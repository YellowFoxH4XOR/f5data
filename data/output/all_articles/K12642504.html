Known Issue

The VIPRION system may erroneously generate a power supply warning message indicating that the voltage is out of range.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP system runs on a VIPRION 2400 chassis.
  • The VIPRION 2400 chassis is using DC power supplies.
  • The input voltage to the DC power supply is within the specification, between 44 and 65 VDC.

Impact

There is no impact. You can safely ignore the errors if the BIG-IP system reports input voltages that are within the specification of 44V and 65V.

Important: Ensure that your data center equipment is supplying the necessary power within the specifications. For more information about the specifications, refer to the VIPRION 2400 manual.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H616055 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Low page.
  • The /var/log/ltm file reports warning messages repeatedly (at an approximately 30-second interval) that appear similar to the following example:

    warning chmand[6495]: 012a0004:4: Voltage on PSU0(53V) is out of range.
    warning chmand[6495]: 012a0004:4: Voltage on PSU1(53V) is out of range.
