Description

After the update, BIG-IP devices may be unable to establish device trust. In addition, you may see the following error:
[McpConnection.cpp:754 getSingle] expected exactly 1 trust_certificate_file_object (got 0)

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • Device Trust

Cause

The devices are missing trust certificates in the /config/filestore/files_d/Common_d/ directory.

Recommended Actions

  1. Verify the content of the /config/filestore/files_d/Common_d/:
    # ls -l /config/filestore/files_d/Common_d/
  2. If the output is missing those two files please jump to third step:

    drwxrwxrwx. 2 root root 4096 2016-12-29 14:40 trust_certificate_d

    drwxrwxrwx. 2 root root 4096 2016-12-29 14:40 trust_certificate_key_d

  3. Remove the none existent trust-domain: 
    tmsh delete /cm trust-domain Root

  4.  

    Restart devmgmt service:
    bigstart restart devmgmtd
