Known Issue

A Session Initiation Protocol (SIP) monitor may fail to retransmit the SIP request and then stop functioning.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • A SIP monitor is configured to monitor SIP pool members.
  • The SIP server fails to respond before the monitor's interval expires.

If the SIP monitor does not receive a response, it should retransmit the UDP SIP request after 0.5, 1, 2, and 4 seconds incrementally (after each retransmission, the interval increases).

Impact

The system may incorrectly reflect the SIP pool member availability status.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H474681 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The SIP pool member status may be incorrectly reflected.
  • The available SIP pool member may remain marked unavailable.
  • The BIG-IP system may attempt to send traffic to an unavailable SIP pool member.
