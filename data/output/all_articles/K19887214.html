Known Issue

When the BIG-IP system performs a SNMP query on the ifXtable object—specified in the interfaces management information base (ifMIB)—the snmpd process stops responding.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The BIG-IP system performs an SNMP query on the ifXTable of the ifMIB.

Impact

The snmpd process stops responding.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The snmpd process stops responding.

Resolution

Status

F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article.
