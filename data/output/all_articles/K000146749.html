K000146749: What is the quota parameter http_loadbalancer.large_idle_timeout?

What Happened?

You want to know the meaning of this parameter in order to keep your quota controlled

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud HTTP Load Balancer 

Resolution/Answer

It is an HTTP Load Balancer with a large IDLE timeout (5 minutes or 300000ms)
The setting is in HTTP Load Balancer > Other Settings > Miscellaneous Options > Idle Timeout.
