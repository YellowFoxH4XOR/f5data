Policy Information

F5 is introducing new Advanced WAF product offerings. The current Advanced WAF listings in public cloud marketplaces will deprecate and become End of Sale (EoS). F5 advises customers migrate to the new product offerings at their earliest convenience, as indicated in the New Advanced WAF product offerings column in the following table.

Current offerings becoming EoSNew Advanced WAF product offerings
F5 Advanced WAF (PAYG, 25Mbps)F5 Adv WAF with LTM, IPI, and TC (PAYG, 25Mbps)
F5 Advanced WAF (PAYG, 200Mbps) F5 Adv WAF with LTM, IPI, and TC (PAYG, 200Mbps)
F5 Advanced WAF (PAYG, 1Gbps)F5 Adv WAF with LTM, IPI, and TC (PAYG, 3Gbps)
F5 Advanced WAF (PAYG, 8vCPU)F5 Adv WAF with LTM, IPI, and TC (PAYG, 3Gbps)

F5 Per-App VE - Advanced WAF (PAYG, 200Mbps)F5 Per-App-VE Adv WAF with LTM+IPI+TC (PAYG, 200M)
F5 Per-App VE - Advanced WAF + LTM (PAYG, 25M)F5 Per-App-VE Adv WAF with LTM+IPI+TC (PAYG, 25M)
F5 Per-App VE - Advanced WAF + LTM (PAYG, 200M)F5 Per-App-VE Adv WAF with LTM+IPI+TC (PAYG, 200M)

The new Advanced WAF offerings are available now. The current offerings will no longer be available to new subscribers beginning April 1, 2021, and will be removed July 1, 2021.

Customers who are currently running instances deployed from these deprecated offers may continue to run their instances. However, they won’t be able to deploy new instances from those deprecated offers after July 1, 2021, after which they can only deploy instances from the new offerings.
