Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If the PVA2 receives a high volume of fragmented traffic, the PVA2 may hang.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR61225, CR68118, and CR61244 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.1.2, 9.2.4, and 9.3. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
