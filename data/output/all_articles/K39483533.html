Known Issue

Standby BIG-IP systems may retain mirrored persistence entries indefinitely.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a virtual server configured with a persistence profile.
  • A mirroring IP address is configured on the BIG-IP system.

Impact

Standby system performance is degraded, and the system may stop responding or restart due to memory exhaustion.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • You observe that, prior to expiration, the age of persistence entries on the standby system resets to 0 and the value is retained indefinitely.
  • You notice the standby system’s performance becomes degraded over time.
  • The standby system stops responding or restarts due to memory exhaustion.
