Topic

In November 2013 (for Virtual Edition (VE) and appliances), and December 2013 (for VIPRION), F5 introduced a tiered licensing model to consolidate purchasing options for its solutions and to significantly reduce customer costs when customers deploy application services together versus individually.

Description

When installing a Good, Better, Best license on a BIG-IP system, you must also provision resources for any modules that you get in addition to the BIG-IP LTM module, which is the base module for all of the Good, Better, Best licenses. Additional modules are part of the Base registration key that F5 provides you when you use the Good, Better, Best licensing schema.

Recommendations

While the aforementioned platforms have the resources available for every module, certain module combinations may require more resources than are available when under load, which may have an impact on the system's traffic handling capabilities. Before provisioning a module, check the Current Resource Allocation section of the Resource Provisioning page in the Configuration utility to verify that you have enough available resources. Additionally, when you plan the scalability of your module usage, F5 recommends that you work with your F5 Sales Associate to properly assess the ability of your F5 platform and the platforms intended use.

Note: For more information about the new F5 licensing options, refer to F5 Perpetual Licensing.
