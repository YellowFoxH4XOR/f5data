Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP LTM OpenSSL filter may not properly identify SSLv2 ciphers during the SSL handshake. As a result, when an iRule attempts to get the SSLv2 cipher name, the TMM process may crash.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR72968 and it was fixed in BIG-IP versions 9.1.3, 9.3.0, and 9.4.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.
