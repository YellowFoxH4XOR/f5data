Description

  • GARPs from a newly active BIGIP may be bridged for a brief time while peer transitions to standby
  • Bridged GARPs cause switches/routers to update CAM/ARP tables with incorrect MAC addresses.
  • Incorrect MAC addresses cause network outages

Environment

  • Viprion chassis with at least two blades.
  • Vlan groups configured in default or non default route domain in /Common or custom partitions


Cause

Known issue caused by ID1144845.


 Recommended Actions

  1. Add all IP address objects configured on the BIGIP to each relevant vlan group proxy exclusion list.
  2. If vlan groups are configured in route domains in custom partitions a hotfix may be needed, see ID1156105.
