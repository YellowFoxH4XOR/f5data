Description

  • When attack signatures are updated in Nginx App Protect what is the staging period?

Environment

  • Nginx App Protect configured
  • Updated Attack Signatures

Cause

Q: When attack signatures are updated in NAP what is the staging period?

A: There is none, they are immediately enforced.


Q: Can the staging period for updated signatures be modified?

A: No but this will considered in a future release - no ETA and no target version can be provided at this time.
