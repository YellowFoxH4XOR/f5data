K000139777: Not receiving ASM signature and Threat Campaigns update notifications

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-05-24
Updated
2025-08-08
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP ASM: 15.1.2
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000139777

Description

Customers are no longer receiving ASM  signature and Threat campaigns update notifications via email even though they are subscribed to email notifications.

Environment

  • BIG-IP ASM
  • BIG-IP ASM VE
  • MYF5.com

Cause

Historically these notifications were available by subscribing to the email notifications found here Subscribing to email notifications regarding F5 products however, to provide customers with immediate, reliable notification when new Signature and Threat Campaigns Updates are available, F5 has transitioned away from email alerts which may be caught by spam filtering or other rules

Recommended Action

Configure and use the built-in Live Update functionality to alert the administrator of Pending available updates. Please see https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K82512024 for more information, including required outbound firewall rules and instructions for configuring the live update system to use a proxy server.
