K000135257: The MAC address field for some devices in the BIG-IQ GUI are not populated

Source

Description

The MAC address field for some devices in the BIG-IQ GUI are not populated.


Environment

  • BIG-IQ CM
  • Non-managed BIG-IP devices
  • Managed BIG-IP devices


Cause

Only the MAC address field of managed Big-IPs are not populated. That is the default behavior. MAC address will only be populated for non-managed Big-IP.



Recommended Actions

None. Expected behavior



Additional Information

You may notice this behavior after re-activating the license on a formerly non-managed BIG-IP device that has become managed. Under this circumstance it is expected that the MAC address field will become unpopulated.
