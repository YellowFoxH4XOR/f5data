Description

The device reboots unexpectedly.

  • The tmm logs show HSB reset errors, "HSB XLMAC phy reset due to fcs errors"
  • The LTM logs show "Link: 9.1 is DOWN". This link is internal interface between switch and HSB.

Environment

  • High Speed Bridge (HSB)
  • Reboot

Cause

This issue may be the result of a traffic pattern the Big-IP is processing. This reset behavior was introduced in v12.1.3/13.1.0 and is tracked as Bug ID 748443. The device attempts to automatically recover by resetting its HSB. If those attempts are unsuccessful, it automatically reboots. Without this reboot, the FCS errors could remain undiagnosed for some time and impact traffic. 

Recommended Actions

If the Big-IP recently rebooted unexpectedly, check for the HSB reset logs in /var/log/tmm or Link 9.1 issues in /var/log/ltm.
