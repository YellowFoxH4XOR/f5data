Description

Users may need to verify how many licenses have been generated using their Strongbox account to ensure they do not exceed their license generation limits. The Strongbox website does not provide a direct interface to view the total count of generated licenses, which can cause concern about potentially exceeding the maximum allowed license generations (typically 24 licenses per Strongbox serial number).

Environment

Strongbox licensing system
Number of licenses

Cause

The Strongbox portal does not currently display a counter or summary showing the total number of licenses generated for a given account or serial number.

Recommended Actions

To obtain information about the number of licenses generated with your Strongbox account, contact F5 Strongbox Support via email at StrongBox@f5.com with the following information:

  •   Last Name
  •   First Name
  •   Email (no group email addresses)
  •   Country Code
  •   Phone Number
  •   Strongbox Serial Number

F5 Strongbox Support will provide you with the license generation count for your Strongbox account.

Additional Information

Strongbox accounts typically have a limit of 24 license generations per serial number. Monitoring your license generation usage helps prevent exceeding this limit and ensures proper license management for your F5 products.
