Topic

This article explains how to use Client Certificate Constrained Delegation (C3D) for inbound traffic use cases with BIG-IP SSL Orchestrator.

Description

C3D lets you perform client authentication tasks and mutual authentication in an end-to-end TLS handshake. You can configure C3D with BIG-IP SSL Orchestrator for the inbound-traffic use case with BIG-IP SSL Orchestrator.

Inbound traffic use case with BIG-IP SSL Orchestrator

You may require mutual TLS authentication and inbound inspection of traffic for servers in your internal network protected by BIG-IP SSL Orchestrator. When you configure BIG-IP SSL Orchestrator for C3D, you enable decryption of SSL traffic on the BIG-IP system, allowing you to inspect for content such as malware. To do so, refer to SSL Orchestrator Advanced Use Cases: Client Certificate Constrained Delegation (C3D) Support on DevCentral.

Outbound traffic use case with BIG-IP SSL Orchestrator

SSL Orchestrator does not support C3D integration for outbound (forward proxy) traffic use cases. C3D creates (forges) a new client certificate from a local CA certificate and private key. For C3D or any such technology to work in a forward proxy, the remote internet hosts need to trust this local certificate authority.
