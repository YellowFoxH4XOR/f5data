Policy Information

F5 Networks announces the end of sales for the SSD (Solid State Drive), effective December 30, 2000.

The primary use for the SSD was by request for an appliance with "no moving parts". The assumption was that standard IDE drives were less reliable than the SSD technology. F5 Networks' direct experience is that IDE drives have proven to be considerably more reliable than SSD controllers. Greater than 50% of SSD controllers have been returned to manufacturing (RMA) due to reliability issues compared with less than 1% for IDE drives. In addition, the operating environment and reliability is virtually the same for both media types as shown by the following technical information obtained directly from the IDE and SSD manufacturers.

One of the drives that currently ship in BIG-IP is the Seagate ST-315320-A. This specific drive and all other drives in production have the following operating environment characteristics.

  • 500,000 hours Mean Time Between Failure (MTBF)
  • 63 Gs operating shock
  • Ambient operating temperature of 0° to 55°C (32° to 131°F)

For a complete listing of the operational specifications please follow this link: http://www.seagate.com/support/disc/manuals/ata/vailpmb.pdf

The Lexar Media PC card (160mb) is the optional Solid State drive. The operating environment characteristics for this SSD are:

  • 500,000 hours Mean Time Between Failure MTBF
  • 50 Gs operating shock
  • Ambient operating temperature of 0° to 60°C

For a complete listing of the operational specifications refer to the following link: http://www.digitalfilm.com/products/pc_main.html

The operating environment and reliability is virtually the same for both media types.

SSD was placed on an indefinite hold back in June because of significant reliability issues. SSD, part number F5-SS-HD will be removed from the F5 Networks price list on December 15th.

Current SSD Service Customers

Current service contracts for systems with SSD will be honored by F5 Networks. At the end of contract term, the user should contact F5 support to maintain continuous support. In the event that an SSD unit fails it will not be replaced with identical hardware. The BIG-IP controller in question should be returned to manufacturing through the standard process and will be replaced with an IDE based replacement unit that will be functionally equal to or better than the returned unit.

For more information or sales assistance, contact F5 Networks product management or your local Sales representative.
