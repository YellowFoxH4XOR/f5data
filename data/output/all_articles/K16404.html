Issue

Old Behavior

Prior to October 2013, the following platform types were shipped with 600 GB hard drives:

  • BIG-IP 11000 (E101)
  • BIG-IP 11050 (E102)
  • BIG-IP 11050F (E102)
  • BIG-IP 11050N (E103)
  • VIPRION PB300 Blade (A108)
  • VIPRION PB300N Blade (A110)

New Behavior

Due to product availability, F5 no longer ships the BIG-IP 11000 series platforms or VIPRION PB300 series blades with 600-gigabyte (GB) hard disk drives. As of October 2013, new BIG-IP 11000 series platforms and VIPRION PB300 series blades include 1-terabyte (TB) hard disk drives.

For example, the following fdisk -l command output illustrates an 11050 platform with the 1-TB hard disk drives:

Impact

If a BIG-IP 11000 series platforms or VIPRION PB300 series blade is configured with RAID and has had one drive replaced with the larger drive (for example, as the result of an RMA), the array size should equal the smallest drive in the array. For example, if the 600-GB drive and the 1-TB drive is installed in the same device, the drives appear as a 600-GB RAID pair.

If two BIG-IP 11000 series platforms or VIPRION PB300 series blades are configured as a high-availability pair, and one member of the pair has 1-TB drives and the other has the older 600-GB drives, the high-availability and failover functionality of the pair is unaffected by the disparity in drive sizes (excluding disk-space specific considerations, such as storage of log archives). The new drive does not negatively affect the functionality, performance, or cosmetic finish of the noted platforms.
