What Happened?

  • What is burst multiplier?

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud (XC)
  • HTTP Load Balancer

Resolution/Answer

The burst multiplier setting allows you to define a multiplier on the configured Rate Limit.

The main objective of the burst multiplier is to help with sudden increases in requests and prevent unintended blocks, as the Rate Limit may be reached it gives a margin for a burst in requests.

 Additional Information

Example:

  • Rate Limit: 10
  • Period: Per Second
  • Burst Multiplier: 10

Rate Limit * Burst Multiplier = 100 requests per second allowed

By setting the Burst Multiplier to 10, the system allows up to 100 requests per second during a burst, based on a base rate of 10 requests/second. F5 XC tracks requests at millisecond granularity, enabling precise enforcement. During a burst, requests may be accepted rapidly until the burst capacity is exhausted. Afterward, the system enforces the base rate of 1 request every 100 milliseconds. Requests exceeding this rate will be denied.
