Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The FirePass Webtop doesn't show logo and banner by default check box is a master group setting located under the User Experience tab on the Users: Groups: Master Groups page. However, when you update this setting for one master group, FirePass incorrectly implements the new setting for all master groups.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR72340 and it was fixed in FirePass 6.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the FirePass release notes.
