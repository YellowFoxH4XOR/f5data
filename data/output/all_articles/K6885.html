Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) loses connectivity with MCP, the pvad process may crash while clearing the configuration.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR68540 and CR68453 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM versions 9.2.5, 9.3, and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
