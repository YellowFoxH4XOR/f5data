Description

Pools with FQDN nodes may intermittently not be populated despite successful DNS queries.

This results in a pool that appears to be down or offline and can disrupt traffic.

FQDN nodes may also show No records returned.

Environment

  • FQDN node
  • Multiple FQDNs that resolve to the same A record

Cause

Issue may be caused by ID860005

Recommended Actions

Upgrade to a version where ID860005 is fixed, or request an engineering hotfix from F5 Technical Support.
Note: Refer to related content for additional information about this issue.

Additional Information

FQDN nodes are only populated by A or AAAA record responses. The BIG-IP will not recursively resolve requests, for example if a CNAME is returned to the initial query.
