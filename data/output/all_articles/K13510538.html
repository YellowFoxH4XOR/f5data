Description

Traffic is sent to pool members in priority groups which should not be active.

Environment

  • Priority Group Activation
  • Small amount of long-lived application connections

Cause

In an environment with a low amount of connections, a lower priority pool member may remain in the active priority group longer than the Slow Ramp Time setting indicates.

Refer to the following article for more information about this issue and Slow Ramp Time:

K14804: Overview of the Slow Ramp Time pool setting

Recommended Actions

In low connection environments, you may need to avoid the combination of Priority Group Activation and Slow Ramp Time.

To resolve the immediate issue, set Slow Ramp Time to 0 make a change to the pool member in the lower priority group, to ensure it is marked offline. For example, temporarily apply a monitor that will fail.
