Description

You may observe the warning message 'Unable to Notify Tenant' is logged every 10 seconds in /var/log/ltm.

Environment

  • You installed the BIG-IP 15.1.8 as a tenant on CX410 or rSeries platforms.
  • BIG-IP F5OS

Cause

ID 1182613

 Recommended Actions

There is no functional impact for those warning messages, but /var/log/ltm will get filled with the message and consumes disk space.
An Engineering Hotfix is available now, and you can submit the support case for requesting the engineer hot fix for ID1182613.
