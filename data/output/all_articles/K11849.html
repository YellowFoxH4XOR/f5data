Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The USB driver module may fail to load properly on the BIG-IP 1600, 3600, and 3900 platforms. As a result, the /dev/ttyUSB0 device is not available when USB devices such as the LCD panel and indicator LEDs attempt to use it.

When this issue occurs, the BIG-IP system may experience one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The front panel control buttons do not function (with one exception: if you hold down the X key, the unit shuts down).
  • The LCD front panel fails to display the full menu and may instead display the following:

    F5 Networks
    Hold the x key to
    turn power off

  • The indicator LED lights may not operate as expected.

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 328288 (formerly CR138016) to this issue, and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 10.2.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP release notes.

Note: F5 previously reported that this issue had been fixed in BIG-IP versions 10.1.0-HF2 and 10.2.0. However, F5 has determined that the fix was ineffective and has reopened the ID.
