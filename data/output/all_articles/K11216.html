Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The bigd process, which is responsible for the health checks of nodes and pool members, is not compatible with IPv6 link-local unicast addresses. As a result, the BIG-IP system will mark nodes and pool members configured with IPv6 link-local unicast addresses as down. IPv6 global unicast addresses and unique local unicast addresses are unaffected.

IPv6 link-local unicast addresses are network addresses that are only intended for communications within one segment of a local network. IPv6 hosts are required to automatically assign each of their interfaces a unique link-local unicast address based on the MAC address of the interface. An IPv6 link-local unicast address has the FE80::/10 prefix. IPv6 unique local unicast addresses are intended for use in local communications and are globally unique, but they are not routable on the global Internet. IPv6 unique local unicast addressing scheme is defined by RFC4193 and has the FC00::/7 prefix.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as ID 333340 (formerly CR111992).

Resolution

Workaround

You can avoid this issue by not configuring nodes or pool members using IPv6 link-local unicast addresses; instead use IPv6 global unicast addresses or IPv6 unique local unicast addresses.
