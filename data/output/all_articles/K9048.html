Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Generating a SIGINT or SIGQUIT signal on the serial console during login causes all system daemons to shut down and restart. As a result, the BIG-IP system becomes inoperative.

For example, if you connect a serial terminal to the BIG-IP serial console, and generate one of the following signals while the password prompt is displayed, the runsvdir daemon and all system daemons shut down and restart:

SIGINT (Ctrl-C to generate a SIGINT signal)
SIGQUIT (Ctrl-4 to generate a SIGQUIT signal)

Note: Sending the SIGQUIT signal on the serial console during login may also cause the chmand process to continually attempt to restart once per second. If this behavior occurs, you must reboot the BIG-IP system.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR99065 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 9.4.7. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, ASM, GTM, Link Controller, PSM, or WebAccelerator release notes.

This issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-9.3.1-HF5 issued for BIG-IP version 9.3.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
