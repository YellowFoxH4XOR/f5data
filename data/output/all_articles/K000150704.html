K000150704: Velos - All pool members flapping on Tenant after upgrade to 17.1.2.1

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-04-03
Updated
2025-09-12
Applies to
  • F5OS
  • F5OS-C: 1.8.X, 1.8.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000150704

Description

  • When upgrading the VELOS chassis to F5OS-C v1.8.0 and the tenant to BIG-IP v17.1.2.1, all pools were observed to flap, resulting in service disruptions.
  • ltm logs indicated errors such as "No members available for pool".
  • Packet captures show TCP SYN messages without corresponding TCP SYN/ACK responses.

Environment

  • F5OS-C v1.8.0
  • Velos
  • Tenant v17.1.2.1
  • Monitor flapping

Cause

Seen behaviour is the result of a known issue tracked with the Bug ID1818185.

Recommended Actions

As a workaround, you can reboot the blades once the partition upgrade to v1.8.0 has been completed and all the blades are online.

Additional Information

None
