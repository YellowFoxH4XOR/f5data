Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP GTM gtmd process may take up to 390 MB of RAM when persistence is enabled, and it may take up to 45 percent of the CPU when expiring persistence records.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR113610 and it was fixed in version 10.0.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM release notes.

Note: This issue still exists within the 9.4.x software branch.

This issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-9.3.1-81.1-HF8 issued for BIG-IP version 9.3.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
