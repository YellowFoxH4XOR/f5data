Known Issue

An incorrect power supply status may be reported after you remove the D113 platform's number 2 power supply.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have one of following the BIG-IP D113 platforms: 10000, 10200, 10050, 10250, 10055, 10255, 10150 NEBs, 12250.
  • You have removed the number 2 power supply.
  • You use either tmctl or system_check commands to view power supply status.

Impact

You can safely ignore this issue. When you view the power supply status, power supply number 2 appears to be installed.

Note: The erroneous status information does not indicate a problem with the hardware.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When you use the following system_check command, power supply 2 appears to be installed when you view the STATUS:

    system_check -d | grep "Chassis power supply"
    Chassis power supply 1: status FAN=good; VINPUT=good; VOUTPUT=good; STATUS=good
    Chassis power supply 2: status FAN=good; VINPUT=good; VOUTPUT=good; STATUS=good

  • When you use the following tmctl command, power supply 2 appears to be installed when you view the input_status and output_status:

    tmctl chassis_power_supply_status_stat
    name index status input_status output_status fan_status current_status
    ---- ----- ------ ------------ ------------- ---------- --------------
    pwr1 1 1 1 1 1 1
    pwr2 2 2 1 1 2 0
