K000137425: BIG-IP may not respond to IPv6 Neighbor Solicitation from a link-local address outside the fe80::/64 range

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-10-31
Updated
2023-11-09
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 15.1.8
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000137425

Description

The BIG-IP device may not respond to IPv6 Neighbor Solicitation from a link-local address outside the fe80::/64 range


Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • IPv6

Cause

According to RFC 4291 under section 2.5.6, link-local addresses should have the following format:


  | 10 bits  | 54 bits     |     64 bits   |

 +-----------+-------------+----------------+

  | 1111111010 |      0  |    interface ID   |

 +-----------+-------------+----------------+

 

Thus, only fe80::/64 may be used. The BIG-IP will only accept addresses in the fe80::/64 as valid link-local addresses.


Recommended Actions

Please ensure you are using valid link-local addresses (that fall under the fe80::/64 range).


Additional Information

https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc4291#section-2.5.6
