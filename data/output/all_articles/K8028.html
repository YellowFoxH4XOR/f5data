Issue

When using non-F5 or unauthorized SFP optical transceivers during the PVAx and I2C tests, the End-User Diagnostics (EUD) may report the following error message:

The PVA2 DRAM appears to be bad!

Completed 1 passes with 1 errors

You can prevent the EUD from generating possible false errors by removing all SFP transceivers, and running the EUD utility again. Some SFP transceivers can be incompatible because they respond to I2C addresses outside the SFP-MSA specifications for those devices. As a result, these addresses cause communication loss from conflicts with other devices on the F5 system.
