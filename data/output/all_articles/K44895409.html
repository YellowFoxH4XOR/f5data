Known Issue

In some circumstances, inconsistencies in the contents of the BIG-IP hardware store busy (HSB) synchronous random access memory (SRAM) may not trigger a failover to the standby host.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You are running ePVA accelerated FastL4 traffic

Impact

ePVA accelerated traffic may drop.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You may see errors with a format similar to the following in the /var/log/tmm* files:

    Device error: hsb_lbb* tre2_crc_errs count *

    For example:

    Device error: hsb_lbb0 tre2_crc_errs count 249

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 725791 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. Workaround

To work around this issue, you can disable hardware accelerated ePVA . To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: There may be a small network performance impact from this change. 

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility as an administrative user.
  2. Navigate to Local Traffic > Profiles > Protocol > FastL4.
  3. Click the FastL4 profile associated with your virtual server
  4. Under PVA Acceleration, click None.
  5. Click Update.
For more information on ePVA and compatible F5 platforms, refer to K09948701: Overview of the FastL4 profile and K12837: Overview of the ePVA feature.
