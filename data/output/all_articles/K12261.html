Topic

FirePass, BIG-IP Edge Gateway, and BIG-IP APM systems do not currently support the Opera browser, although some features may work with this browser. F5 Product Management continues to monitor the browser market and may consider adding Opera support to future releases of FirePass, BIG-IP Edge Gateway, and BIG-IP APM.

If you have an immediate need for the Opera browser to be supported by FirePass, BIG-IP Edge Gateway, or BIG-IP APM, contact your local F5 Sales Representative. Given that this is not a supported browser today, do not contact F5 Technical Support for issues involving the Opera browser. As our Product Management and Product Development organizations learn more about this browser and determine our long-term plans concerning this browser, F5 will update this article.
