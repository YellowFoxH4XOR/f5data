Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When using the Quality of Service (QoS) or Topology load balancing methods, the BIG-IP GTM system assigns scores to wide IP pool members and makes load balancing decisions based on the assigned scores. When multiple pool members have equal scores and the pools are using the QOS or Topology load balancing methods, the traffic distribution is heavily concentrated on the first few members in the pool.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR71556 and CR71555 and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.3.0 and 9.4.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM Release Notes.
