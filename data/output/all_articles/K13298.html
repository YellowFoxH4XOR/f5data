Known Issue

The version of ssldump installed on the BIG-IP system cannot decrypt packet captures of sessions encrypted using the TLS 1.2 protocol.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • A packet capture contains an encrypted session.
  • The session is encrypted with the TLS 1.2 protocol.

Impact

Application data cannot be decrypted by ssldump.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • When decrypting a packet capture file with ssldump, the system does not display the encrypted data in clear text, but the data is denoted as application_data, and it appears similar to the following example:

    5 8 0.1024 (0.0000) C>SV3.3(1) ChangeCipherSpec
    5 9 0.1024 (0.0000) C>SV3.3(32) Handshake
    5 10 0.1055 (0.0030) S>CV3.3(1) ChangeCipherSpec
    5 11 0.1055 (0.0000) S>CV3.3(32) Handshake
    5 12 2.8030 (2.6975) C>SV3.3(22) application_data
    5 13 2.8055 (0.0025) S>CV3.3(5059) application_data
