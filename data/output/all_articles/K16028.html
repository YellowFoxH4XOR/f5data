Known Issue

Power supply information and status may be incorrectly reported by certain platforms.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • After the BIG-IP system has booted, you add or remove a power supply.
  • Your BIG-IP system uses one of the following platforms:

    • BIG-IP 2000 series (C112)
    • BIG-IP 4000 series (C113)
    • BIG-IP 5000 series (C109)
    • BIG-IP 7000 series (D110)
    • BIG-IP 10000 series (D113)

    Impact

    If a power supply is added or removed after the BIG-IP system is already running, the tmsh show sys hardware command displays outdated power supply information.

    Symptoms

    As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

    • The power supply information that displays in the output of the tmsh show sys hardware command reflects only the power supplies that were present when the system started up.
