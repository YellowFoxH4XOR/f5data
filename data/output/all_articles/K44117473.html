Known Issue

IPsec child Security Associations (SA) fail when you use Elliptic Curve (ECP) Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS).

This issue occurs when all of following conditions are met:

  • You have configured IPsec tunnels to use the Internet Key Exchange version 2 (IKEv2) protocol.
  • You have enabled ECP PFS in an IPsec policy.
  • You have configured ECP PFS in an IPSec IKE peer configuration.
  • A child SA expires or is deleted.

Impact

After the first child SA expires or is deleted, the IKEv2 tunnel goes down and another SA cannot be negotiated.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When you attempt to create an IPsec policy entry with ECP PFS, you observe an error message similar to the following example in the /var/log/ltm log file and in the Configuration utility:

    01070734:3: Configuration error: ECP Group for perfect forward secrecy is not supported

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can use Modular Exponential (MODP) instead of ECP. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround:  Performing the following work around should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility as the admin user.
  2. Navigate to Network > IPsec > IPsec Policies.
  3. Click the relevant IPsec policy.
  4. Under Perfect Forward Secrecy, click the MODP encryption of your choice.
  5. Click Update.
  6. Navigate to Network > IPsec > IKE Peers.
  7. Select the relevant IKE peer.
  8. Under Perfect Forward Secrecy, click the same MODP encryption as in step 4.
  9. Click Update.
