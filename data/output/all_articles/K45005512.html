Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may send extraneous Open Shortest Path First (OSPF) retransmissions and ospfTxRetransmit traps.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured the OSPF routing protocol.
  • You have configured the BIG-IP system to send SNMP traps.
  • OSPF instability/networking flaps are occurring.

Note: The more routes that are flapping, the more likely you are to observe this condition.

Impact

There is no impact on the OSPF processing itself. The additional traffic does not cause failing adjacencies or loss of routing information.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When you use OSPF with a high load and network recalculation, you could encounter a race condition that could lead to the system sending out additional OSPF retransmissions. This might also cause the system to send SNMP traps, if they are configured on the system.
  • The additional OSPF retransmissions may cause the system to send additional SNMP OSPF-related traps, which might cause additional load on the external network monitoring system.
