Description

After issuing a show /sys log command, a failover occurs, with logs similar to the following in /var/log/ltm:

HA daemon_heartbeat mcpd fails action is restart all

Environment

  • tmsh show /sys log

Cause

ID622619

Recommended Actions

Upgrade to a version where bug ID622619 is fixed.

Additionally, avoid excessive use of local logging, as this leads to large log files and may cause general system instability. If large amounts of logging are required, consider configuring High Speed Logging.
