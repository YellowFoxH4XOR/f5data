Description

An AFM Policy not syncing between devices, even though they may display In Sync


Environment

  • BIG-IP AFM
  • AFM Firewall Policy
  • Non-floating Self-IP with AFM Firewall Policy applied

Cause

A behavior change in 11.6.0 prevents policies applied to non-floating Self-IPs from syncing between devices.

Note: See Behavior Changes in BIG-IP AFM 11.6.0 Release Note in Related Content for more information.
 

Recommended Actions

Create and manage firewall policies for non-floating Self-IPs separately from those used for floating Self-IPs.

Please note that if the policy is assigned to a non-floating Self-IP and then reassigned to a floating Self-IP the policy will not sync, in that case, generate a new policy and assign it directly to a floating Self-IP.
