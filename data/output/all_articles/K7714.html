Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The internal trunk ports on the BIG-IP system connect the switch subsystem and the PVA ASIC chip to the host motherboard. The BIG-IP LTM system uses the internal trunk ports to pass load-balanced traffic from the switch subsystem to the host motherboard. By default, the BIG-IP LTM system uses an internal trunk distribution hash based on TCP or UDP source and destination ports. The BIG-IP 6800 platform does not support UDP port-based hashing.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR65570, and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.1.3, 9.3, and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
