Topic

You should consider using this procedure under the following condition:

  • You want to manually assign the correct peer relationship when the Enterprise Manager incorrectly associates the peers.

Description

During the device discovery process, the Enterprise Manager looks at the failover addresses configured on the device as well as their corresponding MAC addresses. The Enterprise Manager uses this information to determine the peer associations between a high availability pair. If two HA pairs that are managed by the Enterprise Manager have failover addresses that are identical, the Enterprise Manager may incorrectly associate a peer from one HA pair to a peer in another HA pair.

Note: It is possible for managed devices to have the same failover addresses in multiple private networks.

Consider the following example. The Enterprise Manager manages bigip1, bigip2, bigip3 and bigip4. The managed devices bigip1 and bigip2 are configured as an HA pair, while bigip3 and bigip4 are configured as another HA pair. The managed devices are configured with the following management and failover addresses.

Managed deviceManagement addressFailover address
bigip1172.28.22.151192.168.10.1
bigip2172.28.22.152192.168.10.2
bigip3172.28.22.153192.168.10.1
bigip4172.28.22.154192.168.10.2

In this scenario, the Enterprise Manager may incorrectly associate bigip1 with bigip4 as an HA pair and bigip2 with bigip3 as another HA pair.

When the incorrect peer association occurs, the Enterprise Manager 2.3.0 HF2 and later will report the affected managed devices as having Multiple Peers before providing the option to manually associate a peer on the managed device properties page. In earlier versions of Enterprise Manager, if the two HA pairs are running different BIG-IP versions, the Enterprise Manager will report the affected managed devices as Peer device version mismatch.

If you encounter this behavior on your Enterprise Manager, you can rectify the incorrect peer association by modifying the Peer Address of the affected managed devices and replacing it with the appropriate peer management IP address.

Prerequisites

You must meet the following prerequisite to use this procedure:

  • Administrative access to the Enterprise Manager

Procedures

To manually assign the peer, perform the following procedure:

Note: For Enterprise Manager 2.3.0 HF2 and later, the option to manually associate a peer will only appear on the managed device properties page after the Enterprise Manager system has determined that an incorrect peer association has occurred.

Impact of procedure: None.

  1. Log in to the Enterprise Manager Configuration utility.
  2. Click Enterprise Management Devices.
  3. Click Device List.
  4. Click on the affected managed device.
  5. Navigate to the Peer Properties section under the managed device's Properties page.
  6. In the Peer Address box, enter the management IP address of the correct peer.
  7. To save the modification, click Save Changes.
  8. Repeat steps 2 to 6 for the affected peer device.
