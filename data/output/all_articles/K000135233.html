Description

Inspecting ltm log we can observe:

 

# egrep 'iAppsLX_save_pre' var/log/ltm | more

 

May 13 10:03:54 [hostname] err .iAppsLX_save_pre[3682]: Failed task: /shared/iapp/build-package/b1f8d7a8-6378-4cad-a95f-41e43b5dd6f7: Failed to wait for restnoded rpm-spec-creator: java.util.concurrent.Tim

TimeoutException: Unavailable: [http://] localhost:8100/mgmt/shared/iapp/rpm-spec-creator

May 13 10:03:54 [hostname] err iAppsLX_save_pre[3682]: Failed to get getRPM build response within timeout for f5-iappslx-access-aad-apps

May 13 10:03:54 [hostname] info .iAppsLX_save_pre[3682]: Exporting: f5-iappslx-waf-app-protection - /var/config/rest/iapps/f5-iappslx-waf-app-protection

 

Environment

Installed iApps on BIG-IP

Cause

Known issue ID1303197 might cause restjavad task not to complete.

Recommended Actions

Possible workaround is to restart restnoded + restjavad daemons:

 

# bigstart restart restjavad restnoded
