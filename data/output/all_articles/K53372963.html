Known Issue

iOS BIG-IP Edge Client users may be unable to establish per-app Virtual Private Network (VPN) connections.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • iOS BIG-IP Edge Client users attempt to establish a per-app VPN access session with a BIG-IP APM access profile.
  • The access policy of the access profile is configured with the On-Demand Cert Auth agent.
  • The access policy does not include an assigned webtop resource.

Impact

You are unable to establish an access session.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • iOS BIG-IP Edge Client users attempting to establish an access session receive a TCP reset from the BIG-IP APM virtual server.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 456927 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:

Type of FixFixes Introduced InRelated Articles
ReleaseNoneNone
HotfixNoneNone

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can modify the access policy to have an assigned webtop and a webtop resource. For example, you can assign a full webtop and a network access resource to the access policy.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
