Known Issue

The Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) may allow configuration of conflicting failover settings.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You use the tmsh utility to configure or modify a traffic group failover configuration.
  • You create or modify the traffic group to use auto-failback and a high availability (HA) group configuration.

The tmsh utility incorrectly allows a user to configure two mutually exclusive failover methods, auto-failback and an HA group, concurrently without warning.

Impact

Failover will use the configured HA group method.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • After a failover event, auto-failback does not occur.

Workaround

To work around this issue, do not configure auto-failback with an HA group configuration. For information, refer to K15067: The Auto Failback setting should be disabled when an HA group is configured.
