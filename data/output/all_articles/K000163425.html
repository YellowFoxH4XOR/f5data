K000163425: DNS Express Missing Records After IXFR from AD-Integrated DNS Servers

Type
Support Solution
Published

Updated
2026-09-30
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP DNS: 17.5.1
Source

Description

BIG-IP DNS may contain incomplete DNS Express zone data after an apparently successful IXFR from an AD-integrated authoritative DNS source. Records present in the authoritative zone and available through AXFR may be absent from dnsxdump until a full zone refresh occurs.

 

Environment

  • BIG-IP DNS with DNS Express enabled
  • AD-integrated authoritative DNS servers
  • NOTIFY-driven zone updates
  • IXFR processing enabled
  • Complete zone data available through AXFR
  • Multiple authoritative DNS servers providing zone transfers

 

Cause

An AD-integrated authoritative DNS server may omit changes received through directory replication from its local IXFR journal. The records remain present in the zone and are included in AXFR, but may be absent from the IXFR response provided to DNS Express. Consequently, DNS Express can retain incomplete zone data even though the IXFR transfer is logged as successful. A full AXFR refresh loads the complete zone.

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

The missing records are not lost during IXFR rather they were not even initiated/included from the authoritative DC server's zone transfers since the changes are provided from AD integrated server and was not included in IXFR. So the recommended action for this set up will be to force a full transfer.
