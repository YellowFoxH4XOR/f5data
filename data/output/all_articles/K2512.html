Known Issue



F5 Networks has identified a memory timing issue on the BIG-IP 2000, 5000, 1000, 2400, 5100, and 5110 units that were shipped before March 8th, 2003. This issue affects approximately 1% of all systems. There is currently no software fix for this issue.

You may experience one or more of the following symptoms on your BIG-IP 2000, 5000, 1000, 2400, 5100, or 5110 systems:

Note: The BIG-IP 2000, 5000, 1000, 2400, 5100, and 5110 share a common motherboard. These models of BIG-IP are also known as the D45, D50, D39, D44, and D51 respectively.

Primary symptom:

  • System panics and core dump fails and I receive the following error message:

    • (cpu-0): page fault

Possible secondary symptoms:

  • System hangs at various places during boot sequence.

  • I receive a core dump and the following error message:

    uid 0: exited on signal 11

  • System panics and core dump fails and I receive the following error message:

    (cpu-0): init not found

Contact F5 Networks Technical Support if you are experiencing this problem.
