Known Issue

A BIG-IP device group member may report a 'Not All Devices Synced' status after a reboot.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • A change is successfully synchronized from a device group member (for example, BIG-IP-1) to other devices in the device group.
  • The device that initiated the synchronization operation (BIG-IP-1) is rebooted.

Impact

Attempts to initiate a configuration synchronization (ConfigSync) operation fail.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • A rebooted device reports a 'Not All Devices Synced' synchronization status.
  • Other device group members report an 'In Sync' synchronization status.
  • Attempts to initiate a ConfigSync operation fail.

Workaround

To recover from this issue, you can make a change (or click the Update button for an object) on either device and then attempt to perform a ConfigSync. For example, navigate to a local traffic object (such as a pool) using the Configuration utility, and click the Update button. The Sync status should change and allow you to perform a ConfigSync operation.
