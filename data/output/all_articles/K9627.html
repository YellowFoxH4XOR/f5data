Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Running the qkview utility with a verbose level setting of 175 or greater causes the bigpipe vlan fdb show command to be executed. As a result, a switchboard failsafe outage may occur on SCCP enabled platforms.

Note: For more information, refer to K3454: Overview of the SCCP.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR114525 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 9.4.7. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, ASM, GTM, Link Controller, PSM, or WebAccelerator Release Notes.
