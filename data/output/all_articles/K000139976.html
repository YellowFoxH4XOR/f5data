Description

During failover attempt in Azure Cloud, you can notice that traffic was not redirected to previously standby BIG-IP.

After reviewing logs in var/log/restnoded/restnoded.log , you can observe following logs:

Wed, 29 May 2024 18:20:38 GMT - finest: [f5-cloud-failover] Listing Storage Keys
Wed, 29 May 2024 18:20:39 GMT - finest: [f5-cloud-failover] Storage Account Information:  storageaccountname
Wed, 29 May 2024 18:20:39 GMT - finest: [f5-cloud-failover] Creating storage account container
Wed, 29 May 2024 18:20:39 GMT - finest: [f5-cloud-failover] Function error, retrying: Forbidden Retries left: 50
and:

Wed, 29 May 2024 18:24:54 GMT - severe: [f5-cloud-failover] Failover initialization failed: Forbidden StorageError: Forbidden

Environment

  • BIG-IP

  • Azure Cloud

Cause

There is an issue with accessing storage account.

Recommended Actions

Verify if storage account listed in CFE declaration exists, and if it is accessible for BIG-IP.

Steps to verify current CFE declaration are listed in below article: 

CFE Troubleshooting on F5 Cloud Docs
