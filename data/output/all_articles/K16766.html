Known Issue

The Client SSL connection is not forwarded to the destination when the SSL session is reused.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP PEM system is configured with a classification on the client SSL traffic.
  • The incoming client SSL connection re-uses an SSL session.

    Note: The default local traffic policy _sys_CEC_SSL_client_policy has SSL session reuse feature enabled by default.

Impact

The affected client SSL traffic will either be interrupted or dropped.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The affected client SSL connection is not forwarded to the destination server.
  • The client may experience the connection being interrupted or dropped.
