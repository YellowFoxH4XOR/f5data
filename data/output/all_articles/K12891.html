Topic

Many organizations require users to provide some kind of consent before being allowed to login to a corporate resource. The consent prompts are commonly called legal disclaimers or banners. You can require your users to agree to specific terms before they are prompted for their login credentials. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Creating a disclaimer page

  1. Log in to the FirePass Administrative Console.
  2. Navigate to Users: Endpoint Security: Pre-Logon Sequence.
  3. In the New Sequence section, enter a name for your disclaimer page in the Create new sequence field.

    For example:

    Disclaimer_Page
  4. From the Based on drop-down menu, select template : Empty.
  5. Click the Create button.
  6. In the Select Sequence to Use section, click the edit link next to the sequence you just created.
  7. Click on the (+) after the Sequence Start box.
  8. Select Decision box in the panel that appears on the right.
  9. Click the Apply Changes button.
  10. Under Inspectors, click on the Decision link.
  11. In the Message Text field, enter the terms you want your users to agree to.

    For example:

    Unauthorized access and use of this system is not permitted and is strictly prohibited by security policies, regulations, state and federal laws.

    UNAUTHORIZED USERS ARE SUBJECT TO CRIMINAL AND CIVIL PENALTIES AS WELL AS COMPANY-INITIATED DISCIPLINARY PROCEEDINGS.
  12. In the Value field next to the Green Icon and Text Type, enter the text that you want users to select to continue the logon process.

    For example:

    I agree
  13. In the Value field next to the Red Icon and Text Type, enter the text that you want users to select to decline consent.

    For example:

    I do not agree
  14. Click the Save button.
  15. Click the Back to console link located in the upper right corner of the Pre-Logon Sequence Editor screen.
  16. Select the Sequence that you created in Step 2.
  17. Click the Apply button.
