Description

You want to extend the disk space on your BIG-IQ VE and wonder where is the limit of this kind of operation. 

Environment

  • BIG-IQ

Cause

About the storage, the limit is 1.9TB. This limit is because of TMOS, which architecutre is responsible for BIG-IP and BIG-IQ system. So no, you can't extend the storage size of 1.9TB.

Recommended Actions

When you calculate how many BIG-IQ VE instances your solution requires based on data storage, it's important to understand how much of that 1.9Tb is available for data storage.
