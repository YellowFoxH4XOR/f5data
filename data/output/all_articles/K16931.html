Known Issue

The BIG-IP system sends the same SSL/TLS alert message for all types of fatal SSL/TLS connection issues.

Impact

Incorrect diagnostic information may prolong troubleshooting SSL/TLS connection failures.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When you view packet captures, the BIG-IP system sends only the SSL/TLS alert message handshake_failure(40).
