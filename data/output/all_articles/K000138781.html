Description

Does Velos/rSeries support UDLD?

 

Environment

  • F5OS-A / F5OS-C
  • rSeries / Velos

 

Cause

  • F5 does not support UDLD. UDLD is Cisco proprietary protocol.

 

Recommended Actions

  • Recommend to disable UDLD on Cisco side, as it may result unexpected behavior.
