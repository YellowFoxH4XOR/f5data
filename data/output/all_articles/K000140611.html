K000140611: DNS response from the BIG-IP contains no answer from the authority "this.name.is.invalid. hostmaster.this.name.is.invalid".

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-08-07
Updated
2024-08-07
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP DNS: 15.1.10
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000140611

Description

DNS response is received from the BIG-IP containing no answer and the authority section shows the response is coming from "this.name.is.invalid. hostmaster.this.name.is.invalid."


Environment

BIG-IP DNS 15.1.10.4

Cause

A zone is automatically generated when a WideIP is created. However, when a WideIP is deleted, the related zone is not deleted.

Recommended Actions

Manually delete the Zone of interest.


1. Log in to the Configuration utility.

2. Navigate to DNS›› Zones: ZoneRunner: Zone List.

3. Mark the checkbox for the zone of interest.

4. Click on Delete button and confirm action.
