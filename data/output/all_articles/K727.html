Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The snmp_dca health monitor is not compatible with the output provided by the Microsoft http_server MIB. The snmp_dca health monitor expects only an integer and the http_server MIB may provide a string.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR27202 and CR41873.
