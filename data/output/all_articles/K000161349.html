K000161349: BIG-IP LTM Tenant Stuck in "TMM Not Ready" State After Reboot Due to internal ARP Storm

Description

After rebooting a BIG-IP LTM tenant on F5OS-C, the standby unit remained in a "TMM not ready" state, with the cluster member showing as unavailable and the GUI displaying a yellow status. The issue persisted across multiple reboots, preventing HA redundancy. Log messages included repeated "Inet port exhaustion" errors and abnormal SEP (Switch Extender Port) mapping entries

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM Tenant
  • F5OS-C
  • Version: 17.5.X
  • Version: 17.1.X

Cause

A race condition in the sdag (Switch DAG) context during TMM startup causes an ARP storm. The sdag context may not be properly dropped, resulting in continuous ARP looping for the peer HA device's IP address. This prevents TMM from reaching a ready state, leaving the tenant in "TMM not ready" status.

for f in tmm[0-9]* ; do     tmctl -w 300 -f ${f} tmm/cmp -s cmp_state,queue_drops,neighbor_update_out,neighbor_update_in,dag_update_out,dag_update_in,state_transition_out,state_transition_in | sed 's/^/'"${f}"': /'; done | sed -n -e 1,2p -e '/^tmm.:  *[0-9]/p'
