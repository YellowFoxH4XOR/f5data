Description

When users access VPN with mobile iOS devices some applications may not work. A packet capture shows the iOS client sends a DNS query through BIG-IP APM and gets a DNS response back, however, after the iOS client receives the DNS resolution, there is no connection attempt seen from the iOS client to reach the application.

Other mobile devices like Android work fine.
 

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM.
  • DNS assigned by split tunneling.
  • iOS client.

Cause

Known bug on iOS confirmed by Apple Support.

Note: This link takes you to a resource outside of AskF5, and it is possible that the document may be removed without our knowledge.

https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/715416


Recommended Actions

Not an F5 issue. Suggest to reach out to Apple Support for more information.
