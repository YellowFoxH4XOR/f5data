Description

After configuring a SIP monitor, you may observe that no traffic is leaving the BIG-IP,
As the part of the issue SIP pool members are marked as down.

Environment
  • SIP monitor is in use
  • UDP mode for SIP monitor is enabled
  • Pool members are in non-default route domain
Cause

ID 781041

Recommended Actions

Upgrade to a known fixed version.
