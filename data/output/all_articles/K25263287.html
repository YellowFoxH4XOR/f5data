Known Issue

In rare circumstances, the embedded Packet Velocity Acceleration (ePVA) chip may attempt to process non-ePVA connflows. Due to this improper internal connflow tracking, the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may stop responding and dump core.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is equipped with an ePVA chip.
  • This issue may occur when you change the virtual server type. For example, you change the virtual server type from FastL4 to standard.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from the TMM restart, and devices configured as a high availability (HA) pair may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP system temporarily fails to process traffic as it recovers from the TMM restart, and devices configured as an HA pair fail over.
  • Your BIG-IP system generates a TMM core file to the /shared/core directory.

Workaround

To mitigate this issue, you can avoid switching server profile types while passing production traffic.

Impact of workaround: Performing this workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
