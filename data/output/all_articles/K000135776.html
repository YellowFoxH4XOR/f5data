K000135776: [F5OS] services are restarting continuously on tenant

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-08-08
Updated
2024-08-28
Applies to
  • F5OS
  • F5OS-A
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000135776

Description

The platform_agent and other processes are restarting continuously on a tenant.

 

Environment

  • F5OS
  • VLAN to tenant
  • Post reboot

 

Cause

Bug ID1183901

 

Recommended Actions

Use VLANs with a maximum of 31 characters. 
Removing VLANs with names that are in excess of 31 characters should stop the crashing.

 

Additional Information
