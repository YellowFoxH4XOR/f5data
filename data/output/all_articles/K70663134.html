Known Issue

The gtmd process may restart due to a race condition.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • A race condition occurs under certain conditions, such as a sync event.

Impact

A connection outage occurs due to the gtmd process producing a core file and restarting.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The gtmd process produces a core file, which causes a SIGSEV due to a race condition.
