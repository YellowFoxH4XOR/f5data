Recommended Actions

The following two links are the most relevant ones but they are more specific to HTTP and will require some customization to meet the requirements in the link from IBM.

1) REVERSE PROXY

This flow from the link looks like a normal virtual server flow where the backend servers are hid behind the bigip.

 

Note: This link takes you to a resource outside of MyF5, and the third party could remove the document without our knowledge.

https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/secure-proxy/6.2.0?topic=architecture-about-reverse-proxy

On BIG-IP, you may achieve it by using a combination of "Rewrite profile", HTML Profile" and "local traffic policy" which allows you to connect to a single IP and do the URI mapping between frontend and the backend. However, this will only support HTTP traffic and not FTP/FSTP.

Reference: https://techdocs.f5.com/en-us/bigip-14-0-0/big-ip-local-traffic-manager-implementations-14-0-0/configuring-the-big-ip-system-as-a-reverse-proxy-server.html

2) FORWARD PROXY

This one requires usage of SWG I-APP and APM module to establish a tunnel and forward the traffic to the INTERNET. Again, it only covers HTTP.
