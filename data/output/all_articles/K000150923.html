Description

I have logs with "rsp_body": "UNKNOWN" or "req_body": "UNKNOWN" and want to know why.

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud HTTP Load Balancer - Logs

Answer/Recommended Actions

Currently we log request bodies for less than 25% of requests, since logging of payloads creates significant networking, management and storage overhead, because bodies may be potentially large and contain sensitive information.

This is the reason why most of web servers don't log payloads of HTTP requests at all or heavily sample it like we do.

This is the reason why some might have it but the majority won't.
