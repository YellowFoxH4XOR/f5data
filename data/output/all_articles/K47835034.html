Known Issue

Responses transmitted from the BIG-IP management interface may be too large to reach the destination.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The maximum transmission unit (MTU) on an intermediate device is less than the MTU on the BIG-IP management interface.
  • The Auto Last Hop feature is enabled on the BIG-IP management interface.

    Note: The auto-lasthop module is automatically loaded at boot time and enabled on the MGMT interface by default.

The Auto Last Hop feature on the BIG-IP management interface ignores the path MTU data. As a result, responses sent over the management interface may have the Do Not Fragment flag and the responses may be too large for the intermediate device to receive them.

Impact

Responses transmitted from the BIG-IP management interface may be too large to reach the client and the connection fails.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You experience connectivity issues to the BIG-IP management interface.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 554506 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. Workaround

None

Although no workaround exists, you can disable Auto Last Hop and configure a default gateway to avoid this issue. For more information about the Auto Last Hop feature, refer to K52592992: Overview of the Auto Last Hop feature on the management interface.
