3. Charter

3.1 Mission Statement

Develop and promote security leadership, awareness, knowledge, vulnerability management, and security readiness to support F5’s commitment to our core values, shareholders, and customers' success.

3.2 Constituency

Depending on the specific role being fulfilled, the constituency is anyone interested in the security of F5 products and services, or the impact thereof on the networks in which they are used.

The F5 SIRT primarily handles emergency security response for incidents for users of F5 products, as well as serving as the point of contact for external parties (such as security researchers) to report possible vulnerabilities in F5's products or services.  The F5 SIRT's scope is global, with the exception of countries blocked by export compliance.  There are no geographic restrictions on our services, aside from export compliance.  The F5 SIRT coordinates with other Security teams within F5 as needed.

3.3 Sponsorship and/or Affiliation 

The F5 SIRT resides in the F5 Global Services organization and ultimately reports to the EVP.

3.4 Authority

The F5 SIRT operates under the auspices of, and with authority delegated by, F5, Inc via the leadership of the EVP.

4. Policies

4.1 Types of Incidents and Level of Support

All incidents are considered normal priority unless they are labeled CRITICAL or EMERGENCY.

4.2 Co-operation, Interaction and Disclosure of Information

While there are legal and ethical restrictions on the flow of information from F5 SIRT, many of which are also outlined in the F5 Privacy Notice, and all of which will be respected, the F5 SIRT acknowledges its indebtedness to, and declares its intention to contribute to, the spirit of cooperation that created the Internet.

Therefore, while appropriate measures will be taken to protect the identity of members of our constituency and members of other sites where necessary, the F5 SIRT will otherwise share information, in compliance with applicable law and customer agreements, freely when this will assist others in resolving or preventing security incidents.
The F5 SIRT supports the Traffic Light Protocol (TLP) and communications tagged with WHITE, GREEN, AMBER, or RED will be handled appropriately. 

4.3 Communication and Authentication

In view of the types of information that the F5 SIRT will likely be dealing with telephone and video conferencing will be considered sufficiently secure to be used.  Unencrypted e-mail will not be considered secure but will be sufficient for the transmission of low-sensitivity data.  If it is necessary to send highly sensitive data by e-mail, the use of PGP is strongly encouraged.  Similarly, encryption should be used for any network file transfers (HTTPS/FTPS).

5. Services

5.1 Incident Response

The F5 SIRT is primarily responsible for responding to security incidents that impact F5’s end user customers.  The F5 SIRT’s scope of responsibility includes all F5 products and services, commercial and open source, which have not yet reached End of Technical Support (EoTS). This includes all legacy acquisition products and brands including, but not limited to, NGINX, Shape Security, Volterra, and Threat Stack.  The F5 SIRT coordinates with other Security teams within F5 as needed.

5.2 Proactive Activities

The F5 SIRT collaborates with multiple external entities, including but not limited to, VINCE, CVE.org, CISA, and FIRST.

6. Incident Reporting Forms

Suspected security vulnerabilities in F5 products, services, or online properties should be reported as per K4602: Overview of the F5 security vulnerability response policy.
