Topic

The leakage current tests performed on platforms manufactured by F5 Networks are defined and conducted in accordance with the IEC 60950 safety standard specific to IT and non-medical equipment. The IEC 60950 safety standard specifies an upper leakage current limit of 3.5mA. The leakage current rating for all F5 Networks platforms is less than 3.5mA.
