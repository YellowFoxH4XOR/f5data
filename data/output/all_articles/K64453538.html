K64453538: Traffic acceleration with CRYPTO::encrypt and CRYPTO::decrypt iRule commands

Description
This article discusses whether traffic processed with CRYPTO::encrypt and CRYPTO::decrypt iRule commands are hardware accelerated or not.

Environment

  • BIG-IP 14.1.0 and above releases
  • iRule commands - CRYPTO::encrypt / CRYPTO::decrypt

Cause
If CRYPTO::encrypt or CRYPTO::decrypt commands are used in an iRule to encrypt or decrypt the data, then the traffic will not be hardware accelerated. It will be handled in the software by TMM process.

Recommended Actions

If there is desire to ensure traffic is always hardware accelerated, that's not possible when making use of the CRYPTO iRule commands.
