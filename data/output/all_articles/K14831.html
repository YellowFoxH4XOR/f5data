Known Issue

BIG-IP hardware compression may process compression requests slower.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are on one of the following BIG-IP platforms:
    • 2000
    • 2200
    • 4000
    • 4200
  • Your BIG-IP system is under heavy compression load.

Impact

A delay may occur before the system compresses the data, potentially causing connections to time-out.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H438051 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • In the /var/log/ltm log file, you might observe an error message similar to the following example:

    01010025:2: Device error: (null) Watchdog on unit 1!

  • You might see connection timeouts on connections where compression is taking place.
  • The tmctl compress command shows high values for cur_enqueued for the qa-dc provider, and the value either remains relatively static, increases, or decreases slowly.
