K2615: Reasons why changes to a router statement may not be retained

Topic

3-DNS router objects do not accept a user-specified prober. To ensure their relevance, 3-DNS must obtain metrics for router objects from within the router's data center.

The principal 3-DNS Controller in the synchronization group will assign a device in the data center to probe a router. The 3-DNS Controller ignores changes to the prober definition in the wideip.conf file.
