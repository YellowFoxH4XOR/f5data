Topic

BIG-IP monitors the CPU temperature and the system and CPU fan conditions on the 1000, 2400, 5100, and 5110 Application Switches by querying the system's management bus (smbus) every two minutes.

If the fan speed is less than 1000 RPM or the CPU temperature is greater than 67 degrees Celsius, the alarm LED will blink red and BIG-IP will log an error in the /var/log/bigip file. If the processor exceeds the maximum operating temperature of 75 degrees Celsius, the processor(s) have a built-in thermal diode that will shut down the processor.

BIG-IP contains UL listed power supplies, which are designed to shut down when they get too hot (before they burn themselves up) without providing special notification.
