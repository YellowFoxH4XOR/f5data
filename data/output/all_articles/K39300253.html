Description

VLAN names on a vCMP guest do not match those on the vCMP host.

Environment

  • vCMP
  • VLANs

Cause

VLAN was created on the guest with a different name.

Recommended Actions

VLANs should never be created on the guest unless they are in a non-Common partition. They should always be created and assigned to the guest on the host. The guest will then inherit them from the host, with no further VLAN configuration required on the guest.

If the VLAN is in the Common partition, delete the VLAN from the guest and assign it from the host.
