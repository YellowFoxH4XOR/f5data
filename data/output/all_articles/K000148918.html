K000148918: Unexpected reboot of BIG-IP VE with log gaps

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-12-17
Updated
2024-12-17
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 16.1.4
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000148918

Description

BIG-IP VE instance unexpectedly rebooted. When reviewing log files, such as /var/log/ltm or /var/log/kern.log there are missing logs.

  • The log gaps might be short, ranging between a few seconds to up to an hour.
  • The log gaps are 'filled' with NULL sequences highlighted in red as shown in the following image: 

  • No other error is found.

Environment

  • BIG-IP VE

Cause

This points to a kernel panic event and it is most likely related to the underlying hypervisor.

Recommended Actions

A kernel panic rarely leaves useful information in the BIG-IP's logs, but it sometimes outputs some information to the console. It is then recommended to review the hypervisor logs around the timeframes of the gaps.

Additionally, you could load the systemd journal files and try to look there if something got logged there.
