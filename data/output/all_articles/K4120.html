Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If you try to assign the Oracle health monitor to a node that does not exist, you will receive errors similar to the following:

java.lang.NullPointerException:
at com.f5.eav.MonitorWorker.execute( DBDaemon.java:227)
at com.f5.eav.TreadController.execute(ThreadController.java.167)
at com.f5.eav.PoolThread.run(PoolThread.java:35)

This problem is cosmetic.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR28035.
