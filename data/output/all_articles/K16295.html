Policy Information

With the release of the BIG-IP 12250v and 10350v, F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of the BIG-IP 6900-NEBS, 11000, 11050, and 11050-NEBS, effective April 1st, 2016. F5 is providing advanced product notification to customers and partners announcing intent to retire the products listed in the following table:

Technical Support

F5 maintains generous life cycle policies that allow customers to enjoy many years of support and new software releases. Customers whose platforms are designated End of Life (EoL), and who are currently under maintenance contracts, will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the service contract renewal date expires.
