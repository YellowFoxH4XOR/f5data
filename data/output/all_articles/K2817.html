Product specifications for the BIG-IP 1000, 2400, 5000, and 5100 application switches are available at the following location:

https://support.f5.com/kb/en-us/archived_products/big-ip.html

The BIG-IP 1000, 2400, 5000, and 5100 switch appliances have the following environmental characteristics:

  • Non-operating thermal temperature is a minimum of 40 degrees Celsius to a maximum of 70 degrees Celsius.
  • Non-operating humidity is 90 percent RH (non-condensing).
  • Maximum operational altitude is 6,000 feet; maximum non-operational altitude is 40,000 feet.
  • Average acoustic noise is 63 decibels.

    Note: The BIG-IP 5000 switch appliance is the quietest switch in this product family, typically running at fewer than 63 decibels.

  • The maximum thermal rate change is 30 degrees Celsius per hour.
