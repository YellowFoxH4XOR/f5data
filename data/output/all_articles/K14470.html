Known Issue

In rare conditions, the logd service may consume 100 percent of the CPU and produce a core file.

Impact

Users cannot log in to the Configuration utility.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H425240 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The logd service consumes 100 percent of the CPU.
  • The /var/core directory contains logd core files.
  • Users are unable to log in to the Configuration utility.
  • Messages similar to the following example appear in the /var/log/apm log file:

    err apd[<pid>]: 01490000:3: AccessPolicyD.cpp func: "sendAccessPolicyResponse()" line: 1228 Msg: efc35810: duplicated request dropped

    err apd[<pid>]: 01490000:3: AccessPolicyD.cpp func: "process_request()" line: 759 Msg: EXCEPTION AccessPolicyD.cpp line:1197 function: writeSessionVarToSessionDb - Error:Set pipeline: mc_finish_set_pipeline failed
