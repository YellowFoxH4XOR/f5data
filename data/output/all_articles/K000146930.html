K000146930: Why are the Alert policy rules not working as expected?

Source

What Happened?

  • You have some alert policies configured and you need to know how they work
  • You disabled the ServiceEndpointHealthcheckFailure alert in your Default namespace but you continue to receive the alert in your logs on the F5 Distributed Cloud XC console

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud Console
  • Alert Policies

Resolution/Answer

The alert policy only controls what alerts should/should not be sent to the alert receiver associated with the policy.

All alerts are displayed in the Console regardless of the alert policy.
