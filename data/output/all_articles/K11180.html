Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The High-Speed Bridge (HSB) is the interface between the BIG-IP switch fabric and the host processor complex. On rare occasions, the HSB on BIG-IP 8900 platforms may lockup and cause TMM to crash. When this issue occurs, HSB-related error messages may be logged to the /var/log/ltm file that appear similar to the following example:

01230112:2: Interface 0.3: soft resetting due to transmitter failure.
01010025:2: Device error: hsb interface 2 soft resetting due to transmitter failure
01230112:2: Interface 0.7: soft resetting due to transmitter failure.
01010025:2: Device error: hsb interface 6 soft resetting due to transmitter failure
01010025:2: Device error: hsb interface 2 disable tx ring 0 timed out
01010025:2: Device error: hsb interface 6 disable tx ring 0 timed out
01010025:2: Device error: hsb interface 2 disable tx ring 1 timed out

In addition, error messages may be logged to the /var/log/tmm file that appear similar to the following example:

notice hsb2: tx watchdog check 4
notice hsb2: tx watchdog check 5
notice hsb interface 2 soft resetting due to transmitter failure
notice *** TMM 2 - PDE 2 - transmitter failure ***
notice ifdown failed to disable hsb2. Rebooting
notice hsb2: No.1 reset due to transmitter failure.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR119615 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 10.2.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, ASM, GTM, PSM, Link Controller, WebAccelerator, APM, WAN Optimization, or Edge Gateway release notes.

In addition, this issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-9.4.8-396.1-HF3 issued for BIG-IP 9.4.8, Hotfix-BIGIP-10.0.1-402.7-HF4 issued for BIG-IP 10.0.1, and Hotfix-BIGIP-10.1.0-3372.0-HF1 for BIG-IP 10.1.0. You may download these hotfixes or later versions of the hotfixes from the F5  Downloads site.
