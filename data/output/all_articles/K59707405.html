Description

This article will help in verifying IN-TMM probes are being sent after enabling IN-TMM on a device.

Environment

BIG-IP with IN-TMM Monitoring Enabled

Cause

Verify IN-TMM probes are being sent when IN-TMM is enabled

Recommended Actions

  1. Verify IN-TMM is enabled. See:K11323537: Configuring In-TMM monitoring
    # getdb bigd.tmm

  2. Enable Debug. See: K97244114: Enabling Debug on BIG-IP System Processes For F5 Support
    # tmsh modify sys db bigd.debug value enable

  3. Look for in-tmm probes in /var/log/bigdlog

    For Example:

    IN-TMM Probe: tmm desired=1 tmm probe=1 tmm instance=0 tmm mid=1
    Non-IN-TMM Probe: tmm desired=1 tmm probe=0 tmm instance=-1 tmm mid=0

    Note: When tmm probe=1 the monitor is sent by TMM and when tmm probe=0 it is sent by bigd.

Additional Information

In some cases Probes might not be sent IN-TMM even after it has been enabled.
