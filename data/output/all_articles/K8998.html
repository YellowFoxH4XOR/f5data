Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The Learning function of the BIG-IP ASM system may be disabled, if currently enabled, when the system is power cycled.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR101627 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.4.6. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM release notes.

This issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-9.4.4-94.0-HF3, issued for BIG-IP 9.4.4, and in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.5-HF2, issued for BIG-IP 9.4.5. You may download these hotfixes or later versions of the hotfixes from the F5 Downloads site.

For information about the F5 hotfix policy, refer to K4918: Overview of F5 critical issue hotfix policy.

For information about how to manage F5 product hotfixes, refer to K6845: Managing BIG-IP product hotfixes (9.x).
