Known Issue

In rare instances, the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) process may intermittently, but persistently, produce a core file that includes an Assertion "valid xfrag" failed error.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You have at least one BIG-IP system in a device group.

Impact

TMM may produce a core file and temporarily fail to process traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • An error message similar to the following example appears in the /var/log/ltm file:

    panic: ../kern/xbuf.c:2273: Assertion "valid xfrag" failed.

  • Your BIG-IP system fails over to another system in the device group.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can reboot the BIG-IP system.

Impact of workaround: If your BIG-IP system is in a standby state and not actively passing traffic, performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
