Description

When you reactivate a license after an add-on key has expired, the expired add-on key will be automatically removed from the license file. This applies to all types of expired add-on keys, including evaluation and time-limited add-on licenses.

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • F5OS (R-series and Velos)
  • Evaluation add-on license
  • Time-limited license
  • License re-activation

Cause

If add-on keys have expired prior to re-activation, they will be automatically deleted during the license re-activation process.

Recommended Actions

Note: No manual deletion or deactivation of the expired add-on key is required.

The actions or steps required to address the topic covered in the description.

  1.  Wait until the time-limited or evaluation add-on license has reached its expiration date.
  2. Reactivate the license through the GUI or CLI.
  3. The expired add-on will be automatically removed from the newly generated license file.
