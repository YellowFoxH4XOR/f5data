Topic

For BIG-IP to pass the GLOP multicast protocol, you must have any_ip enabled; however, BIG-IP cannot participate as part of a GLOP multicast.

For specific instructions about enabling any_ip, refer to the BIG-IP Administrator Guide.

For more information about GLOP addressing, refer to RFC2770.
