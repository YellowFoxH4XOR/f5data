K000153093: ISP links remain down

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-08-20
Updated
2025-10-03
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP DNS: 17.0.0, 17.1.0, 17.0.X, 17.X.X, 17.5.X, 17.5.1, 17.5.0, 17.1.X, 17.1.2, 17.1.1
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000153093

Description

Links are down after migrating from LC to BIGIP DNS. The issue may be also observed after upgrade to v.17.x.

Iquery works fine, but links are marked down with (no reply from big3d: timed out) message.

Environment

  • BIG-IP DNS
  • v17.x
  • Links

Cause

Bug ID 2016613

Recommended Actions

While in v17.5.1, run:

tmsh modify gtm global-settings general monitor-disabled-objects yes

Once monitoring has been restarted, monitor-disabled-objects can be returned to the default state of 'no'.

Additional Information

None
