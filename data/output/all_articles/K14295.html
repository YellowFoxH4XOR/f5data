Known Issue

Fragmented IPv6 packets may be dropped when the fragments ingress using different TMM processes.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your system runs on a VIPRION PB200 blade.
  • Your system uses a FastL4 virtual server type.
  • You are passing IPv6 traffic over this virtual server.

Impact

You may experience intermittent packet drops.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H388839 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • When analyzing tcpdump traffic on an affected virtual server, you may see intermittent ICMPv6 Time Exceeded messages.
