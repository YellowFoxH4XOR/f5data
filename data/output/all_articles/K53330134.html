Description

This article offers a step-by-step guide on how to update the email address associated with your MyF5 account (example: name@company.com to newname@company.com. 

Environment

  • MyF5 portal
  • New email address

Cause

Email address change.

Recommended Actions

If your email is already registered in MyF5, it cannot be modified. However, you can register a new email address and request to mirror its access so you can retain access to the cases previously associated with your old email. Follow these steps:

 

  1. Create a new account by registering your new email address at https://account.f5.com/myf5/signin/register
  2. Email F5 Support at supportone@f5.com and request the following (if applicable) :
    1. Associate the new email ID with the accounts linked to your old email.
    2. Transfer ownership of any existing open cases to your new email ID.
    3. Deactivate the old email id (optional)

Additional Information

For security purposes, if you change companies, you will not be able to retain access to the cases previously associated with your old email.
