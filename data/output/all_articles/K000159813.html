Description

You can request email notifications to check the expiration of Support Entitlement for F5 registration keys and/or F5 system serial numbers on Product Information Request portal.

Environment

Hardware

Standalone VE

Service Contract

Support Entitlement

Product Information Request

 

Cause

None

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

F5 does not currently provide automated email alerts for service contract expiration. Customers are responsible for:

  • Manually checking support contract expiration dates through the validation portal.
  • Implementing their own internal tracking systems for service contract renewals.
  • Setting calendar reminders for renewal dates

Alternatively, you can request an email notification for the information on Product Information Request portal.

  1. Navigate to https://secure.f5.com/validate/validate.jsp
  2. Enter the email address where you want to receive the report
  3. Enter the registration key or serial number of your F5 device
  4. Click "Submit Info Request"
  5. An auto-generated email will be sent to the provided email address with all the license details

 Additional Information

For assistance with service renewal, please reach out to the F5 Account team or the Renewal team. If you need help identifying the appropriate account team or renewal team, please Contact F5 Support for assistance.
