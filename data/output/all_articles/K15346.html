Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may incorrectly respond to Address Resolution Protocol (ARP) requests after entering standby state.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The BIG-IP system has transitioned to standby state (for example, after experiencing a gateway failsafe event).

Impact

Connections to local traffic objects may be sent to the standby BIG-IP system.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H461173 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • Connection requests to virtual servers, secure network address translations (SNATs), and network address translations (NATs) may be sent to the standby BIG-IP system.
  • Packet captures show that the standby BIG-IP system incorrectly responds to ARP traffic.
