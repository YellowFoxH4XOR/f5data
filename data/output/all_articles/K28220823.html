K28220823: EMS ELK Maximum TDR TPS

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-08-25
Updated
2023-02-21
Applies to
  • Traffix SDC: 5.X.X, 5.1.X, 5.0.X, 5.2.0, 5.2.X, 5.0.0, 5.1.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K28220823

Description

EMS (Element Management System) introduced ELK as a Splunk replacement in 5.1 CF30 and above. Effectively, TDR (Transaction Data Record) and Trace features are migrated to ELK.
EMS ELK cluster supports up to 4,000 TDR TPS. ELK cluster may not be able to store extra TDR records that exceed the maximum TPS.

Environment

  • EMS 5.1 CF30 and above
  • TDR

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

N.A

Additional Information

Please take note if TDR exceed 4k TPS, there might be performance issue on elastic search and may led to corruption of shard.
