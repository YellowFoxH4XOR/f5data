Known Issue

There is a hard limit on SessionDB messages sizes sent on the backplane on chassis platforms. These messages are never sent, which causes backplane communication to lock up. 

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP system is a VIPRION chassis with more than one blade.
  • Client traffic triggers the creation of SessionDB messages larger than approximately 400 KB.

Impact

You may experience a traffic disruption. The BIG-IP system may fail over to another host in the device group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP system becomes unresponsive to client traffic.
  • The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) process runs out of memory from buffering SessionDB data and restarts.

Workaround

To work around this issue, avoid sending large SessionDB data.

Impact of workaround: Performing this workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
