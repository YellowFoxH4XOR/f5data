Description

Is ID1582593 affecting the FastL4 profile with the setting "tcp-pva-whento-offload establish"?

Environment

  • FastL4 profile
  • tcp-pva-whento-offload establish

Cause

ID1582593

Recommended Actions

Yes, ID1582593 is affecting the FastL4 profile with "tcp-pva-whento-offload establish" configured.

You may not have observed the issue primarily because the connection hasn't sent enough packets to be fully accelerated.

You may observe the issue once a sufficient number of packets are sent and the connection becomes fully accelerated.
