K000130466: SSL ciphers used in the clientssl-insecure-compatible and serverssl-insecure-compatible SSL profiles (12.x - 13.x)

Type
Knowledge
Published
2023-01-04
Updated
2023-01-04
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000130466

This article applies to BIG-IP 12.x through 13.x. For information about other versions, refer to the following article:

  • K13214: SSL ciphers used in the clientssl-insecure-compatible and serverssl-insecure-compatible SSL profiles (11.x)
  • K11631: SSL ciphers used in the clientssl-insecure-compatible and serverssl-insecure-compatible SSL profiles (10.2.x)

You can use the clientssl-insecure-compatible and serverssl-insecure-compatible profile types to allow negotiation of weak Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) ciphers for a BIG-IP virtual server. The cipher lists for the clientssl-insecure-compatible and serverssl-insecure-compatible profiles includes some deprecated ciphers, such as DES-CBC-SHA and all MD5 cipher suites.

  • BIG-IP 13.0.0 through 13.1.0
  • BIG-IP 12.0.0 through 12.1.3
