Known Issue

BIG-IP monitors may experience delayed checks for unavailable pool members.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • The pool member is monitored with a health monitor that uses TCP. For example, a TCP, HTTP, or HTTPS monitor.

    Note: This issue has primarily been reported to affect HTTPS health monitors.
  • The pool member is unresponsive to the health monitor.

The bigd process should continue to monitor a pool member after the system has marked the pool member as Down. However, when a down pool member continues to be unresponsive after being marked Down, the bigd process may check the pool member less frequently than expected. As a result, the bigd process may experience a delay of several minutes before sending a health check and discovering that the affected pool member has recovered.

Impact

The system may not mark recovered pool members Up in a timely manner.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • The bigd process reports a pool member as Down.
  • A tcpdump of the health monitor traffic for an affected pool member shows no health monitor SYN packets being sent to the node.
  • A recovered pool member, which is reachable from the BIG-IP system using diagnostic commands such as ping or telnet, continues to be reported as Down.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can disable and re-enable the affected pool member. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: In some cases this workaround may not force the bigd process to resume checking the affected pool member, and require that you restart the bigd process. To do so, use the Restart bigd process procedure, later in this article.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.
  2. Navigate to Local Traffic > Pools > Pools List.
  3. Click the name of the pool you want to modify.
  4. Click the Members tab.
  5. In the Current Members list, select the check box for the pool member you want to modify.
  6. Click Disable.
  7. In the Current Members list, select the check box for the pool member again.
  8. Click Enable.

Restart the bigd process

To restart the bigd process, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Restarting the bigd process temporarily interrupts the BIG-IP monitor processing, which may cause an interruption in traffic processing.

  1. Log in to the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) by entering the following command:

    tmsh

    Note: If you are currently logged in to the tmsh shell, you can skip this step.

  2. Type the following command to restart the bigd service:

    restart sys service bigd
