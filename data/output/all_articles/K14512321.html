Topic

This article applies to BIG-IP 13.x. For information about other versions, refer to the following articles:

  • K36415004: SSL ciphers used in the clientssl-secure and serverssl-secure SSL profiles (14.x)

Description

The clientssl-secure and serverssl-secure profiles are predefined profile types that you can use with valid and trusted SSL certificates to protect high-value sites. By default, the profiles only enable secure SSL ciphers and disable mid-stream SSL renegotiation. You can associate the clientssl-secure and serverssl-secure profiles with SSL virtual servers.

The SSL ciphers supported in the clientssl-secure and serverssl-secure profiles may have changed across versions. The following sections list the SSL ciphers supported for the clientssl-secure and serverssl-secure profiles in BIG-IP 13.x:

Ciphers in BIG-IP 13.x

In BIG-IP 13.x, the clientssl-secure and serverssl-secure profiles SSL profiles allow the following SSL ciphers.
