Known Issue

When upgrading an ARX system, the upgrade of the CIFS browsing service may stall due to activity in the exports table. When the upgrade stalls, CIFS services may be locked until the service is cleared.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • You upgrade to 6.x or later from 5.x or earlier.
  • You are using a CIFS namespace.

Note: Upgrade of the ARX system will complete successfully prior to the service being cleared.

Impact

CIFS services may fail to start after a failover.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • In the ARX syslog, you may see error messages containing the following content:

    Error <nnnn> upgrading CIFS browsing
  • The CIFS service fails to start after failover

Workaround

Recovery

To recover from this issue, you must simultaneously reboot both the senior and backup ARX systems. To do so, execute a dual reboot by issuing the reload command on both the ARX senior and ARX backup systems simultaneously.

Impact of workaround: Dual reboot incurs additional downtime of approximately 10 to 15 minutes.

Note: The senior ARX system will return to the senior position upon recovery from the dual reboot.

Prevention

You can prevent this issue by performing the following procedure when upgrading a redundant pair of ARX systems:

Impact of workaround: Dual reboot incurs additional downtime of approximately 10 to 15 minutes.

  1. Upgrade the backup ARX system and reboot.

    For instructions about how to upgrade the ARX system, refer to the ARX Maintenance Guide.
  2. After HA pairing completes, reboot the senior ARX system using the reload command to force a failover to the newly upgraded switch.

    For information about how to use the CLI reload command, refer to the ARX CLI Reference Guide.
  3. After HA pairing completes, execute a dual reboot by issuing the reload command on both the ARX senior and ARX backup systems simultaneously.

    Note: The senior ARX system will return to the senior position upon recovery from the dual reboot.
  4. Initiate the upgrade process on the new backup ARX system and reboot the ARX system after the upgrade completes.
