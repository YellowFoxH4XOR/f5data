Data segregation

F5 data is segregated based on the uploading account. F5 does not provide any larger-scope data-sharing tools, nor do we attempt to organize data or provide sharing between accounts. We only allow the account that uploaded the data to see the data.

The only exception to this is that F5 Support can access accounts (as read only) to see file lists and other relevant account data. This critical access allows F5 Support to help you with access and account issues.

Secure Files retention policy

Fourteen (14) days after closing a support case (without reopening it), the system automatically deletes the associated case folder that stores all customer-provided files.

F5 iHealth retention policy

F5 retains QKView files uploaded to iHealth in accordance with F5 Support’s customer data retention policies, as previously mentioned. In some cases, iHealth retention policies are stricter.  The following policies outline how F5 iHealth handles data that is uploaded but not associated with a support case.

Ingress methodRetention policy
Manual upload using iHealth UI30 days
Programmatic upload using API5 days

iHealth overrides the retention policies of both ingress methods if a valid case is associated with the QKView. Linking a case to a QKView in iHealth updates the retention policy, ensuring the QKView is kept for 14 days after the associated support case is closed.

Additionally, iHealth maintains an internal data store for uploaded QKViews. This ecosystem imports QKViews from the iHealth web service and stores the data for a maximum of two (2) years, with different levels of access afforded to the data depending on its age:

  • 0 to 6 months
    • F5 grants access for case research purposes, escalations, and product improvement purposes to a controlled list of people with a controlled list of job roles able to obtain access. 
  • 6 months to 2 years
    • F5 prohibits access to the data, except for the iHealth team.
    • F5 aggregates data reporting.
    • F5 anonymizes data moved into this tier, removing known identifying information.

You may opt out of having your data used for product improvement by changing your settings in the F5 iHealth application to restrict your account data from being imported into the internal data store.

If you are the account holder who uploaded the QKView, you can override any retention policy by manually deleting the QKView from your F5 iHealth account, which ends the retention period immediately.

Note: F5 is ISO 27001 certified, and our security policy and processes are in alignment with ISO 27001 standards.
