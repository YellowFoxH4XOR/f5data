Description

A clean up process of ASM Policy sweeper is running every 24 hours. 

You observe a log message similar to the following in /var/log/ts/asmcrond.log:

asmcrond|INFO|Jan 12 10:00:52.948|2707|F5::PeriodicTask::Base::run,,Running Task: ActivePolicySweep
asmcrond|INFO|Jan 13 10:00:53.290|2707|F5::PeriodicTask::Base::run,,Running Task: ActivePolicySweep
asmcrond|INFO|Jan 14 10:00:53.704|2707|F5::PeriodicTask::Base::run,,Running Task: ActivePolicySweep
asmcrond|INFO|Jan 15 10:00:53.910|2707|F5::PeriodicTask::Base::run,,Running Task: ActivePolicySweep
asmcrond|INFO|Jan 16 10:00:53.565|2707|F5::PeriodicTask::Base::run,,Running Task: ActivePolicySweep

You wish to know how to decide the time when the clean up process starts.

Environment

  • BIG-IP ASM

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

No action is needed.

The timing of ActivePolicySweep depends on starting time of ASM. 

For example, ASM started at 19:08 as follows.

Jan 20 19:08:22 big-ip.local info perl[28951]: 01310053:6: ASM starting
Jan 20 19:08:35 big-ip.local info perl[28951]: 01310053:6: ASM started successfully

asmcrond|INFO|Jan 20 19:08:32.941|29189|F5::PeriodicTask::Base::run,,Running Task: ActivePolicySweep

After that, ActivePolicySweep runs next 19:08.
