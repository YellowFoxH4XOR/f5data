Description

You received an BIG-IQ alert recently for a subject cert as part of a bundle Certificate for example name: ca-bundle.crt.example. You are able to locate a bundle under ca-bundle.crt but you can't find the 'example' in GUI and determinate which certificate will expire.

An example of the alert  from BIG-IQ:

Certificate ca-bundle.crt.example on Hostname: localhost will expire in -1633 days

Environment

BIG-IP

Certificate

GUI

BIG-IQ

Cause

Undetermined

Recommended Actions

Open CLI and try to find the certificate bundle under the path below:

 

/config/filestore/files_d/Common_d/certificate_d/

None
