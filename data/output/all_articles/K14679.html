Topic

The BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE), when hosted on the Xen Community Hypervisor, has limited virtual functions.

Description

Beginning in BIG-IP 11.4.0, the BIG-IP VE supports the Xen Community Hypervisor as well as Single Root I/O Virtualization (SR-IOV). With SR-IOV support, the BIG-IP VE can detect and use natively the SR-IOV-capable PCI Express device instance (virtual function) that the hosting hypervisor presents.

The Xen Community Hypervisor can present a maximum of only two virtual functions to each guest. As a result of this hypervisor issue, the BIG-IP VE that is hosted on the Xen Community Hypervisor can only detect the two virtual functions presented by the hypervisor.
