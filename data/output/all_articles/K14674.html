Known Issue

The dnatutil utility is not time zone aware.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are using the --start_time or --end_time command line options.
  • The data that the system is querying was created in a different time zone than the one on which dnatutil is being run.

Impact

Resulting output from dnatutil may be offset by the number of hours difference between the capture and processing timezones.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The requested output may be offset by the number of hours difference between the time zone of the host running dnatutil and the time zone where the data was created.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can manually offset the time zone in your dnatutil request by adding or subtracting hours to the --start_time and --end_time option.

Impact of workaround: Performing the following workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.

If the data was created on a host in the GMT time zone, and the host running dnatutil was in the GMT+2 time zone, you would subtract two hours from both the --start_time option and --end_time option to match the time zone of the host on which the data was created.
