Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The hard-coded platform serial number may display extra characters on some BIG-IP units. For example, the following bigpipe platform command output displays extra characters on the end of the chassis serial number:

PLATFORM INFORMATION --
| Marketing Name: BIG-IP 6800
| BIOS Rev: AMIBIOS(C)2003 American Megatrends, Inc.
| base MAC: 00:01:D7:00:00:00
| PVA Version: 2
+-> SYSTEM INFO
| Type: D68
| Chassis serial: bip208100s32ZJ Level 200 part: 200-0244-01 REV Bg

Note: The extra characters located on the end of the chassis serial number are bold.

This issue does not affect system performance, and occurs when random characters are written to the SEEPROM chip during manufacturing.

Note: For more information, refer to K121: Base operating systems of F5 products.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as ID 315577 (formerly CR108164).
