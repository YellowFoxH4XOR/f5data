Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The value returned by the dot1dStaticEntry OID does not have a bounded range, although a range is required. As a result, some SNMP monitoring programs may report an error or fail to query this OID.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR32759 and CR41874.
