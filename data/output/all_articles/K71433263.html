Description

You are an end user of F5 products. You want to authorize a third party vendor to create Support Case on your behalf. This vendor will be using their own business email address for interactions with F5 Support.


Environment

 

  • Third-party Vendor
  • End-User Requester
  • Business email address 
  • Not F5 Reseller/Partner
  • MyF5 portal

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

 

  1. Third-party vendor to submit a request to https://account.f5.com/myf5 for an account.
  2. End-user requester to send an email to supportone@f5.com using the following template.

I work for (Device Owner Company)
My name is (Contact's Full Name)

I authorize adding the following third-party contact
to create and manage their own support case
for all devices at the same location of
Serial # (xxxxxxx)

Third-party Contact to Add:
(FirstName) (LastName)
(+full-phone-number-with-extension)
(business-email@address.com)

Additional Information


None
