Topic

Prepare the BIG-IP system to prevent a Nimda worm attack.

Description

You can associate a BIG-IP local traffic policy to prevent a Nimda worm attack. The Nimda worm is designed to attack systems and applications that are based on the Microsoft Windows operating system. Fortunately, you can easily configure your BIG-IP system to use a simple Local Traffic Policy to prevent the Nimda worm from attacking your systems and applications. In order to use a policy, you will want to create and configure a draft policy, publish that policy, and then associate the policy with a virtual server. The policy forwards a URL containing strings associated with the Nimda worm and resets the connection.
