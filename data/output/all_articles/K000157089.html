K000157089: License activation date and time does not follow local time

Description

License activation dates displayed on F5OS systems and related documentation may not align with the local time zone of the device. For instance, when a license is activated in a time zone ahead of Pacific Time (such as Malaysia), the date shown may reflect the previous day due to time zone differences. No error messages are generated.

Environment

  • Product: F5OS
  • Version: 1.8.2

Cause

F5 licensing servers record all license activation timestamps in Pacific Time (North America), regardless of where the device is physically located or its configured local time. This results in a date discrepancy for users operating in other time zones, such as Malaysia Standard Time (MYT), which is typically one day ahead.

Recommended Actions

  • No technical changes or configuration adjustments are necessary; this behavior is by design within the licensing system.
  • Always convert the displayed Pacific Time activation timestamp to your local time zone to verify actual activation details.

Additional Information

This is not applicable for devices based on Pacific Time zone.
