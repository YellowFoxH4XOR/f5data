K000093030: [F5OS] After PSU re-inserted/swapped, GUI and show components does not show parts number and serial number

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-12-07
Updated
2025-01-27
Applies to
  • F5OS
  • F5OS-A
  • F5OS-C
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000093030

Description

PSU is re-seated/swapped. GUI and show components command do not display serial/parts number.

However, the correct serial and part number are visible in /var/log/platform/pel
 

Asserted | 6 | PSU 1 main output power-good
Na | 6 | PSU 1 is Murata-PS XXXXXX
Na | 6 | PSU 1 serial number: XXXXXXXXXXXXX
Na | 6 | PSU 1 revision: 9151002100-11-0A
Na | 6 | PSU 1 primary  FW version: 9151002100-11-0A
Na | 6 | PSU 1 secondary FW version: 9157002100-11-0A
Na | 6 | PSU 1 input is AC high-line

 

Environment

  • F5OS-A
  • rSeries
  • v1.1.x-v1.6.x

 

  • F5OS-C
  • Velos
  • v1.1.x-v1.4.x

 

Cause

  • Bug ID 1050261

 

Recommended Actions

This is cosmetic issue, it is safe to leave it as it is.

 

When you need to fix this, In maintenance window, restart system_platform-mgr service with the below command will fix this issue.

docker restart system_platform-mgr

IMPORTANT: This may cause system reboot.
