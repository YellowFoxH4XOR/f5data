Topic

Mac OS X users who apply Safari to connect to FirePass or the BIG-IP APM system may not be prompted to present their client certificate when client certificate authentication is being used. You may resolve this issue by installing and configuring the client certificates into the Mac OS X system's Keychain application.

Installing and configuring the Keychain application

  1. Obtain your client certificate from your client certificate administrator.

    Note: This step will be unique in every environment. Contact your client certificate administrator for instructions.

  2. Open the Keychain application on the client workstation by performing the following tasks:
    1. Double-click on your hard drive icon.
    2. Under Places, click Applications.
    3. Expand the Utilities folder.
    4. Double-click Keychain Access.
  3. Under File, select Import Items.
  4. Navigate to your client certificate file you obtained in Step 1.
  5. Leave the Destination Keychain drop-down location to Login.
  6. Click Open.
  7. From the Keychain Access login window, locate the certificate you just imported.
  8. Right-click on the certificate and select New Identity Preference.
  9. Enter the URL that matches the F5 device you want to connect to.

    Important: Make sure that the URL includes trailing forward slash characters ( / ). For Mac OS X versions prior to 10.5.4, you will need to include the full URI, including the path.

    For example:

    https://vpn.example.com/login/

    *.example.com/

    Note: If you do not use a wildcard, make sure to include URLs that the F5 device may respond with.

    Note: The wildcard option is available in Mac OS X versions 10.6 and later.

  10. Select Add.

Note: For command line information about installing and configuring a client certificate, refer to the General Commands Manual at the Mac OS X Reference Library site.
