Description

The Service Check Date of Evaluation/Trial license is the Initial activation date of the license and will not be updated upon reactivation of license.

Environment

  • BIG-IP Virtual Edition
  • BIG-IQ VE Edition
  • FCP
  • Evaluation
  • Trial
  • Licensing

Cause

Service Check Date will not be updated as there is no valid support entitlement.

Recommended Actions

Contact your F5 Sales Representative to purchase a new license.

None
