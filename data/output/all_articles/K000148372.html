K000148372: Unreachable Tenant Management IPs

Support Solution
Published
2024-11-02
Updated
2025-09-08
Applies to

  • F5OS-A: 1.8.X, 1.7.X, 1.5.X, 1.8.0, 1.7.0, 1.5.2, 1.5.1, 1.5.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000148372

Description

Tenants randomly lose their management connection.

Environment

  • F5OS-A

Cause

More than 4x Tenants were configured. Some of the configured Tenants randomly lost the management connection.

The VLAN gateway (Firewall) do not see all Tenants in its ARP Table. 

In the .pcap file, we see that affected Tenants are not receiving ARP responses.

Recommended Actions

Check the configuration on the switch side. If parameter "switchport port-security maximum 4" => it limits to 4 active MAC addresses on this port.  The configuration on switch side causes the problem.
