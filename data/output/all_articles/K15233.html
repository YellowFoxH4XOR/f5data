Known Issue

Throughput statistics viewed on the Configuration utility dashboard do not match statistics reported by the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM).

BIG-IP LTM throughput statistics may not match when you compare the dashboard against other statistics reporting tools. The dashboard throughput statistics include traffic observed on all physical interfaces, Layers 2 through 7 (L2 - L7). However, throughput statistics shown in other tools reflect only traffic passing through TMM.

Impact

Dashboard throughput statistics do not match TMM statistics.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H459146 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Low page.
  • Throughput statistics viewed on the Configuration utility dashboard do not match statistics reported by other statistics reporting tools.
