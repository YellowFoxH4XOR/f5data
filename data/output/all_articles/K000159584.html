On January 20, 2026, F5 will upgrade our current customer support chat platform from Legacy Chat to Enhanced Chat (formerly known as Messaging for In-App and Web), powered by Salesforce.

The upgrade introduces several valuable improvements and enhanced capabilities designed to provide flexibility, convenience, and higher efficiency when contacting F5 support teams.

The upgrade doesn't require customer action.

New capabilities and benefits of Enhanced Chat

Persistent conversations

Unlike previous chat solutions, your chat conversation no longer ends when you navigate away or close your session. With Enhanced Chat, conversations are persistent, allowing you to return at any time and pick up right where you left off.

Benefits:

  • Easily resume interrupted conversations at your convenience.
  • Avoid repeating information when re-initiating contact.

Full access to conversation history

Enhanced Chat provides full visibility into your previous conversations directly within the chat window itself. Quickly and efficiently reference past discussions with F5 support engineers.

Benefits:

  • Immediate access to past conversations, resulting in faster resolutions.
  • Better context and less repeated information.

Flexible, asynchronous communication

Enhanced Chat enables asynchronous interactions, giving you flexibility to step away as required and continue chats when convenient, without session expiration or time constraints.

Benefits:

  • Greater convenience tailored to your busy schedule.
  • Enhanced productivity through flexible customer-support interactions.

Advanced routing and faster responses

Automatic routing to appropriate support specialists ensures quicker responses, efficient troubleshooting, and improved support outcomes.

Benefits:

  • Faster access to qualified specialists.
  • Shorter wait times and increased efficiency.
