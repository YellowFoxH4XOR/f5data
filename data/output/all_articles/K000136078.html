Description

If you want to replicate configuration between vCMP guests and VELOS tenants, then this is possible through BIG-IQ

Environment

  • vCMP
  • VELOS (F5OS)
  • BIG-IQ

Cause

Working as per design

 Recommended Actions

You can replicate and manage VELOS tenants just as if they were vCMP guests on the BIG-IQ.
