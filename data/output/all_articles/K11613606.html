Known Issue

Under rare conditions, the dynamic route may remain in the routing table after you delete the route entry.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You delete the route entry from a routing table.

Impact

The deleted route entry remains in the routing table and your traffic may not route as expected. Under rare conditions, the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may restart, and systems configured as part of a high availability (HA) group may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The dynamic route remains in the routing table after you delete the route entry.
  • The BIG-IP system may generate TMM core files to the /shared/core directory with a time stamp that correlates to the traffic interruption.
