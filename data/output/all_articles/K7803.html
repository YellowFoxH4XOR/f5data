Issue

When attempting to license your FirePass controller through Manual licensing, you may receive one of the following two error messages:

  • Incomplete license file.
    Please contact F5 customer support for assistance.

  • License file is incomplete or has been modified.
    Please contact F5 customer support for assistance.

Typically, this error message indicates that the license file was changed or corrupted after downloading it from F5 Networks.

To fix this issue, re-download the license from the F5 Networks license server, through either Automatic or Manual licensing, and load the license again.
