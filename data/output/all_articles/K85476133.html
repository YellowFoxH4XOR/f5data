Known Issue

The sod process repeatedly logs error messages and fails to bind to the appliance management address to listen for network failover packets.

This issue occurs when any of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP system is starting up.
  • You run the load sys config tmsh command.
  • You recently upgraded your BIG-IP system.

Impact

You may experience excessive logging by the sod process.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • In the /var/log/ltm file, you observe the following error message:

    010c003b:3: bind fails on recv_sock_fd addr 10.0.0.1 port 1026 error Cannot assign requested address.
