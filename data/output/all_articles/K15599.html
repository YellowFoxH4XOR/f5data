Known Issue

BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) guests may become unresponsive.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP VE guest virtual machines are hosted by Microsoft Hyper-V on Windows Server 2012/2012E2.
  • The Microsoft Hyper-V host uses remote EMC storage via iSCSI for the deployed virtual machine guests.
  • Two BIG-IP VE guests on the same host are configured for high availability (HA) failover.

Impact

The BIG-IP VE guests become unresponsive and fail to process traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP VE guests respond to ping, but users cannot log in.
  • BIG-IP VE guests stop responding and fail to process traffic.
  • You cannot power off BIG-IP VE guests, and must reset the virtual machine.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can configure the Windows Hyper-V host to use local storage for virtual machines.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
