Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP ASM may fail to handle server responses that would normally contain content with a defined Content-Length header of 0 bytes. Under certain circumstances, the processing of the response may be dropped or may hang for longer than 60 seconds. As a result, subsequent client requests may be dropped.

Note: This issue has been known to affect Microsoft Outlook Web Access (OWA) 2003 connections.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR72258 and CR86492 and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM 9.3.1 and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM Release Notes.
