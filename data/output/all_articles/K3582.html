Topic

Dropped frames are common on many networks and can occur frequently when upstream switches do not know the destination for a frame.

In this situation, the upstream switches flood all ports in an attempt to find the destination. When a BIG-IP system receives a frame that was broadcast in this manner, it forwards the frame to all VLANs associated with the port; however, if the VLAN that processes the frame is not the correct destination, it will drop the frame.

Because the BIG-IP system counts these errors on individual VLANs, the total reported by the Configuration utility is higher than that received by the operating system and reported by the netstat or ifconfig commands. In most cases, you can ignore these errors. However, if a high number of these errors occur, investigate the equipment that is flooding the frames to the BIG-IP system.
