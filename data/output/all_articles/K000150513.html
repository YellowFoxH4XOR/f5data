Description

The issue involves removing the "Appliance Mode" from a BIG-IP device. Appliance Mode restricts certain administrative functionalities, which can hinder troubleshooting efforts. The mode is applied as part of the licensing configuration.

Environment

  • BIG-IP

Cause

Appliance Mode is a feature that can be included in the license of BIG-IP devices to limit access to certain administrative functions for security or compliance purposes. Once enabled, this mode cannot be disabled directly through configuration changes and requires a clean installation with an updated license that excludes the Appliance Mode add-on.

Recommended Actions

Before proceeding, verify if Appliance mode is enabled by referring to F5 article K12815.

After you confirm that the Appliance mode is active:

  • Contact your F5 sales representative or licensing team to update your license and remove the Appliance Mode add-on,
  • After obtaining an updated license without Appliance Mode, perform a clean installation of the BIG-IP software (K13117),
  • During reinstallation, apply the new license key that does not include Appliance Mode.

Note: There is no alternative method to disable or remove Appliance Mode without performing a clean installation with an updated license.

F5 licenses the Appliance mode option free of charge upon request. For questions regarding licensing the Appliance mode option, contact your F5 Sales Representative.
