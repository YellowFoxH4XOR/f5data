Known Issue

The BIG-IP APM Online Certificate Status Protocol (OCSP) agent may not honor the Authority Information Access (AIA) setting.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The authentication, authorization, and accounting (AAA) OCSP responder is configured with the Ignore AIA setting unselected.
  • The client certificate is configured with the AIA extension.

Impact

OCSP authentication may fail as the incorrect URL is used.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system erroneously uses the URL configured in the OCSP responder and ignores the configuration of the client certificate AIA extension.
  • OCSP authentication fails.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can clear the URL field on the OSCP responder.

Impact of workaround: Performing this workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
