Known Issue

BIG-IP platforms may continually generate power supply read failure error messages.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The power supply is populated and powered on.
  • The BIG-IP platform is one of the following models:

    • BIG-IP 10000s / 10200v (D113)

This issue is related to a lights-out processor (LOP) firmware issue and does not indicate an actual power supply issue.

Note: A related issue occurs on the BIG-IP 5000 / 7000 and 2000 / 4000 series platforms. For more information, refer to the Supplemental Information section.

Impact

The power supply continues to function properly, but the Always-On Management (AOM) and the BIG-IP system lose the ability to poll the power supply sensors.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP system may continually generate error messages to the /var/log/ltm file indicating a power supply read failure for PS1 (obj_id=f4). The error message appears similar to the following example:

    012a0005:5: Tmstat::updateSensorTbls: HAL SenSvc error: BourneSeepromDev: LopDev: sendLopCmd: Lopd status: 1 packet: action=1 obj_id=f4 sub_obj=0 slot_id=ff result=19 len=0 crc=a5c1 payload= (error code:0x19)

  • The BIG-IP system may continually generate error messages to the /var/log/ltm file indicating a power supply read failure for PS2 (obj_id=fc). The error message appears similar to the following example:

    012a0005:5: Tmstat::updateSensorTbls: HAL SenSvc error: BourneSeepromDev: LopDev: sendLopCmd: Lopd status: 1 packet: action=1 obj_id=fc sub_obj=0 slot_id=ff result=19 len=0 crc=7b2d payload= (error code:0x19)

  • The power supply LEDs are solid green, indicating that they are present and operating properly.
  • When the BIG-IP system receives an SNMP query for the system objects OID 1.3.6.1.4.1.3375.2.1.3, the system responds back without the requested status information for the chassis fans, temperature, or power supplies.

Workaround

To temporarily work around this issue, you can reseat the power supply that is generating the error messages and clear the LCD display. To do so, perform the following procedures:

Impact of workaround: There is no impact for appliances containing dual power supplies. If you have an appliance containing a single-power supply, you must arrange to power the unit down before reseating the power supply.

Reseating the power supply that is generating error messages

  1. Determine which power supply is generating the error messages by verifying the power supply identifier in the log message.

    For example:

    obj_id=f4  - indicates power supply #1

    obj_id=fc -  indicates power supply #2

  2. Turn the power switch to the Off ('0') position for the affected power supply, and remove the AC power cord from the power supply.
  3. Loosen the screw and remove the power supply.
  4. Before attaching the AC power cord, slide the power supply into the power supply bay.
  5. Attach the AC power cord to the power supply.
  6. Turn the power switch on the power supply to the On ('1') position.

Clearing the LCD display

  1. Locate the green check button on the front of the unit, to the right of the LCD display.
  2. To clear the currently-displayed alerts, press the green check button on the front LCD panel.

    To clear the LCD alerts remotely, refer to K11094: Clearing the LCD and the Alarm LED remotely.
