Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. It is possible for one or both friction type connectors on the back of the System Control Module (SCM) to become loose when the new SCM is installed on the ARX 6000. The bottom cable connection on the back of the SCM connects to the RAID controller for the bay 2 disk. The top cable connection on the back of the SCM connects to the RAID controller for the bay 1 disk.

Note: For information about the System Control Module (SCM) or disk drives, refer to the ARX 6000 Hardware Installation Guide.

Note: For information about how to locate F5 product manuals, refer to K98133564: Tips for searching AskF5 and finding product documentation.

If SCM cable connections going to the RAID controller are loose, one or both of the disk drives may not be displayed or may appear as degraded in the show chassis diskuse command output. If a loose cable connector results in the ARX system marking a disk drive as defective, the drive may no longer be usable in the system and require replacement, even though there is nothing physically wrong with it.

Important: When replacing an SCM, carefully inspect the cable connectors to ensure they are securely connected.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as ARX ID 33118.
