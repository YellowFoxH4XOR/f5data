Known Issue

BIG-IP APM Protected Workspace may not start.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • A client uses Windows XP to attempt to initiate a session with the BIG-IP APM system.
  • The client's browser has (or recently has had) a Working Set memory utilization that is greater than 132 MB.

Impact

Client browsers are unable to establish a session with the BIG-IP APM system.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H426045 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • Clients attempting to access the BIG-IP APM system are unable to initialize the Protected Workspace and the BIG-IP APM session fails.
