K000133157: VLAN failsafe not sending arp traffic until IPv6 NDP fails

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-03-23
Updated
2023-03-23
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000133157

Description

During VLAN failsafe, at the halfway mark the BIG-IP will send out an ARP request or Neighbor Discovery Packet for the oldest entry in the ARP table or IPv6 neighbor cache only. This may be an IPv6 link local request even if the customer does not have IPv6 explicitly configured. Only when VLAN failsafe has reached the end of the timer will it send out requests for the remaining entries.


This means that you may see IPv6 Neighbor Discovery Packets sent at the halfway timeout mark, despite not having IPv6 enabled, then not see an ARP for your IPv4 entries until the timeout has fully expired. This is expected.


Environment

BIG-IP


Cause

This is simply a clarification of existing behavior
