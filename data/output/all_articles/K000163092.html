Description

F5 Distributed Cloud automatic SSL certificate management renews F5-managed certificates before their displayed expiration date. Certificate rotation may require clients to revalidate the certificate, but significant service disruption is not expected. Successful automatic renewal events may not be recorded in service logs.

Environment

  • Product: F5 Distributed Cloud
  • Feature: HTTP Load Balancer automatic SSL certificate management
  • Certificate type: F5-managed Let’s Encrypt certificate
  • Affected domain: Web_1.example.com
  • Certificate validity: 90 days
  • Renewal behavior: Renewal occurs at approximately two-thirds of the certificate lifetime, or about 30 days before expiration.
  • Certificate configuration: The affected domain uses an F5-managed auto-certificate rather than a custom certificate.

Cause

The certificate was renewed by the scheduled Distributed Cloud automatic certificate-renewal process. The renewal timing is controlled by the service configuration and is intended to provide time to address renewal failures before certificate expiration.

The exact internal event that initiated a successful renewal may not be determinable when successful automatic renewals are not logged.

Recommended Actions

  • Expect an F5-managed certificate to be renewed before its displayed expiration date.
  • Do not rely on the displayed expiration date as an exact prediction of the renewal time.
  • No customer approval or maintenance activity is required for an automatic renewal.
  • The Distributed Cloud Console may not provide:
    • A guaranteed exact future renewal timestamp.
    • A customer-visible renewal schedule.
    • A customer-visible status history for a pending automatic renewal.
  • Contact F5 Support to request per-load-balancer diagnostic logging if additional visibility is required for a specific renewal cycle.
  • If certificate changes must be fully controlled by the customer, use a custom certificate instead of an F5-managed auto-certificate.
  • Verify the domain and load-balancer association before investigating certificate changes.
  • If disruption occurs during certificate rotation, investigate certificate pinning, trust stores, client validation, and application deployment processes.

Additional Information

  • Certificate rotation replaces the active certificate with a new certificate and a new validity period.
  • Successful automatic renewals may not be logged; available logs may contain only renewal failures.
  • Certificate rotation is expected behavior and should not normally cause significant service disruption.
  • Renewal timing can vary according to the deployed service configuration.
  • Use a custom certificate when an application requires customer-controlled certificate changes or approval workflows.
