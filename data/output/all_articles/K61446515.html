Topic

AskF5 is performing system maintenance on June 4, 2017 from 08:45 PDT to 15:00 PDT.

Description

During the maintenance period, AskF5 will be accessible. However, the following functionality will be unavailable:

  • Submitting new service requests
  • Updating existing service requests

You will have access to read-only copies of existing service requests.

For assistance during the maintenance period, contact F5 Support.
