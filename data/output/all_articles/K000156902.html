Description

After upgrading F5OS on Velos chassis to version 1.8.1, you may observe periodic error messages in system logs such as:

msg="LOP RX timeout" timeout="3.5s" srcSlot=CcCpu1 destSlot=Blade2 destAddr="<IP_ADDRESS>:1040" objectId=CpldData subObject=0x00 requestId=0x49 interface="lopc-udp"

Environment

  • Product: F5OS - Chassis
  • Scenario: Upgrade F5OS version 

Cause

This is ID1497885.

A blade may not be properly seated after the upgrade, resulting in the system failing to detect it and generating related error messages in logs and incomplete blade status in platform-hal output.

Recommended Actions

  • If a blade is not detected (all status fields "false" or empty in platform-hal output), reseat the affected blade in the chassis and verify that it is recognized correctly.
  • After reseating, confirm that the blade status fields show "true" and that related error messages have stopped.
  • No further action is required unless there are additional functional issues observed.
