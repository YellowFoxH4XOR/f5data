Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When using the BIG-IP ASM Policy Builder for single learned items, the following error message may be reported within the Configuration utility and the Policy Builder log file:

Returned with error: Some worms encountered serious problems. Please check your settings.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR85390 and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM 9.4.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM Release Notes.

Workaround

If you receive the error message, you must ensure that the proper information is gathered by the Policy Builder and included within the policy. To do so, repeat the operation you were performing that prompted the error message.
