Description

Packet captures of TCP connection between client and virtual server hosted on a BIG-IP system seem to show that BIG-IP is sending segments larger than the advertised maximum segment size.

Environment

  • Application hosted on a BIG-IP system on any platform
  • Packet capture of communication with advertised MSS during the TCP 3WHS, taken on the VLAN level of the BIG-IP
  • The db variable tm.tcplargereceiveoffload is enabled (default)

Cause

TCP Segmentation Offload (TSO) happens at the hardware (NIC) level hence the packet captures will give the impression that MSS is not being honored by the BIG-IP as segments can sometimes be larger than the client's advertised MSS.

Recommended Actions

Take the packet captures on the physical interface level and the segments will properly show as adhering to the MSS values
