Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The Priority Group Activation feature within BIG-IP LTM pools, may incorrectly report an active member is available when the pool does not contain any active members.

This issue can affect both BIG-IP LTM and BIG-IP GTM, as they may direct traffic to a virtual server that does not contain any active or available members.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR66808 and CR68144, and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.1.3, 9.2.4, and 9.3. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
