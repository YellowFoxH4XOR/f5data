Known Issue

Health monitors fail to use the configured alias address or alias service port for fully-qualified domain name (FQDN) node pool members.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You configure a health monitor with an alias address or alias service port.
  • You apply the health monitor to a pool configured with FQDN node pool members.

The health monitor will use the address and service port of the FQDN node pool member, rather than the alias address or alias service port configured in the monitor.

Impact

The BIG-IP system fails to perform health checks to the configured alias address or alias service port expected for FQDN node pool members, and may send connections to unavailable pool members or not send connections to available pool members.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP system fails to perform health checks to the configured alias address or alias service port expected for FQDN node pool members.
  • Your BIG-IP system incorrectly marks FQDN node pool members as up or down.
  • Your BIG-IP system sends connections to unavailable pool members or not send connections to available pool members.

Workaround

To work around this issue, if your environment allows, you can configure the pool to use non-FQDN pool members.

Impact of workaround: The impact of the suggested workaround will depend on the specific environment. F5 recommends testing any such changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.
