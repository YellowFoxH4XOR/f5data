Topic

BIG-IP 5250F, 7200F, 10200F, 11000F, and 11050F FIPS platforms shipped prior to June 30th, 2016, contain an older firmware version, and must be updated to run the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) approved firmware version.

Description

On BIG-IP 5250F, 7200F, 10200F, 11000F, and 11050F FIPS platforms running the Cavium Nitrox XL FIPS cards, the FIPS firmware 2.1 has been moved to the Legacy list by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), because the RNG function does not meet modern-day FIPS standards. For more information, refer to the NIST webpage for Validated FIPS 140-1 and FIPS 140-2 Cryptographic Modules.
