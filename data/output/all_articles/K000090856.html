Topic

MyF5 is the new support portal for F5 that provides personalized and quick customer service. From MyF5, customers can also view all subscription details and obtain licenses for deployable products such as BIG-IP and NGINX.

Description

F5 is continually enhancing MyF5 and implementing new features. Currently, the following features are available within MyF5:

Available now

  • Use live chat for quick help with basic support issues.
  • Get fast access to specialists who have details about your issue when you need it.
  • Get knowledge articles relevant to your issue based on previous searches and your profile.
  • Download new products and trials for BIG-IP and NGINX.
  • Create, view, and manage your own support cases.
  • Manage your own account and users.
  • Track your subscriptions.
  • Unassign base registration keys.
  • Provide feedback about your support cases, site experience, and F5 knowledge to inform future enhancements.
  • New FCP customers can generate and manage base registration keys.
