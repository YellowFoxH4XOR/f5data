K000140904: SSL Orchestrator Topology does not pass traffic to services

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-08-30
Updated
2025-02-07
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000140904

Description

After configuration, the topology may pass traffic, but no traffic is sent to the services (such as ICAP, or WAF).

Environment

BIG-IP SSLO

Cause

After adding the service to the topology, you must create a service chain containing that service, then ensure that the service chain is referenced by the traffic interception rule. If any of these redundant entries are missing, traffic will not be forwarded to the service.

Recommended Actions

Ensure that the service is referenced by a service chain, and that the service chain is referenced by the traffic interception rule.

Additional Information

You can also find troubleshooting suggestions in the SSLO deployment guides, which can be found here: https://clouddocs.f5.com/sslo-deployment-guide/
