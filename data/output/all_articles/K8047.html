Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When a stand-alone BIG-IP GTM system is configured with persistence enabled on wide IPs, BIG-IP GTM may consume large amounts of memory.

As a result, BIG-IP GTM exceeds its maximum memory allocation size of 128 MB. Under normal conditions, the gtmd process regularly initiates a memory sweeper to clear stale persistence records. If the stale persistence records are not cleared, the gtmd process can exceed its maximum allocated memory size.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR85608 and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.3.1 and 9.4.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM Release Notes.
