Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The bigpipe interface command does not include Head-of-line (HOL) packet drops in the packet drop statistics.

HOL packet drops occur when an egress switch port's buffer is full and the ingress port drops packets destined for the egress port in HOL condition. BIG-IP egress switch ports have an HOL register that defines the total buffer space that the port can consume before entering an HOL condition.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR91184 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.4.4 and 9.6.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, ASM, GTM, Link Controller, or WebAccelerator Release Notes.
