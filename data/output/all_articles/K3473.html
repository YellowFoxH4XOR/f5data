Topic

To view the SNMP MIB file for FirePass, perform one of the following two procedures, depending on which version of FirePass you are running:

FirePass versions 5.x and later

  1. From the Administrative console, click Device Management.
  2. Click Configuration.
  3. Click SNMP.
  4. Click the Help button in the top right corner of the screen.
  5. Scroll to the bottom of the screen.
  6. Click the Click here to view the MIB link.

FirePass version 4.x

  1. From the Administrative console, click Server.
  2. Click Maintenance.
  3. Click the Help button in the top right corner of the screen.
  4. Scroll to the bottom of the screen.
  5. Click the Click here to view the MIB link.
