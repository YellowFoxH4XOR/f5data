Known Issue

The VIPRION system may incorrectly display the serial number of the blade after you remove the blade.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You remove a blade from the VIPRION system without rebooting the system.
On a VIPRION system, you may use the BIG-IP Configuration utility to display the serial number of the blades in the chassis by navigating to System > Clusters > Properties. If you move a blade to another slot on the same chassis, the BIG-IP system may not remove the serial number of the blade that was in the original slot.

For example, moving the blade with serial number bld234567s from slot2 to an empty slot3 may result in slot2 still retaining the blade's serial number as shown below:
SLOT_IDSERIAL_NUMBER
1bld123456s
2bld234567s
3bld234567s
4

Impact

None

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Serial numbers associated with slots are incorrect after you remove blades from the slots.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can perform a chassis reboot. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: If the VIPRION system is the active member of a redundant system, rebooting causes a failover. If the VIPRION system is a standalone unit, rebooting interrupts traffic processing. F5 recommends that you perform this workaround during a maintenance window.

  1. Log in to the command line of the primary blade on the VIPRION chassis.
  2. Reboot the VIPRION chassis by typing the following command:

    clsh reboot
