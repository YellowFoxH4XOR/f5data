K35522735: Slot offline for extended period of time after upgrade due to ASM policy upgrade

Type
Support Solution
Published
2020-11-20
Updated
2023-02-21
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP ASM: 12.X.X, 12.0.X, 12.1.6, 12.1.5, 12.1.4, 12.1.3, 12.1.X, 12.1.1, 12.1.0, 12.0.0, 12.1.2
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K35522735

Description

  • Slot offline for extended period of time after upgrade due to ASM policy upgrade
  • Configuration not loaded
  • Slot showing offline status
  • Error messages
    010c0087:5: Waiting in Offline for cluster primary slot alive.
    info tsconfig.pl[21686]: ASM initial configration script launched
    info asm_start[20447]: ASM config loaded
    info asm_start[20447]: Policy history (for policy /[path]/[policyName]) was not found while installing ucs file. Activating current version of the policy.

Environment

  • ASM provisioned with many policies in use

Cause

This is expected behavior

Recommended Actions

After upgrade wait a while for the ASM policies to complete their upgrade and for ASM/BD to move to a ready state.
