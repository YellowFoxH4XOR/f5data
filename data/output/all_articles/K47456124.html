Description
Unable to perform upgrade from ASM to Advanced WAF for users on public Cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP, and Alibaba).

Environment

  • Public cloud platform
  • AWS
  • Azure
  • GCP
  • Alibaba
  • Subscription license
  • You upgraded to BIG-IP 14.1 and later, but the ASM license wasn't migrated to Advanced WAF.

Cause
You need to pick up one of these images to enable the Advanced WAF license.

F5 Advanced WAF with LTM, IPI, and Threat Campaigns (PAYG, 25Mbps)
F5 Advanced WAF with LTM, IPI, and Threat Campaigns (PAYG, 200Mbps)
F5 Advanced WAF with LTM, IPI, and Threat Campaigns (PAYG, 3Gbps)
F5 Per-App-VE Advanced WAF with LTM, IPI, TC (PAYG, 25Mbps)
F5 Per-App-VE Advanced WAF with LTM, IPI, TC (PAYG, 200Mbps)

Recommended Actions

You may want to launch a new instance by using one of the images listed in the Cause section.
