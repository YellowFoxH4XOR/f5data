Technical Support

F5 maintains generous lifecycle policies that allow customers to enjoy many years of both support and new software releases. Customers whose platforms are designated EoL and who are currently under maintenance contracts will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the service contract expires.

Note: WebAccelerator software versions 5.2 and 5.3.1 will reach the End of Technical Support on January 1st, 2011. For information, refer to K7572: WebAccelerator software support policy.
