Description

BIG-IP has a Dynamic DoS Signature feature, and you can see the signature in both bigip.conf and GUI.
You may find that some dynamic signatures are not shown in GUI, although those signatures are in bigip.conf.
However it is expected behavior of Dynamic DoS Signature.
 

Environment

  • BIG-IP ASM or BIG-IP AFM
  • Comparing Dynamic Signatures between bigip.conf and GUI "Security >> DoS Protection >> Signatures >> Dynamic"

Cause

It is by design.

Recommended Actions

When you face the differences of dynamic dos signatures between bigip.conf and GUI, you can ignore it safely.
