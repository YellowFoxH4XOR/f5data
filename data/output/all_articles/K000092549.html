Description

The sub-violation "Header name with no header value" was disabled by the administrator.

After some time, the sub-violation was enabled by the policy builder.

Environment

  • Automatic Policy Building

Cause

Learn is enabled for the violation and therefore

  • When enough samples were logged which did not generate the violation, policy builder, made the suggestion to enable the violation.
  • When enough samples were logged which had false positive on the violation, policy builder, made the suggestion to disable the violation.

As it is Automatic, policy builder accepted the suggestion and applied it to the policy.

Note: Disabling the violation does not disable learning.

Recommended Actions

Disable learning for the violation "Header name with no header value".

Additional Information

This behaviour is not specific to the violation "Header name with no header value" and can happen for any violation. Therefore, when Automatic Policy builder is turned on and policy change for a certain entity or attack signature is no longer required, then disable learn for those parts.
