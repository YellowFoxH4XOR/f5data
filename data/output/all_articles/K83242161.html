Description

RSA app is suddenly failing through the previously-working LTM virtual server.

  • RSA App displays an error similar to, "untrusted/unknown certificate authority"
  • tcpdump capture does not show any problems related to the SSL certificate
  • tcpdump capture does not show any problems related to the SSL handshake (a matching cipher is selected by the BIG-IP virtual server)
  • Additional RSA App errors, which may be encountered:
javax.net.ssl.SSLException: Could not generate DH key pairs Caused by: java.security.InvalidAlgorithmParameterException: Accepted DH prime length is 2048 or higher

        at com.rsa.cryptoj.o.kv.a(Unknown Source:28)

Environment

Relevant environmental factors specific to the topic

  • RSA authentication app, installed on a remote client (often on a mobile phone or notebook)
  • LTM virtual server with a clientssl profile, which has been handling the traffic from the RSA app

Cause

RSA has stated that cipher suites in the new Android apps for CT-KIP have been changed and supports FIPS-approved ciphers that are different than the old Android applications.  DH ciphers with prime length less than 2048 can be negotiated between client and server, but app ends up failing, with an unusual error that suggests the certificate authority is the problem.

Recommended Actions

Configure the Cipher String, within the Client SSL profile, to exclude weak Diffie-Hellman ciphers

DEFAULT:!DH   
or:
DEFAULT:!DHE

You may also want to exclude RSA key exchange (also considered weak).
DEFAULT:!TLSv1:!TLSv1_1:!DHE:!RSA
