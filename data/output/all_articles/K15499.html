Known Issue

The BIG-IP system does not properly validate if a virtual server has a corresponding DNS or SIP profile.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You associate a denial-of-service (DoS) profile with a virtual server.
  • The DoS profile is configured for DNS or SIP protection.

Impact

Virtual servers are not protected against DNS and SIP DoS attacks due to non-validated misconfiguration.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H473307 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium page.
  • Virtual servers are not protected against DNS or SIP DoS attacks, even though you have configured an associated DoS profile with corresponding DNS and SIP settings.
  • Virtual servers with DoS profile do not respond as expected to a DoS attack.

Resolution

Status

Workaround

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.

To work around this issue, you can associate a corresponding DNS or SIP profile when associating a DoS profile configured with DNS or SIP settings.
