Description

How to verify a certificate is not in use by the BIG-IP system by leveraging iHealth.

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • SSL profiles
  • Certificates
  • Diagnostics tab of iHealth

Cause
None

Recommended Actions

When you upload a qkview to iHealth, the configuration on your device is evaluated and compared to a database of known issues, common mistakes, and published F5 best practices. This includes finding configuration objects and certificates that are not in use by the system. Based on the findings a list of heuristics is generated and shown in the Diagnostics tab.
 
Under the heuristic "F5 recommends removing orphaned configuration objects" it will provide a list of all certificates that are not in use by the BIG-IP system. Ensure the certificates that are listed here are not indirectly linked to other objects before deleting them.
