Description

The BIG-IP cannot detect the DC power supply unit.

 

Environment

  • DC Power Supply
  • Dual power supply
  • BIG-IP 10000 series

 

Cause

BIG-IP can detect DC power supply when check with the command show sys hardware as below:

 

admin1@(BIG-IP)(cfg-sync In Sync)(Standby)(/Common)(tmos)# show sys hardware

Sys::Hardware

Chassis Fan Status

Index Status Low Limit(rpm) Fan Speed(rpm)

1 up 1000 6841

2 up 1000 5228

3 up 1000 5048

Chassis Information

Maximum MAC Count 2

Registration Key -

Chassis Power Supply Status

Index Status Current

1 up DC

2 unidentified NA

 

Recommended Actions

  • Pulled out the the power supply (PSU) with status unidentified and inserted again, if the status remain unchanged please perform the below steps.

 

  • Swap the power supply (PSU) between PSU1 and PSU2, for the above example, if the Chassis Power Supply Status 1 become unidentified after swap, may consider to RMA the PSU.

 

  • Alternatively to replace the unidentified PSU with the know good PSU, if the Chassis Power Supply Status become up, may consider to RMA the PSU.
