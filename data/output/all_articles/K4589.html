Known Issue

F5 has discovered that a limited number of BIG-IP systems have been shipped from the factory with the Activity light/LED, which is located directly to the left of the LCD panel, permanently illuminated solid amber as soon as the system is powered on.

Normally, the Activity light/LED will flash with network activity or hard disk activity. When this erroneous condition is present, the Activity light will be permanently solid. This is not an indication of a problem and will not affect the operation of the unit.

The following platforms are affected:

  • BIG-IP 3400
  • BIG-IP 6400
  • BIG-IP 6800

At this time, we do not have a list of serial numbers of affected units, nor do we have a date range in which these systems were manufactured. It is important to note that, since there are no functional issues with these units, there is no need to remove any of units that are already installed in your network.
