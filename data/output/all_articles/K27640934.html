Description

PowerShell snap-in modules are not working after upgrading from version 13 to 14.1.0.5 and getting the error below:

Getting Authentication issues: icontrolsnapin
 

Environment

  • Upgrade

Cause

  • Incompatible PowerShell version
  • A device certificate corrupted on the BIG-IP

Recommended Actions

  • Upgrade PowerShell to a version compatible with the BIG-IP version.
  • Check the device certificate on the BIG-IP

None.
