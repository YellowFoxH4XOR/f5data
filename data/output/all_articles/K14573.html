Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may fail to re-establish a connection and persistence mirroring after a failover.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP system is running on a platform that supports serial failover.
  • The BIG-IP system is configured as part of a Sync-Failover device group that uses serial failover.
  • The BIG-IP system is not configured for network failover.

    Note: A device group that uses only serial failover can have only two members.

  • The BIG-IP virtual servers are configured with connection mirroring, and/or persistence profiles are configured with persistence mirroring.
  • A failover has occurred.

Impact

Users may experience unexpected traffic disruption on connections that were configured with mirroring when a failover occurs.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H425977 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • Failovers between devices in the high availability (HA) pair may result in unexpected traffic disruption, especially when the affected virtual servers are configured for mirroring.
  • Persistence and session table information may be missing on the new standby (previous active system) system after a failover.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can enable network failover and restart the TMM processes. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Restarting TMM causes the BIG-IP system to drop the current connections. You should perform this workaround only during a maintenance window.

To enable network failover, refer to the BIG-IP Device Service Clustering: Administration guide.

Note: For information about how to locate F5 product guides, refer to K12453464: Finding product documentation on AskF5.

After you have completed the process of enabling network failover, restart the TMM processes by using the following command:

bigstart restart tmm
