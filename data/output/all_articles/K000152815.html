Description

HTTP::collect <length> may get following TCL error, when you retry HTTP::collect once after HTTP::release.

TCL error: /Common/http-rule <HTTP_RESPONSE_DATA> - ERR_ARG (line 1)     invoked from within "HTTP::collect $collect_length"

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM All version
  • irule command HTTP::collect, HTTP::release is used in an iRule

Cause

HTTP::collect <length> collects the number of length, the length must be increased on every subsequent HTTP::collect, in other words, more content must be collected to keep the collection going. For example, if you want to collect additional 5000 bytes after 10000 bytes collected, the length needs to be set to 15000 bytes.

HTTP::release is one-shot - it releases the request/response headers, any collected body, and ceases to collect. Once HTTP::release has occurred (either explicitly or implicitly), collection is over and attempts to collect will garner an error.

Recommended Actions

If you want to continue HTTP::collect, you can get an incremental effect by continuing to increase the amount specified to HTTP::collect in HTTP_*_DATA, before some final HTTP::release (explicit or implicit) to forward the request. However, the entire request/response will accumulate at the HTTP collect satellite while collection is ongoing.
