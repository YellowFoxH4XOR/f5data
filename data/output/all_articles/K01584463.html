Known Issue

BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) may fail to boot.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP VE is for KVM Red Hat Enterprise Linux or CentOS.
  • The initial deployment of your BIG-IP VE is a modified configuration that does not include a video device.

Impact

BIG-IP VE fails to boot.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When you attempt to boot the initial deployment of BIG-IP VE without a video device, the device does not boot and fails to display error messages.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 577831 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. Workaround

To work around this issue, you can use a VGA console option for the initial BIG-IP VE deployment.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
