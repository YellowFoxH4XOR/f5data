Known Issue

After an upgrade, Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP) guests may change to an INOPERATIVE state.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is running with vCMP guests.
  • You upgrade the host system from one of the following version ranges:
    • BIG-IP 12.1.4.1 up to, but not including, 12.1.5
    • BIG-IP 13.1.1.5 up to, but not including. 13.1.3
  • The upgrade target version is BIG-IP 12.1.5 or 13.1.3 or later.

Impact

The vCMP guest starts up, reverts to the INOPERATIVE state, and then stops processing traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • After upgrading the vCMP host, the guest changes to an INOPERATIVE state.
  • vCMP guests fail to process traffic after an upgrade.

Workaround

You may be able to work around this issue by booting back to a previously used, unaffected version on the vCMP host, and then installing BIG-IP 12.1.5 or 13.1.3 or later. Otherwise, you can upgrade to a version listed in the Fixes introduced in column.
