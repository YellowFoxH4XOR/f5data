Topic

BIG-IP redundant systems can be configured to use the gateway failsafe and the Virtual Local Area Network (VLAN) failsafe features to ensure maximum availability when issues exist in the surrounding network. Each failsafe feature allows the choice of failsafe action among Reboot, Restart All, or Failover.

Beginning in BIG-IP 10.0.0, when both members of a redundant system are simultaneously affected by the same failsafe condition, and the associated failsafe action is set to Failover, both units will go into a standby state. While the failsafe condition is triggered, both units will remain in the standby state. As a result, when both members of a redundant system simultaneously encounter a failsafe condition, the redundant system is unable to service traffic.

Important: For BIG-IP 10.x, F5 recommends that you use the HA Group feature instead of VLAN failsafe or gateway failsafe. The HA Group feature provides greater granularity in managing failsafe conditions and actions, including the consideration and relative weighting of multiple conditions. For more information, refer to the Configuring HA Groups section of the Configuring High Availability chapter of the TMOS Management Guide for BIG-IP Systems.
