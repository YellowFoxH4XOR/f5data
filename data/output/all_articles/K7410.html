Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When polling the sysObjectID OID on the BIG-IP system, the tmsnmpd process continually causes a core dump.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR74893 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.2, 9.3, and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
