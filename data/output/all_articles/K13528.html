Known Issue

Description

A section of microcode that updates the system BIOS may fail to load at boot time on BIG-IP 3400 platforms. As a result, the BIG-IP system may experience frequent and random reboots.

Impact

Systems affected by this issue will experience system reboots and a subsequent failover event in configurations with an HA pair.
