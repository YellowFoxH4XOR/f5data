K000152473: When updating device certificates (server.crt), the Device Trust Certificate (client.crt) is not updated automatically.

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-07-16
Updated
2025-07-16
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000152473

Description

When updating device certificates (server.crt) on F5 BIG-IP LTM systems, the Device Trust Certificate (client.crt) under System > Certificate Management > Device Certificate Management > Device Trust Certificates is not updated automatically. The client.crt is used for device trust and device-to-device authentication and may have a different expiration date than the device certificate. The GUI only provides import/export options for this certificate, and standard documentation may not clearly outline how to renew or replace a self-signed client.crt.

 

Environment

  • F5 BIG-IP LTM
  • Configuration: Standalone or vCMP host with instances (can be in HA pairs)
  • Device Trust Certificate (client.crt) managed under System > Certificate Management > Device Certificate Management > Device Trust Certificates

 

Cause

Updating the device certificate (server.crt) does not update the Device Trust Certificate (client.crt), as these are managed separately. The process for renewing or replacing a self-signed client.crt is not clearly documented in standard F5 resources, which can lead to confusion about the correct procedure.

 

Recommended Actions

To renew or replace the Device Trust Certificate (client.crt) with a new self-signed certificate.

Generate a new self-signed certificate and key:

#openssl req -x509 -nodes -days 3650 -newkey rsa:2048 -sha256 -keyout /config/httpd/conf/ssl.key/server.key -out /config/httpd/conf/ssl.crt/server.crt

For more information, please refer to: K000140327.

 

Restart the httpd service:

bigstart restart httpd

 

Update client.crt with the new certificate:

cat /config/httpd/conf/ssl.crt/server.crt >> /config/big3d/client.crt

 

Restart the httpd service again:

bigstart restart httpd

 

Verify in GUI:

Go to System > Certificate Management > Device Certificate Management > Device Trust Certificates to confirm that the new certificate appears as expected. If multiple certificates are listed, you may remove any old or expired entries.
