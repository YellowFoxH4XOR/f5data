Known Issue

A PEM policy rule configured with HTTP-redirect may cause the client redirected connection to suspend.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system's PEM policy configuration contains a rule that performs HTTP-redirect action.
  • A client connection matches the flow for the HTTP-redirect action.

Impact

The PEM policy rule with HTTP-redirect action may not work as expected.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • You observe that the BIG-IP system applies the redirect action without closing the original connection. By viewing tcpdump captures, you notice that the unclosed connection stays open and is reset by expiration timeout.
  • The connection on the client appears to be suspended.
