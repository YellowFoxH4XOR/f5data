Topic

Some considerations must be made when deploying BIG-IP 2000/4000 series platforms for Layer 2 (L2) switch operations.

Description

The following BIG-IP platforms are affected:

  • 2000s
  • 2200s
  • 4000s
  • 4200v

These BIG-IP platforms do not include an accelerated hardware-based L2 switch and will use the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) for all L2 switching tasks.

Recommendations

When L2 switching features are required on 2000/4000 platforms, you should consider the following factors:

  • TMM handles all L2 switching and bridging operations for the above platforms; these configurations may incur a significant CPU overhead and should be avoided.
  • Spanning Tree Protocol (STP) and Link Layer Discovery Protocol (LLDP) are supported on the 2000/4000 series hardware platforms beginning in BIG-IP 11.6.0.
