Description

After licensing a BIG-IP with an ELA (Enterprise License Agreement) license and configuring cookie persistence you may observe traffic being load balanced instead of persisting as configured.

Environment

  • BIG-IP licensed with ELA license.
  • FIPS module is part of ELA license.
  • Hardware or Software version is not certified for FIPS (List of certified hardware and software)
  • Encrypted HTTP cookies used for persistence.

Cause

FIPS license activated on software or hardware not certified for FIPS prevents BIG-IP from decrypting encrypted cookies which breaks persistence built for those cookies.

Recommended Actions

  1. Verify if your device is licensed with FIPS module by issuing following command:
    show /sys license detail
  2. The device is licensed with FIPS if you see following line in the output:
    FIPS 140-2
  3. You can remove the FIPS module by reaching out to your Customer Success Manager from whom you obtained your ELA license. 
  4. After obtaining new license, you can activate it by following steps from:
    K7752: Licensing the BIG-IP system
