Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When Windows Vista clients attempt to enter Protected Workspace, Protected Workspace may fail to load and only the Windows Desktop wallpaper remains visible to the user. This issue occurs intermittently, and is due to a synchronization issue between two FirePass Protected Workspace client components.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR86283 and it was fixed in FirePass version 6.0.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the FirePass Release Notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in Cumulative Hotfix HF-601-8 issued for FirePass version 6.0.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the Cumulative Hotfix from the F5 Networks Downloads page.

For instructions about obtaining a hotfix, refer to SOL167: Downloading software from F5 Networks.

For instructions about installing a hotfix, refer to SOL3430: Installing FirePass hotfixes.

Workaround

You can work around this issue by pressing CTRL+ALT+DELETE and then selecting Log off from the on-screen menu.

Important: Selecting Log off will end your Windows session. Any unsaved data will be lost.

Once you log out of your Windows session, log back in to Windows and open a new session to the FirePass controller.
