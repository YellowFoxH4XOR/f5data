Description

Default threshold for "Device is low on memory" in Alerts Settings is N/A.

 

Environment

BIG-IQ.

Cause

Working as designed.

 

At present, memory health check is intentionally disabled, always reporting "healthy" status.

 

Recommended Actions

None.

 

Additional Information

Internal ID2044593 "BIG-IQ Device is low on memory" is not a usable event.
