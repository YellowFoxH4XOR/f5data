K000138753: Interfaces take longer than expected to come up

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-02-28
Updated
2026-04-02
Applies to
  • F5OS
  • rSeries Platforms
  • F5OS-A: 1.5.2, 1.5.1, 1.5.0, 1.7.0
  • rSeries 10000
  • rSeries 5000
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000138753

Description

  • Interfaces take longer than expected to be marked UP (40+ seconds)
  • LACP status remains down until the interfaces are marked UP

Conditions

  • rSeries appliance (r5x00 and above platform)
  • F5OS-A
  • 100G interfaces
  • 40G interfaces

Recommended Actions

For SFP/QSFP interfaces:

  • 25G/10G interfaces take over 10 seconds to be marked UP
  • 40G/100G interfaces take 30+ seconds to be marked UP.

This issue is being tracked as ID 1327137 and fixes were introduced in F5OS-A 1.5.3 and F5OS-A 1.8.0

Additional Information

None
