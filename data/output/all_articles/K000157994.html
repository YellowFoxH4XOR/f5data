Description

F5OS-A and F5OS-C do not support hardware level Flow Control.

The flow-control is supported by software of F5OS and always enabled.  There is no option to disable it.

Environment

  • F5OS-A
  • F5OS-C

Cause

In GUI of F5OS-A 1.5, the option "RX Flow Control" is shown as "On" enabled by default at NETWORK SETTINGS / Interfaces. At the moment, there's no way to disable it.

In GUI of F5OS-A 1.8 and newer version and F5OS-C, the option "RX Flow Control" is not shown. However the behavior is the same as F5OS-A 1.5.

Recommended Actions

There is no specific action is required.
