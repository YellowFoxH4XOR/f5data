Purpose

As part of our normal product lifecycle, F5 will be introducing new FirePass products, the 1200 series, and is therefore announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of the existing 1000 series. The End of Sale date is the first date in a series of product life cycle milestones before a product reaches its End of Life. Customers who recently purchased the 1000 series can be assured that F5 is committed to them as illustrated by the five-year support and investment protection policy outlined in the following solution: K3225: Overview of the F5 End of Life policy.

Technical Support

Customers whose platforms are designated EoS and who are currently under maintenance contracts will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Please contact your sales representative if you have any questions.

EOL Platform List and Dates

For information about End of Life dates for all FirePass platforms, refer to K4309: F5 Networks Platform Lifecycle Support Policy.

EOL Terms and Descriptions

For information about End of Life terms and definitions, refer to the End of Life Cycle Glossary located in K3225: Overview of the F5 End of Life policy.
