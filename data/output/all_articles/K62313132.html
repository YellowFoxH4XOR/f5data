Description

The BIG-IP iSeries platform LCD always displays the date and time in UTC regardless of whether you have customized the BIG-IP system’s time zone setting. In BIG-IP 14.1.0 and later, the string UTC is appended at the end of the date and time displayed on the LCD to make this behavior clearer. Prior to BIG-IP 14.1.0, there is no indication on the LCD that it is displaying the date and time in UTC.

Environment

  • BIG-IP iSeries platform

Cause

None.

Recommended Actions

You should adjust the date and time of the messages/alerts you observed on the LCD to your local timezone as needed.

Additional Information

None.
