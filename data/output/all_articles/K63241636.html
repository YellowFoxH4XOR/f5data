Known Issue

The Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) profile cipher lists have a 768-character limitation.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • A cipher list is specified that is longer than 768 characters.

When configuring an SSL profile on the BIG-IP LTM system, you can use the default cipher string, DEFAULT, or you can specify a custom cipher list.

If you specify a custom cipher list, and the list exceeds 768 characters, the list is truncated after the 768th character and SSL handshakes fail.

Impact

Ciphers entered after the 768-character limit are silently truncated and not applied to incoming requests.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H590595 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • Clients experience SSL handshake failures.
