Description

  • Measured throughput on a virtual appliance may exceed or be lower than the reported interface speed
  • The General Properties section of the virtual appliance may display an incorrect Active Duplex setting when accessing the interface through Network > Interfaces
  • The Media column in the output of the tmsh show network interface all-properties command may show incorrect information

 

Environment

  • BIG-IP VE

Cause

When working with virtual machines it's important to keep in mind that the VM cannot determine the link speed of the physical network connections via its own link state.  The link may be able to display the proper value when using a network interface of type SR-IOV, where it is passed this value from the hypervisor. While the VE is aware of the link between itself and the vSwitch, this may not always be reflected in the reported link speed due to limitations related to determining the upstream link state. However, this does not impact the VE's actual link capabilities. It's important to be aware of the differences in how link state may be reported.

In the case that the VE is using the SR-IOV driver, typically the host system will pass the Big-IP the configured interface speed. In that instance, the interface will report what it passed to it by the hypervisor. The speed is assigned in the hypervisor system, not something you modify on the BIG-IP.  Please note if the speed is greater than 40Gb the BIG-IP VE will display it as 40Gb (display not enforce) even with SR-IOV due to ID904661.

 

 Recommended Actions

  1. If reporting the link speed accurately within the VE is the behavior you want, you must configure a direct link driver such as SR-IOV and the hypervisor must be passing the proper value to the VE and less than or equal to 40Gb (ID904661). 
  2. If you want to understand the actual speed of the link, measure throughput.

 

Additional Information
