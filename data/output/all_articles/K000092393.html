Description

BIG-IP discovery Failing with error in popup window:

Failed to connect to 10.1.1.50 check credentials: Unauthorized(401)

restjavad.0.logs contains:

[ERROR][03 Aug 2022 15:51:32 EDT][/cm/global/tasks/device-trust/945acc9c-9746-4e23-b18e-f967f205e02f/worker BigIPTrustTaskWorker] Failed to connect to 10.1.1.50, check credentials: Unauthorized(401)

 

On BIg-IP during the discovery:

restjavad-audit0.logs:

{"user":"local/null","method":"GET","uri":"http://localhost:8100/mgmt/shared/identified-devices/config/device-info","status":401,"from":"10.1.1.100"}

 Environment

  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management
  • Device Discovery is failing with Unauthorized(401)

 Cause

ID1146373 - Basic authentication for rest admin account failing when a client address is sent null to /usr/bin/pam-authenticator
