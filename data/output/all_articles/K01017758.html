Description

The BIG-IP system configuration may fail to load while upgrading to a newer version.
In /var/log/ltm log files following messaged can be observed

   'Disk limit exceeded. XXXX MB are required to provision these modules, but only XXXX MB are available.'
   'Provisioning (validation) failed.'
   01071008:3: Provisioning failed with error 1 - 'Disk limit exceeded. XXXXX MB are required to provision these modules, but only XXXX MB are available.'
   01420006:3: Loading configuration process failed.

Environment

You are upgrading your BIG-IP to a newer version

Cause

The issue is cased due to Lack of Disk Space on the BIG-IP.

Recommended Actions

To work around this issue, you may follow any of the following procedures.:

Delete unused boot locations/Partitions 
Impact of procedure: Performing the following procedures should not have a negative impact on your system.
Note: You should delete only the partitions that are not in use or you know you wouldn't need it. 

  1. Log in to the Configuration Utility
  2. Navigate to System> Disk Management > Select the HardDrive 
  3. In the "Contained Software Modules" Select the boot location you want to delete 
  4. Click Delete  
  5. Confirm delete
  6. Click Delete
Note: The Active Boot Location, will have a "Yes" on the Active Column. 

Delete unused images/Hotfix on the Software Management Section
Impact of procedure: Performing the following procedures should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the Configuration Utility
  2. Navigate to System> Software management > Image list
  3. In the "Available Images "  section, select the images you want to delete. 
  4. Click Delete  
  5. Confirm delete
  6. Click Delete

Extend the VE or VMCP guest disk space on BIG IP 

If the previous procedures did not work for you, you will need to increase the disk space allocated to your systems. 
Note: This only will apply for VE systems and VCMP guest. 

Please follow the article according to your device 

  • K14952: Extending disk space on BIG-IP VE
  • K24156414: Increasing the disk space for a BIG-IP vCMP guest
