Known Issue

Portal access may not correctly rewrite Internet Explorer-specific JavaScript.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system is configured for Portal access.
  • Content rewritten by the Portal includes the Internet Explorer-specific JavaScript location.href.

    For example:

    <html><body>
    <script>
    location.href/test1.html
    </script>
    </body></html>

  • Users attempt to access content.

Impact

The system does not display content referenced by the location.href.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Web page content that depends on the BIG-IP APM rewrite of JavaScript does not function.
