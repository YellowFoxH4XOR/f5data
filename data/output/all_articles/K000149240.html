K000149240: BIG-IQ marks active BIG-IP as unavailable after upgrade to 17.1.1 and 17.1.0

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-01-09
Updated
2025-09-02
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 8.0.0, 8.X.X, 8.3.X, 8.3.0, 8.2.X, 8.2.0, 8.1.X, 8.1.0, 8.0.X
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.1.1, 17.1.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000149240

Description

BIG-IQ marks active BIG-IP as unavailable after upgrade to version 17.1.1 or 17.1.0 Device status is changing/flapping.

Errors in LTM log of BIG-IQ:

"BIG-IQ Managed Device Unavailable Managed device (10.0.0.1:443) marked unavailable"

Environment

  • BIG-IQ CM
  • Managed BIG-IP Device upgrade to 17.1.0  or 17.1.1
  • Status of BIG-IP device in BIG-IQ changing

Cause

Performance issue of restjavad causing timeouts for BIG-IQ healthcheck requests (ID1602033).

Recommended Actions

Restarting the restjavad process on the BIG-IP may temporarily resolve this issue, however, upgrading to a non-impacted version, 17.1.2, will fix it.

Additional Information

Logs in /var/log/ltm log shows state which is flapping from available to unavailable

"BIG-IQ Managed Device Unavailable Managed device (10.0.0.1:443) marked unavailable"
"BIG-IQ Managed Device Unavailable Managed device (10.0.0.1:443) marked available"
