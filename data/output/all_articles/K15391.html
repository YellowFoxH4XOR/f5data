Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may not properly reorder Datagram Transport Layer Security (DTLS) handshake messages that are out of order.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • DTLS handshake messages are received out of order.

Impact

Clients may fail to establish DTLS sessions with the BIG-IP system.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The BIG-IP system responds with an incorrect DTLS handshake.
