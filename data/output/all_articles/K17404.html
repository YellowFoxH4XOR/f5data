Topic

FirePass support for the Windows Internet Explorer 11 browser is available through the following engineering hotfix:

  • HF-439391-1

Important: This engineering hotfix can only be installed on FirePass systems that have installed HF-70-9.1-1.

To obtain the engineering hotfix, you can contact F5 Technical Support and reference this article number and the associated ID number. Engineering hotfixes are intended to resolve a specific software issue until a suitable minor release, maintenance release, or cumulative hotfix rollup release is available that includes the software fix. For more information, refer to K8986: F5 product support policies.
