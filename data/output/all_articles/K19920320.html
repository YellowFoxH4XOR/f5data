Known Issue

A connection leak occurs on the standby BIG-IP device.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP devices are configured in a high availability (HA) configuration.
  • A virtual server(s) has the spanning enabled attribute.

Impact

A flow leak occurs on the Next Active action.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The standby device has a high connection count.
