Known Issue

Newly added Fully Qualified Domain Name (FQDN) ephemeral pool member attributes may be incorrect.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have created a pool containing FQDN autopopulate enabled pool members.
  • The pool is configured with non-default attributes.
  • An additional FQDN pool member is added to the pool.

Impact

The new FQDN pool member does not inherit the non-default attributes of the FQDN parent.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The new FQDN pool member may not behave in an anticipated manner.
  • When comparing the attributes of the FQDN parent to the newly added FQDN pool members, the configured attributes differ.
