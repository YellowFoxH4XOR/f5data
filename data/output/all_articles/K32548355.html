Description

When trying to resize /appdata as described in this article K74200262 the following message appears:

# lvreduce --resizefs --size -100G /dev/vg-db-sda/dat.appdata
fsck from util-linux 2.23.2 /dev/mapper/vg--db--sda-dat.appdata is in use. e2fsck: Cannot continue, aborting.

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • Resize /appdata
  • Disk space

Cause

There are 2 possible causes:

1) AppData is still in use by a service or there are open files related to AppData.
2) Bug ID1121169 Unable to resize AppData until jitterentropy-rngd is stopped.

Recommended Actions

Please follow one of these options to solve the issue:

1)Identify the open files related to AppData:

#lsof | grep appdata

Then kill the process with killall.

For example to kill mysqld use:

#killall mysqld

2) Umount /appdata and restart jitterentropy-rngd and re-try the resize operation.

#umount /appdata
#systemctl restart jitterentropy-rngd
