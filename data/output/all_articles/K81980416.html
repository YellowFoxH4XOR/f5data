Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may fail to detect an unresponsive high-speed logging (HSL) destination and continue to deliver logs.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • A log publisher is configured to use a remote HSL destination to accept logs.
  • Your remote HSL destination is unresponsive.
Even when unresponsive, the HSL destination is reachable by network route.

Impact

Logs that are targeted for an unresponsive HSL destination are not delivered.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You observe that the log publisher continues to accept and deliver logs to HSL destinations.
