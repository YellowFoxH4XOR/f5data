Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Starting in FirePass 6.0.2 with hotfix HF-602-5, and in version 6.0.3 with hotfix HF-603-3, ports connected to a Cisco 6509 switch that has been hard set to  100Mbps/Full Duplex mode may auto-negotiate to 100Mbps/Half Duplex mode. This issue occurs even when the port on the FirePass controller has been hard-set to 100Mbps/Full Duplex. 

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR122644 and it was fixed in FirePass 6.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the FirePass release notes.

This issue was fixed in cumulative hotfix HF-602-11 issued for FirePass 6.0.2, and in cumulative hotfix HF-603-5-1.1 issued for FirePass 6.0.3. You may download these hotfixes or later versions of the cumulative hotfixes from the F5 Downloads site.

For instructions about obtaining a hotfix, refer to K167: Downloading software and firmware from F5.

For instructions about installing a hotfix, refer to K3430: Installing FirePass hotfixes.

Workaround

As a temporary workaround, you can use a crossover cable to connect the FirePass controller to the Cisco 6509 switch. The FirePass port will use 100Mbps/Full Duplex with a crossover cable. You should replace the crossover cable with a straight-through cable after upgrading to a fixed patch level or to versions 6.1.0 and later.
