Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Load balancing behavior for the BIG-IP ASM and WebAccelerator occurs on a per-connection basis rather than a per-request basis. For example, for non-NTLM traffic, two requests issued on a persistent HTTP connection should be load balanced separately according to the pool's configured load balancing algorithm.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR75534, and it was fixed in BIG-IP versions 9.3 and 9.4.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM, LTM, or WebAccelerator Release Notes.
