K000146623: I'm a tenant owner and get the error "You cannot access this page with your current role"

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-11-08
Updated
2025-02-11
Applies to
  • F5 Distributed Cloud Services
  • F5 Distributed Cloud
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000146623

Description

  • You've been just added as a tenant owner
  • You're able to access everything 
  • On the login page, you see the error "You cannot access this page with your current role. Contact your Administrator and ask them to change your role to access it. "

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud
  • Account Management

Cause

  • The Tenant Owner assigned the role to your user from the start without you logging in first

Resolution

Work with another tenant owner to follow the next steps:

    1. Log in to F5® Distributed Cloud Console
    2. Downgrade your account from the Tenant owner, and assign you to a system namespace admin role
    3. Log out of Distributed Cloud Console
    4. Wait for five mins, re-login to F5® Distributed Cloud Console
    5. Upgrade your account to the tenant owner
    6. Wait for five mins, re-login to Distributed Cloud Console
    7. Check if you can open all pages
