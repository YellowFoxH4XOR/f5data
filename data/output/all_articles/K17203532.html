Topic

Within the customer support portal, you can now upload a qkview diagnostic file and directly associate the file with your service request. The system then directly uploads the qkview to iHealth.

Prerequisites

You must meet the following prerequisites to use these procedures:

  • You ran the qkview utility and downloaded the diagnostic file to your local system.
  • You have an F5 support site single sign-on (SSO) account to access the F5 iHealth diagnostics server.
  • You have an existing service request with F5 Technical Support.

Procedure

Associating a qkview diagnostic file with a service request

Impact of procedure: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to AskF5.
  2. Click My Support at the top right corner of the page.
  3. Under Service Requests, click the service request ID to which you want to associate the qkview diagnostic file.
  4. From the Service Request page, click Attach QKView.

    The Attach QKView dialog appears.

  5. Click Browse Files and navigate to the qkview diagnostic file you want.
  6. Click the qkview file you want to associate with the service request.
  7. Click Attach QKView and wait for the upload to complete.
  8. To close the window, click Exit.
