Policy Information

Updated: August 26, 2020

F5 is announcing the end of sale of the F5 Professional Services products listed in Table 1. The last day to order the affected product(s) is September 30, 2020. Customers with active quotations containing these products may want to consider the replacement products, if applicable, identified in Table 3. Customers with active purchase agreements containing the affected products will continue to receive the services purchased until the products are consumed. In some situations, the replacement part number will be utilized in lieu of the End-of-Sale product part number.

Refer to Table 2 for a description of the end of life milestones, definitions and dates for the affected product(s).
