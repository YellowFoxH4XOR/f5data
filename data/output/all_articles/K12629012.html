K12629012: Big-IQ does not list Big-IP FPS service when discovering BIG-IP version 12

Type
Support Solution
Published
2020-11-05
Updated
2023-02-21
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • BIG-IP Platforms
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 7.X.X, 6.X.X
  • BIG-IP FPS
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K12629012

Description

  • FPS/WebSafe service is licensed and provisioned on the BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP is running version 12.x.x
  • BIG-IQ is required to view and manage FPS alerts
  • when BIG-IP is discovered by BIG-IQ, FPS service is not listed in available option
  • Big-IQ seems not to identify FPS service on Big-IP 12.x.x

Environment

  • FPS
  • BIG-IQ service discovery
  • BIG-IQ Dashboard
  • BIG-IP

Cause

  • None

Recommended Actions

  • None

Additional Information

  • Prior to BIG-IQ, FPS/WebSafe used to use a standalone separate Dashboard.
  • In version 13 and later, websafe/FPS/DataSafe/mobileSafe were integrated to work with BIG-IQ as a primary data collection and reporting system.
  • Despite the lack of service discovery when running version 12, BIG-IQ dashboard will display FPS alerts
