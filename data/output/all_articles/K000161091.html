K000161091: Unable to login to labs on F5 Education Services

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-06-17
Updated
2026-06-17
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 21.0.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000161091

Description

Unable to log in to F5 training labs using the provided admin credentials during self-directed BIG-IP administration training. The login error prevented access to the lab environment required for course completion. Education Services

 

Environment

BIG-IP LTM Virtual Edition (VE)

Accessing F5 training labs as part of self-directed training

Education Services

 

Cause

The issue is outside the remit of technical support and related to the training lab environment managed by F5 Education Services.

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

Please contact F5 Education Services using the provided web form and select "Lab issue" to request assistance with training lab access.

Submit a request - F5 Education Services
