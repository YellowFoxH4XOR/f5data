Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Clearing the Enable Content Assembly on Proxies check box from the Content Assembly Options section in the Assembly tab of a policy may result in inconsistent content assembly behavior.

Content assembly will be disabled for responses to requests that were proxied when the Always proxy requests for this node option is configured from the Proxying Options section in the Proxying tab of the policy.

However, content assembly is not disabled for responses to requests that were proxied by a proxy rule configured from the Proxying Rules section in the Proxying tab of the policy.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR107617, and it was fixed in BIG-IP WebAccelerator version 9.4.7. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP WebAccelerator release notes.

Workaround

To disable content assembly for responses to requests that were proxied by a proxy rule, you must disable Intelligent Browswer Referencing (IBR). To do so, perform the following procedure:

Note: Predefined Acceleration policies are not modifiable; this procedure must be performed on a user-defined Acceleration policy.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP WebAccelerator Configuration utility.
  2. Click WebAccelerator.
  3. Click Policies.

    A new Policies Configuration window opens.
  4. Click on the User-defined Acceleration policy to be configured.
  5. Click the plus ( + ) signs in the policy tree to expand the policy tree and display the policy nodes.
  6. Select the policy node you want to configure.
  7. Select Acceleration Rules from the drop-down menu.
  8. Click on the Assembly menu tab.
  9. Clear the Enable Intelligent Browser referencing option in the Content Assembly Options section.
  10. Click the Save button.
  11. Click the Publish button.
  12. Optionally, type a comment or note in the Comment field.
  13. Click the Publish Now button.
