Known Issue

The sod process may log a benign error message indicating that a traffic group device was not found.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The system is a member of a high availability (HA) group.
  • The management IP address is changed on a remote HA peer device.

When the management IP address is changed on a remote peer HA system, the local sod process fails to remove an internal reference to the old IP address.

Impact

This issue may cause the HA failover next-active device selection to fall back to the static (IP-based) selection algorithm. As a result, your DSC group with more than two devices selects a device other than the intended device to take over services.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • In the /var/log/ltm log file, you observe messages similar to the following example:

    notice sod[PID]: 010c0062:5: Config digest module error: Traffic group device not found.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can perform a configuration synchronization (ConfigSync) to propagate the new management IP peer, and then restart the sod process. To restart the sod process, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Restarting the sod process will disrupt traffic processing and should only be performed during a maintenance window.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP command line.
  2. To restart the sod process, type one of the following commands:
    • BIG-IP device:

      bigstart restart sod

    • VIPRION blade:

      clsh bigstart restart sod
