Known Issue

Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) keys secured by the SafeNet hardware security module (HSM) may be deleted when a secondary blade is rebooted.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a VIPRION system with two or more blades installed.
  • You have configured the VIPRION system to use SafeNet HSM to protect SSL keys.
  • You reboot or restart the mcpd process on a secondary blade.

Impact

SSL traffic protected by HSM SSL keys fail.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • SSL connections fail and HSM protected SSL keys are missing after you reboot or restart the mcpd process on a secondary blade.

    Note: The following commands reboot or restart the mcpd process on a secondary blade:

    • tmsh reboot slot <blade_id>
    • tmsh reboot slot all
    • clsh reboot
    • tmsh restart sys service mcpd
    • clsh bigstart restart mcpd
  • Protected SSL keys are deleted from the SafeNet HSM device.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can ensure your BIG-IP system is unable to connect to SafeNet HSM when restarting a secondary blade. To do so, perform either of the following actions:

  • When connecting to SafeNet HSM, disconnect the cable from the interface used by the BIG-IP system
  • Enable a firewall rule to prevent packets from the BIG-IP system to SafeNet HSM

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround may affect traffic processing and should only be performed during a planned maintenance window.
