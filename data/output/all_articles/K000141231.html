K000141231: Bot Profile with Auto threshold is in partial ready after 7 days period

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-09-27
Updated
2025-02-05
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000141231

Description

In certain scenarios, even when auto thresholds have been configured on bot profiles, the HTTP status may remain in "partial ready" after the expected 7-day learning period. Normally, the status should transition to "full ready" after this period, indicating that the learning phase is complete. However, it may stay in "partial ready" longer than anticipated.

Environment

  • F5 Bot Profiles

  • Auto Threshold Configuration

  • HTTP Status Monitoring

Cause

This is caused due to the Bot Defense Analytics and Optimization System (BADOS) using a prediction mechanism based on percentiles derived from the historical data of each signal, such as requests per second and health metrics. During the initial 7-day learning period, thresholds are set at high values and are gradually adjusted downwards as more data is accumulated. This reduces the risk of false positives. The auto threshold is calculated as three times the 98th percentile and is averaged over 7 days. The "partial ready" status indicates that the learning process is still ongoing and functioning as expected.

Recommended Actions

  1. Monitor the Learning Period:
    • Ensure that the learning window has indeed completed the 7-day period.
    • Check that the historical data is being collected properly without interruptions.
  2. Verify Threshold Adjustments:
    • Confirm that the thresholds are being adjusted as more data comes in.
    • Review the percentile calculations to ensure they are accurate.
  3. Check for Anomalies:
    • Investigate any anomalies or interruptions in the data that might extend the learning period.
    • Look for any unusual traffic patterns that could affect the learning process.
