Description

ASM Event logs displaying 'Attack Type' as N/A.

No information about 'Attack Type' is visible when the request log is viewed from the ASM MySQL database.
 

Environment

  • Seen this on Attack Signatures
  • Attack Signatures are in Staging


Cause

Attack Signatures that are matched will trigger the violation 'Attack Signature Detected'.

However, request will be considered Legal as the Attack Signatures are in Staging.

A Legal request will not have an 'Attack Type'.
 

Recommended Actions

None.


Additional Information
 
When the Attack Signatures are enforced ( enabled ) , generating an illegal request, the appropriate 'Attack Type' will be displayed.
