Description

Cannot see BIG-IP/BIG-IQ products when opening a case.

Only NGNIX products are available

 

Environment

MY F5

Cause

No BIG-IP/BIG-IQ products are associated to the account

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

Contact F5 support and provide your BIG-IP/BIG-IQ serial number/base registration key to be associated to account.
