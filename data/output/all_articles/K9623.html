Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. A BIG-IP ASM policy does not strip the URL fragment from the redirected URL; it considers the fragment as part of the parameter.

Note: This behavior affects requests sent from Internet Explorer 6 browsers. Internet Explorer 7 and Mozilla browsers strip this information before sending the request.

For example, if the redirect request is received from a browser containing the value, test=123#ajb, the policy does not correctly strip the #ajb fragmented section from the URL and passes it to the server.

Note: For more information, refer to RFC1808: Relative Uniform Resource Locators.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR111493 and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM 9.4.7 and 10.0.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM release notes.
