K000147275: Do F5 XC Customer Edges have an end of life date?

Applies to

  • F5 Distributed Cloud
Source

What Happened?

You want to know if the Customer Edges (CEs) have an End Of Life (EOL) date

Environment

 

  • F5® Distributed Cloud (XC)
  • Customer Edge

Resolution/Answer

  • No. F5 XC Customer Edges do not have an end of life date.

Additional Information

Any particular version of CE is supported for 6 months and the customer is expected to upgrade CE software version within that time frame.
