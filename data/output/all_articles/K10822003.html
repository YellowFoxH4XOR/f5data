Description
Why SDC 4.4 reply Diameter Answer with "T" flag, and how to prevent the behavior. 

Environment

  • SDC version 4.4
  • "T" is the retransmission flag

Cause
This is caused by the diameter client resending diameter request with a "T" flag after SDC responds with a diameter answer for the original request message.
In such scenarios, SDC will resend the answer with "T" flag.

Recommended Actions

  1. Diameter client should not resend the request with "T" flag if it receives a Diameter Answer from SDC(for the original request).
  2. Customers needs to fix this behavior on their Diameter Client.

Additional Information
Refer RFC6733 for additional information.
