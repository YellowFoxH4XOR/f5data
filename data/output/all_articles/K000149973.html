Description

 

How to obtain reports on utilization and network statistics for specific pool members on F5 BIG-IP load balancers, and retrieve full log reports for these members.

 

Environment

 

  • BIG-IP LTM

 

Cause

 

The need to monitor and analyze the performance of specific pool members or troubleshoot issues by accessing detailed logs.

 

Recommended Actions

 

Retrieving Logs via CLI:

 

  1. Use the following command in the bash shell:
    1. grep -r pool_name /var/log/*
      1. Note: Replace "pool_name" with your pool

Retrieving Logs via GUI:

 

  1. Navigate to System > Logs > Local Traffic in the GUI interface.
  2. Use the search bar to enter pool and filter relevant logs. Note that there may be multiple pages of results depending on log volume.

 

Additional Information

 

By default, logs rotate after 8 days unless configured otherwise.

The level of logging depends on your current configuration.
