Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. A time shift of 10 seconds or more on one or more blades, may cause the blades to become out of sync with each other. This issue may cause one or more of the blades to be removed from the cluster.

This behavior is most likely to occur when initially adding NTP servers or communicating with a broken NTP server, which causes the time to shift more than 10 seconds on one or more of the blades.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR98904.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-v9.6.1-HF2, issued for BIG-IP 9.6.1, for the BIG-IP 9.6 software branch. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
