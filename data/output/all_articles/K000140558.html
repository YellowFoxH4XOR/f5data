K000140558: Reset multi-factor authentication for MyF5

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-08-02
Updated
2024-08-19
Applies to
  • Support Portal
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000140558

Description

You have the following issues:

  • You are unable to log in to the MyF5 portal using multi-factor authentication (MFA). 
  • You lost or need to reset your MFA technique.

Environment

You are using one or more of the following devices:

  • MyF5 portal
  • Cell phone (iOS or Android)
  • Email client

Cause

You lost your current MFA technique for one of the following reasons:

  • Replaced your phone
  • Lost access to Google Authenticator
  • Changed your email address

Recommended Actions

Send an email to supportone@f5.com from the email address you are using for your MyF5 portal access.

In the email subject line, enter the reason you need to reset your MFA technique, such as:

  • Phone replacement
  • Reset
  • Email change

Also include the following information:

  • First name
  • Last name
  • Your MyF5 account email address
  • Company name

Additional Information

None
