Topic

This article applies to BIG-IP 14.x - 15.x. For information about other versions, refer to the following articles:

  • K26701577: Overview of daemons that are monitored and restarted by the runsv utility (16.x)
  • K69933022: Overview of daemons that are monitored and restarted by the runsv utility (12.x – 13.x)
  • K14934: Overview of daemons that are monitored and restarted by the runsv utility (11.x)
  • K11488: Overview of daemons that are monitored and restarted by the runsv utility (10.x)

The BIG-IP 14.x - 15.x software branches uses the runsv utility to monitor specific daemons. The runsv utility monitors specific daemons and will restart the daemon in the event of a system failure.
