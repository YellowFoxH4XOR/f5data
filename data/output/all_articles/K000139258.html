K000139258: Requests are blocked with violation Cookie not RFC-compliant (cookie has no value)

Description

Requests are blocked with violation Cookie not RFC-compliant (cookie has no value). This violation can also appear in the learning suggestions of the policy builder.


Cookie: ABC.NET_SessionId=g2drnslasdasdady; path=/; HttpOnly; SameSite=Lax; TS011ab22b=01ebdsf86s87f6sd76fs8b5f6d5d900feacce9ca7e45534e7104b814a8s7d68a76d5ed677b2068d9160ec7e1c37285; Path=/; Domain=test.com; HttpOnly;


Environment

  • AWAF policy
  • Cookie

Cause

This is caused by a change in behavior after version 17.1.1 and 15.1.10 to comply with RFC 6265. "HttpOnly" is an attribute applicable to Set-Cookie (response) headers, it should not appear in Cookie (request) headers.


This is tracked by Bug ID1069441.

Recommended Actions

The behavior is expected. RFC 6265 should be followed for cookie handling. In this case, "HttpOnly" should not be present in the cookie header of the request.
