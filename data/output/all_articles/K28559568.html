K28559568: Zone serial differs between two GTM's in same SYNC group

Source

Description

A zone was updated in the BIG-IP GTM/DNS but the same zone is having slightly different serials on different GTM/DNS devices.

  • Even after a while, the zone serial doesn't sync and discrepancy exists between GTM/DNS devices in that sync group.
  • It will continue to work without issues.

Environment

Relevant environmental factors specific to the topic

  • BIG-IP with GTM/DNS
  • A recent change was done to the same Zone on two different GTM/DNS within quick succession.
  • However, the contents of the Zone are identical except for the serial.

Cause

This can happen if a change is done on two GTM/DNS devices within a very short period of time (like a few seconds apart).

Recommended Actions

The actions or steps required to address the topic covered in the description.

  1. Create a dummy record in the zone on one GTM/DNS device (example: txt record).
  2. Wait for the record to get propagated across devices in the sync group.
  3. Then delete that dummy record in that zone and wait for the change to get synced to all devices.
  4. Verify the serial on all GTM/DNS devices, it should be the same.
