Topic

The GUI in BIG-IP GTM versions prior to version 9.4.4 may display certain pages, such as the Pools page, slowly according to the size of the wideip.conf configuration. For example, with a configuration containing thousands of objects, the Pools page may take several minutes to display, or appear to hang indefinitely while loading.

Improvements to the manner and mechanism by which the BIG-IP GUI queries the underlying BIG-IP system for its information have been introduced in the BIG-IP 9.4 code branch in version 9.4.4. As a result, the pages render much faster.

F5 Product Development tracked this request for enhancement as CR92430 and it was resolved in BIG-IP GTM 9.4.4.
