Topic

The BIG-IP system collects response times every second for all load balancing modes that use response time metrics, such as Fastest, Observed, and Predictive.
 
For more information about the Fastest, Observed, and Predictive load balancing modes, refer to K508: Comparison of the observed and predictive load balancing modes to the load balancing modes that comprise them (fastest and least connections).
