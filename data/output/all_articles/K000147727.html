What Happened?

I want to Bring Your Own IP (BYOIP) to F5® Distributed Cloud and I am wondering what will be the ASN assigned after the process?

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud (XC)
  • F5® Distributed Cloud official tech docs

Resolution/Answer

It will always be your ASN, no change will be done.

F5 XC ASN35280 will be seen as a transit provider/as-hop on your ASN

Additional Information

Before proceed with the BYOIP process we will need a LOA (Letter of Authorization) from the Sales team. Once received, the XC Support team will reach out to engineering to finish the process.
