Description

It may be necessary to limit the number of connections an individual client can make to your APM protected environment.   This may be due to licensing limitiations, or to limit resource use (bandwidth, CPU/Memory considerations). 

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM Policy
  • Users using too many connections or Licenses.

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

  1. Navigate to: Access  ››  Profiles / Policies : Access Profiles (Per-Session Policies)
  2. Select your Access profile 
  3. Under "Settings",  modify your "Max Sessions per User" setting to your preferred value (0 is unlimited) 
  4. Select Update to commit the changes
Optional: 
  1. Under "Settings", you may also want to limit the "Max in progress sessions per user" value.  These are attempts to connect to APM that have not completed the APM processing.  (Default is 128)
  2. Select update to commit the changes.
Apply Access policy to apply the changes to your environment.

Additional Information

In high volume or in License restricted environments,  you may not want a single user connecting more than once or twice at a time.  this ensures all allowed users have an opportunity to connect to the APM environment.   This is especially relevant in environments using Concurrent Connectivity licenses (CCUs), like Network Access SSLVPN tunnels.
