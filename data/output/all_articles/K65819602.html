Description

When you try to enable PIM, below error messages may be observed from /var/log/ltm

warning mcpd[5061]: 01071030:4: PIM feature not licensed
notice tmrouted[9472]: 01910002:5: PIM: protocol not licensed.

Environment

  • BIGIP
  • VE
  • PIM

Cause

To enable PIM feature, it is necessary to include Multicast Routing, Routing Bundle and Advanced Protocols license modules

Recommended Actions

To verify the license information run command: tmsh show /sys license

Optional Modules
  BIG-IP VE, Multicast Routing 

You may need to contact your F5 Sale representative to obtain the appropriate license to use PIM feature.
