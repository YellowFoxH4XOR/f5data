Known Issue

Health monitor connections may remain in the open connection state after server side termination.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is configured to monitor SSL node or pool members (servers).
  • The server closes (FIN) the health monitor connection without sending the required SSL close_notify alert.

Impact

The BIG-IP SSL monitor connection remains in the open state.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When you view the SSL-level communication of the BIG-IP SSL health monitor and the destination server, the server terminates the health monitor SSL connection with a FIN, but does not send the expected close_notify alert before terminating the connection with the FIN.
  • When you view the BIG-IP health monitor connection state, the connection remains open 15 seconds after receiving the server side FIN. 

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 409340 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. For information about releases, point releases, or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can ensure that the destination SSL server sends the required close_notify alert when closing an SSL connection. 

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
