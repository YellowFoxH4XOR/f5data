Topic

This error message may occur when the serial ports (com0 and com1) have not been assigned interrupts by the BIOS.

com<#> (<###>): no intr

This problem does not occur unless you have made changes to the BIOS.

To recover from this problem, perform the following steps:

  1. Reboot the system.
  2. Enter the BIOS.
  3. Reset the BIOS to default settings.
  4. Exit the BIOS.
