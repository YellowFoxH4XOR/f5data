Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may restart and produce a core file when processing a suspending iRule command.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are using BIG-IP PEM to process mobile service subscriber traffic.
  • You have configured a PEM policy to use an iRule.
  • The iRule uses a command that suspends iRule processing.

    Note: The term suspends may also be referred to as parking.

  • TMM removes a subscriber's connection flow from the connection table.
  • TMM receives additional traffic from the subscriber after the flow has been removed.

Impact

The BIG-IP PEM system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from the TMM restart, and devices configured as a high-availability pair may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP PEM system temporarily fails to process traffic while TMM restarts.
  • The BIG-IP PEM system fails over to another host in the device group.
  • The BIG-IP PEM system generates a TMM core file to the /shared/core directory.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you may be able to modify your iRule to ensure it does not use a suspending iRule command, depending on your specific environment.

Impact of workaround: The impact of the suggested workaround will depend on the specific environment. F5 recommends testing any such changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.
