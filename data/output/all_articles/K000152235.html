K000152235: Analytics/Statistics/Data missing from Application tab of BIG-IQ

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-06-29
Updated
2025-07-15
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 8.4.X, 8.3.X, 8.4.0, 8.3.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000152235

Description

  • You are unable to see analytics data under Application tab of BIG-IQ.
  • You may have followed the Recommended Action mentioned in K000138870: BIG-IQ is not showing application stats for BIG-IP but the issue still persist.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ

Cause

The managed BIG-IP was not re-discovered and re-imported for a long time. Therefore BIG-IQ does not have the latest configuration of the managed BIG-IP.

Recommended Actions

Re-discover and Re-import all the provisioned modules of the managed BIG-IP devices. 

  1. Login into BIG-IQ.
  2. Click on Devices. This will take you to BIG-IP Devices (Left side panel).
  3. Select the BIG-IP device you want to re-discover and re-import.
  4. Click on More.
  5. Click on Re-discover and Re-import.
  6. Enter any name and you can keep everything in its default settings.
  7. Click on Create.

This will help in Re-discovering and Re-importing all the services for multiple devices.
