Description

  • DoS profile are not replicated/synced to the other member of the HA cluster, although Sync-Only device-group with ASM-sync enabled appears to be in sync

Environment

  • Sync-Failover device group
  • Sync-Only device group with ASM-sync enabled
  • ASM DoS Profiles

Cause

  • The seen behaviour is expected.
  • DOS profiles are linked to bot signatures on /Common hence it needs to be synced across all devices in the group.
  • Like all of the LTM configuration objects, objects under the /Common folder are only synced on the sync-failover device group.

Recommended Actions

DoS Profile needs to be synced via the sync-failover device group.

  1. On GUI go to Device Management  ›  Overview
  2. Select the sync-failover device group
  3. Click Sync
