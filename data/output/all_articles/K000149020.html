K000149020: Can a Subscription F5 device be paired with a Perpetual Virtual Edition

Type

Published
2024-12-17
Updated

Description

You are planning to set up a high availability (HA) active-standby pair with two F5 Local Traffic Manager (LTM) devices and want to know if you can proceed with the HA setup if one F5 LTM has a subscription license and the other has a perpetual license.

Environment

  • F5 Local Traffic Manager (LTM) devices
  • Subscription license
  • Perpetual license
  • HA active-standby configuration

Cause

The synchronization and failover processes in an HA setup rely on consistent features and capabilities between the two units. If one F5 LTM device has a subscription license and the other has a perpetual license, there may be feature parity issues and support discrepancies, leading to potential complications in the HA configuration.

Recommended Action

  • Contact F5 support or your F5 sales representative for guidance on aligning licensing models.
  • Consider converting one license type to match the other to ensure feature parity and seamless synchronization.

Additional Information

Ensuring that both F5 LTM devices in your HA pair have the same licensing model is crucial for a reliable and effective high availability configuration. Aligning your licenses will help avoid potential issues and ensure seamless operation.
