Known Issue

SYN cookie validation may cause the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) to restart with an "Assertion 'valid private' failed" message.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • TMM attempts to validate a SYN cookie received for a non-TCP4 flow.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restarting, and devices configured as a high availability (HA) pair may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP system temporarily fails to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restarting.
  • In the /var/log/tmm file, you observe a message similar to the following example:

    notice panic: ../modules/hudfilter/tcp4/tcp4.c:9901: Assertion "valid private" failed.

  • The BIG-IP system generates a TMM core file to the /var/core directory.
  • If configured for HA, the BIG-IP system fails over to the standby system.
