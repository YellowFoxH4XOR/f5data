F5 hereby announces the End of Sale (EoS) of BIG-IP Cloud Edition and BIG-IP Per-App Virtual Edition (VE), effective January 1, 2026.

This EoS applies to all BIG-IP Cloud Edition and BIG-IP Per-App VE SKUs. This applies to all versions, all software development, and all technical support.

This announcement reflects F5's continued commitment to aligning our portfolio with evolving customer needs and market trends. Transitioning away from BIG-IP Cloud Edition and BIG-IP Per-App VEs allows us to continue refining the F5 Application Delivery and Security Platform and focus on delivering solutions that better support customer initiatives.

BIG-IP Cloud Edition and Big-IP Per-App VEs will officially reach EoS worldwide, January 1, 2026.

Implications and transition guidance

Customers who currently use BIG-IP Cloud Edition and BIG-IP Per-App VEs are encouraged to transition to alternative solutions, such as:

  • BIG-IP Virtual Editions (VE): Offering robust scalability, flexibility, and performance across hybrid and multi-cloud environments to meet application delivery and security needs.
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: Continue leveraging its core visibility, management, automation, and templating capabilities outside of the bundled offering.
  • NGINX One (Console and NGINX Plus): Reliable, performant, lower footprint solution that is viable for per-app deployments and includes centralized visibility and management capabilities.
  • Distributed Cloud (XC): SaaS offering inclusive of traffic management and central management, ideal for modern application architectures and distributed workloads.

These products enable customers to maintain equivalent functionality provided by BIG-IP Cloud Edition and BIG-IP Per-App VEs, while aligning more closely with modern application architectures, multi-cloud use cases, and automation initiatives.

For support with transitioning or identifying the best solution to meet your needs, contact your local F5 partner business manager or sales representative.

Technical support

F5 and its partners remains committed to providing support for BIG-IP Cloud Edition and BIG-IP Per-App VEs during this transition as per our product lifecycle policies. Customers under active maintenance contracts will continue to receive technical support until their agreement expires or until the designated End-of-Technical Support (EoTS) date, whichever occurs first.
