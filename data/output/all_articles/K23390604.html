Description

You want to respond to a limited selection of the configured Wide IPs on your BIG-IP DNS/GTM

Environment

  • BIG-IP DNS or GTM
  • DNS Listener
  • Wide IPs
  • iRule
  • Datagroup

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

  1. Create a datagroup that contains the FQDN names you want the BIG-IP to respond to
  2. Create an iRule to drop requests that don't match this list
  3. Apply the iRule to your Listener
Note: You may wish to create a separate listener to allow both a filtered and unfiltered responses to the requests
