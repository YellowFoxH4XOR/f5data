Description

The BIG-IP APM portal access may incorrectly rewrite application-uri in results of missing / after redirection

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system is configured with a portal access profile.
  • Portal access application-uri ends with / as following example:
    application-uri https://application-uri.local/

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Page will not load properly and and you will see blank page
  • After decoding GET request f5-w-68747470733A2F2F6170706C69636174696F6E2D7572692E6C6F63616C2F$$ you will not see / 
  • HttpWatch will show error: 'net::ERR_CONNECTION_RESET'

Environment

  • APM
  • Portal Access
  • application-uri with / at the end

Bug ID1041989
