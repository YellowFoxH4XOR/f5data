Topic

To calculate Round Trip Times, the 3-DNS Controller sends probe packets on an ephemeral high port between an F5 Networks appliance (such as a BIG-IP) and the requester's local DNS.

These packets are typically ICMP or UDP packets and they are numbered and time-stamped so that the probe can:

  • Detect packet loss.
  • Accurately measure the time interval between sending a request and receiving a matching reply (Round Trip Time).
