Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may not forward all Session Initiation Protocol (SIP) responses to SIP clients.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • An iRule configured with a global variable is assigned to a SIP virtual server.
  • The iRule uses the node command, in the SIP_RESPONSE event, to direct the SIP response from the server.

Impact

SIP responses to clients are dropped by the BIG-IP system.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • F5 iHealth lists Heuristic H504323 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • SIP client connections appear to stop responding.

Workaround

To work around this issue, F5 recommends that you avoid using the iRules node command in the iRule SIP_RESPONSE event.

Impact of workaround: Changing the design of an iRule may have additional effects on traffic processing. Ensure any modification is compatible with your environment.
