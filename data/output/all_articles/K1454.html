K1454: Ports 1313, 1414, 1515, 1616, and 1717 are open and listening on BIG-IP or 3-DNS

Topic

Ports 1313, 1414, 1515, 1616 and 1717 may display as listening for any F5 products that support iControl. These ports are used for internal communication on the F5 Networks product by the CORBA engine as follows:

1313 - CORBA BIGapiServer <-> portal
1414 - CORBA BIGdbServer <-> portal
1515 - LocalLBServer <-> portal
1616 - ITCMSystemServer <-> portal
1717 - ITCMNetworkingServer <-> portal
