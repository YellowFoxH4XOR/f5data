Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. In rare instances, a timeout may occur when the ARX system polls for the status of its chassis fans, even if the fans are functioning properly. When this issue occurs, the show health command erroneously reports a fan failure and the ARX system sends fanFail traps. For example:

ARX001# show health
Mon Oct 12 17:01:32 2009 (160) - fanFail Fan control_failed

Additionally, the show chassis and collect commands hang.

Important: When this issue occurs, a physical inspection of the ARX chassis fans is required to determine whether they are functioning properly.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as ARX ID 37047 and it was fixed in ARX versions 5.0.6 and 5.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the ARX release notes.
