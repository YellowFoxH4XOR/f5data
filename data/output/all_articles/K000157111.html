K000157111: Understanding MyF5 Self Service License Status and how it affects utilization/billing

Description

In the MyF5 Self Service option for the Flexible Consumption Program (FCP), the different statuses of the License Keys determine whether they are included in the utilization/billing reports.

Environment

  • MyF5 Customer Portal
  • Editor/Admin access to MyF5 Subscriptions
  • FCP contract renewed after Jan 13, 2025
  • BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) only
  • List of License Keys on MyF5

Cause

As a MyF5 Self Service customer managing FCP license keys, it's important to know how the different license statuses affect billing/utilization.

Recommended Actions

Refer to table below to determine whether the MyF5 Self Service license keys affect billing/utilization.

StatusDescriptionIncluded in Billing/Utilization
Ready to ActivateThis is the initial status of the license key after creating it. The license key has never been activated on a BIG-IP VE.No
ActiveThis license key is applied to a BIG-IP VE or is included in RegKey Pool on BIG-IQ. The day the license is activated will match the Grant Date on the monthly report.Yes
Ready to ReassignThe license key was assigned to a BIG-IP VE, but was unassigned/revoked from the BIG-IP VE. It's currently not assigned to any BIG-IP VE, but is available to reuse on another BIG-IP VE. Yes
TerminatedThe license key isn't needed anymore. This ends support for this license key. The day it's terminated will match the revoke date on the monthly report.No
