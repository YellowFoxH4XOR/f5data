Known Issue

The apmd process may use CPU excessively after the mcpd process unexpectedly restarts.

Impact

CPU usage increases excessively until the apmd process is restarted.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • Performance graphs show high CPU usage.
  • Output from the top or ps commands shows high CPU usage for the apmd process.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can manually restart the apmd process. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Traffic is disrupted briefly while the apmd process restarts.

  1. Log in to the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) by typing the following command:

    tmsh

  2. Restart the apmd process by typing the following command:

    restart sys service apmd

  3. Exit the tmsh utility by typing the following command:

    quit
