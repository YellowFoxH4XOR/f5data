Known Issue

The VIPRION B2100 blade Alert LED does not blink for error- or emergency-level alert conditions.

This issue occurs when any of the following conditions are met:

  • The system experiences a condition that triggers an error-level alert.
  • The system experiences a condition that triggers an emergency-level alert.

You should expect the Alert LED to blink yellow during an error-level alert and blink red during an emergency-level alert. However, during an error-level alert, the Alert LED light remains solid yellow, indicating a warning level alert. During an emergency-level alert, the Alert LED remains solid red indicating an alert or critical-level alert.

Impact

The system might incorrectly identify the level of an alert condition.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • During an error-level alert condition, the Alert LED is solid yellow instead of blinking yellow.
  • During an emergency-level alert condition, the Alert LED is solid red instead of blinking red.
