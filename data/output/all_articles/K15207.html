Known Issue

iRules recv sideband connections may incorrectly time out.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The recv -timeout option is set to zero, or a low value of approximately 100 or less.
  • The sideband connection receives data immediately before the timeout has expired.

Impact

Valid data received by the sideband connection may be discarded.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Even if the sideband connection receives valid date within the timeout period, the recv status variable shows a value of "timeout".

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can increase the timeout value.

Impact of workaround: The impact of the suggested workaround depends on the specific environment. F5 recommends testing these changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.
