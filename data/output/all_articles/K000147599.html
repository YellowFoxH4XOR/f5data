K000147599: How to submit documents for tax exempt verification

Applies to

  • F5 Distributed Cloud Billing
Source

Description

  • You need to know how to submit documents for a tax exemption

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud Account management 

Answer/Recommended Actions

If you have an account manager:

  1. Reach out your Distributed Cloud account manager
  2. Provide your tax exemption certificate

If you don't have an account manager:

  1. Raise a support ticket (How to create a support ticket via F5® Distributed Cloud Console)
  2. Attach your tax exemption certificate

None
