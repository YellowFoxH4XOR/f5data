K000159932: Time zone change is not reflected in GUI

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-02-09
Updated
2026-02-09
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.5.X, 17.5.1, 17.5.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000159932

Description

Time zone change is not reflected in GUI.

After changing the time zone of the system, the status change of a pool member or a node is specifically logged with a timestamp in the old timezone.

 

Environment

- LTM

- time zone changed via GUI or TMSH

- status change of a pool member or a node logged with the wrong timestamp (old time zone)

 

Cause

ID2119313

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

Restart tomcat:

bigstart restart tomcat

 

Additional Information

none
