Description
The holdup time is the time that a power supply can continue to provide output power or voltage to equipment after the input has ceased.

While this  time will vary based on the load on the system, F5 requires that a power supply maintain a holdup time of not less than 5 milliseconds at 80% load.

Environment

  • i5800 with AC power supplies

Cause
None

Recommended Actions

No action is required.
