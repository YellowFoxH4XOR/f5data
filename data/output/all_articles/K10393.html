Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Firefox 3.5 users may have two Firefox tabs open after entering Protected Workspace when the FirePass controller has a Pre-Logon Sequence configured that includes the Switch to PWS action. Firefox opens a new browser window to complete the Pre-Logon Sequence which has two tabs opened to the FirePass login page. The second tab can be safely ignored.

F5 Networks Product Development tracking this issue as CR131501.

Workaround

To prevent two tabs from opening, the Firefox built-in Session Store feature that cases this behavior can be disabled by following the Disabling crash recovery instructions provided by the vendor.
