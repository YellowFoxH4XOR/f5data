Description

I have several origin servers distributed across the world and I want to setup preferences based on geo location so the customers have as little latency as possible.

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud
  • HTTP Load Balancer

Answer/Recommended Actions

Prerequisite

You need to have 1 Origin pool with all your origin servers added and labeled (optional)

  

Configuration

Under the HTTP Load Balancer you have to enable Show Advanced Fields on the right side to see the option for "Origin Server Subset Rules"

  

Example

In this example you have 2 origin Servers, 1 in Germany, the other one in New York.
In this case you have to create 3 rules.

  • 1 for the traffic coming from Germany
  • 1 for the traffic coming from US
  • 1 for the traffic coming from the rest of the world

Here is an example of what it looks like if you want to configure all the traffic from Germany:


Action setup to Country > Germany
You can also add the label for the RE in Germany
Under Clients > Country Codes List add Germany
Under Regional Edges add the RE from Germany

 

Additional Information

The above example is the simplest one, you can go as advance as you want, for example also add to countries near to Germany like France, Netherlands, Poland. Then it can take the traffic also from there, or you can setup the first rule just for Germany and the second one for countries near Germany so you can go and filter traffic from specific to general based on your preferences.
