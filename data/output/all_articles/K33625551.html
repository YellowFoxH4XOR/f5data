Description

If the ICAP server can't be reached by your ASM, every file attachment shows as malicious

Environment

  • BIGIP ASM configured to relay traffic to an Internet Content Adaptation Protocol (ICAP) server for analysis
  • ICAP servers are not available to the ASM

Cause

This can be encountered when your ASM cannot reach ICAP servers to verify the security of the traffic

Recommended Actions

Verify the paths by which your ASM is expected to send/receive data to/from the ICAP servers

Additional Information

The following error may be observed in /ts/log/bd.log:
Virus detection was not performed due to communication problem.
