Description

The website doesn't allow me to open a ticket, the only option for a product family is the NGINX products. Nothing about BIG-IP.

Environment

  • MyF5
  • Create a Case form

Cause

F5 Support needs to update your MyF5 account. 

Recommended Actions

If you don't see your products in the Create a Case form, or if your product serial number isn't working, you must contact F5 Support by phone or chat.
  • Call F5 Support.
  • Use live chat, available in the lower right on MyF5.

    Note: You must log in to MyF5 to use chat.
