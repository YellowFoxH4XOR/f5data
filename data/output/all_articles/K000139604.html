Description

After a new client configuration the antivirus check is not working.

The APM antivirus endpoint check can check and allow the passage for anti viruses that fulfills certain requirements such as:

  • DB Age Not Older Than (days)
  • Last Scan Time Not Older Than (days)

You might be trying to configure these variables, but it is not working for you.

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM
  • Antivirus endpoint check
  • DB Age Not Older Than (days)
  • Last Scan Time Not Older Than (days)

Cause

The variables associated with these endpoint checks might not be supported or not implemented by the endpoint provider.

You can check this by going to your BIG-IP:

  1. Press the F5 logo, and you will be navigated to the first page
  2. On the right side there is a link to: OPSWAT application integration support charts [Press it]

Here you will see:

'GetLastScanTime', which refers to the Last Scan time

and, 'GetDefinitionState', which refers to the DB age

From this OPSWAT list you can verify whether your antivirus is supported or not.

This could be a reason for your antivirus check failing.

 

Recommended Actions

Set it to 0, or use another AV
