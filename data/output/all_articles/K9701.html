Topic

Running the tcpdump utility on the standby unit of a redundant pair may capture what appears to be load-balanced traffic, which is passing through the active unit. This issue occurs only when the following conditions are true:

  • Connection mirroring is enabled
  • The tcpdump utility is run on a standby unit in a redundant pair
  • A VLAN name or interface 0.0 (all interfaces) is specified as a filter

Note: The traffic appears to be passing through the active unit because the local source and destination MAC addresses in the captured packets are those of the active unit, not the standby unit.

When filtering on a specific interface, such as 1.1, load-balanced traffic is not seen on the standby unit.

This behavior is expected; it is an artifact of the BIG-IP connection mirroring process. Packets on the active BIG-IP that require mirroring are transmitted to the standby unit. The standby unit then processes the traffic through the inbound side of the TCP stack and hands it off to TMM as if it were the active unit. However, the output stack is disconnected, and no trafffic is actually sent on the wire. This process allows the standby unit to maintain full state information for the mirrored connections in order to fail over seamlessly.
