Description
BIG-IP TACACS+ Accounting provides Accounting messages for log in, log out, and when events occur but does not provide any cmd or cmd-arg Attribute/Value (AV) pairs in any Accounting messages. This can impact Accounting visibility in third-party TACACS+ Accounting systems.

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • TACACS+ Accounting configured
  • Third-party TACACS+ Accounting server

Cause
BIG-IP fully conforms to the latest TACACS+ RFC draft (revision 12) in the content and manner of TACACS+ Accounting messages it sends. The BIG-IP does not provide per-command Authorization and Accounting and instead provides per-user Authorization and Accounting. The A/V Pairs "cmd" and "cmd-arg" are part of per-command Authorization and subsequently part of per-command Accounting. As the BIG-IP does not provide that form of Authorization it also does not provide that form of Accounting.

In providing per-user Authorization and Accounting, the BIG-IP transmits a TACACS+ Accounting message whenever an audit message is written to /var/log/audit. The Accounting message utilizes the event "cmd_acct" with the descriptive "reason" attribute as outlined in the TACACS+ RFC draft.

Recommended Actions

Configure third-party TACACS+ Accounting servers to expect per-user, not per-command Authorization and Accounting from BIG-IP devices and utilize the cmd_acct event with "reason" attribute to ensure full visibility of the BIG-IP RFC draft-compliant TACACS+ Accounting messages.

Additional Information

  • K13762: Configuring remote RADIUS or TACACS+ accounting
  • K05146413: BIG-IP TACACS+ Accounting Messages contain multiple "reason" Attribute/Value pairs
  • TACACS+ RFC Draft Revision 12
