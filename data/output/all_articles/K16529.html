Known Issue

The High-Speed Bridge (HSB) driver may incorrectly determine that the HSB chip is locked up.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is under heavy load.
  • The platform contains the HSB chip.

Impact

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may restart and temporarily fail to process traffic. Additionally, the system may experience a failsafe timeout (for example, nic_failsafe timeout), and reboot.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP system fails to process traffic for a brief period of time.
  • The BIG-IP system fails over to another host in the device group.
  • The BIG-IP system logs error messages to the /var/log/tmm file that appear similar to the following example:

    notice hsb0 warning: lost 25 consecutive loopback packets.
    notice *** TMM 0 - PDE 0 - receive failure ***
  • The BIG-IP system logs error messages to the /var/log/ltm file that appear similar to the following example:

    tmm[14139]: 01230111:2: Interface 0.1: HSB DMA lockup on receive failure.
