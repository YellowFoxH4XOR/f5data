Known Issue

When SNMP receives simultaneous queries, the data exchange between mcpd and bigsnmpd is slower.

F5 Product Development tracked this problem as CR48760 and CR53692 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.1.1 and 9.2.0 for the 9.2.0 software branch. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
