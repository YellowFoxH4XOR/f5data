Source

Description

High speed SNMP traffic from one client to F5OS-A can cause other client SNMP requests to timeout

Environment

  • F5OS-A
  • SNMP

Cause

Unknown

Answer/Recommended Actions

Slowdown the speed of SNMP GET requests to allow other client's requests to be served by F5OS-A SNMP service.

Additional Information

SNMP GET requests sent from one client with the speed in mbps around  ~ 3 Mbps or more then other clients requests will not be served and no response will be sent to the GET requests to other client.
