Topic

In accordance with IEEE 802.1q specifications, the FirePass controller supports up to 4094 VLANs, with each physical interface capable of supporting the full 4094. VLAN tags must be unique; the FirePass does not support duplicate VLAN tags across multiple physical interfaces.
