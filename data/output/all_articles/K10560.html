Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. After installing cumulative hotfix HF-603-4, HF-603-4.2, or HF-603-4.3, client devices running Windows Mobile version 6.1.0 may be unable to perform ActiveSync through FirePass. When this issue occurs, the ActiveSync screen on the Windows Mobile device displays a blank page.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR125714 and it was fixed in FirePass 6.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the FirePass release notes.

Workaround

You can work around this behavior by removing cumulative hotfixes HF-603-4, HF-603-4.2, and HF-603-4.3 from the FirePass controller. For instructions about removing a hotfix, refer to K3430: Installing FirePass hotfixes.
