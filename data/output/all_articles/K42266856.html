Security Advisory Description

A vulnerability stemming from failure to properly clean up closed OMAPI connections can lead to exhaustion of the pool of socket descriptors available to the DHCP server. Affects ISC DHCP 4.1.0 to 4.1-ESV-R15, 4.2.0 to 4.2.8, 4.3.0 to 4.3.6. Older versions may also be affected but are well beyond their end-of-life (EOL). Releases prior to 4.1.0 have not been tested. (CVE-2017-3144)

Impact

There is no impact; F5 products are not affected by this vulnerability.

Security Advisory Status

F5 Product Development has evaluated the currently supported releases for potential vulnerability, and no F5 products were found to be vulnerable.

Security Advisory Recommended Actions

None
