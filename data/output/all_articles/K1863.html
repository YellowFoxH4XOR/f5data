Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The snmp_dca health monitor does not have its own help screen. If you use the snmpdca -h command, the snmp_dca health monitor will attempt to display the snmpget help screen repeatedly.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR29421 and CR41904.

Workaround

To display the snmpget help screen, type the following command:

snmpget -h
