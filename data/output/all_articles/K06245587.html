Description

Network outage events that interrupt client connections to the BIG-IP APM device can result in client's initiating secondary APM Sessions. These secondary APM Sessions consume a second Access Session (AS) License, and likely consumes a second CCU (Concurrent Connectivity Unit) License; depending on the type of APM Session.

Environment

Relevant environmental factors:

  • BIG-IP with an APM License containing a limited number of Access Session & CCU license available.
  • More than half of the APM Licenses AS & CCU licenses are already consumed/in-use.
  • A network outage event occurs causing the APM users to loose their connections, and many of them try to establish new APM Sessions.

Cause

When a network outage event occurs, interrupting the client's APM Session, and that APM Session is still considered to be alive but idle on the BIG-IP, and the user/client initiates a new APM Session, the BIG-IP then has 2 active APM Sessions for the given client PC. Eventually, the first APM Session will Idle Timeout and be terminated. But until then, 2 APM Sessions for just one client PC consumes 2 AS and potentially 2 CCU licenses. 

When many users lose their first APM Session due to the outage event, and those many users attempt to establish new APM Sessions while their old ones are still considered active by the BIG-IP, roughly double the number of AS & CCU licenses would need to be available to support such a scenario. And if the APM License does not support roughly double the usual number of consumed APM licenses, then some users will be unable to establish their new APM Sessions, until some of the old now idle APM Sessions expire and are terminated, thereby freeing up those AS & CCU license for use by another client.

Recommended Actions

If the number of AS & CCU licenses supported by your APM Licenses is insufficient to account for a scenario such as described above, an Add-On License can be purchased that will increase the number of AS & CCU's the BIG-IP APM device can support. Once such an Add-On License has been properly applied to the BIG-IP APM device, it's AS & CCU limits will be increased accordingly.
