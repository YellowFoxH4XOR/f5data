Topic

Beginning in BIG-IP 14.0.0, F5 implemented IPv4 and IPv6 dual stack support for the management interface. For more information about configuring the management port, refer to K15040: Configuring and displaying the management IP address.

BIG-IP versions prior to 14.0.0 do not include dual stack support for the management interface. You can assign either IPv4 or IPv6 addresses to the management interface, but you cannot assign both IPv4 and IPv6 addresses.

Note: You can only configure one IPv4 address and one IPv6 address. Configurations with two IPv4 addresses or two IPv6 addresses on the management interface are not supported.

Note: Beginning in BIG-IP 14.1.0, F5 has implemented DHCPv6 support for the management interface.

Note: The BIG-IP management interface does not currently support SLAAC for an IPv6 configuration.
