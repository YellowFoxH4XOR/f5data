Known Issue

BIG-IP APM lease pool statistics may be incorrect.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system is running on a multi-blade chassis.
  • You have restarted a blade or have restarted the TMM service of a blade.
  • You use one of the following methods to obtain statistics regarding BIG-IP APM lease pools:

    • Using the command line TMCTL utility: tmctl leasepool_stat
    • Through the SNMP OID apmLeasepoolStatTable/.1.3.6.1.4.1.3375.2.6.2.1.3.1

Impact

The returned statistics are not correct.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When viewing lease pool statistics, the returned data does not include the statistics of all the blades.
  • When viewing lease pool currently assigned addresses, the values returned are all zeros.
