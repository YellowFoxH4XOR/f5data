Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If a slow client is sending a large POST to a BIG-IP ASM virtual server, the BIG-IP ASM system may buffer the POST before forwarding the data for internal processing. As a result, the client-side connection is reset, which forces the client to reconnect and re-POST the content.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR78665 and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM 9.3.1 and 9.4.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM Release Notes.
