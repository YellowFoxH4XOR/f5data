Known Issue

BIG-IP DNS (formerly known as BIG-IP GTM) does not honor Quality of Service (QoS) pool member load-balancing weights.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You configure the BIG-IP DNS system with a wide IP that uses a pool with the QoS member load-balancing method.

Impact

The pool load-balancing QoS weights does not affect load-balancing decisions.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When analyzing the QoS load-balancing decisions, the packet rate is the dominant weight for the selection.
