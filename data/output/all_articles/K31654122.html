Known Issue

VLAN interfaces configured as untagged forward packets as tagged.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are running an i15800 BIG-IP platform.
  • Your BIG-IP is configured for virtual wire.
  • You configure a VLAN to forward packets as untagged.
  • The VLAN processes traffic.

Impact

Traffic processed on the VLAN appears to fail.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptom:

  • When you analyze traffic forwarded by the BIG-IP system on the untagged VLAN, you observe that the packets are tagged.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 885949 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. Workaround

To work around this issue, you can set the system database variable bcm56xxd.hgvlan_check_disable to enable. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround:  Performing the following procedure will cause the BIG-IP to temporarily stop processing traffic.

Important: The following procedure must only be performed on i15800 platforms. Do not attempt to perform the procedure on other iSeries platforms.

  1. Log in to the TMOS Shell (tmsh).
  2. Enter the following command:

    modify /sys db bcm56xxd.hgvlan_check_disable value enable

  3. Enter the following command to restart the bcm56xxd process:

    restart /sys service bcm56xxd

  4. To exit tmsh, enter the following command:

    quit
