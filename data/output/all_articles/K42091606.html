Topic

You should consider using this procedure under the following conditions:

  • You want to determine your BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) product SKU.
  • You want to determine the permitted software range for your BIG-IP VE system.

Description

Each BIG-IP VE Version Plus license is associated with a permitted range of BIG-IP software releases. The VE license allows you to install and run BIG-IP software that falls within the permitted version range, and disables versions that fall outside of the range. The product SKU for your BIG-IP VE system indicates the software version range that is permitted for your BIG-IP VE system.

When planning to upgrade your BIG-IP VE system, you may need to verify the version range that is permitted for your BIG-IP VE system. To do so, use one of the following methods:

  • Use the product SKU version.
  • Use the Exclusive_version fields in the bigip.license file.

Prerequisites

You must meet the following prerequisite to use this procedure:

  • You have access to the BIG-IP command line.

Procedures

    Use the product SKU to determine the permitted version range your BIG-IP VE system

    The product SKU indicates the version range that is permitted for your BIG-IP VE system. To determine your product SKU version and match the SKU version to the permitted version range for your BIG-IP VE system, perform the following procedure:

    Note: You can also obtain the product SKU for your BIG-IP VE system by reviewing the email from F5 Sales that contains the BIG-IP VE registration keys.

    Impact of procedure: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

    1. Obtain the registration key(s) for the BIG-IP VE system(s).
    2. Go to the F5 Product Information Request page.
    3. Enter your contact email.
    4. For F5 Licenses / Serial Numbers, enter the base registration key(s).

      For example, the base registration key format appears similar to the following:

      AAAAA-AAAAA-AAAAA-AAAAA-AAAAA

    5. Select Submit Info Request.
    6. Review the email from F5 Networks with the subject line starting with: Information for F5 Keys.
    7. In the F5 Product Information field of the email, locate the F5 product SKU and note the following:
      • The SKU appears similar to the following format:

        F5-BIG-VE-XXX-XXX-XXX.

      • The last three characters of the SKU represent the SKU version.

        For example, the version number is V13 for the following SKU:

        F5-BIG-VE-BT-25M-V13

    8. Match the SKU version number to the permitted version range noted in K15643: BIG-IP VE Version Plus license offerings.


    Use the Exclusive_version fields in the bigip.license file to determine the permitted version range for your BIG-IP VE system

    NOTE: Before you attempt to do this, you have to ensure that the Service Check date in your license file is updated to the latest date. Your Service check date can only be updated to the latest date provided you have a valid Support Entitlement and you reactivate the license now.

    If you do not have a valid support entitlement, you will see the following in your latest license file under Licensing Information section

    Service check date : YYYYMMDD
    Service Status : As of YYYY-MM-DD there is no active service contract. This may inhibit your ability to upgrade your software.

    You may refer to K7727: License activation may be required before a software upgrade for BIG-IP  to verify the latest version you may upgrade to based on the Service check date shown on the bigip.license file in conjunction with the SKU/Exclusive_version

    After you have generated the latest bigip.license file, you can use the Exclusive_version fields to determine the permitted version range for your BIG-IP VE system. To do so, perform the following procedure:

    Impact of procedure: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

    1. Log in to the BIG-IP command line.
    2. To display the Exclusive_version fields in the bigip.license file, enter the following command:

      grep Exclusive_version /config/bigip.license

      The following example output indicates you can upgrade the system to BIG-IP VE versions through 17.x.x::

      Exclusive_version : 11.6.*
      Exclusive_version : 12.*.*
      Exclusive_version : 13.*.*
      Exclusive_version : 14.*.*
      Exclusive_version : 15.*.*
      Exclusive_version : 16.*.*
      Exclusive_version : 17.*.*
      Exclusive_version : 5.*.*
      Exclusive_version : 6.*.*
      Exclusive_version : 7.*.*
      Exclusive_version : 8.*.*
