When configuring your FirePass device for RADIUS you should verify the ports utilized by your RADIUS server and configure the same port information on your FirePass device.

If you do not define a port when configuring FirePass for RADIUS authentication and accounting, FirePass will default to the UDP port of 1645 for authentication and UDP port 1646 for accounting. Ports 1645/1646 are the original ports that were adopted for use by RADIUS and have since changed to the official IANA port assignment of 1812/1813 for authentication and accounting, respectively.

Note: FirePass is currently experiencing a known issue related to the use of authentication and accounting ports. For more information, refer to K11701: RADIUS accounting start and stop messages are sent to different ports.

Note: For more information about configuring FirePass RADIUS authentication and accounting, refer to the FirePass Controller Administrator Guide.
