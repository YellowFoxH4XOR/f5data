K54260352: Throughput spikes seen in Graphs not matching TMM clientside and serverside throughput

Type
Support Solution
Published
2021-10-17
Updated
2023-02-21
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 15.X.X, 15.0.X, 15.1.8, 15.1.7, 15.1.6, 15.1.5, 15.1.4, 15.1.3, 15.1.X, 15.1.1, 15.1.0, 15.0.1, 15.0.0, 15.1.2
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K54260352

Description
Upon reviewing the performance graphs in the GUI, you may notice significant spikes in the Throughput(bits) and Throughput(packets) graphs.  This is also present on QKview data (e.g., looking at Graphs in iHealth).
The spikes may report unrealistically high levels of traffic.
Note: Detailed throughput graphs are not affected by this issue.

Environment

  • The BIG-IP device is a physical system.
  • TMM was restarted on the system.

Cause
When the system is up and passing traffic, total bytes/pkts in/out will increase as normal.  If TMM restarts, these counters go back to zero.  However, when TMM comes up again, the counters will jump from zero to the previous known number.  This creates a very large delta number which is translated into a "spike" in the Graphs.

Recommended Actions

This issue is purely cosmetic, however it may cause concern when reviewing the performance graphs.
