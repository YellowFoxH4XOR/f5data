Known Issue

Egress trunks are distributed unevenly on BIG-IP 5000 or BIG-IP 10000 platforms that have an odd number of trunk members.

This issue occurs when one of the following conditions is met:

  • You purposefully configure an odd number of members.
  • A port goes down in a trunk that has an even number of members.

Impact

The system distributes traffic unevenly.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When a trunk on a BIG-IP 5000 or BIG-IP 10000 platform has an odd number of members, the traffic distribution to those interfaces is unbalanced.
  • Some interfaces will see more traffic than others.
