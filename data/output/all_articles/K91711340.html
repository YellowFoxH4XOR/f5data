Known Issue

You may be unable to read non-Latin characters in BIG-IP ASM PDF reports.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You export BIG-IP ASM reports in PDF format.

Impact

You cannot read non-Latin characters in PDF reports.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Non-Latin characters display correctly in the Configuration utility but not in PDF reports.
