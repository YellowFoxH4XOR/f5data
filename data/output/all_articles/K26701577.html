Topic

This article applies to BIG-IP 16.x - 17.x. For information about other versions, refer to the following articles:

  • K41984305: Overview of daemons that are monitored and restarted by the runsv utility (14.x - 15.x)
  • K69933022: Overview of daemons that are monitored and restarted by the runsv utility (12.x – 13.x)
  • K14934: Overview of daemons that are monitored and restarted by the runsv utility (11.x)

BIG-IP 16.x - 17.x uses the runsv utility to monitor specific daemons. The runsv utility monitors specific daemons and restarts the daemon in the event of a system failure.
