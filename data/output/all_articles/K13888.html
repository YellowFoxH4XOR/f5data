Known Issue

The active BIG-IP system incorrectly reports the status of its peer device. This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The BIG-IP systems are configured in a high-availability (HA) configuration using only hardwired failover.

Note: If there were no mirroring IP addresses or configsync IP addresses configured on the HA pair in the systems' previous versions, the BIG-IP systems create dummy_peer_device_objects as a substitute to their peer devices, and their configsync status is reported as Standalone.

Impact

You must access the Standby BIG-IP, in order to verify the status.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • The active BIG-IP system may incorrectly report the status of its peer device as offline, although the peer device's failover status is reported as standby. You can find this status on the device page under Device Management > Devices > Device List.
  • The active BIG-IP system incorrectly reports the status of its backup device as Initializing/Not Synced in its traffic group.

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 374168 to this issue, and has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies To box. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:

Note: Starting in BIG-IP version 11.2.0, the peer device's status is correctly reported as not watched, which is the expected behavior when using hardwired failover exclusively.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can either access the Standby BIG-IP to verify the status, or you can—if applicable to your environment—configure network failover between the HA pair. To do so, perform the procedures described in the Understanding Devices and Understanding Device Groups chapters of the BIG-IP Redundant Systems Configuration Guide.

Impact of workaround: You must have available IP addresses if the BIG-IP systems do not already have the desired network failover IP addresses configured. Additionally, you must ensure that the BIG-IP systems can communicate over the desired network failover IP addresses on port 1026 or 1028 (if mirroring is desired).
