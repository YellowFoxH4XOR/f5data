Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Configuring a Link with a translated self IP address and an ICMP monitor causes the BIG-IP GTM daemon to crash.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR63299, and CR63341, and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.2.5, 9.3, and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM Release Notes.
