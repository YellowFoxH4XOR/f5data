K000146928: Regional Edge not being used for backup route when another RE fails a healthcheck.

Type

Published
2024-11-08
Updated
2025-02-19
Applies to

  • F5 Distributed Cloud
Source

Description

  • You want F5® Distributed Cloud (XC) to prioritize a specific Regional Edge (RE), at least in cases where another RE fails a health check.

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud

Answer/Recommended Actions

In the F5® Distributed Cloud, each Regional Edge (RE) typically exports a public endpoint address to all other REs. If an RE detects a failure in the health check for a public endpoint, it can route traffic through any other RE that advertises a “healthy” endpoint.

However, as of the December 2023 release, the platform now selects a subset of REs to advertise routes, typically between 8 to 12 REs.

If you’ve configured public endpoints but restricted access to specific REs or geographical regions, health checks will only succeed from the REs that you have explicitly allowlisted. With this update, it’s possible that the REs in your allowlist may not be included in the subset chosen to export route information to other REs.

The recommended configuration for such restricted access is to define a virtual site and Advertise routes through these virtual sites to ensure consistent behavior when health checks or failovers occur.
