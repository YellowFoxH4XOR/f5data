Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When attempting to compile scripts larger than 2K in size, such as a custom BIG-IP ASM blocking response page, the system splits the script into multiple messages. TMM is unable to reassemble the multiple messages, and may dump core.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR114063 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 10.0.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM or ASM release notes.

This issue was fixed in BIGIP-9.4.8-379.3-HF1 issued for BIG-IP version 9.4.8. You may download these hotfixes or later versions of the hotfixes from the F5 Downloads site.
