Issue

In a BIG-IP GTM system, an iQuery message takes the form of gzip compressed XML data. In an effort to reduce CPU utilization by the big3d and gtmd daemons, beginning in BIG-IP GTM 9.4.7 and 10.0.0, the value for the deflateInit parameter was changed from 9 (nine) to 2 (two).

The deflateInit parameter is used in gzip to set the desired compromise between speed and compression:  1 gives best speed, 9 gives best compression, 0 gives no compression.

Note: The deflateInit parameter is hardcoded and cannot be changed by a BIG-IP administrator.

This change improves the overall performance of the BIG-IP GTM system by significantly reducing the CPU cycles required by the two daemons. This is mostly noticeable when dealing with large BIG-IP GTM configurations resulting in a complex mesh of iQuery connections between the BIG-IP GTM system and other BIG-IP systems.

Note: This change also applies to other BIG-IP products that use iQuery messages to communicate, such as BIG-IP Link Controller systems in a redundant pair, although the change is trivial.

F5 Product Development has tracked this change in behavior as CR102078. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM Release Notes.

Additionally, this change in behavior was also introduced in Hotfix-BIGIP-9.3.1-66.0-HF5, issued for BIG-IP 9.3.1 and Hotfix-BIGIP-9.4.6-423.0-HF2, issued for BIG-IP 9.4.6. You may download these hotfixes, or later versions of the cumulative hotfixes, from the F5 Downloads site.
