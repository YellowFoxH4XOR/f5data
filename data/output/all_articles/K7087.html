Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Every time a call is made to the libuser library, the libuser library leaks memory.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR56745 and CR62819, and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.1.3, 9.3, and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, GTM, ASM, or Link Controller Release Notes.
