Topic

The Energy Policy Act of 2005 is a statute that was passed by the United States Congress on July 29, 2005, and signed into law on August 8, 2005. Part of the Act extends Daylight Saving Time by approximately four weeks, beginning in 2007. Daylight Saving Time in the United States will begin at 2 a.m. on the second Sunday of March and end at 2 a.m. on the first Sunday of November. In most of the countries of Western Europe, including the countries that are members of the European Union, Daylight Saving Time begins at 1 a.m. GMT on the last Sunday of March and ends at 1 a.m. GMT on the last Sunday of October.

For more information about the Energy Policy Act, refer to the U.S. Department of Energy website.

As of FirePass version 6.0.1, FirePass automatically adjusts to the new Daylight Saving Time standards as outlined in the Energy Policy Act of 2005.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR72049, and it was fixed in FirePass version 6.0.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the FirePass Release Notes. Additionally, Hotfix HF-72049-1 has been issued for FirePass versions 5.5, 5.5.1, 5.5.2, and 6.0.0. You may download these hotfixes from the F5 Networks Downloads site.

For instructions about how to obtain a hotfix, refer to K167: Downloading software from F5 Networks.

For instructions about installing a hotfix, refer to K3430: Installing hotfixes.

Important:  Hotfix HF-72049-1 does not provide compliance with Daylight Savings Time changes for the New Zealand time zone.  If your FirePass controller is configured to use the New Zealand time zone, refer to K7968: FirePass compliance with New Zealand Daylight Saving Time.
