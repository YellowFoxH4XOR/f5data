Environment

  • BIG-IP ASM devices managed by BIG-IQ system.
  • When accessing "Security ›› Reporting : Application : Charts", you see the response "There is no data to display either due to the lack of relevant traffic or due to the settings of the filter."
  • When access "Security ›› Overview : Application : Traffic", no charts for any report, and Security Policy pull down does not show any ASM policy.

Cause

Your BIG-IP device may not be able to collect statistics locally since BIG-IQ statistics collection is enabled for that device.

Recommended Actions

  • You can Disable then re-enable statistics collection for the BIG-IP ASM locally
  • It may be necessary to disable statistics collection for the BIG-IP ASM on the BIG-IQ device
Note: The changes are not immediate and may take some time to propagate to other devices.
