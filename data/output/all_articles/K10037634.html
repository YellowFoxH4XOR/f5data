Known Issue

On rare occasions, the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may restart when you enable mirroring on Clustered Multiprocessing-capable (CMP) devices.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have two or more CMP-capable BIG-IP systems in a high availability (HA) configuration.

    Note: For more information about CMP-capable devices, refer to K14358: Overview of Clustered Multiprocessing (11.3.0 and later).

  • You enable session mirroring between the devices.

Impact

The BIG-IP system fails to process traffic while TMM restarts.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP system fails to process traffic for a brief period.
  • Your BIG-IP system fails over to another host in the device group.
  • TMM produces a core file in the /var/core directory.
