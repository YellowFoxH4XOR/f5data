Topic

The Address Resolution Logic (ARL) tables in the following BIG-IP switch appliances can learn a total of 8191 (8K) MAC addresses:

  • BIG-IP 1000, 2000, 2400, 5000, 5100, and 5110

MAC address limits are cumulative for the entire unit. There are no per-port limits up to the cumulative total for the entire unit.

The MAC addresses are stored in the ARL tables of the switch ASIC chips.

Note: For information about the MAC address limits on BIG-IP 1500, 1600, 3400 (all models), 3600, 6400, 6800, 6900, 8400, 8800, 8900, 8950, and 11050 platforms, refer to K6567: Maximum MAC addresses supported by various BIG-IP platforms.
