Description

You are trying to connect a Digi PortServer to your BIG-IP's console port, but aren't receiving an output

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • Digi PortServer

Cause

There may be an issue with the combined pinout of the cable

Recommended Actions

Review the pinout detailed in Additional Information to verify that your cables are connecting the right pins together 

Additional Information

Digi                F5
1 - DSR ---------- 2 DTR
2 - RTS ---------- 8 CTS
3 - GND ---------- 4 GND
4 - TD  ---------- 6 RD
5 - RD  ---------- 3 TD
6 - SG  ---------- 5 SG
7 - CTS ---------- 1 RTS
8 - DTR ---------- 7 DSR
