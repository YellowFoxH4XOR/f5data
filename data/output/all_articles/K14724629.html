Description

iControl REST does not support to manage ZoneRunner (BIND) functionality.
There is no supported way to do below article through iControl REST

  • K7032: Freezing zone files to allow manual update to ZoneRunner-managed zone files
     

Environment

  • BIG-IP DNS 
  • any version
  • ZoneRunner
  • BIND

Cause
By design
 

Recommended Actions

For assistance in the configuration, operations, and maintenance of BIND, refer to An Overview of BIND 9 Documentation and/or DNS and BIND, 5th Edition by Cricket Liu, Paul Albitz.
