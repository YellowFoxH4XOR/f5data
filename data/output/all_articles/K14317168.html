K14317168: Licensing via BIG-IQ over management to an AWS BIG-IP Instance failed

Type
Support Solution
Published
2019-12-05
Updated
2023-02-21
Applies to
  • Public Cloud
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K14317168

Description

When applying a license from an on-premise BIG-IQ to an Amazon Web Services (AWS) BIG-IP over the management network, the operation may timeout at the Client Hello and fail to license.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IP instance in Amazon Web Services Environment
  • GRE tunnel over the management network.

Cause

Responses transmitted from the BIG-IP management interface may be too large to reach the destination.

Recommended Actions

  1. Configure a self-ip on the BIG-IP instance.
  2. Discover and license the BIG-IP instance via the BIG-IQ utilizing the self-ip (over TMM network).
