Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Normally, when you delete a connection using the bigpipe conn delete command, BIG-IP sends a reset to both the client and the node. However, when you clear a connection that is accelerated by the PVA, BIG-IP does not send a reset to the client or the node. This is a limitation in the PVA architecture.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR22494.
