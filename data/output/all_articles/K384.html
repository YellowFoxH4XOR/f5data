Topic

The watchdog card monitors the CPU for a rare case in which the CPU goes into a tight infinite loop. In the unlikely event that this loop occurs, the watchdog card forces a reboot and the secondary BIG-IP Controller takes over as the active system. The takeover is communicated automatically through the failover cable.

You cannot configure the watchdog card (it has no configuration parameters). This is hard-set in the kernel.
