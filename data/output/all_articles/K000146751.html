K000146751: Latency issue when traffic is not routing through closest PoP

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-11-08
Updated
2024-11-08
Applies to
  • F5 Distributed Cloud Services
  • F5 Distributed Cloud
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000146751

What Happened?

When accessing the application, latency is seen as traffic from one region is routed to another region that is not the closest Point of Presence (PoP) for you.

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud Console

Resolution/Answer

  • The majority of the traffic from/to ASXXX is delivered in selected regions because we are receiving traffic from this provider.
  • We have the capacity to peer with ASXXX in your region, but we don't receive routes from you.
  • You have to establish peering to improve the latency by following Step 4 here Configure BGP Peers
