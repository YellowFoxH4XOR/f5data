Policy Information

F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of the Herculon DDoS Hybrid Defender, effective April 1, 2018. F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of the Herculon SSL Orchestrator, effective July 1, 2018. F5 is replacing these products with corresponding BIG-IP DDoS Hybrid Defender and BIG-IP SSL Orchestrator products. F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of the Herculon iSeries platforms (Herculon i2800, Herculon i5800, and Herculon i10800), effective July 1, 2018. Due to the early retirement of these products, and the availability of equivalent replacement products, F5 is providing an abbreviated Advance Product Notification to F5 customers and partners announcing intent to retire the products listed in the following tables.

Technical Support

F5 maintains lifecycle policies that allow customers to enjoy years of both support and new software releases. In the case of an abbreviated lifecycle, contact your F5 Partner Business Manager of Inside Sales Representative for transition details. Customers whose products are designated End of Life (EoL), and who are currently under maintenance contracts, will continue to receive technical support until the the support contract renewal date expires. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the Service Contract Renewal date expires.
