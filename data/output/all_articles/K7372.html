Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP system internal trunks are created between the switch chips and the TMM and are monitored by the lacpd process. In the event of an internal trunk failure, lacpd notifies the failover daemon through the high availability (HA) table, and the BIG-IP system fails over. In rare cases, the LACP packets used to monitor internal trunk links are sent to non-TMM destinations, which can cause a switchboard failsafe to occur.

When this issue occurs, the following error message is logged to the /var/log/tmm file:

Switchboard failsafe by lacpd

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR76774 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.1.3, and 9.3.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
