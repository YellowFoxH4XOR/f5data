Description

This article explains how many simulated DDoS events a customer can request from F5 Distributed Cloud, both for customers with an active contract and for POC customers in the evaluation phase.

Environment

  • F5 Distributed Cloud
    • XC Routed DDoS
    • XC Load Balancers

Answer

F5 Distributed Cloud offers the ability to initiate simulated DDoS attacks against customers' destinations in order to validate the effectiveness of the configured protections.

Customer TypeSimulation LimitCost
Active contract customer Up to 2 simulations per year of contracted servicesNo additional cost
POC Customer1 simulation for the duration of the POCFree of charge


Notes:

  • Simulations for contracted customers reset annually based on the contracted service period.
  • For POC customers, the free simulation is available only for the duration of the POC.
