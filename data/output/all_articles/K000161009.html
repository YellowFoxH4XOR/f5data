K000161009: The etcd processes of Velos controller becomes out of sync after the software upgrade

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-04-28
Updated
2026-04-28
Applies to
  • F5OS
  • F5OS-C: 1.8.X, 1.8.2, 1.8.1, 1.8.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000161009

Description

The etcd processes of Velos controller becomes out of sync after the software upgrade.

As a result, the cluster will become an unhealthy state and will observe the CLI output and openshift.log similar to the following examples.


# show cluster

...

cluster cluster-status summary-status ""


/var/log/openshift.log

YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS.XXXXXX+XX:XX - ETCD: No controllers are currently active - reset delay in launch of the third etcd docker container


Environment

  • F5OS-C 1.8.2
  • Upon the upgrading, the etcd processes requires a quorum of three and the third etcd process is not being launched. It leaves the etcds on both controllers in an inconsistent state.


Cause

F5 tracks this as ID2162197 and fixed at Engineering HotFix at F5OS-C 1.8.


Recommended Actions

  • There is a workaround to mitigate this issue. 
    • On the active controller, manually launch the third etcd instance, by running this command at the root shell
    • /etc/etcd/launch_etcd.sh
    • Then issue a controller failover.
  • Please contact F5 Global Support and open a case to ask for EHF of ID2162197.
