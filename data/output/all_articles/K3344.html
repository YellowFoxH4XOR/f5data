Known Issue



This is the result of a known issue. The loopback interface is not included in the ifIndex table. SNMP OIDs that references the ifIndex table will not report statistics for IP addresses bound to the loopback interface.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR33583, CR33584, CR41915, and CR41916.
