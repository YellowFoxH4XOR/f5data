Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP WebAccelerator system's pvac daemon may restart and produce a core file if Hot Cache statistics are retrieved. This issue has only been reported to occur under conditions where the Hot Cache is loaded in excess of 35 Mb/s.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR89472 and it was fixed in BIG-IP WebAccelerator version 9.4.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP WebAccelerator Release Notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.3-HF2 issued for BIG-IP version 9.4.3. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Networks Downloads site.

For information about the F5 Networks hotfix policy, refer to K4918: Overview of F5 Networks critical issue hotfix policy.

For information about how to manage F5 Networks product hotfixes, refer to K6845: Managing F5 Networks product hotfixes.
