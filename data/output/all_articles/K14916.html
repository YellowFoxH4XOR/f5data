Known Issue

The BIG-IP Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) process may not propagate IPv6 router advertisements between Virtual Local Area Networks (VLANs) in a VLAN group.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You have IPv6 hosts and routers on different VLANs in a VLAN group sending router advertisement messages.

Impact

IPv6 address auto-configuration may not work.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • IPv6 address auto-configuration does not function correctly on hosts connected to affected VLANs.
  • IPv6 router advertisement messages are not propagating between VLANs.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can put clients and routers on the same VLAN, or use manual configuration for IPv6 routes.

Impact of workaround:  Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
