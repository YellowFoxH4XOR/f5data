Known Issue

In rare situations, the BigDB.dat file may become corrupt.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You have a very large and complex configuration.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic and fail over if configured as part of a high availability (HA) system. 

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The system fails to load the BIG-IP configuration.
