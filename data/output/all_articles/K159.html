Topic

iQuery is an F5 Networks, UDP-based protocol that collects configuration and metric information and exchanges that information between 3-DNS Controllers and other F5 Networks products running the big3d agent.

Port 4353 is registered with IANA as the standard port for the F5 Networks iQuery protocol; however, you can also use port 245. All replies are returned to randomly assigned ephemeral ports, which consist of ports 1024 through 5000. If you do not want to use ephemeral ports, you can change the port setting. Refer to K161: Controlling iQuery port usage to change this setting.

The specific ports that are used for iQuery communication between a 3-DNS and a big3d agent depend on certain settings on the 3-DNS Controller. Use the tables below to help configure firewalls and other devices to allow free traffic flow for your 3-DNS Controllers and servers.

Note: The US version of iQuery allows you to to enable encryption. The International version does not support encryption. iQuery uses the Blowfish CBC encryption algorithm. To be valid, the iQuery packets must comply with CRC-32.
