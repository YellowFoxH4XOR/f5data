K451: Time to complete boot on BIG-IP 2000 and BIG-IP 5000 switch appliances

Topic

BIG-IP 2000 and BIG-IP 5000 switch appliances should complete boot and begin passing traffic within 50 seconds and should offer a shell prompt in approximately one minute, when using a minimal configuration. Complex configurations increase the amount of time it takes for the system to begin passing traffic and responding to user input.
