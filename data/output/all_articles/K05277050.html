Description

After activating the BIG-IQ License, few of the ELA Offerings are showing error message:
Failure to generate key - 51148: - Error generating registration key, "AAAAAAA-AAAAAAA" :

Environment

  • License Reactivation
  • BIG-IQ Pool
  • ELA Offerings

Cause
None

Recommended Actions

Manually reactivate the ELA Offerings:

  1. Click on license Name to expand the ELA offerings.
  2. Click on offering name.
  3. Copy dossier from Device Dossier box. 
  4. Paste the dossier into the Enter Your Dossier box, and then click Next.
  5. Copy the license and paste it into License text on BIG-IQ.
  6. Click on Activate in the bottom right of the screen.

Repeat this for each of the ELA license offerings that shows an error. 
If the error persists, please contact F5 Technical Support for further assistance.
