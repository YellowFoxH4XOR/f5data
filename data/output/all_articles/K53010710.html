Known Issue

Proactive Bot Defense JavaScript challenges may introduce high latencies and cause some browsers to display a blank page.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system has Proactive Bot Defense and fingerprinting enabled.
  • The client is using Internet Explorer 11.

    Note: Internet Explorer 11 is known to be affected by this issue. However, other browsers may also be affected.

Impact

When clients access Proactive Bot Defense challenges, the browser displays a blank page.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The Proactive Bot Defense challenge remains on a blank page, never allowing access to the intended website.
