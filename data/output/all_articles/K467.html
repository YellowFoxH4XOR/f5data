K467: It is possible to partially remove a link by deleting its self IP address and VLAN

Known Issue

This is most likely the result of a known issue. If you delete a link indirectly by removing the self IP address and the VLAN associated with the link, you are not completely deleting the link; therefore, BIG-IP Link Controller will continue to report the link's statistics.

This issue is cosmetic. BIG-IP Link Controller will not use the link for load balancing.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR28072 and CR41833.
