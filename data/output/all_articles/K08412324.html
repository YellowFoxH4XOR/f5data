K08412324: Transfer of large files over remote desktop fails

Type
Support Solution
Published
2020-01-29
Updated
2025-02-06
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP APM: 13.1.1
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K08412324

Description

Transferring files larger than 2 GB over remote desktop fails
 

Environment

  • APM
  • RDP 
  • LTM

Cause

By design, there is a 2GB size limitation to the RDP clipboard
 

Recommended Actions

Follow Microsoft's recommendations for transferring large files to an RDP host.
 

Additional Information

Copying files larger than 2GB over a remote desktop services connection
