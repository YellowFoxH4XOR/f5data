Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The SNMP sysContact and sysLocation settings are synchronized between units in a redundant pair. Since the sysContact and sysLocation settings can be unique to each specific system, the settings should not be synchronized between units in a redundant pair.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR97671 and it was fixed in 9.4.7 and 10.0.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, GTM, Link Controller, or WebAccelerator Release Notes.
