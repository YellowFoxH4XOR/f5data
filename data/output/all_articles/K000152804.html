Description

ConfigSync and failover functionality between BIG-IP tenants on F5OS chassis may fail, with HA status showing as "disconnected." This issue can occur when the self IP addresses assigned to the dedicated HA VLAN are incorrectly subnetted, resulting in insufficient usable IP addresses for proper HA communication. No specific error messages may be present, but HA and ConfigSync will not establish connectivity.

Environment

  • Product: F5OS - Chassis
  • Version: 1.8.0
  • Platform: F5-BIG-BT-R10900, C128
  • Modules: BIG-IP LTM tenants on F5OS
  • Configuration: High Availability (HA) setup using a dedicated VLAN for HA/ConfigSync traffic

Cause

Incorrect subnetting on the dedicated HA VLAN resulted in only two usable host addresses, which is insufficient for proper HA operation between tenants. This prevents the establishment of HA and ConfigSync communication.

Recommended Actions

  • Review the subnet mask and address allocation for the dedicated HA VLAN used for ConfigSync and failover.
  • Ensure the subnet provides enough usable IP addresses for all required HA endpoints (at least one per tenant, plus any floating/self IPs as needed).
  • Reconfigure the HA VLAN with a larger subnet (for example, /29 or /28 instead of /30) to allow for additional usable host addresses.
  • After correcting the subnetting, reassign the self IP addresses to the tenants and verify HA and ConfigSync status.
  • Confirm that all other network and VLAN configurations are correct and that there are no overlapping or conflicting subnets.
