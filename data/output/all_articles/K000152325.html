Description

During an upgrade of an F5 BIG-IP LTM HA cluster, the active unit may unexpectedly transition to standby, resulting in a loss of service. This behavior is accompanied by log messages such as:

warning sod[xxxx]: 01140029:4: HA vlan_fs <VLAN_NAME> fails action is failover and abort tm.
notice sod[xxxx]: 010c005c:5: Failover condition reported ... for traffic group traffic-group-1, it will not be able to go active.
notice sod[xxxx]: 010c0052:5: Standby for traffic group traffic-group-1.
notice sod[xxxx]: 010c0018:5: Standby
notice sod[xxxx]: 010c009f:5: Failover condition reported, this device cannot go active.

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • VLAN fail-safe is enabled

Cause

VLAN fail-safe is enabled on several VLANs, including those used for internal or HA communication between BIG-IP units. When one unit is taken offline (for an upgrade, reboot, or failure), the remaining active unit stops receiving traffic on these VLANs. This triggers the VLAN fail-safe mechanism, causing the active unit to go into standby or trigger a failover, even though this does not represent a true failure of user/production traffic. As a result, both units may become unable to handle production traffic and cause a service outage.

Recommended Actions

1. Review VLAN Failsafe Usage:

Enable VLAN failsafe only on VLANs that carry user or production traffic. Do not enable VLAN failsafe on VLANs used solely for HA or internal communication between BIG-IP units.

2. Upgrade and Maintenance Best Practices:

Before performing upgrades, planned reboots, or maintenance activities that take one unit offline:

  • Temporarily disable VLAN failsafe on the remaining active unit, or at least on any VLAN where the loss of expected network traffic will occur during maintenance.
  • After completing maintenance and restoring normal operation, re-enable VLAN failsafe as appropriate for production/user-facing VLANs.
