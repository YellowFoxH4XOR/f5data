Known Issue

On rare occasions, the BIG-IP system may reboot when a High-Speed Bridge (HSB) transmitter failure occurs.

The conditions for this issue have not been fully determined. This issue may occur on rare occasions when the following condition is met:

  • You have a BIG-IP 5250 platform or a vCMP host with one or more Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP) guests.

Impact

Traffic processing is interrupted while the BIG-IP system restarts, and if the BIG-IP system is in a high availability (HA) configuration, a failover event occurs.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP system has unexpectedly rebooted.
  • Your BIG-IP system fails to process traffic for a brief period.
  • Your BIG-IP system fails over to another host in the device group.
  • In the /var/log/tmm file, you observe a message similar to the following example:

    panic: hsb interface 1 DMA lockup on transmitter failure.

Resolution

Status

Workaround

Although there is no workaround for this issue, BIG-IP 13.0.0 and later includes an internal counter that tracks occurrences of these types of HSB transmitter failures to better diagnose the issue.
