Known Issue

Under high traffic conditions, the datastor process may fail to cache new objects.

In high velocity traffic patterns, the constantly changing content leaves many documents with a high resistance to eviction in the cache. As a result, caching new documents may fail.

Impact

The efficacy of datastor process caching may be reduced.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The datastor process fails to cache new objects.
