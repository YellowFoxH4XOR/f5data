K11211142: How to test e-mail notifications for expired SSL certificates

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-11-15
Updated
2025-10-29
Applies to
  • Virtual Editions
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 8.2.X, 8.1.X
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K11211142

Description
After configuring SMTP e-mail alerts for expiring or expired SSL/TLS certificates, you may wish to test them.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ CM
  • Unmanaged BIG-IP certificates

Cause
Testing a new configuration ensures that the feature is working as expected and that you understand the format of the e-mail notification

Recommended Actions
Test the BIG-IQ CM e-mail alerts for expired certificates by generating an expiring certificate on a BIG-IP

  1. On the BIG-IP, generate a test certificate with a Lifetime of 1 day by navigating to System  ››  Certificate Management : Traffic Certificate Management : SSL Certificate List
  2. Click the Create button and fill in the required fields as desired. Set the Lifetime field to 1 day and click Finished.
  3. On the BIG-IQ, rediscover the device. Under Devices tab, click the link for the BIG-IP that you created the certificate on and then go to the Services tab. Click the Re-discover button near the top
  4. After re-discovering the BIG-IP, click the Re-import button
  5. You should now see the certificate and its expiration date under Configuration  ››  Certificate Management  ››  Certificates & Keys on the BIG-IQ
You should receive an e-mail alert for the expiring certificate after some time.

Additional Information
When you discover a BIG-IP device, BIG-IQ automatically imports metadata about the device's SSL certificates used for traffic management so you can monitor their expiration dates. These SSL certificates display as unmanaged on BIG-IQ. Before you can deploy an SSL certificate and key to a BIG-IP device, you must convert it to managed by uploading its certificate/key to BIG-IQ.
