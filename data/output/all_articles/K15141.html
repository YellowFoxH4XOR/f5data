Known Issue

Bridging traffic between two interfaces on a child VLAN in VLAN group mode may be disrupted for systems in standby mode.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • You are running one of the following BIG-IP platforms:

    • 2000 series platforms
    • 4000 series platforms
  • There is an unresolved loop in the network.

When you have configured the BIG-IP system to bridge traffic between two interfaces assigned to a child VLAN that is a member of a VLAN group, and packets arrive on another child VLAN of the same VLAN group, the system may create conflicting dynamic forwarding database (FDB) entries. The conflicting FDB entries may cause the BIG-IP system to drop packets.

Impact

Bridged traffic may be disrupted.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • The standby system fails to bridge traffic, as expected.
  • Packet captures may show the BIG-IP system dropping packets on the child VLAN.
