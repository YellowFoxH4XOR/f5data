Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) restarts after a Large Scale Nat (LSN) pool member change.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You change LSN pool members while processing traffic.

Impact

TMM restarts, causing all traffic processing to stop.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Changing LSN pool members while processing traffic causes TMM to restart.

Workaround

To mitigate or prevent this behavior from occurring, F5 recommends that you change LSN pool members during a maintenance window when there is little or no traffic. If you have a redundant system, make changes on the standby system, failover, and then synchronize the changes to the now standby system.
