K7283: BIG-IP ASM may truncate responses that do not contain a defined Content-Length header

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. BIG-IP ASM may fail to fully process a server response that does not contain a defined Content-Length header. As a result, the client may receive truncated responses.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR76653 and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM 9.4.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM Release Notes.
