Description

In some cases you may wonder why after 10 or more URLs, within 5 minutes, have been sent through they are not being seen as qualified.

Environment

  • Brute Force Protection, URL qualification
  • Login pages, Client-side integrity challenges (such as CAPTCHA or JavaScript injection) 
  • ASM/AdvWAF

Cause

For a URL to be set as 'qualified' it must receive at least 10 consecutive HTML responses within 5 minutes. But what constitutes a 'qualified' HTML response?
