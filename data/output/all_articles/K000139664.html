Description

Unable to establish HA between BIG-IP tenants running on rSeries r5x00 or above.

Environment

  • rSeries platforms except r2x00 / r4x00
  • One or more tenants is in forced offline state.
  • HA/ConfigSync
  • Self IP connectivity

Cause

ID1291121 / ID1301897.

ID1291121 prevents connections from working as expected while a tenant is forced offline.

ID1301897 prevents TMM from becoming ready when it is restarted while a tenant is forced offline. This might surface after actions that restart TMM, such as provisioning a module.

When reviewing the output of show sys ha-status, note that TMM is not ready-for-world.

As a result of these issues you may also see port exhaustion in the CMI channel used for HA (TCP port 4353). You may also see elevated TMM CPU usage.

Recommended Actions

To recover from the issue, release tenants from the offline state. In some cases you may need to restart TMM or reboot the tenant.

To prevent the issue occurring, do not attempt to establish device trust while one or more tenants are forced offline, and do not take actions that will restart TMM while in forced offline.

Additional Information

ID1291121 can also impact outbound connections from the system to configured services (for example, AAA servers) while forced offline.
