K000159701: Is License Reactivation required when a new support contract starts

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-01-26
Updated
2026-01-26
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.1.0, 17.1.2, 17.1.1, 17.1.X, 17.5.X, 17.5.1, 17.5.0, 17.1.3
Source

Description

You received a Purchase Order for a support contract renewal and want to know if license reactivation is required for the support contract to begin.

 

Environment

  • Support contract
  • Purchase order
  • License reactivation

Cause

None

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

License reactivation is not required on the device for the support contract to start.

The support contract will begin automatically based on the Purchase Order start date.

 

Additional Information

License reactivation may be required when performing software upgrades, not for support contract renewals.

K7727: License activation may be required before a software upgrade for BIG-IP
