Known Issue

Unexpected HA Group failover may occur after you upgrade a device.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is configured with an HA Group that is disabled.
  • You use the HA group Failover Method for the configured Traffic Group.
  • You upgrade your BIG-IP system with a BIG-IP release prior to 11.5.0.
  • You reboot the upgraded device.

Impact

After the upgrade reboot, the BIG-IP system may fail over to a peer device.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • After you upgrade to an affected release, your BIG-IP system fails over to a peer based on the HA Group availability of the disabled HA Group.
  • When you view the configured HA Group, it shows as disabled.
  • In the /var/log/ltm file, you may observe log messages similar to the following example, indicating that the disabled HA Group is active:


    notice sod[<pid>]: 010c006e:5: All devices in traffic group /<partition>/<traffic group> now have a HA group.
    notice tmm[<pid>]: 01340001:5: HA Connection with peer <peer ip address>:1029 for traffic-group /<partition>/<traffic group> established.
    notice tmm1[<pid>]: 01340001:5: HA Connection with peer     <peer ip address>:1029 for traffic-group /<partition>/<traffic group> established.
    notice mcpd[<pid>]: 01071451:5: Received CMI hello from /<partition>/<BIG-IP hostname>
    notice sod[<pid>]: 010c0045:5: Leaving active, group score 10 peer group score 100.
    notice sod[<pid>]: 010c0052:5: Standby for traffic group /<partition>/<traffic group>.
    notice sod[<pid>]: 010c0018:5: Standby

Workaround

To work around this issue, after you complete the upgrade, and before you reboot the system, you can remove the disabled HA Group from any configured Traffic Groups. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) by typing the following command:

    tmsh

  2. Check the configured Traffic Groups for HA Group associations by typing the following command:

    list /cm traffic-group

    Example output:

    cm traffic-group traffic-group-1 {
    ha-group ID504494
    unit-id 1
    }

  3. Remove the HA Group from the Traffic Group by using the following command syntax:

    modify /cm traffic-group <traffic group name> ha-group none

    For example:

    modify /cm traffic-group traffic-group-1 ha-group none

  4. Save the configuration changes by typing the following command:

    save /sys config

  5. To exit tmsh, type the following command:

    quit
