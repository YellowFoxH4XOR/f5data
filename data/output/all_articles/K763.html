Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When you configure virtual servers on a BIG-IP Link Controller, but do not include them in a wide IP, the BIG-IP Link Controller Configuration utility will not display statistics for them in the Link Statistics section. Statistics will be reported in the BIG-IP statistics section.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR35019 and CR41878.

Workaround

To display statistics in the Link Statistics section for virtual servers that are not currently included in a wide IP, create an otherwise unused wide IP to contain them.
