Known Issue

A PEM policy with rules referencing the same rating group may cause the BIG-IP system to send additional Multiple Services Credit Control (MSCC) in the Credit Control Request (CCR).

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured the PEM Gy listener with a PEM policy that contains more than one policy rule referencing the same rating group.
  • Your BIG-IP system is running on the affected version.

Impact

Each policy rule that references the same rating group will cause the BIG-IP system to send an additional MSCC in the CCR diameter message.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • For each policy rule referencing the same rating group, the BIG-IP system will send an additional MSCC in the CCR diameter message. With a tcpdump capture, you may observe the system sending the additional MSCC.

    For example, with two policy rules referencing the same rating group, two quota buckets are created with the same rating group ID for the PEM session. One of the quota buckets may be provisioned, while the other is in a pending state.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can assign the different rules that use the same rating group into separate policies, and associate the policies with the affected PEM listener.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
