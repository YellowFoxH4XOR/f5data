Known Issue

In BIG-IP 4.2 through 4.5, the Broadcom 582x driver does not return an error if the hardware operation times out; however, BIG-IP will trigger a failover if the hardware fails entirely or if the operations returns an error.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR20461.
