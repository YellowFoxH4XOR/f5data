Description

After upgrading the VELOS system controllers, you get an unavailable status for a controller, it's unresponsive, and you're unable to SSH to the management address. Also attempts to power on the host system from the Always-On Management (AOM) menu fail.

Environment

  • VELOS system controller
  • F5OS upgrade
  • Firmware upgrade

Cause

The system is in a degraded state that can only be cleared by re-seating the controller blade.

When you upgrade the software, if required, the system will also perform a BIOS firmware update. After the update the controller is power cycled. This is managed by the LOP through the CPLD. When the graceful shutdown of the controller by the OS times out, then LOP issues the override (forceful) power-off to the CPLD.

For example:
25461 | 02/14/2024 | 17:52:31 | 6 | AOM | 0x81 | override host power-off accepted event

However if the power-down is never completed by the CPLD, the LOP will wait indefinitely for that to happen. Because the CPLD is presumably stuck somewhere in its power-down state machine, it rejects the subsequent power-on and power-off requests issued by the AOM menu.

For example:
25475 | 02/19/2024 | 19:22:48 | 6 | AOM | 0x81 | power-on rejected event
25479 | 02/19/2024 | 19:29:04 | 6 | AOM | 0x81 | override host power-off rejected event
25481 | 02/19/2024 | 19:31:21 | 6 | AOM | 0x81 | power-on rejected event

Recommended Actions

Physically re-seat the affected system controller using the following instructions:

  1. Insert a 5mm hex wrench into the jackscrew receptacle on the affected system controller.
  2. Turn it counter-clockwise to eject the controller. The blade slowly backs out of the chassis as you turn the jackscrew.
  3. Continue turning the wrench until the controller ceases to move any further.
  4. Slide the controller out a few inches.
  5. Reinsert the controller into the slot until the jackscrew contacts the mating receptacle in the chassis.
  6. Turn the jackscrew clockwise to draw the system controller into the chassis to secure the controller in the chassis.

    Note: You may want to use a Torque jackscrew to 4 N*m (35 in*lb) for this step.
