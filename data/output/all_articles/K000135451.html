K000135451: F5 requires multi-factor authentication (MFA) for account login

To provide additional security protections for both you and F5 systems, beginning August 8, 2023, customers are required to set up and use multi-factor authentication (MFA) to access MyF5. MFA is an authentication method that requires you to provide two or more verification factors to gain access to an online account or resource. Rather than asking only for a username and password, F5 will now require a secondary verification factor, which greatly decreases the likelihood of a successful cyberattack.

To set up two-step authentication, you must enroll with one (or more) of the following authentication methods:

  • Auth0 Guardian app enrollment
  • Google Authenticator enrollment
  • Security keys
  • WebAuthn

Note: At this time, F5 does not offer SMS as a verification factor. If you previously enrolled in MFA on MyF5 using only SMS, the system will prompt you to enroll again using email or Google Authenticator as your selected verification factor.

You will only be prompted to authenticate your identity once for each eight-hour user session as long as you use the same browser without logging out. Note that if you switch browsers (for example, you switch from Google Chrome to Mozilla Firefox), you must authenticate on the new browser but the sessions for each browser will last eight hours.

Enroll in MFA

To enroll in MFA for MyF5, you must choose a minimum of one factor (security keys or Google Authenticator). You can choose to add more than one factor.

You will have 10 minutes to enroll one or more of the available MFA factors before your login session is timed out.

After you receive your factor response, you have five (5) minutes to complete setting up the factor before the process times out and you have to try again.

If you enroll more than one factor, you will only have to verify one when you log in the next time, but you will have the option to select the one you want to use.

Auth0 Guardian app enrollment

  1. On your desktop or PC, sign in to MyF5. If you have not previously enrolled in two-factor authentication, you will be prompted to do so.
    1. On the Setup 2-step authentication page, select Auth0 Guardian.
    2. Select iPhone or Android.
    3. Select Next.
    4. A QR code displays.
  2. On your mobile device, download and install the Auth0 Guardian app from your device's app store, if you don't already have it.
    1. Launch the Auth0 Guardian app on your mobile device.
    2. Select the plus (+) sign or select Add Account to scan a new code.
    3. Allow Auth0 Guardian permissions, as appropriate for your device.
    4. Scan the QR code displayed on your desktop or PC screen.
    5. For the verification prompt, ensure the account is successfully added.
    6. Select Finish on your desktop.

    Google Authenticator enrollment

    1. On the Setup 2-step authentication page, for Google Authenticator, select Setup.
    2. On the Setup Google Authenticator page, select iPhone or Android. If you do not already have Google Authenticator installed, you can select the link to download the app to your device.
    3. Scan the barcode or select Can't scan? and follow the prompts, then select Next.
    4. For Enter Code, enter the code displayed in Google Authenticator app and select Verify.
    5. Optional: To add email authentication as another factor for your account, select Setup for the appropriate factor.
    6. Select Finish.

    Security keys

    1. On your desktop or PC, sign in to MyF5. If you have not previously enrolled in two-factor authentication, you will be prompted to do so.
    2. On the Setup 2-step authentication page, select Security keys.
    3. Select Next.
    4. Insert your hardware security key (such as a YubiKey or FIDO2-compliant device) into a USB port on your desktop or PC, or ensure your device is ready for Bluetooth/NFC pairing.
    5. Follow the browser prompt or operating system pop-up to allow security key registration.
    6. Touch the sensor or button on your security key when prompted.
    7. Enter a recognizable nickname for your security key when prompted.
    8. Select Finish.

    WebAuthn

    1. On your desktop or PC, sign in to MyF5. If you have not previously enrolled in two-factor authentication, you will be prompted to do so.
    2. On the Setup 2-step authentication page, select WebAuthn.
    3. Select Next.
    4. Follow the browser prompt or operating system pop-up to register your authenticator (such as Windows Hello, Touch ID, Face ID, or a roaming security key).
    5. Complete the biometric verification or physical security key prompt when requested by your device.
    6. Enter a recognizable nickname for your credential when prompted.
    7. Select Finish.

    Log in to MyF5 using MFA factor

    After you enroll at least one factor in MFA, you can log in by entering your F5 login email address and password and then following the instructions for the MFA method you've enrolled in. If you've added more than one factor, you can select the authentication factor you want to use at the top of the screen.

    Note: If you have more than one MFA factor added to your profile, the one you used last appears as the default.

    If you use the same browser for your entire session, after you log in, you should not be required to reauthenticate on any F5 sites for eight hours or until you log out. However, if you switch between browser types (for example Google Chrome and Mozilla Firefox), you must authenticate your login on each browser.

    If you lose access to your MFA factor and require assistance, you can email SupportOne@F5.com or contact F5 Support.

    Note: If you need to reset your MFA factor, refer to K000140558: Reset multi-factor authentication for MyF5 for further instructions.

    Change MFA configuration using My Profile

    If you want to add, remove, or update your MFA factors, you can do that through the My Profile management page for your MFA account in the Security section.

    Note: This MFA My Profile page is different from your MyF5 profile. You can also go to your MFA My Profile management page when logged in to MyF5. To do so, select the profile icon at the top right and then select User Profile. Under Manage preferences, select Manage security preferences.

    Note: There are some limitations as to what you can change on this page. F5 uses MFA to enhance security across your F5 applications. Because of this, you cannot remove all factors from your account. All accounts require a minimum of one factor. Additionally, if you have set up MFA with your email address only, you cannot change that address.

    Add Google Authenticator

    1. On the Security page, toggle the Google Authenticator App switch on.
    2. Install the Google Authenticator app on your mobile device.
    3. In your mobile device app, tap + and select Scan a QR code to scan your code. If you can't scan the code, select Can't scan the QR code? and follow the prompts.
    4. Enter the 6-digit code from the app and select Verify.
