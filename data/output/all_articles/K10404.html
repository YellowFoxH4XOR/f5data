Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Querying large numbers of object statistics can cause undesired behavior with the big3d process.

When the Data Statistics Collection feature is enabled on Enterprise Manager, and the BIG-IP device from which the Enterprise Manager is requesting statistic information is configured with 6000 or more objects, the following behavior may occur:

  • The big3d process consumes a large amount of CPU time.
  • The big3d process may restart.
  • The mcpd process may restart due to the number of requests made by the big3d process. This results in a number of other services to restart due to their dependence on the mcpd process.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR120875 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 10.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, ASM, GTM, or Link Controller release notes.

This issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-10.0.1-342.0-HF1, which has been issued for BIG-IP version 10.0.1, for the BIG-IP 10.0 software branch. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
