Known Issue

A bridging loop may occur when high-availability BIG-IP systems have Virtual Local Area Network (VLAN) groups that contain multiple VLAN members.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have multiple BIG-IP systems in a high-availability active-standby configuration.
  • You have configured the VLAN group feature with multiple VLAN members.
  • You have disabled the VLAN group's bridge in standby option.
  • The standby device receives a packet matching a monitor traffic flow.

Impact

Excessive network traffic and monitor failures may occur on both the active and standby devices due to a bridging loop.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H506076 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The system erroneously marks pool members down.
  • Packet captures reveal excessive network traffic. The IP address and service port will match monitored devices.
