Known Issue

Duplicate SQL monitors may cause pool member statuses to be incorrectly reported.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • Two or more identical SQL monitors are assigned to a pool.

Impact

Pool members may be incorrectly marked down.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Pool members are incorrectly reported as down.
  • Traffic is directed to another server.

Resolution

Workaround

To workaround this issue, you can ensure you only have one SQL monitor associated with a pool.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
