Policy Information

F5 is closely monitoring global development of the semiconductor shortage and providing updates as warranted.

Hardware order fulfillment

The F5 supply chain, as with all electronics industries, is dependent on partners worldwide to manufacture our hardware solutions. The industry is currently experiencing supply constraints in memory/SSD and semiconductor-related components due to the ongoing growth in AI.

F5's supply chain team has been diligently increasing buffer stocks, qualifying additional suppliers, and leveraging our global supply chain practices to ensure the availability of the materials necessary for manufacturing our hardware products. However, semiconductor manufacturers continue to run near full capacity and lead-times remain near records.

Please work with your F5 sales and support teams to ensure your requirements are identified — both for current and future demands — so that F5 remains positioned to exceed your expectations.
