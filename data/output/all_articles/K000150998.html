K000150998: After upgrading BIG-IP VE to 17.5.0 on Nutanix AHV, the system entered "INOPERATIVE" mode

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-04-24
Updated
2025-09-05
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.5.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000150998

Description

After upgrading BIG-IP VE to 17.5.0 on Nutanix AHV, the system entered "INOPERATIVE" mode.  The TMM (Traffic Management Microkernel) process entered a restart loop, rendering the appliance offline and unable to pass traffic.

Environment

  • F5-VE
  • Nutanix AHV

Cause

BIG-IP VE version 17.5.0 is not supported on Nutanix Acropolis Hypervisor (AHV) at the moment of the published time. Attempting to run this version on Nutanix AHV results in TMM process crashes and the system entering "INOPERATIVE" mode due to incompatibility, likely related to network driver or platform support issues.

Recommended Actions

  • Waiting for the official support release for Nutanix AHV

Nutanix Acropolis Hypervisor (AHV)¶
