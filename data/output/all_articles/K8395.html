K8395: Settings for client certificate requests during logon may not reflect the current state

Published
2008-02-12
Updated

Status

Applies to

  • FirePass: 6.0.2, 6.0.1, 6.0.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K8395

Topic

When you make a change to the Request client certificate during logon check box, which is located on the Device Management: Security: Certificates page, the following warning message initially appears:

Configuration changed.
You must restart the FirePass services for these changes to take effect.
Click here to restart

If you avoid restarting services and navigate away from the page, then return to the page, the change will be retained in the Administrative Console. However, the change will not have taken effect, and the warning will not appear again.

F5 Networks recommends that FirePass administrators perform a service restart soon after making a change to the certificate settings.
