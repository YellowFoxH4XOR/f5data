Description

There may be a time when the device group name is required to be changed for any purpose. The following information will provide the steps to accomplish this task.

Environment

  • BIG-IP devices configured in high availability
  • Device groups configured

Cause


Recommended Actions

Set the standby device offline
  1. Log into the Configuration Utility of the standby device.
  2.  Click on Device Management.
  3. Click on Devices.
  4. Click on the standby device (self)
  5. Click on Force Offline.
  6. Click OK.
Removing the devices from the old device group

Note: If the devices are configured for HA-Order, this must be changed to Load aware before proceeding. See additional information section below.
  1. Log into the Configuration Utility of the active device.
  2. Click on Device Groups.
  3. Select the current device group
  4. Move the devices over to the Available column.
  5. Click Update.
Configuring the new device group
  1. Log into the Configuration Utility of the active device.
  2. Click on Device Groups.
  3. Click on Create.
  4. Type the name of the new device group in the name field.
  5. Change the Group Type to the desired group.
  6. Move the devices into the Includes column.
  7. Click Finished.
Release the standby device from offline
  1. Log into the Configuration Utility of the standby device.
  2.  Click on Device Management.
  3. Click on Devices.
  4. Click on the standby device (self).
  5. Click on Release Offline.
  6. Click OK.

Additional Information

To change the devices to load aware:

  1. Log into the Configuration Utility of the Active unit.
  2. Click on Device Management.
  3. Click on Traffic Groups.
  4. Highlight the devices in the Preferred Order box and move over to Load Aware.
  5. Click save.
