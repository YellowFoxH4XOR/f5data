Topic

F5 uses the IEEE standard 1000baseT copper gigabit interfaces on the following platforms:
  • BIG-IP 5110 (D51)
  • BIG-IP 1500 (C36)
  • BIG-IP 3400 (C62)
  • BIG-IP 6400 (D63)
  • BIG-IP 6800 (D68)
  • BIG-IP 8400 (D84)
  • BIG-IP 8800 (D88)
  • FirePass 4100 (D46)
  • FirePass 4300 (D101)
  • Traffic Shield 4100 (D46)

Industry standards require Category 5e cable as the minimum cable type used for gigabit connectivity. F5 Product Development supports and recommends Category 5e and Category 6 cables.

Note: ANSI/TIA 854 1000BASE-TX is an alternative standard that is not inter-operable with 1000base-T. F5 does not support 1000Base-TX Copper Gigabit Ethernet on any product.

For more information about the IEEE standards for 1000baseT copper gigabit interfaces, refer to the IEEE Standards Association website at the following address:

http://standards.ieee.org
