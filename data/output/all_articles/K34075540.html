Policy Information

This article covers options that you have to protect sensitive data on your system when your F5 device is involved in the F5 Return Material Authorization (RMA) process.  Following are the available options:

  • Send F5 your defective device that contains your data
  • Remove your data before shipping F5 the defective device
  • Keep your sensitive data within the confines of your environment
  • Obtain the highest level of data security compliance

Send F5 a defective device that contains your data

In this scenario, F5 disposes of customer data after receiving your defective device. F5 adheres to a strict reconditioning process and removes all data from a returned system. For additional information, refer to K3189: Methods used to dispose of customer data from units returned under RMA.

Also refer to the F5 Material Data Security Statement.

Remove your data before shipping F5 the defective device

In this scenario, you erase internal drives yourself before returning a defective unit for RMA replacement. For additional information, refer to K15521: Using the Security Erase Unit ATA command to perform a disk erase for SSDs and HDDs.

Also refer to K10519: Restoring the BIG-IP configuration to factory default settings.

Keep your sensitive data within the confines of your environment

If you do not want your sensitive data to leave your environment, F5 offers a Disk Drive and Hardware Retention service. This service enables you to return the hardware, without the hard disk drives, for repairs or maintenance.

For more information, refer to the F5 RMA Disk Retention Option datasheet.

Obtain the highest level of data security compliance

If you want the highest level of data security, F5 offers the RMA Hardware Service with Expedited RMA Service.

For more information, refer to the F5 RMA Hardware Retention Service datasheet.
