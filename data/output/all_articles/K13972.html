Known Issue

The lopd process may temporarily lose connectivity to the lights-out processor (LOP).

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • Your system platform is a VIPRION 2400 chassis with B2100 blades installed.

On rare occasions, the LOP can experience an undiagnosed watchdog restart. The lopd process temporarily loses connectivity to the LOP while it resets. This temporary loss of connectivity can result in several hardware-related error messages being erroneously reported.

Impact

  • Erroneous fan tray removed/inserted messages may be inaccurately interpreted as a hardware failure.
  • Erroneous blade-powered down/up messages may be inaccurately interpreted as a hardware failure.
  • Management access by way of the LOP is unavailable while the LOP restarts.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP system falsely reports a chassis fan tray as removed and then inserted.

    Note: For more information, refer to K14788: The BIG-IP system may log erroneous 'chassis fan tray removed' and 'chassis fan tray inserted' messages.

  • Your BIG-IP system falsely reports a blade as powered down and then up.

    Note: For more information, refer to K14789: The BIG-IP system may log erroneous 'blade powered down' and 'blade powered up' messages.

  • The /var/log/ltm file contains error messages that appear similar to the following examples:

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 376443 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of the article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:

Type of FixFixes Introduced InRelated Articles
Release11.5.0*K2200: Most recent versions of F5 software
HotfixNoneNone

*With LOP firmware improvements in later releases, there have been no reports of this issue in BIG-IP 11.5.0 or later. If you experience this issue in 11.5.0 or later, contact F5 Technical Support.

Workaround

There is no workaround for this issue. However, LOP firmware 1.19 or later may help prevent at least one possible cause of the LOP reset. The LOP firmware 1.19 or later is available in BIG-IP 11.4.0, 11.3.0-HF5, 11.2.1-HF6, 11.2.0-HF6, 11.1.0-HF9, and 10.2.4-HF7.
