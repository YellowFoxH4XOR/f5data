Known Issue

BIG-IP APM may inadvertently rewrite content injected by AJAX.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system is configured for portal access.
  • The web application uses AJAX to inject content into JavaScript variables.

Impact

Web applications may malfunction or fail to render properly.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When you use portal access, requested pages that include AJAX injected content fail to display.
  • Web applications malfunction or fail to render properly in browsers.
