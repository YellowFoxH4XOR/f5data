Description

You can notice an information in /var/log/ltm because of non-SYN packets without an establishment received by the BIG-IP is 500 or above packets per second.

Error Message: 011e0001:4: Limiting unreachable response from 501 to 500 packets/sec for <Traffic Group>

Environment

  • BIG-IP 
  • LTM

Cause

It is because of the exceeded limit of non-SYN packets. Also if your device is working for too long without a restart means that old/stalled connections in the SNAT object are still there. Many of these won't get out of the connection table unless TMM is restarted.

Recommended Actions

You can perform a tmm restart by following command:

tmsh restart sys service tmm

Important:  Performing this command will impact the traffic on your device. F5 recommends to do it during maintenance window.

If some of the services are still down you need to restart them manually by:

bigstart restart 

Also it is a good practice to verify the value of idle-timeouts in your configuration, if it possible lower them a little bit.
