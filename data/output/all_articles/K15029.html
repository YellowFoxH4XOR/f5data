Known Issue

vCMP guests are set to the configured state after an upgrade.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have one or more vCMP guest in the provisioned or deployed states.
  • You are upgrading to BIG-IP 11.4.0 or 11.4.1.

Impact

Traffic may not be processed by vCMP guests after an upgrade.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • vCMP guests are in the configured state after an upgrade.

Workaround

To work around this issue, the administrator can manually change the state of the vCMP guests after an upgrade. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP Configuration utility as the administrator.
  2. Navigate to  vCMP > Guest List.
  3. Select the vCMP guests you would like to modify.
  4. Click the Provision or Deploy button, depending on the which state you want for these guests.
