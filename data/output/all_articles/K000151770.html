Description

Although you have enabled the Advanced WAF (ASM) security policy case insensitivity, requests are blocked.

Why are requests blocked even though you have enabled case insensitivity?


Environment

ASM


Cause

Per definition JSON objects are case sensitive (RFC8259, section 8.1) as they are UTF encoded.

Hence, the case insensitivity setting does not apply to JSON objects.

Note: Links mentioned takes you to a resource outside of MyF5, and the third party could remove the document without our knowledge.


Recommended Actions

The solution is to follow the best practices/standards defined in the RFC.
