Topic

3-DNS Controller versions 3.0 through 4.5.12 and 4.6 through 4.6.2 support the EDGE-FX Cache.

Starting in versions 4.5.13 and 4.6.3, F5 Networks Product Development removed support for the EDGE-FX Cache.
