Known Issue

A configuration synchronization (ConfigSync) operation with a large number of Client SSL profiles may increase Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) CPU usage.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is configured in a device group.
  • A virtual server is configured with a large number (hundreds) of Client SSL profiles.
  • You attempt to perform a ConfigSync.

Impact

The watchdog timer detects that the ConfigSync process does not complete within the expected time, causing TMM to restart. The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restarting.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The system CPU usage increases to 100 percent.
  • The BIG-IP system logs many Clock Advanced by <number> ticks messages to the /var/log/ltm file that appear similar to the following example:

    tmm1[10604]: 01010029:5: Clock advanced by 738 ticks
    tmm3[10604]: 01010029:5: Clock advanced by 797 ticks
    tmm5[10604]: 01010029:5: Clock advanced by 856 ticks
    tmm4[10604]: 01010029:5: Clock advanced by 915 ticks

  • Your configuration may take an unexpectedly long time to sync with the peer.
  • The system may experience a watchdog timeout, causing TMM to restart.
