Known Issue

Mirrored connections may fail during a subsequent failover that occurs after an initial failover.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system configuration uses connection mirroring.
  • A subsequent failover that occurs after an initial failover causes the initially active system to become active again.

A traffic group timing issue may allow for the system to lose connections during the following sequence of events:

When the initially active system, BIG-IP_1, fails over causing BIG-IP_2 to become active, and then a subsequent failover occurs causing BIG-IP_1 to become active again.

Impact

Mirrored connections may fail when a failover occurs.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The BIG-IP system resets mirrored connections following a subsequent failover that occurs after an initial failover.
