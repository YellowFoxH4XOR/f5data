Description
Questions about Digital Diagnostics Monitoring

> Should we expect performance impacts when enabling the feature?
The transceiver has a separate built-in microcontroller that does the monitoring. We do not expect any performance impact to the optical and electrical characteristics of the transceiver.

> Does F5 have a recommendation as to whether it should be enabled on a continuous basis vs. only as required when troubleshooting an issue?
The DDM is meant to continuously monitor the various metrics of the transceiver and trigger any alarm once it cross pre-determined standard-specific thresholds. The values of the thresholds can be seen from the overview: https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K83253027. It is up to the customer to decide whether they want to leave it on long term or to only turn it on for troubleshooting purposes.
