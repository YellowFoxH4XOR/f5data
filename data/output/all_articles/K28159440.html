Description
On B2250 platform, EUD_VF 15.1 incorrectly report the condition of 40 GIG QSFP.

EUD record the log as follow:
ERROR: Unable to read the optics data, check whether your optics are officially supported.

Environment

  • BIG-IP VIPRION B2250
  • Installed OPT-0036-01 optics module
  • EUD_VF Version 15.1

Cause
F5 Networks track this issue as ID665549 and fixed at EUD version 15.2.

Recommended Actions

To fix the issue, upgrade the EUD version to 15.2 and rerun the EUD after the upgrade.
