Known Issue

Attack signature updates may fail after upgrading from BIG-IP ASM 11.5.4.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP ASM system is running version 11.5.4 with HF2 or later installed.
  • You load attack signature update ASM-SignatureFile_20170403_145743.im.
  • You upgrade to BIG-IP ASM 11.6.1, 12.1.0, 12.1.1, or 12.1.2.

Impact

After upgrading the BIG-IP ASM system, the attack signature update process fails to complete.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Attack signature updates fail to load after upgrading to one of the affected versions.
  • In the /var/log/asm file, you observe messages similar to the following example:

    01310027:2: ASM subsystem error (asm_config_server.pl,F5::Sigfile::Update::store_error_and_die): Failed on insert to PLC.NEGSIG_SYSTEMS (DBD::mysql::db do failed: Duplicate entry '47' for key 'PRIMARY')

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can delete the incorrect signature association. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP ASM Advanced Shell (bash).
  2. To delete the corrupted signature association, type the following command:

    mysql -u root -p$(perl -MPassCrypt -nle 'print PassCrypt::decrypt_password($_)' /var/db/mysqlpw) -e "UPDATE PLC.NEGSIG_SIGNATURE_SYSTEMS set system_id = 14 where system_id = (select system_id FROM PLC.NEGSIG_SYSTEMS where system_name = 'Apache'); DELETE FROM PLC.NEGSIG_SYSTEMS where system_name = 'Apache';"

  3. Perform the attack signature update.
