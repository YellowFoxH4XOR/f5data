K000139992: Edge Client graph show the max throughput is 100M

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-06-13
Updated
2024-06-13
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • APM Clients
  • BIG-IP APM: 16.1.4
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000139992

Description

On the Window OS, Edge Client window, click "Show Graph", it will show the graph for VPN tunnel, the Max throughput is 100M.

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM
  • Edge Client
  • Windows OS

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

The max throughput in the graph is 100MB, it is by design.

In the most of deployment, the VPN throughput is more much lower than 100M.

There is no limitation for the max throughput, the real throughput may higher than 100M.

If your deployment, if the max throughput exceed 100M, you want to improve the graph, please contact F5 Support and raise the request.

The max throughput is not related with ''Client Interface Speed''.

Additional Information

None.
