Description

Customer may find this issue during the initial setting up of r-Series device, when inserting the SFP and connecting to peer switch, all fiber port is always down and LCD light cannot be powered up.

If checking the platform logs through/var/F5/system/log/platform.log, you will see there is alert of below show, even though it is testing with the F5 brand SFP.
