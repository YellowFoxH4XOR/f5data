Environment

Cause

Recommended Actions

In order to protect the pools against the CVE-2025-66516, enforce the following signatures ID: 

  • ID200018018
  • ID200018030
  • ID200022020
  • ID200003909

Steps: 

  1. Update Live Update Signatures to ensure you have the latest signature set.
  2. Navigate to the specific WAF policy you wish to protect.
  3. Under the Attack Signatures menu, search for the provided Signature IDs.
  4. Enforce the signatures by disabling staging for each ID.
  5. Repeat the steps 3-4 for all listed Signature IDs.
  6. Apply Policy to activate the changes.
