Topic

The Windows Group Policy inspector was introduced in FirePass version 6.0.3, and it integrates FullArmor GPAnywhere with FirePass.

Note: To use the Windows Group Policy Inspector in a pre-logon sequence, you must install the Group Policy license on the FirePass controller, which can be obtained from your F5 Networks sales representative or reseller. If you do not have this license installed, some of the pages and objects described in this Solution will not appear on your FirePass controller.

Windows Group Policy provides the ability to centrally manage and configure computers and users in an Active Directory (AD) environment. However, Group Policy only applies to devices and users that are connected to the AD. GPAnywhere allows users to create portable policies from Group Policy Objects and apply them to any end point through FirePass. This behavior extends the reach of Group Policy beyond the AD environment. For more information about GPAnywhere, refer to the FullArmor website.

For example, if a user connects to the FirePass controller using a personal laptop (a laptop not issued by the user's company), the laptop will not have the corporate Group Policy installed. By enabling the Windows Group Policy inspector in the FirePass pre-logon sequence, FirePass administrators can enforce a Group Policy on the client system prior to user logon.

Important: The Windows Group Policy inspector enforces a Group Policy on the client system, but it does not change, update, or install a Group Policy on the client system. When the user disconnects from the FirePass controller, the client system reverts to its previous policy settings. For example, if the Windows Group Policy inspector prohibits the user from accessing the Windows Control Panel, the user will be able to access the Windows Control Panel after disconnecting from the FirePass controller.

FirePass provides predefined Windows Group Policy templates, and you can optionally configure and upload custom templates. View the Windows Group Policy templates by navigating to the Users: Endpoint Security: Endpoint Inspectors page, and clicking the Windows Group Policy link. A brief description of each predefined policy is provided in the Predefined Group Policy Templates section. In the Custom Group Policy Templates section you can upload your own custom Group Policies. Selecting the name of a predefined policy in the Predefined Group Policy Templates section displays the settings enforced by that Group Policy.
 
Important: Custom policies are generated by a separate GPAnywhere application; use the output from this application to upload a custom Group Policy to the FirePass controller.
 
To use the Windows Group Policy inspector in a pre-logon sequence, insert the Apply Windows Group Policy action into your pre-logon sequence. In the Edit Action panel, click the Windows Group Policy link. In the Group policy template field, select the name of the of the Group Policy template you want to use. You can use one of the FirePass predefined templates, or a custom template that you previously uploaded. Once you’ve selected the Group Policy you want to use, click the Update button.
 
For more information about configuring a pre-logon sequence, refer to the FirePass Controller Administrator Guide.
