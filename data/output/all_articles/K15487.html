Known Issue

Internal interfaces on a Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP) guest are incorrectly modifiable using the Configuration utility.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You access the Configuration utility on a vCMP guest.

Beginning in BIG-IP 11.0.0, internal interfaces for a vCMP guest, such as 1/0.x, were purposely made visible for users to view statistics for these interfaces. These internal interfaces should not be modifiable, and cannot be modified using the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh). However, when using the Configuration utility on a vCMP guest, users are incorrectly allowed to modify these interfaces.

Note: The internal interfaces are not visible or modifiable from the Configuration utility when connected to the vCMP hypervisor host.

Impact

Modifying the internal interfaces may cause traffic disruption or failover.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When connecting to the Configuration utility on a vCMP guest, you are incorrectly allowed to modify internal interfaces.

    For example, on the Network > Interfaces page, you can disable/enable the internal interfaces, or on the Network > VLAN > VLAN Properties pages, you can add/remove the internal interfaces from a Virtual Local Area Network (VLAN).
  • Modifications to the internal interfaces of a vCMP guest cause traffic disruption and/or failover.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you should avoid modifying internal interfaces.

Impact of workaround: Performing the workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
