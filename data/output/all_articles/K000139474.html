When you need help with an F5 product, you have several options. Especially for those who are new to F5 products, it may be difficult to know where to start looking. This article is intended to help guide you to the answers and solutions you need.

Note: The inspiration to create this article came from conversations with F5 customers during AppWorld 2024.

Search the knowledge base

When looking for answers, the best place to start is the MyF5 knowledge base, a robust and continually updated repository for F5 product knowledge. Contributors from all over the company who have specialized knowledge are able to add to and improve it. So, a good way to start looking for answers is to open MyF5.com and enter your query in the search box.

If you need help with maximizing your MyF5 search, refer to K98133564: Tips for searching MyF5 and finding product documentation to find out the tips and tricks for conducting an effective search.

If you need further assistance about using the MyF5 site, refer to K000089216 MyF5 help, which provides links to documentation about searching, accounts, downloads, and subscriptions. It also has links to articles about how to create a support case if you’re not able to find an article that will allow you to self-solve. The Use the MyF5 site playlist provides several videos to help you with common tasks.

Search for manuals

MyF5 is working to improve the search experience for F5 manuals. In the meantime, you can use this article to help locate the manual you’re looking for: K000130285: F5 Product Manuals Index

Find answers using other resources

Bugtracker is a searchable repository of bug IDs and descriptions, which you can use to identify and get information about known issues in F5 products.

Roles on MyF5

Are you the admin for your company’s F5 account? Your organization’s F5 account must have an administrator. If you are the one responsible for administering accounts for others, you’ll need to get set up with the appropriate account permissions. If the admin role is not already assigned to you and there is no other admin to do so in your organization, you should contact F5 Support to have the role assigned to you for the accounts you manage.

K000136095: About MyF5 account user roles and permissions describes the various roles and permissions available.

K000089214: Add, change, or remove MyF5 account users shows you how to manage user accounts.

Join the F5 community

DevCentral https://community.f5.com/ is F5’s online technical user community, made up of thousands of netizens participating from all over the globe, all of whom have F5 in common. It’s a great place to learn to skills and tips, familiarize yourself with products, ask questions, and otherwise participate in the robust F5 community.

Get training

F5 has a variety of training methods and opportunities. For information about training, refer to F5 training.

If you want to get certified in F5 products, refer to F5 Certification or F5 Education Services.

Troubleshoot your F5 products

There are many troubleshooting articles and methods you can refer to in the MyF5 knowledge base, but one of the most powerful diagnostic tools available for F5 products is F5 iHealth, which lets you upload and analyze a qkview file from your product. For details, refer to: K27404821: Using F5 iHealth to diagnose vulnerabilities.

Find out what you own

If you’re new to F5 but your organization already has F5 products, depending on your F5 account role, you can access information about your F5 accounts, subscriptions, registration keys, and trials.

  • K000090852: View your MyF5 accounts
  • Your subscriptions
  • Your registration keys
  • Your trials

Contact F5 Support

If the variety of self-solve opportunities listed in this article aren’t enough to remedy an issue, you contact F5 Support to help. For contact information, refer to: K000135931: Contact F5 Support.

K2633: Submit a support case walks you through the best practices for everything you need to do to create a case that will maximize the benefits of your time and that of your F5 support engineer.
