K000163424: ConfigSync Failure After Adding, Removing, or Modifying a Monitor on an FQDN Pool in One of HA Peers.

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-09-29
Updated
2026-09-29
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.5.1
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000163424

Description

ConfigSync between BIG-IP LTM HA peers fails after a health monitor is added, removed, or modified on an FQDN pool. The receiving device rejects the configuration with an error similar to:

 

01070712:3: Caught configuration exception (0), Can't find monitor rule: <internal ID>

The device group may enter Sync Failed or Disconnected status.

 

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM HA pair.
  • Sync-failover device group.
  • FQDN pools containing health monitors.

Cause

The ConfigSync failure is associated with Bug ID2461325.

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

Open support case and request ENG EHF with fix for Bug ID2461325.

 

Adding the corresponding monitor configuration to the other peer device or re-adding the monitor to the device where it was removed can restore synchronization as a workaround.
