K000146748: How do I escalate my Support Ticket?

Applies to

  • F5 Distributed Cloud
Source

What Happened?

  • You would like to escalate your support ticket.

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud Console

Resolution/Answer

To escalate a Support ticket:

1. Go to your Distributed Cloud Console and click Support . 

  

2. Click track requests to identify the ticket you would like to escalate

  

3. On the right of the ticket you will see an action button, click escalate.
