K38681063: Process ''bd'' remains offline after making space in /var/ directory

Description

Process "bd" remains offline after making space in /var directory.

Environment

  • BIG-IP ASM
  • After removing files to clear up space in the /var directory, the process "bd" remained offline 

Cause

Due to the /var directory full the process "sod" triggered the fail action "go-offline-downlinks" for process "bd". Regardless of making space in the /var/ directory, the process "bd" will remain offline.

Recommended Actions

Impact of procedure: The following procedure restarts the asm process, which temporarily interrupts traffic processing. 

To come out of offline state, restart the asm process using the command:

tmsh sys service restart asm
