Description

After setting the MTU value of traffic vlans lower than 1280 MTU, rebooting the device causes traffic to cease. 

  • No traffic noted on data plane interfaces.
  • Clients cannot connect to virtual servers, pools are marked down.

Environment

Relevant environmental factors specific to the topic

  • BIG-IP
  • Lower MTU value is needed for networking constraints.
  • Traffic VLANS with MTU < 1280.  Management is not affected

Cause

Setting the MTU lower than 1280 can cause the mac address of the vlans to display as 'none'

Recommended Actions

The actions or steps required to address the topic covered in the description.

  1. Contact F5 support for an Engineering Hotfix to address this issue.

Additional Information

Bug ID 956133: MAC address might be displayed as 'none' after upgrading
