Description

When an APM standalone license is used , the available load balancing methods for pools differ on higher hardware platform and lower hardware platform.

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM
  • License type: APM standalone
  • i2xxx/r2xxx

Cause

This behavior is by design.

Answer/Recommended Actions

  • This is expected behavior when using an APM standalone license on higher-end BIG-IP platforms.
  • To access additional load balancing methods on higher-end platforms, an LTM+APM bundle license is required.
  • For further clarification or if this limitation impacts deployment, contact your F5 account team for guidance or licensing options.

Additional Information

  • The restriction is strategic to maintain separation between APM and LTM feature sets on higher-end hardware.
  • No technical workaround exists to enable additional load balancing methods on higher-end platforms with only an APM standalone license.
