K000141139: F5OS Certificate Management on 1.7.0

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-10-02
Updated
2025-03-21
Applies to
  • F5OS
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000141139

Description

You want to create a new Self-Signed Certificate, generate a Certificate Signing Request (CSR), or installing a CA signed certificate used by the GUI of F5OS after upgrading to 1.7.0.

Environment

F5OS

Certificate Management

Cause

Until 1.5.0, the location of changing the certificate used by the F5OS is located under System Settings --> Certificate Management.

After upgrading to 1.7.0, you can change the certificate under Authentication & Access --> TLS Configuration. 

Recommended Actions

Create a new Self-Signed Certificate, generate a Certificate Signing Request (CSR), or install a CA signed certificate on F5OS by navigating to Authentication & Access --> TLS Configuration.
