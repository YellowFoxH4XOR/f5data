K524: Re-licensing a BIG-IP or 3-DNS Controller after replacing network interface cards (NICs)

Topic

If new NICs are installed in your BIG-IP, make sure that your old license is not rejected after the addition of the NICs. There are cases in which NICs can be added without invalidating the current license of the machine.

If the machine is actually un-licensed after adding new NICs, F5 Support will need:

  • The current license file. This is either the /etc/bigip.license (3.3.1 and below), or the /config/bigip.license. (4.0 and higher)
  • The output of the ifconfig -a command.

F5 Support will be able to provide a new license file provided you have a current support contract, the proper information is provided, and the NICs were installed by an F5 Networks representative.

For BIG-IP and 3-DNS versions 4.5 and later, you must re-authorize your unit. You will receive instructions with your new network interface cards.
