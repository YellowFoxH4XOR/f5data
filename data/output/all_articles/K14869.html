Known Issue

Trunks may fail to pass traffic after you reboot the system.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a BIG-IP 2000 or 4000 series platform.
  • You configure one or more interfaces in a trunk with Link Aggregation Control Protocol (LACP) set to passive.
  • You reboot the BIG-IP system.

Impact

Affected trunks do not pass traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H438018 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium|High screen.
  • Configured trunks do not pass any traffic.
  • The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) drops all LACP packets.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can change LACP from passive to active, or restart the lacpd process. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Changing trunk LACP mode from passive to active

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility as the admin user.
  2. Navigate to Network > Trunks > Trunk List.
  3. Select the trunk to modify.
  4. Under LACP Mode, select Active.
  5. Click Update.

Restarting the lacpd process

Impact of workaround:  All trunks temporarily stop passing traffic; this procedure should be performed only during a maintenance window or when the BIG-IP system is in a standby state.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP system as the root user.
  2. Restart the lacpd process by typing the following command:

    bigstart restart lacpd
