Known Issue

The Configuration utility fails to display the list of configured attack signatures.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You attempt to display the list of attack signatures configured on a parameter.

Impact

Administrators are unable to display and configure attack signatures on a parameter.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H678434 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium page.
  • In the /var/log/httpd/httpd_errors file, you observe an error message that appears similar to the following example:

    err httpd[<PID>]: [error] [client <client_IP>] PHP Fatal error:  Maximum execution time of 30 seconds exceeded in /var/ts/dms/common/classes/Thrift/packages/asmconfig/f5_thrift.php on line <line_ID>, referer: https://<BIG-IP_MGMT_IP>/dms/policy/pl_header_normalization.php
