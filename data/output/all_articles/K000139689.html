Description

Trying to change the configuration of the APM VPE (Virtual Policy Editor) through BIG-IQ, shows (Read only)

Environment

BIG-IQ

APM

Customization type: Modern (Per-session-policy)

Cause

Access policy (Per-session-policy) with customization type: Modern, does not support editing through the BIG-IQ

Recommended Actions

BIG-IQ does support:

Access policy (Per-session-policy) with customization type: Standard

or

Access policy (Per-request-policy) with customization type: Modern and Standard

Additional Information

None
