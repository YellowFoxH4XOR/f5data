Known Issue

The wamd process may not start correctly on BIG-IP 4000 series platforms.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You attempt to provision the AAM module on a BIG-IP 4000 series platform.

Due to a possible race condition, the wamd process may intermittently restart during provisioning of the AAM module on a BIG-IP 4000 series platform and generate a core file.

Impact

The BIG-IP system is unable to provision software modules and successfully complete the startup process.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The wamd process generates a core file to the /shared/core/ directory.
  • In the /var/log/kern.log file, you may observe messages similar to the following example:

    info kernel: wamd[29081]: segfault at 78 ip 000000005793c3e0 sp 00000000ff887d30 error 4 in libumem_mt.so[57928000+2c000]
