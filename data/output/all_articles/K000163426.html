Topic

This Trusted Advisor Program Getting Started guide helps you enable and schedule your F5 Trusted Advisor Program services purchased from F5 or a designated F5 partner. The instructions below apply specifically to the Trusted Advisor Program. For guidance on other services, please refer to the main F5 Services: Getting Started guide.

Description

To enable the F5 Trusted Advisor Program, the Client must complete an Act of Acknowledgement. The Client is defined as either (a) the End Customer or (b) a prime contractor acting on behalf of the End Customer.

The Act of Acknowledgement is an electronic acceptance of the agreement between F5 and the Client (“the Parties”). It specifies:

  • The services to be performed by F5 Professional Services
  • The terms and conditions covering these services

The Act of Acknowledgement must be completed once, for the 12-month agreement period, by the Client’s designated signatory or a designated representative with authority to sign on behalf of the End Customer. If you are not a designated signatory, please direct these instructions to the appropriate person in your organization.

Activate your F5 trusted advisor program service

Follow these steps to activate your F5 Trusted Advisor Program service. The activation is a one-time process. If your service is already active, please proceed to the Scheduling section to request services. If you have questions at any stage, contact F5 by submitting a request via Request Professional Services.

  1. In your browser, open the Trusted Advisor Program Act of Acknowledgement page using the link provided in your entitlement notification email from F5.
  2. The form will appear.
     
  3. Enter the signing authority’s information:
    • Email Address
    • First Name
    • Last Name
    • Job Title (position at the company)
    • Company Name (employer’s name)
  4. Click Continue to Contract.
  5. The Service Description for Trusted Advisor Program will display, with entry fields on the cover page of the document.
     
  6. Verify the Name, Email, Title, and Company fields. Update any incorrect information.
  7. In the Service ID field, enter the value from your F5 notification email. The Service ID will begin with “TAP.”
    If you do not provide a valid Service ID, you will be notified after submission and asked to resubmit using the details from your entitlement notification
  8. Carefully review the entire Service Description.
  9. Click Agree at the bottom of the window to electronically sign the Service Description.
      
  10. A confirmation screen will display.
     
  11. Select Download Record to save a copy for your records, or refer to the confirmation email for a downloadable copy.

Scheduling Services

If you have completed the Act of Acknowledgement for the F5 Trusted Advisor Program, you can now submit a service request to schedule your services. Choose one of the following options:

OPTION 1: CLICKABLE EMAIL TEMPLATE

  1. Select the following link:
    taprequest@f5.com
  2. A new email window will open in your default email client.
     
  3. In the Subject field, update the following placeholders with the relevant details from your entitlement notification:
    • <service-id>, Service ID received in your notification email.
    • <company-name>, name of your employer
    • <brand>, name of the F5 brand(s) included within the service request (i.e. BIG-IP, NGINX, Distributed Cloud)
  4. In the body of the email, update the following placeholders:
    • <requestors-physical-location>: Your city and country
    • <requestors-timezone>: Your time zone (UTC format)
    • <brand>: The F5 brand(s) included (e.g., BIG-IP, NGINX, Distributed Cloud)
    • <product>: The F5 products included in the Service Request (i.e. BIG-IP LTM, BIG-IP DNS, BIG-IP APM, BIG-IP ASM, Advanced WAF, SSLO, NGINX Plus, NGINX One, F5 WAF for NGINX, NGINX Ingress Controller, Distributed Cloud WAF, Distributed Cloud WAAP w/RE, Distributed Cloud WAAP w/CE, Distributed Cloud DDoS, etc.).
    • <service-request>: The details of your request—hardware, software modules/versions, maintenance windows, etc.
  5. Replace <your-email-signature> with your business email signature.

Additional Information

NEED ASSISTANCE?

If you have questions or need help submitting your service request, email taprequest@f5.com and let us know you’d like assistance.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?

After F5 receives your service request, you’ll receive an acknowledgment. We will review your request, confirm how many passes will be used to fulfill it, and provide available scheduling options.
