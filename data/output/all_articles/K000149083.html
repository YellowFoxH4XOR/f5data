This article describes the method for locating the physical product label and finding the serial numbers of the VELOS Power Controller (VPC) and Fan Tray on your VELOS device.

Description

  • Finding the serial number of your VELOS Power Controller
  • Finding the serial number of your VELOS Fan Tray

Finding the serial number of your VELOS Power Controller

In the back of the chassis are 4 separate AC or DC power inputs, and 2 redundant power controllers which are removable.

DC Model:

  1. Slot1 VPC 
  2. Slot2 VPC
  3. AC Power inputs or DC Power inputs
  4. Grounding lug
  5. VELOS Fan Tray

The PSU controllers are accessible in the upper left-hand corner of the back of the system.

Loosen the captive screw (#2 Phillips screwdriver) of the Controller and then gently pull the controller out using the same screw head.  This image is of a CX410 chassis, but the application is similar for a CX1610 chassis.

Finding the serial number of your VELOS Fan Tray

The fan tray is removable if it needs to be replaced but should not be removed for long periods of time, as overheating may occur.  To ensure that you can easily access the fan tray, route the power cords away from the fan tray so that the cords do not drape over or cross in front of it.

Grasp and rotate the quarter turn latches outward to prepare the fan tray for removal.

Remove the fan tray from the chassis by grasping the handles and pulling straight toward you.

The Serial number is located on the left side of the Fan Tray assembly.  Please see the red mark below for reference.
