Known Issue

TMM may core when processing a connection with auto-lasthop disabled.

This issue occurs when one of the following conditions are met:

  • Auto last-hop has been disabled globally on the BIG-IP system.
  • Auto last-hop has been disabled for one or more of the following objects:

    • Virtual server
    • VLAN
    • SNAT
    • NAT

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic and fail over if configured as part of a high availability system.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H425807 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic and failover.
  • The BIG-IP system may log MM SIGSEGV error messages to the /var/log/tmm files that appear similar to the following examples:

    bigip1 notice ** SIGSEGV **
    bigip1 notice fault addr: 0x68
    bigip1 notice fault code: 0x1

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 415334 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies To box, located in the top right corner of the article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:

Type of FixFixes Introduced InRelated Articles
Release11.4.0K2200: Most recent versions of F5 software
Hotfix11.3.0 HF5K9502: BIG-IP hotfix matrix

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can enable auto-lasthop. For information about configuring Auto Last-Hop, refer to K13918: Configuring Auto Last Hop on BIG-IP (11.x - 12.x).

Impact of workaround: The BIG-IP system will route packets back out of the received interface regardless of routing table.
