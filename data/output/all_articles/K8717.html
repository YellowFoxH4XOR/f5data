K8717: Idle UDP connections using a SNAT are not timed out

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Idle UDP connection that are routed through the BIG-IP LTM and use a SNAT are not timed out. As a result, the BIG-IP system may track a large number of UDP flows. Since each connection flow requires a small amount of memory, the BIG-IP connection reaper may need to remove some connections.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR90793 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 9.4.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
