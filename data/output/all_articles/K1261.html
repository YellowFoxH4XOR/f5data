K1261: Modifying the Maximum Transfer Unit (MTU) for BIG-IP or 3-DNS interfaces

Type
Knowledge
Published
2006-02-17
Updated
2023-02-21
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • Legacy Products
  • BIG-IP 4.X: 4.6.4
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K1261

Topic

You cannot change the MTU for BIG-IP or 3-DNS interfaces. If you must deliver packets larger than 1500 bytes to BIG-IP or 3-DNS and the source cannot fragment the packets, you must install BIG-IP or 3-DNS behind a router that is capable of fragmenting the packets.
