Policy Information

In early 2000 F5 introduced a feature within BIG-IP that performed on-the-fly transformation of standard content (URLs) into a proprietary content type supported by Akamai Technologies (ARLs). Because of the limited demand and use for this proprietary content type, F5 is refocusing our efforts towards more highly leverage content conversion needs such as SSL. This will allow F5 to focus on solutions that provide deliver a higher value to our customers and partners. F5 remains dedicated to providing traffic management solutions to support distributed CDN and eCDN deployments.

Product Update

F5 announces plans to end support for the Akamaizer feature in BIG-IP. This applies to all fully licensed BIG-IP products.

Product Support

For those customers using the Akamaizer feature, F5 will continue to provide software and phone support in accordance to the customer's maintenance contracts. Those customers using this feature will be required to stay on version 4.5 PTF 03 or earlier. As of the EoSD date 9/30/2004, F5 will not be able to provide software fixes for any technical issues surrounding the Akamaizer feature.

Questions

If you have questions about F5's decision to discontinue this feature, please contact your local F5 representative or service department.
