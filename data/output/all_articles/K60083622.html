K60083622: Why am I receiving alerts stating a certificate will expire in hours rather than days?

Type
Support Solution
Published
2020-12-29
Updated
2025-02-06
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K60083622

Description
Some alerts from BIG-IQ state "Certificate myCertificate.crt on Hostname: myHost.com will expire in <##> days."
Other alerts from BIG-IQ state "Certificate myCertificate.crt on Hostname: myHost.com will expire in -<##> hours."
 

Environment

  • BIG-IQ configured to alert for certificates expiring on BIG-IPs

Cause
There are two separate alerts that can be configured for certificate monitoring on the BIG-IQ. 

  • One is titled "Certificate expiration" and wlll trigger a configured number of days before the certificate expires. 
  • The other is titled "Certificate expired" and will trigger on "Zero-day" of certificate expiration. 

It may say "Certificate myCertificate.crt on Hostname: myHost.com will expire in 19 hours"  or it may read "Certificate myCertificate.crt on Hostname: myHost.com will expire in -240 hours"  
 

Recommended Actions

  1. Navigate to the Monitoring tab of the BIG-IQ CM, select ALERTS & NOTIFICATIONS, and then select the button labelled "Settings"
  2. Midway down the "Device Alert Conditions" locate two lines "Certificate expiration" and "Certificate expired."
  3. Configure your selections. 
    • If you wish to receive notifications that will remind you of a certificate expiring next month, select Certificate Expiration and set the number of days.
    • If you wish to receive notifications about certificates that have already expired, or will expire in a few hours, select Certificate Expired.
  4. Click Save & Close.
