Issue

You may see the following error in the 3-DNS Controller zone files:

named[23969]: Zone "mydomain.com" (file mydomain.com.bk): 
no NS RRs found at zone top
named[23969]: slave zone "mydomain.com" (IN)
rejected due to errors (serial 1)

Note: F5 is working to eliminate exclusionary language in our products and documentation. For more information, refer to K34150231: Exclusionary language in F5 products and documentation.

DNS standards require that you define at least one authoritative nameserver for each new zone that you create in NameSurfer.

A general guideline is to add the nameserver that you listed as the Master NS in the SOA record as one of the authoritative nameservers. This will eliminate these errors.
