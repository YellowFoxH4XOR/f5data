Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may stop responding when deleting a pool member that is processing traffic.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Two or more pools share the same node as the pool member.
  • The pool member (with the shared node) is deleted while traffic is passing.
  • The One Connect Profile associated with the virtual server that references the pool contains the pool member that was deleted.

Impact

Traffic is disrupted while TMM restarts.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The tmm daemon may stop responding, resulting in a restart.
  • Traffic is disrupted while the tmm daemon restarts.
  • Failover occurs on the redundant systems when TMM restarts on the affected system.
