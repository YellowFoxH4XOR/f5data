Topic

The VIPRION chassis fan speed for the B2100 and B2150 blades may fluctuate, depending on the ambient temperature and system load.

Description

At certain system loads and temperatures, the VIPRION chassis fans may fluctuate from approximately 60% to 100% total fan speed on average every 3 to 5 minutes. The fluctuation occurs at time intervals of roughly 3 to 5 minutes, and occurs at temperatures of roughly 8 to 12°C and 33 to 34°C, depending on the load. When the fluctuations occur, you may hear a distinct change in the acoustic output of the fan.

Recommendations

None. This is expected behavior and no functional performance issues or life expectancy reductions will result from the fan speed fluctuations.
