Description

Verify the End of Life (EOL) and End of Support (EoS) status for F5 BIG-IP Virtual Appliance to identify lifecycle milestones for specific software versions, ensuring proper planning for upgrades, support renewals, or migrations. 

Environment

  • BIG-IP Virtual Edition 

Cause

 None 

Recommended Actions

  1. Identify Your Software Version
    • Refer to article K8759: Displaying the BIG-IP software version

 

Consult the Official F5 EOL/EoS Index:

    • Use the F5 Knowledge Base article K11478 (“End of Life/End of Sale index”) to search for your specific model or version.
    • The index provides links to detailed announcements per platform/version.

  • Review Lifecycle Milestones in Announcements:

    • Each announcement typically includes:
      • End of Sale (EoS) date
      • End of New Software Support (EoNSS)
      • End of Software Development (EoSD)
      • End of Support Contract Renewal (ESCR)
      • End of Technical Support (EoTS)
      • Special notes or migration recommendations

  • Understand F5’s Lifecycle Policy:

    • Refer to K4309 (“The F5 platform life cycle support policy”) for definitions and explanations about each lifecycle milestone.

  • BIG-IP Virtual Editions (VE):

    • EOL/EoTS is determined by software version rather than virtual appliance type.
    • Key articles include:
      • K000139937: Details on EOL/EoTS dates for major releases such as 15.1.x and 16.1.x
      • K5903: Overview of BIG-IP software support policy
      • K84205182: Update and upgrade guidance
    • Example dates:
      • BIG-IP 15.1.x series EOL was December 31, 2024.
      • BIG-IP 16.1.x series EOL will be July 31, 2025.

  • Check Product-Specific Announcements as Needed:

    • Some models have dedicated articles with unique timelines or requirements.
    • For legacy VE licenses or special cases, review license-specific notices such as K05392858.
