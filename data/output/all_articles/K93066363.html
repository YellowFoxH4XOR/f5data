Known Issue

Port/Protocol packet filters may fail to capture IPv6 fragments.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your virtual server is configured to use packet filters.
  • The packet filter is based on a port number.
  • The filter default action is discard.
  • You are using the IPv6 protocol.

Impact

The BIG-IP system is not able to process fragmented IPv6 traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • IPv6 fragments are discarded.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can modify your filter rules. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

For example, you want to filter on a destination port of 50000 similar to the following example:

dst port 50000

Alternatively, you can filter based on raw fragments offsets similar to the following example:

((udp dst port 50000) or (ip6[6]=44 and ip6[40]=17 and ip6[50]=50000))
