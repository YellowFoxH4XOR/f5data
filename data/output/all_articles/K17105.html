Known Issue

A Virtual eXtended LAN (VXLAN) overlay, with three or more BIG-IP VXLAN tunnel endpoints (VTEPs), may add an additional hop to forwarded unicast frames.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your configuration includes a VXLAN overlay with three or more BIG-IP VTEPs.
  • A BIG-IP VTEP uses multicast or unicast replication to flood a unicast frame.
  • The unicast frame's destination media access control (MAC) address is known, but the remote endpoint is unknown.
  • After receiving the unicast frame, one BIG-IP VTEP forwards the frame again to another BIG-IP VTEP.
  • An additional Layer 2 (L2) hop is introduced in the VXLAN tunnel.

Impact

Users experience additional VXLAN tunnel packet latency, due to an extra hop.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You notice that packets have an extra hop when traversing the BIG-IP VXLAN tunnel.
