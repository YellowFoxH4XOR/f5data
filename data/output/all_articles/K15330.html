Known Issue

The switch over daemon (sod) process memory footprint may increase over time. This issue occurs with high availability (HA) clusters and with standalone BIG-IP systems.

Impact

The sod process may exit and restart, or other services may become unresponsive due to memory exhaustion.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H447216 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High screen.
  • The sod process memory footprint continuously grows over time due to leaking memory.
  • The sod process restarts and causes an unexpected failover.

Workaround

To temporarily work around this issue, you can restart the sod process. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Restarting the sod process causes a failover and results in a brief traffic interruption. This workaround only mitigates the issue, and the issue may re-occur.

  1. Log in to the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) by typing the following command:

    tmsh

  2. To restart the sod service, type the following command:

    restart sys service sod
