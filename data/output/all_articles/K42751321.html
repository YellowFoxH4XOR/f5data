Known Issue

For BIG-IP systems with high uptimes, traffic groups may become active on more than one BIG-IP system at a time.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Two or more BIG-IP systems are defined in a sync-failover device group.
  • You have at least one traffic group configured.
  • The BIG-IP systems have a high uptime.

    Note: The uptime required to encounter this issue depends on the number of traffic groups that are configured on the BIG-IP systems.

    For example:

    • On systems configured with seven traffic groups, the interval is approximately 710 days.
    • On systems configured with 15 traffic groups, the interval is approximately 331 days.

Traffic groups remain active on more than one BIG-IP system for a time span of 30 seconds.

Impact

Traffic group members that are active on multiple BIG-IP systems concurrently may cause an interruption to production traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Log files indicate that the affected traffic group is active on more than one BIG-IP system.

    You can examine log files on multiple BIG-IP systems to confirm that when a traffic group becomes active on one BIG-IP system, it becomes standby on other BIG-IP systems. For example, when a traffic group becomes active on a BIG-IP system, you may observe messages similar to the following example in the /var/log/ltm file:

    notice sod[<pid>]: 010c0053:5: Active for traffic group /Common/traffic-group-1.

    When this occurs, the system should log a message similar to the following to the previously active BIG-IP /var/log/ltm file, indicating that the traffic group is no longer active on that system:

    notice sod[<pid>]: 010c0052:5: Standby for traffic group /Common/traffic-group-1.

Workaround

There is no workaround. You can mitigate this issue by scheduling periodic reboots for BIG-IP systems, to prevent high uptimes.

Impact of workaround: Rebooting the BIG-IP system affects traffic processing and you should perform the reboot during a scheduled maintenance window.
