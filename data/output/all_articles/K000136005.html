Description

LACP was not forming with F5 VELOS device and Cisco Nexus 7k. The interface is up without LACP and once it is added to a LAG, it is marked down. 

 

Environment

  • Cisco Nexus 7K switch
  • VELOS platform
  • F5OS
  • LACP interface
  • Global tagging on VLAN

 

Cause

Global tagging on VLAN was enabled on Cisco device

 

Recommended Actions

If global tagging is enabled on the VLAN, the LACP BPDU sent from Cisco will have an 801.Q tagging. Cisco does not support LACP with this tagging.


BPDU from Cisco may look like below.

To resolve the issue, disable global tagging on Cisco switch.
