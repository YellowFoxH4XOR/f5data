Topic



Audio streaming is an integral part of the Windows Terminal Services (WTS) implementation. F5 Networks has tested WTS 2003 and verified that audio will transparently stream through the FirePass Terminal Services Webifyer. If the transport application is configured, audio will also stream through an AppTunnel or an SSL VPN.

Note: Because of the overhead required to encrypt an audio stream, F5 Networks recommends that you do not stream large amounts of audio through a FirePass Webifyer.
