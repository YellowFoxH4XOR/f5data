Topic

When you activate or reactivate the license on a VELOS system controller, the license updates on all tenants in all chassis partitions, which causes a brief disruption of data plane traffic on the tenants.

Description

You can use the VELOS system controller webUI to activate or reactivate the license on a VELOS system. Tenant systems inherit licenses from the license installed on the VELOS system controllers.

When you activate or reactivate the license, such as when you add a new add-on registration key, the newly activated license propagates to all tenants on all chassis partitions. When a BIG-IP tenant refreshes the licensing parameters, the BIG-IP tenant reloads its configuration, causing a brief disruption of data plane traffic.

Updating or reactivating the license on a VELOS system automatically causes all tenants in all chassis partitions to receive the updated or reactivated license. Preventing selected tenants from receiving the updated license is not currently supported.

Recommendations

To avoid a disruption of application traffic on tenants, you should only reactivate the license on a VELOS system during a scheduled maintenance window. To activate or reactivate the license on a VELOS system, perform the following procedure:

Impact of procedure: Traffic processing on all BIG-IP tenant systems in all partitions is briefly interrupted while the systems reload their configurations. F5 recommends that you activate the license on your VELOS system while in a maintenance window.

  1. Log in to the webUI of the VELOS chassis as the chassis administrator.
  2. Go to System Settings > Licensing.
  3. The registration key is auto-populated in Base Registration Key. You can choose to overwrite it with a new registration key. To reactivate the license on a licensed system, select Reactivate.
  4. Add-on keys are auto-populated in Add-On Keys. To add add-on keys to a licensed system, enter them in Add-On Keys.
  5. Select Manual.
  6. Select Get Dossier.
  7. After the VELOS system generates the dossier, copy and paste it into Device Dossier.
  8. Select Click here to access F5 Licensing Server. Alternatively, you can use the F5 license activation portal.
  9. For Enter Your Dossier, paste the dossier and select Next.

    The F5 Product Licensing page produces your license.

  10. Copy and paste the license into License Text.
  11. Select Activate.
  12. The End User License Agreement (EULA) displays.
  13. Select Agree to accept the EULA.
