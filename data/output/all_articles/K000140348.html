Recommended Actions

From bash run:

 

tmsh -q -c 'cd /; show ltm virtual recursive' | awk '/Ltm::Virtual/ {printf $3" "} /Availability/ {printf $3" "} /Destination/ {printf $3"\n"}' | column -t
