Description

You want to break the HA between BIG-IPs but do not want to have any outage.

Environment

  • BIG-IP HA pair
  • Breaking HA
  • BIG-IP hardware appliances, BIG-IP VE, BIG-IP vCMP guests

Cause

When breaking HA between BIG-IP pairs are not done correctly, both BIG-IP will be in STANDALONE status and both will process production traffic. This might cause an outage to your application.

To avoid this issue from happening, you will need to FORCE OFFLINE the standby device before breaking the HA.

Recommended Actions

To ensure there is no outage when breaking the HA, you will need to:

  1. Forcing the standby device offline
  2. Breaking the HA trust

Refer to article K42161405 on how to break the HA pair correctly.

Additional Information

Forcing the standby device offline

You can force the standby device to an offline state, which will prevent devices from entering an active-active state while performing this maintenance. To do so, perform one of the following procedures:

Impact of procedure: The standby device will not be available if a failover action occurs. F5 recommends that you schedule this procedure during a maintenance window to minimize the impact in mission-critical environments. 

BIG-IP 13.x and later

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility on the standby unit.
  2. Navigate to Device Management > Traffic Groups.
  3. Click traffic-group-1 and set the failover method to Failover using Preferred Device Order and then Load Aware.
  4. Click Save.
  5. Navigate to Devices.
  6. Click the device labeled (Self).
  7. Click Force Offline.

    Note: You will need to repeat these steps for each traffic group you have configured and standby device in the device group. 

BIG-IP 11.x - 12.x 

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility on the standby unit.
  2. Navigate to Device Management > Traffic Groups.
  3. Click traffic-group-1 and set the failover method to Load Aware.
  4. Click Update
  5. Navigate to Devices
  6. Click the device labeled (Self).
  7. Click Force Offline.

    Note: You will need to repeat these steps for each traffic group you have configured and standby device in the device group. 

Breaking the trust

To break an established trust, you must remove the devices from the device group before you can delete the device trust. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of procedure: Performing this procedure will remove the HA configuration from your environment. F5 recommends that you schedule this procedure during a maintenance window to minimize the impact in mission-critical environments. 

Note: After performing these procedures, the active device should have a status of online (Active) Standalone and the standby devices should have a status of Forced offline Standalone.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.

    Important: Perform this procedure first on the active device of the device group, then repeat the procedure on each standby device in the device group.

  2. Navigate to Device Management > Device groups.
  3. Click your failover group from the list.
  4. Remove the standby member from the device group by clicking it in the Includes list and moving it to the Available list.
  5. Click Update.
  6. Navigate to the following page for your BIG-IP version:
    • 13.0.0 and later: Device Management > Device Trust > Device Trust Members.
    • 11.x - 12.x: Device Management > Device Trust > Local Domain > Peer List or Subordinate List menu.
  7. Select the check box to the left of each peer device and click Delete.

Repeat this procedure on each standby device in the device group.
