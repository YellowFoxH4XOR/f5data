Description
Failover occurred many times due to TMM crash with SIGABRT.
Log shows sod timing out on the tmm heartbeat and restarting tmm shortly thereafter.

in /var/log/ltm

notice sod[<PID>]: 01140041:5: Killing tmm.0 pid <TMM_PID>

in /var/log/tmm*

notice ** SIGABRT **

A long side of log, graph shows below sysmtom

  1. a huge spike in packets per second (but NOT connections)
  2. a huge spike in system CPU accompanying the pps increase
     

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • Any version
  • i2000/i4000 Series
  • TMM core with SIGABRT 

Cause
One of possible cause is DDOS attack.

Recommended Actions

i2000/i4000 series does not have a conventional switch, many unwanted types of traffic can be processed by the TMM pipeline before getting dropped.
We suggest to consider looking at any firewall rules or ACLs for devices in front of the BIG-IP that could be improved to reduce the amount of incoming traffic not intended for defined virtual server objects, if this has not been done.
 

Additional Information

  • K30911031: TMM crashed (SIGABRT) and becames offline/failover.
