Known Issue

In the rare event that a High-Speed Bridge (HSB) lock-up occurs in a Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP) guest, the system may erroneously not generate a core file.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • An HSB lockup occurs in a vCMP guest.

Impact

You have a limited ability to diagnose failures due to HSB lockups.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Affected vCMP guests may reboot after failure.
  • Affected vCMP guests will not produce a core file for detailed analysis of the cause of the failure.
