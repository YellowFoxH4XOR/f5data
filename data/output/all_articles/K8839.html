Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Multiple DNS requests received on a TCP connection may cause Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) to generate a core file.

This issue occurs when the BIG-IP GTM system buffers multiple DNS requests, and attempts to remove a request from the buffer that has already been processed and removed.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR97639.

This issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.3.1-HF3 issued for BIG-IP 9.3.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
