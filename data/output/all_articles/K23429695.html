Description

A warning message appears when creating a UCS backup file from the configuration utility (GUI) or tmsh. You may encounter warning messages such as below:

tar: files_d/Common_d/certificate_key_d/:Common:example_com.key_73077_1: time stamp 2019-03-19 11:52:49 is 20293071.886230791 s in the future
tar: files_d/Common_d: time stamp 2019-02-01 15:58:48 is 16333430.883247487 s in the future
tar: files_d: time stamp 2019-02-01 15:58:47 is 16333429.883202735 s in the future
tar: conf/ssl.crt/server.crt: time stamp 2019-02-01 15:56:47 is 16333309.877553324 s in the future
tar: conf/ssl.crt: time stamp 2019-02-01 15:56:47 is 16333309.87742293 s in the future
tar: conf/ssl.key/server.key: time stamp 2019-02-01 15:56:47 is 16333309.877337326 s in the future
tar: conf/ssl.key: time stamp 2019-02-01 15:56:47 is 16333309.87728267 s in the future

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • UCS

Cause

The warning messages do not affect UCS creation. The affected files may have incorrect time due to NTP issues at the time of file creation.

Recommended Actions

You can resolve the issue and suppress the warning messages by applying the current time to the affected files. Below are the commands for the example above.

touch /config/filestore/files_d/Common_d/certificate_key_d/:Common:example_com.key_73077_1
touch /config/filestore/files_d/Common_d
touch /config/filestore/files_d
touch /config/httpd/conf/ssl.crt/server.crt
touch /config/httpd/conf/ssl.crt
touch /config/httpd/conf/ssl.key/server.key
touch /config/httpd/conf/ssl.key
