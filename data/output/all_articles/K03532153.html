Known Issue

BIG-IP VE with a rate limit license may unexpectedly stop processing application traffic.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP VE instance is licensed with a bandwidth rate limited license.
  • The combined application and non-application (administrative and HA synchronization) traffic exceeds the bandwidth limit of the license.

Impact

The BIG-IP VE drops application traffic packets.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • In the /var/log/ltm file, you may observe log messages similar to the following example:

    notice tmm[<pid>]: 01010045:5: Bandwidth utilization is 122 Mbps, exceeded 75% of Licensed 10 Mbps

  • When you analyze the amount of traffic bandwidth used by applications that the BIG-IP system processed, the total amount of bandwidth used is below the license limit.
  • When you combine the amount of non-application traffic of the BIG-IP VE with the bandwidth used for processing application traffic, the total bandwidth is greater than the license limit.
