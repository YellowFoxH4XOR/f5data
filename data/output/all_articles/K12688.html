Topic

Under certain conditions, the BIG-IP Edge Portal app may fail to uninstall from an Android-based device. This issue occurs because the application is associated with the device administrator's group on the Android device.

Removing the BIG-IP Edge Portal app

Note: Not all device manufacturers support the Device Administrators setting. For more information, refer to your device provider.

  1. Log in to the Android device.
  2. Navigate to Settings > Location and security.

    Note: The names of these menu options may be different on your Android device.
  3. Select Select device administrators.
  4. Clear the BIG-IP Edge Portal option.

    A confirmation window appears.
  5. Select Deactivate.
  6. Press the back button twice.

    You will be returned to the Settings menu.
  7. Navigate to Settings > Apps.
  8. Select the F5 BIG-IP Edge Portal application.
  9. Select Uninstall.
  10. Select OK.

    A new pop-up window opens.
  11. When the Uninstall finished message displays, select OK.
