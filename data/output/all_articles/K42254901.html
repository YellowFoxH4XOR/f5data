Description

A BIG-IP VE instance may restart and go offline for a short period of time when migrating via VMware vMotion.
In /var/log/ltm, you may observe log messages similar to the following:

notice mcpd[<PID>]: 01070410:5: Removed subscription with subscriber id %promptstatusd
info audit_forwarder[<PID>]: audit_forwarder started.
notice devmgmtd[<PID>]: 015a0000:5: shutdown cleanly

You cannot find the initiator of the reboot in any of the logs.

Environment

  • VMware ESXi
  • BIG-IP HA

Cause

Possible issue with VMware vMotion. The VMware log could contain log messages similar to the following:

This Virtual machine is vSphere HA Protected and HA will attempt to restart it after a failure.

Recommended Actions

Report the issue to the VMware administrator, check the VMware log, address the root cause.
