Description

You are trying to open a support case on the MyF5 portal and getting this error when adding the serial number:

"The value entered is invalid or out of Support. Please call F5 Technical Support if you require assistance."

Environment

  • BIG-IP

  • BIG-IQ

  • BIG-IP Next

  • NGINX

  • MyF5

Cause

  • Your MyF5 user account has not yet been associated with the company account that the device is linked to.
  • The support contract is expired.
  • The serial number provided is not in the correct format.

Recommended Actions

Associate your MyF5 user account with the company account

To associate your MyF5 user account with the company account, contact F5 Support and provide:

  • Your MyF5 user account.
  • Device serial number, last 7 characters of the base registration key, and subscription number.
  • Name of the company that owns the device, VE, or subscription.

Note: The default user role is Viewer. For more information, refer to K000136095: About MyF5 account user roles and permissions.

Check the support contact expiration date

To check the support contact expiry date, use the Product Information Request form.

Check the serial number format

  • Use the serial number for the physical device. To locate the device serial number, refer to K917: Finding the serial number or registration key of your F5 device.
  • Use the last 7 characters of the Base Registration Key for the virtual edition license. For more information, refer to K54213125: Find the registration key of your BIG-IP VE system.
  • Use the subscription number for NGINX and BIG-IP Next that should be provided by the Sales team.

    Note: If you have a Sales Order, refer to Sales Order Details and locate the number starts with the letter “Z”. Remove the "Z' from the key and only use the last 7 characters.

Additional Information

If you see only NGINX product options and do not see any BIG-IP product options when attempting to open a support case, refer to K000153066: BIG-IP/BIG-IQ products not displaying on case creation.
