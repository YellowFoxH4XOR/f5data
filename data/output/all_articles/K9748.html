Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Due to a port binding conflict, TCP Half Open and UDP monitors may fail to send out probe packets at the configured interval when the monitor is assigned to more than one pool member. Probes are sent intermittently as the conflicted port becomes available. With monitor debugging enabled, messages that appear similar to the following example are logged to the /var/log/bigdlog file for each failed connection binding attempt:

19:12:25.716757: (_send_half_open_ping): Cannot bind to 10.10.10.119:80, Invalid argument

Note: You can enable monitor logging by setting the Bigd.Debug db variable to enable.

In some cases, the monitor timeout may expire before the system is able to send the monitor probes. As a result, false outages or flapping occurs, where pool members are marked Offline and Available in rapid succession. The issue is more pronounced at smaller probe intervals, and when many pool members are monitored by a TCP Half Open or UDP monitor type.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR97055, and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.4.5 and 9.6.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.

Workaround

You can work around this issue by avoiding the use of TCP Half Open or UDP monitors, or by upgrading to an unaffected version.
