Description
The basic ELA hardware(HW) comes with no software use rights, as it will be covered within the ELA for intended use. There is a separate software(SW) subscription add-on license that unlocks all of the additional modules for the ELA license. This SW Subscription-on-HW will give the customer access to a catalog of SW modules that they will be able to provision and de-provision on their HW as needed. The following error may be observed if the SW Subscription add-on is missing:
Module not licensed 

Environment

  • ELA license 
  • Hardware
  • Software subscription

Cause
Software(SW) subscription add-on is missing

Recommended Actions

Software subscription add-on needs to be activated on ELA hardware

Additional Information
If you lost the SW subscription add-on which has been activated previously, contact F5 support to retrieve the add-on key.
If you have never activated a SW subscription add-on on this device, contact your F5 sales representative for assistance.
