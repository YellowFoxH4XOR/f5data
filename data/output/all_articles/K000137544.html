Description

Users are unable to view their physical device serial numbers or perpetual license information when logged into the MyF5 portal. 
Note : Although users cannot view physical devices and perpetual license information, users can still open cases through MyF5 portal.

Environment

  • MyF5 portal
  • Physical device
  • Perpetual license

Cause

MyF5 only lists subscription licenses. Physical devices and perpetual licenses are not shown.

Recommended Actions

None
