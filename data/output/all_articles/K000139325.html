Description

This is a guide to upgrade your BIG-IP HA pair, when this BIG-IP HA pair is managed by F5 Container Ingress Services (CIS). 

The objective is to reduce downtime for CIS and BIG-IP while ensuring the most up-to-date configuration is preserved.

Environment

  • Upgrading a BIG-IP HA pair in Active/Standby configuration, managed by CIS.

Cause

 None

Recommended Actions

Consider the compatibility among BIG-IP, AS3 and CIS

  • The CIS version demonstrates greater autonomy from BIGIP and AS3 versions. CIS v2.x works with all current supported BIG-IP and AS3 versions.

https://clouddocs.f5.com/containers/latest/userguide/what-is.html

  • The versions of AS3 and BIGIP have a stronger correlation.

https://clouddocs.f5.com/products/extensions/f5-appsvcs-extension/latest/userguide/prereqs.html

The BIG-IP and AS3 should be upgraded at the same time.  You may upgrade the CIS in the same window, but it can also be upgraded independently either before or after the BIG-IP and AS3 upgrade.

Upgrade steps:

Using K80173021: Simple Step By Step HA Upgrade Process as the base and add CIS related steps during the upgrade process.

Before upgrade, BIG-IP#1 is the active and BIG-IP#2 is the standby. CIS points to management IP of BIG-IP#1.

CIS can point to either BIG-IP in an HA group.

1. Reactivate the license on both BIG-IP units 

2. Upload the software ISOs to both BIG-IP units

3. Upgrade the Standby unit BIG-IP#2.

  • Disable any automatic sync on devices to prevent any sync failures during upgrade

4. Reboot the Standby unit BIG-IP#2.

5. Verify the config on the Standby unit BIG-IP#2.

6. Configure CIS to point to management IP of the upgraded standby BIG-IP #2. CIS will be re-deployed with this change.

7. Failover from the Active to the Standby unit

8. Upgrade the former Active unit BIG-IP#1.

  • Repeat steps 3, 4, & 5 above on the former Active (now Standby) unit

9. Perform a ConfigSync

  • Once both devices are back in sync, reenable any automatic sync on both devices

10. (Optional) If you want CIS to point to BIG-IP #1, change CIS config to point to management IP of the upgraded standby BIG-IP #1, and redeploy CIS.

       Or skip #10 and just keep CIS pointing to BIG-IP #2.

Additional Information

In case the operator has no access to CIS, upgrade BIG-IP HA pair following the steps in K80173021, and inform CIS team that BIG-IP is in upgrading process. CIS pods may report errors during this process but should continue to retry connectivity to BIG-IP.
