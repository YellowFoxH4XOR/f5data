Known Issue

The big3d process may leak memory if the system is unable to process monitor requests in a timely manner.

This issue occurs when one of the following conditions are met:

  • The big3d process is under heavy load due to the number or frequency of monitors being performed.
  • The monitors being processed by the big3d process are timing out or failing to respond in a timely manner.

The big3d process uses a Pending queue and an Active queue to process incoming monitor requests. Objects are created for incoming monitor requests and first placed in the Pending queue. As space in the Active queue becomes available, the system moves the objects to the Active queue for processing. However, if the big3d process is unable to process monitor requests in a timely manner the Active queue can become full. Subsequently, the Pending queue can also become full. When the Pending queue is full, and monitor requests are received, the big3d process deletes the request but fails to completely free the allocated memory.

Note: Beginning in BIG-IP 11.1.0 HF3, the Pending and Active queue sizes were increased. The Active queue was increased from 256 to 2048 slots. The Pending queue was increased from 4096 to 16384 slots. The issue is still possible on versions with the increased queue sizes, but less likely to occur.

Impact

Memory usage for the big3d process increases, and may eventually affect other services and overall system performance.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You notice a progressive increase in the amount of memory that the big3d process uses. You can observe this situation by monitoring the memory consumption of big3d using standard OS tools such as top.

Workaround

To partially mitigate this issue, you may be able to modify your monitor settings to use fewer monitors and/or perform less frequent monitoring. Doing so may help prevent the big3d process from being overloaded, and minimize the chances of the Pending queue being full. Additionally, you should ensure that monitored devices are able to respond in a timely manner.

Impact of workaround: The impact of the suggested workaround depends on the specific environment. F5 recommends that you test any such changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.
