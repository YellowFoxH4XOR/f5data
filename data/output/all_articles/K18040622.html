Description
A BD core may occur due to enforcement of 200004390 200004389 signatures with the combination of Arcsight remote logger enabled.

Environment

  • 200004390 200004389 signatures
  • Arcsight remote logger
  • BIG-IP ASM

Cause
Bug ID 1078765

Recommended Actions

As a workaround, please disable 200004390 200004389 signatures.

If you require further assistance, please contact the F5 Network Support Center.
