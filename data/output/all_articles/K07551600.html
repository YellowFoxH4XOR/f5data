K07551600: GUI gets stuck on 'Sync Initiated' when performing APM policy sync

Type
Support Solution
Published
2021-05-05
Updated
2025-02-06
Applies to
  • BIG-IP APM: 13.X.X
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K07551600

Description

When using a 'Sync-Only' device-group for APM policy sync and initiating the sync on one BIG-IP APM device, the 'Sync Initiated' status remains displayed on the Configuration Utility.

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM
  • Sync-Only APM policy sync device-group

Cause

  • there are 'unexpected' device(s) present in the APM Sync-Only device-group. PolicySync is not expected to perform any action on standby devices.
  • 'asm-sync enabled' in the APM policy sync device group

Recommended Actions

  1. Check the 'Sync-Only'  policy sync device-group and remove the standby devices from it. For policy sync between HA pair, normal sync will do the job.
  2. Disable 'asm-sync' for the APM policy sync device group
 # tmsh modify cm device-group <policy_sync_device_group> asm-sync disabled
 # tmsh save sys config

Additional Information

None.
