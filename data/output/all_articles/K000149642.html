Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • AFM provisioned
  • AFM Protocol Inspection assigned to HTTPS virtual server
  • TMM core

Cause

Seen behaviour is caused by a known issue tracked with the Bug ID1787981

Recommended Actions

To process HTTPS traffic and ensure proper functionality, you need to configure port 443 in the IPS profiles assigned to HTTPS virtual servers.
