Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may log benign error messages during the hotfix installation.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • When you attempt to install/upgrade 11.4.1 HF1, 11.4.1 HF2, 11.4.1 HF3, or any of the 11.5.1 hotfixes.
  • When you use BIG-IP 5050s/5250v, 7050s/7250v, or 10050s/10250v SSD platforms.

Impact

This log message is benign, and may be safely ignored.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • You observe messages in the /var/log/liveinstall.log file that appear similar to the following example:

    info: RPM: kernel-2.6.32-220.el6.f5.707.0
    progress: 34/100
    info: RPM: error: no active volume!
