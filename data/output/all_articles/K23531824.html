Published

Source

Description

There's high CPU usage on BIG-IQ and the following alarm pops up:

Device <device_name> exceeds average CPU usage ratio threshold value of 90.0 percent: 90.06% over the last 10 minutes Time of Alert: YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS.000+00:00 Source: <device_name>  `https://10.10.10.1

Environment

  • CPU usage
  • BIG-IQ CM/DCD

Cause

Incorrectly provisioned VE. Less than the recommend number of cores was provisioned.  

Recommended Actions

Provision the BIG-IQ VE to the at least the minimum number of cores as per its role. Please see "Determine your image and resource requirements" section in the manual below.
