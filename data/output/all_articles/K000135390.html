K000135390: Pools in unknown state on standby device after the upgrade of BIG-IP devices

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-07-13
Updated
2023-07-13
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 16.1.3
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000135390

Description

After upgrading BIG-IP devcies, the standby BIG-IP device marks nodes and pools as an Unknown state where they are available on the active BIG-IP.

Environment

  • BIG-IP Upgrade
  • Nodes
  • Pools

Cause

Unknown

Recommended Actions

Remove and re-add the monitors from the affected pools.

Additional Information

None
