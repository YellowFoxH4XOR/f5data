Description

System experiences a throughput spike out and data plane CPU spike after modifying a bot defense profile. Peer system experiences a throughput spike in.

The spike lasts for around 10 minutes.

Environment

  • Bot defense profile

Cause

The issue was caused by an automatic ConfigSync operation. It is not clear why the bot defense profile in particular caused a spike which lasted for 10 minutes.

Recommended Actions

If the issue is reproducible consistently, open a case with F5 Support for further investigation.
