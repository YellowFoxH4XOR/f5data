Topic

The maximum amperage drawn for a VIPRION chassis varies depending on input voltage.

Description

The different amperage levels drawn by VIPRION chassis per input voltage are listed in the following table. The amperage levels have been validated against the power supply manufacturers' public certifications.

VIPRION PlatformInput Voltage (VAC)Frequency (Hz)Amperage
C2400100 - 12750 - 6017
200 - 24050 - 6010
C440090 - 24050 - 6016
C4800100 - 24047 - 6316
200 - 24047 - 6316

Recommendations

F5 does everything possible to meet each country's compliance requirements. When planning VIPRION installations, you should validate that the power cables and power sources are in compliance with your country's specifications.
