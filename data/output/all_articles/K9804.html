Known Issue

This is a result of a known issue. When you log on to a FirePass controller from a client running a Linux operating system and launch a Network Access Favorite that is configured to force all traffic through the tunnel, you may lose connection to FirePass. This issue is caused by incorrect installation of routing table entries on the client machine.

Important: This issue occurs only on FirePass version 6.0.2 with HF-602-7.2-1 installed.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR109751, and it was fixed in cumulative hotfix HF-602-8. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the cumulative hotfix from the F5 Networks Downloads site.

For instructions about obtaining a hotfix, refer to K167: Downloading software from F5 Networks.

For instructions about installing a hotfix, refer to K3430: Installing FirePass hotfixes.
