Known Issue

The Status Age parameter in an OCSP Stapling profile will not accept a value greater than 86400 seconds (1 day).

Impact

You cannot configure an OCSP Stapling profile to accept valid OCSP responses that are older than 1 day.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Valid OCSP responses greater than 1 day are discarded.
