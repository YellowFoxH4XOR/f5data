Known Issue

The Configuration utility may fail to display the member-specific health monitor configured on a pool member.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have one or more fully-qualified domain name (FQDN) pool members configured on a BIG-IP system.
  • You configure a member-specific health monitor on one of the FQDN pool members.

Impact

The Configuration utility may erroneously display a pool member without a member-specific health monitor configured, even though the specified health monitor is correctly applied to the pool member.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • After you assign a member-specific health monitor to a pool member, the Configuration utility fails to display the health monitor in the Active column of the pool member's configuration.

Workaround

There is no workaround; however, you can view the health monitor configured on the pool member using the TMOS Shell (tmsh). To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP command line.
  2. Log in to tmsh by typing the following command:

    tmsh

  3. To view the health monitor configured on the pool member, use the following command syntax:

    list /ltm pool <Pool_Name> members <Pool_Member>
