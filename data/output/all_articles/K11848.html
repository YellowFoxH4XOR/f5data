Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When ZoneRunner is synchronizing zones, the BIG-IP GTM may log a message to the /var/log/gtm file that appears similar to the following example:

SYNC_ZONES completed from <unknonw>

Note: The word unknown is spelled incorrectly in the log message.

This message is benign and may safely be ignored. This message is a result of ZoneRunner not clearing the timer for synchronizing zones, and sometimes sending two SYNC_ZONE completed messages to the gtmd process instead.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as ID 225996 (formerly CR140601).
