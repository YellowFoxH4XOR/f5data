Known Issue

The Configuration utility may not recognize the configured timeout value and unexpectedly log you out of the system.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You log in to the Configuration utility and leave it idle for approximately 20 to 30 minutes.

Impact

The Configuration utility unexpectedly logs you out of the system and does not recognize the configured timeout value.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The Configuration utility unexpectedly logs you out of the system.
