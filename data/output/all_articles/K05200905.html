Description

The following error is shown when generating a trial license for NGINX Instance Manager:

"Something went wrong :( Please reach out to your account team for help. No account team? That's ok, call Support and let them know it's a trial."

Environment

  • NGINX Instance Manager
  • Trial License
  • MyF5

Cause
None at the time of creation.

Recommended Actions

Perform the following steps to get free trial license for NGINX Instance Manager. 

  1. Log in to your MyF5 account. 
  2. From the Home page, select Go To Trials.
  3. Under Trial offers, for NGINX Instance Manager, select START YOUR TRIAL.  
    Note: You will receive a confirmation email indicating that your NGINX Instance Manager Trial has been activated. This may take several minutes. 
  4. After you receive your email confirmation, return to the MyF5 Home page and select Launch Your Trial. 
If the error persists, please contact F5 Technical Support to assist you further.
