Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When the BIG-IP ASM is installed on an 8800 platform, the CPU utilization reported by the Utilization graph within the Configuration utility does not correspond with the accurate utilization reported by the top utility.

The CPU utilization reported is not proportional in relation to the throughput capability of an 8800 platform. The CPU utilization reported is almost identical regardless of the number of connections and throughput.

For example, decreasing connections from 50 to 1 does not decrease the reported resources consumed, as shown in the following output:

| TPS | top - BD | Throughput | Reqs/connection|
11000  - 80%    - 130Mbps   - 50
10000  - 78%    - 120 Mbs   -  6
 9000  - 77%    - 110 Mbps  -  5
 5700  - 79%    - 79 Mbps   -  3
 2000  - 79%    - 30 Mbps   -  1

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR109746 and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM versions 9.4.7 and 10.0.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM release notes.
