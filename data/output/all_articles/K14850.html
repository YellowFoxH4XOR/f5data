Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) process may become unresponsive, and then restart.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have enabled priority groups on a pool.
  • You have configured the rate limit feature on a pool member.

Impact

TMM may produce a core file and temporarily fail to process traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H438545 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The sod daemon starts the TMM process.
  • You may see a SIGABRT message in the /var/log/tmm file that appears similar to the following example:

    notice ** SIGABRT **
    notice sender pid: <pid> (/usr/bin/sod)
