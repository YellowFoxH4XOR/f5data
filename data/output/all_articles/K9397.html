Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP system does not correctly parse RTSP messages that are fragmented in the middle of the header value. The BIG-IP erroneously treats the fragment containing the first portion of the header as the complete RTSP of header. When BIG-IP receives the fragment containing the remaining portion of the header, it closes the connection by sending a TCP reset to the client.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR110808 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.4.7. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.

Note: This issue does not affect BIG-IP versions 10.0.0 and later.
