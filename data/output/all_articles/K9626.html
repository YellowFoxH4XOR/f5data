Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Changing system time may cause the BIG-IP ASM bd process to core. A large time shift may cause the BIG-IP ASM bd process to behave as if it did not receive a Keep-Alive response from its IO thread.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR114412, and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM 9.4.7 and 10.0.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM Release Notes.
