Description

The Service Check date may not update after a License Activation is done if there is no sales order number associated with the serial number or if the service contract is specifically limited by contract arrangements.

Environment

  • Software Upgrade
  • License reactivation 

Cause

Some unique sales orders are configured in such a way that the Service Check date is a fixed number and it will not update even if you re-activate the license.  Therefore, that unit is upgrade limited to versions supported by their Service Check Date.

Recommended Actions

Follow the below procedure for further assistance.

  1. Verify Product Information Request at Product Information Request.
  2. Entitled Service will be shown as none or have an end date in an email report sent to your desired email address.

    #-------------------------------------------------------------------

    # Service and Warranty Information

    #-------------------------------------------------------------------

      Entitled Service: None
    OR
      Entitled Service      : 07-04-2018 - 07-03-2019 
      Warranty End Date     : 07-03-2019

  3. Contact your sales professional if you feel your license was limited in error.

Additional Information

When viewing the license on the device's CLI, you will notice that the license date updates but the service date does not. For example:

[root@bigip1:Active:Standalone] config # tmsh show sys license | grep "Licensed On\|Service Check Date"
Licensed on 01/30/2020
Service Check Date 12/27/2019

In the GUI go to System > License.  The Licensed date should show the date you attempted to renew the license but the Expiration date may not change.
