Policy Information

With the release of BIG-IQ 6.1, F5 hereby announces the End of Life (EoL) plan for the F5 iWorkflow product.

iWorkflow will officially reach the End of Sale (EoS) product lifecycle milestone effective January 1, 2019.

All iWorkflow SKUs will be removed from the global price list on December 1, 2018.

iWorkflow SKUs will be disabled for sale on January 1, 2019.

Technical support

Customers whose platforms are designated EoL and who are currently under maintenance contracts will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the service contract renewal date expires.

After the EoSD date, 31-Jan-2019, F5 will no longer be providing software fixes for any technical issues and F5 will continue to provide technical support for iWorkflow on a best-effort basis.

Migration considerations

F5 recommends customers currently using iWorkflow, use BIG-IQ 6.1 with Application Services 3 Extension (AS3) Integration as an alternative mechanism for application service deployment automation and flexible service catalog capabilities.

Existing customers who have deployed iWorkflow with an F5 Support contract are entitled to a $0 product upgrade with F5-BIGIQ-VE-M.

For instructions about migrating from the Application Services iApp AS2 using iWorkflow's Service Catalog to AS3, refer to Migration from Application Services iApp AS2 to AS3.
