K401: Proper use of continuous pressure power switches

Topic

Some models of BIG-IP and 3-DNS have a power switch that responds to continuous pressure, as opposed to an absolute two-position power switch. These models require five seconds of continuous pressure on the switch in order to switch the system on or off. If you are experiencing this issue, try pressing the switch for five seconds. If this does not produce results, hardware failure is a possibility; contact F5 Technical Support for assistance.
