Known Issue

Processing iStats commands causes the BIG-IP system to leak memory.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is configured with SSL Orchestrator.
  • Your BIG-IP system uses istats incr and istats set commands.

Impact

Over time, the memory usage of your BIG-IP system grows and may lead to the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) restarting. The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from the TMM restart, and devices that you configure as a high availability (HA) pair may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The memory usage on the affected BIG-IP system increases over time and leads to TMM restarting.
  • When TMM restarts, your BIG-IP system generates a TMM core file to the /shared/core directory.
