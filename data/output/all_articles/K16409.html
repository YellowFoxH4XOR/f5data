Known Issue

The Cavium Nitrox SSL accelerator card may hang and generate an error message.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP platform contains a Cavium Nitrox SSL accelerator card.

    Note: This issues does not affect the BIG-IP 1000, 2400, 51x0, 2000s, 2200s, 4000s, and 4200v platforms.

  • The Cavium Nitrox SSL accelerator encrypts certain SSL records.

Impact

The Cavium Nitrox SSL accelerator card hangs.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • F5 iHealth lists Heuristic H517355 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High screen.
  • In the /var/log/ltm file, you observe error messages that appear similar to the following example:

    01010260:2: Hardware Error(Co-Processor): cn1 request queue stuck

  • The Cavium Nitrox SSL accelerator card hangs, and SSL traffic is temporarily disrupted.

Workaround

You may be able to work around this issue by restarting the BIG-IP system, which often frees up the Cavium Nitrox crypto accelerator chip. You should consider this as a temporary solution and not a permanent fix. The the issue may recur.
