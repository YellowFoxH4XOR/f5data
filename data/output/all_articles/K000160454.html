Description

The F5 BIG-IP LTM high availability (HA) pair experienced repeated failover events, causing disruption to internet traffic at a specific office. The failovers were triggered by the HA group score dropping to zero due to a pool that was configure in the HA group, this pool is intermittently having no available members, as indicated by log messages such as "No members available for pool /Common/pool1."

Mar 24 14:03:49 BIG-IP1.local notice sod[6828]: 010c0052:5: Standby for traffic group traffic-group-1.

Mar 24 14:03:52 BIG-IP1.local notice mcpd[7327]: 01070727:5: Pool /Common/pool1 member /Common/10.10.10.1:0 monitor status up. [ /Common/gateway_icmp: up ] [ was down for 0hr:0min:3sec ]

Mar 24 14:03:52 BIG-IP1.local notice mcpd[7327]: 010719fb:5: HA Group ha-pool score updated from 0 to 20.


 

Environment

BIG-IP LTM version 17.5.1

Platform: F5-BIG-IP (two-device HA pair: Primary Device [Active], Secondary Device [Standby])

HA configuration using HA group with two monitored pools: "pool1" and "pool2"

Each pool configured with a threshold and weight contributing to the HA group score


 

Cause

The root cause was the intermittent unavailability (flapping) of members in a pool configured in a HA group. When all members became unavailable, the HA group score dropped to zero, causing the device to relinquish active status and trigger failover. This behavior is by design when using HA groups tied to pool member status.


 

Answer/Recommended Actions

Investigate and resolve the underlying cause of that pool member flapping to prevent HA group score drops and unintended failovers.

If the HA group mechanism is not required, remove the HA group configuration from both devices to prevent failover events based on pool member status:

Navigate to System > High Availability > HA Group List and remove the HA group.

Continue monitoring pool member status and HA events to ensure stability.
