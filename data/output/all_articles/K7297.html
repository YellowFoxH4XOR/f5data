K7297: Meaning of the Heartbeat setting on the 4100 and 4300 LCD

Topic



FirePass controller models 4100 and 4300 have a liquid crystal display (LCD) panel located on the front of the unit, with buttons to navigate the menus in the display. When the Heartbeat menu option is enabled, a heart-shaped display will appear in the upper-right corner of the display. This setting indicates communication between the main motherboard and the SCCP (switchboard) of the FirePass controller.

Generally, this information is not of use to the end user, and may only be useful in certain troubleshooting scenarios where there is no network connectivity through the switch ports, but the FirePass controller is accessible through the management port or console.

This setting does not pertain to the failover heartbeat of a high availability pair configuration.
