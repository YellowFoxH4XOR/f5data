Known Issue

Topology load balancing may not work as expected.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured the Topology load balancing method for a wide IP.
  • Two or more pools associated with the wide IP share the highest assigned score for a particular load balancing decision.

Impact

When two or more pools share the same assigned score, the resulting load balancing decisions can lead to the system distributing pool selections in an unequal or unexpected fashion.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Pools or pool members experience overload when the system makes load balancing decisions that result in an unequal or unexpected distribution.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can adjust the Topology records and pools to avoid the conditions that cause the issue. For information about making this adjustment, refer to K13412: Overview of BIG-IP DNS Topology records (11.x - 14.x).
