Description

TXT records are a type of DNS record used to associate a string of text with a domain name. They are commonly used for domain validation and email authentication.

TXT records are often required when setting up services such as Google Workspace or Microsoft Office 365, which use them to verify domain ownership. They are also used for email authentication mechanisms like SPF, DKIM, and DMARC to help prevent email spoofing.

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud (XC) Primary DNS

Cause

None

Answer/Recommended Actions

  • To add a TXT record in F5 Distributed Cloud (XC) Primary DNS, use the DNS management interface to create a new TXT record for the relevant domain.
  • Enter the required text string as provided by your service provider (e.g., Google, Microsoft, or your email provider).
  • Save and apply the changes to ensure the TXT record is published and available for DNS queries.
  • For email authentication, follow the specific instructions for SPF, DKIM, or DMARC as provided by your email service.

Additional Information

TXT records are essential for domain validation and email security. For more information on SPF, DKIM, and DMARC, refer to the following:

  • Sender Policy Framework (SPF)
  • [DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM)]) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DomainKeys_Identified_Mail)
  • DMARC Policies https://dmarc.org/overview/)
