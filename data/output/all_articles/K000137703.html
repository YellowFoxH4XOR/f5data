K000137703: Did not receive an activation e-mail after registering for a MyF5 account login.

Source

Description

Did not receive an activation e-mail after registering for a MyF5 account login. 

Environment

  • MyF5
  • Login Access 

Cause

Unknown

Recommended Actions

There are various reasons why an activation e-mail is not delivered, follow the below steps. 

  1. Ensure you have registered at Create Your F5 Account
  2. Check junk mail and spam folders for an activation e-mail from noreply@mail.account.f5.com
  3. Verify with your IT team to see if the email address has been filtered out.
  4. If all of this has been completed and it has been over an hour since the user has registered their email address, contact F5 support.
