Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) or BIG-IP Analytics (AVR) process may restart when a BIG-IP system is processing high volumes of traffic.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have provisioned the AVR module.

    Note: The AVR module is not provisioned by default.
  • The BIG-IP system is processing a very large amount of network traffic.

Impact

Traffic processing is interrupted while TMM restarts, and statistics may not be available while the avrd process restarts.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Traffic processing fails for a short period of time.
  • The BIG-IP system generates TMM core files in the /var/core directory, and the time stamp of the files correlates with the time of traffic interruption.
  • AVR statistics are not available, or may reveal missing data for short periods of time.
  • The BIG-IP system generates AVR core files in the /var/core directory, and the time stamp of the files correlates with the time of missing statistics.
