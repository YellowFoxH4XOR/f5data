Topic

Summary

The BIG-IP upgrade script creates a temporary object called dummy_peer_device_object when upgrading a high availability pair from BIG-IP 10.x to 11.x.

Description

When upgrading a BIG-IP high availability pair from 10.x to 11.x, you may notice a peer device object called dummy_peer_device_object on the Device Management screens in the Configuration utility. The BIG-IP upgrade script creates a temporary placeholder object called dummy_peer_device_object when upgrading a high availability pair from BIG-IP 10.x to 11.x. The dummy_peer_device_object placeholder object represents the peer device and remains in the configuration until the peer system is upgraded to 11.x and the devices join the trust domain.

Once both devices complete the upgrade process and join the trust domain, the dummy_peer_device_object object will be updated to represent the peer device's name.

Recommendations

If the dummy_peer_device_object name remains on one or both of the systems after completing the upgrade process, it may be an indication that the device trust has failed between the devices. In this case, refer to the Troubleshooting device service clustering section in K13946: Troubleshooting ConfigSync and device clustering (11.x).
