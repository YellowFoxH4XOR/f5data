Known Issue

A potential Tcl leak occurs during the iRules suspend operation.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured an iRule and it is in use.
  • The iRule contains a combination of procedure calls or loops that are nested at least five levels deep.
  • The iRule contains a suspend operation within the nested calls or loops.

Impact

Memory exhaustion may result in degraded performance or interruptions in traffic processing.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) Tcl memory usage increases over time and does not decrease.
  • TMM produces a core file.
  • Traffic is interrupted while the system restarts the TMM daemon.
  • Redundant systems fail over.
