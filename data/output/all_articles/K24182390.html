Known Issue

AVR Max metrics display incorrect values.

This issue occurs because of a 32-bit integer overflow during high traffic conditions.

Impact

Metrics that the system displays do not correctly reflect the activity.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • Displayed AVR Max metrics do not correctly reflect the activity.

Resolution

Status
