Known Issue

Some BIG-IP blades fail to connect to SafeNet HSM devices.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system uses a SafeNet HSM device to protect Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) keys.
  • Connectivity from the BIG-IP system to the SafeNet HSM device is temporarily lost and then restored.

After the BIG-IP system disconnects from the SafeNet HSM device, some blades can reconnect to the SafeNet HSM device while other blades fail to connect.

Impact

Some Client SSL connections fail.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Some BIG-IP blades can connect to the SafeNet HSM device, while other blades fail to connect.
  • In the /var/log/ltm file, you observe error messages similar to the following example:

    err pkcs11d[27532]: Nethsm: pkcs11_session_reinit Session initialization error
    err pkcs11d[27532]: Nethsm: pkcs11_session_init C_OpenSession error 0x00000030
