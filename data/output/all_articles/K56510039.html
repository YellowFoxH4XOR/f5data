Description

Does BIG-IQ support Telemetry Streaming for its own health monitoring?

Environment
  • BIG-IQ
  • Telemetry Streaming
Cause

BIG-IQ does not support Telemetry Streaming.
Telemetry Streaming is supported by means of iAppLX on BIG-IP only:  Telemetry Streaming > Prerequisites and Requirements

Recommended Actions

None.

None.
