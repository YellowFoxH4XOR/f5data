Known Issue

The dnatutil utility may fail to parse the /var/log/ltm file for deterministic NAT data.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is licensed with the CGNAT module.
  • The LSN pool name is longer than 20 characters.

Impact

You may be unable to map external addresses and ports back to internal clients.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H427851 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The dnatutil utility may output error messages that appear similar to the following example:

    Specified subscriber or translation address not in configured address set.
    Unable to parse why

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 427107 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies To box, located in the upper-right corner of the article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can rename the LSN pool to be 20 characters or less.
