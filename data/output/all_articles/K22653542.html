Known Issue

Clients sending a malformed cookie may prevent the parsing of a valid persistence cookie.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • An HTTP client sends two or more cookies, one of which is the BIG-IP HTTP persistence cookie.
  • One of the cookies includes a blank space character.
  • The cookie that includes the blank space character precedes the BIG-IP persistence cookie.

    For example:

    Cookie: foo=bar =bar; BIGipServerhttp=60361226.20480.0001

Impact

HTTP clients may not persist to the expected origin web server.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Persistence cookies are ignored for affected requests.
