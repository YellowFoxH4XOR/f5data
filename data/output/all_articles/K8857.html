Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The VIPRION base license does not enable Clustered Multi-Processing (CMP) by default. As a result, the SSL TPS license limits on VIPRION platforms are enforced per blade rather than per licensed TMM instance.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR100233.

This issue has been fixed in cumulative Hotfix-BIGIP-9.6.1-839.0-HF1, which has been issued for BIG-IP 9.6.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the cumulative hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.

For information about installing a hotfix, refer to K6845: Managing BIG-IP product hotfixes (9.x).

For information about downloading software from F5, refer to K167: Downloading software from F5.
