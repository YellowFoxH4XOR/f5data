Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP GTM license does not allow the use of the TCP profile. This prevents a TCP-based listener from being configured to allow BIG-IP GTM to accept TCP-based DNS queries.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR74218 and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.3.0 and 9.4.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM release notes.
