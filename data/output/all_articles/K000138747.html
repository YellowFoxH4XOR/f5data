K000138747: The included rackmount rails for the F5 r2000/r4000 hardware products may appear to not work in racks with circular holes

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-03-05
Updated
2024-03-05
Applies to
  • rSeries Platforms
  • rSeries 2000
  • rSeries 4000
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000138747

Description

The included rackmount rails for the F5 r2000/r4000 hardware products may appear to only work in racks with square holes and not racks with circular holes. However, this is not true. The rackmount rail kit included with the F5 r2000/r4000 hardware products can be installed into square hole or round hole racks.

Environment

  • F5 rSeries r2000/r4000 hardware products.
  • Quick-install rails included with the r2000/r4000 hardware.
  • Racks with square holes or round holes.

Cause

None.

Recommended Actions

The square part springs flat exposing the round pin. For details on how to expose the round pin, refer to the following video or images:
