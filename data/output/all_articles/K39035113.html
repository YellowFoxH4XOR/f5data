Known Issue

The default SNMP community (public) may reappear in a configuration after an upgrade.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have previously removed the default SNMP community (public) from the configuration.
  • You upgrade your BIG-IP 11.3.0 or earlier system, to version 11.4.0 through 11.6.1, and you roll the configuration forward.

Impact

The previously deleted default SNMP community (public) is unexpectedly added back into the configuration.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • After upgrading and rolling a configuration forward, the previously deleted default SNMP community (public) is added back into the configuration.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can delete the default 'public' community again after upgrading to BIG-IP 11.4.0 or later. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.
  2. Click System.
  3. Navigate to the SNMP > Agent > Access (v1, v2c).
  4. Select the public:default check box.
  5. Click Delete.
  6. Confirm the deletion by clicking Delete again.
