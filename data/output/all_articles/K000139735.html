K000139735: The BIG-IP is not generating logs in /var/log/ltm

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-09-01
Updated
2026-09-01
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.X.X, 17.1.X, 17.1.1, 17.1.0, 17.0.X, 17.0.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000139735

Description

The device cannot generate logs locally under /var/log/ltm directory. Also, you observe that the /var partition reports that there 0% free space.

Environment

  • Logs
  • /var parition

Cause

/var partition is full and syslog-ng daemon is stopped 

Recommended Actions

Please clear the /var partition form of old unnecessary files such as qkivew, ucs files, and packet captures as well as restart the syslog-nd daemon using the following command :

tmsh bigstart restart syslog-ng

Additional Information

None
