Note: This article applies to the BIG-IP 1600, 3600, and 8800 platforms, the BIG-IP ASM and FirePass 4100 platforms, and the FirePass 1200 platform. For information about the BIG-IP and FirePass software versions supported by these platforms, refer to K9476: The F5 hardware/software compatibility matrix.

Beginning in May 2009, due to product availability, F5 is changing the size of hard drives installed on the following platforms:

BIG-IP 1600, 3600, and 8800

The BIG-IP 1600, 3600, and 8800 platforms will discontinue using a 160 GB hard drive and begin using a 320 GB hard drive.

BIG-IP ASM and FirePass 4100

The BIG-IP ASM and FirePass 4100 platforms will discontinue using an 80 GB hard drive and begin using a 160 GB hard drive.

FirePass 1200

The FirePass 1200 platform will discontinue using a 40 GB hard drive and begin using a 160 GB hard drive.

Note: The disk drive changes do not affect the behavior of high availability redundant pairs. There is no dependency on disk size for failover to work properly.

Note: RMA replacement hard drives or systems will be replaced with the same size hard drive as existed on the failed unit when possible.
