Known Issue

The Class of Service (CoS) configuration may not persist following a staggered restart of high availability (HA) systems.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured the BIG-IP HA systems to utilize CoS to provide support for traffic prioritization using Differentiated Services Code Point (DSCP) or 802.1p.
  • You restart one of the BIG-IP systems in the HA group, wait a moment, then restart another system in the HA group.

Once systems recover from the reboot, the CoS global settings may be missing from one of the BIG-IP systems.

Impact

CoS configuration information is missing from one of the BIG-IP systems, resulting in inconsistent behavior between active and standby devices.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You observe that there is no existing CoS configuration.

    For example:

    tmsh list /net cos

    net cos global-settings { }
