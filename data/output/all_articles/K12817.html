ARX Cloud Extender (CE) software version lifecycles

Unless specifically declared and announced by F5, ARX Cloud Extender (CE) software releases use the following software version lifeccycles:

  • During the Regular or Extended Support Phases for a Major software release, software support will occur for the most recent maintenance release of the current minor, and the most recent maintenance release of the two previous minor releases.
  • A Major software release is supported for a minimum of three years after First Customer ship date to EoSD.
  • A Major software release is supported for a minimum of two years after it is designated a General Sustaining (GS) release.
  • A Major software release is moved into GS mode after a new Major software release is available.
  • One year after the EoSD date is the End of Technical Support (EoTS) date and the End of Return to Manufacturing (EoRMA) date.

Lifecycle glossary

End of Life TermDescription
End of Sale (EoS)F5 stops selling this product, software version, or platform.
End of Software Development (EoSD)Applicable to both platforms and software releases, EoSD is the date that F5 Product Development has ceased considering the repair/maintenance of confirmed software/firmware defects for the designated platform or software release. F5 will offer only reasonable effort technical configuration support. Unless specifically communicated differently by F5, a platform's EoSD date defaults to three years after EoS. Unless specifically communicated differently by F5, a software release's EoSD date defaults to two years after the release enters its General Sustaining phase.
End of Technical Support (EoTS)F5 will no longer accept technical support calls after this date for this platform, product, or software version. Unless specifically communicated differently by F5, a platform's EoTS date defaults to seven years after EoS. Unless specifically communicated differently by F5, a software release's EoTS date defaults to three years after the release enters its General Sustaining phase.
Maintenance releaseThis is a new version of ARX code with bug fixes only.  It may also include support for new hardware, and in rare cases, support for CSRs based on risk and business assessment. Maintenance releases are scheduled releases.
Hotfix Rollup (HFRU)This is a new build on a released version of the software and it may contain many fixes previously provided to customers in engineering hotfix releases.  Hotfix rollup builds are scheduled, as required, and these builds are the standard method F5 uses for hotfix delivery.
Engineering hotfixThis is a specific fix for a specific customer, based off a specific ARX version and build.  These may contain one or more fixes for a specific customer.  Engineering hotfixes are used less frequently than hotfix rollup builds and F5 builds them ad-hoc on an as-needed basis.
