Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. In version 9.4 of the BIG-IP system, the code that checks for PVA lockup issues was not integrated properly. As a result, the PVA2 chip can lock up, causing the TMM process to crash.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR75339, and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.4.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the LTM, GTM, ASM, Link Controller, or WebAccelerator Release Notes.
