Environment

  • HA (i.e., ACTIVE/STANDBY).
  • SSL connection mirroring.
  • Client-ssl profile uses default.crt and default.key.

 

Cause

Even though cert/key object name may be identical "default.crt" and "default.key" between HA units, actual cert/key contents on each BIG-IP unit may be different, as shown below. You will find checksum, fingerprint, or serial-number differs.

During TLS handshake on SSL connection with mirroring virtual server, HA ACTIVE unit processes TLS message with its own default.crt/default.key. And when client TLS message comes in to HA ACTIVE unit, that specific TLS message can be verified/decrypted on HA ACTIVE unit which has own default.crt/default.key. However, with SSL connection mirroring, the received TLS message is next then copied over to peer HA STANDBY unit and processed on HA STANDBY unit as well.


HA STANDBY unit may have different default cert/key pair from ACTIVE unit even though cert/key name may be identical, therefore, HA STANDBY unit can fail to properly process client's TLS message with its local default.cert and default.key. As a result of STANDBY unit fails to process received TLS message, aforementioned error is recorded on STANDBY unit. While STANDBY unit fails to process mirrored TLS message, ACTIVE unit waits for STANDBY unit's acknowledgement (statemirror.verify) and that can result in 8 seconds delay.

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

Do not use default.crt and default.key on client-ssl profile when SSL connection mirroring is in use. Import valid cert/key pair on one of HA unit, synchronize it to the HA peer, and use the imported cert/key pair on client-ssl profile. With same cert/key objects between ACTIVE unit and STANDBY unit, STANDBY unit will not fail to process mirrored TLS message.

 

K17391 also states "SSL profiles configured with the default certificate and key" is not supported.
