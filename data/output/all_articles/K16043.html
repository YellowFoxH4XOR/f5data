Known Issue

The VIPRION 2100 or 2150 blades may experience a traffic interruption when performing compression and decompression operations.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • A large or corrupt file being decompressed requires multiple Octeon operations to decompress the file.

The likelihood of encountering this issue increases relative to the size of file being decompressed. However, any file larger than 32K after it has been decompressed, or a corrupt file, could encounter this issue.

Impact

The system may experience an interruption in traffic processing as a result of the halted compression and decompression operations.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • A VIPRION 2100 or 2150 blade occasionally stops processing traffic while performing compression and decompression operations.
