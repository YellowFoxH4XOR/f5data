Known Issue

The tmsh show /sys cpu command output may contain incorrect information regarding the percentage of cpu used.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You attempt to view the percentage of cpu used on a BIG-IP system using the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh).

Impact

The system displays incorrect information regarding the percentage of cpu used.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The tmsh show sys cpu command output contains an incorrect field description regarding the sampling interval (which should be 5 seconds) for the percentage of cpu used. The command and output may appear similar to the following example:

    tmsh show sys cpu |egrep "CPU:|Util%" CPU: 0 (clock ticks)   Last 5 sec  Last 1 min  Last 5 min   Total
      Util% (last 10 sec)           -           -           -      10
    CPU: 1 (clock ticks)   Last 5 sec  Last 1 min  Last 5 min   Total
      Util% (last 10 sec)           -           -           -       0
    CPU: 2 (clock ticks)   Last 5 sec  Last 1 min  Last 5 min   Total
      Util% (last 10 sec)           -           -           -       5
    CPU: 3 (clock ticks)   Last 5 sec  Last 1 min  Last 5 min   Total
      Util% (last 10 sec)           -           -           -       1
    CPU: 4 (clock ticks)   Last 5 sec  Last 1 min  Last 5 min   Total
      Util% (last 10 sec)           -           -           -       4
    CPU: 5 (clock ticks)   Last 5 sec  Last 1 min  Last 5 min   Total
      Util% (last 10 sec)           -           -           -       0
    CPU: 6 (clock ticks)   Last 5 sec  Last 1 min  Last 5 min   Total
      Util% (last 10 sec)           -           -           -       4
    CPU: 7 (clock ticks)   Last 5 sec  Last 1 min  Last 5 min   Total
      Util% (last 10 sec)           -           -           -       0

  • The percentage of cpu used displayed in the Total column is incorrect.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can use the tmctl command to display the correct percentage of cpu used. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the command line of the BIG-IP system.
  2. To display the percentage of cpu used, type the following command:

    tmctl cpu_info_stat -s 'cpu_id,five_sec_avg.ratio'

    The output of the command should appear similar to the following example:

    cpu_id five_sec_avg.ratio
    ------ ------------------
         0                 11
         1                  1
         2                  5
         3                  1
         4                  5
         5                  1
         6                  5
         7                  0
