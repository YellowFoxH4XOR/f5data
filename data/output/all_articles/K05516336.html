Known Issue

When you deploy an iApp that creates BIG-IP APM objects, customers may experience an error message if the total object name length exceeds 64 characters.

This issue occurs when the following conditions is met:

  • An iApp-generated tunnel name exceeds 64 characters in length

Impact

The iApp configuration fails.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H562320 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Low screen.
  • In the /var/log/ltm log file, you may observe an error message similar to the following example:

    01071529:3: The tunnel name (/Common/<tunnel name>) cannot be longer than 64 characters.

    In the previous syntax, note the following:

    •  <tunnel name> is a string longer than 64 characters in length.

Resolution

Status

F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can use a shorter iApp application name.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
