K000160273: Change in Behavior : Virtual Wire design difference between i-series and r-series

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-03-09
Updated
2026-03-09
Applies to

  • F5OS-C: 1.8.2
Source

Description
Design of Virtual Wire feature was changed since the release of r-series.

 

Environment

  • BIG-IP i-series
  • BIG-IP r-series
  • TMOS
  • F5OS

 

Cause
On i-series, Virtual Wire represented trunk object on BIG-IP LTM.

On r-series r5000/r10000, Virtual Wire is created and managed by F5OS host and attached to the tenant.
Virtual Wire is created by F5OS and tracked as VLAN 4096 by default as an identifier.

BIG-IP tenant do not see physical interfaces or LAGs directly unless a VLAN is assigned to the tenant (K000133082).
Assigning a VLAN to a tenant is the supported way to make the trunk visible inside the tenant for HA Group purposes.
Assigning a VLAN to the tenant and binding it to the LAGs/trunks is required for the tenant 
to see the trunk and use it in HA Group configuration.
Once the trunk is visible in the tenant, HA Group can monitor it.

In i-series, Virtual Wire can be monitored by BIG-IP as it was represented by trunk object on LTM.
On the other hand, r-series do not have the method of Virtual Wire.
At r-series, upon the case for failover, we monitor the vlan attached to the tenant while deploying.
