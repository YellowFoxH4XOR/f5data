K32755669: How to verify support contract end date

Type
Support Solution
Published
2020-06-25
Updated
2026-09-09
Applies to
  • Support Portal
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K32755669

Description

To determine the expiration date of an F5 product's support contract.

Environment

  • MyF5
  • Virtual Edition
  • Physical F5
  • Maintenance 
  • Support Expiry
  • Warranty
  • Entitled Service

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

  1. Access Product Information Request
  2. Enter the email address where you want to receive the report
  3. Enter the registration key or serial number.
    K917: Finding the serial number or registration key of your F5 device 
  4. Click "Submit Info Request".
  5. An auto-generated email will be sent to the email address provided. It will typically only take a few minutes to arrive and contain the following information:

# Service and Warranty Information

#-------------------------------------------------------------------

  Entitled Service      : 

  Warranty End Date     : 

#-------------------------------------------------------------------

Note: Please refer to Product Warranty for Software and Hardware warranties information

Additional Information

Policies and Warranties

Entitled Service Date: This is the period during which your device is covered by an active support contract. During this time, you are eligible for F5 technical support, software updates, and hardware replacement services as defined by your support agreement.

Warranty End Date: F5 warrants that the hardware component of any Product will, for a period of one (1) year from the date of shipment by F5, be free from defects in material and workmanship under normal use.

Note: Entitled Service overrides Warranty End Date. F5 Hardware and Software is under warranty as long as it's under entitled service.
