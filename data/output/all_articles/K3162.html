K3162: FirePass does not accept a valid SSL certificate or private key

Knowledge
Published
2007-06-20
Updated
2023-02-21
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to

  • FirePass: 6.0.2, 6.0.1, 6.0.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K3162

Topic

FirePass cannot accept certain special characters in an SSL certificate or private key, such as carriage returns and line feeds.

To work around this behavior, F5 Networks recommends that you cut and paste the certificate into a text editor such as Notepad or vi. Copy the certificate from the text editor, and then paste it into FirePass. Repeat these steps for the corresponding private key.

This process reformats the special characters and should eliminate the issue.
