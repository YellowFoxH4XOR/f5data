Known Issue

The ConfigSync status may not be propagated across blades in a VIPRION chassis.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The VIPRION chassis is configured in a DSC trust domain.
  • The VIPRION chassis has more than one blade installed.

Under the conditions previously mentioned, when the primary blade fails, the new primary blade will not have the ConfigSync status information. As a result, the ConfigSync status will be reported as Not All Devices Synced.

Impact

The BIG-IP VIPRION will not have the latest ConfigSync status.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • After a primary blade in a multi-blade VIPRION chassis fails over, the ConfigSync status will be reported as Not All Devices Synced.
  • The VIPRION system's ConfigSync status reports Not All Devices Synced when no configuration changes have been made on devices in the DSC device group.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can make a nominal change to a configuration object on the VIPRION. For example, updating the description field of a Virtual Server object.

Impact of workaround: Performing the aforementioned procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.
