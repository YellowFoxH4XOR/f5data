K000137328: Allowing a bot signature for a Bot defense Profile.

Description

When you come across a situation where a bot signature is a false positive it can be allowed.

Environment

  • A Bot signature needs to be allowed for a specific reason in a specific environment.
  • Unified Bot Defense, Mitigation Settings Exceptions
  • ASM/AdvWAF

Recommended Actions

To allow a bot defense signature...

  1. Go to the bot profile Security ›› Bot Defense : Bot Defense Profiles and click on the profile in question.
  2. Click on Bot Mitigation Settings.
  3. In the section titled "Mitigation Settings Exceptions" click on the "+ Add Exceptions".
  4. At the top of the popup enter the signature you want to allow, check the box next to it when it appears, and click Add.
  5. It will now appear as an entry in the "Mitigation Settings Exceptions" section.
  6. In the dropdown in that entry for the bot signature change it to 'None' and click 'Save' it.
