Known Issue

Applying a security policy may result in multiple failover events.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP ASM system is running on a VIPRION platform.
  • You apply a security policy while a blade is promoted to primary status.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may unexpectedly fail over multiple times to another system in the device group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP system unexpectedly fails over to another system in the device group.
  • The mcpd process may restart and generate an mcpd core file to the /shared/core directory.
  • The scriptd process may restart and generate an mcpd core file to the /shared/core directory.
