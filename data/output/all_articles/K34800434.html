Environment

  • BIG-IP GTM/DNS.

Cause

The hard disk may be failing or be too busy, 

Recommended Actions:

Investigate the health of the hard disks. If this is a virtual system, the shared nature of the hard disk can lead to high I/O.
It may be necessary to run 'fsck', to clear any filesystem errors.
