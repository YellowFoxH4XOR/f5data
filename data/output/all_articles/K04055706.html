Known Issue

You may be unable to configure the management IP address on the iSeries platforms when you use the LCD panel.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are using the following iSeries platforms:
    • HRC-i2000 Series
    • BIG-IP i2000 Series
    • BIG-IP i4000 Series
    • BIG-IP i5000 Series
    • BIG-IP i7000 Series
    • BIG-IP i10000 Series
  • When you use the LCD touchscreen panel to attempt to configure the management IP address of the device, one of the following events occurs:
    • The BIG-IP system has just booted up.
    • The BIG-IP system has just been licensed.

Impact

You may be unable to configure the management IP address of the iSeries platforms.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When you attempt to configure the management IP address of the iSeries platforms, you notice an error message on the LCD display that appears similar to the following example:

    There was an error processing the request.  Redirecting to splash in x seconds.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 619397 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper right corner of this article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can wait for one minute before you use the LCD touchscreen panel to make another attempt at configuring the management IP address of the device. The attempt should be successful once the device has been successfully licensed and is running in the active state.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
