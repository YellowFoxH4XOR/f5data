Known Issue

BIG-IP 8400 and 8800 platforms are equipped with the Packet Velocity ASIC 10 chip (PVA10). When BIG-IP 8400 and 8800 platforms receive packets with an invalid TCP checksum of 0xFFFF, the packets are dropped by the PVA10 chip. TCP segments that contain this invalid checksum can be transmitted by devices with certain Intel network cards, which generate an invalid TCP checksum of 0xFFFF through their offload engine.

Note: For more information about 0xFFFF being an invalid TCP checksum, refer to section 3 of RFC1624.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR73114 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.3 and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.

Workaround

To prevent PVA10 from dropping packets with an invalid TCP checksum of 0xFFFF, disable TCP checksum offloading on the network card driver. For information about disabling TCP checksum offloading, refer to the documentation for your specific network interface and operating system.
