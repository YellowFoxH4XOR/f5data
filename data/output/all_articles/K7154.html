Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The big3d process will crash if an external monitor is configured in the wideip.conf file, but the big3d process cannot find the monitor, or the monitor script does not exist on the BIG-IP GTM system.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR72469 and CR72468 it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.3.0, and 9.4.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM release notes.
