Description

This article applies to the following scenario:

  • You are considering migrating from F5 BIG-IP VE's to F5 BIG-IPs on the AWS cloud.

  • You have questions on implementing the change and on the impact of the change, such as the migration of configurations and on licensing requirements.

Environment

BIG-IP VEs
AWS Cloud BIG-IP VEs

Cause

NA

Recommended Actions

The best resource in considering migrating to AWS is by contacting the F5 AWS team at AWSteam@f5.com. You may also directly contact your F5 Account team.
