K2584: Limitations on the number of IP addresses that can be managed by 3-DNS

Type
Knowledge
Published
2006-02-17
Updated
2023-02-21
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • Legacy Products
  • 3-DNS: 4.6.4
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K2584

Topic

The NameSurfer product shipped with the 3-DNS Controller is licensed to manage 100 unique IP addresses for zone files. There is no corresponding limit on the number of hosts entered in BIND.

Licenses for additional IP addresses may be available from the authors of NameSurfer. If you want NameSurfer to manage more than 100 unique IP addresses, contact your F5 Sales Representative for a quote on an unlimited NameSurfer license.
