Policy Information

The F5 software support policy described in this article applies to the F5 BIG-IP Edge Client and F5 BIG-IP Edge Portal application software available through the Apple iTunes Store and the Android Market.

F5 will accept escalations and provide responses to general questions for the last two releases of the F5 BIG-IP Edge Client and F5 BIG-IP Edge Portal apps. F5 will provide fixes and enhancements based on only the latest release of the F5 BIG-IP Edge Client and F5 BIG-IP Edge Portal apps and include the fixes and enhancements in the next release of the F5 BIG-IP Edge Client and F5 BIG-IP Edge Portal apps available through the Apple iTunes Store and the Android Market.

F5 strongly recommends that you upgrade to the current release of these apps to ensure the latest functionality and fixes.
