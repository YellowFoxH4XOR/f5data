Description: 
You have an excess of large log files and /var is reported as being nearly full.
Note that log files are stored in the /var/log filesystem, and that this is not actually part of the /var filesystem 



Environment:
BIG-IP LTM

Cause:
There is no cause, this is just informational only.

Recommended Actions:
If /var is reported as full or nearly full, this is not due to logging. The log files are retained in a separate logical volume called /var/log which can fill up independently of /var
It is also worth knowing that /var/tmp is also not part of /var

The output from the command 'df -h' shows that /var and /var/log are separate logical volumes.

Additional Information:
The storage location for UCS backups is held in /var/local/ucs and is part of the /var filesystem, and also the most likely cause of the /var filesystem being full.
