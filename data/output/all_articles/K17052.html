Known Issue

BIG-IP connection mirroring may fail.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a BIG-IP device group configured for connection mirroring.
  • Your system uses a FastL4 virtual server with connection mirroring enabled.
  • Gateways or static routes are defined on the BIG-IP system.

Under certain circumstances, when a redundant member is initially brought online, some Layer 4 (L4) flows may not be successfully mirrored to the standby device.

Impact

After a failover event, FastL4 connections are not successfully mirrored.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • After a failover event, the FastL4 connections do not continue as expected.

Workaround

There is no work around for this issue; however, you may be able to mitigate the issue by restarting the tmm process.

Impact of action: Restarting the tmm process interrupts traffic processing; you should perform this task during a maintenance window.
