Description

This article explains why a lack of logs explicitly referencing hard disk failure or hard disk errors does not mean the hard disk has not failed on a Big-IP device.

 

Environment

BIG-IP LTM

 

Cause

Hard disk failure can result on no further write activity on the hard disk taking place, which means the generated log messages cannot then be written to disk. 

The hard disk may then recover upon a hard reboot, thus "hiding" the failure from logs.

 

Recommended Actions

Run an EUD if a hard disk failure is suspected.

 

Additional Information

Sometimes, historical 'DRDY ERR' (drive not ready error) or similar errors might be found in /var/log/kern.log on a device with a failing hard disk, but often in an actually failed device, there may be no such errors logged at all, because the HD might have completely failed and cannot write anything at the point when the error would normally be logged. 

Restarting the device may recover the hard disk until it fails again, depending on the exact hardware failure which is occurring.
