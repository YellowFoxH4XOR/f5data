Known Issue

In some low-resource situations, the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may restart when you enable cookie persistence.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system uses cookie persistence.
  • TMM has a low amount of free memory.

Impact

TMM may produce a core file and temporarily fail to process traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H429319 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium | High screen.
  • The BIG-IP system sends a SIGFPE signal to TMM, resulting in a stack trace and core file.
  • You notice an error message similar to the following example in the /var/log/tmm log files:

    notice panic: ../modules/hudfilter/http/http.c:4088: Assertion "persist shouldn't be NULL for COOKIE_INSERT" failed.

  • The BIG-IP system fails over to another system in the device group.
