Known Issue

The status of a node may not be updated for a health monitor state change.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The mcpd service updates the node state at the same time as the bigd service processes a state change for the node.

Impact

The current status of a node may not accurately reflect monitor node state changes.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The availability of affected nodes is not accurately reflected. Available nodes may be marked down, or unavailable nodes may be marked up.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can restart the bigd process. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Restarting the bigd process temporarily interrupts BIG-IP monitor processing.

  1. Log in to the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) by typing the following command:

    tmsh

  2. Restart the bigd service by typing the following command:

    restart sys service bigd
