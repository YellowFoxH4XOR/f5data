Description

How can I choose a specific RE region instead of a local RE region for site registration?

 

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud Console

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

To select a specific region during site registration:

  1. In the site registration form, navigate to the Connected Region field.
  2. In the field, enter the label with its value to select a specific region to connect to the RE. The example below connects a site to a RE near Frankfurt, Germany.
  3. Complete any other site registration options.
  4. After you finish, click Save and Exit.

 

Additional Information

You can refer to a list of ves.io/region labels in the Shared Configuration service by navigating to Manage > Labels > Known Labels.
