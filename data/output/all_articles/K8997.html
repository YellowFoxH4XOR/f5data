Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If a large response is sent to a client on a connection for which plugin processing was disabled by the the PLUGIN::disable iRule command, the BIG-IP system can prematurely shut down the client-side connection. As a result, the BIG-IP system may fail to send data to the client, and instead establish a new connection to retransmit the missing data.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR101598 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 9.4.6. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM or ASM Release Notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.3-HF4 issued for BIG-IP version 9.4.3, in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.4-94.0-HF3 issued for BIG-IP version 9.4.4, and in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.5-HF2 issued for BIG-IP version 9.4.5. You may download these hotfixes or later versions of the hotfixes from the F5 Networks Downloads site.
