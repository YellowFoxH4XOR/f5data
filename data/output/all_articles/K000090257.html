Description

About subscriptions on MyF5

The Subscriptions page displays a list of all your assets like BIG-IP VE, BIG-IQ VE and NGINX that are purchased via Subscription model, including a brief description of the products contained within each subscription.

BIG-IP VE and BIG-IQ VE products under subscription model has subscription number in the format "A-SXXXXXXXX".
If you own F5 hardware and software outright, these are not subscription products. You cannot yet view details about these products on MyF5.
Note : Refer to this article on our future plans for MyF5 " K000092546 : What's new and planned for MyF5"  

Procedures

View your subscriptions

  • Go to My Products and Plans > Subscriptions.

View details about your subscription products

  1. Go to My Products and Plans > Subscriptions.
  2. Select the subscription you want to view.

About the Subscription Details page

The following table describes the product information on the page.

SectionDescription
TopDescription of your subscription, including the following:
  • Your subscription ID
  • The term length for your subscription
  • When your subscription expires
  • If you have BIG-IP VE or BIG-IQ VE products, the total number of registration keys you have for those products
Product summary—BIG-IP VE and BIG-IQ VE products
  • Basic details about the product or bundle you purchased, including the support type you selected: standard, premium, or premium plus. For more information about support types, refer to Support Services on F5 Support.
  • Registration keys for each product or bundle and their current status.
Product summary—NGINX products
  • Basic details about the product you purchased, including the support type you selected: standard or premium. For more information about support types, refer to Support for F5 NGINX Software.
  • All required certificates, keys, licenses, tokens, and installation instructions for your specific product.

Because you need easy access to usage data for your NGINX Controllers, there is another page that lists of all your NGINX Controllers across your subscriptions. For more information, refer to View your NGINX Controllers and usage on MyF5.
