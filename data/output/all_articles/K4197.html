When the syslog daemon becomes overloaded and cannot process more messages, it logs them to the console by default. In rare cases, a large numbers of messages can interfere with the use of the console.

Starting in version 4.6.3, you can prevent syslog messages from overflowing to the console, by typing the following command:

Important: Do not change internal variables unless you are aware of, and accept, the risks outlined in K920: Overview of internal variables.

bigpipe internal set log_to_console = 0
