Known Issue

Synchronizing autodos history files from an active to standby unit causes high CPU utilization.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a BIG-IP Advanced Firewall Manager (AFM) module provisioned and configured.
  • Your BIG-IP system is configured as part of a high-availability (HA) device group.

This occurs because the autodosd service individually synchronizes each autodos history file every five (5) minutes. The autodosd service is a control plane process which supports the BIG-IP AFM DoS Auto Threshold feature.

Impact

High system CPU utilization may result in temporarily degraded performance or interruptions in traffic processing.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • In an HA environment, you observe that the CPU usage for mcpd increases to 100 percent every five (5) minutes on the active device and 65 percent on standby device.
