K000150430: BIG-IQ: Evaluation always indicates cookieEncryptionPassphrase changes

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-03-27
Updated
2025-03-27
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 8.3.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000150430

Description

  • You are attempting to deploy an LTM configuration update that the LTM includes a Cookie Persistence Profile on the BIG-IQ CM.
  • The Cookie Persistence Profile includes an Encryption Passphrase setting, but you didn't update the Encryption Passphrase.
  • During the evaluation phase, the BIG-IQ CM consistently indicates that the cookieEncryptionPassphrase will be modified, even if no changes are made to the Encryption Passphrase in the Cookie Persistence Profile.

Environment

BIG-IP

LTM

Cookie Persistence Profile with Encryption Passphrase

Cause

F5 filed ID 1826413 to track the issue.

Recommended Actions

You can disregard the cookieEncryptionPassphrase change shown in the evaluation on the BIG-IQ CM GUI. The cookieEncryptionPassphrase will not actually be modified.

Additional Information

None
