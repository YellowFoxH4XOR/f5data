Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Using network failover and serial cable failover with an active-active configuration may result in an address conflict when one of the BIG-IP LTM systems is rebooted.

This issue occurs when TMM updates the heartbeat sent to the peer, but does not update the peer’s unit mask. As a result, the sod daemon incorrectly reads that the unit mask is correct, and that the peer unit is in standby mode.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR88337.

This issue was fixed in HOTFIX-BIG-IP-9.3.1-HF2 issued for BIG-IP 9.3.1 in the BIG-IP 9.3 software branch. You may download this hotfix or a later version of this hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
