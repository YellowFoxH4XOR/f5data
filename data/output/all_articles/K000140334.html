K000140334: BIG-IQ HA Auto-failover configuration fails when performing reboot of both BIG-IQ CMs in short period of time

Support Solution
Published
2024-07-15
Updated

Applies to

  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 8.X.X, 8.3.X, 8.3.0, 8.2.X, 8.2.0, 8.1.X, 8.1.0, 8.0.X, 8.0.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000140334

Description

After performing reboot of both BIG-IQ CMs configured as HA with Autofailover in short period of time, cluster is showing bad state.

Primary device reports offline state or other unknown status

Secondary Device works as read only secondary

CM var/log/ha.log reports  constant  1/3 failure detected

Wed May 29 03:30:05 +03 2024 - MONITOR] INFO: Failure detected (1 / 3)

Environment

  • BIG-IQ HA
  • Autofailover configuration
  • Reboot of Both BIG-IQ CMs in short period of time

Cause

Performing reboot of both devices in cluster one by one in short period of time ( like one minute period) may cause unknown state of the cluster which requires re-establishing HA

Recommended Actions

Reestablish HA by performing following procedure

K000139335: How to restore BIG-IQ HA after auto failover
