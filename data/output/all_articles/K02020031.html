Known Issue

Spurious Layer 7 (L7) mirrored ACK packets may cause the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) to restart and produce a core on an active system.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You enable connection mirroring on BIG-IP systems in an active-standby high availability (HA) configuration.
  • Your active BIG-IP system processes spurious L7 mirrored ACK packets sent from the standby BIG-IP system.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from the TMM restart, and your active system fails over to standby system.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your active BIG-IP system temporarily fails to process traffic and fails over to the standby system.
  • Your BIG-IP system generates a TMM core file to the /shared/core/ directory.
  • The BIG-IP system reports a message to the /var/log/tmm file that appears similar to the following example:

    notice panic: ../modules/hudfilter/tcp4/tcp4.c:4489: Assertion "Must be syncookie" failed.
