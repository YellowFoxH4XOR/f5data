Description

Microsoft teams based URLs below are marked uncategorized or categorized as SSL and http2 by traffic intelligence categorization:

aadcdn.msauth.net
aadcdn.msftauth.net
ts01-b.cloudsink.net

Environment

  • Traffic Intelligence categorization
  • Microsoft Teams

Cause

Classification update build 20220711_1206 doesn't appear to categorize the above-mentioned Teams URLs properly

Recommended Actions

  1. Update classification signature to classify the above-mentioned Teams URLs as MS Teams. Refer to K22180795
