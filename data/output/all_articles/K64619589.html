Description

  • Config-Sync is showing disconnected (if previously connected)
  • Device trust cannot be created. On the peer device the Peer details shows blank for Serial Number
  • Deleting the device group and restarting devmgmtd service does not work.
  • mcpd forceload does not work.
  • Logging this even when devices are in standalone mode.
    • Peer cert verify error: self signed certificate in certificate chain (depth 1; cert /CN=
    • CMI peer <IP> certificate rejected, error 19: self signed certificate in certificate chain
    • Connection error: ssl_shim_vfycerterr:4061: self signed certificate in certificate chain (48)

Environment

  • Primary and DR datacenters in use each with a HA pair.
  • Devices in the primary and DR datacenters are using the same config-sync address

Cause

  • The Primary and DR datacenters have been bridged (layer 2)
  • The devices in the one DC are connecting to the devices in the other and flooding them with invalid CMI connection requests.
  • Device trust cannot be established in this case. The spurious connections affect the setup and operation of the CMI channel.
  • You will not always see address conflict messages.
  • The HA pair in the other datacenter will also be impacted, however once the conflict is removed, the other HA pair should recover.
     

Recommended Actions

Confirm this by Capturing the CMI traffic on port 4353
  • Both affected devices will see tcp syn incoming only
  • The peer mac address is not the expected device.
Change the Config-Sync Address,
Re-establish trust.

Additional Information
This situation can occur if the  DR datacenter configuration was cloned from the Primary. In which case you should check that hostnames are also different before setting them up again.

tmsh show /sys mac-address

will list the mac addresses used by a device.
