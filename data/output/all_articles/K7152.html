Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. BIG-IP LTM system drivers do not work with RoHS-compliant Cavium Federal Information Processing Standard (FIPS) cards. The RoHS-compliant Cavium FIPS cards will fail to initialize on the following BIG-IP platforms:
  • BIG-IP 8400 (D84)
  • BIG-IP 6400 (D63)
  • BIG-IP 6800 (D68)

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR72826 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM versions 9.2.5, 9.3, and 9.4.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
