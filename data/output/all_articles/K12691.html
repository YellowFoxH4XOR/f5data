K12691: Unable to change expired Active Directory passwords on supported Apple iOS devices

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When an Apple iOS user attempts to authenticate against Active Directory and their Active Directory password has expired, they are unable to change their password. The expected behavior is that the Active Directory service sends them a notice that their password has expired and prompts them to submit their old password, their desired new password, and a confirmation of their desired new password. Instead, the service sends the user only the notification that their password has expired and does not prompt them to change their password.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as ID 296159.

Workaround

Users can work around this issue by connecting to the Active Directory service with a client that is not an Apple iOS device to change their password. Once they have changed their Active Directory password, they can successfully connect to the Active Directory service from their Apple iOS device using their new password.
