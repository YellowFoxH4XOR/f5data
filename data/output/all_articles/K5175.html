Policy Information

This End of Life (EOL) document describes the product life cycle EOL dates for affected F5 hardware platforms and descriptions of what is affected.

Purpose

Older platforms may not have optimal configurations to run new versions of software. In order to prevent possible problems F5 may choose at anytime to deem a platform unsuited to run the current version of software. Customers whose platforms are designated EOL can choose to continue using the products indefinitely with older versions of software, however, it should be noted that F5 will only support the latest versions of software on this hardware as per our development support policy.

Platform Upgrade

Customers whose platforms are designated EOL and who wish to utilize the latest software may take advantage of F5's platform upgrade program. Please call your sales representative or channel partner for details on platform upgrades.

Technical Support

Customers whose platforms are designated EOL and who are currently under maintenance contracts will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Customers attempting to run the latest versions of software on platforms designated EOL will void those contracts. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the Service Contract Renewal date expires. Please contact your sales representative if you have any questions.
