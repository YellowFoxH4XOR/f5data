Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Under rare conditions, the AHA compression card driver can send the old compression state information with new data. The issue is most likely caused by format and CRC errors.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR71568 and CR71584 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM version 9.2.5 and version 9.1.3. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.
