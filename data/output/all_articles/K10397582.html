Known Issue

A High Availability (HA) mismatched configuration may trigger an unexpected failover.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP systems are in an active/standby configuration with a traffic group configured for an HA failover group.
  • Device connectivity uses a single network for HA communication.
  • The active BIG-IP device is configured beyond a base configuration.
  • The standby BIG-IP device is configured with a base configuration.
  • The HA communication between the devices is disrupted.
  • HA communication between the devices is restored.

Impact

The active BIG-IP device fails over to the standby BIG-IP device, causing the base-configured BIG-IP device to become the active device.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When restoring HA communication between BIG-IP devices, the BIG-IP device that is configured with a base configuration assumes the active status for the HA pair.
  • When you configure VIPRION chassis as an HA pair, and populate the standby chassis with blades that have only a base configuration, the standby chassis assumes the active status for the HA pair.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can configure the HA system to use multiple networks, and avoid loading only the base configuration on devices that are part of an HA configuration. 

Impact of workaround:  Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
