Known Issue

The HTTPS monitor may erroneously mark a pool member down permanently.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a working HTTPS monitor configured.
  • The HTTPS monitor marked a pool member down due to an incomplete Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) handshake response.

Impact

The monitor marks the pool member down and the resource is unavailable for load balancing.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter all of the following symptoms:

  • A packet trace indicates that the monitor completes a TCP three-way handshake with the pool member, but the monitor does not complete the required SSL handshake.
  • The HTTPS monitor marks the pool member down permanently.

Resolution

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can disable the bigd.reusesocket system database variable. For more information, refer to K13820: Configuring BIG-IP monitor socket reuse behavior.

Impact of workaround: Disabling the bigd.reusesocket database key affects all TCP-based monitors on the system, and in some cases may result in port exhaustion.
