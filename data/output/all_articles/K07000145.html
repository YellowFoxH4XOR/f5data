Description

What happens to unapplied Access Policy changes after the BIG-IP is rebooted?

Environment
  • APM
  • "Apply Access Policy"

Cause

The access policy is applied after rebooting.

Recommended Actions

None.

Additional Information

When editing a Policy in the VPE, changes are immediately made to tmsh and the bigip.conf file, but the policy is not pushed to apmd, and hence not applied.

When the BIG-IP is rebooted, the configuration from tmsh is loaded, and the APM policy resident in tmsh is then pushed to apmd.
