K72539303: Unexpected high CPU spike after adjusting system time backwards or changing NTP settings

Published

Updated

Status
Legacy Content
Applies to

Source

Description:

You may find unexpected cpu_usage_1min and cpu_usage_5mins after adjusting system time or NTP settings.
You may see in logs some high CPU messages  or spike in graph.

e.g 
  • CPU usage of apd process on Blade 2 is high, CPU usage is 185% Please Monitor APD Process
  • warning tmm<x>[<PID>]: 01010040:4: Clock has unexpectedly adjusted by 145484 ms
  • notice ntpd: ntpd startup succeeded


Environment:
  • BIG-IP / BIG-IQ
  • NTP
  • System time change backwards
     
Cause:
When system time is adjusted significantly the CPU usage calculation is affected.
NOTE: This also affect health monitor for pool / node: K27722391: BIG-IP pool member down due to unstable NTP

Recommended Actions:

F5 recommends to set at least 3 NTP servers with over strarum 3 and make sure it to have stable connections to keep adjusted in small amount.
Also when you change NTP settings, or force to sync time, recommend to do so in maintenance window.
