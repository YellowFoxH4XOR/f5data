K12396: The alertd process does not trigger SNMP traps after secondary blade takes over as primary

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. On a VIPRION cluster with two or more blades, if a primary blade is removed from the cluster due to a reboot or going offline, and a secondary blade is elected to be the new primary blade, the alertd process on the new primary blade will not receive the message from the mcpd process regarding the change. As a result, the alertd process will fail to trigger any configured alerts and SNMP traps will not be sent.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as ID 344646.

This issue does not affect BIG-IP 10.0.0 or later.

Workaround

You can work around this issue by restarting the alertd process when it is not triggering alerts as expected. To do so, type the following command:

bigstart restart alertd
