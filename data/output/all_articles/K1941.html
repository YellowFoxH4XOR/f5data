Policy Information

 
F5 Networks announced in December of 2004 the End of Sales/Life for the following F5 products:
  • BIG-IP SSL Accelerator 200
  • BIG-IP SSL Accelerator 600
  • BIG-IP e-Commerce Controller 200
  • BIG-IP e-Commerce Controller 600

Each product has an associated replacement product.

Old ProductReplacement Product
BIG-IP e-Commerce 200Call F5 Networks
BIG-IP e-Commerce 600Call F5 Networks
BIG-IP SSL Accelerator 200Call F5 Networks
BIG-IP SSL Accelerator 600Call F5 Networks

There are some older 4U platforms that do not support the new SSL card. All 2U systems are compatible.

Some older 4Us are incompatible with the new cards. The "agency" label or the ETL label on the back can easily identify systems with the incompatible motherboard. Identification of a compatible system only needs to be done on older (those prior to October 2000) 4Us and is as follows:

Compatible System: ETL label has F35 printed on it.
Incompatible System: No F35 on the ETL label.
