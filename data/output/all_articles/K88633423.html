Known Issue

The BIG-IP LTM system incorrectly formats Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) log entries for route status entries.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • One or more routes are configured.
  • A route status change, such as reachable or unreachable, occurs and results in a TMM log entry.

Impact

This formatting issue is considered cosmetic; there is no functional impact.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The BIG-IP LTM system incorrectly formats TMM log route entries.
