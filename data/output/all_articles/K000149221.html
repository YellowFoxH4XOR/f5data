K000149221: rSeries host went down unexpectedly due to critical event 'thermal fault detected in hardware'

Support Solution
Published
2025-01-08
Updated
2025-02-08

  • F5OS-A: 1.8.0, 1.5.1, 1.5.2, 1.7.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000149221

Description

rSeries unit went down unexpectedly.

After unit has restarted, running the following command on the CLI shows a critical event - "Thermal fault detected in hardware" due to "High unrecoverable threshold Value".

#show system events

65546 appliance thermal-fault ASSERT CRITICAL "Thermal fault detected in hardware" "2024-11-xx xx:xx:xx.947931272 UTC" 
65546 appliance thermal-fault EVENT NA "High unrecoverable threshold, Value= 50C" "2024-11-xx xx:xx:xx.947978055 UTC"

Environment

  • rSeries

Cause

This is expected behaviour, where the unit will perform a thermal shutdown when the inlet temperature exceeds the environmental operating specifications.

Recommended Actions

One should ensure that the unit is placed in the correct environmental conditions aligned with F5 specifications, and that the airflow through the system is correct to ensure proper cooling.

Additional Information

Please refer to F5 hardware platform guides for rSeries to check environmental guidelines and Platform specifications
