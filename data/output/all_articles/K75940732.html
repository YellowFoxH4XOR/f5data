Description

System went offline briefly then came back online.

Many logs similar to the following in /var/log/ltm:

notice mcpd[7306]: 0107168e:5: Unable to do incremental sync, reverting to full load for device group /Common/sync-failover-dg

Environment

  • HA
  • Auto-sync enabled

Cause

Many changes were made in a short period of time, prompting many ConfigSync operations. The incremental ConfigSync cache was filled and mcpd got overloaded.

Recommended Actions

If it is common for many changes to be made in this way, consider increasing the incremental ConfigSync cache size:

For example, to double it:

modify cm device-group <device-group>  incremental-config-sync-size-max 2048

Additional Information

It is not unusual for several daemons to restart if mcpd gets too busy. mcpd may even core.
