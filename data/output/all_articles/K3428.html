Topic

To view FirePass SNMP MIB compatibility, refer to the following sections:

FirePass version 5.x, 6.x and 7.x

To view the compatible SNMP MIBS for your version of FirePass software, perform the following procedure:

  1. Log on to the FirePass Administrative Console.
  2. Click Device Management.
  3. Click Configuration.
  4. Click SNMP.

    Note: The SNMP page may take a few moments to load.

  5. In the top right corner of the page, click Help.

The SNMP online help page lists the FirePass SNMP agent version along with the compatible SNMP MIBs.

FirePass version 4.x
