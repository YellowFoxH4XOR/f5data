Policy Information

F5 has released new BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) products that are compatible up to BIG-IP software 15.x. The new BIG-IP VE products offer the broadest range of performance options and software defined application services—including the best-value Good, Better, Best offers—provide license portability between supported private and public clouds, support pay-as-you-grow license upgrades, and options for license mobility and on-demand license pool spin-up with BIG-IQ.

With these new enhancements to BIG-IP VE, F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of BIG-IP VE compatible up to BIG-IP software 14.x, effective October 1, 2014. F5 is providing advance product notification to customers and partners announcing intent to EoS the products listed in the following table:
