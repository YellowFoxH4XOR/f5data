Known Issue

The BIG-IP APM rewrite profile may not rewrite RSS URLs.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is configured with an access profile.
  • You establish a session, and then access a destination resource that is an RSS feed.

Impact

RSS content does not render correctly.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When analyzing content received from the BIG-IP APM RSS feed, you notice that URLs are not rewritten to reference the BIG-IP APM session.
  • Session-related RSS feeds do not function.
