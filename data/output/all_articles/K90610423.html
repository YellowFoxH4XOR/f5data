K90610423: How to create a Reg Key Pool in BIG-IQ

Updated

Applies to

Source

Description
You want to create a Reg Key (license) pool with your Big-IP license keys.

Note: The use of Reg Key Pools will not work with Base Reg Keys provided via the FCP.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ v7.x and later
  • BIG-IP
  • Licensing

Cause
You need the ability to easily assign and revoke licenses to BIG-IP devices.

Recommended Actions

You will log into BIG-IQ, create a Reg Key Pool (license pool), add each of the BIG-IP License keys and then assign or revoke the licenses as needed.
 

  1. Log into BIG-IQ, Devices—License manager—Licenses
  2. Click on add Reg Key Pool
  3. Give the pool a name and click on OK
  4. Click on add Reg Key, type in the base reg / license key, accept the EULA
  5. Repeat for each of the reg keys / licenses
  6. Place a check mark by the license you want to assign, click on assign
  7. “Managed” choose the device in the drop-down box, click on assign, click on OK
  8. “Unmanaged” fill in IP address, username, and password, click on assign, click on OK.
  9. Under License management, Assignments, view the assigned licenses
  10. Go to Licenses—Pool-name 
  11. Revoke Managed, place a check next to the license you want to revoke, click on Revoke
  12. Click on Revoke, click on OK
  13. Revoke Unmanaged, place a check next to the license you want to revoke, click on Revoke
  14. Type in Username and Password, click on Revoke
  15. Check back under assignments as the licenses are now removed.
