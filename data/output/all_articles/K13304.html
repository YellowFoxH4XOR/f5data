Known Issue

When you remove a Subordinate Non-Authority Device from a Local Trust Domain, the system does not correctly remove the device. As a result, the device cannot be re-added to the Local Trust Domain as a Peer Authority Device.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The device was previously a member of the Local Trust Domain as a Subordinate Non-Authority Device, but has been manually removed from the Local Trust Domain.

Impact

Peer devices cannot be added to a Local Trust Domain.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • When adding an affected device to the Local Trust Domain as a Peer Authority Device, a message similar to the following example is displayed in the Configuration utility:

    01020036:3: The requested unknown (/Common/device_trust_group /Common/my_device) was not found.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can reset Device Trust on the affected device. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: The device will be temporarily removed from its current Local Trust Domain

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility of the device you are unable to add to the Local Trust Domain.
  2. Navigate to Device Management > Device Trust > Local Domain.
  3. Click the Reset Device Trust button.
  4. When prompted, choose the Generate new self-signed authority option in the Reset Device Trust wizard.
  5. Click Next.
  6. Click Finished.
  7. Log in to the Configuration utility of the initial device where the error was generated when trying to add a Peer Authority Device.
  8. Navigate to Device Management > Device Trust > Local Domain.
  9. In the Peer Authority Devices section, click Add.
  10. Enter the credentials of the device for which you reset the Device Trust in Steps 1-6.
  11. Click Next.
  12. The wizard prompts you several more times to confirm data. When you reach the final confirmation screen, click Finish.
