Known Issue

Due to an error in packaging scripts, hotfixes may contain outdated versions of the AOM firmware or software packages.

Impact

The expected AOM-related fix may not perform as expected.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The expected AOM-related fix may not perform as expected.

Resolution

Status
