Known Issue

The iControl SOAP portal prints text beyond its log buffer.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You have turned on logging with trace level for iControl SOAP.

Impact

The iControl SOAP portal can print out a garbage log to /var/log/ltm, and can potentially lead to instability and reading beyond the buffer.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When trace logging is turned on, iControl SOAP can potentially print text beyond its log buffer.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 626589 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:

Workaround

To work around this issue, do not enable logging with trace level (which is not turned on by default).
