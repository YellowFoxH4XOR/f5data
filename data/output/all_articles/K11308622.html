Known Issue

The system might not enforce BIG-IP GTM pool level connection limits.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP GTM system is configured with pool level connection limits.
  • You configure a wide IP with RR load balancing referencing two pools, and at least one of the pools is configured with connection limits.
  • Connections that are load balanced across the pools surpass the configured connection limits of the pools.

Impact

Connections continue to be load balanced to pools that have reached the configured connection limit.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Pools that are configured with connection limits continue to receive connections after the connections surpass the configured limit.
