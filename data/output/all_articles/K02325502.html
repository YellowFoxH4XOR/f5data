Known Issue

The BIG-IP APM Configuration utility may not display policy sync location specific objects.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You clear the Location Specific check box in the list of static objects.

Impact

Location specific objects do not display in the list of static objects, which may lead to confusion.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Configured location specific objects do not display in the list of static objects.

Resolution

Status
