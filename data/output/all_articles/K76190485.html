Known Issue

TCP SYN retransmissions to FastL4 virtual servers, with hardware SYN cookies enabled, may be reset.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The initial SYN packet is received before hardware SYN cookie protection is activated.
  • The retransmitted SYN packet is received after hardware SYN cookie protection is activated.

This issue is most likely to occur with a wildcard forwarding FastL4 virtual server for which the resource router (gateway) has a low Address Resolution Protocol (ARP) timeout of five minutes or less. However, the conditions and timing required for this issue to occur make it highly unlikely to be encountered in a production environment.

Impact

FastL4 virtual servers intermittently experience connection failures.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • FastL4 virtual servers intermittently fail to connect.
  • Connection failures coincide with ARP timeouts on the server used by the FastL4 virtual server.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can configure static ARP entries for servers referenced by the affected FastL4 virtual server. Alternatively, configuring a monitor (such as a gateway monitor) for the virtual server's resources may ensure dynamic ARP table entries are continuously refreshed before they expire.

Impact of workaround: The impact of the suggested workaround depends on the specific environment. F5 recommends testing any such changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.
