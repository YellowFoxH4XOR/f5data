FirePass 1000 platform is 4/2 Amps at 100/220V. The power consumption varies according to system load; however, you can use the following measurements to plan an installation:

Note: All measurements are taken with a line voltage of 120V AC.

ModeVoltageCurrent (A)Watts
Initial power up119.40.46855.9
Standby122.80.41851.3
LAN transmission119.90.44553.4
Off119.90.04585.5
