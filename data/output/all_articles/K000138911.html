K000138911: Latencies/delays in data plane traffic forwarding after upgrade to 15.1.10.2 or 15.1.10.3

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-04-11
Updated
2024-04-11
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 15.1.10
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000138911

Description

  • After updating to version 15.1.10.2 or 15.1.10.3 you may notice delays in VS traffic forwarding
  • Large amount of delays, retransmissions and double ACKs in TCP, compared to the traffic before the upgrade
  • CPU and memory saturation looks the same before and after the upgrade

Environment

BIG-IP LTM VE

Cause

Large Receive Offload (LRO) disabled before the upgrade, but enabled on 15.1.10.2/15.1.10.3 by Hypervisor.

TMM receives segments larger than MSS, which in turn causes BIG-IP to fragment packets when egressing them, causing latency.

Recommended Actions

Perform this procedure to mitigate:

Impact of workaround: The procedure involves restarting a service of the system; therefore, F5 recommends that you perform this procedure during a scheduled maintenance window. 

  •  If possible, compare the output from ethtool before and after the upgrade, check the TSO and LRO settings:

            ethtool -k eth1

  • Disable the TSO and LRO:

            tmsh modify sys db tm.tcpsegmentationoffload value disable

            tmsh modify sys db tm.tcplargereceiveoffload value disable

            bigstart restart tmm

  • Wait for 1-2 minutes before the tmm reload
  • Check the settings of TSO and LRO after tmm reloaded

           ethtool -k eth1

  • Test the traffic on affected Virtual Services.
