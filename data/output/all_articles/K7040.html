Topic

BIG-IP APM

All BIG-IP APM high availability (HA) redundant failover configurations require that both devices in the active-standby failover traffic group are identical hardware models.

FirePass

All FirePass hardware models, except FirePass 600, support HA through a failover pair configuration when licensed for the failover software module.

Identical hardware models in the failover pair are required for failover. F5 does not support mixing of hardware models in a failover pair.
