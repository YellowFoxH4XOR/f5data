Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Under rare conditions, if BIG-IP GTM receives multiple DNS requests from the same IP address and source port, BIG-IP GTM may send DNS response packets from the source IP address of 127.0.0.1.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR76390 and it is was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.3 and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM Release Notes.
