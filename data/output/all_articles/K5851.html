K5851: Incorrect internal masking may cause trunk ports to receive traffic destined for ports in other VLANs

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Trunks may be assigned an internal mask that matches a port in another VLAN. If the port in the other VLAN is untagged, and it receives broadcast or multicast traffic, the trunk will also receive the traffic. In rare circumstances, this can cause a bridge loop.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR49623 and CR51635, and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 4.5.14. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP Release Notes.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can configure VLAN tags for all VLANs.
