Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP Configuration utility displays an incomplete list of supported versions in the Versions field for the Citrix XenApp template. The field lists versions 5.1 and 5.0 even though later versions, such as 6.0, are also supported.

Workaround

You can work around this issue by ignoring the Versions field and continuing with your template configuration.
