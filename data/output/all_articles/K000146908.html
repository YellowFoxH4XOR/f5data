Description

  • I want to unblock an IP which has a bad reputation according to XC IP Reputation provider (BrightCloud)
  • You can't access your Load Balancer (LB) because your source IP is being blocked by our IP Reputation Service
  • How to contact BrightCloud about IPs which have a bad reputation

 

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud Services
  • IP Reputation

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

To request BrightCloud to remove an IP being blocked by IP reputation:

  1. Go to BrightCloud's website
  2. Search for the IP and complete the Captcha
  3. Fill the Request a Change form with the following information:
    1. URL or IP
    2. Your email: your email
    3. Additional comments about this request: Reason for request - For example: One of our customer is being blocked by your IP reputation database. Could you please check and remove the blocking?
  4. Complete the Captcha and hit Submit
  5. BrightCloud will send an email to the email that you entered about the status of this request. This could take up to 24 hours. When an IP address is removed from their suspicious IP reputation list, the update typically propagates throughout their systems approximately every 12 hours.

Additional Information

  • As a temporary workaround, you can add the IP as a Trusted Client Rule in the HTTP load balancer configuration
