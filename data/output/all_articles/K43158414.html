Description

With HTTP/2 profile applied, DOSL7 mitigation sees a large increase in the CPU.

With HTTP/1.1 there is no significant jump in CPU utilisation.
 

Environment

  • The data plane CPU spikes to 100% for a short time ( 10 - 20 seconds )
  • Slows down application performance
     

Cause

Product Engineering opened bug ID1037457.

The cause of the issue is still under investigation. Upon completion, the details will be published on BugTracker.
 

Recommended Actions

The fix is available in Engineering Hotfix. 

Should the symptoms match, please contact Technical Support for further assistance.
