Excessive agency

The excessive agency vulnerability arises when an LLM-based component is granted too much autonomy or functionality, allowing it to perform potentially harmful actions in response to unexpected or manipulated outputs. This kind of attack is similar in principle to a server-side request forgery (SSRF) vulnerability in that the attacker abuses a server-side system to launch attacks on a separate component, such as an API, that may not directly accessible, thus expanding the scope of the attack.

Excessive agency attack scenario

In the following scenario an attacker exploits an LLM agent that is integrated into a document management system. The agent is supposed to read documents from a repository; however, the extension used for this purpose also includes the ability to upload and delete documents. The attacker is able to manipulate the LLM agent using malicious input and delete important documents, leading to data loss or corruption.

General strategies for mitigating excessive agency attacks

To protect your LLMs against excessive agency attacks, best practices recommend the following:

  • Limit the use of third-party extensions that the LLM is allowed to call to the necessary minimum .
  • Implement multiple layers of security to protect your accounts, workloads, data, and assets.
  • Implement strict access controls to limit the actions that an LLM can perform based on user roles and permissions.
  • Perform regular auditing and monitoring of LLM interactions for suspicious activity and investigate any anomalies.

      Summary

      An excessive agency attack occurs when an LLM-based component is granted too much autonomy or functionality, allowing it to perform potentially harmful actions in response to unexpected, or manipulated outputs. This can be a significant threat to the integrity and performance of LLMs and related components such as APIs, applications, and data sources. By implementing F5’s comprehensive security solutions, organizations can effectively mitigate this threat and ensure the reliability of their AI deployments.
