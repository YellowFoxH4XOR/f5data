Description

The BIG-IP 1600, 2000, 3600, 3900, 4000, 5000 and 7000 series platforms use three different AC power supply units (PSUs) that should only be installed in their supported platform(s).

Important: The size and shape of three AC power supplies used in several F5 platforms are the same, so any of them fit into the different BIG-IP 1600, 2000, 3600, 3900, 4000, 5000, or 7000 series platforms. You need to verify the correct power supply designed for your specific series platform model prior to installing it into the device.

BIG-IP 1600, 3600, and 3900 series platforms

The BIG-IP 1600, 3600, and 3900 series platforms use a Power-One AC or Bel Power power supply (F5 part number PWR-0130-xx). You can find the F5 part number (PN) on the label when you remove the power supply from the device, or you can visually identify the power supply without removing it from the device by the gray face plate on the power cord connection side and the vertical pull handle on the right side of the power supply, as shown in the following image:

Figure: Power supply used in the BIG-IP 1600, 3600, and 3900 series platforms

BIG-IP 2000 and 4000 series platforms

The BIG-IP 2000 and 4000 series platforms use a Power-One or Bel Power AC power supply (F5 part number PWR-0187-xx). You can find the F5 part number (PN) on the label when you remove the power supply from the device, or you can visually identify the Power-One or Bel Power AC power supply without removing it from the device by the black face plate with the F5 logo on the power cord-connection side and the vertical pull handle on the right side of the power supply, as shown in the following image:

Figure: Power supply used in the BIG-IP 2000 and 4000 series platforms

BIG-IP 5000 and 7000 series platforms

The BIG-IP 5000 and 7000 series platforms use an Emerson or Artesyn AC power supply (F5 part number PWR-0227-xx). You can find the F5 part number (PN) on the label when you remove the power supply from the device, or you can visually identify the Emerson or Artesyn AC power supply without removal from the device by the red face plate on the power cord connection side and the vertical pull handle on the left side of the power supply, as shown in the following image:

Figure: Power supply used in the BIG-IP 5000 and 7000 series platforms

How to know if the wrong power supply is installed

If you install the wrong PSU model in the wrong platform, the system may report error, alert conditions, or fail the End-User Diagnostics (EUD) tests, or all of these.

If you enter the tmsh show sys alert lcd command, the BIG-IP system may report an error that appears similar to the following example:

crit chmand[7648]: 012a0060:2: Power supplies do not match.

You can also enter the tmsh show sys hardware and review the system output to identify when a wrong power supply is installed.

For example, you may have the wrong power supply model installed if the tmsh show sys hardware command output reports a power supply of a status of 4 and NA (not available) and the output information for the power supply in slot#1 shows N/A for all or some of the fields for model, manufacturer, revision, or serial number, as in the following example:

Recommended Actions

Prior to installing an AC power supply in a BIG-IP 1600, 2000, 3600, 3900, 4000, 5000, or 7000 series platform, ensure that you use only the power supply designed for your specific platform model.

If you are unsure if you have the correct power supply, check the F5 part number (PN) and attributes for the specific appliance as described in this article. If you are unable to physically check, then check error logs, alerts, or diagnostics as in the examples shown.

If you have redundant power supplies, check that both power supplies are the same correct F5 part number (PN) and visually check the power supplies installed against the pictures in this article. Also check that both installed power supplies look identical to each other. If both are not visually the same, there is an incorrect power supply installed.
