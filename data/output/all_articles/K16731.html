Known Issue

Some daemons may continue to incorrectly log old host names after a host name change.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is not rebooted after a host name is changed.

Impact

Logs may contain a mixture of old and new host names.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • After a host name change, logs contain a mixture of old and new host names.

Resolution

Status

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can reboot the system, or restart the affected daemons.

Impact of workaround: The impact of the suggested workaround will depend on the specific environment. F5 recommends testing any such changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.
