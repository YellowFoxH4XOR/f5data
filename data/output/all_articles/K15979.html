Known Issue

The syscalld process may prematurely log OPERATION_TIMEOUT error messages.

Impact

The BIG-IP system high availability (HA) operations (CMI device trust) may take a long time to complete or may fail.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • In the /var/log/ltm file, you observe log messages similar to the following example:

    err syscalld[21190]: 0127000a:3: OPERATION_TIMEOUT -- the operation /usr/bin/tmsh (pid 20489) may be hung or taking a long time.
    err syscalld[21190]: 0127000a:3: OPERATION_TIMEOUT -- the operation /usr/bin/tmsh (pid 20489) resumed after a long time.
