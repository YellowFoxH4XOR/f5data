K000152314: Verify Service Check Date prior to upgrading FCP BIG-IP VE

Published
2025-07-01
Updated

Applies to

  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IP Cloud Edition

Description

When upgrading a BIG-IP VE that is licensed by a catalog (CAT) license offering from the BIG-IQ per the Flexible Consumption Plan (FCP), there are two Service Check Dates to confirm and possibly reactivate before upgrading the BIG-IP. 

Environment

  • BIG-IQ License Manager
  • Flexible Consumption Program (FCP)
  • CAT license offering
  • BIG-IP VE

Cause

Since this BIG-IP VE is part of the Flexible Consumption Program (FCP), its service check date can't be updated locally. It must be updated and refreshed from the CAT license offering on the BIG-IQ.

Background

The Service Check Date is the date the license was activated or the date the service contract ends. This date needs to be newer than the release date of the version you want to upgrade. For example, to upgrade to 17.1.x, the Service Check Date needs to be more recent than 2023-02-08. For a complete list of release dates, please reference Software Release Date Information.

Recommended Actions

  1. Verify the Service Check date on the BIG-IP VE and CAT key on the BIG-IQ. Note: The date on the BIG-IP should match the service check date for the Catalog Key from which this device is licensed.
    • On the BIG-IP command line: tmsh show sys license | grep -i 'service check date’
    • On the BIG-IQ Catalog Key: Devices > License Management > Licenses > [Relevant Catalog Key]

  2. If the service check date is older than the release date of the upgrade version, reactivate the CAT SKU on the BIG-IQ then refresh the BIG-IP VE license offering from the BIG-IQ.

    • Update the Service Check Date for a BIG-IP VE licensed by a BIG-IQ

    Additional Information

    The BIG-IQ has its own service check date that is separate from the CAT license offering. To update the license for the BIG-IQ device follow Update the BIG-IQ device license. This is only necessary when upgrading the BIG-IQ. Its list of Service Check Dates is in License activation may be required prior to software upgrade for the BIG-IQ.
