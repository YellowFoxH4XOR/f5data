Known Issue

The auto-migrate directive for a share farm may stop before the share's free space reaches or exceeds the resume-migrate threshold.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The auto-migrate directive is interrupted by a system task

The auto-migrate directive allows the share farm to migrate files from any of its shares that do not have enough free space, the policy-freespace command sets the minimum free space to maintain at a given share. The policy engine migrates files from the share to other shares in the same share farm, until the share’s free space reaches or exceeds the resume-migrate threshold you set with the same policy-freespace command.

Impact

The auto-migrate directive will stop before the share's free space reaches or exceeds the resume-migrate threshold.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • The auto-migrate directive will repeatedly start and stop
  • The syslog may contain messages that appear similar to the following example:

    SNMPTRAP: policyRuleAutoMigrateComplete (487) nsp 'namespace1', vol '/Vol1', shr
    'ARCHIVE01', nfp rule 'Tier2_Vol1', threshold '30G', available '20972752K', :
    free space is above configured auto-migrate threshold, auto-migrate complete

    The global configuration contained the statement, policy freespace 20G resume-migrate 30G. This message indicates that the migration completed because the available freespace of 20,972,752K was above 30GB.
