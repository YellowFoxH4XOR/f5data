Known Issue

CounterBasedGauge64 MIB values do not work with Network Management Systems.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • You use a Network Management System to manage one or more BIG-IP systems that use Simple Network Management Protocol (SNMP).
  • The network management system collects SNMP data from F5-specific Management Information Bases (MIBs).

Impact

Collected SNMP values are incorrect or incomplete.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When you collect SNMP information, values from F5 specific MIBs appear incorrect or incomplete.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 224903 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies To box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:
