Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. STP BPDU packets received on a 10Gb port on an 8400 or 8800 series platform are not forwarded to other ports when the STP passthrough mode is configured. As a result, STP fails and a bridge loop occurs.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR82508 for the BIG-IP LTM 9.4 software branch, and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.4.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in HOTFIX-BIG-IP-9.3.1-HF2, issued for BIG-IP version 9.3.1 (for the BIG-IP 9.3 software branch). You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.

For information about the F5 hotfix policy, refer to K4918: Overview of F5 critical issue hotfix policy.

For information about managing F5 product hotfixes, refer to K6845: Managing F5 product hotfixes.
