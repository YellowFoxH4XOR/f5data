Known Issue

This is a result of a known issue. When multiple priority groups are configured in a pool and active members are available in another priority group, the connections should be sent to those active members, by default. Instead, the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) resets new connections when the connections should be sent to the next active member in an available priority group.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR73405 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.3.0 and 9.4.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.
