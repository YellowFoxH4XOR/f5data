Issue

Old Behavior

In versions prior to BIG-IP 13.0.0 and 12.1.3.5, you are allowed to create a trunk between mixed media interfaces. For example, you are able to create a trunk between 10 /100/1Gig SFP interfaces and 10Gig SFP+ interfaces.

New Behavior

Beginning in BIG-IP 13.0.0 and 12.1.3.5, the SFP and SFP+ interfaces on the BIG-IP 2000/4000 series platform do not share common media capabilities and are no longer allowed to be configured into a trunk together.

F5 Product Development tracked this change as ID 622453.

Impact

  • You are no longer allowed to create a trunk of mixed media (SFP and SFP+) interfaces on a BIG-IP 2000/4000 series platform.
  • For BIG-IP 2000/4000 series platforms, configurations containing a trunk of mixed media (SFP and SFP+) will fail to load when upgrading from BIG-IP 12.1.3.4 or earlier to BIG-IP 12.1.3.5 or later. For more information, refer to K79412058: Configuration containing a trunk with interfaces of different media fails to load after an upgrade.

Recommended Actions

  • When configuring a trunk on a BIG-IP 2000/4000 series platform, ensure all of the interfaces are the same media.
  • Before upgrading to BIG-IP 12.1.3.5 or later, ensure trunks only contain interfaces of the same media.
