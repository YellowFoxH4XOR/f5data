Known Issue

BIG-IP ASM systems may fail to synchronize security policy configuration with device group members.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The BIG-IP ASM systems are configured to synchronize with device group members.

Impact

One or more BIG-IP ASM device group members may not have the latest security policy configuration.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H517384 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Low screen.
  • In the /var/log/ts/asm_config_server.log file, you may observe log error messages that appear similar to the following example:

    F5::ASMConfig::Handler::handle_error,,Code: 104, Error message = Missing policy
    F5::ASMConfig::Handler::log_error_and_rollback,,Missing policy

  • BIG-IP ASM device group members may not have the latest security policy configuration.
