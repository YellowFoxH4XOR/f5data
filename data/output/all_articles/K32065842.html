Known Issue

BIG-IP representational state transfer (REST) services such as BIG-IQ management and iApps LX may fail if there is not an administrator user named admin.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured the user name of the administrator account to something other than admin.
  • You are attempting to use BIG-IP REST services.

Impact

You are unable to use REST services such as BIG-IQ management or iApps LX.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • REST services to the BIG-IP system appear to fail.
  • In the /var/log/restjavad log files, you observe messages similar to the following examples:

Workaround

To work around this issue, ensure that the administrator is configured with the username admin.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
