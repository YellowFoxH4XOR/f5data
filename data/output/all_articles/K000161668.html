K000161668: Management MAC Address Conflict After Cloning a Tenant to a New F5OS-A (rSeries)

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-06-10
Updated
2026-06-10
Applies to
  • F5OS
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000161668

Description

When cloning a BIG-IP LTM tenant from a production rSeries chassis to a disaster recovery (DR) rSeries chassis that shares the same management network, both tenants may come up with identical management MAC addresses. This results in an ARP conflict on the shared management network, causing loss of management connectivity to the production tenant.

Symptoms:

  • Both the original and cloned tenants appear with the same MAC address in the management network's ARP table
  • Management access to the production tenant is lost or intermittent after the cloned tenant is deployed
  • ARP entries for two different management IP addresses resolve to the same MAC address

Environment

  • F5OS-A (rSeries appliances)
  • BIG-IP LTM tenants deployed on F5OS-A
  • Cloned tenant restored from a production tenant archive onto a separate DR chassis
  • Both production and DR chassis share the same management network (Layer 2/3)
  • Unique management IP addresses assigned to each tenant

Cause

When a tenant is cloned or restored from an archive to a new chassis, the management MAC address assigned to the original tenant is carried over in the tenant configuration. The management MAC address is controlled at the F5OS layer — not within the tenant itself — and is not automatically reassigned when a tenant archive is restored to a different chassis. This results in duplicate MAC addresses on the shared management network, causing an ARP conflict.

Answer/Recommended Actions

All commands below are run on the F5OS-A host (DR rSeries) CLI.

Note: The DR tenant will go offline briefly during this procedure. The production tenant on its separate chassis is not affected.

Step 1 — Take the DR (cloned) tenant offline temporarily:

  1. Enter configuration mode and set the tenant to provisioned state:

    config

    tenants tenant <DR-tenant-name>

    config running-state provisioned

    commit

  2. Monitor until the tenant reaches the Provisioned state:

    end

    show tenants tenant <DR-tenant-name> state status

Step 2 — Bring the DR tenant back online:

  1. Re-deploy the tenant:

    config

    tenants tenant <DR-tenant-name>

    config running-state deployed

    commit

    F5OS-A will now assign a new management MAC address from the DR chassis's own hardware MAC pool, which will be different from the production tenant's MAC.

Step 3 — Verify the new MAC address is unique:

  1. Confirm the management MAC has changed:

    show tenants tenant <DR-tenant-name> state mac-data

  2. Verify the new MAC address is no longer the same as the production tenant's MAC.

Step 4 — Confirm the ARP conflict is resolved:

  • Verify that the two management IP addresses now show two different MAC addresses in the management network's ARP table.
  • If needed, clear ARP caches on relevant network devices to expedite resolution and restore management connectivity more quickly.

Additional Information

  • The management MAC address for a tenant is assigned and managed at the F5OS layer and cannot be changed from within the tenant itself.
  • Cycling the tenant's running state (provisioned → deployed) forces F5OS-A to deallocate and reallocate a management MAC address from the destination chassis's hardware pool, guaranteeing a unique address.
  • This behavior applies when restoring tenant archives across different rSeries chassis that share a management network.
