Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP system does not properly fragment packets on egress when using a FastL4 profile. For example, when the server-side MTU is 1500 and the client-side MTU is 1280, the BIG-IP system does not fragment a 1500 byte packet sent from the server-side to the client-side VLAN.

Note: BIG-IP WebAccelerator, ASM, and WOM-enabled virtual servers do not use the FasL4 profile. However, BIG-IP WebAccelerator, ASM, and WOM products may also have virtual servers that use the FasL4 profile and are not enabled for WebAccelerator, ASM, or WOM.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR116540 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 10.0.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, Link Controller, ASM, PSM, WebAccelerator, or WOM release notes.

Additionally, this issue has been fixed in hotfix-BIGIP-10.0.0-5460.0-HF1 issued for BIG-IP 10.0.0, for the 10.x software branch. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
