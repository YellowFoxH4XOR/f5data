K35727256: Filtering email alerts to only SSL certificates that are about to expire on the BIG-IQ system

Type
Support Solution
Published
2019-08-13
Updated
2025-10-29
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K35727256

Description

You can configure the BIG-IQ system to send an email alert when an SSL certificate on a discovered BIG-IP device is about to expire or has already expired.

In some large customer environments where the BIG-IQ is used to manage multiple SSL certificates from several BIG-IP devices, you may want to limit email alerts to those certs that are about to expire.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management
  • SSL certificates on discovered BIG-IP devices

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

  1. Log in to the BIG-IQ CM device web GUI
  2. Navigate to Monitoring >> Alerts & Notifications
  3. Under Alerts, click on the Settings button
  4. Under Alert Settings, in the Device Alert Conditions section, uncheck the Certificate expired condition (by default, it is Enabled)
  5. click Save & Close.

Additional Information

None
