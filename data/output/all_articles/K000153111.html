Unable to get data from stream streams/tenant-stats/json. Please try again later. If the problem persists, contact Technical Support for further assistance.

Environment

  • F5OS-A
  • You performed downgrade of F5OS-A in the past

Cause

Possibly related to Bug ID 1975401.

Recommended Actions

Follow the workaround of Bug ID 1975401.
