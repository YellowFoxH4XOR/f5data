K626: The nslookup utility may respond slowly

Topic

When started, the nslookup utility attempts to perform a reverse lookup for the namerserver(s) IP addresses listed in the /etc/resolv.conf file. If a listed nameserver does not have a reverse DNS entry associated in the file, the nslookup utility will hang temporarily for approximately 30 seconds. After that point, the program is again reusable.

To resolve this issue, create reverse DNS entries for every nameserver listed in /etc/resolv.conf file.
