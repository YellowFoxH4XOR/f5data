LED ColorIndication
GreenGood connection @ 10Mbps
OrangeGood connection @ 100Mbps
OffNo connection

The following table describes the lights on LED 2 located on the top right of the hardware device, which indicates the data status:

LED ColorIndication
OrangeData being transferred
OffNo data being transferred
