Security Advisory Description

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may crash when processing SSL Orchestrator data in a service-chaining configuration. (CVE-2019-6674)

Impact

F5 SSL Orchestrator

TMM may restart, causing F5 SSL Orchestrator to experience traffic disruption or a failover event.

BIG-IP / BIG-IQ / Enterprise Manager / F5 iWorkflow / Traffix SDC

There is no impact; these F5 products are not affected by this vulnerability.
