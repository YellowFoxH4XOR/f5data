Known Issue

Communication between devices in a high availability (HA) configuration may occasionally fail between VIPRION 2200 or 2400 platforms.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are using either a VIPRION 2200 or VIPRION 2400 platform with more than one blade installed.
  • Multiple VIPRION devices are in an HA configuration.
  • The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) incorrectly identifies which TMM should handle host connections from an HA peer.
  • TCP SYN retransmissions have exceeded between the local and remote TMM processes.

Impact

Periodic failures in an HA communication may affect configuration synchronization (ConfigSync) and other HA-related communication.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • You are unable to perform a ConfigSync between HA peer devices.
  • The HA peer status changes when using the Configuration utility to view the HA peer status at Device Management > Devices > Status.
