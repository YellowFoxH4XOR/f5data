K000147578: How can I give the F5 support team access to my tenant?

Description

  • How can I enable the delegated access for the F5 Distributed Cloud (XC) Support team if I previously disabled it in my tenant?
  • Support is trying to help me but they can't access my tenant, what should I do?
  • Why is support not able to access my tenant?

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud Console

Answer/Recommended Actions

Support is not able to access your tenant if you disabled delegated access for it. To give support access again, please follow these steps:

  1. Login to your F5 Distributed Cloud tenant
  2. Once logged in, go to Account Settings >Tenant Setting > Tenant Overview.                  
    1.       
  3. Under the Support Access section click on Manage Access
  4. Once clicked, below form will appear, uncheck the Pause Access checkbox and makes sure Read Write All is selected under the Access Level
    1. . 
  5. Click Save and Exit
