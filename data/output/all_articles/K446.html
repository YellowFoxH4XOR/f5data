Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When virtual servers are down, 3-DNS displays them correctly as red. 3-DNS never displays pool virtual servers as red, even if they are down or disabled.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR27923, CR27924 and CR41831.
