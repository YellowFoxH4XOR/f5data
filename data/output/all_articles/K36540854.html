Description

When you attempt to add a BIG-IP device to a peer’s device trust, the peer fails to add the BIG-IP device and reports an error message similar to the following example:

This device is already associated with a trust-domain and cannot be added to the domain

Environment

  • BIG-IP devices

Cause

None.

Recommended Actions

If you then attempt to add the peer to the BIG-IP device trust and it also fails with the same error message, you may have to reset the device trust across all devices and re-setup the HA device group on all devices. Resetting device trust causes all devices to lose device service clustering communications with all other peers. Depending on the device group and traffic group configuration, this communications loss may result in an unintentional Active-Active condition that causes a traffic disruption. F5 recommends that you perform the device trust reset during a maintenance window.

To reset the device trust across all devices and re-setup the HA device group, perform the Resetting device trust across all devices procedure in K13946: Troubleshooting ConfigSync and device service clustering issues.
