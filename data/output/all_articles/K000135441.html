Description

The F5 r2000 and r4000 series hardware products do not support CPU hyperthreading and do not dedicate cores to F5OS-A. There are no FPGAs on these two hardware products, hence there is no need to dedicate CPU cores for the data mover function. This also means that the BIG-IP tenants deployed on these two hardware products do not support HTSplit; there is no separate logical cores dedicated to either data plane or control plane. The TMM threads in the BIG-IP tenants are pinned to the host CPUs but are shared with the processes in F5OS-A and do not run at real-time or even higher priority. This is to prevent the F5OS-A control plane processes from being starved of CPU resources. To balance between the BIG-IP tenant performance and the needs of F5OS-A, the system uses a combination of thread priorities and CPU shares mechanism. When there is CPU contention, the system gives more shares to processes with higher weight. Sufficient higher weight is given to the TMM threads so that they have priority on execution but not enough to completely starve the F5OS-A control plane processes.

The F5 r5000, r10000 and r12000 series hardware products have FPGAs and leverage CPU hyperthreading. This means that the BIG-IP tenants deployed on these hardware products support HTSplit. For more information about HTSplit, refer to K23505424: Overview of the HTSplit feature.

Environment

  • F5 rSeries hardware products
  • F5OS-A

Cause

Architectural differences between the various F5 rSeries hardware products.
