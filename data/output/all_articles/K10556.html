Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP WebAccelerator pvac daemon may dump core if the process is stopped while the system is still processing outstanding requests. This issue is the result of a rare race condition and may occur when running the wa_clear_cache command, or when the system is shut down while processing high volumes of traffic. Reproduction of this issue is rare.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as ID 222147 (formerly CR113900).
