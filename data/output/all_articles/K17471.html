Known Issue

BIG-IP SQL monitors do not provide adequate debugging data for diagnosing monitor issues, and there are no available commands that can be used to check the status of outstanding pings and cached connections.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • One of the SQL monitor types is marking a pool or pool member down.

Impact

Diagnosing SQL monitor issues may take longer than expected, or you may be unable to determine why an SQL monitor is marking a pool member down.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The system does not include a utility to check the status of SQL monitor pings and connections.
