To shut down BIG-IP or 3-DNS immediately, type the following command:

halt

To reboot BIG-IP or 3-DNS immediately, type the following command:

reboot

To shut down BIG-IP or 3-DNS at a specific time, type the following command:

shutdown <time>

Replace <time> with a time, in hours and minutes.

For example, to shut the system down at noon, you would type the following command:

shutdown 12:00

To reboot BIG-IP or 3-DNS at a specific time, type the following command:

shutdown -r <time>

Replace <time> with a time, in hours and minutes.

For example, to reboot the system at noon, you could use the following command.

shutdown -r 12:00
