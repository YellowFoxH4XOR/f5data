Known Issue

Modifying the security policy causes the sync status to revert to Changes Pending.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP ASM system is configured as a member of a high availability (HA) device group.
  • Your BIG-IP ASM system is configured with a security policy that is synced to the device group.
  • You modify the security policy using the TMOS Shell (tmsh) setting a configuration object using one or more uppercase characters.
  • You sync the change to the peer device.

Impact

The sync status reverts to Changes Pending.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • After performing a manual sync of the configuration change to the device group, the members of the device group temporarily display In Sync status and then revert back to the Changes Pending status.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can push the synchronization back from the peer device to the originating device. Alternatively, you can temporarily modify the device group sync status. To do so, perform one the following procedures:

  • Pushing the synchronization from the peer device
  • Modifying the device group sync status

Pushing the synchronization from the peer device

To sync the BIG-IP ASM device group members by pushing the configuration from the BIG-IP ASM device that originally received the synchronization update, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility on either BIG-IP ASM HA device.
  2. Click the Changes Pending status in the upper-left corner.
  3. Under Devices, click the BIG-IP ASM device that received the last synchronization push.
  4. To synchronize the device configuration to the group, under Sync Options click Push the selected device configuration to the group.

    Note: For BIG-IP 12.x, click Sync Device to Group.

  5. Click Sync.

Modifying the device group sync status

To automatically sync the BIG-IP ASM device group members by temporarily modifying the device group to use automatic synchronization, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility of the Active BIG-IP ASM HA device.
  2. Navigate to Device Management > Device Groups.
  3. Click the name of the affected device group.
  4. To enable automatic sync for the device group, under Configuration, for Sync Type, click Automatic with Incremental Sync.

    Note: For BIG-IP 12.x, select the Save on Automatic Sync check box.

  5. Click Update.
  6. Click the Changes Pending status in the upper-left corner.
  7. Under Devices, click the active BIG-IP ASM device.
  8. Under Sync Options, click Push the selected device configuration to the group.

    Note: For BIG-IP 12.x  click Sync Device to Group.

  9. Click Sync.
  10. Navigate to Device Management > Device Groups.
  11. Click the name of the device group you previously modified.
  12. To disable automatic sync for the device group, under Configuration, for Sync Type, click Manual with Incremental Sync.

    Note: For BIG-IP 12.x, clear the Automatic Sync check box.

  13. Click Update.
