K000132962: Peer unavailable during HA configuration - "time out waiting for secondary to respond"

Description

This article is for you, if you are trying to configure your BIG-IQ in HA configuration but are receiving the error message:

An error occurred while adding the BIG-IQ: timed out waiting for secondary device

...while trying to pair the devices

or

Adding failed - timed out waiting for secondary to respond

... in the banner

Environment

  • 2 BIG-IQ CM
  • Configuring HA setup

Cause

This error message could indicate that the 2 BIG-IQ CM devices that you are trying to pair have different master-keys, hence, not able to decrypt the config of the other device.

 Recommended Actions

You can verify the master-key by running f5mku -K, this will output your master-key.

 

There are 2 options, to resolve this.

  1. Change the master-key manually:
    1. Get the master key from the first device: f5mku -K
    2. Change the master-key on the second device: f5mku -r <the output from the previous step>
  2. Reset the BIG-IQ devices and set the same master-key to the same value while reinstalling.
