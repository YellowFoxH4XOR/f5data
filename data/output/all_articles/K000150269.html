F5 modified Chapter 11: Update or upgrade BIG-IP VIPRION systems (vCMP) in the BIG-IP update and upgrade guide and removed the "Forcing the guest offline procedure" and replaced it with this one. This video covers one procedure of a much longer process covered in the chapter.

This video demonstrates how to set the Minimum Blades Up setting for Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP) guests when you are upgrading BIG-IP VIPRION systems. The demonstration uses the TMOS Shell (tmsh). This procedure is a precautionary measure that prevents the vCMP guest you are upgrading from becoming active during the upgrade or when it goes back online after you upgrade.
