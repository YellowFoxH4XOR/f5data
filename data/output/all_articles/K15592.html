Known Issue

SIP events in iRules may run multiple times.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • An iRule with SIP related events is associated with a virtual server by way of a persistence profile.
  • You have made changes to the iRule and saved them.

Modifying SIP iRule events associated with a virtual server by way of a persistence profile does not remove old versions of iRules from memory.

Impact

The iRule does not function as expected. The impact to traffic varies, depending on the design of the iRule.

Symptoms

  • The iRule does not function as expected.

Workaround

To mitigate this issue, avoid assigning SIP iRules through a profile, instead, assign them directly to the virtual server.

Impact of workaround: Changing the design of an iRule may have additional effects on traffic processing. Ensure that any modification is compatible with your environment.
