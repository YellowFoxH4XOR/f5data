Known Issue

Some BIG-IP systems with compression enabled may experience rapid increases of memory, which may result in an out-of-memory condition, and the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) subsequently producing a core file.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are running on a BIG-IP 2000 series (C112) or BIG-IP 4000 series (C113) system.
  • Compression is enabled in an active profile.

Impact

Performance may degrade over time and the system may eventually become unresponsive and eventually fail over to another system in the device group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H499781 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • TMM xdata memory steadily increases over time.
  • The BIG-IP system fails over to the standby system if the system is configured for high availability (HA).
  • The BIG-IP system generates TMM core files to the /shared/core directory.
