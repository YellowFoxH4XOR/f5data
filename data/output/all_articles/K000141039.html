Support Solution
Published
2024-09-11

Applies to

Source

Description

APM Access Policy fails during SAML Auth, citing the below error log events:

  • Error messages:

Sep 10 10:42:00 <hostname> err tmm15[4644]: 014d0002:3: tm_unknown_header_value_xb err 0

Sep 10 10:42:00 <hostname> err tmm15[4644]: 014d0002:3: [saml_sp_crypto_get_header:1295] err 12

Sep 10 10:42:00 <hostname> err tmm15[4644]: 014d0002:3: Failed to read header 'APD_SamlCryptoSPSignCert' err 12

 

Environment

Relevant environmental factors:

  • BigIP APM with SAML Auth configured

Cause

Bug-ID's 1353021 & 1354673

 

Recommended Actions

There is no Workaround for these Bugs. And Eng-HF must be requested for the Fixes.
