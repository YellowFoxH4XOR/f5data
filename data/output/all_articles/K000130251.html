K000130251: BIG-IQ is reporting LTM device is unavailable

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-12-28
Updated
2022-12-28
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000130251

Description

Getting messages from BIG-IQ:

/var/log/ltm:

Dec 15 10:46:32 bigiq1.com warning logger[21903]: "BIG-IQ Managed Device Unavailable Managed device bigip1.com (172.16.10.2:443) marked unavailable"

Dec 15 10:54:32 bigiq1.com info logger[13555]: "BIG-IQ Managed Device Available Managed device bigip1.com (172.16.10.2:443) marked available"


/var/log/restjavad:

[INFO][15 Dec 2022 10:46:32 EST][/cm/shared/eventsource/device-state DeviceStateEventWorker] device: bigip1.bcbsnc.com not available for two consecutive cycles, sending availability event

[WARN][15 Dec 2022 10:46:32 EST][/cm/shared/event/alerts AlertCollectionWorker] Managed devicebigip1.com (172.16.10.2:443) marked unavailable

[INFO][15 Dec 2022 10:46:50 EST][/shared/identified-devices/config/device-refresh DeviceRefreshWorker] Device Refresh Failed (enable DEBUG logging for details): https://172.16.10.2:443/mgmt/shared/inflate

[WARN][15 Dec 2022 10:47:14 EST][UserUIInteractionLogsParser] Stream closed

<<SNIPPET>>

[INFO][15 Dec 2022 10:54:32 EST][/cm/shared/eventsource/device-state DeviceStateEventWorker] device: bigip1.com now available after having been unavailable for multiple cycles, sending availability event

[INFO][15 Dec 2022 10:54:32 EST][/cm/shared/event/alerts AlertCollectionWorker] Managed device bigip1.com (172.16.10.2:443) marked available

The message stems from the BIG-IQ polling the desired BIG-IP device with restjavad every 5 minutes and seeing if the BIG-IP device responds.


Environment

BIG-IQ

BIG-IP


Cause

The message stems from the BIG-IQ polling the desired BIG-IP device with restjavad every 5 minutes and seeing if the BIG-IP device responds. When it does not respond, then it is marked unavailable for the next polling cycle.


Recommended Actions

Check the network, most likely a layer 2 or 3 issue.


Additional Information

None.
