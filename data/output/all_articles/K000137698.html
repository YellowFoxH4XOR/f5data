K000137698: ArcSight sends a series of '000000000' values in the ASM remote log messages.

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-11-29
Updated
2023-12-06
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP ASM: 17.1.1
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000137698

Description

You may encounter that ArcSight sends a series of '00000000' in the ASM remote log messages.

Environment

  • ASM logging profile
  • Remote logging
  • ArcSight

Cause

This is caued by ID1393761.

Recommended Actions

Consider using another remote logging format, such as Splunk.
Alternatively, you can contact F5 support to explore the possibility of an Engineering Hotfix to resolve ID1393761.

Additional Information

None
