K000135054: Unable to access to "client.wvd.microsoft.com" via BIG-IP(explicit proxy)

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-06-15
Updated
2023-06-19
Source

Description

The customer cannot access to "client.wvd.microsoft.com" via BIG-IP(explicit proxy). This symptom has been seen since around May 2023. And He gets "503 Service Unavailable" response.

It worked before.


Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • v15.1.6
  • Explicit Proxy configuration

Cause

"client.wvd.microsoft.com" has 9 CNAMEs in the CNAME chain. However dns resolver cannot follow 9 CNAMEs.

Because DNS query does not finish he sees "503 Service Unavailable".


Unable to follow 9 CNAMEs is current limitation.

dns resolver can follow up to 8 CNAMEs.

 Recommended Actions

  1. Track RFE ID130285. To extend number of CNAME to follow , it has been submitted.
  2. Consider upgrading to v17.1.x. dns resolver in v17.1.x can follow up to 11 CNAMEs.
