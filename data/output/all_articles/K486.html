Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. BIG-IP will continue to probe any routers that are defined for a datacenter, even if you have deleted all of the BIG-IP prober factories.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR28099 and CR41834.

Workaround

To work around this issue, add a prober Access Control List (ACL) for the router. For specific instructions, refer to the BIG-IP or 3-DNS Reference Guide.
