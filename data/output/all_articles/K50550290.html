Policy Information

 

Important: This article applies only to existing F5 customers with existing Signaling Delivery Controller (SDC) deployments.

In September 2018, F5 announced the End of Sale (EoS) of the F5 Signaling Delivery Controller (SDC) with an effective date of October 1, 2019 (K44314166: End of Sale announcement for SDC). After many discussions with customers and rounds of internal review, F5 recognizes that many customers need more time to move forward their plans for 4G-to-5G transitions and/or migrating functionality to the F5 BIG-IP platform. These customers do not need an additional platform migration of a mature, integrated platform complicating their planning for the future–a future that F5 wants to help enable.

To support our customers, F5 will continue to offer SDC software to existing customers for existing deployments. At this stage, these software items can be purchased until October 1, 2022. In order to provide SDC software for this extended period, F5 is also planning another release, with newer versions of Java and OS support, as well as certification on newer HP hardware. F5 believes that this will enable you to keep your stable, integrated systems in place, allowing you to focus on future plans for signaling.

Whether your future plans involve pure 5G, a mix of 4G and 5G, or some other arrangement, F5 would love to work with you, as we move forward our products and roadmaps. It is F5’s firm intention to partner with customers to deliver the best platforms that enable you to deliver on your individual vision.
