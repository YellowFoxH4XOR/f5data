K2719: Preventing 3-DNS from probing Local DNS servers

Topic

To prevent the 3-DNS Controller from probing a specific LDNS, perform the following steps:
  1. From the navigation pane of the 3-DNS Controller Configuration utility, select System.
  2. Click the ACL tab at the top of the screen.
  3. Enter the IP address of the LDNS you want to stop probing.
  4. Click Update.

The specified DNS servers will no longer be probed.
