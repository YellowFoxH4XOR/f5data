K000149138: AVR custom monthly report exports data for longer time period

Published
2026-09-08
Updated
2026-09-08
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
Source

Description

When running a custom monthly report from AVR/Analytics on BIG-IP the resulting report contains data from a period longer than 30 days.

 

Environment

AVR custom reporting.

 

    Cause

    Working as designed.

    AVR uses a "Unix" month, which is 30 times 86400 seconds and not just 30 days - the resulting "monthly" report will be according to the Unix calendar.

     

    Recommended Actions

    Use external solutions to monitor statistics or specify different start date to get the data you need.
