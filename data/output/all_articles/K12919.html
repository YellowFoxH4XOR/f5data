Issue

Due to product availability, F5 ships the 3600, 3900, 6900, 8900, 8950, 11050, and EM4000 platforms and PB100, PB200, and PB200N blades with new Compact Flash cards as of May 2011. As a result of this change, Compact Flash cards in these platforms will report usable sizes approximately 250 megabytes smaller than the Compact Flash cards installed in devices built before the change was made.

The following two examples show the output of the smartctl -a /dev/hda command, which demonstrates the change in size:

Compact Flash shipped prior to May 2011:
