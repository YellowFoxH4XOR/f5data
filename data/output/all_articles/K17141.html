Known Issue

The BIG-IP APM sync status may change to Not All Devices Synced after you perform an access policy synchronization.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured two or more BIG-IP APM systems in a Sync-Only device group.
  • You use access policy sync to synchronize the BIG-IP APM access policies.
  • Environmental factors, such as network latency or lack of network time protocol (NTP) configuration, cause access policy sync messages to be lost or arrive unordered.

Impact

The BIG-IP APM system inaccurately reports the sync status, even if all devices have updates reflected within their policy.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The sync status for an access policy indicates the sync completed successfully. However, the system occasionally reports the Not All Devices Synced status.
  • Messages in the /var/log/ltm file indicate failed sync attempts, similar to the following example:

    info mcpd[<PID>]: 01071528:6 Device group '/Common/<policy name>' sync inconsistent, Sync failed on one or more devices in this devicegroup, Sync status may not be consistent...
