BIG-IP and 3-DNS server appliances include support for the following Gigabit Ethernet NICs:

  • Intel 82543 fiber gigabit Ethernet card
  • Hamachi fiber gigabit Ethernet card
  • Packet Engines GNIC-II fiber gigabit Ethernet card

For pricing and availability, contact your F5 Sales Representative.
