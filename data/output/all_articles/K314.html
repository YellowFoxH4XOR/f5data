Topic

3-DNS uses BIND to provide normal DNS services. 3-DNS intercepts all requests for resolution of wide IPs in the kernel. All other DNS requests are passed to BIND for resolution.

As a result, 3-DNS can act as a normal DNS server for any record that does not match a wide IP.
