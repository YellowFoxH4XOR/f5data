Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Pool statistics are incorrectly reported on the BIG-IP LTM 8800 series platforms. Pool statistics report that the current number of connections is higher than the maximum number of connections.

This behavior does not affect traffic handling.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR82850 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.4.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.
