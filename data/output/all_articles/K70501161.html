Description

When you configure a  Local Traffic Monitor with the "Time Until Up" feature, the actual time before the monitored object is marked available may not appear to not match the configured time value.

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM 
  • Configure Time Until Up monitor feature

Cause

Time Until Up is a calculated value based on several factors and can give the appearance as thought the configured Time Until Up value is not being adhered to.

Recommended Actions

In calculating the Time Until Up all of monitor probes should be "success" during "Time Until Up".

After the first monitor "success" of the monitored object, the BIG-IP starts the "Time Until Up" timer. To complete the Time Until Up timer all of probes sent by the BIG-IP must be successful (within the Time Until Up configured time) . However the BIG-IP will not count the last probe result when within the Time Until Up configured time range.


Example monitor configuration:

  - Interval : 10 sec
  - Timeout : 31 sec
  * Start from "Status: DOWN" 

Example Time Until Up configuration of 15 seconds.

Time IntervalResult of Monitor replyNode/Pool Member Status
  Failed Down
00:00:00 Success Down (timer starts)
00:00:10 Failed Down
00:00:15 ----- Up (due to "Time Until Up")
   *BIG-IP does not utilize the last-probe result (Failed on 00:00:10)

Example Time Until Up configuration of 25 seconds.
Time IntervalResult of Monitor replyNode/Pool member Status
  Failed Down
00:00:00 Success Down (timer starts)
00:00:10 Success Down
00:00:20 Failed Down
00:00:25 ----- Up (due to "Time Until Up")
   *BIG-IP does not use the last-probe result (Failed on 00:00:20)

Example Time Until Up configuration of 25 seconds.
 
Time IntervalResult of Monitor replyNode/Pool member Status
  Failed Down
00:00:00 Success Down (timer starts)
00:00:10 Failed Down
00:00:20 Success Down
00:00:25 ----- Down (still down)
   *All probes were not successful within the "Time Until Up" timer duration.

Example Time Until Up configuration of 25 seconds.
Time IntervalResult of Monitor replyNode/Pool member Status
  Failed Down
00:00:00 Success Down (timer starts)
00:00:10 Failed Down
00:00:20 Failed Down
00:00:25 ----- Down (still down)
  *All probes were not successful within the "Time Until Up" timer duration.
