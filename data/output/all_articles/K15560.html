Known Issue

Due to a race condition in the Traffic Management Operating System (TMOS) TCP proxy, SPDY connections may trigger multiple EV_BODY_COMPLETE warning messages in the /var/log/ltm file.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • Your system uses the SPDY profile.

Impact

The /var/log/ltm log file may fill with unwanted warning messages.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H457563 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Low screen.
  • An error similar to the following example appears in the /var/log/ltm file:

    http_process_state_parse_header - Invalid action EV_BODY_COMPLETE during ST_HTTP_PARSE_HEADERS
