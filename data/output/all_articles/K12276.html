Topic

When connected to a Layer 2 (L2) switch with the Link Aggregation Control Protocol (LACP) enabled, the ARX system's channel must also be configured with LACP enabled. If LACP is enabled on the L2 switch channel but not enabled on the ARX channel, the ARX system experiences intermittent connectivity.

Note: For information about enabling LACP for a channel, refer to the Enabling LACP section of the Configuring a Channel (802.3ad Link Aggregation) chapter of the ARX CLI Network Management Guide.
