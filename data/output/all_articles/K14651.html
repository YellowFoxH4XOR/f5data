Known Issue

The big3d process may produce a core file and restart.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • After a failed connection, the big3d process attempts to reconnect to the Master Control Program Daemon (MCPD).

Impact

The system may not accurately report statistics between BIG-IP GTM hosts in a synchronization group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H429387 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The big3d process continually restarts.
  • There are big3d core files in the /var/core directory.
