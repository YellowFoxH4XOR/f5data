Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP LTM SSL certificate request does not contain a NULL parameter in the x509 signature ASN1 sequence.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR69973 and CR70074, and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.3.0 and 9.4.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.
