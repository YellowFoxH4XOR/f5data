Known Issue

BIG-IP 2000 and 4000 series platforms may fail to compress traffic.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • A virtual server is configured with an HTTP Compression profile.

Under rare conditions, the compression firmware may return an error message to the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) during compression operations that prevent new compression jobs from being added to the compression queue.

Impact

The BIG-IP system fails to compress traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H460240 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The BIG-IP system experiences high CPU usage.
  • You observe connection resets on compression-enabled virtual servers.
