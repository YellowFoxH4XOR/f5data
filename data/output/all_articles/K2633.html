Topic

Use this article when your F5 product stops working as expected and you need assistance from F5 Support. If you need to collect information specific to your F5 product to open a support case, refer to K000159030: Collect F5 product-specific information when opening a support case.

Important: If you have an F5 partner support account, please contact your partner directly to submit a case. For more information about F5's partner program, refer to Find a Unity+ Partner.

Note: If you need help with an F5 application that you use to securely connect to your organization, such as F5 Access or BIG-IP Edge Client, you must contact your IT department for support. F5 Support cannot provide help for these end user applications.

Introduction

You create a support case on MyF5 (my.f5.com). To provide you with the best possible service, F5 requests that you provide the following:

  • Serial number, registration key, or subscription number for your product
  • Specific information about your issue, depending on your product
  • Priority level for your case

This article details how to collect this information.

Note: As of November 27, 2024, F5 Distributed Cloud Services (F5 XC Services) and Distributed Cloud Bot Defense customers can create and view support cases using MyF5. Distributed Cloud Bot Defense customers can also edit cases using MyF5; however, that capability is not yet available for F5 XC Services customers. For assistance, refer to F5 Distributed Cloud Services Support Process or call F5 Support for help.

Other support options

You can also do the following to get assistance:

  • Search for resources about your issue on MyF5.
  • If you have a MyF5 account, log in and use live chat, available in the lower right on MyF5.
  • Call F5 Support.
  • If you have an F5 partner support account, contact your partner directly. For more information about F5's partner program, refer to Find a Unity+ Partner.
  • Verify the proper operation of a BIG-IP system using iHealth to diagnose a QKView file.

    For more information, refer to K12878: Generating diagnostic data using the qkview utility.

  • For comprehensive installation assistance, consider services from your local F5 authorized reseller or through F5 Professional Services.
  • If you believe your case is not being handled in accordance with your support contract's service levels, you have the right to request manager escalation. Contact F5 Support and ask to speak with a Technical Support Manager. 

About proactive support cases

If you plan to perform a software upgrade, hardware migration, data center relocation, or other activity that requires a dedicated maintenance window, you may want to open a proactive support case in case a problem arises during the maintenance window. For information about proactive support cases, refer to K16022: Opening a proactive service request with F5 Support.

Prerequisites

Support account

You must have a MyF5 account to open a support case. If you do not yet have an account, refer to K000089224: Register for a MyF5 account.

Active support contract and product serial number or registration key

You must have an active support contract and your product serial number, registration key, or subscription number to open a support case. F5 uses serial numbers, registration keys, and subscription numbers to check the level of support you have for your product.

For information about:

  • Support programs and contracts, refer to Working with F5 Support Services.
  • How F5 Support manages support cases for BIG-IP products with valid support contracts, refer to K50458121: F5 Support case management practices for BIG-IP products.
  • How to find your serial number or registration key, refer to K917: Finding the serial number or registration key of your F5 device.
  • How to view your subscription number, refer to K000090257: View your subscriptions and licensing details on MyF5.

Prepare to open a support case

Describe the issue

Before you open a support case, you need to collect the required information for your F5 product. To do so, refer to K000159030: Collect F5 product-specific information when opening a support case.

Determine the priority level for your support case

F5 Support uses the following support case priority definitions to resolve customer issues as efficiently as possible. Use the following information to determine the priority level for your support case. For more information about priority levels and response times, refer to F5 Technical Support Services Guidelines and Policies.

F5 endeavors to respond to priority 1 issues within 30 minutes by phone. For Support Portal submissions, the initial response target is one hour. Unforeseen events can delay response times.

Procedures

Create a support case using MyF5

To watch a video demo of this procedure, go to  Use Guided Case Support on MyF5.

  1. Log in to MyF5, if you are not already logged in.
    • If you have multiple MyF5 accounts, you must use the account associated with the products for which you want to open a case.
    • If you need to associate a product to your account or if you have multiple MyF5 accounts and you do not know which account to use, call F5 Support, email F5 Support at services@f5.com, or if you are logged in to MyF5, use live chat available in the lower right.
    • If you do not yet have an account, refer to K000089224: Register for a MyF5 account.
    • If you use the Flexible Consumption Program (FCP)
      • For CAT key license offerings from the BIG-IQ, use the CAT key as the serial number, K000133079: Open a Support case for a BIG-IP VE licensed by FCP.
      • For MyF5 Self Service, use the last seven characters of a license key as the serial number, K000148592: Generate Self Service license keys in MyF5.
  2. On the home page, select Support > Create a Case.
  3. In Subject, provide a short description of your issue.
  4. In Description, describe your issue as if you are speaking to a support agent and include the information you collected about your issue.

    For details about the information required to open a case for your product, see the Prepare to open a case section.

  5. Select Next.
  6. Check that the correct product family is selected.
  7. Check that the correct product module is selected.
  8. In Version, select your product version.
  9. Select Next.
  10. Review the AI-generated answer at the top of the page. Select Show more to expand the text box and display the entire generated answer.
    • Under Citations, there is a list of referenced articles that were used to generate the answer. For more information, select one or more of the articles.
    • You can rate the quality of the generated answer. Select the thumbs up or thumbs down icons to submit your feedback.
    • Select the copy icon (next to the thumbs up or thumbs down icons) to copy the entire generated answer.
    • Under Top Results, the system displays links to resources that may also resolve the issue. Select a link to open a resource.
      • The eye icon provides a pop-up preview of the resource.
      • The link icon copies the URL of the resource.
  11. If you did not find a solution to your issue, select Next.
  12. Under Problem Summary, review the information you provided. To edit the information, select Edit Information.
  13. Do the following in the next section of the page:
    • For Is this pre-sales?, if your case is about a trial version of a product, select Yes.
    • In Reason for contact, select your reason for contacting F5.
    • In Serial number or last 7 digits of registration key, enter your serial number or the last seven digits of your registration key.
      • MyF5 uses this information to check the level of support you have for your product. If the system does not recognize the number you entered, or you do not have the required level of support to open a support case, call F5 Support.
      • For more information, refer to K917: Finding the serial number or registration key of your F5 device.
    • In Assign a priority, select the priority level that is appropriate for your issue.
      • For 1—Site down issues, call F5 Support.
  14. Select Next.

  15. Under Additional Information, ensure your contact information is correct and select Add alternate contact if you want to add a colleague who is not on your MyF5 account.

  16. Select Submit.

    The case page opens. To return to it anytime, go to Support > Case management.
