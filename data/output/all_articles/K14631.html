Known Issue

In rare occasions, the BIG-IP User Datagram Protocol (UDP) virtual server may erroneously send Internet Control Message Protocol (ICMP) unreachable packets to the pool member after processing the connection. Depending on the application environment, the pool member may close the existing connection after receiving the ICMP unreachable packet from the BIG-IP system.

Impact

The client may experience abrupt termination of connection to the affected virtual server.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H427295 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Low screen.
  • You may observe in a packet capture of the affected traffic that the BIG-IP system sends an ICMP unreachable packet to the affected pool member after it has forwarded the response from the pool member to the client.
  • The BIG-IP system may terminate the client connection prematurely.
