Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. By default, the BIG-IP GTM dynamically determines the best F5 product to use for probing a particular node or device. However, the BIG-IP GTM may report probing data incorrectly when another BIG-IP GTM or Link Controller system has multiple external interfaces, and one interface is up and the other interface is down. The BIG-IP GTM will incorrectly assume that the other device is capable of initiating probe requests, although the other device has one interface down. As a result, available nodes or devices may appear to be down.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR69082 and CR69083 and it was fixed in BIG-IP versions 9.2.4, 9.3.0, and 9.4.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, GTM, or Link Controller release notes.
