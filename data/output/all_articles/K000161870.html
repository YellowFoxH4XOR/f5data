Description

In virus scanning, one may notice viruses are detected in request_logs sequential files of directory /var/asmdata1/request_log, /appdata/mprov/global/asmdata1/

Environment

BIG-IP ASM with local ASM logging enabled.


Cause

none.

Answer/Recommended Actions

This is likely a false positive resulting from the binary formatting of the request_log sequential files.

If the customer has concerns regarding the integrity of these files, we recommend contacting F5 Support for further verification and assistance.

none.
