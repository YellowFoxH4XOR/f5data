Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. After installing cumulative hotfix HF-601-7, you may experience degraded performance when accessing Portal Access Web Applications. This issue occurs due to an incorrect logging setting configured for one of the Portal Access reverse-proxy engine components.

This incorrect logging setting was introduced in cumulative hotfix HF-601-7, and was fixed in cumulative hotfix HF-601-8.

Workaround

You can work around this issue by installing cumulative hotfix HF-601-8 or by uninstalling cumulative hotfix HF-601-7.

For instructions about obtaining a hotfix, refer to K167: Downloading software from F5 Networks.

For instructions about installing a hotfix, refer to K3430: Installing FirePass hotfixes.
