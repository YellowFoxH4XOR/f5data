With the availability of the F5 rSeries r10000 NEBS platform, F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) for the following:

  • BIG-IP Appliance: AFM 10150s
  • BIG-IP Appliance: AFM 10350v NEBS
  • BIG-IP Appliance: 10150s Better
  • BIG-IP Appliance: 10350v NEBS Better Bundle
  • BIG-IP Appliance: 10150S Best Bundle
  • BIG-IP Appliance: 10350V NEBS Best Bundle
  • BIG-IP Appliance: Carrier Grade NAT 10150s
  • BIG-IP Appliance: Carrier Grade NAT 10350v
  • BIG-IP Appliance: DNS 10150s
  • BIG-IP Appliance: DNS 10350v NEBS
  • BIG-IP Appliance: LTM 10150s
  • BIG-IP Appliance: LTM 10350v NEBS
  • BIG-IP Appliance: PEM 10350v NEBS
  • BIG-IP Appliance: Basic PEM 10350v NEBS

This product notice does not impact any subscription licenses or service agreements. Field Upgrade and Add-on SKUs are not included in this EoS announcement.

The BIG-IP SKUs listed below will reach EoS worldwide effective April 1, 2024.
