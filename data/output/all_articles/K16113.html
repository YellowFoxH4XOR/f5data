Known Issue

Under rare conditions, the BIG-IP AAM system may incorrectly cache optimized images or PDF data. As a result, you cannot serve or replace the data until you restart the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM).

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • BIG-IP AAM-cached content in datastor no longer exists because datastor has restarted, or the cached content has been evicted.
  • The cached datastor content is a BIG-IP AAM-optimized image or linearized PDF.
  • Two requests for the cached content enter on the same TCP connection.

Impact

The origin server may experience higher loads because affected images or PDF files are proxied instead of served from the BIG-IP AAM cache.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Affected documents are proxied with an S10413 X-WA-Info header.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can disable one or more of the features that trigger the issue. To do so, perform one or more of the following procedures:

Impact of workaround: By disabling the following features, you may experience lower cache performance and higher origin server load.

Disabling PDF linearization

  1. On the Main tab, click Acceleration > Web Application > Policies.
  2. Click the name of a user-defined acceleration policy.
  3. Click a node in the Policy Tree.
  4. From the Matching Rules menu, choose Acceleration Rules.
  5. On the menu bar, click Assembly.
  6. From the Content Assembly Options list, select Advanced.
  7. Clear the Enable PDF Linearization check box.
  8. Click Save.
  9. Publish the acceleration policy.
    1. Click Publish.
    2. In the Comment field, type a description.
    3. Click Publish Now.

Disabling image optimization

  1. On the Main tab, click Acceleration > Web Application > Policies.
  2. Click the name of a user-defined acceleration policy.
  3. Click a node in the Policy Tree.
  4. From the Matching Rules menu, choose Acceleration Rules.
  5. On the menu bar, click Assembly.
  6. From the Content Assembly Options list, select Advanced.
  7. Scroll to the Image Optimization Settings area of the screen, and for the Optimize Image setting, select the b>No Optimization option.
  8. Click Save.
  9. Publish the acceleration policy.
    1. Click Publish.
    2. In the Comment field, type a description.
    3. Click Publish Now.

Disabling HTTP Keep-Alives

  1. On the Main tab, click Local Traffic > Profiles > Services > HTTP.
  2. Select the HTTP profile associated with your virtual server.
  3. Change the Maximum Requests field from its current value to 1.
  4. Click Update.
