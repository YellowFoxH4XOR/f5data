Known Issue

Users may be unable to associate a local traffic policy with a virtual server.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You create a local traffic policy with multiple conditions.
  • More than one of the conditions uses the same operand and match type; for example, http-header contains.

Impact

You are unable to associate a local traffic policy with a virtual server.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • You are unable to associate a local traffic policy with a virtual server.
  • When you attempt to associate a local traffic policy with a virtual server, you receive an error message that appears similar to the following example:

    010716d8:3: Compilation of ltm policies for virtual server /Common/vs_name failed; Failed to compile the combined policies.
