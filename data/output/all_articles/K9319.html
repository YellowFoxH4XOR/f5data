Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP WebAccelerator will not linearize PDF version 1.4 and later documents. The BIG-IP WebAccelerator will currently linearize PDF versions 1.3 and earlier.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as ID 222645 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 11.0.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP WebAccelerator release notes.
