Description

You're deploying hardware from a Hybrid-Flex Consumption Program (H-FCP). The license includes the modules from a Best license bundle, but what other capabilities does it provide?

Environment

  • H-FCP (SWonHW)
  • rSeries/iSeries
  • VELOS/Viprion
  • Best bundle license (Golden Key)

Cause

The license for H-FCP includes all the hardware‑specific and maximum platform‑level capabilities that are not part of the Best bundle license itself.

  • Maximum platform‑rated throughput
  • SSL TPS / CPS limits defined by the hardware model
  • Hardware acceleration (crypto, compression, packet processing)
  • Port density and interface capabilities of the appliance
  • Max query capacity of DNS
  • Max CCU and Access Sessions of APM

These are inherent to the hardware and they are fully enabled under H-FCP once the license is activated.

Recommended Actions

Review the datasheets for the specific hardware platform to review full capabilities.
