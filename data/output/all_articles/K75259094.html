Description

After rebooting a standby device, it went active for a short period, resulting in an unwanted active/active situation.

Logs similar to the following may be seen in /var/log/ltm:

warning sod[7109]: 010c0083:4: No failover status messages received for 3.100 seconds, from device /Common/ltm2.example.com (10.10.10.1) (unicast: -> 10.10.50.1)

Environment

  • HA
  • Failover

Cause

Device does not have management IP defined for failover. TMM was not ready and no failover packets from peer were detected as a result.

Recommended Actions

Configure both a TMM self IP and management IP for failover, as per F5 best practices:

  • K14135: Defining network resources for BIG-IP HA features

None
