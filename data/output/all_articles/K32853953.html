K32853953: Warning: Stats have not been collected yet for this device

Description

After adding BIG-IP device to BIG-IQ, warning message is seen: "Stats have not been collected yet for this device".

Environment

  • BIG-IQ 6.0 or later
  • BIG-IP 13.1.0.5 or later

Cause

Missing AVR module. For analytics to work, you need AVR provisioned. This is required since BIG-IP 13.1.0.5.

BIG-IP Version 13.1.0.5 or later:

- You must have AVR provisioned for each BIG-IP device.
- BIG-IQ needs to provide access on Port 443 to receive BIG-IP AVR data.
- There must be a Data Collection Device (DCD) configured to your BIG-IQ

Recommended Actions

1. Provision AVR on the managed BIG-IP

2. Restart avrd (AVR daemon)

# bigstart restart avrd

Impact of procedure: F5 recommends that you perform this procedure during a maintenance window. Reprovisioning involves restarting system processes. These actions interrupt traffic and may result in a failover.  F5 recommends that you perform this procedure during a maintenance window, and start with the Standby device first if part of an HA.
