Description

A 100Gbps LACP interface on an F5OS-based BIG-IP platform was persistently in a "LACP_DOWN" state and unable to transmit frames, even though the link status was reported as UP. LACP PDUs were not being received by the peer Cisco Nexus switch from the affected F5 interface, while other interfaces in the port-channel operated normally. FPGA diagnostics indicated transmission failure at the hardware level, with ATSE TX lanes not ready and TX PLL not locked.

Environment

  • F5OS version 1.8.0
  • Platform: rSeries
  • 100Gbps LACP interface in a port-channel with Cisco Nexus switch
  • LACP configuration: Active mode, long timeout, MTU 9600
  • Peer device: Cisco Nexus switch, port-channel trunk, MTU 9216
  • Interface persistently in LACP_DOWN state, unable to transmit frames
  • FPGA diagnostics: TX Enable state disabled, ATSE TX lanes not ready, TX PLL not locked

Cause

Hardware failure in the ATSE fabric chip prevented transmission from the affected interface. The issue was not related to SFP, cabling, or configuration, and was resolved by a cold reboot of the chassis, which reinitialized the ATSE fabric and restored normal operation.

Answer/Recommended Actions

  1. Perform a cold reboot of the chassis to reinitialize the ATSE fabric chip.
  2. If the issue recurs, consider hardware RMA for the affected device.
  3. Monitor interface status post-reboot to confirm restoration of LACP and transmission functionality.

Additional Information

  • Workaround for Bug ID 1601985 (FPGA MAC TX/RX enable) was applied but did not resolve the issue.
  • Further FPGA diagnostics indicated ATSE TX lanes not ready and TX PLL not locked, confirming hardware-level transmission failure.
  • Cold reboot of the chassis reinitialized the ATSE fabric, restoring interface functionality and LACP synchronization.
  • The issue was isolated to the specific interface and did not follow SFP or cable swaps.
