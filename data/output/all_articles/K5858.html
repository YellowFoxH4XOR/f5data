Known Issue

The fix for the crash described under CR44712 and CR44807 in K4326: Known Issue: Problems that cause crashes, panics, hangs in BIG-IP versions 4.5 - 4.5.14 and versions 4.6 - 4.6.4, may cause a significant performance decrease when the ANIP kernel is used, and the auto_lasthop variable is disabled.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR54742 and CR54743, and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 4.5.14. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP Release Notes.
