Known Issue

If you create or delete a security policy, the policy may not synchronize correctly with peer members of the device group.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP ASM device is configured as a member of a Sync-Only device group.
  • Your device group synchronization settings are configured with Automatic Sync and Full Sync disabled (Incremental configuration synchronization).
  • You create or delete a security policy.

Impact

The security policy change is not fully synchronized with peer members of the sync-only device group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When you compare the security policy configuration that is synchronized to the peer device with the originating device configuration, the peer device security policy configuration is incomplete or missing.

    Use the following TMOS Shell (tmsh) command syntax to view the synchronized security policy configuration on the originating and peer devices.

    tmsh list /asm policy <security policy name> all-properties

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can issue a forced full sync from the originating BIG-IP ASM system to the device group. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility of the intended originating synchronization device.
  2. Navigate to Device Management > Overview.
  3. Click the originating device name.
  4. For Sync Options, click Sync Device to Group.
  5. Select the Overwrite Configuration check box.
  6. Click Sync.
