Known Issue

Under rare circumstances, the BIG-IP ASM bd process may produce a core file.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You perform an extensive reconfiguration to many BIG-IP ASM policies while your BIG-IP ASM system is under heavy load.

Impact

Traffic processing is interrupted while the bd process restarts.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP ASM system temporarily fails to pass traffic.
  • Your BIG-IP ASM bd process restarts and creates a core file in the /var/core/ directory.
