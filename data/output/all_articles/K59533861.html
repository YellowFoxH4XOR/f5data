Description

Issue downloading an F5 image. You are attempting to download F5 software from the MyF5 portal and the website displays the following Download Failure error message:

We are unable to complete your download at this time...

Prerequisites

To download software, you must be logged in to MyF5.

Procedures

Request initial review for Download Failure error

After you have selected Resources > Downloads from the MyF5 home page and received the Download Failure message, you can use the Download Failure form to request an initial review. To do so, complete the following steps.

  1. To access the Download Failure form, select Submit the form.
  2. Fill out the form so F5 can start investigating the download issue.
  3. Select Submit.

    The website displays a message indicating that the form has been successfully sent.

    Note: The response to this request may take up to 72 hours. If you need to follow up on your request, contact F5 support. Refer to K000135931: Contact F5 Support for more information.

  4. F5 reviews the information you have submitted.
  5. If your form is approved:
    1. F5 sends you an email indicating that the issue has been resolved, and you can now download F5 software from MyF5.
    2. Log in to MyF5 again, select Resources > Downloads, and proceed with the steps to download the F5 software you want.

    If your form is declined:

    • Complete the steps in the procedure that follows: Resubmit form to address missing or invalid information.

Complete the form without errors

The following image shows an example of a correctly filled-out Download Failure form. The following table contains descriptions of information required in selected fields of the Download Failure form. Providing complete, accurate information impacts the F5 websites you can access, including MyF5, DevCentral, F5 Labs, and LearnF5.

Resubmit form to address missing or invalid information

You are given three attempts to fill out the Download Failure form with the correct information. If F5 declines your initial form submission, refer to the following procedure.

  1. After step 4 in the previous procedure, if your form is declined, F5 sends you an email indicating that some details appear to be missing or invalid.
  2. Review the information in the email and determine what information is missing or invalid.
  3. From the MyF5 home page, go to Resources > Downloads and select Submit the form.
  4. Fill out and submit the form again (this will be your second submission), addressing the missing or invalid content.
  5. F5 reviews the information you have submitted.
  6. If your form is approved:
    1. F5 sends you an email indicating that the issue has been resolved and you can now download F5 software from MyF5.
    2. From the MyF5 home page, select Resources > Downloads and proceed with the steps to download the F5 software you want.
    If your form is declined:
    • F5 again sends you an email indicating that some details appear to be missing or invalid.
  7. Repeat steps 2 through 6.
  8. After your form has been declined three times, the next time you go to Resources > Downloads on MyF5, the website displays the following error message:

    Based on the information you provided we were unable to allow software downloads at this time, please contact F5 Support
