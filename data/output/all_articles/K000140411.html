Description

While troubleshooting interface issues you may see the following error message in the /var/F5/system/log/platform.log

Error appliance-1 fpgamgr[14] version=1.0 msgid=0x309000000000013 msg="Eye Pattern Failure on Interface." INTERFACE="4.0" Eye_Value=48.

 

Environment

  • rSeries
  • VELOS
  • SFP

 

Cause

This error message may indicate a faulty SFP

Recommended Actions

Verify if the issue is caused by a faulty SFP by following the K62013114: Troubleshooting network interface flapping article.

Once verified that the SFP is faulty, request RMA for a replacement SFP.

Additional Information

  • Eye pattern failure on an interface refers to a situation where the eye pattern, which represents the quality of the signal transmitted over the interface, does not meet the required standards.
  • It is a common issue in high-speed digital communication systems, such as Ethernet or fiber optic networks.
  • Eye pattern failure can be caused by various factors, including noise, distortion, impedance mismatch, or signal integrity issues.
  • When an eye pattern fails, it indicates that the transmitted signal may not be correctly received by the receiving device, leading to data errors or communication failures.
