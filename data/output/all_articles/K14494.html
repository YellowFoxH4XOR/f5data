Policy Information

Product Update

F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of the VIPRION LTM C4400 DC NEBS chassis and LTM B4200 NEBS blade, effective July 1, 2014.

Note: Refer to K14291: End of Sale announcement for the VIPRION B4200 blade and VIPRION C4400 chassis for advance EoS notification on the VIPRION LTM C4400 AC chassis and LTM B4200 blade.

Hardware EoL schedule

With the availability of the VIPRION 4480 AC and DC-NEBS chassis and B4300 and B4340 NEBS blades, F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of the VIPRION LTM C4400 DC NEBS chassis and LTM B4200 NEBS blade. Technical support

F5 maintains generous lifecycle policies that allow customers to enjoy many years of both support and new software releases. Customers whose platforms are designated EoL and who are currently under maintenance contracts, will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the Service Contract Renewal date expires.

After the EoSD date, 1-July-2017, F5 will not be able to provide software fixes for any technical issues and F5 will continue to provide technical support for the VIPRION LTM 4400-DCN chassis and VIPRION LTM 4200N NEBS blade on a best-effort basis.
