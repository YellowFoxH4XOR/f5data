K000135068: BIG-IP objects may not send unsolicited Neighbor Advertisements during failover

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-06-20
Updated
2023-06-20
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000135068

Description

  • During a failover, the BIG-IP may not send out unsolicited Neighbor Advertisements for configured traffic objects.

Environment

  • BIG-IP device group configured for high availability.
  • IPv6 BIG-IP local traffic objects are configured that do not match the subnet of a self IP address on the associated VLAN.

Cause

During a failover, BIG-IP redundant system taking over as the Active unit does not send unsolicited Neighbor Advertisements for BIG-IP local traffic objects that are configured that do not match the subnet of the self IP address on the associated VLAN.

 

 Recommended Actions

  1. Configure a self IP address that belongs to the subnet of the affected BIG-IP local traffic object on the VLAN that is receiving traffic for this object.
  2. If you can not configure an self-IP, enable MAC masquerading.
