Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. BIG-IP ASM stores statistical and attack-related records in a local MySQL database. The clean_db.pl script periodically purges older log records from the database tables. The clean_db.pl process may fail to purge database records containing larger data values. As a result, BIG-IP ASM database tables, such as DCC.ATTACK_EVENTS, may expand in size.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as ID 244939 (formerly CR77867) and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM 9.4.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM release notes.
