Description

A known issue exists on BIG-IP Edge Client 7260 on Windows that may result in failures in the auto-update process. This known issue will cause auto-upgrades from 7260 to future versions to fail. 

This issue does not affect BIG-IP Edge Client on macOS and other platforms or clients.

Environment

  • BIG-IP Edge Client 7260.2025.805.1823 on Windows

Cause

A known issue exists in the auto-update process on Windows. You may observe that the F5 Component Installer Service stops and fails to upgrade.

Recommended Actions

BIG-IP Edge Client 7.2.6.1 that fixes this issue is now available on MyF5 Downloads. F5 recommends you skip 7.2.6.0 and directly upgrade to 7.2.6.1 to avoid running into auto-upgrade issues.
