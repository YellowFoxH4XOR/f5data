Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may crash if high-speed logging (HSL) pool members are deleted and reused.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is configured with an HSL profile.
  • Your BIG-IP system is configured with an HSL pool.
  • A pool member is removed and re-added while the HSL pool is in use.

Impact

Traffic is disrupted while TMM restarts.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • TMM crashes.
  • Redundant pair units change state as TMM restarts on the affected unit
  • Traffic is disrupted while TMM restarts.
