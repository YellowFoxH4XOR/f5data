Known Issue

Certain BIG-IP platforms may fail to reboot.

This issue may occur on one of the following BIG-IP platforms:

  • BIG-IP VIPRION 2400/2200 with the B2250 blade
  • BIG-IP 10000 series

On rare occasions, these platforms may fail to complete a user-initiated reboot entered from either the BIG-IP command line or the Always-On Management (AOM) menu.

Impact

The BIG-IP platform fails to reboot, and subsequently shuts down.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • During a reboot, the serial console displays the following message code:

    0xAF

  • The BIG-IP platform does not complete the reboot cycle and powers down.

Workaround

You may be able to work around this issue by either performing a hardware reset using the R option in the Always-On Management (AOM) menu or by powering on the BIG-IP system after a failed reboot.

For information about accessing the AOM command menu, refer to K14594: Accessing the AOM Command Menu.

If the issue persists, contact F5 Technical Support for a Return Materials Authorization (RMA).
