K000134848: APM with SAML Auth failing Error: SAML Agent: /Common/saml_act_saml_auth_ag failed to parse assertion, error: $fmt

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-05-30
Updated
2025-08-26
Source

Description

SAML Auth failing with the following errors:

 

May 15 13:45:04 BIG-IP.local err apmd[14693]: 01490321:3: /Common/saml:Common:e623cc3c:/Common/saml_act_saml_auth_ag: TMM returned error for crypto operation 'Response/assertion is not signed'

May 15 13:45:04 BIG-IP.local debug apmd[14693]: 01490266:7:/Common/saml:Common:e623cc3c: SAML Agent: /Common/saml_act_saml_auth_ag failed to parse assertion, error: $fmt

 

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM
  • Saml
  • BIG-IP as SP
  • Authentication failing
  • Version 17.X.X

Cause

Based on the following logs. BIG-IP APM is expecting the assertion to be both signed and encrypted. However it seems the assertion received is not signed.

 

/var/log/apm

May 24 17:09:50 BIG-IP.local debug apmd[19397]: 01490266:7: /Common/saml:Common:e722f48a: modules/Authentication/Saml/SamlSPAgent.cpp: '__setVarForCryptoOperation()': 1493: setVarForCryptoOperation: key:'SPWantAssertionSigned', val:'YES'

May 24 17:09:50 BIG-IP.local err apmd[19397]: 01490321:3: /Common/saml:Common:e722f48a:/Common/saml_act_saml_auth_ag: TMM returned error for crypto operation 'Response/assertion is not signed' <=========

 

Recommended Actions

Configure IDP to sign assertion

or

Configure APM to not require assertion
