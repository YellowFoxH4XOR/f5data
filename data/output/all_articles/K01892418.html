K01892418: TMM restart due to delayed keep alives

Applies to

Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K01892418

Description
TMM was killed with SIGABRT by the SOD process that monitors all process's health. TMM misses the keep alive, hence the restart.

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • High I/O

Cause
Bug ID 680804: TMM restart due to delayed keep alives

Recommended Actions
This problem is not likely to persist after a TMM service restart. 

  1. If this problem happens repeatedly, it would be required to take a look at I/O Resources in use at the time of the database load or reload, and see if a way to lower I/O can be found.
