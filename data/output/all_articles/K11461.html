Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If BIG-IP ASM is running on a VIPRION system, the Configuration utility does not display the PCI Compliance menu item which is normally found under Reporting.

Due to this issue, PCI compliance reports are not available on the VIPRION system.

Note: The VIPRION platform supports the PCI Compliance feature.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR138484, and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM 10.2.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM release notes.

Additionally, this issue has been fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-10.1.0-3372.0-HF1 issued for BIG-IP 10.1.0. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.

To view a list of the latest available hotfixes, refer to K9502: BIG-IP hotfix matrix.

For information about installing version 10.x hotfixes on systems with a logical volume management (LVM) disk-formatting scheme, refer to K10025: Managing F5 product hotfixes for BIG-IP 10.x systems.
