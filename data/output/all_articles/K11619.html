Topic

After May 26, 2010, users of BIG-IP LTM 10.1.0 or later may provision the BIG-IP APM-Limited system.

The BIG-IP APM-Limited system includes a free license for ten concurrent user sessions. The BIG-IP APM system is a software component of the BIG-IP hardware platform that provides users with secure authentication, authorization, and access to specific web applications, or the entire corporate network.

For more information about the BIG-IP APM system and associated documentation, refer to the BIG-IP APM on F5.com or the Access Policy Manager datasheet. The BIG-IP APM-Limited system is detailed in the BIG-IP LTM 10.2.0 and TMOS release notes and in the BIG-IP APM 10.2.0 release notes.

To take advantage of this feature, current customers with BIG-IP LTM 10.1.0 or later need to reactivate their license and then provision the BIG-IP APM-Limited system. For provisioning details, refer to the BIG-IP Systems: Getting Started Guide and the Configuration Guide for BIG-IP Access Policy Manager.

Note: For information about how to locate F5 product manuals, refer to K98133564: Tips for searching AskF5 and finding product documentation.

Note: The BIG-IP APM–Limited system is licensed on the same platforms that support the BIG-IP APM module.

Note: If you have the APM-Limited license on a single member of a BIG-IP redundant pair, it does not affect normal high-availability features such as ConfigSync and failover. This situation may occur if one of the units is replaced as part of an RMA. However, if you decide to try APM-Limited, you must complete provisioning and configuration tasks on both members of the pair.
