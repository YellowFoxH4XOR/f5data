Topic

This article applies to all F5 platforms that incorporate field replaceable power supply units (PSUs).

Summary

F5 power supply cosmetic standards.

Description

Surface Definitions Description Cosmetic Standards
ExternalAny surface or feature of a PSU that is visible when fully inserted into the appliance or chassis.F5 inspects externally visible surfaces of a PSU, and ensures that it meets the highest cosmetic standards. These surfaces will not have scratches, pitting, tool marks, etc.
InternalSurfaces of a PSU that are only visible prior to insertion into an appliance or chassis.Internal surfaces may contain limited scratches, tool marks, overspray, etc. These internal blemishes will not affect the operation of the PSU.

Recommendations

F5 expects that the internal surfaces of a PSU may exhibit cosmetic anomalies. If the PSU is functioning as expected, it should not be replaced because of cosmetic anomalies on an internal surface.
