The two models of the Intel Gigabit Fiber (SX) network cards are:

  • 33mhz PCI BUS
  • 66mhz PCI BUS

The 66mhz version is a newer version of the Intel Gigabit Fiber NIC. This card started shipping with the BIG-IP in March of 2002. Due to the improved bus speed, it performs significantly better than the 33mhz version.

To find out what card you have, type the following command:

scanpci -v

The 33mhz card will appear as:

Intel 82542 1000MBit network controller

The 66mhz card appear as:

Intel 82543 1000MBit network controller

Or

Intel 82544 1000MBit network controller
