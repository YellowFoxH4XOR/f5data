Known Issue

The sflow_agent daemon may restart and produce a core file when secondary VIPRION blades are restarted (either by the BIG-IP administrator or an error-caused restart).

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured an sFlow receiver on the BIG-IP system.
  • A secondary VIPRION blade is restarted (either by the BIG-IP administrator or an error-caused restart).

The sflow_agent is an SNMP subagent that handles polling and SNMP accessibility.

Impact

sFlow traffic is temporarily interrupted while the sflow_agent daemon restarts.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H614950 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium page.
  • Your BIG-IP system generates an sflow_agent core file to the strong>/shared/core directory.
  • In the /var/log/sflow_agent.log file, you observe messages similar to the following example:

    Warning: Failed to connect to the agentx master agent ([NIL]):

  • In the /var/log/daemon.log file, you observe messages similar to the following example:

    emerg logger: Re-starting sflow_agent
