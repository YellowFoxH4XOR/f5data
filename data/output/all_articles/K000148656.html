Description

'show system events' may report thermal fault messages, similar to the below ones:

65546 controller-2 thermal-fault EVENT NA "CPU TCTL-Delta at -49.0 degC" "2024-05-10 09:01:07.937560940 UTC"
65546 controller-2 thermal-fault EVENT NA "inlet at +17.0 degC" "2024-05-10 09:01:24.014025660 UTC"
65546 controller-2 thermal-fault EVENT NA "outlet at +19.0 degC" "2024-05-10 09:01:24.023345437 UTC"
65546 controller-2 thermal-fault EVENT NA "inlet at +17.0 degC" "2024-05-10 09:01:25.379098889 UTC"
65546 controller-2 thermal-fault EVENT NA "outlet at +20.0 degC" "2024-05-10 09:01:25.388654787 UTC"
65546 controller-2 thermal-fault EVENT NA "outlet at +19.0 degC" "2024-05-10 09:01:29.778947846 UTC"
65546 controller-2 thermal-fault EVENT NA "inlet at +17.0 degC" "2024-05-10 09:01:29.783657776 UTC"
65546 controller-1 thermal-fault EVENT NA "CPU TCTL-Delta at -49.0 degC" "2024-05-10 09:02:57.110254754 UTC"
65546 controller-1 thermal-fault EVENT NA "inlet at +16.8 degC" "2024-05-10 09:03:21.026848783 UTC"
65546 controller-1 thermal-fault EVENT NA "outlet at +19.0 degC" "2024-05-10 09:03:21.030452452 UTC"
65546 controller-1 thermal-fault EVENT NA "inlet at +16.8 degC" "2024-05-10 09:03:21.280769271 UTC"
65546 controller-1 thermal-fault EVENT NA "outlet at +19.0 degC" "2024-05-10 09:03:21.290129509 UTC"
65546 controller-1 thermal-fault EVENT NA "outlet at +24.0 degC" "2024-05-19 09:05:15.885255928 UTC"
65546 controller-1 thermal-fault EVENT NA "inlet at +17.6 degC" "2024-05-19 09:05:15.945125102 UTC"
65546 controller-2 thermal-fault EVENT NA "CPU TCTL-Delta at -47.0 degC" "2024-05-19 09:15:46.146225877 UTC"
65546 controller-1 thermal-fault EVENT NA "inlet at +17.5 degC" "2024-05-19 09:06:25.009440913 UTC"
65546 controller-1 thermal-fault EVENT NA "outlet at +23.0 degC" "2024-05-19 09:06:31.110132713 UTC"
65546 controller-2 thermal-fault EVENT NA "CPU TCTL-Delta at -48.0 degC" "2024-05-28 10:42:23.727261317 UTC"

However, upon reading the temperature values, these are perfectly fine. Controller-1 and Controller-2 are reporting CPU thermal deltas (e.g., -49.0 degC, -47.0 degC, and -48.0 degC). Inlet temperatures are around +16.8°C to +17.6°C. Outlet temperatures range from +19.0°C to +24.0°C. The operational temperature is from 0 degC to 40 degC. And typical room temperature is 23C, so it is less than room temperature inlet.

Furthermore, in the following text, 'EVENT NA' means there was a non-concerning event. The issue is the word 'fault' is confusing.

Environment

  • F5OS-C
  • Velos

Cause

Bug ID 1624573 was created to reduce the occurrences of the word 'fault' when it was not a 'fault'.

Recommended Actions

If the temperature values are within the range of 0 degC to 40 degC, you can safely ignore these messages.

Additional Information

None.
