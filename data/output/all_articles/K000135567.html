Description

There are specific considerations that need to be made when attempting to integrate BIG-IP devices in Solarwinds monitoring via SNMP.
 

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • Solarwinds
  • SNMP
 

Cause

The credentials referenced by Solarwinds for monitoring must be a local account on the BIG-IP. You cannot use remote authentication (AD/TACACS/etc.) accounts.
