Known Issue

Firefox browsers may stop responding during a BIG-IP APM access session.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Users establish a BIG-IP APM access session using the Mozilla Firefox browser.
  • During the access session the BIG-IP APM processes more than one cookie in a sequence with the Firefox browser.

Impact

The Firefox browser may stop responding.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • Users may find that their access session is excessively slow.
  • The Firefox browser stops responding when downloading content from the BIG-IP APM system.
  • When the Firefox browser stops responding, the browser status may display one the following messages:

    Read <fqdn of the access session>
    Transferring data from <fqdn of the access session>
