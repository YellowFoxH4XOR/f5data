Known Issue

The Switch Card Control Processor (SCCP) provides an option for setting the speed of the serial port; however, this option does not actually change the speed.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR42152, CR43722, CR44686, and CR53603, and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.1.1 and BIG-IP 9.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
