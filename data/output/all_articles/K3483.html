Policy Information

Purpose

As part of our normal product lifecycle, F5 is introducing a new FirePass model, the 4100 series, and is, therefore, announcing the End of Sale of the existing 4000 series. The End of Sale date is the first date in a series of product life cycle milestones before a product reaches its End of Life. Each of these milestones is displayed in the table below and definitions of the milestone are available towards the end of this document. Customers who recently purchased the 4000 series can be assured of F5's commitment to them as illustrated by the five-year support and investment protection policy outlined in the table below.

Technical Support

Customers whose platforms are designated EoS and who are currently under maintenance contracts will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Please contact your sales representative if you have any questions.
