Known Issue

Manually synced Sync-Failover device groups fail to sync in the remote device.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You configure multiple devices within a traffic group to use high availability (HA) order as a fail over mechanism.
  • You also configure the Sync-Failover device groups for manual synchronization.
  • You attempt to remove a device that resides within the HA order traffic group from another device within the Sync-Failover device group.

For example:

You configure the TG1 traffic group to use HA_ORDER as a Sync-Failover mechanism. The HA_ORDER list for TG1 includes DEV1, DEV2, and DEV3.

You want to remove DEV3 from Sync-Failover device group on DEV1. However, validation running on DEV1 prevents you from doing so until you remove DEV3 from the TG1 HA_ORDER list.

The conflict occurs at this point. You are removing a device from a Sync-Failover group that is part of the device trust group, which always syncs automatically. However, when manual sync is configured for the Sync-Failover device group, the operation to remove DEV3 from the TG1 HA_ORDER list is synced as part of a manual sync operation.

After you remove DEV3 from the TG1 HA_ORDER list, the configurations don't match: The TG1 HA_ORDER list on DEV1 does not contain DEV3, but the lists in the other trust group members do. If you don't manually sync now, before you try to remove DEV3 from the DEV1 Sync-Failover device group, the remote trust group syncs fail (because of the automatic sync that is part of device trust configuration changes).

Note: Any similar operation in which you modify the membership of trust elements without using sync causes device trust group sync failure.

Impact

The failed device trust group syncs on remote devices where configuration changes were not initiated.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When the Sync-Failover device group is set to manual sync, and you do not appropriately execute and manually sync local operations, the remote synchronization of the device trust group fails.

Workaround

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

To work around this issue, perform the following procedure:

  1. Remove the device from the HA_ORDER list in the traffic group.
  2. Manually sync, immediately.
  3. Remove the device from the Sync-Failover group.

Note: F5 recommends that you do not use manual Sync-Failover groups; however, if you do and you are working with configurations that have dependencies, you must sync immediately.
