Known Issue

The FastL4 profile is not supported on virtual servers created for a BIG-IP PEM Listener.

Impact

Traffic-steering functionality is unreliable.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You notice unexpected traffic-steering behavior, or other IP traffic anomalies.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 410090 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies To box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:

Type of FixFixes Introduced InRelated Articles
ReleaseNoneNone
HotfixNoneNone

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can modify the affected virtual server and change the type from Performace (Layer 4) to Standard.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
