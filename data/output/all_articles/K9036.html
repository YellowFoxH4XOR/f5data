K9036: Clicking the New Certificate button does not generate a new client certificate

Topic

If you attempt to generate a client certificate for a local user account on the FirePass controller, but FirePass does not create a certificate after you click the New Certificate button, the client certificate database on the FirePass controller may be corrupt. When you experience this issue, you will receive a blank page, or you will receive the same page with no client certificate displayed in the Client Certificate section.

Client certificate database corruption is typically caused by generating self-signed client certificates with expiration dates that are past 1/19/2038. For more information, refer to K8451: Self-signed client certificates with expirations past 1/19/2038 corrupt client certificate database.

If you cannot resolve this issue using one of the workarounds listed in SOL8451, contact F5 Networks Technical Support for further assistance. In order to troubleshoot this issue, F5 Networks Technical Support may request a backup file containing your current configuration. You can create a backup of your configuration on the Device Management : Maintenance : Backup/Restore page.
