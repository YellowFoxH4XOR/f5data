Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If you manually purge the log files by selecting the Click to purge logs right now link on the Device Management: Maintenance: Logs page, FirePass incorrectly modifies the Next purge time displayed on that page. Logs are purged on a specific schedule, which is not affected by a manual log purge. For example, hourly logs are purged at the first minute of each hour and daily logs are purged at 4:01 a.m. each day. If you selected the value 1 day in the Keep logs for setting, and you have not purged the logs within the past 24 hours, FirePass displays a message on the Device Management: Maintenance: Logs page that appears similar to the following example:

Last purge:   Wednesday Oct 28 04:01, 2009.
Next purge:   Thursday Oct 29 04:01, 2009.

However, if you manually purged the logs at 17:53 on October 28, 2009, FirePass adds the time in the Keep logs for setting (one day) to the current time, and incorrectly displays the Next purge time as follows:

Last purge:   Wednesday Oct 28 17:53, 2009.
Next purge:   Thursday Oct 29 17:53, 2009.

The next log purge will occur at the original time of Oct 29 04:01, 2009, since manual log purges do not affect the log purge schedule. When the next automated log purge completes, the time displayed for Next purge will be correct.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as ID 319080 (Formerly CR100623).
