Topic

After launching a Portal Access Mobile E-Mail favorite, the FirePass controller may display the following error message:

Mail accounts are not set up

This error message typically occurs when the remote access client is a PDA or smart phone device. The error message occurs because the administrator has not selected the Enable corporate mail account check box on the Portal Access: Mobile E-Mail page for the user's master group, and the credentials for the email account have not been supplied, either by the administrator, an external server, or the user.

To avoid this error message, either configure the Favorite by selecting the Enable corporate mail account check box on the Portal Access: Mobile E-Mail page for the user's master group or specify a valid source of user information in the Logon Information field on the Portal Access: Mobile E-Mail page.
