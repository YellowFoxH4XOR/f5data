Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Once the gtmd process reaches 128 MB, the system will begin aggressively reaping LDNS persistence entries. If the BIG-IP GTM configuration is large, and the gtmd process exceeds or is close to the 128 MB limit, the system may not add new LDNS entries to the persistence table.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR69682 and CR69683, and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.3.1 and 9.4.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM release notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.3.0-HF3 issued for BIG-IP 9.3. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the cumulative hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
