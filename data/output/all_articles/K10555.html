Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If an external monitor is assigned to monitor virtual servers on a non-local subnet, and the virtual servers become unavailable, the big3d agent assigned to monitor those virtual servers may repeatedly restart and dump a core file.

Note: Healthy virtual servers may appear unavailable due to invalid routes, thus provoking this behavior.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR121796 and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.4.8 and 10.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM release notes.
