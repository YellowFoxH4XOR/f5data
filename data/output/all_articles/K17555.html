Known Issue

Reports that span longer time periods, which use BIG-IP Analytics (AVR) statistics, may not include the latest portion of collected data.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP system is provisioned with the AVR module.
  • A BIG-IP AVR profile is associated with a virtual server, and is gathering statistics.
  • You view Analytics statistics reports with a Time Period of Last Year, Last Month, or Last Week.

The amount of data not included in the reports is relative to the Time Period you selected, and the lack of data becomes noticeable when you view reports that span longer time periods. For example, a Last Year report may omit the last month of data, a Last Month report may omit the last week of data, or a Last Week report may omit the last day of data.

Impact

Reports that use AVR statistics may be confusing or misleading.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When you view AVR reports that span longer time periods, such as Last Year, Last Month, or Last Week, the reports do not include the most recent data collected.
