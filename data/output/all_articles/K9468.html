Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The SOD service causes the system to become active or standby, and provides failover and restart capability through a high availability table. When the SOD service determines that the peer unit is no longer responding, the service initiates failover.

During a SOD-based failover event, the standby BIG-IP system takes 3 to 5 seconds before it becomes active and begins processing traffic.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as ID 249259 (formerly CR112329).

Note: This issue does not affect BIG-IP versions 10.0.0 and later; the sod daemon was rewritten in this version.
