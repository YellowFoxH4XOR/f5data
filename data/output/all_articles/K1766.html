K1766: Reasons why the LDNS statistics page may display LDNS servers with a status of Penalty Box

Topic

Penalty Box status means that the LDNS server did not respond to the last probe from the 3-DNS.

The LDNS server will remain in Penalty Box status until it either responds to a probe or until its path information becomes too old and it is discarded. Until 3-DNS discards the path information for that LDNS, the path information for the most recent successful probe will be used.
