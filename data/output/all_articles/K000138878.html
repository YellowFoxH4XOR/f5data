Description

Error Logs for "Invalid fan instance:" on rSeries device

 

Environment

  • r2000 - r5920 device

 

Cause

The r2000 - r5920 devices have 8 fans. Fans 9-12 do not exist.

 

Recommended Actions

The "Invalid fan instance" messages for fans #9-12 can be safely ignored on the r2000 - r5920 devices. They only have 8 fans, fans 9-12 do not exist.

The r10900 has 12 fans, which is the reason why "show components component fan tray" displays 12 fans total.

 

Messages similar to the following show up in the platform.log file:

 

platform 2024-02-13 08:18:18.590 Z Error appliance-1 snmp-service[14] version=1.0 msgid=0x6a01000000000016 msg="Invalid fan instance:" fan_instance=12.

platform 2024-02-13 08:18:18.590 Z Error appliance-1 snmp-service[14] version=1.0 msgid=0x6a01000000000016 msg="Invalid fan instance:" fan_instance=11.

platform 2024-02-13 08:18:18.589 Z Error appliance-1 snmp-service[14] version=1.0 msgid=0x6a01000000000016 msg="Invalid fan instance:" fan_instance=10.

platform 2024-02-13 08:18:18.589 Z Error appliance-1 snmp-service[14] version=1.0 msgid=0x6a01000000000016 msg="Invalid fan instance:" fan_instance=9.
