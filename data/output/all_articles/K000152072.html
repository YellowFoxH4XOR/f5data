Are you an "Always On" or "Always Available" customer?

This is very important to know, because it determines how we set up your Distributed Cloud service.

  • With Always On, all of your inbound traffic is always going through Distributed Cloud.
  • With Always Available, traffic continues to flow through your normal carriers with Distributed Cloud on standby. If you are under attack, you or the SOC switch your traffic to flow through Distributed Cloud scrubbing centers for mitigation.
