K000152820: Comparability issue between i4800 SFP OPT-0010-xx/SFP+ OPT-0016-xx and Huawei 6857 with Huawei SFP+ 02313URC 10G-850nm, 0.3km MM

Type

Published

  • BIG-IP

  • BIG-IP i4000 Series
Source

Description

Compatibility issue between BIG-IP i4800 SFP OPT-0010-xx/SFP+ OPT-0016-xx and Huawei 6857 with Huawei SFP+ 02313URC 10G-850nm, 0.3km MM. Interface status will show up but no packets will pass through.

Environment

  • i4800
  • OPT-0010-00: SFP 1000Base-SX, 1.25Gig 850nm, 550m
  • OPT-0016-00: SFP+ 10GBase-SR, 10Gig, 850nm, 300m
  • Huawei switch model 6857 with SFP+ 02313URC 10G-850nm, 0.3km MM

Cause

Unknown.

Recommended Actions

Use other switch models to connect to BIG-IP i4800.
