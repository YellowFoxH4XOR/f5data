Security Advisory Description

In BIG-IP APM, on systems running more than one TMM instance, authenticated VPN users may consume excessive resources by sending specially-crafted malicious traffic over the tunnel. (CVE-2020-27724)

Impact

This vulnerability may cause the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) to stop responding, leading to disruption in services and a failover event in high availability (HA) systems.
