K000160564: Ingress packets show "Hardware Action: Drop" in the f5 ethernet trailer

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-05-12
Updated
2026-08-21
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.1.3, 17.1.1, 17.1.0, 17.1.X, 17.1.2
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000160564

Description

Packet captures may show ingress packets with the "Hardware Action: Drop" flag set. This is observed in the packet capture details as:

 

Flags: 0x05, Ingress: In, Hardware Action: Drop

.... ...1 = Ingress: In (1)

.... .10. = Hardware Action: Drop (2)

 

The issue can cause confusion when troubleshooting, as the capture suggests packets are being dropped by hardware DOS protection even when they are not.

 

Some platforms without DoS Hardware offloading like the rSeries r2xxx and r4xxx may always show the HW Action Drop field set on all ingress packets.

Environment

rSeries

Velos

Cause

Bug ID 1410245

Answer/Recommended Actions

  • Be aware that on certain platforms, packet captures may always show the "Hardware Action: Drop" field set on all ingress packets due to Bug ID 1410245.
  • This is a display artifact in packet captures and does not necessarily indicate that packets are actually being dropped by hardware.
  • To confirm actual packet drops, use additional troubleshooting methods such as AFM logs, DoS statistics, or end-to-end testing, rather than relying solely on the tcpdump "Hardware Action" field.
  • No further action is required unless there is evidence of real traffic impact.

Additional Information

  • The "Hardware Action: Drop" indication in packet captures is a known cosmetic issue and does not reflect actual packet drops.
  • This behavior is specific to certain platforms without hardware DoS capabilities.
