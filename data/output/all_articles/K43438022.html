Description
The following symptoms may be seen when the HSB is experiencing a large number of XLMAC errors and the system is unable to recover from the errors. After attempting XLMAC recovery fails, the system fails over to the peer unit, goes offline, and closes downlinks. The TMM logs contain messages similar to the following:

notice The number of the HSB XLMAC recovery operation 11 or fcs failover count 0 reached threshold 11 on bus: 3.
notice HA action is triggered due to XLMAC/FCS errors on HSB1 on bus 3.
notice HSBE2 1 disable XLMAC TX/RX at runtime.
notice HA failover action is cleared. Followed by a failover event message.

Environment

  • BIG-IP

Cause
Bug ID 930825 : It is unknown under what conditions the XLMAC errors occur

Recommended Actions

Please consider upgrading to a fixed version of 16.1.0. You can refer to this article K84554955 to guide you on upgrading your BIG-IP system software.
