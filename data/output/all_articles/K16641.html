Known Issue

An error may occur when the bcm56xxd daemon periodically attempts to read the Layer 2 (L2) information from the switch table.

Impact

There is no impact. You can safely ignore the error message.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H523183 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Low screen.
  • When the bcm56xxd daemon attempts to read the L2 information from the switch table, you notice an error message logged to the /var/log/ltm file that appears similar to the following example:

    012c0011:3: Error: reading L2 entry 68416: Operation failed bs_arl.cpp(1579)
