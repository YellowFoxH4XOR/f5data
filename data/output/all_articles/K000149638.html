Description

Does CDN support mTLS?

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud (SaaS)

Answer/Recommended Actions

mTLS is presumably not available on CDN distribution because it's an illogical use case. A CDN is used to optimize delivery of static content to (usually) anonymous clients, not perform trust authentication of a limited number of known clients.
