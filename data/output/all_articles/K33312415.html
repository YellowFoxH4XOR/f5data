Description

  • After upgrading to V14 or above and enabling in-tmm monitors, pools went down
  • SSL health monitors were failing

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • Upgrade from V12.1.x to V14.x and above
  • HTTPS pools with non-default HTTPS monitors

Cause

ID999669

Recommended Actions

To workaround this issue, upgrade to V13.1.4.x first, this will convert the SSL monitors correctly.
Then upgrading to V14.x or later will then carry over the correct SSL monitor configuration.

Additional Information

The workaround for ID1019261 can trigger ID999669 on upgrade
