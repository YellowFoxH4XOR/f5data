K3036: BIG-IP and 3-DNS may not respond to non-original MAC addresses when auto lasthop is enabled

Known Issue

When the bigpipe global auto_lasthop variable is enabled (default setting), the BIG-IP system does not respond to clients or servers with MAC addresses that match the pattern: xx:xx:00:00:00:00 and xx:xx:ff:ff:ff:ff, where x is a hex value between 0 and f.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR32245.

Workaround

To work around this issue, disable the bigpipe global auto_lasthop variable or change the locally assigned MAC address to end in something that does not match the xx:xx:00:00:00:00 and xx:xx:ff:ff:ff:ff pattern.
