K000148941: [F5OS-C] blade is restarted unexpectedly

Support Solution
Published
2024-12-11

Source

Description

Velos BX110 blade restart multiple times over the year.

 

/var/log/platform/pel on the blade has similar to below log multiple times

MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM:SS | 17232 | AOM | 2 | Na | 6 | host S3 event
MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM:SS | 17233 | AOM | 2 | Na | 6 | host S5 event
MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM:SS | 17234 | AOM | 2 | Na | 0 | +1.8V_VQF power fault event
MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM:SS | 17235 | AOM | 2 | Na | 6 | host reset event
MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM:SS | 17236 | AOM | 2 | Na | 7 | checking NC-SI configuration after host reset

 

/var/F5/partition/log/velos.log on the blade does not show the cause of the reboot before the below log (intended reboot usually have "caught SIGQUIT signal; shutting down" before the below log)

YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS+XX:XX blade-X.chassis.local notice boot_marker: ---===[ BOOT-MARKER F5OS-C blade version x.x.x-xxxxx ]===---

 

After the reboot completed, the below command on Controller and Partition shows no unhealthy status.

show system health

 

Environment

  • F5OS-C
  • Velos
  • Blade BX110
  • Unexpected/silent blade reboot

 

Cause

  • Possible Hardware issue (requires F5 support to check The Partition Qkview to confirm)

 

Recommended Actions

To determine the cause of the issue, please generate fresh Qkview from the PARTITION where those blades belongs to and contact F5 Support.
