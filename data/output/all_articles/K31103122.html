Description

The device performs a failover without any reason. Similar logs are found:

01010025:2: Device error: hsbe2 hsb_lbb7 hde2_crc_errs count 175.
01140029:4: HA dataplane_inoperable_t tmm11 fails action is go offline and down links.
010c0050:5: Sod requests links down.
010c0054:5: Offline for traffic group traffic-group-1.
010c003e:5: Offline
01340011:5: HA unit 1 state change: from 1 to 0.

Environment

  • BIG-IP Failover
  • HSB CRC error
  • i5x00/i7x00/i10x00/i11x00/i15x00

Cause

This behavior could be related to these bugs 1022773 and 1063793. If the issue is found more than once, please open a support ticket to confirm this is not a Hardware issue.

HSB CRC errors may have multiple underlying causes. One known cause is packet corruption/bit-flip, tracked as ID891333, which is addressed through HSB firmware (bitstream) updates.

The fix for ID891333 was included in iSeries i5x00/i7x00/i10x00/i11x00 HSB Firmware release: ID1934401, and was integrated into TMOS v17.1.3.1 and v17.5.1 releases.

(HSB firmware fixes are cumulative and may be delivered via other firmware tracking IDs, and therefore may not be reflected as “Fixed In” on each related bug tracker page (for example, tracking bugs).)

Recommended Actions

A reboot can clear the issue.
