K4539: BIG-IP may proxy ARP responses between VLANs when VLAN groups are configured

Known Issue

When VLAN groups are enabled, BIG-IP will proxy ARP responses between the two VLANs. If you attempt to connect to the standby BIG-IP, you will receive a response directly from the standby unit, but you will also receive a proxied response from the active unit. As a result, you may receive an address conflict warning message.

This issue is generally considered cosmetic, since both paths to the standby unit will work.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR14962, CR15998, and CR20647.

Starting in BIG-IP version 4.2 PTF-02, you can work around this issue by configuring a proxy ARP exclusion list. For specific instructions, refer to K2432: Configuring a proxy ARP exclusion class.
