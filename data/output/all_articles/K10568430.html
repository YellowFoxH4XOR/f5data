Source

Description

ASM Policy not in sync between primary and secondary blade.
Cluster synchronization status as "Error" on the secondary blade.

Environment

  • Multi Bladed Viprion
  • BIG-IP ASM
  • Cluster sync status

Cause
The issue is being tracked under  bug ID1095185: Failed Configuration Load on Secondary Slot After Device Group Sync
/var/log/ts/clustering.log on secondary blade show error as below:
asm_config_server.pl|ERR|May 31 10:01:47.611|15749|F5::Cluster::Sync::apply_sync,,Set active failed
asm_config_server.pl|ERR|May 31 10:01:47.611|15749|F5::Cluster::Sync::apply_sync,,Upgrade config load attempted, but was disallowed (possibly due to cluster load)

Recommended Actions
At the time of publishing this article, there is no workaround or fix for this BUG.

Please contact F5 support with a fresh qkview from the device.
