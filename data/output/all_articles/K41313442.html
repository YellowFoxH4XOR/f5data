Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may incorrectly spawn multiple instances of the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) processes.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is configured as a high availability (HA) pair.
  • You have enabled auto-sync and save-on-auto-sync options in a device group.

Impact

The BIG-IP system will eventually run out of memory and may cause system instability.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H548098 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Low screen.
  • You observe a low memory condition when the system invokes multiple tmsh processes.
  • Your BIG-IP system spawns multiple tmsh processes
  • You observe that the system is waiting to run multiple tmsh processes when you run the top or ps command.
