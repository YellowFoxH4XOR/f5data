Topic

This article details the recommended path to upgrade a BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) Version Plus V16 license to BIG-IP VE Version Plus V23 license. For more information about BIG-IP VE Version Plus license offerings, refer to K15643: BIG-IP VE Version Plus license offerings.

Description

The BIG-IP VE Version Plus license enables you to use your BIG-IP VE system with perpetual usage; however, upgrades are limited to software versions within a pre-established range of software releases. The following table shows supported software versions for BIG-IP VE Version Plus V16 and BIG-IP VE Version Plus V23 SKUs.

BIG-IP VE Version Plus SKU versionSupported software versions
V23BIG-IP 17.1.x - 17.5.x and BIG-IP 21.x -23.x
V16BIG-IP 11.6.0 - 16.x

Because there is no overlapping software version supported by both the V16 and V23 SKUs, F5 recommends the process described in this article when upgrading from a BIG-IP VE licensed with a V16 SKU to a BIG-IP VE licensed with a V23 SKU. F5 recommends performing this process during a maintenance window because your BIG-IP VE system stops passing traffic during the upgrade process.

Prerequisite: Purchase replacement BIG-IP VE Version Plus V23 SKU license

Prior to starting the upgrade process, ensure you have purchased the appropriate replacement BIG-IP VE Version Plus V23 license and have access to the 27-character registration key. Ensure that your new BIG-IP Version Plus V23 license includes access to all existing BIG-IP product modules currently in use on your BIG-IP VE system. Contact your F5 sales representative or one of the F5 channel partners to procure the license.

Step 1: Upgrade from BIG-IP VE 16.x. to BIG-IP VE 17.x

Begin the upgrade process by upgrading your system from BIG-IP VE 16.x to BIG-IP VE 17.x. Your BIG-IP VE Version Plus V16 license does not prevent you from performing this upgrade. Follow standard BIG-IP VE upgrade instructions to complete this upgrade step. For information about upgrading, refer to K84205182: Guide contents | BIG-IP update and upgrade guide.

After you upgrade the system to BIG VE 17.x, traffic temporarily stops flowing. This occurs because the BIG-IP registration key applied to your instance is not compatible with BIG-IP 17.x. At this point, the system is fully upgraded to BIG-IP 17.x, but traffic stops flowing until you apply the new BIG-IP VE Version Plus V23 license key.

Step 2: Apply the BIG-IP VE Version Plus 23 license key

To restore functionality and traffic flow, you need to apply the new BIG-IP VE Version Plus V23 registration key received with your license purchase. Use one of the following methods to apply the license:

Use the Configuration utility

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility on your upgraded system.
  2. Go to System > License.
  3. Select Re-activate or Update License to start the license update process.
  4. Enter the 27-character BIG-IP Version Plus V23 registration key.
  5. Follow the prompts to complete the activation.

Use tmsh at the command line

  1. Access your BIG-IP VE instance via SSH.
  2. Run the following command syntax to apply the new license key:

    tmsh install sys license registration-key <XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX>

    Note: Replace <XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX> with the registration key provided for your BIG-IP VE V23 Version Plus license.

  3. Complete the licensing process by following any prompts in the terminal.

Use REST API:

  1. Send a POST request to your BIG-IP VE instance's /mgmt/tm/sys/license endpoint. Include authentication credentials and provide the BIG-IP VE Version Plus V23 registration key in your API request payload.
  2. Confirm license installation by checking the response status for success.

After you apply the BIG-IP VE Version Plus V23 registration key using any of these methods, traffic flow will resume flowing on your BIG-IP VE instance.
