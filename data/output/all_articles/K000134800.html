Description

 

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is a privacy and security law drafted and passed by the European Union (EU), which also covers the use of cookies.

 

To comply with the regulations governing cookies under the GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive you must:

 

  • Receive users’ consent before you use any cookies except strictly necessary cookies.
  • Provide accurate and specific information about the data each cookie tracks and its purpose in plain language before consent is received.
  • Document and store consent received from users.
  • Allow users to access your service even if they refuse to allow the use of certain cookies
  • Make it as easy for users to withdraw their consent as it was for them to give their consent in the first place.

 

Environment

  • ASM captcha cookies.

 

Cause

In contrast with other captcha services, ASM captcha does not save/collect any personal data, the captcha cookie only contains the text user inserts into the text box. Cookie is validated and ignored, and not being saved on BIG-IP.

Recommended Actions
Nothing from F5 perspective.
