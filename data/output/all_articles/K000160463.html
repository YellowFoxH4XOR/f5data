Description

There some questions that are often asked about DOS protection: 

  • What is the difference between Advanced WAF (AdvWAF) and AFM  DoS Protection?
  • Do they work together or are they independent?
  • Do I get full protection at all layers with one or do I need both?

Environment

  • There is a concern of a possible DoS attack, you have had it happen and want to prevent it the future. or are under a current attack.
  • DoS Profiles
  • AdvWAF, AFM

Cause

The "cause" can vary. The questions are brought about proactively or due to a DoS attack experience.

Answer/Recommended Actions

  1.  What is the difference between Advanced WAF (AdvWAF) and AFM  DoS Protection? The "basic" difference is that AdvWAF is a Layer 7 based protection and that AFM is a Layer 4 based protection. 
    1. AFM handles things such as:
      1. TCP SYN Flood (with SYN cookie protection)
      2. UDP Flood, GRE Flood, ARP Flood
      3. Bad/malformed packet headers (ICMP, IP fragmentation)
      4. Single Endpoint Flood/Sweep
    2. AdvWAF handles things such as:
      1. TPS-based detection — transactions per second thresholds
      2. Behavioral/stress-based detection — server health monitoring + machine learning (BADoS)
      3. Heavy URL protection — resource-intensive URL targeting
      4. Geolocation-based blocking
      5. Proactive Bot Defense (integrated into the DoS profile in older versions)
  2. Do they work together or are they independent? The answer to that is either/or. You can use one or the other to protect at specific points or use both together to provide a wider range of protection.
  3. Do I get full protection at all layers with one or do I need both? That kind of goes back to question 2. But it depends on what the needs of the network are and what you suspect could happen. There is some overlap. For instance Shunning happens at layer 7, but is enforced at lower levels to save on resources. Using Shun with Layer 7 DoS

Additional Information

DoS protection can also be done, in a limited way, using just LTM. A couple of examples are:

  • K14813: Detecting and mitigating DoS/DDoS attacks (15.x and later)
  • K44230544: Mitigating HTTP GET flood attacks
  • K14235020: Mitigating HTTP POST flood attacks

The second 2 provide ways via AdvWAF, or by using an iRule that is added to a virtual server that works at Layer 7 without AdvWAF.
