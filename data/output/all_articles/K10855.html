K10855: Quorum disk free space traps

Topic

If the amount of free space available on the quorum disk drops below 5 MB, the ARX system will send an SNMP trap and log a message that appears similar to the following example:

haPairQDiskFreeSpaceWarningRaise (192) - Quorum Disk free space is low. This condition can cause redundancy to function improperly. You can directly access the quorum-disk filer to clear some disk space.

The amount of free space available on the quorum disk must increase to greater than 5 MB for 2 1/2 minutes (five consecutive 30-second polling intervals) before the condition is considered cleared. The ARX system will then send an SNMP trap and log a message that appears similar to the following example:

haPairQDiskFreeSpaceWarningClear (191) - Quorum Disk free space has returned to an acceptable level.

Note: For more information about ARX system quorum disks and high availability pairs, refer to the ARX CLI Network-Management Guide.

Note: For information about changing the quorum disk share, refer to K8889: Changing the quorum disk share without interrupting production.
