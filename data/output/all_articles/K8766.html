The BIG-IP WebAccelerator uses gzip level 6 compression. You cannot currently configure the level of compression that the BIG-IP WebAccelerator uses.

If a higher level of compression is required, consider offloading compression to the BIG-IP LTM by disabling compression in the BIG-IP WebAccelerator policy and enabling it in the appropriate HTTP profile. You can also do this on standalone BIG-IP WebAccelerator systems.

Note: Due to a known issue, offloading compression to the BIG-IP LTM may not work properly for certain BIG-IP WebAccelerator versions. For more information, refer to K12265: The BIG-IP WebAccelerator does not compress data when compression is enabled in the http-acceleration profile
