K8872: Synchronization takes several minutes to resume after the standby member of a failover pair restarts

Topic

When the standby member of a failover pair comes online after a restart, synchronization may take up to five minutes to resume. This behavior occurs because a cron job, which checks the status of the synchronization agent, runs once every five minutes on the standby controller. If the standby controller is ready to perform synchronization, the cron job starts the synchronization agent on the standby controller. If the standby controller is not ready to perform synchronization, for example if the database on the standby controller is not fully initialized, the cron job exits and does not start the synchronization agent. When the cron jobs runs the second time (five minutes later), the standby controller should be fully initialized. If the standby controller is fully initialized, the cron job starts the synchronization agent and synchronization resumes between the active and standby controllers.
