Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When running the Policy Builder, you can specify either the Live Traffic mode or the System-Generated Traffic mode for the Traffic Source setting. When running in System-Generated Traffic mode, the Policy Builder occasionally creates an incomplete security policy. When this occurs the Policy Builder updates the policy based on information that was extracted from the server response, but no other links are followed. As a result, many parameters, objects, and object types may be missing from the security policy.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR92802.
