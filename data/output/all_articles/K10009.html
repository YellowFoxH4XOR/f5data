Topic

Users who attempt to configure clustering on a FirePass 1000 or 1200 series controller may receive the following error message:

Clustering configuration not supported with this model and/or licensing

The FirePass 1000 and 1200 series controllers do not support clustering. A minimum of a FirePass 4100/4300 series controller is required to successfully configure a clustered environment. If you are currently using hardware prior to the 4100/4300 series and need to deploy a clustered environment, contact your F5 Networks sales representative to discuss your options.
