K15146650: F5 Access running on Android 10 does not send IMEI number to APM

Type
Support Solution

2023-05-19

Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K15146650

Description

Android 10 devices F5 Access App does not submit IMEI info when establishing VPN tunnel with APM.

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM
  • F5 Access
  • Android 10

Cause

In Android 10 IMEI cannot be read by third party apps. App requires READ_PRIVILEGED_PHONE_STATE permission that is only granted to carriers. F5 does not have that privilege.

Recommended Actions

None.
