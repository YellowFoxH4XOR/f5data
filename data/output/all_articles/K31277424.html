Known Issue

Policy synchronization (Policy Sync) advanced configuration lists location-specific objects (LSO) incorrectly in BIG-IP APM.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system is configured as a member of a high availability (HA) device group.
  • You create a webtop resource.
  • Your BIG-IP APM system is configured with an access profile.
  • Using the Configuration utility visual policy editor, you assign the webtop resource in the access policy using the Advanced Resource Assign agent.
  • In the Configuration utility, you navigate to the Policy Sync screen and click Advanced Settings > Static Resources to view non-LSO of the webtop.

Impact

The static non-LSO are not listed as static resources in the Policy Sync Static Resources list.

Note: This is a cosmetic issue.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When you view the Policy Sync Static Resources list, the non-LSO are not listed.
  • When you view the Policy Sync Dynamic Resources list, the static non-LSO are listed.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can avoid selecting the static non-LSO as dynamic objects.

Impact of workaround:  Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
