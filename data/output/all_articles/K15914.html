Known Issue

The tmm process may restart and produce a core file after BIG-IP APM systems are upgraded.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM systems are configured for high availability (HA).
  • Client sessions are terminated by the active unit, while the standby unit is starting up at the same time.

Impact

The tmm process continually restarts on the standby system during start up, and may produce a core file.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • After upgrading, the BIG-IP system tmm process continually restarts on the standby system during the boot process.
  • A tmm core file is generated to the /shared/core directory.
  • The system generates a SIGSEGV fault to the /var/log/tmm file, similar to the following example:

    BIG-IP APM 11.6.0

    notice ** SIGSEGV **
       <snip>
       RIP=0x15cc743

    BIG-IP APM 11.5.1 HF6 

    notice ** SIGSEGV **
       <snip>
       RIP=0x15cc743

    BIG-IP APM 11.4.1 HF6 

    notice ** SIGSEGV **
       <snip>
       RIP=0x145100c
