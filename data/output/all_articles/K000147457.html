What Happened?

You want to deploy a node but need to know what the minimum requirements are to avoid issues like High Load on your node or cluster

Environment

  • Create VMware Site 

Resolution/Answer

To serve requests for ~350 reqs/sec (an estimation) with WAF, Service Policies and the rest of the features, it will need 8CPUs with +16GB of RAM or better, 16CPU.

Additional Information

The minimum resource requirements for each node are 8 vCPUs, 32 GB RAM, and 80 GB disk storage for Mesh nodes or 100 GB disk storage for App Stack nodes.
