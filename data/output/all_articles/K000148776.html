Cause

Insufficient total_xml_memory or additional_xml_memory_in_mb

Recommended Actions

Modify "total_xml_memory" or "additional_xml_memory_in_mb" by following article:

K10803: Customizing the amount of memory allocated to the BIG-IP ASM XML processing engine
