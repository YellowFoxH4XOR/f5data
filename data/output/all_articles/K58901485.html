Description
Emails with 'root' defined in their from-address, are not generated when an SNMP alert is triggered. 

Example:
alert BIGIP_SHELL_BP_CONFIGURATION_LOADED {
snmptrap OID=".1.3.6.1.4.1.3375.2.4.0.28";
email toaddress="j.smith@example.com"
fromaddress="root"
body="The test of this Solution worked!"
}

Environment

  • SNMP traps 
  • Email notification alerts 
  • 'root' string defined in from address in the alert 

Cause
Emails blocked at the organization's mail server endpoint.

Recommended Actions

Follow the below troubleshooting steps to isolate or workaround the issue. 

  1. Log in to the command line.

  2. To back up your /config/user_alert.conf file.
    # cp /config/user_alert.conf /config/user_alert.conf.backup

  3. Update the from address in /config/user_alert.conf with FQDN
    email toaddress="j.smith@example.com"
    fromaddress="root@example.com"

  4. Restart alertd service
    # tmsh restart sys service alertd 

  5. Verify if the e-mails are delivered. 

  6. If the e-mails are not delivered, collect a packet capture on BIG-IP to verify if the e-mails are sent out from BIG-IP.
    # tcpdump -vni eth0 -s0 -w /var/tmp/snmp.pcap port 25

    Note: SNMP traffic is sent from the management port and not on tmm interfaces, filter packet capture with the correct port number for management port and SNMP port number. 

  7. Analyze the captures for 250 OK response with the next packet indicating an email generated 'from' and 'to'.

  8. If the issue still exists, repeat the step-3 to step-7 by replacing the from address with a non-root account.
    email toaddress="j.smith@example.com" 
    fromaddress="j.smith@example.com"

In both the scenario’s above, if the emails are sending out from BIG-IP and not delivered to the destination server, this indicates the traffic is blocked elsewhere in the intermediate devices or the mail server.
