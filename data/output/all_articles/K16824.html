Known Issue

Due to a race condition, the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may produce a core file while attempting to load the DNS cache database.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You configure a validating resolver cache on the BIG-IP system.
  • The resolver caches DNS key records.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restarting. If you have configured the system as a member of a redundant configuration, the device may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP system temporarily fails to process traffic.
  • Your BIG-IP system fails over to another host in the device group.
  • Your BIG-IP system generates a TMM core file to the /shared/core directory.
