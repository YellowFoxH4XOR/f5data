Issue

Due to product availability, F5 no longer provisions the 11050 platform with 320 gigabyte hard disk drives. As of January 2011, 11050 platforms will include 600 gigabyte hard disk drives.

If a 11050 platform is configured with RAID and has had one drive replaced (for example, as the result of an RMA), the array size will equal the smallest drive in the array. A 320 gigabyte drive and a 600 gigabyte drive installed in the same device will show as a 320 gigabyte RAID pair. If two 11050 platforms are configured as a high-availability pair, and one member of the pair has 600 gigabyte drives and the other has the older 320 gigabyte drives, the high availability and failover functionality of the pair will be unaffected by the disparity in drive sizes (excluding disk-space specific considerations, such as storage of log archives). The new drive provisioning will not negatively affect the functionality, performance, or cosmetic finish of the 11050 platform.
