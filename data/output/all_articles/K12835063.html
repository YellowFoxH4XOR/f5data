Known Issue

In rare conditions, the cluster management daemon (clusterd) may intermittently log one or more power notices, and log no other messages to indicate that any other issues exist with the cluster members.

This issue occurs when any of the following conditions are met:

  • You are running one of the following VIPRION blades:
     
    • B4100
    • B4200
    • B4300
  • You are running on one of the following VIPRION chassis
     
    • C4400
    • C4480
    • C4800

Impact

The BIG-IP clusterd process does not affect the cluster state in this situation. You can safely ignore these error messages.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • In the in the /var/log/ltm log file, you observe messages similar to the following example:

    slot7/... warning clusterd[7569]: 013a0009:4: Blade 7: blade 5 powered DOWN.
    slot7/... notice clusterd[7569]: 013a0010:5: Blade 7: blade 5 powered up.
