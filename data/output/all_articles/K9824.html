Topic

It is possible for a BIG-IP GTM to assign a BIG-IP LTM system with limited network connectivity as a prober. Limited connectivity in this case refers to the BIG-IP LTM system's inability to probe the systems it is designated to probe, or to send iQuery communications to remote BIG-IP GTM systems.

Although a number of conditions in a network environment may cause this type of limited connectivity, the most commonly observed root cause is a misconfigured or missing BIG-IP LTM default gateway, or in the case of limited iQuery communications, an SSL certificate exchange issue may be preventing establishment of a secure connection.

Note: The configured default gateway may not be loaded if the BIG-IP license is expired.

A BIG-IP GTM system that load-balances a BIG-IP LTM system with a missing or misconfigured default gateway may result in the following scenario:

  • iQuery communications between the BIG-IP LTM and GTM systems on the same subnet succeed
  • Probe requests to systems on the same subnet as the BIG-IP LTM system succeed
  • iQuery communications between the BIG-IP LTM and GTM systems on another subnet (or in another data center) may fail, resulting in inconsistent status across the BIG-IP GTM sync group for objects configured on or probed by the affected BIG-IP LTM
  • Probe requests to systems beyond the local subnet may fail, resulting in a false unavailable status across the sync group for all affected objects

An SSL certificate exchange issue between a BIG-IP LTM system and any member of a BIG-IP GTM sync group that load balances the BIG-IP LTM system may result in the following scenario:

  • iQuery communications between unaffected BIG-IP LTM and GTM systems succeed
  • iQuery communications between the affected BIG-IP LTM and GTM systems may fail, resulting in inconsistent status across the BIG-IP GTM sync group for objects configured on or probed by the affected BIG-IP LTM

F5 Product Development is tracking a request for enhancement to recognize a missing default route as CR107669.

Note: The BIG-IP system selected to perform a probe may be restricted by network ACLs from accessing the probe destination. You can globally prevent such a BIG-IP system from being delegated specific probing tasks by following the procedure in K10385: Controlling the BIG-IP systems to which GTM delegates probing tasks. Alternatively, you can configure a specific BIG-IP system as the prober for a given host by following the procedure in K6508: Configuring BIG-IP GTM and 3-DNS prober addresses.
