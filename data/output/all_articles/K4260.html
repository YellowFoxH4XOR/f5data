Topic

BIG-IP 9.x supports up to 4 GB of RAM. The amount of physical RAM supported by the BIG-IP platforms may exceed 4 GB; however, 4 GB of RAM is the maximum amount recognized by the BIG-IP 9.x software. F5 does not recommend upgrading BIG-IP platforms to exceed 4 GB RAM.

For more information about adding RAM to your BIG-IP system, contact your F5 Networks Sales Representative.
