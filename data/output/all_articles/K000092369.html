K000092369: VE subscription license status shows "Processing"

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-09-14
Updated
2024-09-30
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000092369

Description

VE subscription license key status shows Processing. This license key cannot be activated on a new VE.

Environment

  •     VE Subscription
  •     MyF5

Cause

Unknown as of now

Recommended Actions

  1.     Contact F5 Technical Support via the my.f5.com customer portal to request an Allow move on this license key.
  2.     After an allow move is done, the status will be updated to "Ready To Activate".

Additional Information

None
