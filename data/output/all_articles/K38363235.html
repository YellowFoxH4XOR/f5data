Known Issue

BIG-IP AFM firewall policies may take a long time to compile.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your configuration contains a lot of firewall contexts (for example, 1500 or more virtual servers with enforced policies configured).
  • There are very few rules per context; for example, five or fewer rules per context.

Impact

Compiling the firewall policies might take several minutes.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • It may take several minutes to compile the firewall.
