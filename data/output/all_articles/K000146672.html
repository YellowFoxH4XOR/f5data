What Happened?

I want to know if the "namespace" can be renamed.

Environment

  • Account Management
  • F5® Distributed Cloud Console

Resolution/Answer

  • No, it currently an immutable field.
  • You can find My Namespaces in the F5® Distributed Cloud Console by going to → General → Personal Management→ My Namespaces
