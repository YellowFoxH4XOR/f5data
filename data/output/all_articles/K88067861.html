K88067861: Error ''Resolver returned no such record'' when viewing TXT record with single backslash on DNS ZoneRunner

Source

Description
When trying to view, modify or delete a DNS TXT record on DNS ZoneRunner you may see the following error.

Resolver returned no such record

The TXT record you are trying to modify contain single backslashes. 

For example:

_dmarc  30 IN TXT  "v=spf1 a\123\456 -all"


Environment

  • DNS ZoneRunner
  • TXT record with single backslash


Cause
The error message - resolver returned no such record is due to presence of  single backslash in TXT RDATA as described in ID994221


Recommended Actions

  1. Refer to K7032: Freezing zone files to allow manual update to ZoneRunner-managed zone files to manually edit the TXT RDATA from the zone file to either use double backslash or remove if not needed.
