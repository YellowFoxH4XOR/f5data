Recommended Actions

SHA3 Certificate support requires OpenSSL 3.1.0 or later.

Currently there are no plans to upgrade OpenSSL on BIG-IP.

For SHA3 Certificate support on BIG-IP, please contact F5 Support to discuss available alternatives and workarounds for your specific use case.
