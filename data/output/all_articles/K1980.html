Known Issue

Up to March 31, 2002, F5 Networks shipped BIG-IP 5000 and BIG-IP 2000 systems that may have contained administration port (interface 3.1) issues. The last serial number that contained this issue was bip038303s.

The affected systems are more susceptible to noise on the management port (interface 3.1), which can be caused by EMI-intensive environments, or a poorly shielded cable. This noise can cause packet loss for that port.

Exposure to this problem in production should be limited since the management port (interface 3.1) is not supported for load balancing traffic, and should only be used for system administration or to reload software. Additionally, the overall impact to the administration and software installation on this interface is minimal since TCP traffic, by nature, will retry when errors occur.

As of March 31, 2002, and after serial number bip038303s, all systems have been revised to correct this issue.
