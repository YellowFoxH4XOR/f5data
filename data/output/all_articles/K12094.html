Known Issue

Description

The ARX system will not update a share farm with changes to a share's filer definition.

If a share without a filer defined is added to the share farm, and a filer is subsequently defined for the share, the ARX system will not update the share farm with the filer information. Additionally, if a filer definition is removed and then re-added in a share that is already in a share farm, the ARX system will not update the share farm with the new filer information. As a result, the share farm will not be aware of the free space available for the share, which will cause placement rules using the share farm as a target to fail.

Impact

The share farm is unable to use the new filer.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • Error messages that appear similar to the following example will appear:
:POLICY_PEP-3-INFO-RULE_TARGET_FULL:: Rule [ns1:/vol1:vol1_T1_to_T2]: The target share-farm [ns1:/vol1:sf_vol1_T2] does not have enough free space available.
  • The show policy details command will indicate a 0 percent placement frequency for the share.

    For example:

  Share Farm:       sf_vol1_T2

                       Placement Frequency   Freespace Status       
     Share Name        Count/Total           Free   Size   Pct Free  
     ----------------  --------------------  ---------------------- 
     vol1_sh1          N/A / 0        0  %   983 GB  1.1 TB  80 %

Workaround

To recover from this condition, configure the ARX system to apply the updated filer information for the share farm by deleting then re-adding the share to the share farm.

To prevent this issue, ensure a filer is defined for a share before adding the share to a share farm.

Supplemental Information

  • For information about deleting and adding a share to a share farm, refer to the share (gbl-ns-vol-sfarm) and share-farm commands in the ARX CLI Reference Guide.
