Known Issue

For BIG-IP version 9.1 through 9.1.3, guest user accounts that contain an uppercase character are unable to refresh information on the Traffic Summary, Performance, and Statistics pages. Additionally, the Refresh buttons are disabled.

This issue was resolved in BIG-IP LTM version 9.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.

To resolve this issue, you can refresh the information on the Traffic Summary, Performance, and Statistics pages. To do so, use the Refresh or Reload button in your browser.
