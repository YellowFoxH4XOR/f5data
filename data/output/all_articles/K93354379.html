Known Issue

The End-User Diagnostic (EUD) HSB Interface Test may fail for the VIPRION B4450 blade.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You run the EUD_CF 15.0.0 HSB Interface Test on a B4450 blade.
  • A 100Gb transceiver is installed and connected to an adjacent device that is passing traffic.

Impact

You may receive a false indication of hardware failure.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 653064 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:

Type of FixFixes Introduced InRelated Articles
ReleaseNoneNone
HotfixNoneNone

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can unplug all cables connected to the front panel switch ports as indicated by the instructions in the Field Testing BIG-IP Hardware: VIPRION B4300/B4400 Blades guide.

Note: For information about how to locate F5 product guides, refer to K12453464: Finding product documentation on AskF5.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
