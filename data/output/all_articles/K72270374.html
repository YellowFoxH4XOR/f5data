Known Issue

When you remotely log access session data, the BIG-IP APM system might log incomplete data.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system is configured with a high-speed logging (HSL) profile. 
  • The BIG-IP APM experiences a high load of access sessions (hundreds of sessions per second).

Impact

The system logs incomplete data for some access sessions.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When you compare log data sent through HSL to log data stored on the BIG-IP APM device (local-db), you notice that the remote log data for some access sessions is missing.
