Topic

The power consumed by the BIG-IP 1000 Switch Appliance is dependent on the amount of traffic managed; however, you can use the following measurements to plan an installation.

Note: All measurements are taken with a line voltage of 120Vac.

System operationPower consumed
Initial power up3.7 amps, 444 Watts for approximately 1 second
During boot up1.6 amps, 192 Watts for approximately 25 seconds
When idle0.87 amps, 104 Watts
Load balancing at 50% capacity1.18 amps, 142 Watts
Load balancing at 100% capacity1.5 amps, 180 Watts

Note: The BIG-IP 1000 Switch Appliance is designed for 100% capacity. This measurement assumes a linear increase of power consumption.
