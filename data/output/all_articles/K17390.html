Known Issue

BIG-IP APM portal access may not correctly detect the client's browser user-agent string.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system is configured with a portal access profile.
  • Devices connect to portal access using Internet Explorer 11.

Impact

BIG-IP APM may not correctly detect the Internet Explorer browser version.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Content processed by portal access does not display correctly.
  • Applications accessed through portal access do not function correctly.
