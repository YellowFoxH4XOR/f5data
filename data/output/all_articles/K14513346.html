Known Issue

The tmsh show /sys ha-group command output may display an incorrect high availability (HA) group score value.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have multiple BIG-IP devices configured in a HA configuration.
  • You create an HA group on the devices but have not applied the configuration to any Traffic Group.
  • The configured objects in the HA group have a non-zero score value.

The tmsh show /sys ha-group command output displays an incorrect total HA group score value of 0 (zero), although the associated objects (for example, pools, trunks, or clusters) have a non-zero score value.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may display an incorrect HA group score value.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When you run the tmsh show /sys ha-group command, you notice from the command output that the HA group score is 0.

    The command and output may appear similar to the following example:

    tmsh show /sys ha-group
    ----------------------------
    Sys::HA Group: test-ha-group
    ----------------------------
    State         enabled
    Active Bonus       10
    Score               0

  • Additionally, when you run the tmsh show /sys ha-group detail command, you notice that the associated objects have a non-zero Score Contribution value.

    The command and output may appear similar to the following example:

    tmsh show /sys ha-group detail
    ----------------------------
    Sys::HA Group: test-ha-group
    ----------------------------
    State         enabled
    Active Bonus       10
    Score               0

      ----------------------------------------
      | Sys::HA Group Pool: test-ha-group:test
      ----------------------------------------
      | Threshold             0
      | Percent Up          100
      | Weight               10
      | Score Contribution   10

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can run the tmsh show /sys ha-group detail command and refer to the Score Contribution value of the associated objects instead. 

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
