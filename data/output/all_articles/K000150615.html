Description

The connection fails to establish a connection through an F5 BIG-IP LTM. The client terminates the connection immediately after acknowledging the TLS handshake. Packet captures show that the client sends a FIN, ACK following the Server Hello Done message from the BIG-IP.

In frame 14 the client (192.168.0.1) is closing the connection with the VIP (10.0.0.1) immediately after an ACK. The issue appears to be that the client does not agree with something from frame 11. If comparing frame 10 (sent by the client to the BIG-IP) and the response (frame 11) and they are identical (as it should be) then the issue is on the client side. The BIG-IP is responding with the exact same TLS parameters.

Environment

  • TLS Negotiation

Cause

The issue lies with discrepancies in how the client interprets or processes TLS handshake responses from the BIG-IP VIP during passthrough mode operation.

  • The client device does not agree with some aspect of the TLS handshake or certificate provided by the BIG-IP.
  • No issues were identified on the BIG-IP itself; it is responding correctly based on its configuration.

Recommended Actions

The administrator must review the TLS debug logs from the client side to identify what parameter mismatch is causing the client to terminate the connections.

Verify if there are specific expectations for cipher suites or protocol settings from the client side.
