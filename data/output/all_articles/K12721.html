Issue

After you reboot a VIPRION blade, the clusterd process may occasionally force an additional reboot for time synchronization. F5 expects this behavior, and the behavior occurs only for blades that have not yet joined the cluster and are not passing traffic. The net effect of this behavior is that the blade does not join the cluster until the system completes the additional reboot.

When the blade completes the time synchronization reboot cycle, the VIPRION system logs a message sequence that appears similar to the following example, to the /var/log/ltm file:

warning clusterd[3836]: 013a0005:4: Clusterd: rebooting system for time synchronization
notice overdog[3690]: 01140029:5: HA cluster_time_sync clusterd fails action is reboot.
emerg overdog[3690]: 01140043:0: Ha feature cluster_time_sync reboot requested.
crit overdog[3690]: 01140102:2: Overdog daemon requests reboot.

Note: If multiple time synchronization reboots occur, you may be experiencing an issue reported in K12722: The clusterd process may request multiple time synchronization reboots on VIPRION systems.
