Topic

The BIG-IP system allows the use of basic regular expressions in rules. However, the BIG-IP system does not allow the use of extended regular expressions with rules.

There are many books and references on the Internet that provide information about regular expressions. You can obtain basic information about regular expressions by viewing the man pages for grep and re_format on the BIG-IP system.
