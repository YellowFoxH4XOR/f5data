Description

After upgrading the BIG-IP Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP) Host to version v17.1.1.3, while keeping the vCMP Guest on version v16.1.3.3, you may experience application traffic issues due to ASM policy loading failures and internal plugin abort errors. The issue primarily results in connection resets with the following message observed in logs:

Internal error (ASM requested abort (plugin abort error))

 

Environment

  • BIG-IP ASM
  • Upgrade
  • Policy is unexpectedly blocking traffic

Cause

Two contributing factors were identified:

  1. Missing Parent Policies and Templates

    • Policies such as /Common/ext_x_x_x_x_parent and templates like EXT-_x_x_x_template might not be present or failed to load.

    • This leads to incomplete ASM configurations, causing ASM to fail during initialization.

  2. AFM Compatibility Issues Across Host and Guest Versions

 

Recommended Actions

Immediate Fix

  • Temporarily remove ASM policies to restore application functionality.

Long-Term Recommendations

  1. Ensure all required parent policies and templates are present and correctly configured before attempting reboots or upgrades.
  2. Upgrade both Host and Guests simultaneously to maintain feature compatibility, especially with AFM-enabled deployments.
  3. Validate ASM policy loading post-upgrade using K000137652: Confirming the ASM configuration has been loaded after an upgrade .
