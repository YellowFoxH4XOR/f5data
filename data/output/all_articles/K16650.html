Known Issue

The clusterd process may restart continuously on a secondary VIPRION blade.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • Your VIPRION systems are configured as part of a high availability (HA) group.
  • In the HA group configuration, you have enabled the Include Cluster option.
  • You restart one of the BIG-IP systems.

Impact

Blade clustering does not function on the VIPRION system.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The clusterd process restarts continuously on the secondary blade.
  • The BIG-IP system logs error messages to the /var/log/daemon file that appear similar to the following example:

    emerg logger: Re-starting clusterd
