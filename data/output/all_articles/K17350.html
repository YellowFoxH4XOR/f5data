Known Issue

Disabling and then re-enabling a chassis blade may result in an inconsistent failover state.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The BIG-IP system is configured to use the HA Group feature.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may experience a traffic disruption.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • One blade in the chassis remains in the incorrect failover state, while other blades are in the correct failover state. For example, one blade remains in the standby state while the other blades in the chassis are active.
  • The system logs error messages to the /var/log/ltm file that appear similar to the following:

    notice clusterd[6820]: 013a0024:5: Blade 2: Changing primary from 1 to 2 (self)
    notice clusterd[6820]: 013a0024:5: Blade 2: Changing primary from 2 (self) to 1
    notice clusterd[6820]: 013a0024:5: Blade 2: Changing primary from 1 to 2 (self)
