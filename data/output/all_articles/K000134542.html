Description

BIG-IP VE does not receive IPv6 multicast traffic sent to all-nodes multicast address or all-routers multicast address. Running tcpdump on BIG-IP does not show those packets, while other non BIG-IP servers running in the same network receive such traffic.

Environment

  • BIG-IP VE
  • Using iavf or ixlv driver for SR-IOV ports

Cause

The issue is due to ID 1293193 & 1211089. BIG-IP VE iavf and ixlv drivers are missing MAC filters for multicast addresses preventing TMM from receiving messages sent to All Nodes and All Routers addresses.

Recommended Actions

Upgrade to fixed versions, 17.1.1 or later, 16.1.5 or later, 15.1.10 or later.
