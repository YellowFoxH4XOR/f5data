Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When the BIG-IP APM has been licensed on an F5 device, messages in the TMM log have extra characters before the log message.

For example:

May 17 18:40:31 local/hostname notice 162fd6370b9332767a11e573bc247cd3162fd6370b9332767a11e573bc247cd316e9b82d5d5d7bc44a7e9b82d5d5d7bc
Scheduler: 11%: 3% yield [ 0% lic, 11% db, 0% po, 3% tcl] after licensing

The extra characters are sessions IDs. They are placed into the print buffer on session logout, but not immediately printed. The extra characters can be safely ignored.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR137466 and was fixed in BIG-IP version 10.2.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP APM release notes.

Additionally, this issue has been fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-10.1.0-3372.0-HF1 for BIG-IP version 10.1.0. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
