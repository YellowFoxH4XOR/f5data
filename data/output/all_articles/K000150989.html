Description

  • After upgrading device, zrd keeps restarting.
  • After a reboot, it may or may not resolve.

 

Environment

  • Upgrade to BIG-IP version 16 or 17
  • BIG-IP software upgrade

 

Cause

Bug ID 264701

 

Recommended Actions

Reboot; If that doesn't work, follow the workaround steps in this article:

  • The zrd process exits upon the 'journal rollforward failed: journal out of sync with zone' error message
