Description

  • This article offers a general overview of the processes involved in user and account authentication within the context of inbound customer calls.

Environment

  • F5 Distributed Cloud (XC)

Answer/Recommended Actions

When a customer contacts the Distributed Cloud support line for assistance, our agents will first conduct a series of security verification questions to ensure the caller is an authorized user associated with the account in question. All incoming calls will be subject to this authentication process. While we cannot disclose the specific security questions used during verification, we strongly recommend that all users confirm they have access to the Distributed Cloud Console and possess the necessary information to complete the verification successfully.

 

It is crucial for users to verify their access to the Console beforehand, as failure to do so may result in delays in the support process or even the inability to authenticate the caller, which would prevent service from being provided.

 

If you have any questions about this process, we encourage you to contact your Customer Success Manager or Account Manager for further assistance and guidance.

 

THIS VERIFICATION PROCESS IS EFFECTIVE ON FEBRUARY 9TH, 2026
