Known Issue

Deleting multiple Virtual eXtended LAN (VXLAN) tunnels may cause the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) process to produce a core file and restart.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The profiles used by the VXLAN tunnels are configured with a flooding type of multipoint.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from the TMM restart, and devices configured as a high-availability pair may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from the TMM restart.
  • BIG-IP systems configured as a high-availability pair may fail over.
