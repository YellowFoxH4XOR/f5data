Provisioning licensed BIG-IP modules

Once you have access to the F5 hardware web interface / GUI you will find that newly shipped hardware will have been licensed before shipping.
Note: For R-Series (F5OS) with tenants and other platforms with VCMP Guests, you will need to log into each Tenant or Guest BIG-IP to provision the licensed modules.

You will need to provision the required software modules before moving on to the configuration.
