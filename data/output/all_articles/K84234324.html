Known Issue

Large access policies may fail to render in the visual policy editor.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The access policy contains 4000 or more elements.

Impact

You are unable to edit the access policy through the visual policy editor.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • You experience a long delay to display the access policy in the visual policy editor.
  • The visual policy editor displays an error message similar to the following example:

    this.pluslmg is undefined
    Loading stopped, Please refresh
