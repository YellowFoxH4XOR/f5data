Description

I want to escalate a support case.

Environment

  • F5 Distributed Cloud Web Application Firewall (WAF) — Managed and SaaS

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

Escalate a support case when any of the following conditions apply:

  • Your business or production environment is impacted.
  • You are experiencing partial or complete service disruption.
  • Your case has not received a response from a support engineer within 72 hours.
  • You require a formal update on your case's progress.

Use the table below to choose the appropriate escalation path.

Avenues to
Escalate		When to Escalate?What to do?What to Expect?
Option A — Escalate button

 

Your case has not received a response from a support engineer for more than 72 hours and you need a formal update on its progress.

Your case is classified as P3 priority.

 
  1. Go to the Distributed Cloud Console and click Support.
  2. Click Track requests to locate the ticket you want to escalate.
  3. Click the Escalate action button to the right of the ticket.

The duty manager or a designated engineer will respond through the support case to acknowledge your escalation. Depending on the nature of your request, a follow-up may be required to provide a more detailed update.

Option B — Support engineer

Your case is classified as P1 or P2 priority, or you are experiencing severe disruption or a hard-down impact on your production environment.  

Contact the support engineer on duty via a case update or by phone (if phone support is included in your support tier). See F5 Distributed Cloud Support Process for contact details, including phone numbers by region.

A phone operator will answer your call and transfer you to the case owner or an available engineer on duty.
Option C — Duty manager

 

Your case is classified as P1 or P2 priority, you are experiencing severe disruption or a hard-down impact on your production environment, and the support engineer assigned to your case is unavailable.

 

Request the duty manager via a case update or by phone (if phone support is included in your support tier). See F5 Distributed Cloud Support Process for contact details.

 

 

A phone operator will answer your call and collect your contact details. The duty manager on shift will call you back. If you have not received acknowledgment from a support engineer, you may request to speak directly with the duty manager.

Note: If your case is currently classified as P2 or P1, use Option B or Option C instead of Option A for faster resolution.

Note: For a description of P1, P2, and P3 priority levels, see F5 Distributed Cloud Services Support Process.

IMPORTANT:

  • For urgent P1 or P2 issues, calling support is the fastest way to reach an engineer immediately (available where phone support is included in your support tier)
  • For account-specific escalation contacts or dedicated support arrangements, contact your F5 sales representative or account manager.
