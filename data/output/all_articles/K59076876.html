Topic

Leap day is February 29th, which occurs once every four years.

Description

Leap day is added to the calendar during a leap year. A leap year occurs once every four years, and the additional day is added to keep the calendar year synchronized with the astronomical or seasonal year.

How the BIG-IP system handles leap days and leap years

F5 has evaluated BIG-IP products that have not yet reached their End of Software Development phase, and has determined that the occurrence of February 29th in a leap year does not affect the functionality or performance of the BIG-IP system.

The occurrence of leap years and leap days is independent of the occurrence of leap seconds. For additional information on F5 products and leap seconds, please refer to K16328: F5 products' handling of leap second adjustments.
