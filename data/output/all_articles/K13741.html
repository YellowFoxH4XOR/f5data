Known Issue

The BIG-IP GTM Configuration utility may not scroll to the top of the page after an update.

This issue occurs when one of the following conditions is met:

  • Your BIG-IP GTM system is configured to show a list of objects that is longer than the page length.
  • You select an object that requires you to scroll past the top of the browser page.

Impact

None

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When you select one of the objects previously described, the browser window does not automatically scroll to the top of the next page, so you need to manually scroll to get to the top of the next page.

Workaround

To work around this issue, set your default number of records per screen to a number that will fit on your screen. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: None

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP Configuration utility.
  2. Click System.
  3. Click Preferences.
  4. In the Records Per Screen field, enter a value representing a number of records that will reasonably fit in your browser window without requiring you to scroll, such as 25.
  5. Click Update.
