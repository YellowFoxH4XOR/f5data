K000151893: Pool monitor sourced from 127.1.1.x

2025-06-24

Applies to

Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000151893

Description

You used :h modifier to capture monitor traffic. You can see that the traffic is sourced from 127.1.1.x. The TMM route towards the pool member points to a gateway pool.

 

Environment

  • Monitoring
  • Packet capture

 

Cause

This is expected behaviour. The traffic from bigd towards TMM is sourced from 127.1.1.x. Then TMM makes a load balacing decision and selects one of the pool member from the gateway pool. The traffic leaving the system through the gateway (from TMM) should have a self IP as a source.

 

Recommended Actions

Collect a packet capture without the :h modifier to see the monitor traffic sourced from a self IP.
