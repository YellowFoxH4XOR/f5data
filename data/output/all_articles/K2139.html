K2139: The BIG-IP virtual servers do not respond to large ICMP echo requests

Type
Knowledge
Published
2006-02-17
Updated
2023-02-21
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • Legacy Products
  • BIG-IP 4.X: 4.6.4
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K2139

Topic

The BIG-IP system does not support ICMP fragmentation to a virtual server if the ICMP packet is larger than 1,472 bytes.
