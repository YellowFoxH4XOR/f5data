Note: For more information about working with DC power supplies on the BIG-IP 1600, 3600, 3900, 6900, or 8900 patforms, refer to the Platform Guide: 1600, Platform Guide: 3600, Platform Guide: 3900, Platform Guide: 6900, or the Platform Guide: 8900.

Note: The DC power supplies for the BIG-IP 1500, 3400, 6400, 6800, 8400, and 8800 platforms do not use terminal block plugs. On these platforms, you connect the DC power source directly to the power supplies.

The DC power supplies for the BIG-IP 1600, 3600, 3900, 6900, and 8900 platforms include a DC terminal block plug. You must connect the wires from the DC power source to the terminal block plug and then insert the terminal block plug into the DC power supply.

Only one terminal block plug is required to operate each DC PSU; therefore, you are supplied with one terminal block plug for each DC PSU you purchase, whether it is a factory option or a field upgrade. However, some customers have shown an interest in purchasing additional terminal block plugs in order to wire multiple racks, or to store them as spare parts.

F5 does not sell additional DC terminal block plugs directly; however, you may purchase them from the vendors who supply F5:

  • For BIG-IP 1600, 3600 and 3900 DC PSUs, contact WAGO Corporation and reference the 721-603/019-042 part number.
  • For BIG-IP 6900 and 8900 DC PSUs, contact Positronic Industries and reference the PLB3W3F7105 part number.
