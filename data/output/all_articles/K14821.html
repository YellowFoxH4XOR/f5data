Known Issue

The syslogd process may cease to send system alerts.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You have enabled syslog ISO8601 timestamps.

Impact

System administrators will not be notified of system events.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H437721 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium|High screen.
  • System alerts including LCD panel messages, alarm LED's, SNMP traps, and alert e-mails are not sent during an error condition.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can disable ISO8601 timestamps. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Timestamps sent by syslogd may not conform to ISO8601.

  1. Log in to the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) by typing the following command:

    tmsh

  2. Disable ISO 8601 datestamps by typing the following command:

    modify sys syslog iso-date disabled

  3. Save the change by typing one of the following commands:

    • BIG-IP 10.1.0 and later:

      save /sys config

    • BIG-IP 10.0.x:

      save config
