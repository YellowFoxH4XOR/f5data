Known Issue

This is most likely the result of a known issue. When the BIG-IP LTM performs an SSL handshake where a client certificate is required in the clientssl profile, the connection can stall or abort when transmitting handshake records. This behavior is due to an internal SSL record queue limit. Client certificate handling requires transmission of additional handshake records, which can exceed the queue limit and cause the connection to abort.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR68371 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM version 9.2.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.
