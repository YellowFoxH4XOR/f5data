Description


The Vendor Broadcom recently purchased the company Symantec and all of its products.
When upgrading the Endpoint Security feature (epsec-1.0.0-1356.0, released late Dec 2022), the detection of the product "Symantec Endpoint Protection" is now failing.

Environment

  • APM deployments with Client side inspection configured
  • Most commonly with Edge Client for SSL VPN
  • Upgrade to epsec version 1356
 

Cause

Because of the change of ownership the F5 detection of the Vendor name incorrectly identified the product with an incorrect detection signature (associated with Broadcom), causing the device to fail previously working endpoint checks.

Recommended Actions

This is fixed in epsec version 1372, released late January.
F5 recommends all users upgrade to this latest release available on Myf5.com
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000090258 : Download F5 products from MyF5
Resources >> Downloads and choose your BIG-IP version
Select the EPSEC product container and choose epsec-1.0.0-1372.0.iso at the bottom

Additional Information

Here is the messages seen in the /var/log/apm using the version 1356
(grep the logs for "av_software_check")

Feb  9 14:10:51 savory.example.com notice apmd[12667]: 014902f7:5: /Common/Wiz:Common:4fbb2fda:/Common/Wiz_act_av_software_check_2_ag: endpoint software detected (sdk=4.3.3295.0,type=av,id=3395,vendor_id=1778,version=,state=1,name=Symantec Endpoint Protection,vendor_name=Broadcom,db_signature=3671,db_version=,db_time=,last_scan=,engine_version=,missing_updates=).

It identifies the product as name=Symantec Endpoint Protection and the Vendor as vendor_name=Broadcom

The sdk=4.3.3295.0 above confirms this was being checked by epsec version 1356 (epsec-1.0.0-1356.0.iso)
The type=av,id=3395 is the antimalware signature that was matched.
And this contains no version information, depending on the exact settings in the APM Access policy, this will cause devices to fail to connect to the APM.

After the upgrade to epsec-1.0.0-1372.0, these are the /var/log/apm messages 

Feb  9 16:05:34 savory.example.com notice apmd[12667]: 014902f7:5: /Common/Wiz:Common:00389b33:/Common/Wiz_act_av_software_check_2_ag: endpoint software detected (sdk=4.3.3348.0,type=av,id=538,vendor_id=240,version=14.3.9210.6000,state=1,name=Symantec Endpoint Protection,vendor_name=Symantec Corporation,db_signature=543,db_version=2/7/2023 rev.23,db_time=1675681200,last_scan=1675911626,engine_version=16.4.1.23,missing_updates=).

The sdk=4.3.3348.0 above confirms this was being checked by epsec version 1372 (epsec-1.0.0-1372.0.iso)
The type=av,id=538 is the correct antimalware signature that is now being matched.

It now correctly identifies the product again as name=Symantec Endpoint Protection
It identifies the vendor correctly vendor_name=Symantec Corporation
And now correctly returns the values:
version=14.3.9210.6000, the current release in early Feb 2023
state=1, product was enabled
db_version=2/7/2023 rev.23
engine_version=16.4.1.23
db_time=1675681200, database was updated on Mon  6 Feb 2023 11:00:00 UTC
last_scan=1675911626, last full scan was run on Thu  9 Feb 2023 03:00:26 UTC
