Known Issue

Firewall rules applied to route domains are not synchronized across all BIG-IP devices in the device group.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • BIG-IP AFM devices are in a device group
  • Route domains are configured on the BIG-IP system
  • Firewall rules are applied to a route domain

Impact

You must manually configure firewall rule settings on all BIG-IP devices in the device group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • After performing a successful configuration synchronization (ConfigSync), you notice changes made to firewall rule settings are not synchronized to other hosts in the device group .
  • Firewall rule settings may not be synchronized across BIG-IP devices in the device group.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 402669 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies To box, located in the upper right corner of this article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:

HotfixNoneNone

Note: In BIG-IP 11.4.0 and later, firewall rules will be synchronized only when the route domain to which the rule applies is configured across all devices in the device group.

Workaround

There is no work around for this issue. However, you can mitigate this issue by manually configuring the same firewall rule settings on all BIG-IP devices in the device group.
