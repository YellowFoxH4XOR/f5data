K000135967: Re-activate FCP License on BIG-IP VE not reachable from BIG-IQ

Description

The Flexible Consumption Program (FCP) license on a BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) needs to be reactivated but the BIG-IP VE is unreachable from the BIG-IQ License Manager. License reactivation is required for updates/upgrades to the BIG-IP VE. 

Environment

  • BIG-IQ License Manger
  • BIG-IP VE
  • FCP

Cause

The FCP license on the BIG-IP VE needs to be reactivated but is unreachable from the BIG-IQ License Manager.

Recommended Actions

  1. From the BIG-IQ License Manager GUI, revoke the license for the unreachable BIG-IP. 
    Note: This will not impact the BIG-IP VE traffic.
    • In Devices tab, select License Management > Assignments.
    • Add check mark to Device Name / Device Address of the BIG-IP VE for which the FCP provisioned license is to be revoked.
    • Click Revoke.
    • Click Revoke again and click ok in the Confirm Revoke message box.
    • The selected BIG-IP VE should now be removed from the Assignments List.
  2. From the BIG-IQ License Manager GUI, reactivate the CAT FCP license per K84488720: Reactivate FCP license offering for BIG-IP VE from BIG-IQ.
    Note: This will not impact the BIG-IP VE traffic.
  3. From the BIG-IQ generate the new license using a script from the command line and apply it to the unreachable BIG-IP VE per K69187707: License an unreachable BIG-IP VE from BIG-IQ.
    Important:     This is traffic impacting as services restart on the BIG-IP VE.
