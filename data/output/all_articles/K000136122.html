Description

After configuring Inbound WideIPs, when you click on the particular member, in the next window the member name is changed. Member address and port remain correct.

 

Environment

  • BIG-IP LC
  • versions affected by ID894081

Cause

The issue is related to ID894081.

 

 Recommended Actions

Upgrade to version not affected by ID894081.

 

Additional Information

The issue is specific to BIG-IP Link Controller. BIG-IP DNS is not affected.
