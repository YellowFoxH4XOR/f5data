K81274242: BIG-IQ statistics collection causes high CPU usage on managed BIG-IP

Type
Support Solution
Published
2020-11-15
Updated
2023-02-21
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ Platforms
  • BIG-IP Platforms
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 7.1.0, 7.1.X, 7.0.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K81274242

Description

When statistics collection is enabled on BIG-IQ, managed BIG-IP devices may experience high CPU utilization. In some cases, BIG-IQ may also be unable to collect statistics due to high CPU usage on the BIG-IP devices.

BIG-IP performance graphs show high CPU utilization on the control plane. On hardware platforms where HTSplit is enabled, this is typically visible on the odd-numbered CPU cores.

When statistics collection for the managed BIG-IP devices is disabled on BIG-IQ, the high CPU utilization on the BIG-IP devices stops.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ v7.1.0 or earlier
  • A BIG-IQ cluster with statistics collection enabled
  • A BIG-IP device containing a large number of configuration objects (for example, more than 1,000 virtual servers or pools)

Cause

The high CPU utilization on the BIG-IP is related to Bug ID 872237.

If statistics collection is not functioning, the issue is related to Bug ID 908505.

Answer/Recommended Actions

Follow the steps below to resolve the issue:

  1. Follow K53001642 to update the BIG-IQ iApp and resolve the high CPU utilization issue on the BIG-IP.
  2. If the BIG-IP continues to experience high CPU utilization after updating the iApp, refer to K91114310 to optimize the BIG-IQ statistics collection settings.
