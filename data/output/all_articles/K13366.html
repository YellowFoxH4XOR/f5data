Policy Information

With the release of the BIG-IP APM and Edge Gateway 11.0.0, F5 is announcing the End of Sale of the FirePass product line, including the appliances, VEs, user licenses and accessories, effective October 1, 2012. F5 is providing advanced product notification to customers and partners announcing the intent to retire the products listed below:

Platform NameEnd of Sale (EoS)
FirePass appliances, VEs, hardware options10/01/2012
User add-on, software options and DR licenses10/01/2013

Note: DR licenses include F5-ADD-FP-300-DR, F5-ADD-FP-1000-DR, and F5-ADD-FP-1500-DR.

FirePass hardware End of Life dates are listed below:

Platform NameEnd of New Software Support (EoNSS)End of Software Development (EoSD)End of Support Contract Renewal (EoSCR)End of RMA (EoRMA)Platform End of Technical Support (EoTS)
FirePass appliances, hardware options10/01/201410/01/201510/01/201810/01/201910/01/2019

Note: For information about the FirePass software End of Life dates, refer to K4156: FirePass software support policy.

Technical Support

F5 maintains generous lifecycle policies that allow customers to enjoy many years of both support and new software releases. Customers whose platforms are designated EoL and who are currently under maintenance contracts will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the Service Contract Renewal date expires.
