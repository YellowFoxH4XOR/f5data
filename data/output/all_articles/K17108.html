Known Issue

Repetitively adding and removing a PEM policy with usage monitoring may cause usage reporting to stop working.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You associate the BIG-IP PEM Gx listener with a policy configured with usage monitoring.
  • You repetitively add and remove the policy.

Impact

The usage reporting may not work as expected when you re-associate the policy with usage monitoring to the Gx listener.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • After the second attempt of adding back the target policy, the usage monitoring reports do not send when traffic is matching the policy.
  • Usage reporting stops working.
