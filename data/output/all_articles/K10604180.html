Description
Unexpected failover may occur when HA Group is disabled and a blade experiences a status change

The following logs will be seen on the pre-failover Active:

Jun 15 14:46:25 slot2 notice mcpd[12063]: 010719fb:5: HA Group ha_gateway score updated from 80 to 60.

The following logs will be seen on the pre-failover Standby:

Jun 15 14:46:25 example.f5.com notice sod[13455]: 010c006d:5: Leaving Standby for Active (best ha score).
Jun 15 14:46:25 example.f5.com notice sod[13455]: 010c0053:5: Active for traffic group traffic-group-1.
Jun 15 14:46:25 example.f5.com notice sod[13455]: 010c0019:5: Active
Jun 15 14:46:25 slot2 notice sod[10113]: 010c006d:5: Leaving Standby for Active (best ha score).
Jun 15 14:46:25 slot2 notice sod[10113]: 010c0053:5: Active for traffic group traffic-group-1.
Jun 15 14:46:25 slot2 notice sod[10113]: 010c0019:5: Active

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • Viprions in non-VCMP configuration with HA device cluster configured
  • HA Group disabled
  • Traffic group failover method set to Best HA Score
  • Blade is disabled (or other action that lowers the HA score)

Cause
Regardless of the status of the HA Group, the Failover Method of the traffic group will take precedence.  If the Standby unit is informed that it has a better HA score, a failover event will occur and the Standby will takeover as the Active.

Recommended Actions
To ensure that there are no unexpected failover events while performing work on physical blades (ie adding/removing, enabling/disabling, etc), all blade work should be performed on the Standby chassis, potentially with the Standby device in a Forced Offline state.
