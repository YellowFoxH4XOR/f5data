Fiber Distributed Data Interface (FDDI) support End of Life Announcement.

F5 Networks announces the end of life support for its FDDI cards and software support, effective September 15th, 2001.

This interface, once widely used in data centers, is being replaced by other networking standards. It is no longer a primary networking standard for our customers, and most have already adopted newer standards. F5 Networks is therefore dropping its support for FDDI.

Current FDDI Service Customers:

Current service contracts for systems with FDDI, will be honored by F5 Networks until the end of the contract term. At the end of contract term, the user should contact F5 Networks Support to maintain continuous support. In the event that an FDDI card fails, it will not be replaced with identical hardware. The FDDI card should be returned to manufacturing through the standard process, and F5 Networks will replace these cards with something of equal value and/or functionality. However, this new card may not be compatible with existing FDDI networks.
