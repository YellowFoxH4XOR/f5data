Known Issue

Changes made to deduplication settings are not synchronized to other BIG-IP devices in the device group.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • BIG-IP WOM is provisioned on all BIG-IP devices in the device group.
  • You have made changes to the deduplication settings on one BIG-IP device in the device group.

Impact

Deduplication settings may be out of synchronization across BIG-IP devices in the device group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H426896 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • After changing deduplication settings on one BIG-IP device in the group, the BIG-IP system continues to report the synchronization status as In Sync.
  • Changes you made to the deduplication settings are not synchronized across all BIG-IP devices in the device group.
  • You observe a message similar to the following example in the /var/log/ltm file:

    iSession: Connection error: isession_dedup_handshake_done:941: peer_ctx NULL:18

Note: BIG-IP WebAccelerator and BIG-IP WOM were combined to create BIG-IP AAM in 11.4.0.

Workaround

There is no work around for this issue. However, you can mitigate this issue by manually configuring the same deduplication settings on all BIG-IP devices in the device group.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
