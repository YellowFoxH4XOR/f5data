Topic

What You'll Learn

How to update an Auto-Scaled BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) web application firewall (WAF) in Amazon Web Services (AWS).

Description

If your business on AWS is growing and you need to process more traffic through your BIG-IP VE WAF, you can update your BIG-IP VE WAF in AWS to allow it to process more traffic, without having to re-deploy the system.

You can also find the video here: Updating an Auto-Scaled BIG-IP VE WAF in AWS.
