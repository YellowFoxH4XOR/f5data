Description: 

SOCAT utility present in the connection table of the BIG-IP tenant.

 

Environment:

 

  • F5OS-A (rSeries 5k/10)
  • F5OS C
  • BIG-IP Tenant

 

Cause :

Internal socat connections on ports 10019 and 4097 are expected for control plane communication between the BIG-IP Tenant and F5OS.

 

Recommended Actions:

When BIG-IP Tenant runs on the hardware mentioned in the environment section above. TCP connections established on port 10019 and 4097 using the “socat” utility can be observed on BIG-IP tenant.  

Those connections are used by TMM on the BIG-IP tenant to communicate with tcam-mgr and dagd processes running in containers on F5OS.

For the communication, It uses IP subnet 100.69.x.0/24. Where 100.69.x.1/24 IP is assigned to BIG-IP Tenant interface and 100.69.x.2/24 is assigned to F5OS interface.
