Description
BIG-IP TACACS+ Accounting logs can be received by TACACS+ RFC Draft Revision 12 compliant Accounting servers, like the Cisco Identity Services Engine (ISE). Each Accounting Server may display the information in a different manner. This describes how to view the Accounting information in the ISE.

Environment

  • Cisco ISE 2.6 Patch 2 or later (due to CSCvp37238)
  • BIG-IP with TACACS+ Accounting configured

Cause
Per design, Cisco ISE easily provides access to Per-Command Accounting logs. It does not provide ready access to Per-Session Accounting logs. The BIG-IP utilizes Per-Session Accounting.

Recommended Actions

To view BIG-IP Per-Session Accounting logs on Cisco ISE 2.6 patch 2+ do the following:
  1. Log into ISE GUI
  2. Go to Reports -> Device Administration -> TACACS Accounting
  3. Locate an entry of interest
  4. Click on the Details icon on the entry to popup more information
  5. Scroll down to the Other Attributes section
  6. Review the section called "AVPair" for all of the BIG-IP Accounting entry Attribute/Value pairs

Additional Information
Please contact Cisco Support if there are additional questions on Per-Session Accounting message display within the ISE GUI.
