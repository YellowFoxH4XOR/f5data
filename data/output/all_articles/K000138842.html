Environment

Relevant environmental factors specific to the topic

  • F5OS-A 
  • rSeries hardware
  • You recently replaced or inserted a power supply 

Cause

An unsupported or incompatible PSU component is installed in the PSU 2 slot. This occurs when AC power supply units from different platform models (such as iSeries and rSeries) are interchanged, or when PSUs designed for one rSeries model are used in another model..  

Recommended Actions

Replace the unsupported PSU in slot 2 with a compatible PSU component designed for your specific rSeries platform model.

Additional Information

Identify the unsupported PSU by reviewing the Platform Event Log (PEL) or by navigating to the UI Dashboard > System Settings > General section, and verify whether the component part number is supported for the hardware in question. For more information, please refer to:

  • K36588206: AC power supply units for iSeries and rSeries platform models should not be interchanged 
  • K96511422: F5 iSeries and rSeries power supplies
