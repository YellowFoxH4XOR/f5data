Known Issue

When using SNMP to poll a FirePass controller, you may receive the following error:

noSuchInstance error on this specific OID

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • The FirePass controller is configured with SNMP.
  • You are polling SNMP for the OID 1.3.6.1.4.1.3375.1.10.200.2.0 Failover Status.

Impact

You are unable to determine or log the FirePass failover status by way of SNMP.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The following error message is logged by the SNMP client

    noSuchInstance error on this specific OID
