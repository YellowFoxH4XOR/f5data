K11549: The BIG-IP WebAccelerator incorrectly parses the 'Pragma: no-cache' directive and appends an extra colon

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP WebAccelerator incorrectly parses the Pragma: no-cache directive received from OWS and appends an extra colon. This may cause some clients or proxies to incorrectly parse the Pragma: no-cache directive and cache the content.

For example:

HTTP 200 OK
Connection: close
Date: Wed, 09 Dec 2009 00:33:16 GMT
Pragma: no-cache: <-- Extra colon appended to Pragma: no-cache directive
Accept-Encoding: gzip
ETag: "pv60d0f322665a4dfae0fa2c3f8680623b"
Vary: Accept-Encoding
Content-Length: 7
Client-Date: Wed, 09 Dec 2009 00:25:07 GMT
Client-Peer: 10.0.0.11:80
Client-Response-Num: 1
X-PvInfo: [S10201.C17205.A188537.RA0.G2F0F7.UF831D2C].[OT/html.OG/pages]

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR133764 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 10.2.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP WebAccelerator release notes.

Additionally, this issue has been fixed in BIGIP-10.1.0-3372.0-HF1 for BIG-IP 10.1.0. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
