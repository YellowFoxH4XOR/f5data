Description

When do I received a DDoS attack notification? How do I receive a DDoS attack notification? What is F5's DDoS notification policy? 

Environment

  • F5 Routed DDoS

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

F5 only sends DDoS attack notifications when the SOC receives a high severity alert and confirms it to be a true attack. The SOC then follows the Real Time Incident Procedure (as documented by your team) to notify and start mitigation actions within 15 minutes for the high severity alert. 

Additional Information

In the event a true attack is missed or there's notification for legitimate traffic, we will work with you to ensure we improve our alerting thresholds accordingly and document to ensure the security posture always improves.
