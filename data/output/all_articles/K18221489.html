Known Issue

The BIG-IP ASM Policy Builder may not honor large Allowed URLs.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have set the Policy Builder configuration for URLs Explicit Entities Learning to Learn-ALL URLs.
  • You configure wildcard Allowed URLs.
  • The BIG-IP ASM system processes URLs larger than 24 bytes that match the Allowed URLs.

Impact

The BIG-IP ASM Policy Builder does not honor large Allowed URLs.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP ASM Policy Builder may not honor Allowed URLs that are larger than 24 bytes.
