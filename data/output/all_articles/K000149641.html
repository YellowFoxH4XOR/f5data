K000149641: Display Allowed URL's in ASM policy by RESTAPI

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-02-08
Updated
2025-02-08
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP ASM: 17.1.1
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000149641

Description

By RESTAPI, display "Allowed URL's" from the ASM policy.

Environment

  • ASM policy
  • RESTAPI

 

Cause

NA

 

Recommended Actions

1)_ Gain access to the BIGIP CLI

 

2)_ Obtain the ASM policy id.

 

config # curl -ksu admin:admin https://localhost/mgmt/tm/asm/policies?\$select=name,id | jq -M . | egrep '"name":|"id":' | head -n 6

           "name": "test",

     "id": "HahpT7vWZCFRMuuPWqX3Bw"

 

3)_ With the policy id, we can define the URL to get the "Allowed URLs". There are additional properties but we've selected only protocol, name, type, and id.

 

config # curl -ksu admin:admin 'https://localhost/mgmt/tm/asm/policies/HahpT7vWZCFRMuuPWqX3Bw/urls?%24select=protocol%2Cname%2Ctype%2Cid' | jq .
