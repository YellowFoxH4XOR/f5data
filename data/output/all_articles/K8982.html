Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The big3d process generates false SSL error messages during certificate reloads.

These errors are not cleared immediately and may show up in other system calls made to SSL. As a result, the big3d process may erroneously close connections and cause rsync failures and connection instability.

Note: These errors are internal and are not visible in any log files.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR100952 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.4.6. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM release notes.

This issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.5-HF2, issued for BIG-IP 9.4.5. You may download this hotfix or a later version of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
