Known Issue

The DNAT utility may report a period of invalid mappings following a system or Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) restart.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • Before TMM has completely started, messages are prematurely logged to the log files that are parsed for Deterministic NAT (DNAT) state information.

Impact

Messages that were prematurely logged may be misinterpreted as invalid mappings.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The logs that are parsed for DNAT state information may contain messages that were prematurely logged before TMM started completely.
  • Messages that were prematurely logged may be misinterpreted as invalid mappings.
