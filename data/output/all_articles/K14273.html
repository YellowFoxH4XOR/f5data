Known Issue

The ARX 2000 and ARX 4000 platforms may record an incorrect battery temperature and send spurious nvramBatteryFail traps.

Impact

The ARX system will stop charging the NVRAM battery. If the NVRAM battery is not charged, and the device loses power or is shut down, the NVRAM transactions may be lost.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • The ARX system traplog may include a message that appears similar to the following example:

nvramBatteryFail (419) : Nvram battery :: fault/temperature

  • The ARX system syslog may include a message that appears similar to the following example:

kwaiBatteryMonitor: Bad KWAI battery temperature reading: temp=158 C.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can reload the ARX system. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Reloading an active switch may cause a service interruption.

  1. Log in to the ARX CLI.
  2. Enable privileged mode by typing the following command:

    enable
  3. Reload the ARX system by typing the following command:

    reload
