In this video, AskF5 shows you how to remove BIG-IP client components from your Windows client system.

BIG-IP Edge Client downloads components to end-user computers at initial logon and whenever a feature or favorite is started. The downloaded client components enable the various features of BIG-IP Edge Client functionality. You may remove these components from Windows systems by performing the steps demonstrated in this video.

Note: You must have administrator privileges on the Windows client system to remove the components.
