Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Certain BIG-IP platforms may not include the NAS-IP-Address attribute in RADIUS Access-Request packets when under significant load. As a result, the RADIUS server may reject the request even if the supplied RADIUS credentials are valid.

This issue affects BIG-IP platforms with multicore processors; specifically, the 1600, 3600, 3900, 6400, 6800, 6900, 8400, 8800, 8900, 8950, 11050, and VIPRION platforms.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as ID 225264 (Formerly CR138923).
