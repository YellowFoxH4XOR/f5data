Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • Run top and zombie process is seen

Cause

Processes in the BIG-IP will launch a child process to do a specific task and when the task has ended this child process will be removed from the process table.

In very rare occasion these processes will still stay in the process table even after they have completed execution. These are referred to as zombie process.

Recommended Actions

Zombie processes will not have any negative impact on the BIG-IP as it does not doing any process and does not take any resources from the BIG-IP.

Additional Information

To view what are the zombie process(es), run the following command.
ps aux | grep Z
