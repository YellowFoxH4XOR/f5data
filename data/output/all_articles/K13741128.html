Known Issue

Egress traffic may be distributed unevenly across trunked interfaces.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system runs on one of the following platforms:

    • 10000s (D113)
    • 10050s (D113)
    • 10055s (D113)
    • 10150s NEBS (D112)
    • 10200v (D113)
    • 10200v FIPS (D113)
    • 10200v SSL (D113)
    • 10250v (D113)
    • 10255v (D113)
    • 10350v (D112)
    • 10350v FIPS (D112)
    • 10350v NEBS (D112)
    • 12250v (D111)
    • VIPRION B2250 Blade (A112)
    • VIPRION B4300 Blade (A108)
  • You configure a network trunk containing an uneven number of member interfaces.

Impact

Egress traffic is not evenly distributed among the network interfaces of the trunk.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When viewing the trunk's interface statistics, you notice that an interface is processing a greater percentage of egress traffic.
  • When the trunk is modified to contain an even number of interfaces, egress traffic is distributed evenly across the interfaces.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 507554 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. Workaround

To work around this issue, you can configure the trunk to contain an even number of interface members.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
