K000138644: Pool member shows UP on one device, but DOWN on the other device of the HA pair

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-02-17
Updated
2024-02-17
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 15.1.9, 16.1.4, 17.1.0, 17.1.1, 15.1.10
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000138644

Description

Under certain circumstances, a pool member's status is not updated correctly on only 1 device of a HA pair.

For example, the following sequence of events may trigger this issue:

  • Pool member status is DOWN
  • Pool member is forced offline
  • Pool member is released from forced offline
  • Incremental configsync occurs
  • Pool member is detected as UP on one device, but remains DOWN on the other device.

 

Environment

  • BIG-IP version v15.1.9 or later, v16.1.4 or later, v17.1.0 or later
  • pool member forced offline
  • pool member released from forced offline
  • incremental configsync occurs

Cause

Bug ID 1297257

Recommended Actions

Follow the workaround of Bug ID 1297257 .
