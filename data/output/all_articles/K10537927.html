Topic

Due to new legal documentation requirements instituted by Apple, new updates to F5 Access for iOS on the French App Store are delayed.

Description

Concerns about online privacy, security, and personal data protection have recently been highlighted as serious issues for many companies. Mobile apps and their publishers are central to these issues, and governments are using different regulatory approaches depending on location. F5 publishes VPN connectivity apps to both the Android and Apple app stores in all countries. To comply with regulations that vary by country, Apple has instituted different rules for different locations.

Recently, Apple has instituted new legal documentation requirements for apps published or updated on the French App Store. F5 is currently working with Apple to comply with these new documentation requirements. In the meantime, updates to F5 Access for iOS on the French App Store are delayed. No other countries are impacted at this time, so updates will continue normally.

For more information, please contact F5 Technical Support.
