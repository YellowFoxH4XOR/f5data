Description

When attempting to log into Central Manager via the API, you get an error: 
{
 "status": 401,
 "message": "SYSTEM-00109: Your account is temporarily locked."
}
Attempting to log into the GUI returns an error "Login failed".

Environment

BIG-IP Next Central Manager

Cause

The number of failed login attempts exceed 5 failed logins in the last 10 minutes. 

Recommended Actions

Wait ten minutes and then log in using the correct Central Manager password.

Or, if time is of the essence and you cannot wait for ten minutes, you can click the "Forgot Password?" link at the bottom of the login screen

You will be prompted to enter the username of the account that this instance is registered to, and an email will be sent to the email address of that account.
