What Happened?

  • To route customer traffic through F5 using routed services, customers are expected to route their traffic through F5 XC.
  • After customers have configured their BGP and started advertising their routes through us, the customer traffic is expected to go through XC.
  • Issue: Even after BGP advertisements are configured the routes are still leaked through others carriers which incase lets traffic to reach customer end without letting traffic traverse through F5.

Environment

  • XC DDoS
  • Routed

 

Resolution/Answer

A route leak is the propagation of routing announcement(s) beyond their intended scope.

In the context of XC:

  • If you intend to route traffic to XC for a period of time but some traffic is still arriving directly from the Internet, this is indicative of a route leak.
  • In the event of an attack, if a route leak occurs, it is possible that malicious traffic will still reach your protected network.
  • The F5 XC SOC can work with you to resolve route leaks. This is typically done during onboarding and testing so that when an attack does happen, route leaks will not be a problem.
  • Route leaks can be identified with looking glass aggregates among other tools

Cause

  • Carriers advertising routes with a BGP community of higher priority.
  • Edge routers taking time to update the routes.
  • Incorrect BGP advertisements.
