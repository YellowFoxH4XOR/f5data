K4045: Configuring BIG-IP to verify the validity of TCP headers for incoming connections

Topic

BIG-IP 4.5.11 and 4.6.3 introduce enhanced functionality for verifying the validity of TCP headers. BIG-IP will now drop packets that have invalid TCP headers. This prevents unintended results when reading invalid headers.

You can view statistics for packets that were dropped due to invalid TCP headers by typing of the following command:

sysctl -n net.inet.tcp.stats

In addition, BIG-IP sends warning messages to syslog once every ten seconds when it drops packets due to invalid TCP headers.
