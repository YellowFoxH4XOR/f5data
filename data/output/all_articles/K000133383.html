Description

The issue can be observed through log entries showing that the session deletion process takes longer than the configured time:


Nov 10 17:22:36 xxxx notice tmm[26891]:/Common/Name:Common:ssid1: Session will be deleted in 15 secs due to user logout request.

Nov 10 17:22:55 xxxx notice tmm2[26891]:/Common/Name:Common:ssid1: Session deleted due to user logout request.


Nov 10 17:24:29 xxxx notice tmm3[28759]:/Common/Name:Common:ssid2: Session will be deleted in 60 secs due to user logout request.

Nov 10 17:25:36 xxxx notice tmm1[28759]:/Common/Name:Common:ssid2: Session deleted due to user logout request.




Environment

F5 APM

Portal with a Logout URI

Cause

The observed behavior is caused by an internal mechanism that deletes sessions in the F5 APM system, which results in a delay between the expected deletion and the actual deletion and log of it. This delay affects all session deletions, not just those associated with the logout URI timeout. The specific behavior can be traced as Bug ID 536197.

Recommended Actions

As this behavior is functioning as designed, there are no required actions to resolve the issue. However, you can consider adjusting their logout process to accommodate this delay, such as waiting a few extra seconds before refreshing or navigating to a new page after logging out. Alternatively, users may remove the logout delay completely to reduce the total time for logout, or make it a few seconds shorter than they intend for it to be.
