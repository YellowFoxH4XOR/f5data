Description

After upgrading BIG-IP SSL Orchestrator, the SSL Orchestrator Configuration page may fail to load, instead displaying an error similar to:

Error retrieving template list!

Could not find template for: f5-ssl-orchestrator-gc.

 

Additionally, you may observe disk space errors in the liveinstall or ltm logs after the upgrade, similar to:

 

Terminal error: Disk full (volume group)

 

Environment

BIG-IP SSL Orchestrator

BIG-IP software upgrade

The appdata volume encountered a disk-full condition following the upgrade.

Cause

Insufficient space in the appdata volume may cause the SSL Orchestrator guided-configuration data application/template, including f5-ssl-orchestrator-gc, to become unavailable after the upgrade.

As a result, the SSL Orchestrator module could not complete REST endpoint registration and the Configuration page will return the template-list error.

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

Follow the recommended actions in the K91895955 and K74200262 to increase disk space on the Big-IP, then attempt the upgrade once again.

Additional Information

K91895955: Unable to upgrade with error ''failed (Disk full (volume group). See SOL#10636)''

K74200262: Reducing the size of the appdata volume with the lvreduce utility
