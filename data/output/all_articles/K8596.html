Topic

F5 has recently discovered and corrected a number of critical issues that affect customers running BIG-IP 9.4.4. F5 recommends that all customers currently running 9.4.4 install Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.4-HF1, or a later version of the cumulative hotfix rollup, as soon as possible.

The following CRs are fixed in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.4-HF1 for BIG-IP 9.4.4:

CR87358K8106OpenSSL SSL_get_shared_ciphers vulnerability CVE-2007-5135
CR92397K7837Overview of FIPS 140-2 EAL Level 2 and 3, RoHS Certification Status
