Known Issue

On rare conditions, the scriptd process may indicate that it is running on a secondary blade, even when the process is running on a primary blade.

Impact

Scripts running under the control of scriptd may not run.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • In the /var/log/ltm log file, you may observe an error message similar to the following example:

    debug scriptd[6876]: 014f000f:7: Becoming secondary cluster member

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can restart the scriptd process. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) as an administrative user.
  2. Type the following command:

    restart sys service scriptd
