Known Issue

Monitor traffic may fail after you install a hotfix or upgrade the system.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have installed one of the following hotfixes or product releases:
     
    • BIG-IP 11.2.0 HF3 through HF7
    • BIG-IP 11.2.1 HF3 through HF5
    • BIG-IP 11.3.0 through HF4
  • You are using a monitor that uses one of the following TCP ports:
     
    • 1028
    • 6123-6128
    • 6698
    • 6699
    • 9090
    • 9781
    • 10001

Impact

The BIG-IP system marks down monitored resources, making them unavailable for handling traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • F5 iHealth lists Heuristic H409679 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High page.
  • The BIG-IP system unexpectedly marks down nodes.
  • The tcpdump captures between the BIG-IP system and the monitored node show no outgoing monitor traffic.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can change the TCP port being monitored to a port unaffected by this issue. Alternatively, contact F5 Support .
