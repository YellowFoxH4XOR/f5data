Known Issue

Upgrading to BIG-IP 11.5.0 may fail to properly set the HA group failover method references in traffic groups. 

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You upgrade from BIG-IP 11.x to BIG-IP 11.5.0.
  • You use the HA group failover method.

Impact

The HA group failover method will not function after upgrade.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP hosts in a device group no longer initiate failover when the health score falls below the defined levels
  • The HA group reference is not set in the traffic group.

Workaround

To work around this issue, after upgrading to BIG-IP 11.5.0, manually associate the existing HA group with a traffic group. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP Configuration Utility as the administrative user.
  2. Navigate to Device Management > Traffic Groups.
  3. Select the traffic group.
  4. Change the failover method to HA Group.
  5. From the HA Group menu, select the HA group you would like associated with this traffic group.
  6. Click Update.
