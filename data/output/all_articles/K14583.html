Known Issue

The Border Gateway Protocol (BGP) address family configuration is not retained for peer group members after you restart the tmrouted process.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You configure the BGP peer group.
  • You add the BGP neighbor to the peer group.
  • You apply the address family parameters to a BGP neighbor that is a member of a peer group.
  • You save the BGP configuration and restart the tmrouted process.

Impact

The BGP address family settings are not retained after you perform a tmrouted restart, which can leave the routing configuration in an unexpected state.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H426077 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The address family specific configuration is not retained after a tmrouted restart.
  • You observe unexpected routing.

Workaround

There is no work around for this issue. However, you may be able to mitigate this issue by configuring address family specific parameters for BGP neighbors outside the context of a peer group.

Impact of workaround: You are unable to configure address family specific parameters for BGP neighbors that are members of a BGP peer group.
