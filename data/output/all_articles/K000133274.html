K000133274: MyF5 error during login - Account has not been activated

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-03-31
Updated
2025-08-30
Applies to
  • Support Portal
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000133274

Description

When attempting to sign in to the MyF5 portal, receive the message: 'Account has not been activated. Please check your email to finish activating your account.' However, the account activation email not received. 



Environment

  • MyF5
  • First time login
  • Activate account

Cause

The activation email may have been intercepted by the recipient's email filter or blocked by their IT department. 

Recommended Actions

  1. Please check your Inbox, Junk, and Spam folders for any emails from noreply@mail.account.f5.com
  2. If you locate the email, click the link provided to activate your MyF5 account.
  3. If the email is not found, reach out to your IT department to ensure that emails from noreply@mail.account.f5.com are not being blocked by security settings.
  4. If the issue continues, contact F5 Support or email supportone@f5.com for further assistance.
