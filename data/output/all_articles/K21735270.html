Description

Device trust establishes trust relationships between BIG-IP devices through certificate-based authentication. Each device generates a device ID key and SSL certificate upon upgrade or installation. A trust domain is a collection of BIG-IP devices that trust each other and can synchronize and fail over their BIG-IP configuration data, as well as regularly exchange status and failover messages. The dtdi.crt file is the identity certificate that a device uses to validate its identity with another device.  The dtca.crt file is the CA root certificate for the trust network.

If the CA root certificate dtca.crt for the trust network has expired, you may find these symptoms below.

  • The log var/log/ltm on both BIG-IPs may show "certificate rejected, error 10: certificate has expired"

Jan 1 18:00:23 F5_device info tmm[13310]: CMI peer 192.168.0.1 certificate rejected, error 10: certificate has expired

  • The expiration-string of list /cm all-properties may show the expiration date of dtca.crt.
    # tmsh list cm cert dtca.crt | grep expiration-string    
    expiration-string "Oct  4 13:48:27 2021 GMT"

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • HA
  • Device Trust CA Certificate

Cause
The CA root certificate for the trust domain has expired.

Expired device trust certificates will not impact ConfigSync, but will generate warning messages and will prevent adding new devices into the trust domain.

Recommended Actions
You may reset the device trust and re-add a device to the trust domain, in order to renew the Device Trust Certificates.

Impact of procedure: F5 recommends that you perform this procedure during a maintenance window. This procedure causes the current device to lose connectivity with all other BIG-IP devices. Depending on the device group and traffic group configuration, the connectivity loss may result in an unintentional Active-Active condition that causes a traffic disruption.

You may find more details in the part of "Resetting the device trust and re-adding a device to the trust domain" in K13946: Troubleshooting ConfigSync and device service clustering issues.

Additional Information

  • These procedures only work for self-signed device trust certificates. If you are using their own CA-signed certificate for device trust, although extremely rare, you may need to ensure the relevant certificates are updated/loaded on to the device.
