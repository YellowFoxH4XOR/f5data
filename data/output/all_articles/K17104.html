Known Issue

BIG-IP APM configuration snapshots may be deleted after a failover event.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The active BIG-IP APM unit in a high-availability system fails over, causing the standby unit to transition to active.
  • Due to the length of the detection interval, the BIG-IP APM system fails to detect and restore missing configuration snapshots after the failover event.

Impact

Users are unable to log in after the standby BIG-IP APM unit becomes active because the configuration snapshot for the access profile is missing.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Users are unable to log in to the BIG-IP APM system following a failover event.
  • Running the configdump -allkeys command produces no output, or is missing configuration data.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can restart the apd process on the newly established active unit following a failover event. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP command line.
  2. Type the following command:

    tmsh restart /sys service apd
