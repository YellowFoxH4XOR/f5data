Topic

What You'll Learn

Use a BIG-IP local traffic policy to prevent a spoof of an x-forwarded-for request. You can associate a BIG-IP local traffic policy to prevent a spoof of an x-forwarded-for request, where bad actors might attempt to thwart security by falsifying the IP address in a header and pass it through the BIG-IP system.
