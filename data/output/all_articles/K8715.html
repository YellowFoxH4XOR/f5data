Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. An incorrectly terminated RTSP connection may cause TMM to crash when using an RTSP filter.

The RTSP filter is used by the RTSP profile and within iRules for RTSP handling.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR90301 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.4.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
