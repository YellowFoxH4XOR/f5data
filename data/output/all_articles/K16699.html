Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may produce a core file and restart during failover due to invalid memory access in IPsec components.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system has an IPsec tunnel enabled.
  • The system is a member of a high-availability pair.
  • The system transitions from standby to active.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restarting, and systems configured as part of a high availability (HA) group may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • If configured for HA, the BIG-IP system fails over to the standby system.
  • The BIG-IP system generates TMM core files to the /var/core directory, which include a time stamp that correlates with the traffic interruption.
