Description

Will the IP assigned to my tenant be changed?

 

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud (SaaS)

Cause

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

No, The IP assigned to your tenant is reserved for you and will not be changed without your request. But we still recommend using CNAME instead of A record.
