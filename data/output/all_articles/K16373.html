Known Issue

Certain BIG-IP platforms may experience switch buffer saturation.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • You are using one of the following BIG-IP platforms:
    • PB200
    • 11000
    • 11050
    • B2100
    • 5000
    • 7000
  • You have configured the system to send traffic over a low-bandwidth link at a higher rate than the destination device can support.

For example, this issue may occur if you have configured the BIG-IP high-speed logging mechanism (HSL) to send logging data over a low-bandwidth link at a higher rate than the logging server can support. Under these conditions, the BIG-IP system buffers the egress traffic on the low-bandwidth link. This behavior may saturate the switch chip buffer and degrade egress traffic for other higher-bandwidth links.

Impact

The BIG-IP system experiences a head-of-line blocking (HOL) condition and may fail to send packets.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP system fails to send packets.
  • If BIG-IP systems are configured in a Sync-Failover device group and using a high availability (HA) group, the HA group score decrements and the systems experience a failover.
  • The statistics for the interfaces between the switch chip and High-Speed Bridge (HSB) indicate a high number of packet drops.
