K60640453: Enforcing attack signatures with iControl

Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K60640453

Description

This article explains how to remove staging from attack signatures using iControl.

One example of doing this is when ASM policies are currently configured to put new signatures into staging status for a period of time after the update is downloaded from F5 and you would like to automate moving them to "enforced" status once they reach the "ready for enforcement" state.

Environment

  • ASM
  • Staging attack signatures

Cause
This is functioning as design as explained in K04853200: How updated ASM attack signatures are enforced.

Recommended Actions

1) Retrieve the policies existent in the equipment (Policy ID, name and enforcement mode)
curl https://10.1.1.1/mgmt/tm/asm/policies/?\$select=id,versionPolicyName,enforcementMode -u admin:admin -k|jq .
 Note: please replace 10.1.1.1 with the actual management IP address from the device.

3) Patch the policy to enforce all signatures ready to be enforced:
curl -X PATCH https://10.1.1.1/mgmt/tm/asm/policies/DvOhBMleI58C8Wr3_ylCcA/signatures?\$select=&\$filter=hasSuggestions+eq+false+AND+wasUpdatedWithinEnforcementReadinessPeriod+eq+false+and+performStaging+eq+true -u admin:admin -k -v -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"performStaging":false}' | jq .

4) Apply the policy after the change
curl -X POST https://10.1.1.1/mgmt/tm/asm/tasks/apply-policy -u admin:admin -k -v -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"policyReference": {"link":https://10.1.1.1/mgmt/tm/asm/policies/DvOhBMleI58C8Wr3_ylCcA}}' | jq .
