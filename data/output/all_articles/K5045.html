Known Issue

This may be the result of a known issue. When a unit in a redundant pair becomes active, it updates the time stamp on all mirrored data to ensure that it does not prematurely reap connections. Once the time stamp is updated, the active unit attempts to mirror the data back to the standby BIG-IP. If the standby BIG-IP is not available to receive the mirroring data, the active BIG-IP may panic.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR49457 and CR49458 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 4.5.13 for the 4.5 software branch. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP Release Notes.

This issue still exists in the 4.6 software branch.
