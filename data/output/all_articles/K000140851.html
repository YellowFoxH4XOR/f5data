K000140851: BIG-IP gives the target IP instead of its self IP address when performing trace route

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-08-27
Updated
2026-07-17
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 15.1.10
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000140851

Description

Trace route shows the target IP instead of the self IP, when AFM proxy ARP is enabled in the NAT policy.

  • The trace route output will be similar to:

 1 192.168.10.254 0 msec 1 msec 1 msec

 2 192.168.20.254 4 msec 2 msec 2 msec

 3 192.168.30.254 4 msec 3 msec 2 msec

 4 dns.google (8.8.8.8) 4 msec 2 msec 3 msec <--- BIG-IP.

 5 192.168.40.254 5 msec 4 msec 3 msec

 6 192.168.50.254 5 msec 4 msec 4 msec

 7 192.168.60.254 5 msec

 8 * * * 

 9 * * * 

 10 dns.google (8.8.8.8) 5 msec 4 msec 4 msec

Environment

  • AFM NAT policy is used.
  • Proxy ARP is enabled in the NAT policy.

Cause

This is a by design behavior.

When the proxy ARP is enabled, the AFM uses the target IP (8.8.8.8, in the above example) as the source IP when sending the ICMP TTL exceeded message out, hence this causes the target IP to be displayed in the trace route hops, instead of the BIG-IP's self IP.

Recommended Actions

Disable the proxy ARP function from the AFM policy.

Additional Information

None
