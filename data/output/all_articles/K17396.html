Known Issue

The snmpd process may restart and produce a core file.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP system is configured for SNMP polling.
  • The BIG-IP system is under heavy load and experiencing high CPU utilization.
  • The snmpd process times out due to slow responses to queries.

Impact

SNMP queries fail while the snmpd process restarts.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • SNMP queries sent to the BIG-IP system fail.
  • An snmpd core file is logged in the /shared/core directory.
  • The snmpd process restarts.
