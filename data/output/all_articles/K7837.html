Topic

In January 2003, the European Union adopted the RoHS (Restriction of Hazardous Substances) Directive. This directive, enacted in the EU on July 1, 2006, forbids the sale of all electrical and electronic equipment containing lead, cadmium, mercury, hexavalent chromium, polybrominated biphenyls, and polybrominated diphenyl ether in the EU after July 1, 2006, subject to certain limited exemptions.

F5 changed its manufacturing practices to ensure compliance by this deadline. This compliance effort resulted in a FIPS hardware card redesign in order to meet the RoHS standard.

The Cavium RoHS FIPS protected Hardware Security Module (HSM) used in the following  platforms has achieved FIPS 140-2 certification:

  • BIG-IP 6400, 6800, 6900, 7200, 8400, 8900, 10200, 11000, and 11050
  • BIG-IP 5250v-FIPS, i5820v-FIPS, 7200v-FIPS, i7820v-FIPS, 10200v-FIPS, and 10350v-FIPS

To view the details of the certification, search for Cavium Networks at the NIST website:

http://csrc.nist.gov/groups/STM/cmvp/documents/140-1/140val-all.htm
