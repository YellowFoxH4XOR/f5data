Description

You may request to reopen a closed F5 Support case if the same issue addressed in the original case reoccurs again within 14 days.
Important: If your case has been closed for more than 14 days, you will need to open a new support case. For additional details, refer to K2633: Instructions for submitting a support case to F5.

Environment

  • F5 Support case
  • MyF5

Cause

The initial issue/question remains unresolved.

 

Recommended Actions

Steps to Contact F5 Support:

1. Via Chat
Log in to MyF5 with your credentials. Look for the speech bubble icon labeled "AskF5" in the lower-right corner of the browser window. Click on this icon to start a chat with support.

2. Via Phone
Call the phone numbers provided on the F5 Support page.

3. Via Email
Send an email to supportone@f5.com.

None.
