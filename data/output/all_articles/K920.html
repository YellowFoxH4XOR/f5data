Topic

Internal variables are available to implement experimental changes to a system. You can modify internal variables using the bigpipe internal command.

Internal variables are typically introduced in patches, so that you can enable fixes for problems that have not yet been fully tested or documented. These internal variables can continue to exist for several versions while F5 Networks evaluates their stability.

Do not change an internal variable unless you are aware of, and accept, the following risks:

  • The changes that you implement on a system using internal variables are not fully tested and may not be stable.
  • Internal variables are not supported by the general F5 Networks Technical Support group or by F5 partners. Any problems that you incur when using internal variables must be escalated to a separate group of engineers. This may cause a delay in resolving the issue.
  • F5 Networks can change or remove internal variables in future releases of F5 products without regard to F5's End-of-Life policy.

    Note: For more information about F5's End-of-Life policy, refer to K3225: End of Life: F5 Networks End of Life Policy.
  • Internal variables are not saved; therefore, they will not be retained once you reboot or reload a configuration.

    Note: For information about saving internal variables, refer to one of the following version-specific Solutions:

    For versions 4.0 - 4.5.10 and 4.6 - 4.6.1, refer to     K2653: Configuring BIG-IP or 3-DNS to retain internal variables.

    For versions 4.5.11 - 4.5.14 and 4.6.2 - 4.6.4, refer to     K4430: Configuring BIG-IP or 3-DNS to retain internal variables.
  • Internal variables will not synchronize to another unit. You must change internal variables for each unit in a redundant pair.
