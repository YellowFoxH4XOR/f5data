Policy Information

With the availability of the iSeries dual-SSD, FIPS, and Turbo SSL models, F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of the BIG-IP 7200-SSL, 7055s, 7255v, 10200-SSL, 10055s, 10255v, 10350v, and 12250v appliances effective August 1, 2019.

Note: The BIG-IP 5250v-F, 7200v-F, 10200v-F, 10350v-F, 10150s-N, and 10350v-N platforms are not included in this EoS announcement.

Transition table

The new F5 platforms provide superior performance and functionality. The recommended replacements for the EoS devices are listed in the following tables.
