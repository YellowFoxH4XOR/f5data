K000135940: Cannot login with root after tenant initial deployment

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-11-26
Updated
2024-11-26
Applies to
  • F5OS
  • F5OS-A
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000135940

Description

Customer may find they cannot login the root with default password after tenant initial deployment

Environment

  • F5OS
  • BIG-IP
  • AUTH

Cause

Appliance mode enabled during the initial tenant configure.
