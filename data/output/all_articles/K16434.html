Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) process may restart and produce a core file. This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • All of the virtual servers in your system are assigned a BIG-IP Analytics profile and a DoS profile.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic, and may fail over if configured as part of a high-availability system.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H516939 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High screen.
  • The TMM process dumps core with a SIGSEGV signal.
  • The BIG-IP system generates a TMM core file to the /shared/core directory.
  • The BIG-IP system fails over to the standby system if configured as part of a high-availability system.
