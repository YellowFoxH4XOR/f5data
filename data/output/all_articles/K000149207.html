K000149207: F5OS-A: Bind mount difference on system_manager container

Support Solution
Published
2025-01-22
Updated
2025-01-22
Applies to
  • F5OS
  • F5OS-A: 1.5.2, 1.5.1, 1.7.X, 1.8.X, 1.7.0, 1.8.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000149207

Description

The bind mount was changed in F5OS-A 1.7.0.

 

Environment

F5OS-A

 

Cause

In F5OS-A 1.7.0 and later, the /var/F5/system on the host is mounted to the /var/F5/system on the container.

 

Prior to F5OS-A 1.7.0.
[root@appliance-1] ~ # docker inspect system_manager | jq .[0].HostConfig.Binds | grep /var/F5/system
"/var/F5/system/shared/tcpdump:/var/F5/system/shared/tcpdump:rw",
"/var/F5/system:/var/F5/partition:z",
"/var/F5/system/shared/tcpdump:/mnt/var/shared/tcpdump:rw",

F5OS-A 1.7.0 and later.
[root@appliance-1] ~ # docker inspect system_manager | jq .[0].HostConfig.Binds | grep /var/F5/system
"/var/F5/system:/var/F5/partition:rw",
"/var/F5/system/shared/tcpdump:/mnt/var/shared/tcpdump:rw",
"/var/F5/system:/var/F5/system:rw",
