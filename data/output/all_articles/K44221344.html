Policy Information

F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of the Anti-Bot Mobile (ABM) SDK, effective December 7, 2021.

The ABM SDK integration with F5 Advanced WAF had been achieved with an F5 technology partner (Appdome) to fuse Advanced WAF bot defense with mobile applications. However, to exceed customer expectations and continuously address the increasingly complex and evolving threat landscape, F5 is upgrading the existing ABM SDK to F5 Bot Defense Mobile SDK.

The upgrade addresses the increased volume and sophistication of attacker tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs). The upgrade has feature parity with the ABM SDK and offers additional capabilities, such as advanced fraud detection. It also enables customers to add new security services from F5, including various current and upcoming integrations for anti-fraud and bot detection and protection.

Technical Support

F5 maintains generous lifecycle policies that allow customers to continue support.

F5 and its technology partner (Appdome) shall continue to support customers who choose to continue using the ABM SDK. However, it is not recommended for customers to continue using the ABM SDK.

Note that customers who choose to continue to use the ABM SDK (and not migrate to the F5 Bot Defense Mobile SDK) and continue using the technology partner (Appdome) for fusion may do so. F5 will continue to provide them with support for critical fixes to Service Requests (SRs) reported for issues found in production only, as determined by F5, up to its End of Technical Support (EoTS) and / or End of Life (EoL) date, either of which will be no earlier than one year from the announced End of Sale date of December 7, 2021 for the ABM SDK.

However, suppose a customer chooses to migrate from ABM SDK to a code-based integration with F5 Bot Defense Mobile SDK and opts out of the technology partner (Appdome) fusion. In that case, they can continue under their existing agreement with F5 based upon their originally purchased license.

Existing ABM SDK customers may request a license renewal for a period of up to one year after the ABM SDK EoS date of December 7, 2021. Customers with active support agreements for ABM SDK that end before the period of 1-year after the above public ABM SDK EoS announcement date may renew their support agreement on a month-to-month basis up until the first anniversary of their renewal, not to exceed the first anniversary of the above ABM SDK EoS date of December 7, 2021.

Suppose an ABM SDK customer has a support agreement that terminates after the one-year support extension date after the publicly announced ABM SDK EoS date of December 7, 2021. In that case, those customers will be offered support credits for the time remaining on their service agreement.

Note: Customers can continue using their already-purchased ABM SDK license up to BIG-IP 16.1.0 (the last version to support ABM SDK).
