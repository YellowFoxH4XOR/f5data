K2084: All FIPS connections fail after the FIPS process is restarted or the FIPS hardware is disconnected and reconnected

Known Issue

When you restart the FIPS server on BIG-IP or disconnect and reconnect the FIPS hardware, the proxyd process does not automatically obtain the new FIPS key. As a result, FIPS connections fail.

F5 Networks Product Development is tracking this issue as CR23748. To check the status of this CR, refer to the most recent BIG-IP Release Notes.

Workaround

To work around this problem, restart proxyd, using the following command.

proxyd
