K6902: The BIG-IP GTM HTTPS health check intermittently marks virtual servers as Down when they are Up

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP GTM HTTPS health check intermittently marks virtual servers Down when they are Up. As a result, the BIG-IP GTM system does not send connections to available virtual servers.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR71749 and CR71750, it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.3 and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM release notes.
