K22483326: Protocols supported by DAG Tunnel

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-04-11
Updated
2023-04-11
Applies to
  • VIPRION Platforms
  • BIG-IP Platforms
  • BIG-IP
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K22483326

Description

DAG Tunnel supports the following protocols:

  • IP-in-IP (any combination of IPv4 and IPv6)
  • EtherIP
  • GRE
  • NVGRE
  • VXLAN
  • IPsec

Environment

  • Hardware BIG-IP or VIPRION platform supporting DAG Tunnel

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

None

Additional Information


DAG Tunnel allows the hardware DAG on certain platforms to use the inner protocol headers to achieve more even distribution of tunnel traffic across TMMs.
