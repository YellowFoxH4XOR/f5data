K32508131: Maximum Supported Password Length

Type
Support Solution
Published
2021-06-21
Updated
2025-02-06
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IP
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K32508131

Description

You would like to confirm the maximum supported password length accepted by the system.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IP

Cause

It's important to review the password policy in place by the administrator. Review the Related Content for articles on security controls.

Recommended Actions

The maximum compatible lengths for various scenarios is listed below.

BIG-IP

AreaEnforcement
Configuration Utility (GUI)up to but not more than 255 characters
REST APIup to but not more than 255 characters
SSHup to but not more than 255 characters

BIG-IQ

AreaEnforcement
Configuration Utility (GUI)up to but not more than 255 characters
REST APIup to but not more than 255 characters
SSHup to but not more than 255 characters
Discover BIG-IP from IQup to but not more than 255 characters

Note: Certain subsystems have their own passphrase / password system, such as hardware-based FIPS which is not covered by this article.
