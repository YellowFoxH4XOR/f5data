Description

Importing configuration from the BIG-IP AFM module into BIG-IQ may fail with the error mentioned in the title of this article:

Failed to copy configuration to working-config; reason: Failed copying from source to target: java.lang.IllegalArgumentException: xx.xx.xx.xx: invalid firewall.  FirewallIPAddress xx.xx.xx.xx/xx cannot be modified once set for self-ip firewalls.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ Configuration Manager 
  • BIG-IP AFM module

Cause

The LTM had a self-ip address x.x.x.x and was imported into BIG-IQ including the LTM and AFM objects.  The self-ip address x.x.x.x was subsequently changed to x.x.x.y.  Re-import of LTM works fine.  AFM import sees an AFM object for a self-ip that has the old address x.x.x.x.

Recommended Actions

  1. On the BIG-IQ, remove the AFM service discovery for the BIG-IP in question and for its peer (if in an HA cluster).
  2. On the BIG-IQ, remove the LTM service discovery for the BIG-IP in question and for its peer (if part of an HA cluster).
  3. Re-discover and re-import the LTM service for the BIG-IP and its peer (if part of an HA cluster).
  4.  Re-discover and re-import the AFM service for the BIG-IP and its peer.
Note The reason that the peer also has to be removed is that the self-ip is a shared object.
