Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The iRule commands in the HTTP namespace (any iRule command prefixed with HTTP::) may be executed from the CACHE_RESPONSE event, but may provide unpredictable results.

In BIG-IP LTM 9.3 and earlier, this issue may cause connections to hang, causing TMM to crash. In BIG-IP LTM 9.4, commands may return either no data, or incomplete data.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR87572.

Workaround

You can work around this issue by avoiding use of the HTTP iRule commands in the CACHE_RESPONSE event.
