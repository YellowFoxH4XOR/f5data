K000139475: Mapping done incorrectly for VE subscription renewal keys

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-05-01
Updated
2025-10-14
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP Cloud Edition
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000139475

Description

You completed the key mapping, but realized you chose the wrong keys.

Environment

  • MyF5 Customer Portal

  • BIG-IP VE Subscription

  • Renewal

Cause

You completed the key mapping, but realized you chose the wrong keys.

Recommended Actions

  1. In the MyF5 portal, go to your subscription
  2. Click "Unassign" on the incorrectly mapped license(s)
  3. Revoke keys from device following this procedure
    • K41458656: Reusing a BIG-IP VE license on a different BIG-IP VE system
  4. Relicense the BIG-IP VE with the correct key
    • K7752: Licensing the BIG-IP system

Additional Information

None
