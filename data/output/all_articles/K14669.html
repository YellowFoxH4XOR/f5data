Known Issue

Renewing an Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) certificate may return an error message.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You attempt to renew an SSL certificate with a Subject Alternative Name (SAN).

Impact

You are unable to renew a certificate with a SAN.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H429318 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • When you attempt to renew a certificate, you observe an error message similar to the following example:

    com.f5.tmui.util.Syscall$CallException: Error: Failure to create certificate new-cert with error: 01020066:3: The requested unknown (/Common/new-cert.crt) already exists in partition Common..

Resolution

Status

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can create a new certificate with a SAN.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
