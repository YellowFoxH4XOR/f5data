Description

You observed following logs on your BIG-IP device:

 

<device_name> crit smartd[5379]: Device: /dev/sda [SAT], <number> Offline uncorrectable sectors

 

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • HDD

Cause

The hard drive on your BIG-IP has some unusable sectors. It's not necessarily indicative of drive failure in the short term.

 

Recommended Actions

If you are not aware of any disk issues and you are concerned, you can open F5 support case.

Before that, please gather following outputs to speed up reviewing process:

 

  • Fresh QKView
  • Logs from /var/log/platform_check
  • You can run a longer test - 'smartctl -t long /dev/sda' - which should identify if there are any other problematic sectors. That can take even 2 hours and it's recommended to do that in maintenance window. Run - 'smartctl -a /dev/sda' - after to review the results.
