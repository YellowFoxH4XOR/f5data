K7942: Weak SSL ciphers cause logins at the Administrative Console to fail

Topic

If you attempt to access the Administrative Console of the FirePass controller, and cannot log in despite entering the correct credentials, you may be using a browser that is configured with SSL ciphers that are weaker than allowed by the FirePass controller's configuration.  

Note: Allowed ciphers are configured in the SSL Cipher Security and SSL Protocol Versions sections on the Device Management: Security: User Access Security page.

This most often occurs when the browser version is out of date, or  the browser is export strength , such as a browser that is required by U.S. export law to use only reduced-strength ciphers.  Consult the documentation from your browser's manufacturer to determine whether you can upgrade to resolve this issue.

Alternatively, your browser may have the capability to use the high-strength ciphers required by the FirePass controller, but the browser has been configured to use only low-strength ciphers.  Consult the documentation from your browser's manufacturer to determine how to configure the needed ciphers.

You are using a browser with a lower-strength cipher than needed if you receive a warning message that appears similar to the following example when you attempt to log in to the standard logon page:

SSL ciphersuite EXP1024-RC4-SHA not permitted. Please see your system admin.
