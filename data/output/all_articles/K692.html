K692: Sync groups allow synchronization across versions

Known Issue

You must remove a 3-DNS Controller from a synchronization group before performing an upgrade. Failure to do so may result in the synchronization of the newer version to the other 3-DNS Controllers in the synchronization group, which can create a variety of problems.

For specific instructions about installing an upgrade and removing a 3-DNS Controller from the synchronization group, refer to the specific 3-DNS Release Notes to which you are upgrading.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR38340 and CR41857.
