K000148374: Device Cert Expiry shows Dec 31 1969 or Jan 1 1970

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-11-03
Updated
2025-02-07
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP GTM
  • BIG-IP LTM
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000148374

Description

Device certificate in Certificate Management -->Device Certificate Management --> Device Certificate shows certificate expiry as Dec 31 1969 or Jan 1 1970 in the GUI.

This might show in DNS --> GSLB --> Server --> Trusted Server Certificates if it is part of an iQuery mesh.

 

Upon verification of the certificate in the CLI, we can see that the expiry has just been set to more than 17 years.

 

[root@test:Active:Standalone] ssl.crt # openssl x509 -noout -text -purpose -in /config/httpd/conf/ssl.crt/server.crt

Certificate:

  Data:

    Version: 3 (0x2)

    Serial Number: 468265328 (0x1be92970)

  Signature Algorithm: sha256WithRSAEncryption

    Issuer: C=SG, ST=SG, L=SG, O=MyCompany, OU=MyOrg, CN=cert.test.com/emailAddress=root@test.com

    Validity

      Not Before: Nov 3 01:42:08 2024 GMT

      Not After : Oct 29 01:42:08 2044 GMT
