K2818: FirePass may be unreachable from certain clients after failover

Topic

FirePass uses gratuitous ARPs to notify its network segment that the MAC address for the virtual IP address of the failover pair has changed from the active system to the standby system. Certain firewalls and routers ignore gratuitous ARPs, which results in the active FirePass system becoming inaccessible from non-local subnets.

To work around this issue, set the ARP timeout at the firewall/router for as long as a failover can be tolerated. This interval will represent the amount of time, after a failover event, in which a client will be unable to contact the FirePass system.
