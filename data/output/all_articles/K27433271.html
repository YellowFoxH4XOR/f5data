Description

The device group "datasync-global-dg" may become "Changes Pending" without any operations.

Environment

  • BIG-IP ASM
  • Configuring HA devices

Cause

It is by design.

Due to internal cleanup process, datasyncd deletes old files, which causes updating CID timestamp of datasync-global-dg.
You will see the similar log below in /var/log/datasync/datasyncd.log.

DATASYNCD_CONF|NOTICE|Oct 01 11:54:09.540|XXXXX|datasyncd_conf.cpp:XXXX|result: result_operation = XXXX obj_delete

 

Recommended Actions

Please run config-sync for datasync-global-dg.
