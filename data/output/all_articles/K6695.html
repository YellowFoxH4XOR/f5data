Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. An FTP Virtual Server configured to mirror connections mirrors only active FTP connections to the peer unit in a redundant pair. PASV (Passive) or EPSV (Extended Passive) FTP connections will not mirror to the peer unit in a redundant pair.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR69705 and CR69739 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.1.3, 9.2.5, 9.3, and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.
