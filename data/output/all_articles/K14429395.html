Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may exit and restart during service provider (SP)-initiated Security Assertion Markup Language (SAML) single logout (SLO).

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system is configured as a SAML SP.
  • The SLO profile is configured for HTTP-redirect binding.

Impact

The BIG-IP APM system temporarily fails to process traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • You experience a traffic disruption or cannot access your BIG-IP APM system.
  • The tmm process generates a core file to the /shared/core directory.
  • In the /var/log/tmm file, you observe a SIGSEGV fault similar to the following example:

    notice ** SIGSEGV **
    <snip>
    notice RIP= 0x2b50a2830028

Workaround

There is no workaround for this issue. However, instead of HTTP-redirect binding, you can configure SLO to use HTTP POST binding.
