Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may fail to establish a tunnel with an IPsec peer.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP system and IPsec peer communicate over a lossy network.
  • The remote IPsec peer prematurely deletes an IPsec Security Association (SA).

When the SA is deleted from the remote IPsec SA peer, and the peer sends an INVALID-Security Parameter Index (SPI) notification to the BIG-IP system, the BIG-IP system may ignore the notification and fail to remove the associated SA. As a result, the IPsec tunnel might not be renegotiated until the SA is manually removed from the BIG-IP system.

Impact

The affected IPsec tunnel may fail to establish.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP system may ignore the INVALID-SPI notification from the peer and fail to remove the associated SA.
  • The affected IPsec tunnel may fail to establish.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can manually delete the SA on the BIG-IP system. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the TMOS Shell (tmsh) by typing the following command:

    tmsh

  2. Display the SAs that have been negotiated with the IPsec peer, and determine the SPI of the affected SA by using the following command syntax:

    show /net ipsec ipsec-sa dst-addr <IPsec_peer_addr>

    Delete the SA by using the following command syntax:

    delete /net ipsec ipsec-sa spi <invalid_spi>

  3. Save the change by typing the following command:

    save /sys config
