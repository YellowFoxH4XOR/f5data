Known Issue

BIG-IP APM portals for Citrix and VMware View may fail to initialize.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • A portal is configured for a Citrix or VMware View resource.
  • The virtual server for the Citrix or VMware View portal is configured with a traffic-group other than traffic-group-1.

Impact

Access to the portal resource fails.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H429390 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High screen.
  • In the /var/log/apm file, you observe error messages similar to the following example:

    err vdi[6497]: 01490000: {5e.C} Remote Desktop '/Common/<portal name>': failed to handle '/f5vdi/citrix/resource': SessionDB:18

    or

    err vdi[6188]: 01490000: {4a.C} View broker: can't get SID from Adp response, error: SessionDB:18

  • Users are unable to access the Citrix or VMware View portal resource.
