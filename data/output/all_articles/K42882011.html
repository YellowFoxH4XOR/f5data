Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) stops responding when the BIG-IP system processes carrier-grade network address translation (CGNAT) traffic.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • A TMM connection flow related to CGNAT traffic expires.

Impact

Traffic is disrupted while TMM restarts.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • TMM stops responding when the BIG-IP system processes CGNAT traffic, resulting in traffic disruption.
