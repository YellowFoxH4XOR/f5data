Description

You may find the following error messages in velos.log on the partition or in platform.log on rSeries 5XXX or higher.

2025-09-04T21:15:51.728761+02:00 100.65.18.1 blade-1(p2) fpgamgr[29]: priority="Err" version=1.0 msgid=0x307000000000029 msg="Remote L2 Entry not found." ENTRY="MAC=64:a0:e7:41:1d:41, NTI=4095, VID=2946, Type=1" BLADE=2.
2025-09-04T21:15:51.728815+02:00 100.65.18.7 blade-7(p2) fpgamgr[14]: priority="Err" version=1.0 msgid=0x307000000000029 msg="Remote L2 Entry not found." ENTRY="MAC=64:a0:e7:41:1d:41, NTI=4095, VID=2946, Type=1" BLADE=2.
2025-09-04T21:15:51.728905+02:00 100.65.18.3 blade-3(p2) fpgamgr[14]: priority="Err" version=1.0 msgid=0x307000000000029 msg="Remote L2 Entry not found." ENTRY="MAC=64:a0:e7:41:1d:41, NTI=4095, VID=2946, Type=1" BLADE=2.
2025-09-04T21:15:51.728873+02:00 100.65.18.8 blade-8(p2) fpgamgr[15]: priority="Err" version=1.0 msgid=0x307000000000029 msg="Remote L2 Entry not found." ENTRY="MAC=64:a0:e7:41:1d:41, NTI=4095, VID=2946, Type=1" BLADE=2.
2025-09-04T21:15:51.728807+02:00 100.65.18.4 blade-4(p2) fpgamgr[14]: priority="Err" version=1.0 msgid=0x307000000000029 msg="Remote L2 Entry not found." ENTRY="MAC=64:a0:e7:41:1d:41, NTI=4095, VID=2946, Type=1" BLADE=2.
2025-09-04T21:15:51.728901+02:00 100.65.18.6 blade-6(p2) fpgamgr[14]: priority="Err" version=1.0 msgid=0x307000000000029 msg="Remote L2 Entry not found." ENTRY="MAC=64:a0:e7:41:1d:41, NTI=4095, VID=2946, Type=1" BLADE=2.
2025-09-04T21:15:51.728872+02:00 100.65.18.5 blade-5(p2) fpgamgr[14]: priority="Err" version=1.0 msgid=0x307000000000029 msg="Remote L2 Entry not found." ENTRY="MAC=64:a0:e7:41:1d:41, NTI=4095, VID=2946, Type=1" BLADE=2.

The above example comes from Velos partition running on blades 1-8.
The message means that blade-2 told the other blades (1,3,4,5,6,7,8) to delete the L2 entry on VLAN 2946, but those blades did not have a copy of the entry in their L2 table. This may also indicate the entry was learned on blade-2, but was never sent to the other blades.

Environment

  • rSeries 5XXX or higher
  • Velos

Cause

  • The F5OS device keeps several copies of the ARP table, some of them in hardware, the rest in software. Occasionally they can get out of sync where we delete an entry from the software table, but one of the hardware tables misses the delete request. These error messages will be logged if there is a mismatch between software and hardware entry.
  • FDB entry (L2 entry) was learned on blade-2, but the entry was not sent to the other blades. When the entry aged out on blade-2, the blades requested other blades to delete the entry, but it was not there.

Recommended Actions

  • If these logs are accompanied by performance issues or an increase in Destination Lookup Failure (DLF), then it requires further investigation of the MAC address being logged.
    Destination Lookup Failure can be investigated according to https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000152328
  • Contact F5 Support for a data collection plan.
