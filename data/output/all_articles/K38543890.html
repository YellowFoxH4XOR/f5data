Description

You are trying to sync BIG-IP and Sync Failed with an error:

load failed due to x5tsha1 mismatch

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM
  • Access Profile
  • SSL Certificate

Cause

  •  A validation error occurred while syncing to a remote device
  •  New access policy is configured with a key/certificate pair that does not exist on peer device 
  •  Peer device throws the below error 

 Sync error on <device hostname> : Load failed from /Common/<name of peer device> 01071c83:3: (/Common/NAME_of_KEY) (/Common/default.crt) load failed due to x5tsha1 mismatch

Recommended Actions

 Export/ import Access Profile
  1. On the BIG-IP with the new access profile navigate to Access  ››  Profiles / Policies : Access Profiles (Per-Session Policies)
  2. Click on the name of the access profile 
  3. Export the new access policy
  4. On per BIG-IP with missing access policy navigate to Access  ››  Profiles / Policies : Access Profiles (Per-Session Policies)
  5. Import the new access policy 
  6. Enter in the filed of 'New Profile Name' the exact access profile name as it exist on peer device
  7. Navigate to Device Management  ››  Overview and manually force sync
OR

 Export/ import SSL certificate
  1. On one of the units, make a backup for the original default.crt and default.key, and copy the .crt and .key from its peer (scp or download and upload with ftp tool).
  2. Then perform 'touch /service/mcpd/forceload' as per the article K13030 and reboot,
  3. After the unit reboots you can verify the Serial Number/Fingerprint of default.crt, they should be the same as its peer.
      /config/ssl/ssl.crt/default.crt
      /config/ssl/ssl.key/default.key
