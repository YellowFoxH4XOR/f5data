Description

Is BIG-IP VE supported on  NodeWeaver KVM.

Environment

  • NodeWeaver KVM Support
  • BIG-IP VE

Cause

F5 does not currently support deployments in NodeWeaver KVM. 

Recommended Actions

As per the BIG-IP VE Supported platforms, the following VE platforms are supported. 

  • VMware ESXi
  • Linux KVM
  • Xen Project
  • OpenStack
  • Microsoft Hyper-V
  • Nutanix are supported
