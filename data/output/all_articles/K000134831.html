K000134831: BIG-IQ statistics collection stops due to an out-of-memory condition in the appiqpostaggregator daemon

Source

Description

“Statistics Overflow Protection Detected” alert appears on BIG-IQ DCDs, and DCD statistics collection has stopped.

Log messages containing “[GC (Allocation Failure)]” are present in the /var/log/appiq/gc_postaggregator.log file on the BIG-IQ CM.

Environment

BIG-IQ 8.x cluster with statistics collection enabled

Cause

This occurs due to an out-of-memory condition in the postaggregator daemon on BIG-IQ CM.

Answer/Recommended Actions

This workaround restarts the postaggregator daemon to recover from the out-of-memory condition.

Impact of workaround: This should not have a negative impact on your system
1. On BIG-IQ CM's CLI:
# bigstart restart appiqpostaggregator
2. Check /var/log/appiq/agentmanager.log on the DCDs and confirm whether the “Statistics Overflow” error continues to appear.
3. If restarting the daemon resolves the issue, apply the workaround for ID913329 to increase the memory allocation for the postaggregator daemon.

Additional Information

If the issue persists, collect new qkview archives and contact F5 Support for further analysis.
