K9253: Users may need to uninstall hotfix prior to installing HF-602-7.2

Type
Knowledge
Published
2008-10-30
Updated
2023-02-21
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • Legacy Products
  • FirePass: 6.0.2
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K9253

FirePass cumulative hotfix HF-602-7.2 can be installed on top of hotfixes prior to HF-602-6. However, if you have already installed cumulative hotfix HF-602-6 or HF-602-7 on your FirePass controller, you must un-install them prior to installing HF-602-7.2 or later. If you installed HF-602-7.1 it will not need to be uninstalled. To uninstall the hotfix, refer to K3430: Installing FirePass hotfixes for instructions.
