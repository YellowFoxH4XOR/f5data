Known Issue

In rare situations, the tmm process may produce a core file in intra-cluster environments.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system runs a clustered configuration.
  • Your system uses a persistence profile.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) restarting, and systems configured as part of a high availability (HA) group may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • If configured for HA, the BIG-IP system fails over to the standby system.
  • The BIG-IP system generates TMM core files to the /var/core directory, with a time stamp that correlates to the traffic interruption.
