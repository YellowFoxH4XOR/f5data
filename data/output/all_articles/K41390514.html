Known Issue

BIG-IP systems configured with asymmetric routing may cause the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) to produce a core file and restart.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) is licensed with a rate limited license.
  • Your BIG-IP system is configured with VLAN-Keyed connections disabled.
  • Your BIG-IP system sends connections processed by a virtual server to a pool where the egress route is on a different Virtual Local Area Network (VLAN) than the ingress route (asymmetric routing).
  • Your BIG-IP system with this configuration processes traffic.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restarting, and systems configured as part of a high availability (HA) group may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP system fails over to the standby system if the system is configured for HA.
  • Your BIG-IP system generates TMM core files to the /var/core directory, with a timestamp that correlates to the traffic interruption.

Workaround

To work around this issue, do not configure the BIG-IP system to process asymmetric traffic.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system; however, the workaround requires that you use symmetric routing.
