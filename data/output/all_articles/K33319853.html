Known Issue

After the system closes the Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) connection and while it is processing a handshake message related to Online Certificate Status Protocol (OCSP), the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) restarts.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The SSL connection is closed.
  • SSL receives the HUDEVT_SSL_OCSP_RESUME_CLNT_HS message.

Impact

The system restarts TMM, disrupting traffic processing.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • After the system closes the SSL connection, TMM restarts while receiving the HUDEVT_SSL_OCSP_RESUME_CLNT_HS message.
