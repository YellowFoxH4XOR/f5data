K000136114: Failed unmounting /var or /sysroot during reboot or halt

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-09-07
Updated
2023-09-07
Applies to
  • F5OS
  • F5OS-A
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000136114

Description

Either one of the following error messages will be seen during reboot or halt in F5OS.

[FAILED] Failed unmounting /sysroot. 

[FAILED] Failed unmounting /var.

[FAILED] Failed unmounting /var/F5/system/cbip-disks.

 

Environment

  • F5OS
  • Reboot or halt

Cause

This is by design. it can be ignored.

Recommended Actions

None

Additional Information

None
