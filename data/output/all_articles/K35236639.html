Security Advisory Description

F5 is aware of the reports of vulnerabilities in the baseband management controllers (BMCs) of Supermicro servers. These vulnerabilities allow attackers to easily connect to a server and virtually mount any USB device to the server, remotely over any network, including the Internet.

For more information, refer to the following resources:

  • Virtual Media Vulnerability In BMC Opens Servers to Remote Attack from Eclypsium
  • BMC/IPMI Security Vulnerability from Supermicro

Note: These links take you to a resource outside of AskF5. The third party could remove the document without our knowledge.

The F5 iSeries appliances, including the following appliances, use a BMC for their Always On Management (AOM). However, the Remote Media and Virtual Media functions are not enabled in the F5 BMC firmware; therefore, the iSeries product lines are not vulnerable to the attack reported by the Eclypsium researcher.

  • i2000
  • i4000
  • i5000
  • i7000
  • i10000
  • i11000
  • i15000

No other BIG-IP product lines use a BMC and are not vulnerable to this attack.

Impact

There is no impact; F5 products are not affected by this vulnerability.

Security Advisory Status

F5 Product Development has evaluated the currently supported releases for potential vulnerability, and no F5 products were found to be vulnerable.

Security Advisory Recommended Actions

None
