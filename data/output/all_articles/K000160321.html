K000160321: Why it's important to relicense

Description

You recently received a new set of license keys, but don't plan to apply them until later. The previous license keys have Flash support which extends support during the migration to the new license keys; however, Flash support lacks the full functionality of a current license key.

Environment

  • New F5 contract
  • Expired license keys from any of the following:
    • BIG-IQ/CAT Flex Consumption Plan (FCP) 
    • VE Subscription
    • Perpetual

Cause

Flash support on license keys only extends support on specific, expired license keys, but there are many limitations that impact workflow, upgrades, and security.

  • Unable to open support cases online 
  • Failed upgrades due to old Service Check Dates
  • At risk running BIG-IP versions no longer supported by security updates

Recommended Actions

Prioritize updating license keys after starting new F5 contracts, to ensure full access to BIG-IP entitlements and to remain in security compliance. 

  1. Best Practices when preparing to update the license on BIG-IP
  2. If the BIG-IP is currently licensed by BIG-IQ, refer to Revoke BIG-IP VE licenses managed by BIG-IQ, otherwise, proceed to the next step   
  3. Apply a license to the BIG-IP

Afterwards, you can open support cases via the MyF5 portal for this BIG-IP, reactivate the license to updates the service check date and upgrade the BIG-IP to supported versions that receive security updates.
