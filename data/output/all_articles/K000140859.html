K000140859: BIG-IP takes long time to respond to SNMP requests.

Support Solution
Published
2024-08-27

Applies to

  • BIG-IP LTM: 15.X.X, 15.1.X, 15.1.10, 15.1.9, 15.1.8, 15.1.7, 15.1.6, 15.1.5, 15.1.4, 15.1.3, 15.1.2, 15.1.1, 15.1.0, 15.0.X, 15.0.1, 15.0.0
Source

Description

  • BIG-IP is responding late to SNMP requests.
  • BIG-IP snmp responses take around 30 seconds to be sent after the request is received.
  • snmp allowed IP list configured with FQDN hosts. Example:

    127.0.0.0/255.0.0.0 example.local 10.1.1.1

  • Messages similar to the following can be seen on var/log/daemon.log:
     
    err snmpd[6486]: warning: /etc/hosts.allow, line 16: can't verify hostname: gethostbyname(10.0.01.example) failed
    err snmpd[6486]: warning: /etc/hosts.allow, line 16: can't verify hostname: gethostbyname(10.0.01.example) failed


Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • snmp allowed IP list configured with FQDN hosts

Cause

Configured dns servers fails to resolve the FQDN hostname. The 30 seconds delay is the result of failed dns queries.

Recommended Actions

Modify the snmp allowed IP list and replace FQDN hostnames with their IP address. To do so, please perform the steps provided on K52219241
