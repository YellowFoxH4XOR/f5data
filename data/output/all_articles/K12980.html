Known Issue

The Charts reporting tool may not report response side violations.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • Violations are generated by response traffic

A violation triggered by response traffic, such as Illegal HTTP status in response, will still display on the Reporting: Requests screen, and on the Policy Building: Manual Traffic Learning screen; however, a reporting chart covering the same period of time will not include the Illegal HTTP status in response violation.

Impact

The Charts reporting tool may not contain correct data.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Violations generated by response side traffic will not be included by the Charts reporting tool.
