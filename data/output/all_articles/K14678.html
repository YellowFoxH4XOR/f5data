Known Issue

BIG-IP APM policy authentication may fail when the Identity Provider (IdP) signs Security Assertion Markup Language (SAML) assertions and responses.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured the BIG-IP APM policy to act as a SAML service provider (SP).
  • The external IdP signs both SAML assertions and SAML responses.

When the IdP signs only the assertion or only the response, the issue does not manifest.

Impact

BIG-IP APM policy authentication fails.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H427628 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • Error messages similar to the following example are logged in the /var/log/apm* files.

    err apd[9376]: 01490195:3: 1af28c42: SAML Agent: /Public/bigiplab-https-apmtest_act_saml_auth_ag failed to parse assertion, error: Canonicalization of SignedInfo

  • Authentication fails.

Workaround

There is no workaround for this issue. To prevent this issue, you can configure the external IdP to sign only the SAML responses or only the SAML assertions.

Note: Please refer to your IdP manufacturer's configuration guide regarding signing of SAML assertions and responses.
