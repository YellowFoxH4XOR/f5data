Policy Information

On December 12, law enforcement officials came to the Moscow office of NGINX (acquired by F5 earlier this year) apparently seeking evidence related to an intellectual property dispute, to which F5 is currently not a party. The officers had a warrant, and F5 is still working to confirm the full nature of the investigation.

NGINX cofounders Igor Sysoev and Maxim Konovalov were interviewed by law enforcement officials, but no employees have been arrested or are currently detained. F5 fully supports our employees and believes these claims against them do not have merit.

Promptly following the event, F5 took measures to ensure the security of our master software builds for NGINX, NGINX Plus, NGINX WAF, and NGINX Unit—all of which are stored on servers outside of Russia. No other F5 or NGINX products or services are developed in Moscow.

F5 remains committed to innovating with NGINX, NGINX Plus, NGINX WAF, and NGINX Unit. Developers are working on new features and no changes have been made to the current roadmaps. In addition, F5 expects to maintain our current support SLAs and will continue to provide the best-in-class support you’ve come to expect. NGINX uses a ‘follow-the-sun’ model and benefits from the full breadth and depth of F5’s support organization. F5 will keep you updated on developments, as appropriate, and please contact F5 Support if you have further questions.
