Known Issue

BIG-IP high availability (HA) systems may experience an unexpected active-active state after a software upgrade.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • Your configured BIG-IP systems contain a total of 30 or more local traffic objects (for example, pools, virtual servers, etc.).
  • The standby device is upgraded and then rebooted.

Impact

The HA systems may experience an interruption to production traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Multiple BIG-IP devices become active simultaneously and attempt to process traffic.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can force a system into the standby state. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. On the device that you want to force into standby mode, log in to the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) by typing the following command:

    tmsh

  2. Force the system into the standby state by typing the following command:

    run /sys failover standby
