K000162149: platform-stats mismatch error after F5OS downgrade from 1.8.3 to 1.5.4

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-07-09
Updated
2026-07-09
Applies to
  • F5OS Products
  • F5OS-A: 1.8.3, 1.5.4
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000162149

Description

After downgrading from F5OS-A 1.8.3 to F5OS-A 1.5.4, the following logs may be seem in the following files:

/var/log/messages:

2026-06-20T13:51:03.421855+09:00 appliance-1 journal: platform-stats: Slab ID 6 does not match table ID 5. at 0x20d89d0
2026-06-20T13:51:03.421512+09:00 appliance-1 journal: platform-stats: Slab ID 6 does not match table ID 5. at 0x20d89d0

/var/log/platform.log:

2026-06-20T14:10:26.650978+09:00 appliance-1 platform-monitor[9]: priority="Err" msg="Notification Stream: Send Error" stream="platform-stats" error="confd error: 'Unknown error', last='Invalid confd_vtype value: 0', errno=5" interface="confd"
2026-06-20T14:10:27.098949+09:00 appliance-1 tmstat-merged[1]: priority="Err" version=1.0 msgid=0x1a01000000000900 msg="TMSTAT error:" context="tmstat_remerge" detail="Invalid argument".

Environment

  • F5OS-A 1.8.3
  • F5OS-A 1.5.4
  • Downgrade

Cause

ID2354425

Answer/Recommended Actions

Reboot the appliance to clear the system states. The logs will cease to populate their resepective log files.
