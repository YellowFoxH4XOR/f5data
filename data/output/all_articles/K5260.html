K5260: A disabled 3dnsd daemon will re-enable itself when failover occurs

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If you disable the 3dnsd process on one unit in a redundant pair and then reboot the other unit, the unit on which 3dnsd was disabled will become active. The unit on which 3dnsd was disabled should remain in standby state, since it cannot respond to queries while 3dnsd is disabled.

F5 Network Product Development tracked this issue as CR22875.
