Known Issue

The IPSEC tunnel may fail when excessive Dead Peer Detection (DPD) messages are exchanged.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • Excessive DPD messages are exchanged.

Impact

The BIG-IP system unexpectedly brings down the IPSEC tunnel.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You observe that DPD ACK messages may be dropped when excessive DPD messages are exchanged.
