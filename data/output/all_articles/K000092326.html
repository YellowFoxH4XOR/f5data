K000092326: SSL Certificate - Invalid Maximum Validity Date Detected

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-08-05
Updated
2022-08-05
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000092326

Description

Default BIG-IP device certificate may be flagged as a vulnerability due to the validity date.

Environment

BIG-IP Device Certificate

Cause

BIG-IP ships with a self-signed device certificate with a validity period of 10 years.

Recommended Actions

Update the device certificate per one of the following articles

K6353 - Updating a self-signed SSL device certificate on a BIG-IP system

K42531434 - Replacing the Configuration utility's self-signed device certificate with a CA-signed device certificate

Additional Information
