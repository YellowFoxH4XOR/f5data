K000150612: Kosovo is not among the list of countries available for ASM Geolocation enforcement

Type

Published
2025-03-31
Updated
2025-03-31
Source

Kosovo is not among the list of countries available for ASM Geolocation enforcement.

The geolocation database uses the ISO 3166-2 standard for countries and regions; this standard classifies Kosovo as a region of Serbia.

The whereis statement in an iRule can identify traffic coming from a Kosovar IP.

https://clouddocs.f5.com/api/irules/whereis.html

Use the whereis <ip> state form of the command.
