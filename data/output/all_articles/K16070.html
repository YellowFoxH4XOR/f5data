Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may mark a valid persistence record as expired, even though the corresponding connection is still active.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The connection and persistence timeouts are within 33 seconds of each other.

Impact

The client connection is not returned to the given pool member as expected.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The client connection is load balanced to a new pool member, and not persisted as expected, even though the connection is active.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can set the timeout in the persistence profile to at least 33 seconds longer than the timeout in the protocol profile.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
