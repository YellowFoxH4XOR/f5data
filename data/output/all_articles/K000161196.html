K000161196: Resolving "Secondary is not actively streaming. Postgres may be down." on BIG-IQ After SSL Certificate Renewal

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-05-10
Updated
2026-05-10
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 8.3.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000161196

Description

Error "Secondary is not actively streaming. Postgres may be down." appears on the secondary BIG-IQ device after SSL certificate renewal. This results in loss of HA streaming replication, GUI inaccessibility on the secondary, and backup failures for managed BIG-IP LTM units. Log messages include:

  • FATAL: the database system is shutting down
  • could not accept SSL connection: sslv3 alert certificate expired
  • HikariCP - Connection is not available, request timed out after 30000ms

Environment

  • Product: BIG-IQ
  • Version: 8.3.0
  • HA configuration: Two-node (Primary and Secondary)
  • Recent action: SSL certificate renewal on both nodes
  • Services involved: Postgres, restjavad, tokumond, webd, guiserver

Cause

The issue is caused by an expired or improperly regenerated HA Postgres SSL certificate on the secondary BIG-IQ device. When the certificate is expired or not correctly updated, Postgres rejects SSL connections, leading to a shutdown loop and loss of HA streaming.

Answer/Recommended Actions

To resolve and prevent this issue:


Regenerate the HA Postgres SSL Certificate


  • On each device, run:
  • ha_generate_certs -f <SELF_DISCOVERY_IP>
  • Replace <SELF_DISCOVERY_IP> with the device’s own discovery IP (found in the BIG-IQ GUI under System > This Device > General Properties).

Proactively Rotate HA Certificates


  • To rotate certificates on both peers without breaking HA:
  • ha_rotate_certs -i <local_discovery_ip> -p <peer_discovery_ip>
  • Replace with the appropriate local and peer discovery IPs.

Verify Certificate Validity


  • On both nodes, check certificate status:
  • openssl x509 -in /var/lib/pgsql/config/server.crt -issuer -subject -startdate -enddate -noout

Restart Services if Needed


  • If the system is already impacted, restart the following daemons:
  • tmsh restart /sys service postgres tokumond restjavad webd guiserver

Check Disk Space


  • Ensure sufficient space on /var and /shared:
  • df -h /var /shared

Verify Firewall Rules for Port 5432


  • Confirm both IPv4 and IPv6 rules allow traffic:
  • iptables -L | grep 5432
  • ip6tables -L | grep 5432

Best Practices for Future SSL Renewals


  • Perform renewals during a maintenance window.
  • Monitor Postgres and HA status after renewal:
  • tmsh show sys service postgres
  • Verify HA streaming status in the GUI (System > BIG-IQ HA).
  • Be prepared to restart daemons if issues arise.

Additional Information

  • The default HA certificate validity is 2 years.
  • BIG-IQ does not automatically rotate the Postgres HA SSL certificate.
  • Disk space exhaustion or missing firewall rules can also cause similar symptoms.
