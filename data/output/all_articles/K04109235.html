Description

LDAP authentication may fail after upgrade to 14.x

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • LDAP Authentication
  • Upgrade 12.x to 14.x

Cause

Unknown at the time of publication.

Recommended Actions

Enable Check Member Attribute in Group in the LDAP config

  1. Log into the Configuration Utility (GUI).
  2. Click System.
  3. Click Users.
  4. Click Authentication.
  5. Check the box Check Member Attribute in Group.
  6. Click Finished.

Additional Information

"Check Member Attribute in Group" should be enabled for LDAP role binding to work
