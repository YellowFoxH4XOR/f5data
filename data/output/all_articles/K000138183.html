K000138183: BX520 blade is difficult to remove from VELOS CX1610 chassis

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-01-10
Updated
2024-01-10
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000138183

Description

When attempting to remove a blade from the chassis, the blade feels stuck and cannot be removed without applying excessive force.

Environment

  • CX1610 VELOS chassis
  • BX520 blade
This is most likely to occur on the bottom blades of each section:

Cause

The blade or blanks above it is causing the blade to catch, making it hard to remove

Recommended Actions

  1. Remove the blade or blanks above the affected blade and try again

Additional Information

None
