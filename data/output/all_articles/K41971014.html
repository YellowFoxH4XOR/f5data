K41971014: BIG-IP requires manual intervention to recover from Thermal Shutdown

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-08-14
Updated
2026-08-14
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K41971014

Description
When a BIG-IP overheats, the system will shut down to prevent damage to internal components.  After a shutdown, it will require someone to manually power it back on.  Please ensure that the cause of the thermal event is resolved before booting the BIG-IP.  Possible causes of overheating include, plugged air holes, fan failure, inadequate data center cooling, incorrect installation (intake faces a hot row, rather than a cold row), or data center air conditioning failure.

Environment

  •  

Cause
This is normal and expected behavior for the BIG-IP.  Because the BIG-IP has no way to monitor the ambient temperature while powered off, it relies on the user to manually power it back on after the thermal event has been resolved.

Recommended Actions

No action is required

Additional Information
Please see the platform guide for your BIG-IP appliance for information on proper operating temperatures.  You can find these on askf5.com.
