Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When you launch a Dynamic App Tunnel or Web Application Tunnel Favorite, the Summary section in the Tunnel's pop-up menu displays the bytes sent and received. The counters display 0 (zero) bytes, even though the Tunnel functions normally.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR62018.
