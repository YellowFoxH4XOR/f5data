Description

  • Bot defense profile

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

Selecting/Filtering for specific event(s)

All events:
$ curl -sk https://${BIGIP_USER}:${BIGIP_PASSWORD}@${BIGIP_MGMT}/mgmt/tm/asm/events/bot-defense-events | jq -rc '.items[]|[.supportId, .classification]'
["5200910928203219045","/Common/Malicious Bot"]
["5200910928203219037","/Common/Malicious Bot"]
["5200910928203219068","/Common/Malicious Bot"]

Specific supportId:
$ curl -sk https://${BIGIP_USER}:${BIGIP_PASSWORD}@${BIGIP_MGMT}/mgmt/tm/asm/events/bot-defense-events/5200910928203219068 | jq -rc '[.supportId, .classification]'
["5200910928203219068","/Common/Malicious Bot"]


Trigger different Bot Signature:

$ curl -sk https://${BIGIP_USER}:${BIGIP_PASSWORD}@${BIGIP_MGMT}/mgmt/tm/asm/events/bot-defense-events | jq  -rc '.items[]|[.supportId, .classification, .botSignature]'
["5200910928203219084","/Common/Malicious Bot","/Common/Bing"]
["5200910928203219076","/Common/Malicious Bot","/Common/Google"]
["5200910928203219045","/Common/Malicious Bot","/Common/Google"]
["5200910928203219037","/Common/Malicious Bot","/Common/Google"]
["5200910928203219068","/Common/Malicious Bot","/Common/Google"]


Filter by Bot Signature Name:

$ curl -sk https://${BIGIP_USER}:${BIGIP_PASSWORD}@${BIGIP_MGMT}/mgmt/tm/asm/events/bot-defense-events?\$filter=botSignature+eq+"/Common/Bing" | jq  -rc '.items[]|[.supportId, .classification, .botSignature]'
["5200910928203219084","/Common/Malicious Bot","/Common/Bing"]
