Known Issue

The TMM process may core when using the CLIENT_CLOSED iRule event in a UDP virtual server.

Impact

There may be an interruption of traffic and possible failover to the standby system.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H379842 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High screen.
  • The /var/log/tmm file may show evidence of a TMM core.

    Note: For more information, please see SOL10062 listed in the Supplemental Information section.

  • TMM core files are visible in the /shared/core directory.
  • You may experience a failover to the standby system.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 377268 to this issue, and has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies To box. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:

Workaround

To work around this issue, you may re-write your iRule to not use the CLIENT_CLOSED event.
