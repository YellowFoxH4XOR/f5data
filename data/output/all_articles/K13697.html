Topic

BIG-IP 10.0.0 introduced the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh), which was available with the bigpipe utility. Beginning in BIG-IP 11.0.0, the bigpipe utility is removed from the product in favor of the tmsh utility.

A partial list of mappings from bigpipe commands to tmsh commands is currently available at DevCentral:

  • Bigpipe Mappings

This list is not a complete reference, but an ongoing resource continually updated by the DevCentral user community.
