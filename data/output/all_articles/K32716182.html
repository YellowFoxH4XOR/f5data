Description

Can not upload Snapshot or qkview to iHealth.

Error messages: Your device may not have access to the Internet. Verify that you can access api.f5.com and ihealth-api.f5.com and try your upload again

Environment

  • Configuration objects and settings: BIG-IP 

Cause

DNS is not properly configured.

Recommended Actions

  1. Check connectivity to iHealth and iHealth domains:
    api.f5.com - 104.219.110.164
    ihealth-api.f5.com - 104.219.110.162
  2. Check DNS configuration and confirm resolution name works
      nslookup api.f5.com
      nslookup ihealth-api.f5.com
  3. If all the connectivity test and DNS are correct, verify if your iHealth Account is working, open a browser to https://ihealth.f5.com and use the same credentials you are using on the BIG-IP.
