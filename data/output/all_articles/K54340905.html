Description
Upgrade/update ISO cannot be installed on the existing volume due to the error:
error: tm_install::Process::Process_full_install -- predicted size for BIGIP1 is 14875024, current location size is 12320768, and vg has 2199552 remaining.
 

Environment

  • ISO installer
  • Preparing to upgrade

Cause
Appdata logical volume occupies most of the volume space.

Recommended Actions

Follow the article:
K74200262: Reducing the size of the appdata volume with the lvreduce utility
