Topic

The BIG-IP ASM system supports regular expressions constructed in accordance with Perl Compatible Regular Expressions (PCREs). Additionally, the BIG-IP ASM system supports RE2 regular expression library for attack signatures in version 11.2.0. For more information, refer to K13814: Attack signatures now support the RE2 regular expression library.
