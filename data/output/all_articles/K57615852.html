Known Issue

Filtering BIG-IP ASM charts reports by time period may not return expected results.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You configured the BIG-IP ASM Charts Time Period filter with a value of Last Month.

Impact

Filtering BIG-IP ASM charts reports by time period returns unexpected results.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Filtering the BIG-IP ASM charts reports by a Time Period of Last Month filter returns results for 30 weeks instead of 30 days.
