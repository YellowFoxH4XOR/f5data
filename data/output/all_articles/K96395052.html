Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may fail to initialize rate shaping rate class and halt traffic.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The rate shaping rate class is configured to use the fred (Flow-Based Random Early Detection) drop policy method.
  • TMM is processing traffic that triggers rate shaping.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may stop processing traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP system unexpectedly stops processing traffic.
  • In the /var/log/ltm file, you observe messages similar to the following example:

    notice rateclass_shaper_input/1534: Rate ceiling limit triggered for rate class: /Common/<RateClass>

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can configure the rate class to use a drop policy method of red (Random Early Detection). To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: The impact of the suggested workaround depends on the specific environment. F5 recommends that you test any such changes during a maintenance window and consider the possible impact on your specific environment.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.
  2. Navigate to Network > Rate Shaping > Rate Class List.
  3. Click the affected Rate Class.
  4. In the General Properties list, click Advanced.
  5. In the Drop Policy list, click red.
  6. Click Update.

Note: The original drop policy (fred) will continue to be applied to any existing connection flows as long as they exist. However, the new drop policy (red) will be applied to any new connection flows.
