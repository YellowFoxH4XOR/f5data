Topic

On some Android devices, the BIG-IP Edge Client may be unable to read the device hardware serial number. When the device operating system does not supply the serial number to the BIG-IP Edge Client, the BIG-IP Edge Client may display the hardware serial number as unknown. The following Android device examples are known to exhibit this behavior:

  • Samsung Galaxy Tab
  • Samsung Galaxy S II

There may be additional products that exhibit this behavior that F5 has not tested.

F5 recommends that users who are affected by this issue contact the manufacturer of their Android device.
