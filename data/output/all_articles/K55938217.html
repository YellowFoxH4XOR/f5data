Known Issue

DNS servers configured with Dynamic Host Configuration Protocol (DHCP) are unavailable after restart when there are more than two DNS servers in the lease.

Note: Because of architectural changes, this issue does not affect BIG-IP 13.1.0 and later.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • DHCP is enabled on the management interface.
  • DHCP server provides two or more DNS servers in its lease.

Impact

Name resolution on the management interface fails due to misconfiguration in DNS information for the management interface.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • DNS servers configured with DHCP are unavailable after restart when there are two or more DNS servers in the lease.
