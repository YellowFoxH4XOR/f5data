Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may produce a core file and restart when the TCP profile Duplicate Selective Acknowledgement (D-SACK) option is enabled.

Impact

Traffic processing may be interrupted as TMM restarts and the system may fail over to the peer device.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H486209 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High screen.
  • A recently created TMM core file is present in the /shared/core directory.
  • Client connections through the BIG-IP system experience a brief service interruption.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can disable the TCP profile's D-SACK option. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Note: By default, the D-SACK (RFC 2882) setting is disabled.

Impact of workaround: The BIG-IP system will not provide notification of duplicate segment receipt through D-SACK.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP Configuration utility.
  2. Navigate to Local Traffic > Profiles > Protocol > [Name of TCP Profile].
  3. Under the Loss Detection and Recovery section, clear D-SACK (RFC 2883) option.
  4. Select Update.
