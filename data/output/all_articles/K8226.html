Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP ASM blocking response page is not served to the client and the connection hangs when the OneConnect feature is enabled. As a result, the client does not receive a response regarding the reason their request was blocked, and it appears that the request is being processed.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR87343, and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM 9.4.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM Release Notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.3-HF1 issued for BIG-IP 9.4.3. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.

For information about the F5 hotfix policy, refer to K4918: Overview of the F5 critical issue hotfix policy.

For information about managing F5 product hotfixes, refer to K6845: Managing F5 product hotfixes.
