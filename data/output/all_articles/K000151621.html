K000151621: What is the impact on the ability to decrypt HTTP Cookies when changing Passphrase

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-05-31
Updated
2025-05-31
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.5.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000151621

Description

Can BIG-IP decrypt HTTP Cookies sent under the old passphrase after updating the passphrase in the HTTP profile?


Environment

BIG-IP with HTTP Profile and encrypted cookies


Cause

BIG-IP does not have a "last used" storage mechanism to permit decryption of old cookies. When a cookie arrives that is encrypted with the old passphrase, the BIG-IP cannot decrypt it. It will make a new load balancing decision (if the cookie is the standard persistence cookie) and issue a new cookie encrypted with the new passphrase.


Recommended Actions

Plan passphrase changes for a maintenance window or period of expected low traffic to minimize impact on users.


Additional Information

None.
