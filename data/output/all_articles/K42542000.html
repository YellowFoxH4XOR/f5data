Topic

What You'll Learn
You can use the HA group feature to ensure high availability (HA) for the BIG-IP system. The HA group feature allows the BIG-IP system to fail over automatically based on the health of resources outside of the BIG-IP system. An HA group is a configuration object you create and assign to a traffic group for devices in a device group. An HA group defines health criteria for a resource (such as an application server pool) that the traffic group uses. With an HA group, the BIG-IP system can decide whether to keep a traffic group active on its current device or fail over the traffic group to another device when resources such as pool members fall below a certain level.
