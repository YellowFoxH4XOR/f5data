Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Large configurations (configurations that include 200 or more wide IPs, virtual servers, nodes, pools, or complex iRules) may cause the mcp process to enter a blocked or locked state until the entire configuration is loaded. In this state, other daemons are unable to communicate with the mcp process and BIG-IP is unable to forward or process application traffic until until after the configuration has loaded.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR100673 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 9.4.6. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, GTM, ASM, Link Controller, or WebAccelerator release notes.

This issue has been fixed in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.5-HF2, issued for BIG-IP version 9.4.5. You may download this hotfix or a later version of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
