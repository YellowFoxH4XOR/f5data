Topic

The failover cable physically connects the units in a redundant pair for hard-wired failover purposes.

BIG-IP 1500, 1600, 3400, 3600, 3900, 6400, 6800, 6900, 8900, 8950, 11000, 11050 platforms, and VIPRION B4100/B4200 series blades

The failover port for the BIG-IP 1500, 1600, 3400, 3600, 3900, 6400, 6800, 6900, 8900, 8950, 11000, 11050 platforms, and VIPRION B4100/B4200 series blades is a male 9-pin DE-9 port. The BIG-IP failover cable for these platforms is constructed using a standard female DE9 9-pin D-sub connector (sometimes incorrectly referred to as DB9) at each end.

Important: Do not confuse the failover cable with the serial cable used for terminal connections to the serial port on F5 products. The two cables used for hard-wired failover and terminal connections are not interchangeable. For information about the serial cable used for terminal connections, refer to K587: Pinouts for serial terminal cables used to connect the 9-pin serial port on F5 products.

The pinouts for the failover cable are listed in the following table.

DTR46DSR

DSR64DTR
RTS78CTS
CTS87RTS

Important: Failover cables should not exceed 15.24 meters, or 50 feet, in length.

Note: For information about purchasing a failover cable, contact F5 Inside Sales.

BIG-IP 2000, 4000, 5000, 7000, 10000, 1200, and iSeries platforms

The current 2000, 4000, 5000, 7000, 10000, 1200, and iSeries of BIG-IP platforms uses a standard RJ-45 port for the failover connection. The failover cable used for these platforms is a variation of a standard CAT 5 crossover cable with pins 7 and 8 crossed. You cannot use Standard CAT 5 crossover cables as failover cables for these systems. For information about the RJ-45 connector pinouts for the failover port, refer to the following table.
