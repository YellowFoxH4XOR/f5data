Description

After a failover event in a BIG-IP LTM High Availability (HA) environment, the Active device displays incorrect pool member statuses for down pool members. Specifically:

  • Pool members that are actually offline appear as "UNKNOWN" (blue square icon) instead of "OFFLINE"
  • Health monitors do not update status for down pool members on the Active device
  • Up pool members display correct status and health monitors update normally
  • The Standby device shows correct statuses for all pool members

This issue is persistent and typically occurs following hardware migration or failover events.

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM with High Availability configuration 
  • Issue can be observed after migration from VIPRION to rSeries Tenant and subsequent failover
  • ConfigSync method: Automatic with Incremental Sync
  • Both devices may show matching commit IDs and are in sync

Cause

The root cause could be a synchronization issue in the MCPD process following a failover, resulting in health monitor status not updating for down pool members on the Primary Device. Forcing an MCPD reload and performing a manual full configuration sync from the device with correct status may help to resolve the issue. 

Answer/Recommended Actions

To resolve health monitor status synchronization issues, perform the following steps during a maintenance window:

  1. Fail over traffic to the peer device to minimize impact.

  2. Force the MCPD process to reload the BIG-IP configuration on the affected device, followed by a reboot:

    • Follow the procedure in K13030: Forcing the mcpd process to reload the BIG-IP configuration

  3. If the issue persists after MCPD reload and reboot:

    • Change the ConfigSync method from "Automatic with Incremental Sync" to "Manual Full Sync"
    • Perform a manual configuration sync from the device with correct pool member status to the affected device
    • Restore the ConfigSync method to the desired setting after resolution

  4. Monitor the environment to ensure the issue does not recur.

Temporary Workaround:

Re-attaching health monitors to affected pools may temporarily resolve the issue for those specific pools, but does not address the underlying synchronization problem.
