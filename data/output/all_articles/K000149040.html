K000149040: Physical Damage on Packaging of the CX410/CX410-N or CX1610/CX1610-N

Topic

This article is to help customers determine what to record and report to F5 if they see physical damage on the outside of their CX410/CX410-N (Cobra) and CX1610/CX1610-N (Chiron) chassis devices.

Important: If physical damage is seen on the outside of either the CX410/CX410-N (Cobra) and CX1610/CX1610-N (Chiron) chassis devices, please stop unpackaging and follow the steps below.

Description

When physical damage is seen on the outside of the shipping container, please stop unpackaging and take pictures of the damage.  Pay close attention to include pictures that show the extent of the damage and location.

If unpacked mid-way, do not set the packaging walls down on their sides.  Keep them upright.

While taking pictures, if you come across a Tip-N-Tell indicator (as seen below), please take clear pictures of the indicators.  If present, they should be applied to two separate sides of the packaging.  Please take pictures of both Tip-N-Tell Indicators and submit to F5 Service.   

If the damage is on a CX1610/CX1610-N packaging, take a picture of the Yellow tri-lingual Do Not Stack pallet cones that are on the top of the packaging. 

In Summary, if external packaging damage is seen:

  1. Stop unpackaging, do not lay shipping walls down on their side
  2. Take pictures of packaging to show extent and location of the damage
  3. Take pictures of both Tip-N-Tell Indicators (if present)
  4. Take a picture of the Do Not Stack pyramid (if present on the CX1610/CX1610-N)
