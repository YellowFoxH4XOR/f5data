Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may produce a core file when the BIG-IP PEM is configured.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP system has the BIG-IP PEM provisioned.
  • A Policy Enforcement Classification profile is associated with a virtual server.

Impact

TMM may produce a core file and temporarily fail to process traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H427515 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The BIG-IP host may fail over to another host in the device group.
  • In the /var/core directory, you may observe a TMM core file.
