Description

We are seeing Stats Last Collection Date 'Jan 1, 1970' In devices tab after enabling stats collection

Analytics Iapp is properly installed on BIG-IP


Stats are being collected from few BIG-IP even when AVR is provisioned

Traffic to DCD from AVR is routed via TMM and non default Route Domain


Environment

  • BIG-IQ DCD cluster
  • BIG-IP LTM with AVR provisioned
  • Route Domains

Cause

AVR communication to DCD does not support sending traffic via different Route Domain than RD0 or Management interface

Recommended Actions

Configure BIG-IP to push AVR statistics via RD0 or Management by:

  • Configuring SelfIPs in RD0
  • Setting proper routing via management or RD0
