Description

This article explains how the BIG-IP system determines when to deactivate syn-cookies after they have been triggered.

 

 Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • Syn-cookie 

 Cause

None

 

 Recommended Actions

After syncookies are triggered the following conditions need to be true in order for syncookies to turn off:

  • The VIP cannot be under an AFM syn flood 
  • syncache_curr on the listener key has to be lower than vs_syn_challenge_threshold
  • The amount of outstanding syncookies per period is lower than the deactivation threshold.

The deactivation threshold is calculated as follows:

(sys db dos.syncookiedeactivate * (default-vs-syn-challenge-threshold / number_of_tmms)) / 100
