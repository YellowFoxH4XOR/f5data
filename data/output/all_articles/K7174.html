Topic

The total number of trunks for each of the platforms listed in the table includes one or more hidden trunks used to carry traffic between the switch chips and the motherboard. As such, the maximum number of user-configurable trunks is always less than the total number of trunks for each platform. For the VIPRION platforms, the limit applies to the entire chassis, regardless of the number of blades installed. For the maximum number of interfaces allowed per trunk on BIG-IP platforms, refer to K1689: Overview of trunks on BIG-IP platforms.

Note: For information about the total number of trunks or link aggregation group (LAGs) configurable on F5 VELOS and rSeries hardware products, refer to K000139024: Troubleshooting LACP LAGs on F5 rSeries and F5 VELOS systems.
