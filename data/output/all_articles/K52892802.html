Known Issue

BIG-IP APM dashboard charts may display incorrect active session data.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system runs on a chassis device with multiple blades.
  • A blade of the chassis device is rebooted.
  • A session event (such as a logon or logout) occurs while the blade is rebooting.

Impact

BIG-IP APM dashboard data about active sessions appears to decrease.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The BIG-IP APM dashboard active session count data temporarily skews and appears to decrease; however, after additional session event activity, the displayed dashboard active session data is no longer skewed.
