Known Issue

BIG-IP APM profile access statistics are not updated after a failover event.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM systems are configured in a high availability (HA) group.
  • You upgrade the standby system to BIG-IP 11.5.3 HF2, 11.6.0 HF6, 12.0.0, or later versions.
  • The active system experiences a failover event.

Impact

On a newly active system, profile access statistics for current active sessions, current pending sessions, and current established sessions are inaccurate.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When viewed using the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh), profile access statistics report zero sessions.
  • In the /var/log/apm file, you observe messages similar to the following example:

    01490559:3: 00000000: Access stats encountered error: SessionDB operation failed (ERR_NOT_FOUND).

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can schedule a maintenance window to upgrade all devices in the HA configuration before accepting new connections. After all devices are upgraded, you can reboot the devices simultaneously to resume access profile statistics collection.
