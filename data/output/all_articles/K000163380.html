Description

snmpd daemon may dump core. Following message will be written in /var/log/ltm.

Sep 10 19:14:06 bigip1.f5net.com err mcpd[7111]: 01071087:3: Killed process for snmpd as current count of messages (45202117) keeps building.

Sep 10 19:14:07 bigip1.f5net.com notice mcpd[7111]: 01070418:5: connection 0xeb64c988 (user snmpd) was closed with active requests

Sep 10 19:14:08 bigip1.f5net.com info snmpd[26585]: 0114002b:6: HA daemon_heartbeat snmpd enabled.

Environment

  • BIG-IP 17.5.x
  • snmp access is enabled

Cause

The logs indicate that mcpd terminated snmpd because snmp's MCP message queue size grew continually and did not clear, triggering mcpd's safety kill condition.

The restart may happen in a busy system, or due to some network issue.

Answer/Recommended Actions

snmpd restarts automatically.
