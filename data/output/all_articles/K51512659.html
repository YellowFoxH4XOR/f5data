K51512659: BIG-IQ license management refuses to remove deployed Big-IP with error message ''The following devices are managed and cannot be assigned an unmanaged license''

Type
Support Solution
Published
2020-09-09
Updated
2023-02-21
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 8.X.X, 7.X.X
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K51512659

Description

When attempting to re-license a re-deployed VE, run into a circular issue, where the VE cannot be licensed because the old VE is still managed, but the old VE cannot be deleted because it still has services configured.

When navigating to:

Big-IQ: Devices -> License Management -> Licenses: [IP:port VE] [user] [passwd]

Big-IQ reports [seemingly] UNmanaged device is Managed:

The following devices are managed and cannot be assigned an unmanaged license:  10.x.x.x
Please remove them from the list and try again.


When navigating to remove the device, the following is reported:

Big-IQ cannot remove the selected device because the device has service configurations on it or services are in the process of being removed.

Big-IQ shows the BIG-IP to be unreachable in the License Management Assignment's Device Type column.

Big-IQ: Devices -> License Management -> Assignments


Environment

  • Big-IQ Licensing Management
  • Redeploying already Big-IQ licensed Big-IP VE (though may be applicable to HW platforms as well)
  • Attempting to Re-license existing BIG-IP or newly Re-deployed BIG-IP VE
  • The BIG-IP shows that it is unreachable License Management Assignment's Device Type column.

Cause

The issue is simply that the Big-IP in question has not been thoroughly removed from the Big-IQ, and must be before continuing.  The confusion comes from unfamiliarity with the Big-IQ and insufficient guidance in the Big-IQ error to gracefully direct the admin to the correct next action of removing all services from the Big-IQ for the given Big-IP in question, before attempting to re-add it to the Big-IQ and thereby permit re-licensing it.

Since the BIG-IP is seen as unreachable from the License Management, it will not be able to properly change the license.

Recommended Actions

To proceed with the re-license

  1. Login to the BIG-IQ.
  2. Navigate to:  Big-IP device -> Services -> click "Remove Service" in the upper right corner of the screen
  3. Re-discover and re-import.
  4. Navigate to:  Big-IP device -> License Management -> Assignments
  5. Select BIG-IP device and click Revoke.
  6. Navigate to:  Big-IP device -> License Management -> Licenses
  7. Click the License Name.
  8. Click the License Devices button.
  9. From the Devices list choose the device you want to change the license.
    Important: Do not enter the IP, user name, and password of the device under unmanaged devices.
  10. Click Assign.
  11. After doing this, the device will appear in Big-IP device -> License Management -> Assignments

Additional Information

"The following devices are managed and cannot be assigned an unmanaged license" error message will be displayed if you enter the IP, user name, and password of the device under unmanaged devices, when the BIG-IP device is already managed by BIG-IQ.
