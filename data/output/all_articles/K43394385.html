Description

Inquiries regarding what features are in a license, or to get a new license key should be directed to F5 Sales Team. 

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IQ
  • Licensing questions
  • New License key
  • New Registration key

Cause

F5 Support has no access to the Bills of Material (BOM) of an F5 product. Therefore, F5 Support is unable to advise on the Stock-Keeping Unit (SKU) of the add-ons for a license. F5 support could not provide new license key either.

Recommended Actions

Contact F5 Sales Team for assistance.
