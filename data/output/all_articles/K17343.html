Known Issue

The Configuration utility may appear to stop responding or "hang" when an administrator creates a new security policy.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • BIG-IP ASM synchronization is enabled for a device group with at least two BIG-IP systems.

Impact

The BIG-IP administrator is unable to create a new security policy through the Configuration utility.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The Configuration utility appears to stop responding for long periods of time, before the session eventually times out and returns a blank page.
