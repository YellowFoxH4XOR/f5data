  • 1500
  • 1600 - Heartbeat option in LCD only when running version 10.1.0
  • 3400

  • 8400

  • VIPRION

BIG-IP switch appliances include an option named Heartbeat in the LCD menu that can be enabled or disabled. When enabled, a small heart icon will be displayed on the LCD display. The Heartbeat option is not used by hardware or software.

Enabling the Heartbeat option will have no affect on the system other than displaying a heart icon on the LCD display.
