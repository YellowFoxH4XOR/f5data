Description

After powering on a newly established BIG-IP Next HA pair, Central Manager reports a yellow triangle (unreachable) for the HA health.

When you click into the HA status to see the individual instances, "SHARED-00001: Internal server error" is reported to the screen nothing is displayed for the nodes.

Environment

  • Two BIG-IP Next instances, managed by Central Manager
  • The BIG-IP Next HA cluster was recently established
  • The BIG-IP Next devices were powered down and then powered back up

Cause

Three possible causes

  1. The devices are still re-establishing HA after the cold start.
  2. SSL certificate error between Central Manager and BIG-IP Next. The error is not reported to screen. 
  3. The floating management IP is unreachable by Central Manager

Recommended Actions

The devices are still re-establishing HA after the cold start.

If you just recently brought up the environment, give it some time to stabilize after the cold start.

SSL certificate error between Central Manager and BIG-IP Next. The error is not reported to screen. 

If you have given sufficient time for the instances to boot and re-establish HA (10+ minutes), the issue could be because Central Manager no longer trusts the ssl certificate presented by the BIG-IP Next instances. In the Instances screen of Central Manager, click the instance name to open the properties screen.

In the instance properties screen, (1) click Certificates and (2) click Establish Trust.

If, after clicking Establish Trust, you see a confirmation dialog asking you to accept the self-signed certificate, this is the likely cause, and the HA health should be displayed momentarily.

The floating management IP is unreachable by Central Manager

The conditions that cause this are unknown, but after a cold start the designated standby device may end up as active and Central Manager is unable to contact the floating management IP address, resulting in a yellow triangle health indicator.

If this occurs, power off the designated standby device. If this was the cause then the cluster status will turn red (unhealthy) as the designated active device becomes active.

Now, power on the designated standby device and give it a chance to re-join the cluster, and it should come up as Green/Healthy after a few minutes.
