Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The mcpd daemon is the messenger daemon that allows userland processes to communicate with TMM and vice versa. When communicating with other processes, mcpd generates empty transactional messages, such as start_transaction and end_transaction. This behavior causes excessive CPU cycles and memory pressure. As a result, the mcpd daemon may crash under certain circumstances.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR91313 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 9.4.5. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.

Additionally, Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.3.1-HF5 has been issued for BIG-IP version 9.3.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Networks Downloads site.
