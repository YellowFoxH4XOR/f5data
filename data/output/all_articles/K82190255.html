K82190255: BIG-IQ upgrade from 5.4 may fail if you are managing 11.x BIG-IP devices

Applies to

Description

When you are attempting to upgrade BIG-IQ 5.4 to 6.x or 7.x, the upgrade may fail when your BIG-IQ is managing BIG-IP devices that are still on version 11.x. This may occur when you still have services other than "management" (like LTM, ASM, and so on) discovered under the managed device in BIG-IQ.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ 6.0 and up
  • BIG-IP 11.x
  • Managed services (like LTM, ASM, and so on) in BIG-IQ

Cause

BIG-IQ 6.0 and above only has limited support for BIG-IP 11.x.

In order for BIG-IQ to manage older BIG-IP platforms, all the discovered services need to be remove from within the BIG-IQ console.

This can be seen in the BIG-IQ GUI under:

  •   " Devices >> BIG-IP DEVICES", then check the "Services" column for anything besides "Management"

During the upgrade process, there is a script that checks for older devices, and automatically removes services from those devices that are incompatible with the newer version of BIG-IQ. Although sometimes the script fails to remove all the services correctly, causing the upgrade to fail.

Recommended Actions

If you know that you are managing 11.x BIG-IP devices, just go into the GUI and manually remove services from those BIG-IP devices prior to starting the upgrade.

This is done by:

  1. going to " Devices >> BIG-IP DEVICES" in the BIG-IQ GUI
  2. check the box next to the device for which you wish to remove services from
  3. then click the "Removal All Services" button
