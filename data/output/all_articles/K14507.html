If you have questions or require assistance with this process, contact an F5 RMA Coordinator by calling F5 Support for your region.

Return instructions

When you receive your advanced exchange replacements, please return the suspected defective hardware within 10 business days by performing the following procedure:

  1. Retain all packaging materials to reuse as return packaging.
  2. Carefully place the parts in the original electrostatic bag and packaging, making sure to package each part separately as they were shipped to you. Do not consolidate parts into fewer boxes. Each individual unit, or part of an RMA, will be monitored by the F5 returns management group. Any damages sustained during shipping as a result of insufficient packaging will be invoiced at full list price.
  3. Clearly write the following information on the outside of the shipping container:
    • RMA work order number
    • Serial number

      Note: Shipments that do not have an F5-authorized RMA work order number may be refused at time of delivery and returned to the shipper by way of collect freight.

  4. A return waybill may have been provided with the replacement parts. If so, contact the carrier indicated on the waybill to arrange collection.
  5. F5 devices are regulated by the Trade and Industry Department (HKTID) and subject to Strategic Commodities Control provisions. HKTID requires that any party transferring controlled commodities submit the Application for Resale/Transfer/Disposal of Products Under Approved Import License (SC047). The F5 return agent will assist with this requirement when contacted for pickup of the F5 device. http://www.stc.tid.gov.hk/english/download/files/sc047.pdf
  6. If a return waybill was provided, skip to step 7. If no return waybill is included, email the F5 return agent at f5return@flashglobal.com and provide the following information to schedule a pickup of the suspected defective hardware:
    • RMA work order number
    • Serial number
    • Contact name and phone number
    • Pickup address
  7. Keep a record of the Pickup Request Number and ship date; you will need these to track misrouted shipments. Failure to provide F5 with adequate proof of return will result in an invoice at full list price for the suspected defective hardware.
  8. In accordance with F5's service contract agreement supporting suspected functional failures, F5 has forwarded parts to you at your request using the RMA process. As per this contract with F5, the RMA equipment is sent to you with the understanding that the suspected malfunctioning hardware will be returned to the F5 RMA processing center within ten (10) business days. Please help F5 improve our quality, reduce cost, and provide you with better service by abiding by this agreement.
