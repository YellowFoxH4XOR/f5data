Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may log Clock advanced by <number> ticks messages, and may also restart, when loading many FIPS keys on a BIG-IP 11050 platform.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a BIG-IP 11050 platform with a FIPS card installed.
  • You have created 200 or more FIPS keys.

Impact

The TMM may restart and temporarily fail to process traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H498134 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The BIG-IP system logs many Clock Advanced by <number> ticks messages, similar to the following example, to the /var/log/ltm file:

    tmm1[10604]: 01010029:5: Clock advanced by 738 ticks
    tmm3[10604]: 01010029:5: Clock advanced by 797 ticks
    tmm5[10604]: 01010029:5: Clock advanced by 856 ticks
    tmm4[10604]: 01010029:5: Clock advanced by 915 ticks

  • The BIG-IP system fails over to another system in the device group.
