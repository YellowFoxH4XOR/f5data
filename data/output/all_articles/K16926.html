Known Issue

The apd and apmd processes may exit and restart if an unexpected error occurs when communicating with the mcpd process. In rare circumstances, communication failures between apd or apmd and mcpd may cause the apd and apmd processes to assert and restart unexpectedly.

Impact

Users are unable to connect to the BIG-IP APM system while the apd and apmd processes restart.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Users cannot access resources using the BIG-IP APM system.
  • The apd and apmd processes exit and generate core files to the /shared/core directory.
  • The BIG-IP APM system generates a SIGABRT stack trace to the /var/log/apm file.
