Known Issue

The websso process may produce core files on the 11000 platform.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are running an affected version on an 11000/E101 platform.
  • You have licensed and configured BIG-IP APM.
  • You stop or restart the websso process.

Impact

There is no impact. The websso process produces a core file when shutting down.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • After a restart of the websso process or reboot of the 11000 platform a core file for websso is located in /shared/core.
