Known Issue

On rare occasions, BIG-IP systems in a high availability (HA) configuration may experience Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) restarts and a failover loop.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have multiple BIG-IP systems in an HA configuration.
  • Your BIG-IP systems belong to a Sync-Failover device group.
  • A failover event occurs on a BIG-IP system in the device group.

Impact

Traffic processing fails while TMM restarts and a failover loop occurs.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Traffic processing stops.
  • Your BIG-IP systems continually fail over to the next active peer in the device group.
  • The newly active BIG-IP system immediately produces a TMM core file in the /var/core directory.

Resolution

Workaround

There is no workaround for this issue. To restore traffic processing, log in to the Advanced Shell (bash) of each BIG-IP system in the device group and reboot the system by typing reboot.
