K7438: FirePass ActiveX controls that are reinstalled on IE7 remain in disabled status

Topic

If you disable a FirePass ActiveX control in Microsoft Internet Explorer 7 (IE7), and then reinstall the FirePass ActiveX control, the control may remain in disabled status. When you attempt to connect to a FirePass resource that requires the FirePass ActiveX control, IE7 may display the following error message:

Sorry. ActiveX control is required to view this page.

You can work around this issue by enabling all disabled ActiveX controls in IE7 that begin with F5. For information about enabling disabled ActiveX controls in IE7, refer to following Microsoft document. The following link will take you to a resource outside of AskF5, and it is possible that the document may be removed without our knowledge:

MSDN Activating Active X Controls

Note: For information about FirePass support of IE7, refer to K6740: Support for Internet Explorer 7.
