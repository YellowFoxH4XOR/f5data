Topic

The connection reaper prevents idle connections from remaining in the connection table after the connections have been idle for longer than the configured idle timeout value. The reaper is implemented as a single system-wide process that sweeps the connection table every 6.4 seconds, plus or minus one second, looking for idle connections. Using a single periodic reaper process rather than an individual idle timer for each connection minimizes the impact on system resources. However, as a result, connections may not be removed from the connection table exactly when their idle time exceeds the configured idle timeout. With each sweep, idle connections are removed from the connection table if their idle time exceeds the idle timeout, or if their idle time will exceed the timeout within the next second. Therefore, an idle connection may be removed from the table up to 6.4 seconds after its idle time exceeds the idle timeout value, or up to 1 second before (which is a window of 7.4 seconds).
