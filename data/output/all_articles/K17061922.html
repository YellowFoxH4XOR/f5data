Known Issue

Subscriber traffic using a NAT64 Large Scale NAT (LSN) pool may fail.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • Hairpin mode is enabled for the NAT64 LSN pool.

Impact

Subscriber traffic using the affected NAT64 LSN pool fails.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Subscriber traffic using a NAT64 LSN pool fails.

Resolution

Status

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can perform hairpinning of traffic on an upstream router.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
