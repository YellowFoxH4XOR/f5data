K000132166: 10G and 25G interfaces on rSeries 5000 and 10000 series systems may fail to establish links with Cisco Nexus switches

Type
Known Issue
Published
2023-01-26
Updated
2023-02-10
Applies to
  • F5OS
  • F5OS-A: 1.2.0, 1.3.0, 1.3.1
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000132166

The 10G and 25G interfaces on F5 rSeries 5000 and 10000 series systems may fail to establish links with Cisco Nexus 9000, 7000, or 5000 series switches. When this issue occurs, the network links either cannot be established or are suspended resulting in no network connectivity between the rSeries system and the Cisco Nexus switch. In some cases, the link fails to connect after a system shutdown or reboot or after an interface shutdown.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have an F5 rSeries 5000 or 10000 series system.
  • You connect a 10G or 25G interface on the rSeries system to a Cisco Nexus 9000, 7000, or 5000 series switch port.
  • The interface on the rSeries system attempts to connect after a shutdown or reboot, or after disabling then re-enabling the interface.

Impact

The network link fails to establish resulting in no network connectivity between the rSeries system and the Cisco Nexus switch.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

Note: This issue may be more likely to occur after upgrading to F5OS-A 1.3.1.

  • One or more 10G or 25G interfaces on the rSeries system is marked down when they are connected and expected to be marked up.

Workaround

If you have not upgraded to F5OS-A 1.3.1, F5 recommends you wait to upgrade until you can upgrade to a fixed F5OS-A version. If you have already upgraded to F5OS-A 1.3.1, avoid cycling the 10G and 25G interfaces on the rSeries system until you have upgraded to a fixed F5OS-A version.

If you are currently experiencing problems with 10G and 25G rSeries interfaces connecting to Nexus switches, you may be able to recover the state by rebooting the rSeries system. If any interfaces are still down on the rSeries system after the reboot, disable then re-enable the interfaces one at a time. For each downed interface, first disable the interface on the rSeries system (not the Nexus switch), then wait 30-40 seconds and re-enable the interface. You may need to perform this task several times before the interface connects again. If the issue persists, create a case with F5 Support.

To disable and re-enable an interface on the rSeries system, perform the following procedure:

Impact of procedure: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the webUI of the rSeries system.
  2. Go to NETWORK SETTINGS > Interfaces.
  3. Select the 10G or 25G interface.
  4. For State, select Disabled.
  5. Select Save & Close to disable the interface.
  6. To re-enable the interface, select the interface again.
  7. For State, select Enabled.
  8. Select Save & Close to re-enable the interface.
