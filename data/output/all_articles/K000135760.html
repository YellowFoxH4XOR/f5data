K000135760: Assets are not currently available on MyF5

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-08-15
Updated
2025-08-21
Applies to
  • Support Portal
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000135760

Description

The My Products & Plans portion of MyF5 does not yet have asset information available. Only Licenses that are purchased directly through the MyF5 portal are visible.

 

Environment

  • My F5
  • My Products & Plans

 

Cause

None

 

Recommended Actions

If you or your company have purchased Subscription licenses, you can reach out to our Customer Success team at CustomerSuccess@F5.com to gain access to those subscription licenses.

 

Additional Information

The MyF5 portal does have the ability to include Asset information, which is a great addition from the old support portal where this was not an option. All other hardware and perpetual license assets are not yet available, but it is on our road map to include these in the future.
