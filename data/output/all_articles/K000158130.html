Description

  • Always-On Management (AOM) unexpectedly resets itself on B2250 blade after several hundred (~600) day AOM uptime.
  • This is the B2250 blade presentation of ID 562296 issue documented in K29384547 for appliances.

Environment

B2250 blade with high power on time. While the blade is powered the AOM is on.

Cause

ID 562296

Recommended Actions

None.

Additional Information

There is lop (lights out processor) in each blade, and this is where the AOM functionality resides. After it restarts chassis fan status may be falsely reported as bad until the lop can connect to the chassis again to get good data.
Unlike in the appliances there is no flapping of management interface.
After the reset the AOM is expected to reset after another several hundred days.
