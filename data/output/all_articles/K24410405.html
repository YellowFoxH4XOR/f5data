Known Issue

The BIG-IP system does not forward Session Initiation Protocol (SIP) SUBSCRIBE messages.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is configured as a SIP Application Level Gateway (ALG) with translation.
  • A SIP client sends a SUBSCRIBE message to receive a notification.

Impact

The SIP client does not receive any notification regarding the subscribed events.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • SIP servers do not receive subscription requests.
