Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If you change the Parent Profile setting for a Client SSL profile, you must click Update twice for the change to take effect. If you only click Update once, the change is saved but not loaded.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR139858.

Note: This issue does not affect BIG-IP 10.1.0 and later.
