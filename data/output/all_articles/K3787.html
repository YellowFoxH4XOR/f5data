Known Issue

This is likely the result of a known issue. BIG-IP may lock up during bootup, just after the BIG-IP kernel version statement is displayed.

When this issue occurs, the LEDs display the following status:

Power LEDGreen
Status LEDOff
Activity LEDYellow
Alarm LEDYellow

This issue is very rare and the cause has not been determined.

You can work around this issue by power-cycling the system. On the next boot, it should work normally.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR29843.
