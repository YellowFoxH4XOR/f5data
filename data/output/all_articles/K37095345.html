Description

You notice issues regarding SNMP, your monitoring devices don't get SNMP answers anymore from the BIG-IP.

On a packet capture you can see the requests coming in, but no response from BIG-IP side:

10.200.70.30 → 192.168.1.101   161   SNMP get-next-request 1.3.6.1.2.1.1
10.200.70.30 → 192.168.1.101   161   SNMP get-next-request 1.3.6.1.2.1.1
10.200.70.30 → 192.168.1.101   161   SNMP get-next-request 1.3.6.1.2.1.1
10.200.70.30 → 192.168.1.101   161   SNMP get-next-request 1.3.6.1.2.1.1

You have already tried restarting the snmp daemon several times with no success to resolve.

You may also notice high CPU on control plane due to SNMPD and MCPD.

Environment

  • SNMP stops responding suddenly
  • SNMP requests from monitoring tool time out
  • High CPU

Cause

With large configurations, it is possible to overburden MCPD and SNMPD, such that client queries time out. This is due to: Bug ID 798885: SNMP response times may be long due to processing burden of requests.

Recommended Actions

To confirm this Bug ID is affecting you can:

  1. Check the daemons consuming more CPU with the top command, and is possible that MCPD and SNMPD are on the top consumers constantly.
  2. Testing an SNMP request with -t option on snmpwalk to indicate a larger timeout, should get a successful response. For example:

    snmpwalk -t 30 -v 2c -c public 192.168.1.101 1.3.6.1.4.1.3375.2.2.11.1.3.1.2

To workaround the issue, it may be necessary to lengthen the timeout and retry values used by the SNMP client (on your SNMP monitoring system).
