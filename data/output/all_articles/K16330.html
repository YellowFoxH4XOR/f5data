Issue

Error Message

Sensors out of range:
PS2: Meas. Present GPIO                             : 0x0000

Message Location

You may encounter this message in the following location:

  • Output from the E --- Display error report option in the AOM Command Menu

Description

This message occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your system runs on one of the following platforms:

    • BIG-IP 2000 series
    • BIG-IP 4000 series
    • BIG-IP 5000 series
    • BIG-IP 7000 series
    • BIG-IP 10000 series
    • BIG-IP 12000 series
    • VIPRION B2250 blade
  • The second power supply is not available.

    Note: This can occur when the second power supply bay is empty, or when the system is unable to communicate with the second power supply.

Impact

For BIG-IP systems with a second power supply installed, there could be an issue with the second power supply (for example, the power supply unit is not seated appropriately). However, this error message is benign for BIG-IP systems that do not have a second power supply installed.

Recommended Actions

  • If your BIG-IP system does not have a second power supply installed, then you can disregard the error message.
  • If your BIG-IP system has a second power supply installed, you should contact F5 Support to further troubleshoot the potential issue.
