Description

TMM keeps restarting and generating cores after upgrading 13.1.3.5

  • Observe traffic disruptions due to multiple TMM restarts
  • See "vdag failed to attach" to /var/log/tmm log file

Environment

  • vCMP Guests running 13.1.3.5 after upgrade
  • VIPRION platform
  • vCMP host running any version other than v13.1.3.5

Cause

This happens due to a known Bug ID 727031.

Recommended Actions

  • Reach out to F5 support and get an Engineering Hot Fix (EHF)
(OR)
  • Upgrade to 14.1.0 or later.

Additional Information

None.
