Description

You could start seeing some daemons restarting by itself because an Out Of memory 
The device run out of Memory 
You could receive the following messages in /var/log/daemon.log:

Jan 01 03:04:33 bigip1.example.com err systemd[1]: Timed out waiting for device dev-md3.device.
Jan 01 03:04:33 bigip1.example.com err systemd[1]: Dependency failed for /dev/md3.
Jan 01 03:04:33 bigip1.example.com notice systemd[1]: Job dev-md3.swap/start failed with result 'dependency'.
Jan 01 03:04:33 bigip1.example.com notice systemd[1]: Job dev-md3.device/start failed with result 'timeout'.

On /var/log/kern.log:

Out of memory: Kill process 14096 (tmm.0) score 14 or sacrifice child

The top output show no swap assigned: 

Mem:  32906560k total, 32680780k used,   225780k free,     6356k buffers
Swap:        0k total,        0k used,        0k free,   442332k cached

Environment

  • After Upgrade to version 14.X
  • Daemons (ex: TMM) repeatedly restarting

Cause

Bug ID 762097

Complete the Workaround in the Bug Tracker for ID762097
