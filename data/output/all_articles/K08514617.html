Known Issue

The BIG-IP ASM system incorrectly reports latency-based denial-of-service (DoS) attacks.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The virtual server is configured with a DoS profile.
  • In the DoS profile, latency-based DoS anomaly detection is configured to operate in Transparency mode.
  • Your BIG-IP ASM system detects high latency before a suspicious entity is detected.

Impact

The BIG-IP ASM system incorrectly reports a latency-based DoS attack has started.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP ASM system reports a DoS attack without any associated IP addresses or URLs with high transactions per second (TPS).
  • Your BIG-IP ASM system reports a DoS attack when high latency is detected.
