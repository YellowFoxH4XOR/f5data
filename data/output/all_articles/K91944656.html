Policy Information

F5 has released new BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) products that are supported up to BIG-IP 18.x. The new BIG-IP VE product offers the broadest range of performance options and software application services, including the best-value Good, Better, Best bundle licenses. With V18, customers can now take advantage of the new features planned in future BIG-IP releases.

With the availability of BIG-IP VE V18 licenses, F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of BIG-IP VE V16 licenses, effective September 30, 2019. F5 is providing advance product notification to customers and partners announcing intent to EoS the products listed in the following table.
