Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Configuring a persistence timeout that is less than or equal to 32 seconds may result in persistence entries being prematurely removed when the persistence sweeper checks the status of the persistence entries.

If mirroring is enabled for the persistence profile, mirrored persistence entries are also affected.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR127125 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 10.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.

This issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-10.0.1-402.7-HF4 issued for BIG-IP version 10.0.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
