Description
One or both of the devices will become disconnected after provisioning APM on Google Cloud.
Similar message will be observed in LTM logs:
err mcpd[5239]: 0107142f:3: Can't connect to CMI peer xx.xx.xx.xx, TMM outbound listener not yet created

Environment

  • LTM VE
  • Google Cloud

Cause
This could be caused by Bug ID 1014361.

Recommended Actions

Provision APM module together with LTM concurrently when deploying the VE on Google Cloud.
