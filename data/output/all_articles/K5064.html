Known Issue

This is most likely the result of a known issue. When several hundred BIG-IP systems are added to a 3-DNS configuration, 3dnsd may crash.

Because a real configuration using several hundred BIG-IP systems is unlikely, this problem is only likely to occur during performance or stress testing.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR47531 and CR47532.
