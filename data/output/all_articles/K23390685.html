Known Issue

The sod process may produce a core file after you upgrade BIG-IP software.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP devices are running 10.x and are configured in a high availability (HA) configuration.
  • You upgraded the HA pair to one of the following BIG-IP 12.x versions:
    • 12.1.1
    • 12.1.0
    • 12.0.0
  • You rebooted the HA pair into the upgraded software version.

Impact

The sod process may restart on both HA devices concurrently after both of the devices boot into the upgraded software volume. As a result, traffic processing may be temporarily affected while the daemon restarts.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The sod process on both devices in the HA configuration may restart and produce a core file, after both of the devices reboot into the upgraded software volume.
  • Both devices log messages to the /var/log/messages file that appear similar to the following examples:

    notice logger: Started writing core file: /var/core/sod.bld1.0.196.core.gz for PID <PID>
    notice logger: Finished writing 13036478 bytes for core file: /var/core/sod.bld1.0.196.core.gz for PID <PID>

Workaround

No workaround is required; the BIG-IP devices should recover automatically after the failed daemon restarts. To avoid this issue on subsequent 10.x upgrades on HA pair devices to the affected software version listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, you can first perform an intermediate upgrade to a supported 11.x software version before upgrading the devices to the desired software version.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.
