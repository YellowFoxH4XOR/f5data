Topic

Important: This Solution applies only to the BIG-IP 4100, 4300, 6400, 6800, 8400, and 8800 platforms.

F5 platforms are now shipped with different power supplies. Platforms that formerly shipped with a 400W power supply now ship with a 425W power supply. Platforms that shipped with a 460W power supply now ship with a 475W power supply.

Platforms going from the 460W to the 475W power supply

On Nov. 8, 2007, F5 switched power supply units (PSU) used for the 4300, 8400, and 8800 platforms from a 460W PSU to a 475W PSU. The 475W PSU is not compatible with the existing 460W PSU; if a system is configured with mixed PSUs, it will cause serious damage to the unit. If you are replacing the power supply in a system, ensure that the matching PSUs are replaced at the same time.

Note: F5 replaces only the Alpha units with Cherokee units in the event of an RMA. Customers may proactively purchase Cherokee units, by referencing the information in the SKU number changes section below.

The new 475W power supplies have the same connectors as the 460W power supplies, and can be installed in any 4300, 8400, and 8800 platform.

Important: The power requirements for the 8800 platform require both 460W power supplies to be operational to provide adequate power for the 8800 platform. Upgrading to the 475W power supplies allows the 8800 platform to operate on a single power supply, allowing the second power supply to provide full power redundancy.

Important: The 475W (Cherokee) power supplies may be incorrectly labeled as 460W. Refer to the photographs at the bottom of this Solution to determine whether a PSU is 460W (Alpha) or 475W (Cherokee).

The following shipping changes came into effect Nov. 8, 2007:

  • 8800 platforms ship with two 475W PSUs
  • 4300 and 8400 platforms will ship with 460W until the current stock is exhausted; after the stock is exhausted, the 4300 platforms will ship with a pair of 475W PSUs, and the8400 platforms will ship with a single 475W PSU

Note: The current stock of 460W power supplies has been exhausted.

SKU number changes

As of Nov. 8, 2007, the new F5-UPG-AC-475W-R SKU is used, and the 460W UPG SKU is obsolete. Customers who purchase this new SKU will receive two of the new power supplies. This SKU will be included in the January 2008 price list.

The F5-UPG-AC-475W-R SKU will replace the following SKUs:

  • F5-UPG-PS-RED-AC-8400-RS
  • F5-UPG-PS-RED-AC-8800-RS
  • F5-UPG-FP-PS-RED-AC-4300-RS

Platforms going from the 400W to the 425W power supply

On March 1, 2008, F5 switched power supply units (PSU) used for the 4100, 6400, and 6800 platforms from a 400W PSU to a 425W PSU. The 425W PSU is not compatible with the existing 400W PSU; if a system is configured with mixed PSUs, it will cause serious damage to the unit. If you are replacing the power supply in a system, ensure that the matching PSUs are replaced at the same time.

Note: F5 replaces only the Alpha units with Cherokee units in the event of an RMA. Customers may proactively purchase Cherokee units, by referencing the information in the SKU number changes section below.

The new 425W power supplies have the same connectors as the 400W power supplies, and can be installed in any 4100, 6400, and 6800 platform.

The following shipping changes came into effect March 1, 2007:

  • 6800 FIPS platforms ship with two 425W PSUs
  • 4100 and 6400 platforms will ship with 400W until the current stock is exhausted.

Note: The current stock of 400W power supplies has been exhausted.

SKU number changes

As of March 1, 2008, the new F5-UPG-AC-425W-R SKU is used, and the 400W UPG SKU is obsolete. Customers who purchase this new SKU will receive one new 425W power supply. This SKU will be included in the January 2008 price list.

The F5-UPG-AC-425W-R SKU will replace the following SKUs:

  • F5-OPT-BIG-PS-RED-AC-4100-RS
  • F5-OPT-BIG-PS-RED-AC-6400-RS
  • F5-OPT-BIG-PS-RED-AC-6800-RS
  • F5-OPT-FP-PS-RED-AC-4100-RS
  • F5-UPG-BIG-PS-RED-AC-4100-RS
  • F5-UPG-BIG-PS-RED-AC-6400-RS
  • F5-UPG-BIG-PS-RED-AC-6800-RS
  • F5-UPG-FP-PS-RED-AC-4100-RS

Changing power supplies

Note: For information about adding and replacing a power supply, refer to K9686: Adding and replacing power supplies.

Changing power supplies is an easy customer field upgrade. To do so, shut down the system completely, and then install both PSUs.

Important: If you are using the 8800 platform, you must upgrade to SCCP version 12.0.5 or later to support full power redundancy. SCCP 12.0.5 is included with BIG-IP version 9.4.3 or can be downloaded separately from the F5 Downloads site. For information about displaying the SCCP version number, refer to K3454: Overview of the SCCP.

Change in PSU appearance

The Alpha power supplies are the older power supplies, which exist in two varieties:

  • 460W, which were available for the 8x00 and 4300 platforms
  • 400W, which were available for the 6x00 and 4100 platforms

  • 475W, which are available for the 8400, 8800 and 4300 units
  • 425W, which are available for the 6400, 6800, and 4100 units
