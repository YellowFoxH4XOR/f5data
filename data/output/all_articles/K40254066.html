Known Issue

Some failover configuration features behave unexpectedly or fail to take effect.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • In BIG-IP 12.0.0, the system loads the multicast failover configuration from the configuration files.
  • In all other affected versions of BIG-IP, you make multiple changes to the CM device configuration that include a unicast address list change.

Impact

In BIG-IP 12.0.0, when only multicast failover is configured on your system, traffic groups may become active on all devices in the device group. However, when unicast failover is also configured on your system, the traffic group unexpectedly switches to a different device.

In all other affected versions of BIG-IP, when you change the unicast address list at the same time you change other CM device properties, sod (the failover daemon) fails to recognize other changes.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • In BIG-IP 12.0.0, your system is configured with both multicast and unicast failover and the traffic group unexpectedly switches to a different device.
  • In all other affected versions of BIG-IP, you change the unicast address list at the same time you change other CM device properties, and the sod process fails to recognize one of the other changes.

Workaround

There is no workaround for this issue. However, you may be able to mitigate this issue in BIG-IP 12.0.0 by restoring multicast failover, and in all other affected versions of BIG-IP, by making CM device unicast address list changes separately from other device changes.

To restore multicast failover in BIG-IP 12.0.0, disable and then reenable it by doing the following:

  1. Determine the interface used for multicast failover by typing the following Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) command:

    list cm device device1 multicast-interface

  2. Disable and reenable the multicast failover by typing the following tmsh commands:

    modify cm device1 { multicast-interface none }

    modify cm device1 { multicast-interface eth0 }

Impact of mitigation: The impact of the suggested mitigation depends on the specific environment. F5 recommends that you test any such changes during a maintenance window and consider the possible impact on your specific environment.

You may also want to consider taking the following alternative action to solve the issue:

  • In BIG-IP 12.0.0, upgrade to 12.1.0.
  • In all other affected versions of BIG-IP, apply the hotfix for your version.
