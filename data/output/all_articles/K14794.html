Known Issue

The BIG-IP GTM Longest Match option does not sort topology records as expected.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your system's topology records contain a wildcard or negation in the source or destination section.
  • You have the Longest Match option enabled.

    Note: The Longest Match option is enabled by default.

When the Longest Match option is enabled, the BIG-IP system automatically sorts any topology records that are added to the Topology List. You expect the system to sort wildcard records to the bottom of the list, and sort records that contain a negation just above any wildcard records. However, as a result of this issue, the system sorts wildcard and negation records before other record types.

Impact

Incorrect sorting of topology records may result in unexpected resolutions from wide IPs that use topology load balancing.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H435086 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • When viewing topology records from the Configuration utility or the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh), you find topology records with a wildcard or negation in the source or destination section sorted before other record types. For example: Region, ISP, State, and so on.
  • You observe unexpected resolutions from wide IPs that use topology load balancing.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can disable the Longest Match option and manually sort the topology records. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: When adding topology records, you must manually sort them.

Disabling the Longest Match option

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP Configuration utility.
  2. From the Main tab, expand the Global Traffic menu.
  3. Click Topology.
  4. From the Topology:Records page, click Change Order.
  5. Under the Change Topology Order settings, clear the Longest Match box.
  6. Manually sort the Topology Record List records.
  7. Click Update.
