Description

While performing SAML authentication, BIG-IP APM throws an error message and denies successful authentication.

With looking at the APM logs, we can spot a message like: "SAML Agent: /Common/apm_accessprofile_act_saml_auth_1_ag SAML assertion is invalid, error: Assertion status is not successful".

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM
  • APM as SAML Service Provider [SP]

Cause

This may be because the SAML response, from the Identity Provider [IDP], is missing the following tag: <samlp:Status><samlp:StatusCode Value="urn:oasis:names:tc:SAML:2.0:status:Success" /></samlp:Status>

Recommended Actions

Please engage the SAML IDP team as to investigate with the "<Status />" may be missing.
