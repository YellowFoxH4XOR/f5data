Topic

You should consider using this procedure under the following conditions:

  • You have the F5 Application Visibility and Reporting module enabled.
  • You have any module enabled that requires Application Visibility and Reporting functionality as part of its normal operations: APM, Adv. WAF/ASM, AFM, PEM.
  • You want to stop the Application Visibility and Reporting module from consuming CPU resources by way of MySQL.
  • You are temporarily experiencing extreme traffic loads.

Description

Under normal conditions, certain modules implicitly or explicitly enable the Application Visibility and Reporting module when they are provisioned. These modules are APM, AFM, Adv. WAF/ASM, PEM, and Application Visibility and Reporting. When Application Visibility and Reporting is enabled, it aggregates data as part of its normal operations. This aggregation processes on an internal schedule every 5 minutes, processing the previous 5 minutes of data.  Every hour, the data from the previous hour is processed and aggregated from the 5-minute data. A similar process occurs every 24 hours and 30 days, processing all the data collected since the previous 24-hour or 30-day aggregation, respectively.

The timing of these aggregation processes is linked to the BIG-IP system's local time and are not changeable. The 24-hour aggregation occurs at either 23:00 (11:00 PM) or 24:00 (12:00 AM) local time depending on whether Daylight Saving/Summer time is enabled or not. The 30-day aggregation occurs on a date near the beginning of the calendar month, also taking place at 23:00 (11:00 PM) or 24:00 (12:00 AM). The next scheduled 30-day aggregation occurs November 3, 2020 at 11:00 PM or November 4, 2020 12:00 AM for time zones without Daylight Saving/Summer time.

The Application Visibility and Reporting aggregation process introduces increased CPU usage. Under normal and high load conditions, this increased CPU does not have any negative effect on traffic or ability to manage the BIG-IP system. With proper scaling and excess capacity, this effect on CPU is reduced even further. Using the BIG-IQ system to manage and collect statistics from managed BIG-IP devices also removes most Application Visibility and Reporting data from the BIG-IP systems in question and eliminates most of the CPU used by Application Visibility and Reporting. In extreme traffic circumstances, the amount of CPU used by Application Visibility and Reporting may impact system performance. If you are experiencing such a "worst case scenario" of extreme traffic levels, it is possible to temporarily halt the aggregation by shutting down the monpd process with the following procedures.

Prerequisites

You must meet the following prerequisite to use these procedures:

  • Have administrative access to the BIG-IP system.

Procedures

  • Stop Application Visibility and Reporting from consuming CPU resources by way of MySQL
  • Restore MySQL functionality

Stop Application Visibility and Reporting from consuming CPU resources by way of MySQL

You can stop Application Visibility and Reporting from consuming CPU resources by shutting down the monpd process on all applicable BIG-IP units. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of procedure: Shutting down monpd stops Application Visibility and Reporting from generating reports (including REST, TMOS Shell [tmsh], Configuration utility, etc.). The Application Visibility and Reporting statistics data are permanently lost during the monpd downtime period.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP command line.
  2. To stop the monpd process, enter the following command:

    clsh bigstart stop monpd

  3. Perform the previous steps on the remaining applicable BIG-IP units.

Note: Under /var/log/avr/avrd.log, error messages may appear similar to the following example: Not OK to write CSV files: too many CSV files (over 1000) in directory /var/avr/loader

Restore MySQL functionality

You can restore Application Visibility and Reporting and MySQL functionality by starting the monpd process after clearing out the /var/avr/loader directory. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of procedure: Re-enabling monpd by performing the following procedure allows data collected after re-enabling to become visible again.

Note: The Application Visibility and Reporting statistics data is permanently lost during the monpd downtime period, and is not visible in any reports.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP command line.
  2. To clear files in the /var/avr/loader directory, enter the following command:

    clsh rm -f /var/avr/loader/*

  3. To start the monpd process, enter the following command:

    clsh bigstart start monpd

  4. Perform the previous steps on the remaining applicable BIG-IP units.
