Description

On  April 15th, 2021 Codecov published a vulnerability titled "Bash Uploader Security Update" and forwarded a description and guidelines document, plus a communicate to all its users and partners.

 

Environment

  • BIG-IP and other F5 products.


Cause

Customer might have doubts about F5 products being affected by Codecov's published vulnerability, due to usage of RedHat, CentOS and Open Source software.


Recommended Actions


The following information has been confirmed:
 

F5 products' OS:  F5 OS and VELOS are NOT VULNERABLE because they do not use CodeCov in our VELOS Software.

F5 was not notified by Codecov (as being affected by Codecov as a product).  However, our company did conduct a thorough and systematic investigation of all our CI/CD pipelines and confirmed no instances of compromise.  In addition, our company took additional steps to ensure our environment remain secure.
