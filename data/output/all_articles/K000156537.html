Description

  • You attempt to download NGINX Plus software from the downloads section of MyF5 but are presented with only NGINX Service Mesh in the Product Line drop down menu like the example below;

Environment

  • NGINX Plus
  • MyF5
  • Downloads
  • Subscriptions

Cause

  • NGINX Service Mesh is available to download for all users. However all other NGINX products will be available if you select an account on your profile which has an NGINX Plus subscription

Recommended Actions

  • If your profile is associated to an account with an NGINX Plus subscription or NGINX Plus trial, switch the account you are downloading from, by selecting the dropdown option under accoun

  • If your Nginx Plus subscription is NOT associated to your profile, Contact Support and provide the subscription ID so it can be associated to your profile.
