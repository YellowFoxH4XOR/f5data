Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP LTM incorrectly reports an invalid staged changeset to Enterprise Manager as successful/verified.

If an invalid changeset or template is created on the Enterprise Manager, and the changeset or template is selected to verify/deploy against a BIG-IP LTM, the BIG-IP LTM will report a successful changeset deployment. When viewing the BIG-IP LTM configuration, the changeset additions are missing from the configuration.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR79061 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.3.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
