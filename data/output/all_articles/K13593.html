Topic

You should consider using this procedure under the following condition:

  • You want to restrict access to only web resources defined on the webtop.

Description

By default, a full webtop resource enables you to enter additional URLs. There are times when you may want to remove the URL box from a BIG-IP APM full webtop. This restricts access to only web resources that are defined on the webtop.

Prerequisites

You must meet the following prerequisite to use this procedure:

  • You have a BIG-IP APM access policy configured with a full webtop.

Procedures

Removing the URL box from the webtop

Impact of procedure: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP Configuration utility.
  2. Navigate to Access Policy > Customization > Advanced.
  3. Expand Customization Settings.
  4. Expand Access Profiles.
  5. Expand the Common section. 
  6. Set Edit Mode to Advanced.
  7. Expand the folder for the webtop you want to customize.
  8. Click apm_full.css.
  9. Click into the Advanced Editor, then press Ctrl + F.
  10. In the find box, enter the following text:

    div#webtop_toolbar .left
  11. Click OK.
  12. Move the cursor to the end of the following line:

    float:left;

  13. Press Enter.

    A new line is created.
  14. Enter the following text:

    visibility:hidden;
  15. Click Save Draft.
  16. For Do you want to save your changes?, click Yes.
  17. In the upper-left corner of the editor, click the Save icon.
  18. Navigate to Access Profiles > Access Profiles List.
  19. Select the check box to the left of the customized Access Profile.
  20. Click Apply Access Policy.

    Note: You may need to clear your browser cache and restart your browser prior to testing to view these changes.
