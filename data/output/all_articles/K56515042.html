Description

After a VLAN was created or deleted, standby device starts to exhibit very high throughput, approximately 24 Gbps, with high service throughput observed in the performance graphs.

Environment

  • BIG-IP 5000, 7000, VIPRION B2100
  • Network interfaces
  • VLAN

Cause

Bug ID 698619: Disable port bridging on HSB ports for non-vCMP systems

Note: The loop is internal and should not affect the external network.

Recommended Actions

Upgrade to non-affected version.

Additional Information

This issue may be seen more frequently when STP is enabled, due to the following bug:

  • Bug ID 686059: FDB entries for existing VLANs may be flushed when creating a new VLAN.
