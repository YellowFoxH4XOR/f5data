Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When the BIG-IP WebAccelerator inserts a no-cache header into a response, the pvac process may not accurately determine the length of the no-cache header field. As a result, the pvac process may insert a corrupt no-cache header, or in rare cases the pvac process may core. 
 
F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR138286 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 10.2.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP WebAccelerator Release Notes.
 
This issue has been fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-10.1.0-3372.0-HF1 for BIG-IP 10.1.0. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
