Known Issue

The BIG-IP ASM Overview screens may not respond or may fail to load.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • A large number of attack signatures are set as In Staging

Impact

The BIG-IP ASM Overview screens may fail to load.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • CPU usage may spike 80 to 90 percent for the httpd process, as shown in the following example of the top command:

    4769 apache 15 0 63076 35m 6348 S 80.5 0.9 0:06.14 httpd
  • CPU usage spikes may display in the Statistics > Performance screen and the Statistics dashboard.
  • The Signatures Staging Status counter may report the following message for 10 or more seconds at a time, during which the Overview screen may be unresponsive:

    Loading data, please wait
  • If the Overview screen is unresponsive for several minutes, the browser may report an HTTP 408 Request Timeout message.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can reduce the number of signatures in staging. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Before disabling staging on active attack signatures, you must ensure the attack signature is appropriate for your environment.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.
  2. Click Application Security.
  3. Click Attack Signatures.
  4. Click Policy Attack Signatures.
  5. Select an Attack Signature that you want to remove from staging.
  6. On the Policy Attack Signature Properties screen, clear the Perform Staging box.
  7. Click Update.
  8. Click Apply Policy.
