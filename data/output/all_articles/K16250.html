Known Issue

The mcpd process may restart continuously. This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The VIPRION system is provisioned as a Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP) host.
  • A vCMP guest is deployed to one or more blades.
  • One of the blades that the vCMP guest runs on is replaced or reimaged.

Impact

A blade that is replaced or reimaged is unable to rejoin the VIPRION cluster.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The mcpd process restarts continuously on the affected blade.
  • The VIPRION vCMP host repeatedly fails to provision resources for the vCMP guest on the affected blade, and reports an error message similar to the following example, to the /var/log/ltm file on the remaining functional blades, for every failed attempt:

    0107131f:3: Could not allocate vCMP guest (unknown) because unexpected failure during allocation of validated configuration.

Workaround

There is no work around for this issue. However, you can recover from this issue by manually restarting the affected blade. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the command line of the affected blade.
  2. To restart the blade, type the following command:

    shutdown -r now

  3. Wait for the affected blade to restart and rejoin the cluster.
