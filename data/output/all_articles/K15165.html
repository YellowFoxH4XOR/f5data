Known Issue

The BIG-IP 2000 and 4000 series platforms may generate higher-than-expected fan speeds.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are running the BIG-IP 2000 (C112) series platform or the 4000 (C113) series platform.
  • The LOP BuC Hybrid (LBH) firmware versions up to and including 4.05.

In LBH firmware versions 4.05 and earlier, the fan speeds were set to a higher range under normal operating temperatures. However, the higher fan speed is not problematic.

To determine the BIG-IP platform and LBH firmware version, you can check the tmsh show sys hardware command output. The BIG-IP platform is found under System Information as Type, and the LBH firmware version is listed as the Firmware version.

For example:

Hardware Version Information
Name lop
Type pic
Model F5
Boot loader version 1.04
Firmware version 4.04

System Information
Type C112

Impact

F5 has determined that the increased fan speed does not produce undue wear on the fans.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H456028 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • You notice an increase in fan noise coming from the BIG-IP platform.
  • You observe higher-than-expected fan speed under normal temperatures.
