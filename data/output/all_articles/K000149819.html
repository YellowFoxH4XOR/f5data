K000149819: "SSL, Forward Proxy, VE” optional module is not visible after reactivating the license.

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-02-17
Updated
2025-02-17
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP DNS: 16.1.X, 17.1.X, 17.1.2, 17.1.1, 17.1.0, 16.1.5, 16.1.4, 16.1.3, 16.1.2, 16.1.1, 16.1.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000149819

Description

Your device had a V16 plus license (F5-BIG-DNS-VE250KV16) that has an SSL, Forward Proxy, VE" under Optional Module. Then, you performed a reactivation to your device with a V18 plus license (F5-BIG-DNS-VE250KV18). and you noticed that under Optional Modules, there is no "SSL, Forward Proxy, VE".


Environment

  • DNS VE
  • Version Plus License


Cause

F5-BIG-DNS-VE250KV18 was released on 6/21/2019.  SSL Forward Proxy (F5-ADD-BIG-SSLFWD-VE) was not included as an option because it went end of sale on 1/1/2019.


Recommended Actions

Refer to this article for more information regarding the replacement of SSL Forward Proxy, VE.

K81031320: End of Sale announcement for BIG-IP SSL Forward Proxy License SKUs
