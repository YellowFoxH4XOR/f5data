Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When 100 or more DNS requests are sent at one time, the BIG-IP GTM listener TCP window can overflow. As a result, some DNS requests are dropped.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR74170 and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.3.0 and 9.4.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM release notes.
