Known Issue

HTTP compression in low-memory conditions may cause the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) to restart and produce a core file.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • As a result of a low-memory condition, TMM is unable to initialize the compression provider.

Impact

If the BIG-IP system is a member of a redundant configuration, traffic fails over to the standby BIG-IP system. If the BIG-IP system is not part of a redundant pair, traffic is interrupted until the TMM process restarts.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP system generates a TMM core file to the /shared/core directory.
  • The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to pass traffic.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can disable HTTP compression for the affected virtual server(s) until you can improve the low-memory condition.

Impact of workaround: The impact of the suggested workaround depends on the specific environment. F5 recommends testing any such changes during a maintenance window, with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.
