K000160960: F5OS-A replacement PSU does not appear in the output of show components component psu or related CLI commands.

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-04-23
Updated
2026-04-23
Applies to
  • F5OS
  • F5OS-A: 1.8.3
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000160960

Description

  • After installing a replacement PSU on an F5OS-A r2000 series (C130) appliance, the power supply is operational (green LED is on, system health and event logs confirm presence and activity). However, the replacement PSU does not appear in the output of show components component psu or related CLI commands. 
  • Attempts to view PSU details result in a syntax error, indicating it is not registered in the system's component inventory.
  • There are no active alarms, and the system health reports both PSUs as "ok".

Environment

  • Product: F5OS-A
  • Version: 1.8.3
  • r2000 series (C130)
  • PSU installed and operational (RMA replacement)
  • System health and event logs confirm PSU presence and activity
  • No errors related to FRU EEPROM corruption found in logs

Cause

ConfD registration failure for the replacement PSU, where the hardware is detected at the BMC and diag-agent levels but not propagated into the system's component inventory.

Answer/Recommended Actions

Restart the system_platform-mgr service:

 

    • docker restart system_platform-mgr
    • Note: Restarting this service may cause a system reboot. Perform this action during a maintenance window.

Additional Information

  • This issue does not impact the operational status of the PSU; it is a display/inventory issue only.
  • No active alarms are present, and the system continues to function normally.
  • The absence of errors such as "wrong common header" or "wrong zero checksum" in platform-hal logs indicates that FRU EEPROM corruption is not the cause.
