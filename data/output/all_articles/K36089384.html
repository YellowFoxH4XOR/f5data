Known Issue

An internal unicast flood may occur when a Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP) guest spans fewer slots than the host's trunk.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a VIPRION system provisioned for vCMP.
  • Your VIPRION vCMP host configuration includes a trunk.
  • You deployed a vCMP guest that spans fewer slots than the vCMP host's trunk.

Impact

Unicast packets being broadcast to all of the vCMP host Traffic Management Microkernels (TMM) may cause traffic processing delays or dropped packets.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Remote clients experience delayed or dropped connections.
  • The vCMP host CPU is abnormally high for brief periods.
  • Packet captures taken from next hop devices reveal a small number of latent or missing packets.

Workaround

You can reduce the occurrence of this issue by ensuring every pool member has a health monitor applied.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
