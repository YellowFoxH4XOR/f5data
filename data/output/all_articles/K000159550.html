Description

BIG-IP system may failed to reload configuration after license renewal, displaying error which is preventing the device from becoming operational. 

You may also encounter those logs in the /var/log/ltm on BIG-IQ:

"01070582:3: Monitor rules are not licensed: min 1 of /Common/[name] /Common/[name2]" 

Environment

  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IP
  • FCP licenses

Cause

Issue may occurred when trying to use the license from the catalog without reactivating it earlier so the Service Check Date is not updated.

Recommended Actions

After log in to BIG-IQ first what I needed is to go Devices -> Licenses -> [catalog name] -> Reactivate.
