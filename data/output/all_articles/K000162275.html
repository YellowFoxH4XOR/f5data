K000162275: NGINX App Protect/WAF Repository Access Denied Due to Subscription Tier

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-07-15
Updated
2026-07-15
Applies to
  • NGINX Products
  • F5 WAF for NGINX
  • F5 DoS for NGINX
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000162275

Description

A 403 error occurs when attempting to access the NGINX App Protect (WAF) repository at https://pkgs.nginx.com/app-protect, while access to the NGINX Plus repository is successful. The certificate subject CN matches the subscription page, and access to NGINX Plus is confirmed, but access to App Protect/WAF is denied.

Environment

  • Product: NGINX App Protect WAF
  • Subscription: NGINX ONE (Standard Tier)

Cause

The certificate and subscription are for the Standard Tier of NGINX ONE, which does not include access to premium products such as App Protect/WAF. The 403 error is expected behavior when attempting to access repositories for products not covered by the subscription.

Answer/Recommended Actions

  • Verify with your F5 account manager whether your subscription is in the Premium Tier.
  • If access to App Protect/WAF is required, upgrade the subscription to the Premium Tier to obtain the appropriate certificate.
  • Contact the sales department to correct the subscription tier if necessary.

Additional Information

Access to App Protect/WAF is a premium feature and not included in the Standard Tier of NGINX ONE. The certificate subject CN matching the subscription page confirms the certificate is valid for the products included in the current tier. Receiving a 403 error when accessing the App Protect/WAF repository is expected if the subscription does not cover this product.
