Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP system may incorrectly drop a RST packet for an established flow when Delayed Acks are enabled in the TCP profile, and the BIG-IP system is holding a pending ACK with no other data to send to the peer.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR99973 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 10.0.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in BIGIP-9.4.8-385.0-HF2, which has been issued for BIG-IP version 9.4.8. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.

For information about the F5 hotfix policy, refer to K4918: Overview of the F5 critical issue hotfix policy.

For information about managing F5 product hotfixes, refer to K6845: Managing F5 product hotfixes.
