K4911: Preventing a disabled 3-DNS system from synchronizing configuration with members of the synchronization group

Topic

A disabled 3-DNS synchronization group member will not collect path-based or SNMP-based metrics from LDNS or host servers. However, a disabled 3-DNS system will continue to synchronize DNS zone and 3-DNS wide IP configuration information with other synchronization group members.

You can prevent the disabled 3-DNS system from synchronizing configuration information with active members of the synchronization group. To do so, perform the following procedure:

  1. Log in to the 3-DNS Configuration utility.
  2. Click System.
  3. Uncheck the Sync Enabled checkbox.
  4. Click the Update button.
