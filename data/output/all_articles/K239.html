Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When BIG-IP handles SIP connections through a SNAT, SIP persistence fails and BIG-IP creates multiple connections for each incoming SIP connection.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR27515 and CR41775.

Workaround

Do not use SIP persistence in conjunction with a SNAT.
