Known Issue

The BIG-IP system modifies source ports on network address translation (NAT) connections instead of preserving them.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You configure a NAT.
  • Your platform is the BIG-IP 2000/4000 series.

Impact

Applications that require the BIG-IP system to preserve the source port are affected.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When your BIG-IP system modifies the source port, applications fail or are treated incorrectly by back-end systems.
  • Your BIG-IP system changes the source port for connections handled by NATs.
