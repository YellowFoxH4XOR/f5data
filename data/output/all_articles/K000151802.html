K000151802: BIG-IQ prompt in CLI shows "REACTIVATE LICENSE"

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-06-12
Updated
2025-06-12
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 8.4.X, 8.4.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000151802

Description

The new BIG-IQ continues to display 'REACTIVATE LICENSE' even after being activated with an evaluation license.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ Lab
  • Evaluation license
  • License activation

Cause

The eval key was generated on October 15, 2024, prior to the BIG-IQ v8.4.0 release on March 26, 2025. As a result, any license from that key appears expired based on the Service Check Date.

in CLI, you will see below message, and Service Check Date remains at the generation date of the eval key.

admin@(test123)(cfg-sync Standalone)(REACTIVATE LICENSE)(/Common)(tmos)# show sys license | grep -i date
Warning: service check date has expired
License Start Date 2025/06/09
License End Date 2025/07/26
Service Check Date 2024/10/16 <<----------
Daily Renewal Notification Start Date 2025/07/22
admin@(test123)(cfg-sync Standalone)(REACTIVATE LICENSE)(/Common)(tmos)# 

Recommended Actions

Contact your Sales or Account team to obtain a new evaluation key generated after the BIG-IQ v8.4.0 release date.
