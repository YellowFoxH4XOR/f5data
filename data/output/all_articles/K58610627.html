Description

What problems, if any, should we expect when upgrading our BIG-IP to 15.1.x?

Environment

15.1.1.0

LTM

Cause

Planning to upgrade the BIG-IP system

Recommended Actions

Review K84554955: Overview of BIG-IP system software upgrades prior to an upgrade.

Take special note of:

  1. Make sure to update your license before you update. K7727: License activation may be required prior to a software upgrade for the BIG-IP or Enterprise Manager system.
  2. It wouldn't be a bad idea to take UCS backup after re-activating your license and before proceeding.  K4423: Overview of UCS archives.

Additional Information

Individual known caveats for 15.1.0.5 can be found in the 15.1.0.5 Release Notes.
Individual known caveats for 15.1.1.0 can be found in the 15.1.1.0 Release Notes.
