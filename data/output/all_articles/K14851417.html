K14851417: GUI won't come up after Virtual Machine reboot. SSH only

DESCRIPTION

After extending disk space and rebooting the VM GUI is not responding
Only SSH management access works

ENVIRONMENT
  • BIG-IQ 6.0.0
  • 4CPU / 16Gb RAM
CAUSE

The following can be seen repeating in the logs in /var/log/guiserver/current:

error: reached maximum attempts (1) for /shared/loader/available, giving up...
warn: availability check failed: failed to connect to REST service: , error: Error: connect ECONNREFUSED 127.0.0.1:8100
error: bigiqUpgrade: failed to query for cm:system:storage-upgrade:storageupgradestate: MongoError: not master and slaveOk=false
error: failed to connect to REST service: , error: Error: connect ECONNREFUSED 127.0.0.1:8100 (attempt #1)
warn: failed to connect to REST service: , error: Error: connect ECONNREFUSED 127.0.0.1:8100 (attempt #0)
error: reached maximum attempts (1) for /shared/loader/available, giving up...

GUI cannot come up due to that.

Starting in 6.x, we recommend a minimum of 8CPU / 32Gb for each device deployed in the config, specially when statistics collection is in place.

RECOMMENDED ACTIONS

Allocate more resources to VM at Hypervisor level
