Known Issue

Under rare conditions, when an IP host is unreachable, the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) restarts and produces a core file.

Impact

The BIG-IP system temporarily fails to process traffic while the TMM process restarts. If you configured your BIG-IP system for high availability (HA), it fails over to a peer system.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP system temporarily fails to process traffic as it recovers from the TMM restart.
  • TMM generates a core file in the /shared/core directory.
  • In the /var/log/tmm file, you observe log messages similar to the following example:

    notice panic: ../modules/hudfilter/hudfilter.c:1065: Assertion "valid node" failed.
    notice ** SIGFPE **
