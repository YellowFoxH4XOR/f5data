Description

A blade serial number is required when requesting a RMA for the following Fiber Gigabit Ethernet modules

  • SFP
  • XFP
  • SFP+
  • QSFP+
  • QSFP28


 

Environment

  • VIPRION

     

Cause
Those parts are blade specific and therefore requires the blade serial number and chassis serial number.

 

Recommended Actions

Provide us with qkview when you suspect those parts need to be replaced.
