Topic



The FirePass 1200 hardware platform complies with GR-63-CORE of the NEBS Criteria for acoustic noise emissions. The following table presents a breakdown of noise measurements at locations around the unit:

Measurement LocationNoise Level (db-A)
Back58
Front53
Right54
Left55

As indicated in the table, the FirePass 1200 does not exceed the maximum allowable sound level of 60 db-A, measured at a distance of .60 meters under normal operating circumstances, as required by GR-63-CORE, Section 4.6, Requirement R4-72 [128].
