Known Issue

The tmrouted process may generate a core file on the BIG-IP system. This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP system is configured to participate in an OSPFv3 routing network.
  • The BIG-IP system is configured as part of a high-availability (HA) device group.
  • A failover occurs on the BIG-IP system.

Additionally, the tmrouted process exits and restarts the routing services. As a result, advertised routes from the BIG-IP system may be withdrawn temporarily until the routing services restart and re-establish adjacency.

Impact

Traffic destined for the BIG-IP system may be disrupted temporarily due to the withdrawal of advertised routes from the system.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H432416 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium|High screen.
  • The tmrouted process produces a core file in the /shared/core directory.
  • Advertised routes from the affected BIG-IP system are withdrawn from the OSPFv3 routing network.
  • The BIG-IP system is not reachable on the OSPFv3 routing network.
