BIG-IP has the following hard-coded limits for concurrent connections:

ProcessorsProcessing ModeConcurrent Connection Limit
1SMP/ANIP5461
2ANIP5461
2SMP10922

This limit is a safety feature, designed to prevent the SSL Proxy from overburdening the CPU, which could result in system instability.
