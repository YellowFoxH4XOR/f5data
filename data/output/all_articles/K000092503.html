Description

The 'Device Management > Overview' ,'Device Management > Traffic Groups > Properties' and 'System > Support' pages stuck on "loading.."

Environment

  • BIG-IP HA
  • Configuration utility
  • CLI

Cause

Out-of-Memory condition is triggered, causing icrd processes to be reaped.

Example:

var/log/restjavad.0.log:
[WARNING][7903][11 Sep 2023 14:54:06 UTC][8100/shared/nsyncd NsyncdWorker] Bad response while waiting for icrd: java.lang.Exception: Unable to process request /tm/cm/device-group. Service is unavailable.
[WARNING][10712][11 Sep 2023 16:26:06 UTC][8100/shared/nsyncd NsyncdWorker] Bad response while waiting for icrd: java.lang.Exception: Unable to process request /tm/cm/device. Service is unavailable.

Recommended Actions

1) Perform the command in CLI below to recover the access

 bigstart restart restjavad


2) You may increase the Management provisioning level by following K26427018: Overview of Management provisioning

3) After increasing Management provisioning level, consider to increase memory for tomcat and restjavad by following K63328426: Increase memory for Tomcat and Restjavad when Out of Memory
