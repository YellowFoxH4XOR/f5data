Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • tcpdump
  • iRule with reject inside FLOW_INIT
  • vip-on-vip traffic

Cause

Seen behaviour is caused by a known issue tracked with the bug ID1821033

Recommended Actions

Change the reject command to drop command or moving the reject command out of FLOW_INIT and putting it inside CLIENT_ACCEPTED.
