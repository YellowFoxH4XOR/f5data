Topic

The Restriction of Hazardous Substances Directive (RoHS) specifies the maximum levels of substances such as lead, mercury, and cadmium in the components of manufactured electronic devices.

The European Union (EU) and the Chinese government require RoHS compliance for products to be exported to their jurisdictions. The RoHS standards used by the two regions are similar, but not identical. FirePass hardware became EU RoHS compliant in June 2006, and China RoHS compliant in March 2007. The China RoHS standard went into effect in March 2007, and did not require any additional FirePass hardware changes to the existing EU RoHS-compliant models. Only the additional labeling required for China RoHS compliance was added.

The following FirePass controller models are not RoHS-compliant:

  • FirePass 600
  • FirePass 1000
  • FirePass 4000

The following FirePass controller models are RoHS compliant for both the EU and China:

  • FirePass 1200
  • FirePass 4100
  • FirePass 4300

Note: Some older FirePass 4100 units are not RoHS compliant. These units will not have RoHS compliance stickers.

EU RoHS-compliance is indicated by a green sticker on the rear of the controller, with the following text:

RoHS Compliant
Directive 2002/95/EC

China RoHS-compliance is indicated by a white Environmentally Friendly Use Period (EFUP) sticker on the top-rear of the controller with the number 50 inside a circular orange recycling icon.

For more information about environmental guidelines, refer to the Environmental Guidelines and Hardware Specifications manual for your specific FirePass hardware platform.
