Description

  • After renewing the subscription for your BIG-IP VE and reactivating the license,  the configuration fails to load.
  • You observe the Error Message below on the Web GUI: 
    The configuration has not yet loaded. If this message persists, it may indicate a configuration problem

 

Environment

BIG-IP VE Subscription

License reactivation

Cause

The registration keys have not been remapped after renewing the BIG-IP VE subscription.

Recommended Actions

1- Follow the steps in K12183049 for mapping the registration keys from the renewed subscription.
K12183049: Mapping MyF5 registration keys after a subscription renewal

2- Reactivate the license
K7752: Licensing the BIG-IP system
