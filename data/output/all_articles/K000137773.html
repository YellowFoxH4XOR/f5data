Description

The AVR statistics are not stored locally on BIG-IP.

There is no data shown in the GUI: Statistics ›› Analytics : Virtual Servers : Traffic Details : Connections

 

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • Managed by BIG-IQ with stats collection enabled
  • AVR provisioned

Cause

When stats collection is enabled on BIG-IQ, stats are sent to BIG-IQ instead of being stored locally

Recommended Actions

View stats in the BIG-IQ Dashboard Monitoring tab.

Additional Information

Disabling stats collection on BIG-IQ will restore local stats collection on BIG-IP.
