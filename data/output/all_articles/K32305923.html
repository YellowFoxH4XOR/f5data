Known Issue

Reprovisioning BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) may cause the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) to produce a core file and restart.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • BIG-IP VE is licensed with a throughput rate limited license.
  • You attempt to license and provision additional modules.
  • BIG-IP VE does not reboot after reprovisioning.

This issue is triggered when provisioning additional modules raises the throughput requirement on your system, which may cause a mismatch of backplane MAC addresses and the number of TMMs.

Impact

BIG-IP VE temporarily fails to process traffic while the TMM process restarts. If BIG-IP VE is configured for high availability (HA), the system fails over to a peer system.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • In the /var/log/tmm file, you observe log messages similar to the following example:

    Unexpected backplane address

  • Your BIG-IP system generates a TMM core file to the /shared/core directory.

Resolution

Status

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can reboot BIG-IP VE after TMM restarts. This allows a hardware abstraction layer (HAL) to correctly reconfigure the backplane MAC addresses on your system.

Impact of workaround: Rebooting BIG-IP VE disrupts traffic processing until the system is back up. F5 recommends that you perform this operation during a maintenance window.
