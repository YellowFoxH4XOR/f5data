Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If you are running tcpdump on a VLAN to capture load balanced traffic, the tcpdump utility fails to capture the first packet in a TCP or UDP stream on PVA2 systems. This issue occurs when FastL4 virtual servers are configured with PVA acceleration set to none. While tcpdump does not record the packet, the packet is processed and load balanced properly by the BIG-IP system, and network traffic is not affected.

This issue occurs consistently on the 3400 and 6400 platforms. This issue also occurs intermittently on the 6800 platform due to the two internal network paths; on the 6800 platform one internal link passes through the PVA, and the second link bypasses the PVA.

Note: Systems equipped with the PVA10, such as the 8400 and the 8800 platforms, are not affected by this issue.

F5 Networks Product Development is tracking this issue as CR103529.
