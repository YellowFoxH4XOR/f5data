Known Issue

Some monitor types may fail to probe when you change the monitor definition.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • An FTP, IMAP, POP3, or SMTP monitor is configured and assigned to a pool or pool member.
  • You change the monitors configuration.

Impact

Pool member or node status may no longer be updated and the BIG-IP system no longer properly tracks the health of the monitored object.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • After you update the monitor configuration, your BIG-IP system fails to send monitor probes for FTP, IMAP, POP3, or SMTP monitors, for monitored objects (pool members or nodes).

Resolution

Status

F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. Workaround

To prevent one of the affected monitors from entering a non-operational state, F5 recommends that you remove the monitor from the pool or pool member before you make a change. Once you make the change, you can assign the monitor back to the original pool or pool member.
