Known Issue

BIG-IP APM logon page form header text may not render correctly.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your access policy contains a logon page.
  • The customization on the logon page form header text property contains quote characters.

Impact

The logon page does not render correctly.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When accessing the access policy logon page, the form header text does not render correctly.
