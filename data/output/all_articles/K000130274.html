Description:

When a device group is configured with incremental sync the following reason are why a device group would fall back to full sync instead of performing an incremental sync.

Environment:
  • Big-IP Device Services Clustering (DSC) HA
  • Device Group(DG) with incremental sync enabled (Automatic or Manual)

Cause:

Any of the following reason will force a DSC HA DG to issue a full sync verse incremental sync:
 
  • Older transactions, required by the out of sync device, are no longer in the incremental cache as they have been removed in order to make room for newer transactions
    • Examples
      • Single transaction with a size greater than the size of the cache
      • Multiple transactions exceeding the cache size
  • File(s) related to transaction is not found on disk
    • Example
      • Introduction of new cert/key pair
      • Introduction of new external datagroup
  • Unknown error or incremental cache corruption

Recommended Actions:

This knowledge article is meant to help understand why incremental sync reverted to full sync
