K000130624: SSL Orchestrator may display a blank page when browsing the "SSL configuration" or "Security Policies"

Published

Applies to

  • BIG-IP SSL Orchestrator: 14.1.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000130624

When browsing the page "SSL Orchestrator >> Configuration", the product shows individual menus such as "Security Policies" or "SSL Configurations".

Upon clicking any of those menus, the browser may display a blank page.

This issue may get its source from the current "security settings" of the BigIP themselves: System ›› Platform ›› Security: Management Port

 

What to do: please make sure there are rules that allow each device's management IP to connect to the peer management interface.
