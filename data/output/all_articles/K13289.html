Known Issue

The TMM process may not properly detect when it has lost connection to the mcpd process.

This issue may occur when the following conditions are met:

  • BIG-IP is running on a VIPRION platform containing more than one blade
  • The mcpd process exits and restarts

The mcpd process is the BIG-IP messenger daemon that allows other processes to communicate with TMM. When the mcpd process dies and restarts, it notifies subscriber processes that they must also restart in order to re-establish a communication channel with the mcpd process. When this issue occurs, a race condition causes the mcpd process to exit preventing TMM from receiving the FIN or RST packet from the mcpd process.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may not process traffic as expected, and configuration changes are not propagated to TMM.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The system may not process traffic as expected.
  • Configuration changes are not propagated to TMM.
  • No error messages are logged to indicate that TMM has lost the connection to the mcpd process.

Workaround

You can work around this issue and re-establish TMM communication with the mcpd process by restarting the TMM process. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Restarting TMM interrupts traffic processing and should be performed in a maintenance window.

  1. Log in to the VIPRION command line.
  2. Type the following command:

    bigstart restart tmm
