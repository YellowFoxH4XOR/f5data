K11587: The pvac daemon may crash when multiple plugin threads are actively processing Edge Side Includes

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When multiple active plugin threads attempt to simultaneously update the list of active Edge Side Includes (ESI) contexts maintained by the BIG-IP WebAccelerator's pvac daemon, the ESI context list may become corrupted. Subsequent attempts to access the list cause the pvac daemon to crash. As a result, BIG-IP WebAccelerator traffic may be disrupted.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR137269, and it was fixed in BIG-IP 10.2.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP WebAccelerator release notes.

Additionally, this issue has been fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-10.1.0-3372.0-HF1 for BIG-IP 10.1.0. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
