Important: You should access the BIOS only when directed to do so by F5 official documentation (including AskF5) or an F5 Support Engineer. If you access the BIOS without such direction, you may void your support contract.

When the system is booting up, press the Delete key as soon as you see the white F5 screen. You may have to press the Delete key several times in order for BIOS to display.
