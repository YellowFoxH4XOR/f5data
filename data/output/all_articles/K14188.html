Known Issue

A BIOS condition on the BIG-IP 1600, 3600, and 3900 platforms may cause system instability.

This issue occurs when one of the following conditions is met:

  • You have a BIG-IP 1600/3600 platform with a BIOS version earlier than build 150.
  • You have a BIG-IP 3900 platform with a BIOS version earlier than build 010.

The affected BIOS versions can experience a rare condition in which a register IP-relative instruction result may be incorrect, causing an unexpected page fault or unpredictable system behavior.

Impact

BIG-IP platforms affected by this issue may experience system reboots and a subsequent failover event in configurations with a high-availability (HA) pair.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • Unexpected page fault or unpredictable system behavior
