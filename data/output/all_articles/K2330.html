The switchboard on the application switch contains dedicated Broadcom chips; therefore, it is not dependent on the speed of the backplane to pass high volumes of traffic.

The application switch also communicates with multiple switch cards so it does not have the extreme port density that would require the type of high-speed backplanes that an enterprise core system might need.

The throughput on the BIG-IP application switch is as follows:

BIG-IP 1000 series = (8 * 100 Mb/s) + (1 * 1Gb/s) = 1.8Gb/s
BIG-IP 2000 series = (16 * 100 Mb/s) + (2 * 1Gb/s) = 3.6Gb/s
BIG-IP 5000 series = (24 * 100 Mb/s) + (4 * 1Gb/s) = 6.4Gb/s

At this time, the maximum volume of L2 traffic processed by the application switch is limited solely by the port density of the switch card.
