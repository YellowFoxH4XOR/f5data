K000140521: An old digital certificate is showing despite being renewed on the BIG-IP

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-09-04
Updated
2025-02-07
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000140521

Description

You have renewed a digital certificate hosted on the BIG-IP, but when testing the service that utilises the certificate, you see that the old certificate details are still seen.

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • Digital Certificate

Cause

Another device such as a Firewall or Load-Balancer is between the client and the BIG-IP and has the old certificate still installed.

Recommended Actions

Update certificates on the device between the client and the BIG-IP.

Additional Information

None
