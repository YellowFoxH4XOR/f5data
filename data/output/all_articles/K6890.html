Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. A FIPS kernel driver failure or FIPS card hardware failure does not cause the BIG-IP LTM system to fail over to the standby unit, as expected.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR69260, CR70301, and CR70302 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM version 9.2.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
