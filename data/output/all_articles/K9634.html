The FirePass 4100 and 4300 platforms offer a dedicated management port. This port is labeled as Management on the FirePass controller chassis, and it is designed for access to the FirePass Administrative Console from a directly connected management workstation. If you want to connect the FirePass controller to a corporate management network, configure one of the non-management ports for that purpose. 

Note: Access to the Administrative Console is not limited to the management port. For more information, refer to K8393: Restricting access to the FirePass Administrative Console.
 
Note: The FirePass 4300 platform supports lights-out management. For more information, refer to the Platform Guide: 4300.
 
The dedicated management port should only be used for accessing the FirePass Administrative Console. F5 Networks does not support configurations that enable user logon, user traffic, or other types of traffic through the management port on a 4100 or 4300 FirePass controller. Additionally, F5 Networks recommends that you retain the default settings for the management port.

Important: Do not configure an IP subnet on the management port that conflicts or overlaps with an IP subnet configured on a non-management port. Additionally, ensure that the IP subnet on the management port does not conflict or overlap with any IP routes you manually configured on the FirePass controller.

The FirePass controller uses the management port for internal SCCP communication. Therefore, if you are viewing traffic statistics for the management port, traffic will be reported even when administrators are not accessing that port.
