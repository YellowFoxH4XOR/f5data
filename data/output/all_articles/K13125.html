Known Issue

The Citrix JICA client shows as not installed after uploading to the FirePass controller.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The Citrix JICA client is installed after installing hotfix HF-70-3.
  • You have navigated away from the Application Access : Terminal Servers : Global Settings page and then returned to the page.

Impact

You are unable to determine the installed status and version of the Citrix JICA client.

Note: The Citrix JICA client is still available to users connecting to the FirePass controller.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • Viewing the Citrix JICA client configuration on the FirePass controller displays as JICA client not installed, even though the client installed correctly.
