Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • BGP, BFD configured
  • Default DAG configured
  • Application using fixed ports

Cause
Based on the log messages, the issue is related to BFD timeouts.  The underlying cause of the timeouts could be related to CPU spikes seen as a result of fixed source port application traffic being pinned to specific TMMs.  When traffic spikes are seen, the utilization on the TMMs overwhelms the BIG-IP, causing the BFD timeouts and causing BGP to flap.

Recommended Actions
Consider changing the DAG value to a value more suitable for fixed source port traffic, such as SP-DAG.  Refer to the linked TechDocs article for more information about modifying the DAG values on a BIG-IP.
