Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. A trunk interface drops a small number of frames during link recovery. For example, if interfaces 1.1 and 1.2 are defined in a trunk on the BIG-IP system, and interface 1.1 becomes temporarily unavailable, the interface drops a small number of frames after it recovers. F5 Networks Product Development has established the upper bound of time in which frames may be dropped to be less than one second after link recovery. This behavior occurs regardless of whether Link Aggregation Control Protocol (LACP) is defined for the trunk.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as ID 249204 (formerly CR106810).
