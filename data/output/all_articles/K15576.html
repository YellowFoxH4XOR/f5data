Known Issue

BIG-IP 2000 and 4000 series platforms may send frames to the wrong egress interface. A Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) race condition may occur, causing the same media access control (MAC) address to appear on multiple interfaces.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your system uses a Virtual Local Area Network (VLAN) configured with multiple interfaces.
  • Packets from the same MAC address have been received recently on multiple ports.

Impact

Connections may fail if the BIG-IP system forwards packets to the wrong interface.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H449181 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • Connectivity through the BIG-IP system fails.
  • The BIG-IP system sends packets to the wrong interface.
  • The tmsh show net fdb command lists multiple entries for a single VLAN:MAC pair, pointing to different interfaces.
