Known Issue

F5 Access may fail to launch from the Recently Closed tabs pop-up window.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your iPadOS device has the F5 Access 3.0.7 client installed and configured.
  • You launch and then close the F5 Access client.

    Note: The F5 Access app will be displayed in the recently closed app bar.

  • You attempt to re-launch the F5 Access client by using the iPadOS Recently Closed Tabs pop-up window.

Impact

The F5 Access client may fail to launch.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • After you had previously launched and closed the F5 Access client, you are unable to launch the F5 Access client using the iPadOS Recently Closed Tabs pop-up window.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 829817 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. For information about releases, point releases, or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can launch the F5 Access client from the iPadOS home screen.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
