Description

After changing the ConfigSync IP utilized by an ASM-enabled device group, ASM (Advanced WAF) policy changes will not synchronize until the asm_config_server processes are restarted.

Environment

HA-enabled ASM or Advanced WAF setup.

Cause

The asm_config_server process does not pick up changes to LTM device sync settings automatically. 

Recommended Actions

To restart the asm_config_server processes with minimum disruption to other processes on the BIG-IP, run the following command:

pkill -f asm_config_server

The asm_config_server processes will restart in about a minute.

None.
