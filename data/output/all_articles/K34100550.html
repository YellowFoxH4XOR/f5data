Known Issue

Setting high-speed logging (HSL) Distribution to replicated may cause the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) to leak memory.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You configure a remote HSL destination with a Distribution setting of replicated.
  • One or more pool members for the selected HSL destination pool becomes unavailable.

Impact

TMM memory may eventually become exhausted, resulting in TMM restarting. The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restarting. If the system is part of a high availability (HA) pair, it may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • F5 iHealth lists Heuristic H637078 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Low screen.
  • Your BIG-IP system performance degrades over time or the system becomes unresponsive.
  • Your BIG-IP system temporarily fails to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restarting.
  • If your BIG-IP system is part of a HA pair, it fails over to the standby system.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can set the HSL destination Distribution to adaptive or balanced.

Impact of workaround: If you set the HSL destination Distribution to adaptive or balanced, the BIG-IP system does not replicate messages to all HSL destination pool members.
