Source

Description

When you attempt to activate a new License key on a BIG-IP device, you receive an error similar to the following:

Error 51071: Invalid Dossier

Environment

  • Unlicensed BIG-IP product
  • Attempting to add/activate an Add-on module license

Cause

The Dossier is blank. A blank dossier can not be handled by the license server.

Recommended Actions

Contact F5 Technical Support to investigate the cause of the mismatched key requirements.
