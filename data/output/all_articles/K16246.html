Known Issue

Deterministic NAT (DNAT) mode may provide incorrect address mappings.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system uses the VIPRION C4800 or C4800N platform.
  • The Large Scale NAT (LSN) pool uses DNAT mode.

Impact

Reverse and forward address mappings do not work as expected.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Reverse and forward mappings do not work as expected.
  • The DNAT table contains an error message that appears similar to the following example:

    'No useable endpoints'
