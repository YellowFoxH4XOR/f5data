K44418420: Configuration Changes in the BIG-IP Are not Reflected on the BIG-IQ

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-08-23
Updated
2025-02-06
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K44418420

Description
You made a change in your BIG-IPs but the change is not reflected in the BIG-IQ. You have even removed and reimported the BIG-IP into the BIG-IQ

Environment

  • BIG-IQ (all versions)
  • BIG-IP (all versions)

Cause
Unknown at time of publication.

Recommended Actions

  1. A quick and easy workaround is to restart the restjavad daemon to refresh the configurations. This does not have any impact on BIG-IP devices. You will only lose momentary access to the Configuration Utility on the BIG-IQ.
    1. Log in to the CLI and run the below command in bash
tmsh restart sys service restjavad
