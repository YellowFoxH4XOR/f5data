Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may stop responding when AAM is under severe pressure due to TMM memory exhaustion.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • AAM is provisioned and processes traffic.
  • Your BIG-IP system's TMM memory is severely exhausted.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restarting, and systems configured as part of a high-availability pair may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP system becomes slow or unresponsive, and the sweeper starts terminating connections and reports error messages similar to the following example:

    011e0003:4: Aggressive mode sweeper: 9 Connections killed
  • The tmm process stops responding and generates a core file in the /shared/core directory.
