Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP LTM system supports an active-active configuration that allows two units in a high availability pair to simultaneously manage traffic. The BIG-IP ASM does not support an active-active configuration. When the BIG-IP ASM is licensed on a BIG-IP system, the BIG-IP LTM system does not support active-active mode.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR76773, and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.4.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM or LTM release notes.
