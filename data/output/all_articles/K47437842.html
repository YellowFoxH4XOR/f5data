Known Issue

Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP) guest provisioning or deployments may stall with a Waiting for other disk activity to finish message.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a VIPRION system licensed and provisioned for vCMP.
  • You have either provisioned or deployed a vCMP guest.

Impact

The vCMP guest fails to move into the provisioned or deployed state.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • You receive a Waiting for other disk activity to finish message while attempting to provision or deploy a vCMP guest.
  • The vCMP guest fails to enter the provisioned or deployed state.

Workaround

There is no workaround for this issue. When the system no longer displays the Waiting for disk activity message to finish message, set the vCMP guest to a configured state, and provision or deploy the vCMP guest again.

Impact of procedure: Performing the recommended procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.
