Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP LTM parser that handles cookies only correctly parses cookies that use the <cookie name>=<cookie value> format.

This issue affects virtual servers using a cookie persistence profile. If the BIG-IP LTM receives a request with a cookie that does not use the <cookie name>=<cookie value> format, and that cookie precedes the BIG-IP cookie, the parser stops and does not read any of the cookies that follow in the list.

As a result, the BIG-IP load balances the request to a new member in the pool and a new BIG-IP cookie is assigned to the packet, thus breaking persistence.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR68429 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM versions 9.1.3, 9.3, and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
