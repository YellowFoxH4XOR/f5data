Topic

The following specifications are for the available Copper Gigabit Ethernet modules and the platforms that support those modules.

Note: F5 Gigabit Ethernet 1000Base-T Copper SFP modules comply with IEEE standards 802.3ab (1000BASE-T).

Important: 1000Base-T network segments have a maximum length of 100 meters (328 feet) and must use a minimum Category 5 (Cat5) cable. F5 recommends a Cat5e, Cat6,or Cat7 cable.

  • F5-UPG-SFPC-R (OPT-0051-01) - 1000Base-T Copper Ethernet Transceiver SFP module specifications
  • F5-UPG-SFPC+-3M-8 (CBL-0138-xx) - 10000Base-T Copper SFP+ CU Active Cable

F5-UPG-SFPC-R (OPT-0051-01) - 1000Base-T Copper Ethernet Transceiver SFP module specifications

Specifications
  • Connector type: RJ45
  • Maximum operating distance: 100 meters (328 feet)
  • Cable specifications: Minimum Cat5; Cat5e, Cat6, or Cat7 recommended
  • DDM support: No
Supported hardware products
  • BIG-IP 800, 1500, 1600, 2000s/2200s, 3400, 3600, 3900, 4000s/4200v, 5000s/5200v, 6400, 6800, 6900, 7000s/7200v, 8400, 8800, 8900, 8950, 10000s/10200v, and 11050 platforms
  • VIPRION B2100/B2150, B4100 (PB100), B4200 (PB200), and B4300 blades
  • BIG-IP i2600, i2800, i4600, i4800, i5600, i5800, i7600, i7800, i10600, i10800, i11600, and i11800 platforms

    Note: The i2600, i2800, i4600, i4800, i5600, i5800, i7600, i7800, i10600, i10800, i11600, and i11800 platforms only support the 1000BASE-T speed setting and will not negotiate to a lower speed.

    Note: The SFP+ slots in i2xxx and i4xxx do not support SFP transceivers. The SFP+ slots in i5xxx and above support SFP transceivers.

  • F5 r2x00, r4x00
Serial number location

F5-UPG-SFPC+-3M-8 (CBL-0138-xx) - 10000Base-T Copper SFP+ CU Active Cable

Specifications
  • Connector type: SFP+
  • Cable Length: Fixed length of 3 meters (9.8 feet)
  • Cable Specifications: SFF-8431 SFP+ MSA compliant with two pair shielded twinax wire construction
Supported hardware products
  • BIG-IP 2000, 4000, 5000, 7000, 8900, 8950, 10000, 11000, 11050 and 12000 platforms
  • VIPRION® B2100, B2150, B4100, B4200, and B4300 blades
  • BIG-IP i2600, i2800, i4600, i4800, i5600, i5800, i7600, i7800, i10600, i10800, i11600, and i11800 platforms
  • F5 r2x00, r4x00, r5x00, and r10x00 platforms
