Description

You may experience device connectivity issues when upgrading BIG-IQ 

Environment

BIG-IQ version 4, 5, 6, 7

Cause

Service ports have changed between versions and have different connectivity requirements. 

Recommended Actions

Verify service port connectivity before upgrading, including any firewall rules that may inhibit connectivity on required BIG-IQ service ports
