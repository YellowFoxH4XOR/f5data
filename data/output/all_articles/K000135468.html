With the introduction of the new NGINX Connectivity Stack for Kubernetes Bundles planned for August 1, 2023, F5 NGINX is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) notification for the existing NGINX Microservices Bundles as of July 1, 2023. This applies to all Microservices Bundle SKUs (SKUs containing NGX-MSB).

This notice applies to the following product SKUs.

Technical Support

F5 maintains generous lifecycle policies that allow customers to continue support and receive product updates. Existing NGINX Microservices Bundle customers can continue to consume NGINX Microservices Bundles past the EoS date. License renewals are not available after the July 1, 2023 EoS date.
