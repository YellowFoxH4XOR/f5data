Known Issue

SessionDB-related accesses initiated by way of iRules may hang and the system may never clean them up.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Either iRules or a native profile uses SessionDB.
  • You have configured a FastL4 profile.
  • You are running on a VIPRION chassis.
  • There is more than one blade in service.

Impact

Performance may degrade over time and possibly cause a failover to another host in the device group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The number of active connections increases over time.
  • Memory that the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) uses increases over time.
  • TMM may restart, causing a failover to a standby host.
