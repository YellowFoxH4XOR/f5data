Known Issue

Upgrading a high availability (HA) device group member causes the peer device to become unresponsive.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP HA device group members run BIG-IP 11.6.0, 11.6.1, 12.0.0, 12.1.0, 12.1.1, or 12.1.2.
  • You perform either of the following actions:
    • You upgrade the peer HA device from BIG-IP 11.6.0 or 11.6.1 to BIG-IP 12.x or later.
    • You upgrade the peer HA device from BIG-IP 12.0.0, 12.1.0, 12.1.1, or 12.1.2 to BIG-IP 13.x or later.
  • After you upgrade the peer HA device, a failover event occurs. 

Impact

The BIG-IP 11.6.x device(s) become unresponsive.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When the failover event occurs, the BIG-IP 11.6.x device logs messages to the /var/log/ltm file that appear similar to the following examples:
