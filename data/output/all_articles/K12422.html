Issue

Previously, the 1600, 3600, 3900, and EM4000 platforms included a retention clip as an additional deterrent to the power cable accidentally dislodging from the socket on the power supply.

Beginning in December 2010, the retention clip is no longer included on the 1600, 3600, 3900, and EM4000 platforms. There is no change in performance or functionality to the power supply. In addition, the power cables are interchangeable with any other 1600, 3600, 3900, or EM4000 device; in the event of an Return Materials Authorization (RMA), the old cables should be retained (as normal) for use with the replacement unit.
