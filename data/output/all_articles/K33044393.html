Known Issue

BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) may become unresponsive under heavy load.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • BIG-IP VE experiences a high packet rate.
  • The packets contain small data payloads.

Impact

BIG-IP VE fails to process traffic and is not remotely accessible.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP VE fails to process traffic.
  • You are unable to access BIG-IP VE using either secure shell (SSH) or HTTPS.

Workaround

To work around this issue, first increase the amount of memory allocated to BIG-IP VE on your hypervisor. If this step does not resolve the issue, you can also perform the following procedures:

Decreasing BIG-IP VE throughput

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure significantly decreases the amount traffic the BIG-IP VE system can process.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP VE Advanced Shell (bash).
  2. To substantially decrease the BIG-IP VE instance throughput, type the following command:

    echo 1048576 > /sys/module/unic/rx_queue_size

Enabling the sysctl vm.panic_on_oom setting

Important: Perform this procedure only if the other two workarounds do not resolve the issue.

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP VE bash.
  2. To enable the sysctl vm.panic_on_oom setting, type the following command:

    sysctl vm.panic_on_oom=1
