Description

Some larger organizations have multiple accounts, and those accounts have multiple users performing different tasks, such as the following:

  • Creating or managing support cases
  • Purchasing or managing F5 hardware, software, subscriptions, and services (assets)

MyF5 has a set of account user roles, and each role corresponds to a set of actions that users with that role can take on one MyF5 account. For more information, refer to K000136095: About MyF5 account user roles and permissions.

However, organizations with large or multiple accounts may want the flexibility to do the the following:

  • Enable one or more users to see all cases and assets across multiple accounts.
  • Divide multiple accounts into subgroups of users and control the cases or assets that users can see or manage in their subgroup.

Definitions

An account group enables you to combine multiple accounts so that you can see and control users, cases, and assets across those accounts. It is comprised of multiple accounts belonging to one organization and has the following additional qualities:

  • F5 Service Delivery Managers (SDMs) create account groups for customers.
  • SDMs assign one or more customers the Asset Manager role for the account group, and the Asset Manager manages the account group.
  • Asset Managers must have the Admin role in all the accounts in an account group.
  • Asset Managers can see all the cases and assets in an account group. 

A contact group enables you to add users from an available users list to a new group and control their access to cases and assets within that group. It is comprised of a subset of users from an account group and has the following additional qualities:

  • Asset Managers create contact groups.
  • You can add to contact groups only users who are on all of the accounts in the account group and have at least the Basic role in all accounts.
  • Account and asset groups can have multiple contact groups.
  • Asset Managers can assign new roles to users in the contact group, regardless of their role in each account.

An asset group enables you to create groups of assets from multiple accounts and associate users from multiple accounts to those assets. It is comprised of selected assets owned by an organization and has the following additional qualities:

  • Asset Managers create asset groups.
  • Asset Managers can assign one or more contact groups to an asset group.
  • Asset Managers assign the contact group a role, and the users in the contact group can access assets in the asset group according to that role. The roles available for asset groups are Viewer, Editor, and Case Manager.

Examples

Use account and contact groups to manage support cases

To provide an example of how to use account and contact groups, let's say you work for a company that has an Information Technology (IT) department in each country office, and each country office has a MyF5 account, such as the following accounts: 

  • My Company, Brazil IT MyF5 account
  • My Company, Mexico IT MyF5 account

The situation is the following:

  • The director of IT for your organization's LATAM region is Carmen, and she is responsible for these MyF5 accounts.
  • Some of the same employees are in both accounts.
  • There are employees in both accounts who manage all security vulnerabilities, while also working on other issues.
  • Aside from Carmen, all of the users in both accounts have the Basic role, which means they can see and manage only the cases they create, and they cannot manage subscriptions.

Carmen wants to solve the following problems:

  • Problem 1: She wants to enable all employees who remediate security vulnerabilities to see each other's cases and collaborate on them.
  • Problem 2: She wants to designate employees to manage subscriptions to increase efficiency.
  • Problem 3: She doesn't like switching between accounts to see all the cases in both accounts.

Solution

To solve these problems, Carmen uses an account group and contact groups.

Create an account group

She starts by requesting that her SDM put the two accounts into an account group called "LATAM IT MyF5" and make her the Asset Manager of the account group. This change enables her to easily access all of the cases in both accounts from one page.

LATAM IT MyF5 account group
  • Brazil IT account
  • Mexico IT account
  • Carmen, Asset Manager

Summary

By creating an account and contact groups, Carmen accomplished the following:

  • She can use an account group to easily access all of the cases in both accounts from one page.
  • She used the Security Vulnerabilities contact group and Case Manager role to enable Marie and Sofia to access each other's cases in both accounts so that they can collaborate on security vulnerability cases.
  • She used the Subscriptions contact group and Editor role to enable Diego and Paulo to manage subscriptions for both accounts.

Use account, contact, and asset groups to manage assets and support cases

To provide an example of how to use account, contact, and asset groups, let's say your organization is a city. The city has multiple agencies, and each agency has an IT department. The IT departments have a MyF5 account. The following accounts are two of the city's MyF5 accounts:

  • My City, Government Agency IT MyF5 account
  • My City, Education Agency IT MyF5 account

Your city also contracts with IT vendors that purchase and manage your F5 assets. You have the following two IT vendors, and their employees are on these accounts:

  • First IT Partners
  • Second IT Partners

The situation is the following:

  • Ajay works for the city as a manager for the IT departments in these two agencies, and he is responsible for their MyF5 accounts.
  • Aside from Ajay, all of the users in both accounts have the Basic role, which means they can see and manage only the cases they create, and they cannot manage subscriptions.
  • Both accounts have IT vendors who purchase assets and manage asset issues.
  • Some of the same vendor employees are on both MyF5 accounts.
  • The employees who work for each vendor have knowledge of only the assets their company procured for each agency.

Ajay wants to solve the following problem:

  • He wants to create groups of assets across both accounts.
  • He wants to designate IT vendor employees who can manage cases and subscriptions for those groups of assets.
  • The vendor employees must be able to see and manage only the cases and assets that their company procured.
  • The vendor employees must not see the cases and assets procured by the other IT vendor.
