Known Issue

Updating a policy macro branch rule does not trigger the apply policy notification after saving the modification.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You configure the BIG-IP APM system with a policy that uses a macro set with at least one branch rule.
  • You modify the branch rules of an associated policy with the visual policy editor.
  • You save modifications of the branch rules in the visual policy editor.

Impact

The BIG-IP APM policy macro branch rule changes do not take effect.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Modifications to policy macro branch rules are not applied.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can manually apply the policy changes after editing a visual policy editor macro. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

Applying a BIG-IP APM policy change

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.
  2. Navigate to Access Policy > Access Profiles > Access Profiles List.
  3. Select the check box to the left of the policy that you previously edited.
  4. Click Apply Access Policy.
