Description

  • Although setting up your ROA to be RPKI-ready is not yet mandatory, it is something recommended to avoid future issues. Why not take this opportunity to work on enabling RPKI for the same prefix’s you’ve registered with ROA’s?

 

Environment

  • Customer IRR Dashboard

 

Procedure

Being RPKI Ready (example from ARIN)

  1. Login to your IRR, and look for your organization information.

  2. Click on your Organization handle, or details to pull up current settings

  3. Under Actions click on Manage RPKI  or look on our RIR for where you can manage RPKI settings

  4. If this has not been setup before, you will first need to select Hosted RPK" or Delegated RPKI. The Hosted version is usually easier to setup, but the needs of your organization should be considered.

    2. Let's proceed assuming you have to setup HOSTED

      • Accept any Terms of Service if you are in agreement

      • Create a Private Key for your organization:

        • >  openssl genrsa -out orgkeypair.pem 2048
      • Extract Public Key from the private key to share with your RIR: 
        • openssl rsa -in orgkeypair.pem -pubout -outform PEM -out org_pubkey.pem
      • Copy entire Public Key and paste into the RIR where it is requesting PUBLIC KEY 
        • -----BEGIN PUBLIC KEY-----
everything in here as well as the the opening and closing lines with the 5 dashes
-----END PUBLIC KEY-----
      • Additional info on Key pairs and processes:
        https://www.arin.net/resources/manage/rpki/hosted/#roarequestkeypair  

  5. Submit your Public Key with the RIR.  Once the request is submitted, it may take 1-2 business days to accept the key, but can often accepted more quickly.

 

 

Accepted

    1. The RIR responds with an acceptance, and should provide a confirmation and any necessary files.

    2. Go back to your ORG settings, and back to ACTION >> Manage RPKI  (or equivalent in your RIR)

    3. Click on Create ROA

    4. Fill in all details:

      • ROA Name:
        Origin AS:  (this is YOUR ASN, so that you are validated to originate the prefix)
        Start Date
        End Date
        Prefix
        Private Key  (upload your private key you created -- if necessary)

      • NOTE: the private key is not stored, it is only used to validate the inital ROA request

    5. Fix any errors… it should prompt you if date range is too broad, or misconfigured prefix, etc.

    6. SUBMIT
      After submission, it might take a day or two for verification and approval. With ARIN a ticket is opened, and you will be notified when the ticket has been completed.  Others may vary.

Validate

There are a few resources you can use to verify your RPKI status is ready once the approval has been completed.  IRR Explorer works, as well as RIPE.  These are some examples:

https://irrexplorer.nlnog.net/
https://rpki-validator.ripe.net/ui/

Additional Information

NOTE: We are using this example because you might see something similar. In this example, F5 has only registered the full /16 summary route, even though we are advertising multiple related /24 prefix’s. While IRR Explorer does pick up the other prefix’s being advertised, it is not necessary that every child prefix is “green” status.

You are not required to register any more than your summarized routes to be RPKI-ready, unless there are special circumstances. For example - there might be additional ASN’s allowed for originating certain child prefix’s. Then you should create another ROA for those.
