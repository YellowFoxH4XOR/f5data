K25872674: Access Policy Manager upgrade of HA pair does not support zero downtime.

Type
Support Solution
Published
2019-09-24
Updated
2025-04-02
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP APM
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K25872674

Description

The purpose of this article is to set a realistic expectation for upgrade planning of Access Policy Manager (APM).

Environment

  • Upgrade planning.
  • BIG-IP Access Policy Manager (APM).
  • High Availability Environments.

Cause

F5 does not support zero downtime when upgrading Access Policy Manager (APM) in an HA pair due to the complexities involved in how APM handles sessions and resource allocation.

Recommended Actions

To ensure that upgrading and transitioning to a new APM failover pair version is successful, follow the below procedures.

Important: During the upgrade, all users currently logged on to the system will have to log on again.

  1. Connect to a standby unit of a failover pair.
  2. Upgrade the standby unit. You can reference K84554955: Overview of BIG-IP system software upgrades
  3. After the standby unit has been upgraded and booted into the new volume. Press Force offline on the [active] unit to trigger a failover to this newly upgraded unit. The newly upgraded unit will take over as the active unit.
    Device Management  ››  Devices  ››  <Device_Name(self)> Force Offline
  4. Once the upgraded unit takes over as active. You must restart the upgraded unit again.
    Note: This extra step or additional restart is required to flush out any of the old sessions which may have been introduced from the the previously active unit while on the older version of the software.
    System  ››  Configuration : Device : General > Reboot
  5. Wait for the upgraded unit to come back up.
  6. Once the upgraded unit becomes the active unit, upgrade the standby or offline unit.
  7.  When the newly upgraded standby unit comes back up, bring it back online by pressing Release Offline.
    Device Management  ››  Devices  ››  <Device_Name(self)> Release Offline
  8. This unit should be standby and the upgrade is now complete.

Additional Information

Not performing the upgrade with the steps outlined above could result in unpredictable APM session state, related issues.
