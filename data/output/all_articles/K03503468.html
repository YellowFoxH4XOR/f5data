Policy Information

F5 announces the End of Life (EoL) plan for DNS-Lite for all BIG-IP platforms. DNS-Lite was available on BIG-IP 11.4.0 through 12.1.0 as a heavily rate-limited, non-licensed, and provisioned evaluation and testing of DNS and GSLB services at no charge. This announcement does not impact any licensed BIG-IP DNS offerings. This applies to all BIG-IP 11.4.0 through 12.1.0 plus all future BIG-IP versions starting September 1, 2016 and any future licensing updates to current BIG-IP deployments will EoL DNS-Lite.

If F5 customers are currently using DNS-Lite for testing and evaluation, F5 recommends a replacement with a 30-day trial BIG-IP Evaluation License. The EoL of the DNS-Lite features allow F5 to focus on developing and supporting core capabilities and trials that are more commonly needed by customers. 

The DNS-Lite feature available on BIG-IP has reached EoL worldwide effective September 1, 2016.

F5 is providing advanced notification to customers and partners announcing intent to retire the feature listed in the following table:

FeatureEnd of Life (EoL)End of Software Development (EoSD)End of Support Contract Renewal (EoSCR)End of Technical Support (EoTS)
DNS-Lite on BIG-IP1-Sep-20161-Sep-2016N/A1-Sep-2017

Because DNS-Lite is a no-charge, non-licensed, provision only testing and evaluation feature is a best effort for all technical support.

Technical Support

F5 maintains generous life cycle policies that allow customers to enjoy many years of support and new software releases. Customers whose platforms are designated End of Life (EoL), and who are currently under maintenance contracts, will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the Service Contract Renewal date expires.
