Known Issue

The SafeNet hardware security module (HSM) client fails to establish sessions to some of the HSM devices in a high availability (HA) group.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You configured your VIPRION system with multiple blades.
  • You configured your multi-bladed BIG-IP system to use multiple SafeNet HSM devices in an HA group for SSL key management.
  • You configured your BIG-IP system to reach the external HSM devices through a Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) interface.

During startup, the pkcs11d process attempts to connect to the external HSM devices over a TMM interface, before TMM has fully initialized. As a result, the SafeNet client session to some of the HSM devices in the HA group fails to establish, and the BIG-IP system does not try to re-establish these connections.

Impact

The system does not fully utilize your SafeNet HSM devices in the HA group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP system experiences poor performance when clients are attempting to establish SSL connections.

  • The BIG-IP SafeNet client fails to establish sessions to some HSM on some BIG-IP blades.

    For example, consider a case in which you configure a system to use eight HSM devices. In the following output from a netstat command, the system establishes sessions for all eight HSM devices on one blade, but it only establishes sessions for three HSM devices on the other blade:

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can re-establish the connection to your SafeNet HSM device by restarting the pkcs11d service. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: The impact of the suggested workaround depends on the specific environment. F5 recommends testing any such changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.

  1. Log in to the TMOS Shell (tmsh) by typing the following command:

    tmsh

  2. Restart the pkcs11d process by typing the following command:

    restart /sys service pkcs11d
