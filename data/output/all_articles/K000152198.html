Description

  • Why do those grey bars mean in the proxy stats?
  • I see some grey bars in the proxy stats

Environment

  • Silverline WAF
  • DDoS
  • Proxy
  • Shape
  • Silverline Portal

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

The grey bars you can see in the portal proxy stats represent time windows for which there is missing data. Missing data does not mean there was a problem with the proxy, just that the graph is extrapolated based on known data points over the period of some missing data.
