Note: The ARX 1000, 4000, and 6000 platforms each support small form-factor pluggable (SFP) optical connectors.

F5 Networks currently ships the Finisar FTRJ8519P1BNL transceiver, a Fiber Channel SFP mini-GBIC transmitting at 850 nm. This transceiver supports distances up to 500 m on 50/125 um MMF, or 300m on 62.5/125 um MMF.

Important: The ARX platforms do not currently support other long-reach transceivers used for longer distance applications.

Only transceivers provided by F5 Networks are supported for use in the ARX platforms. For information about the SFP transceivers currently offered by F5 Networks, contact your F5 Networks Sales Associate.
