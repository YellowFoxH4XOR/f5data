Known Issue

BIG-IP 8900 and 8950 platforms, running High-Speed Bridge (HSB) firmware version 2.1.41.4 or earlier, may experience system lockup and IPv6 transmission issues.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • Your BIG-IP 8900 or 8950 platform runs HSB firmware versions 2.1.41.4 or earlier. The HSB firmware versions map to BIG-IP software versions as follows:

    • 2.1.26.1 (BIG-IP 10.2.0, 10.2.1)
    • 2.1.37.1 (BIG-IP 10.2.2, 10.2.3, 11.0.0, 11.1.0)
    • 2.1.41.4 (BIG-IP 10.2.4, 11.0.0 HF4, 11.1.0 HF5, 11.2.0, 11.2.1)

Impact

The system may not transmit IPv6 packets as expected. The system may experience lockups and resulting failover, or lockups followed by hard system hangs after a reboot.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H512651 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The system transmits IPv6 packets with checksums of 0, instead of the expected complement (all Fs) as per RFC 768.
  • The HSB experiences lockups and the system hangs after a reboot.
  • On rare occasions, the HSB locks up due to arbitration issues with datapath sharing between Packet DMA Engines.
