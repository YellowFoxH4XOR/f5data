Topic

This article applies to BIG-IP 13.x through 17.x. For information about other versions, refer to the following article:

  • K6499: BIG-IP platforms that support FIPS (9.x - 12.x)

BIG-IP hardware product support for the Federal Information Processing Standard (FIPS).

Description

The following hardware products provide FIPS support starting from the BIG-IP 13.x software branch.

Hardware ProductFIPS support in BIG-IP
F5 r5920-DF (C136)417.1.x, 17.5.x
F5 r10920-DF (C137)417.1.x, 17.5.x
BIG-IP i5820-DF (C125)3BIG-IP 13.1.1 - 13.1.3, 14.x, 15.x, 16.x, 17.x
BIG-IP i7820-DF (C126)3BIG-IP 13.1.1 - 13.1.3, 14.x, 15.x, 16.x, 17.x
BIG-IP i15820-DF (D120)BIG-IP 16.1.1 - 16.1.3, 17.x
BIG-IP 5250v-FIPS1 (C109)BIG-IP 13.x, 14.x, 15.x, 16.x
BIG-IP 7200v1 (D110)BIG-IP 13.x, 14.x, 15.x, 16.x
BIG-IP 10350v-FIPS2 (D112)BIG-IP 13.x, 14.x, 15.x, 16.x, 17.x
BIG-IP 102001 (D113)BIG-IP 13.x, 14.x, 15.x, 16.x

1Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP) guest access to FIPS resources is not supported on this hardware product.

2vCMP guest access to FIPS resources was introduced in BIG-IP 13.1.0 on this hardware product.

3vCMP guest access to FIPS resources was introduced in BIG-IP 13.1.1 on this hardware product.

4BIG-IP tenant's access to FIPS resources was introduced in BIG-IP 17.1.0 on this hardware product.

Important: Although the versions listed in the table include FIPS support, it is important to note that they may not be FIPS certified. For information about the certification of FIPS used in these hardware products, refer to K7837: Overview of FIPS 140-2 EAL Level 2 and 3 RoHS Certification Status and the F5 FIPS Cryptographic Module Certifications Page.
