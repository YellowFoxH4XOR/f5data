Description

You are looking in the GUI at the disk usage in System  ››  Disk Management. The bar graph shows only a small amount of free space. Concerns that there is not enough hard drive space for an upgrade. While it shows the the disk as full, this doesn't mean the space is occupied. The new partitioning scheme for iSeries platforms allocates storage for all product modules into a single shared volume called appdata. This scheme makes HD1.1 disk show as full, where space is allocated, but not fully used.

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • V13.* and later

Cause

While it shows the disk as full, this doesn't mean the space is occupied. It shows reservation for disk.

Recommended Actions

This behavior is normal and no further action is required. 
The more granular way to assess your disk space is to use the linux df -h command:
 
For example:
