Known Issue

AVR statistics data may be incorrect or missing.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You change the BIG-IP system time to a time earlier than the configured NTP server.
  • You view ARV reports or graphs with a Time Period of Last Hour.

Impact

AVR statistics reports and graphs indicate incorrect data.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Statistics are missing or incorrect when you view ARV reports or graphs with a Time Period of Last Hour.
