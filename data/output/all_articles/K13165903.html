Known Issue

The mcpd process may restart and produce a core file when you delete all peers from a device service clustering (DSC) domain.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is configured to use HA.
  • You delete all peers from a DSC trust domain while a connection is processing a transaction.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may experience a brief service interruption while the mcpd process restarts.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • You may observe that your active connections are in tear down when you delete all peers from the DSC trust domain.
  • If configured as part of a high availability (HA) group, the BIG-IP system fails over.
  • You notice a core file for the mcpd service in the /shared/core directory, with a correlating timestamp to the traffic interruption.
