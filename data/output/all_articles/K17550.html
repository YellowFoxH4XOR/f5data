Known Issue

Access policies configured with the CRLDP agent may appear to stop responding.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM access policy is configured with the CRLDP (Certificate Revocation List Distribution Point) Auth agent.
  • The BIG-IP APM is unable to access the configured AAA CRLDP servers.

Impact

The system ignores the configured CRLDP AAA profile Connection Timeout value.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Users attempting to establish an access session experience an extensive delay before authentication fails.
