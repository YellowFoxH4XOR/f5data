Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may produce a core file when modifying macro terminals.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You modify or create an access policy macro, changing the default terminal name of Out to a different name.

Impact

The BIG-IP APM system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restarting, and systems configured as part of a high availability (HA) group may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:
