Topic

F5 recommends limiting Neutral to Ground voltage to less than 10 percent of V-line (nominal). High Neutral to Ground voltages indicate that Neutral is floating with respect to Ground. This floating condition may cause common mode currents to flow in the ground connection when equipment is switched on and off. Common mode currents will inject noise onto electronic equipment. You should avoid this situation if possible.

In addition, verify that the Line to Neutral voltage meets the F5 input voltage specification. This information is available in the Platform Guide for your F5 product.

Note: You must ensure a low-impedance bond exists between the chassis ground and plant (power system) ground. A low-impedance ground bond will eliminate a source of issues with RS-232 and other ground referenced communication.
