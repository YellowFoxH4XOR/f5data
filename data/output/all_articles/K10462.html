Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The iControl LocalLB::Pool.get_persistence_record option may return an empty or incorrect value. Tools utilizing this iControl option that require strict WSDL compliance will have responses fail due to the invalid field value returned.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR118233 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.4.8 and 10.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM and Link Controller release notes.

Additionally, this issue has been fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-9.3.1-74.0-HF7, issued for BIG-IP 9.3.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the cumulative hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.

For information about the F5 hotfix policy, refer to K4918: Overview of F5 critical issue hotfix policy.

For information about how to manage F5 product hotfixes, refer to K6845: Managing F5 product hotfixes.
