Description

GTM sync group members deployed on rSeries reset their config upon reboot


Environment

The behavior is as follows:


1. GTM sync group members have their configuration in sync.

2. One of the devices is rebooted and in the meantime a config change is done on the remaining online unit.

3. This change is erased once the rebooted device comes back online.


Cause

ID1105021.
