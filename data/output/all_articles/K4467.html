Topic

A redundant (high availability) FirePass configuration consists of two FirePass controllers: one operating in active mode, and one operating in standby mode. The two controllers maintain communication across a network connection, and session and configuration information is replicated between the active and standby controllers. In the event that the active controller fails, the standby controller automatically becomes active.

Upgrading a redundant failover pair consists of the following processes:

  • Backing up the redundant pair
  • Performing the upgrade

F5 Networks recommends that you read the following instructions in their entirety, before you start the upgrade.

Important: To prevent losing access to the FirePass controllers, F5 Networks recommends that you have direct physical access or remote serial console access to the FirePass controllers when performing the upgrade.

Note: Refer also to K3339: Restoring backup files on a different FirePass unit than they originated on.

Note: You may temporarily increase the Global Inactivity Timeout to two or three hours to ensure your session does not time out during the upgrade process. For more information, refer to K3106: Configuring server-side session timeouts for more information.

Backing up the redundant pair

To back up the redundant pair, perform the following procedure:

  1. Log on to the Administrative Console of the active FirePass controller.
  2. Click Reports.
  3. Click Sessions.
  4. Next to each active session, click the Kill link to terminate the session.

    Note: You have the option to send an email using the Announce function (located on the Users: Announce page) to users in the FirePass local user database, notifying them about the impending downtime.

  5. Click Device Management.
  6. Click Maintenance.
  7. Click Backup/Restore.
  8. Create a backup of the current FirePass configuration.
  9. Log on to the Administrative Console of the standby FirePass controller.
  10. Repeat Steps 2 through 8 on the standby FirePass controller.

    For FirePass hardware that supports the snapshot function, continue to Step 11. For hardware that does not support the snapshot function, go to the section titled Perform the upgrade.

    Note: For specific instructions about performing a snapshot, refer to: K3244: Backing up and restoring FirePass system software.

  11. Create a snapshot of the FirePass image for the active FirePass controller.

    The active FirePass unit will reboot twice during this process and will become the standby unit once the snapshot process is complete.

  12. Create a snapshot of the FirePass image for the FirePass unit that is now active.

    This active FirePass unit will reboot twice during this process, becoming the standby unit once again.

  13. Log on to the Administrative Console of the active FirePass controller.
  14. Click Failover.
  15. Click Settings.
  16. Verify that the redundant pair is synchronizing normally.

Performing the upgrade

Note: You must review the FirePass Release Notes for the version you plan to install before you perform an upgrade. The release notes may contain extra steps that you must perform prior to or during the upgrade.

  1. Log on to the Administrative Console of the active FirePass controller.
  2. Click Device Management.
  3. Click Maintenance.
  4. Click Online Update.

    Note: If your FirePass controllers do not have direct access to the Internet, refer to K167: Downloading software from F5 Networks. From the workstation logged into the Administrative Console, download the upgrade image from the F5 Networks Downloads page and perform a Local Update on the Device Management: Maintenance: Local Update page.

  5. Follow the instructions on the page to perform the upgrade on the active FirePass controller. The active FirePass controller will reboot when the upgrade is complete, becoming the standby unit.

    Note: The total downtime from the point that the controller reboots until the standby controller becomes active should be less than 30 seconds. The active FirePass controller remains active during the download of the upgrade.

  6. Log on to the Administrative Console of the newly active FirePass controller.
  7. Repeat Steps 2 through 4 on the newly active FirePass controller.
  8. Verify that the synchronization and all site functions and applications are working properly.
