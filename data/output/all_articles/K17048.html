Topic

Description

The longevity of locally retained session data depends directly on the access policy complexity, the volume of access sessions, and the configured log level of the BIG-IP APM system. Local logging that is configured to use the Informational log level, with a moderately busy BIG-IP APM device, can reach the Maximum Number Of Log Entries setting's maximum of 5000000 within a single day of activity (regardless of the Log Rotation Period setting).

Recommendations

F5 recommends offloading BIG-IP APM log information to remote syslog servers.
