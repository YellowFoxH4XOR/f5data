Known Issue

There is no limit to the size of log files on the Switch Card Control Processor (SCCP). If log files fill the /var/log partition on the SCCP, the SCCP may become unresponsive or may reject login attempts.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR52506, CR53029, and CR53632 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.1.1 and 9.2.2. To monitor the status of this CR, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
