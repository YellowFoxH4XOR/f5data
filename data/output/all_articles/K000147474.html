What Happened?

I have a Customer Edge (CE) and want to know if pod to pod communication is allowed.

Environment

  •  F5 Distributed Cloud - Regional Edges 

Resolutions/ Answer

Yes, pod to pod communication is allowed on both RE and CE as long as the pods are in same namespace.

The connections can be done via TCP/UDP/HTTP.
