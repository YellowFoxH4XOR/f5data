Description

This article is to provide necessary steps to renew certificate used by ELK for EMS(SDC).

  • Graph on EMS/SDC is blank
  • Sudden surge is disk utilisation by ELK logs

Environment

  • ELK
  • SDC/EMS

Cause

There is expiry for CA certs generated to be ELK.

Recommended Actions

  1.  Login to one of EMS node , refer to (A)
  2. openssl req -new -newkey rsa:4096 -nodes -text -out ca.csr \
    -keyout ca.key -subj "/CN=Elastic Certificate Tool Autogenerated CA"
  3. chmod og-rwx ca.key
  4. openssl x509 -req -in ca.csr -text -days 3650 \
    -extfile /etc/pki/tls/openssl.cnf -extensions v3_ca -signkey ca.key -out ca.crt
  5. openssl req -new -newkey rsa:4096 -nodes -text -out instance.csr \
    -keyout instance.key -subj "/CN=instance"
  6. chmod og-rwx instance.key
  7. openssl x509 -req -in instance.csr -text -days 3650 \
    -CA ca.crt -CAkey ca.key -CAcreateserial -out instance.crt
  8. refer to (C)
  9. repeat (C) for the rest of the nodes (EMS,SDCs)
  10. Restart ELK,FLUENT and Kibana. on All EMSs follow by SDCs.
  11. run the bash command on all the nodes, 

A) Refer to page 74, section 15 of Bare Metal System Maintenance Guide, perform step 1 & 2 only

B) Perform step 2 - 7

C) perform step 3 of section 15.

Additional Information

ps -ef | grep -i td-agent | awk '{print "kill -9 " $2 }' | sh   maybe require if fluent is displaying a lot of logs after the application of the steps above.
