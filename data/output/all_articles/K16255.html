Known Issue

The VIPRION 4800 backplane interfaces may fail to auto-negotiate properly causing connection failures.

Impact

Connections may fail due to the BIG-IP system dropping packets or not responding to remote clients.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Connections may fail due to the BIG-IP system not responding to remote hosts.
  • Connection failures may be consistent based on source and destination IP and port.
  • The command tmsh show net interface -hidden reveals one or more 5.x or 6.x interfaces status as up but the corresponding media as none.

Resolution

Status
