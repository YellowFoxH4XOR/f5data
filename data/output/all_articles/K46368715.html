K46368715: After upgrading to 15.1.2.1 all Mac users cannot connect to VPN

Support Solution
Published
2021-09-13
Updated
2023-02-21
Applies to

  • BIG-IP

  • APM Clients
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K46368715

Description

After upgrading to 15.1.2.1 all Mac users cannot connect to VPN.

  • Mac Edge Client shows being stuck at "Checking host..."
  • APM Session log for the user doesn't show any prevalent failures, other than  "Session deleted due to user inactivity" at the end.
  • Windows OS users are ok, they are Not affected.


Environment

  • Upgraded from 13.1.3.6 to 15.1.2.1
  • Edge Client version:  7211.2021.0107.1
  • MacOS Catalina version: 10.15.7
  • BIG-IP APM, Edge Client

Cause

Unknown at the time of publication.

Recommended Actions

Update Edge Client to the latest version 7213.2021.527.649-5386.0.iso

Additional Information

None
