Known Issue

The system may not apply the domain group policy to computers after establishing a VPN connection.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You establish a Network Access session with Windows Logon Integration.

Impact

Computers maintain their local GPO after they connect to the window domain.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Domain GPOs are not applied to computers that establish an NA session with Windows Logon Integration.
