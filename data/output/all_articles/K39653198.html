Description

A user who connects to APM VPN Gateway is assigned with IP address for PPP tunnel connection from leasepool range configured under network-access resource. 

Environment

  • APM VPN
  • PPP
  • leasepool
  • assigned IP

Cause

BIGIP APM administrator may need to check the assigned IP address to VPN user for troubleshooting or audit purposes.

Recommended Actions

There are couple of methods for checking the assigned IP address for PPP connection to VPN user:
  • on APM, in GUI Configuration Utility, go to 'Access  ››  Overview : Active Sessions', find the user's session id, click on it and find the log line similar to the below. In lab example, the vpn user is assigned with IP 10.2.0.17:
 
2022-08-18 11:23:30/Common/ap_na:Common:614be168: Assigned PPP Dynamic IPv4: 10.2.0.17 ID: 0b296c8a Tunnel Type: VPN_TUNNELTYPE_TLS NA Resource: /Common/na_test Client IP: 10.1.0.9
 
  • on APM, in CLI bash mode, filter on user's VPN session id (in example, it's 614be168) and by text string 'Assigned PPP' in /var/log/apm log file:
 

[root@bigip-apm] config # grep '614be168' /var/log/apm | grep -i 'Assigned PPP'

Aug 18 11:23:30 bigip-apm notice tmm[12373]: 01490549:5: /Common/ap_na:Common:614be168: Assigned PPP Dynamic IPv4: 10.2.0.17 ID: 0b296c8a Tunnel Type: VPN_TUNNELTYPE_TLS NA Resource: /Common/na_test Client IP: 10.1.0.9

[root@bigip-apm] config #
 

  • on client's Windows machine in the right bottom of desktop where application icons exist, right-click on F5 EdgeClient app, choose Details, find Tunnel Details section and locate Client IPv4 address. 
 
  • on client's Windows machine, in Command Terminal type ipconfig /all, find PPP adapter section and locate IPv4 Address

PPP adapter _Common_na_test - 1.1.1.1:

   Connection-specific DNS Suffix  . :
   Description . . . . . . . . . . . : _Common_na_test - 1.1.1.1
   Physical Address. . . . . . . . . :
   DHCP Enabled. . . . . . . . . . . : No
   Autoconfiguration Enabled . . . . : Yes
   IPv4 Address. . . . . . . . . . . : 10.2.0.17(Preferred)
   Subnet Mask . . . . . . . . . . . : 255.255.255.255
   Default Gateway . . . . . . . . . :
   DNS Servers . . . . . . . . . . . : 1.1.1.1
   NetBIOS over Tcpip. . . . . . . . : Enabled
 
  • on client's Windows machine , APM administrator can generate CTU report and search the logs lines similar to:

2022-08-18, 9:23:10:662, 10732,10736,APPCTRL, 0,,,, Session 614be168 established
[...]
2022-08-18, 9:23:31:183, 9284,8256,DIALER, 48, \URDialer.cpp, 1632, CURDialer::OnRASConnected, Assigned IP address for PPP connection - 10.2.0.17
