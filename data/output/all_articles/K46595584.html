K46595584: VELOS tenants (on 2 different chassis) cannot reach either other self-ip in the same VLAN and Incoming ARP requests were not being delivered to the tenant.

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-11-07
Updated
2023-02-21
Applies to
  • F5OS
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K46595584

Description
Data plane was trying to send inbound ARP requests to the Software Rebroadcaster, but wasn't received by the tenants either.
Incoming ARP requests were not being delivered to the tenant.

BIG-IP tenants in an HA pair were unable to ping each other. There is egress ARP requests, but neither one was receiving ingress ARP broadcast packets from the network. Thus, they couldn't resolve the ARP entries for each other and could not connect to each other.

Rebroadcaster shows 0 packets. 
To verify this, load the qkview into iHealth and look at -> Commands/Standard -> system_fpga -> system_fpga - tmctl -d blade -w 180 -a

For example :

Environment

Cause

Recommended Actions

  1. The workaround is to delete the VLAN listeners and recreate them, which occurs when VLANs are removed and re-added to a tenant.
