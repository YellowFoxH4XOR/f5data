Topic

When you use the tcpdump utility to capture traffic on a BIG-IP platform that contains a Packet Velocity ASIC (PVA), the resulting trace may contain Ethernet frames sent between F5 MAC addresses (MAC prefix 00:01:d7) that specify the protocol encapsulation as EtherType 0xf5f5.

F5 specifies EtherType 0xf5f5 in frames that are exchanged internally between the PVA and the PVA management daemon (pvad). While they may appear in unfiltered traces, these packets are not transmitted outside the BIG-IP.

You can filter the frames from the packet trace using the following filters:

  • tcpdump

    not ether proto 0xf5f5

  • Wireshark

    eth.type != 0xf5f5
