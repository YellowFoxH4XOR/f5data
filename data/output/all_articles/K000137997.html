Description

What is a True Forward event in the FCP (ELA) agreement

Environment

  • VE (Virtual Edition)
    • Cloud Hosted
  • BIG-IQ License Manager
  • ELA and FCP

Cause

This article provides reference information on what is a True Forward and how this billing event is calculated inside of the Flexible Consumption programme (FCP/ELA) agreement. We will reference the terms of the FCP or ELA agreement, and while it can be modified, the generic terms are available here .

 

Recommended Actions

A True Forward is a single yearly billing event in your ELA or FCP contract that accounts for any potential overconsumption during the past year to bring it forward. It's is called a True Forward because the billing is forward-looking to the next year and service not yet consumed, rather than settling in arrears. Your potential growth is reviewed during a contractually agreed 3 month window.

  • Commit billing

True Forward Calculation

Your True Forward will consist of a single quote that will include your commit value as well as any potential growth.
This will be calculated during a designated three month window agreed in your signed contract. Usually these would be months 9 10 11 counted from the start of the agreement (ie for an agreement starting in February that would be January, December, November).

The Run Rate reports provided by your F5 CSM will have a table that shows the average of the last 3 months that can help you preview the approaching costs. Please make sure to review these before the start of the True Forward period to make sure all the active licenses are tracked correctly. You can find further explanations on the provided Run Rate and License Activity reports.

 

Commit billing

Commit is the annual payment for services that the customer has committed to paying regardless of actual usage. Your True Forward billing event will always include the Commit payment for the next year which is determined by either the initial sizing of the agreement in Year 1, or the previous True Forward calculation. Commit Billing is paid for in advance for the next year of the agreement.

The commited billing part of the agreement can grow depending on the growth during the measurement period, but can never reduce even if you aren't fully utilising the funds allocated towards the agreed license sizing.

 

Growth billing  

Any potential growth is calculated during the Measurement Period by averaging out specifically the growth in the agreement over the 3 months, and not the full usage. If your usage is found to be more than your entitlement during this period, you will pay all associated charges for such increased usage for the remainder of the term, and your commit will be adjusted going forward to the increased usage level. Growth is not billing for last year's usage in arrears; instead the amount of usage in the previous term informs the amount of growth to charge forwards to the next term.

If there is no growth during the Measurement Period this means no growth is estimated to occur in the upcoming contract year and therefore no additional growth cost is added alongside the commit billing for the next year's True Forward cost.

 

 Following year

Any accumulated growth will be carried forward and will then become your new Commit during the subsequent years of the agreement. The measuring process will repeat again for the next True Forward during the designated months of the following year,

If this is the final year of the agreement and you are at or below your Commit level - there will be no due invoices as you have already paid for the Commit part during the last True Forward.

In the case of Growth during the final year you may need to pay a final settlement fee at the end of the agreement. Please reach out to your Account Manager to discuss this.
