K000139753: BIG-IP VXLAN fragmenting packets even when MTU is set to jumbo frames

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-05-23
Updated
2025-02-07
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000139753

Description

When using VXLAN tunnels in the BIG-IP and setting the MTU to something higher than the normal 1500, for example 9000, and then the BIG-IP receives a 5000 length frame, when forwarding, the BIG-IP fragments it.

Environment

  • VXLAN tunnels
  • Jumbo frames
  • Fragmentation
  • Network route with MTU different than 0 or shorter than the size of the jumbo frame

Cause

Even when the vxlan tunnel has an MTU bigger than the jumbo frame, the BIG-IP honors the route's MTU, not the tunnels MTU

Recommended Actions

Change the route's MTU to 0 or something higher than the jumbo frame lenght

Additional Information

None
