Description

Your BIG-IP fails to establish an OSPF connection with it's peer

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • OSPF
  • Self-IP

Cause

BIG-IP is corrupting the inbound MAC address on the OSPF DBD packet from the peer device

Recommended Actions

If you need to prioritize recovery, rebooting the device may resolve the issue

Alternatively, you may collect packet captures of the OSPF handshake to determine what step the issue occurs at
