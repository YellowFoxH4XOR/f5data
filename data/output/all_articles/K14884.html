Known Issue

Citrix Receiver for Apple iOS and Google Android may fail to connect through the BIG-IP APM system.

This issue occurs when one of the following conditions is met:

  • You configure the BIG-IP APM system with Citrix StoreFront integration.
  • You configure the BIG-IP APM system to use two-factor authentication.

Impact

Connections through the BIG-IP APM system that originate from Apple iOS and Google Android devices fail when using Citrix Receiver.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • The Citrix StoreFront application responds with an HTTP 404 - Not Found error message.
  • Proxy connections to the BIG-IP APM system from Apple iOS or Google Android devices that use Citrix Receiver fail.
