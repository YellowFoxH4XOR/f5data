Policy Information

With the release of BIG-IP 14.0.0, F5 introduced new SSL Orchestrator functionality which requires a refresh of the former BIG-IP SSL Forward Proxy License SKUs. F5 added a new set of ADD SKUs and changed the SKU names from SSL FWD ADD to SSLO ADD.

To run SSL Orchestrator on BIG-IP 14.0.0 and later, and access new feature functionality, you must use the new SKUs. The new SKUs support earlier versions, as far back as BIG-IP 12.1.0.

With the introduction of new SSLO ADD SKUs, F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of the BIG-IP SSL Forward Proxy License SKUs.

Technical Support

F5 maintains generous lifecycle policies that allow customers to enjoy many years of both support and new software releases. Customers whose platforms are designated End of Life (EoL), and who are currently under maintenance contracts, will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the Service Contract Renewal date expires
