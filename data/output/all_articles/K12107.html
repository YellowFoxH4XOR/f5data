Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP system fails to forward router advertisement packets across VLANs in a VLAN group. For example, if the VLAN group contains child VLANs external and internal, and a device sends an ICMPv6 router advertisement packet on VLAN external, the BIG-IP system fails to forward the packet across the VLAN group to VLAN internal.

The following packet capture output shows both OSPFv3-hello packets and ICMP6 router advertisement packets arriving on the external VLAN:

# tcpdump -ni external ip6

IP6 fe80::201:d7ff:fe2c:1408 > ff02::5: OSPFv3-hello 36: rtrid 10.10.10.56 area 10.10.0.0 dr 10.10.10.56
IP6 fe80::201:d7ff:fe2c:1408 > ff02::5: OSPFv3-hello 36: rtrid 10.10.10.56 area 10.10.0.0 dr 10.10.10.56
IP6 fe80::201:d7ff:fe2c:1408 > ff02::1: ICMP6, router advertisement, length 56
IP6 fe80::201:d7ff:fe2c:1408 > ff02::1: ICMP6, router advertisement, length 56

The following capture output shows only the OSPFv3-hello packets on the internal VLAN:

# tcpdump -ni internal ip6

IP6 (hlim 64, next-header: OSPF (89), length: 36) fe80::201:d7ff:fe2c:1408 > ff02::5: OSPFv3-hello 36: rtrid 10.10.10.56 area 10.10.0.0 V6/E/R ifid 0.0.0.30 pri 1 int 10 dead 40 dr 10.10.10.56 nbrs
IP6 (hlim 64, next-header: OSPF (89), length: 36) fe80::201:d7ff:fe2c:1408 > ff02::5: OSPFv3-hello 36: rtrid 10.10.10.56 area 10.10.0.0 V6/E/R ifid 0.0.0.30 pri 1 int 10 dead 40 dr 10.10.10.56 nbrs

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as ID 338827 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 10.2.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, ASM, PSM, WebAccelerator, APM, WOM, or Edge Gateway release notes.

This issue has also been fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-10.2.0-1789.0-HF2 issued for BIG-IP version 10.2.0. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
