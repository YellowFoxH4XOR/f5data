Description

While the BIG-IP APM Access Reports provided comprehensive Session logging already, it is sometimes beneficial to create a Custom Report that contains the specific columns of data needed, and with specific filters to restrict the log messages that appear in the Custom Report.

Environment

Relevant environmental factors:

  • BIG-IP
  • APM (Advanced Policy Manager) module Licensed & Provisioned

Cause

N/A

Recommended Actions

To create an APM Custom Report:

  1. In the GUI, navigate to "Access > Overview > Access Reports".
  2. When the pop-up window appears, click "Cancel".
  3. Under "Browser Reports" on the left, click the "Custom Reports" bar at the bottom.
  4. Under "Report Names", right click to reveal the "Create New Report" option and click it.
  5. Give the new Custom Report a "Report Name:".
  6. Under the "Report Fields" tab, from the "Available Fields" selection list, check the boxes for all the Fields you'd like to appear in the report. The one Field you MUST have is under the "User" section and it's called "Access policy result". But you'll like what to add a bunch of others or the only field the Custom Report will display is the Access Policy Result.
  7. Under the "Report Constraints" tab, select from the "User" section the "Access policy result" option. Then select an "Operator" such as "includes". And then specify a "Field Value" to filter the list, such as "Full" for example.
  8. Under the "Sort Fields" tab, select the Field you would like the Custom Report to sort by.
  9. Then click the "Save" button to save the new Custom Report.
