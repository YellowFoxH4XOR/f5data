Description

After restoration of a BIG-IP HA device you receive the following error when trying to establish device trust:
Unable to add peer. This device does not have a ConfigSync address set. Update the ConfigSync address for this device

Environment

  • HA-Pair
  • Replacement of Peer device

Cause
Though the error complains about configsync IP, the underlying issue is actually related to the HA certificate.
Note: The synchronization certificate is a different from the certificate used by the device for Configuration utility access GUI).

Recommended Actions

To fix the issue, reset device trust using the following steps:

Impact: The following steps will break HA trust so both devices may go Active-Active, please perform the change under a maintenance window or place the new device in the force offline state to avoid outage from split brain scenario.

  1. Log on to the Configuration utility.
  2. Go to Device Management > Device Trust > Local Domain
  3. Click Reset Device Trust
  4. Under Certificate Signing Authority , select Generate New Self-Signed Authority
  5. Click Update.
  6. Perform the same steps (1-4) on the peer device.
  7. To reestablish device trust between the HA devices refer to: BIG-IP LTM BIG-IP Device Service Clustering > Administration Managing Device Trust.
