Known Issue

A BIG-IP ASM device group may be unable to perform a ConfigSync operation.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • BIG-IP ASM devices in a device group are licensed exclusively for the BIG-IP ASM system; the BIG-IP LTM module is not licensed.
  • The configuration being synchronized contains at least one pool member.

Impact

The ConfigSync operation will fail.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H380475 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • ConfigSync will fail and error messages that appear similar to the following example will be written to the /var/log/ltm file:

    Sep 14 02:34:51 rip-2 err mcpd[5416]: 01070356:3: Ratio load balancing feature not licensed.
    Sep 14 02:34:51 rip-2 notice mcpd[5416]: 0107140c:5: CMI_DGS (inconsistent_auto_resync --> .) full load for device group /Common/device-group-failover-foo fails.
    Sep 14 02:34:51 rip-2 notice mcpd[5416]: 01071406:5: CMI_DGS (inconsistent_auto_resync --> inconsistent_help) on event Load_fails for device group /Common/device-group-failover-foo.
    Sep 14 02:34:51 rip-2 notice mcpd[5416]: 01071407:5: CMI_DGS (inconsistent_help --> .) for event Xa_success for device group /Common/device-group-failover-foo state not changed.
