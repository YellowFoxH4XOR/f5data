Policy Information

Product Update

F5 hereby announces the End of Life (EoL) plan for the Advanced Client Authentication (ACA) for all F5 BIG-IP platforms. This applies to all fully-licensed ACA products and bundles that include ACA.

ACA will be officially End of Sale effective December 1, 2013.

Software EoL Schedule

ProductEnd of Sale (EoS)End of Software Development (EoSD)End of Support Contract Renewal (EoSCR)End of Technical Support (EoTS)
BIG-IP Advanced Client Authentication Module1-Dec-20131-Dec-20131-Dec-20141-Dec-2015

Technical Support

F5 maintains generous lifecycle policies that allow customers to enjoy many years of both support and new software releases. Customers whose platforms are designated EoL and who are currently under maintenance contracts will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the Service Contract Renewal date expires.

After the EoSD date, 1-Dec-2013, F5 will not be able to provide software fixes for any technical issues and F5 will continue to provide technical support for ACA on a best-effort basis.

Migration Options

F5 recommends that if you are currently using ACA, to use BIG-IP Access Policy Manager (APM) as an alternative mechanism for client authentication. BIG-IP APM is a flexible, high-performance access and security solution that provides unified global access to your business-critical applications and networks.
