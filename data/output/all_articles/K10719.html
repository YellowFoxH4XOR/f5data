Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If you generate a self-signed client root certificate after installing cumulative hotfix HF-603-5, most of the certificate fields will appear blank on the confirmation screen. Additionally, an invalid date is displayed in the Expiration field on the confirmation screen. For example:

Expiration        0//2000

This is a cosmetic issue; the self-signed certificate will contain all of the information you entered on the previous screen.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR130140 and it was fixed in FirePass version 6.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the FirePass release notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in cumulative hotfix HF-603-5-1.1 issued for FirePass version 6.0.3. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the cumulative hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
