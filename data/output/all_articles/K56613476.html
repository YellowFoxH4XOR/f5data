Known Issue

A large number of webtop resources may cause the access policy to fail.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system is configured with an access policy that includes a webtop.
  • A large number of resources are assigned to the webtop (approximately 221).

Impact

The BIG-IP APM access policy fails to run.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Users are unable to access the webtop page.
  • The BIG-IP APM access policy fails to run.
