Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The RADIUS health monitor does not comply with RFC 2865. RFC 2865 specifies that an Access-Request must contain an NAS-IP-Address attribute or an NAS-Identifier attribute or both attributes.

The RADIUS health monitor does not contain either of these attributes.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR65703, and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.3 and 9.4.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
