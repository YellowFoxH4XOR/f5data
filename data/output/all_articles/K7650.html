Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. A large amount of path probing can cause the connection from the big3dshim process to the big3d process to stall. The connection between the two daemons stalls when the big3dshim process is attempting to write information to the big3d process. As a result, the objects that the big3dshim process is attempting to write to the big3d process are reported by BIG-IP LTM as being in an unknown state.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR69156, CR67753, and CR67755 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.1.3, 9.2.4, 9.3.0, and 9.4.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.
