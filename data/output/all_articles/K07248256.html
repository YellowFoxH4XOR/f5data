K07248256: Creating ASM Child Policy fails with error ''Could not update the Policy '/partition/policyName'. Inherited values may not be changed.''

Type
Support Solution
Published
2021-07-27
Updated
2023-10-17
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP ASM: 16.0.0, 15.1.X, 14.1.X, 15.1.8, 15.1.7, 15.1.6, 15.1.5, 15.1.4, 15.1.3, 15.1.2, 15.1.1, 15.1.0, 14.1.5, 14.1.4, 14.1.3, 14.1.2, 14.1.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K07248256

Description

  • Creating an ASM Child Policy fails and displays a pop-up error message.
  • Error messages:
    "Could not update the Policy '/partition/policyName'.  Inherited values may not be changed."

Environment

  • ASM Provisioned
  • ASM Parent policy created with signature staging disabled.
  • Creating a new child policy with that policy as a parent.

Cause

This is a known bug, ID986937.

Recommended Actions

Workaround:

  1. Create the policy without assigning it to the parent.
  2. Assign it to the parent policy on the Inheritance Settings page.

Additional Information

This bug is fixed in 16.0.1.2 and later. An engineering hotfix containing a fix may be requested on other versions by contacting F5 Support.
