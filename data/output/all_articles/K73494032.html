K73494032: Disk partition /var (slot #) has only % free

Type
Support Solution
Published
2021-07-09
Updated
2025-04-02
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K73494032

Description

The system is reporting the /var space to be nearly full.

 

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • Tenant
  • Guest

 

Cause

UCS files may take up significant space, especially when APM EPSEC update files are left on the system.

 

Recommended Actions

Download any UCS backup archives to your workstation and delete them from the BIG-IP system.

Delete any APM EPSEC update files: K21175584: Removing unnecessary OPSWAT EPSEC packages from the BIG-IP APM system 

 

Additional Information

  • K14403:  Maintaining disk space on the BIG-IP system
  • K000133778: Unable to delete Epsec Packages from GUI or CLI after removing them manually
