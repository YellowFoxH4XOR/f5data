Known Issue

The subscriber fails to establish a call when the system drops the Session Initiation Protocol (SIP) Application Layer Gateway (ALG) INVITE message.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your system is configured with a Carrier-Grade NAT (CGNAT) SIP ALG profile.
  • A subscriber attempts to establish a call after a SIP register message.
  • The BIG-IP system receives a SIP INVITE message.

Impact

The subscriber fails to establish the SIP calls.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Subscribers are unable to establish the SIP calls.
  • The BIG-IP system drops SIP INVITE messages.
