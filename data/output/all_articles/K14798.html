Known Issue

The SASP monitor may immediately close its subsequent TCP connections to the Group Workload Manager (GWM) server.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The configuration contains a resource that uses the SASP monitor.
  • A recovery has occurred from a previous connection failure between the SASP monitor and the GWM server.

When a connection failure occurs between the SASP monitor and the GWM server, the GWM server is unable to reset its connection to the SASP monitor. During this time, when the connection times out, the SASP monitor continues to attempt to reconnect to the GWM server. When the connection recovers, the SASP monitor sends a FIN packet to the subsequent connection, even after the connection is established.

Impact

Incorrect SASP monitor operation disrupts traffic to the pool members.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • The SASP monitor sends a FIN packet to subsequent connections, even after they have been established.

Workaround

There is no work around for this issue. To recover from this issue, you can temporarily remove the SASP monitor from the affected resource pool, and then add it back.
