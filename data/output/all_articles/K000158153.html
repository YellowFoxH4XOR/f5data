Description

In very rare circumstances F5OS-A running on appliances may be missing the /var/export/chassis mount which can cause disk space on the root filesystem to fill up.

Example logs from /var/log/sw-mgmt.debug:

20-Sep-23 14:43:22 - CRITICAL: sw-mgmt: priority=critical msgid=0x3501000000000182 msg=The disk is reaching full capacity. Deport unused ISOs before importing any new ISOs. Stopped Import.

Other symptoms may include: 

* Daemon is not running, not healthy:

 dev-partition_image-export_chassis.device
  Loaded: loaded
  Active: inactive (dead)

systemd[1]: Job dev-partition_image-export_chassis.device/start timed out.
systemd[1]: Timed out waiting for device dev-partition_image-export_chassis.device.
systemd[1]: Job dev-partition_image-export_chassis.device/start failed with result 'timeout'.
systemd[1]: Job dev-partition_image-export_chassis.device/start timed out.
systemd[1]: Timed out waiting for device dev-partition_image-export_chassis.device.
systemd[1]: Job dev-partition_image-export_chassis.device/start failed with result 'timeout'.


* orchestration-agent timeouts in platform.log: 

orchestration-agent[7]: priority="Err" version=1.0 msgid=0x506000000000040 msg="Timeout waiting for response from NodeAgent" TENANT="myTenant" NODE=1.
orchestration-agent[7]: priority="Err" version=1.0 msgid=0x506000000000042 msg="Failed to allocate memory for tenant" TENANT="myTenant" node=1.

* System logs may show repeated timeouts and dependency failures related to dev-partition_image-export_chassis.device and var-export-chassis.mount. 

The issue is associated with Bug ID 1388921.

 

Impact

  • Disk full errors,
  • Unable to upgrade
  • Tenant fails to deploy with "Insufficient memory on node, retrying tenant deployment" error.

Environment

  • Product: F5OS - Appliance
  • Symptoms observed after tenant deployment attempts
  • Systemctl and platform logs show device and mount failures for partition_image and VDO volumes

Cause

Bug ID 1388921

Recommended Actions

Contact F5 Support for assistance capturing qkview, creating the partition, and mitigating the disk usage.
