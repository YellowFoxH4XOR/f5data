Description

After upgrading one of the BIG-IP devices in a HA pair to newer software version, you may observe a message similar to the following: 

   01340005:3: HA Connection with peer 10.1.10.1:1029 for traffic-group /Common/traffic-group-1 received unexpected TLS handshake.

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • HA
  • Software upgrade

Cause

BIG-IP devices in an HA pair are not running the same version of code, and they are not successfully establish mirroring connection.

Recommended Actions

Upgrade the peer device(s).
Note: HA communication issues may leave both devices in an unintended HA state, such as both staying in the online state. When this happens, you can set one device in the offline state, as you proceed with the upgrade process.

If the message still appears even after upgrading the peer devices(s), contact F5 Support.
