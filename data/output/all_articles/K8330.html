Topic

Extended Validation SSL (EV SSL) certificates are a special type of SSL certificate that requires the issuing Certificate Authority (CA) to perform a validation of the requesting organization’s identity before issuing the certificate.

Note: For more information about EV SSL certificates, refer to the CA/Browser Forum.

Installing an Extended Validation SSL certificate

To install an EV SSL certificate on your FirePass controller, perform the following procedure:

  1. Log on to the FirePass Administrative Console.
  2. Navigate to the Device Management: Security: Certificates page.
  3. In the Renew/Replace SSL Server Certificate section, click the Install link.
  4. In the Paste the new certificate in the PEM format (for Apache + mod_ssl) here field, paste the EV SSL certificate.
  5. In the Paste the corresponding cryptographic key in PEM format here field, paste the private key corresponding to the EV SSL certificate.
  6. In the Enter password here field, enter the password for the private key.
  7. In the Optionally, put your intermediate certificate chain here (in the PEM format) field, paste the Extended Validation Intermediate CA certificate.

    Note: If you do not have an Extended Validation Intermediate CA certificate, do not proceed to the next step. Contact the CA who issued your EV SSL certificate to obtain the appropriate Extended Validation Intermediate CA certificate. Your CA may instruct you to enter a certificate chain (multiple certificates) in this field, for example an Extended Validation Root CA certificate and an Extended Validation Intermediate CA certificate. The appropriate certificate chain for your EV SSL certificate can only be provided by the issuing CA.
  8. Click the Go! button.

    Important: Do not proceed to the next step if you receive an error message similar to the following example:

    Your certificate chain cannot be fully verified

    For steps to resolve this error message, refer to K8374: Error Message: Your certificate chain cannot be fully verified.
  9. If no certificate errors are reported, click the Save button.
  10. Click the Web Services tab.
  11. Click the Configure link next to the web service you want to configure.
  12. In the Certificate menu, select the certificate you just installed.

    Note: The Certificate menu will not appear unless the Use SSL check box is selected.
  13. Click the Update button.
  14. Click the Finalize tab.
  15. Click the Finalize Changes button.
  16. Click the Apply Changes and Restart button.
  17. Click the Restart button to restart the FirePass controller.

Note: If you have completed this procedure, and your users receive an SSL error message when accessing Dynamic and Web Application Tunnels, refer to K9164: Extended Validation SSL certificates may cause error messages when accessing Dynamic and Web Application Tunnels.
