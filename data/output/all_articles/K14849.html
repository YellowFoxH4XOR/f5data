Known Issue

Virtual servers that you configure to strictly preserve source ports may drop session connections.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You configure a virtual server with the Source Port option set to Preserve Strict.
  • Your BIG-IP system has Clustered Multiprocessing (CMP) enabled.

In this situation, the returned packet for a given request may reach a different Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) and drop the connection.

Impact

Users or applications may experience dropped connections.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H438625 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High screen.
  • You experience excessive dropped sessions.
