Topic

BIG-IP ASM does not currently support the use of Unicode extensions such as extended characters, properties, scripts, or blocks to be specified within a regular expression.

Note: BIG-IP ASM does support all 7-bit (ASCII) Unicode extensions within regular expressions.

The following syntax is commonly used when specifying Unicode extensions within a regular expression:

\p{..}
\P{..}
\X

As a result of specifying Unicode extensions within a regular expression, the behavior may prevent through traffic. Additionally, when the syntax is used within the RegExp Validator, the behavior may return a false positive.
