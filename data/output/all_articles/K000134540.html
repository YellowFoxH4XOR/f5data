Description

BIG-IQ Scheduled Backups  for  APM devices are failing.

BIG-IP reports full  /var  and BIG-IP UCS files  are larger than 100MB

Environment

  • BIG-IQ CM
  • BIG-IP APM
  • Scheduled backups configured on BIG-IQ for BIG-IPs
  • Full /var on BIG-IP
  • UCS files larger than 100MB

Cause

Size of UCS backup caused /var partition full and interupt process of creating and removing backup from BIG-IP

Recommended Actions

 Remove unused epsec definitions to decrease size of the ucs file

K21175584: Removing unnecessary OPSWAT EPSEC packages from the BIG-IP APM system
