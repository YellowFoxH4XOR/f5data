Description

BIG-IP VE in AWS rebooted unexpectedly. The following messages are found in /var/log/kern.log:

err kernel: ena: reading reg failed for timeout. expected: req id[1] offset[88] actual: req id[57006] offset[0]

err kernel: ena: reading reg failed for timeout. expected: req id[2] offset[8] actual: req id[57007] offset[0]

err kernel: ena: Reg read32 timeout occurred

Environment

  • BIG-IP VE in AWS

Cause

Likely a busy hardware device, or hardware failure.

Recommended Actions

Monitor for recurrence. If another reboot occurs for the same reason, migrate the BIG-IP VE to another host. (Please check with AWS.)

Additional Information

The log messages match this AWS article https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AWSEC2/latest/UserGuide/troubleshooting-ena.html :

 

> Register read timeout

> The ENA architecture suggests a limited usage of memory mapped I/O (MMIO) read operations.

> MMIO registers are accessed by the ENA device driver only during its initialization procedure.

>

> If the driver logs (available in dmesg output) indicate failures of read operations, this may be caused

> by an incompatible or incorrectly compiled driver, a busy hardware device, or hardware failure.
