Description

HA connections over port 1029 are lost every ~4 hours

 

You see similar log messages in /var/log/ltm:

Nov 2 15:46:52 192.168.0.1 notice tmm[18641]: 01340011:5: HA unit 1 state change: from 0 to 1.
Nov 2 15:46:52 192.168.0.1 notice tmm2[18641]: 01340011:5: HA unit 1 state change: from 0 to 1.
Nov 2 15:46:52 192.168.0.1 notice tmm1[18641]: 01340011:5: HA unit 1 state change: from 0 to 1.
Nov 2 15:46:52 192.168.0.1 notice tmm1[18641]: 01340007:5: HA Connection with peer 192.168.0.2:32769 for traffic-group /Common/traffic-group-1 closing.
Nov 2 15:46:52 192.168.0.1 notice tmm2[18641]: 01340007:5: HA Connection with peer 192.168.0.2:32768 for traffic-group /Common/traffic-group-1 closing.
Nov 2 15:46:52 192.168.0.1 notice tmm[18641]: 01340007:5: HA Connection with peer 192.168.0.2:32770 for traffic-group /Common/traffic-group-1 closing.
Nov 2 15:46:52 192.168.0.1 notice tmm3[18641]: 01340011:5: HA unit 1 state change: from 0 to 1.

Environment

BIG-IP LTM
Microsoft Hyper-V hypervisor

Cause

This can be caused by a hypervisor that uses dynamic mac addresses for the VE instead of static mac addresses, every time the mac address changes ha connections can be lost for a short amount of time

Recommended Actions

Disable dynamic mac address behavior of hypervisor, use static mac addresses

Additional Information


Some additional settings in the Microsoft Hyper-V hypervisor that may be necessary to set:

Hyper-V Load-Balancing Algorithms:  Hyper-V Port Load-Balancing Algorithm
Virtual Machine Queues (VMQ):  Off
Mac Address Spoofing:  Enabled
Ipsec task offloading:  Disabled
