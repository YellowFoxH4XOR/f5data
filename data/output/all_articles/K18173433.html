Policy Information

With the release of Container Ingress Services 1.10, F5 hereby announces the End of Life (EoL) plan for the BIG-IP Controller for Mesos Marathon and BIG-IP Controller for Cloud Foundry. BIG-IP Controller for Mesos Marathon and BIG-IP Controller for Cloud Foundry will officially reach the End of Sale (EoS) product lifecycle milestone effective September 30, 2019.

ProductEnd of Sale (EoS)End of Software Development (EoSD)End of Technical Support (EoTS)End of Life (EoL)
F5 Controller for Mesos MarathonSeptember 30, 2019September 30, 2019December 31, 2019December 31, 2019
F5 Container Connector - Cloud FoundrySeptember 30, 2019September 30, 2019December 31, 2019December 31, 2019

Technical support

Customers whose container integrations are designated EoL and who are currently under an active BIG-IP maintenance contract will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Customers are encouraged to transition their container integrations before the service contract renewal date expires.

After the EoSD date, September 30, 2019, F5 will no longer be providing software fixes for technical issues, and F5 will continue to provide technical support on a best-effort basis.

Migration considerations

F5 recommends that customers currently using BIG-IP Controller for Mesos Marathon or BIG-IP Controller for Cloud Foundry, use Container Ingress Services supported Kubernetes or OpenShift controllers.

For instructions about deploying supported container integrations, please visit: https://clouddocs.f5.com
