K000161630: MyF5 FCP Subscription shows Map Subscription, missing option to Generate License

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-06-08
Updated
2026-06-09
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP Cloud Edition
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000161630

Description

The FCP subscription in MyF5 displays a Map Subscription link and the option generate license keys is missing. As a result, the customer can't create any new licenses for their BIG-IP VEs from this FCP subscription.

Environment

  • Flex Consumption Program (FCP)

  • MyF5 Self Service Licenses
  • Editor/Admin access to MyF5 FCP Subscription

Cause

The Map subscription option is for renewing VE Term Subscriptions, not FCP subscriptions. This FCP subscription needs to be corrected by F5. 

Recommended Actions

Contact your Customer Success Manager directly or email the Customer Success team (customersuccess@f5.com). If urgent, call F5 Support to open a support case. Provide the following information:

  • Customer
  • Contact Name and Email 
  • FCP Subscription number from the MyF5 Customer Portal
  • Screen shots of the issue

Additional Information

The Map subscription option is grey unless the MyF5 account has editor/admin access to the FCP subscription. Regardless, the Map subscription option is for renewing VE Term Subscriptions, not FCP subscriptions. It shouldn't appear on FCP subscriptions.
