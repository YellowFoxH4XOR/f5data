Known Issue

Generating a BIG-IP qkview snapshot may fail.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • A filestore mapping becomes corrupt. For example, files listed in the filestore are missing from the filesystem.

Impact

You are unable to generate a qkview snapshot.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The qkview processing does not complete.
  • In the /var/log/ltm file, you observe an error message that appears similar to the following example:

    err mcpd[4596]: 0107134e:3: Failed while making snapshot: (Failed to link files existing(/config/ssl/ssl.crt/ca-bundle.crt) ew(/config/.snapshots_d/certificate_d/1389867940_:Common:ca-bundle.crt_1) errno(2)(No such file or directory).) errno(2) errstr(No such file or directory).
