Policy Information

Important: This Solution contains information about BIG-IP 4.x software support running HP DL 380 G3 and G4 platforms.

F5 Networks announces the End of Sale (EoS) and support for the BIG-IP Consolidated Client products.

In September 2004, F5 Networks introduced BIG-IP version 9.0 software and corresponding hardware platforms. As a result, F5 will begin the End of Life (EoL) cycle for older BIG-IP software, and announce the EoS of the existing BIG-IP Blade Controller platforms and software.

Customers whose platforms are designated EoS or EoL can continue using the products with the existing software versions, or choose to migrate to the latest F5 Networks software and hardware architecture and product.

Product support

F5 Networks will continue to provide software and phone support, according to maintenance contracts, for customers using the BIG-IP Consolidated Client product. Customers with HP DL 380 platforms are required to continue running version 4.6.x. Future major versions of BIG-IP, such as 9.x, will not be supported on this platform. F5 Networks will not provide software fixes for technical issues surrounding 4.6.x support on HP platforms (the Consolidated Client product) as of the EoSD date of 12/31/2006.

Product migration

CCI Blade Controller Software is based on BIG-IP version 4.x software. If you want to migrate to BIG-IP version 9.x software, contact your local F5 sales representative.

If you have questions about F5 Network's decision to discontinue this feature, contact your local F5 sales representative.

For information about End of Life terms and definitions, refer to the End of Life Cycle Glossary located in K3225: F5 Networks End of Life Policy.
