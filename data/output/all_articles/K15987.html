Known Issue

You may detect some disabled SFP switch ports as not having a link status of down on the connected device.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The SFP module switch port has been disabled on the BIG-IP system.

Some Finisar OPT-0010-00 SFP modules may continue to provide higher optical power output when the port has been disabled. As a result, the remote connected device may indicate a false positive link state. The issue is a result of the optical transmitter in the SFP/SFP+ module not disabled when the switch port is in a disabled state. This issue may occur with certain optical SFP/SFP+ modules, including the indivudual modules with the following part numbers: OPT-0010-00 (1G-SR) OPT-0011-00 (1G-LR) OPT-0016-00 (10G-SR) OPT-0017-00 (10G-LR) For a list of F5 supported Fiber Gigabit Ethernet SFP, XFP, SFP+ and QSFP+ modules, refer to K6097: Specifications of the Fiber Gigabit Ethernet SFP, XFP, SFP+, QSFP+, and QSFP28 module ports on BIG-IP system platforms.

Impact

Link status may be incorrectly reported as up on remote connected device.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Remote connected device may incorrectly detect the link state as up for a port that has been disabled on the BIG-IP system.

Workaround

To work around this issue, when disabling an affected switch port on the BIG-IP system, you can also disable the connected port on the remote device.

Impact of workaround: The impact of the suggested workaround depends on the specific environment. F5 recommends that you test any such changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.
