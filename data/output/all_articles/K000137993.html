F5 NGINX is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) notification for the existing NGINX Controller Application Delivery and NGINX Controller Application Delivery Security as of January 1, 2024. This applies to SKUs containing NGX-CTRL-AD or NGX-CTRL-AS. End of Software Development (EoSD) and End of Technical Support (EoTS) are effective September 30, 2025.

F5 maintains generous lifecycle policies that allow customers to continue support and receive product updates. Existing NGINX Controller Application Delivery customers can continue to consume the product past the EoS date. License renewals are not available after September 30, 2024.
