K4048: BIG-IP now checks TCP FINs for valid sequence numbers

Applies to

Source

Topic

Starting in version 4.5.11 and 4.6.3, BIG-IP verifies that FINs that are received from a client, and are destined for a delayed binding connection, have a valid sequence number. This verification ensures that a connection will not be disrupted due to invalid FIN packets.
