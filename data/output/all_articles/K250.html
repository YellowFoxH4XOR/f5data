Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The purpose of the status LED is to report whether the unit is in an active (green) or standby (yellow) state. When the power supply fails for a system that is equipped with redundant power supplies, the activity LED flashes yellow and the status LED erroneously changes to green. The color of the status LED should not change.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR28012 and CR41788.
