Description

BIG-IP management plane failure with GUI displaying "REST Framework is not available" and CLI root login failing with "Permission denied" despite valid credentials working on peer device.

Environment

  • Virtual machine on hypervisor infrastructure

Cause

To prevent recurrence, consider the following actions:

  • Log and Storage Review: Review hypervisor and storage logs for any disk or I/O errors; investigate datastore latency or storage disconnect events.
  • Snapshot Management: Avoid long-running or orphaned snapshots that may contribute to the issue.
  • Monitoring Configuration: Configure monitoring for disk I/O errors, datastore latency, log gaps, and configuration sync failures.

 

Recommended Action

To restore normal operation:

1. Schedule a maintenance window

Failover and reboot may cause a brief traffic interruption.

2. Fail over to the peer device

Fail over traffic to the healthy unit in the HA pair.

This restores full traffic and management functionality on the active unit.

3. Reboot the affected VE from the hypervisor

Reboot the affected BIG-IP VE from the hypervisor console or management interface (not from BIG-IP).

This resets the virtual disk state and restores write capability.

4. Verify after reboot

Confirm the following:

  • CLI login works.
  • GUI loads without REST errors.
  • Configuration synchronization succeeds.
  • Logs are being written normally.

 

Additional Information

  • In Virtual Edition deployments, disk issues usually stem from the hypervisor or storage layer. If they continue, contact your virtualization or storage vendor for support.
