K42204639: BIG-IQ - adding two DCDs caused two previously added DCDs to disappear

Applies to

Source

Description

      Adding two DCDs in a remote subnet  caused two previously added DCD's to disappear 

Environment

  • BIG-IQ

Cause
 
     No route to original DCDs from the two new DCDs

Recommended Actions

  1.  DCD and CM cluster member's networking should be fully meshed

Additional Information

        Elasticsearch (ES) can elect any DCD or CM as the ES master so DCDs and CMs must be able to route to all cluster members.
