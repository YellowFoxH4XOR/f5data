Description

The following errors are seen in the ltm log:

Connection error: ssl_hs_ciphprivdec:4197: crypto_req_process (80)
Connection error: ssl_hs_sign_srvkeyxchg:13916: ssl_signature (80)

dmesg may show errors similar to this:

c6xxvf 0000:02:01.0: Failed to send Compat Version Request.
c6xxvf: probe of 0000:02:01.0 failed with error -22
c6xxvf 0000:03:01.0: Failed to send Compat Version Request.
c6xxvf: probe of 0000:03:01.0 failed with error -22

These indicate that the qat driver probe failed, marking these devices offline.

lspci will show the matching devices:

02:01.0 Co-processor [0b40]: Intel Corporation Device [8086:37c9] (rev 04)
03:01.0 Co-processor [0b40]: Intel Corporation Device [8086:37c9] (rev 04)

As a result SSL connections are not processed.

Environment

  • VE

Cause
This is caused by a communication failure between the VE host and the VE guest.  The Physical Device on the host that communicates with the Virtual Device on the guest was not accessible at boot.  Unfortunately, as this happens very early in the boot process, this is difficult for the BIG-IP to recover from short of a reboot.  In extreme cases, the device may be locked up in hardware and only a reboot of the actual hypervisor will resolve.

Recommended Actions

  1. Begin by rebooting the VE guest.
  2. If that does not work, reboot the VE host
