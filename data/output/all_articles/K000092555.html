Topic

Learn what to expect when F5 moves from AskF5 and My Support (support.f5.com) to MyF5 (my.f5.com), F5's new support site.

Description

Everything on AskF5 and My Support is moving to MyF5. For more information about MyF5, refer to the following articles:

  • K24530640: MyF5: the new digital support experience from F5 Support
  • K000089216: MyF5 help

AskF5 will remain online until March 2023, but the few remaining pages will redirect you to MyF5.

The following table provides more details about when and how information and features are moving.

Everything is moving to MyF5
ContentMoving dateMoving detailsMore information
Knowledge and resourcesMoved on February 21, 2023

Visible on December 12, 2022		F5 displays articles, manuals, videos, and all other resources on MyF5 by December 12, 2022 and then moves articles to MyF5 by February 21, 2023.

Consequently, for most articles that have titles that begin with K<number>, you see the following, unless F5 updated the article more recently than February 21, 2023:
  • On article pages, the date that displays next to Updated Date is Feb 21, 2023 or February 22, 2023, depending on your geographical location.
  • In search results, the date that displays next to Modification Date is Feb 21, 2023 or February 22, 2023, depending on your geographical location.
Articles retain the same identification number on MyF5 that they had on AskF5. The following example displays links on the old and new sites:
  • AskF5: support.f5.com/csp/article/K4602
  • MyF5: my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K4602
  • Use search on MyF5
Product downloadsMoved on November 21, 2022Download all F5 products from MyF5. MyF5 replaces F5 Downloads.

F5 Downloads will remain online until February 2023, but F5 will not add new files to it after this move.
  • MyF5 Downloads

    Note: You must log in to MyF5 to access this page.

  • K000090258: Download F5 products from MyF5
AccountsMoved on November 16, 2022 All customers are moving to MyF5. F5 is moving customers to MyF5 in groups. Before F5 moves your account, F5 sends you emails to notify you when your account is moving. The last group moves on this date.

If your account moved and you have not done so already, you must register to use MyF5 to access your support account and support cases.
  • K000089224: Register for a MyF5 account
  • MyF5 accounts section on K000089216: MyF5 help
Support case managementMoved on November 16, 2022Your support cases move with your account, and you must manage support cases using MyF5 after your support account moves. 

My Support, F5's previous case management tool, will remain on online until March 2023, but it contains just read-only information.
  • Support cases section on K000089216: MyF5 help
Knowledge Centers MyF5 replaces the Knowledge Centers that were on AskF5 with the Product Manuals and Release Notes search hub. If you saved links to Knowledge Centers, the system will redirect you to this page with search filters already selected to display the appropriate resources.

Policies and product-specific announcements are not available on the Product and Release Notes page. You can find policies in a regular search on MyF5, and you can sign up for TechNews email notifications to get product announcements.
  • AskF5 search tips on YouTube.

    Note: AskF5 and MyF5 have the same search experience.
