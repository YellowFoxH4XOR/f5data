Description

Support for IPsec tunnel High Availability (HA).

Environment

  • 2 BIG-IP devices in a High Availability (HA)  Active Standby Configuration.
  • IPsec tunnel to remote site.
  • Mirroring is configured.

Cause
For successful HA mirroring and failover of IPsec connections, the following must hold true:

  • IKEv2 is used.
  • The IPsec tunnel must be configured with a floating Self IP as the "Local Address".
  • The configuration is synchronised with the peer device.

Recommended Actions

  • Expect and plan for a tunnel renegotiation on failover.

Additional Information

  1. High Availability is not supported with IKEv1.
  2. Multi-bladed systems must use Version 16.1 or later.
