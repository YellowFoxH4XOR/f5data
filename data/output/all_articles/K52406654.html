Description

After importing Certificates & Keys from BIG-IP to BIG-IQ, the status remains on "associating".

Environment

  • BIG-IQ
  • Import BIG-IP certs and keys

Cause

ID1161601

Recommended Actions

Certificate status gets stuck in intermediate state when there are more than 100+ certificates on Big-IP.
This issue is cosmetic and the certificates have indeed loaded into BIG-IQ.
No action needed.
