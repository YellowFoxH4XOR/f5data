K2826: FirePass 1000 will boot into BIOS configuration if it is rebooted with a serial terminal attached

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Microsoft Windows Hyperterminal, and possibly other terminal emulators, may send a CTRL+BREAK sequence. If FirePass 1000 receives a CTRL+BREAK sequence while it is booting, it will enter the BIOS phase.

To resolve this issue, disconnect the cable and reboot the FirePass system. Wait until the BIOS phase is complete, which is after the second beep or at least one minute after the reboot, before you connect the serial cable and start the terminal emulation software.
