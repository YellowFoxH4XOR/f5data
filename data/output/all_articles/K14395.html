Known Issue

When a big3d SSL certificate is close to or at expiration, the BIG-IP system does not send an SNMP trap.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • A big3d SSL certificate is close to or at its expiration date.
  • BIG-IP devices are configured to send an SNMP trap.
  • A connection is made to a host's bid3d daemon from a remote host through iQuery.

In the event of a big3d SSL certificate close to or at expiry, BIG-IP versions that are unaffected by this issue send traps that appear similar to the following examples:

SNMP_TRAP: SSL cert from IP <IP_ADDRESS> WILL EXPIRE on <DATE>

SNMP_TRAP: SSL cert EXPIRED from IP <IP_ADDRESS>

This issue occurs because the /etc/alertd/bigip_big3d_error_maps.h file is not included in the affected versions.

Impact

The big3d certificates from affected hosts may expire without notifying administrators.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • There will be no notification of big3d SSL certificates that are close to or at expiration.
  • BIG-IP products and modules that rely on iQuery to pass information between hosts may be unable to communicate.
