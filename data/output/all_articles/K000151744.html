Policy Information

With the availability of the F5 rSeries r5920-DF and r10920-DF, F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) for the BIG-IP iSeries i5820-DF and i7820-DF.

This product notice does not impact any subscription licenses or service agreements. Field Upgrade and Add-on SKUs are not included in this EoS announcement.

Refer to K4309: F5 hardware product lifecycle support policy for information about BIG-IP and F5 platform lifecycles.
