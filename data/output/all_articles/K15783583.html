Description

Before software upgrade on Big-IP device, it is recommended to perform some pre-upgrade and post-upgrade checklist.

Environment

  • Software Upgrade
  • Checklist 

Cause
None

Recommended Actions

The pre-upgrade checklists are good to take in order to understand the current status of a device before upgrade. Most of the pre-upgrade checklist revolves around the health of the device.

Below is the list of most of the checks that could be performed. The list is not exhaustive, there could other checks as well mandated by the InfoSec policy of the enterprise.
  • Before Upgrade
  • During/After Upgrade
    Before Upgrade

    1. Ensure you have taken a UCS backup of the device along with the output of the below command, and save it off the box. 
      # f5mku -K
      For additional information refer: K9420

    2. Ensure you have taken a qkview of the device and save it off the box.
      For additional information refer K13132

    3. Ensure you have the root account and admin account details.

    4. Ensure the HA is working fine if the devices are configured for HA between the 2 boxes i.e. failover, failback, and sync should be working fine. Please make sure the HA units are in "In Sync" before the upgrade.
      Disable State Mirroring prior to upgrading, and re-enable once the upgrade is finished.

    5. Ensure all the applications hosted on the F5 are working fine.

    6. Take the screenshot of the Network MAP. This section will provide you details of how many VIPs and Pools are marked green before the upgrade.

    7.  Ensure your service contract is still valid, else upgrade will fail.
      For additional information refer K7727

    8. Ensure that you re-activate your license prior to the upgrade process.
      For additional information refer K7752

    9. Ensure you have deleted the unused HD from the software management.

    10. Ensure that your configuration is valid with the command:
      # tmsh load sys config verify

    11. Ensure that you have important files available (certificates, chain trusts, etc).

    12. Ensure your iRules are compatible with the version you are upgrading to.
      One such article for changes in irules is: K14204621

    13. You could take the qkview from pre XX.XX device and upload to ihealth and view upgrade advisory.

    14. Create a pro-active case with F5 to assist in troubleshooting should you encounter any issues.
      For additional information refer to K16022

    15. Ensure the NTP is working fine.
      For additional information refer to K10240

    16. Run "tmsh save sys config" this is required to ensure any changes made via CLI are saved before booting into another volume.



      During/After Upgrade

      1. Ensure you are creating the new software volume on the day you plan to switchboot. If you are creating software volume before hand then ensure any changes made post creating the new volume, is populated to the new volume using the command 
        # cpcfg <options> <destination_location> 
        For additional information refer K14724

      2. Ensure you upgrade the standby unit first. After upgrade, make this device active and test all your applications.

      3. Once the upgraded device is made Active, ensure the Network Map shows the equal number of VIPs and pool as green as seen in the previous version.

      4. Once all testing succeeds, you could go ahead with the upgrade of the current standby unit. In case any application is failing, you could choose to call in to troubleshoot or failback to standby device to restore services.

      5. Sync the device in the end after the upgrade is successful

      None
