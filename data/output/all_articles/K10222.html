K10222: The RSA Authentication field is pre-populated with existing credentials when using the VPN standalone client

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When RSA authentication is configured, the SecurID tokencode field is pre-populated with the previous tokencode on the VPN standalone client. Users may incorrectly assume that the information that has been pre-populated is correct and press Logon, rather than submitting the new credentials. This may result in the user locking themselves out of their account.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR121875 and it was fixed in FirePass 6.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the FirePass release notes.

This issue has been fixed in cumulative hotfix HF-603-4 issued for FirePass 6.0.3. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the cumulative hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.

For instructions about obtaining a hotfix, refer to K167: Downloading software from F5.

For instructions about installing a hotfix, refer to K3430: Installing FirePass hotfixes.

Workaround

To work around this issue, instruct your users to manually enter their SecurID tokencode each time they are prompted.
