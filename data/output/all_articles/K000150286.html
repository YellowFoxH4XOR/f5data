Description

The customer was having error on their nginx Pods and having application connection issues:</p><p>

</p><pre class="ql-syntax">2025/03/06 17:32:57 [error] 65#65: *3573336 upstream timed out (110: Connection timed out) while reading response header from upstream, client: 172.31.8.7, server: ingress.edf.tractorsupply.com, request: "POST /api/passcode/v1/validate HTTP/1.1", upstream:


2025/03/06 17:32:57 [error] 65#65: *3573336 no live upstreams while connecting to upstream, client: 172.31.8.7, server: ingress.edf.tractorsupply.com, request: "GET /api/productfinder/v1/store/2295/sku/1082747 HTTP/1.1", upstream: "http://vs_default_edf-prod1-ingress-vs_vsr_prod1-gateway_edf-apim_edf-apim/api/productfinder/v1/store/2295/sku/1082747", host: "ingress.edf.tractorsupply.com"

</pre><p>



Environment

Nginx+ R31


Cause

The upstream timed out (110: Connection timed out) while reading response header from upstream” means that nginx successfully connected but then didn’t get the response from upstream. And the no live upstreams error means that the same happened with all servers in the upstream group nginx and returns 502 to initial client request.


And on your config, you have 60 seconds of timeout, and this is the client closed connection before timeout rt=54.832 urt=54.831, so the client prematurely closed connection earlier from it’s side.


This means the issue was on the upstream side/application, it was closing the connection and not sending the reponse.


Recommended Actions

Check on their upstream server/applicatin why it is closing the connection prematurely.


Additional Information

Also, as I saw on the call with the customer, they found that the issue was at the RabbitMQ applications and it got fixed the issue on the customer side, so it was not an NGINX issue.
