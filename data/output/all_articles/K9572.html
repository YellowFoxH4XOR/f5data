Policy Information

With the release of the BIG-IP ASM 3600 platform, F5 is announcing the End of Sale of the BIG-IP ASM/TrafficShield 4100 platform, effective August 1, 2009. F5 is providing Advanced Product Notification to customers and partners announcing intent to retire the products listed below:

Important: This article is only announcing the End of Sale for the TrafficShield/ASM 4100 full appliance and StrongBox demo appliance. This notice does not apply to other F5 products, such as FirePass, that support the 4100 platform.

Technical Support

F5 maintains generous life cycle policies that allow customers to enjoy many years of both support and new software releases. Customers whose platforms are designated EoL and who are currently under maintenance contracts will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the Service Contract Renewal date expires.
