Issue

On BIG-IP 1000, 2000, or 5000 series switch appliances, you may see log entries in the /var/log/messages file that appear similar to the following example:

Feb 11 08:24:17 hostname kernel: ubsec: InitDevice() failed
Feb 11 08:24:17 hostname kernel: ubsec: Device failed fffffc Status=-4 (UBSEC_STATUS_DEVICE_FAILED)
Feb 11 08:24:17 hostname kernel: ubsec:  MCR [e69d8419] ctrl[0c000000] stat[04000000] dma[00dea300]
Feb 11 08:24:17 hostname kernel: ubsec:  --------------- Register Dump ----------------
Feb 11 08:24:17 hostname kernel: ubsec: Command Timeout de
Feb 11 08:24:17 hostname kernel: ubsec1 at pci0 irq 10 maddr 0xfebb0000-0xfebbffff : BCM5820
Feb 11 08:24:17 hostname kernel: ubsec0 at pci0 irq 10 maddr 0xfeab0000-0xfeabffff : BCM5820

This error message indicates serious issues with the SSL hardware. RMA the appliance or the SSL card immediately.
