K000134622: FIPS EUD results may produce false positive

Type

Published
2023-05-16
Updated
2025-02-07

Legacy Content
Applies to

Source

Description

The EUD results may show passed when there is a failure with the FIPS card. Below is the sample expected and  failed FIPS results. 
 
  • Expected FIPS results:

********** FIPS Test Started at 02 21 2013 09:41:54 **********

02 21 2023 09:41:54    Cavium FIPS card detected
02 21 2023 09:41:54    Test Begin: FIPS Test
02 21 2023 09:41:54    Checking for test module
02 21 2023 09:41:54    Cavium FIPS testing...
02 21 2023 09:41:54    Test Complete: FIPS Test PASSED
 
  • FIPS results indicating a FIPS card failure

 ********** FIPS Test Started at 02 21 2023 09:41:54 **********

Test Complete: FIPS Test PASSED
 

Environment

  • BIG-IP FIPS

Cause

Undetermined

Recommended Actions

Contact F5 Technical Support
