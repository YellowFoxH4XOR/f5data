Description

How to read ELA (FCP) Run Rate reporting

Environment

  • VE (Virtual Edition)
    • Cloud Hosted
  • BIG-IQ License Manager
  • ELA and FCP

Cause

This article provides reference information on reading the usage reporting you receive from an F5 CSM

 

Recommended Actions

This report should be sent to you by the Customer Success Manager in charge of the ELA or FCP agreement on a regular basis. It’s intended to provide a one-page overview of your historic F5 license usage. The report consists of 3 main sections covering information over the past year:

  • Report Header
  • Monthly run rates
  • Year to date run rate

 

Report Header


Most of these fields should be self-explanatory except for:

  1. The term will state the initial 3 year period that was agreed and the actual start of the agreement (if waiting for hardware shipments).
  2. A legal contract ID allows us to easily reference the specific agreement that was signed by both parties in case there’s multiple parallel agreements or subscriptions.
  3. These are all order numbers placed on your behalf during the length of the agreement. This allows to find specific sets of provided keys during the duration.

 

Monthly run rates

 

  • Monthly run rates show the list price of the licenses used during the 3 past months
  • The trending usage adjustment covers the associated costs that would be incurred during the True Forward calculation period if you were in it at the moment of the report
    1. Annual Usage Value shows the agreed upon level of use that the agreement can’t drop below. In case of underutilisation this will still be the list price cost incurred.
    2. Trending annual growth represents any current license use above the agreed Annual Usage Value
    3. In the case of over utilisation Trending Next Year Annual Usage Value will represent the next years Annual Usage Value, or stay the same if at commit or underutilising.
  • Actual Annual Growth calculation is done once a year during a specific 3-month period of the agreement (usually months 9, 10, 11).

 

Year to date run rate

 
  • The green line represents the Annual Usage Value from the Monthly run rate table.
  • Any usage under the green line represents underutilisation of the agreement and means not everything scoped in the initial agreement is yet to be deployed, or is not tracked.
  • Any overage is represented over the green line and represents growth of the agreement under existing agreement. Going over the line has no impact on your spend unless done during the 3 months of the True Forward Period.
