Known Issue

The BIG-IP WebAccelerator system may not match special characters entered within the value fields of the policy editor.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You have entered special characters within the policy editor. 

    For example, the accented 'e' character é.

Impact

Your policies may not perform as expected.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Policies may not match strings that contain special characters.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can use UTF-8 strings instead of special characters. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround:  Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

When entering strings within the policy editor, you must use the UTF-8 strings.

For example, take the following word:

résumé

To match that word, enter the UTF-8 equivalent instead, as follows:

r%C3%A9sum%C3%A9
