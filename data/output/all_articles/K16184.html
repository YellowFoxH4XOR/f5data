Known Issue

Description

IDE DMA timeouts may result in unresponsive processes.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • You have one of the following platforms:
    • VIPRION B4100 (A100), or B4200 (A107) blade
    • Virtual Edition (VE) configurations deployed with IDE storage drivers (Xen, Hyper-V)

Impact

The I/O request may fail to complete and result in unresponsive processes.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Some processes become unresponsive and enter the 'D' (uninterruptible) state.
