Known Issue

A BIG-IP High Availability (HA) pair may fail to retain mirror addresses after you upgrade the system.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You upgraded the HA pair from BIG-IP 10.2.x directly to an affected version listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article.

    Note: This issue does not occur if you upgrade the HA pair to BIG-IP 12.0.x first and then subsequently upgrade them to BIG-IP 12.1.x.

Impact

After you upgrade the affected HA pair to BIG-IP 12.1.x, the HA pair fails to mirror connections between the two systems.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • After you upgrade an HA pair to BIG-IP 12.1.x, using the BIG-IP Configuration utility, you observe the Primary and Secondary Local Mirror Addresses are set to None on the Mirror Configuration page for each device. Similarly, you observe that the device configuration stanza is missing the mirror IP configuration in the /config/bigip_base.conf files for each of the affected devices.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 640704 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:

Workaround

There is no workaround for this issue. To recover from this issue, you can reconfigure the primary and secondary local mirror addresses on each affected device. For more information about configuring local mirror addresses, refer to K13478: Overview of connection and persistence mirroring (11.x - 12.x).

Impact of action: Performing the suggested action should not have a negative impact on your system.

If you have not upgraded your BIG-IP 10.2.x HA pair directly to BIG-IP 12.1.x, you can avoid this issue by upgrading the HA pair to BIG-IP 12.0.x first, and then upgrading them to BIG-IP 12.1.x.
