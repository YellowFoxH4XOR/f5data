Environment

VCMP Guest

Cause

Unknown

Recommended Actions

Put the guests that aren't showing in the Guest Status Tab into the Configured state and redeploy.

Impact of procedure: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your vCMP host. The vCMP guest cannot process traffic until it is restarted.

  1. 1. Login to VCMP Host GUI.
  2. 2. Navigate to VCMP --> Guest List.
  3. 3. Select the vcmp guests that are not showing.
  4. 4. For Requested State, select Configured.
  5. 5. Wait for the guest to properly shutdown.
  6. 6. For Requested State, select Deployed.
