K13486: The ARX managed volume root backing share may change after restoring the global-config file

Topic

When multiple shares are configured for a managed volume, the first share imported into the managed volume becomes the default root backing share. However, the global-config file sorts share names in alphabetical order not in the order they were imported.

Therefore, if you are required to rebuild an ARX configuration using the global-config file, the shares will now be imported in alphabetical order. This behavior may cause a different share to become the root backing share. As a result, files placement after restoration may not occur as expected.
