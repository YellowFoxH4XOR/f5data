Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If the BIG-IP LTM system receives a corrupt SSL record containing certain data patterns during an SSL handshake, the SSL handshake parser can loop until it causes heartbeat failure. As a result, TMM can crash.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR75649, and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.3 and 9.4.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
