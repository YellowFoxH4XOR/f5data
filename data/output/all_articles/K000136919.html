Description

Upon receiving delivery of a newly purchased F5 appliance, you notice that some screws are missing.

Environment

Newly purchased F5 appliance

Cause

The missing screws maybe the result of shipping or manufacturing issue.

Recommended Actions

Double check the accessory box
Raise a support case to request for a replacement of the missing screws.
Note: When raising a support case, provide as much information as possible regarding the specific screws required. Include pictures of the relevant part if possible.
