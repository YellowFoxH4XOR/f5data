Known Issue

Statistics collected by TMM for the total number of PVA assisted connections may not be correctly counted.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are using a platform with a PVA processor, such as the 3400, 3410, 6400, 6800, 8400, or 8800 platform
  • The PVA is accelerating connections on one or more of your virtual servers

Impact

This is a cosmetic issue that will only affect customers analyzing PVA statistics.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The total number of PVA accelerated connections displays a 0 (zero) or a low value. For example, the tmsh show sys pva-traffic command displays 0 for the total number of PVA assisted connections.
