K26557974: Schedule of some cron job are changed after upgrading

Type
Support Solution
Published
2021-07-27
Updated
2025-02-06
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K26557974

Description

Customer noticed that schdule of following cron job are changed after upgrading from 14.1.2.6 to 14.1.4.2.

 /usr/bin/updatecheck -a
 /usr/bin/phonehome_upload
 /usr/bin/copy_rrd save

e.g)
 // 14.1.2.6
 MAILTO=""
 */10 * * * * /usr/bin/diskmonitor
 0 */4 * * * /usr/bin/diskwearoutstat
 35 03 * * * /usr/bin/updatecheck -a       
 35 03 21 * * /usr/bin/phonehome_upload
 1 * * * * /usr/bin/copy_rrd save

 //14.1.4.2
 MAILTO=""
 1-59/10 * * * * /usr/bin/diskmonitor
 0 */4 * * * /usr/bin/diskwearoutstat
 39 05 * * * /usr/bin/updatecheck -a
 39 05 21 * * /usr/bin/phonehome_upload
 5 * * * * /usr/bin/copy_rrd save
 

Environment

Upgrading from 14.1.2.6 to 14.1.4.2.

Cause

- Schedule of updatecheck and phonehome_upload are set based on date/time when upgrading.
- Schedule of copy_rrd is set randomly when upgrading.
Thus, those schedule are changed when upgrading. It's not bug

Recommended Actions

None
