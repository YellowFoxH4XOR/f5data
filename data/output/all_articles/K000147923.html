Understanding Router Monitoring Responsibilities 

Environment

  • DDoS
  • Local Router
  • Netflow

Resolution/Answer

At F5 we prioritize the security and performance of your network infrastructure through our Router Monitoring Service. This involves analyzing data flows to predict potential attacks.

It is essential to clarify the scope of responsibility regarding router monitoring.

While F5 diligently monitors data flows and provides proactive insights into potential threats, it is important to note that when a customer's router is down and no data flow is received, F5's monitoring responsibility is limited. 

Customer Responsibility 

In instances where the customer's router is offline or experiencing connectivity issues where F5 collectors do not receive flow data, it becomes the customer's responsibility to monitor their infrastructure and ensure the on-prem router exports the necessary flow data for proper monitoring by F5 collectors. This includes and not limited to ensuring the uptime and functionality of the on-prem routers.

Additional Information

For further clarification or assistance regarding F5 Router Monitoring responsibilities , please reach out to the SOC.
