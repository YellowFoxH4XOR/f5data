K5142: BIG-IP may use a source port that is already used by another BIG-IP feature when performing a health check

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. BIG-IP does not perform a sufficiently rigorous check to ensure that source ports used by a health check are not simultaneously in use by a BIG-IP virtual server, NAT, or SNAT. As a result, health checks may occasionally attempt to use a source port that is already in use. When this occurs, the health check will fail and the node will be marked down until the health check runs again successfully.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this problem as CR37986 and CR42114.
