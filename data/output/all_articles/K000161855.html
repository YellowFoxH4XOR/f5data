K000161855: 100G Link Fails to Come Up with OPT-0060 PAM4 BiDi Optics on F5OS-A r5600

Applies to

Source

Description

When using F5 OPT-0060 (40G/100G PAM4 BiDi) optics on F5OS-A r5600, interfaces may fail to come up at 100G in direct loopback or device-to-device scenarios. System logs show persistent "DDM low alarm set" and "Receiver power low alarm" errors on all lanes at 100G, while the same optics and cable work normally at 40G. The issue can be triggered by a specific sequence of optic removal/insertion and port state transitions, especially when DDM receive power is low. After a system reboot and optic replacement, the 100G link may come up successfully.

Environment

  • Platform: F5-BIG-LTM-R5600 (r5000 series, C129)
  • Software: F5OS-A 1.8.3-23493
  • Optics: F5 OPT-0060 (40G/100G PAM4 BiDi, Rev A1)
  • Direct loopback or device-to-device connection
  • Sequence involving optic removal/insertion and port enable/disable
  • DDM receive power below threshold at 100G

Cause

A rare corner case in the PAM4 optic initialization sequence is triggered when an optic is connected with low DDM receive power, the port is disabled, the optic is removed, the port is enabled, and the optic is reinserted. This leaves the 100G link unable to recover until a full system reboot. 

Answer/Recommended Actions

  • If 100G interfaces fail to come up and DDM receive power low alarms are observed on all lanes, perform a full system reboot to reset the PAM4 link initialization state.
  • Avoid removing and reinserting optics while the port is disabled and DDM receive power is low.
