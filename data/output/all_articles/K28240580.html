Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may enter an infinite restart loop after running the bigstart restart command.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system runs on the iSeries 2000 or 4000 platform.
  • While TMM is actively processing traffic, you run the bigstart restart command.
  • You synchronize the datasync-global-dg device group for a high availability (HA) configuration.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restarting, and devices configured as a HA pair may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • If configured for HA, your BIG-IP system fails over to the standby system.
  • Your BIG-IP system generates multiple TMM core files to the /shared/core directory, which include a time stamp that correlates with the traffic interruption.

Workaround

There is no workaround for this issue. However, you may be able to prevent the issue by modifying the TMM configuration. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Note: The following procedure may not be effective in all scenarios; results depend on the conditions during which this issue has occurred.

Impact of procedure: The bigstart restart command temporarily interrupts traffic processing. If the system is configured as part of a HA pair, the device may fail over. F5 recommends that you perform this procedure during a scheduled maintenance window.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP command line.
  2. Use a text editor to create the /config/tmm_init.tcl file.
  3. In the file, add the following line of text:

    Important: Add the line of text exactly as shown. Any errors can prevent TMM from restarting.

    HSB::failures_before_reset 100

  4. Save the file.
  5. Ensure that all TMM traffic flows are stopped.
  6. Type the following command:

    bigstart restart tmm
