Description

You have added new HDx into BIG IP VE, by mistake
You wish to remove the HDx (Never used it)
From BIG IP perspective:

From ESX perspective:

Environment

  • BIG IP VE
  • Another HDx created by mistake

Cause

Another HDx created by mistake and you wish to remove it.

Recommended Actions

Important: Deleting HDx from BIG IP will cause lost your data (for HDx)

  1. Generate a UCS backup file of your configuration before activity will take place (In case you will required to restore your configuration). To assist, please use:
    • K13132: Backing up and restoring BIG-IP configuration files with a UCS archive
  2. On Hypervisor's Host:    
    • Power Off the relevant BIG IP VE
    • Go to Edit Settings and remove the HDx you added before( "Hard Disk #"), by hover the mouse on the relevant HD - It will show you 'X' sign to delete the HD from Hypervisor, click on it.

Note: Be sure you remove the extra HD created, which it was NOT in use at all!
