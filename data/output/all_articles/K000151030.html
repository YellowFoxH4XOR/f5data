Description

Could the execution of the mitigation procedure for CVE-2024-11187 on the BIG-IP system potentially result in service disruption, particularly considering that the final step involves restarting the BIND (named) service? Using command:

 # bigstart restart named

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • High availability (HA) environment
  • Service: BIND (named) DNS service
  • Configuration: Systems where DNS services are managed by the BIG-IP named process

 

Cause

Restarting the named (BIND) service as part of the mitigation for CVE-2024-11187 results in a temporary interruption of DNS services. This occurs because the service is stopped and then started, momentarily halting DNS resolution on the device.

 

Recommended Actions

If your BIG-IP is in production and actively handling DNS traffic, schedule the restart during a maintenance window or at a time of low traffic to minimize potential disruption.

  • The # bigstart restart named command does not require a full system downtime or a failover event.
  • Does not trigger a failover event, and does not disrupt general traffic processing unless DNS services provided by "named" are critical to traffic flows. 
  • It stops and then starts the BIND (named) DNS service.
  • During the restart, there will be a brief period when the DNS service is unavailable.
  • The impact is typically brief (seconds), but any active DNS queries at that moment may be dropped or experience a timeout, while the named process restarts.
  • Consult F5 article K000150814 for further information on the vulnerability and mitigation procedures.
