Known Issue

The BIG-IP system does not send a Key Exchange (KE) in the payload when IPsec renegotiation uses CREATE_CHILD_SA, and Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS) is enabled.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured the BIG-IP system as an IPsec tunnel endpoint.
  • You have configured IPsec tunnels to use the IKEv2 protocol.
  • You have enabled PFS mode in an IPsec policy.
  • The initial Security Association (SA) with a remote tunnel endpoint has timed out.

Impact

SA renegotiation between BIG-IP systems will not use new keying information, and SA renegotiation between the BIG-IP systems' third-party endpoints will fail.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • You notice that the BIG-IP system fails to send KE in the payload during the CREATE_CHILD_SA exchange with another BIG-IP system during SA renegotiation.
  • You notice that the SA renegotiation fails between the BIG-IP system and third-party endpoints.
