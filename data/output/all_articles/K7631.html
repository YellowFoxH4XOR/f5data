Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. BIG-IP LTM is incorrectly reported to BIG-IP GTM as being available and enabled when the BIG-IP LTM system is configured with a virtual server that is associated with a pool that does not have enabled members. As a result, the BIG-IP GTM system may direct traffic to a virtual server that does not contain active or enabled members.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR65706 and CR65705 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.1.3, 9.3.0, and 9.4.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.
