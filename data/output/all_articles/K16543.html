Known Issue

Setting up configuration synchronization (ConfigSync) on clustered standalone configurations may degrade Configuration utility and Master Control Program Daemon (MCPD) performance.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system runs 11.5.1 HF3 or 11.5.1 HF4.
  • Your system uses standalone or Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP) guests with three or more blades.
  • Your configuration includes the ConfigSync IP address.

Impact

System performance may be degraded.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You notice that Configuration utility and mcpd process performance is severely degraded.
