Issue

Due to product availability, F5 will no longer provision the 1600 & 3600 platforms with 320 gigabyte hard disk drives. As of February 22, 2011, the 1600 & 3600 platforms will include 500 gigabyte hard disk drives.

If two devices are configured as a high availability pair, and one member of the pair has a 500 gigabyte drive and the other member has the older 320 gigabyte drive, the high availability and failover functionality of the pair will be unaffected by the disparity in drive sizes (excluding disk-space specific considerations, such as storage of log archives). The new drive provisioning will not negatively affect the functionality, performance, or cosmetic finish of the platforms.

For example, the following fdisk -l command output illustrates a 1600 platform provisioned with the new 500 gigabyte hard disk drive:
