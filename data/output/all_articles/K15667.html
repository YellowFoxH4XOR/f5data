Policy Information

F5 has released new BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) V18 Version Plus (V+) licenses that are compatible with software up to BIG-IP 18.x.x. The new BIG-IP VE V+ licenses offer the broadest range of performance options and multi-cloud application services, including the best-value Good, Better, Best offers, provide license portability between supported private and public clouds, support bring your own license (BYOL), pay-as-you-grow, and subscription licenses, and include options for per-app VE and on-demand license pool spin-up with the BIG-IQ system.

With these new enhancements to BIG-IP VE and support for new licensing models, F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of the following legacy BIG-IP VE licenses in the following table, effective April 1, 2015. In addition, F5 is announcing the End of Technical Support (EoTS) for these legacy BIG-IP VE licenses to align and coincide with EoTS of BIG-IP 13.x software, which is currently targeted for December 2023.

Important: If you are a current customer using the following legacy BIG-IP VE licenses, you can run and continue to renew support for any version up to BIG-IP 13.1.x that has not gone to EoTS. Any other upgrades to versions greater than 13.1.x (which BIG-IP software may not prevent you from doing) are not supported and your support maintenance contract cannot be renewed. Customers interested in and requiring v14.x and higher code version must upgrade and buy the latest Version Plus perpetual licenses, move to Subscription license, or Enterprise License Agreement (ELA).

Note: F5 does not support current add-on licenses for legacy VE SKUs. Customers should trade in or upgrade their legacy SKU for the latest a Version Plus license.

Note: This policy article applies to the legacy pre-Version Plus VE licenses as listed in the following table.

For the latest BIG-IP 13.x software end of support dates, refer to K5903: BIG-IP software support policy. F5 is providing advance product notification to customers and partners announcing End of Sale and End of Support for the products listed in the following table.

Technical Support

F5 maintains generous lifecycle policies that allow customers to enjoy many years of both support and new software releases. Customers whose platforms are designated EoS and who are currently under maintenance contracts will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the Service Contract Renewal date expires. This EoS notice applies only to VE licenses. For specific details about End of Software Development (EoSD) and EoTS dates, refer to K5903: BIG-IP software support policy.
