Description

When configuring new deployment of a BIGIP VE on ESXi, which HDD provisioning should it be?

Environment

  • BIG-IP Virtual Edition on ESXi
  • New  BIG-IP® VE deployment
  •  Virtual Disks provisioning

Cause

Virtual Disks provisioning options:

  • Thick
  • Thin

Recommended Actions

For production environments, virtual disks should be deployed Thick. Thin deployments are acceptable for lab environments
