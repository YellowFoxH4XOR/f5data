Environment

  • A previous resizing of the directory.

Cause

When resizing a directory  "--resizefs" was omitted.

Recommended Actions

1. Run the following command to reflect the change in "df -h" and "lvdisplay".
         resize2fs /dev/mapper/vg--db--sda-set.1._var

2. Reboot the BIG-IP:
        reboot
