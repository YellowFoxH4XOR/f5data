K2004: BIG-IP failover on failure of nCipher FIPS cards

Topic

Beginning in BIG-IP version 4.5 PTF-04, BIG-IP will fail over when a nCipher FIPS card fails. In prior versions, BIG-IP does not fail over if a FIPS card fails.
