Description

Network firewall rules matching EU or AP Address/Region do not allow, drop, or reject as expected and do not appear to work.

Environment

  • BIG-IP AFM
  • Rules matching geolocation codes for EU or AP

Cause

The geolocation codes are based on MaxMind databases and they have an explanation related to EU and AP country codes:

"Please note that 'EU' and 'AP' codes are only used when a specific country code has not been designated. Blocking or re-directing by 'EU' or 'AP' will only affect a small portion of IP addresses. Instead, you should list the countries you want to block/re-direct individually."

Recommended Actions

F5 recommends to use country codes instead of, or in addition to, EU and AP continent codes. For a list of country codes refer to the ISO 3166 page.
