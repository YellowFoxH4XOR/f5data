K000151558: Interfaces 1-10 suddenly went down and "Optical Power Fault" is reported in system events

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-05-28
Updated
2025-05-28
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000151558

Description

Interfaces 1-10 suddenly went down, and the system is unable to initialize optics in these ports, even after reboot or power cycle. The following logs can be found in platform.log.

2025-05-08T15:52:19.477061+09:00 appliance-1 fpgamgr[9]: priority="Err" version=1.0 msgid=0x303000000000038 msg="Optic not initialized, unplug and re-install." portgroup="1" socket=0.
2025-05-08T15:52:23.480163+09:00 appliance-1 fpgamgr[9]: priority="Err" version=1.0 msgid=0x303000000000038 msg="Optic not initialized, unplug and re-install." portgroup="2" socket=1.
2025-05-08T15:52:24.483119+09:00 appliance-1 fpgamgr[9]: priority="Err" version=1.0 msgid=0x303000000000038 msg="Optic not initialized, unplug and re-install." portgroup="7" socket=6.
2025-05-08T15:52:39.284889+09:00 appliance-1 fpgamgr[9]: priority="Err" version=1.0 msgid=0x303000000000038 msg="Optic not initialized, unplug and re-install." portgroup="8" socket=7.

System events indicate power fault in hardware, more precisely in Network Service Engine (NSE) optical component.

# show system events
65545 appliance power-fault ASSERT CRITICAL "Power fault detected in hardware" "2025-05-08 01:28:14.947110010 UTC"
65545 appliance power-fault EVENT NA "Asserted: NSE optical power fault" "2025-05-08 01:28:14.947125862 UTC"

Environment

  • rSeries 5XXX or 10XXX
  • Interfaces 1-10 suddenly went down
  • System events indicate power fault in hardware, more precisely in Network Service Engine (NSE) optical component

Cause

Hardware fault.

Recommended Actions

Follow https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K13428255 to capture AOM diagnostic information.

If upon review of the captured information, there is 0V on the 3.3V line, this is a cause for NSE optical power fault and the device needs RMA.

5.0VSB : +5.065 V
3.3VSB : +3.322 V
2.5V_ASW : +2.507 V
5.0V : +5.052 V
3.3V_OPT_NSE : +0.004 V <----------
3.3V_OPT_ASW : +3.322 V

If there is no other indication of hardware fault, contact F5 Support for further troubleshooting steps.

Additional Information

According to https://clouddocs.f5.com/training/community/rseries-training/html/rseries_performance_and_sizing.html

In rSeries 10XXX, interfaces 1-10 are managed by NSO, 11-20 are managed by ASW.
In rSeries 5XXX, all interfaces are managed by ASW.

The article shows an example of Network Services Socket (NSO) failure. NSE optical component is part of NSO. The logs would be similar if an optical component in Appliance Switch (ASW) fails.
