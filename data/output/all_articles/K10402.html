Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP ASM may generate false positives when it handles a request parameter that appears to contain multiple encoded characters.

In the following example, the same encoding is used even though different characters are represented; however, the request would trigger a false positive:

POST /test_form.asp HTTP/1.1
Accept: */*
Content-Type: application/x-www-form-urlencoded
Host: testhost.askf5.com
Content-Length: 20

param_name=%20%20%25

Note: The encoded characters do not have to be contiguous; they can be separated by spaces or other characters.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR120643 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.4.8 and 10.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM release notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-10.0.1-342.0-HF1, which has been issued for BIG-IP 10.0.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
