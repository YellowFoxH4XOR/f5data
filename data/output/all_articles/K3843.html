Policy Information

Product Update

F5 hereby announces the End of Life (EoL) policy for FirePass Client Operating System support features. This applies to all fully licensed FirePass products running on any of the supported FirePass hardware platforms.

Under this policy, unless an explicitly stated EoL is announced by F5 for a particular client Operating System version, F5 will offer support for FirePass client Operating System ONLY while the Operating System vendor actively supports that particular version of the client Operating System.

From the date the Operating System vendor ends support for a particular client Operating System, F5 will no longer support that particular client Operating System. The End of Life Policy for the FirePass client Operating System support feature will allow F5 to focus on developing and supporting product capabilities on other more current and vendor supported client Operating Systems.

Questions

If you have questions about F5's decision to discontinue this feature, contact your local F5 representative or service department.

EoL Terms and Descriptions

For information about End of Life terms and definitions, refer to the End of Life Cycle Glossary located in K3225: F5 Networks End of Life Policy.
