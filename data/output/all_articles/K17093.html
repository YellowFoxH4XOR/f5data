Known Issue

When a High-Speed Bridge (HSB) transmitter failure occurs, registers are not dumped to the log file to determine the cause of the lockup.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are using a BIG-IP system with an HSB.
  • The HSB has a hardware failure or lockup.

Impact

There may not enough information in the log files to determine the cause of the HSB lockup.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H516140 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • In the /var/log/ltm log file, you may observe error messages similar to the following example:

    info bcm56xxd[<pid>]: 012c0015:6: Link: 4.3 is DOWN
    info bcm56xxd[<pid>]: 012c0012:6: Reset HSBe2 (bus 1) HGM2 MAC completed on higig2 link 4.3 down event.
    info bcm56xxd[<pid>]: 012c0015:6: Link: 4.3 is UP
    crit tmm5[<pid>]: 01230111:2: Interface 0.6: HSB DMA lockup on transmitter failure.
