Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP ASM _asm_clientside iRule erroneously forces a load balancing decision for some connections. This can cause NTLM authentication connections processed by the ASM modules to fail.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR70020, CR70078, and CR70022 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM and ASM versions 9.2.4, 9.3, and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM and ASM Release Notes.
