Description

Customers often need clarification on whether to contact F5 Support or F5 Professional Services for various types of assistance. This article explains the key differences between these two service offerings and provides guidance on which team to engage based on the nature of your request.

Environment

  • F5 Support: Reactive support for operational issues
  • F5 Professional Services: Proactive consulting and implementation services

Cause

The distinction between F5 Support and F5 Professional Services scope of work is not always clear to customers, leading to confusion about which team to contact for specific needs.

Recommended Actions

  1. Understand the difference

F5 Support and F5 Professional Services serve different but complementary roles. 

 

    1. F5 Support : Handles break-fix issues, troubleshooting, technical questions and operational problems with existing deployments. Support is included with your F5 product subscription.

  1. How to Contact
    1. Reach out to F5 Support through one of the following channels:
      • Create a case : https://my.f5.com/manage/s/createcase
      • Call : K000135931: Contact F5 Support
      • Chat : Available upon signing in to MyF5 Portal (lower right corner)
    1. Reach out to F5 Professional Services through one of the following channels:
      • Contact your F5 Account Team
      • Visit: Request F5 Professional Services
      • Contact your F5 Sales Representative
    • Note : If you don't know your F5 Account Team or Sales Representative, contact F5 Support and ask to connect you with your account team
