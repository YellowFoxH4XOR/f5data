Description

You find your BIG-IP is unable to re-establish a connection over a BGP route after applying a new license or changing your device's provisioning

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • BGP Routes

Cause

Bug ID 945265 causes the BIG-IP to advertise an incorrect default route via BGP

Recommended Actions

  • To immediately restore connectivity to the route, refer to Bug ID 945265 in Related Content for a workaround
  • To permanently address the issue, upgrade to a version not affected by this ID

None
