Description

A high CPU spike was observed on odd cores of the BIG-IP ASM VE system, occurring hourly. Additionally, the mysqld process showed elevated CPU usage during these spikes.

Also /var/log/restjavad.log logs indicated errors such as below :

restjavad.log:[SEVERE][8100/tm/asm/owasp/generate-score OWASPPolicyScoreGenerateWorker] Unexpected exception when generating score for policy xxxxxx in step SAVE_SCORE, reason: java.lang.IllegalArgumentException: Duplicate item. Key already exists: policyId: xxxxx

Environment

  • BIG-IP ASM
  • No AVR is provisioned in device

 

Cause

The root cause of the corruption appears to be a one-time race condition or data inconsistency during operations, which triggered duplicate key conflicts and subsequent failures.

Recommended Actions

Restarting the restjavad service resolved the issue by resetting the REST storage and repopulating it with correct data, eliminating duplicate key conflicts and subsequent failures.

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

 

#tmsh restart sys service restjavad
