Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. An unreachable filer may cause access failure for all volumes in a VPU Domain. The ARX system's bespd daemon is responsible for probing and marking shares online/offline. Once bespd has marked a share offline, vcifs will stop attempting to connect to the offline storage. However, with a large number of shares, it can take a long period of time for an unreachable share to be marked offline. Until a share is marked offline by bepsd, any client requests attempting to reach an offline share will hang until the request times out. It is possible for there to be enough requests waiting for timeout to exhaust the number of vcifs threads available. As a result, the ARX system would be unable to service requests for unaffected filers in the VPU Domain.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as ID 38802 and it was fixed in ARX 5.2.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the ARX release notes.
