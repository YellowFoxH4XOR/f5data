Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. TMM may core multiple times when SSL persistence is enabled on a virtual server. As a result, TMM restarts each time it cores.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR79515 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.3.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.
