Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. After the Always-On Management (AOM) subsystem is rebooted, and while it is initializing, the AOM generates the following error message:

May 25 12:50:47 AOM2 user.warn kernel: b44: probe of ssb0:0 failed with error 19

The error message is benign and does not affect the operation of the unit. The message only indicates that the AOM is checking an unused network port.

Note: The AOM is a separate subsystem that provides lights-out management for the BIG-IP system. For more information about the AOM, refer to K9403: Overview of the Always-On Management (AOM) subsystem.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR122959 and it was fixed in AOM version 10.1.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the Always-On Management Release Notes.
