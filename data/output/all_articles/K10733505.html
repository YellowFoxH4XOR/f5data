Known Issue

Copper small form-factor pluggable (SFP) modules may prevent other interfaces from becoming operational.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system uses the 10000 series platform with the D112 or D113 platform ID.
  • You have at least one copper SFP OPT-0015 module on the BIG-IP device.
  • You have one SFP module plugged into ports 1.1-1.8 and the other SFP module plugged into ports 1.9-1.16.
  • One of the following actions occurs:

    • You restart the BIG-IP system.
    • You restart the bcm56xxd process on the BIG-IP system.
    • You modify the state of the interface to disable or enable.

Impact

BIG-IP system interfaces fail to function, and connections are lost.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP system reports corrupted serial numbers from the affected SFP modules.
  • You cannot modify the state of the affected interface to disable or enable.
  • Your fiber SFP modules are not available on the device.
  • In the /var/log/ltm file, you observe error messages similar to the following example:

    info bcm56xxd[12345]: 012c0016:6: soc_phy_i2c_select_mux(0):soc_i2c_start() failed(-9) by 0x55afcae0
    info bcm56xxd[12345]: 012c0016:6: soc_phy_i2c_select_mux(1):soc_i2c_start() failed(-1) by 0x55afcae0
    err bcm56xxd[12345]: 012c0011:3: Failed to read pluggable eeprom offset=0 (port=16): Internal error
    err bcm56xxd[12345]: 012c0011:3: I2C SFP operation failed: dev:0x19 at :628, errmsg(Ok)
