Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may restart when the incorrect flow is assigned to a fragmented IPv4 packet.

This issue occurs when one of the following conditions is met:

  • The connection is re-accepted.
  • You use the FLOW_INIT iRules event.

Impact

Traffic is disrupted while TMM restarts.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Failover occurs if you have configured a redundant system.
  • Traffic disruption occurs as the system reboots.
