Issue

Due to product availability, F5 no longer ships the 6900, 8900, and 8950 platforms with 320-gigabyte (GB) hard disk drives. As of July 2011, new 6900, 8900, and 8950 platforms include 1-terabyte (TB) hard disk drives. Field replaceable drives that are replaced by way of a Return Materials Authorization (RMA) also ship with the new larger drive size. These drives begin shipping as Field Replaceable Units in late June 2011.

If a 6900, 8900, or 8950 platform is configured with RAID and has had one drive replaced with the larger drive (for example, as the result of an RMA), the array size equals the smallest drive in the array. In this situation, the 320-GB drive and the 1-TB drive installed in the same device show as a 320-GB RAID pair.

If  two 6900, 8900, or 8950 platforms are configured as a high-availability pair, and one member of the pair has 1-TB drives and the other has the older 320-GB drives, the high-availability and failover functionality of the pair is unaffected by the disparity in drive sizes (excluding disk-space specific considerations, such as storage of log archives). The new drive does not negatively affect the functionality, performance, or cosmetic finish of the 6900, 8900, and 8950 platform.

Important: For BIG-IP WOM systems, refer to K12952: BIG-IP WOM symmetric disk deduplication cannot provision disks that are larger than 500 GB.
