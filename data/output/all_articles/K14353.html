Known Issue

When a client’s Maximum Segment Size (MSS) is low in relation to the serverside MSS, a computational error may cause TMM to assert and restart.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The client's MSS is low; for example, the MSS is 500 or less. 
  • The serverside MSS is high; for example, the MSS is 1400 or greater. 
  • The BIG-IP system is conducting packet manipulation. For example, an HTTP header insert is requested.

Impact

The restart of TMM will impact traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • TMM fails with an Assert/SIGABRT argument
  • The /var/log/tmm file may include a log entry that appears similar to the following example:

    notice 2: lib/c/xbuf.c:522: xfrag_prealloc_get: Assertion `valid xf' failed.
