Description

This article explains how to show the iRules LX statistics when they are not displayed in the Local Traffic tab even though the iRule LX services are running properly.

To verify this issue, navigate to:

Statistics  >>  Module Statistics: Local Traffic  >>  iRules LX  >>  Local Traffic.

On the screen you will see this message:

Status: No records to display.

Environment

  • iRules LX
  • BIG-IP LTM
  • Command line
Cause

Unknow at the time of publication

Recommended Actions

Reboot the BIG-IP following this procedure:
  1. Log in to the command line.
  2. Enter the following command:
     reboot

Note: The BIG-IP restart process can take an average of four to eight minutes, depending on the BIG-IP device, resources, and configuration.
