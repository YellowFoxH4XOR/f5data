Known Issue

Standby BIG-IP systems may overwrite gratuitous Address Resolution Protocol (ARP) information sent by a node or pool member with gratuitous ARP information proxied by the active BIG-IP system.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • High availability redundant BIG-IP systems are configured to use a VLAN group.
  • A node or pool member sends gratuitous ARP on one VLAN of the VLAN group.
  • The active BIG-IP system proxies the gratuitous ARP to the other VLANs of the VLAN group.
  • The standby BIG-IP system receives the proxied gratuitous ARP after it receives the original gratuitous ARP directly from the node or pool member.

When a node sends a gratuitous ARP broadcast on one VLAN of a VLAN group, the active BIG-IP system will proxy the gratuitous ARP broadcast onto the other VLANs of the VLAN group. Standby BIG-IP systems will first update their ARP table with the gratuitous ARP information sent by the node. However, the gratuitous ARP information sent by the node will be subsequently overwritten with the proxied gratuitous ARP information. If the proxied gratuitous ARP information was modified by the active BIG-IP system, subsequent health checks sent to the node will egress the standby BIG-IP system from the wrong VLAN, and the active unit will forward the health checks to the node's VLAN. If the node responds directly to the standby unit, the connection is reset by the standby BIG-IP system if the VLAN-Keyed Connections option is enabled (the default setting). As a result, health checks may time out and the node or pool member will be marked down.

Impact

The standby BIG-IP system's health monitor traffic may fail and mark the monitored nodes down.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • Health monitors fail on the standby BIG-IP system, and the node or pool members are marked down on the standby unit.
  • The standby BIG-IP system's ARP entries will have the proxied gratuitous ARP information for the node.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you may be able to use proxy exclude lists to prevent the node address from being proxied. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: The active BIG-IP system will not proxy ARP and gratuitous ARP information to the other VLANs of the VLAN group. Devices on the other VLANs will not receive ARP and gratuitous ARP information sent on the local VLAN of the VLAN group.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.
  2. Click Network.
  3. Click VLANs.
  4. From the top menu, click VLAN Groups.

    For BIG-IP 10.x, select Lists from the top VLAN Groups box.
  5. Select the affected VLAN group.
  6. From the top menu, click Proxy Exclusion List.
  7. Click Create.
  8. Enter the IP address of the node you want to exclude from being proxied.
  9. Click Finished.

Repeat steps 7 through 9 for each IP address that you want to exclude from being proxied.
