K000151319: Warning message "Warning generated: /Common/f5.apg_analytics definition:556: warning" in ltm log after initial boot

Description

  • During the initial boot of BIG-IP, warning messages similar to the following may appear in the system logs:

    /var/log/ltm:
    01071859:4: Warning generated : /Common/f5.apg_analytics definition:556: warning: ["\/" has no meaning.  Did you mean "\\/" or "/"?][{^(?:https?:\/\/)?(?:[^@\/\n]+@)?(?:www\.)?([^:\/?\n]+)}]
    01071859:4: Warning generated : /Common/f5.ibd definition:1399: warning: ["\/" has no meaning.  Did you mean "\\/" or "/"?][{\/}]

    /var/log/messages and /var/log/user.log:
    /Common/f5.apg_analytics definition:556: warning: ["\/" has no meaning.  Did you mean "\\/" or "/"?][{^(?:https?:\/\/)?(?:[^@\/\n]+@)?(?:www\.)?([^:\/?\n]+)}]
    /Common/f5.apg_analytics definition:557: warning: ["\/" has no meaning.  Did you mean "\\/" or "/"?][{^(?:https?:\/\/)?(?:[^@\/\n]+@)?(?:www\.)?([^:\/?\n]+)}]
    /Common/f5.apg_analytics definition:557: warning: ["\/" has no meaning.  Did you mean "\\/" or "/"?][{^(?:https?:\/\/)?(?:[^@\/\n]+@)?(?:www\.)?([^:\/?\n]+)}]
    /Common/f5.apg_analytics definition:937: warning: ["\/" has no meaning.  Did you mean "\\/" or "/"?][{(\/.*[^\}])}]
    /Common/f5.apg_analytics definition:962: warning: ["\/" has no meaning.  Did you mean "\\/" or "/"?][{(\/.*[^\}])}]


  • These warnings are typically observed only during the initial boot process and are not repeated during subsequent configuration loads

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • Upgrade
  • Initial boot
  • mcpd warning message

Cause

Bug ID 1971981

Recommended Actions

This is a parsing warning and does not indicate a functional error or misconfiguration. These warning messages can be safely ignored as they do not impact the operation or configuration of the BIG-IP system.

No further action is required unless additional symptoms or errors are observed.
