Description

LNR & DNR missing in Ukraine's State definition for topology load balancing

Environment

  • Topology load-balancing
  • DNS GTM

Cause

Donetsk People's Republic(DNR) and Luhansk People's Republic(LNR) are not available as options under Ukraine's state definition. 

Recommended Actions

The closest match for Donetsk People's Republic and Luhansk People's Republic are Donets'ka Oblast and Luhans'ka Oblast respectively.
