Topic

Current versions of ARX support NFSv2, NFSv3 UDP, and NFSv3 TCP. However, NFSv4 and PC versions of NFS (PCNFSD or HCLNFSD) are not currently supported.

F5 Product Development is tracking a request to add support for NFSv4 as ID 30568.
