Known Issue

Numerous configuration changes to a dynamic bandwidth control policy within a short period of time may cause a much lower maximum allowable rate.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured a dynamic bandwidth control policy.
  • Your BIG-IP PEM is using the policy to process traffic.
  • You modify the policy's Maximum Rate and Maximum Rate Per User values numerous times within a short period of time.

Impact

Subscribers experience a lower than expected maximum rate.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Subscribers are experiencing a lower than expected maximum rate.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you should avoid making numerous configuration changes to bandwidth controller policies while processing traffic. You can restart the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) to resolve the issue. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure temporarily halts traffic processing, and you should perform the procedure only during a maintenance window.

  1. Log in to the command line of the BIG-IP system.
  2. To restart TMM, type the following command:

    bigstart restart tmm
