Topic

The following NIC interface drivers are built into the PXE install kernel:

  • AMD PCnet-PCI Ethernet based on DEC DE500 chips
  • SMC EtherPower 10 and 100 Mb/s Ether and other cards based on DEC 21040/21140 chips
  • DEC/Intel 21143-based boards
  • SMC EtherPower II EPIC-based Ethernet adapters
  • Intel EtherExpress Pro100B
  • Broadcom/3Com Gigabit Ethernet adapter
  • Packet Engines GNIC-II (Hamachi) - Gigabit Ethernet
  • SysKonnect SK-NET Gigabit Ethernet
  • Intel E1000 Gigabit Adapter
  • Alteon Tigon Gigabit Adapter
  • Adaptec Starfire
  • 3COM EtherLink XL 3C900/3C905 (boomerang) PCI Ethernet adaptors
  • TI Thunderlan-based Ethernet adapters (Compaq/Racore)

Of this list, F5 Networks has only tested with the following:

  • EtherExpress Pro100B
  • Broadcom/3Com Gigabit Ethernet adapter
  • Intel E1000 Gigabit Adapter

Note: F5 Networks cannot guarantee that all revisions of the specified NIC cards will work on all hardware platforms. In some cases, the same revision of a NIC card works on one hardware platform and not on another.
