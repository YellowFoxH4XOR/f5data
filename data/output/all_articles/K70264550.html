Issue

Old Behavior

In versions prior to 12.0.0, the BIG-IP system uses cron to manage scheduled jobs. Cron executes all scripts within the following directories:

  • /etc/cron.hourly
  • /etc/cron.daily
  • /etc/cron.weekly
  • /etc/cron.monthly

The scripts within these directories are executed at the following times:

  • Hourly: On the first minute of every hour
  • Daily: At 04:02 every day
  • Weekly: At 04:22 every Sunday
  • Monthly: At 04:42 on the first day of every month

New Behavior

In version 12.0.0 and later, the BIG-IP system uses anacron to manage scheduled jobs for daily, weekly, and monthly periodic tasks. anacron executes the daily, weekly, and monthly jobs at random intervals between the hours of 03:00 and 22:00, with a random delay of up to 45 minutes, plus an additional delay of 5 minutes for daily jobs, 25 minutes for weekly jobs, and 45 minutes for monthly jobs.

The BIG-IP system still uses cron to process hourly scheduled jobs. The scripts in the /etc/cron.hourly directory are processed by cron on the first minute of every hour.

anacron executes scripts in the following directories:

  • /etc/cron.daily
  • /etc/cron.weekly
  • /etc/cron.monthly

Impact

Daily, weekly, and monthly scheduled jobs are now executed between the hours of 03:00 and 22:00, with a random delay of up to 45 minutes, plus an additional delay of 5 minutes for daily jobs, 25 minutes for weekly jobs, and 45 minutes for monthly jobs.

Recommended Actions

You can view cron log messages in the /var/log/cron file.
