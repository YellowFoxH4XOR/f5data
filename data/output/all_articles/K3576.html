Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. F5 has determined that some BIG-IP 1500 systems produced before November 11, 2004 are equipped with internal Gigabit Ethernet interfaces that may drop frames. These frames are dropped silently and are not reported in any system counters.

Customers will generally experience this issue as a small drop in performance, occasional lost connections, increased TCP retransmissions, or an increase in issues with applications that use UDP. In the worst cases, no more than .1 percent of Ethernet frames are affected. Customers can correct this issue by updating the firmware for those interfaces.

If you are experiencing a higher than normal rate of TCP retransmits and dropped UDP packets when traffic is passed through a BIG-IP 1500, contact F5 Technical Support for new firmware that will resolve this issue.
