K5256: Messages logged on 3-DNS when retrieving BIG-IP statistics

Topic

3-DNS uses iQuery to communicate with BIG-IP. When 3-DNS opens an iQuery connection to BIG-IP, it logs the following message to the /var/log/3dns file:

<time stamp> <host name> 3dnsd: STATE:iqmTcpConnect: Connection in progress to <IP address>:iqmgmt.c:502

After 3-DNS successfully connects to BIG-IP, 3-DNS logs the following message to the /var/log/3dns:

<time stamp> <host name> 3dnsd: STATE:iqmFinishConnect: Connection complete to <IP address>:iqmgmt.c:366

If 3-DNS is unable to complete a connection to BIG-IP, it will retry the connection once every minute and will log the message Connection in progress each time it retries the connection. For example, the log messages may appear as follows:

Nov 1 12:00:00 askf53dns 3dnsd: STATE:iqmTcpConnect:
Connection in progress to 10.10.91.1:iqmgmt.c:502
Nov 1 12:01:00 askf53dns 3dnsd: STATE:iqmTcpConnect:
Connection in progress to 10.10.91.1:iqmgmt.c:502
Nov 1 12:02:00 askf53dns 3dnsd: STATE:iqmTcpConnect:
Connection in progress to 10.10.91.1:iqmgmt.c:502
