What Happened?

  • We want to ensure our process is followed in the event of a DDoS attack.
  • We want notification emails to be sent in the event of a DDoS attack.

Environment

  • F5®Distributed Cloud (XC) DDOS

Resolution/Answer

RTIPs (Real Time Incident Procedures) are per customer instructions that tell the F5 SOC DDoS team how to contact a customer during an attack.

 

Customer Responsibility:
Customers are responsible for creating, updating, and maintaining their RTIPS using the XC console. 

 

No RTIPS Scenario:
If no RTIPS are configured, F5 SOC will notify the tenant owner via email. 

 

Incident Response Instructions and Recommended Format
Include clear and concise steps for F5 to follow in case of an alert. 

Example: 
'Please follow regular mitigation processes and notify SOC@example.com, NOC@example.com, ADMIN@example.com'

 

 

IMPORTANT
For AA (Always Available) customers, if any special requirements apply to route origination, such as: the customer must authorize each Route advertisement, the customer will initiate the advertisement, only advertise certain prefixes, etc. Please add these instructions to the RTIPs. Otherwise SOC will follow the standard procedure which is to advertise a prefix at the discretion of our analysts. This includes advertising when a prefix is under attack and/or if further inspection of the traffic is required.

 

Account Passphrase
Define your organization's security passphrase under RTIPs.
Passphrase Example: Secret123
