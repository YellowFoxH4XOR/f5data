K6182: Traffic may be sent to a last hop pool member that is down, even though reselect is enabled

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When BIG-IP LTM is configured with a last hop pool, and reselect is configured as the action to take when a pool member is marked down, the BIG-IP LTM system should direct traffic to an available last hop pool member when the member that was previously used becomes unavailable. Instead, the BIG-IP LTM system continues to use the unavailable pool member until the connection becomes idle.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR56001 and CR55761, and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.1.2 and 9.2.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
