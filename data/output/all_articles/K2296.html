K2296: Reasons why BIG-IP or 3-DNS licenses may not activate following an upgrade

Topic

After upgrading BIG-IP or 3-DNS, the software attempts to connect to the F5 Activation Server and authenticate the system for activation (this can happen automatically or manually; refer to the BIG-IP or 3-DNS Release Notes for details).

In rare instances, this activation may fail. Some common reasons for failure include connection issues or an incorrect license file.

If activation fails, open the /var/log/messages log file and look for a line similar to the following:

Feb 6 14:54:11 bng9823d1 kernel: Authorization fails: Error 0x120d0a0401

Take note of this line and contact F5 Networks Technical Support to troubleshoot the activation issue.
