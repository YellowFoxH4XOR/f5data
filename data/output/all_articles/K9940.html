Topic

When booting the BIG-IP 1500, 3400, 6400, 6800, 8400, and 8800 platforms, the Status LED indicator on the BIG-IP system's front panel is initially yellow, and remains yellow until the boot to the Traffic Management Operating System (TMOS) is complete. When the boot to the TMOS is complete, the Status LED turns green.

When booting the BIG-IP 1600, 3600, 6900, and 8900 platforms, the Status LED indicator on the BIG-IP system's front panel turns green immediately and remains green through the boot to the TMOS.

For all platforms, once the TMOS has booted, members of a redundant pair will negotiate active/standby status. For devices remaining in the active state, the Status LED remains green. For devices transitioning to the standby state, the Status LED turns yellow.

Note: For more information about the LED indicators on the front panel of the BIG-IP system, refer to K4263: Front panel LED indicator behavior for legacy platforms.
