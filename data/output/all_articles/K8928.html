Topic

The VIPRION chassis Performance Extreme license option enables Maximum Compression and Maximum SSL TPS.

If the Performance Extreme license is enabled, the VIPRION system is licensed to perform the maximum compression level and maximum SSL TPS supported by the platform. If the Performance Extreme license option is not enabled, the VIPRION system is not licensed to perform compression and maximum SSL TPS.
