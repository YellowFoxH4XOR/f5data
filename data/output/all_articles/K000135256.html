Six-month advanced notification

With the availability of the FIPS Ready F5 rSeries r10920-DF platform, F5 is announcing the six-month advanced notification of the End of Sale (EoS) for the BIG-IP 10350v-F platform to customers and partners on July 1, 2023. This product notice does not impact any subscription licenses or service agreements. Field Upgrade and Add-on SKUs are not included in this EoS announcement. The actual EoS is effective January 1, 2024.

For the specific SKUs and products impacted, end-of-support lifecycle dates, and replacement SKUs, refer to the following table.
