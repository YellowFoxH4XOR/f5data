Known Issue

The format strings listed in the redirection section of the bigpipe pool man page are incorrectly joined together.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this problem as CR35796 and CR42104 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 4.5.14. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP Release Notes.
