K000150317: F5 Access authentication failing with iOS v 18.1.1

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-03-11
Updated
2025-03-11
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • F5 Access
  • BIG-IP APM
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000150317

Environment

F5 Access on iOS 18.1.1


Cause

The issue arises due to a behavior in iOS version 18.1.1 that prevents proper processing of client certificates by the F5 Access during authentication with BIG-IP APM policies requiring certificate-based authentication.


Specifically, the common name (CN) from the client certificate is not extracted and passed as the logon username, leading to failed authentication attempts.


Recommendations

To resolve this issue and restore VPN connectivity:


Upgrade iOS Devices: Upgrade affected devices running iOS version 18.1.1 to version 18.3.1 or later, where this behavior has been corrected.
