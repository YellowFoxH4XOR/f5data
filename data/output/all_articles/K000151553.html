There are some related questions and answers for your reference:

        1. Can XC do the verification of HMACs or signatures on payloads or headers and must be capable of being used in conjunction with OAuth 2.0 bearer tokens.?

A: No

 

2. Does XC has the ability to integrate with a secure key manager for cryptographic operations?

A: No

 

3. Does XC Support for Oauth 2.0 to enable secure and standardized authentication and authorization for APIs?

A: No

 

4. For SSO intention, Does XC support Oauth 2.0?

A: Yes.
