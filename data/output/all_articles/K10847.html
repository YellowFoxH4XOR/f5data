Topic

The CE marking (also known as CE mark) is a mandatory conformity mark on many products shipped to a market in the European Economic Area (EEA). The CE marking certifies that a product has met EU consumer safety, health, or environmental requirements. CE labels are located on the FirePass 1200, 4100, or 4300 platforms, on either of the sides or rear of the chassis. An EC Declaration of Conformity set of documentation is also shipped as part of the bill of materials for all F5 hardware products.
