Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may accumulate learning_manager.pl zombie processes.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP ASM system does not have external internet connectivity.
  • You perform a manual signature update.

Impact

Zombie processes have a minimal effect on BIG-IP CPU and memory resources.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The BIG-IP process list displays multiple instances of the learning_manager.pl process.
