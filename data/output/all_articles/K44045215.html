Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may restart and produce a core file when processing a suspending iRules command during a CLIENT_CLOSED event.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured a virtual server that uses an iRule.
  • The iRule uses a command that suspends iRules processing within the CLIENT_CLOSED event.

    Note: The term suspend is commonly referred to as park or parking.

  • TMM attempts to process an incoming packet while the connection flow is closed.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from the TMM restart, and devices configured as a high availability (HA) pair may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • F5 iHealth lists Heuristic H616054 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium page.
  • The BIG-IP system fails over to another host in the device group.
  • The BIG-IP system generates a TMM core file to the /shared/core directory.
  • The BIG-IP system temporarily fails to process traffic while TMM restarts.

Workaround

To work around this issue, depending on your specific environment, you may be able to modify your iRule to ensure it does not use a suspending iRules command within the CLIENT_CLOSED event.

Impact of workaround: The impact of the suggested workaround depends on the specific environment. F5 recommends testing any such changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.
