Description

After upgrading to v16.1.0 from 16.0.x, both devices in HA pair show offline.

  • After upgrading from 16.0.x to 16.1.0, devices were working fine for some time.
  • After a day or two, both devices go offline.

Environment

  • BIG-IP 16.1.x
  • KVM/ESXi hypervisor is being used.

Cause

This can happen because on KVM, Xen Project,OpenStack and ESXi based hypervisors because 16.1.x is not officially supported yet.

Recommended Actions

Downgrade to any of the officially supported versions. Refer the link below to check when it will be officially supported.
BIG-IP VE Supported Platforms
