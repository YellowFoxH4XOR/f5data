K12612: BIG-IP ASM Overview statistics do not support floating point values

Topic

In BIG-IP ASM 10.x, the graphs that are displayed on the ASM Overview screen do not support floating point values. All values that are reported have been rounded and stored as an unsigned integer for the purposes of statistics graphing. This may result in unexpected displays of data when viewing graphs.

For example:

  • The Requests/Sec axis of the ASM Traffic Statistics graph displays its values in fractional increments, but the line graphing Blocked, Alarmed, and Dropped requests will jump from integer to integer.
  • The Top Requested URLs and Top Requesting IPs tables may display the TPS for a given URL as zero if the URL or IP has been requested, or requesting (respectively) at a rate of less than one TPS.

This behavior is by design.
