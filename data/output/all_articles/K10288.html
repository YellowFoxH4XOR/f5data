Topic

 

Important: This article applies to BIG-IP 10.x through 11.3.0. Starting in BIG-IP 11.4.0, all modules are supported on all platforms. For BIG-IP 11.4.0 and later, refer to the release notes for your specific version for exceptions. For more information, refer to K55201922: Locating product release notes.

BIG-IP 10.x through 11.3.0 software restricts the number and types of modules that are allowed, based on specific platform requirements. The system enforces these restrictions through licensing and provisioning.
