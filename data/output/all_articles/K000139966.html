Description

F5OS-A has below log time to time:

/var/F5/system/log/platform.log 

appliance-1 alert-service[8]: priority="Notice" version=1.0 msgid=0x2201000000000029 msg="Received event." event="65536 appliance hardware-device-fault EVENT NA "RAS AER unknown errors, count=1" "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS.xxxxxxxxx UTC"".

appliance-1 alert-service[8]: priority="Notice" version=1.0 msgid=0x2201000000000029 msg="Received event." event="65536 appliance hardware-device-fault EVENT NA "No RAS AER unknown errors detected" "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS.xxxxxxxxx UTC"".

 

SNMP monitor also detect this. What is this?

 

Environment

  • F5OS-A

 

Cause

This is by design.
It was AER correctable error just once occurred, and automatically corrected.
This is just a informational (priority="Notice")

 

Recommended Actions

As it is corrected, safely ignored.
When it goes to higher than 200, it will change to warning, and also will have information in show system health command output.
