Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may report being out of synchronization with its peer after synchronization successfully completes.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • The device group is configured to use the incremental synchronization option.

    Note: The incremental synchronization option is the default setting for device groups.
  • The BIG-IP system restarts, or system services restart, after receiving an incremental synchronization from the peer device.

If you reboot or restart the services (for example, if you use the tmsh restart sys service all command) on a BIG-IP device to which incremental changes were propagated, the changes persist, but the configuration time stamp may be different on the restarted device. As a result, the restarted device may report an incorrect synchronization status.

Impact

The incorrect synchronization status may cause confusion.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • The incremental synchronization changes are propagated to the device group.
  • After the restart, the BIG-IP device from which the changes were propagated reports the following synchronization status:

    In Sync
  • After the restart, the BIG-IP device to which the changes were propagated reports the following synchronization status:

    Not All Devices Synced
  • The BIG-IP devices show different commit ID time stamps after the system restarts.
