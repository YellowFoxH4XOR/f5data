Security Advisory Description

Under certain conditions, Behavioral DoS (BADoS) protection may fail during an attack. (CVE-2018-5526)

Impact

BADoS protection does not function as intended.

Security Advisory Status

F5 Product Development has assigned IDs 714350 and 714369 (BIG-IP) to this vulnerability.
