Topic

This article applies to existing BIG-IP ASM and BEST bundle customers.

For BIG-IP 14.1.x and later, the system adds F5 Advanced Web Application Firewall (WAF) features after you reactivate the BIG-IP ASM license. For information about eligible SKUs, refer to K72212499: End of Sale announcement for BIG-IP ASM.

For BIG-IP 13.1.x, the system adds some of the Advanced WAF features after you reactivate the BIG-IP ASM license.

For BIG-IP 13.1.0.2 and later, the system adds the DataSafe feature after you reactivate the license.

For BIG-IP 13.1.0.8 and later, the system adds the Guided Configuration feature after you reactivate the BIG-IP ASM license.

For information about how you can verify that the Advanced WAF features are enabled, refer to From ASM to Advanced WAF: Advancing your Application Security on DevCentral.
