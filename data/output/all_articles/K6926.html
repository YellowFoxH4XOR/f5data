Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When the BIG-IP ASM parses Set-Cookie headers in HTTP responses from the server, the parsing of headers is currently case sensitive. As a result, the BIG-IP ASM may log illegal modified cookie violations to the forensics logs.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR72347 and CR62860, and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM 9.3.0 and 9.4.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM release notes.
