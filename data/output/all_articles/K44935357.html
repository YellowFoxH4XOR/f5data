Description

You are considering to purchase a BIG-IP/BIG-IQ platforms, or upgrade your current BIG-IP/BIG-IQ platforms, or need to know the spec for BIG-IP/BIG-IQ Virtual Editions, and need to know the best platform for your environment.

Environment

  • BIG-IP platform/Virtual Edition
  • BIG-IQ platform/Virtual Edition

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

Due to the complexity of sizing devices to your unique network or deployment,  F5 recommends you contact one of the following Sales organizations to assist further.  

  • F5 Sales
  • one of F5's many Partners
  • your existing F5 reseller.

Support is unable to assist with Sizing considerations or discussions, other than to analyze current load/resources and identify whether the devices is overloaded or not.  If you are facing a degraded system state that you suspect is related to sizing,  Please open a support case at https://support.f5.com.


Additional Information

For Sizing considerations related to Hypervisor or virtual devices, you can find more information on the host requirements for various devices in the following articles: 

 

  • K14810: Overview of BIG-IP VE license and throughput limits
  • K15796: Hardware requirements to host BIG-IP VE on private cloud platforms
  • K14946: Overview of BIG-IP VE image sizes
  • Manual page: System requirements for BIG-IP VE modules
For BIG-IQ,  please see the following recommendations for host requirements, and suggested limits for devices added to BIG-IQ: 
  • Manual Chapter: 8.0.0 - BIG-IQ Application Service Sizing Guidelines
  • Manual Chapter: 8.1-8.2 - BIG-IQ Sizing recommendations
