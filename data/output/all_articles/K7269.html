Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP ASM system can silently drop packets over 1500 bytes on its internal interfaces if the Do Not Fragment bit is set in the IP header. Furthermore, all retransmissions of the dropped packet will also be dropped.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR75231 and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM 9.3.0 and 9.4.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM Release Notes.
