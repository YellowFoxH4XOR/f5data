K000160253: The effect on BIG-IP VE devices when the license is released

Type
Knowledge
Published
2026-03-05
Updated
2026-03-05
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000160253

Description

When a BIG-IP VE instance needs to be rebuilt, upgraded, or moved, there may be a requirement to release or allow the move of the BIG-IP VE base registration key. This is often needed if the Revoke button is not functioning or is not visible.

Environment

  • BIG-IP VE
  • Release/Revoke BIG-IP VE license

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

The BIG-IP VE license release or allow-move procedure does not have any negative impact on BIG-IP VE instances until the base registration key is reused.


Additional Information

None
