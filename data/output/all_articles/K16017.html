Known Issue

BIG-IP CGNAT subscribers may experience service disruptions.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have an LSN pool configured to use deterministic mode.
  • You have a FastL4 virtual server configured to use the LSN pool.

When an LSN pool is configured in deterministic mode, each subscriber gets a predetermined set of translation endpoints that are used for source address translation. Under the conditions mentioned previously, when a translation request is processed for a new connection, certain ranges of source ports in the LSN pool may be used more frequently than other ranges, which may lead to high levels of port reuse.

Impact

CGNAT subscribers may experience service disruptions due to high levels of port reuse.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H501797 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • Service to certain CGNAT subscribers may be disrupted.
  • tcpdump shows tcp resets from affected CGNAT subscribers.
