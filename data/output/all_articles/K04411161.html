Description

You received an alert from a non-F5 device, similar to the following:

Audit: P2P Torrent Traffic 5 attack detected but not blocked.

You would like to know if ASM can protect against attacks utilizing the BitTorrent protocol.

Environment

  • Security alert
  • BitTorrent traffic

Cause

Third party alert.

Recommended Actions

ASM cannot protect against attacks that utilize BitTorrent.

ASM is primarily intended to protect from HTTP-based attacks and BitTorrent uses an entirely different protocol that runs on top of UDP.

Find out more detail about the specific attack and consider if other BIG-IP modules such as AFM could help.
