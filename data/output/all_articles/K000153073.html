K000153073: Discontinuation of BIG-IP Next Hardware and VE Systems

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-08-19
Updated
2025-08-19
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000153073

Description

F5 has made the strategic decision to discontinue the development of BIG-IP Next hardware and VE (Virtual Edition) systems. Customers may have questions regarding product availability, support continuity, and migration paths.

Environment

  • F5 BIG-IP Next hardware appliances.

  • F5 BIG-IP Next VE systems.

Cause

F5’s decision to discontinue BIG-IP Next hardware and VE systems stems from a strategic realignment of resources toward other solutions that align with the company’s long-term vision.
The focus is shifting towards modern app delivery strategies, software-defined infrastructure platforms, and enhancing the capabilities of current-generation BIG-IP solutions and NGINX products.

 

Recommended Action

To ensure a smooth transition for customers impacted by the discontinuation of BIG-IP Next hardware and VE systems, F5 recommends the following actions:

  • Review Product Lifecycle Information:
    • Ensure you are aware of the end-of-life (EOL) dates for BIG-IP Next hardware and VE systems. The final release of BIG-IP Next (20.3.0) reached End of Software Development (EoSD) and End of Technical Support (EoTS) on April 30, 2025. As such, BIG-IP Next has already reached the end of its software lifecycle. 
  • Evaluate Alternative Solutions:
    • Explore other F5 offerings, such as the traditional BIG-IP platform, NGINX, or F5 Cloud Services, which can help meet your application delivery and security needs.
  • Consult F5 Sales: 
    • Coordinate with F5’s Account or Sales team to receive personalized guidance on migration plans.

 

Additional Information

F5 remains committed to providing innovative solutions to meet evolving customer needs. For additional questions or concerns about this discontinuation:

  • Contact F5 Support

  • Review knowledge base articles and lifecycle guidelines on the F5 website.

  • If applicable, inquire about trade-in programs or discounts for transitioning to alternative solutions.
