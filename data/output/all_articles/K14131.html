Policy Information

F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of the following vCMP add-on SKUs, effective February 20, 2013. F5 is providing advanced product notification to customers and partners announcing intent to retire the products listed in the following table:

Automatic License migration to Maximum Guests

The new vCMP replacement add-ons provide superior performance and functionality. Unique to this offering, F5 will provide a free license migration service to customers with these add-ons. F5 will contact owners of the EoS add-ons and then migrate their existing vCMP add-on licenses to the newer vCMP Max SKUs, per system. These changes will take effect upon the next system activation event.

Technical support

For customers using the above EoS add-ons, F5 will continue to provide software and phone support in accordance with the customer's maintenance contracts, and until their successful license migration.

Note: For more information about this announcement, contact your local F5 Partner Business Manager or Inside Sales Representative.
