Description

I am having some issues with Health-Check and need to know how to check the Health-Check logs from the F5® Distributed Cloud (XC) portal.

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud Services
  • F5 Distributed Cloud HTTP Load balancer

Answer/Recommended Actions

To check the Health-Check logs:

  1. Go to the Origin Pool page
  2. Find the record
  3. Click the three dots at the end of the record
  4. Click Show Child Objects
    1.   
  5. Click View under Status in the Endpoint section.
  6. Click the Status Object by the RE on the pop-up page
  7. Choose the records you want to check.
  8. Check the details of the Health Check records, like the response code, status, and error message. 

Additional information

  • Health-Check logs provide valuable information about the status and connectivity of your origin servers.
  • Reviewing these logs can help you troubleshoot issues related to server availability and performance.
