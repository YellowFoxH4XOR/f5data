K000135662: vCMP guest pair showing Disconnected sync status after reactivating license on the vCMP Host

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-07-31
Updated
2025-02-07
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 14.1.5
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000135662

Description

After reactivating the license on the vCMP Host, prior to doing an upgrade, all vCMP guest pairs are showing a status of Disconnected.


You'll see logs similar to the below on the vCMP guest residing on the vCMP Host where the license was reactivated

Jul 24 14:04:27 guest1.bigip.local warning pfmand[8396]: 01660005:4: Disconnected from hypervisor.

Jul 24 14:09:26 guest1.bigip.local warning pfmand[8396]: 01660005:4: No connection to hypervisor.

Jul 24 14:14:27 guest1.bigip.local warning pfmand[8396]: 01660005:4: No connection to hypervisor.


Environment

  • vCMP System
  • Reactivate license
  • Sync status Disconnected

Cause

This is a bug

Bug ID 721892: Pfmand on vCMP guests does not recover after service interruption


 Recommended Actions

Currently, there is no fix for the bug available, yet. You can workaround the issue, however, by rebooting the affected vCMP Host.
