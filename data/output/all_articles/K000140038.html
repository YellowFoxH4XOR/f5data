Support Solution

Description

You are experiencing a behavior where, after rebooting your F5OS appliance post-upgrade after version 1.1.0, you've observed that the tenants on the system are shut down prior to the appliance reboot. Once the F5OS appliance finishes rebooting, it then restarts the tenants, placing them in a deployed state.

Environment

  • Upgraded to F5OS versions after 1.1.0
  • Tenants configured and running prior to reboot
  • Appliance in a production environment

Cause

This is expected behavior in F5OS versions above 1.1.0. The system has been designed to ensure that tenants are safely shut down before the F5OS appliance reboots, to prevent any inconsistencies or corruption of tenant configuration.

Recommended Actions

  • Plan for a maintenance window when rebooting the F5OS appliance, as tenants will be temporarily unavailable during this process.
  • Ensure all tenant data and configurations are saved before initiating a reboot.
  • Monitor the tenants' status post-reboot to confirm they have been restarted and are in a deployed state.
  • If tenants do not come back online after reboot, contact F5 technical support for further assistance.
