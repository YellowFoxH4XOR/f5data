K000159853: MyF5 account locked after multiple failed login attempts

Description

When attempting to log in to the MyF5 portal, you receive a message indicating that your account is locked.

Environment

  • MyF5 portal
  • Account authentication
  • Account locked

Cause

Your MyF5 account becomes locked when you make 10 or more consecutive failed login attempts. This is a security feature designed to protect your account from unauthorized access attempts.

Recommended Actions

There are a few ways to unlock your MyF5 account.

  1. Automatic Unlock Email
    When you make 10 or more attempts to log in, F5 automatically sends you an email to help you unlock your account.
    • Check your email inbox (including spam/junk folders) for an unlock email from F5 (noreply@mail.account.f5.com). 
    • Click the unlock link provided in the email. Follow the instructions to unlock your account.
  1. Manual Unlock Request
    If you did not receive the automatic unlock email, visit the Unlock Account page and follow the on-screen instructions. 

  • Figure: MyF5 'Account Unlock' request page 
  1. Contact F5 Support
    If you are still unable to unlock your account after trying the above steps, email F5 Support at supportone@f5.com for assistance.
