Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • HA pair
  • iRule with HTTP::respond during a SERVER_CLOSED event

Cause

Seen behaviour is caused by a known issue tracked with the Bug ID 1002321 "HTTP::respond on closed (aborted) HTTP when called in SERVER_CLOSED."

Recommended Actions

You should review iRule logic to ensure you don't send an HTTP::respond during a SERVER_CLOSED event
