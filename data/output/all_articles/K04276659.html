Description
BIG-IP does not have support for UDLD (UniDirectional Link Detection) is not supported, and when either the Tx or Rx fiber in an optical transceiver is not operative or has been removed, the interface can show the link UP still.

Misconnected ports are not correctly shut down, and you might experience loss of traffic.  Also, the incorrect status makes it more difficult to diagnose the problem.

What is UDLD (UniDirectional Link Detection) ?

  • UDLD  (UniDirectional Link Detection) is a proprietary Data Link Layer (L2) protocol that works with the Phtsical Layer (L1) mechanisms to determine the physical status of a link. At the Physical Layer auto-negotiation takes care of the physical signaling and fault detection
  • UDLD performs tasks that auto-negotiation cannot perform, such as detecting the identities of neighbors and shutting down misconnected ports
  • When you enable both auto-negotiation and UDLD, Physical Layer and Data Link Layer detections work together to prevent physical and logical unidirectional connections and the malfunctioning of other protocols.
  • UDLD works by exchanging protocol packets between the neighboring devices. In order for UDLD to work, both devices on the link must support UDLD and have it enabled on respective ports.

Environment

  • BIG-IP platforms
  • Only a single strand of fiber (tx or rx) in an optical connection is active

Cause

This issue is tracked with BugID 759525

Recommended Actions

To workaround this issue, replace the optical fiber on the interface to have two working fiber strands.
