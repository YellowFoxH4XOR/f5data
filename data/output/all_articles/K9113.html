Topic

Beginning in BIG-IP GTM 10.1.0,  the system supports Domain Name System Security Extensions (DNSSEC) when the optional DNSSEC feature module is licensed.

Note: For more information about F5's DNSSEC, refer to F5 DNSSEC Deployment Guide and technical article A Living Architecture.

Note: For configuration information, refer to the Securing Your DNS Infrastructure chapter in the BIG-IP Global Traffic Manager: Implementations manual, and the Working with DNSSEC Keys and Zones chapter in the Configuration Guide for BIG-IP Global Traffic Manager. For information about how to locate F5 product manuals, refer to K98133564: Tips for searching AskF5 and finding product documentation.

According to a memorandum dated August 22, 2008, the Federal Government must deploy DNSSEC to the top level .gov domain by January 2009. The policy requires that the top level .gov domain be DNSSEC-signed, and processes be developed to enable secure delegated subdomains.

Note: For more information about this mandate, refer to the following document from the Executive Office of the President of the United States. The link takes you to a resource outside of AskF5, and it is possible that the document may be removed without our knowledge:
