Description

BIG-IP appears to be non-responsive. It boots up with an error “Host is not available” on the LCD screen. Console is non-responsive, and rebooting the device yields no results. Upon restart console does show up with “Press Esc for AOM management” but nothing further, and pressing Esc also does nothing. All settings and options on the LCD screen are greyed out, including anything related to reboot, or power off. 

Environment

F5-BIG-LTM-R4600, C131

F5-BIG-LTM-R2600, C130

Cause

Hardware failure

Raise an RMA with F5 support.
