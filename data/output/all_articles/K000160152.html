Description

This page provides guidance on the MyF5 Self Service licensing for Flex Consumption Program (FCP).  This is a step-by-step onboarding guide for new FCP contracts signed in January 2025 or later.

Environment

  • FCP contract renewed after January 2025
  • BIG-IP Virtual Edition
  • MyF5 Customer Portal
  • Editor/Admin access to MyF5 FCP Subscriptions

Step by Step Onboarding

Required Prerequisites [0 day]

  • Register for F5 FCP Onboarding Webinar via the email you received from your Customer Success Manager (CSM).
  • Ensure all Admins/Editors can log onto MyF5 and access the active FCP subscription
  • Immediately schedule a maintenance window to migrate traffic using this guide to complete your installation
  • Register F5 Notifications with our Preference Center for notifications, security alerts and threat intelligence feeds

Licensing [30 days]

  1. Attend F5 FCP Onboarding Webinar
  2. Login to MyF5 and Generate Self Service license keys in MyF5
  3. Start creating license keys and begin your deployment. Generate Self Service license keys in MyF5 for products included in the FCP contract or Create Custom Self Service license keys in MyF5.
  4. Ensure your BIG-IP is healthy and without errors: Best Practices when preparing to update the license on BIG-IP
  5. Apply a license to the BIG-IP VE or if you are using BIG-IQ to manage previous licenses,  Migrate BIG-IP VE from BIG-IQ LOAD/CAT license offering to MyF5 Self Service license key.  For environments in closed networks (Federal/Government) License in a Closed Circuit Network (CCN)
  6. Create a unique identifier for Cost Code/Cost Center, General Ledger code, Business Unit, Project, etc: Add Chargeback Tag to Self Service license keys in MyF5
  7. If using BIG-IQ for previous licenses, ensure they are revoked from the BIG-IQ so you are not getting double billed for “active” licenses: How to revoke BIG-IP Virtual Edition licenses managed by the BIG-IQ

Managing and Reporting [60-90 days]

  1. Confirm your previous licenses are revoked: How to revoke BIG-IP Virtual Edition licenses managed by the BIG-IQ
  2. Available edit options for MyF5 Self Service License Keys
  3. Import Self Service license keys from MyF5 to RegKey Pool on BIG-IQ
  4. Manage MyF5 Self Service license keys from a RegKey Pool on BIG-IQ
  5. Unassign Self Service license keys in MyF5
  6. Terminate Self Service license keys in MyF5
  7. Understand your monthly FCP license consumption:  Read the License Activity Report and Read the Run Rate report
