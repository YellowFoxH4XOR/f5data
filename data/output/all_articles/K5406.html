K5406: BIG-IP and 3-DNS support for jumbo Ethernet frames

Topic

Typical Ethernet frames are limited to 1500 bytes. Frames in excess of 1500 bytes exceed the MTU of typical Ethernet devices and require fragmentation.

Frames between 1500 bytes and 9000 bytes, inclusive, are known as jumbo frames.

The 4.x versions of BIG-IP and 3-DNS do not support jumbo frames. If BIG-IP or 3-DNS receive a jumbo frame, they discard it.

BIG-IP cannot pass jumbo frames, even when IP forwarding is enabled on the BIG-IP.
