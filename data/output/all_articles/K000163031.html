K000163031: BIG-IP Edge Client stalls during antivirus validation on macOS 26.2

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-08-21
Updated
2026-08-21
Applies to

  • APM Clients
  • BIG-IP APM: 17.5.X, 17.5.1, 17.5.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000163031

Description

On macOS 26.2 (Tahoe) running on Apple Silicon, BIG-IP Edge Client build 7261 can remain at “Awaiting Connection” or stall during antivirus validation. APM reaches the Antivirus Check, but session.policy.inspectionhost.status remains awaiting_connection and eventually times out due to inactivity.

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM 17.5.1
  • BIG-IP Edge Client for macOS build 7261
  • macOS 26.2 (Tahoe) on Apple Silicon
  • VPN access policy configured with antivirus checks and endpoint inspection (EPSEC/EPI)

Cause

An incomplete or inconsistent state involving BIG-IP Edge Client, the EPSEC package, or the endpoint inspection process can prevent the antivirus check from completing.

Answer/Recommended Actions

  • Install the latest available BIG-IP Edge Client and EPSEC package.
  • Fully uninstall and reinstall BIG-IP Edge Client and F5 Endpoint Inspector.
  • Verify that all relevant client logs are generated.
  • Be sure to perform a full AV scan on the system before attempting to connect to the APM VPN.
  • Confirm successful antivirus-check completion in the APM logs before testing VPN connectivity.
  • If the issue persists, collect detailed client and BIG-IP logs, along with packet captures, for further analysis.
