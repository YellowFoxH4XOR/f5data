K000162073: SWG Reports and URL Request Logs Empty After Upgrade to BIG-IP 17.1.3 with SSLO

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-07-03
Updated
2026-07-03
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP APM: 17.1.3
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000162073

Description

After upgrading BIG-IP to version 17.1.3, the "Access ›› Overview : SWG Reports : Overview" page displays "No data" and URL Request Logs are empty. This occurs even after licensing and provisioning SWG, URLDB, and AVR modules, and adjusting log settings. The issue was not present on version 15.x, where SWG reports were available without explicit provisioning of these modules. Attempts to reproduce and resolve the issue in lab environments with various configurations have not restored SWG report functionality on 17.1.3.

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM, version 17.1.3
  • SWG, URLDB, and AVR modules (tested with and without provisioning)
  • Explicit proxy and SSLO configurations
  • Upgrade from version 15.x (where reports were available)
  • Logging settings: "Enable URL Request Logs" and "Log Allowed Events" enabled
  • Reports missing from "Access ›› Overview : SWG Reports : Overview" and "Event Logs : URL Request Logs"

Cause

BZ 2339765 - SSLO dashboard needs to implement something equivalent to SWG report

Answer/Recommended Actions

  • There is currently no workaround to restore SWG reports for SSLO access profiles on BIG-IP 17.1.3.
  • The absence of SWG report data is due to a product change: a previous bug (ID857961) in 15.x allowed SWG reports to display URL data when SSLO was used, even without SWG/URLDB provisioned. This was fixed in later versions.
  • Product Engineering is tracking the need for equivalent SWG reporting on the SSLO dashboard under BZ 2339765.
  • Monitor the status of BZ 2339765 for updates or future software releases that may address this gap.
  • If SWG reporting is critical, contact your F5 account team to discuss possible Options and status of this ID.
  • At time of publication, there is no current Engineering Hotfix available - projected in the weeks to come.

Additional Information

  • Attaching access profiles and PRP to explicit proxy will not affect SWG report data; what matters is the profile on the transparent proxy.
  • The previous behavior on 15.x was due to a bug and is not expected in 17.x and later.
  • The fix for equivalent SSLO reporting requires significant changes and may take several weeks to implement.
