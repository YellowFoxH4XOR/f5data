Known Issue

In rare situations, the sod process may terminate the bcm56xxd process under heavy load.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is under heavy load.
  • The bcm56xxd process does not receive enough CPU cycles to finish its operations and activates the watchdog daemon.

Impact

The BIG-IP device goes offline and may fail over to another host in the device group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • There are multiple bcm56xxd core files in the /var/core directory.
  • In the /var/log/ltm log file, you observe error messages similar to the following example:

    notice sod[8584]: 01140041:5: Killing /usr/bin/bcm56xxd pid 7073.
