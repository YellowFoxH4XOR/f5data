Description
This is about the message "error: ssh_msg_send: write" in /var/log/ltm.

Environment
  • BIG-IP
  • /var/log/ltm

Cause
This may be because a user connected to BIG-IP, but have something wrong.

Recommended Actions
You many need to check /var/log/audit, and /var/log/sel in order to determine who tried to connect to the BIG-IP, and what they did.
For example, you may see the following logs. In this case, a user connected to BIG-IP, and entered a username, but a password.

/var/log/ltm
Oct 13 16:09:49 LTM-1 err sshd[3489]: error: ssh_msg_send: write

/var/log/audit
Oct 13 16:09:49 LTM-1 info sshd(pam_audit)[3489]: 01070417:6: AUDIT - user user123 - RAW: sshd(pam_audit): User=user123 tty=ssh host=1.1.1.1 failed to login after 1 attempts (start="Wed Oct 13 16:07:49 2021" end="Wed Oct 13 16:09:49 2021").

/var/log/secure
Oct 13 16:09:49 LTM-1 err sshd[3489]: pam_unix(sshd:auth): conversation failed
Oct 13 16:09:49 LTM-1 crit sshd[3489]: pam_unix(sshd:auth): auth could not identify password for [user123]
Oct 13 16:09:49 LTM-1 info sshd(pam_audit)[3489]: User=user123 tty=ssh host=1.1.1.1 failed to login after 1 attempts (start="Wed Oct 13 16:07:49 2021" end="Wed Oct 13 16:09:49 2021").
Oct 13 16:09:49 LTM-1 info sshd(pam_audit)[3489]: 01070417:6: AUDIT - user user123 - RAW: sshd(pam_audit): User=user123 tty=ssh host=1.1.1.1 failed to login after 1 attempts (start="Wed Oct 13 16:07:49 2021" end="Wed Oct 13 16:09:49 2021").
