Description

About trial options

You can use MyF5 to start a free BIG-IP or NGINX trial. You receive unlimited BIG-IP or NGINX trial usage for 30 days for each instance that you start.

Procedures

Start a trial using MyF5

You can start a BIG-IP or NGINX trial using MyF5, and also you can start an NGINX trial on the NGINX site. To use MyF5, do the following:

 Access and download trial software and obtain trial registration keys on MyF5.

  1. Go to My Products and Plans > Trials.

    Under Trial Offers, you can see the trails available to you on MyF5.

  2. Select the trial you want.

Access your trial 

After you start a trial, F5 sends you an email confirming your trial subscription and fulfills your subscription within 30 minutes.

Check your trial fulfillment status

After you request your trial and while you are waiting to start using it, you can check its fulfillment status by doing the following:

  1. Go to My Products and Plans > Trials.
  2. Under My Trials, you can see your trail status in the Status column.

Download and license your trial

After F5 fulfills your trial, you can access your trial license and download the software.

  1. Go to My Products and Plans > Trials.
  2. Under My Trials, select the trial you want to download.
  3. Select the add license (+) option.

    The wording for the option varies by product.

  4. Follow the steps that display to download and license your trial
