Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If you impersonate a user on the Users: Impersonate User page, and the user’s master group has Client Certificate Two-Factor Authentication enabled, FirePass does not display any resources on the impersonated webtop.

Note: This issue does not occur when client certificate authentication is the primary authentication method for the user’s master group.

F5 Networks Product Development is tracking this issue as CR94098.

Workaround

You can work around this issue by disabling Client Certificate Two-Factor Authentication. To disable Client Certificate Two-Factor Authentication, perform the following procedure:

  1. Log on to the FirePass Administrative Console.
  2. Navigate to the User: Groups: Master Groups page.
  3. Click the name of the master group you want to configure.
  4. Click the Authentication tab.
  5. Clear the Require client certificate for user logon check box.
  6. Click the Update button.
