Known Issue

The gtmd process reports a SIGSEGV when persistence is enabled.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • Persistence is enabled for the wide IP pools.

Impact

The gtmd process may occasionally restart.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • F5 iHealth lists Heuristic H716416 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium page.
  • The gtmd process reports a SIGSEGV and produces a core file.
  • The gtmd process restarts, which prevents clients from receiving answers to requests.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can disable persistence on wide IP pools. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.
  2. Navigate to DNS > GSLB > Wide IPs > Wide IP List.
  3. Click the wide IPs you want to change.
  4. Click Pools.
  5. Under Pools, in the Persistence list, click Disabled.
  6. Click Update.
