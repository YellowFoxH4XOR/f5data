Known Issue

Reconfiguring iApp application services may cause the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) to restart and produce a core file.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You reconfigure iApp application services.
  • The reconfiguration includes switching templates.
  • The previous and new templates both contain iRule procedures with the same name.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restarting, and devices configured as a high-availability pair may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • After you reconfigure an iApp application service, the BIG-IP system's TMM process restarts.
  • The BIG-IP system temporarily fails to process traffic as it recovers from the TMM process restarting, and systems configured as part of a high availability (HA) group fail over.
  • The BIG-IP system generates a TMM core file to the /shared/core/ directory, which includes a time stamp that correlates with the traffic interruption.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can modify the iApp application service templates to generate iRule procedures that have unique names per iApp.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
