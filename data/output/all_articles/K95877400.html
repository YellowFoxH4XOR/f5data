Policy Information

April 1, 2022

One-year advanced notification

With the availability of BIG-IP VELOS CX410 and BX110 (available since March 2021), with this article, F5 is announcing the one-year advanced notification of the End of Sale (EoS) for the VIPRION B2150, B2250, C2200-AC, C2200-DC, C2400-AC, and C2400-DC platforms to customers and partners. This applies to all VIPRION chassis, blades SKUs (including the YK versions), and the associated software modules. This product notice does not impact any subscription licenses or service agreements. All the Field Upgrade and Add-on SKUs will still be available until the product reaches EoL. The actual End of Sale will be effective April 1, 2023.

Refer to K4309: F5 platform life cycle support policy for the specific SKUs/products impacted, end-of-support lifecycle dates, and replacement products/SKUs.

Thank you,

BIG-IP Product Management
