K000162318: Upgrading to F5 Insight 1.2.0 fails in air-gapped environments

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-07-17
Updated
2026-07-17
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • F5 Insight: 1.2.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000162318

Description

Upgrades to F5 Insight 1.2.0 will fail in environments without external network access due to an unresolved internal initialization dependency required during the upgrade process. In order to avoid this issue in air-gapped or network-restricted environments, F5 recommends you evaluate migration paths to F5 Insight 1.2.1 instead.

Environment

  • You are upgrading to F5 Insight 1.2.0.
  • Your environment is fully air-gapped with no external network access.

Cause

This issue is caused by an unresolved internal initialization dependency in F5 Insight 1.2.0.

Answer/Recommended Actions

If your environment is fully air-gapped, do not attempt to upgrade to F5 Insight 1.2.0. Instead, you can skip 1.2.0 and upgrade directly to F5 Insight 1.2.1 where this issue is fixed.

Additional Information

To download F5 Insight 1.2.1, go to the F5 Downloads site and select F5 Insight in the BIG-IP Group. The download files are located in the 1.2.0-LTS Product Version container. Select F5Insight_ova to see the list of files for F5 Insight 1.2.1.
