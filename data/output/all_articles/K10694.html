Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Mirrored persistence records may fail to time out on the standby BIG-IP system when a short timeout of 30 seconds or less is configured for the persistence profile.

For example, when configuring a Source Address Affinity persistence profile with a 15 second timeout and the Mirror Persistence option enabled, persistence entries may remain on the standby unit even after the entries have timed out on the active unit. The records will begin timing out as soon as failover occurs, and the standby unit is marked active.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as ID 248943 (formerly CR96945).

Workaround

To work around this issue, set the persistence timeout to an interval longer than 32 seconds.
