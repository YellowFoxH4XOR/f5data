Known Issue

The Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) may report the bits and packets processed counts incorrectly for Packet Velocity ASIC (PVA) statistics.

Impact

Incorrect statistics may be misleading.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The tmsh show /sys pva-traffic global command incorrectly reports bits and packets processed counts of approximately 34 billion and 16 million, as indicated in the following example:

    -------------------------------------------------
    Sys::PVA
    -------------------------------------------------
    PVA Traffic ClientSide ServerSide
    Bits In 34.3G 34.3G
    Bits Out 34.3G 34.3G
    Packets In 16.7M 16.7M
    Packets Out 16.7M 16.7M
    Current Connections 0 0
    Maximum Connections 1 1
    Total Connections 3 3

    Miscellaneous
    Cur PVA Assist Conns 0
    Tot PVA Assist Conns 3
    HW Syncookies Generated 0
    HW Syncookies Detected 0
