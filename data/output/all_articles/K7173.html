K7173: Maximum number of configurable trunks in BIG-IP version 4.x

Topic

The following platforms support a maximum number of six (6) trunks. The maximum trunk limit on the platforms includes hidden trunks used to carry traffic between the switch chips and the motherboard. As a result, the maximum number of user configurable trunks two (2). The maximum number of interfaces per trunk is eight (8).

  • BIG-IP 1000 (D39)
  • BIG-IP 2000 (D45)
  • BIG-IP 2400 (D44)
  • BIG-IP 5000 (D50)
  • BIG-IP 5100 (D51)
