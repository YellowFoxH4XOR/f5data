Description

BGP "as-origination-interval" is not adhered to after Force Offline or reboot event causing long convergence times for failover event.
 

Example bgp config:
 

﻿router bgp 64000

neighbor AS65 peer-group

neighbor AS65 remote-as 65000

neighbor AS65 as-origination-interval 1

neighbor AS65 advertisement-interval 30

....

 

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • BGP
  • Force Offline/reboot

Cause

BugID 1256777

Issue occurs when "as-origination-interval" is defined in peer-group

Recommended Actions

As a workaround, you may define "as-origination-interval" outside of the peer-group:


Example:
 

router bgp 64000

neighbor AS65 remote-as 65000

neighbor AS65 as-origination-interval 1

neighbor AS65 advertisement-interval 30

....
