Known Issue

iSession connections may be intermittently established as unencrypted even though they are configured to be secure.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP AAM local-endpoint and destination AAM remote-endpoint do not use Server SSL profiles.
  • The remote virtual server has a Client SSL profile with allow-non-ssl enabled.

Impact

An unencrypted iSession connection may be established, and this is inconsistent with configuring data-encrypt as enabled in the server-side iSession profile.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Unencrypted iSession connections are established.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can configure the Client SSL profile with allow-non-ssl as disabled (the default value) to reject non-SSL connections. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility as an administrative user.
  2. Navigate to Local Traffic > Profiles > SSL > Client.
  3. Select the SSL client profile associated with your virtual server.
  4. Select Advanced Configuration.
  5. Clear the Non-SSL Connections option.
  6. Click Update.
