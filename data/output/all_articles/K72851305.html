Known Issue

There are multiple known issues related to iPadOS Split View functionality when the F5 Access client is installed.

Multiple F5 Access client issues occur when any of the following conditions are met:

  • Your iPadOS device has the F5 Access 3.0.7 client installed and configured.
  • You are running the iPadOS device in Split View mode.
  • You attempt to view the VPN configuration, connection information, or go to Settings > Support on the iPadOS device.

Impact

The F5 Access client does not function correctly when the iPadOS device is using Split View.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The F5 Access client may terminate when you go to Settings > Support on the iPadOS device.
  • The F5 Access does not display VPN configuration information when the iPadOS device is in the landscape orientation.
  • The F5 Access client does not display connection information when you tap the i icon when the iPadOS device is in landscape orientation.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 829801, 829813, and 829797 to these issues. F5 has confirmed that these issues exist in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. Workaround

To work around these issues, you can use the iPadOS device in the portrait orientation or in a full-screen mode.

Impact of workaround:  Performing the recommended workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
