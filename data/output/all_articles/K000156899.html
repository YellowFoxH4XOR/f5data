K000156899: LTM CA Cert Missing missing after upgrade

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-10-07
Updated
2025-10-07
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.1.2
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000156899

Description

Upgrading BIG-IP LTM can result in SSL handshake errors due to the absence of the CA certificates in the default ca-bundle.crt file, even though the certificate's in question expiry date is in 2027.

Environment

  • Product: BIG-IP LTM
  • Configuration: SSL profiles requiring missing CA certificate for successful SSL handshakes.

Cause

During a BIG-IP upgrade, ca-bundle.crt is replaced with the version included in the new software release, which removes any manually added or updated certificates unless they are re-applied after the upgrade.

Recommended Actions

  • After upgrading BIG-IP, manually update ca-bundle.crt to restore any required custom or non-default CA certificates.
  • Use Certificate Bundle Manager to automate updates and retention of necessary CA certificates after upgrades.

Refer to F5 article K60612439 ("Manually updating the BIG-IP default CA certificate bundle file"): https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K60612439
