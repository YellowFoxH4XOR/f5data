K31403303: F5 Access VPN client keeps using expired/revoked certificate

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-09-20
Updated
2023-09-20
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP APM
  • F5 Access
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K31403303

Description
You are using a MDM to push Certificates to mobile devices for the F5 Access Client to authenticate to the BIG-IP.
At various times, the clients are seen to be using the older certificate rather than the new.

Environment

  • MDM configured to regularly expire certificates and push updated certificates to the Mobile device.

Cause

  • The client is using cloud backup and is restoring the old certificate.

Recommended Actions

  1. Review your mobile device configuration.
  2. If you cannot resolve the issue, contact your MDM supplier for support. F5 Access does not control or manage certificates. It is solely a consumer of the services provided by the mobile platform as configured by the MDM.
