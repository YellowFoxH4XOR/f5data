Known Issue

Security policy history files may be missing after you perform a synchronization.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP ASM system is configured as a member of a Sync-Failover device group with Application Security Synchronization enabled.
  • You create a security policy and synchronize the change to the peer devices of the device group.
  • You note that the contents of the /var/ts/dms/policy/policy_versions/ subdirectories on all device group members contain the same security policy history files.
  • You modify the security policy and synchronize the change to the peer device group members.

Impact

The system removes the security policy history.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When you view the file contents of the /var/ts/dms/policy/policy_versions/ subdirectories, the security policy history files appear and disappear on alternating synchronizations on peer members of the device group.

Workaround

To work around this issue, when you note that the security policy history files are missing on the peer devices, you can manually synchronize the originating device configuration to the device group members. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround:  Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility of the intended originating synchronization device.
  2. Navigate to Device Management > Overview.
  3. Click the originating device name.
  4. Select the Sync option Sync Device to Group.
  5. Select the Overwrite Configuration box.
  6. Click Sync.
