Description

When APM and APM Lite are both provisioned but only APM Lite is licensed, the Guided Configuration will be missing from the WebUI.

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM and APM Lite
  • Guided Configuration

Cause

Expected behavior

Recommended Actions

To access the Guided Configuration, Access Policy Manager Full must be licensed.

Additional Information

You can identify the license you have by the following:

License KeyIDModule
mod_apmAccess Policy Manager Full (No l)
mod_apmlAccess Policy Manager Limited
