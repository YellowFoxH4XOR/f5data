Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may fail to take the appropriate VLAN failsafe action after detecting a loss of network traffic on the VLAN.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • The system is running base version 11.2.1 or 11.1.0 HF5 - HF8 or 11.2.0 HF2.
  • The VLAN failsafe timer has expired.

This issue is related to the fix for the issue described in SOL13933 (ID 384940). For more information, refer to K13933: The BIG-IP system may continue to generate VLAN failsafe traffic after receiving responses to VLAN failsafe requests.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may not fail over to the HA peer as expected.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H404170 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The ha-status output indicates that the VLAN failsafe failure status is set to yes, but the system does not trigger the failsafe action.

    For example:

    tmsh show /sys ha-status

    ---------------------------------------------------------------------
    Sys::HA Status
    Feature Key Action Fail
    ---------------------------------------------------------------------
    vlan-failsafe VLAN-1 failover yes

  • The BIG-IP system may not fail over to the HA peer, as expected.
