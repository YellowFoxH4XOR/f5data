Note: The FirePass End of Software Development (EoSD) announcement described in this article also applies to FirePass Virtual Edition (VE).

F5 is announcing that the End of Software Development (EoSD) for FirePass 7.x is effective October 1, 2015 in accordance with the guidelines contained within K8986: F5 product support policies and K4156: FirePass software support policy.

As of October 1, 2015, F5 will no longer provide security updates, software fixes (hotfixes), or consulting services for FirePass version 7.x. In addition, service contracts enter an Extended Support phase beginning with the EoSD. During the Extended Support phase, F5 will offer only configuration support and RMA services. Defect correction will not be provided. F5 may offer a customer access to certain software updates, which may include security or stability related software releases.

All configuration support for FirePass version 7.x during the Extended Support phase is considered reasonable effort. 'Reasonable effort' is defined to include technical phone support and hardware support, and is limited to system configuration support. No response time targets, guarantees, or SLAs shall apply. No escalations to Engineering Services or Product Development shall be allowed. RMA support is included, as long as the platform has not exceeded its End of Technical Support (EoTS) or End of RMA (EoRMA) support date.

The effective End of Technical Support (EoTS) and End of RMA (EoRMA) date is October 1, 2016 for the FirePass version 7.x software, completing its End of Life (EoL) cycle.
