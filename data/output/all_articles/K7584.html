Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When a change is made to the IP Group Filters of a Network Access Favorite, the change may not take effect until you perform a Service Restart, or you reboot the FirePass controller.

IP Group Filters are packet filters applied to the connection of a Network Access Favorite in a specific resource group. IP Group Filters are configured on the IP Group Filters tab of the Network Access: Resources page.

Note: The tab becomes visible only after you select the Use packet filter to access LAN check box.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR77068 and it was fixed in FirePass version 6.0.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the FirePass release notes.

Additionally, cumulative hotfix 601-1 has been issued for FirePass version 6.0.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the cumulative hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.

For instructions about how to obtain a hotfix, refer to K167: Downloading software and hardware from F5.

For instructions on installing a hotfix, refer to K3430: Installing FirePass hotfixes.

Workaround

You can work around this issue by clicking the Restart Services link located on the Device Management: Maintenance: Restart Services page.

Important: Restarting services can cause an interruption or a dropped connection for FirePass clients, depending on the type of favorite being used. For Network Access Favorites, the Network Access client attempts to reconnect automatically. Depending on the length of time required to restart services, the client may prompt the user to continue to attempt to reconnect.
