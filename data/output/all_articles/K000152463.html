K000152463: Trial licenses request by Government End User

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-08-07
Updated
2025-08-07
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.5.X, 17.1.X, 17.5.1, 17.5.0, 17.1.2, 17.1.1, 17.1.0
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 8.4.X, 8.4.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000152463

Description

Government organizations attempting to request a BIG-IP VE trial license may find their requests denied, even after providing full company details and accurate address information. 

This restriction is in place to ensure F5’s compliance with export licensing laws and regulations, which may be influenced by international trade laws, U.S. export controls, and other legal requirements.

These laws can restrict the distribution of certain software or technology to government organizations in specific countries or regions, or may require additional licensing or authorization that is not available for trial or evaluation purposes. 

Environment

  • Platform: BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE)
  • Licensing method: Trial license request via My F5 portal
  • User type: Government end users

Cause

F5 does not permit BIG-IP VE trial downloads by government end users due to export compliance requirements. This policy applies regardless of whether all requested information is provided accurately.

Recommended Actions

If you are a government user and need access to F5 products, you are advised to contact your F5 Sales representative. They can guide you through the appropriate process, which may involve additional compliance checks or licensing steps that are not available through the standard trial license request process.

Additional Information

If you need more detailed information about export compliance requirements, your F5 Sales representative or F5’s legal/compliance team can provide guidance specific to your organization and region.
