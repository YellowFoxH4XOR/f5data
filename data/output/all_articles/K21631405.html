Description

After BIG-IP upgrade the following logs are being raised in every 20 minutes

  • <19>Mar  2 18:00:02 ltm1.f5.com [mail.err] err sSMTP[3187]: Unable to connect to "localhost" port 25.
    <30>Mar  2 18:00:02 ltm1.f5.com [daemon.info] info crond[3749]: sendmail: Cannot open localhost:25
    <19>Mar  2 18:00:02 ltm1.f5.com [mail.err] err sSMTP[3187]: Cannot open localhost:25
    <19>Mar  2 18:20:01 ltm1.f5.com [mail.err] err sSMTP[18283]: Unable to connect to "localhost" port 25

  • /etc/cron.daily/logrotate:

error: error creating output file /var/lib/logrotate.status.tmp: Permission denied

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • K01630449: Overload of emails after upgrade to v15 with Subject: nice -n 19 ionice -c 3 /usr/bin/rest_logintegrity is not solving the issue

Cause

SElinux permission issue.

  1. One of the following two options:

# touch /.autorelabel && reboot
