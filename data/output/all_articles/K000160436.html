Description

After reseating an SSD drive following RAID failure alerts, the F5OS system's show system raid command indicated the drive was present, but underlying Linux mdadm commands revealed multiple RAID arrays remained in a degraded state with partitions from the reseated SSD marked as removed.

# cat /proc/mdstat
Personalities : [raid1]
md125 : active raid1 nvme0n1p2[0]
   113878016 blocks super 1.2 [2/1] [U_]
   bitmap: 1/1 pages [4KB], 65536KB chunk
md122 : active raid1 nvme0n1p4[0]
   1048512 blocks super 1.0 [2/1] [U_]
   bitmap: 1/1 pages [4KB], 65536KB chunk
md124 : active raid1 nvme0n1p1[0]
   455646208 blocks super 1.2 [2/1] [U_]
   bitmap: 4/4 pages [16KB], 65536KB chunk
md121 : active raid1 nvme1n1p3[1] nvme0n1p3[0]
   1046528 blocks super 1.2 [2/2] [UU]
   bitmap: 0/1 pages [0KB], 65536KB chunk
md123 : active raid1 nvme0n1p5[0]
   111848448 blocks super 1.2 [2/1] [U_]
   bitmap: 1/1 pages [4KB], 65536KB chunk

Environment

  • Platform: F5-BIG-BT-R10900, C128
  • F5OS version: 1.8.3
  • Two NVME SSDs configured in RAID1 arrays (ssd1/nvme0n1 and ssd2/nvme1n1)
  • Multiple mdadm RAID volumes: md121, md122, md123, md124, md125
  • RAID failure alerts

Cause

The RAID arrays may be degraded because the partitions from a reseated nvme drive were not automatically reintegrated into the mdadm RAID volumes after the physical drive reseat and reboot. While the reseated drive itself was recognized by the system, mdadm may require explicit commands to re-add each partition to its corresponding RAID array.

Answer/Recommended Actions

This issue is resolved by manually adding the missing partitions back to the arrays using mdadm --manage --add commands.

Once the arrays are complete, automatic resynchronization of all arrays to healthy [UU] status will occur.

mdadm /dev/md122 --manage --add /dev/nvme1n1p4
mdadm /dev/md123 --manage --add /dev/nvme1n1p5
mdadm /dev/md124 --manage --add /dev/nvme1n1p1
mdadm /dev/md125 --manage --add /dev/nvme1n1p2

- Monitor the resynchronization progress using cat /proc/mdstat

Note: mdadm resynchronizes one volume at a time; other volumes will show "resync=DELAYED" status until their turn

Verify all arrays show [UU] status after resynchronization completes

If resynchronization fails for any volume, an RMA of the NVME SSD may be required.
