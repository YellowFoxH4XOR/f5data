Description

F5 Access 3.2.3 for Android release introduces new and updated icons new icons to provide a refreshed, modern, and consistent visual experience.

Details

The following new icons introduced may differ on different physical device. Android automatically selects the correct density folder at runtime based on the physical screen of the device.

ImageFunctionDescription
DisconnectShows After the F5Access Android is in connected state
ConnectShows when the F5Access Android is in connected state
Logo iconShows when the F5Access Android is appearing in Logon phase
Foreground notification barAppears on the taskbar (top of mobile device), once F5 Access is connected.
Playstore iconPlaystore icon in the Play console
Tile iconAppears in the beginning of phone screen

Environment

F5 Access 3.2.3
