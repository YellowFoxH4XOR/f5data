Policy Information

F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of the F5 vSocial, vDeface, and vApp scanning services, effective January 1, 2016. With vSocial, vDeface, and vApp services, customers can monitor and detect unauthorized social media pages and app stores. Initially offered through Versafe as part of its web fraud protection solutions, these services were inherited by F5 with the acquisition of the Tel Aviv-based company. As a result of low demand and increased interest in client-side threat detection, F5 will cease to sell these services on January 1, 2016, and conclude all supporting services on April 1, 2016, to instead focus resources on malware threat detection as part of the Web Fraud Protection and Application Protection strategies. Limited support for these services will continue until the End of Life (EOL) date April 1, 2016, when all services will conclude. This EoS announcement does not impact any BIG-IP customers or customers of other Web Fraud Protection services.

Existing WebSafe and MobileSafe customers will otherwise see no changes to their current antifraud solutions. F5 will continue to offer and develop malware detection and other capabilities for these solutions, enabling customers to accurately detect and guard against phishing threats, browser-based malware, at-risk devices, and credential theft. WebSafe and MobileSafe are core components of the BIG-IP platform, and an essential part of F5’s security strategy. F5 is providing advance product notification to existing customers and partners announcing the intent to retire the associated products. The F5 SKUs that are affected are as follows:

Primary SKUs affected:

SKUsDescription
F5-VS-VSOCIAL-1YRMonitor up to 50 Apps in appstore. Protect up to 50 pages in social networks.
F5-VS-VDEFACE-1YRDetect defacement in up to 50 pages.
F5-VS-VAPP-1YRVERSAFE SUBSCRIPTION: VAPP 1 YR (10 K USERS) (LEGACY ONLY)

Transition Options

F5 does not plan to support social media, webpage, and app store scanning services in the foreseeable future. Existing customers who currently use vSocial, vDeface, and vApp will need to transition from these services by the EOL date of April 1, 2016. F5 will work through their partners and resellers to assist customers making such transitions and those who have recently expressed interest in these services. Customers are advised to contact F5 or their reseller for more information.

Technical Support

After the End of Software Development (EoSD) date of 1 January, 2016, F5 will not provide fixes for any related technical issues, and all services will be concluded at 12:01 a.m., April 1, 2016 (EOL).
