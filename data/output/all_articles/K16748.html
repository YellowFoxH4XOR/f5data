Known Issue

The HA group trunk score may be inaccurate and cause a failover event after rebooting the BIG-IP system. The HA group trunk score may erroneously consider down links as available when calculating the HA group trunk score.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have multiple BIG-IP systems in a high availability (HA) configuration.
  • You have configured the BIG-IP systems to monitor trunk link availability using the HA group feature.
  • You have one or more of the trunk interfaces has a down status.
  • You have rebooted the BIG-IP system.

Impact

An unexpected failover event may occur due to an incorrect HA group trunk score.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The HA group trunk score calculation is incorrect, or greater than expected.
  • An unexpected failover event occurs after rebooting a BIG-IP device.

Workaround

There is no workaround for this issue. To resolve the inaccurate HA group trunk score, you can reload the BIG-IP configuration.

Impact of action: Using the following command should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP command line.
  2. Reload the configuration by typing the following command:

    tmsh load sys config
