K8964: The PLUGIN::enable iRule command may cause TMM to crash if the BIG-IP ASM bd process crashes

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If a BIG-IP ASM virtual server refers to an iRule that utilizes the PLUGIN::enable or the PLUGIN::disable iRule command, and the BIG-IP ASM bd process crashes while parsing traffic, TMM may crash.

When the BIG-IP ASM bd process is parsing header information while additional traffic is being passed through to the iRule containing the PLUGIN::enable iRule command, the plugin may cause TMM to reference information that had been removed.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR100363 and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM 9.4.6. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM release notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-9.4.4-94.0-HF3, issued for BIG-IP 9.4.4, and in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.5-HF2, issued for BIG-IP 9.4.5. You may download this hotfix or a later version of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
