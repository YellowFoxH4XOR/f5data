Topic

When the SX gigabit port of a BIG-IP (1000, 2400, or 5100 series) switch is connected to a Nortel Passport 8600 switch with an 8608SX GBIC, the link status will appear as up on both units, but the Nortel switch will not receive traffic that is sent by the BIG-IP.

This specific issue is caused by the Nortel Passport 8600 switch with the 8608SX GBIC, which uses a transceiver/PHY chip that requires an Ethernet preamble no longer than 56 bytes.

By definition, the IEEE 802.3 Standard for Information for CSMA/CD access methods defines the preamble at 56 bytes in length and also states that, to be compliant, the device must allow for preambles longer or shorter than the definition. The Nortel Passport 8600 with 8608SX GBIC is not compliant with this requirement. Your device must be connected to an IEEE 802.3 compliant switch in order to be used with SX gigabit interfaces on the BIG-IP 1000, 2400, or 5100 series switches.

Note: This issue applies only to the SX gigabit ports for the BIG-IP 1000, 2400, or 5100 series switches and the Nortel Passport 8600 switch with an 8608SX GBIC. Copper gigabit interfaces and 10/100 interfaces are not affected. No other hardware platform or configurations are known to be affected by this issue.

To ensure that this issue is not being caused by an unrelated problem, troubleshoot as follows:

  • Verify that the link lights are illuminated and the status is up on both the BIG-IP and the Nortel switch.
  • On the affected port, use the tcpdump utility to confirm that:

    • Traffic is sent and received on the BIG-IP
    • The appropriate interface statistics on BIG-IP are being incremented.
    • Traffic is not received by the Nortel switch.
  • Confirm that the Nortel switch shows no inbound traffic being received. If the switch is not receiving traffic, you should see dropped/error frames on the Nortel connected port statistics.
  • If possible, try connecting the same BIG-IP SX gigabit port to another port that is currently working properly to confirm that the port is functional. For example, try connecting two BIG-IP systems together.

Contact F5 Technical Support if you are experiencing this issue.
