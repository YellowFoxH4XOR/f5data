K000139436: BigIP Next SPK - vlan and interfaces are not visible since deploying f5-tmm again.

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-04-29
Updated
2025-02-07
Applies to
  • BIG-IP Next
  • BIG-IP Next SPK
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000139436

Description

With deploying POD f5-tmm again, you notice that some interfaces and VLANs are no longer visible.


Typically, the output of the commands [root@server]# oc exec -it deploy/f5-tmm -n spk-ingress2 -c debug -- bash "/ip a" or [root@server]# oc describe f5-spk-vlans.k8s.f5net.com -n spk-ingress2 is missing interfaces and VLANS.


Also, you may notice the following log records:

[root@server]# oc logs po/f5-spk-cwc-6449ff5d9-tl4lr -n default

ERROR: logging before flag.Parse: E0415 04:48:03.316652 1 rabbitmqapi.go:77] Failed to establish connection to rabbitmq server. Error tls: failed to verify certificate: x509: certificate has expired or is not yet valid: current time 2024-04-15T04:48:03Z is after 2024-04-12T07:50:38Z

ERROR: logging before flag.Parse: I0415 04:48:03.316683 1 amqp.go:117] error: failed to setup exchange

ERROR: logging before flag.Parse: E0415 04:48:03.316697 1 cm20.go:50] Failed to create AMQP. Error Failed to Setup exchange: Failed to open channel

ERROR: logging before flag.Parse: I0415 04:48:06.319260 1 amqp.go:112] DEBUG: exchange name - CWC-SPK


Environment

  • BigIP Next SPK

Cause

This may be because we hit ID 1550625.

Recommended Actions

Please upgrade to v1.7.7 or more recent.

Additional Information

You may also look at the article K000137668.
