Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If a VLAN group is configured to operate in opaque mode, the BIG-IP will incorrectly respond to all ARP requests received on its configured VLANs.

This behavior causes the BIG-IP to overwrite ARP tables of other devices on the same VLAN with the BIG-IP's MAC address. As a result, all traffic on the VLAN will be directed toward the BIG-IP, which can disrupt network communication.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR89635 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 9.4.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, ASM, GTM, Link Controller, or WebAccelerator Release Notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.3-HF4 issued for BIG-IP version 9.4.3. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Networks Downloads site.

For information about the F5 Networks hotfix policy, refer to K4918: Overview of F5 Networks critical issue hotfix policy.

For information about managing F5 Networks product hotfixes, refer to K6845: Managing F5 Networks product hotfixes.

Workaround

You can work around this issue by setting the VLAN group option to transparent mode.
