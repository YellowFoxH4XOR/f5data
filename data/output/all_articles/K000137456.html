K000137456: ASM policies deleted and out of sync between devices after upgrade

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-11-03
Updated
2023-11-03
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP ASM: 16.X.X, 16.1.X, 16.1.4, 16.1.3, 16.1.2, 16.1.1, 16.1.0, 16.0.X, 16.0.1, 16.0.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000137456

Description

  • ASM policies deleted/missing after upgrade.
  • Number of ASM policies is different between devices on the HA pair.

Environment

  • BIG-IP ASM
  • HA pair
  • Auto-sync enabled

Cause

The difference in configured asm policies between devices after upgrade is the result of having auto-sync enabled during the upgrade.

Recommended Actions

We recommend to disable auto-sync before the upgrade as described on K84554955

Additional Information

None
