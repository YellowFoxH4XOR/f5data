K000133171: HTsplit feature supports on F5 rSeries platform

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-03-29
Updated
2023-03-29
Applies to
  • rSeries Platforms
  • BIG-IP
  • rSeries 2000
  • rSeries 4000
  • BIG-IP LTM: 15.1.5, 15.1.6, 15.1.7, 15.1.8, 17.1.X, 17.1.0
  • rSeries 5000
  • rSeries 10000
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000133171

Description

F5 r2000/r4000 Series platform does not support the HTSplit feature, but F5 r5000/r10000 Series platform supports it.

Environment

  • r2000/r4000
  • r5000/r10000
  • HTSplit feature

Cause

None.

 Recommended Actions

F5 r2000 and r4000 Series platform CPUs do not support the HTSplit feature, for details you can check the hardware specifications in the F5 manuals below:

  • Manual Chapter : Hardware specifications - r2000 Series
  • Manual Chapter : Hardware specifications - r4000 Series
Both F5 r5000 and r10000 Series platform CPUs support the HTSplit feature, for details please check the hardware specifications separately:
  • Manual Chapter : Hardware specifications - r5000 Series
  • Manual Chapter : Hardware specifications - r10000 Series

Additional Information

You can also use the tmsh show sys tmm-info | grep Sys::TMM command to determine if your platform supports the HTSplit feature, for details please check K23505424.
