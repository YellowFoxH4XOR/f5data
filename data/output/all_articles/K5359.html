Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIND sample configuration files that are included on 3-DNS have not been modified by F5 Networks. The sample configuration files refer to directories that are not used on 3-DNS.

Refer to the 3-DNS documentation, rather than the BIND sample configuration files, when you create a configuration.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR30228.
