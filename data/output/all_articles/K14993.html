Known Issue

The VIPRION B4300 blade serial console port may display garbled output and/or stop responding.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You configure a terminal emulator to use a different baud rate, and then change the baud rate back to match the VIPRION blade's serial console port baud rate.

Impact

You cannot access or reliably use the VIPRION blade through the serial console port.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • You cannot access the VIPRION blade through the serial console port.
  • The serial console port displays garbage characters.
  • The serial console port stops responding to any keystrokes.
  • The serial console appears mostly normal, but skips one to two characters at times.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can reboot the affected VIPRION blade.

Impact of workaround: The BIG-IP system experiences an interruption in traffic processing while the VIPRION blade reboots.
