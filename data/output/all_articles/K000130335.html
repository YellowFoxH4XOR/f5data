K000130335: Page Not Found, links not working, support.f5.com KB article is no longer accessible.

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-12-23
Updated
2025-12-29
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • Support Portal
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000130335

Description

Knowledgebase (KB) article https://support.f5.com/csp/article/<K number> is no longer accessible.

Articles with the support.f5.com domain may not automatically redirect to the my.f5.com domain.

Environment

  • MyF5 support portal
  • KB articles with http://support.f5.com in the URL

Cause

This issue is now resolved. support.f5.com should now be automatically redirected to my.f5.com.

Recommended Actions

Workaround if necessary:

  1. Attach the K number to https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/
    For example:
    You can manually change the following URL:
    https://support.f5.com/csp/article/K14513
    To:
    https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K14513

Additional Information

In December 2022, KB content from support.f5.com was migrated to a new platform on my.f5.com
