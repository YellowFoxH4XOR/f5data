Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Due to an initialize bug that does not properly adjust the count on the pool members, BIG-IP LTM always selects the same snatpool member regardless of the status or availability of the other pool members.

This issue only affects SNAT pools with members that contain IP addresses on the same VLAN and network. Therefore, this issue does not affect SNAT automap pools or pools that contain members that are on different VLANs and networks.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR57636 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.2.3. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
