K000163241: BIG-IP QKView Upload Times Out After Reaching 100%

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-09-30
Updated
2026-09-30
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000163241

Description

When uploading a QKView to iHealth from BIG-IP, the upload may reach 100% and then report a timeout. This behavior can occur through both the TMUI and CLI workflows.

The QKView may nevertheless be successfully uploaded and become available in iHealth despite the timeout reported by BIG-IP.

Environment

  • Product: BIG-IP LTM
  • Version: BIG-IP 17.5.1.6
  • Platforms tested:
    • BIG-IP VE
    • BIG-IP i4800
  • Upload methods:
    • TMUI
    • ihealth.sh
  • Outbound HTTP/HTTPS proxy configured

Cause

The timeout occurs after the QKView file has been transferred, while the BIG-IP workflow waits for iHealth to complete diagnostic processing and return the results.

Consequently, the timeout does not necessarily indicate that the QKView upload failed. If the QKView is visible in iHealth, the file transfer was successful and the failure occurred during the subsequent processing or results-retrieval stage.

The behavior is associated with Bug ID1054405, which documents the QKView upload workflow reporting an error even though the upload succeeds. The related timeout behavior was tracked under Bug ID2140417 (no public bug tracker article was available for this bug during creation of this article). 

Recommended Actions

  1. Verify that dedicated iHealth API credentials are configured under:
    System > Support > Manage iHealth Credentials
  2. Confirm that the proxy permits HTTPS connections to:
    • identity.account.f5.com:443
    • ihealth2-api.f5.com:443
  3. Retry the QKView upload through TMUI or ihealth.sh.
  4. If the upload reaches 100% and then times out, search iHealth for the QKView before retrying the upload.
  5. If the QKView is present in iHealth, consider the upload successful despite the BIG-IP timeout.
  6. If the QKView is not present in iHealth, verify proxy connectivity and access-control rules, then retry the upload.
  7. If automated uploads continue to time out, manually upload the QKView to the relevant F5 Support case.
  8. At the time this article was prepared, no software fix had been provided for this behavior. For the latest status, available workarounds, or assistance confirming whether a QKView upload completed successfully, create an F5 Support case and reference Bug ID1054405 and Bug ID2140417.

Additional Information

Error 200202 can also be encountered during this workflow. Refer to Bug ID1054405 and KB K84223474: “Error uploading to iHealth” banner in WebUI, “Error 200202” in logs for information about that behavior.
