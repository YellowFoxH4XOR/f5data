Known Issue

Enabling Virtual Local Area Network (VLAN) failsafe may cause the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) to enter an infinite loop and eventually restart.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system has the VLAN failsafe feature enabled.
  • The neighbor table includes a large amount of entries.

Impact

TMM enters a loop, resulting in degraded performance or temporary interruptions in traffic processing. 

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H459149 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The BIG-IP system fails over to the standby system, if configured as a high-availability pair.
  • The BIG-IP system generates a TMM core file to the /shared/core directory.
