Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. In rare circumstances, the BIG-IP WebAccelerator MySQL database may become corrupt after synchronizing policies in a symmetric deployment. The corruption is caused by loss of clock synchronization between the WebAccelerator systems, and is exacerbated by multiple config and policy syncs. As a result of policy or application corruption, the pvac process may not start, may not be listening on port 8081, or may become unstable and restart repeatedly.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR118628, and it was fixed in BIG-IP 10.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP WebAccelerator release notes.

Workaround

Troubleshooting database corruption and attempting recovery are time-consuming and difficult. It is usually easier and less resource-intensive to remove the existing configuration and rebuild it. If database corruption is suspected, contact F5 Technical Support. If necessary, they can run a script which clears the database, restoring the database to its default state.
