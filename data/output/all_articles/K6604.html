Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If you edit a third party device and assign a specific BIG-IP or BIG-IP GTM system as the Statistics Collection Server, the configuration change is not written to the wideip.conf file. As a result, specifying an F5 product to monitor the third party device can be problematic. When the Auto Select feature is selected, the BIG-IP GTM will use any F5 device in the data center to probe a given third party device.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR69164 and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.2.4 and 9.3.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM release notes.
