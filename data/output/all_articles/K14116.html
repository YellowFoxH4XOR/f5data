Known Issue

The BIG-IP ASM system may fail to display detailed information for violations.

The system clears out detailed information for older records without removing the record itself.

Impact

The BIG-IP ASM system may not provide details for the affected violation.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • When you click Recent Incidents under the Attack Signature Detected section on the Policy Building > Manual > Traffic Learning page, the BIG-IP ASM system presents another window without the details. In addition, the BIG-IP system reports a message similar to the following example:

    No records to display
  • When you click Occurrences under the affected violation type on the Policy Building > Manual > Traffic Learning page, the BIG-IP ASM system presents another window without the details. In addition, the BIG-IP system reports a message similar to the following example:

    No records to display
