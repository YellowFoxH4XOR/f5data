Known Issue

Disabling iRules events preceding a suspending command may cause connections to stop responding or fail.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • An iRules event is disabled while it is executing.
  • An iRules event is disabled while pending execution.

Impact

Connections to the BIG-IP system may stop responding or fail.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) process may fail to complete the execution of the iRule.
  • Connections to the BIG-IP system stop responding or fail.
  • In the /var/log/ltm file, you observe an error message that appears similar to the following example:

    tmm info tmm[30823]: 01220009:6: Pending rule event CLIENT_ACCEPTED aborted on flow 10.10.0.1:59609->10.10.0.51:80 (listener: /Common/vs1)

Resolution

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can modify the iRule. If an iRule wants to disable an event, move the execution of the disabling to the end of the iRule.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
