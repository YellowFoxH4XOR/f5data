K000136051: SNMPD restarts when an Add-On license expires.

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-09-14
Updated
2023-09-14
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 15.1.8
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000136051

Description

  • In a monitoring tool, you may receive an alert, about SNMPD Re-starting.
  • No SNMP functionality will be available during the restart.
  • Log Messages in /var/log/kern:

info snmpd[13697]: 0114002c:6: HA daemon_heartbeat %snmpd disabled.

info snmpd[31358]: 0114002b:6: HA daemon_heartbeat %snmpd enabled.


Environment

  • Add-On license.
  • Daemon Re-start.

Cause

When the Add-On license expires, the BIG-IP needs to refresh the current licensing.


 Recommended Actions

If the Add-On license is still needed, please contact your F5 Sales Representative to renew it.


Additional Information

None
