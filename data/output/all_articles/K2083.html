K2083: BIG-IP virtual servers may not respond to large ICMP echo requests

Type
Knowledge
Published
2006-08-23
Updated
2023-02-21
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • Legacy Products
  • BIG-IP 4.X: 4.6.4
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K2083

Topic

The BIG-IP system does not fragment ICMP requests that are larger than the interface Maximum Transfer Unit (MTU) when the requests are destined for a BIG-IP virtual server. As a result, BIG-IP virtual servers may not respond to large ICMP echo requests.
