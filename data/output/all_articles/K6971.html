Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The eventd process memory usage can grow over time if the eventd process experiences a high amount of activity and cannot contact the subscriber. As a result, the use of excessive memory by the eventd process can cause the BIG-IP system to fail over.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR72794 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.1.3, 9.3, and 9.4.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, GTM, ASM, or Link Controller release notes.
