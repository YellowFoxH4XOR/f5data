Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. To identify datacenters for which application persistence should be applied, the update process erroneously uses a dc tag rather than the datacenter tag, which the parser is expecting. As a result, Distributed Application Persistence does not synchronize between members of a BIG-IP GTM sync group.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR123411 and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.4.8 and 10.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM release notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-9.3.1-74.0-HF7 issued for BIG-IP 9.3.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the cumulative hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
