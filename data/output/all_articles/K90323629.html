Known Issue

The ISTATS::remove iRules command fails to remove an iStats variable.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • An iRule invokes the ISTATS::remove command to remove an iStats variable.

Impact

The value of the iStats variable remains defined, and the affected iRule may not perform as expected.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When an iRule invokes the ISTATS::remove command to remove an iStats variable, the iStats variable is not removed.

Resolution

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can use iStats-triggers and iCall scripts to invoke the iStats command line tool indirectly.

Impact of workaround: The impact of the suggested workaround depends on the specific environment. F5 recommends testing any such changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.
