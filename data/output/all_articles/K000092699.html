Description

  • This Article explained cause and impact of highwatermark log
  • Each Diameter peer(Server/Client) had request queue of 8k
  • When queue reach 8k, Diameter peer will be put out of service
  • Error message found in CPF.log

 

2017-01-14 22:33:40,989 WARN [10383] NETTY NIO channel /135.164.45.1:28835=>/135.164.34.164:52729 exceeded the high watermark: highWaterMark=8000, newWriteBufferSize=8000, messageSize=1, highWaterMarkCounter=1, queue size=8000. [Message Executor_22_14] [AbstractNioChannel$WriteRequestQueue.offer()]

 

2017-01-14 22:33:40,989 WARN [11780] Peer DiameterPeer(Lab_Gy C Out of Service 1484462020989) changed state to busy due to BusyChannelWriteQueue [Message Executor_22_14] [PeerImpl.setBusy()]

 

Environment

  • SDC
  • Diameter Peer (Server/Client)

 

Cause

Diameter peer is facing network delay or had issue responding to request proxy from SDC, the queue start to build up.

 

 Recommended Actions

  1. Investigate vendor diameter peer/network for performance issue.
  2. Disable/Renew Peer will help to clear the queue temporary

Additional Information

Only CPF places Diameter Peer out of service but FEP is still connected to Diameter peer.

Subsequent SDC will response with 3004 error for subsequent request designated for Diameter Peer that is in out of Service state.
