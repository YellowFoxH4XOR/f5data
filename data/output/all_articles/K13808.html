Known Issue

The monpd process may log extraneous statistical error messages to the ERR syslog level.

Impact

None

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The system generates extraneous error messages to the /var/log/avr/monpd.log file, similar to the following example:

    monpd|ERR|<date> [stat_bridge_thread::load_file, ] Some rows of load_stat_geo_1340868600.1 not loaded
    monpd|ERR|<date> [stat_bridge_thread::load_file, ] Some rows of load_stat_ip_1340868600.1 not loaded
