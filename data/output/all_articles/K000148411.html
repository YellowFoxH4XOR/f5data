K000148411: Management routing not working on VCMP guest on Viprion

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-11-27
Updated
2024-11-27
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP APM: 17.1.1
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000148411

Description

After configuring management route on VCMP Guest on Viprion, you may observe that traffic is still being routed via TMM interface. This can be inconsistent, you can observe some traffic being routed through management, while some traffic being routed through TMM.

Environment

  • BIG-IP

  • VCMP

Cause

If traffic is being sent through blade for which management IP is not configured, traffic will be sent through TMM interface.

Answer/Recommended Actions

Configure management IP for each blade used by VCMP Guest.

Additional Information

None
