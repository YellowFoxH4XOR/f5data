Known Issue

On rare occasions, running the tmsh show /sys conn command may cause the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) to stop responding and produce a core file.

Note: The tmsh show /sys conn command displays active connections on the BIG-IP system.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restarting, and systems configured as part of a high availability (HA) group may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP system temporarily fails to process traffic while TMM restarts.
  • The system sends a SIGSEGV to TMM, and you observe messages in the /var/log/tmm* file that appear similar to the following example:

    notice ** SIGSEGV **
