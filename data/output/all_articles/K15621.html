Known Issue

In rare circumstances, the datastor service may temporarily stop processing data.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • Your system's datastor cache storage services experience very heavy load for a sustained period of time.

Impact

HTTP sessions which rely on the datastor service may end prematurely.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • A datastor core file appears in the /shared/core directory.
  • HTTP clients receive a TCP reset from the BIG-IP system.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can lessen the load on the datastor caching services by modifying the active policies on the affected BIG-IP WebAccelerator or BIG-IP Advanced Acceleration Manager to exclude caching classes of objects that have a low popularity and classes that either change quickly on the server or are rarely requested.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
