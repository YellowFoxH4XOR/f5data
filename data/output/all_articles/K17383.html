Known Issue

The BIG-IP ASM Configuration utility pages may be slow to respond, and take 1 to 2 minutes to load.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • Many policy configuration changes have been made on the system over time (for example, several thousand policy configuration changes).

Impact

Configuration utility pages may take 1 to 2 minutes to load.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Configuration utility pages are slow to load; in some cases load times may take 1 to 2 minutes.
  • The top command indicates that the mysqld process consumes higher than normal CPU usage.
