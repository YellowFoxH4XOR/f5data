Description

This article explains how to revoke license on BIG-IQ for an unmanaged BIG-IP device when you lost the credentials

Environment

  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IP
  • Revoke license
  • Unmanaged device
  • Lost of BIG-IP credentials

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

  1. Referring to K84120003: Remove license from BIG-IQ using restcurl on step 1 to step 3
  2. Ensure that the BIG-IP is not reachable from BIG-IQ (shutdown the BIG-IP)
  3. Replace the command on step 4 with the following to delete the license assignment and revoke license

restcurl '/cm/device/licensing/pool/regkey/licenses/L8471-22580-09186-08717-5656797/offerings/1404a598-ba4e-4d68-bcee-f6cf419753d3/members/1b366659-9065-4042-abee-dc08c7fbe359' -u admin:admin -X DELETE -d '{"username":"admin","password":"admin","id":"1b366659-9065-4042-abee-dc08c7fbe35"}'
