K10899: Active sessions may not appear on the Reports: Sessions page after a log purge

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If you configure a frequent log purge schedule, logs pertaining to active user sessions may be purged. For example, if you configure an hourly log purge schedule, users that have been logged on to FirePass for longer than an hour will no longer appear on the Reports: Sessions page after a log purge. Additionally, activity generated by those users will no longer appear on the Reports: App Logs page, even if the activity occurs after the last log purge.

Note: The log purge schedule is configured on the Device Management: Maintenance: Logs page in the Purge logs section.
 
F5 Networks Product Development is tracking this issue as CR60097.
