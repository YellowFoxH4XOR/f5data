F5 iWorkflow software support policy

F5 provides 18 months of support for any iWorkflow version starting from the first customer ship date of a given iWorkflow release. Customers who require support beyond the standard 18-month period should contact their F5 account manager.

The following table lists the support periods with their associated severity levels of customer found defects, and the amount of time from the first customer ship that F5 will support the product for the particular support period.

Support periodSeverityAmount of time from the first customer ship
Software DevelopmentAll6 Months
Vulnerability SupportSecurity Patches18 Months
Tech SupportAll18 Months

At the End of Software Development (EoSD) standard 6-month support period, the release enters its End of Software Development (EoSD) phase for the next 12 months. Software branches of the iWorkflow system that have reached their EoSD date are no longer supported with active software development. These software versions will continue to receive reasonable effort configuration support until they reach their End of Technical Support (EoTS) date. However, F5 is no longer liable to provide software fixes (hotfixes) nor consulting services for that major release branch.

For software releases, unless specifically declared and announced by F5, one year after the EoSD date is the EoTS date. The EoTS date marks the end of the software version's support phase. After the EoTS effective date, F5 is no longer liable to provide security updates nor software fixes (hotfixes) for that software branch.
