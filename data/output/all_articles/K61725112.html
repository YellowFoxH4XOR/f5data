Known Issue

In rare circumstances, the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may cause CPU usage to suddenly climb to 100 percent and remain there indefinitely.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • A TCP flow exists in the FIN_WAIT_2 state.
  • TMM marks client and server-side flows as EXPIRED.

Impact

TMM performance and traffic processing may slow down.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You notice that one or more TMM processes show 100 percent CPU usage.
