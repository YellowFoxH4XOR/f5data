K20950501: SFPs missing after a reboot

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-08-14
Updated
2026-08-14
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K20950501

Description

After a reboot SFPs are suddenly missing

Environment

Applicable on any appliance or chassis blade where SFPs are used.

Cause

The Big-IP will not poll for SFPs until TMM is ready for world.  

Recommended Actions

Determine why TMM is not going ready and once this has been resolved, the SFPs should populate as expected.

Additional Information

https://cdn.f5.com/product/bugtracker/ID752766.html
Note: This ID is only one possible reason why TMM may not be ready for world, and is not the root cause of this issue.
