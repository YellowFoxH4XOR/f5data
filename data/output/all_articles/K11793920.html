K11793920: BIG-IP LTM policies may not correctly handle multiple data groups

Type
Known Issue
Published
2018-02-16
Updated
2025-02-06
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 13.1.0, 13.0.1, 13.0.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K11793920

Known Issue

You may encounter a situation when a policy is in use, and the conditions reference two or more data groups, but only the conditions that refer to the first data group have any effect on the system.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a BIG-IP policy associated with a virtual server.
  • The associated policy has two or more rules associated with it.
  • Two or more of these rules reference different data groups using TCP address matches in datagroup.

Impact

The policy rules do not match as expected.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • BIG-IP LTM policy conditions do not check against second and subsequent data groups, resulting in the policy not working as intended.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 688744 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. For information about releases, point releases, or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can, if possible, rework the policy so that it does not refer to more than one data group-based condition.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
