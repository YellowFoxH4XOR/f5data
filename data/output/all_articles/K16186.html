Known Issue

Health Monitor traffic fails on a standby BIG-IP system when the system obtains health status through an IPSec tunnel.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have multiple BIG-IP systems in a high-availability configuration.
  • You have configured an IPSec tunnel to use a floating self IP as the local endpoint address.
  • Health monitor traffic reaches pool members through the IPSec tunnel.

Impact

After a failover event, there may be brief connection failures as pool members will not be immediately available.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Monitored pool member status shows Available on an Active device, but Offline on a Standby device.
  • You observe a brief period of connectivity failures after a failover event.
