Note: The P95 Billing page is also included in the 3-DNS Controller, but this statistics page is primarily beneficial to BIG-IP or BIG-IP Link Controller users.

P95 is a common ISP billing method in which the customer is charged on the 95th percentile of the total traffic utilized. This means that with P95 billing, the highest 5% of traffic usage periods are discarded so that customers are not charged for abnormally high spikes in network traffic.

The BIG-IP Link Controller's P95 Billing page is a statistical page that graphically depicts traffic patterns through ISP links. It also designates the P95 level of traffic with a horizontal line through the chart. This statistics page is for reporting purposes only, no load balancing decisions are made based on this data.

The P95 billing graphs populate as soon as traffic begins to generate through the links. You can select the time frame for what the graphs should represent. The default setting is five weeks; however, you can specify a value of up to two months.

Note: The scales are dynamically generated to fit the traffic. Since the graph measures bandwidth on a scale of five weeks to two months, it may take up to several days of traffic to notice the graphing.

For more information about how to configure the BIG-IP Link Controller P95 Billing page, refer to the BIG-IP Link Controller Solutions Guide.
