Description

Interfaces are not forwarding the traffic after the VE upgrade.

Environment

  • Interface is showing down or not forwarding the traffic after the upgrade
  • Mac doesn't match up with the mac in the vmware
  • Upgrade from BIG-IP 13

Cause

Mac addresses don't match up with the mac in the VMWare after the upgrade.

Recommended Actions

Reorder the interfaces in Hypervisor, to match the BIG-IP VE order

Additional Information

For more detailed information for this issue, please refer to:

  • BIG-IP VE preserves the NIC ordering for five or more network interfaces on VMware
