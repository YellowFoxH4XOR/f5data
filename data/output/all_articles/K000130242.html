Description

This article explain reason for sudden Tripo Crash and restart. 
The following messages are logged on /var/log/rsyslog/nodename.SyswatchDog.log 
Aug  9 02:02:34 Lab-C-08-Tripo-01 SysWatchDog[3174]: Tripo started successfully
Aug  9 02:02:45 Lab-C-08-Tripo-01 SysWatchDog[3174]: WebStatMgr crashed. Tripo is shutting down
Aug  9 02:03:37 Lab-C-08-Tripo-01 SysWatchDog[4179]: #########################
Aug  9 02:03:37 Lab-C-08-Tripo-01 SysWatchDog[4179]: #   WatchDog STARTING   #
Aug  9 02:03:37 Lab-C-08-Tripo-01 SysWatchDog[4179]: #########################
 

Environment

  • SDC version 5.x
  • Tripo
  • user/external process initiated telnet to port 5555 where TRIPO service is running on

Cause

Bug SDC-374

 Recommended Actions

Avoid performing scanning or performing access to port 5555. 
IP tables can be put in placed to avoid external IP from scanning/accessing port 5555.

Additional Information

Issue is fixed from version 5.1 CF 31
