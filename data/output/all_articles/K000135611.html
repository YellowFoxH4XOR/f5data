Description

  • Blades on a Viprion chassis failing to forward inter blade traffic through the backplane.
  • Monitors report pool members and nodes down.
  • Error message(s) logged in /var/log/tmm
notice Oops @ <hex number>:<number>: MPI out of memory

Environment

  • Multi blade Viprion chassis operating as full individual system or vCMP system.
  • For vCMP systems guests spanning more than one blade.
  • Any BIGIP module.

Cause

Over utilisation of backplane to process traffic leads to MPI memory exhaustion resulting in all traffic utilising the backplane failing.
Some of the causes of backplane over utlisation could be some blades not physically wired to trunk(s).

 Recommended Actions

  1. Ensure traffic volume is within BIGIP Viprion specification based on the product datasheet.
  2. All blades in the system MUST be wired and included in the trunk(s) connected to the network. 

Additional Information

To fairly distribute traffic among available blades in a chassis number of trunk interfaces MUST be in the powers of 2.
See the manual for further details.
