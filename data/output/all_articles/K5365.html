K5365: Systems that are licensed for both BIG-IP and 3-DNS do not send SNMP traps for failed 3-DNS ECV checks

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If you configure SNMP traps for failed 3-DNS ECV health checks on a BIG-IP that is licensed with the 3-DNS module, no SNMP traps will be sent. The log messages associated with the ECV failure are logged to the /var/log/3dns file, but the system bases the SNMP trap on the contents of the /var/log/bigip file.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR28210 and CR42082.
