K000132652: After Upgrade getting Provisioning warning

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-02-17
Updated
2023-02-27
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 16.1.3
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000132652

Description

All of your modules, with the exception of AVR, are reporting as unlicensed, despite reactivating the license before performing the upgrade.


Additionally, the GUI is throwing a provisioning warning, and your vCMP guests are reporting down.


Environment

vCMP

BIG-IP 5250

after upgrade


Cause

BIG-IP version isn't compatible with your hardware.


Recommended Actions

Downgrade to the highest compatible for your hardware. You can find that in The F5 hardware/software compatibility matrix


Additional Information

none
