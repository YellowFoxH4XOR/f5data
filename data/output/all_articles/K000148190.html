K000148190: Why am I getting a "Payload Too Large" Response?

What Happened?

  • When making a request to my application, I am getting a "Payload Too Large" response page

 

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud Console
  • Load Balancer

 

Resolution/Answer

  1. Locate to your Load Balancer (LB) in the Console
  2. Click the Manage Configuration Option from the "..." on the right-hand side of the Load Balancer List
  3. Click Edit Configuration
  4. Scroll To or Click Other Settings
  5. Click Edit Configuration under More Options
  6. Click Configure under Miscellaneous Options -> Buffer Policy
  7. You then have two options:
    1. You can Increase the Max Request Bytes to a higher value (up to a maximum of 10485760 bytes)
    2. Disable the Buffer Policy Altogether by Enabling the Checkbox next to Disable
  8. Click Apply
  9. Click Apply
  10. Click Save and Exit

 

Additional Information

  • When you make a request to your Load Balancer (LB) and the request body is too large to buffer, the Load Balancer (LB) will return a 413 Response with a page saying "Payload Too Large"
