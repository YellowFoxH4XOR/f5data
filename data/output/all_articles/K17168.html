Known Issue

The health monitor may report an unknown status for the ephemeral nodes instead of offline as expected.

This issue occurs when the following condition are met:

  • You configure fully qualified domain name (FQDN) pool members.
  • You modify monitor rules several times that are associated with FQDN pool members.

Impact

The health monitor may report an unknown status for ephemeral nodes instead of offline as expected.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can restart the bigd process. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: When bigd is restarted monitoring is stopped until the bigd service has finished restarting. During the restart time monitored instances retain the last known state.

  1. Log in to the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) by entering the following command:

    tmsh

  2. To restart the bigd service, type the following command:

    restart sys service bigd
