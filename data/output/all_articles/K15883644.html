Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may incorrectly identify a TCP retransmission as a spurious retransmission.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The virtual server is configured with a TCP profile.
  • A connection to the virtual server experiences packet loss.

The BIG-IP system may occasionally treat a TCP retransmission as if it were a spurious retransmission and fail to re-acknowledge the original packet that was previously acknowledged.

Impact

Affected TCP connections may experience poor performance or fail.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Affected TCP connections may experience poor performance or fail.
