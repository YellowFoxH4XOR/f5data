K21572604: Passive FTP data channel times out

Type
Support Solution
Published
2021-11-18
Updated
2023-02-21
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K21572604

Description

Passive FTP data channel creation fails during 3-way handshake.

Environment

BIG-IP LTM
Hardware SYN Cookies enabled

Cause

This is due to a manifestation of ID1040017.  

With hardware SYN cookie mode enabled, final ACK validation during flow accept fails and ACK packets are dropped.  In this case an error is logged in LTM such as:

"A Enforced Device DOS attack start was detected for vector IP uncommon proto"

Recommended Actions

Disable hardware SYN Cookie.

To obtain an engineering hotfix for your version please contact F5 support.

Additional Information

It is possible the data channel functionality never recovers without a reboot or tmm restart due to ID1012581.
