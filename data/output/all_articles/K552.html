Topic

If you are local to BIG-IP (that is, directly plugged in to the BIG-IP system with a keyboard and monitor) and logged in at the console, you can log in on four different channels. To access the channels, use the Alt key in conjunction with a function key (F1 through F4).

For example:

Pressing Alt and F4 will take you to channel 4.

These different channels can assist you in certain processes. For instance:

  • If you have started a process you cannot exit, you could go to another channel and kill the process.
  • If you need to perform simultaneous tcpdumps on multiple interfaces.
  • If you want to generate traffic while performing a tcpdump.
  • If you need to quickly shift back and forth between BIG-IP 1 and BIG-IP 2.

Note: This feature requires that you log out of all console channels when you have finished a process.
