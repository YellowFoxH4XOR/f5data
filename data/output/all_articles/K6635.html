K6635: BIG-IP ASM-enabled virtual servers allow for reuse of NTLM connections to pool members

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Connections from the BIG-IP ASM virtual servers to those pool members that require NT LAN Manager (NTLM) authentication currently allow flow reuse.

Connections to pool members that require NTLM authentication should be handled on their own non-pooled connection.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR69313 and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM 9.2.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM release notes.
