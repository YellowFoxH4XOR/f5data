Known Issue

Most failures on a BIG-IP system cause the device to go into the offline state; a device in the offline state cannot become active.

If a high availability (HA) feature fails, which causes a failover, the device goes into a standby state.  For example, when the pool-min-up-members fails, the BIG-IP system goes into a standby state, and as this condition persists, the system cannot become active.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You configure a device group with both active and standby systems.
  • A failover condition occurs that causes the failed system to go into a standby state.
  • The failover condition persists.

Impact

If a system fails over to an apparent standby system, the failover may be unsuccessful and disrupt traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • A standby host is unable to become active.
