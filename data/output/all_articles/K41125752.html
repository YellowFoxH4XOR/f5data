Using the latest maintenance and point release of BIG-IP software ensures you have access to advanced capabilities, the highest quality software, and the most secure releases.

F5 recommends that you upgrade your BIG-IP to the most recent maintenance and point release for BIG-IP 17.1.x.

Important: BIG-IP 15.1.x will reach end of life (EoL) at the end of 2024, and BIG-IP 16.1.x will reach EoL seven months after 15.1.x. For additional information, refer to BIG-IP 15.1.x and 16.1.x are reaching End of Technical Support soon! and K5903: BIG-IP software support policy.
