Description

The F5 BIG-IP device may experience a split-brain (active-active) scenario where both nodes in a High Availability (HA) pair believe they are the active device. This situation can lead to conflicting traffic distribution and potential service disruption.

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • Device Service Clustering (DSC)
  • Split Brain
  • Queries

Cause

A split-brain scenario can be triggered by various factors, including:

  1. Network Connectivity Issues:
    1. Loss of Heartbeat Link: When the primary and secondary devices lose communication with each other, they might both assume they are the active device.
    2. Network Partitions: Isolation of devices due to network partitions can lead to a split-brain situation.
  2. Configuration Errors:
    1. Incorrect HA Configuration: Misconfigured HA settings, such as incorrect IP addresses or VLAN configurations, can contribute to split-brain.
    2. Device Trust Issues: Problems with device trust certificates or keys can disrupt communication and lead to split-brain.
  3. Hardware Failures:
    1. Failed Network Interfaces: Hardware failures on the HA link or other critical network interfaces can cause connectivity issues and trigger  a split-brain.
    2. Power Outages: Simultaneous power loss or power fluctuations in both devices can cause them to both attempt to become active upon reboot.
  4. Software Bugs or Glitches:
    1. Although rare, software bugs or glitches in the BIG-IP software can sometimes lead to unexpected behavior and split-brain situations.

Recommended Actions

To prevent a split-brain scenario, consider the following solutions and best practices:

  1. Ensure reliable network connectivity between HA devices, particularly the heartbeat link. 
  2. Verify that HA configurations, including IP addresses and VLAN settings, are correct and consistent across devices.
  3. Regularly check the health and status of network interfaces and replace any faulty hardware promptly.
  4. Use uninterruptible power supplies (UPS) to protect devices from power outages and fluctuations.
  5. Keep the BIG-IP software up-to-date to benefit from the latest bug fixes and enhancements.
  6. Regularly monitor device trust certificates and keys to ensure they are valid and correctly configured.

Additional Information
