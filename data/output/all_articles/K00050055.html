Known Issue

IPsec IKEv1 tunnels may fail and stay down indefinitely if the IKEv1 racoon daemon stops responding and the tmipsecd process does not restart it.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The racoon daemon has previously stopped responding multiple times.

Impact

IPsec IKEv1 tunnels cannot be established.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • IPsec IKEv1 tunnels cannot be established.
  • There is no indication that the racoon daemon has stopped responding.
  • Attempts to reconfigure IPsec do not resolve the issue.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can restart the tmipsecd process. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the TMOS Shell (tmsh) by typing the following command:

    tmsh

  2. Restart the tmipsecd service by typing the following command:

    restart /sys service tmipsecd

  3. To exit tmsh, type the following command:

    quit
