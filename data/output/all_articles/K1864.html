Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The ifRcvAddressAddress OID contained in the ifRcvAddressEntry table does not have a size restriction. This may cause some SNMP monitoring programs to report an error.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR29606 and CR41905.
