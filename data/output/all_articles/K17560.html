Known Issue

BIG-IP ASM chart reporting may incorrectly report that there is no data to display.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You navigate to Security > Reporting > Charts and attempt to view reporting data for various time periods.

Due to the way that the system stores data internally, when you view larger sets of data (such as with longer time periods), some information may not be reported at all.

Impact

Reports of BIG-IP ASM activity may be confusing or misleading.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Viewing reports for the time period Last Week displays chart data, but when the time period is switched to Last Month, you may observe a message similar to the following example:

    There is no data to display either due to the lack of relevant traffic, or due to the settings of the filter.
