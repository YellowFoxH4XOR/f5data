K45000869: High CPU utilization of odd cores due to mcpd

Type
Support Solution
Published
2020-03-19
Updated
2023-02-21
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 12.1.2
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K45000869

Description

BIG-IP experiences CPU spikes to ~80% of odd cores(1, 3, 5...) approximately every 5 minutes.
Most CPU resources are drained by mcpd.

Multiple audit_forwarder processes are running.

It can be verified via ps command executed from BIG-IP bash:
ps aux | grep audit_forwarder

141526636  26636  23286   0.0   0.2   32.1M   22.3M ?        S    Mar04   00:00:16.38 /usr/bin/audit_forwarder
141526637  26637  23322   0.0   0.2   32.1M   22.3M ?        S    Mar04   00:00:16.50 /usr/bin/audit_forwarder
Environment
  • BIG-IP LTM
  • high CPU usage due to mcpd
  • multipe audit_forwarder processes running
     
Cause
Bug ID 628703


Recommended Actions
As a workaround you can restart syslog-ng process.
From BIG-IP bash: 
bigstart restart syslog-ng
