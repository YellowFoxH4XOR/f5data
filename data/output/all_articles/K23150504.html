Known Issue

One or more VIPRION blades may experience Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) restarts after an intra-chassis failover event.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP VIPRION system chassis has multiple blades installed.
  • Your BIG-IP VIPRION Network Mirroring option is set to Intra-cluster mirroring. This is the default setting.
  • The BIG-IP system is provisioned to process PEM subscriber traffic.
  • Your configuration includes one or more custom PEM RADIUS protocol profiles.
  • One or more RADIUS subscriber connection attributes are corrupted or missing.
  • A VIPRION blade either fails or reboots.

Note: If the VIPRION system is processing large amounts of traffic, multiple blades may experience TMM restarts.

Impact

Traffic processing will be interrupted while the TMM processes restart.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP VIPRION system fails to process traffic for a brief period.
  • TMM has produced a core file in the /var/core directory.
