Issue

This article applies to BIG-IP 11.5.2 through 11.5.7, 11.6.0 HF5 and later, and 12.0.0 and later. For information about other versions, refer to the following article:

  • K14210: vCMP guests with less than 6.5 GB of memory support a maximum of two provisioned modules for some versions

Old Behavior

In BIG-IP 11.3.0 through 11.5.1, and 11.6.0 through 11.6.0 HF4, you are only able to provision two modules on a Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP) guest with less than 8 GB of memory.

New Behavior

Beginning in BIG-IP 11.5.2, 11.6.0 HF5, and 12.0.0 and later, vCMP guests with less than 8 GB and more than 4 GB of memory now support provisioning for potentially more than two software modules. Module provisioning is now constrained by the memory and disk usage of each specific module. 

Recommended Actions

F5 provides recommendations for module provisioning for each release in the corresponding release notes. This information is located within the Platform Support section and differs based on the amount of available memory. For information about the amount of memory allocated to each vCMP guest for vCMP-capable platforms, refer to K14218: vCMP guest memory/CPU core allocation matrix.
