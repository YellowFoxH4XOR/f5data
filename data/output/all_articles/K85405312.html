Known Issue

The IPsec tunnel may fail to establish when an Internet Key Exchange version 2 (IKEv2) protocol is used.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured your BIG-IP system as an IPsec tunnel endpoint.
  • You have configured IPsec tunnels to use the IKEv2 protocol.
  • The remote IKEv2 peer sends a NON_FIRST_FRAGMENTS_ALSO notification as a part of a child SA negotiation.

    For example, a Cisco ASA device sends an IKEv2 request with a NON_FIRST_FRAGMENTS_ALSO notification to the BIG-IP system.

Impact

The BIG-IP system fails to establish an IPsec tunnel with a remote peer.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Your BIG-IP system fails to establish an IPsec tunnel with a remote peer.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can use an IKEv1 protocol instead of IKEv2 to establish an IPSec tunnel with the remote peer.

Impact of workaround: The impact of the suggested workaround depends on the specific environment. F5 recommends testing any such changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.
