Description

Encountering an error message stating 'Unable to reach the licensing server' when attempting to activate the license through the 'Automatic' method in the GUI.

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • License activation and reactivation 

Cause

The BIG-IP cannot establish a connection to the licensing server.

Recommended Actions

To reactivate the license, use the 'Manual' activation method by following these steps:

Impact of procedure: Traffic processing is briefly interrupted while the BIG-IP system reloads the configuration. When licensing redundant BIG-IP systems, F5 recommends that you activate the license on your BIG-IP devices while they are in standby mode.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.
  2. Select Activate.
    Note: If the BIG-IP system is already licensed, navigate to System > License and select Re-activate. 
  3. Select Manual > Next
  4. Copy the dossier and connect to the F5 Product Licensing page at secure.f5.com.
  5. On the F5 Licensing Tools page, select Activate F5 product registration key.
  6. Paste the dossier into the Enter Your Dossier box, and then select Next.
    The F5 Product Licensing page produces your license.
  7. Copy the license and paste it into the License box in the Configuration utility.
  8. Select Next and wait for the configuration to reload

    Additional Information

    For additional details on licensing the BIG-IP system, refer to K7752: Licensing the BIG-IP system.
