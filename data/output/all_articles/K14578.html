Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may repeatedly report a critical PSU failure.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The BIG-IP system is one of the following platforms:
    • BIG-IP 2000 series (C112)
    • BIG-IP 4000 series (C113)
    • BIG-IP 5000 series (C109)
    • BIG-IP 7000 series (D110)

Impact

These error messages are benign.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H439090 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Low screen.
  • You may observe intermittent system_check messages in the /var/log/ltm file that indicate a fan failure. These messages appear similar to the following example:

    010d0006:0: Chassis power supply 1 has experienced an issue. Status is as follows: FAN=bad; STATUS=bad.

  • You may observe the chmand process log a sequence of messages in the /var/log/ltm file that indicate the Power Supply GPIO status changes to Bad and back to Good approximately 5 seconds later. These messages appear similar to the following example:

    crit chmand[6532]: 012a0013:2: Blade 0 hardware sensor critical alarm: Power supply 1 GPIO status(SPAFFIV03G): Bad
    notice chmand[6532]: 012a0016:5: Blade 0 hardware sensor notice: Power supply 1 GPIO status(SPAFFIV03G): Good
