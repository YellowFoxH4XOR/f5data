Topic

Everyone can start using MyF5 by early 2023

By early 2023, F5 will welcome all its partners and customers to MyF5 (my.f5.com), your portal to our retooled, world-class support organization. MyF5 will make it easier for you to get the most from your F5 technologies and access our world-class F5 Support professionals.

If you have not yet received an email notification that F5 Support is moving your support account to MyF5, you will soon. We invite you to visit MyF5 now, but you do not need to sign up for MyF5 yet. When you receive the email notification that your account is moving to MyF5, it will include instructions about how to sign up and get started. F5 Support will email you using the contact information in your My Support account, so you may want to make sure it is current.

Description

MyF5 provides personalized customer service

When you join MyF5, you will be able to:

  • Create, view, and manage your own support cases.
  • Add, remove, and manage your support account users.
  • Download all F5 software and track details and usage for your subscriptions.
  • Find knowledge articles based on your previous searches and your profile.
  • Get fast access to specialists who have details about your issue when you need it.

Introducing F5 Support Live Chat

F5 is also pleased to introduce our Live Chat support channel within the MyF5 portal. You can use Live Chat to get quick help with basic support issues—like licensing and site navigation—and opening support cases for complex problems that require a support professional.

These features are just the beginning!

MyF5 will continue to add new capabilities that further improve your support experience. For example, in the coming months, MyF5 will add the following enhancements:

  • Consolidated knowledge articles and resources on MyF5, so you no longer change between sites when you are looking for information
  • A self-serve, AI-powered knowledge base that enables you to troubleshoot problems more quickly by returning the most relevant articles

The wait is nearly over!

If you are not yet on MyF5, we will see you there soon!
