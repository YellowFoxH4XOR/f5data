K32559134: Discovery Task for Address Space stopped or expired and does not run again

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-08-11
Updated
2026-07-07
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP APM: 17.X.X, 16.1.X, 17.0.0, 16.1.3, 16.1.2, 17.0.X, 16.1.0, 16.1.1
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K32559134

Description

You observed that Zoom or O365 discovery task:

  • doesn't run with the selected frequency
    or
  • was canceled
    or
  • its status shows 'Expired'
    and
  • you cannot click 'Delete', 'Restart' or other buttons.

Environment

  • BIG IP APM
  • Network Access Address Space

Cause

This can be BIG-IP REST framework problem.

Recommended Actions

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system. This does not your affect HA configuration meaning failover is not expected.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP system's Advanced Shell (bash).
  2. To restore your BIG-IP REST framework, enter the following command:

    clear-rest-storage

    Note: clear-rest-storage completely clears the stored state for the REST framework. It first stops restjavad and restnoded, then deletes the index and storage directories, and then restarts those services.
