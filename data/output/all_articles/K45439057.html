Description

Unexpected failovers are occurring in a BIG-IP VE environment.

Messages similar to the following may appear in /var/log/ltm:

notice tmm[16072]: 01010029:5: Clock advanced by 10893 ticks
sod[6009]: 010c0083:4: No failover status messages received for 3.800 seconds, from device BIGIP-A.example.com (192.168.1.2) (unicast: -> 10.10.20.1).

Environment

  • BIG-IP VE
  • Failover
  • High CPU/CPU starvation
  • VM Snapshot/Backup

Cause

The snapshot process freezes the whole BIG-IP VE at points, including TMM, so no traffic is passing at that time. When TMM starts running again, it sees that it essentially jumped in to some point in the future, hence the clock advances.

Recommended Actions

K00421514: Periodic failover while taking BIG-IP VE snapshots from hypervisor

None
