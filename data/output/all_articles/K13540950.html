Description

An unmanaged virtual edition Big-IP needs to be upgraded and its FCP license is managed from a BIG-IQ

Environment

  • These steps are unique for BIG-IQ License manager, as this is a cut down version of BIG-IQ and as such does not allow you to "manage" the BIG-IP devices in the traditional way
  • FCP CAT License(s) in use 

Cause

The process to upgrade BIG-IP Virtual device is manual and involves reactivating the license and each of its offerings, updating each of the BIG-IP devices from the BIG-IQ, then upgrading the software on each individual BIG-IP device.

Recommended Actions

  1. Reactivate the License to get a new service check date
  2. Update each of the BIG-IP devices with a fresh License
  3. Upgrade the BIG-IP device

Reactivate the License to get a new service check date

License Automatically

  1. Click on Devices, expand (+) License Management and click on Licenses
  2. Place a check mark in the box next to the license you need to reactivate and click on the Reactivate button
  3. Choose Automatic as the activation method
  4. Click on Reactivate in the bottom right side of the screen
  5. The screen will revert back to the license list and you will notice each of the offerings is now also updated automatically
    Note: This will generally take about 10 minutes.

License Manually

  1. Click on Devices, expand (+) License Management and click on Licenses
  2. Place a check mark (√) in the box next to the license you need to reactivate and click on the Reactivate button
  3. Choose Manual as the activation method
  4. Click on Generate Dossier
  5. Right click and select all, then right click and copy the dossier text
  6. Click on the License server link (https://activate.f5.com) click on the Activate F5 product registration key link
  7. Paste the dossier text into the box, right click and choose paste
  8. Click on Next
  9. Agree to the terms and conditions, click on Next
  10. Copy the license text, right click in the license text box select all, right click copy
  11. Return to the BIG-IQ GUI and paste the license, right click in the box and paste
  12. Click on Reactivate bottom right of the screen
  13. You will now notice the license has a yellow triangle beside it and status of pending
  14. Click on the license name to expand it
  15. You will need to Reactivate each of the offerings, click on the first offering name
  16. Copy the Device dossier
  17. Paste it into the license server as you did above
  18. Agree to the license terms if presented click Next
  19. Copy the license text
  20. Paste the license text into the BIG-IQ GUI
  21. Click on Activate in the bottom right side of the screen
  22. Repeat for each offering

Update each of the BIG-IP devices with a fresh License

Note: A soft restart of services is done during this process (We advise doing this out of hours or on standby unit and failing over)

  1. Click on Devices, expand (+) License management and click on Licenses
  2. Click on the name of the license to expand and show each of its offerings
  3. Review the list of offerings and for each of them which has 1 or more assigned click on the offering name
  4. Place a check mark (√) besides the BIG-IP device name and click on Refresh
  5. Fill in the Admin user name and Password and click on OK
  6. Confirm refresh, by clicking on OK

Upgrade the BIG-IP device

  • You will need to check and provision Hard drive space.
  • You will need to upload the ISO image to each of your BIG-IP devices
  • For more guidance see K84554955: Overview of BIG-IP systems software upgrades or raise a Technical Support case.
