Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may reset the server-side TCP connection to a diameter server. This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The BIG-IP system receives a Capabilities-Exchange-Answer (CEA) diameter message in two or more TCP segments from a diameter server during the capabilities exchange process.

Impact

The diameter traffic is disrupted.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • A tcpdump capture of the affected traffic shows the BIG-IP system responds with an RST packet after it receives a CEA message in two or more TCP segments.
