Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When the End-User Diagnostics (EUD) detects a chassis fan as bad during the cpldsensor test, the test incorrectly indicates a passed test result.

For example, the following cpldsensor test output indicates a passed test result even though Fan6 is detected as bad:

09 17 2008 15:34:33 Test Begin: cpldsensor test
09 17 2008 15:34:33 Fan1: GOOD
09 17 2008 15:34:33 Fan2: GOOD
09 17 2008 15:34:33 Fan3: GOOD
09 17 2008 15:34:33 Fan4: GOOD
09 17 2008 15:34:33 Fan5: GOOD
09 17 2008 15:34:33 Fan6: BAD
09 17 2008 15:34:33 Test Complete: cpldsensor test: PASSED

Note: This issue affects EUD versions 10.0.9 through 11.1.x. For information about determining the EUD version, refer to K8002: Determining the End-User Diagnostics version.

If you notice that the cpldsensor indicates a passed test result even though a chassis fan was detected as bad, contact F5 Networks Customer Support to report the issue.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR107750 and PDT637, and it was fixed in EUD version 11.2.0.
