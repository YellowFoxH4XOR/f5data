Known Issue

The lsndb delete client command may fail to delete all entries from the Large Scale NAT (LSN) database.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP system is a chassis-based platform.
  • A large number of entries (for example 500,000) exist in the LSN database.

Impact

There is no impact; however, users may question why the lsndb command fails to remove the required entries.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The lsndb command fails to remove entries from the LSN database.
