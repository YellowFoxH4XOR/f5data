Known Issue

The standby BIG-IP ASM will erroneously send a Reporting Charts email.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • BIG-IP ASM is configured in a high availability pair
  • The Reporting Charts Scheduler feature is configured with an email address in the Send To (E-Mails) field

Normally only the active unit should send Reporting Charts emails. As the standby unit in a high availability (HA) pair will not be passing any traffic, there is no need to generate reports from the device. As such, the email generated by the standby device correctly contains no data.

Impact

Both the active and standby members of a high availability pair send Reporting Charts emails.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • Two emails will be sent each time the Charts Scheduler has been configured to send a chart, one from each member of the HA pair.
  • The erroneous email from the standby member of the HA pair can be identified by a lack of data.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can disable the SMTP service on the standby device. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: The standby BIG-IP system will be unable to send mail.

Note: Any config-sync operation from active to standby will re-enable the SMTP service on the standby, and disabling the work around. Once disabled, SMTP will remain disabled after failover and will need to be re-enabled to allow the now active device to send charts.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP ASM Configuration utility on the standby device.
  2. Navigate to the Application Security > Options > SMTP Configuration menu.
  3. Clear the Enable SMTP Mailer check box.
  4. Click Update.
