Known Issue

The IPsec tunnel may fail when NAT traversal (NAT-T) is enabled with IKEv1 negotiation.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured your BIG-IP system as an IPSec tunnel endpoint.
  • The IKE Peers NAT Traversal option is enabled on the Internet Key Exchange (IKE) peers configuration.
  • Your network address translation (NAT) devices are detected during IKE negotiations.

Impact

The BIG-IP system fails to establish IPSec tunnels when NAT-T is enabled.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • You observe that your BIG-IP system fails to establish an IPSec tunnel when NAT-T is enabled.
  • You observe that IKE uses incorrect source port (UDP 500), when viewing a packet capture taken with the tcpdump utility. For more information regarding tcpdump, refer to K411: Overview of packet tracing with the tcpdump utility.
