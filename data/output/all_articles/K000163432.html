K000163432: BIG-IQ 8.4.2 HA database/replication failure during every manual failover, leaving both CMs at “Waiting for BIG-IQ services to become available” when ssh-rsa is disabled in SSH HostKeyAlgorithms

Description

BIG-IQ 8.4.2 manual HA failover consistently failed because the promotion process could not establish the required SSH session to stop services on the current Primary Device. The failure occurred immediately after bigstart stop restjavad tokumond postgres, leaving PostgreSQL in a read-only state, replication incomplete, and both BIG-IQ CMs potentially displaying “Waiting for BIG-IQ services to become available.”


 

Environment

  • BIG-IQ HA Central Managers configured for manual failover with DCDs
  • Customized BIG-IQ SSH configuration restricted HostKeyAlgorithms to ECDSA algorithms and excluded ssh-rsa
  • Manual promotion and failover testing performed in both directions


 

Cause

The customized SSH configuration excluded the legacy ssh-rsa host-key algorithm required by the BIG-IQ 8.4.2 HA promotion workflow. During promotion, the Secondary must establish SSH connectivity to the current Primary to stop restjavad, tokumond, and PostgreSQL. Because SSH negotiation failed immediately, the service-stop operation did not complete, the HA replication workflow terminated before configuring streaming replication, and the former Primary remained in a read-only database state. The resulting incomplete HA transition caused service startup failures and loss of GUI availability.


Issue Tracked under ID 1623073


 

Answer/Recommended Actions

BIG-IQ requires ssh-rsa enabled on SSH HostKeyAlgorithms for all HA operations ( Setting up HA cluster, Promoting Secondary to Primary)
