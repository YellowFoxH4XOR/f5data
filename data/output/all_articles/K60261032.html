Known Issue

The BIG-IP ASM system may fail to log violation details for an illegal meta character violation.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP ASM security policy is configured with the Shift JIS application language.
  • The security policy detects an illegal meta character.

Impact

Illegal meta character violation details are not logged.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When viewing logged violations for illegal meta characters, details about the violations are missing.
