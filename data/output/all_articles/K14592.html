Topic

This article covers the compatibility of BIG-IQ products with BIG-IP releases.

Description

The BIG-IQ products support several modules and provide central management for the BIG-IP system.

Before June 13, 2016, the BIG-IQ product line contained the Centralized Management and Cloud and Orchestration products. Starting in BIG-IQ 5.0.0, the BIG-IQ product line contains only the Centralized Management product, and F5 will release the Cloud and Orchestration product under a new product line. To determine the compatibility of products, select the article that applies to your BIG-IQ version:

  • For BIG-IQ Centralized Management 4.6.0 and later, refer to K34133507: BIG-IQ Centralized Management compatibility matrix.
  • For BIG-IQ versions prior to 4.6.0 and BIG-IQ Cloud and Orchestration 1.0.0, refer to K45824491: BIG-IQ 4.0.0 - 4.5.0 and BIG-IQ Cloud and Orchestration compatibility matrix.

Note: For the considerations on the upgrade compatibility of BIG-IQ or iWorkflow product offerings, refer to K21232150: Considerations for upgrading BIG-IQ or F5 iWorkflow.
