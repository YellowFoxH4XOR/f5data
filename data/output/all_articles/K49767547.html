Known Issue

After upgrading a BIG-IP device that is licensed for FIPS 140-2 compliant mode or full-box FIPS license, the BIG-IP device reports its FIPS mode as FIPS 140-3 compliant mode, however FIPS 140-2 and FIPS 140-3 mode is not properly enabled on the BIG-IP device.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP device is running a BIG-IP version earlier than BIG-IP 16.1.2.2, regardless of hardware or software platform type.
  • Your BIG-IP device is licensed with FIPS 140-2 compliant mode or full-box FIPS license.  Or your BIG-IP system is licensed by BIG-IQ license manager and the license includes the FIPS 140-2 compliant mode feature flag.
  • You upgraded your BIG-IP device to BIG-IP 16.1.2.2.

Additionally, if you upgrade a BIG-IP device that is licensed for FIPS 140-2 compliant mode or full-box FIPS license to BIG-IP 16.1.2.2 without first re-activating the device license, you will be prompted to reboot after the upgrade is completed. After the reboot, FIPS mode will be disabled on the BIG-IP device.

Impact

After the upgrade is completed and the BIG-IP device reboots to BIG-IP 16.1.2.2, the BIG-IP device reports that FIPS 140-3 compliant mode is enabled, but the FIPS mode is not FIPS 140-2 or FIPS 140-3 compliant in this version. 

If you did not re-activate the license prior to upgrade, the BIG-IP device prompts you to reboot, and then starts with FIPS mode disabled.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The mcpd process logs a message similar to the following example to the /var/log/ltm file indicating that it is FIPS 140-3 compliant but the FIPS mode is not FIPS 140-2 or FIPS 140-3 compliant:

    warning mcpd[7294]: 01071859:4: Warning generated : Changing HTTPd SSL-ciphersuite configuration from
    "ECDHE-RSA-AES128-GCM-SHA256:ECDHE-RSA-AES256-GCM-SHA384:ECDHE-RSA-AES128-SHA:ECDHE-RSA-AES256-SHA:ECDHE-RSA-AES128-SHA256:ECDHE-RSA-AES256-SHA384:
    ECDHE-ECDSA-AES128-GCM-SHA256:ECDHE-ECDSA-AES256-GCM-SHA384:ECDHE-ECDSA-AES128-SHA:ECDHE-ECDSA-AES256-SHA:ECDHE-ECDSA-AES128-SHA256:
    ECDHE-ECDSA-AES256-SHA384:AES128-GCM-SHA256:AES256-GCM-SHA384:AES128-SHA:AES256-SHA:AES128-SHA256:AES256-SHA256"
    to default FIPS-approved ciphersuites for FIPS 140-3 compliance.

  • If you did not re-activate the license prior to upgrade, the BIG-IP device prompts you to reboot, and then starts with FIPS mode disabled.

Resolution

Workaround

If you are upgrading a BIG-IP device that has a FIPS 140-2 compliant mode license applied, do not upgrade to BIG-IP 16.1.2.2. You should upgrade to BIG-IP 16.1.3 or later if your intention is to upgrade to the BIG-IP 16.x branch.
