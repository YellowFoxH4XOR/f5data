Description

You are looking to understand the limitations associated with the Packet Tester feature within the Application Firewall Manager (AFM). The Packet Tester is a valuable tool for verifying and troubleshooting firewall policies, but it is important to be aware of its constraints to use it effectively.

Environment

AFM (Application Firewall Manager) with Packet Tester feature

 

Cause

None

 

Recommended Actions

The following are known limitations of the Packet Tester feature in AFM:

  • Packets are injected after hardware processing, meaning that any drops caused by hardware features will not be tested. This includes hardware-accelerated DoS and hardware-based IP Intelligence enforcement.
  • Packets are injected after stateless packet filter rules have been applied. Therefore, any drops caused by this feature will not be tested.
  • Packets are injected after tcpdump processing. As a result, generated traffic will not be visible in tcpdump captures.
  • Only one packet can be injected at a time. Even when scripting TMSH (TMOS Shell) commands, this will only result in a low packet per second rate.
  • Packet types that can be tested are limited. Invalid packet type DoS (Denial of Service) vectors cannot be tested with the Packet Tester.

 

Additional Information

When using the Packet Tester, it is crucial to recognize these limitations to avoid misinterpretation of the results. This tool is designed for specific testing scenarios and may not cover all aspects of packet processing, especially those handled by the underlying hardware or prior to the packet reaching the Packet Tester's point of injection in the processing pipeline.
