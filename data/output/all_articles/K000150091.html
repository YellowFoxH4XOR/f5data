In this video, AskF5 shows you how to upgrade a pair of VIPRION Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP) host systems with a new version of BIG-IP software. It also demonstrates how to upgrade a vCMP guest high availability (HA) device group that is running on the vCMP host.

The demo uses BIG-IP 15.1.0 and BIG-IP 14.1.0.5
