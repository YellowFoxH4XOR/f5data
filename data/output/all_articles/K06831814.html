Known Issue

A translucent Virtual Local Area Network (VLAN) group may use an unexpected source MAC address.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have set up your BIG-IP system in a high availability (HA) configuration.
  • You have configured a VLAN group in Translucent mode.

Impact

In an HA configuration, switches in the network may have forwarding database (FDB) entries for the standby system that is assigned to the port of the active system.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When a VLAN group is configured in Translucent mode, IPv6 neighbor discovery packets that your BIG-IP system sends may have the locally unique bit flipped in the source MAC address.
