Description

Arrow indicators may not work properly to collapse and expand sections of the iRule.


For instance,



When you try to click the "down" arrow near the number 1, it may not collapse. 



Environment

  • GUI
  • iRule

Cause

This issue has been documented as ID1287769, and it could be caused by the iRule's regular expression.


Recommended Actions

None.
