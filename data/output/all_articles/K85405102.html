K85405102: The BIG-IP APM LocalDB users are not synced between the active and standby

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-04-06
Updated
2025-04-18
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP APM
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K85405102

Description

All or some previously existing BIG-IP APM LocalDB users are disappeared from the standby device after you performed the software upgrade.

Environment

  • Software upgrade
  • BIG-IP APM in HA
  • The BIG-IP APM LocalDB users are synced previously in the HA peer before the upgrade

Cause

Bug ID 761303: Upgrade of standby BIG-IP system results in empty Local Database

Recommended Actions

To work around this issue, you may need to trigger a full database dump from the active to standby, the detailed steps you may check on below.

After rebooting into the volume with the upgraded installation from standby, do the followings steps:

Note: The below steps may help the standby device to return the LocalDB users to its original synced state.

  • Force stop the localdbmgr daemon:
# bigstart stop localdbmgr

Note: You may not find any of the LocalDB users on your standby BIG-IP APM from the GUI during the time when the daemon was stopped.

  • Wait at least 15 minutes.

  • Restart the localdbmgr daemon:

Impact of procedure: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

# bigstart restart localdbmgr
