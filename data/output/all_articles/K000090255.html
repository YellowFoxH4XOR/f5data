Description

If your F5 product stops working as expected, and you are unable to find the help you need by searching on this site, you can create a support case to receive help from F5 Support.

You can watch the following video of the procedure in this article.

Prerequisites

You must have a MyF5 account to create a support case. For more information, refer to K000089224: Register for a MyF5 account.

Procedure

Create a support case using MyF5

If your F5 product stops working as expected, and you are unable to find the help you need by searching on this site, you can create a support case to receive help from F5 Support.

  1. On the Home page, select Case Management.
  2. Select Create new case.
  3. Indicate whether you are opening a case for an BIG-IP or NGINX product by selecting the appropriate button.

    Note: If you do not own BIG-IP products, the BIG-IP option does not display.

  4. In Product, select the product for which you are creating a case.
  5. In Version, select your product version.
  6. In Subject, provide a short description of your issue.

    Note: On the right, under Suggested solutions, MyF5 provides resources from its knowledge base that may help solve your issue. For more information, refer to About suggested solutions.

  7. In Description, provide the details about your issue.

    Note: On the right, under Suggested solutions, MyF5 provides resources from its knowledge base that may help solve your issue. For more information, refer to About suggested solutions.

  8. Enter your serial number or the last seven digits of your registration key.

    Note: MyF5 uses this information to check the level of support you have for your product. If the system does not recognize the number you entered, or you do not have the required level of support to open a support case, call F5 Support.

  9. Select Next.
  10. Complete the rest of the fields, noting the following:
    • Hostname is a required field for BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE).
      • In Assign a priority, choose one of the following:
        PriorityDescriptionSecurity issueInitial response
        1 —
        Site down
        • Conditions prevent high-level commerce or critical business activities, including remote access to critical applications.
        • Your F5 instance cannot power up, or it cannot pass traffic, affecting business-critical applications.
        		Critical business impact due to an attack or vulnerability.30 minutes (phone only)
        2—
        Site at risk
        • Conditions significantly impair business activities, including remote access to critical applications.
        • Conditions put your network or commerce at risk.
        		Severe business impact due to an attack, vulnerability, compliance, or data at risk.Two hours
        3—
        Performance degraded
        • Your F5 instance is degraded and may impact your traffic or business-critical applications.
        		Potential or partial business impact related to mitigation, audit results, or vulnerability.Four hours
        4—
        General assistance
        • General questions and troubleshooting for non-critical F5 instances.
        • Requests for features or solutions that are not currently part of your solution.
        		General security concerns not related to an immediate need.24 hours
    • Use Customer ticket number for your company's internal case number for this issue (optional).
    • Use Add alternate contact when you want to add a colleague who is not on your MyF5 account (optional).
  11. Select Submit.

    Your new case displays on the Case Management page.

About suggested solutions

When you create a new case, you describe your issue using the Subject and Description fields. As you are typing in those fields, on the other side of the screen, under Suggested solutions, MyF5 provides search results for resources in its knowledge base that match the information you are entering.

To help you solve your issue as quickly as possible, MyF5 displays these resources before you submit your case, because they may contain the information you need to resolve your issue immediately and on your own.

Search results under Suggested solutions have two icons:

  • Top icon—the content type, such as manual, video, non-diagnostic article, bug, or release note. Mouse over the icon to see the content type.
  • Bottom icon—the Quick View, which displays a preview of the page that you can use instead of opening the article in a new tab.
