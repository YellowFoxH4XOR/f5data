K23291085: F5 SSL Orchestrator may fail to process IMAPS traffic when the service chain includes ICAP service

Known Issue

F5 SSL Orchestrator may fail to process IMAPS traffic. This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • A service chain that includes an Internet Content Adaptation Protocol (ICAP) service is configured on SSL Orchestrator.
  • A TCP service chain classifier is configured to use this service chain for processing IMAPS traffic.

Impact

The system fails to process IMAPS traffic. As a result, the affected IMAPS client is unable to connect.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When you attempt to establish IMAPS connections through SSL Orchestrator, the IMAPS connections appear to hang until they are timed out.
  • If you enable debug logging on SSL Orchestrator, you observe that the system fails to correctly process and return IMAPS traffic that is forwarded to the service chain that contains an ICAP service.

Workaround

There is no work around for this issue. However, you can avoid this issue by creating a separate service chain with no ICAP service defined, and use this service chain specifically in the TCP service chain classifier for processing IMAPS traffic. For more information about creating service chains and classifiers, refer to the Creating Services Service Chains and Classifier Rules chapter of the F5 SSL Orchestrator: Setup manual.
