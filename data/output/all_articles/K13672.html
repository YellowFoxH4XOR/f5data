Known Issue

FirePass fails to properly mitigate hard disk or file system errors. FirePass logs file system errors to the console. However, the system fails to perform failover or reboot actions after encountering the errors.

Impact

The following events may occur during hard disk failure:

  • The FirePass controller may fail to service connections without warning
  • High availability functions may not perform as expected

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • The FirePass controller may exhibit unusual behavior without logging error
    messages

    For example, the following behaviors may occur without warning:

    • The system experiences random, unexplained crashes
    • The system becomes unresponsive
    • Users have issues saving or updating FirePass settings
  • The console may display EXT3-fs errors and does not perform a reboot or failover

Workaround

None.

If you have reason to suspect a failing hard disk, you may be able to mitigate this issue by running the fsck utility.
