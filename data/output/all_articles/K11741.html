Topic

Some of the early 1600 and 3600 platform chassis support rack mounting use only the provided stationary front mounting brackets (also referred to as ears). These early platforms do not support the use of rack mounted sliding rail kits. The receiver holes in the BIG-IP chassis do not align with the hole for the bolt that secures the sliding rail safety lock mechanism, and the safety lock cannot be installed. The safety lock ensures safe operation of the sliding rail by preventing it from extending beyond the end of the supporting rail.

Important: Do not install any system on a rack mounted sliding rail kit unless the entire kit is installed according to the manufacturer's recommendations, and the unmodified safety lock mechanism is securely in place. Operating the sliding rail without the intended locking mechanism in place may result in personal injury or damage to your BIG-IP system.

If you RMA a BIG-IP 1600 or 3600 platform, you will receive in return a platform that supports at least the same rack mounting options supported by the original system. In most cases, you will receive an RMA replacement platform that is identical to the original platform. In some cases, however, if you return an early platform that supports only front bracket rack mounting, you may receive a replacement platform that can be mounted either by the supplied front bracket or on a sliding rail.
