K47102031: Unable to open support case after VE subscription renewal

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-07-13
Updated
2025-02-06
Applies to
  • Virtual Editions
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IQ
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K47102031
Description 
You have recently renewed your F5 VE subscription and need to raise a support case. You are advised by support that you do not have valid support entitlement in place. 

Environment 
  • F5 VE Subscription renewal 
  • You have mapped your license keys from old to new subscription 
  • Unable to create a new support case 
Cause 
When you go through the renewal / license key mapping process, the new support contact is put in place at the same time. This can cause issues where the “start date” of the renewal is in the future, this would show in F5 system as no support contract in place 

Recommended Actions 
For urgent support issues, whilst speaking to a support engineer, please ask them to create (1 free call) and refer them to this solution,  
For non-urgent issues please reach out to your Customer Success manager, who can assist in opening a new case and ensuring you get access to an engineer.
