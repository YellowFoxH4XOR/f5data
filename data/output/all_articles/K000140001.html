K000140001: BIG-IQ HA issue after reboot

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-06-25
Updated
2024-06-25
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000140001

Description

Reboot active and standby BIG-IQ CM cause HA (High Availability) broken

  • active BIG-IQ CM shows Postgres replication slot is not found
  • active BIG-IQ CM HA Pair State shows Failed - Secondary is not actively streaming. Postgres may be down
  • standby BIG-IQ shows HA Pair State shows Failed - Secondary is not actively streaming. Postgres may be down


Environment

BIG-IQ


  • When try to reset BIG-IQ CM HA to standalone you may see "ERROR: Ipv4 chain bigiq_ha" doesn't exist and "ERROR: Ipv6 chain bigiq_ha" doesn't exist in /var/log/ha.log


Cause

Missing rules in /etc/sysconfig/iptables and /etc/sysconfig/ip6tables for either active or standby BIG-IQ CM



Recommended Actions

The actions or steps required to address the topic covered in the description.

  1.  Compare the iptables and ip6tables rules between active and standby BIG-IQ CM for tcp port 5432 , 443 and 22
  2. You may find missing rules in iptables and ip6tables for active BIG-IQ CM or standby BIG-IQ CM
  3. Update missing rules for /etc/sysconfig/iptables and /etc/sysconfig/ip6tables

Notes: if you found that iptables/ip6tables rules in standby BIG-IQ CM is more than active BIG-IQ CM, you may copy the iptables/ip6tables rules in standby BIG-IQ CM and upload to active BIG-IQ CM


Additional Information

You may stop the iptables/ip6tables services before replacing /etc/sysconfig/iptables and /etc/sysconfig/ip6tables


systemctl status iptables

systemctl stop iptables

systemctl start iptables



systemctl status ip6tables

systemctl stop ip6tables

systemctl start ip6tables


iptables -L

ip6tables -L
