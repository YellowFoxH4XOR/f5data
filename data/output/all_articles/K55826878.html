Known Issue

The connection stalls after the system issues HTTP::collect in the HTTP_REQUEST_DATA of the iRule.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • If the system runs, in HTTP_REQUEST/RESPONSE_DATA, iRule commands that result in parking, the system receives the final piece of body while parked, and if the system issues HTTP::collect before or after parking, either the connection stalls, or the collect has no effect and the system implicitly releases the request.

Impact

The iRule does not function as expected, resulting in either a stalled connection or incomplete collect results.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The client connection may stall and reset.
