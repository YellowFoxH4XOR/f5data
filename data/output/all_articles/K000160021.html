K000160021: How come IP Inteligence blocked category without an active IPI module being active.

Description

Traffic was blocked due to the IP Intelligence category without a valid IPI license.

Environment

  • ASM
  • IP Intelligence (IPI)

Cause

The IP Reputation database persisted on the system after the IPI license expired, allowing continued use of IPI-based blocking by iRules and policies. However, after a system upgrade the database was not carried over to the new boot location, causing IPI functionality to fail and resulting in errors.

Answer/Recommended Actions

Understand that IPI-based blocking may continue with an outdated database after license expiration until the system is upgraded or rebooted.

To restore IPI functionality, ensure a valid IPI license is installed and the database is downloaded after any upgrade or reboot.
