K5030: 3-DNS will not respond to requests until it receives an updated persistence table from the synchronization group

Known Issue

This may be the result of a known issue. If a 3-DNS Controller or Link Controller belongs to a sync group and uses persistence, it will not respond to requests until it obtains an updated persistence table from another member of the sync group. A member of a sync group that has been updated to a newer version may not be able to communicate with other members of the group that are running older versions. As a result, the upgraded 3-DNS Controller or Link Controller may wait interminably for an updated persistence table and will never answer requests.

F5 Networks Product Development is tracking this issue as CR26784. Monitor future 3-DNS or BIG-IP Link Controller Release Notes for the status of this CR.

Workaround

To work around this issue, remove the 3-DNS Controller or the Link Controller from its sync group before performing an upgrade. For instructions about how to remove a 3-DNS Controller or Link Controller from sync group, refer to K5027: Removing a 3-DNS Controller or BIG-IP Link Controller from a synchronization group.
