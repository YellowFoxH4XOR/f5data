Description:

This article introduces the compensation mechanism of static ratio load balance algorithm when pool member status changes.

Environment:

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • Static Ratio load balance method is used in the pool

Cause:

None

Recommended Actions:

None

Additional Information:

There are 2 internal properties that contributes to the decision of connection distribution, one is lb_value, another is bias_value.
When a new connection comes to the virtual server, tmm will assign the pool member that has the lowest lb_value.
The lb_value will be set to 0 when the ratio method is used.
The bias_value will be set once the ratio value is modified and the ratio method is used.
If the ratio value is 99:1, then the bias_value is 1:99.
After that, the delta of lb_value equals to the multiplier of the number of new connections that assigned to the specific pool member with the bias_value of that member.
No matter pool member is set force-offline or is marked down by monitor, the lb_value of that pool member will be frozen, until the member becomes up again.
When the member is marked up again, its lb_value will change, and use the lowest one in the other available members.
So generally speaking, the ratio of newly established connections that are distributed to all available members should just follow the setting and is not affected by the number of historical total connections.
