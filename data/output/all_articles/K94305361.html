Topic

This article explains the pay-as-you-go (PAYG) offerings for i11000-DS (i11400-DS, i11600-DS, and i11800-DS) series platforms.

Description

The BIG-IP i11000-DS series is an upgradable platform that offers three-tier PAYG licensing, including i11400-DS, i11600-DS, and i11800-DS. The assigned PAYG license defines the data-plane performance of the BIG-IP i11000-DS series. Typically, you achieve iSeries PAYG licensing by disallowing certain features from accessing hardware acceleration and instead having them process in the software of the ix600 model. In addition, the ix600 model throttles CPU speeds, which helps set the maximum performance metrics that distinguish the ix600 from the ix800 models.

The i11000-DS model has a three-tier PAYG offering by which you can start with the i11400-DS and, through license upgrades, unlock more performance on the same hardware platform by upgrading to an i11600-DS or i11800-DS license. Implementing the three-tier PAYG is different on the i11000-DS series than on other iSeries models. In addition to using the techniques previously described for other iSeries models, the i11000-DS series also disables some CPUs on the i11400-DS and i11600-DS models to obtain the levels of performance that distinguish the two models from each other. The published data sheet numbers are based on these newer PAYG strategies.

Another difference in the i11000-DS series is that it offers vCMP support across all three tiers of PAYG. Other iSeries models only offer vCMP on the ix800 model, and it is disabled on the ix600 model. Due to some CPU cores being disabled on the i11400-DS and i11600-DS models, you must take care when calculating vCMP resources because a portion of the overall CPUs are available for assignment to vCMP guests. For more information, refer to K14218: vCMP guest memory/CPU core allocation matrix.
