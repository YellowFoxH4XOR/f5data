Description
ARP entries not completed for one vCMP Guest, but they are working fine for other vCMP Guest.

The HSB (High Speed Bridge) is showing a huge amount of pauses.
When running the following tmctl command on the vCMP host:

tmctl -f /var/tmstat/blade/bcm56xxd switch_internal -s name,counters.rx_pause
name counters.rx_pause
