Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The current redundancy state may not be immediately updated in the Configuration utility when the BIG-IP system transitions from an active to standby failover state.

For example, if you click the Force to Standby button to make the current unit become the standby unit, failover occurs immediately; however, the applet that reflects the current redundancy state in the upper left corner of the Configuration utility may continue to display State: Active for up to 30 seconds. After 20 to 30 seconds the system updates the redundancy state to State: Standby.

Note: Due to architectural changes introduced beginning in BIG-IP 10.1.0, the Configuration utility update delay is up to 5 seconds.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as ID 334388 (formerly CR135719).
