Known Issue

The Configuration utility may display an incorrect BIG-IP APM synchronization status.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP APM system is configured with a Sync-Only device group that contains more than two devices.
  • Synchronization of the BIG-IP APM policy is initiated to the members of the device group using the Configuration utility.

When a device group member initiates a synchronization to peer members, the members receive the update and display that they are in synchronization. However, the device group member that initiates the synchronization does not correctly set the synchronization action as complete and continues to display that the synchronization has only been initiated.

Impact

The Configuration utility does not correctly display when the BIG-IP APM devices have completed synchronization.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H440734 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The device group member that initiates the synchronization displays the following symptoms in the Configuration utility:
    • The synchronization status located in the upper-left corner continually displays "Sync initiated" after executing a synchronization.
    • The Device Management > Overview screen displays "Initiated - Status Unavailable" for some device group members.
  • The receiving members of the BIG-IP APM synchronization device group display that they are "In Sync" after receiving and applying a synchronization from a peer device.

Workaround

There is no workaround for this issue. However, after the peer members receive the synchronization, you can clear the Configuration utility status message on the affected device by performing the following procedure:

Impact of procedure: Deleting the policy psync-data before the synchronization completes can cause out-of-sync devices to appear as synchronized.

  1. Log in to the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) by typing the following command:

    tmsh

  2. Verify that the peer members of the device group have received the updated configuration synchronization.
  3. Clear the BIG-IP APM policy synchronization status by typing the following commands:

    delete /apm policy psync-status all

    delete /apm policy psync-data all
