Topic

This article describes what you can do as a VPN user when experiencing connection issues with the BIG-IP Edge Client.

Description

Troubleshooting BIG-IP Edge Client connection issues as a VPN user is different from a BIG-IP APM administrator. As a VPN user, you only have access to your client (Windows, MAC, Linux) device and do not have access to the BIG-IP APM server.  Refer to the steps in this article to identify or narrow down any client side issues. By performing the first level of troubleshooting, you may be able to resolve the issue faster, before contacting your IT administrator.

Note: Every organization has its own unique IT policy which translates to a unique access policy configured on the BIG-IP APM. When an issue persist, you should contact your IT administrator for further assistance.

Procedures

Perform the following procedures to troubleshoot BIG-IP Edge Client connection issues:

ProcedureDescription
Review the (error) messages on the BIG-IP Edge Client console

The following lists some common errors and causes:

  • Waiting to Connect to Server: There are network issues. Verify your client's network connections.
  • Failed to initialize local Tunnel Server: There are issues on the client device ranging from Operating System component errors, network port conflict issues due to third party software, BIG-IP Edge Client installation issues. Rebooting the client device, reinstalling the faulty OS component, uninstalling the third party software, or reinstalling BIG-IP Edge Client may resolve this issue.
  • Failed Validation: When the security software installed on the client system does not meet the security policy requirements configured on the BIG-IP APM system, the VPN establishment will fail. 
  • Access was denied by the access policy. This may be due to a failure to meet access policy requirements: Your user account or client device does not meet the requirements set out by your organization. These may include but not limited to unpatched OS, unauthorized software installed and so on. For more information, refer to K65142400: Error Message: Access was denied by the access policy.

For a full list of error messages, refer to K95925417: Error messages when connecting to BIG-IP APM Network Access VPN.

Verify if the issue is local to your client device or user account by connecting from another client device if possible or checking with other users if they are facing the same issue.

When the issue is isolated to your client device, follow the subsequent procedures in this table to identify the root cause.

Recall the last change you made on your device. In some cases, installing a new software may render your device not compliant to your organizations policy. Revert the change and attempt connection again.

Verify Network connections

You can perform basic network troubleshooting to ensure that your client device is connected to the internet and to the BIG-IP APM server.

  • BIG-IP Edge Client provides network information on your client device. On the BIG-IP Edge Client console, select View Details and select Connection Details, Routing Table, IP Configuration, Miscellaneous as appropriate.
  • Verify that you can access other online websites
Verify third party softwareThird party network (firewalls), security (AV) and other third party VPN software can interfere with VPN connections. Review these software installed on your client device.
Verify operating system files and driversEstablishing VPN requires operating system components and drivers. When these fail, BIG-IP Edge Client may remain in Initialization state. Reinstalling the faulty OS components, rebooting the device, or in some cases, depending on the severity, re-imaging the operating system may be required. 
Change the target BIG-IP APM server on Edge ClientYour IT organization may provide a list of VPN servers you can connect to. Attempt to connect to a different one to isolate the issue. On the BIG-IP Edge Client console, select Change Server. then select a different server.
Verify SSL connection errorsWhen you encounter SSL errors, such as The SSL certificate contains errors, note K000133271: BIG-IP Edge Client prevents connection to BIG-IP APM servers with untrusted SSL certificates. 

This can happen if you manually change the target BIG-IP APM server to an IP address or server name that is not configured by your IT department. Consult your IT administrator for more information.

Attempt to connect from the browser as a workaroundIn some cases, such as BIG-IP Edge Client installation issues, you may be able to successfully connect through a browser instead. When you do so, required VPN components may be installed successfully. Reboot your client device before attempting to connect through BIG-IP Edge Client again.
Advanced troubleshooting
Review logs

Review the following logs on your client device for more information:

  • Operating system logs: In Windows refer to Event viewer. 
  • BIG-IP Edge Client logs: On the BIG-IP Edge Client console, select Diagnostics Report and View Logs.

For more information, refer to K00819308: Gathering F5 VPN client logs and K12321: Enabling Network Access debugging for Mac OS X and Linux.
