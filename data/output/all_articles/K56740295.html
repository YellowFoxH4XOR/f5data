Description
From 12.0.0, BIG-IP DNS systems allow configuring SOA Negative Caching TTL setting for wide IPs. As a result, the same zone may return different SOA negative caching TTLs from the perspective of DNS resolvers. The SOA negative caching TTL value in the BIND zone needs to be consistent across this BIND zone and all related wide IPs.

Environment

  • With at least a BIND zone has been created with SOA record in place, BIG-IP systems allow wide IPs to be created with respective SOA Negative Caching TTLs.

Cause
If SOA negative caching TTLs in BIND zone and wide IPs are inconsistent, with queries to wide IPs and those names not present in wide IPs, the negative caching TTL on resolvers will be overwritten quite frequently and they may not follow what has been specified by SOA record in BIND zone or wide IPs.

Recommended Actions

SOA negative caching TTLs in BIND configuration and related wide IPs need to be consistent.
