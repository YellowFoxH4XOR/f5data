Known Issue

On rare occasions, the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may restart when Address Resolution Protocol (ARP) cache nexthop objects experience a double free error.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP configuration includes one or more standard virtual servers with an associated TCP or User Datagram Protocol (UDP) profile.
  • Your BIG-IP system receives a new connection request from a remote client.
  • Your BIG-IP system attempts to create an associated server-side connection.
  • The response to the new server-side connection attempt is delayed.

Impact

The BIG-IP system fails to process traffic while TMM restarts.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP system fails to process traffic for a brief period.
  • Your BIG-IP system fails over to another host in the device group.
  • TMM produces a core file in the /var/core directory.
