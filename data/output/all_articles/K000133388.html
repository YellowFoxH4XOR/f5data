Description

Is ca-bundle involved with ASM signature update?

Deleting Root Certificates out of ca-bundle has an impact on automatic ASM Signature updates and license/update checks.

 

Environment

  • BIG-IP

 

Cause

The ca-bundle is the collection of root certificates that are used to verify TLS certificates presented by websites.
Deleting root certificates out of the ca-bundle will prevent the BIG-IP from validating certificates that are presented to it when connecting to other sites over TLS.

 

Recommended Actions

You can download the latest ca-bundle from the MyF5 Downloads portal. On MyF5 Downloads, set the Group to Certificate Authority Bundle to find and download blended-bundle.crt.
