Description

This article describes the actions to wipe all BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) configuration and restore the BIG-IP to default factory settings or decommission the virtual device.

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • Virtual Edition (VE)
  • Wipe all configuration/settings
  • Decommission of sites or devices

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

  1. Backup the UCS and Master Key in case there's a requirement to restore the same configuration on another device.
    1. To perform a UCS backup refer to K13132: Backing up and restoring BIG-IP configuration files with a UCS archive.
    2. To get the BIG-IP Master Key, refer to The f5mku utility: Reference.
  2. Choose one of the following steps:
    1. To restore the BIG-IP VE to factory default settings, refer to K13127: Restoring the BIG-IP configuration to factory default settings.
    2. To complete a BIG-IP VE decommissioning process, remove the virtual appliance from the VM/KVM host. Refer to the respective vendor's documentation for instructions on removing VM/KVM guests.
