Known Issue

VIPRION systems may log extraneous stpd messages.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • Your VIPRION chassis has one or more empty slots.

Impact

The specific error messages are benign, and should not impact system performance.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The BIG-IP system repeatedly logs error messages to the /var/log/ltm file that appear similar to the following example:

    err stpd[7287]: 01290003:3: Attempt to send 15 bytes to inettcp://127.3.0.4:4401 timed out after 10 seconds
    err stpd[7287]: 01290003:3: Attempt to send 15 bytes to inettcp://127.3.0.5:4401 timed out after 10 seconds
    err stpd[7287]: 01290003:3: Attempt to send 15 bytes to inettcp://127.3.0.6:4401 timed out after 10 seconds
    err stpd[7287]: 01290003:3: Attempt to send 15 bytes to inettcp://127.3.0.7:4401 timed out after 10 seconds
