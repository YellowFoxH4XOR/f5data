Topic

Hardware hard-wired failover is not supported on the WebAccelerator 4500 platform. Network failover must be used when configuring WebAccelerator 4500 systems as a redundant pair.

For information about configuring network failover, refer to the Configuring High Availability chapter of the TMOS Management Guide for BIG-IP Systems.
