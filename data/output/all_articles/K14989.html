Known Issue

When using an iRule with the POLICY::targets and/or POLICY::controls commands with an unsupported or mistyped parameter, the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may produce a core file.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You use POLICY::targets or POLICY::controls commands with an unsupported parameter.

Impact

TMM may produce a core file, temporarily fail to process traffic, and fail over to another device in the device group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Your BIG-IP system sends a SIGSEGV signal to TMM, resulting in a stack trace and TMM core file in the /shared/core directory.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can make sure that invalid parameters are not passed to POLICY::targets and POLICY::controls iRules commands.

Impact of workaround: The following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

Supported parameters for the POLICY::controls and POLICY:targets commands include:

POLICY::controls [ acceleration | asm | avr | caching | classification | compression | forwarding | l7dos | request-adaptation | response-adaptation | server-ssl ]

POLICY::targets [ wam | asm | http-cookie | http-header | http-host | http-referer | http-set-cookie | http-uri | log | tcl | tcp-nagle ]
