Environment

  • rSeries tenant
  • Restart or reboot

Cause

Tenant is rebooting repeatedly due to VLAN failsafe action, as shown by messages similar to the following in /var/log/ltm:

warning overdog[pid]: 01140029:4: HA vlan_fs /Common/Internal fails action is reboot.
crit overdog[pid]: 01140102:2: Overdog daemon requests reboot.

This is expected behaviour if VLAN failsafe is enabled and there is no layer 2 connectivity in the VLAN in question.

Recommended Actions

To prevent the tenant from rebooting constantly, you can disable VLAN failsafe for the VLAN(s) in question.

Refer to K13297 for more information about VLAN failsafe:

  • K13297: Overview of VLAN failsafe

You may need to investigate why VLAN failsafe is being triggered:

  • Is this expected?
  • Are any other systems able to respond in the VLAN?
  • Are interfaces up?

Further troubleshooting may be required once the tenant has stabilised.

Additional Information

When a tenant is experiencing reboot issues and is found to be in a long-running Running state from F5OS, this typically indicates a problem within the tenant that is independent of F5OS.
