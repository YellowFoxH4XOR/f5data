You can find the serial number on the barcode sticker on the FirePass controller or view it from the Administrative Console. The serial number begins with the letters bip and ends with the letter s.

Note: Some older versions of FirePass may have the original uRoam serial number. This number should be located in the same place on the FirePass controller, but is a six or seven digit number that starts with a 000. No letters are included in the original uRoam serial numbers.

Serial number barcode sticker

For models 4100 and 4300, the white serial number barcode sticker is located on the lower right, front corner of the system, below the LCD panel. On earlier versions, the barcode sticker is located on the right side of the case next to the rack mount bracket.

For model 1200, the white serial number barcode sticker is located on the lower left, front corner of the system.

For model 1000, the white serial number barcode sticker is located on the lower left, front corner of the system.

For model 600, the white serial number barcode sticker is located on the lower right, front corner of the system.

For model 4000, the white serial number barcode sticker is located on the lower left, front corner of the system.

For the original uRoam model, the white serial number barcode sticker is located on the back of the system.

Administrative Console

To view your serial number in the Administrative Console, perform one of the following two procedures based on the version of your FirePass controller:

FirePass versions 5.x, 6.x, and 7.x

  1. Log in to the FirePass Administrative Console using the admin account or an account with administrator privileges.
  2. From the navigation menu on the left, click Device Management.
  3. From the navigation menu on the left, click Current Settings.

    The serial number is located in the Serial Number field in the FirePass Version section of the page.

FirePass versions prior to 5.0

  1. Log in to the FirePass Administrative Console using the admin account or an account with administrator privileges.
  2. From the navigation menu on the left, click Server.
  3. From the navigation menu on the left, click Settings.

    The serial number is located in the Serial Number field.
