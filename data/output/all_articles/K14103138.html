Description

Would like to regenerate the LTM device certificate that is presented when connecting to the GUI.

Will this affect LTM Device Trust, ConfigSync, or Failover functionality?

Environment

  • Device certificate
  • GUI
  • ConfigSync

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

None

Additional Information

Renewing or replacing the device certificate will not affect LTM HA/DSC functionality; separate DSC device trust certificates are used for this, which have no direct relation to the device certificate that is presented while accessing the management IP/port.

However, regenerating the device certificate does affect DNS synchronization and iQuery communication.

For more information, refer to:

  • K15664: Overview of BIG-IP device certificates
