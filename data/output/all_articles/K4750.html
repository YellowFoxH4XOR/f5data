Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When SYN cookie processing is active, the number of FTP connections that may be established is severely limited.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR50613 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 4.5.13. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP Release Notes.
