K1578: Identifying the power supply revision installed in a BIG-IP 5000 or BIG-IP 2000 switch appliance

Topic

The revision number is located on the back or the top of the power supply.

Power supplies may be unmarked or marked with Rev 1, Rev 2, or Rev A.

Unmarked power supplies are revision 1.
