Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may fail to load the configuration and become unstable after creating an SNMP community.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • An SNMP community is created in a partition other than the /Common partition and the system is subsequently rebooted.

Note: This issue is more likely to occur with bladed systems and/or Clustered Multiprocessing (CMP) configurations.

Impact

The BIG-IP system does not load the configuration and is unstable after a reboot.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • After creating an SNMP community in a partition other than /Common, the BIG-IP system fails to load the configuration and becomes unstable after a reboot.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can remove the affected SNMP community from the non-Common partition.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
