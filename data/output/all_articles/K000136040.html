F5 will no longer offer a BiDi solution for Bourne platforms and VIPRION B2250/B4450, with the current inventory of 40G BiDi (F5-UPG-VEL-QSFP+BD and F5-UPG-QSFP+BD) expected to be fully depleted by February 1, 2024, based on historical demand. F5 cannot guarantee all orders can be fulfilled due to the fixed number of optics available. After February 1, 2024 (or after we run out of stock), customers must consider other optics technologies.

  • For VELOS, the F5-UPG-VEL-QSFP+BD can be replaced with F5-UPG-VELQSFP28SRBD*.
  • For iSeries and rSeries, the F5-UPG-QSFP+BD can be replaced with F5-UPG-QSFP28-SRBD*.

*F5-UPG-VELQSFP28SRBD and F5-UPG-QSFP28-SRBD are dual rate 40G/100G BiDi optics compatible with existing 40G BiDi transceivers. “Dual rate” in this technology refers to the fact that it can support 40G and 100G data rates. The default mode is 40G, which can be switched to 100G through device configuration.
