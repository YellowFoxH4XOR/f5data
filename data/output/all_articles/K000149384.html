Environment

  • F5OS
  • Tenant
  • platform_agent logs

Cause

None, this is expected behavior and those logs are for informational only.

Recommended Actions

Since those logs were informational only, it could be safely ignored. It would be helpful that those logs could occur when:

  • The tenant starts up
  • The tenant associated VLAN has been changed
  • There is a status change on any underlayer interface or LAG
