Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The big3d process does not honor the LDNS probe queue limit, causing the big3d process to consume too much system memory when processing a high number of LDNS probe requests. As a result, the big3d process crashes in some circumstances.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR67749, CR67750, and CR69155, and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.2.4, 9.3, and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM and LTM Release Notes.
