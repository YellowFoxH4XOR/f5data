K000141238: Unable to upload to identity.account.f5.com

Type
Knowledge
Published
2024-09-29
Updated
2026-01-10
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 8.0.0, 8.X.X, 8.4.X, 8.4.0, 8.3.X, 8.3.0, 8.2.X, 8.2.0, 8.1.X, 8.1.0, 8.0.X
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000141238

Description

When you are trying to submit the report or upload a qkview directly from the BIG IQ you can face the following error:

curl: (35) Unknown SSL protocol error in connection to identity.account.f5.com:443

Environment

  • BIG IQ

Cause

Required URLs might be blocked by the local firewall.

Recommended Actions 

Ensure the following URLs are allowed on the firewall:

  • ihealth-api.f5.com
  • ihealth2-api.f5.com
  • identity.account.f5.com
  • api.f5.com

Additional Information

None
