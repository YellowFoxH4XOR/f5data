Known Issue

You may be unable to highlight and copy the session ID of an active session.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system is actively processing access sessions.
  • Using the Configuration utility, you navigate to Access Policy > Manage Sessions.
  • In the Total Active Sessions list, you attempt to highlight and copy a Session ID.

Impact

You cannot highlight the Session ID.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You are unable to copy or highlight a session ID.
