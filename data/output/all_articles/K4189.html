Topic

The IP Classifier database (/usr/local/3dns/include/netIana.inc) that is used in 3-DNS versions 3.0 through 4.6.2 is more than two years old. While still useful, the locations of many addresses have changed.

The IP Classifier database that is used in 3-DNS version 4.6.3 was generated in December, 2004 and contains significantly more accurate information.

F5 Networks recommends that all customers who use the non-customized topology entries extensively, upgrade to 3-DNS version 4.6.3 or later to take advantage of the updated database.

For information about upgrading, refer to the 3-DNS Controller Release Notes.
