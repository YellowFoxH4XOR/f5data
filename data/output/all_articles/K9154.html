Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP system continues to send monitor traffic to nodes or pool members after they are forced offline by an administrator. Instead, the BIG-IP system should temporarily stop sending monitor traffic when a pool member or node address is forced offline, and resume health checks to the node or pool member when it is enabled.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as ID 215109 (formerly CR103321), and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 10.0.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.

This issue still exists in the BIG-IP LTM 9.x software branch.

Note: For more information about the behavior change in BIG-IP 10.x, refer to K12197: The BIG-IP system no longer sends monitor traffic to nodes or pool members with a status of Forced Offline or Down.
