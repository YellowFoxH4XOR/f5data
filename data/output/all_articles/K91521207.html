K91521207: Unable to see capacity of second disk on VE install of Big IP or Big IQ

Type
Support Solution
Published
2021-10-12
Updated
2025-02-07
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IP
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K91521207

Description

When installing BIG-IP or BIG-IQ in a virtual environment, one may create the virtual machine with more than one disk.  However, after booting and configuring the virtual device, only one disk is visible.

Environment

  • Big IP VE
  • Big IQ VE
  • Any supported virtualization hypervisor (e.g., ESXi, HyperV, etc.)

Cause

This is expected behavior.  F5's products do not recognize more than one disk.  

Recommended Actions

Instead of adding a second disk to the virtual machine, you should expand the first disk as per your needs.  Precise steps on how to do this are dependent on your hypervisor of choice.
