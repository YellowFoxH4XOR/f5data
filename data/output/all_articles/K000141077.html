Description

When exporting and importing a security policy on a bigip system the learning suggestions are not retained. This means that the imported policy will not show any learning data.

Environment

  • Security Policy Learning Suggestions

Cause

This behavior is by design, Learning suggestions are specific to the policy and the bigip unit and cannot be migrated/imported.

Recommended Actions

Finish building the Security Policy and then export it for use on another bigip.
