Issue

In ARX versions prior to 3.2.2, the threshold for the probuf nsmResourceThreshold propkt resource was 80 percent. Beginning in ARX 3.2.2, 4.1.1, and 5.0.0, the threshold for the propkt resource was increased to 100 percent.

Usage in excess of 80 percent for the propkt resource is not unusual or indicative of an issue. However, the lower threshold caused frequent nsmResourceThreshold traps and emails similar to the following:

Traplog

- nsmResourceThreshold (415)  : resource:propkt  threshold:80 slot:4 proc:4
- nsmResourceThresholdClear (416)  : resource:propkt  threshold:90 slot:4 proc:4

Email

Subject: ::trap id:415(nsm-resource-threshold)::cn:::switch:arx1-sea::loc:::desc:ARX-6000

Software Version: Version 3.02.001.10856 (Aug 26 2008 17:52:56) [nbuilds]
Chassis Serial Number: 0208080000

Group:       chassis
Event:       nsm-resource-threshold (ID: 415)
Total Events:1

Time(UTC):   2009-06-25T 23:15:11.615

Events:
 Fastpath resource is at 80%% utilization for propkt  resource on core 4.4 Name: propkt

F5 Product Development tracked this change as ARX ID 32816.
