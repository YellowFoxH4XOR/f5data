Description

This article describes impact on performing license reactivation done on BIG-IQ for:

  • Pool License
  • ELA License
  • BIG-IP License

 

Environment

  • BIG-IQ CM or License Manager
  • Licensing BIG-IP and reactivating license

 

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

Reactivating license on BIG-IQ does not have impact on BIG-IPs until Refresh button is pushed for specific BIG-IP.

At the time when Refresh was pushed, BIG-IQ is refreshing license on specific BIG-IP and updating service check-date with configuration reload.

Note: Refresh process is traffic impacting
