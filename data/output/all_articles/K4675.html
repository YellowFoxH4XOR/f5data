Topic

The FirePass controller has been certified by RSA for native SecurID authentication and SecurID authentication through RADIUS.

For information about how to configure FirePass to work with RADIUS and RSA SecurID Authentication, refer to the RSA SecurID Ready Implementation Guide or the FirePass Controller Administrator Guide.
