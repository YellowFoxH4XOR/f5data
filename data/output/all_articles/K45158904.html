K45158904: BIG-IQ error message: ''Status of HA Certificate on this device has changed from EXPIRING SOON to EXPIRED'' or ''VALID to EXPIRING SOON''

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-10-25
Updated
2025-04-25
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K45158904

Description

You may receive e-mail notification or log warning in /var/log/restjavad.1.log that:

  • Status of HA Certificate on this device has changed from EXPIRING SOON to EXPIRED. Please renew the certificate as per guidelines.
  • Status of HA Certificate on this device has changed from VALID to EXPIRING SOON. Please renew the certificate as per guidelines within 30 days

Environment

  • BIG-IQ system in standalone or HA mode
  • Any version where HA certificate is present
  • HA certificate is expired or expiring soon
  • BIG-IQ managing one or more BIG-IP devices

Cause

This message is generated when the internal HA certificate, stored at /var/lib/pgsql/config/server.crt, is expired or expiring soon. This certificate is used for configuration database replication between BIG-IQ systems. In standalone deployments, the certificate is not actively used.
 

Recommended Actions

  • You can safely ignore this message. The HA certificate warning does not impact production traffic, high availability, or the management of any BIG-IP devices managed by BIG-IQ. It is only related to internal BIG-IQ database replication, and certificate expiration is not checked during replication.
  • In standalone BIG-IQ deployments, the HA certificate is not used for any operational purpose. The warning can be ignored in this scenario.
  • This HA certificate is unrelated to the device trust certificates used by BIG-IP devices for HA and synchronization. No action is required on managed BIG-IP devices in response to this warning.

If you prefer to remove the warning by updating the expired certificate, follow these steps:

  1. Check the certificate status: 
    openssl x509 -in /var/lib/pgsql/config/server.crt -text -noout
  2. Renew the HA certificate: 
    ha_generate_certs -f <local_discovery_IP>
    Replace <local_discovery_IP> with the appropriate local discovery IP address.

Important:
If your BIG-IQ is configured in HA mode, renewing the certificate may break the HA configuration. In this case, you will need to re-establish HA between the affected systems:

  1. On the primary unit, go to BIG-IQ HA and click "Remove Standby."
  2. On both the secondary and primary units, run: 
    ha_reset <local_discovery_IP>
  3.  Pair the units again by adding the standby BIG-IQ HA option.
