Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may fail to send log messages for form-based client-initiated SSO and SAML SSO.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP APM system is configured for SAML SSO or for form-based client initiated SSO.
  • The BIG-IP APM system processes either SAML SSO or form-based client initiated SSO traffic.

Impact

SSOv2 log messages are missing, which makes troubleshooting difficult.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Log messages are not written in /var/log/apm for client-initiated form-based or SAML SSO.
