Known Issue

Dashboard line charts contain too many annotations.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You read a line chart with a time period other than the last five minutes.

Impact

Users may find it difficult to read dashboard line charts.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The dashboard lines charts are difficult to read because there are many annotations; red-filled circles on the bottom part of the diagram.

Resolution
