Known Issue

The Enterprise Manager system may fail to generate an alert for managed devices that are not synchronized. This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The affected managed devices are running BIG-IP 11.x and later
  • The affected managed devices are configured in a device group
  • An initial ConfigSync operation was previously performed between the affected managed devices

Impact

The ConfigSync status of a managed device can not be determined from the Enterprise Manager.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The Enterprise Manager generates an alert when the configuration between the managed devices is not synchronized for the first time. However, the Enterprise Manager system fails to generate an alert for subsequent instances when the configuration is not synchronized.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can verify the ConfigSync status on the affected managed devices. To do so, perform one of the following procedures:

Impact of workaround: None.

Verifying the ConfigSync status (BIG-IP 11.2.1 and later)

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility of the affected managed device.
  2. Click Device Management.
  3. Click Overview.

    You can now view the ConfigSync status between members of the device group.

Verifying the ConfigSync status (BIG-IP 11.0.0 - 11.2.0)

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility of the affected managed device.
  2. Click Device Management.
  3. Click Device Groups.
  4. Click Device Group List.
  5. Click the affected device group name.
  6. Click the ConfigSync tab.

    You can now view the ConfigSync status between members of the device group.
