Known Issue

New TCP connections to FastL4 virtual servers and self IP addresses may fail when SYN cookie protection is activated.

This issue occurs when the following condition has been met:

  • The Software SYN Cookie Protection and Generate Internal Sequence Numbers options are enabled in a FastL4 profile.
  • The SYN Check Activation Threshold (default threshold of 16384) has been reached and SYN cookie protection is activated.

After the three-way TCP handshake establishes, the virtual server sends invalid TCP sequence numbers to the client. As a result, the client ignores the TCP packets from the BIG-IP system and the connection eventually times out.

Impact

  • Connections to virtuals or self IP addresses fail when SYN cookie protection has been activated.
  • For BIG-IP 10.1.0 and later, new management traffic connections to self IP addresses may fail when SYN cookie protection has activated.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H383269 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • After the three-way TCP handshake establishes, the BIG-IP system sends invalid TCP sequence numbers to the client. As a result, the client ignores the TCP packets from the BIG-IP system and the connection eventually times out.
  • This issue does not affect failover or mirroring traffic exchanged between BIG-IP redundant pairs; however, this issue may affect ConfigSync operations.
