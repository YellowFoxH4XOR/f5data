Known Issue

Inserting a blade into a VIPRION chassis may overwrite the configuration and cause a software upgrade/downgrade.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The primary blade in a VIPRION cluster is disabled.
  • No other blades in the VIPRION cluster are enabled.

If the primary member in a cluster is disabled, and you insert a new or replacement blade, the newly inserted blade becomes the enabled primary member and subsequently overwrites the remaining blades in the cluster with the installed software version and configuration data of the new or replacement blade.

Impact

The initial VIPRION cluster configuration and software version are overwritten by the newly inserted blade.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • After inserting a new blade in the VIPRION cluster, you notice that the configuration has been modified.
  • After inserting a new blade in the VIPRION cluster, you notice that the BIG-IP software version has changed.

Workaround

There is no workaround for this issue; however, you can prevent the issue by ensuring that the primary member in the VIPRION cluster is not disabled before inserting a new blade.
