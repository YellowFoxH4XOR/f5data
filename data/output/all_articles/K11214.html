Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When an OWS responds with a no-transform directive in the Cache-Control header, and the matching response node's Client Cache setting on the Lifetime page in the policy is set to Do not change or with a Maximum Age value, then the Cache-Control header in the response sent to the client will contain two no-transform directives.

For example:

HTTP/1.1 200 OK
Server: Apache/2.2.3 (CentOS)
Content-Type: text/html; charset=UTF-8
Date: Wed, 09 Dec 2009 19:15:01 GMT
Content-Length: 176
ETag: "pv575a9d224061ef5f3dc94428d310dca1"
Last-Modified: Wed, 09 Dec 2009 18:27:40 GMT
Cache-Control: private, no-transform, no-transform            
X-WAM-RC-MASQ: On
X-PvInfo: [S10201.C11048.A7276.RA0.G0.U3590B6FE].[OT/html.OG/pages]
Connection: Keep-Alive
Vary: Accept-Encoding
Accept-Ranges: none

This issue is cosmetic and does not affect BIG-IP WebAccelerator functionality or client behavior.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as ID 243220 (formerly CR133767 and CR136012) and it was fixed in BIG-IP 10.2.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP WebAccelerator release notes.

Note: Cache-Control handling was rewritten in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.5-HF2. In BIG-IP versions prior to Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.5-HF2, the no-transform directive is dropped from the response.
