Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may drop bursty egress traffic more aggressively than expected.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system processes high traffic that stresses the Memory Management Unit (MMU) memory.
  • You have one of the following BIG-IP platforms:
    • BIG-IP 5000 or 7000 series
    • VIPRION B2100/2150 or B4200 blades
Note: The MMU manages the internal/external packet buffering for the switch and schedules the packet for transmission.

Impact

The BIG-IP system drops packets more aggressively on busy ports.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The tmsh show /net interface command output displays a high drop count in interface statistics.
