Policy Information

F5 is simplifying licensing for the BIG-IP AAM system, and is introducing a single upgrade SKU for the BIG-IP AAM system across all BIG-IP appliance platforms, in addition to the Good, Better, Best (GBB) licensing. For more information about the BIG-IP AAM End of Software Development (EoSD) announcement, refer to K70113407: End of Software Development for BIG-IP AAM.

Effective October 1, 2015 the following licensing will change:

  • BIG-IP AAM add-on modules change from a per-platform SKU to a single SKU across all platforms.
  • ADC SKUs are eliminated in favor of GBB licensing.
  • VIPRION AAM add-on modules continue on a per-chassis platform basis.
  • BIG-IP AAM standalone SKUs are eliminated, with the exception of the BIG-IP 2000 series appliance and the Virtual Edition.

The following SKU will be introduced to upgrade any BIG-IP appliance platform to BIG-IP AAM, with the exception of the BIG-IP 2000 series. This is the recommended replacement for the discontinued BIG-IP AAM module SKUs.

Dates for EoSD, EoSCR, EoRMA, and EoTS will follow the dates for each associated platform.

Technical Support

F5 maintains generous lifecycle policies that allow customers to enjoy many years of both support and new software releases. Customers whose platforms are designated EoL and who are currently under maintenance contracts will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the Service Contract Renewal date expires.
