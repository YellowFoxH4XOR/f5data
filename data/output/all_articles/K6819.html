K6819: BIG-IP GTM persistence entries are not synchronized to the peer if the peer is taken offline and is then brought online again

Known Issue

This is the result of known issue. When persistence is enabled within a synchronized pair of BIG-IP GTM units, both units communicate their persistence records to the other unit on a constant basis. If one unit within the pair is taken down for maintenance or brought down by a problem, the active BIG-IP GTM system is handling and recording LDNS requests in its persistence table during the period of down time. When the peer unit is brought back online, the persistence records are not synchronized from the active unit that had not gone down to the newly active unit.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR70934 and CR71019, and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.2.5, 9.3, and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM Release Notes.
