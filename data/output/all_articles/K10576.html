Topic

After you configure FirePass to proxy ActiveSync connections, users can configure their ActiveSync software to connect to the Exchange server through FirePass. To do so, users must enter the FirePass hostname in the Server field instead of entering the Exchange server hostname when configuring ActiveSync on an iPhone.

Note: For instructions about configuring FirePass to proxy ActiveSync connections, refer to K7812: Configuring FirePass to proxy ActiveSync.

Note: Detailed instructions for configuring ActiveSync on the iPhone are located at the following location. The following link will take you to a resource outside of AskF5, and it is possible that the document may be removed without our knowledge:

http://support.apple.com/kb/HT2480

Note: The instructions also apply to iPhone version 3.x software.

In Step 3, enter the FirePass hostname in the Server field instead of the Exchange server hostname. If you are unsure of what to enter in the other fields, such as the Domain and Username fields, contact your Exchange administrator.
