Description

If your existing BIG-IP Advanced WAF system is licensed with a Threat Campaigns subscription, the System > License page from the BIG-IP Configuration utility will indicate the expiration date of the Threat Campaigns subscription while the show sys license command from the tmsh utility will list the Threat Campaigns subscription under the Time Limited Modules with an expiration date.

If you subsequently activate add-on Threat Campaigns subscription license on the same BIG-IP Advanced WAF system, the system will add to the existing expiration date of the Threat Campaigns subscription. For example, if the expiration date of the Threat Campaigns subscription on your existing BIG-IP Advanced WAF system is 14 August 2027 and you subsequently activated 3 units of the 1 year Threat Campaigns subscription add-on license on this system, the new expiration date for the Threat Campaigns subscription will be 17 August 2030.

Note: The sequence of add-on license activation does not affect the new expiration date. In this example, you can activate 3 units of the 1 year Threat Campaigns subscription add-on license at different times or you can activate all 3 units together at the same time, the expiration date will still be 17 August 2030.

Recommended Actions

Note that if your BIG-IP Advanced WAF systems are in a high availability (HA) configuration, you must obtain a unique add-on license for each device. You can activate the Threat Campaigns subscription add-on license using the automatic or manual activation methods. For information on the procedures to activate add-on license on the BIG-IP system, refer to the Activating an add-on module section of the K7752: Licensing the BIG-IP system.
