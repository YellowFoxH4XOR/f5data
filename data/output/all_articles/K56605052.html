Description

You may see logs similar to the following in /var/log/urldb-trace.log

2020-11-09 05:38:24; THREAD: 84646700; ERROR; WsFileMutexLock: Failed to lock mutex. Mutex file /var/urldb/rtu/710919/websense.ndx.710919.byte.chk already exists.

Environment

  • SSLO/SWG

Cause

These logs are due to failure to load the master database.

Recommended Actions

These logs shouldn't be of great concern as they are not indicative of a problem. Also most of the time, we see there will be retries and typically the master DB gets reloaded successfully.
