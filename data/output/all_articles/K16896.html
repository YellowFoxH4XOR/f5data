Known Issue

BIG-IP 2000 and 4000 series platforms do not support Stream Control Transmission Protocol (SCTP).

Impact

The BIG-IP system cannot load balance client connections that use SCTP.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Client connections that use SCTP to create new connections are failing.
  • Error messages appear in the /var/log/ltm file, similar to the following example:

    crit tmm2[6789]: 01010201:2: Inet port exhaustion on 192.168.10.1 to 192.168.10.100:3456 (proto 132)
