Topic

The BIG-IP system includes the following routing protocols through the use of ZebOS:

  • BGP4
  • BGP4+
  • IS-IS
  • OSPF2
  • OSPF3
  • RIPng
  • RIPv1
  • RIPv2

BIG-IP upgrades to ZebOS:

  • ZebOS was upgraded to 7.10.6 in BIG-IP 12.1.0
  • ZebOS was upgraded to 7.10.4 in BIG-IP 12.0.0 and 11.6.0
  • ZebOS was upgraded to 7.10.2 in BIG-IP 11.5.0
  • ZebOS was upgraded to 7.8.4 in BIG-IP 11.3.0
  • ZebOS was upgraded to 7.8.1 in BIG-IP 11.0.0
  • ZebOS was upgraded to 7.5.1 in BIG-IP 9.6.0 and 10.0.0
