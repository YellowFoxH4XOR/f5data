Description

After upgrading BIG-IP from version 17.1.1.4 to 17.5, the following error may appear in the GUI:
"Provisioned but unlicensed GTM"
Additionally, a warning message may be displayed:
"A valid service contract is required to complete installation."
This issue persists even after reactivating the license and removing auto-provisioned iRules.


Environment

  • GUI Error
  • Provisioning Error
  • Licensing


Cause

The error occurs because the device’s support contract has expired. BIG-IP requires a valid service contract to complete installation and update the service check date, especially when provisioning modules such as GTM. Even if the license is reactivated, an expired or missing support contract will prevent successful provisioning of unlicensed modules after an upgrade.


Recommended Actions

  1. Ensure that the device has a valid, active support contract before performing upgrades or provisioning additional modules.
    K55311915: What is my license expiry date and what is F5 Product Information Request?

  2. If the support contract has expired and not able to meet the minimum service check date requirement for the intended version, you will not be able to perform the upgrade. Please get in touch with F5 renewals team in your region. 

    NA: GSSE@f5.com 
    EMEA: EMEARenewals@f5.com 
    APAC: APACRenewals@f5.com
