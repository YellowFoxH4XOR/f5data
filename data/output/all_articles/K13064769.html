Known Issue

The BIG-IP Edge Client always connected mode might malfunction when you have enabled the captive portal feature.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured the captive portal feature in the access profile.
  • You click Cancel in the captive portal detector window.

Impact

The BIG-IP Edge client stops, and users are unable to establish a VPN connection.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When you attempt to close the captive portal detector window, the BIG-IP Edge Client goes into the Always Disconnected mode.
  • The BIG-IP Edge Client stops, and users are unable to establish a VPN connection.
