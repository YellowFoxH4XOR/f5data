Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP WebAccelerator comm_srv process may restart under high load.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR97174 and it was fixed in BIG-IP WebAccelerator version 9.4.6. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP WebAccelerator Release Notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.5-HF2 issued for BIG-IP version 9.4.5. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Networks Downloads site.

For information about installing a hotfix, refer to K6845: Managing F5 Networks product hotfixes.

For information about F5 Networks hotfix policy, refer to K4918: Overview of F5 Networks critical issue hotfix policy.
