Known Issue

The BIG-IP Edge client may inadvertently send cookies from the previous session to the logon page.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP APM system is configured as a Security Assertion Markup Language (SAML) service provider (SP) and authenticates users using an external Identity Provider (IdP).
  • Users disconnect from network access after successful authentication.

Impact

Users may automatically reconnect to network access without entering their credentials.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Users automatically reconnect to the network access without entering their credentials.
  • During authentication, users receive a logon failed error message without entering their credentials.

Resolution

Status

Workaround

There is no workaround for this issue. However, you can mitigate the impact by manually exiting the BIG-IP Edge client after the user disconnects.
