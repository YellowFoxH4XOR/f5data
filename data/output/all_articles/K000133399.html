K000133399: Hybrid FCP Hardware conversion and license reactivation process

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-04-05
Updated
2025-02-14
Source

Description

You are moving existing F5 hardware under the Hybrid Flexible Consumption Program (H-FCP).
You are required to reactivate the existing license key. This action modifies the existing license to a FCP full license (sometimes referred to as Golden Key).

Environment

  • BIG-IP Hardware
  • H-FCP

Cause

To ensure you continue to get support and access to the full FCP catalogue of BIG-IP Modules and subscriptions, you will need to reactivate the existing Hardware license.
This removes the need to use an add on key or a new base registration key. 

Note: Upon license reactivation both the i-Series and r-Series x600 platforms convert to x800.

 Recommended Actions

Important: Reactivating the license will cause a soft restart and will cause traffic interruption / outage!

Automatic Method 
(BIG-IP has access to Internet / activate.f5.com)

  1. On the BIG-IP GUI, in the menu on the left, select System-License
  2. Scroll to the end of the page and click on "Re-activate"
  3. Activation method place a dot in "Automatic"
  4. This step will restart the services and cause outage: Click on "Next"
  5. After the restart, note "BIG-IP system configuration has changed" message and click "Continue"
  6. Review "active Modules" list and notice ELA / FCP additional lines.

Manual Method

  1. On the BIG-IP GUI, in the menu on the left, select System-License
  2. Scroll to the end of the page and click on "Re-activate"
  3. Activation method place a dot in "Manual"
  4. Click on "Next"
  5. In the text box "Step 1: Dossier" right click in the text and click select all, right click again and click on copy
  6. "Step 2: Licensing server" click on the blue link to open  and navigate to the F5 License server web page
  7. On the F5 license server web page, In the text box "Enter your Dossier" right click and click on paste
  8. Click on "Next"
  9. "Your License" right click in the license text box, click on select all, right click and click on copy
  10. Return to the BIG-IP GUI, "Step 3: License" in the text box, right click and click on paste (paste in the license file)
  11. This step will restart the services and cause outage: Click on "Next"
  12. After the restart, note "BIG-IP system configuration has changed" message and click "Continue"
  13. Review "active Modules" list and notice ELA / FCP additional lines.
