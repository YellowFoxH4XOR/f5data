Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP LTM system may process new data on a connection after a FIN has been received. The BIG-IP LTM system experiences this issue after the following sequence of events:
  • A client sends a TCP FIN to the BIG-IP LTM in order to close the client's side of the connection.
  • The client later retransmits that same TCP FIN with new data.

    Under these circumstances, the BIG-IP LTM system will erroneously continue to process the new data, and the malformed TCP stream may cause TMM to core dump.

    F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR68403, CR68402, and CR68401 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.2.4, 9.3, and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
