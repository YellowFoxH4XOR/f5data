Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The LB::reselect 127.0.0.1 portion of the client-side BIG-IP ASM iRule causes the loopback address to be added to the persistence table instead of the pool member IP address. As a result, persistent connections fail when the request is sent through the BIG-IP ASM virtual server.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR59050 and CR67652, and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM 9.2.4, 9.3.0, and 9.4.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM release notes.
