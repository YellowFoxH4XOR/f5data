Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The bigpipe proxy show command displays the number of connections that are managed by the SSL proxy as well as connections that are queued, but not yet handled by the proxy; therefore, the numbers reported by the bigpipe proxy show command are higher than the actual connections.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR21750 and CR42057.
