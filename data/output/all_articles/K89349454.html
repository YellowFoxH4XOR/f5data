Known Issue

External monitors may cause abnormally high CPU usage.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The BIG-IP system is configured with external monitors that use Secure Sockets Layer (SSL).

Impact

High CPU usage can cause the BIG-IP system to experience a decrease in performance.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP performance graphs indicate a higher than expected percentage of CPU usage.
  • Administrative tasks are adversely affected and may take additional time to complete.
  • You observe a decrease in performance for client connections through the BIG-IP system.
