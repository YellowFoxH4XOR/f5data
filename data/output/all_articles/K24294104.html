Description

You want to physically move your BIG-IP system to a new location in your data center.

Environment

  • BIG-IP appliance
  • Data center

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

If you have a high availability (HA) environment and want to maintain traffic through the process, you can fail over in advance and verify traffic is working on the peer device.

  1. Shut down the platform. To do so, refer to K7369: Shut down and restart the BIG-IP system.
  2. Detach cables from the platform.
  3. Unmount the platform and Quick Install rails.

    Note: Reference the Platform Guide specific to your device. Refer to K98133564: Tips for searching MyF5 and finding product documentation.

  4. Relocate rails to the new location.

    Note: Reference the Platform Guide specific to your device. Refer to K98133564: Tips for searching MyF5 and finding product documentation.

  5. Mount the BIG-IP system on the relocated rails.
  6. Reattach cables to device.
  7. Boot the device and re-establish connectivity to peer and pool members.
  8. Fail over and verify traffic continues working on newly relocated device.

Optional: Repeat on peer device.

Additional Information

None
