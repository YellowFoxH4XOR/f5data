Topic

Summary

This article recommends considerations for BIG-IP APM and FirePass when experiencing an antivirus software scan check issue.

Description

You can configure the BIG-IP APM and FirePass systems to pre-scan a client device through browser plugins or the BIG-IP Edge Client to detect that the device is running current known vendor antivirus software and OPSWAT libraries.

Client-side antivirus software and OPSWAT libraries are continually updated as separate entities. OPSWAT works with many antivirus vendors to ensure compatibility. However, with a wide range of antivirus vendors, there is always a possibility that an antivirus vendor's code or an OPSWAT update may change how these entities interact. Antivirus vendors and OPSWAT work diligently to avoid such a scenario. In some rare cases, the antivirus vendor's code or OPSWAT libraries may be updated through an unannounced change. As a result, you may experience unexpected results when BIG-IP APM or FirePass scans a client device, triggering a scan failure.

Recommendations

Prior to troubleshooting a BIG-IP APM or FirePass antivirus software scan check issue, you should ensure the following:

  • The client device is running the current release of the vendor's antivirus software.
  • The client device has current antivirus virus definitions.
  • You have updated the BIG-IP APM or FirePass system with the most recent OPSWAT libraries.
