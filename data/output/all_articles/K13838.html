Security Advisory Description

A cross-site scripting (XSS) vulnerability exists on the BIG-IP ASM traffic overview page. Malicious request URLs may be exposed in the Configuration utility without proper sanitization. (CVE-2012-2975)

Impact

Privileged (root) access may be granted to unauthenticated users.
