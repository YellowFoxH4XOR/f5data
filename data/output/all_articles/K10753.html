Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP WebAccelerator does not correctly parse regular expressions containing commas in the Matching Rules section.

For example, if you add the following regular expression to the Matching Rules section of a BIG-IP WebAccelerator policy, the expression will be truncated after the comma:

'^[0-9][0-9]{0,5}$'

After refreshing the Configuration utlity page, the example regular expression will be truncated to the following:

'^[0-9][0-9]{0

Note: This issue can result in the pvac process restarting.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR97268.
