Known Issue

Safari 11 displays F5 Endpoint Inspector and F5 VPN launch confirmation dialog boxes twice.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM access policy configuration uses endpoint inspection or network access.
  • You installed F5 Endpoint Inspector or F5 VPN components on the client system.
  • You use Safari 11 to connect to the BIG-IP APM system.

Impact

None. The extra dialog box does not affect system functionality.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • After accepting the confirmation dialog box, you are prompted with another confirmation dialog box.
