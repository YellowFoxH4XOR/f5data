K2527: Configuring a BIG-IP or 3-DNS Controller to not send probes

Topic

When the 3-DNS Controller needs to send a probe to measure Quality of Service (QoS) metrics, it can send the probe from any BIG-IP or 3-DNS Controller systems that are configured for probing.

To exclude a certain system, or set of systems, from sending the probes, perform the following steps:

Note: You must have at least one system probing from each Data Center.

  1. From the navigation pane of the 3-DNS Controller's Configuration utility, click Servers.
  2. Select the specific BIG-IP or 3-DNS Controller that you want to exclude from probing.
  3. Clear the check box next to Probers factory.
  4. Click Apply.

The specific system will no longer be a candidate for sending probes.
