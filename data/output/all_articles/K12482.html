Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Users accessing Domino Web Access (DWA) through a Portal Access favorite may be unable to send emails that have attachments. The email may appear to have the file attached, but when the user attempts to press the Send button, they receive an error message that appears similar to the following example:

Error: Unable to upload file: "<File_Name>"

This issue appears most frequently on DWA version 8.5.2; however, this issue may exist in other versions as well.
