Description 

F5® announced the End of Life (EoL) of the Silverline platform, effective June 30, 2025. We sent End-of-Sale emails in March 2024, and End-of-Life in August 2024.

Environment 

The Silverline platform and its WAF & DDOS solutions will no longer be supported after the EoL date of June 30th, 2025. Users are advised to transition to our future platform with a global network and 5x global points of presence Distributed Cloud Platform – the designated replacement.  

 

Cause 

This change is being driven by the need to enhance and consolidate various components of F5’s application security architectures. The migration to F5’s Distributed Cloud Platform will provide customers with a unified platform that offers improved security, functionality, and compliance capabilities.  

 

Recommended Actions 

Customers using the Silverline platform should migrate to an analogous F5® Distributed Cloud Platform solution. F5 offers a range of products and services on its Distributed Cloud Platform that can meet your needs and ensure a smooth transition.

 Contact your account team or your white glove managed migration team member. 

Questions 

For more information about this announcement, please contact f5distributedcloudteam@f5.com.
