Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may exit and restart when you modify Security Assertion Markup Language (SAML) configurations.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system is configured as a SAML Identity Provider (IdP).
  • An internal error occurs in the tmconf library while your BIG-IP APM system processes a newly added SAML single sign-on (SSO) configuration.

Impact

When TMM restarts, if the BIG-IP APM system is configured for high availability (HA), the system cannot process traffic and fails over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system generates a TMM core file to the /shared/core/ directory.
  • Your BIG-IP APM system fails over to the standby system if configured for HA.
  • In the /var/log/tmm file, you observe a log message similar to the following example:
