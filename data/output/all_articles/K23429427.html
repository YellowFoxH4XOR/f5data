Description
After starting cron daemon and then changing timezone cron jobs do not start at the expected time.
Instead starts at the time of PT time zone which is the default time zone when VEs were built.

Environment

  • crond
  • Time Zone

Cause
Change to timezone does not get picked up by crond.

Recommended Actions

To work around this issue, you can restart the cron daemon after you change the time zone of the BIG-IP system.
To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP command line.
  2. Restart the cron daemon by typing the following command:
  • Versions with systemd:  systemctl restart crond
  • Versions with sysvinit:  bigstart restart crond

K42515525: The sysvinit system has been replaced with the systemd software suite

Additional Information
For version before v12:
K14113: Cron job date/time may not be updated when the system time zone is changed
