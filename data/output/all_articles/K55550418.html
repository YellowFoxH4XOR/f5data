Policy Information

In December 2019, Russian police visited the NGINX Moscow office seeking evidence in connection with a criminal investigation tied to an intellectual property dispute. Several NGINX employees were questioned in connection with the investigation. Recently, the criminal investigation in Russia was officially dismissed due to a lack of evidence.

On June 9, 2020, a new civil suit complaint related to the intellectual property dispute was filed by Lynnwood Investments in the United States District Court for the Northern District of California. As with the criminal investigation, we believe this new civil lawsuit is also without merit. F5 is now reviewing the details of the complaint with its legal advisers. The complaint names F5, several NGINX co-founders and leaders, and former NGINX investors as parties to the suit.

This new civil suit is an ongoing legal matter and will take some time to resolve in the courts, but F5 is confident in its position regarding this case and will not be distracted from developing market-leading application solutions for our customers and partners:

  • NGINX source code is secured and stored outside of Russia.  Promptly following the event in December 2019, F5 took measures to ensure the security of our master software builds for NGINX Open Source, NGINX Plus, NGINX ModSecurity WAF, and NGINX Unit—all of which are stored on servers outside of Russia.
  • No commercial products are developed in Russia.  Developers in Moscow are only contributing to open source projects. F5 remains committed to innovating and releasing new commercial offerings with the development and release process outside of Russia.
  • Since December 2019, NGINX has maintained 13 on-time releases. This includes NGINX Open Source 1.18 and 1.19; NGINX Unit 1.16; NGINX Ingress Controller 1.7; NGINX Plus R21 and R22 (released today); NGINX Controller 3.0, 3.1, 3.2, 3.3, 3.4, and 3.5 (released today); and we worked with our F5 security colleagues to release NGINX App Protect 1.0.
  • F5 expects to maintain our current support service level agreements. We will continue to provide the best-in-class support you’ve come to expect. NGINX uses a "follow-the-sun" model and benefits from the full breadth and depth of the F5 Support organization.

F5 will keep you updated on developments, as appropriate, and please contact F5 Support if you have further questions.
