K000160484: Understanding Gaps in Platform Logs on F5OS Devices

Source

Description

Gaps observed in the platform logs (/log/platform.log) on F5OS devices. During certain periods, no new entries appear in the log file. Visible log messages may include:

  • Network manager mac masquerade reply returned OK
  • Receive listener request from tenant client
  • Listener mac masq request

Environment

  • Product: F5OS
  • Software Version: 1.8.3
  • Log file: /log/platform.log

Cause

None

Answer/Recommended Actions

  • Gaps in the platform.log are expected behavior in this environment.
  • The platform.log mainly records events that are related to configuration changes of the host platform. These are usually triggered by specific actions or changes, such as tenant boot, shutdown, or failover (active/standby transitions).
  • If no such events occur during a period, the log will not record new entries, resulting in apparent "gaps."
  • No action is required to prevent these gaps, as they do not indicate a malfunction or data loss.

Additional Information

  • The observed log behavior is normal and correlates with tenant lifecycle events.
  • Similar log gaps may be observed during periods of system stability when no significant events occur.
  • There is no indication of missing or lost log data; the log simply reflects the absence of relevant events during those times.
