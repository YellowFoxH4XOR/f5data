K12797032: How to upgrade BIG-IQ License manager to BIG-IQ VE Max

Type
Support Solution
Published
2021-05-25
Updated
2025-04-02
Applies to
  • Virtual Editions
  • BIG-IQ
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K12797032
Description

You currently have the limited version of BIG-IQ and want to upgrade to the full version of BIG-IQ.
The VE Max version of BIG-IQ allows you to consume from your existing Commitment value and is charged per managed device, instead of a fixed fee separate subscription.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ
  • ELA
  • License
Cause

You need to upgrade the existing BIG-IQ License Manager to VE Max full version.

Recommended Actions

You will log into BIG-IQ GUI, edit, and delete the current license, replace, and activate the new license key.
Existing ELA Load licenses and reporting data will not be lost during this process.
This process will not impact and existing BIG-IP which have been licensed by the BIG-IQ.



Automatic method
  1. Log into the GUI
  2. System--Licensing
  3. Click on "Reactivate"
  4. Highlight and delete existing "Base Registration key" (also removes "Add-On Keys")
  5. Paste in the new VE Max license key into "Base Registration Key"
  6. Select “Automatic” 
  7. Click on "Activate"
Manual method
  1. Log into the GUI
  2. System--Licensing
  3. Click on "Reactivate"
  4. Highlight and delete existing "Base Registration key" (also removes "Add-On Keys")
  5. Paste in the new VE Max license key into "Base Registration Key"
  6. Select “Manual”
  7. Click on “Generate Dossier”
  8. Select and copy the “Device Dossier” text
  9. Open https://activate.f5.com” in a browser, click the link “Activate F5 product registration key”
  10. Paste the dossier into the box on screen
  11. Click “Next”
  12. Copy the full license text (select all, copy)
  13. In the BIG-IQ GUI, paste the license text into the box “License Text”
  14. Click on “activate”
