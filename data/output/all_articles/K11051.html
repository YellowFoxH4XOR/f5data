Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If a user running Windows 7 closes an App Tunnel and then immediately launches a second App Tunnel, the second App Tunnel may fail to open. When this issue occurs, a pop-up window briefly appears and displays the following two error messages, one at a time, before closing:

Checking connectivity...Please wait.
Now it's safe to close this window

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR134231 and it was fixed in FirePass 7.0.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the FirePass release notes.

Workaround

Although the second App Tunnel may initially fail to open, users can work around this issue by waiting a few seconds and then selecting the App Tunnel favorite again to launch the connection.
