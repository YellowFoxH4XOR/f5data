Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. In rare cases, the BIG-IP ARP system send queue contains a reference to a stale packet pointer. As a result, the TMM process will crash.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR77954, and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.4.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
