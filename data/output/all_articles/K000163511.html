K000163511: Renewal of certificate can show an unexpected validity period after an update attempt

Type
Article
Published
2026-10-01
Updated
2026-10-01
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000163511

Description

After update the F5OS-A management TLS certificate and extend its validity period. The validity shown in /var/system/httpd/ssl/f5os.crt did not reflect the certificate used by the active management TLS service.

 

Environment

F5OS-A version higher than 1.8.0
Certificate update 

 

Cause

Fix of ID1493981

Recommended Actions

We use now /etc/auth-config/default not /var/system/httpd/ssl/f5os.crt

Additional Information

None
