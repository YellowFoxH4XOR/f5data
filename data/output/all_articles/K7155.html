K7155: BIG-IP LTM does not flush SSL data queues when packet fragments less than the MSS value are received

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP LTM SSL receive queue only delivers complete maximum segment size (MSS) fragments to the upper protocol layers. When partial MSS fragments reach the head of the decrypted receive queue, the SSL queue may stall and cause the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) to core dump.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR70730 and CR70755 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.3.0 and 9.4.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.
