K4922: Reasons why a rule stops processing after a discard, accumulate, hash, cache, or redirect statement

Topic

iRules will not continue to execute a list of statements after reaching a terminal statement. As a result, if you wish to use a non-terminal statement with a terminal statement, the iRule must execute the non-terminal statement prior to executing the terminal statement.

The following iRule statements are terminal statements:

  • discard
  • accumulate
  • hash
  • cache
  • redirect

The following iRule statements are non-terminal statements:

  • log
  • if
  • use
