Description

You see that your VDI pool members are returning traffic to the BIG-IP after the initial connection had timed out

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • Horizon VDI iApp 

Cause

As the connections have already timed out, the BIG-IP sends ICMP UNREACHABLE packets in response to the unhandled UDP traffic being sent by the pool members

Recommended Actions

Create a virtual server, with a source defined to this pool, in conjunction with an iRule to drop this traffic and prevent further ICMP UNREACHABLE responses to the pool member
