K88210403: Error ''Cannot decrypt SAML Assertion'' on APM SAML SP

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-11-13
Updated
2025-02-07
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP APM
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K88210403

Description

Error "Cannot decrypt SAML Assertion" is dumped when APM SAML SP receives an encrypted assertion

Environment

  • APM configured as SAML SP
  • APM SAML SP receives an encrypted assertion

Cause

After receiving the encrypted assertion these logs are dumped

modules/Authentication/Saml/SamlSPAgent.cpp: 'SamlSPAgentexecuteInstance()': 2078: Cannot decrypt SAML Assertion
SAML Agent: /Common/AP-SAML-SP_act_saml_auth_ag failed to process encrypted assertion, error: RSA decrypt
 

Recommended Actions

You need to verify that certificate added on APM SAML IdP connector matches the private key on your SAML IdP and also that the certificate has not expired.

bigip.conf:
apm aaa saml-idp-connector /Common/OAS_IdP {
    idp-certificate /Common/OAS_IdP__saml_idp_metadata_cert.crt
