K000151597: Noticeable slowness on BIG-IP after upgrade

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-06-09
Updated
2025-06-09
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.1.2
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000151597

Description

You encounter a situation where your device is facing performance degradation right after you upgraded it to another version.

Environment

  • BIG-IP VE
  • License

Cause

Performance degradation may be because of throughput limitation imposed by the initial trial license on the new F5 VM. This may be a reason of packet drops and performance degradation.

Recommended Actions

To solve this problem you need to apply a license with higher throughput which will allow F5 to manage the expected traffic.
