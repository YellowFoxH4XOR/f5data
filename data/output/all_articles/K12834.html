Issue

Beginning in ARX version 5.1.0 the temperature threshold for the ARX 2000 and 4000 platforms was increased from 45 to 62 degrees Celsius. If the ambient temperature exceeds 62 degrees Celsius, the ARX sends a tempFail (740) SNMP trap and logs a show health event.

Note: The temperature threshold for the ARX 500, 1000, and 6000 platforms was not changed.

You can use the show chassis command to view temperature information for your ARX system. The Temperature Details portion of the show chassis command output will appear similar to the following example:

Note: The ambient temperature is reported by the Local values.
