Description

Below log is observed in /var/log/restjavad.*.log

[ProcessManager] Maximum child processes of 3 has been reached

Environment

  • BIG-IP V12.0.0 and later on released version before  the version below

    17.5.0, 17.1.1, 16.1.5, 15.1.10

     

Cause

By design.
Local daemon "ICRD" is logging this message. It spawns extra processes as its incoming request queue gets bigger, up to a maximum of 3 processes. These logs indicate that it's getting a flood of requests all at once and that parallel execution will be limited. This is by design -- to prevent a resource shortage -- it should not affect any functionality of the BIG-IP to process live traffic.
 

Recommended Actions

None

Additional Informationthere is change introduced due to ID1040573, so in newer version, you may see maximum of 5 icrd_child.
