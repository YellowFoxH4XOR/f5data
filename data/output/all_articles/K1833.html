K1833: Configuring wildcard virtual servers in a VLAN that is part of a VLAN group

Topic

VLAN-based wildcard virtual servers will not work on a VLAN that is a child of a VLAN group.

Packets are only processed at the IP layer on a VLAN group; therefore it is not possible to have wildcard virtual servers on a VLAN that is a child of a VLAN group.
