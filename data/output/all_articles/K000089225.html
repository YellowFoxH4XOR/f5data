K000089225: Troubleshoot subscriptions and licenses on MyF5

Topic

Use this article when you encounter any of the following issues when working with F5 or NGINX subscriptions or licensing on MyF5:

  • Your subscriptions don't display on MyF5
  • You receive an "Action Needed" or "Activate Subscription" message
  • You receive a "Service is down" or "Order is being fulfilled" message when accessing your license
  • You don't see any licensing information in your MyF5 account
  • Your NGINX product expiration dates are later than your subscription end date

Your subscriptions don't display on MyF5

SituationPossible causesResolution
You are a new MyF5 customer.Your order is still processing.Check back later.
F5 Sales team created a MyF5 account for you.The email address you used to register on MyF5 and the email address the sales team used to create your order are different.The email address you used to register must match the email the sales team used to create your order.
You have multiple MyF5 accounts.MyF5 users can have access to multiple accounts that have unique subscriptions and products.Switch to your other account. For more information, refer to Switch between MyF5 accounts in K000090852: View your MyF5 accounts.
Your coworker created their own MyF5 account. Ask your coworker to add you to their account so that you can access their subscription. For more information, refer to Add MyF5 account users in K000089214: Add, change, or remove MyF5 account users.
Your email is correct and none of the above situations apply. Contact your sales account manager.

You receive an "Action Needed" or "Activate Subscription" message

SituationPossible causesResolution
You renewed your BIG-IP or BIG-IQ subscription.F5 needs to know which registration keys you will continue using in your renewed subscription. If you changed the mix of products in your renewed subscription, you may no longer need some registration keys.Select the message to complete your renewal. Assign any required existing registration keys. Then, you can view the details of your renewed subscription and access newly assigned registration keys.

You receive a "Service is down" or "Order is being fulfilled" message when accessing your license

SituationPossible causesResolution
You can see your subscription but BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) or BIG-IQ VE registration keys or NGINX licenses do not display.If it has been less than one day, your order is still processing.Check back later.
If it has been more than one day, your order may have an issue.Contact your sales account manager.

You don't see any licensing information in your MyF5 account

SituationPossible causesResolution
Your subscription is no longer active. None.

Your NGINX product expiration dates are later than your subscription end date

SituationPossible causesResolution
Your NGINX subscription expired, but your NGINX products continue to work.Some NGINX products continue to work for a limited time beyond their subscription end date.To minimize interruption of service, F5 recommends you work with your sales account manager to renew your subscription.
