Description
With an increased demand for more employees to work from home, this article is designed to help those customers who already use APM, within a BIG-IQ Enterprise License agreement / ELA, in place and need to increase the capacity to allow more remote workers to gain access to their internal systems.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ
  • APM
  • ELA

Cause
A standard 'Best' license allows up to 500 Concurrent Connections (CCU) and you want to increase this to allow more remote users access.
When using ELA it is not possible to use an add on key, we need to revoke and replace the license (does not damage the existing configuration)

Recommended Actions
In this example we are using the F5-BIG-MSP-BT-200M, this is part of the Load-4 SKU and provides 500 CCU, customers looking to increase CCU to 2500 CCU can follow the process below to change the license of their APM to F5-BIG-MSP-BT-M-1G (also part of the Load-4).

  1. Log into BIG-IQ
  2. Navigate to Devices (top of screen), then click on Licenses Management (left side menu) this expands to show (licenses, assignments and reports) click on Assignments
  3. Look for the exiting APM License “F5-BIG-MSP-BT-200M”, click in the box to the left side of the license, see a check mark appear, then click on Revoke towards the top of the screen.
  4. Check the details and fill in as needed, IP address of the BIG-IP, admin username and password, then click on Revoke bottom right of the screen.
  5. Click on the popup box to confirm the Revoke of the license
  6. Notice the license is now removed form the list in the BIG-IQ and that the APM / BIG-IP no longer has a license (if you review the GUI)
  7. In the left menu under License management, click on Licenses, click on the Load-4 offering
  8. In this example we are looking for the 2500 CCU license called F5-BIG-MSP-BT-M-1G, click on the blue link.
  9. Click on License Devices
  10. Set unit to measurement to Yearly, fill in device IP address, port (443), admin username and the password, click Assign at the bottom right side of the screen.
  11. Click on Assignments, on the left menu, you will see that the new license has been applied to your APM / BIG-IP device.
  12. Log into your APM device, click on System and then License, notice the CCU increase

Additional Information
There are several licensing options available within the ELA with regards to the amount of CCU and Throughput options, for more information please contact your Customer Success manager.
