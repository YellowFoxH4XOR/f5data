Topic

Note: For information about the information you need when opening a support case for other F5 products, refer to K2633: Instructions for submitting a support case to F5.

F5 Technical Support can help resolve issues more quickly when you provide a full description of the issue and the details of your configuration. To help you gather all the required information, use the following guidelines to prepare for opening a case:

Platform Serial Number

F5 Technical Support requires that you provide the platform serial number for your ARX appliance when you open an ARX support case. For information about locating your ARX serial number, refer to K8239: Finding the serial number for your ARX.

General Information

Provide the following information when you open a case with F5 Technical Support:

  • A full description of the issue, including the following:

    • The symptoms of the issue
    • The approximate time the issue first occurred
    • The number of times the issue has recurred
    • Any error output provided by the ARX appliance
    • Steps to reproduce the issue
    • Any changes you have made to the ARX appliance before the issue first occurred
    • Any steps you have taken in an attempt to solve the issue
  • A description of the impact the issue is having on your system and site, using the following definitions:

    • Site Down - Your network or application is down or critical business functions have stopped due to the issue
    • Site at Risk - Your primary appliance has failed resulting in no redundancy and the situation is placing the site is at risk of failure
    • Performance Degraded - The performance of your network or application has been severely reduced due to the issue
    • General Assistance- The subject of the case does not currently impact your network or application
  • The hours that you are available to work on the issue with an F5 Technical Support representative, and any alternative individuals to contact who are aware of the issue, in the event you are unavailable
  • Remote access information, if possible

Note: Remote access to your network environment is important, because it is the most effective method for collecting information and troubleshooting technical issues. If you cannot provide remote access information, F5 Technical Support will work directly with you to resolve the issue over the phone; however, this method can often be more time consuming and may require file transfers, replication, and additional testing.

Product-specific information

When you open the case with F5 Technical Support, provide the following information (if appropriate for your issue):

  • An ARX diagnostic information file.

    Note: For more information about collecting diagnostic information, refer to the Collecting Diagnostic Information section of the CLI Maintenance Guide, or refer to SOL8294: Providing an ARX diagnostic information file to F5 Technical Support.

  • A capture of the relevant traffic.

    Important: Please provide concurrent client-side and back-end captures from the ARX.

    Note: For more information about collecting a capture, refer to the Capturing IP Traffic in a File section of the ARX CLI Maintenance Guide.

  • The network diagram.

    Note: A Visio diagram is preferred.

Additional information

The following, additional information is not required when opening a case with F5 Technical Support; however, this information may expedite the resolution of your case:

  • Evaluate whether certain volumes or shares are affected, or all volumes and all shares are affected
  • Evaluate whether certain files or types of files (file size, file type, file location) are affected, or all files are affected

Performance related issues:

If the case entails a performance-related issue, provide as much of the following information as possible:

  • Provide supporting data that documents performance changes:

    • Current and previous performance measurements
    • Explain what means you used to gauge the performance
  • Provide performance comparison for client connection by way of the ARX and client connection directly to the back-end filers
