Question

On Manage > Tunnels in XC Portal, what are the definitions of the terminology used for different IP addresses related to your GRE Tunnels?

 

Environment

  • XC DDoS Protection
  • Routed configuration, using GRE Tunnels

 

Answer

Portal Navigation

Navigate to Routed DDOS >  Manage > Tunnels

 

Definitions

TermDefinitionPortal Appearances
Customer Endpoint IP (Destination)

Customer publicly routable IP leveraged to terminate GRE tunnel.

(Important: Cannot be included in the protected subnets.)

Manage > Tunnels
Cust InterconnectGRE tunnel interface IP leveraged for BGP neighbors (only routable over GRE tunnel).Manage > Tunnels
F5 Endpoint IP (Source)F5 publicly routable IP leveraged to terminate GRE tunnel.Manage > Tunnels
F5 InterconnectGRE tunnel interface IP leveraged for BGP neighbors (only routable over GRE tunnel).Manage > Tunnels
