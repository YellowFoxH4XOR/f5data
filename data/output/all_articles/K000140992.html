Description

Customization Logout page on Webtop

 

Environment

BIG-IP APM

 

Cause

Customizing the text for the Logout page on Webtop

 

Recommended Action

1. Login to BIG-IP GUI

2. Go to Access => Profiles/Policies => Customization: General

3. There will be 2x Tabs 'Branding' and 'Text' at the top of the list of profiles.

4. Click on 'Text'

5. Click on your desired Access Policy

6. Expand 'Logout' and then click on 'General'

7. There is a list of names that can be edited/modified to logout page on the webtop

7.1 You should put a "single space" in the box and save, it will remove the message all together.

8. Click Save icon and the Apply Access Policy

 

Additional Information

Access Policy Manager Customization
