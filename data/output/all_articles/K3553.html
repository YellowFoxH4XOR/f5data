Topic

You cannot alter the transmission mode of operation for the Fiber Gigabit port on the BIG-IP system. The Fiber Gigabit ports have automatic link detection and operate only in multimode.

Note: For information about platforms that use SFP and XFP modules, refer to K6097: Specifications of the Fiber Gigabit Ethernet SFP, XFP, SFP+ and QSFP+ module ports on BIG-IP system platforms.
