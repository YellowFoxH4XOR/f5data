Known Issue

When adding or enabling blades on a VIPRION chassis, the bigd process sends pool monitor traffic before the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) is ready.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a VIPRION platform.
  • You have performed one of the following tasks while the primary blade is actively passing traffic:
    • You disabled and re-enabled a secondary blade using either the Configuration utility or the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh).
    • You manually removed and re-installed a secondary blade.

Impact

For a brief period of time, client connections may be dropped while TMM becomes ready to process bigd monitor traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • After installing or enabling a secondary blade, client connections fail for a brief period of time.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can remove all monitors from the pool members and add the monitors back once TMM becomes ready to process all traffic.

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Remove all of the pool member monitors using either the Configuration utility or tmsh.
  2. To verify TMM availability, type the following command:

    tail -f /var/log/ltm | grep command

  3. Verify the HA reports tmm ready message, similar to the following example from a four-slot VIPRION chassis:

    slot1/VIP4400 notice sod[5676]: 01140044:5: HA reports tmm ready
    slot2/VIP4400 notice sod[5677]: 01140044:5: HA reports tmm ready
    slot3/VIP4400 notice sod[5678]: 01140044:5: HA reports tmm ready
    slot4/VIP4400 notice sod[5679]: 01140044:5: HA reports tmm ready

  4. Add the pool member monitors back using either the Configuration utility or tmsh.
