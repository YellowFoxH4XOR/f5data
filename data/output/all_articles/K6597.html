Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When TMM processes certain traffic combinations of MTUs, packet latency, packet rate, and TCP options, TMM can corrupt the process and cause the process to core dump.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR69126 and CR67869, and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.1.3, 9.2.4, and 9.3. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
