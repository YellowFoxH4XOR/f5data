K000135906: Error Message in Zone Runner : Resolver returned no such record

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-09-26
Updated
2025-04-02
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP DNS
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000135906

Description

Unable to modify some records in Zone Runner.
Click records in DNS>>Zones:ZoneRunner : Resource Record List, but show Resolver returned no such record.


Environment

  • DNS
  • Zone Runner
  • NS resource record
 

Condition

NS record exists in Zone Runner

For example, example.com and its subdomains are managed by Zone Runner, A record of www.example.com is added in this Zone Runner.
If NS record of www.example.com exists in this Zone Runner, other types of records for www.example.com is not able to be modified and GUI shows Resolver returned no such record.


Recommended Action

This behavior is expected.
Once you configure NS record for some domains in Zone Runner, these domains will be managed and resolved by other name servers based on NS records, and Zone Runner will show Resolver returned no such record.
