Source

Description

You are trying to sync an APM policy and got the below error message: 

PolicySyncMgr: Failed to write to Definition file /var/apm/apmpolicysync/policyxxxx_obj_list.xml

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM 
  • Policy sync

Cause

/var is full

Recommended Actions

  • Check what files are consuming more space on /var and delete them.
  • Once you have space available, please try to sync your policy again.

Additional Information

None
