K000140330: Trial License Keys are not showing up on MyF5 portal

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-07-14
Updated
2024-07-14
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000140330

Description

The Trial Licenses you have requested have already been sent via email, however it is not showing up on MyF5. After logging on, the main page shows "0 Trials" and when you go to My Products & Plans > Trials, it is showing "You have no active trials" at the bottom of the page.



Figure: MyF5 main page

 

Figure: MyF5 Trials page

Environment

  • MyF5 portal
  • BIG-IP VE & BIG-IQ VE Trial Licenses

Cause

Unknown at the time of publication.

Recommended Actions

 ​​​​​​​1. Go to  My Products & Plans > Trials

 2. Click on Start Free Trial button for BIG-IP VE & BIG-IQ VE product

Figure: MyF5 Trial Offers page

 

3. Scroll down the page and it should now show that you have an Active Trial. You can view the keys by clicking on the Product name.

 Figure: MyF5 Trials Page showing an Active Trial

 

Note: Try to logout and login again if the page did not refresh.

4. If the issue was not resolved, please send an email to support@f5.com with your MyF5 login ID and the description of the issue. 

Additional Information

None.
