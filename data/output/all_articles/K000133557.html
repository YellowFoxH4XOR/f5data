Description

At AskF5, we could get to knowledge center or follow breadcrumbs to get documentation such as platform guides. For my.f5.com there is not a clear path necessary to obtain documents

Environment

MyF5 Support Home page
Searches for F5 platform guides

Cause

Migration from AskF5 (support.f5.com) to MyF5 (my.f5.com ) support portal

Recommended Actions

if you go to my.f5.com and click on "Product Manuals and Release Notes," you will get over forty-five thousand results. You then need to click on the magnifying glass icon at top right and it opens up the search bar - then search for the platform. For example, if you search for "4400", the Viprion 4400 Series chassis platform guide will come up.
