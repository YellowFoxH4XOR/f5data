Issue

In BIG-IP versions prior to 9.0.0, persistent connections are sent to the next available pool member in the configuration when the pool member in a persistent session becomes unavailable.

Beginning in BIG-IP LTM 9.0.0, persistent connections are load balanced using the pool load balancing method when the pool member in a persistent session becomes unavailable.
