Known Issue

The BIG-IP APM email agent does not send mail messages to unverified recipients.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a BIG-IP APM access policy configured to send email messages.
  • A recipient's email server does not provide a 200 OK response to a verify (VRFY) request.

The version of cURL included in BIG-IP 13.0.0 uses a VRFY request to confirm that recipients are valid before sending mail. However, many email servers consider VRFY a potential information leak and respond with a 252 - Not Verified response instead of a 200 OK response. As a result, the BIG-IP APM system does not send the message. Additionally, the BIG-IP APM system does not log an error indicating the email message was not sent.

Impact

The access policy email agent does not send mail messages or log errors about the affected email not being sent.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP APM email agent does not send email messages to some of the recipients configured in an access policy.
  • After upgrading to BIG-IP 13.0.0, some email recipients that received BIG-IP APM access policy emails before upgrading to 13.0.0 no longer receive email messages.
