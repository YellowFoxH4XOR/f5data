Source

Description
Connection to management IP may be lost after upgrade VE to 14.1.0 or later version, after booting into the new software volume, below symptoms may be observed:

  • Failed to ping management ip
  • Cannot ssh to manament ip
  • Webui is not available 
  • Tcpdump on mgmt interface shows there is traffic in but no traffic sending out from mgmt

Environment

  • BIG-IP VE
  • Management Interface
  • Auto Last Hop

Cause
It is caused by the function change of auto last hop on the management interface, refer to solution link below for detail:
K55225090: BIG-IP VE no longer supports Auto Last Hop for management connections

Recommended Actions

Configure the management route manually.

Additional Information

None
