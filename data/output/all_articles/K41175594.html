Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may unexpectedly transition from Forced Offline to Active status following a reboot.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You perform a user configuration set (UCS) restoration from a UCS archive created when your BIG-IP system is in Forced Offline status.
  • You reboot your BIG-IP system following the UCS restoration.

Following the UCS restoration and the reboot, your BIG-IP system should maintain the Forced Offline status.

Impact

The BIG-IP system transitions into Active status following a reboot, resulting in unexpected behaviors during system installations and upgrades.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Your BIG-IP system transitions from Forced Offline to Active status following a reboot.
