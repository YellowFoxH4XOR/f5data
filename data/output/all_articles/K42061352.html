Description

Sometimes it is necessary to manually trigger a Failover between BIG-IP HA Peers in a Traffic Group. For example, when preparing to Re-Activate their Licenses or to otherwise prepare the BIG-IP for maintenance. 

When performed, the BIG-IP currently in the Active HA Role is Forced into the Standby HA Role. And the BIG-IP that had been in the Standby HA Role, will move into the Active HA Role. 

The newly Active HA Peer BIG-IP broadcasts all it's IP's and MAC addresses to adjacently connected devices such as switches via Gratuitous ARP's, or GARP's. And those adjacent devices use that information to update their MAC forwarding tables. At which point, the adjacent devices will begin sending production traffic to the newly Active HA Role BIG-IP.

Environment

Relevant environmental factors:

  • Two or more BIG-IP's in an HA Pair or Cluster arrangement.

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

To initiate a Manual Failover from the currently Active HA Peer BIG-IP in the Traffic Group, the following steps can be performed:

  1. In the GUI, click the Active link in the upper left hand corner next to the F5 Ball and Online. This is a shortcut link that takes you directly to Device Management > Traffic Group and into the Traffic Group for which the BIG-IP is currently the Active HA Peer BIG-IP.  
  2. Near the bottom of the Traffic Group's config page, click the Force to Standby button to initiate the Manual Failover. And watch the HA Role of that BIG-IP move into the Standby HA Role in the upper left hand corner next to the F5 Ball and Online.
