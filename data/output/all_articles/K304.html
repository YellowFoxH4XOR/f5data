K304: RAM requirements for cookie persistence

Topic

Using cookie persistence (for any duration) does not require any additional memory on the BIG-IP Controller, regardless of the number of cookies issued.

Because the cookies are stored on the connecting client and presented to the BIG-IP Controller, the controller directs them to the appropriate node when it sees the cookie. The cookies do not take up any additional RAM on the BIG-IP Controller.
