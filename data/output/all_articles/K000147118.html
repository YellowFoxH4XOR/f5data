Description

  • Where do you find your http load balancer (LB) rate limiting policy reflected in the dashboard.

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud Console
  • Http Load Balancer 

Answer/Recommended Actions

To find out your rate limiting policy rules hits in the dashboard you will have to go to:

  1. Load balancers > namespace in which your LB is  > Virtual hosts > Http Load Balancer
  2. Select Security Monitoring on the desired LB and you will find this under Top Service Policies Hit
