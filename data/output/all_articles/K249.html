Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. On BIG-IP 5100 and 2400 models, the bigpipe interface command erroneously reports the media type for fiber gigabit Ethernet ports as 1000BaseTX. The media type is actually 1000BaseSX.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR29602 and CR41786.
