K000164027: PCIe Receiver Error Messages (Corrected AER Errors) Logged on VELOS Blade Running F5OS 1.8.x

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-10-28
Updated
2026-10-28
Applies to
  • F5OS Products
  • F5OS-C: 1.8.X, 1.8.2, 1.8.1, 1.8.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000164027

Description

The system logs repeated corrected PCIe errors for a blade. Example messages:

  • kernel: nodename=blade-X(pX) pcieport 0000:97:02.0: AER: Multiple Corrected error received: id=9710
  • kernel: nodename=blade-X(pX) pcieport 0000:97:02.0: PCIe Bus Error: severity=Corrected, type=Physical Layer, id=9710(Receiver ID)
  • kernel: nodename=blade-X(pX) pcieport 0000:97:02.0: device [8086:347a] error status/mask=00000001/00000000
  • kernel: nodename=blade-X(pX) pcieport 0000:97:02.0: [ 0] Receiver Error

These messages report corrected (recoverable) Receiver Errors on the PCIe link. They do not indicate uncorrected or fatal errors.

Environment

  • Product: F5OS-C (VELOS chassis)
  • Version: F5OS 1.8.2
  • Affected PCIe devices: 0000:97:02.0 (root port) and 0000:98:00.0 (endpoint)
  • Conditions: Corrected Receiver Errors are logged at a low rate.

Cause

  • F5 hardware engineering reviewed the logs and determined that these specific corrected Receiver Errors occur at a low rate and do not cause functional failures.
  • Logging of these correctable errors is tracked in Bug ID 2695517: "Suppress correctable PCI e receiver errors in console output." No fix or release timeline was available at the time of publication.
  • This is related to a known issue, Bug ID 1538361: "Monaco: Intermittent corrected PCIe Bus Error at 97:02.0 and 97:04.0.".

Answer/Recommended Actions

  1. Check the logs for the error types. Confirm that the entries are only corrected Receiver Errors. Example search:
    • grep "Corrected error received" <system message log>
  2. Check for signs of functional impact. Confirm that none of the following are present:
    • Uncorrected or fatal PCIe errors
    • PCIe link recovery failures
    • Traffic, interface, or blade service disruption
  3. If no functional impact is present, treat the corrected Receiver Errors as expected and ignore them until Bug ID 2695517 is resolved in a released version.
  4. If uncorrected or fatal errors, link recovery failures, or service impact appear, open a support case and include the relevant logs and a qkview.
  5. Optional: If you want to rule out a physical seating issue, you can reseat the blade. Physically remove and reinsert the blade, record the time of the action, and then review the logs to see whether the corrected errors continue. This step does not resolve the error if the behavior is expected. It only helps determine whether the errors persist after reseating.

Additional Information

  • Expected behavior: Occasional corrected PCIe Receiver Errors logged at a low rate, with no uncorrected errors, link recovery events, or service impact. Corrected errors are recovered by the hardware.
  • Unexpected behavior: Uncorrected or fatal PCIe errors, repeated PCIe link recovery, or any traffic or service disruption on the blade. These require a support case.
  • Log entries that match this article do not require a software upgrade or rollback unless other symptoms are present.
