Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may erroneously determine that the hardware crypto queues are unavailable, and trigger a high availability (HA) failover event.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are using Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) hardware acceleration on one of the following platforms:
    • BIG-IP i2000 Series (C117)
    • BIG-IP 2000 Series (C112)
    • BIG-IP 4000 Series (C113)
    • BIG-IP 5000 Series (C109)
    • BIG-IP 6900 FIPS (D104)
    • BIG-IP 7000 Series (D110)
    • BIG-IP 8900 FIPS (D106)
    • BIG-IP 10000 Series (D113), BIG-IP 10350N (D112)
    • BIG-IP 11000 FIPS (E101)
    • BIG-IP 12250v (D111)
    • VIPRION B2250 blade (A112)
    • VIPRION B4300 blade (A108)
       
  • Your BIG-IP system is under heavy load.

Note: A similar issue exists with other BIG-IP platforms. For more information, refer to K24036315: Hardware crypto queues may erroneously become unavailable under heavy load.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may become unresponsive and fail over to a standby system in the device group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • In the /var/log/ltm log file, you observe error messages that appear similar to the following example:

    notice tmm3[<pid>]: 01010029:5: Clock advanced by 1052 ticks 
    crit tmm2[<pid>]: 01010025:2: Device error: crypto codec cn-crypto-2 queue is stuck.
    warning sod[<pid>]: 01140029:4: HA crypto_failsafe_t cn-crypto-2 fails action is failover.
    notice sod[<pid>]: 010c0052:5: Standby for traffic group /Common/traffic-group-1.

  • You experience a failover event to the standby system.
