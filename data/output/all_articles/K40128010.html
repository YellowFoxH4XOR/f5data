Known Issue

BIG-IP does not retrieve LDAP attributes during remote role group matching.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP system is configured for remote LDAP, ClientCert LDAP, or Active Directory authentication.
  • Remote role groups are configured with an attribute string.
When you configure a remote role group with an attribute string, such as memberOf or gidNumber, the BIG-IP system fails to fetch all the needed attributes for authentication. This issue may cause unexpected group matching or authentication failure.

Impact

Remote role group mapping based on LDAP attributes fails or matches unexpectedly.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H636988 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen. 
  • Less specific remote role groups are matched or no remote role group is matched.
  • The default remote role is incorrectly applied to users when authenticating.
  • Users fail to authenticate and are unable to access the BIG-IP Configuration utility.
