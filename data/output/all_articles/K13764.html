Known Issue

DHCP Relay type virtual servers may be unable to properly relay requests when Clustered Multi-Processing (CMP) is enabled on the BIG-IP system.

Impact

DHCP clients may be unable to connect to the network with a valid IP address.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H392805 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • DHCP server may not receive DHCP requests from the clients on the other VLAN.
  • DHCP client may not receive DHCP server responses that use a valid IP address.
