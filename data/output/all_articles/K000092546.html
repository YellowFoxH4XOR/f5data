Support case management

Your support cases move with your account, and you must manage support cases using MyF5 after your support account moves.

My Support, the previous F5 case management tool, will remain on online until March 2023, but it contains just read-only information.

Moved November 16, 2022Support cases section in K000089216: MyF5 help
Accounts

All customers are moving to MyF5. F5 is moving customers to MyF5 in groups. Before F5 moves your account, F5 sends you emails to notify you when your account is moving. The last group moves on this date.

If your account moved and you have not done so already, you must register to use MyF5 to access your support account and support cases.
