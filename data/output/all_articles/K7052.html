The typical licensing for a FirePass high availability failover pair consists of one FirePass controller with a standalone license with failover support enabled, and a second FirePass controller with a failover-only license. However, two FirePass controllers with standalone licenses can be configured to form a failover pair. F5 Networks supports the use of two FirePass controllers with standalone licenses as a failover pair if they meet the following three conditions:
  • The Failover Support module is enabled on both licenses.

    Note: A typical standalone license will have the Failover Support module enabled. To verify whether the Failover Support module is enabled, click on the Click here to view license details link on the Device Management: Maintenance: Activate License page.
     
  • Both FirePass controllers have identical options activated in their licenses.
  • Both FirePass controllers have identical hardware platforms.

The steps to configure a FirePass high availability failover pair are the same, regardless of which licensing method you use.
