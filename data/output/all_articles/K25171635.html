Known Issue

The qkview utility may erroneously include files in the /lib64 and /usr/lib64 directories.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You run the qkview utility on an affected BIG-IP software version; affected versions are listed in the Applies To box, located in the upper-right corner of this article.

Impact

The qkview utility may take more time than usual to complete, due to the additional files included.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You notice that the qkview utility takes more time than usual to complete, due to the additional files included.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 480811 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies To box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:
