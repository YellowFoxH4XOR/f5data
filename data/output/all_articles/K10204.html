Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When using the Deployment Wizard in any deployment scenario other than Manual, the learning suggestions for all violations do not display in the Traffic Learning or New Entities screens until the Deployment Wizard is stopped.

Similarly, if you use a deployment scenario other than Manual, and you add custom violations to the policy, the learning suggestions do not display until you stop the Deployment Wizard. For example, if you begin building a policy using the Web Services deployment scenario, and you add custom checks for non-XML violations, the learning suggestions do not display until you stop the Deployment Wizard.

F5 Product Development tracked these issues as ID305835 and ID305836 (formerly CR120813 and CR120814) and they were fixed in BIG-IP ASM 10.2.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM release notes.
