Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Non-TCP connections with varying protocol-specific timeout requirements may be inappropriately subjected to the TCP handshake timeout.

For example, the idle timeout for a connection through a BIG-IP NAT should be 300 seconds. However, the idle timeout for a GRE-based connection (such as PPTP) through a NAT, alternates between five and 300 seconds during the course of the connection. BIG-IP LTM inappropriately subjects the GRE-based connection to the TCP handshake timeout of five seconds, causing the connection to terminate after five seconds of inactivity.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR96419 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.4.5. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
