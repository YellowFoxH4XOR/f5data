Description

BIG-IP LTM device is not sending sFlow data to the configured receiver

  • Not seeing any traffic at the sFlow receiver

  • No error messages or log messages.

  • Tcpdump on the LTM dose not show any traffic being sent to the sFlow receiver

Environment

Relevant environmental factors specific to the topic

  • Sflow Receiver configured on the BIG-IP LTM and sFlow collection enabled in the an HTTP profile assigned to the virtual server
  • BIG-IP LTM does not have a default route configured

Cause

The IP address of the sFlow receiver must be reachable from a self IP address on the BIG-IP system.
A specific self-ip route is required to send traffic from the BIG-IP LTM.

Recommended Actions

  • Follow article to add default route, or specific route for the destination of the sFlow receiver to the configuration on the LTM:
    K13833: Managing static routes in BIG-IP and BIG-IQ
  • After adding the additional route for sflow to follow, you may need to remove and re-add the sflow configuration.
