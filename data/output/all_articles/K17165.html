Known Issue

A disabled VIPRION blade may incorrectly re-enable itself when another blade becomes primary.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are using two or more blades in a VIPRION chassis.
  • A secondary blade becomes primary, then is quickly disabled.

If a secondary blade becomes primary and then is quickly disabled, that blade does not send a cluster packet for 10 seconds. A new primary blade is not elected for 10 seconds (the heartbeat timeout) instead of the expected time (immediately). The other blades (including the new primary blade) do not receive the message that the blade was set to disabled, so the blade re-enables itself without a user request.

Impact

The disabled blade re-enables itself.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Management interfaces used for accessing the Configuration utility and the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) may appear to stop responding for 10 second intervals.
  • You may view messages in the /var/log/ltm file that appear similar to the following example:

    emerg load_config_files: "/usr/bin/tmsh -n -g load sys config partitions all base" - failed. -- Unexpected Error: Saving andloading configuration is only allowed on the primary slot.

Workaround

There is no workaround for this issue. To mitigate this issue, wait 10 seconds after disabling a blade before you disable another blade.
