Known Issue

TCP window scaling may fail on the server-side connection.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP system has a virtual server configured with a TCP profile.
  • You have configured the TCP profile Connection Setup with the Verified Accept property enabled.

Impact

Server-side connections may experience latency and delay.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H522923 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • On the server-side connection, the BIG-IP system ignores the back-end maximum segment size (MSS) option.
  • Server-side connections experience latency and delay.
