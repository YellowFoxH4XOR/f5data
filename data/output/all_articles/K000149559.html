K000149559: botd service is taking too long to start after upgrade

Support Solution
Published
2025-01-30

Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP ASM: 17.X.X, 17.1.X, 17.1.2, 17.1.1, 17.1.0, 17.0.X, 17.0.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000149559

Description

After upgrading from version 16.1.4.2 to 17.1.2 on BIG-IP ASM VE, the botd service takes an unusually long time (30-60 minutes) to start after a reboot or restart of services, causing traffic issues and disruptions. The botd status shows "waiting for asm to release ready semaphore" during this delay.

Environment

BIGIP ASM

Cause

The root cause was insufficient disk space on the Virtual Edition (VE), which led to delays in module provisioning (mprov.pl) during boot-up, causing process restarts that interfered with ASM's upgrade activities and delayed the startup of dependent services like botd.

Recommended Actions

To clean up disk space follow the procedure in

K14403: Maintaining disk space on the BIG-IP system
