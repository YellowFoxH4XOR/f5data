Description

After logging into MyF5 portal, you are unable to view your existing case.

Environment

  • MyF5 account user roles and permission
  • Case management

Cause

 Cases cannot be viewed unless you are the primary contact, or assigned the appropriate role to view the case. 

Recommended Actions

  • For users who do not have visibility to the case, please contact F5 support and request the change of the primary contact.

    Note    : There can be only one primary contact per case. However i    f the customers request we can add their email in CC to loop in to have visibility of the emails in case.

  • Users who want to view all cases that are opened for their account, are required to have a "Case Manager" or an "Admin" role. Please contact your account manager first, for approval for these roles. Thereafter, please contact F5 support to get your roles updated. You may refer to this article to understand roles and permission.

Additional Information 

To view your current role and permission for an Account, you can check it by clicking the profile icon on the upper right corner then "My Accounts".
