Description

The delete button is not displayed in the VPE Macro terminal editor.

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM
  • VPE
  • APM Macro

Cause

This is caused by the known issue ID996029

Recommended Actions

There are two options to workaround this behavior:
  1. Redo each one of the current Terminal entries in the Macro
  2. Remove the macro from the policy, remove the desired endings and then add it back to the policy
