Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. F5 has identified an operational issue with the LCD panel. The LCD panel and keypad backlight can be manually toggled OFF and ON using the system's LCD panel. If the LCD panel backlight is toggled OFF and then ON using the keypad, only the LCD backlight will be restored. The keypad backlight, however, will not be restored until the system is power cycled.

As of December 2006, the LCD module was revised to ensure backlight longevity and color control. The following F5 platforms that shipped after December 2006 are affected by this issue:

  • BIG-IP 1500 (C36)
  • BIG-IP 3400 (C62)
  • BIG-IP 6400 (D63)
  • BIG-IP 6800 (D68)
  • BIG-IP 8400 (D84)
  • BIG-IP 8800 (D88)
  • FirePass 4100 (D46)
  • FirePass 4300 (D101)

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR83142 and it was fixed in SCCP 12.0.5.

For information about determining the SCCP version, refer to K8359: Determining the SCCP version.
