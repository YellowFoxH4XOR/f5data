K55402235: Ansible playbooks broke after upgrading to v15

Published
2022-09-27
Updated
2026-01-16
Applies to

Source

Description

After running an ansible playbook, Ansible fails with error: "msg": "Expecting value: line 1 column 1 (char 0)"

Environment

  • Ansible Playbook
  • BIG-IP v15.1.5.1

Cause

You have scripts running that restarts the restjavad process, to "reset" the API, that step was killing their own tokenized session, which lead to log message: "Ansible error : "msg": "Expecting value: line 1 column 1 (char 0)". For example, tmsh restart /sys service restjavad.

Recommended Actions

Review and update your script to remove the piece of code that restarts the restjavad process.
