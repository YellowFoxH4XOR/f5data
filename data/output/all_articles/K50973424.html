Known Issue

Tunnel synchronization may restore deleted tunnels.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You perform a full load synchronization.

Impact

Deleted tunnels are restored to the configuration.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • After a full load synchronization, the system adds previously deleted tunnels to the default route domain for the partition that contains them.
