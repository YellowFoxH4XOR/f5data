Customers who are interested in moving to the newer management platform, BIG-IQ, are requested to evaluate feature availability and work with their account teams to appropriately select the BIG-IQ SKU.

Technical Support

Customers whose platforms are designated EoS and who are currently under maintenance contracts will continue to receive technical support until the Service Contract Renewal date expires.
