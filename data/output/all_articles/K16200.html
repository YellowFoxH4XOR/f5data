Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may take up to five seconds to detect that client-side or server-side connections have not been offloaded to the embedded Packet Velocity Acceleration (ePVA) hardware.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured a Fast Layer 4 (L4) virtual server type.
  • The pva.acceleration database key is set to guaranteed.

Impact

Connections that the BIG-IP system does not detect as offloaded to the ePVA are reset.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You may experience unexpected connection resets.
