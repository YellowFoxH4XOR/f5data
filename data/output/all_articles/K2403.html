To restart the ITCMPortal processes, you can perform the following steps.

  1. Stop the ITCMPortal daemons by typing the following commands:

    bigstart shutdown tomcat

    bigstart shutdown portal

  2. Start the ITCMPortal and dependant daemons by typing the following commands:

    bigstart startup tomcat

    bigstart startup portal

    bigstart startup corbalocallb

    bigstart startup corbasystem

    bigstart startup corbanetwork

    bigstart startup corbamgmt
