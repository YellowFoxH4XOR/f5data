Description

With using a service as DUO to add another authentication factor to the APM policy, users are not able to successfully complete the 2FA/MFA step.

Instead, users may be asked again for credentials from the authentication service / identity provider.

Also: some users may have noticed that if they submit the onetime code to DUO within less and 10 seconds, the authentication process work flawlessly.

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM
  • EdgeClient v7.2.4.2 or newer
  • Apple MacOS v13.4.1 or newer.

Cause

This may be due to bug ID 1328933.

Recommended Actions

F5 recommends you open a Support ticket to be provided by an hotfix to this issue.
