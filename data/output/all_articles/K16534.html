Known Issue

BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) may perform poorly or drop connections when provisioned with a number of vCPUs that are not a power of 2.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You have provisioned a BIG-IP VE with a number of vCPUs that is not a power of 2:

    For example:

    2, 4, 8

Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) threads are allocated based on the number of configured vCPUs. If the number of TMM threads is not a power of 2 on the BIG-IP VE system, the system can experience lower throughput, performance degradation, and dropped connection flows.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may drop connection flows or experience performance degradation and decreased throughput.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Poor performance
  • Dropped TMM flows

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can provision the number of vCPUs for the BIG-IP Virtual Edition instance to be a power of 2. This can be 2, 4, or 8 - depending on your license. Please refer to K14810: Overview of the BIG-IP Virtual Edition license limits for further information on license limits.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.

Provisioning vCPUs is dependent on the hypervisor that you use. Please refer to your hypervisor documentation for further instructions on allocating vCPUs.
