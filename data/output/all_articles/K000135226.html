Description

When try to deploy ASM policy in BIG-IQ CM, get the following error message in /var/log/restjavad.0.log

 

restjavad.0.log:[ERROR][24 Apr 2023 14:05:31 PDT][/cm/asm/tasks/difference-config/71cd2d3c-9299-42c0-b2b1-67257c748b8a/worker AsmDifferenceTaskWorker] Difference operation failed: Object /Common/Java Servlets/JSP Signatures (High/Medium Accuracy) does not exist, URI https://localhost/mgmt/cm/asm/current-config/signature-sets/9e49711f-fc2c-35a7-b847-a713129be9ff [KeyNotFoundException]

 

Environment

  • BIG-IQ CM
  • BIG-IP ASM

Cause

No sync between two BIG-IP ASMs

 

Recommended Actions

Enable BIG-IP ASM synchronization on the device group

 

Additional Information

None.
