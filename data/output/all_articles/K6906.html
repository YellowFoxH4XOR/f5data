Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When the BIG-IP GTM system is configured with wide IP persistence, it will synchronize its persistence records with other BIG-IP GTM systems in the network when it receives an LDNS request. Under certain circumstances, the BIG-IP GTM system does not update its synchronization status. As a result, the BIG-IP GTM drops persistence requests from the LDNS server.

After the BIG-IP GTM system receives a DNS request, it adds a persistence entry to its persistence table, and synchronizes the entry to the other BIG-IP GTM systems in the network. It is possible that the BIG-IP GTM system receiving the synchronization does not update its synchronization status from Waiting for peer to Ready. When the BIG-IP GTM system is in Waiting for Peer or Need Peer synchronization status, it drops persistence DNS requests.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR72106 and CR72107, and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.2.5, 9.3.0, and 9.4.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM release notes.
