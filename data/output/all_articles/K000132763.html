Description

UCS Restoration from CM to DCD Failed with error

 

Broadcast message from systemd-journald@bigip1 (Wed 2023-01-01 01:01:01 +08): logger[16485]: Re-starting guiserver

2023 Feb 01 01:01:01 bigip1 logger[16485]: Re-starting guiserver

 

Environment

  • Restoration is triggered from CM to DCD.
  • Noticed the following repeating logs: logger: [XXXXX: ]Re-starting guiserver

 

Cause

  • The error you are seeing is expected.
  • This process is not recommended.

Recommended Actions

  • As per manual...

UCS backup management for the BIG-IQ system

Restore BIG-IQ from the UCS backup file

 

It's important to back up your BIG-IQ system on a regular basis so in the event you have an issue, you can restore your BIG-IQ and return to managing your BIG-IP devices with limited interruption.

Use your BIG-IQ UCS backup to restore your BIG-IQ configuration.

Restore only the active BIG-IQ in a high availability (HA) configuration.

 

  • Furthermore as per this article K45246805

 

K45246805: Restore the BIG-IQ system with a UCS file backup stored remotely

 

This procedure applies to BIG-IQ Centralized Management only.

 

For restoring Data Collection Devices (DCD), you should remove and re-associated the DCD instances from the BIG-IQ Centralized Management system. For more information, refer to K02014651: Removing a DCD association from BIG-IQ Centralized Management.
