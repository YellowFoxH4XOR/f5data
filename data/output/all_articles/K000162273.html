K000162273: Why 17.5.1.7 was not released to MyF5 downloads

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-07-15
Updated
2026-07-17
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000162273

Description

The BIG-IP software release 17.5.1.8 includes all fixes delivered in 17.5.1.7. This is documented in the 17.5.1.8 release notes.

Environment

  • BIG-IP 17.5.1.7

Cause

None

Answer/Recommended Actions

Version 17.5.1.7 remained in Limited Availability (LA) and was superseded by 17.5.1.8 shortly after its creation.
 
For that reason, 17.5.1.7 did not appear on MyF5 Downloads and was not announced for General Availability (GA).

None
