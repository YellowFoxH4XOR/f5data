Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. On the Device Management: Configuration: SNMP page in the Hosts section, the FirePass Administrative Console incorrectly displays the SNMPv3 informs field as SNMPv2 informs. This issue is cosmetic, and does not affect the functionality of the feature. If you need to configure one or more SNMPv3 trap hosts, enter that information in the SNMPv2 informs field.

F5 Networks Product Development is tracking this issue as CR111307.
