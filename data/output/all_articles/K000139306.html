K000139306: After failover or reboot traffic is not sent to the active BIG-IP

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-04-16
Updated
2024-04-16
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 15.1.5
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000139306

Description

After failover or a reboot, traffic is not sent to the active BIG-IP. BIG-IPs use MAC Masquerade.

Traffic may recover after a short period.

Environment

  • BIG-IP with MAC Masquerade
    • HA communication may fail
  • switch with a MAC address-based security feature

Cause

When BIG-IP fails over with MAC Masquerade, the switch will see the BIG-IP's MAC address move to a different port. In certain conditions, such as a loss of failover communication, the MAC address may appear on both ports for a brief period.

Recommended Actions

Disable this switch feature on ports connected to BIG-IP.

Additional Information

To ensure BIG-IP failover works during startup before TMM is ready, which helps prevent conflicts, F5 recommends that you configure the management address as well as at least one Self IP for Network Failover. See K14135, linked below.
