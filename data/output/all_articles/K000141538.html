Description

Challenges and recommendations related to using optical SFP modules with varying speeds in a High Availability cluster of F5 BIG-IP devices.

Environment

  • In a High Availability (HA) cluster of F5 BIG-IP devices, optical SFP modules with varying speeds are going to be installed.

Cause

The primary concern with using different speed transceivers in Active/Standby cluster arises during failover situations. If the Active device, equipped with a high-speed QSFP transceiver, fails, the Passive device with an SFP transceiver assumes the active role. This can lead to several issues:

  1. Bandwidth limitation: The SFP transceiver has a lower throughput capacity which can restrict the amount of traffic it can handle, particularly if the Active device is managing high-bandwidth applications.
  2. Network congestion: during failover, a sudden influx of traffic directed to the Passive device may overwhelm its capabilities, resulting in congestion, increased latency, and packet loss.

Recommended Actions

To mitigate the risk associated with using mixed-speed transceivers in F5 BIG-IP HA clusters use uniform transceiver speeds to guarantee consistent performance.
