Description

The subscription is renewed, then mapped on MyF5 but the service check date fails to update when the license is reactivated shortly after mapping is completed.

Environment

  • Software upgrade
  • License Reactivation
  • Service Check Date
  • License reactivation attempted shortly after subscription mapping is completed on MyF5.

Cause

The service check date may fail to update during license activation because the backend synchronization is not yet complete after the subscription renewal and mapping on MyF5. The job that updates service contract dates runs periodically, so immediate license reactivation after mapping may result in outdated/unchanged service check dates. 

Answer/Recommended Actions

  1. After renewing the subscription and mapping registration keys in MyF5, wait a few hours to allow backend synchronization jobs to complete.
  2. Reactivate the license keys after sufficient time has passed (typically a few hours).
  3. If the issue persists even after waiting several hours, Submit a support case
