Known Issue

The receiving members of a BIG-IP GTM synchronization group may fail to load synchronized DNS zone information.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP GTM systems are configured in a synchronization group.
  • The Synchronize DNS Zone Files setting is enabled.
  • The ZoneRunner utility is used to make zone changes on one of the synchronization group members.

When using the ZoneRunner utility to make changes to an existing DNS zone, the changes are loaded on the local BIG-IP GTM device and synchronized to other members of the BIG-IP GTM synchronization group. However, the receiving BIG-IP GTM synchronization group members may fail to load the zone information from the .jnl file.

Impact

BIG-IP GTM synchronization group members may fail to resolve zone information as expected.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H434066 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The zone changes appear in the .jnl files on the receiving BIG-IP GTM synchronization group members; however, the zone information does not appear in ZoneRunner.
  • BIG-IP GTM synchronization group members may fail to resolve zone information as expected.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can restart the named process on the receiving BIG-IP GTM synchronization group members. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the command line.
  2. Restart the named process by entering the following command:

    bigstart restart named
