Known Issue

Flow reporting does not occur for unclassified subscriber flows.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have created a BIG-IP PEM policy with one or more classification rules.
  • The BIG-IP PEM policy has flow reporting enabled.
  • Your BIG-IP PEM system processes an unclassified subscriber flow.

Impact

Subscriber data usage reports are inaccurate.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You notice TCP connections are missing from the BIG-IP PEM reporting logs when compared with tcpdump packet captures.
