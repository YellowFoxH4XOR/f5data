Description

The BIG-IP web GUI (Configuration Utility) becomes inaccessible or unresponsive, while CLI access remains functional. This issue occurs when the syslog-ng process enters a problematic state due to repeated connection failures to unreachable remote syslog servers. The issue may also cause system logging to stop, and in rare cases, can lead to system failover or unexpected reboot.

You may see the following type of logging in the messages log:

Feb 10 17:42:54 bigip1.local notice syslog-ng[2993]: Syslog connection established; fd='33', server='AF_INET(10.234.18.75:5140)', local='AF_INET(0.0.0.0:0)'
Feb 10 17:42:54 bigip1.local notice syslog-ng[2993]: Syslog connection established; fd='35', server='AF_INET(10.234.18.12:514)', local='AF_INET(0.0.0.0:0)'
Feb 10 17:42:57 bigip1.local notice syslog-ng[2993]: Syslog connection broken; fd='33', server='AF_INET(10.234.18.75:5140)', time_reopen='60'
Feb 10 17:42:57 bigip1.local notice syslog-ng[2993]: Syslog connection broken; fd='35', server='AF_INET(10.234.18.12:514)', time_reopen='60'
Feb 10 17:43:57 bigip1.local notice syslog-ng[2993]: Syslog connection established; fd='60', server='AF_INET(10.234.18.75:514)', local='AF_INET(0.0.0.0:0)'
Feb 10 17:43:57 bigip1.local notice syslog-ng[2993]: Syslog connection established; fd='66', server='AF_INET(10.234.18.12:514)', local='AF_INET(0.0.0.0:0)'
Feb 10 17:43:57 bigip1.local notice syslog-ng[2993]: Syslog connection broken; fd='60', server='AF_INET(10.234.18.75:514)', time_reopen='60'
Feb 10 17:43:57 bigip1.local notice syslog-ng[2993]: Syslog connection broken; fd='66', server='AF_INET(10.234.18.12:514)', time_reopen='60'

Environment

  • BIG-IP (all modules)
  • syslog-ng

Cause

This issue is caused by Bug ID 1040277

Answer/Recommended Actions

Ensure syslog-ng server configuration is valid, and that the server is reachable. If a remote server is not reachable remove it from the BIG-IP syslog configuration.

If the system has encountered this issue it's important that syslog-ng is restarted if that (or equivalent such as reboot) hasn't already occurred, to resume its normal service and reduce risk of further issues.

bigstart restart syslog-ng
