Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP WebAccelerator may specify the incorrect content length or range for a proxied PDF response, causing PDF linearization to fail, or a corrupt and unopenable PDF file.

Workaround

You can ensure the PDF files are not corrupted by disabling Intelligent Browser Referencing (IBR) in the acceleration policy for the node that matches PDF files. To do so, perform the following procedure:

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP WebAccelerator Configuration utility.
  2. Click WebAccelerator.
  3. Click Policies
  4. Click on the desired acceleration policy to edit.

    Note: Pre-defined Acceleration Policies cannot be modified. You must copy the pre-defined Acceleration Policy and create a User-defined Acceleration Policy.
  5. From the left navigation frame, click the + button to expand the Site node.
  6. Click the + button to expand the Static node.
  7. Click the + button to expand the Documents node.
  8. Select the PDF node.
  9. Select Acceleration Rules from the menu.
  10. Click the Assembly tab.
  11. Clear the Enable Intelligent Browser Referencing check box.
  12. Click Save.
  13. Click Publish.
  14. Click Publish Now.
