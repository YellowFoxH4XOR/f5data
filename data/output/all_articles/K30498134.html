Policy Information

This article applies to SSL Orchestrator 12.x - 13.x. For information about newer versions, refer to the following article:

  • K51003252: F5 SSL Orchestrator software support policy (14.x and later)

To view the F5 software support policy for other F5 products, refer to K8986: F5 product support policies.

SSL Orchestrator products

SSL Orchestrator is delivered by the SSL Orchestrator iApp template for the BIG-IP system together with an SSLO type license.

Note: SSL Orchestrator and its associated hardware platforms were previously known as Herculon. For more information, refer to K11552143: End of Sale announcement for Herculon DDoS Hybrid Defender, Herculon SSL Orchestrator, and Herculon iSeries platform.
