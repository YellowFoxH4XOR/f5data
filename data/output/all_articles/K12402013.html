Known Issue

The Configuration utility may be unable to access Local Traffic Manager policies and policy rules.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You use the Configuration utility or Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) to create a Local Traffic Manager policy that contains one of the following characters in the policy name or policy rule name.
    • The forward slash character (/).
    • The percent symbol (%).
  • You use the Configuration utility to access or delete the affected policy or policy rule.

Impact

The Configuration utility does not execute the action.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When you use the Configuration utility to access or delete the policy or policy rule, the intended action is not executed and the page refreshes or a blank page is displayed.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can use tmsh to access or delete the Local Traffic Manager policy or policy rule.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
