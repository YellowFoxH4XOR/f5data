Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The Summary page incorrectly reports information for BIG-IP systems as follows:

  • The Total Servers number reports redundant pairs as a single unit.
  • If unit 1 in a redundant pair is in a standby state, the redundant pair is reported as Unavailable, rather than Up.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR33735 and CR41849.
