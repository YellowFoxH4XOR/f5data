K57250405: Endpoint inspection for APM 13.1 is ''End of Software Development ''at the end of 2022 and may stop working

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-12-08
Updated
2025-02-06
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP APM: 13.1.X, 13.1.4, 13.1.3, 13.1.1, 13.1.5, 13.1.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K57250405

Description

  • The OPSWAT OESIS libraries for BIG-IP APM 13.1 will stop being updated in 2023.
  • Anyone planning to continue using Endpoint inspection in 2023 should plan to upgrade to a newer BIG-IP APM Version
  • Current versions of Epsec have licence expiry dates of 2025, and should be regularly updated.

Environment

Relevant environmental factors specific to the topic

  • v3v4 OPSWAT OESIS Library 
  • Anti Virus and Firewall Endpoint Inspection features
  • Patch Management and other features are no longer current.

Cause

https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K5903 confirms that the BIG-IP software 13.1 reaches 
End of Software Development on December 31, 2022
End of Technical Support on December 31, 2023

Recommended Actions

As the Endpoint Inspection feature is supplied by a third party to F5, there will be no new updated to this software in 2023 and it may stop working abruptly when the licence certificates expire.
In order to ensure continued active support it is recommended to plan to upgrade to a more recent BIG-IP software before 2023, Upgrading to 15.1 or 16.1 is recommended.

Additional Information

The final supported 13.x version BIG-IP 13.1.5.1 was released with a bundled epsec-1.0.0-1263.0.iso
an update for that was released in early December 2022
