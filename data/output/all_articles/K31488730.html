Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may restart and produce a core file when processing a corrupt Message Passing Interface (MPI) message. This issue occurs under rare internal conditions.

Impact

The tmm process consumes 100 percent of CPU and produces a core file, and systems configured as part of a high availability (HA) group may fail over. In addition, the BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restarting.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The tmm process consumes 100 percent of CPU.
  • The BIG-IP system dumps a TMM core file in the /shared/core directory.
  • The BIG-IP system fails over to its peer.
  • In the /var/log/tmm log file, you observe messages similar to the following example:

    notice MPI NET: Unknown msgtype 64 from 192.168.1.1:58991
