Known Issue

Note: For information about the Assist mode, refer to K4832: Overview of PVA Acceleration.

This is the result of a known issue. If your BIG-IP LTM virtual servers are configured to use the FastL4 profile in Assist mode, incorrect client-side throughput statistics reported by the pvad process will cause a large spike in client throughput to be reported on the throughput graph. As a result, the autoscale feature of the graph autoscales to accommodate the spike, and the throughput graph does not accurately track client-side throughput.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR58721, and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.3 and 9.4.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
