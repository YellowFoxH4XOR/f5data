Description

How to customize the APM Webtop's logout button that appears after you log in to the APM VPN home page (Landing page)

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM
  • Webtop
  • Web access / Clientless

Recommended Actions

1. To change the Color of the Logout button and the label on top of it:


Log in to the Configuration utility.
Navigate to the Customization page:

BIG-IP 13.x and later
Access > Profiles / Policies > Customization > General

BIG-IP 12.x
Access Policy > Customization > General

BIG-IP 11.x
Access Policy > Customization > Advanced

Under Branding Tab:

  •  Navigate to Customization Settings > Webtops 
  •  Click (+) of the Webtops you want to customize
  •  Navigate to Full Webtop Custom Header
  •  Toggle - Custom Header - ON
  •  Logout Button Color - Pick the color
  •  Logout Button Text Color - Pick the color
  •  Click Save

2. To change the label text of the Logout button:


Log in to the Configuration utility.
Navigate to the Customization page:

BIG-IP 13.x and later
Access > Profiles / Policies > Customization > General

BIG-IP 12.x
Access Policy > Customization > General

BIG-IP 11.x
Access Policy > Customization > Advanced

Under Text Tab:

  •  Navigate to Customization Settings > Access profiles
  •  Click (+) of the Access profile you want to customize
  •  Navigate to Common, select Page Header Settings
  •  Modify Disconnect Text from Logout to your custom text
  •  Click Save
