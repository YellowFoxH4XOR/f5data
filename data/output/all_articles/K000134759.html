Topic

This article is to inform customers about cosmetic differences of the front panel for PWR-0149 DC Power Supplies that are utilized in BIG-IP 10XXX platforms. 

Description

The visual difference between the two front panels does not impact the functionality of the power supply.  If you have two power supplies installed in your appliance where one has the Power One words in the front panel and one power supply where there are no words in the front panel, it is acceptable and will not impact the functionality of the platform.
