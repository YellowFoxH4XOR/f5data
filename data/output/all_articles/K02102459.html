Known Issue

You may be unable to delete a topology region.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP GTM system is configured with topology regions.
  • You attempt to delete a topology region that is referenced by a topology record.

    Note: This action should fail due to the topology record association.

  • You attempt to delete a topology region that is not referenced by any topology records.

    Note: This action should be successful.

Impact

You are unable to delete the topology region.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Attempts to delete a topology region after a previous failed attempt (due to the topology record association) fail, and fail at all subsequent attempts.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can restart the mcpd service. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: The BIG-IP GTM system will not process traffic while the mcpd service is restarting.

  1. Log in to the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh).
  2. Type the following command to restart the mcpd service:

    restart /sys service mcpd

  3. To exit the tmsh utility, type the following command:

    quit
