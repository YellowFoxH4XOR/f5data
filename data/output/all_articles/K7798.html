Issue

When attempting to download your license to your FirePass controller, either through Automatic licensing or Manual licensing, you may receive the following error message:

Incorrect license date.
Please contact F5 customer support for assistance

Typically, this error message indicates that the date on the FirePass controller is incorrect. You may have the time on the FirePass controller set to a date that is older than the Licensed date value in the license file.

You can fix this issue by setting the correct time and date on the FirePass controller. To do so, perform the following procedure:

  1. Log on to the Administration Console with the admin account, or an account with privileges to configure the time and date.

  2. Click Device Management.

  3. Click Configuration.

  4. Click Time.

  5. Select the correct time zone from the Time Zone menu.

  6. Click Apply.

  7. Configure either an NTP server in the NTP Server section or manually set the time in the Set date and Time Manually section.

  8. Click Apply.

You can verify that the time was set correctly by looking at the section heading at top of the Device Management: Configuration: Time page. The current time zone and time are displayed.
