Known Issue

In rare circumstances, TMM may core when running BIG-IP WebAccelerator.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP WebAccelerator system is under very heavy load.
  • Multiple clients concurrently read and abort a cached document.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may crash and fail over to another host in the device group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • You may notice TMM core files in the /shared/core directory.
  • You may have unexpected failovers to devices in the BIG-IP device group.
