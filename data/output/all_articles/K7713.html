Topic

The LCD backlight that F5 ships with a BIG-IP switch appliance has a mean life of 10,000 hours. In previous versions of the BIG-IP software, the LCD backlight stays lit indefinitely when the unit is powered on. Over time, this causes the LCD backlight to dim until it is no longer useful.

Beginning with BIG-IP 9.4.1, the LCD backlight has been enhanced to extend the backlight life. The new feature automatically turns the backlight off after 15 minutes of inactivity on the LCD keypad. The backlight is restored by pressing any key on the keypad, which also restarts the 15 minute timer.

F5 has enhanced the following BIG-IP platforms in order to extend the life of the LCD backlight:

  • BIG-IP 1500 (C36)
  • BIG-IP 3400 (C62)
  • BIG-IP 6400 (D63)
  • BIG-IP 6800 (D68)
  • BIG-IP 8400 (D84)
  • BIG-IP 8800 (D88)

F5 Product Development tracked this enhancement as CR75943, and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.4.1 with SCCP version 12.0.4.

Note: BIG-IP 9.3.1 was released after BIG-IP 9.4.1, but includes this enhancement as well as it shipped with SCCP version 12.0.5.
