Known Issue

10 GB SFP+ active copper direct attach cable detection may fail.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are running one of the following BIG-IP platforms:
    • i2000 series platform
    • i4000 series platform
    • HRC-i2800 platform
  • A physical interface of your BIG-IP system uses a 10 GB CBL-0138-01 SFP/SFP+ active copper direct attach cable.

Impact

The interface does not establish a link.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The interface does not establish a link to the upstream device (switch).
  • In the /var/log/ltm directory, you observe an error message similar to the following example:

    err pfmand[<pid>]: 01660016:3: Interface <interface> detected a non 10GbE optic
