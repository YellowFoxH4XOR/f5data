Description

iRule logic can be defined at multiple strategic points within an SSL Orchestrator configuration. An iRule attached at the service level specifically targets traffic flowing across this service. iRules are an extremely powerful tool for manipulation and observation of real time traffic flows. You could, for example, insert an iRule to produce additional logging, insert or remove headers, or to inspect and/or act on some values in the request or response. Keep in mind here that an iRule inserted at a service is interacting with the flow at that service and should not be used to alter traffic flow (routing, NATs, nodes, virtual, etc). iRules can be used to generate a reset or blocking page, and are primarily intended to work at the application layer (ex. HTTP).

Prior to SSL Orchestrator 16.1.x-9.x, you must disable strictness (or unprotect configuration) in the Services tab before you can add an iRule to an SSL Orchestrator service. You must refer to the warnings and directions in the Disabling Strict Updates section of K00805840: SSL Orchestrator known issues and workarounds. Once you have disable strictness (or unprotect configuration), you can proceed to add an iRule to the SSL Orchestrator service. To add an iRule to a service in SSL Orchestrator, perform the following procedure:

Impact of procedure: F5 recommends that you test any such changes during a maintenance window and consider the possible impact on your specific environment. Additionally, adding an iRule increases the resources used by the associated virtual server. Depending on the type and volume of the connections, this processing may introduce noticeable latency. F5 recommends testing any such changes in an appropriate environment.

  1. Log in to your F5 SSL Orchestrator Configuration utility.
  2. Navigate to Local Traffic > Virtual Servers.
  3. Click the virtual server with a name matching ssloS_<service name>-t-4 or ssloS_<service name>-u-4 (for UDP traffic, if needed).
  4. Click the Resources tab.
  5. For iRules, click Manage.
  6. Under Resource Management, find the iRule in the Available list and move it to the Enabled list, then click Finished.
