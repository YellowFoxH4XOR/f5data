Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When you enable telnet and FTP using the Configuration utility, the ports are opened, but the telnet and FTP servers do not start.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR35320 and CR41805. 

Workaround

To work around this issue, enable telnet and FTP using the config command.
