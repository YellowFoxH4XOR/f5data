Known Issue

Multiple IPsec iSession tunnels may cause the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) process to restart and produce a core file.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is configured with multiple IPsec iSession tunnels that use the same Traffic Selector.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from the TMM restart, and devices configured as a high availability (HA) pair may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from the TMM restart.
  • If configured for HA, the affected BIG-IP device fails over to a configured peer device.
  • Your BIG-IP system generates a TMM core file to the /shared/core directory that includes a time stamp that correlates with the traffic interruption.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can configure a separate traffic selector for each tunnel.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
