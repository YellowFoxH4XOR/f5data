Policy Information

A third-party network equipment manufacturer has reported a clock function issue, where loss of clock functionality exists. F5 has conducted research as to whether F5 equipment may be affected by the same semiconductor devices, and has determined that equipment that F5 manufactures is not affected.

F5 has received no direct communication regarding clock functionality from manufacturers whose semiconductor devices are used in F5 products.

The F5 manufacturing quality group closely monitors the field reliability performance of all platform families, and have received no reported issues related to loss of clock functionality.

While investigating this issue, F5 has encountered recent Internet publications, including a device errata that discusses possible loss of clock functionality. You can locate the errata document that a semiconductor device manufacturer has published by searching the Internet for a document titled “System May Experience Inability to Boot or May Cease Operation.” This document refers to the loss of clock functionality on a specific System on Chip (SoC).

If the device referred to in the previously mentioned errata is the cause of the third-party equipment manufacturer’s clock functionality issue, then F5 has determined that F5 products are not affected. F5 has never embedded the device that is discussed in the errata in any F5 platform.

Customer satisfaction is critical to F5, and F5 engages in a closed-loop corrective action quality assurance program.  F5 will provide further communication on this issue as relevant facts become available.
