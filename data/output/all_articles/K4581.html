Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The driver for the network interface LED on the HP BL20p G2 and BL30p blade servers and the HP DL380 G3 server appliance does not work correctly. As a result, the amber and green link and traffic indicators blink in an alternating pattern regardless of the actual traffic or link states.

This issue is cosmetic.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR29269 and CR29296.
