Description

 

Logs are being generated by an AFM policy that is in staging in a route domain context for the same packet which an IPI policy had already dropped at the global context.

Sample log messages would be similar to the following:

Feb 26 12:34:56 bigip.local info tmm[17772]: 23003142 "Feb 26 2024 12:34:56","172.16.50.4","bigip.local","Advanced Firewall Module","F5","17.1.1.3.0.0.5","Global","","/Common/ip-intelligence","10.1.10.20","10.1.10.204","37026","80","No-lookup","No-lookup","unknown","unknown","/Common/External","TCP","0","AFM_test_IP_blacklist","Drop","custom_category","","","","","","","","","","0000000000000000"
Feb 26 12:34:56 bigip.local info tmm[17772]: 23003137 "172.16.50.4","bigip.local","Route Domain","/Common/0","No-lookup","10.1.10.20","No-lookup","10.1.10.204","37026","80","/Common/External","TCP","0","","","","","","","","Staged","/Common/AFM_test_FW_policy","/Common/AFM_test_rule_list:AFM_test_DROP_HTTP_fw_rule","","Drop","Policy","","","0000000000000000","unknown","unknown","unknown","unknown","","","","","No-lookup","No-lookup"

Environment

 

  • BIG-IP AFM

 

Cause

 

The AFM firewall log message is being generated because the firewall policy in the route-domain context is still in staging. A staged policy allows the administrator to evaluate the effect that a policy has on traffic by analyzing the system logs without actually modifying traffic based on the firewall rules. If the BIG-IP administrator is satisfied with how the rules in the staged AFM firewall policy behave, that policy may be moved from staging to enforced. No duplicate logs will be generated from the enforced AFM firewall policy for a packet that would have already been dropped by IPI in the global context.

 

Recommended Actions

 

Move the AFM firewall policy in the route domain context from staging into enforced mode.
