Known Issue

Client connections to a NAT address on the BIG-IP system are reset.

Impact

The NAT address will not pass traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • Client connections to the NAT address fail.
  • A tcpdump reveals that the BIG-IP system resets connections to the NAT address.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 404157 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies To box, located in the top right corner of the article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:

Type of FixFixes Introduced InRelated Articles
Release11.4.0K2200: Most recent versions of F5 software
Hotfix11.3.0 HF5K9502: BIG-IP hotfix matrix

Workaround

Depending on your NAT address requirements, you may be able to work around this issue by configuring a SNAT to handle outbound connections.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
