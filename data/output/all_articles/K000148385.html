K000148385: Policy Diff take too long to complete

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-11-06
Updated
2024-11-06
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP ASM: 17.1.1
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000148385

Description

Compare ASM policies take too long to complete


Environment

Use Policy Diff to compare ASM policies

  • duplicate attack signatures sets found in ASM policy


Cause

Duplicated signature sets are causing the prolong time needed for policy diff to complete


Recommended Actions

Delete duplicate attack signatures sets

  1. Navigate to Security ›› Application Security : Policy Building : Learning and Blocking Settings
  2. Click on Attack Signatures
  3. Click on Change button
  4. Remove any duplicate attack signature sets
  5. Click Change button
  6. Click save button
  7. Click Apply Policy

None
