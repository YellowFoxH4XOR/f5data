Description

In the event of a BGP failure, it is important to collect the data needed to observe the failure and subsequently determine cause.  Here are some steps for you to gather this information quickly. 

Environment

  • Big IP - any platform
  • BGP communication with any device
  • Command line access

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

  1. Enable zebos logging to see the status of the daemon responsible for BGP routing and what was happening on it during the time of the issue.  See K10168, and look for the debug logging section roughly half way into the article.
  2. Log BGP neighbor status changes.  See K13804 for instructions.
  3. Collect a packet capture of tcp port 179 between Big-IP and the peer router[s] to see if traffic was flowing correctly at the time of the issue; use the following syntax:
                     tcpdump -vni 0.0:nnn -s0 -w /shared/tmp/filename-$HOSTNAME.pcap port 179

After performing the above steps, then generate a qkview and be prepared to provide both the packet capture and the qkview to F5 for support as need be.
