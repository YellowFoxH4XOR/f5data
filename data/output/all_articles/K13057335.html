Description

After changing BIG-IP date/time, you may face the following issues:

  • Device group keeps future(wrong) commit time
  • Device group keeps In Sync status after modifying any configurations
  • Unable to run config sync

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM 
  • HA configuration
  • Change the date/time to future, and then back it to correct(current) date/time

Cause

It is due to changing date/time. 

Recommended Actions

To recover it, you may have to follow K13030.
This will clear the commit ID/Time, then run config sync manually.

Additional Information

None.
