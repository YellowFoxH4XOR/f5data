What You'll Learn

How to create a local traffic policy to mitigate a Shellshock attack.

Description

A Shellshock attack refers to a class of exploits that misuse the bash shell through a specifically crafted URL. You can associate a BIG-IP local traffic policy with a virtual server to mitigate a Shellshock attack, where the policy examines requests for a pattern of "() {" in the URI.
