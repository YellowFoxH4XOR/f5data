Known Issue

Changing partition on the Forward Zones page results in an error.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Using the Configuration utility, you create a DNS resolver and forward zone within a partition other than the Common partition.
  • While you are on the Forward Zones page, you change to a different partition.
  • You click on the Properties menu.

Impact

Changing partitions on the Forward Zones page results in an error.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Changing partitions on the Forward Zones page results in an error similar to the following example:

An error has occurred while trying to process your request.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can return to the DNS Resolver list and then change the partition.

Note: This error message is cosmetic and does not impact traffic or the configuration in any manner.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
