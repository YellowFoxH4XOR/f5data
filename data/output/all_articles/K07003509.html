Known Issue

Dynamic routing does not function on interfaces with long names.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are using a dynamic routing configuration on your BIG-IP system.
  • You have an interface, vlan group, or tunnel name which is16 characters or longer.

Impact

Dynamic routing functionality will be impacted on affected interfaces

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • No dynamic routing traffic will be seen on the affected interfaces

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can use interface or VLAN names that have 15 characters or less.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
