Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. A high volume of network traffic on the management (MGMT) port can cause the Switch Card Control Processor (SCCP) to reboot, which causes the BIG-IP system to reboot. For example, under rare circumstances large quantities of broadcast, multicast, or other management traffic bridged through the SCCP can cause the system to reboot.

The following platforms contain the Switch Card Control Processor (SCCP):

  • BIG-IP 1500 (C36)
  • BIG-IP 3400 (C62)
  • BIG-IP 6400 (D63)
  • BIG-IP 6800 (D68)
  • BIG-IP 8400 (D84)
  • BIG-IP 8800 (D88)

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR73564 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 9.1.3, 9.3.1, and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, ASM, GTM, or Link Controller Release Notes.
