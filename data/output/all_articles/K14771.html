Known Issue

The BIG-IP 2200s and 4200v platforms may experience instability when hardware compression is enabled.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The hardware compression card processes large amounts of data.

BIG-IP 11.2.1 and 11.3.0 include a version of the hardware compression SDK, which may cause instability when hardware compression is enabled.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may experience instability.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H431319 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium|High screen.
  • The system logs error messages to the /var/log/ltm file.

    For example:

    crit tmm1[18601]: 01010025:2: Device error: (null) Watchdog on unit 1!
    crit tmm[19408]: 01010025:2: Device error: (null) Watchdog on unit 0!
  • The BIG-IP system may experience instability.
  • The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may exit with a SIGSEGV violation.
