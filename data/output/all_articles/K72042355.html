Known Issue

The sflow_agent may stop responding during a BIG-IP system restart or system shutdown.

This issue occurs when one of the following conditions is met:

  • You restart the BIG-IP system.
  • You shut down the BIG-IP system.
  • The sflow_agent daemon is exiting.

Impact

The sflow_agent daemon may stop responding and sflow data is not be available through SNMP. If the BIG-IP system is in the process of restarting or shutting down, there is no impact.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The sflow_agent daemon may stop responding and produce a core file.
