K4071: The snmpdca log is now rotated

Type
Knowledge
Published
2006-02-17
Updated
2023-02-21
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • Legacy Products
  • BIG-IP 4.X: 4.6.4
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K4071

Topic

In versions prior to 4.5.11 and 4.6.3, the snmpdca log is a single file and will increase in size without limit until deleted.

Starting in versions 4.5.11 and 4.6.3, BIG-IP rotates the snmpdca log and the log never contains more than 24 hours of data.
