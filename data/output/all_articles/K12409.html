Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When making changes to an access profile's language settings on the Properties screen, you will not be prompted to apply the access policy and the change will not be detected until you make a change to a different setting. BIG-IP APM will not register that you have made a change because the system does not inspect the language settings. 

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as ID 345915.

Workaround

You can work around this issue by making a change to a different setting in the applicable access profile. To do so, perform the following procedure:

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP Configuration utility.
  2. Expand Access Policy.
  3. Click on Access Profiles.
  4. Click on the name of the access profile you want to change.
  5. Make a change to the Language Settings section.
  6. Make a change to any other setting.

    For example, change the SSO Configuration drop-down menu from None to HTTP_Basic.
  7. Click the Update button.
  8. Undo the change you made in Step 6.

    For example, change the SSO Configuration drop-down menu from HTTP_Basic back to None.
  9. Click the Update button.
  10. Click the Apply Access Policy link in the upper left-hand corner.
  11. Click on the Apply Access Policy button.
