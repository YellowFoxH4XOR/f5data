Policy Information

F5 has released new BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) 18 Version Plus (V+) licenses that are compatible with software up to BIG-IP 18.x.x. The new BIG-IP VE V+ licenses offer the broadest range of performance options and multi-cloud application services, including the best-value Good, Better, Best offers; provide license portability between supported private and public clouds; support bring your own license (BYOL), pay-as-you-grow, and subscription licenses; and include options for per-app VE and on-demand license pool spin-up with the BIG-IQ system.

With these new enhancements to BIG-IP VE and support for new licensing models, F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of the following legacy BIG-IP VE licenses in the following table, effective January 1, 2018. In addition, F5 is announcing the End of Technical Support (EoTS) for these legacy BIG-IP VE licenses to align and coincide with EoTS of BIG-IP 15.x software, which is currently targeted for December 2024.

Important: If you are a current customer using the following legacy BIG-IP VE licenses, you can run and continue to renew support for any version up to BIG-IP 15.1.x that has not gone to EoTS. Upgrading to versions greater than 15.1.x (which BIG-IP software may not prevent you from doing) are not supported, and your support maintenance contract cannot be renewed in these circumstances. If you require BIG-IP 16.x or later, you must upgrade and buy the latest Version Plus perpetual licenses or move to a Subscription license or an Enterprise License Agreement (ELA).

For the latest BIG-IP 15.x software end of support dates, refer to K5903: BIG-IP software support policy. F5 is providing advance product notification to customers and partners announcing End of Sale and End of Support for the products listed in the following table.
