K000162811: F5OS-A: MAC Address Flapping Caused by IPv6 MLD Traffic from SR-IOV VF Interfaces

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-08-06
Updated
2026-08-06
Applies to
  • F5OS Products
  • rSeries Platforms
  • F5OS-A: 1.8.X, 1.8.4, 1.8.3, 1.8.0
  • rSeries 2000
  • rSeries 4000
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000162811

Description

F5OS-A SR-IOV Virtual Function (VF) interfaces may transmit IPv6 Multicast Listener Discovery (MLD) reports after receiving MLD queries. The reports may use an unspecified IPv6 source address (::) and VF-related MAC addresses, causing upstream switches to relearn MAC addresses repeatedly and report MAC/CAM-table flapping.

Observed traffic may include:

  • ICMPv6 Multicast Listener Reports
  • ICMPv6 Router Solicitations
  • ICMPv6 Neighbor Discovery traffic
  • Repeated packets sourced from VF-related MAC addresses

Environment

  • r4000 or r2000 platform
  • Untagged MLD queries coming in to the front panel ports

Cause

The issue is tracked under Bug ID 2447237.

Recommended Actions

Immediate workaround

Restart NetworkManager:

systemctl restart NetworkManager
