Description

It is possible that a BIG-IP is reboot in a way that barely leaves any traces or clues as to why it happened, and one of the reasons is a reboot triggered from the ESXi hypervisor.

This can be identified by looking at /var/log/vmware-vmsvc.log file, which contains the following relevant messages:

[Aug 19 15:36:18.017] [ message] [powerops] Executing script: '/etc/vmware-tools/poweroff-vm-default'
<snip>
[Aug 19 15:36:55.616] [ message] [powerops] Executing script: '/etc/vmware-tools/poweron-vm-default'

Note: These messages will not be found  when the cause of the reboot is other than a reboot from the ESXi hypervisor. The log file /var/log/vmware-vmsvc.log was introduced on version 12.x

Environment

  • BIG-IP VE running on ESXi.
  • No recent changes made on the BIG-IP VE.
  • Silent reboot

Cause

The BIG-IP was reboot from the hypervisor.

Recommended Actions

Perform a review on the hypervisor to look for any signs of the BIG-IP VM being reboot.
