Known Issue

In unique circumstances, if you use the TCP::collect iRules command, connections may stall.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You use the TCP::collect command within an iRule that is associated with a virtual server.
  • The first packet that the system receives after the SYN carries data.
  • The initial sequence number in the SYN, plus the length of the data in the first packet, plus 1, is greater than or equal to 2^31.

Note: BIG-IP APM VDI profiles use the TCP::collect command internally; therefore, virtual servers with VDI profiles may be affected.

Impact

  • TCP connections may fail and disrupt traffic.
  • The BIG-IP Configuration utility pages may stop responding.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • You observe excessive SSL connection handshake failures.
  • In the /var/log/ltm log file, you observe an error message similar to the following example:

    warning tmm3[<pid>]: 01260009:4: Connection error: ssl_hs_vfy_pms:10370: Invalid PMS (80)

  • The BIG-IP Configuration utility may stop responding when administrative users attempt to access the Device Management > Overview page.
