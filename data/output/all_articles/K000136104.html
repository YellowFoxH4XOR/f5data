K000136104: Virtual Servers and Pools health status became unknown unexpectedly

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-09-27
Updated
2023-09-27
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 16.1.3
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000136104

Description

You have a device group and you notice that most of the virtual servers, pools, and nodes health status unexpectedly change to unknown in active and standby devices. Besides this, you do not notice any impact on your applications from the end user's perspective, and there is network connectivity between the Virtual Servers and pool members. A reboot seems does not solve the problem.

Environment

  • LTM, High Availability.
  • Virtual Servers, Pools, and nodes.
  • Health Monitors.

Cause

Unknown at the time of publication.

Recommended Actions

After discarding any possible network connectivity issue in your LAN environment, restart the bigd daemon (responsible for providing system health checks).

bigstart restart bigd

If this does not solve the problem, continuing with the force load of the mcpd to reload the configuration is known to fix the problem:

K13030: Forcing the mcpd process to reload the BIG-IP configuration

Note: Apply these steps on the standby box or execute them during the maintenance window as these actions can have an impact on traffic processing.
