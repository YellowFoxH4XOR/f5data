Known Issue

ZebOS IS-IS may not propagate routes to peer devices when the metric-style is configured as wide.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP system is licensed with Advance Routing and ZebOS IS-IS is enabled.
  • The ZebOS IS-IS metric-style setting is configured as wide.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may not propagate routes to peer devices. This may result in services outages or routing issues.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • With ZebOS ISIS debug enabled, you may see an error message in the log file /var/log/zebos.log that appears similar to the following example:

    IS-IS: SPF[ipi:L2:IPv4]: IP extended reachability with invalid prefix (0.0.0.0)
