Known Issue

The BIG-IP APM configuration may become unavailable after a failover event.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have installed BIG-IP APM in chassis systems.
  • The BIG-IP APM systems are configured in a Sync-Failover device group.

If the active BIG-IP APM system in a high availability (HA) chassis configuration fails over to the standby system, the previously active system may lose the ability to process the access policy. When this issue occurs, a subsequent failback event can cause a traffic disruption.

Impact

The BIG-IP APM system is unable to process the access policy and users are unable to connect to the BIG-IP APM system.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H509860 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium page.
  • Users are unable to connect to the BIG-IP APM system after a failover event.
  • Users are presented with an error message similar to the following example when attempting to connect to the BIG-IP APM system:

    Access policy configuration has changed on gateway. Please login again to comply with new access policy configuration

Workaround

You may be able to work around this issue by re-applying the access policy in the BIG-IP APM system. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP APM Configuration utility.
  2. Navigate to Local Traffic > Access Policy > Access Profiles.
  3. Next to the name of the access policy you want to re-apply, select the check box.
  4. Click Apply Access Policy.
