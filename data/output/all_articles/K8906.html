Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Additional monitor instances are started on the VIPRION system after changing the monitor properties. For example, after modifying the monitor properties, such as the interval or timeout settings, BIG-IP LTM starts additional instances of the same monitor on each active blade.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR99829.

Additionally, Hotfix-BIGIP-9.6.1-HF1 has been issued for BIG-IP 9.6.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
