Description

The in-discards counter is constantly increasing on the standby F5OS controller management interface.

 

Environment

- Velos chassis running two controllers

 

Cause

The in-discards counter incrementing on the management port of the standby controller is expected, and the standby controller is functioning as designed.

Management traffic follows a specific path: from the customer's management switch to the management NIC, and then to the control plane switch (inside the controller).

Each controller's control plane switch is cross-connected to the other via an internal LAG (Link Aggregation) port. This design enhances resilience and provides redundant links for each controller's management port.

To prevent Layer 2 (L2) loops from occurring, the standby controller disables forwarding on its management port, resulting in the port discarding incoming frames (in-discards).

After a failover, both active and standby controllers show an increase in ingress packets, but only the active controller will show an increase in egress packets. As forwarding is disabled on the standby controller, it will not have any egress packets.

 

Recommended Actions

None.
