Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. BIG-IP WebAccelerator systems containing large configurations and processing high levels of traffic may experience a slow memory leak when serving large files from the cache. As a result, this behavior may cause the pvac process to dump core.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR129586 and it was fixed in BIG-IP WebAccelerator version 10.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP WebAccelerator release notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in BIGIP-10.0.1-378.0-HF3 issued for BIG-IP WebAccelerator version 10.0.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the cumulative hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
