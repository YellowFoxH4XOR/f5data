Known Issue

The output from the tmsh show running-config command incorrectly includes default objects.

Impact

Locating certain objects, such as specific user-configured objects, may be more difficult due to additional output.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When running the tmsh show running-config command, you notice that default objects (in addition to user-configured objects) are displayed.
