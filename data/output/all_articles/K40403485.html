Known Issue

BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) may incorrectly report the bandwidth utilization exceeded log message.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • BIG-IP VE is licensed with limited throughput.
  • The system is running multiple Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) processes.
  • Only some of the total number of TMM processes are handling a majority of the traffic.

When you license BIG-IP VE with limited throughput, it may incorrectly report that bandwidth utilization exceeded due to how it performed bandwidth checking. Total throughput is divided equally among TMM processes, BIG-IP VE currently applies the threshold limit against the individual TMM throughput slice. As a result, BIG-IP VE reports an error message if a TMM process exceeds its throughput slice, even though other TMM processes may not have reached their bandwidth limit and the total system throughput has not exceeded.

Impact

There is no impact; this log message is benign.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • BIG-IP VE reports a message in the /var/log/ltm file that appears similar to the following example:

    notice tmm[15529]: 01010045:5: Bandwidth utilization is 4141 Mbps, exceeded 75% of Licensed 5000 Mbps
