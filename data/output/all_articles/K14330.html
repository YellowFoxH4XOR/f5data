Known Issue

vCMP hosts with high disk I/O may experience instability.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP host is provisioned for vCMP
  • You have deployed one or more vCMP guests 
  • One or more of your vCMP guests is performing disk I/O intensive operations, such as MySQL operations

This issue occurs due to disk I/O requests on vCMP guests configured to run in real-time, starving all other concurrent I/O requests of system resources. BIG-IP modules that make use of MySQL include ASM and WebAccelerator.

Impact

Your BIG-IP host may unexpectedly reboot and fail over to a standby device in the device group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • You may experience system reboots due to a system watchdog timeout.
  • You may see Clock Advanced messages in the /var/log/ltm file. For example:

    notice tmm[11021]: 01010029:5: Clock advanced by 137 ticks
  • One or more daemons may experience a heartbeat failure as documented in K9233: Overview of daemon heartbeat failover for BIG-IP VIPRION systems.
