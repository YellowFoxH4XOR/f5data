Known Issue

BIG-IP APM webtops may fail to render Citrix resources.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP APM system is configured to integrate with Citrix XML Brokers.
  • The BIG-IP APM Citrix resource is configured with a pool as the Destination.
  • The BIG-IP APM Citrix resource has Auto Logon enabled with Kerberos Single Sign-On.

Impact

Users are unable to access Citrix applications through the BIG-IP APM system.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H458170 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium|High screen.
  • Citrix applications brokered by the BIG-IP APM system fail to render in the webtop.

Workaround

Impact of workaround: Performing the following workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.

If you use a single Citrix XML Broker for the BIG-IP APM Citrix resource, you can work around this issue by specifying the IP address or host name of the Citrix XML Broker as the Destination in the General Properties of the resource, rather than a pool. There is no workaround when you use a pool as the Destination.
