Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When connectivity to the quorum disk is lost, the recovery actions involve sequential unmount and mount operations, which issue a sequence of add/remove NAT actions that are propagated to the respective high availability peer NSM. 

If propagation of these actions encounter latency longer than the unmount/mount operation sequence, a race condition may occur. As a result, an NSM may core and reboot.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as ARX ID 36139 and it was fixed in ARX version 5.2.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the ARX release notes.
