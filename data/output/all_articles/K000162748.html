K000162748: BGP Route Advertisement May Be Delayed After Graceful Restart

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-08-04
Updated
2026-08-04
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.1.3, 17.5.1, 21.1.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000162748

Description

BGP route advertisements may be delayed after a graceful restart when a peer is configured with advertisement-interval 0. Route advertisement delays can affect network convergence time and potentially impact traffic routing during BGP graceful restart scenarios.

The issue is related to improper handling of peer End-of-RIB (EOR) list during graceful restart when advertisement interval is set to 0.

Environment

  • Product: Advanced Routing Module (ARM)
  • Feature: BGP
  • Condition: BGP graceful restart
  • Peer setting: Advertisement interval (advertisement-interval) configured to 0

Cause

  • BGP routes are not advertised immediately after a graceful restart.
  • Route advertisement experiences unexpected delays.
  • Network convergence may be slower than expected after BGP graceful restart.

 

Recommended Actions

  • Verify the BGP session state when route convergence is delayed after graceful restart.
  • Confirm whether affected peers use advertise-interval = 0. Consider a different value.
  • Apply the available fix for this issue.

Additional Information
