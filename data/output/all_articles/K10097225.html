Known Issue

BIG-IP Analytics may fail to include statistics from a previously active device when generating a report.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:
  • The affected system is configured as part of a device group.
  • The affected system was previously the active device.
  • The affected system is now the standby device when you generate the report.

Impact

BIG-IP Analytics generates an inaccurate report as a result of excluding statistics from a previously active device.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • BIG-IP Analytics generates inaccurate reports that exclude statistics from a previously active device.
