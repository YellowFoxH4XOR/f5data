Description

When you run tmsh show sys service you see that httpd and tomcat are down
If you review the contents of /var/log you find that logging has stopped

When you restart httpd you see the following Alarm clock message.
# bigstart restart httpd
Stopping httpd:                                            [  OK  ]
/bin/bash: line 1: 19814 Alarm clock             /usr/sbin/httpd -DTrafficShield -DAVRUI -DWebAccelerator -DSAM
Starting httpd:                                            [FAILED]

There is also intermittent Broadcast messages of
logger[26961]: Re-starting tomcat

Environment

  • BIG-IP vCMP Guest

Cause

Unknown at time of publication

Recommended Actions

  1. Restart syslog-ng, and then restart httpd and tomcat:
    tmsh restart sys service syslog-ng
    tmsh restart sys service httpd tomcat
