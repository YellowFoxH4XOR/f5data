Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Fragmented ICMP ping requests will not pass through a SNAT or a virtual server.

In addition, once a SNAT has received a fragmented ICMP ping request from a node, the SNAT may fail to respond to any subsequent ping requests from that node until the SNAT receives an ICMP ping request from another node that is not fragmented.

Fragmented ICMP ping requests that are sent to BIG-IP's self IP address rarely fail.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR30731.
