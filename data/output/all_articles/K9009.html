Topic

You can invalidate cached content in a BIG-IP WebAccelerator cache by using a perl iControl script to perform the desired ESI invalidation. This script is maintained on the DevCentral website in the BIG-IP WebAccelerator codeshare:

https://devcentral.f5.com/Wiki/WebAccelerator.InvalidateCachedContent.ashx

Note: A separate DevCentral login is required to access this content; you will be redirected to authenticate or register (if necessary).
