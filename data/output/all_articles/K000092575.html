Description

After software upgrade LDAP authentication failed, from the logs we noticed the following log messages,

/var/log/ltm

Nov 1 04:41:48 slot2/mibigip.com info nslcd[11219]: caught signal SIGTERM (15), shutting down

Nov 1 13:01:43 slot1 err nslcd[11463]: [8b4567] <authc="userx"> ldap_result() timed out

Nov 1 13:03:35 slot1 err nslcd[11463]: [7b23c6] <authc="userx"> ldap_result() timed out

Nov 1 04:07:41 slot1/mibigip.com err httpd[23185]: pam_ldap(httpd:auth): error reading from nslcd: Timer expired

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • Software Upgrade
  • LDAP configured for management authentication

Cause

Issue is unknown at the time of publication

 Recommended Actions

Please follow the workaround below to resolve the issue

Impact of procedure: Using the following commands should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Restart the nslcd daemon with the below command 

          # systemctl restart nslcd 

       2. Wait for 15 minutes and then try to authenticate again.
