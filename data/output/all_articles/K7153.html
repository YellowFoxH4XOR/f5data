Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The xbuf cache holds data that the TMM sends and receives on the network. Under certain conditions, the xbuf_scanf process can append extra characters to an IP address or MAC address, which will cause problems with persistence and load balancing.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR70635 and CR70685 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.1.3, 9.2.5, 9.3, and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
