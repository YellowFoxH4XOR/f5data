Known Issue

IPsec tunnel traffic from BIG-IP systems to peer systems may intermittently fail and then later resume after some time.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The peer IPsec device is a Cisco system.

This issue occurs when the systems negotiate more than one pair of IPsec SAs.

Impact

The IPsec tunnel intermittently fails to pass traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • IPsec traffic intermittently stops and then recovers after a certain period of time
