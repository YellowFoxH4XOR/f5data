K11210: The deletion of pre-logon sequences must be manually synchronized between the active and standby controller

Updated

Applies to

  • FirePass: 7.0.0, 6.1.0, 6.0.3
Source

Topic

When a pre-logon sequence is deleted on the active FirePass controller, you need to manually synchronize the active FirePass controller with the standby. However, newly created pre-logon sequences will synchronize automatically.

As a precautionary measure, F5 Networks recommends that you manually synchronize the active and standby units after the removal of a pre-logon sequence. Manual synchronization will prevent potential issues in the event that the standby FirePass controller is promoted to an active state. Additionally, in the event that a pre-logon sequence is accidentally removed, the FirePass administrator may recover quickly.

Note: For a manual synchronization to work on FirePass version 6.0.3, cumulative hotfix HF-603.4.3 or later must be installed.
