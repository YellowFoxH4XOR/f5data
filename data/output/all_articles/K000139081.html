K000139081: BIG-IP VE with 10 Mb/s throughput lab licenses and 8 TMM threads have lower maximum throughput on any thread

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-03-28
Updated
2024-04-04
Applies to

  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.1.X, 16.1.X, 15.1.X, 15.1.5, 15.1.6, 15.1.7, 15.1.8, 15.1.9, 15.1.10, 15.1.0, 16.1.1, 16.1.2, 16.1.3, 16.1.4, 17.1.0, 17.1.1, 16.1.0, 15.1.1, 15.1.2, 15.1.3, 15.1.4
Source

Description

Throughput on any single TMM thread is limited to a maximum 1 Mb/s for a flow or group of flows sharing the same thread where the BIG-IP VE has 8 TMM threads and is licensed with a 10 Mb/s throughput lab license.

Environment

  • BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE)
  • Lab license
  • 8 TMM threads

Cause

VE with 10 Mb/s throughput licence cannot allocate more than 1 Mb/s throughput to any TMM thread if there are eight TMM threads or greater.

Recommended Actions

If it is important to be able to have more than 1Mb/s throughput on an individual flow, or group of flows on a TMM thread, reduce the number of TMM threads to 4 or fewer. With 4 TMMs, up to 6 Mb/s should be available on an individual thread if other threads use less, so that total bandwidth use still is 10 Mb/s or less, and with 2 TMMs up to 8Mb/s should be available on busier thread. With a single thread, the full 10Mb/s should be available.

You can do this via either one of these methods:

Note: Both methods are service affecting when carried out.

A) Reduce number of vCPU cores allocated to the VE in the hypervisor, while the VE is not running.

B) Use this DB setting:

tmsh modify sys db provision.tmmcount value 4

Followed by a restart of the TMM process:

tmsh restart sys service tmm
