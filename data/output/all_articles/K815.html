Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The Solutions Guide is installed on the BIG-IP Link Controller; however, the link from the Configuration utility page was omitted.

F5 Networks Product Development is tracking this issue as CR24946 and CR41890. 

Workaround

You can view the BIG-IP Link Controller Solutions Guide in PDF or HTML format on AskF5:

BIG-IP Link Controller manuals

Or use the following link, replacing <IP address> with the IP address for your BIG-IP or 3-DNS Controller:

https://<IP Address>/doc/lc_solutions.pdf
