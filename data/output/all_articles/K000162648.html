K000162648: Packet drops interface tx_hw_drop and tmm/ndal_tx_stats on BIG-IP VE, with no rx drops.

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-07-29
Updated
2026-07-29
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000162648

Description

BIG-IP VE may have packet drops for outbound traffic

  • Interface tx_hw_drop 
  • TMM statistics tmm/ndal_tx_stats

Environment

  • BIG-IP VE running on any hypervisor
  • High volume of traffic ingress BIG-IP VE

Cause

The hypervisor Physical NIC not draining TX packets from the BIG-IP VE.

Answer/Recommended Actions

  1. Advise customer to tune Physical NIC TX ring buffer.
  2. Increase BIG-IP VE NIC TX ring buffer as a temporary measure.
  3. See  article K28710001 for details.

Additional Information

See article K28710001 for increasing BIG-IP VE TX ring buffer. Note that the article references ESXi but this applies to any hypervisor provided the correct NIC PCI device ID is used. For instructions on how to find PCI device ID see K10142141.
