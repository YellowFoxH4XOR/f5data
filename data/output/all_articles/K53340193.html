Description

The article describes behavior that could be observed on a BIG-IP Virtual Edition. Mcpsync warning log messages appear announcing that the smartctl was not found, which could definitely raise some questions.

Environment

  • BIG-IP VE / VCMP
  • Upgrading VE / VCMP devices from older versions to 15.1.6 or higher versions
  • Mcpsync warning log messages similar to: 
    warning mcpsync[30336]: tm_install::TMOS::TMOS_binary: cannot find program: smartctl

Cause

ID1154021. These log messages are something that is left over from hardware code used for checking hdd quality. It is the self-monitoring, analysis, and reporting technology tool. The warnings that appear after upgrading can be ignored as they cannot cause any harm.

Recommended Actions

None at the moment. ID1154021 is still under investigation regarding a possible fix.

None.
