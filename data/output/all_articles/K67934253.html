Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may drop fragmented IPv4 packets when packet filtering is enabled.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is running 11.5.4, 11.6.0 HF6, or 11.6.1.
  • The packet filtering feature has been enabled.
  • Your BIG-IP system receives a fragmented IPv4 packet.

Impact

Established connections may appear to stop responding or new connections may fail to establish.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Intermittently, established connections appear to stop responding or new connections fail to establish when processed by your BIG-IP system.
  • Your BIG-IP system does not forward IPv4 fragments with a non-zero fragment offset field.
