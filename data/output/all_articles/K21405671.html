Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may erroneously assert an active signal on the serial failover cable.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is configured as part of a high availability (HA) pair with a peer unit using hardwired serial cable failover.
  • The HA status of the affected BIG-IP system is standalone.

The HA status of a system can become standalone if the configuration is reset or if the system is replaced by a Return Materials Authorization (RMA) unit. When the affected system asserts an active signal while the status is standalone, the peer unit will transition from active to standby.

Impact

Traffic processing is disrupted.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Traffic is interrupted.
  • The HA status of the affected system is standalone and the peer system's HA status is standby.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can temporarily disable serial cable failover on the peer active unit by modifying the failover.usetty01 database variable. To do so, refer to K13819: Disabling hardwired failover using tmsh.

You can re-enable serial cable failover on the peer active unit when the affected BIG-IP system is ready to rejoin the HA configuration. To do so, refer to K13819: Disabling hardwired failover using tmsh and modify the value of the failover.usetty01 database variable to enable instead of disable.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
