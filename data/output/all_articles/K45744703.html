Issue

Error Message

warning clusterd[<PID>]: 013a0016:4: Blade <blade number> quorum state decreasing from <state> to <state>.

In this error message, note the following:

  • <PID> is the process ID for the clusterd process.
  • <blade number> is the blade number being reported.
  • <state> is the reported blade's quorum state.

For example:

warning clusterd[6492]: 013a0016:4: Blade 2 quorum state decreasing from RUNNING to QUORUM.

Message Location

You may encounter this message in the following location:

  • The /var/log/ltm file

Description

This message occurs when one of the following conditions are met:

  • A blade (primary or otherwise) is offline.
  • You remove, disable, or shut down a blade from the chassis or you shut down the entire cluster.
  • The clusterd process is disconnected from its local mcpd process.
  • A member of the cluster claims another blade to be primary; this creates a primary blade conflict in the cluster.

Impact

This is a notification of the change in the quorum state.

Recommended Actions

If the blade shutdown or offline condition is unexpected,  the blade may be experiencing other issues. You can review the logs to determine the events prior to the unexpected shutdown or offline condition that may have potentially caused the behavior.
