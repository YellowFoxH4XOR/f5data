Known Issue

PEM subscriber sessions may not work as expected with RADIUS.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • PEM subscriber traffic arrives on a non-default route domain (route domains other than route domain 0).

Traffic arriving on a Virtual Local Area Network (VLAN) that is associated with any non-zero route domain fails to match the RADIUS session. This may cause issues with identifying the subscriber traffic and applying the correct policies.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may have issues identifying subscriber traffic and applying the correct policies.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The system fails to identify subscriber traffic, and does not apply the correct policies.
