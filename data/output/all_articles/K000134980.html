Source

Description

Adding Secondary BIG-IQ to Primary hangs on "Configuring Database Replication"

errors in /var/log/ha_pgbasebackup.log:

pg_basebackup: could not connect to server: SSL error: certificate verify failed

 

Environment

  • BIG-IQ 7.x CM
  • Unable to add secondary BIG-IQ CM
  • BIG-IQ deployed more than two years ago

Cause

Big-IQ V7.1, the SSL certificate has not been updated for quite some time.

Note: By default the ha certificate will be valid for 2 years. 

Recommended Actions

Regenerate certificates on both devices by using following commands:

  • On BIG-IQ-01:

ha_reset -f 10.0.0.1

ha_generate_certs 10.0.0.1

  • On Big-IQ-02:

ha_reset -f 10.0.0.2

ha_generate_certs 10.0.0.2
