Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) process on some Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP) guests may produce a core file shortly after the BIG-IP system starts.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have deployed vCMP guests.
  • You have installed vCMP guests running a mix of versions BIG-IP 12.1.0 through 12.1.2 and any other BIG-IP versions on a single vCMP host.

Impact

The affected vCMP guest may temporarily fail to process traffic and fail over to another vCMP guest, if one is configured.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The vCMP guest stops responding and fails over to a standby guest or another host in the device group.
  • You observe a TMM core file in the /shared/core directory.
