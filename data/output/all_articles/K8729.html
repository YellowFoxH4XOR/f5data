Topic

On August 1, 2007, F5 began shipping the BIG-IP 6400 platform with new AMD Opteron processors, which run at 2.2 GHz. Prior to August 2007, the BIG-IP 6400 platform shipped with dual AMD Opteron processors, which ran at 2.0 GHz. The 2.0 GHz CPUs have since reached their End of Life.

F5 has thoroughly tested the new Opteron CPUs on the 6400 platform; the new CPUs introduce a small performance improvement over the previous CPUs, but are otherwise identical to their predecessors.

Note: BIG-IP 6800 and 6400 platforms shipped after July 2008 require a hotfix to run version 9.1.x. For more information, refer to K9002: BIG-IP 6800 and 6400 platforms shipped after July 2008 require a hotfix to run version 9.1.x.

F5 Product Development is tracking this change as CR99299.
