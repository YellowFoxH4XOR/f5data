Description

You might experience services down, and log messages indicating that inode in /var is full
You are also experiencing high swap

Environment

  • LTM
  • APM
  • F5 TMOS VE
  • inode full in /var
  • Services (such as: httpd_sam, httpd_apm) might be down

Cause

Not enough memory

Recommended Actions

Increase memory 8GB per provisioned module
