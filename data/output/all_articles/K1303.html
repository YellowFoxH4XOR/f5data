K1303: Behavior of BIG-IP 2000 and BIG-IP 5000 switch appliances during power interruptions

Topic

During a power outage, or other power event, the power supply of the BIG-IP 5000 or BIG-IP 2000 enters into standby mode to protect its components. This behavior occurs only if the power outage or event lasts .5 to 3 seconds.

When the power supply goes into standby mode, the power LED on the front of the BIG-IP displays red and the unit is not responsive.

To return BIG-IP to an operational state, turn the power switch off, and then back on again.

Important: For complete redundancy and high availability, F5 Networks recommends that you deploy BIG-IP 2000 and BIG-IP 5000 switch appliances in active/standby pairs with each using a different power source.
