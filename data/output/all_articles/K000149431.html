K000149431: Resolving PSU Status Power Supply Mismatch Alert on F5OS rSeries Systems

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-01-24
Updated
2025-01-24
Applies to
  • F5OS
  • F5OS-A: 1.7.X, 1.8.X, 1.8.0, 1.7.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000149431

Description

The system experiences a critical fault with PSU1, indicated by the "PSU Status Power Supply Mismatch" alert. Example logs show the following errors:

priority="Err" msgid=0x098200000000001a msg="Component Attribute Changed" component="appliance attribute="psu:status:power-supply-mismatch" severity="Critical" value="0x00000000" interface="diag-controller"
priority="Err" msgid=0x098200000000001c msg="Component Health Changed" component="appliance/hardware" severity="Critical" interface="diag-controller"

Additionally, the PSU in slot 2 is identified to belong to a different series (r5000 series) which should not be interchanged with the r2000 series PSUs.

Environment

  • F5OS-A
  • PSU Mismatch alerts
  • System has  PSUs from differing series installed 

Cause

The issue is due to the installation of a PSU from the r5000 series in an r2000 series platform, causing a "PSU Status Power Supply Mismatch" fault.

Recommended Actions

Make sure the PSU in all slot 1 and 2 are using the correct part-number meant for the F5 hardware version as per article K36588206 suggestion.
