Description

Client machines are fitted with disks compliant with OPAL 2.0 standard.

The "Hard Drive Encryption" check, from the APM Access Policy, fails to detect the hard drives are encrypted.

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM
  • EPSEC / client side checks
  • Hard Drive Encryption

Cause

EPSEC "Hard Drive Encryption" will attempt to detect some encryption software running within the context of the Operating System.

While OPAL 2.0 standard will perform encryption at BIOS/firmware level.

This is not a supported scenario.

Recommended Actions

Please relax the "Hard Drive Encryption" constraint, from the APM AccessPolicy.

None.
