Getting started in the certification program

To get started, create a new account in the Education Services Portal (certification.f5.com).

Where to schedule an F5 exam

F5 Certified! exams are delivered online and in-person. For details about scheduling exams, refer to When can I schedule/take my exam?

All test delivery providers require a valid, signed, government-issued identification for exam check-in. Please review the requirements for the test delivery provider before checking in for an appointment. The name on your ID must match the name listed on your F5 Certified account. If it does not match, email F5 Certified! support at support@mail.education.f5.com.

You will receive a preliminary pass/fail grade immediately after completing your exam. Your validated score will be posted in the Education Services Portal within 48 hours.

Understanding the exam retake policy

The exam retake policy can be found here: What is the F5 Exam Retake Policy?

Understanding scaled scoring

All F5 exams are scored on a scaled-score basis, where your score will range from a possible 100 to 350 points. All F5 exams are calibrated for a passing score of 245 on that scale.

There are advantages to the scaled score model. A scaled cut score remains stable across all forms and versions of the same exam. Exam forms that contain different items are all aligned to the same specifications. However, when individual items differ in difficulty, forms may also differ in difficulty. Scaled scores take these differences into account and ensure that the reported scores across exam forms and versions have the same meaning regardless of difficulty. Fair and consistent decisions can then be made about exam results regardless of the exam form or version.

Rescheduling exams

You can schedule, reschedule, or cancel exam appointments via the Education Services Portal.

Any changes to an exam registration must be completed at least 24 hours before your exam appointment. Cancelling or rescheduling an exam less than one business day before your appointment, or missing an exam appointment, may result in forfeiting your exam fees. F5 cannot reschedule your exam. For detailed instructions, refer to How do I reschedule or cancel an exam appointment?

Finding your digital badges

After achieving certification, you can access digital badges for your email signature or social media profiles. Badges include meta data about your certification and are only accessible while your certification is current.

  1. Select Login to Credly at the top of the home page.
  2. Select Sign in to Credly.

The first time you access your badge, you need to create a Credly account (or sign in if you already have an account).

Understanding certification expirations

F5 Certifications are valid for two years.

You will receive an email notification 90 days before a certification expiration date. The email will include exam scheduling and eligibility information. These reminder emails will be sent every 30 days until your certification expires.

Certification expiration dates and status can be found in your profile in the Education Services Portal.

For information about renewing a certification, refer to How do I renew or extend my Certification?

Important: Achieving or renewing an F5 certification will automatically renew or extend any prerequisite certifications.

Your expiration date is exactly two years from your active date, which is the date you achieved certification. If you pass the expiration date without recertifying, you are no longer certified; however, you have a 30-day grace period to reactivate your certification.

The final renew date for your certification is the end of the 30-day grace period.

Note: When you reach your expiration date, you are not certified, eligible for higher-level exams, nor eligible for any benefits tied to certification.
