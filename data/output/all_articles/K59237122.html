Known Issue

Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) certificate forging causes the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) to reset.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The server side completes the SSL handshake.
  • The client-side SSL starts to forge a server certificate.

Impact

Some contexts change due to race condition and traffic is disrupted while TMM restarts.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • In an SSL offload environment, a rare race condition within SSL certificate forging causes TMM to reset.
