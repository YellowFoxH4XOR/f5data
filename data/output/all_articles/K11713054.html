Description

You can integrate the MobileSafe software development kit (SDK) with apps on mobile devices to detect and prevent malware attacks. You can obtain a copy using a download link provided by your local F5 representative. For more information, contact your local F5 representative.

For information about MobileSafe SDK support for the Android and iOS operating systems, refer to the following table.
