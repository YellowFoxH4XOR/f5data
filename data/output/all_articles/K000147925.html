K000147925: How to ensure route prefixes are not filtered on a BGP session with F5XC?

Description

  • You acquire new ranges of IP addresses, or you become a transit for a new peer, or an existing peer of yours might also acquire new IP addresses.
  • Once you or your peer start using the new IP addresses, you will be announcing them on the BGP sessions you have with us
  • For security reasons, best documented by the MANRS initiative, it is important that (just like any other peer on the Internet) we filter the routes/prefixes that you announce to us and we re-advertise, and only accept what is legitimate
  • Every 24h, a process starts on our side that looks up information about our peers in the Regional Internet Registries databases and picks up information to update filters on our routers.
  • Below the steps you need to take to ensure that our filters match the routes you want us to accept.

Environment

  • Regional Internet Registry (RIR) interface

Procedure

Ensure that:

  1. Any peer that you advertise routes for is present in your AS-SET
  2. Any route that you advertise as originating from your AS has a corresponding route object and a valid ROA.
  3. Any of the routes you advertise on behalf of your peers follows the same rules as above.

Once this is done, you can expect our filters to be updated within the next 24 hours.
