Known Issue

BIG-IP APM 13.1.3.6, 14.1.4, 15.1.2.1, and 16.0.1.1 require APM Clients 7.1.8.5, 7.1.9.8 or 7.2.1.1, or later, in their respective software branches for VPN browser clients to be able to establish a connection.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is running the following BIG-IP versions or later, in their respective software branches.
    • 13.1.3.6
    • 14.1.4
    • 15.1.2.1
    • 16.0.1.1
  • Your BIG-IP system is running the following APM Clients versions, or earlier, in their respective software branches:
    • 7.1.8.4
    • 7.1.9, 7.1.9.7
    • 7.2.1

For more information about BIG-IP APM and APM Clients versioning, refer to K13757: BIG-IP Edge Client version matrix.

Impact

Network access VPN users are unable to establish a connection when using the browser.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Network access VPN users observe that the browser-based VPN components fail to launch and a VPN connection cannot be established.

  • You may observe an error message similar to the following in a pop-up dialog from your browser:

    F5 VPN - Your session could not be established

    Your session could not be established.

    The session reference number: nnnnnnnn

    Application will be closed
