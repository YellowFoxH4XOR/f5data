Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The standby BIG-IP LTM does not re-establish mirrored FastL4 connections under certain conditions. This issue occurs when the standby BIG-IP LTM marks one or more nodes as down and then marks the node or nodes as up. As a result, the Action On Service Down setting is set to reject in the pool. Under these conditions, the FastL4 connection flow is not re-mirrored from the active BIG-IP LTM to the standby system, and the connections will be lost in the event of a failover.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR64672 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM versions 9.1.3, 9.2.4, 9.3.0, and 9.4.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.
