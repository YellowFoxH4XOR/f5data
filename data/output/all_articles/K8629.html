K8629: OPSWAT hotfixes no longer require a restart

Issue

Starting in FirePass 6.0.1 with cumulative hotfix HF-601-11 and FirePass 6.0.2 with cumulative hotfix HF-602-4, FirePass does not require a restart after installing an OPSWAT hotfix. Previously, FirePass automatically restarted after installing an OPSWAT hotfix.

Important: OPSWAT hotfixes released prior to 4/11/2008 require a restart after installation. OPSWAT hotfixes released after 4/11/2008 will not require a restart.

Note: For more information about OPSWAT hotfixes, refer to K6664: Obtaining and installing OPSWAT hotfixes.

F5 Product Development tracked this enhancement as CR94462 and it was included starting in FirePass 6.0.3. For information about upgrading, refer to the FirePass release notes.
