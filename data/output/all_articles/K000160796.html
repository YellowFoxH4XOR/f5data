Source

Description

/var is full, free disk space is 0%

 

 

Environment

BIG-IP

APM and SSLO may be provisioned

 

 

Cause

These temporary directories are from when RPM packages are installed and get extracted by restjavad. Usually related to iApps, SSLO, APM Guided configuration, among others.

 

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

Safe to delete these files.

 

# bigstart stop restjavad

# rm -rf /var/config/rest/tmp.*

# bigstart start restjavad

 

 

Additional Information

None
