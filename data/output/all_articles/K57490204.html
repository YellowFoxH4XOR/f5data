Description

When the CPF sends delete operation to Tripo, but the operation fails due to incorrect timestamp between CPF and Tripo.  It means that the time is not synchronized or incorrect between Tripo and CPF machines. You will see below logs in CPF logs.

2018-03-13 02:12:49,933 WARN  [11332] External storage server error: Key:abctest101;1520872156;5468;1304335464 OpCode:op_delete Status:st_invalid_timer Error message is:Timestamp out of date. [Tripo-EpollEventLoop-thread 4_2_23] [AsyncStorageProvider.answerReceived()]

Environment

  • SDC

Cause

NTP time is not synchronized or incorrect between CPF and Tripo machines.

Recommended Actions

Fix or correct the NTP timings between CPF and Tripo.

Additional Information

None
