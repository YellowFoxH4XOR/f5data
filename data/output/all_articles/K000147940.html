What Happened?

  • Does F5® Distributed Cloud (XC)  platform have a blackhole service for BGP?
  • I want to advertise some prefixes to be blackholed as soon as they enter your backbone when trying to reach my subnets. Is that possible?

Environment

  • BGP

Resolution/Answer

Yes, F5 XC offers this. You can use this community to blackhole prefix: 65535:666

 

Additional Information

You can check the Blackhole of an ASN using the following command:

whois [ASN_NUMBER] | grep Blackhole
