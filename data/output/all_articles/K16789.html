Known Issue

On rare occasions, the BIG-IP system may take a longer amount of time to boot up and become active. This issue occurs more frequently on older platforms that run newer versions of the software.

Impact

The system is unable to pass traffic while waiting to become ready.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP system takes several minutes longer than normal after boot-up to go from offline to active.
  • Due to a timing issue during system load, it takes longer for the system to become ready to pass traffic after deployment or reboot.
