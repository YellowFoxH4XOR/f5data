Description

  • You load a ucs archive on your Active BIG-IP unit
  • A failover suddenly occurs

Environment

  • BIG-IP units in HA pair
  • Network failover configured
  • Load UCS

Cause

When a ucs archive is loaded, specific system services restart, and the device temporarily loses network failover connectivity.

Recommended Actions

 F5 recommends that you perform ucs load activities when the unit is Standby or during a maintenance window.

Additional Information

K13132: Backing up and restoring BIG-IP configuration files with a UCS archive
