Description

You have renewed the subscriptions and want to know if you need to reactivate the license.

Environment

  • MyF5
  • Subscriptions
  • Renewal

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

If the license registration key and license type are the same then there is no need to reactivate on the BIG-IP.

You do not need to reactivate license unless there's additional products / change in functionality. e.g go from 1G to 5G license.

Additional Information

K12183049: Mapping VE registration keys after a subscription renewal

K7752: Activating the license using the automatic activation method

Upon completion of mapping of registration keys, the existing subscription keys will be shown under the renewal subscription. Thus, you do not need to replace your keys unless you are adding additional products as part of renewal.  This resets the term date on the keys to coincide with the renewed subscription.
