Known Issue

Deleting a data group in use by an iRule does not trigger a validation error.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The iRule references a data group associated with a virtual server.
  • You delete the data group.

Impact

The system allows you to delete the data group without any warning. As a result, connection flows end.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The iRule ends due to failed commands, causing connection flows to end.
