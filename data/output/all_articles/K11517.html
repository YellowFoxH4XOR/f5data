Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP WebAccelerator pvac process may crash when disk caching is disabled and a document eligible for normalization is requested. When disk caching is disabled, the normalized cache is also disabled, but normalization is not forcibly disabled. As a result, when a document eligible for normalization is requested, pvac attempts to perform normalization. Since the normalized cache is not available, pvac crashes.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR138440 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 10.2.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP WebAccelerator release notes.

Additionally, this issue has been fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-10.1.0-3372.0-HF1 for BIG-IP 10.1.0. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
