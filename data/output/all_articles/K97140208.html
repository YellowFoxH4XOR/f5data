Known Issue

The FQDN ephemeral nodes may not repopulate, as expected, after you re-create them.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP systems are configured in a high-availability (HA) configuration.
  • You configure DNS so that two FQDN nodes resolve to the same IP addresses.
  • You configure two pools that use either of the aforementioned nodes.
  • You modify the DNS entry for the overlapping node to use a different IP address.

Impact

The FQDN ephemeral nodes may not repopulate as expected.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You observe that FQDN ephemeral nodes are not updated on the standby device.
