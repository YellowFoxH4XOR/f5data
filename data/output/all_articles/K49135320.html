Description

BIG-IQ fails to add Secondary BIG-IQ to cluster  (System > BIG-IQ HA > Add Standby) with error in the WebUI after clicking Add:
An Error occurred while adding the BIG-IQ: ssh_exchange_identification: read: Connection reset by peer

/shared/ha/add-peer-task/1d41bf07-e61e-47b1-b1e8-abfa60f1b86d/worker AddPeerTaskWorker] ssh_exchange_identification: read: Connection reset by peer

TCPDUMP  collected between BIG-IQ CM devices shows RST send in both directions to both CMs by IPS Device

Environment

  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management
  • Configuring BIG-IQ HA
  • IPS/Firewall between BIG-IQ devices
     

Cause
The traffic is blocking by a signature "SSH User Authentication Brute Force Attempt" on IPS and the paten for this signature as mention below.

EXAMPLE
*************
40015 - User Authentication Brute-force Attempt
If a session has the same source and destination but triggers our child signature, 31914, 20 times in 60 seconds, we call it is a brute force attack.
The child signature, 31914 is alert on every connection on ssh server.

Recommended Actions

  1. Verify IPS sygnatures on Your IPS system and mark it as false positive in terms of BIG-IQ

Additional Information

BIG-IQ is opening about 40-44 ssh sessions during time of creating HA. This is by design.
