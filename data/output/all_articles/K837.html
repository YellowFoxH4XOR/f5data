Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Failover does not occur when gateway failsafe is triggered for active-active units in a redundant pair.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR22728 and CR33581.

Workaround

To work around this issue, use VLAN failsafe instead of gateway failsafe. For information about configuring VLAN failsafe, refer to K3537: Configuring VLAN failsafe.
