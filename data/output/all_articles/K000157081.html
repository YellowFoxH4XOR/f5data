Description

F5 strongly recommends running the latest version of software on your BIG-IP appliances and Virtual Editions (VEs) to enhance the security, performance, and overall total cost of ownership of your BIG-IP systems. To ensure a smooth and successful upgrade process to F5 WAF and ASM, refer to the articles listed in the following table. These resources also provide valuable insights into the most common issues encountered during upgrades, serving as a helpful reference to streamline your efforts.

You can also use F5 iHealth to periodically diagnose known vulnerabilities on your BIG-IP system. For more information, refer to K27404821: Using F5 iHealth to diagnose vulnerabilities.

Answer/Recommended Actions

To ensure a smooth and successful upgrade process to F5 WAF and ASM, refer to the articles listed in the following table:

 

ArticleDescription

K02693745: Prepare to update or upgrade the BIG-IP system | BIG-IP update and upgrade guide

Successfully upgrading the BIG-IP system requires some planning and preparation, such as checking the current health of the system and backing up the configuration to a safe place on your network. Some steps are optional but recommended to ensure a better experience e.g. Disable auto-sync. Failure to perform these procedures may lead to unexpected down time and extended maintenance windows.

K000157049: Post Upgrade ASM Configuration Fails To Load When /var Directory Run Out Of Space

Starting with BIG-IP 17.x, the system uses a manifest generation process to maintain its configuration. This process relies on available space in the /var directory, so it’s important to monitor and minimize its usage. The reference article list a plan of action to follow in case policies fail to load due to /var space starvation.

K82512024: Managing BIG-IP ASM Live Updates (14.1.x and later)

Information on how to configure, manage, and troubleshoot Live Updates for the BIG-IP Application Security Manager. It covers enabling automatic or manual updates, connectivity requirements, licensing prerequisites, and methods to apply new attack signatures and security data efficiently. The article also includes guidance on verifying updates, handling failures, and maintaining system security without requiring a full software upgrade. 

K000137652: Confirming the asm configuration has been loaded after an upgrade

Details how to verify that your ASM configuration was successfully retained and applied following an upgrade of the system, outlining steps to check policy versions, compare configuration states, and troubleshoot common issues when settings revert or fail to load. 

K13636984: Ensuring the BIG-IP system has sufficient volume disk space available for a software upgrade

Goes over some steps for review to ensure there is enough volume disk space for the upgrade.
