Topic

On BIG-IP 6400, 6800, 8400, and 8800 platforms and FirePass 4100 and 4300 platforms equipped with redundant power supplies, both power supplies are engaged when power to the unit is active. The power supplies may receive power from different power sources.

All F5 platforms that utilize dual power supplies use a common power bus configuration. When the system is powered on, both power supplies are powered up supplying power to the common bus. Each supply draws power from the individual main plug for that supply. If you use different sources for each power supply, both sources would have to fail for a loss of power to the appliance.

Electrical current is drawn from both power supplies. If one power supply fails, the full electrical current is drawn from the remaining power supply, without interruption to the system. A brief time is required for the single remaining power supply to increase its electrical output. However, this time period is within milliseconds, and not long enough to interfere with system operation.
