Description

  • BIG-IP DNS Wide-IP or BIG-IP DNS Pool is failing to answer DNS queries.
  • The Wide-IP or Pool is marked with a Yellow status icon.
  • The Wide-IP or Pool shows a status of either "Availability: Unavailable - Pool/Pool member limits" or "Availability: Pool limit(s) exceeded on GTM"

Environment

BIG-IP DNS Wide-IP or Pool with "Limit Settings" enabled.

Cause

The BIG-IP DNS system is operating correctly, as it has been configured.

Recommended Actions

Try to determine if there has been an increased volume of requests for the affected BIG-IP DNS Wide-IP or Pool.
  • If the increased volume is legitimate, consider disabling or increasing the configured connection limit.
  • If the increased volume is due to a flood attack, you may be able to mitigate this upstream or with functionality offered by the Advanced Firewall Module.
