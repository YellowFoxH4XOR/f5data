Source

Description

You want to configure our F5 XC Global Log Receiver and you want to export the logs to a syslog server. After configuring it you get a similar error to this one:

Environment

  • Global Log receiver

Cause

It is not possible to configure. Our Global Log Receiver do not support exporting to a syslog server endpoint

Additional Information

If the port of the endpoint is not the default one, please check on your side to have it also allowed in your firewall.
