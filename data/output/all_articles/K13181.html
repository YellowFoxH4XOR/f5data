Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may experience uneven distribution of connections between TMM processes.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The platform is either a BIG-IP 6900 or BIG-IP 8900 or BIG-IP 8950
  • The source port of the client connection is divisible by four

Due to algorithms used to choose a TMM process in a Clustered Multi-Processing (CMP) environment, connections from client source ports that are divisible by four will prefer a certain subset of the available TMM processes. If a client application uses such ports disproportionately rather than a broad distribution of ports, the impact of this issue can be exaggerated accordingly.

Impact

System performance may degrade.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • Connection count may be unevenly distributed between TMM processes.
  • Incorrectly favored TMM processes may prematurely reach configured connection limits.

    On a CMP device, connection limits per pool member or node are distributed among available TMM processes. If one TMM is receiving a disproportionate amount of the connections, that connection count for that TMM may reach the connection limit configured by the BIG-IP administrator, causing connections to be reset. For information about CMP and connection limit distribution, refer to K8457: Connection limits configured on pool members or nodes for a CMP system are enforced per TMM instance.
