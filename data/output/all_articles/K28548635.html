    Additional Information

    • Impact migrating WAF deployment from HOT to COOL storage account:-
      • The main difference between HOT and COOL storage accounts is the frequency in which data is accessed by the given deployment
      • The BIG-IP WAF not only process traffic but also parse, normalized and check for violations for each and every HTTP requests against configured security policy.
      • This validation also include the application of signature. Attack signatures are rules or patterns that identify attack sequences or classes of attacks on a web application and its components. 
      • AWAF is heavily reliant not only on readily available Attack signatures but its ability to access and update the attack signatures frequently.
      • Violation reports and event logs also relies on frequent interaction, reference of data on the system.
