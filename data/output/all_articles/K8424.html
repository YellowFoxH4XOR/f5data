Multiple unspecified vulnerabilities in the Java Runtime Environment in Sun JDK and JRE 6 Update 1 and earlier, and 5.0 Update 13 and earlier, allow context-dependent attackers to gain privileges through an untrusted application or applet, as demonstrated by an application or applet that grants itself privileges to read local files, write to local files, or execute local programs.
