In this video, AskF5 shows you how to create SSL Subject Alternative Name (SAN) certificates and certificate signing requests (CSRs).

This demo uses the BIG-IP 15.1.0.

Note: After the CSR is signed by the certificate authority, you’ll need to import the certificate. To choose a procedure, refer to K17379: Managing BIG-IP SSL certificates and keys.
