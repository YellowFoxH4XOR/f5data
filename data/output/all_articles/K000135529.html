Recommended Actions

At the time of writing, a planned update of the Journeys tool to add support for 17.x migration was in development. In the meantime, the UCS platform-migrate option can be used, provided that the source and target are both running the same v17.x software. For current status of the Journeys tool update, contact F5 Support.
