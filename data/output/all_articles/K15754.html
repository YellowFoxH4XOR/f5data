Known Issue

The DNAT utility (dnatutil) reports inconsistent results when parsing Deterministic NAT (DNAT) data from BIG-IP platforms equipped with Intel Hyper-Threading (HT) Technology CPUs. For specific information about hyper-threaded platforms, refer to K14358: Overview of Clustered Multiprocessing (11.3.0 and later).

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The affected Large Scale NAT (LSN) pool is configured with DNAT mode, and using dnatutil for reverse mapping.

Impact

The dnatutil utility fails to properly perform reverse mapping for the DNAT data.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H486444 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High screen.
  • The dnatutil utility reports reverse mapping results that are inconsistent with the lsndb list all command results. For example, the lsndb list all command reports that client 192.168.0.42:42767 has a translation to 172.28.21.196:62806. However, dnatutil reports that the translation address and port 172.28.21.196:62806 is reverse-mapped to client 192.168.0.46.
