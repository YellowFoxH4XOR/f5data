Description

Not all encrypted and decrypted packets are captured when running tcpdump in environment where BIG-IP is terminating IPsec.

Environment

  • BIG-IP terminating IPsec traffic (IKEv1 or IKEv2) and passing unencrypted traffic

  • IPsec policy for affected traffic in tunnel mode. Interface (routed) mode is not affected.
  • Standard or Fastl4 virtual server (VS).

Cause

Working as designed

Answer/Recommended Actions

None

Additional Information

All encrypted (ie: peer to peer) traffic is captured by tcpdump with a suitable filter.

Tcpdump captures unencrypted traffic that is either egress from BIG-IP to a local vlan after being processed by the VS or is ingress from a local vlan  to BIG-IP prior to VS processing.

Tcpdump captures decrypted traffic that is ingress from the wide area network (WAN) and has yet to be processed by a VS.

Tcpdump does NOT capture unencrypted traffic that is egress from BIG-IP after being processed by the VS prior to being encrypted.

For example, if the client makes a TCP connection to a standard VS from behind the remote IPsec peer, tcpdump will show the encrypted SYN ingress , the decrypted SYN ingress, but NOT the SYN/ACK egress from the standard VS.. All subsequent three-way handshake (3whs) packets will be output. 

The same applies to the remainder of the TCP flow and also to UDP and ICMP packets.
