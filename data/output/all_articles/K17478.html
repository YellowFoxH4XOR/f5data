Known Issue

Large configurations may cause transaction timeout events on secondary blades.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • Your configuration takes more than one minute to transfer to secondary blades on a multi-bladed system.

Impact

The BIG-IP system does not process the mcpd transaction, and your configuration is not loaded properly.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your configuration is not loaded due to an incomplete mcpd transaction.
  • When you insert or remove a secondary blade from the system, the BIG-IP system logs messages to the /var/log/ltm file that appear similar to the following example:

    mcpd[12345]: 01070430:5: end_transaction message timeout on connection 0x5ea9a9c8 (user mcpd-primary)
