Description

You are regularly losing connectivity to several virtual servers

In /var/log/ltm, you may see that large numbers of pool members 

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • Trunk

Cause

Large amounts of traffic for unknown vlans may cause the interfaces to lose monitor traffic to the pool members

Recommended Actions

Review interfaces statistics to see if the interfaces or trunks are dropping large numbers of packets. If the virtual servers are becoming unavailable at a regular interval, reviewing the statistics before and after may help you to determine if this is the issue affecting your device.
