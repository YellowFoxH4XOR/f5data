Description

Connection error: ssl_basic_crypto_cb:972: alert(20) Packet after MAC or padding FAILED

 

Environment

-SSL errors

-ssl_basic_crypto_cb:972: alert(20)

-Packet after MAC or padding FAILED

-Client Ciphers

-tcpdump captures

-BIG-IP running v15.1.10.2

 

Looking at the logs it shows that all the failing connections were between client (xx.xx.xx.14, xx.xx.xx.148) and VIP xx.xxx.xxx.140:443

 

 

MMM DD HH:MM:SS <HOSTNAME> warning tmm[5471]: 01260009:4: (null connflow): Connection error: ssl_basic_crypto_cb:972: alert(20) Packet after MAC or padding FAILED

MMM DD HH:MM:SS <HOSTNAME> warning tmm[5471]: 01260009:4: xx.xx.xx.148:38114 -> xx.xxx.xxx.140:443: Connection error: hud_ssl_handler:1991: alert(20) codec alert

 

Looking at the packet traces - some frames are missing but with more than 1GB of payload in 10s tcpdump on BIG-IP may not be able to collect everything. In the future you may want to use a specific filter to limit amount of data sent to tcpdump.

 

 

What we can see is that client xx.xx.xx.148 is sending Client Hello with 2 set of supported ciphers - in one there are 25 ciphers (+TLS_EMPTY_RENEGOTIATION_INFO_SCSV (0x00ff)) in another just one TLS_ECDHE_RSA_WITH_AES_256_CBC_SHA384 (0xc028) + TLS_EMPTY_RENEGOTIATION_INFO_SCSV (0x00ff)

 

 

From what we can see the problem affects TLS_ECDHE_RSA_WITH_AES_256_CBC_SHA384

 

 

Because some packets are missing we don't see all handshake messages and but if you use a filter 'tcp.port == 42414' - there is one connection with full SSL handshake, Application Data sent from the client and Fatal alert sent from F5.
