Description
Once you will modify IPsec tunnel options, the tunnel will disconnect and stay down. Even if you will revert the change, IPsec tunnel will not come up. 

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • IPsec
  • BIG-IP 15.0.0 - 15.1.3.1, 16.0.x

Cause

  • Bug ID 964941
  • Bug ID 969713


Recommended Actions

As an imediate workaround reboot BIG-IP or restart tmm (IKEv2) with the command:
Important: Restarting tmm will cause of traffic disruption. Please have maintenance window to do this.
tmsh 
restart sys service tmm

A restart of tmipsecd is also required if IKEv1 is in use:
tmsh 
restart sys service tmm tmipsecd

NOTE: We strongly recommend to upgrade to fixed versions later than 15.1.4 or 16.1.0.
 

Additional Information
IKEv1 is handled by racoon (tmipsecd daemon), and it also needs to be restarted to workaround the issue when the configuration related to IKEv1 is changed.

  • K33404337: The tmsh and bigstart utilities can no longer be used to individually manage the racoon service
  • K48615077: BIG-IP daemons (15.x - 16.x)
