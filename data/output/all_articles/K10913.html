K10913: Internet Explorer 7 and 8 may appear to hang when connecting to FirePass

Topic

Internet Explorer 7 or 8 may appear to hang while connecting to FirePass. The connection appears to hang for ~10 seconds during the installation of InstallerControl components. This issue occurs if the browser does not have access to the Certificate Revocation List servers. After the session appears to hang, users may receive the following error message:

A webpage is not responding on the following website: <FirePass IP Address>

After another 10 seconds, this message disappears and the ActiveX installation process continues normally. The delay in connection occurs because the browser is attempting to connect to the Certificate Revocation List servers, and the browser waits for that connection attempt to fail before proceeding with the ActiveX installation process. 

To eliminate this delay, users should clear the Check for publisher's certificate revocation option in their browser's setting.

Important: Prior to making a change to the browser's setting, confirm that the change will not be a violation of your Corporate security policies.

This configuration is located in the Tools: Internet Options: Advanced tab.

Note: For more information about changing the browser settings, refer to the Microsoft documentation.
