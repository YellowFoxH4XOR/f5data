Environment

  • External Datagroup
  • A device group made up of four units

Cause

This is caused due to a potential sync failure when syncing datagroups across large device groups (bug id 1036225)

This is a rare race that can occur between one device in the group receiving a configsync update and thereafter sending it on to update another device in the group.

Recommended Actions

The failure has no impact on future config-syncs. The next config sync will be successful, whether it is triggered manually or automatically via data-group update
