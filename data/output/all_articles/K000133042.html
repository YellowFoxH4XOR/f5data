Environment

  • F5OS rSeries appliance
  • System upgraded to a version after F5OS-A 1.3.0

 

Cause

From 1.3.1 onwards, we can expect tenants to take extra time to come up. This is due to:

  •   Introduction of fix for  "Bug ID 1185369: F5OS rSeries appliances will not launch tenants after upgrade to F5OS-A 1.3.0"
  •   BIOS update that needs to complete before K8s can be started

 

 Recommended Actions

Expected Behaviour. This delay could last up to 20-25 minutes so just have to wait.
