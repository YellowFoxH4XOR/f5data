Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. SmartPhones running Windows Mobile 6 and later may go into a continuous reboot cycle after installing the FirePass SSL VPN client software.The Smartphone device will require a hard reset to restore functionality and will result in deleting all data on the device.

Note: For instructions on resetting your device, refer to your Smartphone manufacturer.

F5 Networks Product Development is tracking this issue as ID 317647 (formerly CR131339-1) for FirePass version 6.1.0,  and as ID 294154 (formerly CR131339-2) for FirePass version 7.0.0.

This issue has been fixed in cumulative hotfix HF-610-1, which has been issued for FirePass version 6.1.0. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the cumulative hotfix from the F5 Networks Downloads site.
