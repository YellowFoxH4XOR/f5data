Recommended Actions

Types of F5 Licenses:

 

Perpetual Licenses: These licenses are purchased once and can be used indefinitely, as long as the hardware or software remains operational.

  • For physical hardware, the license is valid until the device reaches its End of Life.
  • For virtual hardware, the license is tied to specific versions of the BIG-IP software.

Subscription Licenses: These licenses are based on a subscription model, requiring annual renewal to maintain access to the F5 product.

  •  This model provides a steady revenue stream for F5 and ensures that customers always have access to the latest updates and support.

F5 StrongBox Evaluation Program:  provides a cost-effective way for F5 UNITY Partners to allow their customers to test drive F5 technology

  • The evaluation cycle can be chosen as either 30 or 45 days

F5 StrongBox Lab License is designed for educational and training purposes, allowing institutions to use F5 technology in a lab environment.

  • The lab license typically has a fixed duration, often one academic year, but this can vary based on the agreement with F5.

 

F5 StrongBox Pay as You Go license is designed for customers who prefer a flexible, usage-based pricing model.

  • This model can be more cost-effective for customers who don't need to use the technology continuously, as they only pay for what they use.

Evaluation Licenses: These are temporary licenses provided to customers for a limited period, typically to allow them to test and evaluate the F5 product before making a purchase decision.

Demonstration Licenses: These licenses are usually given to partner engineers or sales teams to showcase the capabilities of F5 products. They are not tied to a specific customer but are meant for demonstration purposes only.

 

Additional Information

Viewing the type of BIG-IP license your system contains:

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.
  2. Go System > License.
  3. Locate License Type.
    • If the License Type is Evaluation or Demonstration, the License Expiration Date is also listed.
    • If the License Type is Production, no License Expiration Date is listed.
