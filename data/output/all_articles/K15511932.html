Security Advisory Description

F5 is aware of the reports of the alleged Super Micro (Supermicro) hardware compromise.

F5 does not use Super Micro boards in any of our BIG-IP, BIG-IQ, Enterprise Manager, ARX, FirePass, or VIPRION hardware. F5 designs our own boards and controls the manufacturing tests and processes used by our contract manufacturer. We take multiple steps to validate and ensure the integrity of all firmware and software loaded on our systems during the manufacturing process.

Impact

There is no impact to F5 products.

To determine if your hardware has been evaluated for this compromise, refer to K4309: F5 platform lifecycle support policy.
