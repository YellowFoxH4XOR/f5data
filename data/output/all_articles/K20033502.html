Known Issue

The BIG-IP device group may report an incorrect sync status after a reboot.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have two or more BIG-IP devices configured in a device group.
  • You perform all of the following steps on one of the device group members:
    • You modify the configuration on the device, triggering the devices to become out of synchronization.
    • You manually initiate a synchronization of the modified configuration to the device group using the Overwrite Configuration option from the Configuration utility.

      Note: The device group devices display that they are in the synchronized state.

    • You proceed to reboot the device.

Impact

The BIG-IP device group members report an incorrect sync status although the configuration of the device group is synchronized. The modified device configuration is synchronized across the device group members. No configuration is lost as a result of this issue.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The device group member with the modified configuration reports a sync status of "Not All Devices Synced" after the reboot.
  • Other devices in the device group report sync status of "Changes Pending".
  • You notice that all the device group members maintain the same updated configuration data.

Resolution

Workaround

There is no workaround. To resolve the incorrect sync status, you can initiate a synchronization of the device configuration to the device group without using the Overwrite Configuration option. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.
  2. Navigate to Device Management >? Overview.
  3. In the Device Groups section, click the name of the device group you want to synchronize.
  4. In the Devices section, click the appropriate device.
  5. From the Sync Options section, select the desired synchronization operation.

    Note: Do not select the Overwrite Configuration checkbox.

  6. Click Sync.
