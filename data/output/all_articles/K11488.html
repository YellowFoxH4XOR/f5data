Topic

This article applies to BIG-IP 10.x. For information about other versions, refer to the following articles:

  • K14934: Overview of daemons that are monitored and restarted by the runsv utility (11.x)
  • K7657: Overview of daemons that are monitored and restarted by the runsv utility (9.4.x)

The BIG-IP LTM 10.x software branch uses the runsv utility to monitor specific daemons and will restart the daemon in the event of a system failure. To view daemons that are monitored using the runsv utility in BIG-IP 10.x, refer to the following table:
