Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may not forward SIP 200 OK answers to INVITE or re-INVITE messages.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a virtual server that references a SIP profile.
  • Your SIP profile has the Dialog Aware option enabled.
  • The BIG-IP system receives and forwards either a SIP INVITE or re-INVITE message.
  • The BIG-IP system receives a SIP 200 OK answer in response to the INVITE or re-INVITE message.

Impact

SIP calls may fail prematurely due to the system not forwarding SIP 200 OK messages.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Client SIP connections are prematurely disconnected.
