K26364249: BIG-IQ email alerts: ''out-of-date analytics iApp installed''

Source

Description

Getting email alerts that look like the following message from your BIG-IQ system indicating that the "f5.bigiq-analytics" iApp is out of date:

==========================================================================================
Device <some.managed.device.hostname> has an out-of-date analytics iApp installed: 6.1.0

Time of Alert: <Date-time>
Source: <BIG-IQ Hostname> | https://<device IP>

Additional Information:
==========================================================================================

Environment

  • The BIG-IQ system
  • Stats collection is enabled on your managed BIG-IP systems
  • Email alerting is configured on the BIG-IQ system

Cause

This appears to be related to a known bug; refer to Bug ID 692135.

Recommended Actions

To get the alerts to stop, disable and then re-enable stats collection in the BIG-IQ system for the specific BIG-IP device that is called out in the alert.

Disabling and re-enabling stats on the BIG-IQ system causes it to re-deploy the analytics iApp on the BIG-IP system, which will update it with the most current version.
