K43873105: BIQ Stats Last Collected showing date of Dec. 31, 1969

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-02-25
Updated
2026-02-12
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K43873105

Description

  • After a device is discovered and stats are enabled the "Stats Last Collected" column shows Dec 31, 1969

Environment

Relevant environmental factors specific to the topic

  • Big-IQ collecting stats from a Big-IP

Cause

There are 2 related causes of this issue.    AVR on the Big-IP is required for the Big-IQ to collect stats.  And AVR must be able to reach all DCDs on port 443.

Recommended Actions

The actions or steps required to address the topic covered in the description.

  1. Verify that the Big-IP has the AVR module provisioned.
  2. Verify that the Big-IP can reach the DCD on port 443

Additional Information

When the administrator updated the configuration factors below, F5 also recommend to update the statistics parameter of BIG-IQ CM as well. 

Points to verify to BIG-IQ DCD to collect statistics data.
 - BIG-IP update provisioned module such as AVR.
 - BIG-IP update offbox-tcp-addresses to include IP address of BIG-IQ DCDs
 - Network connectivity between BIG-IPs and BIG-IQ DCDs via port 443

To update the statistic collection feature clearly, please perform the following steps on each BIG-IPs through BIG-IQ CM
 - Disable statistics collection for the device once
 - Re-enable statistics collection
 - Re-confirm that the offbox-tcp-addresses at BIG-IP reflect the BIG-IQ DCDs' IP addresses 

If the statistics data is still not collected, please collect the qkviews (qkview -s0) from BIG-IQ CM and DCDs, along with BIG-IPs.
