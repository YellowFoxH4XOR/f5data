Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. In extremely rare circumstances, the BIG-IP ASM blocks modified domain cookies even when the Violation setting is turned off or is not selected within the policy.

Note: This issue does not exist in the BIG-IP ASM 9.4.x branch due to architectural differences from the 9.3.x maintenance branch.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR89652.

This issue has been fixed in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.3.1-HF1 issued for BIG-IP ASM 9.3.1. You may download this hotfix or a later version of this hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.

For information about the F5 hotfix policy, refer to K4918: Overview of F5 critical issue hotfix policy.

For information about managing F5 product hotfixes, refer to K6845: Managing F5 product hotfixes.
