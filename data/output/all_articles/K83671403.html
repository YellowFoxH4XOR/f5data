K83671403: When uploading image to BIGIP vCMP host, ansible playbook fails with ''Failed to create the resource.''

Type
Support Solution
Published
2021-04-09
Updated
2025-05-21
Applies to
  • VIPRION Platforms
  • F5 Automation and Orchestration
  • F5 Modules for Ansible
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K83671403

Description

bigip_software_install and bigip_software_image  modules fail on multiblade platforms.

Environment

  • BIG-IP Viprion
  • You installed Ansible 2.7 or lower on the control machine.
  • BIG-IP version 12.x or later
  • You are trying to upload a image to a BIG-IP vCMP host but the playbook fails with an error similar to this:

Cause

Recommended Actions
