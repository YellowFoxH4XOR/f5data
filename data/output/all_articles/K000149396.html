K000149396: Old statistics disappeared after reboot of BIG-IQ DCD.

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-01-21
Updated
2025-01-21
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 8.X.X, 8.3.X, 8.3.0, 8.2.X, 8.2.0, 8.1.X, 8.1.0, 8.0.X, 8.0.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000149396

Description

When the BIG-IQ DCD is restarted due to configuration changes, maintenance work, etc., there may be a situation where old statistics may appear to have disappeared as soon as the device is started up. 

Environment

  • BIG-IQ DCD

Cause

Probably some of the processes were still loading which cause the problem with loading the statistics.

Recommended Actions

Just wait a few more minutes for device to load properly, then check again.

Additional Information

None
