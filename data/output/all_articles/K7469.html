Topic

Virtual servers using acceleration (PVA2) may report a 0 (zero) for connection or statistic information when viewed from the command line. This behavior is expected.

The 6800 platform has been designed to optimize data throughput by default. There are four interfaces on the 6800 platform: two travel through the PVA, and two bypass the PVA and travel directly to the host.

The switch determines which switch interface to use based on a source and destination hash. Half of the connections sent to the 6800 platform will be software-only acceleration, which allows the system to scale to a maximum throughput of approximately 4 Gps. Connections that bypass the PVA and that are software-only accelerated will not display connection or statistic information for the virtual server under the PVA fields.
