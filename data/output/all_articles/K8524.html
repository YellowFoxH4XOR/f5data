Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The UDP Immediate timeout setting does not work as expected.

The correct behavior of the UDP Immediate timeout setting is to immediately remove the UDP connection flow from the connection table after the packet has left the BIG-IP LTM system. However, the UDP connection flow remains in the connection table. This behavior may cause port conflicts.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR95134 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM version 9.4.5. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in hotfix BIGIP-9.4.4-94.0-HF3 issued for BIG-IP version 9.4.4. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Networks Downloads site.
