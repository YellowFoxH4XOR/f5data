K000158969: "On Emergency attempting sync to prevent inconsistent state" message in asm_config_server.log

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-12-31
Updated
2026-06-23
Source

Description

On rare occasions, a standby unit may attempt to sync its configuration back to a primary unit unexpectedly, with a message like the following reported to /var/log/ts/asm_config_server.log:

asm_config_server.pl|INFO|Jun 31 12:34:56.789|12345|F5::ASMConfig::Handler::spawn_relay_handler,,On Emergency attempting sync to prevent inconsistent state.

This can result in corruption of the ASM configuration.

Environment

One of the following modules is provisioned:

  • AFM
  • ASM

Cause

When the ASM config sync process detects a critical inconsistency or a failed/timed-out sync recovery state, the system attempts an "emergency" sync to prevent further divergence between the active and standby units. This is a last-resort mechanism to avoid policy corruption, but may not be successful.

Here are some possible causes:

  1. Improper sync configuration when using multiple sync groups.
  2. The times on the active/standby unit are not in sync.
  3. The asm_config_server daemon is overwhelmed.

Answer/Recommended Actions

  1. Refer to K000151506 for recommendations of configured ASM HA.
  2. Check that units in the HA group are synced to NTP.
  3. Check the health of the asm_config_server daemon.
