Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When analyzing the performance graphs of the VIPRION system, you may notice oddities with some of the lines drawn at the blade level. For example, the line corresponding to the current connections to one of the blades may contain flat horizontal segments. This issue is caused by the primary blade intermittently failing to acquire the performance data from the other blades. Additionally, because the data gathered by the primary blade is incomplete, the performance graphs at the cluster level, which are based on the data from all the blades, will also be inaccurate.

This issue is cosmetic and does not affect traffic.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR123474 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 10.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, ASM, GTM, PSM, Link Controller, WebAccelerator, or WOM release notes.

This issue has been fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-10.0.1-378.0-HF3 issued for BIG-IP 10.0.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
