Description

When configuring a new access policy for VPN, DNS requests are not resolved by the Edge Client, rendering your client unable to connect to the CRL.

You are connected to the VPN with one of your Windows clients and you notice that the DNS resolution for some of your internal domains is not working, and as a consequence, CRL can not be reached. You take a packet capture and you notice that there is no response back for some DNS requests although the Primary and secondary DNS servers are present in the configuration of the Network Access resource and there are no problems with the DNS exclusions.

You check the VPN client logs on the Client Troubleshooting Utility for Windows and notice the following log message:

2023-12-20,12:59:42:470, 5996,10600,, 2, , 2598, DNSRelayProxy::ManageArrays, Too many pending requests. Drop oldest request.

Environment

  • VPN.
  • Edge Client.
  • Windows 10
  • BIG-IP APM
  • DNS

Cause

This means there are more than 20 requests in the client-side queue that have not yet been acknowledged by the DNS servers. The client sends DNS requests to the Primary and Secondary DNS servers configured on the Network Access Resource but does not get any response back. The queue overflows after 20 pending requests and it deletes the oldest request from the queue.

Recommended Actions

This is not a BIG-IP issue, the BIG-IP is not to receiving DNS responses. Please troubleshoot further on your DNS infrastructure and all your firewalls as there could be some misconfiguration such as a missing route.
