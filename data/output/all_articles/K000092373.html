Description

APM Logon page not displaying - redirected to /my.logout.php3?errorcode=21


 Environment

• Access policy with Logon Page

• Device with high uptime (around 100 days up or more)

• httpd_apm & httpd_sam processes are up and running

 Cause

High uptime may causes unexpected behavior between internal APM processes communication.

 Recommended Actions

Reboot the BIG-IP device.

 Additional Information

None.
