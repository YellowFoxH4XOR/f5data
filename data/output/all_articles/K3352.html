Introduction

A redundant FirePass configuration consists of two FirePass controllers: one operating in active mode, and one operating in standby mode. The two controllers maintain communication across a network connection, and session and configuration information is replicated between the active and standby controllers. In the event that the active controller fails, the standby controller automatically becomes active.

The process of upgrading a redundant configuration consists of the following processes:

  • Back up the redundant pair
  • Perform the upgrade

F5 Networks recommends that you read these instructions in their entirety, before you start the upgrade.

Important: To prevent losing access to the FirePass controllers, F5 Networks recommends that you have direct physical or remote serial console access to your FirePass controllers when you upgrade your systems.

Note: Refer also to K3339: Restoring backup files on a different FirePass unit than they originated on.

Back up the redundant pair

Perform the following steps to back up the redundant pair:

  1. Access the Administrative Console of the active FirePass through a URL in one of the following forms:

    • https://A.B.C.D/admin
    • <https://A.B.C.D/stats>
Where A.B.C.D is the IP address of the controller interface, not the IP address of the virtual IP address that is shared by the FirePass pair, or the corresponding hostname of the virtual server.
  • Click Reports.
  • Click Sessions.
  • End each open session, by clicking the Kill link.

    Note: You have the option to send an email using the Announce function (located on the Users, Announce page) to those users located in the user database, notifying them about the impending downtime.

  • Click Device Management.
  • Click Maintenance.
  • Click Backup/Restore.

    The Backup/Restore page appears.

  • Create a backup of the active FirePass configuration.
  • Repeat steps 1 through 8 on the standby FirePass controller.

    For FirePass hardware that supports the snapshot function (task b from the Maintenance menu), continue to step 10. For hardware that does not support the snapshot function, go to the section titled Perform the upgrade.

    Note: For specific instructions about performing a snapshot, refer to: K3244: Backing up and restoring FirePass system software.

  • Create a snapshot of the FirePass image for the active FirePass controller.

    The active FirePass unit will reboot twice during this process and will become the standby unit once the snapshot process is complete.

  • Create a snapshot of the FirePass image for the FirePass unit that is now active.

    This active FirePass unit will reboot twice during this process, becoming the standby unit once again.

    Important: You cannot use the Telnet Access feature from the Administrative Console to create a snapshot, because the snapshot function will restart the FirePass controller in recovery mode, which is only accessible through the serial console.

  • Log in to the Administrative Console of the active FirePass controller.
  • Click Failover.
  • Click Settings.
  • Verify that the pair is synchronizing normally.

Perform the upgrade

Note: You must review the FirePass Release Notes for current changes before you perform an upgrade. The Release Notes are also available on the Online Update page.

  1. From the Administrative Console of the active FirePass controller, click Device Management.
  2. Click Maintenance.
  3. Click Online Update.

    Note: If your FirePass controllers do not have direct access to the Internet, refer to K167: Downloading software from F5 Networks.  From the F5 client system logged into the Administrative Console, download the upgrade image from F5 and perform a Local Update instead of an Online Update.

  4. Follow the instructions on the page to perform the upgrade on the active FirePass unit. The active FirePass unit will reboot when the upgrade is complete, becoming the standby unit.

    Note: The total downtime from the point that the controller reboots until the standby unit becomes active should be less than 30 seconds. The active FirePass unit remains active during the download of the upgrade.

  5. Log in to the Administrative Console of the newly active FirePass unit.
  6. Repeat steps 2 through 4 on the newly active FirePass unit.
  7. Verify that the synchronization and all site functions and applications are working properly.
