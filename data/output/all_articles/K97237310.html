Known Issue

The ssldump utility may fail to decrypt captures and shut down in rare circumstances.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The ssldump utility is decrypting traffic where a Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) record header spans TCP segments.

Impact

The ssldump utility may fail to fully decrypt the capture starting at the frame where the SSL record spans a TCP segment. Depending on the remaining data in the TCP stream, the ssldump utility may shut down.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The ssldump utility fails to decrypt a capture.
  • In rare circumstances, the ssldump utility shuts down.
  • The ssldump utility displays an output similar to the following example:

    1 25 0.4781 (0.0000)  S>CShort record
    Unknown SSL content type 224
    1 26 0.4781 (0.0000)  S>CShort record
    Unknown SSL content type 142
    ...
    1 30 0.4781 (0.0000)  S>CShort record
    1 31 0.6141 (0.1359)  S>CV231.213(45857)  application_data
