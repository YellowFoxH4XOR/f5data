Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may reach 100 percent CPU utilization when you do not license the BIG-IP system for the PEM module and a virtual server references the radiusLB-subscriber-aware RADIUS profile.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is not licensed for the PEM module.
  • Your BIG-IP configuration has a virtual server that references the radiusLB-subscriber-aware RADIUS profile.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may experience a significant decrease in performance.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • You observe a significant decrease in performance for client connections through the BIG-IP system.
  • Administrative tasks are adversely affected and may take additional time to complete. 
  • The BIG-IP performance graphs indicate very high or 100 percent TMM CPU usage.

Workaround

There is no workaround for this issue. However, you can avoid this issue by not associating the radiusLB-subscriber-aware RADIUS profile with a virtual server when the PEM module is not licensed.
