Known Issue

Deleting zones from large databases (for example, 100m records) may take a considerable amount of time (for example, up to 30 minutes).

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You are dealing with a large DNS database.

Impact

The zxfrd process takes an excessive amount of time to delete records and may delay transfer requests.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When you delete a zone or a large number of resource records, the zxfrd process might reach 100% CPU for a considerable length of time.
