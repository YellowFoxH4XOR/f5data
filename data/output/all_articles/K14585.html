Known Issue

The configuration synchronization status does not propagate to secondary blades in a chassis.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Multi-bladed VIPRION chassis platforms are members of a device group.
  • The primary blade in one of the chassis changes. For example, the primary blade reboots or fails and then another blade in the cluster becomes primary.

Multi-bladed VIPRION chassis configured in a device group can synchronize their configurations. As a result, when you make a change to one unit, the change propagates to the other units in the device group. When you synchronize the configurations, both units report a status of In Sync. If the primary blade in one of the chassis changes, that unit reports a status of Not All Devices Synced.

Impact

Synchronization may cease between chassis devices.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H442255 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The chassis in which the primary blade changed may report Not All Devices Synced while the other chassis reports In Sync.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can make a configuration change to one of the devices and perform a ConfigSync.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
