Description
Unable to ping VIP or floating self-ip on ESX or ESXi VE HA pair.

Environment

  • ESX or ESXi Hypervisor
  • HA pair
  • MAC masquerade

Cause
When configuring Traffic-group MAC masquerading for BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) on VMware ESX or ESXi servers, you must configure the virtual switch's Forged Transmits and Promiscuous Mode settings to Accept. By default, the Promiscuous Mode and Forged Transmits settings are disabled. For information about enabling the Promiscuous Mode and Forged Transmits settings on the virtual switch, refer to the VMware knowledge base article listed in the Supplemental section, or in the VMware documentation for your specific VMware version.

Recommended Actions

  • See Configure promiscuous mode on a virtual switch or port group on VMware.com for guidance.
