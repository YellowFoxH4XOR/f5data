Policy Information

Note: For information about the End of Sale announcement for the BIG-IP 6400 and 6800 non-FIPS platforms, refer to K9484: End of Sale announcement for the BIG-IP 6400 and 6800 platforms.

Note: For information about the End of Sale announcement for the BIG-IP 6400 NEBS platform, refer to K11043: End of Sale announcement for the BIG-IP 6400 NEBS platform.

With the FIPS certification of the BIG-IP 6900 and 8900 platforms, F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of the 6400 and 6800 FIPS SKUs, effective Oct 1, 2010. F5 is providing Advanced Product Notification to customers and partners announcing intent to retire the following products:

Technical Support

F5 maintains generous life cycle policies that allow customers to enjoy many years of both support and new software releases. Customers whose platforms are designated EoL and who are currently under maintenance contracts will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the Service Contract Renewal date expires.

For more information about this announcement, contact your local F5 Partner Business Manager or Inside Sales Representative.
