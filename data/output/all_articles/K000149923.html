Description

To set up your XC routed DDoS services, we need key information about your network(s). The F5 Security Operations Center (SOC) requires the information in the following downloadable document:

  • F5® Distributed Cloud DDOS Discovery Questionnaire

Once it's filled out, we can configure your Distributed Cloud DDoS services so we can mitigate future DDoS attacks on your network(s). 

Environment

  • Onboarding
  • F5® Distributed Cloud (XC) DDOS Discovery Questionnaire

Cause

None

Answer/Recommended Actions

Download and fill out this F5® Distributed Cloud DDOS Discovery Questionnaire with as much detail as possible before our initial Technical Onboarding Call. 

If you have any questions or issues, a SOC Analyst can address these on your Technical Onboarding Call. Thank you for taking the time to provide us with this vital information.
