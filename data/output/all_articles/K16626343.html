Known Issue

The system does not properly enforce the connection queue limit on bladed systems.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • Connection queues (configured within the pool) are enabled on bladed systems.

Impact

The system does not properly enforce limits and may consume more memory than it should.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The connection queue depth may be larger than the configured limit.
