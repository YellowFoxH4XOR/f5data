Description

When building and running an HA Pair  (Device service cluster), do the individual member devices have to have the same  Admin / Root passwords? 

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • DSC or High Availability (HA) configured

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

 Device service clustering (DSC) or "High-availability" (HA) clusters do not require passwords to match on each device during base configuration.
  • On initial setup, Root/admin passwords can be different. 
  • Upon initial Sync between the HA members,  the Root/Admin password from the active device will be written to the peer device.
  • After initial sync, the Root/admin passwords will be the same as the Active device.

Additional Information

Manual:  Device service clustering
