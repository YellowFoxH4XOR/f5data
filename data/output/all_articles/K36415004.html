Topic

This article applies to BIG-IP 14.x. For information about other versions, refer to the following articles:

  • K14512321: SSL ciphers used in the clientssl-secure and serverssl-secure SSL profiles (13.x)

Description

The clientssl-secure and serverssl-secure profiles are predefined profile types that you can use with valid and trusted SSL certificates to protect high-value sites. By default, the profiles only enable secure SSL ciphers and disable mid-stream SSL renegotiation. You can associate the clientssl-secure and serverssl-secure profiles with SSL virtual servers.

The SSL ciphers supported in the clientssl-secure and serverssl-secure profiles have changed across versions. The following sections list the SSL ciphers supported for the clientssl-secure and serverssl-secure profiles in BIG-IP 14.x:

  • Ciphers in BIG-IP 14.1.0
  • Ciphers in BIG-IP 14.0.0
