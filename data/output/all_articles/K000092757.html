K000092757: VCMP guest restarted automatically after experiencing NMI watchdog: BUG: soft lockup - CPU#<#> stuck for <##>s!

Support Solution
Published
2022-11-29
Updated
2022-11-29
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000092757

Description

You notice that the VCMP guest restarted automatically.


The following are logged in /var/log/ltm in the VCMP guest:

emerg kernel: NMI watchdog: BUG: soft lockup - CPU#<#> stuck for <##>s! [<proc>:<>]


And the following are logged in /var/log/ltm in the VCMP host:

alert vcmpd[15111]: 01510014:1: vCMP guest guest01 heartbeat timeout at the halfway mark.

alert vcmpd[15111]: 01510013:1: vCMP guest guest01 lost heartbeat.

alert vcmpd[15111]: 01510011:1: vCMP guest guest01 powered off.

notice vcmpd[15111]: 01510006:5: Guest (guest01): Stopping VM.

notice vcmpd[15111]: 01510006:5: Guest (guest01): Starting VM.

alert vcmpd[15111]: 01510012:1: vCMP guest guest01 detected heartbeat.

alert vcmpd[15111]: 01510010:1: vCMP guest guest01 powered on.


The guest was rebooted automatically by the host.


Environment

  • BIG-IP (including Intel-based platforms)
  • VCMP

Cause

Unknown at time of publication.


Recommended Actions

The guest experienced a rare CPU lockup.


If the guest was restarted automatically by the host, the issue should be resolved and not expected to occur again. Please monitor the guest and other guests on the same host. You may consider running EUD on the host, but it is not strictly necessary.
