Known Issue

The BIG-IP ASM system experiences excess logging during periods of high CPU utilization.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP ASM system is experiencing high CPU utilization.
  • You configure your security policy using automatic Policy Builder.
  • Automatic Policy Builder applies adjustments to the security policy.

Impact

The Async process produces excess logging.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • In the to /var/log/ts/asm_config_server.log file you may observe repeating log messages similar to the following example:

    asm_config_server.pl|INFO|<date and time>|<pid>|F5::ASMConfig::Handler::pass_call_to_asynchronous_process,,Async process socket is full - ignoring

  • When you view CPU statistics, you observe high CPU utilization.
