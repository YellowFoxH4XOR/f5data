Topic

The ITU-T K.20 standard covers the Resistibility of Telecommunication Equipment installed in a Telecommunications Centre to Overvoltages and Overcurrents.

The ITU-T K.21 standard covers the Resistibility of Telecommunication Equipment installed in Customer Premises to Overvoltages and Overcurrents.

F5 has not conducted tests to determine if F5 hardware platforms meet either ITU-T K.20 or ITU-T K.21 recommendations.
