Description

How to get to check the light levels on the SFPs on F5OS?

 

Environment

  • F5OS-C / F5OS-A
  • Velos / rSeries
  • SFP

 

Cause

  • n/a

 

Recommended Actions

SFPs' the light levels can be retrieved by Confd command:

show portgroups

NOTE: For F5OS-C, you will need to log in to the partition's CLI.

You will see similar to below output
