Description

After upgrading or patching or changing settings of a BIG-IP LTM VE (Virtual Edition) instance host settings, the virtual machine may fail to boot. The console displays errors such as the following:

systemd-fstab-generator[503]: Failed to create mount unit file <...> as it already exists. Duplicate entry in <...>

The system is inaccessible via GUI or SSH, and the console is stuck on these errors, preventing further input.

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM VE

Cause

This issue occurs due to changes in device UUIDs or disk mappings at the hypervisor level after a hypervisor settings change, upgrade or patch. The guest OS (BIG-IP VE) may be unable to locate the correct disk devices referenced in its fstab file, resulting in duplicate or incorrect entries that trigger systemd-fstab-generator errors and prevent booting.

Such problems can arise a hypervisor reorders or renames virtual disk devices, if there are duplicate UUIDs from disk cloning, or from other misconfigurations affecting device mapping.

Recommendations

Check the hypervisor for changes that can affect the guest access to disk locations then reboot guest.
