Topic

Starting in BIG-IP version 4.5, you can use the Configuration utility to view the expiration dates of the SSL Certificates you have installed for SSL Proxies.

To do so, perform the following procedure:

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP Configuration utility.
  2. From the left navigation pane, click Proxies.
  3. At the top of the screen, click the Cert Admin tab.

    The SSL Certificate Administration page will appear and display information for each key pair and certificate installed. The expiration dates for certificates that have already expired are displayed in red.
