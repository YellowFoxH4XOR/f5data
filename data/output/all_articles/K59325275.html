Known Issue

The jitterentropy-rngd process may quietly fail to start with no errors at start-up.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The jitterentropy-rngd fails to start.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may stop responding indefinitely at start-up or unresponsive genkeys processes may eventually cause increased memory and CPU utilization, potentially leading to unpredictable system failures over time.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • At start-up, your BIG-IP system stops responding at a message that appears similar to the following example:

    Generating /var/named/config/rndc.key ( 09:08:10 ) ...

    or

  • The system boots successfully with the init.d script reporting OK when it should be FAILED.
  • When crontab invokes the genkeys and genkeys-1024 processes every hour, they may start but never terminate.
