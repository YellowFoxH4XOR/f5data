K000134607: Why was my case closed for EoTS (End of Technical Support)?

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-05-19
Updated
2024-03-28
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000134607

Description

As indicated in K8986: F5 product support policies, F5 no longer provides technical support services such as troubleshooting or configuration advice for products that have reached it's End of Technical Support (EoTS) date. 
 

  • For hardware products, this date typically coincides with End of Life (EoL).
  • For software products, this date typically coincides with End of Software Development (EoSD).
  • For service products, this date typically coincides with End of Life (EoL).

This is also addressed in the following sections of the F5 Maintenance Terms and Conditions

Environment

  • Support Policy
  • Products lifecycle

Cause

No Support Services continues for F5 products that have reached End of Technical Support (EoTS).

Recommended Actions

1. Check which products have reached EoTS:

  • For hardware products, refer to the section "End of Life F5 Hardware Products" 

K4309: F5 hardware product lifecycle support policy

  • For software products, refer to the section "Branches of BIG-IP software that have reached EoL"
K5903: BIG-IP software support policy


2. Where applicable, please upgrade to a supported version and/or product. Refer to the same articles above to find the supported versions.

3. Visit MyF5 support page to search for Knowledge articles that may have fixes and recommendations for products that have reached EoTS,
 

Additional Information

Should you need further advise, please reach out to your F5 Account Manager.
