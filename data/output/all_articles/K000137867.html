Description

ASM shows heavy CPU use due to Obfuscator process

 

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • ASM
  • Advanced WAF

 

 

Cause

After a recent upgrade you might notice that Obfuscator consuming more CPu and taking longer to finish a task.
 

Recommended Actions

 

Compare prior upgrade and after upgrade datasyncd logs:

 

Navigate to this path - /var/log/datasync/datasyncd.log


Notice that Obfuscator taking just over 4 mins to complete on v15.

 

DATASYNCD|NOTICE|Sep 07 22:03:13.221|2987|datasyncd_main.c:6030|extgen_obfuscator_run: running obfuscator on pid 23449
