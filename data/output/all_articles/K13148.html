Topic

F5 ships BIG-IP, BIG-IQ, BIG-IP Next, F5 VELOS and F5 rSeries systems with default values for several system settings. This article provides a summary of these settings to assist you with accessing the devices upon receipt from F5.

The settings described in this article also apply to BIG-IP Virtual Edition, and BIG-IQ Virtual Edition systems.

Note: If your product is not listed in a particular table, either the product family does not ship with a default setting of the type specified in the table, or the product may no longer be supported. For more information about supported platforms, refer to K4309: F5 platform lifecycle support policy.

Note: For BIG-IP and BIG-IQ products launched in Azure or Amazon cloud environments, root accounts are disabled by default. User account names and passwords may vary. Check the configuration guide for your deployment.
