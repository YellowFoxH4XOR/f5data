Known Issue

BIG-IP APM policy synchronization with five or more devices may interrupt access to the Configuration utility.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured five or more BIG-IP APM systems in a device group.
  • A BIG-IP APM administrator uses the Policy Sync feature to synchronize an access policy to the device group.

Impact

Connection to the Configuration utility on the BIG-IP system is temporarily lost while the policy synchronization completes.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • After performing policy synchronization, the Configuration utility becomes inaccessible.
  • BIG-IP APM administrators are momentarily unable to access the Configuration utility of any device group members.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can configure the BIG-IP APM systems in two device groups rather than one, with a single BIG-IP APM system configured as a member of both device groups. This will allow you to synchronize the access policy to one device group first, then using the BIG-IP APM system common to both device groups, create a copy of the synchronized access policy and synchronize the copy to the members of the other device group.

For more information about configuring device groups, refer to the BIG-IP Device Service Clustering: Administration guide.
