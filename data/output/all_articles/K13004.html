Topic

The DoS Attacks reporting component of the BIG-IP ASM Anomaly Detection feature may report that BIG-IP ASM system is detecting denial-of-service (DoS) attacks. However, when you expand the IP Addresses and URLs menus, no data may be reported in either menu.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • BIG-IP ASM detects DoS attacks for a duration of approximately 65 seconds or less

Due to the methods that the BIG-IP ASM system uses to detect and collect data during a DoS attack, an attack may begin and end before the BIG-IP ASM system has completed collating the reporting data. In this circumstance, the BIG-IP ASM system reports only the Start Time and End Time events in the Anomaly Statistics: Dos Attacks reporting screen, and the IP Addresses and URLs fields will remain blank.

F5 is tracking enhancements to this reporting behavior as ID 366997 and was addressed in 11.3.0.?
