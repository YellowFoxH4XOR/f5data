Description

Dynamic multi-connect client peers are unable to connect to SDC. It gives below error in Fep log.

2021-08-19 18:14:55,671 WARN  [12294] Peer aaa://abc_SN_1:5403;transport=sctp is rejected because it exceeded allowed maximum number of peers of 5000 [Silo-EventLoop-thread-3-2_23] [PeerTableImpl.incrementBusinessPeersCounter()]

Environment

  • SDC

Cause

Multiple reconnection of client peers when there is issue with diameter clients(could be due to network or diameter clients) and it trigger CPF-25116

 Recommended Actions

  1. The actions or steps required to address the topic covered in the description. 
  2. upgrade to 5.1 CF 24 or above
  3. Workaround with engineering script solution found under additional information section.

Additional Information

***Procedure needs to be performed during a planned maintenance.
