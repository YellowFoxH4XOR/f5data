Description

in-tmm monitors with a longer time-until-up  that is greater than 150 seconds, the monitored node is not recovered after an extended downtime.

Environment

  • BIG-IP 21.0.x, 17.5.x, 17.1.x
  • in-tmm monitor is enabled

Cause

The issue is tracked by

(Bug alias 2163625) in-tmm monitor: time until up attribute for arp monitor is taking more time than configured value

Answer/Recommended Actions

You may workaround by setting a shorter time-until-up than 150.
