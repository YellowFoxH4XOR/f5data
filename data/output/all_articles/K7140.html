K7140: The big3d daemon crashes when it attempts to write to big3dshim before the SSL handshake has completed

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Under heavy path probing, the big3d daemon queue can fill up quickly. If the big3dshim process is restarted during a period of heavy probing, the big3d daemon will attempt to write probe responses to the big3dshim before it has completed its Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) connection. Since the connection has not been completed, the parser is not ready to read the probe responses. As a result, the big3d daemon will crash.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR67748 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.1.3 and 9.2.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
