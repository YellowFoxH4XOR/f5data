Description

You may have difficulty removing 10 gigabit SFP+ Ethernet modules (part number OPT-0016-00) from a BIG-IP system. You may be unable to fully open the bail when you remove SFP+ modules that were manufactured before September 2012. Attempting to remove the SFP+ module without the bail fully opened may cause damage to the SFP+ module. A manufacturing change introduced in September 2012 has corrected this issue.

When removing an affected SFP+ module with a bail that cannot be easily opened, you may be able to use a small screwdriver to move the bail to the fully-open position. After the ball is opened, you can safely remove the module.
