Known Issue

Note: Only the following platforms are affected by this issue:
  • BIG-IP 1500
  • BIG-IP 3400
  • BIG-IP 6400
  • BIG-IP 6800
  • BIG-IP 8400
  • BIG-IP 8800

This is the result of a known issue. A heartbeat failure can occur when the bcm56xxd daemon timer wraps after 97 days of uptime. As a result of the heartbeat failure, TMM initiates a failover.

The error messages or logs that are commonly associated with this issue can take the form of a TMM stack trace or multiple TMM and bcm56xxd restart messages. The TMM and bcm56xxd restart messages are logged to the /var/log/daemon.log directory.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR78171 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.4.1 and Enterprise Manager 1.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, GTM, ASM, Link Controller, WebAccelerator, or Enterprise Manager Release Notes.
