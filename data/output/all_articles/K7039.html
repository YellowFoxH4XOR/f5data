Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When using a Legacy Hosts Favorite configured with Java-based VT-100, VT-220 or VT-320 emulation, the keystrokes typed into the emulator may not display immediately. The delay is typically less than 100 milliseconds.

Important: Many factors in addition to this known issue may delay the display of typed characters.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR67250, and it was fixed in FirePass version 6.0.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the FirePass release notes.

Additionally, a hotfix has been issued for version 6.0.0 of FirePass. For version 6.0.0, this issue was fixed in cumulative hotfix 600-4. You may download this hotfix or a later version of the cumulative hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.

For instructions about obtaining a hotfix, refer to K167: Downloading software from F5 Networks.

For instructions about installing a hotfix, refer to K3430: Installing hotfixes.
