Description

When configuring device groups for configuration synchronization (sync) in BIG-IP environments with ASM (Adv. WAF) enabled, certain sync group configurations can lead to sync failures. This is a known limitation, not a software bug, and is documented under ID602243.

Environment

  • Adv. WAF
  • Configuration synchronization (sync) in BIG-IP environments.

Cause

This issue occurs when there are two device groups configured: a sync-failover device group and a sync-only device group, and the auto-sync setting is not consistent between them (for example, auto-sync is disabled on the sync-failover group and enabled on the sync-only group, or vice versa). Additionally, ASM sync is enabled on the sync-only device group. This configuration mismatch leads to synchronization failures due to the way ASM sync interacts with device group settings.

Recommended Actions

  • Use a single sync-failover device group with ASM sync enabled.
  • If both a sync-failover device group and a sync-only device group are required, ensure that the auto-sync setting is the same (either enabled or disabled) for both groups..
