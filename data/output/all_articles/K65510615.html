Description

When performing a manual failover, it keeps failing back to the original active node.

Environment

  • HA groups are configured

Cause

The pool in the HA group is down. Therefore, both BIG-IPs have an HA Score of 0, but the Active Bonus value keeps the original active BIG-IP as active.

Recommended Actions

Either determine why the nodes the pool are down or remove this pool from the HA group configuration.
