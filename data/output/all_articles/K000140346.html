Description

You have encountered a limitation with the number of scheduled reports. After successfully creating 100 scheduled reports, an attempt to create the 101st report resulted in the following error message:

01071d71:3: Can't create scheduled-report. You currently have 100 scheduled-reports set, while this is above max allowed scheduled-reports.

Environment

  • ASM

  • 100 scheduled reports already configured

Cause

The limit of 100 scheduled reports is a hardcoded constraint within the ASM system. There is no configurable db variable available to extend this limit.

Recommended Actions

None
Due to the hardcoded nature of this restriction, there is no direct method to increase the number of scheduled reports within ASM. This maximum is consistent across various licenses and VE types, including High-Performance VEs.

Additional Information

Users of ASM who utilize scheduled reports extensively must consider this limitation when organizing their reporting structure. If the limit has been reached, it may be necessary to evaluate the current reports for consolidation opportunities or to prioritize which reports are most critical for your operational needs.
