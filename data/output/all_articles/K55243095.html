K55243095: BIG-IQ CLI shows standby Centralized Management as active:standalone

Type
Support Solution
Published
2020-06-23
Updated
2025-02-06
Applies to
  • Virtual Editions
  • BIG-IQ
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K55243095

Description
   Command line interface on a Standby High Availability BIG-IQ Centralized  Management shows as Active:Standalone

Environment

  • BIG-IQ 7.1.0

Cause

This is functioning as designed.

Recommended Actions
None

Additional Information

Because the BIG-IQ failover mechanism is different from BIG-IP, TMOS and TMSH do not see the two units as active/standby DSC peers.
The standby unit will have a prompt that looks like:

[root@big_iq2.example.com: Active: Standalone] config #

Active or standby status may be determined on the System>BIG-IQ HA  tab or at the top of the Graphical Interface.
