Known Issue

BIG-IP APM access policies may not function on the VIPRION P8 platform.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The VIPRION P8 chassis does not contain a blade in slot 1 of the chassis.

Impact

The configured BIG-IP APM access policies are not accessible.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You notice log messages in the /var/log/apm file that appear similar to the following example:

    err tmm12[22891]: 01490514:3: 00000000: Access encountered error: ERR_NOT_FOUND.
    File: ../modules/hudfilter/access/access_license.c, Function: access_retrieve_sdb_license_count, Line: 1102
    err tmm12[22891]: 01490553:3: tmm.license.global_access: Access license count sync error: could not read key, error: ERR_NOT_FOUND

    notice apd[7893]: 01490005:5: d0bc6e08: Following rule 'fallback' from item 'Start' to ending 'Allow'
    err apd[7893]: 0149013e:3: d0bc6e08: Allow agent: Logon denied due to validation error, Error Code: 3009 (Nonexistent ACL assigned)
    notice apd[7893]: 01490000:5: ** SIGSEGV **
    notice apd[7893]: 01490000:5: ** STDERR **
    notice apd[7893]: 01490000:5: ** END STDERR **

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 426939 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies To box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:

Workaround

To work around this issue, ensure that slot1 of the VIPRION P8 chassis is populated with a blade.

Impact of workaround: For more information about the impact of this workaround, refer to the VIPRION Systems: Blade Migration guide.
