K000152589: The BIG-IQ is unable to gather a statistics form the manage BIG-IP devices. The Stats Last Collection Date' in BIG-IQ GUI showing Jan 1, 1970

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-07-16
Updated
2025-07-16
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 8.3.X, 8.4.X, 8.4.0, 8.3.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000152589

Description

The Stats Last Collection Date' in BIG-IQ GUI showing Jan 1, 1970 for manage BIG-IPs and BIG-IQ is unable to collect statistic form affected devices.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IP
  • AVR

Cause

AVR is unable to establish correctly connection to the BIG-IQ

Recommended Actions

Impact of workaround: Restarting avrd does not impact traffic flow, but analytics data collection and reporting will be interrupted. Always perform this action during a maintenance window to avoid operational surprises.

1. Remove all Services from affected BIG-IPs

2. Remove all BIG-IPs from the BIG-IQ

3. Restart the avrd process on the affected BIG-IPs with below command:

bigstart restart avrd

4. Add back again all BIG-IPs to BIG-IQ, re-discover and re-import configuration

5. Enable again collection of the statistics to refresh stats collection

None
