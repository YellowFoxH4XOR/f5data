Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may report inaccurate throughput statistics for Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP) guests.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system has an embedded Packet Velocity Acceleration (ePVA) chip. For more information about platforms with the ePVA chip, refer to K12837: Overview of the ePVA feature.
  • The system processes traffic with a virtual server that has an associated FastL4 profile.
  • You attempt to use the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) or Configuration utility to view the throughput statistics for vCMP guests.

Impact

You are unable to determine the correct throughput statistics for vCMP guests.

Note: The guest statistics are hidden in the guest and at the host level under vCMP guest statistics. Therefore, you may observe lower than expected statistics under the vCMP guest and the guest statistics that are visible from the host.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Your BIG-IP system reports lower than expected throughput statistics for vCMP guests.
