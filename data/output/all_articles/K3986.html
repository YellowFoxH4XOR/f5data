Topic

By default, when using dynamic ratio load balancing, the BIG-IP Link Controller chooses links for load balancing based on the amount of inbound traffic on the link. When inbound and outbound traffic are related and exist in a typical ratio of request-to-response traffic, this method works well.

If links are used primarily for outbound traffic, dynamic ratio may not be the most effective method for choosing links. Starting in versions 4.5.11 and 4.6.3, you can change the method used for collecting the metrics so that it is calculated based on the total of inbound and outbound traffic.

To make this change, add the following line to the globals section of the wideip.conf file:

link_use_total_metrics yes
