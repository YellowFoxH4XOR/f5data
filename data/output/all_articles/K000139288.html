K000139288: An unexpected reboot of a Velos system can be caused by overheating.

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-04-15
Updated
2024-04-15
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000139288

Description

You may have noticed the tenants on your Velos system all suddenly stopped at the same time. A look at the logs shows they all experienced a reboot at the same time.

  • In either the event-log.log, /var/log/platform/pel or velos.log you may see log entries similar to this:

/var/F5/partition<number>/events/event-log.log

65546 blade-2 thermal-fault EVENT NA "VQF2 at +49.1 degC" "2024-04-13 03:23:58.578022756 UTC"

/var/F5/partition<number>/log/velos.log

2024-04-08T15:33:06.485651+02:00 controller-2(p2) alert-service[22]: priority="Notice" version=1.0 msgid=0x2201000000000029 msg="Received event." event="65546 blade-3 thermal-fault EVENT NA 'ATSE1 at +64.6 degC' '2024-04-08 13:31:41.273845912 UTC'".

/var/log/platform/pel

 VQF2 at +64.4 degC (state 1 -> 0)

10/17/2023 00:30:39 | 3936 | AOM | 2 | Na | 6 | VQF2 action: fan step 1

10/17/2023 00:32:10 | 3937 | AOM | 2 | Na | 6 | VQF2 at +70.0 degC (state 0 -> 1)

10/17/2023 00:32:10 | 3938 | AOM | 2 | Na | 6 | VQF2 action: fan step 2

Environment

  • Temperature in the location the device is stored on, or maybe another device blowing hot exhaust at the F5 device.

Cause

The specifications for a Velos system environment should be between 0 and 40 degrees Celsius. If it is not it can cause the Velos to crash and reboot.

Recommended Actions

The actions or steps required to address the topic covered in the description.

  1. Monitor at all times the temperature where the Velos system is stored.
  2. Ensure no other device is blowing its exhaust air onto the Velos system.
