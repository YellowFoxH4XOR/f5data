K000137972: Received an email from cert@f5.support

Type

Published
2023-12-20
Updated

Source

F5 has been tracking malicious activity that targets companies using a phishing campaign. The campaign spoofs F5 domain and lures recipients to download malware. This is not a legitimate email from F5.

You can reduce your risk by blocking email from @f5.support.

F5 is committed to keeping customers safe and informed and reminds you of the following:

  • All F5 security communications originate from F5SIRT@F5.com.
  • Software downloads are available only from MyF5 (my.f5.com).
  • Ensure you are running the most current, applicable version of BIG-IP software.

If you have any questions about the authenticity of a communication purporting to be from F5, you can directly contact your account representative or contact F5 Support.
