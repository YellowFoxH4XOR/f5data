Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Enterprise Manager is unable to install hotfixes on compact flash (CF) only devices. This restriction is due to physical space restrictions of the CF media and the minimum space requirements specified by the hotfix.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR80484 and it was fixed in version 9.4.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, GTM, ASM, Link Controller, or WebAccelerator release notes.
