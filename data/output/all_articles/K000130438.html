K000130438: Configuration change on one NAT policy may affecting another NAT policy

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-01-03
Updated
2026-07-17
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP AFM: 15.1.5
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000130438

Description

There are 2 NAT policies NAT_A and NAT_B using 3 common DNAT rules with different untranslated addresses respectively.

Configuration change on NAT_A, such as re-ordering rules on policy NAT_A, may affecting NAT_B.

That NAT policy NAT_B will not do any translation.


Environment

  • AFM
  • NAT

Cause

This is caused by ID1215609.

Recommended Actions

Any configuration changes on affected policy NAT_B, such as re-ordering rules on policy NAT_B, may resolve this issue.
