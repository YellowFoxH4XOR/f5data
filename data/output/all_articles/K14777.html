Policy Information

F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of all ARX, ARX Cloud Extender, and Data Manager Products, effective November 1, 2014. ARX software that is embedded on these products will reach End of Software Development (EoSD) and End of Technical Support (EoTS) according to the following dates. F5 is providing Advance Product Notification to customers and partners, announcing intent to retire the products listed in the following tables:
