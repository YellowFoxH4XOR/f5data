Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. BIG-IP VE Trial does not return intermediate certificates to clients as part of the Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) handshake process. Configuring a Client SSL profile to use a certificate chain file will not produce any effect. Due to this issue, clients may not trust the SSL certificates presented by the BIG-IP VE Trial system.

Note: This issue does not affect BIG-IP VE 10.2.0 or later.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as ID 225348 (formerly CR139149).
