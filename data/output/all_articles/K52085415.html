K52085415: office 365 environment are rejecting message error ''bare line feeds are illegal''

Updated

Applies to

Source

Description

Office 365 emails are getting rejected with an error message "bare line feeds are illegal". 


Environment

  • Office 365 environment
  • Email system


Cause

The cause of this issue is breaks in the email. When emails contain breaks, it causes this error message to appear.


Recommended Actions

Remove all breaks from the email, including the use of the <br> tag.

Old Example(Not Working):

This is my email

<br>

Here is your OTP

<br>

Thank you

New Example(Working):

This is my email<br>Here is your OTP<br>Thank you
