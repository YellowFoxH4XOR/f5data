Description

Certificate validation requires publishing a DNS TXT record supplied by the certificate authority. CAA records can restrict certificate issuance to authorized certificate authorities. CAA and TXT records are configured in DNS and are not embedded in the CSR or certificate.

Environment

  • Product: BIG-IP DNS
  • DNS management utility: ZoneRunner
  • CSR generated on BIG-IP DNS and submitted to a certificate authority
  • DNS record types:
    • CAA for Certificate Authority Authorization
    • TXT for domain-validation data

Cause

CAA and TXT records must be created in the authoritative DNS zone used for certificate validation.

Answer/Recommended Actions

  1. Generate the CSR on BIG-IP DNS and submit it to the certificate authority.
  2. Start the DNS validation process and obtain the exact TXT record name and value supplied by the certificate authority.
  3. In the authoritative DNS zone, open the ZoneRunner resource-record management page:
    • Log in to the BIG-IP Configuration utility.
    • Go to DNS > Zones > ZoneRunner > Resource Record List.
    • Select the applicable view name and zone name.
  4. Create the TXT record:
    • Enter the record name supplied by the certificate authority.
    • Enter the required TTL.
    • Select TXT as the record type.
    • Enter the validation value supplied by the certificate authority in the text field, enclosed in double quotation marks where required.
    • Create the record.
  5. Configure CAA records when certificate issuance should be restricted to specific certificate authorities:
    • Add the CAA records required for the domain.
    • Authorize the certificate authority required by the certificate-management policy.
    • Ensure that the CAA records are created in the appropriate authoritative DNS zone.
  6. Confirm that the TXT and CAA records are published by the authoritative DNS service and can be queried.
  7. Complete the certificate-authority validation process and import the issued certificate into BIG-IP DNS after validation succeeds.

Additional Information

  • CAA records allow domain owners to specify which certificate authorities may issue SSL/TLS certificates for their domains.
  • Certificate authorities that are not authorized by the domain’s CAA policy may be prevented from issuing certificates.
  • TXT records are used for DNS-based validation.
  • The DNS records must be created in the authoritative DNS zone used by the domain-validation process.
