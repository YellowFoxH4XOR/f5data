Topic

The air inlet sensor measures the temperature of the air entering the BIG-IP chassis. If the incoming air is too warm, the system is prone to overheating, which can result in poor performance, system malfunctions, and hardware failure. The following table lists the recommended range of air inlet temperature readings for each platform that contains an air inlet sensor.

PlatformRecommended inlet air temperature
16000 to 55 °C
36000 to 55 °C
39000 to 55 °C
69008 to 60 °C
89008 to 60 °C
89508 to 60 °C
110008 to 49 °C
110508 to 49 °C
11050 NEBS8 to 56 °C

If the air inlet sensor for your platform consistently reports temperatures in excess of the maximum recommended temperature, investigate the environment in which the BIG-IP system is installed. Begin by ensuring the air inlet is not blocked by cables or other equipment. If necessary, reduce the ambient air temperature in the vicinity of the air inlet.

If the air inlet sensor reports excessive temperatures and the environment meets the published specifications, open a case with F5 Support to investigate the possibility of a bad sensor.

Note: For location of the air inlet and environmental specifications for your platform, refer to the appropriate Platform Guide.
