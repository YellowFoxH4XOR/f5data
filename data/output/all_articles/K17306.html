Known Issue

Under rare conditions, the bcm56xxd process may restart due to parity errors. This issue only affects the following hardware platforms:

  • BIG-IP 6900 (D104)
  • BIG-IP 8900 (D106)
  • BIG-IP 8950 (D107)
  • BIG-IP 11000 (E101)
  • BIG-IP 11050 (E102)
  • BIG-IP 5000 series (C109)
  • BIG-IP 7000 series (D110)
  • BIG-IP 10000s/10050s/10200v/10250v (D113)
  • BIG-IP 12250v (D111)
  • VIPRION blade B2100 (A109)
  • VIPRION blade B2150 (A113)
  • VIPRION blade B2250 (A112)
  • VIPRION blade B4100 (A100)
  • VIPRION blade B4200 (A107)
  • VIPRION blade B4300 (A108)
  • VIPRION blade B4340N (A110)

Note: The bcm56xxd process is the switch driver daemon for the BIG-IP system.

Impact

The system interrupts network traffic processing while the bcm56xxd process restarts.

Note: Restarting the bcm56xxd process does not take long, however, using the bigstart restart command to restart your system may take from 5 to 10 minutes, depending on your BIG-IP hardware modules.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H499660 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High screen.
  • Your BIG-IP system fails to process traffic for a brief period of time.
  • In the /var/log/ltm file, you observe error messages similar to the following example:

    bcm56xxd[6789]: 012c0016:6: unit 0 L2_ENTRY_ONLY entry 120673 parity error.
    info bcm56xxd[6789]: 012c0012:6: Exiting on parity errors.
    notice mcpd[5678]: 01070406:5: Removed publication with publisher id BCM56xxPublisher.
    info bcm56xxd: 012c0013:6: BCM56xxd starting. debug=0, foreground=1, packet=1, bcm_debug=0x7, soc_debug=0x0

Note: The following types of parity error messages may be logged if a parity error is detected by the broadcom chip in an area that is not used by user data.

These type of errors will not trigger a driver restart, and are not user impacting, however, they can generate a large number of log entries.

F5 recommends rebooting the affected device during a scheduled maintenance window to clear the state.

  • 012c0016:6: unit 0 FP_TCAM entry 1127 TCAM parity error
  • 012c0016:6: unit 0 SER parity failure without valid count
  • soc_xgs3_mem_dma: EGR_SERVICE_COUNTER_TABLE_X.epipe0 failed(NAK)
