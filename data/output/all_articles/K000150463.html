K000150463: Investigate and resolve F5 alerts

Description

A service account was identified as expired, and assistance was required to locate it. The account is not found on the BIG-IP system itself but resides in an external Active Directory (AD) environment.


Environment

  • Product: BIG-IP LTM
  • Version: 17.1.1


Cause

A service account does not exist within the BIG-IP system's local configuration or logs. Instead, it resides in the organization's Active Directory environment. This suggests that the service account is used for LDAP-based authentication between the BIG-IP system and AD but is not actively configured or referenced in the current setup.


Recommended Actions

  • Verify Service Account Location:
    • Confirm with your domain administrators whether the service account exists in your Active Directory environment.
    • Check if this account is still required for any ongoing integrations or configurations.
  • Reset or Remove Service Account:
    • If necessary, reset the password for this service account through your AD management tools.
    • If no longer needed, consider removing or deactivating this service account to maintain security hygiene.
  • Review Configuration on BIG-IP System:
    • Ensure that all configurations referencing LDAP authentication are up-to-date and valid.
    • Use commands like grep on configuration files to search for specific references:
    • grep -ri examplesvcaccount *.conf
  • Collaborate with AD Team:
    • Work closely with your Active Directory team to troubleshoot and validate any dependencies related to this service account.
    • Schedule meetings as needed to align on next steps and ensure proper resolution.
  • Understand Service Accounts in F5 Configurations:
    • When configuring a BIG-IP system to authenticate against AD, ensure that:
      • A dedicated service account is created in AD with minimal permissions required for querying user information.
      • Secure communication methods (e.g., LDAPS) are used between BIG-IP and AD servers.
    • Proper mapping of AD groups to roles on the BIG-IP system is implemented for streamlined user management.
  • Documentation & Maintenance:
    • Maintain clear documentation of all active accounts used by your systems.
    • Periodically review and update passwords for critical accounts like LDAP service accounts.
