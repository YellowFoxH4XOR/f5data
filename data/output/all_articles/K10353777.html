Known Issue

Upgrading the BIG-IP APM system may reset log rotation report preferences.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You upgrade your BIG-IP APM system from a version prior to 11.3.0, to a later version.

Impact

BIG-IP APM report log rotation preferences are not carried forward.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • After upgrading your BIG-IP APM system, custom report preference values for the following are not carried forward:
    • Log Rotation Period
    • Maximum Number Of Log Entries

Resolution

Status

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can configure the report preferences after the upgrade.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
