K000137919: Network Devices connected to Big-IP are receiving runts errors on their interfaces.

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-03-28
Updated
2024-03-28
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 15.1.3
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000137919

Description

The network devices (like switches) that are connected to the Big-IP are receiving increasing runt errors on their interfaces. Any received frame with its size less than 64 bytes is illegal, and called as a "runt".

Environment

  • Runt errors
  • Link flapping

Cause

In most cases, such frames arise from a collision, and while they indicate an illegal reception, they may be observed on correctly functioning networks. A receiver must discard all runt frames.

From /var/log/ltm, link is flapping and half-duplex is detected during the flap which indicate physical layer 1 issues are happening :
