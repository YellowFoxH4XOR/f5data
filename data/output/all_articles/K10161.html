Topic

The Activity LED on 8400 platforms does not illuminate during normal operations. The LED illuminates while the system is power cycling, and turns off after the boot process has completed. This is expected behavior.

If the Activity LED light on an 8400 platform is solid green during normal operations, the LED is not operating as expected, and may be considered defective. When this erroneous condition is present, the operation of the unit is not affected, and there is no need to remove any units that are already installed in your network.
