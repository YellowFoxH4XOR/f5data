Known Issue

The dnatutil utility may produce inconsistent or incorrect results.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You enable the carrier-grade network address translation (CGNAT) module.
  • You use the dnatutil utility with the --start_time or --end_time flag for timestamp specification.

Impact

The dnatutil utility output may be incorrect.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The timestamp does not match correctly, and you may receive incorrect or inconsistent reverse lookup results.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can avoid using the --start_time or --end_time flags with the dnatutil utility.
