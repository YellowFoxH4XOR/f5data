Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The forwarding database (fdb) table is not updated correctly when a VLAN is connected to both a copper and a fiber port, and the copper port is disconnected on one of the following hardware platforms:

  • BIG-IP 1000
  • BIG-IP 2400
  • BIG-IP 5100
  • BIG-IP 5110

As a result, traffic may be halted until the ARP table expires, or until the BIG-IP system receives traffic on the fiber port. At that time, the fdb is correctly updated and traffic is correctly handled by the switch fabric.

This behavior typically only affects traffic initiated by the BIG-IP system, such as monitor traffic. However, since most network implementations have constant two-way traffic, this behavior is typically seen only during connectivity tests performed during initial implementation.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR91157.
