Issue

In BIG-IP 10.2.0 HF2, when you upload a Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) certificate to the BIG-IP system, you can now include chain certificates in the same file as the SSL certificate. Previously, chain certificates were uploaded and configured separately.

Note: For more information about chain certificates, refer to K788: Overview of chain certificates.

F5 Product Development tracked this enhancement as ID 336848.
