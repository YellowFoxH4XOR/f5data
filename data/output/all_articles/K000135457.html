Environment

  • BIG-IP VE
  • APM module

Cause

After the testing of the APM package provisioning and subsequent unprovisioning on both the Active and Standby boxes. Despite unprovisioning the APM package, the epesec packages associated with it persisted on the device. This persistence is causing synchronization failures and impacting the expected functionality of the system.

 Recommended Actions

Important: The BIG-IP system experiences a temporary traffic interruption as it recovers from the TMM restart, and devices configured as a high-availability (HA) pair may fail over. F5 recommends changing the Management memory size allocation during a scheduled maintenance window.
  1. Reprovision the APM on the device.
  2. Find the epsec package causing the issue by following command.

find /config -xdev -type f -exec du {} \; | sort -rn | head -20

 

Move the Epsec Package to the Temporary (tmp) Folder and then again try to sync again.
