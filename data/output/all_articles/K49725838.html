Known Issue

BIG-IP 7000 and 10000 series platforms with dual solid-state drives (SSDs) may continuously restart after upgrading to version 11.6.0.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You have dual SSDs, in an affected platform, configured with RAID 1 mirroring.
Note: Support for RAID 1 mirroring with dual SSDs in the 7055, 7255, 10055, and 10255 platforms was introduced in BIG-IP 11.5.3, 11.6.2 and 12.0.0. However, BIG-IP 11.6.0 was released before those versions, and does not include support for RAID 1 mirroring with dual SSDs in the affected platforms.

Impact

When users attempt to boot into the 11.6.0 boot location, the BIG-IP system continuously restarts.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • If you upgrade an affected platform from BIG-IP 11.5.3 or 11.5.4, to 11.6.0, the BIG-IP system continuously restarts when attempting to boot into the 11.6.0 boot location.
