Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When multiple pools are configured with connection limits, and one or more of the pools connection limit is reached, the Configuration utility may report pools whose connection limits have not been reached as unavailable.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR86241 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.4.7. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
