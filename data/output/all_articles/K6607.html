Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The PVA chip is now reset by the PVA management daemon, pvad, if five contiguous LBDB busy messages are received.

This results in the pvad process performing a warm start of the PVA. This warm start retains the PVA's connection table and allows established connections to be preserved, such as TCP connections that are resilient to dropped packets through the use of retransmits. UDP connections will likely suffer lost packets as it does not provide for retransmissions at the protocol level.

New connections, either those handled by the PVA or through software, cannot be accepted during the reset interval as traffic cannot be forwarded to or through the PVA until the reset has completed.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR60725 and CR69144, and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.1.2 for the BIG-IP 9.1 software branch and in version 9.2.4 for the BIG-IP 9.2 software branch. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
