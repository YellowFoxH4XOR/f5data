Description

FQDN-based AFM firewall rule stopped working

Environment

  • BIG-IP AFM
  • Firewall Rules that contain overlapping, mixed case, FQDN entries

Cause

The issue, caused by ID881985, causes FQDN matching to fail when a given FQDN is present in multiple rules, using different cases.

In the example below, cloud.example.com and ltm1.example.com are present in separate rules, written using different cases and will trigger the issue. The other duplicate, bigip1.example.com, is only ever written in lower case and doesn't trigger the issue.

FQDNID 
big-iq1.example.com6 
cloud.bigip1.example.com2Rule List: example_rule_list, Rule: example_rule_1
Cloud.bigip1.example.com5Rule List: example_rule_list, Rule: example_rule_2
ltm1.example.com3Rule List: example_rule_list, Rule: example_rule_3
LTM1.example.com8Rule List: example_rule_list, Rule: example_rule_4
bigip2.example.com7 
bigip1.example.com1These entries are not mixed case and do not trigger the issue
bigip1.example.com4These entries are not mixed case and do not trigger the issue
example.com10 
info.example.com9 

Recommended Actions

  1. Review your FQDN rules and replace any FQDNs present more than once with a single case.

    tmsh show security firewall fqdn-info fqdn all | grep -i FQDN | sort | awk '{ print $2 }'

  2. After setting redundant FQDNs to a single case, reboot the device.

    reboot
