Topic

You should consider using this procedure under the following conditions:

  • You need to install the power supply unit due to a Return Materials Authorization (RMA).
  • You need to swap out a power supply unit or remove it for testing.

Description

BIG-IP platforms can support up to two power supply units, however, some platforms come with only one power supply by default. For BIG-IP platforms with two installed power supplies, you can hot swap power supplies. In the event of a power supply failure, you can install a replacement power supply without powering down the system, as long as there is at least one power supply operating during the replacement process.

Important: VIPRION platforms with blades installed may require more than one power supply to ensure the blades are powered. For more information, refer to the AC power redundancy provisioning section of the Platform Specifications chapter in the Platform Guide for your VIPRION platform. For information about how to locate F5 product manuals, refer to K98133564: Tips for searching AskF5 and finding product documentation.

Important: The BIG-IP iSeries platforms use two different AC power supply units (PSUs).

  • The i2000/i4000 series platforms use a General Electric (GE) AC power supply (part number PWR-0334-xx).
  • The i5000/i7000/i10000/i11000 series platforms use a Murata AC power supply (part number PWR-0306-xx).

You should install only the PSU designed for your specific iSeries platform model. However, the size and shape of the two PSUs are the same and allow either of the PSUs to physically fit into the different i2000/i4000/i5000/i7000/i10000/i11000 series platforms. For more information, refer to: K36588206: AC power supply units for different BIG-IP iSeries platform models should not be interchanged.

Prerequisites

You must meet the following prerequisites to use this procedure:

  • You have the replacement power supply unit, and your BIG-IP platform is running redundant power supplies with at least one working power supply.
  • You are aware of the warnings and cautions required when handling power related equipment.

Important: BIG-IP platforms and their power supplies are sensitive to electrostatic discharge. F5 recommends that you use proper electrostatic discharge grounding procedures and equipment when you install or maintain the unit.

Important: Running the BIG-IP platform without power supplies installed in all available bays in the platform can affect cooling and electromagnetic interference. If you need to run the unit with fewer power supplies, you must install a blank supply bracket into any empty power supply bays. The blank supply bracket is required to maintain proper airflow in the system. If you do not have a blank supply bracket, leave all supplies installed and unplug any unused power supplies.

Important: F5 strongly recommends that you use only one power supply type (AC or DC) in a platform. Do not mix power supply types.

Procedures

To install a replacement power supply in your BIG-IP platform, perform the following procedure:

Note: The following procedures are generic. For platform specific instructions, refer to the Platform Guide for your VIPRION Platform. For information about how to locate F5 product manuals, refer to K98133564: Tips for searching AskF5 and finding product documentation.

Impact of procedure: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Prepare the system for power supply unit replacement:
    • For a single-supply system, use the touchscreen LCD to halt and power off the system.
    • For a dual-supply system, make sure that you have an operational supply already installed in your system if you want to hot swap a supply while the system is running. If you do not want to hot swap, you can perform the replacement with the system powered off.
  2. Before removing the power supply unit from your system, disconnect the AC power cord from the power supply by pulling one or both of the power cord locking tabs away from the power supply.
  3. Remove the failed AC power supply unit by squeezing the ejector latch and pulling the handle straight toward you.
  4. Make sure that the power switch on the new power supply is powered off.

    Important: If the power supply is installed when the power switch is on, the power supply may not function until you shut off the power switch and power it on again.

  5. Ensure that the power cord is not connected to the new power supply before adding it to the unit.
  6. If one is installed, remove the cover plate from the power supply bay.
  7. Slide the new power supply into the power supply bay.
  8. Tighten the screw into place. The power supply is connected to the system when you tighten the screw completely.

    Note: Depending on the model and revision of the power supply, you might need either a Phillips or a slotted screwdriver to replace the power supply.

  9. Attach the power cord to the new power supply.
  10. Attach the power cord to the power source.

    Note: As a safety precaution, the socket outlet must be installed near the equipment and be easily accessible.

  11. Turn the power switch on and make sure the new power supply functions properly.
