Topic

F5 has released 10.2.1 Hotfix-BIGIP-10.2.1-511.0-HF3. BIG-IP 10.2.1 HF3 corrects several issues that affect customers running 10.2.1, and adds support for the Appliance Mode feature.

Note: For information about Appliance Mode, contact your F5 Sales Representative.

Important: If you are currently running 10.2.1 HF-2 and have installed Engineering hotfixes, check with F5 Technical Support to confirm whether the Engineering hotfixes are included in 10.2.1 HF-3 before installing the hotfix.

The following IDs are fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-10.2.1-511.0-HF3 for BIG-IP 10.2.1:
