Description

Guidance on how to gracefully shutdown BIG-IP VE which installed on Hypervisor (example ESXi).

Environment

  • BIG-IP VE
  • Hypervisor
  • Guidance How-to
  • Shutdown Gracefully

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

You may use Shell or TMOS command "# shutdown" to gracefully power off down the F5 BIG-IP VE.

1. SSH into BIG-IP VE
2. You can either initiate the command via Linux Shell or TMOS, and the BIG-IP VE will goes into power off status
example Linux Shell
# shutdown

example TMOS
(tmos)# shutdown

3. Else, you may also use Shell command to halt the BIG-IP VE, and then will need to go to the hypervisor steps below to shutdown the BIG-IP VE.
example Linux Shell
# halt

4. Extra information, you may not be able to shutdown the BIG-IP via Configuration Utility (GUI), instead, you may only initiate reboot.
    Login to BIG-IP VE GUI > System > Configuration > Device > General > Click on the Reboot [button]
     

5. Lastly, please go to hypervisor to perform necessary steps to either shutdown or reboot the BIG-IP VE.
Notes: F5 doesn't manage the hypervisor (example VMware EXSi), you may want to check further with your team who managed the virtual host where the BIG-IP VE is installed on.

Example steps can be found from VMware documentation portal.
