K000163596: RADIUS Monitor Fails After Upgrade to 21.1.0

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-10-08
Updated
2026-10-08
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 21.1.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000163596

Description

A RADIUS monitor on BIG-IP LTM may incorrectly mark a RADIUS service down after an upgrade. Monitor logs may include:

  • Received 20 characters of packet of length 20
  • [RADIUS_monitor] response authenticator verification FAILED
  • EAV failed
  • visit DOWN

Environment

  • Product: BIG-IP LTM
  • Software branch: BIG-IP 21.1.x
  • Monitor type: RADIUS

Cause

This issue is associated with Bug ID 2395777. The RADIUS monitor incorrectly handles the response authenticator, causing verification to fail and the monitored service to be marked down.

Recommended Actions

  1. Contact F5 Support to obtain the applicable Engineering Hotfix for the BIG-IP software branch.
  2. Install the Engineering Hotfix according to the supplied instructions.
  3. Test the RADIUS monitor.
  4. Confirm that the monitor successfully validates the RADIUS response and that the service is no longer incorrectly marked down.
  5. Follow the F5 critical issue hotfix policy when testing and deploying the fix.
