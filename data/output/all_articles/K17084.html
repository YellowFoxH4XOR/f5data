Known Issue

The Diameter monitor fails when a monitored pool member's response arrives in two separate packets.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your pool is configured to monitor pool members by using the Diameter monitor.
  • The pool member's Diameter response arrives in two separate packets.

Impact

The pool member status remains as Down or Offline, and pool members are unavailable for load balancing.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • A monitored pool member status remains as Down or Offline after applying the Diameter monitor.
  • When viewing packet captures, you notice that the BIG-IP system sends TCP FIN and RST packets after receiving the pool member's first response packet.
