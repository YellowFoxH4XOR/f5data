Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) process produces a core file and restarts when the single sign-on (SSO) log level is configured to Debug.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system is configured for SSO.
  • You set the SSO log level to Debug.

Impact

Traffic is disrupted while the TMM process produces a core file and restarts. Systems configured as part of a high availability (HA) device group may fail over to a peer device.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The TMM process generates a core file to the /shared/core directory.
  • Your BIG-IP system generates a SIGSEGV fault message in the /var/log/tmm log file.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can configure the the SSO log to a less verbose level, such as Info or Notice.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
