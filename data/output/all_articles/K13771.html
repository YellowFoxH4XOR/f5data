Known Issue

The VIPRION 2400 may incorrectly report excessively large figures for the throughput statistics. This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The VIPRION 2400 is configured with a virtual server that has ePVA enabled

Typically a TCP virtual server assigned with a FastL4 profile enables the ePVA. This issue occurs because the VIPRION 2400 fails to properly compute statistics for the ePVA connections.

Impact

Throughput statistics are not readily available.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H377637 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Low screen.
  • When comparing deltas between intervals of SNMP counters for throughput statistics, the figures are reported to be higher than expected. For example, the delta of ltmVirtualServStatClientBytesOut is 130 Gbps within 30 seconds when the expected value is 6 Gbps.
  • The throughput performance graph or throughput graph in the dashboard may report figures that are much higher than what the pool members received. For example, the graph reports 130 Gbps of throughput when the pool members report only 6 Gbps of throughput combined.
