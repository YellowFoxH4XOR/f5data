Known Issue

BIG-IP Advanced Routing daemons may not start on a secondary blade on standby units.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are running two VIPRION hosts in a high-availability configuration.
  • Each VIPRION host has more than one blade.
  • The primary blade on the standby host is restarted or the mcpd process on that blade is restarted.

Impact

There may be a routing failure in the event of a failover.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptom:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H573035 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Low screen.
  • In the event of a failover, one or more of the following daemons may not start on the secondary blade of the standby host:
    • BIG-IP 11.x: oamd, ospf6d, ripd, ripngd
    • BIG-IP 12.x: mribd, oamd, pimd, ripd, ripngd

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can restart the tmrouted daemon on the newly active host after a failover event. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: You may have temporary loss of routing and connectivity until you run this command.

  1. Log in to the newly active BIG-IP host as the root user.
  2. Type the following command:

    bigstart restart tmrouted
