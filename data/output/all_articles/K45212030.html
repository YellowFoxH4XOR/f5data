Known Issue

Enabling encrypted cookies may cause cookie persistence to fail.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is running 10.2.4 HF12 or 10.2.4 HF13.
  • The Encrypt Cookies field in the HTTP profile is configured with the cookie name of the cookie used for persistence.

Impact

Requests are not persisted to a pool member as expected.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Requests do not persist to a pool member as expected.
