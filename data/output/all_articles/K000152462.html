Description

This KB article explains how to verify if apmd was restarted.

Environment

  • APM

  • apmd

Cause

The BIGIP administrator may require to check if apmd daemon was restarted.

Answer/Recommended Actions

To verify whether the apmd daemon was restarted, check in CLI /var/log/daemon.log. When the daemon was restarted, you should see the log line similar to:

Jul 1 13:48:17 bigip.local emerg logger[22669]: Re-starting apmd

None
