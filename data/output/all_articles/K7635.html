Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If you modify the origin address of a SNAT after the SNAT has been created and associated with the specified VLANs, SNAT listeners associated with those VLANS are not correctly updated. As a result, changes made to the SNAT for specific origin IP addresses are not enabled.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR66374, CR70506, CR68132, and CR66375, and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM versions 9.1.3, 9.2.4, 9.3.0, and 9.4.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.
