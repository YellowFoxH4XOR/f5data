Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Under rare conditions the AHA compression hardware driver can cause data corruption by reading invalid data.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR71826 and CR71827, and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.1.3 and 9.3.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.
