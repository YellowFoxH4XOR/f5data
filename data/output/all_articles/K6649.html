Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The asmqkview process currently uses the bzip archive format, which consumes a higher CPU percent than other archive formats. As such, the asmqkview process can use the maximum amount of CPU0, causing resource issues for the BIG-IP ASM system.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR69315 and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM 9.2.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM release notes.
