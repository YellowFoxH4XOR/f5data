Known Issue

This is a result of a known issue. If you are using network failover, the standby unit may take up to 40 seconds or more to become active if the previously active unit reboots or is unreachable.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR81981 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.3.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
