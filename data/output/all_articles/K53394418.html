Known Issue

BIG-IP PEM may return DHCPv6 responses to the wrong DHCP client.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • Multiple DHCP clients send DHCPv6 solicit messages to BIG-IP PEM in DHCP relay mode.

Impact

DHCPv6 responses may be returned to the wrong DHCP clients.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Packet captures indicate that the BIG-IP system returns DHCPv6 responses to the wrong DHCP clients.
  • DHCP clients are unable to obtain a valid address.
