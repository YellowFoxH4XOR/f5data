K62045676: Redeploy of SSLO topologies may fail after an upgrade if the SSLO units cannot communicate over the Management port

Type
Knowledge
Published
2023-03-31
Updated
2023-03-31
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K62045676

Description

Redeploy of SSLO topologies may fail after an upgrade if the SSLO units cannot communicate over the Management port
The Big-IP may return an error similar to: 

[SSLO] Internal error: proxy deploy returned failure :{"templateId":"f5-ssl-orchestrator-topology","state":"ERROR","errorMessage":"checkBlockState timed out after trying for 90 seconds."}

 

Environment

SSLO Orchestrators in an HA pair
 

Cause

SSLO currently presumes management connectivity as part of it's HA environment
 

Recommended Actions

Ensure the management ports on the two units in an HA environment can communicate before attempting to redeploy your SSLO topologies.
