Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The Clear LDNS Statistics button in the Configuration utility does not work.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR31239 and CR41909.

Workaround

To clear the LDNS statistics, reload the configuration by typing the following command:

3ndc reload
