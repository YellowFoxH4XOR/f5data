K000150469: ASM Events are NOT synced from Active to Standby

Type
Knowledge
Published
2025-06-11
Updated
2025-06-11
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP ASM
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000150469

Description

Detected violations reported by support-ids will NOT sync to the standby Big-IP .

Also, events generated by Bot profiles and DoS profiles are NOT synced from active to standby

So, when the Big-IP fails over, previously occurred violations can not be found on the now active Big-IP and thus have to be verified on the standby.

 

Environment

All version of Big-IP ASM and Big-IP Advanced WAF.

 

Cause

This is by design and expected behavior.

 

Recommended Action 

None
