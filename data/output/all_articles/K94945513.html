Description

After a configuration change is made through GUI you hit "update", however, the GUI stays in "Loading...." and eventually displays a message "Secure Connection Failed".  The update is never made.

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • Configuration Utility

Cause

Unknown at the time of publication

Recommended Actions

A quick workaround is to restart the configuration utility daemons:

#bigstart restart httpd restjavad
