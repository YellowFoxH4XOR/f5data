Topic

Note: BIG-IP does not support MLDv2. For a list of the supported protocols availible with the Advanced Routing Module, refer to K6654: Routing protocols and extensions included in the BIG-IP Advanced Routing Module.

IPv6 routers use the Multicast Listener Discovery (MLD) version 2 Protocol to discover the presence of multicast listeners on the router's directly attached links. Multicast listeners are devices prepared to receive multicast packets. The MLD also discovers which multicast addresses are of interest to neighboring devices.

Note: For more information about MLDv2, refer to RFC3810.
