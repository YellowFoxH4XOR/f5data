Device is booting in a loop or randomly restarting.

Messages similar to the following are logged to /var/log/ltm:

notice chmand[5414]: 012a0005:5: AOM total reset count: 1
notice chmand[5414]: 012a0005:5: AOM power-on reset count: 1
notice chmand[5414]: 012a0005:5: AOM pin reset count: 1
info chmand[5414]: 012a0006:6: Latched Event:0x15: AC power fail event
info chmand[5414]: 012a0006:6: Latched Event:0x3: LOP was reset due to standby power up event

Environment

  • BIG-IP 2000/4000/5000/7000/10000/12000 series

Cause

External power failure.

Recommended Actions

Investigate if there was an external power failure.

Perform the troubleshooting procedures in:

  • K52015891: Troubleshooting BIG-IP power supply issues

None
