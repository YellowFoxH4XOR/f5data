Topic

When BIG-IP ASM does not have adequate available jobs or enough free memory to allocate for new transactions, BIG-IP ASM will reset new incoming connections and existing connections until enough resources have been made available to meet the level of incoming connections.

When BIG-IP ASM resets existing connections, it will reset both client and server side sessions. Connections on the server side are chosen for reset from the least recently used connections, and those that have received no response from the server, in order to minimize the effect of connection reaping on active connections.
