Known Issue

Users are unable to close the BIG-IP Edge Client logon window.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP Edge Client is connected in Always connected mode.
  • The login page is customized and redirects users to perform additional steps on a different page.

Impact

Users are unable to enter their login credentials after they are redirected to a different page.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, users may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When users attempt to close the BIG-IP Edge Client logon window, the following message appears:

    Your system administrator requires you to login in order to get access to the network. To change these settings, contact your system administrator.

  • Users are unable to establish the network access connection.
