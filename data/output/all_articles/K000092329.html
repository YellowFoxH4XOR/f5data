K000092329: Update Bot Signatures NGINX App Protect system

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-08-12
Updated
2023-12-07
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000092329

Description

The BOT Signatures will be updated with the upgrade. It does not exist feature to update them separately.

 

Environment

  • NGINX App Protect
  • BOT Signatures

Cause

None.


Recommended Actions

The BOT signatures are contained in the upgrade.
 

Additional Information

The ability to update the Bot Signatures externally from the full upgrade is not being implemented yet.
