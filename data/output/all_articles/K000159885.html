K000159885: Recurring "Failed to post device to device-group tm-shared-all-big-ips" Warning Messages in restjavad Logs on BIG-IP HA Pair

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-02-04
Updated
2026-07-30
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • F5 Distributed Cloud Services
  • BIG-IP LTM
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000159885

Description

  • BIG-IP systems in a high availability (HA) configuration may generate repeated warning messages in the restjavad logs similar to the following:

    [WARNING][8100/tm/shared/bigip-failover-state BigipFailoverStateWorker] Failed to discover <peer_IP_address>: java.lang.IllegalStateException: Failed to post device <UUID> to device-group tm-shared-all-big-ips: java.lang.IllegalArgumentException: A device with ip address '<peer_IP_address>' has already been discovered in this group.
  • Despite these warnings appearing in the logs, HA failover status remains normal and operational on both devices. The warnings typically recur at regular intervals (such as every 20 minutes or every few days).

Environment

  • BIG-IP 
  • Device Service Clustering (DSC) configured with two or more devices
  • HA failover status: Normal (Active/Standby functioning correctly)
  • Possible recent activities: UCS restore, device trust changes, platform migration, or tenant provisioning

Cause

Corruption or duplication of device objects in the REST framework's device group database (tm-shared-all-big-ips). This can occur when:

 

  • Duplicate or stale device entries exist with conflicting machineId values
  • UCS files containing REST database information are restored to devices
  • Devices are cloned or migrated without regenerating unique REST identifiers
  • Device trust operations are performed improperly

Recommended Actions

Impact of procedure: The following steps do not impact business traffic or HA failover functionality. Brief interruption to GUI/REST API access may occur during restjavad restart. Perform these actions during a maintenance window and back up current configurations before proceeding.

Perform the following steps on both devices in the HA pair (one device at a time, starting with the standby device):

  1. Generate a UCS backup - https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K13132
  2. Log in to the BIG-IP system's Advanced Shell (bash).
  3. Delete BIG-IP REST device-group-key-pairs tm-shared-all-big-ips with the following command:

    restcurl -X DELETE /shared/device-group-key-pairs/tm-shared-all-big-ips

Additional Information

    Note: Do not use "clear-rest-storage" as this may delete configuration for modules using "Guided Configuration".  See K67220984
