Known Issue

Some VIPRION blades may experience link flapping when a secondary blade is disabled.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are using two or more VIPRION 2100 blades in a VIPRION 2400 chassis.
  • The blades are connected to a Cisco switch and an external port negotiates a 1 Gigabit interface speed.
  • A secondary blade is disabled as a cluster member.

Impact

External monitors may see the blade as down rather than disabled.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • In the /var/log/ltm log file, you observe error messages similar to the following example:

    info bcm56xxd[<pid>]: 012c0015:6: Link: 1/1.7 is DOWN
    info bcm56xxd[<pid>]: 012c0015:6: Link: 1/1.7 is UP
    info lacpd[<pid>]: 01160009:6: Link 1/1.7 added to aggregation

  • External monitors may detect that the VIPRION blade is down.
