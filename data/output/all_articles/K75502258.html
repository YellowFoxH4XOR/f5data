K75502258: How do I verify what version of the BIG-IP Edge client I am using?

Type
Support Solution
Published
2020-12-21
Updated
2023-02-21
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP APM
  • APM Clients
Source

Description

This article will explain to you how to verify the version of the edge client installed.

Environment

Relevant environmental factors specific to the topic

  • BIG-IP APM 
  • Connecting with BIG-IP Edge client

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

The actions or steps required to address the topic covered in the description.

  1. Open the BIG-IP Edge client application.
  2. Click on the View details button
  3. Click on the Miscellaneous tab
  4. You should now be able to see the version information.
