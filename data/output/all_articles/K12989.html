Known Issue

Description

FirePass clients accessing web applications through a Portal Access favorite may experience intermittent failures.

This issue occurs when the all of the following conditions are met:

  • User accesses a web application through a Portal Access favorite.
  • The web application depends on cookies for its functionality.

Impact

Portions of the web application that are dependent on cookies may not function as expected.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • Users may be repeatedly prompted for their credentials even after entering correct username and password.
  • FirePass may insert duplicate cookies into the response.
  • FirePass may remove the existing cookie values and insert null-value cookies into the response.
  • OWA users may receive an error message that appears similar to the following example:

    You may have already opened your mailbox on a different browser tab. If you feel you've received this message in error, try refreshing your browser
