Known Issue

The tmstat command reports CPU statistics that incorrectly show spikes or negative values.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The system experiences an internal timing error associated with statistics collection.

Impact

The system displays incorrect CPU statistics.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • On blade systems, the results from the tmstat and tmstat cpu commands may spike high or go negative due to an issue with how the system collects per-blade statistics.
