The FirePass device can be connected to various Out of Band (OOB) or Lights Out Management (LOM) console servers to manage the device without network connectivity. Some frequently used examples of console servers devices are Cyclades, Alterpath, KVM, and Dell DRAC. Most console servers connect using a RJ45 cable; however, the physical port on the FirePass device is a DB9 port. A standard DB9 to RJ45 cable can be used.

Note: For more information about configuring this connection, refer to K9286: Accessing the FirePass Maintenence Console.
