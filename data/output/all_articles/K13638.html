Topic

The front panel indicator LEDs may temporarily flash at the end of the hardware startup sequence on the following platforms:

  • BIG-IP 1600, 3600, and 3900

There is a variation in timing during the hardware startup sequence that may cause LEDs to flash at the end of the hardware startup sequence on a certain percentage of the listed platforms.

If you have multiple platforms that are affected, you may notice a variation in the behavior of LEDs during startup between devices.

Impact

None. The variation in behavior does not constitute a hardware failure.
