K000159963: High Latency on Internal Interface

Type

Published
2026-07-07

Description

When pinging the self-IP address on an internal interface of an appliance running F5OS, ICMP echo replies from the appliance to a directly connected device may show slightly higher latency compared to replies from the device to the appliance self-IP. For example, a device pinging the appliance self-IP typically receives replies in less than 1ms, while the appliance pinging the device may see response times greater than 1ms. This behavior may be observed in latency-sensitive environments.

Environment

  • F5OS Appliance
  • Software Version: F5OS 1.5.1
  • Internal Interface, ICMP (ping) testing
  • Direct connection between device and appliance interface on the same VLAN
  • No intermediate devices; HA pair configuration

Cause

The observed latency difference is expected and normal for F5OS appliances. The slightly higher latency when the appliance pings an external device is due to additional processing incurred by F5OS when handling self-IP ping replies. This is inherent in the F5OS design and does not indicate a performance issue.

Recommended Actions

No action is required unless other symptoms or impacts on production traffic are observed. The minor latency difference is normal for F5OS and does not indicate a defect or misconfiguration.

Additional Information

This behavior is specific to F5OS appliances and is a result of the way F5OS processes ICMP echo replies for self-IP addresses. Users should expect this minor latency difference and can consider it normal for F5OS 1.5.1 and similar versions.
