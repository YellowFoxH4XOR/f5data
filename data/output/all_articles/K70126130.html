Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may generate Address Resolution Protocols (ARPs) after transitioning to the standby state.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured a BIG-IP high availability (HA) pair.
  • You have configured a Virtual Local Area Network (VLAN) group with the Bridge in Standby option in the default disabled state.
  • The system receives an ARP just before transitioning or failing over to the standby state.

Impact

The system generates unexpected ARP requests that might result in packet loops.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H652387 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Low screen.
  • The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) generates unexpected ARP requests after entering the standby state.
