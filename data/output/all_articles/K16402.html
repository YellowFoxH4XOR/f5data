Known Issue

The rsync daemon may not restart after configuration changes are made to the BIG-IP system.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have multiple BIG-IP devices configured in a device group.
  • You make a configuration change to one of the BIG-IP devices in the device group.

Impact

Configuration changes made to one BIG-IP device may not synchronize to all other BIG-IP devices in a device group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Configuration changes made to one BIG-IP device are not synchronized to other members of the device group.
