K000159665: Service Check Date is showing old date after Subscription renewal

Description

After successful subscription renewal in MyF5 portal, Service Check Date of license is still showing that of the expired support contract, that is not of current date.

 

Environment

  • MyF5
  • License Reactivation
  • Subscription Renewed
  • Service Checked Date of previous Subscription expiry

 

Cause

Backend data synchronization issue.

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

Contact F5 Support to assist in resolving this issue.
