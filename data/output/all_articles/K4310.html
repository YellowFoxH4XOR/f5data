Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When you perform a specific sequence of actions in the FirePass Administrative Console, the right browser frame of the Administrative Console may occasionally appear blank.

F5 Networks Product Development is tracking this issue as CR45396.

Workaround

You can work around this issue by right-clicking the content window in your browser and clicking Refresh from the menu.
