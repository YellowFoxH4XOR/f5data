Description

The F5 rSeries hardware is shipped with the serial failover port located at the front of the platform. However, this is not supported for HA failover of the rSeries device. Because HA failover is not supported between rSeries platforms. The F5OS layer in rSeries appliances doesn't have the concept of HA between systems; redundancy and failover is configured at the tenant level. 

In addition, no failover cables are shipped with the rSeries hardware.

Environment

  • rSeries platforms
  • Hardware failover
  • Tenant failover

Cause

Support for high availability HA, failover in rSeries platforms.

Recommended Actions

When configuring BIG-IP HA failover on rSeries platforms, consider the following:

  • HA including serial (hardwired) failover is not supported within or between rSeries platforms at the appliance or F5OS level.
  • Per K8665 HA is supported between heterogeneous platforms for the tenants. However, "Features that rely on similar resources, such as connection and persistence mirroring, may not be supported for device groups consisting of heterogeneous hardware, virtual platforms or BIG-IP software versions'". To do so...
    • "Beginning in BIG-IP 11.4.0, F5 supports redundant devices in a device group on different hardware or virtual platforms through the load-aware failover feature. If you are configuring devices with different capabilities/capacities, for example a BIG-IP iSeries and a BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) tenant running on a F5 rSeries, you must configure the ha-capacity setting on both devices. This setting specifies a number that represents the relative capacity of the device to be active for a number of traffic groups; the higher the number the greater the capacity to take on connections." 
    • The other consideration is when using a specific APM HA configuration (Should only be used for DR type setups)... K7040: BIG-IP APM and FirePass do not support mixing hardware platforms within an HA pair
  • To implement HA between the tenants, you set up device service clustering (DSC) on the BIG-IP tenants. For more information, refer to the following:
    • Tenant high availability (HA) overview section in the Tenant Management chapter of the F5 rSeries Systems: Administration and Configuration manual.
    • The BIG-IP Device Service Clustering: Administration manual.

      Note: For information about how to locate F5 product manuals, refer to K12453464: Finding product documentation on AskF5.
