Known Issue

The standby BIG-IP system may generate a packet with EtherType 0x23f5. This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The traffic group is configured with a MAC masquerade address
  • The BIG-IP system is configured with a trunk
  • The BIG-IP system receives a packet destined for the masqueraded MAC address from a downstream switch

Impact

When the standby device responds to the packet from the downstream switch, the downstream switch now sends all traffic destined for the masqueraded MAC address to the standby devce. As a result, traffic is disrupted.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • Traffic is disrupted because traffic is directed at the standby device instead of active device
  • Traffic captures at the interface level shows the standby device sending out packets with EtherType 0x23f5 using the masqueraded MAC address as the source mac address and 00:00:00:00:00:00 as the destination MAC address
