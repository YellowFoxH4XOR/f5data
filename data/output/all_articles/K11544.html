Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When compressing content, the pvac process unnecessarily clears the 64K deflate buffers used by the zlib utility upon initiation of each gzip deflate operation. As a result, BIG-IP WebAccelerator system performance may be negatively impacted.
 
F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR138174 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 10.2.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP WebAccelerator release notes.

This issue has been fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-10.1.0-3372.0-HF1 for BIG-IP 10.1.0. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
