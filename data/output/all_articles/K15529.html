Known Issue

BIG-IP systems licensed for WebAccelerator or BIG-IP WOM are not licensed for BIG-IP Application Acceleration Manager (AAM) after an upgrade.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your system is licensed for WebAccelerator or BIG-IP WOM, and was activated before BIG-IP 11.4.0.
  • You upgrade to BIG-IP 11.4.0 or later.
  • Your license file includes the mod_woc or mod_wa flags.

Impact

Users cannot access BIG-IP AAM after an upgrade.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H457565 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High screen.
  • BIG-IP AAM is shown as not licensed in the BIG-IP License Manager.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 444388 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. Workaround

To work around this issue, you can re-activate your license after you install BIG-IP 11.4.0 or later. To do so, follow the instructions outlined in K7752: Licensing the BIG-IP system.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
