Description

You may notice the front panel LED(s) brightness variation from the following components: Status/Alarm LED, F5 Logo Ball, and port LEDs.

Environment

This article applies to the following platforms:

  • iSeries
  • rSeries
  • VELOS

Cause

F5 does multi source procurement for components used on the platform to improve stability of supply chain which include LED light pipe. This involves comprehensive testing to ensure components such as luminance and color variation are within product specification. All light pipes are confirmed to meet specification. Based on qualification testing, there is luminance difference between suppliers. The following table shows luminance specification and the result of measurements.

Luminance specification and measurements

The luminance variation may be relatively large with certain LED combination, however both supplier parts are within specification.

Recommended Actions

The luminance difference is not linear to human perceptive brightness difference. The following chart shows the relation between luminance (x-axis) and perceived brightness (y-axis). 45% luminance change will be perceived as 17% brightness change, and 175% luminance change will be 41% of perceived brightness change. 
 


You may still notice a brightness difference by comparing different units. However, that is within F5's product specification and request for an RMA may not be accepted due to this brightness difference.
