Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. BIG-IP LTM systems utilizing the FIPS module for SSL key management may experience a memory leak in the iControlportal.cgi daemon.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR74557, and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.1.3, 9.3, and 9.4.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
