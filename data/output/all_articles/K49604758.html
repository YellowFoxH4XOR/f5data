Policy Information

This Acceptable Use Policy ("Acceptable Use Policy") sets forth guidelines for acceptable use of F5’s cloud-based application services marketed as Silverline services (the “Silverline Services”). By using the Silverline Services, the customer (“Customer”) acknowledges that it has read, understood and agrees to comply with the terms of this Acceptable Use Policy. Any defined terms used herein and not otherwise defined herein shall have the meaning as set forth in the Silverline Master Services Agreement by and between F5 and Customer. Customer agrees to ensure that there is no breach, compromise or violation, by Customer employees, consultants, agents, customers, suppliers or independent contractors (“Customer Representatives”) of this Acceptable Use Policy and will be responsible for any such non-compliance.

1.1 Customer agrees to use the Silverline Services for lawful purposes only.

1.2 Customer will not and will not allow a third party to:

(i) decompile, reverse engineer, disassemble or otherwise attempt to derive, analyze or use any source code or underlying ideas or algorithms related to the Silverline Services by any means whatsoever (including, but not limited to review of data structures or similar materials produced by the Silverline Services);
(ii) remove or alter any product identification, markings, copyright or other notices;
(iii) use or allow the use of the Silverline Services by or for the benefit of third parties, including without limitation by renting, leasing, lending, timesharing, hosting, outsourcing, subscription service or using for service bureau purposes;
(iv) sell, distribute, transfer, assign, give, translate or market the Silverline Services (or any interest thereto);
(v) publish any results of any benchmarking or other tests run on the Silverline Services; or
(vi) modify or create derivative works based on the Silverline Services.

1.3 The below is conduct deemed by F5 to be inappropriate, improper or harmful to F5's reputation, network or Silverline Services and therefore prohibited when using the Services.

(i) causing, aiding, encouraging, or facilitating a domain or URL hosted by F5 for Customer or any Customer end user to point to or otherwise direct traffic to any material in violation of any applicable law or regulation;
(ii) using, encouraging, aiding or facilitating the use of the Silverline Services to (including by pointing to web sites or locations that) create, transmit, distribute or store material that: violates trademark, patent, copyright, trade secret or other intellectual property laws, violate the privacy, publicity or other personal rights of others, include tools designed for compromising security (including but not limited to password guessing programs, cracking tools or network probing tools), violate U.S. export control, data protection or anti-terrorism laws, or impair the privacy of communications;
(iii) using, encouraging, aiding or facilitating the use of the Silverline Services in connection with any material, information or content that: may be obscene, pornographic, harassing, threatening, abusive or discriminatory or which encourage hatred against an identifiable group; are defamatory, knowingly contain viruses; or constitute a criminal offense or give rise to civil liability;
(iv) attempting to penetrate or manipulate, or encourage, aid or facilitate the penetration or manipulation of, the security features of the F5 Silverline network or any other system (including but not limited to unauthorized access to or use of data, systems or networks, probing, scanning or testing the vulnerability of a system or network, breaching security authentication measures, unauthorized monitoring of data or traffic, interfering with the service of any user, host or network by any means; forging any TCP/IP packet header or any part of a message header);
(v) posting article(s) or substantively similar articles(s) to an excessive number of newsgroups using a F5 hosted domain or posting such messages through a Silverline Services; or
(vi) sending unsolicited and/or mass e-mailings, whether or not such activities provoke complaints from the recipients (F5 has a zero tolerance policy on the sending of SPAM, junk e-mail or unsolicited commercial email (emails may not contain forged headers or fake contact information).

1.4 If Customer becomes aware of any activity that would violate this Acceptable Use Policy, Customer shall notify F5 pursuant to the notice provisions in the applicable customer agreement for the Silverline Services, shall take all reasonable action to prevent content from being routed to or passed through the F5 Silverline network and agrees to use reasonable efforts to cooperate with F5 to resolve the violation accordingly.

1.5 In addition to F5’s other rights or remedies available to F5, F5 may (i) immediately, temporarily suspend services where continued use of services (a) violate applicable law (b) will have a material adverse impact on the F5 Silverline network, or (c) F5 reasonably believes suspension is necessary to mitigate any damage or liability resulting violation of this Acceptable Use Policy; and (ii) notify the appropriate law-enforcement agencies of any violation of this Acceptable Use Policy.

F5 reserves the right to modify or replace this Acceptable Use Policy at any time and in F5’s sole discretion. F5 will indicate at the top of this Acceptable Use Policy the date such document was last updated. Any changes will be effective immediately upon posting the revised version on www.askf5.com or a successor website (or such later effective date as may be indicated at the top of the revised Acceptable Use Policy). The customer’s continued use of the Silverline Services following the posting of any changes to this Acceptable Use Policy will constitute your acceptance of such changes. If the Customer does not agree to the changes, it must stop using the Silverline Services. In addition, F5 may provide other methods by which Customer may accept this Acceptable Use Policy or changes to this Acceptable Use Policy.
