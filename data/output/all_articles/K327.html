Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When you change the load balancing mode for a wide IP pool to QoS, the QoS values for VS Capacity and Kilobytes/Second may also change.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR20213, CR22131, CR26154, and CR41811.

Workaround

To work around this issue, review the QoS settings for the wide IP pool when you change the load balancing mode to QoS, and correct the values as required.
