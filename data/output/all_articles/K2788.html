The following are the specifications of the Fiber Gigabit Ethernet ports that are included on BIG-IP models 2400 and 5100 platforms:
  • The ports use the Gigabit Ethernet Protocol at 1.25G Baud
  • The optical transceivers are designed for bi-directional multi-mode communication
  • The lasers are 850 nm (multi-mode) emitters
  • The receiver is sensitive to light between 770 nm and 860 nm
  • The spectral width of the transmitter is 0.85 nm RMS maximum
  • The minimum optical input power is -17 dBm and the maximum is 0 dBm (BER <1.0E012)

Note: The optical transceivers used on the 5100 platform are the Agilent HFBR-53A5VEM and the Stratos SDX-19-4-1-T-L.

Given the optical power of the transmitters of -7 (+/-3) dBm, you can use cables that meet the following specifications:

  • 500 meters of 62.5 um core MMF fiber
  • 750 meters of 50 um core MMF fiber
