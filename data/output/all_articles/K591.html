Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The random load balancing mode incorrectly distributes connections in a fixed ratio. The ratio begins with zero and increments by one for each available pool. For example, if you have three pools, 3-DNS will load balance connections to the pools using a ratio of 0:1:2.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR32762 and CR41845.
