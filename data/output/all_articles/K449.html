Topic

The following error messages may appear in the 3-DNS Controller logs:

big3d: CFG:ProcessIQMessage: invalid iQuery message from 64.210.248.164:iqslave.cpp:1190

named[11453]: CFG:MasterProcessReply: Received iQuery unimplemented message from 64.0.99.140:iqmaster.c:1629

Note: F5 is working to eliminate exclusionary language in our products and documentation. For more information, refer to K34150231: Exclusionary language in F5 products and documentation.

These two messages can occur under the following circumstances:

  • The versions of the big3d daemon on the BIG-IP Controller and the big3d daemon on the 3-DNS Controller are different versions.
  • The big3d agent is communicating with a non-crypto-enabled agent using encrypted communications. This is typically due to a misconfiguration of the 3-DNS.
  • Packet corruption exists.

To verify that iQuery communication is working properly, check the 3-DNS Configuration utility statistics page to ensure all of the BIG-IP and 3-DNS Controllers are displaying as green or yellow.

If these messages are infrequent and do not seem to be causing a problem, you should not be concerned.
