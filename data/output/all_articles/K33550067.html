Known Issue

On rare occasions, a race condition may cause incoming Clustered Multiprocessing (CMP) messages to be overwritten.

Upon receiving a CMP message, the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may process an internal event that sends another CMP message, which overwrites the first incoming message before it is processed.

Impact

Features that use CMP messages may behave inconsistently. Monitors may incorrectly mark pool members down, and connections may appear to stall.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Address Resolution Protocol (ARP) lookups fail, causing intermittent traffic failure.
  • Persistence record lookups fail, causing the system to lose the session state.
