Topic

Note: Blade Controller Software, based on the version 4.x software, will continue to be supported and customers will still be able to use the software and receive the benefits of its use. Current blade server platforms do not support the necessary minimum hardware requirements for optimal use of key functions within the version 9.x software. Customers who wish to migrate to version 9.x software should contact your sales or support representative for details on specific migration paths available.

Platform Upgrade

Customers under active F5 Networks Support Contracts whose platforms are designated EOL and who wish to utilize the latest BIG-IP software should investigate upgrade options. For specific information about platform upgrades, contact your hardware vendor.

Technical Support

Customers whose platforms are designated EOL and who are currently under maintenance contracts will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of their service contract. Customers attempting to run the latest versions of BIG-IP software on platforms designated EOL will void those contracts. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their platforms before the Service Contract Renewal date expires. Please contact your F5 Networks sales representative if you have any questions.
