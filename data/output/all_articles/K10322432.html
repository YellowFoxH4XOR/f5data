K10322432: No traffic forwarded to pool members with ASM Policy and iRule applied

Description

No traffic forwarded to pool members with ASM Policy and iRule applied.
 

Environment

  • ASM policy is in transparent mode
  • iRule contains HTTP::disable
  • No traffic forwarded to the pool member
  • When ASM policy is removed, the traffic is forwarded to the pool member

Cause

With the HTTP::disable in the iRule, ASM stops functioning.

ASM requires HTTP profile, hence in the iRule, HTTP::disable shouldn't be used.
 

Recommended Actions

In the iRule, the 'HTTP::disable' can be removed or the line can be commented.
