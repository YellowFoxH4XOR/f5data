Known Issue

Diameter responses on the client side may contain pool member information.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • A virtual server is configured with a diameter profile.
  • The virtual server processes a diameter request message.

Impact

Diameter sessions may terminate unexpectedly.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Pool member information, such as the host IP address and the origin host, is sent to a client in the response.
