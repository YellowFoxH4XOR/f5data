Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP LTM may log the following messages when the mod_jk2 Apache module is initialized during normal operation of the Configuration utility web server:

httpd[<PID>]: [error] jk2_init() Can't find child <PID> in scoreboard
httpd[<PID>]: [error] mod_jk child init 1 -2

These error messages are benign; they provide information about expected conditions that occur when the system is working correctly.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as ID 314620 and ID 249555 (formerly CR54909 and CR54995).
