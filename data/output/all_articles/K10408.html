Topic

FirePass does not support the Simple Network Time Protocol (SNTP) for synchronizing the FirePass internal clock with an external time server. FirePass does support the Network Time Protocol (NTP), and you can configure NTP time servers on the Device Management : Configuration : Time page in the FirePass Administrative Console.
