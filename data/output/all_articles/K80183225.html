Known Issue

Half-closed congested Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) connections with an unclean shutdown may stall.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Outbound SSL communication is congested.
  • The client issues a FIN packet without a close notification.

Impact

The connection flow may stall.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The connection may stall or stop responding.
