Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. BIG-IP may wait as long as thirty seconds before deleting connections that have timed out.

If a client connects from the same source port after the connection timed out, but before BIG-IP deletes it, the connection continues and BIG-IP sends the client to the node it previously used, rather than load balancing the new connection. This can cause connections to clump on nodes in environments where clients make repeated connections using the same source port.

When configuring connection timeouts, be aware that the connections may continue as much as 30 seconds longer than the value you specify.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR29988 and CR41780.
