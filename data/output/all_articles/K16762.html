Known Issue

Deterministic NAT (DNAT) state information may be incomplete when the BIG-IP system uses high-speed logging (HSL).

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your VIPRION system has one or more B2250 blades installed.
  • The HTSPLIT feature is enabled.

    Note: This feature is enabled by default in BIG-IP 11.5.0 and later.

  • The Large Scale NAT (LSN) pool uses DNAT mode.
  • You have configured the BIG-IP system to send log messages to a remote HSL server.

Impact

Subscriber reverse and forward mappings may be missing, or incorrect, in the remote high-speed logs.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Reverse and forward mappings are missing or incorrect in the high-speed logs.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can use the BIG-IP system's local log entries, in the /var/log/ltm file, to forward and reverse map subscriber connections.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
