Description

  • In a HA environment both Active and Standby Devices have high CPU. Both Devices show extreme ARP rate. (100Kpps +)
  • You may see a device ARP Request for an  IP not in the configuration.
  • You may see a device ARP-Tell for a Self IP the device does not possess.

 

Environment

  • HA Pair
  • A VLAN with multiple interfaces configured directly not as part of a trunk.

Cause

  • L2 Looping. The Interfaces are not participating in STP.

Recommended Actions

  1. Either use only one interface on the VLAN or
  2. Reconfigure the Interfaces into a trunk and apply the trunk to the VLAN.
  3. Not preferred: fix the STP as sole solution. STP is not a replacement for using trunks. It might be disabled on the BIGIP interface, globally on the BIGIP or on the Switch. STP is enabled by default on BIGIP.
