K5408: BIG-IP does not allow directed ARP requests to pass through a VLAN group

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. BIG-IP was designed to pass only multicast ARP requests through a VLAN group. Directed ARP requests use a unicast destination, and are subsequently dropped by BIG-IP.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR43356 and CR43357.
