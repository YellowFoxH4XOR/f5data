What Happened?

I want to see Origin Pools and Origin Servers associated with my Load Balancers.

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud Console
  • HTTP Load Balancer
  • Origin Pool

Resolution/Answer

Here are the steps for viewing list of origin pools attached to LBs:

  1. Click the gear icon at the top right of the load balancers page
  2. Type "origin" in search bar
  3. Select "Origin Pools" as shown in the below screenshot
  4. Click apply

 

Here are the steps below for viewing list of origin servers attached to origin pools:

  1. Click the gear icon at the top right of the origin pools page
  2. Type "serv" in search bar
  3. Select "Origin Servers" as shown in the below screenshot
  4. Click apply
