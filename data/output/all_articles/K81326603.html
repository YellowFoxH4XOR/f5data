Topic

Overview of the new partition scheme for BIG-IP iSeries platforms

Description

F5 introduces a new partition scheme in the BIG-IP iSeries platforms. This scheme creates a single, shared logical volume that all TMOS modules, applications, and Virtual Clustered Microprocessors (vCMPs) use. The new changes allow more flexible and efficient disk storage for F5 product modules.

Note: The new partition scheme is available to only BIG-IP iSeries platforms running BIG-IP 12.1.1 and later. If you upgrade to BIG-IP 12.1.1 on platforms other than iSeries, the storage layout does not change.

In previous versions and platforms, BIG-IP system disk storage is designed so that the system allocates F5 product module storage in advance, on a per-module basis. If you add extra storage to the system, the extra storage cannot be used for application data other than datastor unless you re-partition the entire system. The new partitioning scheme supports additional storage without re-partitioning.

Currently only available on the BIG-IP iSeries platforms, the new partition scheme creates a logical volume named appdata, the size of which is based on the total available storage that is present on the platform. Once the installer reserves 36 GB for base installation images and 5 GB for swap, it allocates approximately 75 percent of the total available storage to the appdata volume. In addition to the appdata volume, the new scheme allocates 10 percent of the total available storage to the share volume and 5 percent of the total available storage to the log volume.

A change in behavior, introduced in BIG-IP 12.1.2, causes the installer to allocate an additional 10 percent to the appdata volume. The increased appdata volume size may prevent the installation of more than three TMOS images. For more information, refer to K41812306: The appdata volume on BIG-IP iSeries platforms is now larger.

The following example shows the space allocation on a BIG-IP i5600 platform with a 480 GB solid-state drive (SSD) running BIG-IP 12.1.2:

# lvs
  LV            VG        Attr      LSize   
  dat.appdata   vg-db-sda -wi-ao--- 303.52g
  dat.boot      vg-db-sda -wi-a---- 448.00m
  dat.log       vg-db-sda -wi-ao---  20.23g
  dat.share     vg-db-sda -wi-ao---  40.47g
  dat.swapvol   vg-db-sda -wi-ao---   5.00g
  set.1._config vg-db-sda -wi-ao---   3.17g
  set.1._usr    vg-db-sda -wi-ao---   3.29g
  set.1._var    vg-db-sda -wi-ao---   3.00g
  set.1.root    vg-db-sda -wi-ao--- 440.00m
  set.2._config vg-db-sda -wi-a----   3.17g
  set.2._usr    vg-db-sda -wi-a----   3.29g
  set.2._var    vg-db-sda -wi-a----   3.00g
  set.2.root    vg-db-sda -wi-a---- 440.00m
  set.3._config vg-db-sda -wi-a----   3.17g
  set.3._usr    vg-db-sda -wi-a----   3.29g
  set.3._var    vg-db-sda -wi-a----   3.00g
  set.3.root    vg-db-sda -wi-a---- 440.00m
