Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When disk caching is disabled, the pvac process still attempts to read objects from disk. As a result, the BIG-IP WebAccelerator performance may be negatively impacted.

Note: Disk caching is disabled by modifying the pvsystem.conf file.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR138188, and it was fixed in 10.2.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP WebAccelerator release notes.

Additionally, this issue has been fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-10.1.0-3372.0-HF1 for BIG-IP 10.1.0. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
