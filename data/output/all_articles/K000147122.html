K000147122: I am unable to do a reverse lookup from any DNS provider outside F5

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-11-08
Updated
2024-11-08
Applies to
  • F5 Distributed Cloud Services
  • F5 Distributed Cloud DNS Management
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000147122

Description

  • You had a reverse zone added to the allowlist by F5
  • You have created your zone with correct PTR records
  • You have activated your zone
  • When you do a reverse lookup from F5 nameservers, they resolve without issue
  • You're unable to do a reverse lookup from any DNS provider outside F5

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud DNS

Cause

  • You have not updated your registrar to point to our F5's nameservers

Resolution

  • You need to go to your domain name registrar and there you can change your nameservers to point into F5's nameservers (ns1.f5clouddns.com and ns2.f5clouddns.com)
  • Depending on who your registrar is, the process may vary
