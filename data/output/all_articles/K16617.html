Known Issue

The mcpd service may restart when the BIG-IP GTM system loads many virtual servers.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP GTM configuration contains many virtual servers.
  • You attempt to load the configuration.

Impact

Attempts to load the configuration fail; the BIG-IP system temporarily stops processing traffic, and may fail over if paired with other BIG-IP systems.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H518600 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High screen.
  • When you attempt to load the configuration, the mcpd service times out.
  • When the configuration fails to load, the mcpd service restarts.
