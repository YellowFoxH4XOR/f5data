Description

The support contract end date in the initial Service Agreement Acknowledgment (SAA) is different from the support end date found at the F5 Product Information request page(https://secure.f5.com/validate/validate.jsp).

Sample screenshot from an SAA showing that a support contract end date of August 09, 2023.



Sample screenshot from the email received from F5 Product Information request page for the same device showing a support contract end date of October 7, 2023.




Environment
  • F5 Product Information page
  • Service Agreement Acknowledgment
Cause

The support contract end date may be extended based on the product license activation date of the unit or 90 days from shipment, whichever is earlier.

This information can also be found on the Service Agreement Acknowledgment (SAA), as below:
