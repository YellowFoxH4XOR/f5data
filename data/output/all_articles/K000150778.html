Description

Unable to disable the signature 200020003 under Cookie

Environment

ASM

Signature

Cause

Signature can be disabled under cookie only if the scope of the signature includes cookie

Signature 200020003 has only following scope :

Signature Scope: Header, URI, Request Content

Recommended Actions

Disable the signature under the URL in question .

Additional Information

None.
