Known Issue

Certain BIG-IP platforms may send Link Aggregation Control Protocol (LACP), Spanning Tree Protocol (STP), and Link Layer Discovery Protocol (LLDP) protocol data units (PDUs) with a Virtual Local Area Network (VLAN) ID of zero.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system runs on the i2x00 or i4x00s platforms.
  • Your BIG-IP system uses the LACP, STP, or LLDP protocols.

Impact

The LLDP, STP, or LACP protocols may fail due to third-party devices rejecting packets.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Affected packets have a VLAN ID of 0 (zero).
  • Third-party devices reject affected packets.
