Policy Information

F5 is announcing that the End of Software Development (EoSD) for ARX 6.x is effective November 1, 2017 in accordance with the guidelines contained within K8986: F5 product support policies and K8466: ARX software support policy.

As of November 1, 2017 F5 will no longer provide security updates, software fixes (hotfixes), or consulting services for ARX 6.x. Additionally, service contracts enter the Extended Support phase beginning with the EoSD in accordance with the guidelines contained within K4309: The F5 platform life cycle support policy.

During the Extended Support phase, F5 will offer only configuration support and RMA services. Defect correction will not be provided. F5 may offer a customer access to certain software updates, which may include security or stability related software releases.

All configuration support for ARX 6.x during the Extended Support phase is considered reasonable effort. 'Reasonable effort' is defined to include technical phone support and hardware support, and is limited to system configuration support. No response time targets, guarantees, or SLAs shall apply. No escalations to Engineering Services or Product Development shall be allowed. RMA support is included, as long as the platform has not exceeded its End of Technical Support (EoTS) or End of RMA (EoRMA) support date.

The effective End of Technical Support (EoTS) and End of RMA (EoRMA) date is November 1, 2019 for the ARX 6.x software, completing its End of Life (EoL) cycle.

Customers should view the corresponding software release notes to determine if a particular software release is suitable for their platform or software version. Access to and compatibility of these software updates are not guaranteed and will be made under the F5 reasonable effort support guidelines.

Additionally, if a platform is not compatible with the latest General Sustaining (GS) software release, the platform may be restricted from operating that version through the F5 software licensing process.
