Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may incorrectly display duplicate if_index statistics, which may cause the nsm daemon to restart.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP system is configured as part of a high-availability (HA) configuration.
  • Tunnels are configured.
  • If dynamic routing is configured, additional impact may occur.

Impact

You view unreliable or confusing statistics through SNMP polling. If dynamic routing is also configured, an nsm daemon restart may lead to loss of dynamic routes.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP system presents duplicate if_index statistics of network objects, either viewed internally or polled through SNMP.
  • If dynamic routing is configured, the nsm daemon restarts, causing loss of dynamic routes.
