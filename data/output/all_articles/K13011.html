Known Issue

B2100 blades may fail to recognize and properly initialize 1 GB SFP interfaces.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • 1 GB SFP interfaces are installed on the B2100 blade

Note: The 10 GB SFP interface is not affected.

Note: This applies to only the VIPRION 2400 platform.

Impact

The affected 1 GB SFP interfaces are unable to establish a link.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • Link light never reports a green or up status
  • The following error is reported in the /var/log/bcm56xxd log file:

    8706: p=17 SPI-ROM load didn't complete (0xfb)
    8706: p=18 SPI-ROM load didn't complete (0xfb)
    8706: p=19 SPI-ROM load didn't complete (0xfb)
    8706: p=20 SPI-ROM load didn't complete (0xfb)
    8706: p=21 SPI-ROM load didn't complete (0xfb)
    8706: p=22 SPI-ROM load didn't complete (0xfb)
    8706: p=23 SPI-ROM load didn't complete (0xfb)
    8706: p=24 SPI-ROM load didn't complete (0xfb)
