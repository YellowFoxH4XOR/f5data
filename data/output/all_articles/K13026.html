Known Issue

Using ActiveSync Auth may result in duplicate sessions or instability on the FirePass device when ActiveSync devices are connected.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • ActiveSync Auth is enabled on the Device Management > Customization > URI-based Customization tab.
  • Users connect to an ActiveSync device such as an iPhone, Android, or Nokia.

Impact

The FirePass device may experience a higher than usual load average or database corruption. In a high availability (HA) deployment, both FirePass devices may become Active nodes, rather than an Active node and a Standby node.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Users experience slower than usual response times from the FirePass device.
  • Users cannot authenticate to the FirePass device intermittently.
  • Users cannot access the active HA FirePass device using the common IP address.
  • In the Configuration utility, when you navigate to Device Management > Monitoring > System Health, the System Health page reports errors with the database.
  • In the Configuration utility, when you navigate to Device Management > Monitoring > System Load, the System Load page reports higher than expected average loads.
  • In the Configuration utility, when you navigate to Reports > Sessions, the Sessions page displays duplicate sessions for the same user.
