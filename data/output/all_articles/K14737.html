Known Issue

The BIG-IP WebAccelerator or BIG- IP AAM system may not send an eTag for requested content.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP WebAccelerator or BIG-IP AAM system is configured to cache the compressed static content from the origin web server (OWS).
  • The requested content is already present in the BIG-IP WebAccelerator or BIG-IP AAM cache, and needs to be re-validated.
  • The OWS does not send a Last-Modified date header in its response.

Impact

The OWS may experience an increased number of GET requests, which may have otherwise been satisfied by a conditional GET.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • Requests that may have been satisfied with a conditional GET will instead use a full GET request.

Resolution

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can configure your OWS to return Last-Modified headers for all content that it serves.

Impact of workaround:  Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

To perform this change, refer to the configuration guide for your OWS software.
