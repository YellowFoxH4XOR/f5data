Description

Backend servers report a significantly higher number of sessions or requests originating from the BIG-IP ASM self IP compared to the number of requests logged by the WAF. Symptoms include:

  • Backend servers observe millions of sessions from the WAF self IP.
  • BIG-IP ASM logs show a much lower request count for the same period.
  • All backend requests appear to originate from the WAF self IP, with request content visible.
  • Discrepancy between backend and WAF logs, especially during high traffic or attack periods.
  • Error/log messages such as "skipped ... requests due to rate limit" may appear in ASM logs.

Environment

  • Product: BIG-IP ASM (Advanced WAF)

Cause

  • Log rotation and rate limiting: During high traffic spikes, BIG-IP ASM may not log or forward all requests due to configured rate limits or log storage constraints (e.g., 2GB or 3 million rows).
  • Requests exceeding the logging rate limit are not logged locally or forwarded to remote logging servers.
  • Backend may see all requests, but Advanced WAF logs only a subset, leading to perceived discrepancies.

Answer/Recommended Actions

Confirm Traffic Statistics:

  • Use tmctl to review accumulated statistics for virtual servers:

tmctl -f blade0-performance-10800-YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS virtual_server_stat "name=/Common/<virtual_server_name>" -s clientside.tot_conns,serverside.tot_conns,tot_requests

  • Subtract accumulated values between time periods to determine request counts.

Understand Logging Limitations:

  • ASM logs are retained for a limited time and may be subject to rate limits.
  • If logs show "skipped ... requests due to rate limit," not all requests were logged or forwarded.

Remote Logging Best Practices:

  • Forward logs to a remote logging server for extended retention and visibility.
  • Monitor for rate limit messages to ensure all critical events are captured.
