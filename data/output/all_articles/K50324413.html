Known Issue

The firewall NAT intermittently resets traffic.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have licensed and provisioned the BIG-IP AFM module.
  • You have configured a firewall NAT.

Impact

The system experiences a destination translation failure.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Traffic using the firewall NAT intermittently fails to connect.
