Description

One of the main objectives of the F5 support organization is to have a quick initial response and resolution to customer issues and due to the pure nature of our products Technical Support Alliance Network (TSANet) has become an effective tool for our support organization.

 

TSANet was established in 1993 and is an industry not-for-profit organization that facilitates vendor-to-vendor support collaboration on behalf of mutual customers.

F5 has been the Elite member since 2004. TSANet has over 750 member companies worldwide.

 

The value in TSANet comes in the legal framework and code of conduct which allows members to collaborate seamlessly and securely. TSANet also taughts a named escalation path to ensure prompt resolution to mission critical issues.

With F5s global support organization, streamlining the existing engagement procedures are already in place with TSANet. This leads to higher customer satisfaction and quicker resolution times than with vendors who are not TSANet members.

Furthermore, collaborating with a vendor with no contract agreement requires the customer to play a stronger role in resolving the issue. The big advantage of being a part of TSANet is the method is efficient, repeatable and consistent, which is important to F5, having multiple engineers. F5 has a TSANet awareness program and representation in each of its global regions.

 

To check whether your company is a public member or not, consult the TSANet members list.
