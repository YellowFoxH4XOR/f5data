Description

  • Pool availability state flapping on a regularly timed interval.
  • No pool members are available for about 5 minutes. After 5 minutes the pool regain available members
  • Log messages similar to the following are logged to /var/log/ltm:
    err tmm[11661]: 01010221:3: Pool /Common/<pool name> now has available members
    err tmm[11661]: 01010028:3: No members available for pool /Common/<pool name>
Environment
  • The inband monitor eavesdrop on the conversation between a client and the server upon an attempt by a client to connect to a pool member.
  • Client connection failed 3 times within 30 seconds. Pool member failed to respond within 10 seconds which causes the failure.
  • The inband monitor marks the pool member as down and waited 5 minutes until it tries again to test connection to the marked down pool member.
  • Per configuration, if pool members do not response in time (10s) after 3 failures (30s) the pool member will be down for 300 seconds (5 min).

Cause

When seeing this pattern of pool members in this up/down flipping, this is typically a matter of choosing the most appropriate configuration for your environment, specifically Response Time (default = 10), Failure Interval (default = 30). Retry Time (default = 300) is why it comes back after it is down.

Recommended Actions

Ask yourself what will be the expected time for a pool member response? 

Could it be that 10 seconds is too short. If so, update the 'response-time' lets say 15 seconds and failure-interval to 45 seconds.

You can also reduce the retry-time to 180 seconds, which means the health monitor will test the connection after 3 minutes instead of 5 minutes, so you will have the pool member back online in shorter time - unless it continues to fail the health check.

Adjust these settings to tune to what works better in your environment.
