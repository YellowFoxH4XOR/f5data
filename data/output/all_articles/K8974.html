Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. A BIG-IP initiated midstream renegotiation connection will close when the maximum record delay counter counts down to 0 (zero), if the maximum record delay counter was not reset to zero in the previous successful handshake.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR100632 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.4.6. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.3.1-HF4, issued for BIG-IP 9.3.1, and in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.5-HF2, issued for BIG-IP 9.4.5. You may download these hotfixes or later versions of the hotfixes from the F5 Downloads site.

For information about the F5 hotfix policy, refer to K4918: Overview of F5 critical issue hotfix policy.

For information about how to manage F5 product hotfixes, refer to K6845: Managing F5 product hotfixes.
