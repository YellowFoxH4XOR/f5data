K55045050: BIG-IP APM upgrade access policy Email agent sends email with content type of text/html

Issue

Old Behavior

In versions prior to BIG-IP APM 15.1.0, when you configure an access policy with the Email agent, the system sends email in plain text without a content-type header.

New Behavior

Beginning in BIG-IP APM 15.1.0, when you configure an access policy with the Email agent, the system sends email that contains the following content-type headers:

  • Content-Transfer-Encoding: 8bit
  • Content-Type: text/html; charset=UTF-8

F5 Product Development tracked this change as ID 788473.

Impact

Email recipients expecting to receive email from the BIG-IP APM system as plaint text may not parse email received as HTML in the expected manner.

Recommended Actions

When creating email content for the BIG-IP APM Email agent to send, consider the required HTML formatting for the content.
