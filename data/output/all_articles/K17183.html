Known Issue

The HTTPS monitor may incorrectly mark pool members down due to SSL SessionTicket Extension.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The monitored pool member is running an older version of OpenSSL that does not support the SessionTicket Extension.

Impact

The affected pool member is unable to process production traffic

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • You notice that the BIG-IP system incorrectly marks an HTTPS pool member down.
  • A packet capture between the BIG-IP system and the affected resource shows the BIG-IP system sending the SessionTicket TLS extension in its ClientHello message, and the affected resource fails the TLS session with an SSL alert number 47 error.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can use a TCP monitor or an external health monitor instead of the HTTPS monitor for the affected pool members. For information about authoring an external health monitor, refer to HTTPS - Monitor SSL Handshake on DevCentral.

Impact of workaround: The impact of the suggested workaround depends on the specific environment. F5 recommends that you test any such changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.
