Known Issue

Management network routes may be deleted after a change to the routing table.

This issue occurs when one of the following conditions are met:

  • You have used the TMOS Shell (tmsh) net route command to create a route that is within the management network subnet.
  • Your BIG-IP system is configured to use dynamic routing and has received a dynamic route update that is within the management network subnet. 

Impact

The system may lose a management network route and you may lose access to the BIG-IP system management interface.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • You are unable to access the BIG-IP system management interface.
  • You notice routes missing from the management interface routing table.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 645723 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table.

Workaround

To avoid this issue, you can filter dynamic routing updates and avoid creating static routes using tmsh net route that match the network interface subnet.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround may limit or impact routing to your system management interface depending upon your networking requirements.
