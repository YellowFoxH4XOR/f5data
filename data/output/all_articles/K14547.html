Known Issue

Copying an existing BIG-IP APM policy may fail.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The destination policy name character length exceeds 62 characters.

Impact

Creating a copy of the policy will fail.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • If a user attempts to copy a policy with a destination name that exceeds 62 characters, they will receive the following error message:

    coapi_errstr: 01070277:3: The requested access policy item (<truncated name>) was not found.
  • A copy of the BIG-IP APM policy is not created.

Resolution

Workaround

To work around this issue, specify a destination policy name that is 62 characters or less.

Impact of workaround: None.

Creating a BIG-IP APM policy by copying an existing policy

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP Configuration utility.
  2. Click Access Policy.
  3. Click Access Profiles.
  4. For an existing policy, select the Policy link to the right of the policy name.
  5. For the Copied Profile Name, enter a destination policy name that is less than 63 characters.
  6. Click Copy.
