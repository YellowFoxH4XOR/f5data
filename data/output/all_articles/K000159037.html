K000159037: Unable to upload BIGIP-17.5.1.3-0.0.19.iso image since BIG-IQ 8.4.0-0.14.139.14

Description

After BIG-IQ upgrade to 8.4.0-0.14.139.14 it is not possible anymore to upload BIGIP-17.5.1.3-0.0.19.iso image via GUI.

Command line upload directly to /shared/images is successful and the image is visible on GUI after that.

 

Environment

BIG-IQ 8.4.0-0.14.139.14

GUI upload of BIG-IP image BIGIP-17.5.1.3-0.0.19.iso to further deploy to managed devices.

 

Cause

ID2140961 Unable to upload BIGIP-17.5.1.3-0.0.19.iso image since BIG-IQ 8.4.0-0.14.139.14

 

Recommended Actions

Request BIG-IQ 8.4.0.HF-139.20-ENG with the fix.
