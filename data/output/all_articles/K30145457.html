Known Issue

Attempts to load a configuration may fail after a peer is removed from a Sync-Only device group.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM configuration uses a Sync-Only device group.
  • Your system synchronizes to the device group.
  • You delete a device from the Sync-Only device group that has not been previously synchronized to the device group.
  • You attempt to load the configuration on the device that was deleted from the device group.

Impact

The configuration fails to load.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When attempting to load the configuration, error messages similar to the following example are displayed:

    Validating configuration...
    /config/bigip_base.conf
    /config/bigip_user.conf
    /config/bigip.conf
    01070712:3: Values (/<partition>/<peer device>) specified for Policy Sync Status (/<partition>/<policy sync name>/<peer device> /<partition>/<peer device>): foreign key index (device_FK) do not point at an item that exists in the database.
    Unexpected Error: Validating configuration process failed.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can remove the peer device from the Sync-Only device group on which the previous policy synchronization was performed.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
