Known Issue

The front panel LCD display may stop updating the status, and the Status LED blinks amber.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are running one of the following hardware platforms:
     
    • BIG-IP 2000 series
    • BIG-IP 4000 series
    • BIG-IP 5000 series
    • BIG-IP 7000 series
    • BIG-IP 10000 series
  • The LED/LCD module stops receiving updates from the BIG-IP system for 3 minutes or longer.

    This condition may occur if data transfers stall between the BIG-IP system and the LED/LCD module, as well as over the connecting USB bus.

Impact

The front panel LCD does not display updated information, and the Status LED blinks amber. However, there is no impact to BIG-IP system operations, and traffic is not disrupted.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The front panel LCD does not display current BIG-IP system information.
  • The Status LED blinks amber.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can manually generate USB bus traffic. To do so, perform either of the following actions:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following actions should not have a negative impact on your system.

  • Press one of the buttons on the LCD display to navigate the LCD menus.
  • Generate USB bus traffic by typing the following command at the BIG-IP system console:

    /sbin/lsusb -v -d 0451:3410.

Either of these actions will generate USB bus traffic and trigger a recovery.
