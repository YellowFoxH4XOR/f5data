Known Issue

The End-User Diagnostics (EUD) utility displays an incorrect power supply serial number.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You run the EUD utility on a BIG-IP 5000 series platform with a PWR-0227 power supply installed.
F5 has determined that the EEPROM for some power supplies have been programmed with an incorrect value by the power supply manufacturer. As a result, when running the EUD on a BIG-IP 5000 series platform, the terminating Z of the power supply serial number may not display. 

Impact

This issue is cosmetic. This issue is not indicative of a power supply failure and does not affect the power supply functionality.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When running the EUD on a 5000 series platform, the terminating Z of the power supply serial number does not display.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 680750 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article.
