Known Issue

Access policy modifications may cause performance degradation for profile webtops.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system is experiencing high system resource usage.
  • An access policy is created or modified, and then applied to the running configuration.

Impact

Users experience degraded webtop performance.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When accessing their access profile webtops, users experience slow page rendering.
  • When viewing RAM cache eviction rates, administrators notice a significant spike in the amount of evictions correlating with the time of the webtop performance issue.
