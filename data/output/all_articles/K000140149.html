Description

For UDP traffic, nothing is being sent when service is going down, even though BIG-IP is configured for "Reject" as Action On Service Down.

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM

Cause

This is expected behavior, when UDP pool member goes down, connection entry for that pool member is silently torn down with no ICMP unreachable being sent, and next packet will be directed to another pool member if any is available, if there are no available pool members, then ICMP unreachable will be sent.
