K17252: The bigd process no longer sends monitor traffic to pool members when the BIG-IP system is offline or a VIPRION blade is disabled

Issue

Old Behavior

In BIG-IP versions prior to 11.5.3, 11.6.0 HF6, and 12.0.0, the bigd process sends monitor traffic to the pool members when the BIG-IP system is offline or a VIPRION blade is disabled, or when the member in a high availability (HA) pair is offline, including forced offline.

New Behavior

In BIG-IP 11.5.3, 11.6.0 HF6, and 12.0.0, the bigd process no longer sends monitor traffic to the pool members when the BIG-IP system is offline or a VIPRION blade is disabled, or when the member in a HA pair is offline, including forced offline. As a result of this change, you may observe that the bigd process is restarting when the system is offline, however, monitoring is paused until the system is released from offline mode.

Note: The new behavior described in this article applies to Virtual Editions, standalone units, and VIPRION blades.
