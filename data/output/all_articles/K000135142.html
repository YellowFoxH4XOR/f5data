K000135142: Total peer limitation in Traffix SDC

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-06-25
Updated
2024-10-01
Applies to
  • Traffix SDC: 5.2.X, 5.2.0, 5.1.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000135142

Description

  • The total limit of connected peers is 1250, regardless of number CPF or FEP.
  • Having an amount of peers which exceeds beyond this number is likely to have an impact on performance. However, its not possible to know the definitive performance impact since we need to consider dimensioning. Performance impact varies from different customers that has different implementations(example: transformation and flow of traffic).

Environment

  • Traffix SDC 5.x

Cause

By design.

 Recommended Actions

None.

Additional Information

None.
