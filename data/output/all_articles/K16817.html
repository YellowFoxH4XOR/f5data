Known Issue

The datasyncd process may continually restart.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • An 11.6.0 hotfix was installed on the BIG-IP system.
  • The BIG-IP system is provisioned with either the BIG-IP ASM or Fraud Protection Service (FPS) module.

Impact

The BIG-IP ASM system goes offline and does not process traffic while the datasyncd process is restarting.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The datasyncd process continually restarts for a 30 minute period following the hotfix installation.
  • The BIG-IP ASM system goes offline after installing the hotfix.
