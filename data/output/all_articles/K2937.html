Known Issue

Node traffic statistics may be incorrect on BIG-IP 1000 and BIG-IP 2400 switch appliances. These platforms do not count fragmented packets in traffic statistics. In addition, these platforms may not resume incrementing traffic statistics after you reset the statistics.

F5 Product Development tracked this problem as CR24085, CR29087, CR29312, and CR30498 and it is scheduled to be fixed in a future release.
