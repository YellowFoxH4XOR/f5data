Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When SYN cookies are activated for connections traversing a FastL4 virtual server or a self IP address, the BIG-IP system may send an incorrect MSS value to the destination. Instead of sending an MSS value that is equal to or less than the MSS value provided by the client, the BIG-IP system sends an MSS value based on the MTU of the VLAN that the traffic arrived on.

This behavior may cause the client or upstream device to drop the packet and send a Need to fragment message, indicating that the destination host should resend the packet with the correct MTU value. However, some networks may be configured to drop ICMP unreachable Need to fragment messages, causing the client connection to stall and eventually time out.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as ID 241502 (formerly CR127666) and it was fixed in BIG-IP 10.2.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM or Link Controller release notes.

In addition, BIG-IP iHealth may list Heuristic H383270 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Low screen.
