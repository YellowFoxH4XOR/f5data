Description

When hosting a VE on a VMWare ESXi, what F5's recommendations are to modifying the ESXi's Power Management Policy settings.

Environment

  • ESXi Power Manangement Policy
  • BIG-IP Virtual Edition

Cause

TMOS is a real-time OS and not an interrupt driven OS. Therefore reducing and interfering with the resources used by the OS will have a significant impact.

According to VMWare's Host Power Management in VMware vSphere "vSphere chooses a suitable deep C-State (like C2) based on its prediction of when the CPU cores need to be active again." This suggests that CPUs can be put to sleep which will potentially interfere with TMM as it would most likely be aborted and restarted. The BIG-IP requires constant and dedicated resources considering that TMM/BIG-IP was never designed for "sleep". Additionally, in VMWare's documentation there is no information to what happens to RAM and the hard disk, however, the impact of the CPU alone is enough to reach the conclusion that it is best practice to avoid modifying the ESXi's Power Management Policy.

Recommended Actions

It is highly advised not to modify the VMWare ESXi's Power Management Policy and to leave it at its default "High Performance" value.
