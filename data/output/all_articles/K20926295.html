Description
When trying to connect to a VPN using the browser, a pop up saying "Loading cache cleanup control. Please wait..." comes to the screen. This does not stop the access to the policy because it has a "Click here to skip loading and continue" option, but if not clicked it might hang.  

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM
  • Browser 
  • VPE Policy contains a "Windows Cache and Session Control" event

Cause
The VPE Policy Object "Windows Cache and Session Control" has become obsolete. For more information, refer to K20926295: ''Loading cache cleanup control'' pop up when trying to connect to VPN using browser.

Recommended Actions

As a BIG-IP administrator, remove the "Windows Cache and Session Control" VPE Object from your Policy.

As a VPN user, select the "Click here to skip loading and continue" option. Depending on your organization's corporate requirements and policy, you may or may not be able to proceed with the login. Contact your IT administrator for more information.
