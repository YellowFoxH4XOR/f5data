Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If a virtual sever with dependent virtual servers is marked down, the dependent virtual servers should be immediately marked down by the BIG-IP GTM system. However, the BIG-IP GTM system waits approximately 60 seconds before marking down dependent virtual servers. As a result, the BIG-IP GTM system responds to wide IP queries with unavailable virtual servers.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR92752.
