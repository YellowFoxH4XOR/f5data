Description
BIG-IQ VE Max (Full version of BIG-IQ / NOT License Manager) is billed per managed device and this is consumed from the annual commit value.
F5 requires an inventory report showing each managed device once a month.
Note:This procedure is not needed if you are running Second Sight data collector.
 

Environment

  • BIG-IQ VE Max
  • ELA / FCP
  • Managed devices (Hardware and Virtual BIG-IP)

Cause
You need to create an inventory report and send it to F5.

Recommended Actions
You will log into BIG-IQ and generate the Inventory Report and send it to your Customer Success Manager.

  1. Log into BIG-IQ.
  2. Click on Devices on the top menu.
  3. Click on BIG-IP DEVICES in the menu on the left.
  4. Click on "Export Inventory".
  5. Excel file is saved to Downloads folder.
  6. Please email a copy to your Customer Success Manager.

Additional Information
This process lists only BIG-IP devices Managed by BIG-IQ.
