K3996: The sfd daemon now restarts automatically

Type

Published
2006-02-17
Updated
2023-02-21
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • Legacy Products
  • BIG-IP 4.X: 4.6.4
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K3996

Topic

In versions of BIG-IP prior to 4.5.11 and 4.6.3, sfd will not restart if it fails or is stopped.

Starting in versions 4.5.11 and 4.6.3, sfd will restart automatically.

For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP Release Notes.
