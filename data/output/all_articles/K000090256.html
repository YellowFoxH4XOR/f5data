For assistance, refer to F5 Distributed Cloud Services Support Process or call F5 Support for help.

Description

About closed support cases on MyF5

You cannot change anything in a case after F5 closes it. When you need more assistance with a closed case, do one of the following:

  • If F5 closed the case within the last 14 days, you can contact F5 Support to reopen it using chat on MyF5, a support case, or call F5 Support .

    Note: You must log in to MyF5 to use chat.

  • If F5 closed the case more than 14 days ago, open a new case.

Procedures

View your support cases on MyF5

Sign in to MyF5 and do one of the following:

  • To see only your open cases, on the homepage, under Open Cases, select Cases.
  • To see all of your cases, regardless of status, on the homepage, select Support > Case Management.

About the Case Management page

When you view your support cases on the Case Management page, you see the following:

  • Cases you created (and are therefore the owner of) across all the MyF5 accounts you have access to.
  • All the cases in accounts for which you have the Case Manager role.

    For more information about user roles and permissions, refer to K000136095: About MyF5 account user roles and permissions.

In the Account Name column, you can see the account associated with each case, and in the Contact Name column, you can see who created each case.

Filter cases on the Case Management page

If you have numerous cases, you can select Filters to open Filter options. From there, you can filter your cases by any of the following criteria:

  • Priority
  • Status
  • Account name
  • Contact name
  • Date opened

View support case details and activity on MyF5

  1. Select Case Management.
  2. Select the case you want to see.

About case status

At the top of the page, under Status, F5 assigned a status to your case. Each status has the following meaning:

  • New—You created a support case and it is open.
  • Solving—A member of F5 Support is working on your case.
  • Need information—F5 Support is awaiting information from you.
  • Resolution provided—F5 Support is awaiting confirmation that you accept the solution F5 provided.
  • Closed—F5 closed the support case. For more information, refer to About closed support cases on MyF5.

Download a list of your support cases from MyF5

When you have many support cases, you may want to download a list of them into a spreadsheet.

Note that the Case Management page can display a maximum of 2000 records; however, you can download the entire list of your cases, including lists that exceed 2000 records,into a spreadsheet.

  1. Select Case Management.
  2. Select Download All.

Edit a support case on MyF5

  1. On the homepage, under Open Cases, select Cases.
  2. Select the case you want to edit.
  3. Under Case Information, select Edit case information.
  4. Under Edit case information, make your changes, and then select Save.
