Description

The rSeries appliance may unexpectedly encounter power cycle repeatedly. The following logs may appear in /var/log/pel or /var/log/sel:

09/14/2024 19:50:54 | 30284 | AOM | 255 | Na | 0 | main powerdown fault event
09/14/2024 19:50:54 | 30285 | AOM | 255 | Na | 0 | miscellaneous power fault event
09/14/2024 19:50:54 | 30286 | AOM | 255 | Na | 0 | +5.0v power fault event
09/14/2024 19:50:54 | 30287 | AOM | 255 | Na | 5 | system power fault recovery attempt 1 of 3
09/14/2024 19:50:54 | 30288 | AOM | 255 | Na | 6 | AOM host power button event
09/14/2024 19:50:54 | 30289 | AOM | 255 | Na | 6 | power-on accepted event
09/14/2024 19:50:55 | 30290 | AOM | 255 | Asserted | 0 | MISC +5.0V fault
09/14/2024 19:50:57 | 30291 | AOM | 255 | Deasserted | 6 | MISC +5.0V fault
09/14/2024 19:50:58 | 30292 | AOM | 255 | Na | 5 | system power fault recovery successful

Environment

  • F5OS-A
  • rSeries appliance
  • Unexpected reboots

Cause

Hardware Fault on the Mainboard Power Rail.

Recommended Actions

If there has been a recurring series of these same events repeatedly on the system, check for these logs and reach out to F5support.
