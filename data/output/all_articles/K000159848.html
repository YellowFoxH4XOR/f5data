Description

In MyF5 Self Service for the Flex Consumption Program (FCP), the activation status of the license key impacts what edit action options are available. 

Environment

  • MyF5 Customer Portal

  • Editor/Admin access to MyF5 FCP Subscription
  • FCP Contract started January 2025 or later
  • BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE)
  • List of License Keys in MyF5 FCP Subscription

Cause

As a MyF5 Self Service customer managing FCP license keys, it's important to know what edit options are available for the MyF5 Self Service License keys. These edit options vary according to the status of the license key.

Recommended Actions

Refer to the table below to determine which edit options are available for the MyF5 Self Service license key.
