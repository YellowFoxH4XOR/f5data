Description
When there is the need of performing a clear-rest-storage for License Manager BIG-IQ, these are the expected effects on the licensed BIG-IP devices. 

It makes the BIG-IQ forget about everything it has done to date.

It does not affect the BIG-IPs. The BIG-IP license will stay intact and the Big-IP will continue to report "Managed by BIG-IQ". However, you will need to discover/import all managed devices and then relicense each of the devices again as well as get an allow-move on the volume license along with the offerings in order to license the devices again.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ License Manager
  • clear-rest-storage
