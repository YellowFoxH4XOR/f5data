K5430: Wide IP delegation as an NS record from authoritative DNS to 3-DNS Controller is not supported

Type
Knowledge
Published
2006-05-08
Updated
2023-02-21
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • Legacy Products
  • 3-DNS: 4.6.4
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K5430

Topic



F5 Networks does not support directly delegating a wide IP as an NS record from the authoritative DNS to the 3-DNS Controller. Use of an NS record to delegate a wide IP from an authoritative DNS to the 3-DNS Controller could result in name resolution failure from the 3-DNS Controller.

F5 Networks recommends using a CNAME and subdomain zone delegation method.

For additional information about zone delegation and use of a CNAME, refer to K973: Configuring a DNS server to transfer zones to 3-DNS.
