Description

When trying to make a search in "Zone Runner : Resource Record List" following error is displayed in BIG-IP Configuration Utility:

RCODE returned from query: 'SERVFAIL'.

Environment

  • BIG-IP GTM or DNS

Cause

One of the zones can't be loaded due to errors.
You can observe similar logs in /var/log/daemon.log

error: zone www.example.com/IN/internal: journal rollforward failed: journal out of sync with zone
error: zone www.example.com/IN/internal: not loaded due to errors.

Recommended Actions

Follow below article in order to clear affected Zone journal:
  • K10066: The zrd process exits upon the 'journal rollforward failed: journal out of sync with zone' error message
