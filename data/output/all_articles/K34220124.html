Known Issue

Hardware-accelerated SSL traffic may encounter errors and performance problems.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system uses Cavium Nitrox SSL hardware acceleration.
  • You are experiencing a high SSL traffic load.

During high SSL traffic load, the Cavium Nitrox SSL hardware accelerator card may need more time than the default timeout to perform encryption and decryption. When the default value of the device-request timeout factor is too low, the system reports a false alert indicating that the crypto request queue is stuck.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may experience SSL connection failures or reduced performance.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H597735 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Low page.
  • In the /var/log/ltm log file, you observe error messages that appear similar to the following examples:

    tmm3[13326]: 01010025:2: Device error: crypto codec cn-crypto-3 queue is stuck.
    tmm3[13326]: 01010260:2: Hardware Error(Co-Processor): n3-crypto1 request queue stuck

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can modify the device.request.timeoutfactor database key. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system. This procedure will mitigate future occurrences. A restart of the BIG-IP system is required to clear a currently occurring condition.

  1. Log in to the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) as an administrative user.
  2. Type the following command to increase the device.request.timeoutfactor to 200:

    modify /sys db device.request.timeoutfactor value 200
