Known Issue

When three or more BIG-IP GTM systems are configured in a synchronization group that is handling a large number of LDNS entries and synchronizing LDNS persistence information, the BIG-IP GTM system may not answer DNS queries.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR78495 and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.3.1 and 9.4.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM Release Notes.

Workaround

You can work around this issue by restarting the gtmd process on the BIG-IP GTM system that is not answering DNS queries. To do so, type the following command:

bigstart restart gtmd

Note: Data synchronization may take 15 minutes or more, depending on the number of entries and the amount of persistence information that is being synchronized.
