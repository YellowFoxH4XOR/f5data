This article shows you how to complete the customer survey and submit feedback for articles on AskF5.

Description

For a brief demonstration of the following procedures, watch the following video:

You can also view the video at the following location: Submitting feedback to AskF5.

To submit feedback, you must enable the Functional Cookies setting in your cookie preferences to use the feedback links on AskF5. If you prefer to disable Functional Cookies, you will not be able to use these feedback tools. However, you can still submit feedback by sending an email to TellAskF5@f5.com.

Modifying your cookie preferences

To update your cookie preferences to use the feedback tools, perform the following procedure:

  1. From any page on AskF5, go to the footer section and select Cookie Preferences.
  2. Under Functional Cookies, select YES (red).
  3. Select SUBMIT PREFERENCES.
  4. Select CLOSE.

Submitting feedback to AskF5

You can provide feedback to AskF5 using the following tools:

Taking the AskF5 survey

  1. To take the survey, open your article and select the Take the AskF5 survey tab on the right side of the page.

    Note: If you are not logged in to AskF5 with your user credentials, you will be prompted to complete the CAPTCHA verification to proceed.

  2. After you answer all of the questions and submit your feedback, select Close.

Leaving feedback for an article

  1. To leave feedback, open your article.
  2. Go to the end of the article and select Leave feedback on this article.

    Note: If you are not logged in to AskF5 with your user credentials, you will be prompted to complete the CAPTCHA verification to proceed.

  3. After you answer all of the questions and submit your feedback, select Close.
