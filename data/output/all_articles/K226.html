Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. In rare cases, BIG-IP may crash when you change the target of a virtual server from a rule to a pool or from a pool to a rule, while traffic is passing through the virtual server.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR27090 and CR41774.
