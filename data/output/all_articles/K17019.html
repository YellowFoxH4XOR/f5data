Known Issue

Tunnel endpoints that have mismatched IPsec policy configurations may cause the racoon process to restart.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured the BIG-IP system as an IPSec tunnel endpoint.
  • The BIG-IP system's IPsec policy phase 2 configuration does not match the IPsec phase 2 policy configuration of the peer tunnel endpoint.
  • The phase 2 tunnel negotiation fails.

Impact

The racoon process may restart and temporarily fail to process tunneled packets.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Connectivity through the IPsec tunnel fails.
  • The racoon process generates a core file in the /var/core directory.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can ensure that IPsec policies for encryption and authentication match on both remote and local BIG-IP system tunnel endpoints.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
