Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. An SQL exception may occur when modifying or updating a pool object within the Configuration utility on the BIG-IP GTM system.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR78767 and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.3.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM Release Notes.
