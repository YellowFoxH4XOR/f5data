K000132631: iOS and Android devices may disconnect early or exhibit added latency with VMware Horizon

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-08-26
Updated
2026-08-26
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP APM: 15.1.5
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000132631

Description

When iOS and Android mobile devices are connecting to VMware Horizon view, there may be added latency observed, compared to that of a desktop device.

Additionally, users may notice VMware session may disconnect early or timeout unexpectedly.

Environment

  • Big-IP APM
  • VMware View

Cause

This is a default behavior with VMware and iOS devices.

For VMware Horizon on iPads, the Horizon Client suspends data transmission when you switch to another app. Data transmission resumes when you switch back to Horizon Client.

By default Horizon Client runs in the background for up to three minutes on iOS 9.0 and later device

Similar behavior can be observed on Android devices as well.

Recommended Actions

Expected behavior between VMware and mobile devices
