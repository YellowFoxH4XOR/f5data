Description

  • When restoring F5 BIG-IP VE instances from snapshots in AWS and Azure, the device serial number changes, causing the license to become invalid. For AWS pre-licensed images, F5 Support cannot transfer or reissue licenses; this must be handled by AWS. For BYOL (Bring Your Own License) or Azure instances, F5 can assist with license release and re-activation.

Environment

  1. F5 Big-IP in AWS and Azure pre-licensed images

Cause

NA

Recommended Actions

  1. Issues must be handled by AWS and Azure
