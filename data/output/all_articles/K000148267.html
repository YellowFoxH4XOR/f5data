Description

This page provides guidance on the MyF5 Self Service licensing for Flex Consumption Program (FCP).

Environment

  • New FCP Contract
  • Big-IP Virtual Edition
  • MyF5 Customer Portal
  • Editor/Admin access to MyF5 Subscriptions

Starting in January 2025, new FCP customers can generate and manage Self Service license keys via their MyF5 Customer Portal.
