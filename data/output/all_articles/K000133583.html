Policy Information

With the availability of the F5 rSeries r2000, r4000, r5000, and r10000, F5 is announcing the advanced notification of the End of Sale (EoS) for the BIG-IP iSeries i850, i2000, i4000, i5000, i7000, i7000-D, i10000, i10000-D, and i11000 platforms, effective January 1, 2024. This applies to all iSeries SKUs (including the YK SKUs), except for the exceptions noted below.

This product notice does not impact any subscription licenses or service agreements. Field Upgrade and Add-on SKUs are not included in this EoS announcement.

The actual EoS will be effective January 1, 2024. F5 expects to be able to support the existing backlog and demand for the iSeries appliances. However, fulfillment is contingent on global supply availability without guaranteeing that new orders can be fulfilled through the EoS date.

See the table below for the specific SKUs/products impacted, end-of-support lifecycle dates, and replacement SKUs.

Exceptions for this announcement:

  • FIPS platforms: i5820-DF, i7820-DF, i15820-DF
  • TurboSSL Platforms: i11400-DS, i11600-DS, i11800-DS
  • i15000 Platforms: i15600, i15800

For information about BIG-IP platform lifecycles, refer to K4309: F5 hardware product lifecycle support policy.
