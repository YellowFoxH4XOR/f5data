Known Issue

If you view the pools or pool members of a discovered BIG-IP device, the system may produce an error.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have discovered one or more remote BIG-IP devices.
  • You have upgraded the BIG-IQ system from 5.3.x to 5.4.x.
  • You attempt one of the following tasks using the BIG-IQ user interface:
    • View the pools or pool members of a discovered BIG-IP device.
    • Re-import the discovered BIG-IP device configuration.
  • You configure the pools or pool members n the remote BIG-IP device's /Common partition.

Impact

Users are unable to view the pools or pool members of a discovered BIG-IP device or re-import the BIG-IP device configuration.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You notice the following error when you attempt to view the pools or pool members of a discovered BIG-IP device, or re-import the device configuration:

    Found unexpected BSON type UNDEFINED.
