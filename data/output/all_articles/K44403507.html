Policy Information

F5 provides self-solve tools with access to a wealth of knowledge and technical support for BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) implementations on the Amazon Web Services (AWS) Marketplace for the U.S. Intelligence Community (IC).

Chat Support

Chat Support is available globally, in English only, when you are logged in to MyF5. After you log in, select Chat with an Expert at the bottom of the page.

Getting Started page

Getting Started in AWS on F5 Cloud Docs contains resources for BIG-IP VE on AWS.

MyF5

On MyF5 you can access product manuals and release notes, articles (how-to, known issues, security advisories, and policies), and more.

DevCentral community

DevCentral is F5's extensive community of experts, developers, and users addressing technical issues related to F5 products.

Continental U.S. First-Line phone support

  • 24x7x365 telephone access to F5 Support
  • U.S. citizens on U.S. soil agents to handle direct telephone Service Request communications with the customer
  • Priority 1 and Priority 2 Service Request opened by phone in to assigned toll free number
    • Immediate transfer to a product support engineer on case open
    • If no product support engineer is immediately available, customer will receive a callback within 1 hour
  • Priority 3 and Priority 4 Service Request opened by phone-in to assigned toll free number
    • Dispatched to queue for assignment
    • Email Initial Response acknowledgement from engineer
  • SRs opened on WebPortal do not qualify for CONUS handling and such SRs may be accessed by non-US nationals
  • BIG-IP LTM/GTM (BIG-IP DNS), BIG-IP AAM, BIG-IP APM, BIG-IP ASM, BIG-IP AFM, F5 SSL Orchestrator, F5 Privileged User Access, BIG-IQ, and F5 BIG-IP Advanced WAF covered software products
      • Classified data not in scope
      • All support data and information that is communicated to F5 by any means may be accessed on internet-based systems by non-U.S. nationals and non-CONUS-based engineers
      • Lack of system support data may extend time to isolation and resolution

      Definitions:

      For escalations and issue resolution F5 U.S. national engineers on U.S. soil may need to engage product developers that do not reside on U.S. soil and may not be U.S. citizens.
