Known Issue

The BIG-IP Analytics DNS virtual and client IP stats page may display statistics as aggregated values.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You attempt to view BIG-IP Analytics DNS statistics.
  • You select to view the statistics by virtual or client-ip.

Impact

The DNS statistics may show incomplete results.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • On the analytics DNS page, the system shows the virtual and client IP stats as Aggregated instead of displaying the detailed statistics.
