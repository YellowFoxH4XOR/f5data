Policy Information

F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of Signaling Delivery Controller (SDC), effective October 1, 2019. This applies to all SDC licenses and bundles.

F5 is providing advanced product notification to customers and partners announcing intent to retire the products listed in the SDC SKU List.

Technical support

F5 maintains generous lifecycle policies that allow customers to enjoy many years of both support and new software releases. Customers whose platforms are designated End of Life (EoL) and who are currently under maintenance contracts will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the Service Contract Renewal date expires. To review the SDC software support policy and dates, refer to K14402: Traffix software support policy.

Questions

For more information about this announcement, contact your local F5 Partner Business Manager or Inside Sales Representative.
