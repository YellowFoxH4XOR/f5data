K000137323: F5OS Next tenant name must be the same when in HA

Description
When deploying Next on F5OS, if two Next instances are intended to be in High Availability (HA), they must share the same tenant name.

Environment
  • Applies only two Next instances running on F5OS.
  • The VE (non F5OS) editions are not affected by this limitation.

Symptom
When you setup HA and the two Next instances have different tenant names, you will get an x509 (certificate) error in the HA manager logs. HA will not form correctly.

Workaround
Give the two F5OS Next instances the same tenant names. This limitation is planned to be removed in future software.
