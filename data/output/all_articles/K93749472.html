K93749472: BIG-IQ sends yellow alert when syncgroup is changed

Type
Support Solution
Published
2020-10-26
Updated
2023-02-21
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ Platforms
  • BIG-IQ
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K93749472

Description
When changes are made to the DNS sync group on the BIG-IP,  BIG-IQ may send a yellow alert that the  sync group is not in sync

Environment

  • BIG-IQ

Cause
        Propagation of change to the sync group takes time. If the notification time is below the propagation time the BIG-IQ will send an alert that the sync group is not in sync.
        The GREEN > YELLOW notification may be sent 15 minutes after the change, and in one instance the notification was sent three minutes after a YELLOW > GREEN notification was recorded.

Recommended Actions

  1.  Increasing the interval to something in the range of 240 to 480 may reduce alert frequency
  2.  A setting of zero is not recommended.

Additional Information
        In GUI  Monitoring -> ALERT & NOTIFICATIONS -> Settings -> Set "DNS sync failed"
