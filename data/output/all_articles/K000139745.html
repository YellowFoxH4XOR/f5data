Description

You may see the following message in your ltm log: 

"Chassis fans are not at 100% speed with <number of blades> powered blades"

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • Fan

Cause

The 'Chassis fans are not at 100%' message means the unit is cool enough that the fans do not have to run at 100%.

Recommended Actions

None, this is normal behaviour.

Additional Information

None
