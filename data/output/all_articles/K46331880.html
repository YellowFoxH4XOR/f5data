Known Issue

iApp custom template properties may not be displayed.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is configured with a custom iApp template.
  • Your custom iApp template references more than 150 application services.
  • From the Configuration utility, you attempt to view the properties of the iApp template.

Impact

The iApp template properties page does not display the properties of the template, and the template cannot be edited.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When you attempt to view the affected iApp template properties, the page is blank.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 488610 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies To box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:

HotfixNoneNone

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can create a duplicate of the iApp template and then reassign sufficient application services to the new template to ensure that each template's assigned applications do not exceed 150.

Impact of workaround: The suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
