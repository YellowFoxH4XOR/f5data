Description
In order to understand reporting from both BIG-IP tools as well as third party monitoring tools it is important to know the differences and similarities is how the values are calculated, collected, and where they are collected from.  This is important for observed High CPU usage, spikes or alerts via BIG-IP or 3rd Party monitoring tools.

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • Unexpected CPU usage

Cause
Differences in the polling method, averaging method, and granularity
