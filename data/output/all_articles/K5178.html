Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The full_debug script does not check for duplicate entries in the syslog.conf file. When you enable full_debug, the script enables full logging for the local2 facility. By default, 3dns already logs the local2 facility at the warning level. As a result, all messages at the warning level or higher will be duplicated in the log file. In addition, if you have configured a higher level of logging, all of the additional levels will be duplicated.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR31388.

Workaround

To work around this issue, refer to K5046: Reasons why the same message may appear in a log file multiple times.
