Description

  • SDC supports NTP authentication.
  • The NTP config is managed by Salt and it involves modification and customization of the ntp.conf file.
  • F5 Technical Support is best used for troubleshooting existing/previously working configurations. New configurations, design implementation, as well as config optimization and upgrade planning are handled by the F5 Professional Services team.

Environment

  • SDC 5.1
  • NTP
  • Salt

Cause

By design.
