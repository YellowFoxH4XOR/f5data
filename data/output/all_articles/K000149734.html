Description

  • You are currently running a End of Software Development/End of Life versions.
  • You plan to upgrade your BIG-IP to a supported version.
  • You require version 11 or 12 software image so that you can load them in your lab, upgrade the configuration to supported version, and load the configuration back into a production device.

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • Upgrade

Cause

You could not find version 11 or 12 in Downloads | MyF5.

Recommended Actions

We do not provide End of Life software version to customer which are not available in the Downloads | MyF5. For reference BIG-IP software support policy

What you could alternatively do is - look into the /shared/images of your current version 11 or 12 device and see if the ISO image is present. If yes, download it and load into your lab.

Else upgrade your current version 11 or 12 device to a minimum version available in Downloads | MyF5. After the upgrade, take that configuration and load it in your lab.

Additional Information

The version you plan to upgrade to may be limited by your BIG-IP hardware device. Please refer K9476: The F5 hardware/software compatibility matrix
