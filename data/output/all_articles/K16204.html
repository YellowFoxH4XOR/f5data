Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may produce a core file under heavy traffic conditions when connection limits are specified.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Pool members or nodes are configured with connection limits.
  • The BIG-IP system is under heavy load.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restarting. If the BIG-IP system is configured as part of a high-availability (HA) group, a device failover may occur.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP system fails over to the standby system if the system is configured for HA.
  • The BIG-IP system generates TMM core files to the /shared/core directory.
  • You observe messages similar to the following example in the /var/log/ltm file:

    notice sod[7056]: 01140029:5: HA daemon_heartbeat tmm fails action is go offline down links and restart.
    notice sod[7056]: 010c0050:5: Sod requests links down.
    notice sod[7056]: 01140045:5: HA reports tmm NOT ready.
