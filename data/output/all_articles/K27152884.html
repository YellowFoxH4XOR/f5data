Description
One or more blades in a Viprion chassis cannot communicate with the other blades.  This may manifest as certain VLANs being unavailable or a reduction in capacity, or no impact may be noticed.  tmsh show sys cluster may show everything is fine but you may see error logs similar to this in /var/log/ltm:

iHealth may report that the CMP states on the blades do not match.

Environment

  • Viprion with 2 or more blades.

Cause
The root cause is unknown at this time

Recommended Actions

The only resolution is to reboot the entire chassis. 
  • To reboot from the TMUI go to System, Configuration, Device, General and select Reboot Chassis.
  • To reboot from the CLI you will need to issue: clsh reboot
After rebooting you should verify that all blades show the new uptime. You can do this from the CLI by issuing: clsh uptime
If a blade reports a longer uptime, or fails to return any value, it will need to be rebooted manually.  You can do this by sshing directly to the management IP of that blade and issuing a reboot.  It is best practice to reboot the entire chassis again after this blade has been rebooted and verify that the blades have all come up together.
