Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may stop responding due to a memory leak in the BIG-IP WebAccelerator module or BIG-IP Advanced Acceleration Manager (AAM).

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You make configuration changes to nodes in the BIG-IP WebAccelerator or BIG-IP AAM configuration.

Over time, very small amounts of memory may leak with each configuration change, causing TMM to eventually fail due to lack of memory.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may fail over to a standby system in the device group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H481967 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High screen.
  • The BIG-IP system sends a SIGSEGV signal to TMM, resulting in a stack trace and core file.
