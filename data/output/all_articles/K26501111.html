Topic

This article explains the software-defined networking (SDN) offerings for BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE).

Description

For BIG-IP VE with license SKU V12 and V13, an additional SDN add-on license is required to enable SDN services. You can verify the add-on license by performing the following procedure:

  1. Log in to the TMOS Shell (tmsh) by entering the following command:

    tmsh

  2. To verify that the license includes SDN add-on license, enter the following command:

    show sys license | grep 'SDN Services'

    The command output appears similar to the following example:

    SDN Services

For BIG-IP VE with license SKU V16 and later, the SDN license is included in the VE base license by default. No add-on license is required to enable the SDN services, and it is the expected behavior that no SDN Services appears in the license output.
