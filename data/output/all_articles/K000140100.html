Description

On rare occasion when enabling ha-group unexpected ha-failover can happen due to HA monitor fault with race condition. The following logs will appear in /var/log/ltm:

 

notice sod[7270]: 010c0091:5: HA monitor fault on device bigip1 for traffic-group traffic-group-1.

...

notice sod[7270]: 010c0092:5: HA monitor fault cleared on device bigip1 for traffic-group traffic-group-1.

 

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • Multi-blade vcmp guests on Viprion system
  • Enabling ha-group

Cause

BugID 1587897

Recommended Actions

To avoid undesirable affect of unexpected failover on production traffic perform ha-group configuration during maintenance window.

Additional Information

K16947: F5 recommended practices for the HA group feature
