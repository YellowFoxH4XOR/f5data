Known Issue

The BIG-IP APM system may enforce access control lists (ACLs) inconsistently.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP APM system experiences a high packet rate and is under heavy load.                                                             
  • The BIG-IP APM system processes many concurrent connections during the launch of an application protected by the ACL.

Impact

ACLs are not applied to some user traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The BIG-IP APM system fails to enforce ACLs for some user traffic.
