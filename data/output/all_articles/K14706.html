Policy Information

With the availability of BIG-IP AAM, F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of BIG-IP WebAccelerator, effective October 1, 2014. F5 is providing advance product notification to customers and partners announcing intent to retire the products listed in the tables below. F5 will migrate customers who purchased a software add-on and upgraded to BIG-IP 11.4.0 or later to BIG-IP AAM; in addition, the End of Software Development (EoSD), End of Support Contract Renewal (EoSCR), and End of Technical Support (EoTS) dates will not apply. The following dates and milestones apply only to BIG-IP WebAccelerator; they do not apply to customers who upgrade to BIG-IP AAM 11.4.0.

The EoS for the 1600 and 3600 platforms is effective January 1, 2014. For all other platforms, the effective date is October 1, 2014.

Customers who have purchased SKUs in the table below should contact their account manager or F5 Technical Support when upgrading to BIG-IP 11.4.0 to have their system converted to the equivalent BIG-IP AAM SKU. This conversion will allow for continued hardware and software support beyond the dates listed.

Note: The BIG-IP 1600 and 3600 are not eligible for the conversion.

Technical Support

F5 maintains generous life cycle policies that allow customers to enjoy many years of both support and new software releases. Customers whose platforms are designated End of Life (EoL), and who are currently under maintenance contracts, will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the service contract renewal date expires.

After the EoSD date, October 1, 2014, F5 will not be able to provide software fixes for any technical issues, and F5 will continue to provide technical support for the BIG-IP WebAccelerator on a best-effort basis. Customers who upgrade to BIG-IP AAM 11.4.0 or later will continue to receive technical support.
