Policy Information

F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of the Web Fraud Protection (WFP) dashboard effective January 7, 2019. The End of Life (EoL) date is July 8, 2019.

F5 is providing Advanced Product Notification to customers and partners announcing intent to retire the following product.

ProductEnd of Sale (EoS)End of Software Development (EoSD)End of Technical Support (EoTS)
WFP dashboard7-JAN-20197-JAN-20198-JULY-2019

Technical Support

F5 maintains generous lifecycle policies that allow customers to enjoy many years of both support and new software releases. Customers whose platforms are designated EoL and who are currently under maintenance contracts will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the service contract renewal date expires.

After the EoSD date, January 7, 2019, F5 will not be able to provide software fixes for any technical issues, and F5 will continue to provide support for the WFP dashboard on a best-effort basis.

Migration Options

If you are currently using the WFP dashboard, F5 recommends using BIG-IQ Centralized Management with an FPS license or cloud dashboard as an alternative mechanism for visibility into fraud alerts. BIG-IQ Centralized Management is an application-centric security management and monitoring solution that aggregates fraud alerts.
