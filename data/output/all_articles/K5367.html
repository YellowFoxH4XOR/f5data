Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When BIG-IP is unreachable or when a version 4.2 3-DNS probes a BIG-IP running version 4.5 or later, the BIG-IP statistics page may display unusual values in the BIG-IP statistics page. The statistics page may display large, incorrect values, negative values, or may display valid statistics in the wrong field.

There is no work around for this problem.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR29189, CR30464, CR36766, and CR41840.
