K82381341: BIG-IP APM may fail to verify the signature from the SAML IDP with the message: Digest of SignedInfo mismatch

Description
BIG-IP APM is configured to perform SAML authentication. After successful authentication to the SAML IDP, BIG-IP APM fails to very the signature of the SAML response.
A typical snippet from /var/log/apm is similar to:
err apmd[19048]: 01490000:3: modules/Authentication/Saml/SamlSPAgent.cpp func: "verifyAssertionSignature()" line: 5831 Msg: ERROR: verifying the digest of SAML Response
debug apmd[19048]: 01490266:7: modules/Authentication/Saml/SamlSPAgent.cpp: 'verifyAssertionSignature()': 5883: Verification of SAML signature #1 failed
err apmd[19048]: 01490204:3: SAML Agent: /Common/saml_auth_ag failed to process signed assertion, error: Digest of SignedInfo mismatch

When viewing the SAML IDP response encoding contains &#13 strings similar to the following:
<ds:SignatureValue>
BKsdDb4BP9yW6tJSk84EQLXME+ydwUZSj78FDK/M/oTnyl6kqTkwAzEhd1rjgqcNm4dRkDOIoWYA&#13;
RbYaSiJXNrQujhZmd2awu5N6QaHITe0o502LO0farL4ZLkNGz9QGzn0vWCwu9/uPe9+FAxirDF6l&#13;
Ad9KKumCtx+ULhYQ4C/mxNuVOfviEQaVI1RhBef+UNqW40YeXYdmJFzObdjiqJ7jZNPhJBTyEj9m&#13;
U5KYiz5uZ8JwwRv+G4AVIn6meuNC8BL6ZH3/2IJiNm6pX6g6Xy3zOTLKQE3+jUseu7H/tOEzztYs&#13;
XvQjY5WmsHDZl3/G2zeghy0GpenstuOmpiQVpg==
</ds:SignatureValue>

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM
  • SAML SP

Cause
This may be due to the way the SAML response is encoded, back from the SAML IDP.

Recommended Actions

F5 suggest configuring the SAML IDP in such a way there's no trailing chars or strings, attached to the signature.

Additional Information
None.
