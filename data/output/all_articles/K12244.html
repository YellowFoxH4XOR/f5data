Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Under rare circumstances, the BIG-IP GTM pool Alternate Load Balancing method is overwritten with a corrupt value in the /config/gtm/wideip.conf file. As a result, the gtmd process may crash while trying to parse the configuration during wide IP ConfigSync or during gtmd start-up.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as ID 336260 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 10.2.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM release notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-10.2.0-1789.0-HF2, which has been issued for BIG-IP 10.2.0. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
