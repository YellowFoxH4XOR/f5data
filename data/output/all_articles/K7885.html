Known Issue

This is a result of a known issue. Responses to client requests that exceed the licensed-enforced compression limit are switched to null-compression or no compression. The responses are truncated and result in corrupted files.

This behavior occurs when the licensed compression rate is exceeded, which causes the BIG-IP system to switch to null compression while data is still being transferred.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR81731 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.4.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.
