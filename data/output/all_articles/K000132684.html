Description

You have scanned your F5 product for potential vulnerabilities and several of them have been reported as a results of scan.

Environment

  • All F5 products
  • Third-party vulnerability scanners (like Qualys)

Cause

Some of the vulnerabilites reported by third-pary scanners may be false-positive. In cases where potential vulnerable code is present, the F5 product might never use the vulnerable function, or root/admin access might be required for anybody to exploit the system.

Recommended Actions

If your vulnerability scanner reports that your F5 system may be affected please go to MyF5 website  and search for particular CVE you are concerned about.

If there is no information about concerning CVE, please open a support ticket providing particular vulnerability number in case description.
