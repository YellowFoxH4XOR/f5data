Description

The TCP Connection Overload Protection is a feature that is designed to protect the tenant from getting overwhelmed by new concurrent TCP connection requests and it is available in all platform that has the F5 Data Mover component. By dropping new TCP connections at the platform level, the tenant's TMM CPU usage will be kept stable during peak period or during unexpected extreme high new TCP connections event.
