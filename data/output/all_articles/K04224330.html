Known Issue

If you configure inbound traffic and use Layer 3 (L3) or HTTPS services, you may need to take some additional steps to configure this combination.

Impact

Regardless of traffic flow, packets always enter an L3 service on its inbound side. Therefore, a simple L3 or HTTP service with a single default route back to the BIG-IP system returns traffic to the incorrect interface.

Note: This issue does not apply if SSL Orchestrator is set up to handle only inbound or outbound traffic; it only applies to HTTPS or L3 services.

Workaround

There are two ways to work around this issue:

  • You can set up the service device to route traffic back to the BIG-IP system based on the destination address. This option may require setting up policy based routing on the service device. You should contact the service device vendor for assistance.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround has an unknown impact on your system. You should contact the service device vendor.

  • You can create multiple services for the security devices but swap the To and From service networks.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround may require duplicating rules or policies but should not have a negative impact on your system.
