Description

The BIG-IQ generated the utilization reports for the CAT license, but sending the reports failed.

Environment

  • Flex Consumption Program (FCP)

  • CAT/LOAD license offerings
  • Error banner across the Reports page
  • Report generated for missing dates
  • Report not sent to F5
  • Empty "Last Submission Time" Column

Cause

This can occur due to various network connectivity, firewall updates, or certificate issues. 

Recommended Actions

  1. Resolve the connectivity issue (Troubleshooting connectivity after api.f5.com and callhome.f5.com IP address changes), certificate issue (api.f5.com is rejecting FCP report submissions with error), or investigate with your local IT team.
  2. Submit the utilization report directly from the BIG-IQ to F5. On the BIG-IQ:
    1. Navigate to Devices Tab > License Management > Reports. 
    2. Note the dates of the generated reports where the "Last Submission Time" column is empty.
    3. Check the box to the left of the most recent report, that contains the relevant dates.
    4. Click "Send to F5".
    5. Confirm the date and time populates the "Last Submission Time" column.
  3. This sends the report directly to F5 for processing.
