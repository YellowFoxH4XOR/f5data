What Happened?

Environment

  • HTTP Load Balancer 
  • Automatic TLS certificate 

Resolution/Answer

Full process of ACME challenge

  1. In case of HTTPS load balancer with automatic TLS certificate, F5 Distributed Cloud generates certificates for the domains using the LetsEncrypt Automatic Certificate Management Environment (ACME) server.
  2. LetsEncrypt issues a DNS challenge for a TXT record to be created under the domain with specified text message.
  3. When you get a certificate from Let’s Encrypt, Let’s Encrypt servers validate that you control the domain names in that certificate using challenges, as defined by the ACME standard.
  4. This challenge asks you to prove that you control the DNS for your domain name by putting a specific value in a TXT record under that domain name. After Let’s Encrypt gives your ACME client a token, your client will create a TXT record derived from that token and your account key, and put that record at:_acme-challenge.<YOUR_DOMAIN>Then Let’s Encrypt will query the DNS system for that record. If it finds a match, you can proceed to issue a certificate.

How to force ACME challenge

Every time, when you change LB domain list new challenge will be created. Once challenge is issued, it can take up to 24h for challenge to validate. 

Note: if you use your DNS instead of Distributed Cloud DNS with "Allow Application Load Balancer Managed Records", 24h are counted once you will add correct ACME challenge record in your DNS. 

 

If you don't want to change domain on your LB and certificate is not issued, please contact Distributed Cloud support. There is possibility to recreate challenge from our side.
