K000160024: Lack of logs from BIG-IP on BIG-IQ

Description

After configuration of fetching the logs from BIG-IP to BIG-IQ you may encounter a problem when the logs are not visible on the BIG-IQ. There is also possibility to observe some logs which may indicates this issue:

[INFO][12 Feb 2026 14:26:49 IST][/cm/shared/stats-mgmt/stats-node-task StatsNodeTaskCollectionWorker] checkAgentHealth failed, rescheduling: java.net.ConnectException: Connection refused

Environment

  • BIG-IQ
  • AVR module
  • BIG-IP

Cause

Issue may be related to the asymmetric routing, mostly it is because when the BIG-IQ DCD and the BIG-IP are using the management-route, even though they are in the same subnet.

Answer/Recommended Actions

For the beginning it is good to remove the management-route when both of the devices are in the same subnet. Also it is good to verify the connectivity bidirectional by using commands below:

from BIG-IQ DCD to BIG-IP: 
telnet <ip.address BIG-IP> 443 
from BIG-IP to BIG-IQ DCD:
telnet <ip.address BIG-IQ DCD> 443
