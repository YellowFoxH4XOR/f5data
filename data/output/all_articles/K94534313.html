Known Issue

The system may not honor the maximum data size allowed on representational state transfer (REST) messages.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • You manually adjust the maximum data size of 32 MB to a higher limit by using, for example, the /mgmt/shared/server/messaging/settings/8100 REST endpoint.
  • You request or receive more than 32 MB of data by way of iControl REST.

Impact

The system may be unable to transfer more than 32 MB of data by way of iControl REST.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The system is unable to transfer more than 32 MB that result in requests to iControl REST or restnoded.
  • In the /var/log/restjavad.x.log file, you observe error messages similar to the following example:

    Exception: java.lang.IllegalArgumentException: 47177925 is more than 33554432
