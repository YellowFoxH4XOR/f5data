Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. BIG-IP does not properly initialize the variables that contain the SNAT pool statistics. As a result, the initial SNAT pool statistics are incorrect. The statistics will normalize over time.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR26953, CR27061, CR29407, CR32944, and CR41768.
