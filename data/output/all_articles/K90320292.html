Known Issue

BIG-IP ASM guest CPU count settings may create an invalid VCMP configuration.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP platform is configured for VCMP.
  • You configure a BIG-IP ASM VCMP guest allocating 10 CPUs.

Impact

The BIG-IP ASM VCMP guest does not reliably process traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The newly created BIG-IP ASM VMCP guest fails to start.
  • The BIG-IP ASM VMCP guest sporadically sends a tcp reset to client requests.
  • In the /var/log/ltm file, you may observe error messages similar to the following example:

    err tmm4[<pid>]: 01480024:3: Can't bind the flow, waiting for config response on channel mem://bd10
    err tmm4[<pid>]: 01230140:3: RST sent from <virtual ip address:dest port> to <client ip address:src port>, [0x1b41b3c:459] Internal error (ASM requested abort (open error))

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can allocate either 8 or 12 CPUs to the BIG-IP ASM VCMP guest.
