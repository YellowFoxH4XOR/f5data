Description

  • This page offers helpful answers to frequently asked questions from F5 customers regarding notifications about service status, incidents, scheduled maintenance, and related topics.

Environment

  • https://www.f5cloudstatus.com/

FAQs

  1. How can I get notified about maintenance or incidents?
    1. Go to https://www.f5cloudstatus.com/ and click the "Subscribe To Updates" blue button at the top of the page.
    2. Enter your email or distribution list.
    3. Check your inbox for a one-time password (OTP) and enter it on the site.
    4. Choose the regions and services you want updates for.
      1. NOTE: You can also subscribe using webhook, RSS, or Atom Feed if that works better for you.
  2. Should I allow any specific email address to receive status notifications?
    1. Generally, you won't need to take any extra steps. However, if you suspect you're not receiving notifications, we recommend adding noreply@statuspage.io to your allowlist or safe sender list to ensure alerts aren't blocked or sent to spam.
  3. I’m unsure which location or service to select for my subscription.
    1. Contact your F5 Technical Account Manager (TAM), Customer Success Manager (CSM), or F5 Support to ensure you choose the appropriate components or services based on your deployment and/or configuration.
  4. If I'm subscribed to multiple components, will I receive separate notifications for each one?
    1. No - you'll receive a single, consolidated notification even if multiple components you're subscribed to are affected. This helps reduce noise while still keeping you informed.
  5. Can I view F5 maintenance updates and notifications without subscribing via email?
    1. Yes, all maintenance and incident details are available on the StatusPage. However, if you don’t subscribe, you won’t receive real-time notifications, such as alerts sent 60 minutes before maintenance begins, when it starts, and once it’s completed.
  6. I’m getting an overwhelming number of notifications from StatusPage emails.
    1. A better digital world is built on innovation. At F5, we collaborate with our customers and partners, bringing together diverse perspectives, experiences, and technologies to modernize application delivery and security. To support this mission, we conduct regular updates and periodic maintenance. We recommend that you subscribe to the components relevant to your environment. For guidance in selecting the appropriate services, please get in touch with your assigned Account Manager or F5 Support.
  7. How do I manage or unsubscribe from status email notifications?
    1. At the bottom of any alert or email notification, click "Manage your subscription", where you can check or uncheck options to subscribe or unsubscribe from all updates.
  8. What kind of updates will I receive after subscribing?
    1. You'll receive notifications for incident updates, such as:
      1. Investigating
      2. Identified
      3. Ongoing updates are approximately every 30 minutes or as new information becomes available.
    2. Maintenance notifications such as:
      1. Scheduled
      2. In Progress
      3. Completed
  9. I need historical information about past incidents or maintenance. Where can I find it?
    1. Historical incidents and maintenance records are available in the StatusPage archive. Navigate to the F5 Cloud Status page and review past events with detailed timelines and resolutions.
