Topic

In April 2007, the New Zealand government adopted a new standard for Daylight Saving Time.  Daylight Saving Time in New Zealand will begin at 2 a.m. on the last Sunday in September and end at 2 a.m. on the first Sunday in April.  The amount of time change is 30 minutes.

Information about the Energy Policy Act is located at the following New Zealand Department of Internal Affairs web site:

http://www.dia.govt.nz/diawebsite.nsf/wpg_URL/Services-Daylight-Saving-Index?OpenDocument

As of FirePass version 6.0.2, FirePass automatically adjusts to the new Daylight Saving Time standard.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR85974 and it was fixed in FirePass version 6.0.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the FirePass Release Notes.

Additionally, Hotfix HF-85974-1 has been issued for FirePass versions 5.5, 5.5.1, 5.5.2, 6.0.0, and 6.0.1. You may download this hotfix or a later version of this hotfix from the F5 Networks Downloads page.

For instructions about obtaining a hotfix, refer to K167: Downloading software from F5 Networks.

For instructions about installing a hotfix, refer to K3430: Installing hotfixes.
