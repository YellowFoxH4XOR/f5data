F5 Technical Support can help resolve issues more quickly when you provide a full description of the issue and the details of your configuration. To help you gather all the required information, use the following guidelines to prepare for opening a case:

General Information

Provide the following information when you open a case with F5 Technical Support:

  • A full description of the issue, including the following:
    • The symptoms of the issue
    • The approximate time the issue first occurred
    • The number of times the issue has recurred
    • Any error output provided by the system
    • Steps to reproduce the issue
    • Any changes you made to the system before the issue first occurred
    • Any steps you have attempted to solve the issue
  • A description of the impact the issue is having on your site, using the following definitions:
    • Site Down - Your network or application is down, or critical business functions have stopped due to the issue.
    • Site at Risk - Your network or application is severely and negatively impacted by the issue.
    • Performance Severely Degraded - The performance of your network or application has been severely reduced due to the issue.
    • Performance Impaired - Your network or application is suffering from reduced performance, but otherwise continues to work as expected.
    • General Assistance Required - The subject of the case does not currently impact your network or application.
  • The hours that you are available to work on the issue and any alternative contacts that work on the issue if you are not available.
  • Remote access information, if possible.

    Remote access to your network environment is important, because it is the most effective method for collecting information and troubleshooting technical issues. If you cannot provide remote access, F5 Technical Support will work directly with you to resolve the issue over the phone; however, this method can often be more time consuming and may require file transfers, replication, and additional testing.

SSL VPN client log file information

Collect the following information from the affected system(s) and provide it when you open the case.

Note: For information about sending this information to F5, refer to K2486: Providing files to F5 Technical Support.

To collect the SSL VPN client log file, perform the steps specific to your operating system.

Microsoft Windows systems

To collect the SSL VPN client log file for Microsoft Windows systems, perform the following procedure:

  1. Open the SSL VPN client.
  2. From the menu, click Setup.
  3. Click Trace Logs.
  4. Click the link Click here to view logs.
  5. Cut all of the information and paste it into your email or case notes.

Apple Mac OS X  and Linux systems

To collect the SSL VPN client log file for Mac OS X or Linux systems, refer to the following Solutions:

For FirePass version 7.0.0 or later, refer to K12321: Enabling Network Access debugging for Mac OS X and Linux

For FirePass version 6.1.0, refer to K3367: Enabling FirePass Network Access debugging for Mac OS X and Linux

Include all of the information from the log file in your email or case notes.

Windows Mobile Devices

On Windows Mobile devices, the SSL VPN client log files are located at the following location:

\Temp\f5SSLVPNlog.txt
\Temp\comdriverlog.txt

Cut all of the information from these log files and paste it into your email or case notes.

Client routing information

To collect the client routing table and interface status, perform the steps specific to your operating system.

Microsoft Windows systems

To collect the client routing table and interface status for Microsoft Windows systems, perform the following procedure:

  1. Log in to the command line.
  2. View the routing table by typing the following command:

    netstat -rn

  3. Cut all of the information and paste it into your email or case notes.
  4. View the interface status by typing the following command:

    >ipconfig /all

  5. Cut all of the information and paste it into your email or case notes.

Apple Max OS X and Linux systems

To collect the client routing table and interface status for Apple Max OS X and Linux systems, perform the following procedure:

  1. Log in to the command line.
  2. View the routing table by typing the following command:

    netstat -rn

  3. Cut all of the information and paste it into your email or case notes.
  4. View the interface status by typing the following command:

    >ifconfig -a

  5. Cut all of the information and paste it into your email or case notes.

Apple iOS devices

On the Apple iOS device, the BIG-IP Edge Portal app, and the BIG-IP Edge Client app, you can email the client files to F5 Technical Support.

Perform the following procedure:

Note: You must have an email account configured on the Apple iOS device to perform this procedure.

  1. On the Apple iOS device, open the BIG-IP Edge Portal app or the BIG-IP Edge Client app.
  2. Click Settings in the lower right corner.
  3. Click Report a Problem.

    An email window will open and attach the following log files:

    BIG-IP Edge Client
    Edge-Client-App.log
    Edge-Client-Plugin.log
    BIG-IP Edge Portal
    EdgePortal.log
  4. In the To field, enter the email address to where you are sending the logs.
  5. Click Send.

Android devices

On the Android device, the BIG-IP Edge Portal app, and the BIG-IP Edge Client app, you can email the client files to F5 Technical Support.

Perform the following procedure:

Note: You must have an email account configured on the Android device to perform this procedure.

  1. On the Android device, open the BIG-IP Edge Portal app or the BIG-IP Edge Client app.
  2. Click the Menu button.
  3. Click the Settings option.
  4. Click Report a Problem.

    This opens the Send Log... dialog menu. 
  5. Select your email app.

    This opens an email window and attaches the following log file:

    EdgePortalLog.zip
  6. In the To field, enter the email address of where you are sending the logs.
  7. Click Send.
