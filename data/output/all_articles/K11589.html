Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. A race condition during connection closure may prevent plugin-initiated connections, such as Edge Side Includes requests, from shutting down properly. However, plugin-initiated connections are marked as NOEXPIRE, which prevents the reaper from deleting the zombie flows. As a result, BIG-IP WebAccelerator performance may become degraded.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR137378 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 10.2.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP WebAccelerator release notes.

Additionally, this issue has been fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-10.1.0-3372.0-HF1 for BIG-IP 10.1.0. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
