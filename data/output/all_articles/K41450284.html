Description

Beginning in BIG-IP 16.1.0, F5 moved to a four-year Long-Term Stability Release Standard Support phase, from the previous five-year phase. This change applies to BIG-IP 16.1.0 and subsequent x.1.0 releases.

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • Software lifecycle policy

Cause

F5 moved to a four-year Long-Term Stability Release Standard Support phase is characterized by the introduction of new features, as well as security updates, stability improvements, and new hardware support.
