Known Issue

The BIG-IP system does not delete temporary directories and files used for Kerberos delegation.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You configure the BIG-IP system to authenticate with Kerberos delegation.

Impact

The system may experience excessive disk space and memory utilization.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Over time, the /var/run/krbdelegate/ directory may fill with temporary directories and files, resulting in disk space exhaustion.
