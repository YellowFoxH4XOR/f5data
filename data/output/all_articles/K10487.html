Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP system ignores connection limits specified for SNAT translation addresses. As a result, the BIG-IP system may allow as many connections as the SNAT is capable of handling.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR86294 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 9.4.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.

Additionally, this issue has been fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-9.3.1-74.0-HF7, issued for BIG-IP version 9.3.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the cumulative hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.

For information about the F5 hotfix policy, refer to K4918: Overview of F5 critical issue hotfix policy.

For information about how to manage F5 product hotfixes, refer to K6845: Managing F5 product hotfixes.
