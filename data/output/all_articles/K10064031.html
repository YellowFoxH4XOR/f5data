Description

The Trellix products do not appear in Support Tables as of the latest SDK 4.3.3109.0 (September 13, 2022)

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM
  • EPSEC package version lower than 1.0.0-1526
  • Client Side Security AV check
  • McAfee / Fireeye Merger

Cause

Trellix currently does not have any products in the category of Antivirus, which is why Trellix doesn't appear under support tables. 

Despite FireEye/McAfee merging into Trellix, the products under vendor FireEye/McAfee before they merged into Trellix would still be maintained under vendor FireEye/McAfee.

Until a brand new Trellix Product in the Antivirus category is released, it will not appear as a Vendor-ID / Product-ID in the support tables.

All pre-existing McAfee / FireEye products classified as Antivirus will remain under their original either McAfree or FireEye Vendor-ID as they were originally categorized.

Recommended Actions

Please follow the article below, upgrade the EPSEC package to the latest version (higher than 1.0.0-1526).

K48955220: Installing an OPSWAT Endpoint Security update on BIG-IP APM systems

Additional Information

Please read the file readme_minimum_version.txt, upgrade BIG-IP and APM Clients version if necessary.
