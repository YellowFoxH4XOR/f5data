Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If an NFS share for a virtual server runs out of free space, the ARX system may deny client access to other virtual servers until free space is restored to the affected share.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as ID 345278 and it was fixed in ARX versions 5.1.9 and 5.2.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the ARX release notes.
