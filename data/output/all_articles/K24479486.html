Known Issue

Hardware-accelerated SSL traffic may encounter errors and performance problems.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system uses Intel Cave Creek SSL hardware acceleration.
  • You are experiencing a high SSL traffic load.
  • You are using one of the following platforms: 2x00, 4x00, i850, i2x00, i4x00, or HRC-i2800.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may experience SSL connection failures or reduced performance.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • In the /var/log/ltm log file, you observe error messages that appear similar to the following example:

    tmm3[13326]: 01010025:2: Device error: crypto codec cn-crypto-3 queue is stuck.
    tmm3[13326]: 01010260:2: Hardware Error(Co-Processor): n3-crypto1 request queue stuck

Resolution

Status

F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. Workaround

To work around this issue, you can increase the crypto.queue.timeout database key, allowing more time to the BIG-IP system to process a request. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system. This procedure will mitigate future occurrences. You must reboot the BIG-IP system to clear a currently occurring condition.

  1. Log in to the TMOS Shell (tmsh) as an administrative user.
  2. Enter the following command:

    modify /sys db crypto.queue.timeout value 300

  3. To clear a currently occurring condition, reboot the BIG-IP system by entering the following command:

    reboot
