Description

This article explains how F5 verifies and associates contacts to company accounts, why your email domain matters, and what to do if you encounter issues during this process. It is intended to help users understand the requirements and steps for successful account association.

When associating a contact to a company account in F5 systems (such as MyF5), it is important to ensure that your email domain matches the company account name. This process helps maintain account security and ensures only authorized users have access. 

Environment

  • F5 MyF5 portal and account management
  • F5 product trials
  • Corporate email addresses (e.g., @company.com)
  • Personal email addresses (e.g., @gmail.com, @yahoo.com)

Cause

F5 requires that the domain of your email address matches the company account name to ensure only authorized users are associated with company accounts.

  • If your email domain does not match, administrator approval is required.
  • Personal email addresses are not permitted for company account association.
  • These policies help protect company assets and maintain account security.

Recommended Actions

  1. Use a Corporate Email Address:
    Always use your company-issued email address when requesting association with a company account.

  2. Administrator Approval for Mismatched Domains:
    If your email domain does not match the company account, contact your account administrator to approve your request. 

  3. Do Not Use Personal Email Addresses:
    Personal email addresses (such as Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook, etc.) cannot be added to company accounts.

  4. For Product Trials:
    If you do not have a corporate email, contact an F5 sales representative to discuss trial options.

Additional Information

If you need further assistance, please reach out to your account administrator or F5 Support.
