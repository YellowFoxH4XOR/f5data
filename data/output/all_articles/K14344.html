Known Issue

NTLM client authentication requests may fail when the internal process that handles NTLM authentication on the ARX system, exhausts its allocated memory due to a memory leak.

Impact

NTLM clients are unable to authenticate.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • The NTLM client (sbmclient) gets a STATUS_NO_LOGON_SERVERS response from the ARX CIFS services.
  • Authentication fails and blocks NTLM client access.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you must reload the active ARX system and fail over to the standby peer. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Services will not be available until failover completes.

  1. Log in to the ARX CLI.
  2. Enable privileged mode by typing the following command:

    enable
  3. Reload the ARX system by typing the following command:

    reload
  4. When prompted, confirm the reload request by typing:

    Yes
