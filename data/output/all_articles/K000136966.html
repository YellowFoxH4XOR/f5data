Description

How to install certificates from a third-party certificate authority (CA) in F5OS.

Environment

  • F5OS
  • Certificates

Cause

Installing certificate bundle

Recommended Actions

To watch a video demo of this procedure, go to  Install an SSL certificate signed by a third-party CA on F5OS .

  1. Generate a certificate signing request (CSR) and submit it to your certificate authority.

    Note: If you generate the CSR on the system, the private key won't automatically change after generating CSR. The new private key is in the  /etc/auth-config/certs directory.

  2. Download the certificate bundle from the certificate authority. (It consists of the server certificate, root certificate, and intermediate certificate).

  3. In the F5OS system, go to: AUTHENTICATION & ACCESS > TLS Configuration.

  4. Under CA Bundles, select Add. Enter a name for the bundle.
  5. Open your CA Bundle file. Copy the contents and paste into the TLS CA Certificate field.
  6. Select Save and Close.
  7. Under TLS Certificate & Key, expand (SHOW), to reveal the text-input fields for the signed TLS Certificate and TLS Key (private key). These fields accept PEM-formatted certificate data.
  8. Open your signed certificate in a text editor. Copy the contents and paste into the TLS Certificate field.
  9. Open your private key in a text editor. Copy the contents and paste into the TLS Key field. If the private key is encrypted with a passphrase, enter the passphrase into the TLS Key Passphrase field.
  10. Select Save.
