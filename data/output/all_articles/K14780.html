Known Issue

The incremental-config-sync-cache option may not display contents properly.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You issue the tmsh show cm device-group <device group> incremental-config-sync-cache command.

The incremental-config-sync-cache option is intended to show additional details about the incremental configuration synchronization (ConfigSync) cache for a device group. However, when specifying this option, contents of the incremental ConfigSync cache may not be shown. Additionally, contents of the incremental ConfigSync cache may be shown even when not using the incremental-config-sync-cache option.

Impact

The incremental ConfigSync cache may not display.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • Contents of the incremental ConfigSync cache for the device group are shown when issuing the tmsh show cm device-group <device group> command without the incremental-config-sync-cache option.
  • No incremental ConfigSync cache contents are shown when using the tmsh show cm device-group <device group> incremental-config-sync-cache command.
