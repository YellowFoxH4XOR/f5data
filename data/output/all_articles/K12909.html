Topic

FirePass does not support the Link Aggregation Control Protocol (LACP). IEEE 802.3ad link aggregation technology, such as the BIG-IP Trunk or Cisco Etherchannel, allows grouping of several physical Ethernet interfaces to create one logical interface for the purpose of providing fault-tolerance and high-speed links between switches, routers, and servers.

If you require LACP support in your environment, consider migrating to the BIG-IP APM platform. For more information about BIG-IP APM, contact your F5 Sales Representative. For information about using LACP on a BIG-IP APM system, refer to the TMOS Management Guide for BIG-IP Systems.
