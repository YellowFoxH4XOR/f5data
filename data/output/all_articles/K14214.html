Known Issue

Pool members may not receive connections when Request Queuing is enabled for the pool.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The affected pool's connection queue is not empty.
  • A new pool member is added, or a disabled pool member is enabled.

Impact

The newly added or enabled pool member does not receive any connections.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H406992 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • When the new pool member is added or newly enabled to the pool, the pool member does not receive any request from the pool connection queue.
  • The remaining requests in the pool connection queue are served only by the previously enabled and available pool members.
