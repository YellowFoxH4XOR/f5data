Known Issue

This may be the result of a known issue. Elements of the BIG-IP API cannot successfully free memory if the API's initial attempt to do so fails. As a result, BIG-IP's memory usage will increase by a small amount over a long period of time.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR31994 and CR41783.
