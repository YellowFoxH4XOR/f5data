Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. ICMP or ICMPv6 traffic traversing a CMP-enabled forwarding virtual server may be dropped after failover between blades in a VIPRION chassis. This issue is caused by the method in which the BIG-IP system process ARP responses on chassis-based systems.

F5 Networks Product Development is tracking this issue as CR133604.

Workaround

You can work around this issue by defining static ARP entries for affected hosts. To do so, perform the following procedure:

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.
  2. Click Network.
  3. Click ARP.
  4. Click Create.
  5. Enter the MAC address and IP address for the host.
  6. Click Finished.

Note: For more information about CMP, refer to K7751: Overview of Clustered Multi-Processing.
