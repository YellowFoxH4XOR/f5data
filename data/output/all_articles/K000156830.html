K000156830: How do I know BIG-IP SSL Orchestrator is enabled?

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-10-06
Updated
2025-10-17
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000156830

Description

You want to confirm whether BIG-IP SSL Orchestrator is enabled on your system. This article provides steps to verify its status.

Environment

  • BIG-IP SSL Orchestrator
  • BIG-IP platform
  • Applicable to versions supporting SSL Orchestrator

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP Configuration Utility (GUI):

    • Open your browser and access the management IP address of your BIG-IP device.
    • Enter your administrator credentials to log in.

  2. Navigate to System > Resource Provisioning:

    • In the left navigation panel, go to System.
    • Click on Resource Provisioning.
    • Look for the SSL Orchestrator entry in the list of modules.
      • If "SSL Orchestrator" is listed and set to "Provisioned", SSLO is enabled.
      • If it is set to "None", it is not enabled.

Additional Information

If you are unable to locate or verify SSL Orchestrator configuration, check with your licensing team to confirm whether the module is licensed and activated.
