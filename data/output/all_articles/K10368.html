Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Network packet captures on interfaces 1.1 through 1.4 on FirePass 4100 and 4300 platforms cannot capture packets from tagged VLANs. This issue affects only the ability to see the packet in a network packet capture. The packet itself is still being correctly tagged and will reach its target destination.

Typically, users expect to see the tagged packet when running a packet capture on the related interface. Users can view the packets on other parts of the network to confirm that the packets are passing. This issue is related to the network drivers.

Important: The 4100 and 4300 platforms are the only platforms affected by this issue.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR126001.
