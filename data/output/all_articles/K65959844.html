Description

Alarm LED is red, what is the cause?

Environment

  • Front Panel of BIG-IP platforms
  • any version

Cause

The Alarm LED on the front panel of your BIG-IP hardware is lit when the system records log messages of Alert or Critical severity. The LED then remains lit until it is manually cleared.

Recommended Actions

  • Login to bash of your BIG-IP system to review the log files (in /var/log/), looking for messages of Alert or greater severity.

    Note: Log messages in /var/log that corresponds to the LED Alarm on the front panel that occurred more than 10 days ago, might no longer be visible in the logs

  • Upload a QKview to iHealth, then review the Diagnostics for any High or Critical items that could have triggered an Alert or greater log message:
    K27404821: Using F5 iHealth to diagnose vulnerabilities

Additional Information

The LED with remain lit until it is manually cleared.
Manually clear following each platform's manuals.
