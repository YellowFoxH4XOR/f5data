Description

  • After upgrading from 15.1.2.1 to 15.1.6.1, Horizon Client won't connect to APM device.
  • User side error: Horizon Client:  The username or password is not correct.  Please try again. (error: Failed to resolve KDCs by domain name)
  • After the upgrade, the configuration worked fine for over 12 to 24 hours.   No changes done on the APM side after the upgrade.
  • /var/log/ltm shows:
Aug 27 12:00:39 <xxx-hostname> err zxfrd[8030]: 0153100e:3: Transfer of zone xxx.net failed with rcode (SERVFAIL).
Aug 27 12:00:39 <xxx-hostname> err zxfrd[8030]: 01531018:3: Failed to transfer zone xxx.net from 127.10.0.1, will attempt AXFR.

   Note: xxx.net will be your actual domain.

Environment

  • Vmware: Horizon Client
  • APM, DNS, LTM

Cause

Microsoft DNS Servers didn't have entries allowing F5 self IPs to do zone transfers; thus, the SERVRFAIL response in the rcode.

Not BIG-IP APM. 

Recommended Actions

  1.  Check DNS servers to allow zone transfers.
    • If seeing zone transfer (zxfrd) AXFR error, then, check if the zones are Secondary. 
    • If so, validate the Primary has nothing blocking zone transfers.
  2.  On BIG-IP APM CLI:  bigstart restart   Note: This will restart all services.
