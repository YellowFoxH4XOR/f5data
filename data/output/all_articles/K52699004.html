Known Issue

When you remove a folder by manually editing the bigip_base.conf file, objects load without error, but the system does not recreate the folder.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • A BIG-IP configuration has objects in a folder.
  • You remove a folder by manually editing the bigip_base.conf file.

Impact

You may experience an inconsistent configuration between devices in the same device group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • After modifying the bigip_base.conf file, BIG-IP hosts in a device group incorrectly display as in-sync.
  • Objects do not sync to peer devices after a configuration synchronization (ConfigSync) operation.
  • Configurations do not load after you perform a system restart or mcpd process restart.

Resolution

Status

F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. Workaround

To work around this issue, avoid manually removing folders from the bigip_base.conf file.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
