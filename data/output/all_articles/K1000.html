K1000: Maximum number of zones that can be configured on 3-DNS when NameSurfer is not configured

Type
Knowledge
Published
2007-01-13
Updated
2023-02-21
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • Legacy Products
  • 3-DNS: 4.6.4
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K1000

Topic

A 3-DNS Controller can handle any number of zones when NameSurfer is not configured; the number of zones is limited only by the amount of RAM installed in the 3-DNS Controller.
