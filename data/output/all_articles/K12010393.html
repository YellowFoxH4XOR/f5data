Known Issue

Devices connected to 10GBase-SR (Short Range) 10G Ethernet transceiver modules may report CRC errors or fail to link.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • A VIPRION B2100 or B2150 blade contains one or more 10GBase-SR (Short Range) 10G Ethernet transceiver modules (OPT-0016-00).
  • The VIPRION blade is powered on, rebooted, or restarted by the bigstart restart command.
When a VIPRION B2100 or B2150 blade boots, there is a brief window during which the transmitter on the optics module is enabled, but the accompanying initialization of the Broadcom switch has not yet occurred. During this window, which lasts about 20 seconds, the system can transmit random data that may be reported as errors by the link partner. In some cases, the link may fail to initialize after the startup process.

Impact

The link to connected equipment may not initialize correctly after a system has completed booting; the connected unit may report the link down or report CRC errors for the link.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • During the boot process, connected equipment may report CRC or other errors with the link.
  • On rare occasions, the link fails to initialize with connected equipment after the boot process.

Workaround

To work around this issue, if the link fails to initialize, you can restart the Broadcom daemon. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Restarting the Broadcom daemon drops all links on the system and causes them to re-initialize, affecting any system traffic that is using the already-initialized links.

  1. Log in to the command line.
  2. Restart the Broadcom daemon by typing the following command:

    bigstart restart bcm56xxd
