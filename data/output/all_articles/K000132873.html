K000132873: Adding a BIG-IP to multiple BIG-IQ Centralized Management

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-03-07
Updated
2023-03-07
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 8.0.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000132873

Description

  • When you want to add a BIG-IP to multiple BIG-IQ Centralized Management.
  • The BIG-IP will not be available on the 1st BIG-IQ CM that you added the BIG-IP but it is available on the latest BIG-IQ CM.

Environment

  • 2 Standalone BIG-IQ Centralized Management
  • 1 BIG-IP
  • Device Discovery

Cause

This is by design, you can managed a BIG-IP from only 1 BIG-IQ Centralized Management

 Recommended Actions

  1. Add a BIG-IP from only 1 BIG-IQ Centralized Management device.

Additional Information

None
