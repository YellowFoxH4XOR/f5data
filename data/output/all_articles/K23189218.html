Description

How to enter domains that can bypass the VPN connection when per-app VPN is connected.

Environment

  • iOS Client
  • F5 Access VPN 3.0.10
  • MDM Intune VPN profile                 
  • Per-App VPN
  • Split Tunnel

Cause

Misconfiguration

Recommended Actions

  1. To get split tunnel working with per-app VPN configure Intune VPN profile setting Excluded Domains.
