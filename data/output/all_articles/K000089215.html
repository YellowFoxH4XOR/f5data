Topic

Use this article when you want to learn how to leave feedback for MyF5 articles.

Description

You can easily leave feedback for MyF5 by using the feedback form located at the bottom of MyF5 articles.

You may also see the following pop-up survey during your visit to our site.

Figure: Site survey pop-up

Leave feedback using the pop-up survey

  1. At the bottom of a MyF5 article, locate the Support list of links.
  2. Select Leave Feedback.
  3. Select a topic to leave feedback about.
  4. Enter the feedback in the text box.
  5. Select whether you would like to be contacted about the feedback.
  6. If you selected Yes in the previous step, then provide your email address.
  7. Select Submit.

F5 wants to create content to help you self-serve, and this pop-up survey helps us understand the kind of content that makes your MyF5 experience better and easier.

Note: The pop-up site survey and feedback form do not appear for content hosted on clouddocs.f5.com or techdocs.f5.com. They also may not appear if you are using a browser plug-in such as DuckDuckGo. You must turn off such plug-ins for the feedback forms and surveys to display.

Procedure

To watch a video demo of this procedure, go to Leave feedback on MyF5 articles.

Leave feedback about an article on MyF5

  1. At the bottom of a MyF5 article, for Was this information helpful?, select Yes or No.
  2. For How can we improve this content?, enter your recommendations or other comments about the article.
  3. For Would you like us to follow up with you?, select Yes or No.
  4. If you selected Yes in the previous step, for Email, enter an email address where F5 can contact you regarding your feedback.
  5. Select Submit.

Note: If you encounter any difficulty with using the form, you can instead send your feedback to MyF5@F5.com.
