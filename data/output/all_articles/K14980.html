Known Issue

Network traffic responses for the BIG-IP system may be directed to the wrong Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM).

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system initiates network traffic that uses the same source and destination ports. For example, network time protocol (NTP) traffic uses the same source and destination ports.
  • Your platform uses HDAG hardware. The following platforms are affected by this issue:

    • BIG-IP 5000s/5200v (C109)
    • BIG-IP 7000s/7200v (D110)
    • BIG-IP 10000s/10200v (D113)
    • VIPRION Blade 4300 (A108)
    • VIPRION Blade 2250 (A112)

      Note: As this involves traffic sourced from the BIG-IP system, the issue can affect both Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP) guests and hosts.

Impact

The BIG-IP system drops response traffic that is sent to the wrong TMM.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H438063 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium | High screen.
  • Services that use the same source and destination ports, like NTP, do not function properly.
  • When reviewing the packet captures, you notice that the initiating traffic from the BIG-IP system is sent out from a TMM (for example, tmm0) and the response packet is sent to a different TMM (for example, tmm2).

    For information about capturing internal TMM information with tcpdump, refer to K13637: Capturing internal TMM information with tcpdump.
