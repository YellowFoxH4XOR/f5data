Topic

The ARX system provides support for Windows-Management Access through the functions of the Shared Folders snap-in for the Microsoft Management Console (MMC).

Important: F5 does not officially support the functions of any other MMC snap-ins.

Note: For information about Windows-Management Access and MMC snap-ins, refer to the ARX CLI Storage-Management Guide.
