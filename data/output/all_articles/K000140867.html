Description

You may notice that the ASM device remains in a 'change pending' status, even immediately after syncing the configuration to the peer device.

Environment

  • Change pending 
  • Manual sync
  • Session Tracking

Cause

This is expected behavior. Enabling any of the 'Block All Thresholds' settings in the Session Tracking feature within a Manual Sync Group may cause the active device to remain in a constant 'Changes Pending' state.

Recommended Actions

You can either ignore this symptom or switch the device group sync type from Manual to Auto-sync.
