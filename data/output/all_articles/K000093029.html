K000093029: BIG-IQ VE does not support disk provisioning larger than 1.9 TB

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-12-13
Updated
2025-03-03
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 8.X.X, 8.3.X, 8.3.0, 8.2.X, 8.2.0, 8.1.X, 8.1.0, 8.0.X, 8.0.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000093029

Description

BIG-IQ VE does not support disk provisioning larger than 1.9 TB.

 

Environment

  • BIG-IQ DCD provisioned with more than 2 TB
  • BIG-IQ DCD not booting up

Cause

Provisioning system with more than 1.8 TB is not supported

Recommended Actions

Recreate new system with maximum of 1.9 TB of HD
