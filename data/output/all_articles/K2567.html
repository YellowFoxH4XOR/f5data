Topic

The 10/100 trunk group limits are as follows:
  • 8 links per trunk
  • 5 trunks
  • 2 Gigabit port trunks

Note: 10/100 links cannot be configured in the same trunk group with Gigabit links. F5 recommends that trunks contain 2, 4, or 8 links.
