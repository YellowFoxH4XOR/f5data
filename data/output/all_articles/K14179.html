Known Issue

A Client SSL profile with renegotiation may cause TMM to crash.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The 6900 or 8900 platform is installed with FIPS HSM.
  • The Client SSL profile is configured with FIPS security type.
  • Renegotiation is enabled on the affected Client SSL profile.
  • The client attempts to renegotiate the SSL session.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from a TMM restart. If the device is configured as a high-availability pair, the device may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • TMM generates a SIGSEGV core file in the /var/core directory.
  • The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic.
