Description

You observe the BIG-IP dtca.crt and dtdi.crt are expired for HA units as below screenshots show:

And per K82959634: Failover still works after device trust certificates are expired you expect that the config-sync will not work any more, but you finally find that both config-sync and failover are working well.

Environment

  • LTM
  • DSC
  • Device cert is 3rd party CA signed cert

Cause

If the BIG-IP device cert is 3rd party CA signed, then the device trust is built bases on the 3rd party CA signed cert.

If both the root CA cert and device cert are valid, then both config-sync and failover works well even though the dtca.crt and dtdi.crt are expired.

Recommended Actions

This is expected behavior, you can navigate to System >> Certificate Management : Device Certificate Management : Device Trust Certificates to see the certs of local machine, the peer member, and the root CA.
