Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may erroneously drop a diameter response message for a client request. This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The affected client request processing is temporarily suspended by an iRule command.
  • The system receives the diameter response message for the affected client request while it is in the temporary suspended state.

Impact

Missing diameter response messages may disrupt the diameter connection.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H486546 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High screen.
  • Diameter connections fail on the BIG-IP system.
  • A tcpdump capture of the affected traffic shows that the BIG-IP system fails to forward the diameter response message to the client.
