Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. While processing a moderate amount of traffic, BIG-IP platforms containing a Packet Velocity ASIC (PVA) chip show packet loss on the internal links. For example, with a moderate amount of bulk traffic on a BIG-IP 6800 platform, the internal links that go through the PVA (interfaces 4.1 and 4.2) show an approximate 5 percent packet loss, while interface 4.3 or 4.4 shows no dropped packets.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR64013 and CR64014 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.3 and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
