Known Issue

BIG-IP Edge Client may fail to establish per-app Virtual Private Network (VPN) connections.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • iOS BIG-IP Edge Client users attempt to establish a per-app VPN connection.
  • The access profile is configured with an iRules event agent.
  • The access policy does not include an assigned webtop.

Impact

Users are unable to establish per-app VPN access sessions.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • iOS BIG-IP Edge Client users attempting to establish an access session receive a TCP reset packet from the BIG-IP APM virtual server.
  • Users are unable to establish per-app VPN access session.

Resolution

Status

F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. Workaround

There is no workaround for this issue; however, you can mitigate the issue by adding a webtop to the access profile.

Note: For more information about configuring webtops, refer to the BIG-IP Access Policy Manager: Portal Access guide. For information about how to locate F5 product guides, refer to K12453464: Finding product documentation on AskF5.
