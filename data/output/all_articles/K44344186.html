Description

The following log message may be found in /var/log/onboard.log and/or /var/log/cloudlibs-install.log, indicating a failure from another script:

info: [pid: 10880] [lib/util.js] ERROR signaled from other script. Exiting

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • f5-cloud-libs scripts

Cause

A prior error drops a 'signal' in /tmp/f5-cloud-libs-signals but it's not getting cleared.
Any future runs of the script detects the 'signal' and results in the logged ERROR.

Recommended Actions

Manually clear the 'signal' by deleting the files in /tmp/f5-cloud-libs-signals/

None
