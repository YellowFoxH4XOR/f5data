Topic

Each F5 platform contains a lithium coin cell battery on the motherboard that is used to power non-volatile memory when the system is powered off. The battery is used to keep the real-time clock running and store the system BIOS settings. The lithium coin cell battery is the only battery on the motherboard. The coin cell batteries are expected to last 10 years, but often last much longer. These batteries are not rechargeable.

If the battery loses charge and the system is power cycled, the BIOS clock time becomes incorrect. Additionally, the system reports a CMOS checksum error and the prompts you to accept the default settings at system start up. If the lithium coin cell battery on your system has lost its charge, contact F5 Technical Support.
