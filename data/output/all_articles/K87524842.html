Known Issue

The tcpdump :p option may not work for certain types of flows when you use the option to capture traffic from multiple associated peers.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are using the tcpdump utility with the :p option.
  • The flows that the system is capturing have multiple peers.
  • You are trying to capture one of the following types of flows:
    • HTTP/2
    • SIP (Message Routing Framework)
    • SIP (Message Based Load Balancing)
    • Diameter (Message Routing Framework)
    • Generic Message
    • MQTT

Impact

You are unable to use the tcpdump :p option for diagnostic purposes. 

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The tcpdump utility is not capturing peer traffic.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 631657 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. Workaround

To work around this issue, you can make modifications to your tcpdump command. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

Instead of using the :p option, you can create a targeted filter to individually include the host addresses or other details specific to the peers.  For more information on performing such a targeted capture, refer to K11555: Gathering data in preparation for a traffic-impacting change to the BIG-IP system.
