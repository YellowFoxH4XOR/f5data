Description

Tenant blade can get stuck in "upgrade needed" status when newly inserted blade has older version installed and there is image pending on inactive tenant volume.

Environment

  • F5OS Chassis (e.g., F5-VEL-LTM-CX410-AC, F101)
  • F5OS version: 1.6.x, 1.7.x, 1.8.x
  • BIG-IP tenants versions: 15.1.x, 16.1.x, 17.1.x. 17.5
  • Multi-blade configuration, including addition of new hardware revision blades to existing partitions/tenants with previously installed version on a blade.

Cause

The status for inactive image will stay "waiting from product image" as long as there is no relevant image in /shared/images folder. This will also persist after reboot.

At this state, as long as one of the blade has on older image on it, it will not upgrade to the latest version of blades cluster and will stay in "upgrade needed" status.

NOTE: if the newly added blade doesn't have any version on it, it will be upgraded properly (will not be stuck in "upgrade needed" state), even though the inactive volume is waiting for product image.

Recommended Actions

There are 2 possible actions that can be taken:

  1. Upload the missing image. Once the image is uploaded the installation on inactive partition will finish and then the blade will be upgraded as well to the version of the rest of the blades in cluster (no failover needed).
  2. Perform mcpd forceload - this is not really recommended due to service disruption nature of this action.
