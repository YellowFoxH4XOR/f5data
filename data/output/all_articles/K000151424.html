Description

Licensing BIG-IP from BIG-IQ is Failing with error:

{"code":400,"message":"Device at 10.0.0.100 is not reachable. Please ensure that you've entered the correct IP address and restjavad services on the device are running.",

Pcaps contains RST in both directions to BIG-IP and BIG-IP

Environment

  • BIG-IQ CM or License Manager
  • Licensing BIG-IP
  • Firewall between devices

 

Cause

Firewall Rejects SSL communication after inspections and sending resets RST to both ends

Recommended Actions

Review Firewall inspection policy and permit traffic to enable BIG-IP BIG-IQ Communication

None
