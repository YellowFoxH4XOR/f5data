Description

Uneven CPU load on VELOS

Environment

  • VELOS

Cause

CPU load is getting imbalanced due to VELOS platform not distributing traffic evenly to the TMMs.
The VELOS platform's DAG hash algorithm does not perform well when the traffic favors even-numbered source ports.

Contact F5 Support and reference this article number.
