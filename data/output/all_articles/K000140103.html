Description

The L7 mirroring of diameter connections may occasionally fail after a failover event.

 

Environment

  • Diameter MRF
  • L7 Mirroring

 

Cause

ID1578637

 

Recommended Actions

Manually delete the affected connections. Reduce the likelihood of messages being dropped on the standby device by increasing the mirrored-message-sweeper-interval:

modify ltm message-routing diameter profile router mirrored-message-sweeper-interval 5000

 

Ensure that the HA connection does not have excessive latency or packet loss. The "Buffered" stat in the ha-mirror statistics table should be low. See K54622241 for details.

Ensure that the Maximum Pending Messages setting in the Diameter router profile is high enough to handle the message load.
