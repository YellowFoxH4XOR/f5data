K000139743: Kerberos SSO failing after minor upgrade

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-05-29
Updated
2026-02-25
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP APM: 16.1.4
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000139743

Description

After upgrading to 15.1.9 or higher on the 15x branch, or 16.1.4.1 or higher on 16x.

  • Kerberos SSO fails with errors saying it cant decrypt a ticket
  • Kerberos: can't decrypt S4U2Self ticket for user x@domain.com - Cryptosystem internal error (-1765328206)

Environment

Upgrade to 15.1.9 or higher, or 16.1.4.1 or higher.

Cause

F5 has upgraded krb5 library (1.14 to 1.18.2) in v15.1.9 and 16.1.4.1 as part of BZ752077-1. With it comes stricter security requirements.
As per this, "the arcfour-hmac for FIPS mode is not preferred for the version 1.17 and later. Hence the enctype was not accepted at the time of authentication." and leading to this error.

In captures we see TGS-REP has this ENC type eTYPE-ARCFOUR-HMAC-MD5 (23)

Also delegation accounts must be in SPN format on both the APM and the KDC, not UPN or sAMAccountName format.

ex. host/serviceaccountname.my.domain.com

Recommended Actions

In the AD Servers under : Active Directory users and computers --> delegation user (right-click) --> properties --> account --> account options.

  1. Make sure delegation account is in SPN format (service/accountname domain), not samaccountname format.
  2. Under account options 2 check-boxes are made to be check-marked which are :

a. This account supports kerberos AES 128 bit encryption
b. This account supports kerberos AES 256 bit encryption.

After making these changes please restart your AD server.
