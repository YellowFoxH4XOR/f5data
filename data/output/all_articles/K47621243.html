Policy Information

  • F5 hereby announces the End of Sale (EoS) plan for the BIG-IP Link Controller (LC) for all BIG-IP platforms.
  • BIG-IP LC officially reaches the End of Sale product lifecycle milestone effective January 1, 2023.
  • BIG-IP LC SKUs will be removed from the global price list on October 1, 2022. SKUs will be disabled for sale on January 1, 2023.

Technical Support

F5 maintains generous lifecycle policies that allow customers to enjoy many years of both support and new software releases. Customers whose platforms are designated EoL and who are currently under maintenance contracts will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date.
