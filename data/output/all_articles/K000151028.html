Description

Some options are missing under "Security >> Network Firewall", you might see only "IP intelligence" is available in the list.

Environment

ASM or Advanced WAF is in used

 

Cause

AFM is not provisioned in your system.

Active Rules, Policies, Rule Lists, Schedules are available when AFM is provisioned.

 

Recommended Actions

Provision the AFM, having the licensed AFM without provisioning will not show all of the options.
