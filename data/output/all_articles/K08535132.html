Description
The following daemons, dwbld, autodosd, autodiscd, do not support remote logging.

Environment

  • These daemons are all related to the AFM process and may not be running if AFM is not provisioned
  • Remote logging or High Speed Logging is configured.

Cause
Due to the logging mechanism used for these daemons they are not exported either via the High Speed Logging mechanism or Syslog.  The information contained in these logs is not considered useful to the administrator in the general daily maintenance of the BIG-IP and is largely intended to assist Product Engineering with troubleshooting.

Recommended Actions

No action is needed

Additional Information
Note that all of these are listed as available filters under the high speed logging configuration.  This is in error and is tracked under 

Bug ID 1162385: Unsupported daemon entries dwbld, autodosd, autodiscd listed in the HSL filter source list
