Known Issue

DHCPv6 relay pool members may not have their status properly updated by a monitor.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a DHCPv6 relay virtual server configured.
  • You are relaying IPv6 DHCP requests to multiple pool members.
  • The pool members are being monitored and the monitor changes the initial state of the pool member.

Impact

DHCP requests may fail to receive a response.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • A pool member that is initially marked up, but subsequently marked down, erroneously continues to receive traffic.
  • A pool member that is initially marked down, but subsequently marked up, erroneously receives no traffic.
