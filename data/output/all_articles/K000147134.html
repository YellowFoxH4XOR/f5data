K000147134: How do I get a new link to reset my password?

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-11-08
Updated
2024-11-08
Applies to
  • F5 Distributed Cloud Services
  • F5 Distributed Cloud
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000147134

Description

  • You created an account/were added to an account in F5® Distributed Cloud Console
  • The link to set your password may have expired
  • You don't remember your password
  • You did not receive any welcome email

Environment

  • User Management
  • F5® Distributed Cloud Console  

Cause

  • Forgot password

Answer/Recommended Actions

To reset your password

1. Go to the F5® Distributed Cloud Services website
2. Click on Login button on the right side of the top navigation bar

 

For free or Individual accounts

3. Click on the For Free or Individual accounts square to expand it 
4. Click on Forgot Password?
5. Enter your email in the textbox and click Submit

 

For Team and Organization accounts

3. Click on the I'm a member of a team account square to expand it and enter your tenant/account name in the textbox

4. Click Next
5. Click on Forgot Password?
6. Enter your email in the textbox and click Submit

 

NOTE: It's important to follow the process in the correct setting (Free and Individual/ Team and Organization) since an email can be connected to more than one account.
