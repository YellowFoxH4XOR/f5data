In versions of BIG-IP prior to 9.0, the SNMP port is open by default. In BIG-IP 9.1.2 and 9.2 and beyond, the SNMP port is open by default.

To open the SNMP port for BIG-IP 9.0 through 9.1.1, perform the following procedure:

  1. From the main tab of the Configuration utility, click Network.
  2. Click Self IPs.
  3. Click the Self IP address you will use for SNMP access.
  4. Click Port Lockdown.
  5. Click Allow Custom.
  6. Click the UDP option.
  7. Type the SNMP port number (161 is standard) in the text field.
  8. Click Add.
  9. Click Update.
