Source

Topic

FirePass does not support use of IPv6. F5 Product Development is considering adding support for IPv6 in future versions but a specific target release has not been identified. If you have an immediate need for IPv6 support, contact your F5 Sales Representative to discuss possible options.

F5 Product Development is tracking this Request for Enhancement as ID 266753. Since IPv6 is not supported, please do not contact F5 Support regarding IPv6 issues.
