K000149062: What is the maximum number of Service Policies?

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-12-19
Updated
2024-12-20
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000149062

Description

  • How many Service Policies can I use in Active Service Policies?
  • How many Service Policies can I attach to Load Balancer?

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud (XC) Console
  • XC Load Balancer
  • Service Policy

Answer/Recommended Actions

  • Service Policies > Apply Namespace Service Policies - Maximum of 32 Service Policies
    • Namespace Service policies are configured in Manage > Service Policies > Active Service Policies

 

  • Service Policies > Apply Specified Service Policies - Maximum of 16 Service Policies
    • Specified Service Policies are configured directly in Load Balancer Settings

Additional Information

  • Common Errors
  • Specified Service Policies limit
  • Active Service Policies limit
