K000136092: logger command does not generate logs in /var/log/ltm file

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-09-05
Updated
2023-09-06
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 15.1.5
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000136092

Description

You find a logger -p command (as per K11127) does not write logs locally to /var/log/ltm. Non-TMM daemons stopped logging to log files, and many files including /var/log/audit are very small. The /var/log/ltm only has logs from TMM.

You may find logs similar to below in /var/log/daemon.log.

Apr 29 03:05:01 bigip.local alert auditd[1845]: Audit daemon is low on disk space for logging
Apr 29 03:05:15 bigip.local alert auditd[1845]: Audit daemon has no space left on logging partition
Apr 29 03:05:15 bigip.local alert auditd[1845]: Audit daemon is suspending logging due to no space left on logging partition.

Environment

  • Disk partition /var is filling up.
  • Restarting syslog-ng daemon does not make any difference.

Cause

Due to low free space in /var partition, systemd-journald.service may stop and audit daemon suspends logging to disk. Daemons that depend on systemd-journald are also affected.

Recommended Actions

Free /var partion as much as possible and restart systemd-journald.service to resume the logging.
Impact of workaround: Logging via systemd-journald will not be available while systemd-journald.service is restarting.

systemctl restart systemd-journald.service
