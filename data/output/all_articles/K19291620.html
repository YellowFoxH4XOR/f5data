Description

You can use this article to force the hostname in the browser to include www.

  • For instance if you have a website at https://mywebsite.com and the site also exists at https://www.mywebsite.com you can use a local traffic policy to force all the traffic through https://www.mywebsite.com

Environment

This article will apply to all BIG-IP instances on version 12.1 and higher

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

  1. The first thing you will need to do is create a new Local Traffic Policy by going to the following:

    Local Traffic  ››  Policies
     
  2. Then click Create in the top right to create a new policy
     
  3. You will need to give the Policy a Name. Something such as: force_www_mywebsite.com would be sufficient. The description is optional. The Strategy and Type can stay set to the default values: Execute First Matching Rule and Traffic Policy. 
     
  4. Click Create Policy
     
  5. After that you will see a Rules section that shows up. Please click Create to the right of Rules.
     
  6. You will need to name the rule. An example would be add_www
     
  7. Under the section Match all of the following conditions: you will see a + symbol on the right hand side. Please click that.
     
  8. Now we are defining what traffic is going to match our rule. Here you are going to define the hostname of your site without the www. In our example it would look like this:

    HTTP Host  >  host  >  is  > any of  > mywebsite.com

    NOTE: You need to type your hostname into the lower section without www and then hit add and it will bring it up.
     
  9. After you have defined what traffic to look for you need to then define what is going to happen to the traffic. Under the section Do the following when the traffic is matched: click the + symbol on the right hand side
     
  10. The way the traffic will be handled in this instance is the traffic will be redirected to http://www.mywebsite.com or https://www.mywebsite.com pending on if you want the redirect to be secure or not. In our example we are going to force it to the secure site and it will be defined like this:

    Redirect  >  to location  >  tcl:https://www.mywebsite.com[HTTP::uri]

    NOTE: We are adding "tcl:" to the beginning of the location so that we can add the existing URI that was originally requested from the client. This allows the BIG-IP to insert the originally requested URI after the hostname
     
  11. Then click Save at the bottom to save your rule. 
     
  12. You will then be brought back out to the Policy page where you can see the rule or rules you have defined. Next to the Save Draft there is a down arrow. Click the down arrow and select Save and Publish Policy.
     
  13. Once you have published your policy you will then need to apply that to the virtual server in question. Once you have navigated to the virtual server you will see a Resources tab at the top. Please click that.
     
  14. Then click Manage to the right of Policies and move your new policy to the Enabled side and click Finished. Now the site should be forced to use www in it's hostname. You can test this by opening a new Private Browsing Window or Incognito Window and navigating to the site.
