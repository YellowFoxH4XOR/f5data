K000092895: Licensing Error: Error 51078, Incompatible Products and Add-ons - Keys are not valid for this BIG-IQ platform : XXXXXXX-YYYYYYY(BIG-IP) When reactivating BIG-IP FCP license via the BIG-IP command line

Description

You want to update the service check date on your BIG-IP.
Your BIG-IP device is licensed by a BIG-IQ FCP license.
You log into the BIG-IP command line and generate the dossier, but when you attempt to activate in the license portal you get the following error
Licensing Error: Error 51078, Incompatible Products and Add-ons - Keys are not valid for this BIG-IQ platform : XXXXXXX-YYYYYYY(BIG-IP)

Environment

  • Reactivate license (most likely due to upgrade)
  • FCP
  • BIG-IQ managed BIG-IP

Cause

Due to the way FCP licenses work, you need to use the BIG-IQ to reactivate the licenses on the BIG-IP.

 Recommended Actions

This is a two step process starting with updating the load / catalog service check date, then refreshing the license on the BIG-IP(s)
Please use the procedure in the following article:Upgrading FCP BIG-IP VE through BIG-IQ License manager
