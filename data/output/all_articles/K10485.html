K10485: The BIG-IP WebAccelerator fails to insert a Connection: Keep-Alive header in proxied responses

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP WebAccelerator only inserts the Connection: Keep-Alive header in responses served from the cache, and fails to insert Connection: Keep-Alive headers in proxied responses. As a result, the BIG-IP WebAccelerator generates a greater number of TCP connections for certain client connections.

In version 9.x, the BIG-IP WebAccelerator attempts to reduce the number of TCP connections open to the server by adding a Connection: Keep-Alive header to all responses.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR124830.
