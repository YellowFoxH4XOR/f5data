K000161012: How to Delete Your MyF5 User Account

Description

If you would like to request a deletion of your MyF5 account and a removal of associated personal data from F5 systems, you can do so by submitting a request via F5 Privacy Web Form.

Environment

  • MyF5 user account deletion

Cause

None

Answer/Recommended Actions

  1. Navigate to the F5 Privacy Web Form.
  2. Complete and submit the form with the required information regarding your deletion request.
  3. Your request will be automatically forwarded to the F5 IT team responsible for processing data removal.
  4. Confirm your request using the email sent to you by F5 after submission. 
                                     
                                             Figure: Request Confirmation

  5. F5 IT team will process your request and remove your personal data from F5 records accordingly.
