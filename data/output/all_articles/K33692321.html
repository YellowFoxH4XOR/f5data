Known Issue

Renewing the NT Lan Manager (NTLM) Machine Account password may not update the serviceId for NTLM authentication.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You renew the NTLM Machine Account credentials on your BIG-IP APM system.

The symptoms of this issue might not occur right after you renew the account because the connection can be reused.

Impact

Users cannot access the configured policy resources.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • You cannot access the configured policy resources.
  • In the /var/log/ltm file, you observe error messages similar to the following example:

    err nlad[12384]: 01620000:3: <0x566d4b90> nlclnt[71601c70a] init: Error [0xc000006d,NT_STATUS_LOGON_FAILURE] connecting to DC.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can restart the eca daemon. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP command line.
  2. Type the following command:

    bigstart restart eca
