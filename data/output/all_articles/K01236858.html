Product Alternatives

Reducing online fraud against web and mobile applications is a challenge. Despite having numerous fraud tools, most enterprises still lose millions to fraud annually. Worse, fear of fraud also causes enterprises to impose friction on legitimate users.

F5 is committed to enhancing customers’ anti-fraud capabilities and has a modern, next generation solution—F5 Distributed Cloud Account Protection. Distributed Cloud Account Protection takes a different and far more effective approach to detecting and preventing fraud than WebSafe and MobileSafe did. Distributed Cloud Account Protection uses machine learning as a more effective way to understand the evolving nature of fraud attacks and provides a superior anti-fraud outcome compared to other fraud solutions.

Distributed Cloud Account Protection delivers compelling, durable, and measurable improvements in fraud detection. More information on Distributed Cloud Account Protection can be found at:

https://www.f5.com/products/security/account-protection
