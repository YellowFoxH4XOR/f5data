Known Issue

Connections may stop passing traffic after a route update.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a pool on a non-translating virtual server.
  • A routing update occurs.

Impact

Connections may fail after routing updates, although new connections are not affected.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When a pool is used with a non-translating virtual server, routing updates lead to an incorrect lookup of the nexthop for the connection.

Workaround

To work around this issue, use a route to direct traffic to the ultimate destination rather than using a pool to indicate the nexthop.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
