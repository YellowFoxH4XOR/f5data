Topic

The FirePass Controller and the BIG-IP SAM device currently do not support Windows Embedded Compact (WinCE, or Windows CE) version 6.0. WinCE is a core OS component used for building various embedded systems like robots, industrial controls, and GPS navigation systems. Custom versions of WinCE 6.0 may be supported. To check whether your custom build is supported, contact F5 Networks Technical Support, and provide the SDK and emulator image for your specific build.
