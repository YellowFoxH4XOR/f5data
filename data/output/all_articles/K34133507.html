Topic

This article covers the compatibility of BIG-IQ Centralized Management 8.0.0 and later with BIG-IP releases.

Description

BIG-IQ products support several modules and provide central management for the BIG-IP system.

For the compatibility overview of BIG-IQ with BIG-IP releases, based on version, point release, and hotfix level, refer to the following sections. If a version of BIG-IP software is listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article, but does not appear in the table as a minimum supported version, maximum supported version, or a version in between, then it is not supported. For example, BIG-IP 17.x versions are not supported by BIG-IQ 8.1.0 and earlier.

  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management 8.4.2
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management 8.4.1
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management 8.4.0
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management 8.3.0
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management 8.2.0
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management 8.1.0
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management 8.0.0

You can review the significant feature changes for the respective version by referring to the New features and Behavior changes sections of the BIG-IQ Centralized Management release notes.

Note: For information about how to locate F5 product manuals, refer to K98133564: Tips for searching AskF5 and finding product documentation.
