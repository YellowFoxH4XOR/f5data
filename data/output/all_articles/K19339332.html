Source

Description
The BIG-IP GTM local MAC address is flapping on the local switch preventing the BIG-IP GTM from resolving DNS queries.

Environment
  • BIG-IP GTM
  • BIG-IP 11.6.5
  • Multiple Self-IP addresses in the same vlan
  • Standalone Device
Cause
The condition was triggered when one of the two self-ip addresses was converted to a floating self-ip address.  The traffic group was previously configured with a MAC masquerade address.

Recommended Actions
Remove MAC masquerade from traffic-group.

Additional Information
None
