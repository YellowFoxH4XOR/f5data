K000149419: Does F5 send notifications for all maintenances or service interruptions?

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-01-23
Updated
2025-01-23
Applies to
  • F5 Distributed Cloud Services
  • F5 Distributed Cloud Audit Logs & Alerts
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000149419

Description

  • Sometimes I get an alert triggered with no reason and I want to know why the alert was triggered.
  • This alert is similar to the ones I receive when  F5® announces a Maintenance time.

Environment

  • Alerts

Cause

There was a backend service restart, after it restarts there will be a period of time the internal metric is showing as empty. 

Answer/ Recommended Actions

No, F5® does not always provide notifications for every maintenance activity. The decision to notify depends on factors such as the type of work, its potential impact on our services, and the overall scope of the changes.

Recommended Actions

To be 100% sure you can raise a ticket asking if this was a false positive or if there was a service restart in the backend that had to be done urgently. In order to receive the answer as fast as possible please do not forget to add the following information to the ticket raised:

  • Name of the alert triggered, for example: "CertifiedHardware in CustomerEdgenode has been changed."
  • Time when this alert was triggered
  • Your time zone
