Topic

Summary

Clients may need to re-mount the volume after a snapshot reconstitution.

Description

Re-associating filer snapshots with ARX snapshots is called snapshot reconstitution. There may be situations where back-end snapshots become disassociated with their ARX snapshots. Snapshot management is performed on a share by share basis. In order to browse all managed snapshots, you must complete actions on all of the shares in the volume.

Some clients may need to unmount and re-mount the volume after the snapshot reconstitution is complete in order to view all available files. This behavior is due to a limitation on how the client is requesting the volume data; this behavior is not an issue with the ARX system.

Recommendations

When reconstituting files, you should consider the following factor:

  • You may use the ARX drop cifs-service command to force clients to unmount and re-mount after performing snapshot reconstitution. For more information, refer to the ARX CLI Reference Guide.
