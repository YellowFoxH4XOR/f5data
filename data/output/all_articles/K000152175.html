K000152175: BIG-IQ reports contain only top 10 items

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-06-26
Updated
2025-09-30
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000152175

Description

When generating BIG-IQ reports only 10 top items are pulled.

Environment

BIG-IQ 

 

Cause

ID598494 Only 10 dimension values are exported even when from 11 through 100 of them are represented in dimension section in Inspector page GUI 

 

Recommended Actions

The workaround is to either use export to CSV or use this TMOS option:

https://clouddocs.f5.com/cli/tmsh-reference/v16/modules/analytics/analytics_pool-traffic_report.html

 

Additional Information

None.
