K7639: A Java-based Legacy Host session cannot be resumed after switching screens

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When you launch a Java-based Legacy Host Favorite and then view an alternate page, such as the Webtop, you cannot resume your original Legacy Host session. When you attempt to resume the Legacy Host session, you may receive a blank screen or an error message that appears similar to the following example:

java.lang.NumberFormatException: For input string: ""
at java.lang.NumberFormatException.forInputString(Unknown Source)
at java.lang.Integer.parseInt(Unknown Source)
at java.lang.Integer.parseInt(Unknown Source) at CliJava.init(CliJava.java:151)
at sun.applet.AppletPanel.run(Unknown Source)
at java.lang.Thread.run(Unknown Source)

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR80564 and it was fixed in FirePass version 6.0.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the FirePass release notes.
