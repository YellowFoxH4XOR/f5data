Known Issue

The OSPF6 daemon (ospf6d) may stop responding when processing specific link-state advertisements (LSAs).

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system uses OSPFv3.
  • Another system initiates a graceful restart.

Impact

OSPFv3 routing stops while the daemon restarts and the protocol re-converges.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When OSPFv3 is configured, and another router in the network performs a graceful restart, the ospf6d process stops responding.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can disable graceful restart on other network systems. This prevents the interruption of ospf6d process responses on the BIG-IP system.

Impact of workaround: Disabling graceful restart can cause a routing interruption on a system that restarts.
