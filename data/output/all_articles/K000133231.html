K000133231: Combining Behavioral DOS and TPS/Stress Based DOS

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-03-28
Updated
2023-03-28
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP ASM: 17.0.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000133231

Description

When using Advanced WAF Layer 7 DOS protection profiles it is not recommended to combine Behavioral detection with TPS or Stress-Based protection in most cases. Behavioral DOS is most effective when presented with unfiltered samples of the current traffic on the virtual server. When TPS or Stress-Based protection engages mitigation prior to Behavioral DOS detecting a threat the lack of samples from potential bad actors may produce unexpected results, such as incorrect detection of malicious behavior.


Environment

BIGIP Advanced WAF

DOS Profile with Application DOS enabled

TPS or Stress-Based protection enabled

Behavioral DOS protection enabled


Cause

Due to sampling required to accurately fingerprint malicious behavior, the ADM daemon responsible for Behavioral DOS will not see an accurate cross section of the traffic if other DOS mitigation measures are employed.

Recommended Actions

Use either a combination of TPS/Stress-based detection or Behavioral DOS detection. If combining the two is desired, ensure TPS/Stress-based thresholds are set loose enough for your application traffic needs that Behavioral DOS will be the primary mitigation and detection measure.
