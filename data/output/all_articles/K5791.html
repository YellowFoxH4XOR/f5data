In BIG-IP 9.1.x, all Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) handshakes are accelerated in hardware on the BIG-IP 1500, 3400, 6400, 6800, and 8400 platforms.

The following ciphers are fully accelerated on the BIG-IP 1500, 3400, 6400, 6800, and 8400 platforms:

Note: Crypto operations are offloaded by the hardware when the ciphers are fully accelerated.

  • RC4-MD5
  • RC4-SHA
  • AES128-SHA
  • AES256-SHA
  • DES-CBC3-SHA
  • DES-CBC-SHA
