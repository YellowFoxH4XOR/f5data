The user-configurable Admin E-Mail Address field in FirePass enables you to specify the e-mail address(es) of your FirePass administrator(s). Navigate to Device Management: Configuration: Admin E-Mail to specify the email address(es) you would like to receive alert messages. This field has no limit on the number of e-mails addresses that can be configured; however, F5 Networks recommends configuring less than 30 addresses in this field. E-mail addresses should be separated with a comma ( , ) only.

If multiple parties are required to be listed as FirePass administrators to receive admin e-mail alerts from FirePass, F5 recommends using a mail distribution list on your mail server, which is referenced using a common e-mail address. The mail distribution list enables efficient management of notifications and reduces the number of individual addresses that need to be configured and managed on your FirePass Controller.
