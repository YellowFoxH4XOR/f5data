Recommended Actions

If you cannot find a resolution an issue using the self-help resources, you can engage F5 support.

F5 Support (open a Service Request)
F5 Support is designed to provide support for specific break-fix issues for customers with active support contracts. F5 Support does not perform the installation of new software. However, F5 Support can assist you if you encounter an issue performing an upgrade or update. For more information about F5 scope of support, refer to Support Policies.

F5 Support resources are available 24 hours a day, seven days a week and are distributed around the world in multiple support centers. Live support is provided by our professional network support engineers. Hours of availability may vary depending on the service contract with F5.


Proactive Support Case
Customers with valid Standard or Premium Support contracts can open a proactive service request with F5 Support prior to any software upgrade, hardware migration, data center relocation, or any other activity that requires a dedicated maintenance window.  For information, refer to K16022: Opening a proactive service request with F5 Support

When opening a proactive service request with F5 Support, you should note a few important points about the proactive service request process:

  • Opening a proactive service request does not guarantee that a network support engineer (NSE) will be available to work with you during the maintenance window or help you complete maintenance tasks. The service request is in place in case you require F5 Support assistance due to a failure that occurs during the maintenance window.
  • F5 does not actively monitor proactive service requests for email or F5 Web Support updates. If you need F5 Support assistance, you must call F5 Support using one of the F5 Support phone numbers and mention the proactive service request number.
  • F5 treats requests for assistance on proactive service requests as "high site at risk" or "urgent site down" and prioritizes such requests along with other new high and urgent service requests received at that time.
  • F5 recommends that you create your proactive service request at least two weeks in advance, if possible.
  • F5 recommends that you use Proactive in the title, if possible, to help identify the new proactive service request.
  • F5 provides assistance for Standard Support contracts within Standard Support hours only. For more information, see Standard and Premium Support for contract details.

Assisted services
If you do not feel confident in performing the software installation yourself, or want additional "hands on" assistance with the preparation for a software upgrade or update, F5 offers a fee based Product Upgrade and Migration Services.  An F5 consultant will help you define the most efficient migration process and ensure that it is aligned with your business goals. Your consultant will also manage the deployment and educate your team on how best to support the right solution for your unique environment. For additional information about F5 Professional Services, refer to Professional Services That Fit Your Needs.

Additional Information

Upgrade vs. Update
Installing a new version of F5 software falls into the following two categories:

  • Upgrade: A version that introduces new features, hardware support, and specific performance improvements.
  • Update: A version that introduces product defect and security fixes.

F5 makes this distinction because upgrades and updates require different considerations before you install.  For more information, refer to the Introduction chapter of the BIG-IP update and upgrade guide.
