Description

Can NGINX Amplify retention policy be extended and/or can the metrics be exported to a third party collector?


Environment

NGINX Amplfy

Cause

NGINX Amplify Design

Recommended Actions

The retention policy cannot be extended and metrics cannot be exported.

Additional Information

The default retention policy is 2 weeks
