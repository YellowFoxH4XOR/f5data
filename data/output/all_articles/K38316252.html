Known Issue

Tagged Virtual Local Area Network (VLAN) interfaces fail to pass traffic.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your deployment uses BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE).
  • The hypervisor uses SR-IOV (Single root I/O virtualization) to interact directly with underlying Network Interface Card (NIC) adapters.
  • You configure a BIG-IP VLAN for the interface as a tagged VLAN.
  • You initiate a connection to or from BIG-IP VE on the tagged VLAN.

Impact

The connection fails to establish.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When viewing network-level communication, connections that you initiate from BIG-IP VE are sent appropriately tagged to the hypervisor. The hypervisor does not receive response traffic.

Resolution

Status

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can configure VLAN tagging at the hypervisor.

Impact of workaround:  Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
