Description

There are multple EPSEC files in the folder /config/filestore/files_d/Common_d/epsec_package_d/, some of them are duplicated, some of them are not used.

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM

Cause

EPSEC packages may be re-installed after upgrade creating duplicates in the filestore.

Old EPSEC packages are not removed automatically and are saved in the UCS.

 Recommended Actions

Follow the article K69803943: UCS backup file increasing in size, remove the unexpected iso files.

None.
