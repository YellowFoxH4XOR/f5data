Known Issue

The Configuration utility may calculate and display an incorrect high availability (HA) group score.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • A particular HA group object (for example, a Pool) has no available members, and the Minimum Member Count option is not used (by default).

Impact

You can safely ignore this issue; the issue is limited to what the Configuration utility calculates and displays to the user.

Note: Internally, the BIG-IP system uses the correct HA group score to determine the role of the unit. F5 cautions you to not be misled by the issue and to not take unnecessary corrective action because of it.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The Configuration utility may calculate and display an HA group score of 0, while the correct HA group score is greater than 0.
