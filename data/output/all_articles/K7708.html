Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Hotfix installations may fail on BIG-IP legacy switch platforms due to space limitations on the compact flash drive.

This issue applies to the following BIG-IP legacy switch platforms:

  • BIG-IP 1000 (D39)
  • BIG-IP 2400 (D44)
  • BIG-IP 5100 and 5110 (D51)

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR59856 and it was fixed in 9.3.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, GTM, ASM, or Link Controller release notes.
