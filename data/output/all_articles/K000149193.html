K000149193: Adding a self IP address fails on F5OS tenants with "Cannot get device index for <VLAN> in rd0 - ioctl failed: No such device"

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-01-21
Updated
2025-01-21
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.0.0, 17.1.0, 17.0.X, 17.X.X, 17.1.X, 17.1.2, 17.1.1
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000149193

Description

You attempt to add a self IP address to a VLAN in your BIG-IP tenant and see the following error message:

Cannot get device index for <VLAN> in rd0 - ioctl failed: No such device

Environment

  • r2000 and r4000 appliances
  • F5OS (all versions)
  • BIG-IP tenant (affected versions)

Cause

This issue is described in ID1783649 and is triggered by the conditions described in ID1596313.

Recommended Actions

To resolve this issue you must reboot the tenant or restart mcpd by issuing:

bigstart restart mcpd

To avoid a recurrence until ID1596313 is resolved, please avoid any of the known triggers such as:

  • Renaming a LAG in use by a tenant on the F5OS host.
  • Removing and re-adding all members from a LAG currently used by a running tenant on the F5OS host.
