Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may drop encapsulated traffic from a tunnel. This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP system is configured with either an IP tunnel or an APM Network Access tunnel
  • A listener object (such as a virtual server or self IP) is configured to process the encapsulated traffic from the tunnel
  • The affected listener object is associated with a traffic group
  • The traffic group is configured with a MAC Masquerade Address

Impact

The BIG-IP system fails to process encapsulated traffic from an established IP tunnel or APM Network Access tunnel.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The client is able to establish the tunnel to the BIG-IP system, however the BIG-IP system does not respond.

Workaround

There is no work around for this issue. However, you may be able to mitigate this issue by removing the MAC Masquerade Address from the affected traffic group. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Depending on your environment, removing the MAC Masquerade Address from a traffic group may temporarily disrupt traffic to other listener objects in the same traffic group. F5 recommends you make the change during a maintenance window.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP Configuration utility.
  2. Depending on your version, click Device Management (11.3.0) or Network (11.2.0 - 11.2.1).
  3. Click Traffic Groups.
  4. Click the name of the affected traffic group.
  5. Remove the MAC address from the MAC Masquerade Address field.
  6. Click Update.
