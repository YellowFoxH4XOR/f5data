Known Issue

BIG-IP AAM deduplication cache synchronization fails.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • BIG-IP AAM is provisioned on BIG-IP platforms with varying Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) thread counts.
  • Symmetric deduplication using SDDv2 is configured between the dissimilar BIG-IP platforms.

Symmetric data deduplication reduces the amount of bandwidth consumed across a WAN link for repeated data transfers.

Impact

The symmetric data deduplication cache is not synchronized between the BIG-IP AAM endpoints.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H465126 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High screen.
  • You notice error messages similar to the following example in the /var/log/ltm file:

    iSession: internal error: isession_rx_isession_msg:5561: Error parsing isession msg: ERR_NOT_FOUND
    iSession: internal error: isession_tdr2_rx_ctrl_dispatch:737: dispatching received SDDv2 control message: ERR_NOT_FOUND
    iSession: internal error: isession_dedup_rx_ctrl_conn:1672: processing received isession control message: ERR_NOT_FOUND
  • You observe a negative acknowledgment of deduplication hits due to a discrepancy between the BIG-IP AAM endpoint caches.

Workaround

You may be able to mitigate the issue by manually forcing a cache refresh for the remote endpoint, which resets the deduplication cache. However, this may terminate active iSession tunnels and will reduce the deduplication hit rate until the cache is repopulated.
