K000140571: User’s cannot send iMessage messages when traffic passes through an SSL Orchestrator

Description

iMessage does not work when routed through SSLO


Environment

BIG-IP SSLO

Cause

SSLO does not support iMessage


iMessage protocol bulk messaging happens over a continuously open channel on TCP 5223, and that data is point-to-point encrypted. This means that the BIG-IP cannot intercept and decrypt this data. If and only when the channel drops, a TCP 443 connection is established to restart the channel and exchange P2P keys. That 443 connection to *.courier.apple.com is mutually authenticated and uses post Quantum Cryptography. The BIG-IP does not support Post Quantum Cryptography.


Recommended Actions

Do not use iMessage with SSLO
