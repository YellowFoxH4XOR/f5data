Description

Error Message: error: 51075 - Invalid Dossier - Unable to decrypt

Environment

Relevant environmental factors specific to the topic

  • Licensing
  • BIG-IP
  • GUI

Cause

  • The inbound dossier cannot be decrypted by the F5 Licensing server.
  • The dossier cannot be decrypted using the F5 private key. 
  • Either the dossier was produced using a non-F5 public key, or the dossier is corrupt.

Recommended Actions

  • Double check your dossier.
  • Confirm there is no white space or carriage return
  • Re-generate the dossier
