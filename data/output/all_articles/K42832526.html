Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may fail to process traffic after the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) process restarts.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is configured to use external hardware security module (HSM) for FIPS crypto services.
  • The TMM process is restarted on the affected BIG-IP system.

Impact

The BIG-IP system fails to process traffic that relies on external HSM for crypto services and does not recover.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • After restarting the TMM process, your BIG-IP system is unable to process traffic and connections fail on the virtual server.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can reestablish the connections by restarting the pkcs11d service and the TMM process. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Restarting TMM interrupts traffic processing. You should perform this action only during a maintenance window. If the system is configured as part of a high availability (HA) pair, the device may fail over.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP command line.
  2. To restart the pkcs11d service and TMM process, type the following command:

    For a standard BIG-IP appliance:

    bigstart restart pkcs11d tmm

    For a VIPRION system:

    clsh bigstart restart pkcs11d tmm
