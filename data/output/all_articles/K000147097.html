Description

  • Where can I see the health status of my service?
  • Where can I find the DNS Load Balancer health indicators?

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud DNS Load Balancer
  • F5 Distributed Cloud Console

Answer/Recommended Actions

  • You can find it on the DNS Load Balancers Overview dashboard
  • It provides a quick and easy way to see the overall health of your service
  • It gives you the ability to quickly find problem areas so they can be fixed with minimal downtime
  • The portal shows a health status for your services

  • You can quickly see if you have any service problems and if so quickly scroll down the list of services to see which ones are having a problem. 

 

Different health states and their description

StateDescription
HealthyAll individual load-balancing pools/services are healthy
DegradedOne or more pools/services are degraded
UnhealthyAll pools/services health check are failing
N/AAll services are currently inactive
