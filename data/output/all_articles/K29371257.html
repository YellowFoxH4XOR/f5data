Environment

  • Devices configured in HA
  • PEM may or may not be provisioned
  • AFM provisioned

Cause

The daily automatic DPI signature update increments the commit ID of the device doing the update which results in a perceived change between the peers regardless of whether the update succeeds or not.

Recommended Actions

  1. If PEM is not provisioned or in use you can safely disable the daily automatic signature update using the following command:
    # tmsh modify ltm classification signature-update-schedule auto-update-disabled
