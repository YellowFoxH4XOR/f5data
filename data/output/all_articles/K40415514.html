Known Issue

The qkview utility may stall during denial-of-service (DoS) data collection.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have licensed and provisioned BIG-IP ASM on the BIG-IP system.
  • You have configured a virtual server with a DoS profile.

Impact

The qkview utility fails to complete.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • You are unable to generate a qkview snapshot file on the BIG-IP ASM system.
  • The qkview utility stalls when running the asm_tables_dumps.pl process.
