Topic

F5 Product Development is now tracking product issues using the Bugzilla tracking tool, which is a single web-based tracking system for all products.

As a result of the change, AskF5 articles documenting F5 product issues will designate the tracking number as follows, where <XXXXXX> is the Bugzilla tracking number associated with the issue:

ID XXXXXX

In cases where a CR was initially created to track a product issue and the issue was moved to the Bugzilla tracking tool, you may see the following designation, where ID <XXXXXX> is the Bugzilla tracking number associated with the issue, and CRXXXXXX is the legacy tracking number associated with the issue:

ID XXXXXX (formerly CRXXXXXX)
