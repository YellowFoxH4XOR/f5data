Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may generate ICMPv6 Packet Too Big messages that contain the link-local address (prefix fe80::/10) as the source address.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The BIG-IP system generates an ICMPv6 Packet Too Big message in response to receiving a packet from an upstream device that exceeds the MTU of a downstream device.

When performing path MTU caching for IPv6 connections, ICMPv6 Packet Too Big messages generated by the BIG-IP system will use the link-local address (prefix fe80::/10) of the egress interface, rather than the source IP address of the connection flow. The BIG-IP link-local address will not be routable to the upstream device.

Impact

IPv6 connections may stop responding or fail.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H421074 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High screen.
  • IPv6 connections may stop responding or fail.
  • A tcpdump will show ICMPv6 Packet Too Big messages generated by the BIG-IP system contain the link-local address (prefix fe80::/10) as the source address.
