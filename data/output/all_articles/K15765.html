Known Issue

Hardware compression may cause excessive memory consumption and a Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) restart.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You use hardware compression with one of the following platforms:
    • 2000s/2200s
    • 4000s/4200v
  • Compressed data objects vary significantly in size.

Impact

Service may be interrupted while the TMM restarts.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H486521 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • TMM memory statistics continually increase.
  • Your system experiences a service interruption.
  • Your system fails over.
  • A TMM core file logs to the /shared/core directory.
