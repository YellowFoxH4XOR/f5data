https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000138910

Description

Upgrade BIG-IP from BIG-IQ failed with the following error:

Couldn't list volumes on 172.16.137.244: Service not available, try again or check device health (HTTP status 500)

Environment

  • Upgrade
  • BIG-IQ
  • Disk volume

Cause

It could be due to restjavad unresponsive

Recommended Actions

1) Delete unuse software volume

K000135806: How to delete the volume after software upgrade failed - with error 'failed to delete volumeset'

2) Increase Management Provisioning to 500

K26427018: Overview of Management provisioning

3) Restart restjavad with the following command in CLI bash:

bigstart restart restjavad

 

Additional Information

K33265170: Deleting a boot location volume to free up disk space
