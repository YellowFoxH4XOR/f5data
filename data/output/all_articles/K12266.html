Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. As of October 1, 2010, the BIG-IP ASM web-scraping detection engine may incorrectly classify the Bing search engine as a web-scraping bot.

In order to prevent known search engine crawlers from being incorrectly identified as web-scraping bots, the BIG-IP ASM maintains a list of User-Agent data to exempt from web-scraping violations. However, Bing has changed its User-Agent data, which can cause the BIG-IP to classify it as web-scraping traffic.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as ID 343292 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 10.2.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM release notes.

In addition, this issue has been fixed in cumulative hotfix Hotfix-BIGIP-10.2.1-473.0-HF1, which has been issued for BIG-IP 10.2.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the cumulative hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
