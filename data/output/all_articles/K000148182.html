What Happened?

Can we configure domain names with special characters as domain names having special characters in it cannot be entered in the console?

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud Console

Resolution/Answer

  • You need to first convert the domain name and you can use a Punycode converter site such as https://www.punycoder.com/
     
    e.g. www.santé.fr will be entered as www.xn--sant-epa.fr. in the XC console.
