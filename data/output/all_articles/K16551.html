Known Issue

BIG-IP high availability (HA) systems may experience performance and stability issues for mirrored connections. The initial TCP window size for mirrored connections is 64 KB. In some cases, the smaller window size may lead to performance and stability issues for mirrored connections.

Impact

Systems may intermittently terminate the mirroring channel.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP systems are slow to establish the mirroring channel.
  • The BIG-IP system intermittently reaps mirrored connections and log messages, similar to the following example, to the /var/log/ltm file:

    tmm tmm[<PID>]: 011e0002:4: sweeper_update: aggressive mode activated. (117504/138240 pages)
