Known Issue

A memory leak may occur when Large Scale NAT (LSN) pools have a small number of translation IP addresses.

Impact

Client connections may experience decreased performance, or possible outages, due to out-of-memory conditions.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Client connectivity appears to be slow, and may lose connectivity.
