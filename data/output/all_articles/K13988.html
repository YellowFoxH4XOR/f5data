Topic

Due to the increased memory footprint of the BIG-IP software beginning in BIG-IP 11.x, certain hardware platforms may not support all BIG-IP module combinations.

Description

To view BIG-IP module provisioning exceptions for BIG-IP 11.x, refer to the following table.

VersionsDocumentDescription
11.4.0 and laterRelease notesFor general guidelines about module support for BIG-IP 11.4.x and later, refer to the release notes for your software version. For information about how to locate F5 product documentation, refer to K12453464: Finding product documentation on AskF5.
K15173vCMP guests running on the VIPRION B2100 blade do not support certain provisioning combination.
K16585Single-core vCMP guests support only a limited number of products and product combinations.
K14760High Density vCMP.
K95553400vCMP Guests with more than 4GB and less than 8GB RAM can now provision more than two modules (11.5.2 and later, 11.6.0 HF5 and later, and 12.0.0 and later)
11.3.0K14422The 1600 and 3600 platforms support a maximum of two provisioned modules when running BIG-IP.
11.3.0K14210vCMP guests with less than 6.5 GB of memory support a maximum of two provisioned modules for some versions.
11.0.0 - 11.2.1K14118The BIG-IP 11000 and 11050 platforms cannot be provisioned with the BIG-IP LTM and Link Controller module combination.
