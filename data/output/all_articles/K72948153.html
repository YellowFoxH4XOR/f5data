K72948153: Upgrading the CIS from 2.0.0 to 2.x

Type
Support Solution
Published
2021-06-10
Updated
2023-02-21
Applies to
  • F5 Automation and Orchestration
  • F5 Container Ingress Services
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K72948153

Description

  • Upgrading CIS version 2.0.0 or earlier to 2.1.0 or greater.
  • After deployment of the configmap against the new controller, the pool member objects are missing from the BIG-IP.

Environment

  • F5-Ingress-Controller
  • BIG-IP
  • Container Ingress Services (CIS)
  • The configmap resides in different namespaces as the services.

Cause

  • A change in behavior now requires that each configmap resides in the same namespace as the service.
  • This also requires that these services all reside within the same tenant or BIG-IP Partition.

Recommended Actions

The actions or steps required to address the topic covered in the description.

  1. Collate services to the same namespace that need to be accessible by the BIG-IP Partition.
  2. Verify that the configmap resides in the namespace as the services.
