Policy Information

United States of America Only: F5 hereby announces the End of Sale (EoS) plan for the BIG-IP Link Controller (LC) in the United States for all BIG-IP platforms. This does not impact regions outside of the United States. This applies to all Link Controller licenses and bundles that include Link Controller. The End of Sale of the previously mentioned items will allow F5 to focus on developing and supporting core capabilities that are more commonly needed by customers.

United States of America Only: BIG-IP Link Controller has reached the End of Sale in the United States effective October 31, 2015.

F5 maintains generous lifecycle policies that allow customers to enjoy many years of both support and new software releases.
