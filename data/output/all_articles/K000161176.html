K000161176: Our production loadbalancers are performing unwanted fail-overs, resulting in short outages

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-05-08
Updated
2026-05-08
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.5.1
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000161176

Description

BIG-IP LTM may experience unwanted failovers and short outages due to TMM (Traffic Management Microkernel) process crashes. Log messages may include:

panic: ../kern/tmm_cmp.c:3464: Assertion "valid dest" failed.

Symptoms:

  • HA connections lost between peers
  • TMM processes killed and restarted
  • Devices unexpectedly transition between Active, Standby, and Offline states

Environment

  • Tmm
  • High Availability (HA)

Cause

Bug ID 1012009: MQTT Message Routing virtual may result in TMM crash.

Answer/Recommended Actions

Upgrade BIG-IP LTM to a fixed version to resolve the issue. Fixed versions are available.

Additional Information

None
