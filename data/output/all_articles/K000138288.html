Description

F5 Support Services practices KCS® (Knowledge-Centered Service), a methodology that helps organizations leverage knowledge to enhance Support in daily operations and improve our customers' capability to self-solve wherever and whenever they wish to.  KCS encourages trusting all internal and external stakeholders to create value (in this case, content) from the interactions they have, which in turn helps create an abundance of knowledge and value.

Benefits of F5 practicing KCS and providing a knowledge base

In other methodologies, Support members and customers may have to rely on finding the right person who possesses the needed knowledge to solve a given problem. When KCS is applied at F5, the knowledge base is collectively owned by all members of the organization.  As such it contains a healthy mix of contributors and content to serve as a centralized repository and a consistent platform for customers and F5 Support to use in an effort to solve known questions and issues that arise.

KCS also encourages growth and evolution of this knowledge base by empowering members of F5 Support to continuously improve knowledge articles during problem solving (How F5s world class Support troubleshoots issues). By identifying opportunities to create, improve, and publish knowledge during problem-solving process, KCS allows other team members and customers to immediately benefit from the organization's new knowledge, thus enabling other team members to reuse the new knowledge to solve their customers questions more consistently, accurately, and efficiently. 

KCS also provides customers a direct benefit.  With it, customers are enabled to focus their attention on unique and more complex issues because you have access to the solutions for common and known issues, in many cases, without having to rely on contacting F5 Support (K000135931: Contact F5 Support).

How does it work?

KCS expects that, wherever possible, interactions with the F5 Support organization:

  1. Reuse: Employ the knowledge base in order to reduce redundancy and guesswork in problem solving by reusing solutions others have already captured.
  2. Improve: It also requires that F5 Support staff improve on content that exists if there an opportunity to update, clarify, or improve the content to make it more reusable for the next reader
  3. Create: Add new knowledge when a content gap is found as a result of solving an issue. 
    Note: While adding new knowledge requires extra intention in the problem-solving process, the return on investment can be exponential in the time saved both for our organization as well as for our customers.

Customers also directly contribute to the knowledge base by interacting with the knowledge workers during problem solving. This ensures that the knowledge base remains relevant and appears in a context that makes sense to customers (more on that in How F5s world class Support troubleshoots issues).

What kinds of content at F5 follow the KCS methodology?

Support solutions

Support Solutions are knowledge articles written directly by Support agents during interactions with customers (cases, chat, virtual meetings, emails, etc.) and published on the knowledge base, which is integrated as part of the Support portal, MyF5. The KCS methodology ensures that the issues resolved for customers appear in Support solution articles and thus benefit all who use the portal.

Knowledge

Knowledge are articles that are written within the Support organization that tend to be written in reflection of the Support Solutions.  Often times these articles stitch together or connect multiple Support Solutions together as well as provide best practices for how to approach a challenge.

Additional Information

KCS core principles are as follows:

  • Abundance: Share more, learn more.
  • Create value: Work tasks; think big picture.
  • Demand-driven: Knowledge is a by-product of interaction.
  • Trust: People will choose to make good decisions and judgement.

These core principles are mutually valuable, and when successfully adhered to, they can help organizations realize their full Support potential.
