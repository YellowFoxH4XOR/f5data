K75378150: Clients Authenticating with PIV or CAC cards no longer being prompted for card

Source

Description

System no longer being prompted for PIV card

Environment

  • Windows 10
  • APM

Cause

Windows clients are caching the certificate provided by the client.

Recommended Actions

Clearing the Windows CAC Certificate Cache

  1. Open the “Start Menu”

  2. Open the “Control Panel”

  3. If your Control Panel is in Classic View

  4. Select “Internet Options”.

  5. Open the “Content” Tab

  6. Click the “Certificates” button

  7. Select the “Personal” tab

  8. “Remove” the highlighted certificate

  9. Click “Close” to close the certificate window

  10. Click “OK” to close the internet options

  11. Remove your CAC from the card reader

  12. Reinsert your CAC & login to the desktop if necessary
