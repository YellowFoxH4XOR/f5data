Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may fail to capture relevant traffic to the logs.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You configured a capture filter in the Capture Filter section of the affected Analytics profile.

Impact

The BIG-IP system does not capture all relevant traffic to the logs.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You observe the System > Log > Captured Transactions page appears to display only a fraction of the expected transactions.
