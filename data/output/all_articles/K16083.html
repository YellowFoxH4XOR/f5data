Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) process may produce a core file during VIPRION system startup or restart. This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have enabled health monitors on the VIPRION system prior to startup or restart.
  • The VIPRION system receives traffic before the TMM processes transition to ready for Clustered Multiprocessing (CMP) processing.

Impact

The VIPRION system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restarting.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H499973 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The VIPRION system fails over to the standby system if the system is configured in a redundant configuration.
  • The VIPRION system generates TMM core files to the /shared/core directory.

Workaround

To prevent this issue from occurring, you can disassociate all health monitors from the pools/nodes prior to restarting the VIPRION system. To do so, refer to the following guides appropriate for your version:

  • The About Pools / About Nodes chapter of the BIG-IP Local Traffic Management: Basics guide (11.6.0)
  • The Pools / Nodes chapter of the BIG-IP Local Traffic Manager: Concepts guide (11.4.1 - 11..5.1)

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system during the restart. However, you have to reassociate the health monitors once the VIPRION system is up and running.
