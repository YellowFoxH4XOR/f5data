Description

The following is information about the integration of F5OS (F5 Operating System) with IBM QRadar SIEM, specifically regarding the ability to forward F5OS host logs to QRadar in the LEEF (Log Event Extended Format) format.

Environment

  • F5OS
  • Log destination: IBM QRadar SIEM

Cause

F5OS does not support the LEEF log format for log forwarding to external SIEMs such as IBM QRadar.

Recommended Actions

NA

Additional Information

There are no current plans on the F5OS roadmap to support LEEF format for log forwarding.
