K4319: BIG-IP now times out node-side connections that have not transmitted any data based on the bigip_max_syn_timeout variable

Applies to

  • BIG-IP 4.X: 4.6.4
Source

Topic

Beginning in BIG-IP version 4.5.12, delayed binding virtual servers will time out the node side of the connection when it has not transferred any data, based on the value of the bigip_max_syn_timeout variable.

This means that the client and node side connections of a delayed binding connection will be subject to the same timeout period.

This should result in a reduction of stale connections on nodes, since the connections will be reset more quickly.
