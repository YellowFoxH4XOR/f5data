Description
BIG-IP Viprion blades became out of sync following restart of services after updates to management provisioning

These log messages may be observed:

    Mar 17 10:16:12 slot2/example.f5.com err tmm[11045]: 01340004:3: HA Connection detected different cmp_state: local cmp_state f, remote cmp_state 2. Connection will be retried.
    Mar 17 10:16:13 slot2/example.f5.com err tmm[11045]: 01340004:3: HA Connection detected different cmp_state: local cmp_state f, remote cmp_state 2. Connection will be retried.
    ...
    Mar 17 10:25:34 slot2/example.f5.com err tmm[11045]: 01340004:3: HA Connection detected different cmp_state: local cmp_state f, remote cmp_state e. Connection will be retried.
    Mar 17 10:25:34 slot2/example.f5.com err tmm[11045]: 01340004:3: HA Connection detected different cmp_state: local cmp_state f, remote cmp_state e. Connection will be retried.

This error (or a similar failed script error) may also observed:

    Mar 17 10:15:15 slot2/example.f5.com info mprov:29159:[29271]: Invoked as: /usr/bin/mprov.pl (pid=29159) --file /var/run/config/.mprov.temp --quiet --list

    Mar 17 10:25:41 slot2/example.f5.com err mcpd[33846]: 01071392:3: Background command '/usr/bin/mprov.pl --quiet --commit afm host ltm tmos ui ' failed. The command was signaled.

Environment

  • BIG-IP Viprion with multiple blades
  • Changes made to Management resource provisioning requiring restart of services

Cause
This error appears to be related to a failed, or hung, script invoked by MCPD.  

Recommended Actions
Restart services on the blade that is out of sync:

  1. Connect to the BIG-IP CLI.
  2. SSH to the correct blade:
ssh slot<x>
  1. Restart services on the blade:
bigstart restart
  1. Confirm the status of the blade:
tmsh show sys cluster
