Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Although 3-DNS allows you to configure SNMP version 2 probing for Foundry hosts, 3-DNS only sends SNMP version 1 probes.

There is no way to configure 3-DNS to send SNMP version 2 probes to Foundry hosts at this time.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR27758 and CR42072.
