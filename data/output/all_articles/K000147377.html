K000147377: How can I receive notifications for planned maintenance of F5 Distributed Cloud?

Description

  • You want to receive a notification for a planned maintenance

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud Console

Resolution/Answer

There are two cases to consider:

  • You are a tenant user: by default, you will receive e-mail notifications of a maintenance, at the e-mail address used to log in.
    • If you don’t, or would rather not, please review your Notification Preferences in the Administration service, section Personal Management, subsection My account, where you can turn these notifications back on/off.
  • You want to receive maintenance and outage notifications on an address that doesn’t belong to a tenant user: you can visit https://www.f5cloudstatus.com/ and subscribe to updates on that page with the corresponding e-mail address. 

Other means to get updates on maintenance

It is also possible to get updates using the F5 Cloud Status API, or using our RSS feed.
