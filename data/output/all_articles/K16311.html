Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may drop Internet Content Adaption Protocol (ICAP) payload during extended iRule processing.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • An ICAP profile and either a Request Adapt or Response Adapt profile are assigned to a virtual server.
  • An ICAP internal virtual server has an iRule that uses the ICAP_REQUEST event and contains commands that may take an extended period of time to complete, or that may run asynchronously.

    Note: For more information about asynchronous commands in iRules, refer to K12962: Some iRules commands temporarily suspend iRule processing.

A long-running iRule in ICAP_REQUEST can cause the loss of payload while the iRule is running, resulting in the system omitting the beginning of the payload in the request to the ICAP server. Note that headers are unaffected, and the system will still send those once the ICAP iRule completes.

Impact

The system may omit the beginning of the payload of an ICAP request.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Your ICAP servers may report the ICAP request content as unrecognized.

Workaround

There is no workaround for this issue. You can mitigate this issue by ensuring that, when possible, you keep iRule duration short by minimizing processing in the ICAP_REQUEST event and avoiding unnecessary processing, or move the processing elsewhere in your iRule logic.

Impact of workaround: Changing the design of an iRule may have additional effects on traffic processing. Ensure that modifications are compatible with your environment.
