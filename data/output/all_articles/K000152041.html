K000152041: The health monitor status doesn't indicate the pool member has been forced down

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-06-30
Updated
2025-07-16
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.1.2
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000152041

Description

When you are adding a new pool member in forced offline state via TMSH, the GUI may display the pool member’s health monitor status as "Checking (Monitor Instance Disabled)". This can be confusing or misleading for administrators who may think that the pool member is still being monitored which should not be the case when the pool member is in forced offline state. 

 

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • Adding new, forced-offline pool member to the pool via tmsh

Cause

In fact the monitoring is indeed disabled (as indicated by “Monitor Instance Disabled” text), however the BIG-IP does not have a previous health monitor status and therefore it cannot display an icon that would correspond to the previous monitor status – either UP or DOWN. The health monitor status will remain as "Checking (Monitor Instance Disabled)" until the pool member will be put into enabled or disabled state.  

 

Recommended Actions

This is only a cosmetic problem and requires no further action from the administrator. The monitoring is disabled although the health monitor status may confuse system administrators.
