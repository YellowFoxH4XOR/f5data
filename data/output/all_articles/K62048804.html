Description

Customizing range of date, the resolutions of AVR-related charts may be low.

Environment

  • BIG-IP AFM, ASM, AVR

Cause

This is by design. 
AVR aggregates data with the following resolutions:

1 Hour: every 5 minutes (12 data points)  
1 Day: every 1 hour (24 data points)
1 Month: every 1 day (30 data points)
Older: every 1 month      

For example, if specified range is Last Day, the chart will be plotted by 1 hour data.    

  

However, if specified start time is over 1 day from current time, the chart will be plotted by using 1 day data instead of 1 hour data because of aggregation.
