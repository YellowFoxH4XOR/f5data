Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When all of the following circumstances occur, an active unit may fail back to standby mode after the standby unit reboots:

  • Only one BIG-IP system in a pair can directly access the gateway; the other BIG-IP system accesses the gateway through its peer
  • Gateway failsafe is enabled
  • VLAN failsafe is enabled on the internal VLAN
  • Both hardware failover and network failover are enabled
  • The BIG-IP system that is dependent on its peer to reach the gateway is active
  • The BIG-IP system that is dependent on its peer to reach the gateway cannot reach the gateway and becomes standby
  • The BIG-IP system that is dependent on its peer to reach the gateway is able to reach the gateway again

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR29394, CR29395, and CR41985.
