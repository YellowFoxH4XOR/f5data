Description

Even though Centos will reach End of Life on June 30, 2024 F5 will continue to support all products that use Centos as the base Operating System in accordance with the product lifecycle. Arrangements have been put in place for CVE support to continue without any disruption or concern.

This is useful for customers looking to understand long-term support plans for products using Centos 7.x as the base Operating System. Given that Centos 7.x reaches end of life (EOL) on June 30, 2024.
