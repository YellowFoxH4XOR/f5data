Known Issue

The BIG-IP does not forward IPsec ESP (protocol 50) packets.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • A client attempts to establish an IPsec tunnel connection through the BIG-IP via an IP forwarding virtual server.

In BIG-IP 10.x versions, clients are able to establish an IPsec ESP tunnel connection through an IP forwarding virtual server. BIG-IP version 11.0.0 introduced functionality for the BIG-IP to be an IPsec SA endpoint. When the BIG-IP is not configured as an IPsec SA endpoint, it will incorrectly drop the IPsec ESP (protocol 50) packets instead of forwarding them through the IP forwarding virtual server.

Impact

Clients are unable to establish an IPsec ESP tunnel connection through the BIG-IP.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H379837 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • Clients are unable to establish an IPsec ESP tunnel connection through the BIG-IP.
  • BIG-IP drops IPsec ESP (protocol 50) packets.
