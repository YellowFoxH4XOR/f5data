Description

The TMM pod in SPK 1.7.14 was repeatedly crash-looping on a DL360 Gen11 node with Mellanox ConnectX-6 LX NICs.

The log reported the error:

"Too many pci buses encountered (32 max)
 tmm_start: device_init failed (ERR_UNKNOWN)."

Disabling unused PCIe slots allowed the pod to start, confirming the issue was due to a PCI bus resource limit.

Environment

SPK version 1.7.14

Cause

The root cause was a hard-coded limitation in SPK 1.7.14 restricting the system to a maximum of 32 PCI buses.

When the number of PCI buses exceeded this threshold, TMM failed to initialize, resulting in a crash-loop. This was confirmed by both log evidence and successful resolution after reducing the number of active PCIe devices.

 

Recommended Actions

Upgrade SPK to version 1.9.x or later, where the PCI bus limit has been increased to 128.

As a temporary workaround, disable unused PCIe slots or NICs to ensure the number of PCI buses does not exceed 32.

Confirm the environment meets hardware compatibility requirements for the upgraded SPK version.
