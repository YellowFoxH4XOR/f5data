Policy Information

F5 is announcing that the End of Technical Support (EoTS) for all BIG-IP and 3-DNS 4.x software branches became effective December 31, 2009 in accordance with the guidelines contained within K8986: F5 product support policies and K5903: BIG-IP software support policy.

The EoTS concludes the Extended Support phase. As of December 31, 2009, F5 will no longer provide technical support, configuration support, security updates, software fixes (hotfixes), or consulting services for any BIG-IP or 3-DNS version 4.x product.

F5 will continue to offer RMA services for platforms that have not exceeded the platform's EoTS or End of RMA (EoRMA) support date in accordance with the guidelines contained within K4309: The F5 platform life cycle support policy.

All BIG-IP and 3-DNS 4.x documentation available on the AskF5 Knowledge Base has moved to the End-of-Life Products section and will no longer be included in a standard AskF5 search. For information about searching for End-of-Life Product software, refer to K11204: Searching for End-of-Life Product documentation.

Note: Some platforms running BIG-IP 4.x software are capable of upgrading to the BIG-IP LTM or GTM software. For information, refer to the software release notes to determine if a particular software release is suitable for the platform. Access or compatibility of these software updates are not guaranteed and will be made under F5 reasonable effort support guidelines.

For more information about this announcement, contact your local F5 Partner Business Manager or Inside Sales Representative.
