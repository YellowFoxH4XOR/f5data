Known Issue

When you customize the webtop title, the system may not display the customized title.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system is configured with a full webtop.
  • You customize the webtop by changing the displayed webtop title.
  • You assign the webtop as an access session resource.
  • Users establish an access session.

Impact

The webtop renders the default title.

Note: You can safely ignore this issue; it does not affect system functionality.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When a user accesses the webtop, the webtop title does not reflect your customization.
  • When a user accesses the webtop, the system displays the title F5 Dynamic Webtop.
