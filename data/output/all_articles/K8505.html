Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When a TCP RST is generated through the use of an iRule, the ACK number is incorrect.

For example, the ACK number sent is the sequence number + 2, when it should be the sequence number + 1.

As a result, clients may reject the generated RST packet due to the incorrect ACK number specified, rather than disconnect or drop the connection.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR91692.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in HOTFIX-BIG-IP-9.3.1-HF2 issued for BIG-IP version 9.3.1, for the BIG-IP 9.3 software branch. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
