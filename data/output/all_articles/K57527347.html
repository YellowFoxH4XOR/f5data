Known Issue

You may be unable to read Japanese characters encoded in SHIFT-JIS in PDF exports.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You try to export requests containing Japanese characters using the SHIFT-JIS encoding.

Impact

You are unable to export PDF files from the BIG-IP ASM Configuration utility.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • PDF files are created but you cannot read them.
