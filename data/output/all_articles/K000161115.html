K000161115: Pool Member become offline even if the ping and curl working fine

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-05-07
Updated
2026-05-07
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP ASM: 17.0.0, 17.1.0, 17.0.X, 17.X.X, 21.X.X, 21.1.X, 21.1.0, 21.0.X, 21.0.0, 17.5.X, 17.5.1, 17.5.0, 17.1.X, 17.1.3, 17.1.2, 17.1.1
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000161115

Description

A pool member was reported as offline in the BIG-IP configuration, resulting in disrupted traffic forwarding and impacted services. The device was able to reach the pool member using both curl and ICMP, but the pool member remained offline.

Environment

  • Monitors

Cause

The pool member was marked offline due to a monitoring or probing issue. 

Answer/Recommended Actions

Remove and re-add the monitor to the affected pool member to reset health checks on the pool member.

Additional Information

None
