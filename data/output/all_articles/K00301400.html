Known Issue

Dynamic offloading in the embedded Packet Velocity Acceleration (ePVA) chip may cause immediate flow eviction and re-offloading.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a FastL4 profile with the PVA Offload Dynamic option enabled.
  • Two flows experience a hash collision.

Impact

The system may delay packet processing on a connection.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You observe that the ePVA re-offloads, evicts, and again re-offloads colliding flows within a short period of time.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can modify the FastL4 profile to disable the PVA Offload Dynamic option.

Impact of workaround: If you disable the PVA Offload Dynamic option, flows evicted from ePVA processing are not dynamically re-entered into the ePVA.
