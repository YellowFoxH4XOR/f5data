Policy Information

F5 is announcing the End of Life (EoL) of the BIG-IP Edge Portal 1.0.2 for Android and BIG-IP Edge Portal 1.0.3, effective July 1, 2018.

Product Support

BIG-IP Edge Portal is no longer supported on iOS 11 and on Android 8, and is no longer available on the Apple App Store and the Google Play store.

Recommended Actions

BIG-IP Edge Portal was a browser platform primarily intended to deliver a consistent web app experience to end users. With the standardization of browser rendering engines, cross-compatibility is not a large concern as it once was. End-users can use standard Safari, Chrome, or Firefox browsers on mobile and be assured of full support for BIG-IP APM-delivered web apps. For the VPN tunnel use case, customers can use F5 Access for iOS and F5 Access for Android to establish a full or split VPN. Alternatively, customers can use Per-App VPN solution using an Enterprise Mobility Management (EMM) or Mobile Device Management (MDM) solutions provider.
