Known Issue

Portal access webpages may fail to display.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured the access profile with portal access, and the associated portal rewrite profile includes a list of more than 26 bypass URIs.
  • A user attempts to access the portal.

Impact

The configured portal resources do not display the portal content.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • When you access a portal, the system does not display portal access webpage content.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can reduce the number of URIs in the rewrite profile bypass list. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

Reducing the list of bypass URIs in the affected rewrite profile

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.
  2. Navigate to Access Policy > Portal Access > Rewrite Profiles.
  3. Click the name of the affected rewrite profile.
  4. In the Rewrite List box, select the bypass URIs that you want to remove, reducing the total number of URIs to approximately 26.

    Note: To select multiple bypass URIs, press and hold the Ctrl key.

  5. Click Delete.
  6. Click Update.
