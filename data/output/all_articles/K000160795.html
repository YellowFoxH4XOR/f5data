Description

You are implementing IaC with our F5 Distributed Cloud platform and you want to know if the F5 terraform provider has drift detection.

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud (XC)

  • F5 XC Terraform

Answer/Recommended Action

No, We have no drift detection / protection in our terraform provider as for today.  You can use lifecycle ignore workarounds in some cases, otherwise the fix should be in the new provider GA Q4 CY26.

Additional Information

Official documentation: https://registry.terraform.io/providers/volterraedge/volterra/latest/docs
