Known Issue

Access sessions initiated with a Chrome browser may appear to not initialize.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM access profile access policy includes one or both of the following:
    • A network access resource
    • Endpoint Security Client-Side agents
  • Users attempting to establish an access session are using the Google Chrome browser 60 or higher.

Impact

The access session appears to not initialize.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • When you attempt to establish an access session, the status indicators for Network Access Application and Endpoint Inspection continually display the status Waiting.

Workaround

Impact of workaround:  Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.

To work around this issue, you can wait 60 seconds and refresh the access profile webpage or use a different vendor browser to establish your access session.
