Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may drop fragmented IPv6 packets.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system has packet filtering enabled.
  • Your BIG-IP system processes fragmented IPv6 packets.

Impact

The BIG-IP system drops fragmented IPv6 packets when packet filtering is enabled.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When packet filtering is enabled, fragmented IPv6 packets are dropped.
  • Affected IPv6 connections fail.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can disable packet filtering on the affected BIG-IP system and perform the desired packet filtering on an upstream or downstream device.

Impact of workaround: The impact of the suggested workaround depends on the specific environment. F5 recommends testing any such changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.
