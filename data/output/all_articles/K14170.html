Known Issue

BIG-IP systems may experience a TMM memory leak causing system instability.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a high-availability (HA) configuration.
  • You have persistence mirroring enabled.
  • The HA communication channel is interrupted.

Under these conditions, when a persistence update is made to the standby system, it is possible for the active unit to create duplicate persistence entries that do not expire and remain in memory.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may eventually reboot due to lack of memory.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H380608 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Low screen.
  • Small incremental increases in TMM memory usage over time.
  • Lower performance and stability due to a growing persistence table.
