K000136113: F5OS-C Physical interface status flaps randomly

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-09-07
Updated
2026-04-21
Applies to
  • F5OS
  • F5OS-C: 1.6.X, 1.5.X, 1.6.2, 1.6.1, 1.6.0, 1.5.1, 1.5.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000136113

Description

Blade interfaces of F5OS-C may flap and marked interfaces DOWN and UP intermittently, you will find log in the qkview.

/qkview/subpackages/partition#_manager/qkview/filesystem/var/F5/partition/log/velos.log
20YY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS.XXXXX+00:00 192.168.1.10 blade-1(p2) fpgamgr[14]: priority="Debug" version=1.0 msgid=0x301000000000030 msg="" text="atse_link_callback link 16 (DID:0x8010) state:DOWN".
 

Environment

  • VELOS platforms, version 1.5.1
  • Port groups configured in 4x10GB or 4x25GB mode.
  • Physical interface status are disabled.

Cause

F5 tracks this behavior as ID1345977 .

 Recommended Actions

F5 recommends one of the following:

  • upgrading to F5OS-C 1.8.0 where this issue is fixed.
  • a workaround as enabling all physical interfaces on the blade, even those that may be unplugged or unused. 

Additional Information

  • K000135859: [F5OS-C] error message: "Unable to read optic EEPROM - device is unusable" and "Optic REMOVED."
