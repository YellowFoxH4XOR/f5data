Known Issue

A source route for the cluster IP address is not included in the IP routing table on the primary blade.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The management ports for all blades in a multi-bladed system or Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP) guest are configured with valid IP addresses.

Impact

  • Services that receive traffic from the management interface of the primary blade in a physical cluster may not recognize the cluster as the source of the traffic.
  • Services that depend on traffic sourced from the management interface of a vCMP guest may fail.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H460207 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Low screen.
  • The primary blade in a clustered system may send management traffic sourced from the blade IP address associated with the management port instead of from the cluster IP address.
  • Traffic sourced from the management interface of a vCMP guest may fail with an ICMP 'destination unreachable' message.
