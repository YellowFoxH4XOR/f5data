Known Issue

The BIG-IP system fails to include Subject Alternative Name (SAN) extensions when generating a certificate signing request (CSR) from the Configuration utility.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP configuration has a Certificate Authority (CA) signed Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) certificate with one or more SAN extensions.
  • You attempt to renew the SSL SAN certificate from the Configuration utility by generating a new CSR.

Impact

SAN extensions are missing from new CSRs.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When you view the contents of a new CSR, no SAN extensions are present.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can use the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) to renew the SSL SAN certificate. To do so, refer to K13471: Creating SSL SAN certificates and CSRs using the Configuration utility or tmsh.
