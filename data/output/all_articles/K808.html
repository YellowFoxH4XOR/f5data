Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The timeout period that you configure for the reverse ECV health monitor determines the frequency at which the monitor reports the node status.

For example, if you configure the reverse ECV health monitor with a timeout period of 30 seconds and the monitor successfully completes the check in the first second, the monitor will wait another 29 seconds to mark the node as up.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR571, CR37500, CR37746 and CR41784.

Workaround

To work around this issue, configure the timeout option for the shortest amount of time in which the nodes can respond while maintaining a ratio of 1:3+1 for the frequency and timeout.

Note:. For more information, refer to K350: Best Practice: Recommended health monitor frequency and timeout values.
