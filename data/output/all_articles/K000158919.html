Description

You may be facing some kind of an issue or you simply have questions about disk space on a BIG-IP. 

  • Where can I find the information?
  • The BIG-IP is not upgrading properly, or a module may not be acting correctly.
  • I am seeing some log entries I have a question about.
  • An upgrade fails due to disk issues..

Environment

Disk space on any type of BIG-IP is showing an issue or you simply have questions.

Cause

The causes here can vary from:

  • iRule logging filling up /var/log
  • Not enough actual disk space allotted. 
    • Most frequently seen in virtual BIG-IPs.
  • Too many old images
  • There are some APM or AdvWAF files that may build up.
  • Too many old ucs archive files

That is not an inclusive list, but is some of the most common that are seen. 

Recommended Actions

  1. A common issue is caused by having too many old ucs archives, old software images, or too many old installed OS versions. These are among the most common causes of disk issues. By simply deleting most or all of them in many cases disk issues are resolved. 
    1. There are some basic best practices that are common to all 3;
      1. Keep the most recent file/image and delete the rest. 
      2. For installed OS versions keep the last version and the one currently in use in the event you need to fall back.
      3. Always keep a recent ucs stored off the BIG-IP in a safe location.
  2. Since there are varying factors it would be a good idea to understand how a BIG-IP works with disk space:
    1. K14403: Maintaining disk space on the BIG-IP system
    2. K17477: Disk usage by the BIG-IP modules with application volumes
  3. With that the next step would be to gather the data needed to move forward to answer your questions or address any issues you may be having:
    1. K000136959: Troubleshooting a full partition, disk, or filesystem.
  4. The most common issue involves disk space issues while upgrading:
    1. Prior to any upgrade it would be a good idea to inspect the system and deal with any potential issues proactively:
      1. K000148391: Verifying free disk space for software upgrade
    2. If you come across something or something happened during the upgrade process:
      1. K33265170: Deleting a boot location volume to free up disk space
      2. K91895955: Unable to upgrade with error ''failed (Disk full (volume group). See SOL#10636)''
      3. K000157359: Installation failed not enough disc space /config
  5. If you run into a directory that is full:
    1. K000132482: Recover /var disk partition free space that is filling or full
    2. K14952: Extending disk space on BIG-IP VE
    3. K000157183: APM Disk Space issue: /config is full
    4. K15485103: err diskmonitor[<PID>]: 011d0004:3: Disk partition /shared has only x% free
    5. K32060221: /var getting full due to ASM policy history files

This is not an all inclusive list, but is intended to get your through some common questions and issues. If after going over this you have any questions of the issues has not been resolved open a case with F5 Support. K2633: Submit a support case
