Known Issue

Email sent by the BIG-IP APM system may appear incomplete.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM access policy is configured with the Email agent.
  • The receiving email server expects the RFC 5322 standard for email formatting.

Impact

Emails are not formatted correctly.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The receiving SMTP server processing of the email excludes the body of the email.
  • The receiving SMTP server processing of the email excludes the subject of the email.
  • The receiving SMTP server generates the email containing the email header as part of the message body.
  • The receiving SMTP server processing of the email excludes the date header of the email.
