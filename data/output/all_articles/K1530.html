Known Issue

The KB-LK switch on the F35 chassis is not connected to anything. Because of this, the motherboard cannot support the keyboard lock switch (also known as, keyboard inhibit) function.

Note that the F35 hardware platform was discontinued on 10/01/01. Refer to K1943: End of Life Announcement: BIG-IP Enterprise (F35) platform for additional information.
