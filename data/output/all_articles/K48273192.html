Description
Activating license of BIG-IP from BIG-IQ may fail at AWS environment.
 

Environment

  • Activating license of BIG-IP from BIG-IQ on AWS
  • BIG-IP is configured as single NIC deployment

Cause
F5 track this issue as ID808129

Recommended Actions

There are 2 methods of workaround 
  • To deploy BIG-IP to have 2 or more NIC
                        (or)
  • Restarting restjavad on BIG-IP
    #tmsh restart sys service restjavad
