Description

You may see the following message on your browser: "Bad Request - Invalid Hostname".

 

Environment

BIG-IP LTM

IPv6 to IPv4

Cause

Not using the setting: NAT64 on the VIP, the BIG-IP won't translate properly. 

Recommended Actions

Go to the VIP in question
Change "Configuration" from Basic to Advanced
Enable NAT64
Click Update

NOTE: The BIGIP is acting as the NAT64 gateway. It intercepts the IPv6 packet and performs the necessary address translation. It translates the IPv6 source address to a corresponding IPv4 address and the destination IPv4 address to the appropriate IPv6 address.
