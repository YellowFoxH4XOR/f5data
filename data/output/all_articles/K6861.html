Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If the big3d utility receives an error during SSL renegotiation, the big3d daemon is prevented from sending any further messages to the requesting system. This also causes the big3dshim daemon to update its idle timer, preventing a reset that would correct the issue and allow the big3d daemon to renegotiate the SSL connection.

The occurrence of this behavior is rare; when this issue occurs, it will only happen once in a 24-hour period.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR70620 and CR70619, and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.2.5 and 9.3. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM Release Notes.
