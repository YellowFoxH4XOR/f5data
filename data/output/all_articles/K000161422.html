K000161422: BIG-IQ CM Upgrade Issue – Rolling Upgrade Stopped Before CM Update

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-05-26
Updated
2026-05-26
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 8.4.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000161422

Description

Rolling upgrade of BIG-IQ Central Manager and Data Collection Devices may stop prematurely after upgrading some or all DCDs, without proceeding to upgrade the Central Manager. The Central Manager remains on the previous version while one or more DCDs are running the newer version. The upgrade process may report the error:

Timed out trying to recover the cluster. Rolling back.

Attempts to rerun the upgrade on a different partition may also fail, requiring restoration from a snapshot.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ Central Manager with Data Collection Devices
  • Upgrade

Cause

The rolling upgrade process halted due to a cluster recovery timeout on one of the DCDs, which triggered a rollback and prevented the upgrade from continuing to the Central Manager.

Answer/Recommended Actions

To upgrade the BIG-IQ Central Manager independently after a partial rolling upgrade failure:

  1. Remove all DCDs from the BIG-IQ Central Manager.
  2. Perform a standalone upgrade of the BIG-IQ Central Manager to the target version.
  3. Verify that the Central Manager has successfully upgraded and is operational.
  4. Re-add the DCDs back to the Central Manager.
