Description

If APM with Endpoint Protection checks is being used and there are new laptops and they can not connect the your corporate VPN.

Environment

  • APM 
  • Access Policy with Endpoint Protection checks
  • Antivirus Check

Cause

A Full Scan by the AV is needed since this populates all the client registry details used for Endpoint Protection checks for (most) Malware products.

Recommended Actions

We always recommend when ever you have this issue and log files show a client device failing 'last_scan'. To run a full scan.
