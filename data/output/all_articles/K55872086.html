Description

You may encounter an error message as below.
  • Error message: TCL error: /Common/example <SERVER_CONNECTED> - Unknown error (line 2) (line 2) invoked from within "SSL::enable sslprofile"
  • This might happen when you use SSL::enable and SSL::profile in the iRule to enable the SSL feature for your traffic.

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • SSL Profiles
  • iRules

Cause

The SSL::profile or SSL::enable requires an SSL profile.

Recommended Actions

To use the iRule SSL::profile or SSL::enable, you should add one or more SSL profiles to your Virtual Server. To attach the SSL profiles to your Virtual Server, you may follow the steps of section Creating a virtual server for client-side and server-side HTTPS traffic from  Manual Chapter : Managing Client- and Server-Side HTTP Traffic Using a CA-Signed Certificate
