Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may fail to process Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) traffic after re-establishing connectivity to SafeNet Hardware Security Modules (HSMs).

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is configured to use SafeNet HSMs.
  • One of the following events disrupts connectivity to SafeNet HSMs:
     
    • You perform a manual restart of the pkcs11d service without restarting the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM).
    • A network issue affects the connection between your BIG-IP system and a SafeNet HSM device.
    • You reboot the SafeNet HSM device without restarting both the pkcs11d service and TMM on your BIG-IP system.

Impact

SSL clients are unable to connect to the affected virtual server configured with a SafeNet HSM certificate key pair.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H585245 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High screen.
  • In the /var/log/ltm file, you observe error messages that appear similar to the following example:

    warning tmm1[PID]: 01260009:4: Connection error: ssl_hs_vfy_sign_srvkeyxchg:9678: sign_srvkeyxchg (80).
    info tmm1[PID]: 01260013:6: SSL Handshake failed for TCP 10.10.0.1:59513 -> 10.10.1.150:20001.
    warning pkcs11d[PID]: 01680022:4: Crypto operation [2] failed.
    crit tmm1[PID]: 01260010:2: FIPS acceleration device failure: fips_poll_completed_reqs: req: 56 status: 0x1 : Cancel.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can re-establish the connection to your SafeNet HSM device. If the pkcs11d service was restarted manually, you should also restart TMM. If the issue was caused by a SafeNet HSM reboot or a network disruption between the BIG-IP system and the SafeNet HSM device, you can restart the pkcs11d service, followed by TMM, when the network issue gets resolved. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Restarting TMM interrupts traffic processing, so you should perform this procedure during a maintenance window. If the system is configured as part of a high availability (HA) pair, the device may fail over.

  1. Log in to the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) by typing the following command:

    tmsh

  2. To restart the pkcs11d service, type the following command:

    restart /sys service pkcs11d

  3. To restart TMM, type the following command:

    restart /sys service tmm
