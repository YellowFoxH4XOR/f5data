Known Issue

Simple Object Access Protocol (SOAP) monitors fail when the specified 'URL Path' value begins with a colon character.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured a SOAP monitor to check the availability of pool members.
  • The SOAP monitor's 'URL Path' value begins with a colon character.

Impact

Monitored pool members are marked as down or offline, and are unavailable for load balancing.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The SOAP monitor continuously reports the pool member statuses as offline or down.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can precede the colon character in the 'URL Path' value with one of the following characters:

  • slash character (/)
  • backslash character (\)

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
