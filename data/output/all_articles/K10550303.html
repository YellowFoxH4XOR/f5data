Known Issue

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP device is a BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE).
  • You configure your BIG-IP Virtual Local Area Network (VLAN) with interface tagging.
  • You configure the VLAN with CMP-Hash set.
  • The VLAN is actively processing traffic.
  • You modify the CMP-Hash configuration settings.

Impact

The issue impacts the traffic processing performance of the VLAN.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • After you modify the CMP-Hash setting, the VLAN performance is lower than you would expect.
  • When you view the tmctl command counter for TMM, you notice in the flow redirect statistics that the counter does not increment.

    The following is an example tmctl command:

    tmctl  tmm/flow_redir_stats

Workaround

To work around this issue, when possible, you can configure the hypervisor to use VLAN tagging, and remove interface tagging from the BIG-IP VLAN configuration.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
