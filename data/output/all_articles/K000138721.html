Description

Potentially questionable images listed in the /usr/local/www/tmui/tmui/skins/Default/images/ directory on VE images


Environment

BIG-IP LTM VE

Traffic Intelligence

Classification Engine (CEC)


Cause

These icons show up in Traffic Intelligence::Applications::Application List - open the category with the + sign to the right of the name and you'll see the icons there.


The images are icons of those sites and from the F5 library called the Classification Engine (CEC).


Recommended Actions

This is by design, the images are icons of sites the Traffic Intelligence::Applications::Application List category which is from the F5 library called the Classification Engine (CEC).
