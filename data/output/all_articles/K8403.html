Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When mirroring is enabled for a FastL4 virtual server, the BIG-IP LTM allows an unmatched, non-SYN packet to pass through the virtual server. This occurs even if the Loose Initiation setting is disabled in the FastL4 profile.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR91670 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM version 9.4.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in HOTFIX-BIG-IP-9.3.1-HF2 issued for BIG-IP version 9.3.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Networks Downloads site.

For information about the F5 Networks hotfix policy, refer to K4918: Overview of F5 Networks critical issue hotfix policy.

For information about how to manage F5 Networks product hotfixes, refer to K6845: Managing F5 Networks product hotfixes.
