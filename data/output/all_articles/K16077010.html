NFV Solutions and F5 VNF Manager software support policy

F5 provides two years of support for any NFV Solutions or VNF Manager version starting from the first customer ship date of a given release. Customers who require support beyond the standard two-year period should contact their F5 account manager.

At the end of a release’s Standard Support phase, the release reaches its End of Software Development (EoSD) and End of Technical Support (EoTS) milestones. Software versions that have reached their EoSD/EoTS date are no longer supported with active software development. F5 will not provide software fixes (hotfixes) or consulting services for a version that has reached the EoSD/EoTS milestone.

Note: For software releases, unless specifically declared and announced by F5, the EoTS date marks the end of the software version’s support phase.
