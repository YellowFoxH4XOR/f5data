Description

Below messages similar to the following are logged to /var/log/ltm:

err tmm1[PID]: 01220001:3: TCL error: _cgc_pick_clientside <CLIENT_DATA> - unknown cgc sni: BIGIP.HOSTNAME (line 1)     invoked from within "CGC::sni $tls_servername"
err tmm3[PID]: 01220001:3: TCL error: _cgc_pick_clientside <CLIENT_DATA> - unknown cgc sni: BIGIP.HOSTNAME (line 49)    invoked from within "CGC::sni $tls_servername"
 

Environment

  • DSC (Device Service Cluster) - the trust established between members of the same device-group.
  • CGC (CMI Group Communications) - the encrypted communications stream between DSC members.
 

Cause

The errors are coming from the internal iRule for config-sync listener( (<configsync-ip>:4353)). 
Part of the iRule logic checks the SNI extension in the ClientHello from the external client. If it's anything different from the expectation, the error messages will be logged.
 

Recommended Actions

If  further troubleshoot which IP address is causing the log messages is needed, at least until the next "unknown cgc sni" error shows in the logs
1. Collect periodic outputs of "netstat -pano" and save them to a temporary file: 

while true; do (date; netstat -pano|grep 4353) >> /var/tmp/netstat.out; sleep 5; done

2. Do a tcpdump to capture the packet: 

tcpdump -nni 0.0:nnn -s0 -C 100 port 4353 -w /var/tmp/port4353_localhost -W 5 -vvv
