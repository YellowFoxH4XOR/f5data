Topic

Some F5 Application Security Edition (-AS) units received additional functionality they would not be entitled to receive without the purchase of an optional F5-ADD-BIG-P1 add-on license upgrade. This functionality includes all of the features that are activated in the Performance Pack or BIG-IP Enterprise. As of October 5, 2006, the licensing has been corrected to prevent further occurrences.

Products with the following SKUs are affected:

  • F5-BIG-LTM-6400-AS
  • F5-BIG-LTM-6400-AS-RS
  • F5-BIG-LTM-6400-AS-LIC
  • F5-BIG-LTM-6800-AS
  • F5-BIG-LTM-6800-AS-RS
  • F5-BIG-LTM-6800-AS-LIC
  • F5-BIG-LTM-8400-AS
  • F5-BIG-LTM-8400-AS-RS

The correct feature set for the BIG-IP 6800 ASE product is as follows:

  • LTM
  • ASM Unlimited
  • Ram Cache
  • Rate Shaping
  • Routing Modules

For affected units, the following advanced features have been enabled:

  • Client Authentication
  • Hardware Compression 1Gbps
  • 20 000 SSL TPS

If you are in possession of an affected unit, you may keep these advanced features. If you re-activate your device after upgrading, you will not lose the added functionality. Even if your unit requires an RMA, the replacement unit will maintain the functionality. However, future activations of BIG-IP 6400, 6800 and 8400 Application Security Edition units will not receive these optional features without the purchase and licensing of an add-on license.

If you want to remove the extra features, contact your F5 Customer Support Center to create a support case and request the additional features be removed. Your unit will require relicensing.

If you are interested in enabling your BIG-IP-6400-AS, 6800-AS or 8400-AS product with Client Authentication, Hardware Compression 1Gbps or 20,000 SSL TPS, you may do so with the additional purchase of the Performance Pack 1 license (SKU # F5-ADD-BIG-P1).
