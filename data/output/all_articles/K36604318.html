Description

You upgraded your instance to a new version and booted into it.

After the new version comes up no pools are marked as green.

Testing including tcpdump's shows the bigip is NOT sending monitor requests to pools.

You boot back to your previously working instance, yet the monitors still fail.

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • Monitors configured

Cause

Instance was placed in Forced Offline mode, which persists across reboots

Recommended Actions

Release offline
