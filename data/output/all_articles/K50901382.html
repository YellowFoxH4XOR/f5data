Description

Your vulnerability scanner has detected that the BIG-IP is vulnerable to  tls-dh-primes.

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • Vulnerability scanner has detected BIG-IP is vulnerable to tls-dh-primes

Cause

tls-dh-primes are related to DHE group.

BIG-IP mitigates tls-dh-primes by rotating DHE groups. The DHE rotation has been done since BIG-IP 11.x. You can read further in this article K14818.

No changes is needed as BIG-IP is not vulnerable to tls-dh-primes.

Recommended Actions

If needed, you can disable DHE group and this will ensure that the vulnerability scanner does not mark the BIG-IP to be vulnerable to tls-dh-primes.

To mitigate tls-dh-primes on TMM (production traffic), you will need to remove DHE on TMM. Refer to K01770517 to mitigate DHE on TMM.

To mitigate tls-dh-primes on the Configuration Terminal (GUI), you will need to remove DHE on the Configuration Terminal. Refer to K02321234 to mitigate DHE on Configuration Terminal.

Additional Information

None.
