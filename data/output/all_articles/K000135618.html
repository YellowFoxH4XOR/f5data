When you log in to MyF5, your default account appears under Account on the home page. Your subscriptions, trials, and open cases for that account also display. These account elements are only accessible when you select the account under which they were set up. If you have more than one account, you can access your other accounts and their associated elements by selecting the down arrow icon on the list and choosing the account you want.

You can manage your accounts on the My Accounts page. On that page, you can set a different account to display by default, add an active account to the list, remove an account from the list, or remove yourself as a user from an account.

Manage your accounts

You can manage your accounts on the My Accounts page, which you can access by selecting the profile icon on the top right of the page and then selecting My Accounts.

On the My Accounts page, you can view details regarding your MyF5 account(s), including the name, account ID, associated subscriptions, number of registered users, the number of open cases, your assigned role for that account, and whether the account is visible on the home page and other locations.

Set different account as default

If you have multiple accounts, the default account will display on the home page. If you want to set another account as the default, do the following:

  1. Under Account Name, select the check box next to the account you want to set as default.
  2. Select Set as default.

Add an account to the My Accounts list

If you want to add an active account to your list of accounts, do the following:

Note: You can only add accounts to the list if they already exist. If you need to set up a new account, contact your MyF5 administrator or contact F5 Support.

  1. On the My Accounts page, under Account name, select the check box next to the account you want to add to the list.
  2. Select Add to Account List.

Remove an account from the Account list

If you want to remove an account from your account list, do the following:

Note: Removing an account from the account list does not remove you as a user for that account. It only hides the account from your list of accounts when viewed on the MyF5 home page and other locations. To remove an account and remove yourself as a user for that account, refer to the Remove account procedure in this article.

  1. On the My Accounts page, under Account name, select the check box next to the account you want to remove from the list.
  2. Select Remove from Account List.

    Note that under the Account List column, the account should indicate Hidden after you remove the account from the list. The account still exists and you are still associated with it as a user, so you can re-add it to the list by following the Add an account to the My Accounts list procedure in this article.

Remove an account

If you want to remove yourself as a user from one of the accounts listed on the My Accounts page, do the following:

Important: If you remove yourself as a user from an account using this process, you will not be able to add the account back to your list. You will need to contact your MyF5 administrator to do so.

  1. Under Account Name, select the check box next to the account you want to remove.
  2. Select Remove Account.
  3. In the pop-up confirmation message, select Delete Account.
