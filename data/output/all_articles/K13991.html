Known Issue

The LCD panel may not display the correct high-availability state of the device. The information and version screens of the LCD panel will display a status of active after the system has been rebooted.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The system is part of redundant failover group
  • The system is rebooted

Impact

The system may be erroneously identified as the active system in a redundant high-availability configuration.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • After the system has been rebooted and enters the standby state, the information and version screens of the LCD panel display a status of active.
