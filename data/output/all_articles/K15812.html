Known Issue

The LCD status display and status LEDs on the front of some BIG-IP appliances may stop displaying updated information.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are running one of the following hardware platforms:
    • BIG-IP 10000s (D113)
    • BIG-IP 10200v (D113)
  • The LCD screen does not receive any updates from the system for 3 minutes or longer.

Impact

The BIG-IP LCD screen does not display updated information.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP LCD screen does not display updated information.
  • The amber status LED blinks.

Resolution

Status

Workaround

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

To work around this issue, press any button on the LCD console on the front of the BIG-IP appliance to resume normal operation.
