Known Issue

Pool member state changes may impact BIG-IP system performance.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is running version 11.4.1 HF6.
  • Your BIG-IP system is configured to monitor pool members.
  • Your BIG-IP virtual server referencing the monitored pool is configured with source_addr persistence.
  • Connections are load balanced to the monitored pool.
  • The pool monitor detects continuous fluctuation (flapping) of pool member availability.

Impact

System CPU utilization may increase.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H499710 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High screen.
  • The CPU levels of the BIG-IP system may rise disproportionally to the volume of traffic that the system is processing.
  • When you view the BIG-IP persistence table, you observe many duplicate persistence entries.
  • In the  /var/log/ltm file, you may observe repeating log messages similar to the following examples:

    notice mcpd[5903]: 01070727:5: Pool /<partition>/<pool member name or ip> member /<partition>/<pool member name or ip>:<port> monitor status up. [ /<partition>/<monitor name>: up ]  [ was down for 0hrs:0mins:23sec ]
    notice mcpd[5903]: 01070638:5: Pool /<partition>/<pool member name or ip> member /<partition>/<pool member name or ip>:<port> monitor status down. [ /<partition>/<monitor name>: down ]  [ was up for 0hrs:0mins:37sec ]
    notice mcpd[5903]: 01070727:5: Pool /<partition>/<pool member name or ip> member /<partition>/<pool member name or ip>:<port> monitor status up. [ /<partition>/<monitor name>: up ]  [ was down for 0hr:0min:9sec ]
