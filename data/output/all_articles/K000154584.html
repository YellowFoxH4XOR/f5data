K000154584: Require management access on rSeries

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-09-03
Updated
2025-09-03
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000154584

Description

On F5OS appliances, including rSeries platforms, qkview files and other diagnostic data required for troubleshooting cannot be obtained if the BIG-IP system does not have a configured management IP address. The absence of a management IP prevents access to essential support tools.


Environment

Product: F5OS - Appliance


Cause

Diagnostic utilities like qkview rely on the management interface to operate. Without a management IP address, these tools are unable to collect or export troubleshooting information from the appliance.


Recommended Actions

Ensure that a management IP address is always configured on F5OS appliances to enable access to diagnostic and support tools.

Avoid operating rSeries appliances without a management IP, as this configuration limits troubleshooting and support capabilities.
