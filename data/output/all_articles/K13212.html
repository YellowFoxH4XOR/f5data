Topic

This article applies to BIG-IP 10.x. For information about other versions, refer to the following article:

  • K13213: SSL algorithms that are hardware accelerated (11.x - 12.x)

All Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) handshakes are accelerated in hardware on BIG-IP platforms that support version 10.x.

The following bulk cipher combinations are fully hardware accelerated version 10.x platforms:

Note: Crypto operations are offloaded by the hardware when the ciphers are fully accelerated.

BIG-IP 10.2.3 through 10.2.4

  • RC4-MD5
  • RC4-SHA
  • AES128-SHA
  • AES256-SHA
  • DES-CBC3-SHA
  • DES-CBC-SHA
  • EXP1024-RC4-MD5
  • EXP1024-RC4-SHA
  • EXP1024-DES-CBC-SHA
  • EXP-RC4-MD5
  • EXP-DES-CBC-SHA
  • AES128-SHA256
  • AES256-SHA256

BIG-IP 10.0.0 through 10.2.2

  • RC4-MD5
  • RC4-SHA
  • AES128-SHA
  • AES256-SHA
  • DES-CBC3-SHA
  • DES-CBC-SHA
  • EXP1024-RC4-MD5
  • EXP1024-RC4-SHA
  • EXP1024-DES-CBC-SHA
  • EXP-RC4-MD5
  • EXP-DES-CBC-SHA
