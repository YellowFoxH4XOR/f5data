Known Issue

iSession endpoints may fail to establish a communication channel.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • iSession endpoints are both multi-blade BIG-IP chassis systems.
  • At least one of the iSession endpoints is configured in a redundant pair.

Impact

The iSession tunnel fails to pass traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The iSession tunnel fails to pass traffic.
  • The standby BIG-IP system indicates that the iSession connection to the remote endpoint is down, although the active BIG-IP system indicates the connection is up.

Workaround

To recover from this issue, you can restart the mcpd process. To do so, type the following command from the BIG-IP command line:

Impact of workaround: Restarting the mcpd process interrupts all traffic processing on the BIG-IP system. You should perform this workaround during a maintenance window.

bigstart restart mcpd
