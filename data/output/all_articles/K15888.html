Known Issue

In rare occurrences, certain BIG-IP platforms may be susceptible to parity errors. This issue occurs on the following hardware platforms:

  • BIG-IP 10000s/10200v/10250v (D113)
  • VIPRION blade B4300 (A108)
  • VIPRION blade B4340N (A110)
  • VIPRION blade B2250 (A112)

Impact

The affected BIG-IP platform is unable to perform parity error recovery.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H493717 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Low screen.
  • In the /var/log/ltm file, you observe an error message similar to the following example:

    info bcm56xxd[1366]: 012c0016:6: unit 0 L2_ENTRY_ONLY entry 130152 parity error

Workaround

There is no work around for this issue. However, you may be able to mitigate hardware parity error-induced issues by restarting the affected BIG-IP system. To do so, reboot the system by typing the following command from the BIG-IP command line:

reboot

Impact of workaround: Rebooting the system may cause a failover event where high-availability is configured. F5 recommends that you perform this procedure during a scheduled maintenance window.
