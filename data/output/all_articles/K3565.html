Topic

The FirePass Administrative Console does not currently report installed hardware options. To confirm that your FirePass is equipped with an SSL accelerator card (non-FIPS), observe the system messages during bootup. Systems equipped with an accelerator card will display the following messages:

Nitrox/Nitrox-Lite driver ver 2.8
2Nitrox/Nitrox-Lite cores enabled.
