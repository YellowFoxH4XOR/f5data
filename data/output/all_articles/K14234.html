Known Issue

Creating a Security Assertion Markup Language (SAML) Identity Provider (IdP) connector using the BIG-IP IdP template creates an incorrect Single Sign On Service URL.

Impact

User authentication fails for SAML webtop objects.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP APM SAML IdP fails to authenticate users.
  • The IdP connector endpoint incorrectly has redirectpost instead of redirectorpost in the URL path.

Resolution

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can correct the Single Sign On Service URL path. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: None.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.
  2. Click Access Policy.
  3. Click SAML.
  4. Click the External IdP Connectors tab.
  5. Click the check box next to the name of the IdP connector to edit.
  6. Click Edit.
  7. Click Endpoint Settings.
  8. Replace the Single Sign On Service URL text portion redirectpost to redirectorpost.
  9. Click OK.
