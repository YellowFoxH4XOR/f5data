Cause

None

Recommended Actions

In order to confirm the Blade in the slot has gone bad, perform the following action:

Impact of procedure: Powering down and re-Seat the blade on a running system can interrupt traffic processing. You should perform this procedure during a maintenance window.

  1. Re-Seat the blade in the slot.
    • Remove the blade from the slot, and re-insert.
    • Check the status after the blade powers back up.
      • #tmsh show /sys cluster all-properties
    • If status is still "Slot Failed" then it may need replacing.

Additional Information

Slot positions in a BIG-IP Viprion 2400 Chassis

  • Upper left slot    (1)
  • Lower left slot    (2)
  • Upper right slot  (3)
  • Lower right slot  (4)
