Topic

This article outlines the requirements as well as service statistics locations for when you access the BIG-IP Analytics (AVR) data within the BIG-IQ system. For more information, refer to the BIG-IQ Centralized Management: Monitoring and Reports manual.

Note: For information about how to locate F5 product manuals, refer to K98133564: Tips for searching MyF5 and finding product documentation.

Description

The visibility of statistics in the BIG-IQ system depends on the version of your managed BIG-IP devices. BIG-IP devices running BIG-IP 13.1.x or earlier have limited statistics visibility support in the BIG-IQ system. For the statistics visibility to work, ensure that your managed BIG-IP devices, as well as your BIG-IQ setup, adhere to the following requirements.

managed BIG-IP device versionRequirements
BIG-IP 13.1.0.5 or later
  • Each managed BIG-IP device must have AVR provisioned.
  • The BIG-IQ system must provide access on Port 443 to receive AVR data.
  • There must be a Data Collection Device (DCD) configured to your BIG-IQ system.
  • Ensure that the licenses for your managed BIG-IP devices do not include Appliance Mode. A managed BIG-IP device running in Appliance Mode will not allow for statistics visibility in the BIG-IQ environment.
Prior to BIG-IP 13.1.0.5
  • Ports 22 and 443 on each managed BIG-IP device must be open for the BIG-IQ DCD to retrieve data.
  • There must be a Data Collection Device (DCD) configured to your BIG-IQ system.
  • Ensure that the licenses for your managed BIG-IP devices do not include Appliance Mode. A managed BIG-IP device running in Appliance Mode will not allow for statistics visibility in the BIG-IQ environment.
