  • Multiblade vCMP guest
  • GTM

Cause

  • Slot2 (secondary blade) connection became unstable.
  • Configuration changes made to the primary blade are not syncing as a result of ID1060145

Recommended Actions

Additional Information

ID1060145 being hit was due to the fix for the slot's instability, Root cause is unavailable.
