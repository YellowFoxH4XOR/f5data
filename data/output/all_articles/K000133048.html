K000133048: BIGIP VE license not operational after reboot when mgmt MAC is changing

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-03-17
Updated
2025-02-07
Applies to
  • Virtual Editions
  • Private Cloud
  • Public Cloud
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IP
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000133048

Description

Error in ltm.log:
"01070608:0: License is not operational (expired or digital signature does not match contents)." after reboot of BIG-IP licensed from BIG-IQ.

After Reboot MAC address of management interface changed which can be spotted by reviewing /var/log/cloud-init-output.log

 

ci-info: | mgmt | True | 10.1.1.100 | 255.255.255.0 | global | 00:15:5d:06:0a:24 |

ci-info: | mgmt | True | fe80::215:5dff:fe06:a24/64 | . | link | 00:15:5d:06:0a:24 |

ci-info: +--------+-------+----------------------------+---------------+--------+-------------------+

 ...

2023-02-12T00:05:56+01:00 test.localhost notice boot_marker : ---===[ HD1.1 - BIG-IP 16.1.3.3 Build 0.0.3 ]===---

ci-info: | mgmt | True | 10.1.1.100 | 255.255.255.0 | global | 00:15:5d:06:0c:0d |

ci-info: | mgmt | True | fe80::215:5dff:fe06:c0d/64 | . | link | 00:15:5d:06:0c:0d |

Environment

  • BIG-IQ CM
  • Licensing
  • BIG-IP

Cause

MAC address change after reboot caused License not operational

Recommended Actions

  • Verify why VE HOST is changing MAC address of Mgmt interface.
  • Revoke and assign license to BIG-IP device following Using BIG-IQ License Manager to remotely manage BIG-IP Virtual Edition licenses

 

Additional Information

None
