Description

 

DNSSEC DDOS attack - DNSSEC queries for random non-existent sub-domains are hitting BIG-IP.

 

<random-string>.example.com

 

BIG-IP is signing proof of non-existence records as a response which is putting stress on the system.

 

 

Environment

  • BIG-IP DNS
  • BIG-IP AFM
  • DNSSEC
  • DDOS Attack

 

Cause

DNSSEC DDOS Attack
