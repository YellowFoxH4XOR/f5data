Issue

The following error indicates that the operating system cannot communicate with the hard drive:

recal failed after controller reset: status 51 <rdy,seekdown,err> error 4<ab wd0>

A common cause for this error is a bad or loose IDE cable, or an overheated hard drive, in which case the operating system usually crashes or locks up before any serious damage occurs. In most cases, the hard drive is the reason for the error because it has so many moving parts and is therefore more likely to fail.

To troubleshoot this issue, verify that the room temperature is at a reasonable level and make sure that the BIG-IP system is not located near a heater, or beneath a halogen light. If the room and hard drive temperature is at an acceptable level, and the hard drive and cables are fine yet the error persists, contact F5 Support for further assistance.
