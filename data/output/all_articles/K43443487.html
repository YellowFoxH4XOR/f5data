Description

You may find that device group "datasync-device-<device_name>-dg" becomes "Not All Device Synced" status, and then it backs to "In Sync" status without any operations everyday.
You can also see the config-sync log for "datasync-device-<device_name>-dg" in /var/log/ltm.
 

Jan 16 19:12:35 bigip01 notice mcpd[xxxx]: 010714a0:5: Sync of device group /Common/datasync-device-bigip02-dg to commit id xxx 1234567890123456789 /Common/bigip02 0 from device /Common/bigip02 complete.

Environment

  • BIG-IP ASM
  • Configure HA
  • Check the Sync status of "datasync-device-<device_name>-dg"

Cause

It is by design. 
It is due to datasyncd key generation process. 
 

Recommended Actions

You can ignore it safely.
The sync status will be "In Sync" automatically after a while.

Additional Information

You can also check the log in /var/log/datasync/datasyncd.log.

DATASYNCD_CONF|NOTICE|Jan 16 19:12:32.010|xxxxx|datasyncd_conf.cpp:xxxx|force_sync_devicegroup: sent. device-group /Common/datasync-device-bigip02-dg
