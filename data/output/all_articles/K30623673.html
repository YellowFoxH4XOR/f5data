  • BIG-IP ASM: 15.X.X, 15.0.X, 15.1.8, 15.1.7, 15.1.6, 15.1.5, 15.1.4, 15.1.3, 15.1.X, 15.1.1, 15.1.0, 15.0.1, 15.0.0, 15.1.2
Source

Description

  • "Illegal host name" and "Host name mismatch" violations are missing from Learning and Blocking Settings

Environment

  • ASM license (not Advanced WAF)
  • Learning and Blocking Settings
  • ASM

Cause

  • "Illegal host name" and the "Host name mismatch" violations are only available with an Advanced WAF license

Recommended Actions

  1. Existing ASM, or BEST bundle customers, under a valid support contract running BIG-IP version 14.1 or greater can reactivate the licenses to upgrade to Advanced WAF (Adv WAF). If you are running a BIG-IP Virtual Edition you must have a V16 or a more recent license.
  2. Contact your F5 Account Team to request an Advanced WAF license for your device.
