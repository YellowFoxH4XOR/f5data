Known Issue

A pool member may remain in the Slow Ramp Time phase on VIPRION platforms.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a VIPRION hardware platform with intra-cluster mirroring enabled.
  • A pool is configured to load balance connections using the Ratio load balancing mode.
  • The system has the Slow Ramp Time feature enabled.

    Note: This is the default setting.
  • A pool member has recently become available for load balancing. For example, this condition may occur after a health monitor marks the pool member down and then up again, or if a new pool member has been added.

Impact

A pool member receives significantly fewer connections than other pool members.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • A pool member's current connection count remains significantly less than other pool members after becoming available for load balancing.
