K1485: Use of ports higher than 1028 on 3-DNS

Type
Knowledge
Published
2006-02-17
Updated
2023-02-21
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • Legacy Products
  • 3-DNS: 4.6.4, 4.6.4
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K1485

Topic

3-DNS may open ephemeral ports for outbound traffic from the 3-DNS Controller, such as big3d probing. In addition, 3-DNS may listen on high ports when named is configured to allow recursive queries.
