Question

I would like to test moving our traffic over to XC Distributed Cloud.

  • Is there a mechanism to test moving Routed traffic over?
  • Can we schedule that for a particular maintenance window?
  • Network routing test
  • Router advertisement testing

Environment

  • XC DDoS
    • Routed
  • XC Portal
    • Route Advertisement

 

Answer

Yes!

You can test without SOC intervention by advertising the route to us, provided that you have successfully configured GRE tunnels and have activated BGP peering via the Portal: Routed DDOS > Manage > Route Advertisement.

Unless you have already discussed and implemented Route Origination with the SOC, any prefix you advertise to us will be advertised to our carriers. With Route Origination, you can manage the advertisements yourself via the Portal: Routed DDOS > Manage > Route Advertisement. Note that the SOC has to make configurations for this to work properly.

If you want additional assistance, give the SOC 24-hour notice via XC Portal (Support > Contact Support) and include:

    • maintenance window
    • details of the test
    • ASN and prefix you wish to test
