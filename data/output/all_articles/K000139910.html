Source

Description

  • On a VIPRION appliance, the error 013a0019:5: Blade 4 turned Yellow: TMM not ready. The error can be found in the /var/log/ltm folder,
  • This error message still occurs after two or three reboots in the slot, it comes back for a few hours and then the problem reappears.
  • You may see tmm core files in the /shared/core directory.
  • You may also see the following error in /var/log/ltm:

01010025:2: Device error: hsb interface 19 prod_fifo_level 0x000000ce is nonzero after clear.

01010025:2: Device error: hsb dma check clear for hsb unit 19 failed

01230017:2: Unable to attach to PCI device 83:00.02 for Interface 4/0.20

01010025:2: Device error: hsb hsb21 could not initialize its dma engine

01010020:2: MCP bulk Connection aborted, exiting

01010020:2: MCP Connection aborted, exiting

01140045:5: HA reports tmm NOT ready

Environment

  • VIPRION

Cause

Possible faulty blade.

Recommended Actions

Please contact F5 support for further analysis.

Additional Information

None.
