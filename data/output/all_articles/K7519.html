Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Clearing the attack events information from the BIG-IP ASM database may not clear all of the data. As a result, the attack events information is not displayed in the Configuration utility. However, the attack events information is retained within the UCS configuration backup file.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR78355 and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM version 9.4.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM release notes.
