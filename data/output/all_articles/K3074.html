K3074: Differentiating between 300 TPS nCipher SSL Accelerators and 1600 TPS nCipher SSL Accelerators

Topic

F5 Networks ships two models of the nCipher nForce Secure SSL Accelerator PCI cards: 300 and 1600.

The nForce PCI 300 card provides SSL acceleration in BIG-IP systems running version 4.2 software.

The nForce PCI 1600 card provides SSL acceleration in BIG-IP systems running version 4.5 or later.

Important: The 1600 series cards will not function in a system running version 4.2 software.

A gray tamper resistant material covers the components on both cards. You can differentiate between the two models by performing the following procedure:

  1. Hold the card with the serial interface side facing left and the teeth pointing toward you.
  2. Look at the side of the card with the heat sink(s) to the right on top of the gray epoxy. If the card only has one heat sink, it is a 300 series card; if the card has more than one heat sink, it is a 1600 series card.

    Additionally, 300 series cards have a rectangular bump in the upper left corner of the epoxy area, and the 1600 series cards do not.
