Description
VIPRION chassis with two blades, one of which was marked offline. LEDs on the front of the blade were all red, and the management IP was unreachable.

Environment

  • VIPRION chassis with two blades
    • One blade marked offline
  • LEDs on the front of the blade were all red

Cause
None

Recommended Action
There are numerous strategies to test the hardware status of the blade. 

  • Confirm that the blade was properly seated and that the problem was specific to this blade by reseating the affected blade in a different slot.
  • Test /EUD/ capture information to verify hardware health of the blade. 
  • Confirm that the blade hadn't been administratively disabled and that the failure was in the blade rather than the slot.

Additional Information
Document the test and capture the console output of EUD/tests to assist with troubleshooting with F5 Support.
