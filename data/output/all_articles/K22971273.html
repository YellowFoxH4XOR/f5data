K22971273: Does base-mac address change on vCMP guests?

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-12-20
Updated
2024-12-20
Applies to
  • VIPRION Platforms
  • BIG-IP
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K22971273

Description

MAC Masquerade remains intact and does not change when vCMP guests are rebooted.  However, the base-mac changes according to the order in which vCMP guests are rebooted.

Environment

VCMP guests

  • Bladed (Viprion) & non-bladed appliances
  • MAC Masquerade
  • Base-mac

Cause

Big-IP systems, including vCMP hosts, contain a pool of available MAC addresses that can be assigned to interfaces, trunks, and Virtual Local Area Networks (VLANs). Base-MAC assignment may change after rebooting vCMP guests.  MAC Masquerade remains intact and does not change. 

Recommended Actions

The actions or steps required to address the topic covered in the description.

  1. Reboot the vCMP guests one at a time or in the order they were deployed.
  2. Clear the ARP cache on upstream devices after rebooting the VCMP guests.

Additional Information

Before rebooting the vCMP guest:

Base MAC: tmsh show sys hardware | grep -i "base mac"
  Base MAC       aa:bb:cc:dd:ee:ff

MAC Masquerade: tmsh show sys mac
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sys::MAC Address
Entry              Component              Object-ID                          Property
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
aa:bb:cc:f5:f5:f5  cm traffic-group       traffic-group-1                    mac
  
After rebooting the vCMP guest(s):

Base MAC: tmsh show sys hardware | grep -i "base mac"
  Base MAC       aa:bb:cc:dd:ff:ee
  
MAC Masquerade: tmsh show sys mac
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sys::MAC Address
Entry              Component              Object-ID                          Property
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
aa:bb:cc:f5:f5:f5  cm traffic-group       traffic-group-1                    mac
