Description

When using the Ratio (ratio-member) load balancing method in BIG-IP LTM, you may observe that new connections are not distributed evenly among pool members according to their configured ratios. Instead, connections may consistently be directed to a single pool member, even when all members are available and have the same ratio. This behavior is closely related to the lb_value assigned to each pool member.

Environment

  • Ratio load balance
  • lb_value

Cause

The lb_value is an internal metric used by BIG-IP to determine which pool member should receive the next new connection. When a new connection arrives, the system assigns it to the member with the lowest lb_value. Over time, the lb_value for each member increases as it receives connections, in proportion to its configured ratio.

However, if a pool member becomes unavailable (for example, due to a monitor failure), its lb_value stops increasing while the other members continue to accumulate higher values. When the unavailable member returns to service, its lb_value is much lower than the others. As a result, all new connections are directed to this member until its lb_value catches up, causing a temporary but significant imbalance in traffic distribution.

Resetting pool statistics does not reset the lb_value. Therefore, the imbalance can persist until the values naturally equalize through traffic distribution.

Recommended Actions

  1. How to check lb_value for pool:

    You can check the lb_value for each pool member using the following command:

    tmctl pool_member_stat pool_name=<pool_name> -s pool_name,addr,lb_value

    Example output:

    pool_name           addr                        lb_value
<pool_1>            <member_1_ip>               1000
<pool_1>            <member_2_ip>               100

    In this example, new connections will be assigned to the member with lb_value 100 until it catches up with the other member.

  2. How to reset the value for lb_value:

    To resolve the imbalance caused by lb_value discrepancies, you can reset the load balancing method for the pool. This will reset the lb_value for all members:

    1. Change the pool's load balancing method to another method (e.g., round-robin): 
      tmsh modify ltm pool <pool_name> load-balancing-mode round-robin
    2. Then change it back to ratio-member: 
      tmsh modify ltm pool <pool_name> load-balancing-mode ratio-member

    This action will reset the lb_value for all pool members, allowing the ratio load balancing to function as expected.

Additional Information

    • The lb_value mechanism is designed to ensure long-term adherence to the configured ratios, but temporary imbalances can occur after member failures or maintenance events.
    • Resetting pool statistics does not affect lb_value; only changing the load balancing method will reset it.
    • Always perform these changes during a maintenance window or with awareness of potential impact on active connections.
