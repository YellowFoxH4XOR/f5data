Known Issue

ePVA acceleration of IPv6 has been disabled on a VIPRION chassis with B2250 blades.

A firmware update in BIG-IP 11.6.0 inadvertently disabled the ability to enable ePVA acceleration for IPv6 virtual servers on the affected platform.

Impact

ePVA full acceleration cannot be enabled for IPv6 virtual servers.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • ePVA full acceleration cannot be enabled for IPv6 virtual servers.
