Chapter 0: Guide introduction and contents

Introduction

Generative AI refers to a class of artificial intelligence algorithms that can create unique content, such as text, images, and code. A large language model (LLM) is a specific type of generative AI that processes and generates human language. LLM applications are trained on large amounts of text and code, enabling them to understand and respond to language in a human-like way. Examples of LLM applications include chatbots, code development apps, content creation apps, language translation and localization apps, and virtual assistants, to name a few.

LLMs form the foundation for a wide range of innovative applications. For example, a healthcare application may use an LLM to analyze medical research and patient data to provide personalized treatment recommendations. By training the application on large amounts of information, the application helps doctors make more informed decisions and improve patient outcomes. Or consider a platform that allows users to create and interact with AI-powered virtual companions. The LLM enables the creation of realistic virtual personalities with unique backstories, interests, and conversational styles. These virtual companions can provide company, offer emotional support, and even assist with tasks such as learning new languages or planning trips.

As generative AI applications become more prevalent, IT stakeholders must determine how to manage security and privacy for their LLMs and related components such as APIs, applications, and data sources. F5 offers a portfolio with security measures that can help mitigate some LLM vulnerabilities and extensive measures that provide a defense-in-depth approach to help reduce the risks associated with OWASP Top 10 for LLMs.

About the OWASP Top 10 for LLMs

The OWASP Top 10 for LLMs (2025) defines the most serious security risks for LLMs. For more information, refer to the OWASP Top 10 for LLMs.
