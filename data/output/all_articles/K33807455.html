K33807455: How to exclude certificate expiry notification for SMTP in BIG-IQ

Source

Description
You have setup an SMTP Server and you want to exclude certificate expiry notification for SMTP in BIG-IQ

Environment

  • BIG-IQ Central Management
  • You specify an SMTP server so F5 BIG-IQ Centralized Management can send email alert
  • You want to exclude certificate expiry notification

Cause
Certificate expiry notification is enable by default

Recommended Actions

  1. At the top of the screen, click Monitoring
  2. On the left, click ALERTS & NOTIFICATIONS
  3. At the top of the screen, click the Settings button
  4. Under Device Alert Conditions look for "Certificate expiration"
  5. Unselect the Enabled check box for Certificate expiration
