K000150603: Client/Server SSL connection monitoring with SNMP OIDs sysClientsslStatTotConns5s, sysClientsslStatTotConns1m, and sysClientsslStatTotConns5m

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-03-28
Updated
2025-03-28
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.1.1
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000150603

Description

The SNMP OIDs sysClientsslStatTotConns5s, sysClientsslStatTotConns1m, and sysClientsslStatTotConns5m on BIG-IP are all gauges and not counters, and the values reflect the current maximum concurrent connections per second during the relevant time periods. 

For example, sysClientsslStatTotConns5m will show a value of 10 if the system sees 10 new unique connections per second over the past 5 minutes. The value will not show 3000 (ie. 10 * 60 * 5), which is what would be expected if it was showing a total number of unique connections over the past 5 minutes.

 

Environment

BIG-IP LTM

 

Cause

sysClientsslStatTotConns5s, sysClientsslStatTotConns1m, and sysClientsslStatTotConns5m are all gauges and not counters, and although it is not mentioned in the name, they return the max value of the number of connections per second over their respective time periods.

 

Recommended Actions

None.

 

Additional Information

Sample from F5-BIGIP-SYSTEM-MIB.txt:

3722 SYNTAX Gauge
3723 MAX-ACCESS read-only
3724 STATUS current
