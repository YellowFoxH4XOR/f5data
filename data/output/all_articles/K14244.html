Known Issue

Non-LACP 'Slow Protocol' Ethernet frames received by a BIG-IP interface are erroneously forwarded to the BIG-IP lacpd process.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • Your BIG-IP host receives non-LACP Slow Protocol type Ethernet frames

Impact

Error messages are benign, but may result in an increased size of log files.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You may see error messages in the /var/log/ltm file. The error messages appear similar to the following example, where <pid> is the unique process ID of the lacpd process running on your BIG-IP host, and <interface> is the switch interface port on which the BIG-IP received the Slow Protocol frame:

    info lacpd[<pid>]: 01160008:6: LacpSystem.cpp:324 - Invalid LACPDU received on <interface> with ethertype: 8809 and frameLen: 100

    info lacpd[<pid>]: 01160008:6: HalMsgHandler.cpp:267 - Unknown LACPDU from hal interface: <interface> port:425
