Known Issue

Log rotate may not prevent the system from overusing the /var/log partition.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is a Virtual Edition (VE).
  • A log file in /var/log exceeds 485 MB.

    Note: The value of max-file-size for the log rotate function is 1024000(K)=1 GB, where the allocated hard drive space for the /var/log partition on BIG-IP VE devices is 485 MB.

Impact

The /var/log hard drive partition becomes fully utilized.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Running the BIG-IP VE for an extended period of time with the /var/log partition fully utilized affects performance.
  • When configured for high availability (HA), the BIG-IP VE system fails over to the peer device.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 582127 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. Workaround

To work around this issue, you can increase the size of the /var/log partition. To do so, refer to K14952: Extending disk space on BIG-IP VE.

Impact of workaround: You must shut down the BIG-IP VE instance during the disk provisioning steps, and the system will not be available for traffic processing.
