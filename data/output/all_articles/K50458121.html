Policy Information

F5 product support engineers (PSEs) in F5 Support routinely exceed our customers' expectations. However, if you have expectations that differ from the practices F5 follows, it could lead to unnecessary frustrations. Not understanding how support cases are managed can lead to different expectations between you and the PSE managing your case.

This article provides insight into how F5 manages cases to better set and ultimately exceed your expectations for interactions with F5 Support. By providing you with insight into how F5 manages cases, F5 hopes to appropriately inform you about how to request alternative case management options when necessary.

This article applies to F5 Support cases for BIG-IP products with a valid Support contract. For information about the Support services available from F5, refer to Working with F5 Support Services.

Note: Service provided by F5 Partners may have different case management practices. If you obtain support through an F5 Partner, directly discuss support practices with the F5 Partner.

Note: F5 products other than BIG-IP (such as NGINX, Silverline, and so on) may have different product specific case management practices.

F5 Premium Support

Case ownership

F5 Premium Support is transactional (case-by-case). F5 attempts to limit each case to one specific issue.

Whenever possible, once a support case is assigned to a PSE, that PSE will manage it for the life of the case. This provides an efficient method of case management for F5 and you, our customers. Providing a single point of contact reduces information loss and reduces the need to request duplicate information due to different engineers working on a case.

If you have known interactions between two cases, be sure to mention that to the PSE assigned to your case. That way, F5 can associate cases in our system so that our PSEs know to check for the related case. Doing so helps reduce conflicting advice and prevents you from having to explain your issue multiple times.

F5 PSEs work a variety of schedules in many different geographic regions. F5 recommends that you take note of the shift schedule of the PSE assigned to your case, which is typically shown in their email signature. Please note that when you send an email regarding your support case, only the case owner is notified. If the PSE is off shift when the email arrives, they will review it when they return to work. If you call and the PSE assigned to your case is not available, they will be notified so that they can call you back when they are available. However, if the PSE does not return your call before their scheduled shift is over, or if you cannot wait for the PSE's return, you can request the next available PSE to assist you by calling F5 Support.

Transferring case ownership

If necessary, it is possible for F5 to transfer a case to a different PSE. This may happen in one of the following circumstances:

  • The assigned PSE's schedule is inconvenient or does not fit the times you need to work on your issue. In this case, you can request F5 to reassign the case to a PSE who is available during the times you require. For example, you may open a case in the evening and you want to work on it the next morning.
  • Situations requiring work to continue overnight or over a weekend may require an engineer to hand off the case to a PSE who is working those hours.

If you need to have a support case handed off to another PSE due to a scheduling issue, communicate this with the PSE assigned to your case so that they can request a transfer to an incoming PSE.

Communicating business impacts and scheduling requirements

To ensure that your support case is managed properly, make sure that the PSE assigned to your case is aware of any scheduling impacts or requirements you have. These may include the following:

  • Specific or tight schedule on a maintenance window
  • Specific management goals or timelines your organization must meet
  • Other changes made in the system while F5 is working an F5 issue

Preferred contact method

Ensure that your profile has your preferred contact method set, including your email and phone number.

If you want a specific support case to have a different preferred contact method, request that the PSE update it for that case.

Duty managers

If required, you can call in and ask for a duty manager. You may have to wait or request a callback. Note the following:

  • Duty managers do not watch individual cases on a regular basis.
  • If you are already working with an individual manager, try to directly contact that person. If they are unavailable, you may need to call in and ask for the duty manager on shift.

Case escalations

If your support case requires escalation to another level of support, the PSE assigned to your case will maintain ownership of the case and continue to be your point of contact.

When performing a technical escalation, F5 assigns an Engineering Services engineer who takes over the technical parts of the escalation that require research or technical depth beyond the PSE's scope. The PSE will continue to work on the case and, as needed, serve as the point of contact between you and the Engineering Services engineer as F5 Engineering Services engineers do not typically communicate directly with customers.

Part of a PSE's role is to be the primary point of contact with you. This division of labor enables Engineering Services and Product Engineering to focus on the testing and in-depth analysis that must take place in order to resolve complex issues. During escalation, F5 PSEs continue as the point of contact and manage the cases while also being responsible for providing customers with updates. If Engineering Services or Product Engineering need something during the escalation, they work with the PSE, who passes on requests for whatever else is needed from you. This allows Engineering Services and Product Engineering resources to continue working the issue while the PSE coordinates.

F5 devices encompass a vast range of technologies. A single individual often does not have all the knowledge required to fully support all aspects of the selection of products and features that are part of your deployment. Frequently, F5 will need to research questions before answering. Sometimes F5 needs a team to provide input when dealing with tough issues. Additionally, Engineering Services engineers need time to perform their deep technical dives and reproductions. For this reason, F5 enables the PSEs to be the front-end representative to combine all the input and write or speak to you to provide a clear answer or plan a course of action.

Premium Plus Support

You may want to consider enrolling in Premium Plus Support. Premium Plus Support is a relationship support model rather than a transactional support model like Premium Support.

  • Premium Plus Support uses a similar support case management model; however, Premium Plus cases are assigned to our Premium Plus engineers.
  • Premium Plus engineers are high-level support engineers who work on a limited number of specific accounts. As a result, your cases are more likely to be owned by a Premium Plus engineer who is familiar with and understands your specific account and deployment requirements.
