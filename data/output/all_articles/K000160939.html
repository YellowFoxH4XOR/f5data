Issue

Beginning in BIG-IP 21.1.0, CCM and CCM8 ciphers are removed from the DEFAULT cipher suite.

Old Behavior

In versions prior to BIG-IP 21.1.0, the DEFAULT cipher suite included the CCM and CCM8 cipher suites. When SSL profiles were configured to use DEFAULT, BIG-IP could negotiate CCM or CCM8 with peers that supported and selected those cipher suites.

New Behavior

Beginning in BIG-IP 21.1.0, the DEFAULT cipher suite no longer includes the CCM and CCM8 cipher suites. As a result, SSL profiles configured with DEFAULT will no longer negotiate CCM or CCM8 unless those cipher suites are explicitly configured in the cipher string, where supported.

The DEFAULT cipher suite intentionally only includes ciphers that have gone through extensive performance analysis. However, the CCM and CCM8 ciphers were erroneously added in BIG-IP 16.1. To maintain the highest standards for cryptographic security and performance, the CCM and CCM8 ciphers will no longer be included in the DEFAULT cipher suite, but will remain available on the BIG-IP as needed.

Impact

The connections may be impacted if their environments rely on CCM or CCM8 for TLS negotiations. In such cases, connections that previously succeeded using the DEFAULT cipher string may fail after upgrading, if no other mutually supported cipher suite is available between BIG-IP and the peer system.

Recommended Actions

If your environments currently rely on the CCM and CCM8 ciphers, and are applying these by virtue of the DEFAULT cipher suite, are encouraged to manually re-add these ciphers as required to the cipher string:

DEFAULT:AES128-CCM:AES128-CCM8:AES256-CCM:AES256-CCM8:ECDHE-ECDSA-AES128-CCM:ECDHE-ECDSA-AES128-CCM8:ECDHE-ECDSA-AES256-CCM:ECDHE-ECDSA-AES256-CCM8:DHE-RSA-AES128-CCM:DHE-RSA-AES128-CCM8:DHE-RSA-AES256-CCM:DHE-RSA-AES256-CCM8
