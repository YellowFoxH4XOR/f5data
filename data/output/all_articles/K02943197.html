Description

This article intend to solve an issue of wrong trunk status on VELOS platform
When migrating vCMP guests to VELOS tenants which have Vlan group configured with the following conditions:

  1. HA group is configured with two trunks on both Tenants (Active and standby).

  2. There is no connectivity in partition1 for one of the trunks, as port is down, LACP is not active, but still you can see that the trunk is on tenant which is configured on that partition

Environment

Relevant environmental factors specific to the topic

  • VELOS tenants
  • HA group is configured with two trunks on both Tenants (Active and standby).
  • Trunk is configured with LACP

Cause

This behavior is caused due to bug ID1076965: Incorrect link aggregation group (LAG) / trunk information passed to tenant

Recommended Actions

  1. Configure a new LAG in the the F5OS partition or change a LAG type from LACP to STATIC or from STATIC to LACP
  2. Reboot the traffic blades
