Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP GTM ZoneRunner utility does not allow the forward slash (/) character in zone names or resource record names. The forward slash character is necessary to add classless IN-ADDR.ARPA delegation as defined in RFC 2317.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR73425 and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.3.1 and 9.4.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM Release Notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.3.0-HF3 issued for BIG-IP 9.3.0. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the cumulative hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
