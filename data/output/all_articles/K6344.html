Topic

The SNMP OID sysStatTmSleepCycles reports the number of sleep cycles for traffic management CPU usage that are not in use by TMM. The numerical value for OID sysStatTmSleepCycles is .1.3.6.1.4.1.3375.2.1.1.2.1.43.0.

TMM must share CPU resources with other processes—when TMM is in a sleep cycle, other processes are free to use the CPU. As a result, the OID sysStatTmSleepCycles only applies to single processor systems such as the BIG-IP 1000, 1500, and 3400.

In multiple processor systems, the second CPU is dedicated entirely to TMM and does not have to share any cycles with other processes. Under these conditions, the SNMP OID sysStatTmSleepCycles will always report 0 (zero).
