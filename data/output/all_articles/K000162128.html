F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) for the Field Upgrade, Copper SFP+ 10G Direct Attach 8-Pack 3M, effective January 1, 2027.

Customers are encouraged to transition to the recommended optical module, F5-UPG-SFP+-R, which provides a more stable solution for 10G connectivity.

F5 provides Advanced Hardware Replacement (RMA) and Technical Support for this product until the End of Life (EoL) date, provided you maintain an active support contract.
