Security Advisory Description

Eight new vulnerabilities in Intel processors have been mentioned in several sources and are referred to collectively as Spectre-NG. F5 is aware of these vulnerabilities and is investigating as information becomes available. As Intel officially recognizes and announces the vulnerabilities, they will be added to the following table.

To locate relevant articles for these vulnerabilities, refer to the following table. If no article is listed in the Article column, an article has not yet been published. F5 Technical Support has no additional information on these issues.
