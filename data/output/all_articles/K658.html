Known Issue

This is a result of a known issue. BIG-IP does not include a scroll bar for the Symbolic Link Generation page. If your configuration includes more client certificates than can be displayed on one page, you cannot see the Completed message, or any other progress messages, because they appear below the viewable region of the page.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR34056 and CR41851.
