Topic

Use this document when configuring or troubleshooting IPsec and you need to verify the settings of an IPsec tunnel between a BIG-IP system and a third-party device.

Description

IPsec connectivity issues result when there is a configuration mismatch between the IPsec endpoints. If you are in the process of configuring or troubleshooting an IPsec tunnel between a BIG-IP system and a third-party device, you should verify that the BIG-IP settings match the corresponding IPsec settings on the third-party device at the other end of the tunnel. Before opening a support case with F5 Technical Support, you should collect the following data from the BIG-IP system and the third-party IPsec device:

  • From each BIG-IP device, collect a QKView file.
  • From the third-party IPsec peer, collect the following data:
    • The make, model, and version of the IPsec peer.
    • The complete IPsec configuration.

Note: For information about configuring IPsec for the third-party device at the other end of the tunnel, refer to the manufacturer's documentation for your device.

Prerequisites

  • Administrator access to the BIG-IP.
  • The ipsec-policy uses "transport" or "tunnel" mode.
    • While the settings mentioned in this document mostly apply to "interface" mode as well, there is no description of the tunnel objects that also need to be made for "interface" mode to work.
    • Interface mode tunnels are referred to as "route based" tunnels by some vendors.

Procedures

Verifying the IPsec settings between the BIG-IP system and the third-party device

To verify that your Internet Key Exchange (IKE) peer IPsec settings match those from the third-party device, refer to the following tables:

  • IKE peers version 1
  • IKE peers version 2
  • IPsec policy
  • Traffic selectors

Impact of procedure: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.
