Topic

When cookie persistence is enabled, BIG-IP inserts or modifies a cookie that has a specific name and format. You cannot rename a cookie or modify its attributes, such as Comment, Domain, Max-Age, Path, Secure, or Version.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this request as CR26137.
