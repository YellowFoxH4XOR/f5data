Topic

Customers that use APM Webtops and have Users with visual disabilities, may require to use some type of Screen reader software, such as JAWs, NVDA, and ChromeVox.

With the Release of 15.1, APM has introduced a new Type of Desktop Customization called Modern.

The new Modern Customization does support ADA Compliance.

There is no way to convert a Policy from Standard to Modern, so the only way you can do this, is to rebuild a Policy that uses Modern Customization
