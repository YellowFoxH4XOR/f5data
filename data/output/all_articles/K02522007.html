Description

On the Google Cloud, HA failover, but the service traffic does not come to the active device.

Environment

  • Cloud platform like Google
  • HA

Cause

The system account "admin" was disabled or removed.

Recommended Actions

Perform the workaround in K39460110: Automation Toolchain Extensions, FAST, CFE Will Not Work if the admin Account is Disabled.
