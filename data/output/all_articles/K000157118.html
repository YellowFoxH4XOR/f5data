Description

The command tmsh show sys hardware output do not display the serial number or model information for the power supply unit (PSU) on F5 VIPRION C4480 / C4800 chassis with B4450 blades. When attempting to retrieve PSU details for inventory or RMA purposes, no PSU serial number / model is shown in system outputs or on physical PSU labels for these platforms.

Environment

  • VIPRION C4480 / C4800
  • PSU
  • tmsh show sys hardware

Cause

This is by design. Certain VIPRION platforms, including C4480 and C4800 chassis with B4450 blades, do not provide PSU serial numbers due to hardware and firmware limitations. The PSU for these platforms does not have a serial number on physical labels.

Recommended Actions

  • For RMA or field replacement processes, use "XXXXXX" in the FRU Serial Number field when a PSU serial number is required for C4480/C4800 platforms.
  • No further action is required; this is expected behavior for the affected hardware.
