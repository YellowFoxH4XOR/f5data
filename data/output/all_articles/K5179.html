Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Although pool names consisting of only of numerals are valid, the 3dpipe command utility will report a parse error when it encounters such numeral pool names.

To avoid this issue, do not use pool names consisting of only numerals.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR20183.
