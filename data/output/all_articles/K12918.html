Note: This announcement does not apply to ARX VE Trial Ware 5.3.0. For information about the End of Software Development (EoSD) for ARX VE Trial Ware 5.3.0, refer to K12922: End of Software Development for ARX VE Trial Ware 5.3.x.

F5 is announcing that the End of Software Development (EoSD) for ARX 5.x is effective August 1, 2013 in accordance with the guidelines contained within K8986: F5 product support policies and K8466: ARX software support policy.

As of August 1, 2013 F5 no longer provide security updates, software fixes (hotfixes), or consulting services for ARX 5.x. Additionally, service contracts enter the Extended Support phase beginning with the EoSD in accordance with the guidelines contained within K4309: The F5 platform lifecycle support policy.

During the Extended Support phase, F5 offers only configuration support and Return Materials Authorization (RMA) services. Defect correction is not provided. F5 may offer a customer access to certain software updates, which may include security or stability related software releases.

All configuration support for ARX 5.x during the Extended Support phase is considered reasonable effort. 'Reasonable effort' is defined to include technical phone support and hardware support, and is limited to system configuration support. No response time targets, guarantees, or SLAs shall apply. No escalations to Engineering Services or Product Development shall be allowed. RMA support is included, as long as the platform has not exceeded its End of Technical Support (EoTS) or End of RMA (EoRMA) support date

The effective EoTS and EoRMA date is August 1, 2014 for the ARX 5.x software, completing its End of Life (EoL) cycle.

Customers should view the corresponding software release notes to determine if a particular software release is suitable for their platform or software version. Access to and compatibility of these software updates are not guaranteed and is made under F5's reasonable effort support guidelines.

Additionally, if a platform is not compatible with the latest General Sustaining (GS) software release, the platform may be restricted from operating that version through the F5 software licensing process.
