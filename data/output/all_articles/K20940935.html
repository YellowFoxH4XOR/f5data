Description

When creating a  TXT record with a special character you receive message "Bad Characters. Only the following special characters are allowed: period, asterisk, forward slash, dash, colon, underscore, question mark, equals, at sign, comma, ampersand and double quote (.*/-:_?=@,&")"

Environment

  • BIG-IP DNS (formally known as GTM)
  • ZoneRunner
  • TXT records

Recommended Actions

  1. To create the TXT record with a special character that doesn't match (.*/-:_?=@,&") create the TXT record with double quotes.
          Example: "v67SgyzOv9xfHn0ZOqOCQwvqbrOY+vrg=" 

          Note there is a "+" character which won't save unless you add double quotes at the beginning and end of the TXT record.

Additional Information

As a best practice, it's recommended to always use double quotes when creating a TXT record.
