New Behavior

Beginning in BIG-IP 15.1.0, Client and Server SSL profile statistics provide additional columns: Offload and Software. SSL Hardware Acceleration has been changed to SSL Handshake with just Software and Offload counts.

The new behavior enables you to determine how the BIG-IP system handles various SSL requests for a given cipher or algorithm.

Depending on your BIG-IP system, you can also view SSL handshake statistics and see whether the hardware or software handled them.

Note: The SSL Handshake Offload statistic is incremented only for BIG-IP systems with a Cavium Nitrox SSL acceleration card, and in cases where the full SSL handshake is offloaded to the Cavium card. For more information, refer to K75983426: SSL Hardware Acceleration statistics show Partial Acceleration instead of Full Acceleration.
