Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Installing version 9.4.x on a BIG-IP 1000, 2400, 5100, or 5100 platform causes the amber warning LED to turn on (and stay on indefinitely). This is a fault in the 9.4 software branch and does not indicate a warning event. Attempting to turn off the LED does not cause any change in the LED.

This issue is cosmetic; ignore the warning LED on the BIG-IP platforms mentioned in this article.

Since the BIG-IP 1000, 2400, 5100, and 5100 platforms have reached their End of New Software Support (EoNSS) and End of Software Development (EoSD) dates, F5 Product Development will not address this issue.

Note: For more information, refer to K4309: The F5 platform lifecycle support policy.
