K000162829: BIG-IP remains Offline because of ASM security policy restoration delays

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-08-07
Updated
2026-08-26
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000162829

Description

A BIG-IP tenant can remain Offline and ASM policy restoration and subsystem initialization may delay the transition to a healthy state.

Environment

  • BIG-IP ASM policies configured and active
  • BIG-IP is in the Offline state
  • Software upgrade

Cause

BIG-IP ASM restores active security policies sequentially. Initialization was delayed while policies are restored.

Recommended Actions

  • Monitor the BIG-IP ASM and system logs while policies are restored.
  • Allow sufficient time for BIG-IP ASM policy restoration and service initialization to complete.

Additional Information

  • BIG-IP ASM policy restoration can take additional time when multiple active policies are present.
