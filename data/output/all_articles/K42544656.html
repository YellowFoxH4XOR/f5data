Known Issue

User defined Advanced Filters configured for Analytics reports may not be saved when you are performing an upgrade.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured custom Analytics reports using Advanced Filters on the ASM Charts pages.
  • You perform an upgrade from an affected version.
  • You boot into the newly upgraded boot location and roll forward the configuration.

Impact

Previously saved user defined Advanced Filters used for ASM Charts reports are not present following an upgrade.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • User defined Advanced Filters used for ASM Charts reports are not present after you perform an upgrade.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can use drill downs rather than filters to view the information you want to see.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
