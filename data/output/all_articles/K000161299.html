Description

This article explains how to check if you have the rights to open a support case for a specific serial number.

Environment

  • BIG-IP (including VE and hardware platforms) 
  • BIG-IQ
  • rSeries
  • VELOS
  • NGINX

Cause

None

Answer/Recommended Actions

To verify your rights and entitlement:

  1. Log in to the MyF5 portal at my.f5.com.
  2. Click on "Support" and select "Create a Case."
  3. Enter the serial number of your device.
  4. Click "Next."
  • If you are able to click "Next" after entering the serial number, this means you have the necessary rights to open a support case and there is valid support entitlement for that serial number.
  • If you are not able to proceed past the serial number entry, you may not have the required rights, or the serial number does not have valid support entitlement.
