K36551228: BIG-IQ support for SHA and AES algorithms with SNMPv3

Published

Applies to

Source

Description

Does BIG-IQ version 8.2 support SHA-256 and AES-192 or AES-256 with SNMPv3?

Environment

BIG-IQ v8.2.

Cause

N/A.

Recommended Actions

BIG-IQ 8.x still runs Net-SNMP version 5.7.2. Please refer to https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K15861#v8.

SHA-256, AES-192, AES-256 are supported starting 5.8.

http://www.net-snmp.org/wiki/index.php/Strong_Authentication_or_Encryption.

Additional Information

None.
