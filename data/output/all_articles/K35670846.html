Known Issue

Citrix applications may intermittently fail to render on BIG-IP APM webtops.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system is integrated with Citrix XML Brokers, and Citrix applications are published on the BIG-IP APM webtop.
  • Your BIG-IP APM access profile is configured to use Kerberos single sign-on (SSO).

Impact

Citrix applications intermittently fail to start from the BIG-IP APM webtop.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • After authenticating to the BIG-IP APM system, Citrix applications intermittently fail to start from the BIG-IP APM webtop.
  • Log messages similar to the following example appear in the /var/log/ltm file:

    err tmm[<pid>]: 011f0007:3: http_process_state_ingress_data - Invalid action EV_PREPEND_HEADERS during ST_HTTP_INGRESS_DATA (Server side: vip=<partition>/<vip_name> profile=http server_ip=<server_ip>)
