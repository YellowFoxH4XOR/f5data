Known Issue

Modifying an in-use rate class may prevent BIG-IP LTM from applying the rate shaping feature to existing connections.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a rate class associated with a virtual server.
  • You modify an in-use rate class.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may experience traffic flows outside the bounds of the rate shaping limits set.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Existing connections no longer have the rate shaping feature applied.
