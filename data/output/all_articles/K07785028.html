Known Issue

BIG-IQ Virtual Edition (VE) guests may fail to startup on Hyper-V hypervisors.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You attempt to deploy a BIG-IQ VE guest on a Microsoft Hyper-V hypervisor.
  • The VE guest's virtual hard disk (VHD) has not completed the full first-time initialization. 
  • The VE guest's IDE CD/DVD is not configured to mount a valid media source.

Impact

The BIG-IQ VE guest fails to startup.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IQ VE guest fails to startup.
  • At the VE guest console, you notice messages similar to the following example:

    udevadm settle - timeout of 180 seconds reached, the event queue contains:
       /sys/devices/pci0000:00/0000:00:05.0/ide1/1.0/block/hdc (345)

    udevd[567]: worker [789] failed while handling '/devices/pci0000:00/0000:00:05.0
    /ide1/1.0/block/hdc'

    Setting up Logical Volume Management

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can either ensure the BIG-IQ VE guest's IDE CD/DVD is configured to mount a valid media source, or perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Connect to the Hyper-V hypervisor.
  2. Shut down the BIG-IQ VE guest.
  3. Remove the IDE CD/DVD IDE device from the guest configuration.
  4. Start the BIG-IQ VE guest again and allow the VHD to complete first-time initialization.
  5. Add the CD/DVD device back to the guest configuration.
  6. Restart the BIG-IQ VE guest.
