K52661028: VCMP Host may show OFFLINE if it is placed in a device group

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-05-02
Updated
2023-02-21
Applies to
  • BIG-IP Platforms
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K52661028

Description

VCMP Host hoses OFFLINE in the GUI and the Guests are also offline

  • VCMP host is not available 
  • HA status indicator shows DISCONNECTED; OFFLINE
  • Logs are listed in /var/log/ltm
Apr 20 10:01:55 somedevice.com  notice sod[4666]: 01140041:5: Killing /usr/bin/tmm pid 11951.
Apr 20 10:01:55  somedevice.com warning sod[4666]: 01140029:4: HA daemon_heartbeat tmm fails action is go offline down links and restart.
Apr 20 10:01:55  somedevice.com notice sod[4666]: 010c0050:5: Sod requests links down.
Apr 20 10:01:55  somedevice.com notice sod[4666]: 010c0054:5: Offline for traffic group traffic-group-1.
Apr 20 10:01:55 somedevice.com notice sod[4666]: 010c003e:5: Offline

Environment

Relevant environmental factors specific to the topic

  • VCMP Host has been placed in an HA device group
  • Device Management ------ Device Groups
  • VCMP

Cause

HA High Availability is not supported for VCMP host devices.

Recommended Actions

The actions or steps required to address the topic covered in the description.

  1. Reset the device trust to remove the HA configuration
    1. Device Management  ››  Device Trust : Local Domain    Click on "Reset Device Trust
  2. SSH to the device command line and run:    bigstart restart vcmpd
