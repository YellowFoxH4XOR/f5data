Description

Several pool members are constantly flapping. Seeing Monitor instance <monitor-name> <IP:port> UP --> DOWN from <IP> (state: timeout) in logs.

  • GTM keeps marking certain servers down.
  • While the actual nodes behind the LTM are up and the LTM doesn't mark them down at any time.
  • Monitor instance <monitor-name> <IP:port> UP --> DOWN from <IP> (error) in /var/log/gtm
  • Only one or two IPs are marking them down.

Environment

  • BIG-IP GTM/DNS monitoring LTM pools
  • BIG-IP LTM

Cause

If only one or two IPs are marking the pools down, while others are marking them up, can be due to a misconfiguration.

Recommended Actions

The actions or steps required to address the topic covered in the description.

  1. From the logs find out which IPs are responsible for marking them down. This can be done by checking /var/log/gtm
  2. After finding the offending IPs, investigate why these IPs are marking the pool down.
  3. iQuery connections are recommended to be established on self-IPs only and not management IPs.
  4. If there is an iQuery established over management IP, reconfigure the iQuery to be established over Self-IP. For more details refer to K13312.
