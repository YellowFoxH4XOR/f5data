Known Issue

The tmrouted process may continually restart when it fails to resolve the interface index for a Virtual Local Area Network (VLAN), VLAN group, or tunnel.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is configured with dynamic routing.
  • Your BIG-IP system has either of the following:
    • A VLAN, VLAN group, or tunnel in a non-default partition.
    • A VLAN, VLAN group, or tunnel with a name greater than 15 characters in length.

Impact

Dynamic routing fails to function. Routed monitors may not function properly and may incorrectly mark pool members down.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • In the /var/log/ltm log file, you observe an error message similar to the following example:

    err tmrouted[<pid>]: 01910201:3: failed to get interface index for /Partition/vlan10 in rd 1

  • The tmrouted process restarts.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can shorten the VLAN, VLAN group, or tunnel name to be less than 15 characters in length, or move the interfaces in to the Common partition.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
