Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When using the ping command to send ICMP echo request messages from a VLAN with a trunk containing interfaces on the same blade, to a VLAN with a trunk containing cross-blade interfaces, the initial ping replies are duplicated. Subsequent pings from the single-blade VLAN do not cause duplicated ICMP replies.

When sending a ping request from the VLAN with cross-blade interfaces to the single-blade VLAN, no duplicate ICMP replies are generated.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR79205.
