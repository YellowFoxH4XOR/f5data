Description

This issue is caused by a race condition when you remove licenses from devices in the BIG-IQ WebGUI. As a result, removed licenses appear multiple times in the assignment page.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ running 6.x and 5.x with Tokumon database
  • User deletes licenses from devices through BIG-IQ GUI

Cause

This condition may be caused  by Database indexing (toku) corruption 

Recommended Actions

To work around this issue, you can restart tokumond daemon. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround:  F5 recommends that you perform the following procedure during a scheduled maintenance window. This procedure may take 1-2 hours and the BIG-IQ will not be available during this time. Restarting  tokumond process from command line will trigger the database to re-indexed.

         #bigstart kill tokumond

Now, wait for 1-2 hours to ensure procedure is completed and no messages are logged to /var/log/restjavad.log.
