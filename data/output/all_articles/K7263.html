Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP system uses the postfix mail system to deliver locally generated email messages. When changing the time zone from PST to another time zone, the postfix control program keeps a stale copy of time zone information. As a result, if the BIG-IP system is configured to send SNMP trap alerts as email notifications, the email alerts will be sent with a PST timestamp.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR60976 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM version 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.
