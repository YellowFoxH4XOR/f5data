Known Issue

Rebooting a BIG-IP device may result in a Not All Devices Synced sync status.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • You configure the BIG-IP systems in a redundant pair with an In Sync sync status.
  • You create or modify an object on one of the devices (BIG-IP_A), and synchronize the object to the other device (BIG-IP_B).

After you reboot the device (BIG-IP_A), the system displays a Not All Devices Synced sync status, and recommends synchronizing the configuration to the group. The other device (BIG-IP_B) continues to display the In Sync sync status. Attempting to synchronize the rebooted BIG-IP system's configuration to the group does not change the sync status discrepancy.

Impact

The incorrect sync status may cause confusion.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP device group members may show a different sync status. For example, the rebooted device may show a Not All Devices Synced sync status, while the non-rebooted device may show an In Sync sync status.
  • The sync status does not change after performing additional synchronizations.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can force a ConfigSync operation from the rebooted device. To do so, perform one of the following procedures:

Impact of workaround: The following workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.

BIG-IP 11.2.1 through 11.3.0

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility of the rebooted device.
  2. Navigate to Device Management > Overview. 
  3. In the Device Group section, click the device group name
  4. In the Devices section, click the device name.
  5. Click Sync device to group.
  6. Click Overwrite Configuration.
  7. Click Sync.

BIG-IP 11.2.0

  1. Log in to the command line of the rebooted device.
  2. Type the following command:

    Note: The following command temporarily designates the device group member as the synchronization leader and forces the device to initiate a ConfigSync operation.

    tmsh modify cm device-group device-group-failover devices modify { device_A {set-sync-leader}}
