Description

You would like additional information regarding the usage of the OID ltmDnsCacheStatFailureSend that is located in F5-BIGIP-LOCAL-MIB.txt.

Since v14.x, ltmDnsCacheStatFailureSend is no longer visible in the WebUI or TMSH.

ltmDnsCacheStatFailureSend is "response send failure" that is triggered when out of TMM memory while trying to resolve and sending out queries.
