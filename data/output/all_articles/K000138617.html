Description

In BIG-IP Next Central Manager version 21.0, the Security menu has a sub-menu for SSL Orchestrator.  SSL Orchestrator is Early Access but the GUI does not indicate this.

Environment

  • BIG-IP Next Central Manager
  • Viewing the Security menu

Cause

The GUI does not indicate that SSL Orchestrator is Early Access.

Recommended Actions

SSL Orchestrator is Early Access and is not supported by F5 Support.  You should contact your Account Team if you wish to discuss SSL Orchestrator.

Additional Information

The BIG-IP Next SSL Orchestrator functionality is currently in the Early Access period and thus is not under full F5 support. Learn more about early access features at K000135952: About Early Access .
