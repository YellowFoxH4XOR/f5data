Description

By default, UCS files backed up from managed BIG-IP devices are stored to /shared/ucs_backups local to BIG-IQ.

On rare occasions, the /shared/ucs_backups mount point residing on the /dev/md17 filesystem for a BIG-IQ 7000 Series Platforms may fail to mount.

Note: Under normal circumstances, the /shared/ucs_backups mount point residing on the /dev/md17 filesystem should always be mounted for BIG-IQ 7000 Series Platforms. 

If the /dev/md17 filesystem mount point of /shared/ucs_backups fails to mount, scheduled UCS backup files from managed BIG-IP devices will instead be stored to the ucs_backups subdirectory located under the /shared mount point of the /dev/md1 filesystem. 
Please reach out to F5 Support for assistance if this issue or similar symptoms are encountered

Environment

  • BIG-IQ 7000 Series Platform
  • Managed BIG-IP
  • Backup Management
  • /shared/ucs_backups

Cause

Unknown

Recommended Actions

  1. Manually mount the /shared/ucs_backups mount point residing on the /dev/md17 filesystem by typing the following command.

    # mount -t ext3 /dev/md17 /shared/ucs_backups
Additional Information

BIG-IQ UCS storage structures differ between BIG-IQ hardware (7000 Series) and Virtual Edition (VE) platform types. 

Virtual Editions consists of a single partition of /shared with the subdirectory of ucs_backups:
