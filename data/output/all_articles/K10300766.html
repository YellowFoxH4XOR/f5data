Description

You created a custom certificate bundle in System  ››  Certificate Management : Traffic Certificate Management : Bundle Manager List and want to use it for your SSLO topology

However, when you navigate to SSL Orchestrator  ››  Configuration  ››  SSL Configurations  ››  ssloT_<SSL-configuration>, you notice that the custom certificate bundle is not available for selection in the Trusted Certificate Authority drop-down menu.

Note: ssloT_<SSL-configuration> is the name of your SSL configuration

Environment

  • custom certificate bundle
  • SSL profile
  • Trusted Certificate Authority

Cause

This is functioning as designed. SSL Orchestrator does not show objects under System ›› Certificate Management : Traffic Certificate Management : Bundle Manager List but only under System ›› Certificate Management : Traffic Certificate Management : SSL Certificate List.

Normal operation is to update the certificate bundle as below:

When you create or update the Certificate Bundle you must enter an "Update Interval" value and/or enable the "Update Now" checkbox and then click the 'Update' button at the bottom of the Certificate Bundle properties page.

Following the update, the certificate bundle name will be listed under System ›› Certificate Management : Traffic Certificate Management : SSL Certificate List and that is the list of objects that SSL Orchestrator will render in its configuration page.

Recommended Actions

1. Navigate to System  ››  Certificate Management : Traffic Certificate Management : Bundle Manager List and click on the desired Bundle
2. Tick the Update Now checkbox and then click the Update button
3. Navigate to System  ››  Certificate Management : Traffic Certificate Management : SSL Certificate List, you should now see the custom Bundle you created, in there.
4. The custom certificate bundle is now available for selection under the Trusted Certificate Authority drop-down menu in the SSLO Configuration.
