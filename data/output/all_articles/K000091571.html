Topic

Use this article when you encounter any of the following issues working with support cases on MyF5:

  • Issues opening a new support case

Note: As of November 27, 2024, F5 Distributed Cloud Services (F5 XC Services) and Distributed Cloud Bot Defense customers can create and view support cases using MyF5. Distributed Cloud Bot Defense customers can also edit cases using MyF5; however, that capability is not yet available for F5 XC Services customers. For assistance, refer to F5 Distributed Cloud Services Support Process or call F5 Support for help.

Issues opening a new support case

SituationPossible causesResolution
You do not see your products in the Create a Case form.Your product serial number is not associated with your MyF5 account.Contact F5 Support by doing one of the following:
  • Use live chat, available in the lower right on MyF5.

    Note: You must log in to MyF5 to use chat.

  • Call F5 Support
