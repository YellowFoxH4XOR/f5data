Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may produce a core file and restart when you use the gateway failsafe feature.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You configure the gateway failsafe feature with the following settings:
    • You enable min-up-members-checking.
    • You set min-up-members to 0.

Impact

The BIG-IP system temporarily fails to process traffic as it recovers from the TMM restart, and devices configured as a high-availability (HA) pair may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP system unexpectedly fails over to another system in the device group.
  • In the /shared/core directory, you observe a TMM core file.

Workaround

To work around this issue, set min-up-members to greater than 0 when you use gateway-failsafe-device.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
