Known Issue

The BIG-IP APM apd service may exit and produce a core file after you delete a pool.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You create an access policy where a policy agent references a pool.
  • You manually delete the referenced pool.
  • Users attempt to establish an access session that is processed by the pool-dependent access policy.

Impact

Users are unable to establish access sessions.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The apd process exits and generates core files to the /shared/core directory.
  • The BIG-IP system fails over to the standby system, if configured as part of a high-availability system.
  •  In the /var/log/apm file, you may observe log messages similar to the following example:
