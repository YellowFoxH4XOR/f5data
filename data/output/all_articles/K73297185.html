Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may display an aborted sync status.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have four or more BIG-IP devices configured in a Sync-Only device group.
  • The BIG-IP devices are divided into Sync-Failover device group pairs.
  • You manually initiate a synchronization.

Impact

The BIG-IP device group members report an incorrect sync status, even though the device group configuration synchronizes across the device group members. No configuration is lost as a result of this issue.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The device group member with the modified configuration reports a sync status of Sync initiated.
  • The other devices in the device group report a sync status of Aborted.
  • When reviewing the individual devices, the configuration synchronization (ConfigSync) does update the configuration.
  • The Sync initiated sync status does not clear, requiring you to manually cancel the synchronization.
