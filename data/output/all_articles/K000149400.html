K000149400: F5OS A-1.8.0 upgrade takes up to 10 additional minutes to complete

Description

You may observe an increased time when upgrading your F5OS-A system; this can be up to 10 additional minutes. 

 

Environment

  • F5OS-A
  • Upgrade

 

Cause

ID 1624629 



 


Recommended Actions


None, informational only


 


Additional Information


 


No functional impact. Upgrade takes more time but improves the upgrade experience. F5OS-A 1.8.0 has implemented a controlled method for upgrading firmware to enhance the stability of the process. While the firmware upgrade is in progress, the services bring-up is kept on hold, which can result in an increased upgrade time by up to 10 minutes. The exact duration may vary depending on the platform and the types of firmware versions involved. Although the upgrade process takes longer, this approach improves the overall upgrade experience by ensuring greater stability.
