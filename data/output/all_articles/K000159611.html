Description

BIG-IP R5600 equips SSD mechanically mounted that not accessible by the administrator.

BIG-IP R5600 does not have a field replaceable SSD. 

SKU "F5-UPG-SSD-R5XX" is only for use in FIPS supported products BIG-IP R5920-DF.
