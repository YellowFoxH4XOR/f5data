Known Issue

Use of a BIG-IP APM visual policy editor macro may create an inaccessible access policy.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • An access policy is created using the macro Client Classification and Prelogon Checks.

Impact

Users are unable to successfully log on.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP APM access policy does not correctly detect that anti-virus is installed and running on user devices.
  • All users attempting to access the BIG-IP APM access policy are processed with the access policy fallback method.
  • Users attempting to log in to the access policy may see a browser message similar to the following example:

    Access was denied by the access policy.
    This may be due to a failure to meet access policy requirements.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can correct the macro-created AV settings. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

Modifying the macro-created Antivirus settings

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.
  2. Navigate to Access Policy > Access Profiles > Access Profiles Lists.
  3. Open the visual policy editor by clicking Edit to the right of the Access Profile name.
  4. Click the name Antivus of the Antivirus box.
  5. Click Add new entry.

    Leave the entry settings at their defaults (Any) or select the appropriate Platform, Vendor ID, Product ID, State, Version, Engine Version, and DB Version for your environment.

  6. Click Save.
  7. Click the name Antivus of the Antivirus box.
  8. Click the Branch Rules tab.
  9. Click the word change in the Successful branch.
  10. Click the Advanced tab.
  11. In the Advanced text box replace the existing syntax with the following syntax

    expr { [mcget {session.check_software.last.av.result}] == 1 }

  12. Click Finished.
  13. Click Save.
  14. Click Apply Access Policy in the upper left corner of the visual policy editor.
