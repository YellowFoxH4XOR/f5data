Description

What configuration objects are synchronised between BIG-IQ CM devices in a High Availability setup?

Environment
  • BIG-IQ CM in HA
Cause

There is a script "/usr/bin/ha_sync_files" on each BIG-IQ CM which specifies what objects are replicated and those are:
  • Local Users
  • License Reports
  • Content of directories listed in call to /shared/file-object-configuration (default is /var/config/rest/fileobject)
  • Big-IP and Big-IQ UCS Backup Files (full content of /shared/ucs_backups)
  • Client certificate authentication files
