Description

Under certain circumstances, you may observe the following message in /var/log/ltm whenever the bigd daemon senses an excessive load on the system.

err bigd[<PID>]: 01060146:3: Bigd PID <PID>, instance <Instance ID>, overloaded.
notice bigd[<PID>]: 01060147:5: Bigd PID <PID>, instance <Instance ID>, overload condition cleared.

<PID> refers to bigd's process ID, whereas <Instance ID> refers to the bigd instance's ID.

An overloaded bigd daemon may result in odd or unpredictable pool member up/down behavior, such as monitor flapping.

Environment

  • BIG-IP version higher than 11.6.1 or 12.0

Cause

The message was implemented as part of Bug ID 517053, an enhancement to detect bigd overloading issue.

Potential causes of the message:

  • BIG-IP is configured with a very large number of monitor instances
  • BIG-IP is configured with a short interval time
  • BIG-IP is configured with a huge number of higher-overhead monitors
  • BIG-IP is overloaded due to high traffic
  • BIG-IP is under-provisioned with insufficient CPU cores (VE or VCMP guest)

Please note that the message helps you to detect bigd overloading conditions, but does not change the problem or mitigation steps.

Recommended Actions

To stop the message from being logged, you need to identify the underlying cause and eliminate it.

Potential mitigation may include:

  • Decreasing the number of monitor instances
  • Increasing the interval time in order for probes to occur less frequently
  • Replacing higher-overhead monitors with simpler, lower-overhead monitors
  • Reducing the amount of traffic
  • Provisioning more CPU cores to the BIG-IP (VE or VCMP guest)
