Known Issue

BIG-IP i2000 and i4000 series platforms reboot when all 10 GB ports are not connected to a peer link.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • You have one of the following BIG-IP Shuttle series platforms: i2600, i2800, i4600, or i4800.
  • You did not connect or improperly connected the BIG-IP 10 GB ports to a peer device link.

Impact

The BIG-IP system reboots and interrupts traffic processing.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • F5 iHealth lists Heuristic H694005 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High page.
  • Your BIG-IP system reboots.
  • A failover event occurs if your BIG-IP system is in a high availability (HA) configuration.
  • A bad_tlp_status message closely follows a completion_time_out_status message in the /var/log/sel file, similar to the following example:

    CPU 0 PCI/DMI Error B:D.F 0:3.2: corerrsts: bad_tlp_status
    CPU 0 PCI/DMI Error B:D.F 0:3.2: rperrsts: error_fatal_nonfatal_received
    CPU 0 PCI/DMI Error B:D.F 0:3.2: rperrsts: non_fatal_error_messages_received
    CPU 0 PCI/DMI Error B:D.F 0:3.2: uncerrsts: completion_time_out_status

Workaround

To prevent the issue from occurring, you must populate all 10 GB ports with optic cables and ensure they are connecting to a working peer link. A single 10 GB empty or improperly connected port can cause a system reboot.

There is no workaround for this issue. If you cannot prevent the issue from occurring, you should upgrade to a version listed in the Fixes introduced in column or contact F5 Technical Support to request an engineering hotfix.

Important: A device Return Materials Authorization (RMA) will not prevent this issue.
