Topic

When you are using the cloud marketplace, such as Amazon Web Services (AWS), Azure, and Google, and you want to change the billing model of your BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) instance, you can create a new BIG-IP VE instance and move the configuration over to the new instance.

Description

Depending on your business requirements, you may want to move your deployed BIG-IP VE instance to a different billing model. In the cloud marketplace, you must create a new instance with your desired billing model and move the configuration to the new instance. However, you are not required to create a new BIG-IP VE instance if you want to add or reduce resources, such as CPU and memory.

F5 offers following two billing models to deploy BIG-IP VE in the cloud marketplace.

  • Bring Your Own License (BYOL) model

    You purchase a BIG-IP VE license beforehand and use the license to activate the BYOL BIG-IP instance. Your throughput and modules are associated with the license you purchased.

  • Pay As You Go (PAYG) model

    This model enables you to pay as you go. The BIG-IP VE instance is already licensed with the respective throughput (25 Mbps, 200 Mbps, 1 Gbps, 5 Gbps) and modules through the Good-Better-Best PAYG billing model.

Actions that require you to create a new BIG-IP VE instance

Note: You must refer to your cloud service provider documentation for the additional steps required to create your new BIG-IP VE instance.

  • You convert your BYOL BIG-IP VE instance to a PAYG BIG-IP VE instance.
  • You convert your PAYG BIG-IP VE instance to a BYOL BIG-IP VE instance.
  • You change the throughput for your PAYG BIG-IP VE instance.
Note: You cannot mix BYOL and PAYG licenses. Either re-deploy BYOL instance only , or re-deploy PAYG instance with new licenses for your desired features.  For example, if you deployed ASM + LTM in PAYG model in the AWS marketplace, and later wanted to add IP Intelligence (IPI) subscription. You have to re-deploy the PAYG instance with IPI subscription.
 

Note: For more information about migrating your configuration to a new BIG-IP instance on AWS, refer to K35355528: Migrating an existing AWS BIG-IP instance to a new instance.

Actions that do not require you to create a new BIG-IP VE instance

  • You modify the BIG-IP VE instance resources (CPU or memory).
  • You want to upgrade your current pre-licensed PAYG VE instance, and the BIG-IP VE instance is running BIG-IP 12.1.3.3, 13.1.0.2, or later. 
  • You want to upgrade or apply a hotfix or point release for your BIG-IP VE version.
  • You want to change the throughput for your BYOL BIG-IP VE instance by applying an add-on throughput license.
  • You deployed a 1-slot disk instance and want to upgrade or downgrade.

    Note: You can extend the disk space for an additional slot. Refer to the procedure Extending disk space for upgrading software, or installing hotfix or point releases on an additional slot in K14952: Extending disk space on BIG-IP VE for re-sizing the disk.
