K8875: Graceful shutdown of connection may lead to leaked or orphaned connections

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When an idle TCP connection timeout is reached, the BIG-IP LTM will gracefully reap the connection. As a result, the BIG-IP LTM sends the FIN packet to the client and moves the connection into a FIN-WAIT status.

If the client does not respond with a FIN-ACK packet, the BIG-IP LTM does not shut the connection down and instead leaves the connection in the FIN-WAIT state. This behavior leaks the connections, which allows the connections to remain on the system and occupy resources (even though the connection flow is closed).

The BIG-IP LTM will not attempt to expire the connection a second time and the connection flow will remain in the connection table. This behavior may lead to a high number of leaked or orphaned connections running on the system.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR100821 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 10.0.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-9.6.1-HF1 which was issued for BIG-IP version 9.6.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
