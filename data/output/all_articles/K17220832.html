Description

  • The BIG-IP sends syslog logging records to Splunk.
  • Splunk displays them grouped under a timestamp header.

Environment

  • A BIG-IP sending syslog records to Splunk, commonly using High Speed Logging.

Cause

This is not an F5 issue. The syslog record format does not contain a hierarchy. The presentation is entirely up to Splunk.

Recommended Actions

  1.  Contact Splunk for Splunk support.

Additional Information

There is also a splunk  "F5 App" to integrate BIG-IP analytics and telemetry. That does not involve syslog and is not relevant to this.
