Description

The BIG-IQ DCD having GUI loading issue.

  1. Found the "/var/log" disk space full with "df -h" command line.
  2. Found failed to flush  error logged to "/var/tmp/restjavad.out"
    log4j:ERROR Failed to flush writer,
    java.io.IOException: No space left on device
    --
    log4j:ERROR Failed to flush writer,
    java.io.IOException: No space left on device
    --
    log4j:ERROR Failed to flush writer,
    java.io.IOException: No space left on device

Environment

  • BIG-IQ DCD
  • GUI not loading
  • Disk space full
  • CLI

Cause

The "esnodelog-xx.log" log has causes the "/var/log" disk drive space filled which causes the BIG-IQ DCD GUI loading issue

Recommended Actions

Clear up spaces on "/var/log" disk, may follow steps recorded in KB article K14403 attached under related content. 

Steps below are extracted from the attached related KB article K14403.

  1. The following "find" command can locate the 20 largest directories/files in the /var partition:
    # find /var -xdev -printf '%h\n' | sort | uniq -c | sort -k 1 -nr | head -20

  2. The results will display up to 20 largest directories and files the /var partition, and in this case you will see a lot of "esnodelog-xx.log"
     Example: 
    -rw-r--r--.  1 elasticsearch elasticsearch  16G Oct 30 23:59 eslognode-202x-10-30.log
    -rw-r--r--.  1 elasticsearch elasticsearch  12G Oct 31 23:59 eslognode-202x-10-31.log
     
  3. You may delete those large "esnodelog-xx.log" log file from the BIG-IQ DCD.
