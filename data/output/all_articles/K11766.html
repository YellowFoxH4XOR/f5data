Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP ASM Charts Scheduler may not send reports that are scheduled to be sent every 6, 12, or 24 hours. When the scheduled reports are sent manually, the .pdf file containing the report data is not attached to the email message.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as ID 211662 (formerly CR128246) and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 10.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM release notes.
