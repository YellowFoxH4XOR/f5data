Description

This article answers common questions regarding session timeout settings and user account management in the XC Console:

User Account Management

  • Can user accounts be disabled manually?
  • Is there a way to disable user accounts automatically due to inactivity?

Environment

  • XC Console
  • Administration
    • User Management

Cause

  • None

Answer/Recommended Actions

Manual User Account Disabling

User accounts can be disabled manually via the XC Console. Users with the appropriate permissions can navigate to the console and disable user accounts as needed.

Automatic User Account Disabling

The XC Console does not support automatic disabling of accounts due to inactivity.

If your organization uses a connected Single Sign-On (SSO) provider, you may be able to implement inactivity policies on the provider’s platform to disable user accounts at the source.

Additional Information

The XC Console's account management policies are fixed configurations within the platform. For advanced account control strategies, organizations may consider integrating SSO solutions or third-party identity providers that support customizable account policies and session management.
