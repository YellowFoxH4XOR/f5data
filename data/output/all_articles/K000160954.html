K000160954: [F5OS] CMP mismatch can cause long live connection instability

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-04-23
Updated
2026-04-23
Applies to
  • F5OS
  • F5OS-A: 1.8.3
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000160954

Description

On F5OS VELOS, a tenant may experience BGP flapping or instability of other long-lived pass-through TCP sessions after chassis replacement, restore, or tenant reconfiguration when the HA peers have different tenant CMP state or blade membership. Affected systems may log repeated TMM errors such as:

 

`01340004:3: HA Connection detected different cmp_state: local cmp_state 7, remote cmp_state 1. Connection will be retried.`

 

Although HA may appear up, the CMP mismatch can cause HA mirroring and state synchronization failures, resulting in intermittent session resets.

Environment

F5OS on VELOS CX410

BIG-IP tenant deployed on VELOS in an HA pair

Tenant configured to run across multiple blades

Scenario occurred after VELOS chassis RMA replacement and tenant configuration restore

Cause

Tenant CMP state mismatch between HA peers due to inconsistent blade provisioning or blade availability. When one tenant has a different number of working/provisioned blades than its HA peer, HA connection mirroring and state synchronization retries occur, disrupting long-lived TCP sessions.

Answer/Recommended Actions

1.Check the tenant cluster/member state on both HA peers and confirm that the tenant is provisioned across the same number of blades on each side.

2. Review TMM logs for repeated messages similar to:

    • 01340004:3: HA Connection detected different cmp_state: local cmp_state 7, remote cmp_state 1. Connection will be retried.

3. Verify whether any tenant blades are offline, unlicensed, or in quorum-related states.

4. If blade membership differs between HA peers, re-provision or redeploy the tenant so both HA peers use the same blade count and blade layout.

5. Ensure previously offline blades are restored to available/active state before returning traffic to the affected tenant.

6. After correcting blade provisioning, confirm:

  • HA error messages no longer appear
  • Cluster members on the tenant show the expected blades as available/active
  • BGP or other long-lived pass-through TCP sessions remain stable during failover and failback testing

Additional Information

This issue can affect pass-through traffic even when BGP is not configured directly on the BIG-IP tenant. The instability is caused by HA mirroring/CMP inconsistency at the TMM level, which can interrupt long-lived TCP flows such as BGP over TCP/179. This issue can occur when certain blades are offline on the affected tenant with quorum-related status. Session stability returns after reprovisioning the tenant to restore the offline blades so that both HA peers have matching blade membership.
