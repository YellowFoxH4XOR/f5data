Known Issue

BIG-IP ASM Overview Traffic charts may show no traffic.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The BIG-IP ASM system is receiving very low traffic levels.

For example, if the BIG-IP ASM system is handling very low levels of traffic, or if the data is spread across many objects, traffic statistics over time can average out to zero. This situation can cause the charts in the BIG-IP ASM Traffic section to show zero averages for traffic statistics.

Impact

You cannot see accurate traffic information in the ASM Traffic Statistics and ASM Network Statistics charts.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Data from requests are not reflected in the Application Security > Overview > Traffic charts, but are reflected in other reports in the BIG-IP ASM system.

Workaround

There is no workaround for this issue. The system populates BIG-IP ASM Traffic Summary charts once it detects enough traffic.
