Description

After renewing a BIG-IP / BIG-IQ subscription, the system continued to display an outdated license Service Check Date, despite the subscription being active and valid for several more months. Attempts to map the renewed subscription failed because most registration keys appeared as "XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXXXX," preventing proper mapping and license reactivation.

Environment

  • BIG-IP / BIG-IQ subscription renewal
  • Subscription renewal involved merging multiple expired subscriptions 

Cause

The issue is caused by merge of multiple subscriptions into a single new subscription in MyF5 portal during renewal.

Answer/Recommended Actions

Open a case with Support to escalate the issue and reference this article.
