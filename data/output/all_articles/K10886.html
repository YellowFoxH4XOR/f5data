Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If you execute the TCP::close iRule command within the CLIENTSSL_HANDSHAKE iRule event, TMM may dump core with a SIGSEGV fault.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR133819.

Workaround

You may be able to work around this issue by using the reject iRule command in place of the TCP::close command to reset the connection.
