Issue

Old Behavior

Previous to ARX 6.1.1, the ARX 1500 and 2500 platform Status and Alarm LEDs blink green to indicate that the system is functioning properly.

New Behavior

Beginning in ARX 6.1.1 the Status and Alarm LEDs on the ARX 1500 and 2500 platforms indicate the system's status as displayed in the following table:

Alarm LEDStatus LEDModule State
OffGreenOnline
OffYellowARX 1500/2500 is powering up and the ARX software is coming up. This occurs
after the LED driver is loaded. It also occurs immediately following a reload command, waiting for the
interview to commence, or immediately after the interview process has finished.
RedYellowAfter boot up: System Record is missing; cli command, such as show proc,
returns an error message.
RedGreenSwitch interview is in progress.
RedOffOne of the power supplies failed. (Look at the power supply LED at the rear to determine the failure.)
OffOffThe switch has no power, or the system is in an early boot state.

F5 Product Development tracked this change as ID 370246.
