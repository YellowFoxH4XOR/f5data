Topic

The Packet Velocity ASIC (PVA) requires that pool members of virtual servers, configured to be accelerated by the PVA, have current ARP entries.

F5 recommends that you configure a health monitor for these pool members to ensure ARP entries are present. For additional information about configuring health monitors, refer to the BIG-IP manual for your specific version.

Alternatively, you can also ensure the presence of ARP entries by adding static ARP entries for each pool member to be used by an accelerated virtual server. For additional information about configuring a static ARP entry, refer to K5946: Adding a static ARP entry.
