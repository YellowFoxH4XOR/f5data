K11734122: BIG-IP Upgrades of more than 2 Major Versions are not supported

Type
Support Solution
Published
2021-11-10
Updated
2023-02-21
Applies to
  • BIG-IP Platforms
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K11734122

Description

If you need to upgrade your BIG-IP by more than two major versions, you first need to upgrade an interim version.

Environment

  • BIG-IP 
  • Upgrading

Cause

Since Version 12, BIG-IP no longer supports upgrades of more than 2 major versions. Refer to K13845

Recommended Actions

  1. You just need to upgrade to an interim version first.
  2. When choosing the interim version, make sure that the interim version is released after the version you are upgrading from.
  3. In most cases, just choose the latest release of the interim version, but also consider that this was not released after the final version that you are upgrading too.
  4. For example, if you are upgrading from 12.1.6 to 15.1.3.1, then we would choose 14.1.4.3 as the interim version, as this was released after 12.1.6, but before 15.1.3.1.
  5. For Release Date information, refer to Software Release Date Information K33062581
