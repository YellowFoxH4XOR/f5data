Known Issue

The command history and audit logs contain additional double quotation marks (") and backslash (\) (escape) characters.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • From the TMOS Shell (tmsh), the command you enter includes escape characters.

Impact

  • Commands repeated from the history may not match the content originally entered and interpret the content as displayed.
  • The audit logs may contain additional double quotation marks or escape characters when compared to the original command.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The same command in the tmsh command history contains additional escape characters.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 572079 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. For information about releases, point releases, or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table.

Workaround

To work around this issue, when repeating commands from the history contain escape characters, F5 recommends removing the additional escape characters before running the command.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
