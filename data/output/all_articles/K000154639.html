Description

When multiple IP Intelligence add-on licenses (each valid for a specific duration) are activated on an F5 BIG-IP device at the same time, the system handles the stacking of these subscription add-ons and the durations will be combined. 

 

  • If the BIG-IP device already has an active IP Intelligence subscription, activating (renewing) additional add-ons will append their total duration to the current expiration date.
  • If the device has no prior active IP Intelligence subscriptions and multiple IP Intelligence add-ons are activated at the same time, the subscription term will be the total duration of all the add-ons, beginning from the date of activation.
  • The system will randomly select one of the add-ons to be displayed as a Time Limited Module in the GUI or in the output of the show sys license command from tmsh utility; however, the total subscription period reflects the cumulative duration of all activated add-ons.

 

Environment

  • Activating or Renewing IP Intelligence License

 

Cause

  • Subscription add-on licenses such as IP Intelligence are stackable. When more than one subscription add-on license is activated on a device at the same time, their durations are added together, extending the total subscription period accordingly.

Recommended Actions

  • For information on the procedures to activate add-on license on the BIG-IP system, refer to the Activating an add-on module section of the K7752: Licensing the BIG-IP system
