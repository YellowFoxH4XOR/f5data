K000135196: LTM Bandwidth Controller Configuration not working on v16 and later

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-09-26
Updated
2023-09-26
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.X.X, 16.X.X, 17.1.X, 17.1.0, 17.0.X, 17.0.0, 16.1.X, 16.1.3, 16.1.2, 16.1.1, 16.1.0, 16.0.X, 16.0.1, 16.0.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000135196

Description

Bandwidth Controller Policy (BWC) functionality prior to 16.x was associated with the AAM license and provisioning.
After 16.x this functionality is available in LTM, but may not provide the expected or desired results due to the default context.

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • Bandwidth Controller Policy

Cause

LTM virtual servers can have a Bandwidth Controller Policy applied. 
In LTM, this policy (by default) applies to server-side ingress traffic. 

Recommended Actions

Use an iRule to attach the Bandwidth Controller Policy after SERVER_CONNECTED to ensure that client-side ingress traffic is limited.
 

when SERVER_CONNECTED {
  BWC::policy attach /Common/static
}
