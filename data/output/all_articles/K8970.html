Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If a partially accelerated SSL connection encounters an SSL alert after a midstream renegotiation, and an APPDATA record is still in queue, memory corruption or handshake failures may occur. This issue occurs when the connection is terminated, and the system attempts to remove the record a second time.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR100631 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.4.6. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.3.1-HF4, issued for BIG-IP 9.3.1, and in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.5-HF2, issued for BIG-IP 9.4.5. You may download these hotfixes or later versions of the hotfixes from the F5 Downloads site.
