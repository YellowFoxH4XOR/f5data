Known Issue

This may be the result of a known issue. In rare cases, gateway failsafe may trigger immediately after it is enabled.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR35752 and CR41930. This problem was resolved in BIG-IP version 4.5.13 for the 4.5 software branch. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP Release Notes.
