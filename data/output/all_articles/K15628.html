Known Issue

When you restart the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) process on a standby host, the system may not reestablish one or more mirrored high availability connections.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have one or more BIG-IP hosts in a high availability failover configuration.
  • You have connection mirroring configured.

Impact

In the event of a failover, the system may terminate some connections with a TCP RST.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H481007 on the Diagnostics > Identified > High screen.
  • On failover, the system may terminate some connections with a TCP RST.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can restart all TMM processes on the standby host. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Failover redundancy will be lost temporarily.

  1. Log in to the standby host as the root user.
  2. Stop the TMM process by typing the following command:

    bigstart stop tmm

  3. Wait 5 seconds.
  4. Start the TMM process by typing the following command:

    bigstart start tmm
