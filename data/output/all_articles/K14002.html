Known Issue

The BIG-IP WebAccelerator system may not properly import policies.

This issue occurs when either of the following conditions is met:

  • You are importing a BIG-IP 10.x policy which has two or more leaf nodes of the same name into a BIG-IP WebAccelerator 11.x system.
  • You are upgrading a BIG-IP WebAccelerator 10.x system to 11.x  and the 10.x policies have two or more leaf nodes with duplicate names.

BIG-IP WebAccelerator 11.x does not support duplicate leaf node names.

Impact

The BIG-IP WebAccelerator policy import or upgrade may fail.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • When importing a policy with duplicate leaf node names into a BIG-IP WebAccelerator 11.x system, you may see the following error in the Configuration utility:

    There was 1 error.
    (1) Error when attempting to import policy.

  • When upgrading from 10.x to 11.x and rolling forward your configuration, the system will correctly migrate the first node of policies where leaf nodes have duplicate names and will silently delete subsequent duplicate nodes.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you must rename duplicate leaf node names on a BIG-IP WebAccelerator 10.x system before exporting a policy or upgrading. To do so, perform the following procedures:

Impact of workaround: This procedure requires booting a BIG-IP system into a legacy 10.x partition. You may perform this procedure on a standby system.

Renaming leaf nodes in BIG-IP WebAccelerator 10.x

  1. Boot into a BIG-IP 10.x partition with your legacy configuration installed.
  2. Log in to the BIG-IP Configuration utility.
  3. Click WebAccelerator.
  4. Click Policies.
  5. Select the policy you would like to change.
  6. Select a duplicate leaf node you would like to rename, and click Rename.
  7. Select a new name for this leaf node, then click Rename to confirm.
  8. Repeat Steps 4 and 5 for every duplicate policy name.
  9. Click Publish, then click Publish Now.
  10. Exit the policy editor.

From this point you may either export the policy by pressing Export next to your policy, or attempt to upgrade to 11.x to carry this configuration forward.

Importing your exported policy on BIG-IP 11.x

  1. Boot into your 11.x partition.
  2. Log in to the BIG-IP Configuration utility.
  3. Click WebAccelerator.
  4. Click Policies.
  5. Click Import.
  6. Under Policies File click Browse.
  7. Select the exported 10.x XML policy file.
  8. Under Policy Name, choose a policy name under which you would like to import this policy.
  9. Click Import.
  10. Next to the policy you have just imported, click Publish.
