Known Issue

The autodosd service may intermittently produce a core file.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP device is licensed and provisioned with the BIG-IP AFM module.
  • Your BIG-IP device has an uptime of several days.

Impact

The autodosd service may produce a core file and restart.

Note: There is no impact to the autodosd service state.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The autodosd service generates a core file in the /shared/core directory.
  • In the /var/log/ltm file, you observe log messages that appear similar to the following example:

    err mcpd[<pid>]: 01071087:3: Killed process for %autodosd as current count of messages (<numeric value>) keeps building.
