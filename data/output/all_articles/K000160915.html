Description

F5 is announcing a product revision for the F5 rSeries r5000 and F5 rSeries r10000 platforms. This revision introduces a targeted hardware design update intended to improve long-term component availability and platform lifecycle longevity, while maintaining the same platform architecture, performance profile, and customer experience.

What is changing

This product revision includes the following update: The dedicated bandwidth engine hardware component, BE2, has been replaced with an integrated software-based solution that delivers equivalent platform functionality and performance. This change is part of F5's ongoing effort to simplify hardware design, improve component resiliency, and support long-term product availability.

Software requirement

The revised rSeries r5000 and rSeries r10000 platforms require the following F5OS software: F5OS-A 1.8.4 or later

What is not changing

The following remain unchanged:

  • Product names and platform identity: rSeries r5000 and rSeries r10000
  • Product SKUs
  • Platform architecture, form factor, and deployment model
  • Core system performance and customer use cases
  • Published performance benchmarks
  • Management interfaces and operational workflows
  • Licensing and support entitlements
  • Existing deployment and management practices
  • Existing automation toolchain environments

Customer impact

This product revision is designed to be straightforward for customers:

  • Revised hardware ships with supported software preinstalled.
  • Existing operational workflows remain unchanged.
  • No changes are required to licensing or support entitlements.
  • Existing deployment, management, and automation practices continue to apply.
  • BIG-IP tenants can be deployed across both revised and prior-revision rSeries r5000 and rSeries r10000 platforms.

Deployment guidance

Customers deploying revised rSeries r5000 or rSeries r10000 hardware must ensure systems are running F5OS-A 1.8.4 or later. For customers deploying BIG-IP TMOS tenant high availability pairs on F5 rSeries platforms, tenants can be provisioned across both revised and prior-revision rSeries r5000 and rSeries r10000 platforms.

Effective date

Beginning July 1, 2026, all rSeries r5000 and rSeries r10000 orders will begin shipping with revised hardware. RMA shipments will align with the hardware revision being replaced.

Our commitment

This rSeries product revision reflects F5's continued commitment to delivering resilient, high-performance platforms while improving component sustainability and ensuring a seamless customer experience.

Hardware revision and ordering

When do the revised rSeries r5000 and rSeries r10000 platforms begin shipping?

Beginning July 1, 2026, all rSeries r5000 and rSeries r10000 orders will begin shipping with revised hardware.

Are the product SKUs changing?

No. Place orders using the existing rSeries r5000 and rSeries r10000 SKUs.

Is there a functional difference between the prior-revision rSeries r5000/rSeries r10000 platforms and the revised platforms?

No.

Are there performance differences between the prior-revision platforms and the revised platforms?

There is minimal performance difference, and the published performance benchmarks are unchanged.

Can a revised rSeries r5000 or rSeries r10000 platform form a high availability pair with a prior-revision platform?

Yes. A high availability pair can be formed between BIG-IP tenants running on both prior-revision and revised rSeries r5000 and rSeries r10000 platforms.

What unit will I receive through RMA?

The RMA process ensures replacement units align with the hardware revision being replaced, up until the End of Technical Support (EoTS) date for F5OS-A version 1.8.x, which is October 2027:

  • A prior-revision rSeries r5000 or rSeries r10000 unit will receive a prior-revision replacement unit.
  • A revised rSeries r5000 or rSeries r10000 unit will receive a revised replacement unit.

Are any hardware appliances or chassis other than rSeries r5000 and rSeries r10000 impacted by this announcement?

No. This announcement applies only to the rSeries r5000 and rSeries r10000 platforms. The following platforms are not impacted:

  • rSeries r12000
  • rSeries r5920-DF
  • rSeries r10920-DF
  • VELOS chassis
  • VELOS blades

Software requirements

Is there an F5OS software version requirement for revised rSeries r5000 and rSeries r10000 platforms?

Yes. Revised rSeries r5000 and rSeries r10000 platforms require F5OS-A 1.8.4 or later.

What is the difference between F5OS-A 1.8.4 and F5OS-A 1.8.3?

F5OS-A 1.8.4 includes additional product defect and CVE fixes, in addition to support for the revised rSeries r5000 and rSeries r10000 platforms. For more information, refer to the F5OS-A 1.8.4 release notes.

Is F5OS-A 1.8.4 an LTS release?

Yes. F5OS-A 1.8.4 is part of the F5OS-A 1.8.x Long-Term Support release.

What happens if someone accidentally tries to run 1.8.3 on a newer revision r5000/r10000? Does it fail to install or install but run in some degraded way?

The revised rSeries will not boot into 1.8.3 and will result in an error message, preventing accidental installation.
