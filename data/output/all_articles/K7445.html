Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Time zone data for some Canadian time zones were not updated for 2007. As a result, BIG-IP systems set to the following time zones may display the incorrect system time:
  • America/Dawson
  • America/Edmonton
  • America/Goose_Bay
  • America/Inuvik
  • America/Moncton
  • America/Nassau
  • America/St_Johns
  • America/Thule
  • America/Vancouver
  • America/Whitehorse
  • America/Yellowknife

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR76611 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.1.3, 9.3.0, and 9.4.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, GTM, ASM, or Link Controller release notes.
