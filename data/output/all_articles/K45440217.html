Known Issue

The BIG-IP system erroneously prevents you from provisioning the BIG-IP LTM, GTM, and ASM module combination on some VIPRION blades.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You attempt to provision the BIG-IP LTM, GTM, and ASM modules.
  • You are using the VIPRION 2100 with B4200N (A109) blade.

Impact

You cannot provision the BIG-IP LTM, GTM, and ASM modules together.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The system displays a provisioning error message in both the Configuration utility and the /var/log/ltm file:

    0107100d:3: The module combination (asm gtm ltm) cannot be provisioned on this platform (A109)

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 402469 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:

Workaround

To work around this issue, provision the BIG-IP Analytics module in addition to BIG-IP GTM, LTM, and ASM.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
