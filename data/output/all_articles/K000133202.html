IMPORTANT: The iSeries and rSeries platforms use three different AC power supply units (PSUs) that should only be installed in their supported platform.  The size and shape of the three AC power supplies are the same, so any of them will physically fit into the different iSeries and rSeries platforms.  You need to verify the correct power supply designed for your specific Series platform model prior to its installation into the device.

The i5000/i7000/i10000/i11000 and r5000 series platforms use the following AC power supply:  
Murata 650W AC power supply (part number PWR-0306-xx).  This CUSTOMER P/N can be seen on the label when removed from the device.

You can visually identify the Murata AC power supply without removal from the device by the black vertical (|) pull handle and silver face with rectangle shaped perforations near the power cord connection.
