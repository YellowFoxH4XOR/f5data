Topic

BIG-IP and 3-DNS display one of three status indicators in the interface column in the output of bigpipe interface show:

UPThe interface is a member of a VLAN and is up.
DNThe interface is a member of a VLAN and is down.
DSThe interface is a member of a VLAN and is disabled or is not a member of a VLAN.
