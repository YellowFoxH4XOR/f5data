Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When you manually create a policy with a wildcard entity and run the Policy Builder, the Policy Builder will remove wildcard entities.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR91568 and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM 9.4.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM Release Notes.
