K58450599: Newer BIG-IP Edge Client versions contain more details in the Subject field of its Digital signature certificate

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-06-28
Updated
2024-08-29
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP APM: 17.X.X, 17.0.0, 16.1.3, 16.1.2, 15.1.6, 17.0.X, 15.1.5

Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K58450599

Description

The Subject field of the Digital signature certificate of BIG-IP Edge Client 7215 and later versions (7220 and 7221) contains more parameters/details, when compared to versions prior to 7215 (i.e., 7214/7213)

Edge Client 7215/7220/7221:
------
CN = F5 Networks Inc
SERIALNUMBER = 601692492
2.5.4.15 = Private Organization
O = F5 Networks Inc
1.3.6.1.4.1.311.60.2.1.2 = Washington
1.3.6.1.4.1.311.60.2.1.3 = US
L = Seattle
S = Washington
C = US
------


Earlier Edge Client versions (7213/7214/7214 Update 1):
------
CN = F5 Networks Inc
O = F5 Networks Inc
L = Seattle
S = Washington
C = US
------

To view the Digital signature certificate of the Edge Client installed on your Windows PC:
1. navigate to C:\Program Files (x86)\F5 VPN
2. right click on f5fpclientW.exe, then click Properties
3. in the Properties window, click the Digital Signatures tab
4. in the Signature list, click the F5 Networks signature, then click Details
5. in the Digital Signature Details window, click View Certificate
6. in the Certificate window, navigate to the Details tab.
7. examine the Subject field of the certificate

Environment

  • Edge Client versions 7215/7220/7221

Cause

Those 'additional' details/parameters you see in the digital signing cert of 'newer' Edge Client versions is a result of fixing ID1095221 : [APM Clients] Upgrade to Openssl 1.1.1n in vendor and main branch

This is what ultimately added support for TLS1.3 in Edge Client 7.2.2

It also resolved a pair of OpenSSL Vulnerabilities for CVE-2022-0778.

Recommended Actions

None. Unless you are specifically using these new CN fields supported by the newer version of OpenSSL 1.1.1, it will have zero impact.
