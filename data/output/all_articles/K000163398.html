Published

Updated
2026-09-23
Applies to

  • F5 Distributed Cloud Routed DDoS
Source

Description

DDoS reports can be requested for time ranges and automatic delivery (Daily, Weekly or Monthly).

Environment

  • Distributed Cloud

  • Routed DDos

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

Open a support ticket with the SOC and provide the desired time range for the DDoS report (Daily, Weekly or Monthly).

The SOC will create the report and will be available in the Console from that point onwards.

Additional Information

  • Generated DDoS reports are stored under: Routed DDoS > Visibility > Reports
  • Reports are Available in PDF format
