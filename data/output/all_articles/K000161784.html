K000161784: After upgrading BIG-IP to 17.5.1.6 unable to see statistics on BIG-IQ

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-07-20
Updated
2026-07-20
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 8.4.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000161784

Description

After upgrading BIG-IP to 17.5.1.6 you are unable to see BIG-IP statistics in BIG-IQ

Environment

  • BIG-IQ
  • Managed BIG-IPs

Cause

BIG-IQ IP address was not allowed in BIG-IP httpd and sshd.

Answer/Recommended Actions

Allow BIG-IQ IP address in httpd and sshd allow list of BIG-IP and re-added the BIG-IP to BIG-IQ.

Additional Information

None
