Policy Information

Because of customers demand, F5 Networks incorporated basic routing capabilities on BIG-IP with a feature called GateD. Over time customers requested a more featured routing package that closely mimicked industry standard IOS configuration. Rather than enhancing GateD, F5 Networks choose to OEM a different 3rd party routing software from IP InfusionTM. This integration was completed in 2002. As a result, customers can add support for BGP, OSPF and RIP on any version of BIG-IP running 4.5 or later.

Product update

F5 Networks announces the End of Life plan for the GateD feature in BIG-IP. This applies to all fully licensed BIG-IP products.

Product support

For those customers using the GateD feature, F5 Networks will continue to provide software and phone support in accordance to the customer's maintenance contracts. After the EoSD date, 2/28/2004, F5 Networks will not be able to provide software fixes for any technical issues with GateD. Customer will be encouraged to upgrade to the latest version of BIG-IP software running the new IP InfusionTM modules.

Questions

If you have questions about F5 Networks's decision to discontinue this feature, please contact your local F5 Networks sales representative or service department.
