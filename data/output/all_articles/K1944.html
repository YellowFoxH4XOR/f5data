Policy Information

BIG-IP and 3-DNS have had the requirement for no-crypto versions of the software. These versions were used for shipments into regions with import restrictions and did not include any cryptology software. This requirement has been removed and, therefore, F5 will no longer produce no-crypto versions of BIG-IP and 3-DNS after version 4.2. All PTF releases of BIG-IP 4.2 will have no-crypto versions.

Product update

F5 announces the End-of-Life (EOL) for distribution of BIG-IP and 3-DNS no-crypto versions as of April 1, 2002.

This does not affect any other standard BIG-IP and 3-DNS product or price.

Transition issues

  1. Current service contracts for BIG-IP and 3-DNS no-crypto software versions will be honored by F5 until the end of the contract term. At the end of contract term, the partner should contact F5 to inquire about renewing the support contract.
  2. If a BIG-IP or 3-DNS no-crypto software version system fails, the system should be returned to F5 manufacturing after completing the RMA process. F5 will repair or replace the system with a system of equal or greater value and/or functionality.
  3. Upgrades of BIG-IP or 3-DNS no-crypto versions after 4.2 will be to crypto versions.

Take action

Be sure to update all price lists, quotes, product literature, presentations, web sites, and so on, to reflect the change.

Product in stock

If you have existing BIG-IP or 3-DNS systems with no-crypto software versions in stock you may continue to sell the product to customers. Please contact your F5 representative if you have existing stock of this product.

Questions

If you have questions about a specific BIG-IP no-crypto software version situation, please contact your local F5 representative or email partners@f5.com.
