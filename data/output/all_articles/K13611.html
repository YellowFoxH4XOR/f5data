Topic

The port speed and link indicator LEDs may exhibit slight variations in color and brightness on the following platforms:

  • BIG-IP 1600, 3600, 3900, or PB200 VIPRION Blade platform

There is a variation in LED color and luminosity that may be visible; however, the LEDs are within specifications.

If you have several F5 platforms in the affected list, and they are near each other, you may notice a difference in color or brightness of the LEDs.

Impact

None. The variation in LED color and luminosity does not constitute a hardware failure.
