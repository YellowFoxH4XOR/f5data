Description

You want to use iRulesLX but are unable to find where you access LX Workspaces or LX Plugins.
  • You navigate to Local Traffic > iRules. and do not see an option for LX Workspaces or LX Plugins

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • iRulesLX

Cause
iRules LX is not licensed or provisioned.

Recommended Actions

Verify iRulesLX is licensed and provisioned
  1. Navigate to System > Resource Provisioning
  2. From here, you will be able to see whether you have iRulesLX licensed and whether or not it is provisioned.
    • If iRulesLX is not licensed, contact F5 Sales or your third party vendor to get an Add-on license for iRulesLX
    • If iRulesLX is licensed, but not provisioned, provision it at either minimal or nominal.  Note: See Related content for more information about Resource Provisioning
Navigate to Local Traffic > iRules. From here, you should see LX Workspaces and LX Plugins
