Environment

Aspen Mesh, Kubernetes Network Policies
 

Cause

Kubernetes Network Policies not allowing egress traffic towards port 15012
 

Recommended Actions

Adjust the network policy in your namespace and/or environment to permit outbound (egress) communication towards port 15012.
 

Additional Information

NetworkPolicies are an application-centric construct which allow you to specify how a pod is allowed to communicate with various network "entities".
