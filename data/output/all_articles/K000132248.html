K000132248: Statistics are not displayed correctly when two SNAT Lists have matching Address Lists enabled on different VLANs

Published

Applies to

Source

Description

SNAT statistics may display incorrect value when two SNAT lists have matching Address Lists enabled on different VLANs.

Environment

  • LTM
  • SNAT

Cause

This is documented as ID1224373.

The BIG-IP is configured with a pair of SNAT Lists, with one Enabled On a particular VLAN, with the other Disabled On that VLAN. Meanwhile the SNAT's have equivalent Address/Prefix origin lists.
