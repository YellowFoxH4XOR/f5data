Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. TMM does not properly handle a connection after receiving a negative (404) response to a HEAD request that does not specify Content-Length or chunked encoding transfer. If the TMM were to act properly, as stated in RFC2616 Section 4.4, responses to HEAD requests (and other requests that do not have an entity body) are terminated by the first blank line after the HTTP header. However, TMM waits for the server to close the connection. As a result, this behavior causes a delay and affects the BIG-IP LTM performance, which defeats the purpose of sending HEAD requests.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR68238, and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.1.3, 9.3, and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
