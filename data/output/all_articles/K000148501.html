Description

  • What impact does the streamlining of F5's case management tools have to me?

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud

Cause

  • None

Answer/Recommended Actions

There should be no impact when it come to case creation. You can continue to use F5 Distributed Cloud Console (XC) to submit support cases.

For more information on how to reach out to support: How to contact F5® Distributed Cloud Support

Key changes:

  • Open tickets: You can continue working your active tickets through the XC Console as you do today. All remaining open tickets will be migrated to the new system in early 2025.
  • Closed tickets: Existing closed tickets will remain in the old system and cannot be reopened after November. You will need to create a new ticket and reference the previous one to follow up.
  • Historical Ticket Access: During 2025 the old system will be decommissioned. You will lose access to historical tickets in the console. 

 

Register for a MyF5 account

If you already have a MyF5 account, you should be able to see the Distributed Cloud product listed under Support and Create a Case.

If you do not already have a MyF5 account, follow the steps below to complete your MyF5 registration:

  1. Go to the following link for MyF5 Login - https://account.f5.com/myf5 

  1. Click on Don't have an account? Sign Up

  1. Add in your details and ensure that the email address you input matches the one that you are using in the F5 Distributed Cloud Console.   

    1. If you register with a different email address, there is a risk you will not be able to see all support cases and will need to contact F5 Support. 

  1. Once you have completed your registration, please refer to the following article for how to submit a support case through MyF5. 

    • K2633: Submit a support case
    • If you have questions regarding MyF5, our F5 Customer Care team will be available to assist with any queries you may have.  

    1. USA/Canada: +1-888-882-7535 (888-88-ASKF5)  

    1. INT: +800-275435 (800-ASK-4-F5)
