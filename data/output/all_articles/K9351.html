Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Route Health Injection (RHI) will not advertise 0.0.0.0/0 virtual addresses when the Advertise option is enabled.

When the Advertise option is enabled on a 0.0.0.0/0 virtual address, the tmrouted process will filter the address and prevent it from being injected.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR109706 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 10.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM release notes.
