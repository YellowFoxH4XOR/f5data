Known Issue

Rare instances of an iSession connection may cause Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) to stop responding.

This issue may occur when iSession endpoints are configured on the BIG-IP system.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may stop responding and fail over to a standby host or another host in the device group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H440743 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • TMM may stop responding and produce a core file that includes a panic string similar to the following example:

    panic: ../base/pool.c:1062: Assertion "No pool members still in use" failed.

  • You may observe a TMM core file in the /shared/core directory.
  • The BIG-IP system may stop responding and fail over to a standby host or another host in the device group.
