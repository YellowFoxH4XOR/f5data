Topic

The Energy Policy Act of 2005 is a statute that was passed by the United States Congress on July 29, 2005, and signed into law on August 8, 2005. Part of the Act extends Daylight Saving Time by approximately four weeks, beginning in 2007. Daylight Saving Time in the United States will begin at 2 a.m. on the second Sunday of March and end at 2 a.m. on the first Sunday of November. In most of the countries of Western Europe, including the countries that are members of the European Union, Daylight Saving Time begins at 1 a.m. GMT on the last Sunday of March and ends at 1 a.m. GMT on the last Sunday of October.
