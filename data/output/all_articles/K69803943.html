Description
The UCS backup is unusually large, or increases by over 100Mb after system upgrade when generating a new UCS file.
Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • APM provisioned
  • Multiple EPSEC packages

Cause
EPSEC packages may be re-installed after upgrade creating duplicates in the filestore
Old EPSEC packages are not removed automatically and are saved in the UCS.

Recommended Actions

Remove unnecessary EPSEC packages from the configuration.
See K21175584: Removing unnecessary OPSWAT EPSEC packages from the BIG-IP APM system

Additional Information
