Policy Information

In alignment with security best practices, the Fraud Protection Service (FPS) will no longer accept email-based submissions to the F5 Security Operations Center (SOC) that contain active links to potentially malicious content. Any email submissions that contain fully formed URLs may be dropped by F5 email security protocols. All submissions that contain a URL must be escaped to include hxxp and [.]TLD.

For example:

hxxp://www.badwebsite[.]com

This change is effective August 31, 2018 at 23:59 UTC. After this date, any email-based submissions that do not adhere to the new policy will be blocked and won't reach the SOC.

Impact

If you fail to properly escape email-based submissions of incidents that contain active links, F5 blocks the email. Therefore, the SOC does not receive the notification and shutdown actions do not occur.
