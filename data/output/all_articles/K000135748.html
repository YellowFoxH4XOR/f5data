Description

You are attempting to download F5 software from MyF5 portal and receive the following error: 

"You have limited access in MyF5 while viewing your PAYG or BYOL account purchased through a cloud marketplace".
 

Environment

  • MyF5 portal
     

Cause

Your account is associated with a Marketplace offering account.
 

Recommended Actions

To use a different account if you need to download. You can go to Create your F5 account on MyF5.

Additional Information

MyF5 portal is still undergoing changes and you should be able to download using your account in future.
