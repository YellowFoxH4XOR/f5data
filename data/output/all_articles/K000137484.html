Description

According to the article K14513: MAC address assignment for interfaces, trunks, and VLANs (11.x and later) , you know that each BIG-IP system has a pool of available MAC addresses that can be assigned to interfaces, trunks, and Virtual Local Area Networks (VLANs), and the BIG-IP VE platform default have 64 MAC addresses of MAC address pool. The VXLAN tunnel MAC address is the same as VLAN, it is also from the MAC address pool. the VLAN group MAC address is through flipped local bit of  VXLAN tunnel MAC address, it is similar to selecting a MAC address for MAC masquerade to traffic group, you can get more detail from K3523: Choosing a unique MAC address for MAC masquerade.
The following table illustrates the conversion for VLAN group MAC address from VXLAN tunnel MAC address:
