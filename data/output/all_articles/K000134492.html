Description

After upgrade of the BIG-IP DNS system, the health status of GSLB objects changing, They remain as blue [Status Unknown]
DNS name resolution may not be working

Frequently-reoccurring gtmd core crashes are found in the /var/log/messages log file. 

May 3 03:07:25 bigip notice logger[18657]: Started writing core file: /var/core/gtmd.bld0.34.3.core.gz for PID 12086
May 3 03:07:41 bigip notice logger[18715]: Finished writing 42785503 bytes for core file: /var/core/gtmd.bld0.34.3.core.gz for PID 12086


The gtmd process is constantly re-starting, every 5-6 seconds, as observed in the /var/log/gtm logs:

May 3 03:07:24 bigip emerg logger[16883]: Re-starting gtmd
May 3 03:07:30 bigip emerg logger[16976]: Re-starting gtmd
May 3 03:07:36 bigip emerg logger[17071]: Re-starting gtmd

Environment

BIG-IP DNS

Cause

Issue might be caused by  ID1137485/ID1137493.
Bug ID 1137485: Gtmd produces excessive logging and may also crash (SIGSEGV) repeatedly

Recommended Actions

Use version mentioned as Fixed, from the Bug Tracker article, or open a ticket with F5 Support, to obtain an Engineering Hotfix.
