K000135205: Spanning Single-Bladed Guests to Different Blades on F5 Viprion: Step-by-Step Guide for Slot Expansion and RMA Hot Swap

Description

This knowledge article provides a step-by-step guide on how to span guests to different blades on an F5 Viprion device if they only existed in a single blade. This process allows for expanding a guest from one slot to another and performing a hot swap with a new RMA blade, while ultimately leaving the guest only in the desired slot.
 

Environment

F5 Viprion device.
 

Procedure

Expanding the guest to slot 2:

    Before starting the migration/expansion, ensure that a copy of the UCS is saved externally and in the /var/tmp directory for backup purposes.

    Verify that the "Initial Image/Hotfix" on the Guest config on the Viprion Host matches the currently running version. If they differ, follow these steps to fix the issue:

  •     Change the guest to "Configured" mode.

  •     Update the "Initial Image/Hotfix" on the Guest config page to match the current software version.

  •     Redeploy the guest.

    While the guest is in deployed mode, change the "Number of Slots" from 1 to 2.

    Move slot 2 from "Available" to "Selected".

    Click on "Update" to save the changes.

    Monitor the guest status until it turns green, indicating successful replication of all configurations. Confirm the replication logs via the CLI.

    Expected log entries similar to this:

    Jun 22 09:47:57 VIP2400-lab.local alert vcmpd[7449]: 01510012:1: vCMP guest Guest_Test detected heartbeat.
    Jun 22 09:47:57 VIP2400-lab.local info vcmpd[7449]: 01510007:6: Guest (Guest_Test): VS_STARTING->VS_RUNNING
    Jun 22 09:47:57 VIP2400-lab.local alert vcmpd[7449]: 01510010:1: vCMP guest Guest_Test powered on.

Replacing the blade in slot 1:

    Perform a hot swap by replacing the blade in slot 1 with the RMA blade.

    Wait for the new blade to join the cluster and ensure that all configurations, including the Guest configuration, are replicated to the newly installed slot. Verify the config on the new slot and confirm its presence via CLI. Use article K13965 as a reference during this process.

Reseting back the guest to only slot 1:

    While the guest is in deployed mode, change the "Number of Slots" from 2 to 1.
    Move slot 2 from "Selected" to "Available".
    Click on "Update" to save the changes.

    Expected log entries similar to this:

    Jun 22 09:49:18 slot2 info vcmpd[8860]: 01510007:6: VDisk (Guest_Test.img/1): Syncing with MCP - [filename:Guest_Test.img slot:1 installed_os:1 state:4]
    Jun 22 09:49:18 slot2 info vcmpd[8860]: 01510007:6: Guest (Guest_Test): VS_STOPPING->VS_STOPPED
    Jun 22 09:49:18 slot2 info vcmpd[8860]: 01510007:6: Guest (Guest_Test): VS_STOPPED->VS_INACTIVE

    Confirm from the CLI that the guest is now only in slot 1.
