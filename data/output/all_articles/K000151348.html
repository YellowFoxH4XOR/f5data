Description

What is the correct sequence of steps when applying an add on license to an HA pair?

 

Environment

  • BIG-IP HA pair
  • License activation

 

Cause

Not applicable

 

Recommended Actions

1. Backup the Configuration:

  • Before making any licensing or configuration changes, back up the current configuration on both devices.
  • tmsh save sys ucs <filename>.ucs
  • Verify Current Configuration and Status:
  • tmsh load sys config verify
  • Check the HA pair status to ensure both devices are operational and synchronized before proceeding with the licensing steps.
  • tmsh show cm sync-status

2. Activate Addon License on the Secondary/Standby Device by following the steps in this article: Licensing the BIG-IP system

3. Force Primary (active) unit to standby and Activate Addon License on the Primary Device

  • Log In to the Primary (Active) Unit:
  • Navigate to Device Management > Devices > Choose (self) from the list
  • In the window on the screen, scroll to the bottom of the page and click "Force to Standby".
  • Note: Verify that the previous standby system is operational and no issues are noticed as it has the upgraded license and is now handling traffic. If the secondary unit is not processing traffic correctly, force it to standby and contact F5 Support.

4. On the Primary (now standby system), active the license according to the steps in this article: Licensing the BIG-IP system

5. Perform Device Synchronization

  • After the addon license is activated on both devices, synchronize the HA pair to ensure consistency in their configuration. Preferably synchronize from the active box.
  • Confirm Device Status:
  • Run the following command to check the redundancy and sync status:
  • show cm sync-status
  • Verify that both devices are communicating and in sync-active or sync-standby state.
  • Force Synchronization:
  • On the active device, push the configuration to the standby unit:
  • GUI: Navigate to Device Management > Devices, and under Device Actions, choose Sync to Group.
  • From CLI:
  • tmsh run cm config-sync to-group <device-group-name>
  • Verify Sync Completion:
  • Ensure that both devices are synchronized after performing the config sync.
  • show cm sync-status

 

6. Test the Setup

  • Test failover between the devices to confirm that the licensing and configuration are correctly applied to both devices.
  • Check the status of the addon module(s) to ensure they are working properly on both units.
  • Contact Support Services | F5 if you encounter any problems.
