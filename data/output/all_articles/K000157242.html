F5 is ending the sale of F5 Distributed Cloud Small Customer Edge (CE) Nodes for CE deployments. This change marks a significant shift in the F5 product portfolio, because organizations that previously relied on Small CE Nodes will need to plan transitions for these deployments. The End of Sale (EoS) applies to all Small CE Node models and their associated SKUs, effective July 1, 2025.

Note: F5 will support Small CE Nodes until July 1, 2028, or end of contract, whichever comes first. F5 recommends that you plan for resizing and migrating Small CE Nodes as soon as possible, even though Small CE Nodes remain supported through the stated support period.

If you're looking for equivalent functionality or continued support, F5 recommends Medium CE Nodes, which are now offered at the previous price point of Small CE Nodes. This adjustment is intended to ensure a seamless transition and continued value while streamlining offerings on the F5 Distributed Cloud platform to better meet evolving market demands.

Further details on impacted SKUs, alternative options, and support transition plans are outlined in the following sections.

What's changed

  • The following Small CE Node SKUs are no longer available for purchase:
    • F5-V-O-MCE-STK-ND4C
    • F5-V-O-MCE-STK-N4-B
    • F5-V-O-MCE-STK-N4-B2
    • F5-V-O-MCE-MSH-N4-B2
    • F5-V-O-MCE-MSH-ND4C
    • F5-XC-MCE-MSH-ND4C-M
    • F5-V-O-MCE-MSH-N4-B
  • Medium CE Nodes are now priced at the previous Small CE Node price.
  • Large CE Nodes are now priced at the previous Medium CE Node price.
  • These changes apply to both Term Subscription SKUs and FCP-B SKUs.

Why these changes are necessary

These changes reflect our commitment to delivering the best possible experience across all deployment scenarios. By standardizing Medium CE Nodes as the new entry point, F5 is ensuring that you have the performance headroom and flexibility needed for emerging networking and security use cases—without the risk of under-sizing.

Resizing and migration guidance

If you currently operate Small CE Nodes, F5 recommends that you proactively plan and begin the transition to Medium or Large CE Nodes.

F5 recommends the following phased approach:

  1. Begin transition (starting January 2026).

    You should begin transitioning after the December 2025 release of F5 Distributed Cloud Services.

    • Before resizing, upgrade to CE software version crt-20250701-0199 or later to benefit from improved stability and resiliency enhancements.
    • Begin transition planning to move from Small CE Nodes to Medium CE Nodes starting January 2026, aligning activities with scheduled maintenance windows or renewal cycles.
    • Please note that this procedure will temporarily impact application traffic. You should plan this activity during a maintenance window, with an expected downtime of approximately 30 minutes per CE Node.
  2. Complete transition by July 1, 2028 (or end of contract).
    • Complete resizing to Medium CE Nodes by July 1, 2028, or end of contract, whichever is first.
