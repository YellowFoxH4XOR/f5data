K000135754: Standby BIG-IP going offline after full sync

Support Solution

Environment

  • LTM
  • HA pair
  • in-tmm monitors are configured
  • ssl-profile is changed

Cause

Seen behaviour is caused by a known issue tracked with the Bug ID 1305697.

Recommended Actions

As a workaround you can disable in-tmm monitors, and avoid performing a full sync after modifying in-tmm ssl monitors. If required an EngHF with the fix can be provided.
