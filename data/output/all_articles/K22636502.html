Description

During reboots of blade or whole chassis, one blade takes dramatically longer than the others to boot

Environment

  • BIG-IP VIPRION Chassis and blades

Cause

This issue could potentially be caused by pending hard drive failure

Recommended Actions

  1. Perform EUD to test for hardware issues
  2. Review boot logs in /var/log/ltm and for boot errors and /var/log/kern for ATA errors
  3. Generate a qkview and contact F5 Support for assistance
