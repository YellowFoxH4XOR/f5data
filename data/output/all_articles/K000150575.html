With the availability of the F5 VELOS CX1610 chassis and the F5 BX520 blade, F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) for the F5 VIPRION C4480 and C4800 chassis and the F5 VIPRION B4450 and B4460 blades.

This product notice does not impact any subscription licenses or service agreements. Field Upgrade and Add-on SKUs are not included in this EoS announcement.

For information about BIG-IP and F5 platform lifecycles, refer to the K4309: F5 hardware product lifecycle support policy.
