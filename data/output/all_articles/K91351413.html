Known Issue

The Log out button on the BIG-IP Configuration utility may not function as expected.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • You have a BIG-IP system running one of the following software versions:
    • 12.0.0
    • 11.6.1 HF2
    • 11.6.1 HF1
    • 11.5.4 HF2
  • You are using the Internet Explorer 8.0 browser to access the Configuration utility.
  • You attempt to log out of your BIG-IP system by clicking the Log out button located in the top right corner of the page.

Impact

The affected Internet Explorer browser fails to redirect the Configuration utility to the Login page to re-authenticate the user.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When you click the Log out button located on the top right corner of the Configuration utility, you do not get redirected to the Login page to be re-authenticated by the BIG-IP system.
  • When you close the Internet Explorer browser and open the Configuration utility in a new Internet Explorer browser session, you get a login prompt, as expected.

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 516841 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can close the existing Internet Explorer browser and open a new Internet Explorer browser session to get re-authenticated by the Configuration utility.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.
