Description

Unable to delete an inactive ASM security policy
When trying to delete an ASM security policy the following error message appears on the GUI:
01071726:3: Cannot delete policy '/Common/example_https_virtual'. It is in use by ltm policy '/Common/example_https_virtual'.
 

Environment

  • ASM security policy
  • LTM policy

Cause

Can't delete an ASM policy if it's still referenced by a rule in an LTM policy.

Recommended Actions

To delete the policy you will need to delete first the LTM traffic policy associated to it.

  1. On GUI go to Local Traffic  ››  Policies : Policy List
  2. Locate LTM policy "asm_auto_l7_policy__example_443" and delete it
  3. Then should be able to delete the inactive asm policy
