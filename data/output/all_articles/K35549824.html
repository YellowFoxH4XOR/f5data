K35549824: NFV Solutions and F5 VNF Manager software upgrade policy

Type
Policy
Published
2018-10-30
Updated
2023-08-30
Applies to
  • Network Function Virtualization
  • F5 VNF Manager
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K35549824

Policy Information

Following the purchase of a perpetual license for an F5 NFV Solution, customers may upgrade their current version to the next newer version of the solution, using the prescribed upgrade path and method, for a period of four years from the date of purchase (when the customer receives the VNF Manager serial number), contingent upon the product being actively supported. Customers using a subscription model may upgrade continuously, as long as the subscription is active.
