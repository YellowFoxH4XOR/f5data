Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Rebooting a blade in a VIPRION system after changing the blade's management address may lead to a race condition that causes the local syslog daemon to lose awareness of the cluster.

In a VIPRION chassis, syslog is meant to be aware of the other members of the cluster: Once the blades are initialized, each blade sends relevant syslog messages to each of the other blades in the cluster. Each blade in the cluster is configured to receive these messages from other cluster members. However, after changing the management address and rebooting, the blade no longer sends local messages to the other blades and does not process the messages received from the other blades.

You may be able to identify whether your system is affected by this issue by examining the prefix of the messages logged by the blade. Blades that are cluster-aware log both local and remote messages in the following format, specifying the slot from which the message originated:

<date> <slot>/<hostname> <syslog message>

For example:

Dec 11 15:09:26 slot2/bigip1 err logger: demo

When this issue occurs, locally originated messages are instead logged in the following format, indicating local rather than the slot location:

<date> local/<hostname> <syslog message>

For example:

Dec 11 15:09:26 slot2/bigip1 err logger: demo

Note: During blade initialization, it is normal to see messages with the local/<hostname> prefix. Once the blade initialization is complete, the blade will begin logging with the expected slot location prefix.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR130868 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 10.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, ASM, GTM, PSM, Link Controller, WebAccelerator, or WOM release notes.

Workaround

You can work around this issue by typing the following command while logged into the blade that is exhibiting the behavior:

confpp unix_config_syslog

Note: Message processing on the affected blade may be temporarily interrupted. Message processing on other blades is not affected.
