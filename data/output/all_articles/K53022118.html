Description

After upgrading BIG-IP VE to 14.1.0 and later versions, you will find that the management-IP doesn't respond from remote network.
BIG-IP VE management-IP is still able to respond from local network.

Environment

  • BIG-IP Virtual Edition
  • Upgrading to 14.1.0 and later versions
  • Management route is not configured

Cause

It is due to behavior change beginning in BIG-IP VE 14.1.0.
Note: For information, refer to K55225090.

Recommended Actions

You have to configure a default management route or other specific management routes for BIG-IP VE to communicate with devices on a remote network.
For information about configuring a management route on the BIG-IP system, refer to K15040.

Additional Information

None.
