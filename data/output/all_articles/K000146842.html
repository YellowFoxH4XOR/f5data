What Happened?

  • We want to ensure our process is followed in the event of a DDoS attack.
  • We want notification emails to be sent in the event of a DDoS attack.

Environment

  • F5®Distributed Cloud (XC) DDOS

Resolution/Answer

To set up real time incident procedures for DDoS attacks:

  1. Open a new ticket with XC Support to "Update RTIP" with the specified procedure you want the SOC to follow.
  2. The SOC will confirm the update the RTIP and contact information for you account 

For AA (Always Available) customers, if any special requirements apply to route origination, such as: the customer must authorize each Route advertisement, the customer will initiate the advertisement, only advertise certain prefixes, etc. Please add these instructions to the RTIPs. Otherwise SOC will follow the standard procedure which is to advertise a prefix at the discretion of our analysts. This includes advertising when a prefix is under attack and/or if further inspection of the traffic is required.
