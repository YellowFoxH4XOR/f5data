Topic

Summary

The volume free space reported by ARX may be incorrect.

Description

By default, the ARX system will calculate a volume’s free space automatically. If a volume contains multiple shares from the same back-end storage device, the ARX system will only count the free space reported by one of the shares on the device toward the total volume free space. As a result, the volume free space reported by the ARX system may be less than the actual free space on the volume.

Recommendations

You can use the freespace calculation manual command to indicate which shares within a volume should be counted, and you can optionally use the freespace adjust command to adjust the reported free space for specified shares. Additionally, you can use freespace ignore to ignore specified shares in the free-space calculation.

Supplemental Information

  • For more information about using the freespace commands, refer to the ARX CLI Reference Guide.
