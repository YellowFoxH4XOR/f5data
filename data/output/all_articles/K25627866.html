Known Issue

VMware View client users may be unable to change their password.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM access policy is configured with AD (Active Directory) AAA Cross Domain Support.
  • VMware View client users that attempt to authenticate, reside in a different AD domain than the domain configured in the access policy AD Auth agent.
  • The user's password has expired in AD.

Impact

VMware View client users are unable to update their expired password.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Users with expired AD passwords are presented with the VMware View update password screen, they enter a new password, and the system presents the screen again.
