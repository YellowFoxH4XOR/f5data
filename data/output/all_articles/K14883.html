Known Issue

The BIG-IP 800 system may not detect the insertion or removal of the small form-factor pluggable (SFP) module.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You use the BIG-IP 800 (C114) platform.
  • You insert or remove the SFP module after the BIG-IP system boot sequence starts.
  • Your BIG-IP system runs on an affected version.

Note: This issue does not affect BIG-IP 800 systems that run on any BIG-IP 11.4.0 builds.

Impact

The link status for the SFP port does not update accordingly.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP system cannot detect the insertion or removal of the SFP module.
  • The link is not established after the SFP module is inserted.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can restart the bcm56xxd process after inserting or removing the SFP module. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Restarting the bcm56xxd process disrupts the traffic that passes through the BIG-IP system. You should perform this procedure only during a maintenance window or when the system is in a standby state.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP command line prompt as the root user.
  2. Restart the bcm56xxd process by typing the following command:

    bigstart restart bcm56xxd
