Known Issue

The BIG-IP ASM bd process may stop responding when processing a malformed cookie.

Impact

The bd process restarts, dumps core, and the BIG-IP ASM may temporarily fail to process traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H426641 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • In the /var/log/ts/ts.log file, you may observe an error message similar to the following example:

    nwd.pl|ERR|<date>|44705|F5::NwdUtils::Nwd::log_failure,,Watchdog detected failure for process. Process name: bd, Failure: Insufficient number of threads (required: 8, found: 0).
    exec_core.pl|ERR|<date>|34188|main::add_ts_log_line,,ASM core restarted for recovery purposes

  • The system generates a bd core file to the /shared/core directory.
