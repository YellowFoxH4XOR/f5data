Description

Consider this article when you need to use a custom service port for a health monitor.

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • Pools and Monitors

Cause

Certain applications require monitoring services on a different port than application services. 

Recommended Actions

To accommodate this need you will create a custom health monitor and modify the Alias Port

  1. Log into the Configuration Utility
  2. Navigate to Local Traffic --> Monitors 
  3. Click on Create... in the top right
  4. Name your monitor and choose your Type of Monitor from the drop-down
  5. Configure your monitor as you would for your application service
  6. Scroll down to Alias Port --> Change the * to your desired Custom Service Port
  7. Click Finished
Apply this health monitor to the desired pool.
