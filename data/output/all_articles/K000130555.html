Description

When defining an LTM HA Pair for GSLB:Servers do you define the HA Pair with the local MGMT IPs?  Or the LOCAL Self IPs?  Or a FLOAT?

Environment

  • BIG-IP DNS
  • iQuery

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

The LTM HA pair should be added as one GSLB Server, with the two LTMs added as Devices independently using its non-floating self-IP. GTM/DNS initiates iQuery connections to all LTMs regardless of their HA state.

While big3d does listen on the management interface, it's standard practice to add your LTM devices via self-IP, not the management IP.

Additional Information

Adding an LTM HA pair as a single Device with two addresses under the Server object would cause issues. The gtmd process on the DNS device would view the HA pair as a single device with two possible iQuery connections to choose from.

Issue would arise when the gtmd sends bigip monitor probe requests to the iQuery connection to the Standby LTM device. By design, the mcpd daemon of the Standby LTM device would not give a response when being probed for the status of the Virtual Servers on that device. This in turn causes big3d to not respond to bigip monitor probe requests, leading to "no reply from big3d" error.
