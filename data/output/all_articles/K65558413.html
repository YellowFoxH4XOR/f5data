Description

You may suspect unsuccessful DNS queries and you would like to know how to get statistics about this.

Environment

  • DNS
    • queries
  • tcpdump
  • tshark

Cause
This technique will assist you in gathering statistics of the DNS queries received during the packet capture. This provides the number of DNS query types received and how many were unsuccessful.

Recommended Actions

Perform a packet capture using tcpdump then evaluate the results with tshark.
  1. Take a packet capture

    Example:

    tcpdump -s0 -ni 0.0:nnn -w /var/tmp/DNS_statistics.pcap dst port 53 and dst host 192.168.5.5

    Note: You'll need to modify the Host IP address to align with your environment.

  2. Open the packet capture and apply the following tshark filter:

    tshark -r /var/tmp/DNS_statistics.pcap -qz "\
    io,stat,60,\
    COUNT(dns.qry.type)dns.qry.type and dns.qry.type==1,\
    COUNT(dns.qry.type)dns.qry.type and dns.qry.type==28,\
    COUNT(dns.qry.type)dns.qry.type and dns.qry.type==5,\
    COUNT(dns.qry.type)dns.qry.type and dns.qry.type==15,\
    COUNT(dns.qry.type)dns.qry.type and dns.qry.type==2,\
    COUNT(dns.qry.type)dns.qry.type and dns.qry.type==12,\
    COUNT(dns.qry.type)dns.qry.type and dns.qry.type==6,\
    COUNT(dns.qry.type)dns.qry.type and dns.qry.type==33,\
    COUNT(dns.qry.type)dns.qry.type and dns.qry.type==16,\
    COUNT(dns.qry.type)dns.qry.type and dns.qry.type==252,\
    COUNT(dns.qry.type)dns.qry.type and dns.qry.type==251,\
    COUNT(dns.flags.rcode)dns.flags.rcode and dns.flags.rcode==0,\
    COUNT(dns.flags.rcode)dns.flags.rcode and dns.flags.rcode!=0"
