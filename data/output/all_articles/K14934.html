Topic

This article applies to BIG-IP 11.x. For information about other versions, refer to the following article:

  • K69933022: Overview of daemons that are monitored and restarted by the runsv utility (12.x)
  • K11488: Overview of daemons that are monitored and restarted by the runsv utility (10.x)

The BIG-IP 11.x software branch uses the runsv utility to monitor specific daemons. The runsv utility monitors specific daemons and will restart the daemon in the event of a system failure.
