Known Issue

Internet Control Message Protocol (ICMP) health monitors fail when the Virtual Local Area Network (VLAN) DAG Round Robin feature is enabled.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have enabled the DAG Round Robin feature on a VLAN.
  • You have applied an ICMP health monitor to a pool member.

Impact

Pool members appear as offline and are unavailable for load balancing.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H503986 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • Pool members are unreachable only when connecting through the BIG-IP system.
  • When you apply an ICMP type monitor to a pool, the status shows the pool as offline.

Workaround

None. You must disable the DAG Round Robin feature before applying the ICMP monitor, or choose a different monitor type.
