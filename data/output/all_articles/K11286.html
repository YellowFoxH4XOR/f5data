Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP 3900 platform fails to forward broadcast and multicast Ethernet frames between ports on the same VLAN. Due to this issue, the BIG-IP 3900 platform cannot be used as an L2 switch.

Consider the following example:

  • A BIG-IP 3900 system is configured with the internal VLAN.
  • Interfaces 1.1 and 1.2 are assigned to the internal VLAN.
  • Host1 is connected to interface 1.1 and Host2 is connected to interface 1.2.
  • A connection between Host1 and Host2 cannot be established because the BIG-IP system fails to flood ARP broadcasts to all ports on the internal VLAN.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR136964, and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 10.2.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, ASM, GTM, PSM, Link Controller, WebAccelerator, APM, WAN Optimization, or Edge Gateway release notes.

In addition, BIGIP-10.1.0-3402.0-HF2 has been issued for BIG-IP version 10.1.0. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.

For information about the F5 hotfix policy, refer to K4918: Overview of the F5 critical issue hotfix policy.

For information about installing version 10.x hotfixes on systems, refer to K10025: Managing F5 product hotfixes for BIG-IP version 10.x systems.
