Description

Cumulative Microsoft Update March 2020 breaks VPN functionality

  • After installing Cumulative Microsoft March 2020 on windows 10 machines, VPN stopped working
  • After removing the cumulative update from windows machine, VPN works correctly

Environment

  • BIG-IP VPN
  • Cumulative Microsoft March 2020 Update installed on Windows 10

Cause

This issue is connected to Microsoft bug KB4535996. For more information, refer to the Known issues in this update section of the KB4535996 page.

Note: The previous link take you to resources outside of AskF5. The third-party could remove the document without our knowledge.

Recommended Actions

This issue is resolved with the solution described in KB4554354.

Note: The previous link take you to resources outside of AskF5. The third-party could remove the document without our knowledge.

The recommended action is to apply the new patch.
