K156: Account permissions required to perform administrative tasks at the command line

Topic

To perform administrative tasks at the command line, such as installing software or running bigpipe commands, you must log in using the root account, or an account with permissions equivalent to the root account.

The admin account does not have sufficient permissions to perform administrative tasks at the command line.
