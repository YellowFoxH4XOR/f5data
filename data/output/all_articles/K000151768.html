Description

After relicensing a system with ASM or Advanced WAF provisioned, it is best practice to do a full restart to make sure all of the relevant components recognize the new license state.

Environment

One of the following modules is provisioned:

  • ASM
  • Advanced WAF

Cause

All of ASM's internal processes are not automatically restarted after a licensing change.

Recommended Actions

  • Reboot the affected unit.

Note: This is disruptive to traffic passing through the unit and will result in a failover in HA environment.

 

  • As an alternative, you can all restart of the processes on the system: bigstart restart
