Topic

This article documents program-specific procedures required to complete an Evaluation Return. Perform these procedures only if you are directed to this article as part of the return process for the Worldwide Evaluation Program.

For all other returns, or replacement of potentially defective hardware outside of this program, refer to K12882: Overview of the F5 RMA process.

Return the hardware within the specified time frame on your signed Master Agreement.

Return instructions

  1. Retain all packaging materials to reuse as return packaging.
  2. Carefully place the parts in the original electrostatic bag and packaging, making sure to package each part separately as they were shipped to you. Do not consolidate parts into fewer boxes. Each individual unit or part of an Evaluation Return will be monitored by the F5 returns management group. Any damages sustained during shipping as a result of insufficient packaging will be invoiced at full list price.
