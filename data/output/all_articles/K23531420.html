Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may incorrectly process traffic as untagged.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are running a BIG-IP i2000 series or a BIG-IP i4000 series platform.
  • Your BIG-IP system process traffic on an interface associate with a Virtual Local Area Network (VLAN) of tag value 1.

Impact

Traffic is incorrectly processed by the BIG-IP system as untagged.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Traffic is incorrectly processed by your BIG-IP system as untagged.

Workaround

There is no workaround for this issue. However, you can alternatively configure the VLAN with a tag value different than 1.
