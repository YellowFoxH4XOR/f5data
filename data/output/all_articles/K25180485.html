Known Issue

Listing BIG-IP DNS (formerly known as BIG-IP GTM) pools or wide IPs in a particular partition may return objects from all partitions.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have configured, and are making use of, multiple administrative partitions.
  • You have configured BIG-IP DNS pools or wide IPs.
  • You attempt to list the pools or wide IPs using either the BIG-IP Configuration utility, iControl, or Traffic Management Shell (tmsh).

Impact

You may be able to view objects that are in partitions to which you do not have access.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When you list wide IPs or pools from within a partition, the system displays listings from all partitions instead of the current one.
