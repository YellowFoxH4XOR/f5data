Known Issue

Fully-qualified domain name (FQDN) ephemeral nodes may not be updated after a DNS record change.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You configure multiple FQDN nodes on the BIG-IP system.
  • Two or more of the FQDN nodes resolve to the same IP address.
  • You configure a pool using the FQDN nodes.
  • You modify the DNS record for one of the FQDN nodes.

Impact

FQDN ephemeral pool members are not updated after a DNS record change.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You notice that after changing the DNS record for an FQDN node, the changes are not reflected in the pool configured with FQDN nodes.
