K22595813: Do BIG-IP / BIG-IQ support NTP version 3, 2 or 1 (NTPv3, NTPv2 or NTPv1)?

Description

This article explains if the BIG-IP is compatible with NTP version 3, 2 or 1 (NTPv3, NTPv2 or NTPv1).
At the time of publication NTP version 4 is used by default.

Environment

  • BIG-IP / BIG-IQ
  • NTP
     

Cause

None
 

Recommended Actions

  • By default the BIG-IP communicates with the ntp version 4, but also retains compatibility with ntp version 3, as defined by RFC 1305.
  • BIG-IP is also compatible with ntp version 1 and 2 as defined by RFC 1059 and RFC 1119.
    Note: The above link brings you to a third party website not maintained by F5 Networks.
  • There are some NTP servers not to respond to NTPv4 request from the BIG-IP / BIG-IQ.
    Please refer to: K09705600: BIG-IP fails to sync NTP with NTPv3 servers.
