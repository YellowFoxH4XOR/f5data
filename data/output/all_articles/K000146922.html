What Happened?

I want to know why upgrading the master node will take down all other nodes.

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud
  • Manage Site

Resolution/Answer

It is related due to the control plane components that manage the cluster state and communication. This can lead to loss of coordination, preventing nodes from functioning properly until the master is fully operational again.
