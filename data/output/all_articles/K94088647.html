Description

This article provides a guide on the requirements to open Signaling Delivery Controller (SDC) Service Request (SR). The main objective is to enable F5 Support to be able to address your suspected product issue effectively. F5 Support supports F5 technologies and would render all necessary assistance to address suspected product issues.

We also understand that your business is unique and dynamic. Some activities may be outside the scope of F5 support. F5 Professional Services (PS) can design, customize, and implement a solution that is right for your IT infrastructure, supports your business goals, and is agile enough to move your business forward. Additionally, F5 Support does not support Signaling Delivery Controller (SDC) feature customization and tailored scripts nor verify and validate Method Of Procedure (MOP), as well as migration, optimization and audit. This includes F5 PS deployed solutions that are implemented at the customer request. F5 can render consultative assistance when necessary. However, F5 is unable to take any responsibility for any feature customization and tailored scripts deployed nor MOP used to perform a change. As such, F5 is also unable to guarantee any Root Cause Analysis (RCA).  When such a need arises, we recommend you to talk to a F5 Account Representative so that F5 PS can be engaged.

Please adhere to the guidelines described below so that all needed firsthand analysis is performed prior to opening the SR. If otherwise, similar to SR that is outside of the scope of F5 Support, we may have to reject and close your SR. Please feel free to open a new SR once you have sufficient analysis performed on suspected SDC product issue.

F5 Support will provide the necessary assistance to resolve issue to SR that follows the guidelines of this article, however F5 does not guarantee RCA nor its timeline.

Recommended Actions

The actions or steps required to address the topic covered in the description.

  1. If the issue arises within 14 days from a set of new implementations:
    1. If this is led by F5 PS, please have F5 PS to open the SR with F5 Support. F5 would render all assistance to assist on your suspect product issue. F5 PS would continue to interface with you on the issue
    2. If F5 PS is not involved and there is no clear evidence of a suspected product issue, please talk to a F5 Account Representative
  2. Problem statement (only one problem description per request)
    • Describe the problem fully (start time/date, if issue is still recurring) and its current impact (Including business impact)
  3.  Collect TTAs from systems involved as soon as possible, as soon as the issue happen(delay might result in logs data being rotated)
    • /opt/traffix/scripts/tta-ng.sh -n 10
  4. Please provide PCAP(if issue is traffic related)
    • Provide your troubleshooting/analysis on the PCAPs (identifying specific flow/frames) that clearly indicate problem is with SDC.  
    • Provide screenshot/Error message discover
  5. What were the last changes made prior to the issue occurring?
    • Were there any configuration changes on SDC and in your network?  
    • Were there any traffic changes?  
    • If there are, we would first recommend that these changes been roll back to validate whether the issue still persist.
    • If there are customized features or tailored scripting used, depending on the level of complexities, we may first recommend that these unsupported changes be removed first.  Please see K31353435
  6. Using the above information, analyze and describe the problem
    • Refer to specific date/Time and specific log such as cpf.log and fep.log with data on your suspicion that the issue is SDC product related
    • Please take note issues may be due to diameter peer/networks which is not on SDC, if it’s not an issue on SDC, partner can resolve without opening SR
  7. If there is suspicion to traffic flow issue, indicate the RT (route id that is expected to be using) ,
    • Share with us the expected traffic flow and compare this to current suspected erroneous traffic flow
    • Explain any transformation rules (incoming/outgoing) that are being used (please indicate the transformation id number) 
  8. Do continue with the group investigations and supply data and analysis as the Service Request evolves
  9. The above steps will provide the first level analysis to describe the problem, and would expedite our process to identify any product related issues quickly.  
    • It is difficult to proceed with Service Request when the information is incomplete which may bring about delays or even having an inconclusive Service Request.
