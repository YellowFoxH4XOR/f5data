Topic

You should consider using this procedure under the following condition:

  • You want to configure the clusterd process to communicate over the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) backplane in addition to the management backplane.

Description

The VIPRION system is a cluster of blades that work together to process network traffic. By default, each blade in a VIPRION system communicates by sending intra-blade User Datagram Protocol (UDP) datagrams with the multicast address of 224.0.0.245 as follows:

  • In BIG-IP 13.1.0 and later, the clusterd process communicates by sending intra-blade UDP datagrams over both TMM and management backplanes by default.
  • In BIG-IP versions prior to 13.1.0, the clusterd process communicates by sending intra-blade UDP datagrams only over the management backplanes by default.

Note: The blades are connected to the management backplane by the mgmt_bp interface, and connected to the TMM backplane by the tmm_bp interface.

In BIG-IP 12.1.0 the clusterd.communicateovertmmbp database variable was introduced to allow you to configure clusterd to communicate over the TMM backplane in addition to the management backplane. Configuring the clusterd process to communicate over both the TMM and management backplanes provides a redundant communication channel. In 13.1.0 and later, you can use the clusterd.communicateovertmmbp database variable prevent the system from sending intra-blade administrative traffic over the TMM backplane, if desired.

Prerequisites

You must meet the following prerequisite to use this procedure:

  • You can access the TMOS Shell (tmsh).

Procedures

Configuring the clusterd process to communicate over the TMM backplane (12.1.0 - 12.1.4)

In BIG-IP 12.1.0 - 12.1.4 you can configure the clusterd process to communicate over the TMM backplane, in addition to the management backplane. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Important: For vCMP systems, you should employ this procedure on the vCMP Guests.

Impact of procedure: After configuring the clusterd process to communicate over both the TMM and management backplanes, it may be more difficult to detect failure of the management backplane.

  1. Log in to tmsh by typing the following command:

    tmsh

  2. To configure the clusterd process to communicate over the TMM backplane, type the following command:

    modify /sys db clusterd.communicateovertmmbp value true

Configuring the clusterd process to communicate over the management backplane only (13.1.0 and later)

You can prevent the system from sending intra-blade administrative traffic over the TMM backplane, and configure the clusterd process to communicate only over the management backplane. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of procedure: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to tmsh by typing the following command:

    tmsh

  2. To configure the clusterd process to communicate over the management backplane only, type the following command:

    modify /sys db clusterd.communicateovertmmbp value false
