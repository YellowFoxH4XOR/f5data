Known Issue

BIG-IP Edge Client may exit during Network Access tunnel reconnection.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • BIG-IP Edge Client has established a Network Access tunnel session with the BIG-IP APM system.
  • BIG-IP Edge Client loses network connectivity to the Network Access tunnel, and the reconnection is protracted.

Impact

During protracted reconnections, BIG-IP Edge Client exits and requires a restart.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • BIG-IP Edge Client exits during a protracted reconnection and needs to be restarted.
