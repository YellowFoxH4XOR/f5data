Description

Cookie persistence is not properly working on Chrome. Other browsers might also be impacted.

The pool member (node) is running Tomcat 9.0.27.

Environment
  • Cookie persistence
  • Chrome browser
  • Tomcat webserver 9.0.27

Cause

The issue is related to Tomcat bug 64226. The Tomcat webserver will return in a CET timezone thus causing the cookie to have expired.

Recommended Actions

The fix is to upgrade Tomcat webserver to at least 9.0.34.

  1. Consult your webserver administrator to perform this upgrade task.
    Important: Upgrading the Tomcat webserver might impact your application. Please consult your application developer. F5 will not take responsibility for any unexpected outcome.
  2. You might want to engage with Apache to get further details on Tomcat bug 64226.
