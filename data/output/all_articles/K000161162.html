Description

After renewing BIG-IP VE subscriptions and updating licenses using BIG-IQ, devices may become disconnected from their device group and experience failures with high availability (HA) and configuration synchronization (config sync). Symptoms include:

  • Devices showing as disconnected from the device group after license renewal
  • Config sync failures with "Host Unreachable" errors
  • Issues with HA communication between BIG-IP devices

These issues can occur following license changes or provisioning of new modules.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ version 8.3.0 managing BIG-IP VE devices
  • Devices configured in HA pairs with config sync enabled
  • License

Cause

Bug ID 1014361: Config sync fails after provisioning APM or changing BIG-IP license

Answer/Recommended Actions

Restart the mcpd process on affected BIG-IP devices as a workaround for Bug ID 1014361
