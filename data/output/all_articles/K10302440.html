K10302440: WideIP shows availability as OFFLINE although its pool is unmonitored and yielding responses

Type
Support Solution
Published
2020-04-02
Updated
2025-02-06
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP DNS: 13.1.X, 13.1.4, 13.1.3, 13.1.1, 13.1.5, 13.1.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K10302440

Description
A WideIP may show its availability as Offline although its primary pool is Unmonitored (Blue square). In this state, it continues to deliver GSLB DNS responses from its Unmonitored pool.

show gtm wideip a example.f5support.local
-----------------------------------------------
Gtm::WideIp::A example.f5support.local
-----------------------------------------------
Status
  Availability : offline
  State        : enabled
  Reason       : No enabled pools available

Environment

  • BIG-IP DNS
  • WideIP
  • GSLB pool
  • Fallback pool
  • No monitors applied

Cause
A pool with no attached monitor has availability Unknown which makes it eligible to deliver DNS responses.  However, in the case that there is a Fallback pool with no monitor, its availability would be Offline, propagating this availability to the WideIP.

Recommended Actions

  • No action needed, but this behavior can lead to administrator confusion.
    or
  • Follow F5 recommended best practice to apply monitors to your pools in order to allow the WideIP to respond intelligently.
