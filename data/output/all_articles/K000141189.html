K000141189: Will ID1161913 impact an upgrade from BIG-IP v16.1.4 to v16.1.5?

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-09-26
Updated
2024-09-26
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.1.X, 17.1.1, 17.1.0, 16.1.4
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000141189

Description

You need to upgrade from v16.1.4 to v16.1.5, the previous upgrade from 15.1.8, 15.1.8.1, 15.1.8.2, or v15.1.9 to any 16.x (prior to 16.1.4), had challenges due to ID1161913.

Will this happen again while upgrading from v16.1.4 to v16.1.5 ?

Environment

  • Upgrade
  • BIG-IP v16.1.4
  • ID1161913

Cause

None. Bug ID1161913 is fixed in v16.1.4 and v17.1.x

Recommended Actions

This version you are upgrading from is not affected by the mentioned Bug ID1161913.

Follow the recommended upgrade procedure based on your setup.

Additional Information

None
