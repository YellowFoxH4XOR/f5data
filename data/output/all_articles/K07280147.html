Environment

  • vCMP Host
  • vCMP guest
  • Failover

Cause

Possible corruption of the binary configuration image.

Recommended Actions

The actions or steps required to address the topic covered in the description.

  1. Backup the configuration of your host and guests following article K13132
  2. Get the master key of your host and guests following article K9420
  3. Perform an mcpd forceload following article K13030
  4. After the forceload, confirm in /var/log/ltm confirm if the logs are still being generated.

Additional Information

If you are using auto-sync, change it to manual sync before you perform the mcpd forceload.
