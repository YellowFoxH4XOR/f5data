BIG-IP 16.1.x reached the End of Life (EoL) milestone on July 31, 2025.   
BIG-IP 15.1.x reached the End of Life (EoL) milestone on December 31, 2024. 

Important:  BIG-IP 16.1.x FIPS Validated platforms and VE FIPS Addon license will not reach the EoL milestone until July 31, 2026.

BIG-IP Long-Term Stability Releases have a Standard Support Phase of four years from the date the x.1.0 version for a given major version was released.

At the end of a releases Standard Support phase, the release enters the End of Software Development (EoSD) and End of Technical Support (EoTS) phase.

When a version reaches EoSD, F5 no longer develops maintenance releases for a software product or software product version. F5 does not release any software product fixes, including fixes for bugs or CVEs, using maintenance or EHF releases.

When a version reaches EoTS, F5 no longer provides technical support services, such as troubleshooting or configuration advice.

The EoTS and EoSD milestone combine to mark the EoL milestone. EoL is the point at which the hardware, software, or service product is no longer supported by F5 in any way.

F5 strongly encourages you to plan ahead and upgrade to the latest BIG-IP release, 21.0.x or 17.1.x, so that your organization's systems continue to receive technical support and software development support, including bug fixes and security (CVE) updates. You can download the 21.0.x or 17.1.x release by selecting RESOURCES from the top navigation bar on MyF5 and choosing Downloads from the menu.

For information on downloading software, refer to K000090258: Download F5 products from MyF5.

For information on upgrading your system, refer to the K84205182: Guide contents | BIG-IP update and upgrade guide. To speak with F5 regarding assistance with your upgrade, refer to: Upgrade BIG-IP.

For more information on F5 software lifecycles and the BIG-IP software support policy, refer to K5903: BIG-IP software support policy and K8986: F5 product support policies.
