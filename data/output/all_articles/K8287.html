Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The switchboot utility does not report the new build number after a hotfix rollup is installed.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR65654.

This issue has been fixed in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.3.1-HF1 issued for BIG-IP version 9.3.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.

For information about the F5 hotfix policy, refer to K4918: Overview of F5 critical issue hotfix policy.

For information about managing F5 product hotfixes, refer to K6845: Managing F5 product hotfixes.

To view a list of the latest available hotfixes, refer to K9502: BIG-IP hotfix matrix.
