Known Issue

Client connections that rely on dynamically discovered routes may fail to remirror after the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) restarts.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have multiple BIG-IP systems in a high availability (HA) configuration.
  • You have configured mirroring between the BIG-IP systems.
  • You have configured the BIG-IP system to use a dynamic routing protocol.
  • A client connection is made that relies on a dynamically discovered route to reach an endpoint.
  • One of the BIG-IP systems experience a restart of the TMM.
  • The BIG-IP system that experiences the TMM restart becomes the active device.

Impact

Client connections may fail after a failover event.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Client connections fail after a failover event.

Workaround

To avoid this issue, create static routes for client connections that must remain active after any failover event. For more information refer to K13833: Managing static routes (11.x - 12.x)

Impact of workaround: The impact of the suggested workaround depends on the specific environment. F5 recommends that you test any such changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.
