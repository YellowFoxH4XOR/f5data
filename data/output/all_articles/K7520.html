Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The attack events tables are not cleared in the correct order. As a result, new records may be removed before old records.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR78357 and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM version 9.4.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM release notes.
