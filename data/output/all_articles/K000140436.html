Description

You can view your F5 Distributed Cloud (XC) product usage for term subscriptions on MyF5. Term subscriptions are product-based commitments that grant customers access to F5 Distributed Cloud products for a period ranging from one to three years.

Prerequisites

You must have an F5 Distributed Cloud term subscription to use this article.

Procedure

The XC Product Usage page displays all your F5 Distributed Cloud term subscriptions.

To view your F5 Distributed Cloud product usage, do the following:

  1. Go to My Products and Plans > XC Product Usage.
  2. Select the subscription in which you want to see your product usage.

    The product usage page for your subscription displays.

Status column

Definitions of statuses that display in this column:

  • At Commit—You used 100% of your entitlement for this product.
  • Overused—You used more than your current entitlement for this product.
  • Underused—You used less than your current entitlement for this product.
  • New Product—This product is new to you, and F5 does not yet have data about you using it.
  • No Usage—You have not used this product.
