Description
Just received a new or RMA replacement device. Didn't receive or have misplaced the email from F5 containing the base registration key of the device

Environment

  • New Appliance
  • RMA replacement
  • Virtual Edition
  • Base Registration key

Cause
None

Recommended Actions

For Virtual Edition, 
Contact F5 Technical Support  with your Serial Number which starts with a "Z" followed by 7 characters. This information can be found from your Sales Order.

For Existing Appliance:
Contact F5 Technical Support with your Chassis Serial Number.

For Brand New Appliance (factory-installed system):
For BIG-IP: K3782 : Finding the serial number or registration key of your BIG-IP system - Locating the registration key on an unlicensed system.
For BIG-IQ: K14383: Find the serial number or registration key of your BIG-IQ system - Locating the registration key on an unlicensed system.
