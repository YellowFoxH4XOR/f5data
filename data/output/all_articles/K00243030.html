Description
After installing a new Virtual Edition license on the BIG-IP device, the Configuration Utility and the Shell prompt show that the unit is "Offline".

Environment

  • Higher version VE 
  • VE Plus License Offering
  • Successful license activation

Cause
The reason for the unit to remain "Offline" even when the license seems to be successfully activated is a failure to meet the VE Offering license versions.
For example, a BIG-IP VE SKU F5-BIG-xxx-xxx-V16 enables customers to have perpetual usage with upgrades up to and through the last major, minor, maintenance, and point release of BIG-IP 16.x.x

Recommended Actions

  • Check what software version the unit is running on:
  1. Log in to the BIG-IP command line using SSH
  2. Issue the command: #tmsh show sys license
  3. Observe the output: "Licensed Version     11.6.X"
  4. Based on the output, refer to K15643 to determine if the version is being supported by the current VE Offering.
  • Contact your F5 Sales representatives to upgrade to a newer VE Offering if your current software does not support your desired upgrade version.
