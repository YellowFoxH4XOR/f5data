K000152933: Why do the Routed DDoS usage statistic change depending on time range?

Description

  • Why are the Routed DDoS statistics on the Distributed Cloud Console different depending on the time range selected?
  • Why are Routed DDoS statistics on the Distributed Cloud Console different than what we observed on our routers?
     

Environment

  • F5®Distributed Cloud Console
  • Routed DDoS​​​​​​​

Answer/Recommended Actions

This is due to the data roll ups changing depending on the time range select. The longer the range, the more the statistic will be average across wider time window buckets. 

To see the most accurate values, limit the time range to 24 hours or less as this will ensure the data rollups do not affect the average volume statistic.
