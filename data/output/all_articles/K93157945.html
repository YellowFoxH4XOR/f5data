Description
Venafi is a third-party certificate management product that has been introduced into BIG-IQ specifically so that certificate and BIG-IP admins have a centralized certificate renewal and deployment process.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ 7.x
  • Venafi certificate management account

Cause
This article offers a link to the customer experience of deployment on BIG-IQ with Venafi.  It illustrates the steps required to:

  • Add Venafi as a third party certificate manager on the BIG-IQ
  • Deploy a new application with a machine identity provided by Venafi to a BIG-IP
  • Import an existing application from BIG-IP and migrate the certificates to BIG-IQ certificate management.
