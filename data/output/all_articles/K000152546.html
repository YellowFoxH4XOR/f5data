K000152546: MGMT IP shows 0.0.0.0 when Cisco Port Security is used

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-07-14
Updated
2025-07-15
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000152546

Description

When restrictions on the number of MAC addresses are enforced on your rSeries or VELOS system's management interface, you may see connectivity fail, and '0.0.0.0' show for your management IP.

 

Environment

  • rSeries
  • VELOS
  • Management Interface

 

Cause

With port security configured to only allow one MAC address, it may block your rSeries or VELOS system, as there are multiple MACs on your system which may respond. This condition will remain until you turn off port security for the number of MAC addresses off on the switch, and shut/noshut the port.

 

As the command "show system mgmt-ip" shows your current mgmt IP address, not your configured mgmt IP, when the port is oper-status down, "show system mgmt-ip" will instead provide 0.0.0.0 for output.

 

Recommended Actions

As your rSeries or VELOS can use more than one MAC address to respond to queries, you will want to remove restrictions on the number of MAC addresses used on the port from your switch. Once this is completed, shut/no-shut the port on the switch side. Please follow your manufacturer's recommendations when performing these steps.
