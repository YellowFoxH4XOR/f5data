K05260343: High CPU Utilization Observed

Description

When viewing Control Plane CPU graphs in the Dashboard, you will often see high utilization and/or a wide range of utilization from moment to moment.

You will see this behavior on a wide range of BIGIP systems when viewing Control Plane statistics by graph:

  • Statistics  ››  Analytics : CPU
  • Statistics  ››  Dashboard

Environment

BIG-IP

Cause

This is expected behavior. Your BIGIP system is optimized for moving your network traffic without delay or interruption. Tasks are split up between those which move your network traffic ("tmm", your data plane) and the rest ("control plane") which provide support for moving your traffic. The tasks assigned to your control plane are processed at a "normal" priority instead of the "high" priority used for tmm tasks. This can mean that when using a visual aid which does not account for priority, the control plane can vary widely over a period of time where the tmm graphs can look far more sedate. 

Recommended Actions

When looking into CPU utilization, we recommend using "top" if looking for a perspective from the Linux host, or "tmsh show sys tmm-info" for utilization data at the "tmm" level.
