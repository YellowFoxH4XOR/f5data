K81404460: BIG-IQ DNS Sync Group alerts may not indicate an issue with the DNS sync group

Type
Knowledge
Published
2021-04-02
Updated
2023-02-21
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 7.X.X, 5.X.X, 8.X.X, 6.X.X
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K81404460

Topic

Occasionally, BIG-IQ users may receive multiple "DNS Group Alert" messages throughout the day.  This may happen when:

  • DNS Groups are configured on the BIG-IQ
  • Health alerts on the BIG-IQ appear to 'flap' from green --> yellow --> green throughout the day
  • There are frequent updates to the sync group.

Symptoms

As a result of the above conditions:

  • The customer may receive excessive DNS Group alert messages.

Description

Prerequisites

You must meet the following prerequisite to use this procedure:

  • You must have an administrative role for alert management on the BIG-IQ

Procedures

Verify that there is not a reasonable cause for the alerts

You may wish to review the actual BIG-IPs reported in the alerts to ensure that there is not an actionable issue.
Review the log files on one or more of the BIG-IPs reported in the DNS Sync group for obvious errors.  
For example, you may find that an out-of-date iApp may cause some flapping.  

Increase the notification time of the alert

If you see that the DNS Sync Groups are routinely moving from green --> yellow --> green status without any alarming cause:
Go to the Monitoring tab of the BIG-IQ --> Alerts and Notifications --> Settings.
Find the listing for "DNS Sync failed" and change the threshold value to 1800 seconds.
