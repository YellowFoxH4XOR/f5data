Known Issue

The BIG-IP APM Secure Web Gateway (SWG) Category Lookup may experience performance degradation.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system is configured for SWG per-request policy.
  • The SWG per-request policy contains a Category Lookup action.
  • You have configured a custom category with a large number (1,000 and more for significant performance degradation) of unique glob patterns.

Impact

The BIG-IP APM SWG system processes HTTP requests slowly.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP APM SWG system experiences high CPU usage.
  • Your BIG-IP APM SWG system processes HTTP requests slowly.

Workaround

There is no workaround for this issue. However, you can mitigate the issue by compressing large collections of glob patterns into fewer patterns.
