Description

  • TMUI (WebUI) is unresponsive
  • TMUI (WebUI) shows no content on main menu (no "Local Traffic", "Network", "System", etc. links)

Environment

  • i4600 or i4800 hardware
  • Upgrade to v14.1.4.1
  • Only one reboot occurred (to boot into the new boot volume)

Cause

Unknown. Further problems can occur, such as unexpected failover.

Recommended Actions

Reboot the BIG-IP once after the upgrade.

Additional Information

None.
