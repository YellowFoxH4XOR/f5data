K000156525: BIG-IQ v8.4.0 may lose connectivity on its TMM self-IPs when using IPv6

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-09-17
Updated
2025-10-14
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 8.4.X, 8.4.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000156525

Description

BIG-IQ v8.4.0 may lose connectivity on its TMM self-IP that is configured with IPv6 on a KVM hypervisor and when virtio driver is being used by TMM.

Logs similar to the following can be seen on BIG-IQ's /var/log/tmm before connectivity stops:

<13> Feb 26 06:25:05 big-iq notice virtio[0:9.0]: MAC filter[1]: 33:33:ff:NN:NN:NN - deleted
<13> Feb 26 06:25:05 big-iq notice virtio[0:9.0]: MAC filter[1]: 33:33:ff:NN:NN:NN - added
<13> Feb 26 06:27:19 big-iq notice virtio[0:9.0]: MAC filter[1]: 33:33:ff:NN:NN:NN - deleted
<13> Feb 26 06:27:19 big-iq notice virtio[0:9.0]: MAC filter[1]: 33:33:ff:NN:NN:NN - added
<13> Feb 26 06:29:18 big-iq notice virtio[0:9.0]: MAC filter[1]: 33:33:ff:NN:NN:NN - deleted
<13> Feb 26 06:29:18 big-iq notice virtio[0:9.0]: MAC filter[1]: 33:33:ff:NN:NN:NN - added

The last 3 octets of the MAC being derived the the last 3 octets of the TMM interface's IPv6 self-IP.

BIG-IQ would be unable to receive replies to IPv6 NDP Neighbor Solicitation requests, resulting in connectivity failure.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ v8.4.0
  • KVM hypervisor
  • VirtIO driver used by TMM
  • IPv6 configured on TMM Self-IP

Cause

BIG-IQ v8.4.0 is built on top of TMOS v15.1.0.1 which makes it susceptible to ID1079769.

Recommended Actions

If bug ID1079769 has been hit on BIG-IQ v8.4.0, the workaround is to change the TMM driver from virtio to sock using the steps in K10142141.

Additional Information

None.
