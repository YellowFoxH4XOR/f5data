Known Issue

On rare occasions, a race condition may prevent the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) from removing Clustered Multiprocessing (CMP) peer connection flows.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP platform uses CMP with two TMM instances.
  • The connection is aborted early in the connection flow.

Impact

Over time, abandoned (zombie) connection flows eventually exhaust system memory and lead to performance degradation.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • You observe abandoned connections in the tmsh show /sys connection command output.
  • The system performance degrades.
  • TMM memory consumption steadily increases over time and the system eventually produces a core file.
  • Your BIG-IP system unexpectedly closes the CMP connection flows.
