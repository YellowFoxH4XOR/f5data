Topic

Starting in version 4.5.12 and 4.6.3 you can configure internal variables that are common to BIG-IP and 3-DNS on 3-DNS stand-alone systems using the bigpipe internal command.

Before you enable any internal variable, refer to K920: Overview of internal variables.
