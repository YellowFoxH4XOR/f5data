Description

You want to use the newer Microsoft Intune Compliance Retrieval service in place of the older Intune NAC as an MDM in your Access Policy.

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM
  • MDM Intune

 

Cause

Microsoft will ends support for Microsoft Intune NAC in March 2024:
New Microsoft Intune service for network access control
Note: This link takes you to a resource outside of AskF5, and the third party could remove the document without our knowledge.

 

Recommended Actions

Follow the steps in this DevCentral post to migrate to Compliance Retrieval Service:

Migrating F5 BIG-IP APM From Legacy NAC Service to Compliance Retrieval Service
 

Additional Information

Microsoft Intune Compliance Retrieval service is supported starting with the following versions:
F5 BIG-IP Access Policy Manager 14.1.5.2 and higher.
F5 BIG-IP Access Policy Manager 15.1.7 and higher.
F5 BIG-IP Access Policy Manager 16.1.3.1 and higher.
F5 BIG-IP Access Policy Manager 17.0 and higher.
