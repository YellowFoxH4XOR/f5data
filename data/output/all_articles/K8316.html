Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If the BIG-IP GTM listener receives a User Datagram Protocol (UDP) packet with a payload of less than 12 bytes, it causes a process loop and the BIG-IP GTM system is unable to process the packet. As a result, the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) times out while waiting for a response from the listener.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR89666, and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.4.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM Release Notes.

Additionally, Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.3-HF2 has been issued for BIG-IP 9.4.3. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
