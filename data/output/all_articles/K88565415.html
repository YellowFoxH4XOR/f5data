Description

vCMP host disk partition /appdata has only 1% free after attempting to upgrade vmcp guests
 

Environment

  • vCMP host
  • BIG-IP LTM

Cause

Upgrading the guests increased the size of the images in /appdata
 

Recommended Actions


Follow the steps in K13355459: Increasing the size of the appdata volume with the lvextend utility
