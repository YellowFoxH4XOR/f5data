Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When a response is bypassed mid-response due to buffer shortages, and the BIG-IP WebAccelerator attempts to insert the S10413 bypass pv tag, the pvac process references transaction objects that have already been freed and dumps core.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR136407 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 10.2.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP WebAccelerator Release Notes.

This issue has been fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-10.1.0-3372.0-HF1 for BIG-IP 10.1.0. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
