F5 Networks does not have specific performance data for ciphers higher than 128-bit; however, F5 Networks has tested ciphers higher than 128-bit with OpenSSL tools and measured session times to provide the following relative performance information:

  • RC4 performs at the same speed, regardless of the key size (up to 128-bit)
  • AES 128 is approximately 4 times slower than RC4
  • AES 192 is approximately 5 times slower than RC4
  • AES 256 is approximately 6 times slower than RC4
  • DES is approximately 3 times slower than RC4
  • 3DES is approximately 3 times slower than DES
  • AES 256 is approximately 20% slower than AES 128
  • AES 192 is approximately 10% slower than AES 128
