K000147065: F5® XC DNS Nameservers are not propagating the new domain

Type
Support Solution
Published

Updated
2025-02-19
Applies to

Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000147065

Description

I moved my DNS records to the F5® Distributed Cloud (XC) platform and after waiting for a couple of hours I see that the DNS is not propagating, only a couple of servers are resolving.

I also had DNSSEC enabled on our domain when I bought it from the registrar.

 

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud
  • DNS zones

Answer/Recommended Actions

There are 2 way to solve it:

  1. You remove the DS record from the parent, leaving the zone unsigned
  2. You re-enable DNSSEC in XC, and copy the DS record in the parent

Cause

The DNSSEC validation is failing because you have a DS record in the parent but DNSSEC is disabled on your XC DNS configuration.

You can check it with this tool; https://zonemaster.net/en/run-test

 

Additional Information

  • DNS propagation takes time. Often 24 to 72 hours.
  • A side note for this kind of DNSSEC issues there are Nameservers with DNSSEC enabled and disabled, for example:
    • dig www.example.com @9.9.9.9 => SERVFAIL
    • dig www.example.com @9.9.9.10 (Quad9 with DNSSEC disabled) returns the records
