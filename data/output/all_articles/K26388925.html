Description

The following symptoms may be observed:

  • TMM crashes and is unable to process traffic
  • Errors similar to the following are printed at the command line:

    emerg kernel: BUG: soft lockup - CPU#5 stuck for 67s!

Environment

  • BIG-IP platforms with both High-Speed Bridge (HSB) chips and AMD CPUs. This includes the BIG-IP 6900, 89x0, and 110x0 platforms, and the VIPRION B4100, B4200, and B4200N blades.

Cause

Unknown at time of publication.

Recommended Actions

Reboot the system to clear this condition.

Additional Information

This issue is rare and causes a HSB lockup on AMD-based BIG-IP platforms. If the issue happens continuously, run an EUD test and contact F5 Support.

Note: This article specifically does not apply to Intel-based BIG-IP systems. To determine if your BIG-IP system contains an AMD processor, review the Platform Guide for your specific BIG-IP device.
