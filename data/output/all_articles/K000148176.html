Description

  • You removed Secret Policies by mistake and want to restore it

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud (SaaS) 

Answer/Recommended Actions

To recover the deleted secret policy, follow the below steps:

  1. Go to Shared Configuration ->  Secret ->Secret Policies
  2. Toggle Show Deleted (next to search bar)
    - You will see all the deleted policies being greyed out and marked as Pending Delete. For ex Policy ves-io-allow-volterra mentioned on the below screenshot.
  3.  On the right side of the policy under Actions column click the horizontal ellipsis i.e. "..."
  4. Click Restore object to restore it
