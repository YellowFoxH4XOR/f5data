Policy Information

F5 is committed and currently working to eliminate exclusionary language in supported products and product documentation—specifically, revising slave to secondary and master to primary, when master appears in the context of master/slave, and all forms of black list and white list to deny list and allow list, respectively.

F5 follows guidance from the Inclusive Naming Initiative. For more information about recommended language replacements, refer to the Word Lists section.

Due to the extent of our code base and documentation, this process is a large undertaking and an ongoing effort. There are situations in which we cannot update certain articles because third parties use these terms.

F5 Support has no additional information about these changes.
