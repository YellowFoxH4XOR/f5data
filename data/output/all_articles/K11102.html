Topic

The ARX system does not limit the maximum number of configurable file-placement rules. The policy engine processes rules concurrently. However, a maximum of one scan per volume and up to four concurrent scans per VPU/domain are allowed.

Note: For information about understanding policy, rules, and volume scans, refer to the ARX CLI Storage-Management Guide.
