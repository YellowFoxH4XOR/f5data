Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When an administrator deletes the client root certificate from the FirePass controller, a service restart is required before the deletion takes effect. However, the Administrative Console does not indicate that a service restart is required. Client root certificates are configured  from the Device Management: Security: Certificates page of the Administrative Console.

After an administrator clicks the Delete link to remove the client root certificate, the FirePass controller displays the following warning message:

Are you sure you want to delete the currently installed client root CA cert?
This will completely remove all issued user client certificates, certificate database, and associated CRLs.

Clicking the Delete button will perform the deletion, but this will not take effect until a service restart is performed, even though the Client Root Cert(s) field on the Device Management: Security: Certificates page displays Not installed. Until the restart, the old configuration continues to be in effect, allowing users with certificates issued by the root certificate to be used for login and other functions. A service restart is performed from the Device Management: Maintenance: Restart Services page. Additionally, a full restart of the FirePass controller will cause the change to take effect.

F5 Networks Product Development is tracking this issue as CR89537.
