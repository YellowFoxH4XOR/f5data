Description

After upgrading to Apple iOS 16.5 or 16.5.1, the F5 Access clients may occasionally fail to establish a VPN connection.
By checking the client report, you may find similar error messages in the report:
xxxx-xx-xx,xx:xx:xx:xxx, 2303,20743[com.apple.usernotifications.UNUserNotificationServiceConnection.call-out],PacketTunnel, 1, RemoteEventNotification.swift:59, sendLocalUserNotification(_:object:userInfo:completionHandler:), [error sending a notification to the container app: (nil)
xxxx-xx-xx,xx:xx:xx:xxx, 2303,19211[com.apple.NSXPCConnection.user.endpoint],PacketTunnel, 48, PacketTunnelProvider.swift:1279, stopTunnel(with:completionHandler:), Stopping tunnel for a reason: 14

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM
  • F5 Access
  • Apple iOS 16.5 or 16.5.1
  • SSL VPN

Cause

This is being tracked as F5 issue ID1331181.

This issue is caused by the behavior change introduced by Apple iOS 16.5 or 16.5.1, for details please check the link New 60s timer when instantiating PacketTunnelProvider.
Note: The above links take you to resources outside of F5. Third parties could remove these documents without our knowledge.

Recommended Actions

 The current workaround: you have to establish the VPN login process within 60 seconds.

Additional Information

Apple iOS versions that are below 16.5, such as 16.4.1 or 16.4, are not affected by the recent behavior change.
