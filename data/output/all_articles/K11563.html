K11563: Windows Mobile device fails to synchronize with the error:0x80072F0D when attached to FirePass

Topic

Your Windows Mobile device may not trust the FirePass SSL certificate. As a result, your Windows Mobile device may fail to synchronize and show the following error message when connected through your FirePass controller:

error:0x80072F0D

To resolve this error you need to install the issuing certificate authority public SSL certificate; alternatively, if you are using self-signed certificates, you will need to install the SSL certificate from your FirePass controller onto your Windows Mobile Device as a trusted authority.

Note: For information about installing certificates on your Windows Mobile device, refer to your phone carrier for instructions on your specific device.

Note: For more information about extracting a FirePass self-signed certificate, refer to the FirePass Controller Administrator Guide.
