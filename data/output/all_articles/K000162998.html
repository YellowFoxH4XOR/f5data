K000162998: BIG-IQ number of process increased by 30% after upgrade

Description

SNMP monitoring and system commands such as top or ps may report an unexpectedly high process count on BIG-IQ Centralized Management. The additional entries may be runsv supervisor processes associated with managed services.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management
  • SNMP process-count monitoring
  • Linux process-monitoring commands, including top and ps

Cause

runsv processes supervise managed services and start or restart them when required. A supervisor process may be present for each monitored service, such as restjavad, avrd, or restnoded or other. These processes are included in the total process count but are not additional application worker processes.

Answer/Recommended Actions

The increased process count is expected when it consists of runsv supervisor processes. Increase the process-count threshold in the SNMP monitoring system to account for the expected supervisors.
