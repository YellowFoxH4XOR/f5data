Topic

This article applies to F5 systems with RJ-45 console port connections. For information about F5 systems with 9-pin serial console port connections, refer to the following article:

  • K587: Pinouts for serial terminal cables used to connect the 9-pin serial port on F5 products

For other F5 products including VELOS and rSeries, also refer to Cable and Adapter Pinouts.

This article provides the pinouts for the RJ-45-based console cables and adapters used for F5 platforms that have an RJ-45 console port.

Description

The pinouts listed in the RJ45/DE-9 console cable and RJ45M/RJ45F rolled adapter sections that follow, are applicable to the following F5 platforms:

  • BIG-IP 2000/4000/5000/7000/10000 Series platforms
  • BIG-IP i2000/i4000/i5000/i7000/i10000/i11000 iSeries platforms
  • VIPRION 2100/2150/2250/4100/4200/4300 blades
  • Enterprise Manager 4000
  • ARX 1500 and ARX 2500

Note: DE-9 refers to the standard 9-pin D-sub connector, and it is sometimes erroneously referred to as DB9.
