Topic

The FirePass 4000 was introduced in 2002 as a higher-end FirePass platform with clustering capabilities and support for 100 to 1,000 concurrent user sessions. Other new features included increased main storage, and optional feature card plug-in slots. The last date of sales for the FirePass 4000 was September 30, 2004.

The FirePass 4000 is currently in the Extended Support phase of its product lifecycle. This means that:

  • No new product feature enhancements are being developed for this product platform. Future releases starting at release 6.0.2 and beyond will not support the FirePass 4000.

    Important: The last versions of FirePass software to support the FirePass 4000 are version 5.5.2 of the maintenance release branch and version 6.0.1 of the feature release branch.

  • New software is not tested to ensure backwards compatibility on the FirePass 4000.

The FirePass 4000 was superseded in September of 2004 by the new FirePass 4100 series, which was designed for medium to large size enterprise businesses and provided superior reliability and functionality. New features of the FirePass 4100 include a 100 percent increase in disk capacity, increased concurrent user capability from 1,000 to 2,000 maximum concurrent user sessions, a redundant AC power supply option, an SSL hardware accelerator card add-in feature, and an ergonomic physical design that aligned the FirePass 4100 to the same look and feel as the BIG-IP product suite.

A high performance FirePass 4300 series version designed for medium to large scale enterprise businesses was introduced in March of 2007. The FirePass 4300 supports a quad core CPU for maximum performance, a built-in redundant AC power supply, and optional fiber ports. The FirePass 4300 is ideally suited for high bandwidth concurrent user performance utilizing the full FirePass features with a recommended maximum of 2000 concurrent user sessions supported on a single device.

With the FirePass 4000 now at the end of its Regular Support phase, F5 recommends that current FirePass 4000 customers under existing service contracts take advantage of the following upgrade pricing promotion to migrate to the FirePass 4100 and/or 4300 platforms and leverage the feature roadmap and performance advantages of the new platform.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the primary benefits of the 4100 and 4300 platforms over the 4000?

The 4000 is at the end of its Regular Support phase of its product lifecycle. This means that there will be no new operational features supported and that newly-developed software is no longer regression tested on this platform. The 4100 and 4300 platform offers a stable and future growth platform foundation that offers the following advantages over the previous generation 4000:

  • Higher levels of performance
  • More concurrent user options – 100 to 2000 per device
  • The features and benefits of future FirePass software versions (that is: 6.0.2 and beyond) are targeted to include:  Vista support, IE7 support, GTM integration, seamless Citrix, Gigabit Ethernet support

Based on my present and future concurrent user requirements and the best price/performance offering, what platform should I upgrade to – 4100 or 4300?

From a price/performance standpoint and based on the number of concurrent users in your enterprise, if you have 1,000 concurrent users or above, it would be price advantageous to select the 4300 platform over the 4100. Plus, customers get additional features on the 4300 at no extra cost – such as the built-in redundant AC power supply.

How many concurrent users can be supported on the 4100 and 4300 in single unit mode and clustering mode and what is the optimum concurrent user recommendation?

The 4100 and 4300 platforms can support 100 to 2,000 concurrent users per unit and 20,000 users in a 10 unit cluster. The performance may vary depending on the access policies and modes employed. Based on customer price/performance requirements, the recommended number of concurrent users for a 4100 device is approximately 500 users and 2,500 users in a 5 device cluster; the recommended number of concurrent users for a 4300 device is 2,000 users and 10,000 users in a 5 device cluster.

Will there be a disruption to my FirePass network service during the migration from a 4000 to either a 4100 or 4300?

Disruption can be minimized by first installing the 4100 or 4300 in a test configuration while the current 4000 remains in normal business operations. Once the new unit has been configured and tested, the 4000 traffic can be switched to the new units with minimum disruption to service.

Can I migrate my current FP 4000 configuration to a 4100 or 4300?

Customers may be required to upgrade their FirePass software and configuration. The current supported versions of FirePass software for the 4100 are versions 6.0.1, 6.0.2, 6.0.3, 5.5, 5.5.1 and 5.5.2. The current supported versions of FirePass software for the 4300 are versions 6.0.1, 6.0.2, and 6.0.3. If you are already using one of these supported software versions and are migrating to a 4100, no upgrade is required. If not, F5 Technical Support recommends that you upgrade to one of these supported software versions prior to migration. For customers migrating to the 4300 platform models, we recommend upgrading to the latest FirePass 6.x software version prior to migration.

If I upgrade to the FirePass 4100 or 4300 now, will I have to upgrade to yet another platform in the near future to take advantage of new features?

The FirePass 4100 and 4300 platforms will continue to offer new feature as well as maintenance upgrades. In addition, it is expected that the 4300 platform will also support the Traffic Management Operating System (TMOS).

Can my existing service contract be transferred to the new 4100/4300 or will I be required to initiate a new service contract?

Customer Support maintenance contracts are available in three levels: Standard, Premium, and Premium Plus. The normal support contract term is one year renewable until the End of Support Contract Renewal date (1 year prior to the End of Technical Support date). In the case of the 4000, depending on the remaining term of a year's support contract, a calculated credit rate will be applied in the form of a discount toward the purchase of a new 4100 or 4300 unit's service contract. The discount applied will change, subject to the return date of the replaced unit.

What should I do with the old product that is being replaced? Can I keep the 4000 in the network until the new 4100/4300 has been tested and migrated into production?

F5 will allow you up to 60 days after you receive the new unit(s) to return the old FP4000 unit(s) to F5. During this period you are entitled to maintain the FP4000 in your current network while you test, configure, and activate the new 4100/4300 into production

What are the future release features of the 4100/4300 that would compel me to migrate?

Version 6.0.2 and later versions of FirePass software include key features such as Network Access support for Windows Mobile 5 and 6 devices, enhanced web application access, Network Access support for Intel-based Mac platforms, and broader web application interoperability, as a benefit of the next generation reverse proxy.

What type of technical assistance can I expect for the migration effort?

F5 offers a range of services including Professional Services Consulting, Customer Support, and Global Training to address your migration needs. The Professional Services consulting team can provide both migration services and assistance if issues arise as a result of an upgrade through custom consulting engagements.
