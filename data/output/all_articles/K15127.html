Known Issue

In the DoS Layer 7 remote log, the value of the dos_attack_tps field that displays during an ongoing attack always appears as 0.

For example, the field displays as follows:

dos_attack_tps="0 tps"

Impact

The dos_attack_tps output is misleading and may cause users to misdiagnose a Layer 7 DoS attack.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The remote log value of the dos_attack_tps field is 0 as displayed in the following output:

    Sep 10 10:04:32 172.29.42.131 action="Blocking",hostname="a.b.c",bigip_mgmt_ip="1.1.1.131",client_ip_geo_location="",client_request_uri="",configuration_date_time="Jul 30 2013 20:00:40",context_name="/Common/vs2",context_type="Virtual Server",date_time="Jul 30 2013 20:09:26",device_product="ASM",device_vendor="F5",device_version="11.3.0",dos_attack_detection_mode="TPS Increased",dos_attack_event="Attack ended",dos_attack_id="120302513",dos_attack_name="DOS L7 attack",dos_attack_tps="0 tps",dos_dropped_requests_count="0",dos_mitigation_action="No Prevention",errdefs_msgno="13003140",errdefs_msg_name="Application DoS Event",severity="7",partition_name="",profile_name="/Common/dos",source_ip=""
