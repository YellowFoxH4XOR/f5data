Known Issue

High-Speed Bridge (HSB) lockups can occur after a HiGig MAC reset.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are running on the BIG-IP 5000-series, 7000-series or 10250 platforms.
  • Your BIG-IP system performs a HiGig MAC reset.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may trigger a failsafe event and subsequently restart.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP system fails over to another device in the high availability (HA) group.
  • In the /var/log/ltm log file, you observe error messages similar to the following example:

    bcm56xxd[<pid>]: 012c0015:6: Link: 4.1 is DOWN.
    bcm56xxd[<pid>]: 012c0012:6: Reset HSBe2 (bus 1) HGM0 MAC completed on higig2 link 4.1 down event.
    bcm56xxd[<pid>]: 012c0015:6: Link: 4.1 is UP. ...
    tmm2[<pid>]: 01230111:2: Interface 0.3: HSB DMA lockup on transmitter failure.
