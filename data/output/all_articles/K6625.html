K6625: A high number of DNS requests sent to a wide IP with persistence enabled may cause the gtmd process to crash

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The gtmd process may restart when a high number of DNS requests are sent to a wide IP with persistence enabled. BIG-IP GTM persistence records are sent to sync group members when the expiration time changes. When gtmd is forced to process a large number of DNS requests, it may receive insufficient CPU time and fail to respond to heartbeat messages in a timely manner. As a result, the gtmd process can crash.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR68950 and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.2.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM release notes.
