Known Issue

Description

You may experience delays when using the BIG-IP Configuration utility to save configurations.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have BIG-IP AAM provisioned with a large configuration.
  • You are saving new or updated BIG-IP LTM settings using the BIG-IP Configuration utility.

Impact

None

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When saving your BIG-IP LTM configuration, you may experience a delay of 20 seconds or more before receiving a response from the BIG-IP Configuration utility.
