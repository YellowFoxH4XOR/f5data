K000149755: F5OS 1.7.0 or before traffic failures with tenant running BIG-IP 17.1.2 and above

Description

When running F5OS-A 1.7.0 or earlier or F5OS-C 1.6.x or earlier and a tenant running 17.1.2 or later; this often manifests as health monitors intermittently failing, particularly ICMP monitors.  However, the traffic failures are not limited to ICMP traffic nor to health checks -- it can affect load-balanced traffic and other protocols (i.e. UDP and TCP).

Environment

  • VELOS or r5000-series, r10000-series, or r12000-series appliance
  • F5OS-A 1.7.0 or earlier or F5OS-C 1.6.x or earlier
  • Tenant running BIG-IP v17.1.2 or later

Cause

Bug ID 1785385: Intermittent traffic failures when tenant is running BIG-IP v17.1.2 or above and host is on version prior to F5OS-A 1.8.0 or F5OS-C 1.8.0

Answer/Recommended Actions

Upgrade to F5OS 1.8.0 or later to resolve issue for all tenants

OR

Install an engineering hotfix (EHF) containing the fix for ID1785385 in all tenants to resolve this issue on a per-tenant basis.

Contact F5 support for an EHF on 17.1.2 to fix ID1785385
