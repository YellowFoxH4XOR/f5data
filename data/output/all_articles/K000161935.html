Environment

  • BIG-IP VE
  • Google Cloud Platform (GCP)
  • quickstart GDM v2 template from GitHub (f5-google-gdm-templates-v2)
  • GCP Organization Policy with constraints/compute.managed.restrictProtocolForwardingCreationForTypes enabled

Cause

The GCP Organization Policy constraint constraints/compute.managed.restrictProtocolForwardingCreationForTypes is configured to restrict the types of protocol forwarding deployments (internal or external) allowed in the environment. If set too restrictively (e.g., only INTERNAL is allowed), the deployment of BIG-IP VE that requires EXTERNAL forwarding rules will be denied by GCP.

Answer/Recommended Actions

  1. Review the GCP Organization Policy for the constraint constraints/compute.managed.restrictProtocolForwardingCreationForTypes in the affected project or organization. 
  2. Ensure that the allowlist for this constraint includes the required forwarding type (INTERNAL, EXTERNAL, or both) needed for the BIG-IP LTM deployment.
  3. If necessary, update the Organization Policy to permit the required forwarding rule type.
  4. Retry the BIG-IP VE deployment after updating the policy.

Additional Information

The F5 GDM v2 template relies on modules that create forwarding rules as part of the deployment process. There is no supported method to bypass these modules within the template. The error is not caused by the F5 GDM template itself but by the GCP Organization Policy settings in the environment. Successful deployment in other environments may occur if the Organization Policy is less restrictive.
