K10098: An online volume may be listed as offline for an ARX policy

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. An ARX policy rule's volume scan may complete while files are still being copied. If the volume becomes unavailable and is marked offline while the files are still being copied, another policy rule volume scan will not be triggered when the volume becomes available. As a result, the policy rule will erroneously indicate the volume is offline, and the migration will fail.

You can use the the show policy command to view the policy rule status. If the policy is marked offline, the command output will appear similar to the following example:

                                                            Status                
Namespace       Volume         Rule          Vol. Scan        Migration           
--------------  -------------  ------------- -------------------------------- 
policy          /v0            allfiles      Offline: The volume is offline.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as ID 34445 and it was fixed in ARX versions 4.1.3 and 5.0.6. For information about upgrading, refer to the ARX Release Notes.

Workaround

You can work around this issue and allow the migration to continue. To do so, you must disable and re-enable the policy rule, which will trigger another volume scan.
