Policy Information

Important: This article applies only to existing F5 customers with existing Signaling Delivery Controller (SDC) deployments.

In September 2018, F5 announced the End of Sale (EoS) of the F5 Signaling Delivery Controller (SDC) (refer to K44314166: End of Sale announcement for SDC). EoS was later extended to October 2022 (refer to K50550290: SDC EoS extension).

Following discussions with SDC customers, F5 decided to provide more time for migration away from SDC. As a result, SDC support dates have been updated. To review the SDC software support policy and dates, refer to K14402: Traffix software support policy.

Questions

For more information about this announcement, contact your local F5 Partner Business Manager or Inside Sales Representative.
