K000140169: MS SCOM agent and/or F5 Management Pack for SCOM causing restjavad issues on Big-IP

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-06-27
Updated
2024-06-27
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.1.1
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000140169

Description

Customers who had Microsoft SCOM Agent (System Center Operations Manager Agent) installed on their Big-IP v15.x seemingly with no problems have reported problems with restjavad when they upgrade to v17.x, until they uninstalled the SCOM agent from Big-IP.

The problems often manifested as admin GUI slowness on the Big-IP due to the underlying restjavad problems.

Environment

Big-IP v17.x

 

Cause

Microsoft SCOM agent is not supported on Big-IP, and F5 Monitoring Pack has not been supported on Big-IP for many years now. F5's current solution in BIG-IP monitoring and management is BIG-IQ (effectively, the F5 Management Pack has been superseded by Big-IQ).

 

Recommended Actions

Admins who have either Microsoft SCOM Agent or the F5 Management Pack installed on their Big-IP machines should ensure they uninstall them first before upgrading to v17.x or above, and use Big-IQ for monitoring or managing their Big-IP machines if required.
