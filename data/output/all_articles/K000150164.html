In this video, AskF5 shows you how to upgrade or update software on a standalone BIG-IP system. After the installation, you must reboot your BIG-IP system to the newly updated or upgraded software volume.

Important: Upgrading or updating a standalone BIG-IP system requires down time for your network traffic because there is no high availability (HA) peer to handle traffic. F5 recommends that you perform this procedure during a maintenance window.

F5 has more videos and free training to help you upgrade or update your BIG-IP systems.

To see a list of videos, refer to K02721907: Frequently asked questions for upgrade and update videos.

This demo uses BIG-IP 14.1.0 on a standalone system.
