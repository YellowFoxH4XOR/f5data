Topic

F5 offers the following support for ZoneRunner, BIND, and the named process:

ZoneRunner support

ZoneRunner is an F5 product used for zone file management on the BIG-IP DNS (formally known as GTM) system. ZoneRunner will receive the same levels of support as BIG-IP DNS.

BIND support

F5 support for BIND is limited to issues that impact security, critical defects, and configuration changes needed to support DNS Express within the BIND feature. Enhancements and non-critical changes to BIND are not supported by F5. F5 does not provide support for the actual configuration of BIND zones through the command line or troubleshooting support for zones other than those created and managed by the BIG-IP software. F5 will provide support for the configuration of BIND through ZoneRunner. F5 does not support modifications to the BIND /var/named/config/named.conf file, except those required to support DNS Express, as the modifications may adversely impact the ZoneRunner daemon (zrd).

For assistance in the configuration, operations, and maintenance of BIND, refer to https://www.isc.org/downloads/bind/doc and/or DNS and BIND, 5th Edition by Cricket Liu, Paul Albitz.

Note: These links take you to resources outside of AskF5, and it is possible that the documents may be removed without our knowledge.

named process support

F5 will address any security vulnerability discovered in association with the named process. Additionally, F5 will occasionally offer upgraded versions of BIND, which include the named process, in conjunction with upgrades of the BIG-IP software.

Any known issues related specifically to BIND, however, will not be supported or fixed by F5. F5 will determine if the compromised portion of BIND directly impacts BIG-IP functionality and security.
