Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Under certain conditions, when the mcpd process exchanges a high volume of messages with the bcm56xxd switch driver process, bcm56xxd may fail to forward one or more LACP messages to lacpd. As a result, LACP timeouts may occur.

Note: For more information about the mcpd, bcm56xxd, and lacpd processes, refer to K8035: Overview of BIG-IP daemons.

F5 Networks Product Development is tracking this issue as ID 226418, ID 327810, and ID 328331 (formerly CR141447).

This issue has been fixed in Hotfix-BIGIP-9.4.8-407.0-HF4 issued for BIG-IP version 9.4.8, in Hotfix-BIGIP-10.1.0-3402.0-HF2 issued for BIG-IP version 10.1.0, and in Hotfix-BIGIP-10.2.0-1755.1-HF1 issued for BIG-IP version 10.2.0. You may download these hotfixes or later versions of the hotfixes from the F5 Networks Downloads site.
