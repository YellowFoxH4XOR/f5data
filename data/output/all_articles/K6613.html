Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Under rare circumstances, the BIG-IP LTM FastHTTP profile advances the server-side sequence number too far when dealing with a client in the FIN_WAIT_1 state sending incorrect TCP sequence numbers. This behavior occurs after the following sequence of events:

  • A client sends a FIN ACK packet that does not ACK the entire server response
  • The client follows the FIN ACK packet with a bare ACK, advancing the TCP sequence number past the FIN in the first packet
  • If a new client request is then sent down the same server-side connection, the BIG-IP LTM will generate bad sequence numbers

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR69130, CR68969, CR68968, and CR68526 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM versions, 9.1.3, 9.2.4, 9.3, and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
