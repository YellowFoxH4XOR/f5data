Topic

You may notice changes to the appearance of the BIG-IP APM webtop if you upgrade to BIG-IP 14.0.0 or later from an earlier version.

Description

Beginning in BIG-IP 14.0.0, resource items on the BIG-IP APM full webtop are displayed as tiles. This change is cosmetic and made as part of the improvements to the BIG-IP APM software. It is not possible to revert to the previous look and feel by making configuration changes. To organize resources on a full webtop, you can consider configuring webtop sections. For more information, refer to the Webtop Sections chapter of the BIG-IP Access Policy Manager: Third-Party Integration  manual.
