K51432772: Adding the SAML 2.0 Assertion of AuthnRequest ForceAuthn=True

Type
Support Solution
Published
2021-11-16
Updated
2025-02-06
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K51432772

Description

Users may require that  AuthnRequests generated by the BIG-IP as an SP, contain the attribute ForceAuthn=True

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM
  • BIG-IP as an SP

Cause

SAML authentication may not function as required or fail due to AuthnRequests not containing the attribute ForceAuthn=True

Recommended Actions

  1. For a user to add the SAML 2.0 Assertion of AuthnRequest ForceAuthn=True, to their APM SAML SP service, they can navigate in the GUI to Access >> Federation >> SAML Service Provider >> Local SP Services and select their SAML SP service and click 'Edit'
    Select the 'Advanced Settings' option, then click the checkbox for 'Force Authentication'
