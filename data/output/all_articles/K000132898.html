Description

You would like to modify your BIG-IP licensing using BIG-IQ but, when you are marking device in assignments section Change License, option button is greyed out. 

Environment

  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management

Cause

The problem occurs when licenses are assigned to unreachable devices.



When the devices are licensed as managed or unmanaged, then the Change License option can be used.

This is because the BIG-IQ can directly affect licenses that resides on the reachable devices (managed or unmanaged).

Recommended Actions

Changing the license for an unreachable device requires license revocation on the BIG-IQ and assignment of new one.

This also requires a transfer of the new license to the unreachable device as outlined in K69187707 .
