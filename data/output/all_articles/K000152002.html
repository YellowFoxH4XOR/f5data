K000152002: Advance End of Sale announcement for BIG-IP Virtual Edition V18 licenses

Type
Policy
Published
2025-07-01
Updated
2026-03-13
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000152002

F5 is introducing BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) V23 Version Plus (V+) licenses, which will replace the existing BIG-IP VE V18 Version Plus licenses. These V23 licenses will be compatible with BIG-IP 17.1.x to 23.x and will be released in two phases depending on the BIG-IP modules licensed:

  • Phase 1 (July 2025): The first phase release includes V23 licenses for the following:
    • BIG-IP LTM VE
    • BIG-IP Advanced WAF VE
    • BIG-IP DNS VE
    • All “Better” and “Best” bundle VE licenses
  • Phase 2 (October 2025): The second phase release will include V23 licenses for all remaining product modules and bundles, including:
    • BIG-IP APM VE
    • BIG-IP AFM VE
    • BIG-IP SSL Orchestrator VE
    • BIG-IP CGNAT VE
    • BIG-IP PEM VE
    • BIG-IP DDoS Hybrid Defender VE
    • BIG-IP VNF "Better" and "Best" bundle VE licenses

With the availability of BIG-IP VE V23 licenses, F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of BIG-IP V18 licenses. The EoS dates for these licenses will align closely with the availability of their replacement V23 licenses. As such, all V18 licenses with V23 replacements released in Phase 1 will reach EoS on October 1, 2025. All V18 licenses with V23 replacements available in Phase 2 will reach EoS on January 1, 2026.
