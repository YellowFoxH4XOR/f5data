Known Issue

This may be the result of a known issue. If BIG-IP attempts to allocate an invalid structure as a memory buffer, it can result in a crash. When this type of crash occurs, it will generally happen repeatedly and at regular intervals.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR34409 and CR41769.
