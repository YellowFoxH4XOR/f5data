Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. In a FirePass failover pair configuration, deleting a master group on the active controller should delete the master group on the standby controller. However, in some instances, the deleted group may later reappear on the Users: Groups: Master Groups page on the standby controller.

This issue is cosmetic. Although the name of the deleted master group may appear on the standby controller, none of the settings previously configured for that master group are retained. Therefore, users cannot authenticate to that master group if the standby controller becomes active.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR112334 and it was fixed in FirePass version 7.0.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the FirePass Release Notes.

Additionally, this issue has been fixed in cumulative hotfix HF-603-1 issued for FirePass version 6.0.3. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the cumulative hotfix from the F5 Networks Downloads site.
