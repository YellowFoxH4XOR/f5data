What Happened?

I would like to know if all the origins matching route is down will traffic fail to the default pool?

Environment

  • Origin Pools
  • HTTP Load Balancer
  • Health monitor

Resolution/Answer

When the health check fails for all origin pools pointed by the matching route, the traffic will go to the default pool.
