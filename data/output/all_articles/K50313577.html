K50313577: BIG-IP LTM 15.1.2 Device went into fail safe fault mode while upgrading

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-05-05
Updated
2025-05-05
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 15.1.2
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K50313577

Description
       Traffic was not seen from switch after reboot or failover

Environment

  • HA LTM 
  • Dual switches not peered
  • BGP used for routing

Cause
        Delay in shifting traffic was due to slow BGP convergence

Recommended Actions

  1.  Reduce bgp timers

Additional Information
        None
