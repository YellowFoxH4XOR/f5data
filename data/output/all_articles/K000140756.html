K000140756: Do r-Series devices use 13th or 14th Gen Intel core processors?

Type

Published

Updated
2024-08-20
Applies to

Source

Description

There is a concern about r-Series devices using 13th or 14th Intel core Processors.


Environment

  • Intel processors
  • 13th and 14th Generation.
  • r-Series devices.

Cause

None. Informational only.

Recommended Actions

The short answer is that r-Series devices DO NOT use 13th or 14th Gen Intel core processors anywhere.


Additional Information

None.
