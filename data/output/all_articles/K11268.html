K11268: Loading a large configuration may result in PVA acceleration remaining at None for all virtual servers

Type

Published

Source

Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When loading a configuration, the system initially sets PVA acceleration to None for all virtual servers. If the configuration is very large (hundreds of virtual servers or thousands of pool members), a communications failure between MCP and pvad may prevent the system from promoting the virtual servers to their configured PVA acceleration mode for an extended period of time (days or months).

F5 Networks Product Development is tracking this issue as CR135205.

Workaround

You can work around this issue by restarting the pvad process, thereby forcing an mcpd update. To do so, type the following command:

bigstart restart pvad

Once the update is complete, the system will promote virtual servers to their configured PVA mode. The update may take up to 5 minutes for a very large configuration.
