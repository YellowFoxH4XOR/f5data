Description

F5 does not support updating VMware Tools on Virtual Edition (VE) instances of BIG-IP or BIG-IQ deployed on VMware ESXi or vCloud Director.

The VMware Tools suite of utilities are designed to enhance the usability of the graphical interface console screen, such as video resolution, color depth, mouse movement, drag-and-drop, and sound of the vSphere client. The graphic functionality of the BIG-IP VE console page is limited to command-line information and does not use the enhancements provided by VMware Tools.

For more information about VMware Tools, refer to the following VMware knowledge base article: Overview of VMware Tools.
