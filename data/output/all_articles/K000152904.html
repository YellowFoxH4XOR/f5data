Description

What is an auto mitigation in the context of the routed DDOS service, and what are its advantages?

Environment

  • Routed DDOS

  • Distributed Cloud

Cause

None

Answer/Recommended Actions

Auto Mitigation is a customized defensive posture managed by the SOC team that allows customers who opt-in to benefit from automated mitigation once a high-severity alert has been triggered when traffic is routed through F5 scrubbing centers. This automation helps to significantly reduce the time needed to start a mitigation, in contrast to manual mitigations which are started once a SOC analyst has reviewed an alert and determined whether it is actionable.

This configuration only modifies the way manual mitigations are triggered, other settings like denylists, firewall rules, etc. won't be affected.

 

  • Does auto mitigation mean no manual intervention is applied?
    • No, the SOC team continues to review all alerts and mitigations, and will continue to monitor any incident until it has subsided.
  • Does enabling this configuration incur additional costs?
    • No, auto mitigations are part of the service but it is an opt-in configuration. Please contact the SOC for additional details.
  • Does auto mitigation start even if the alert is later determined to be a false positive?
    • Yes, any time an alert reaches a high severity status, an auto mitigation will be started. If our analysts determine the alert to be a false positive, the mitigation will be stopped. The SOC team regularly reviews detection and alerting thresholds in order to reduce the number of false positives and avoid unnecessarily starting mitigations.
  • Does auto mitigation adapt to multiple vector attacks?
    • Yes, the SOC has carefully crafted templates that will automatically enable/disable various countermeasures depending on the type of attack traffic observed. However, a SOC analyst can make modifications to the mitigation to optimize its effectiveness based on traffic inspection and feedback from customers.
