Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If you generate a self-signed client certificate for a local user account on the active controller in a FirePass failover pair, the client certificate is not synchronized to the standby controller. If your pre-logon sequence or master group authentication settings require a valid client certificate, local users will be unable to authenticate if the standby controller becomes active.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR111944 and it was fixed in FirePass 6.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the FirePass release notes.

Additionally, cumulative hotfix HF-603-1 has been issued for FirePass version 6.0.3. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the cumulative hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.

For instructions about obtaining a hotfix, refer to K167: Downloading software and firmware from F5.

For instructions about installing a hotfix, refer to K3430: Installing FirePass hotfixes.
