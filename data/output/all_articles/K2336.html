K2336: Reasons why Sun servers may receive large numbers of TCP resets after a BIG-IP redundant pair fails over

Topic

ARP entries are stored in the Sun server's cache and are not overwritten by gratuitous ARPs from the BIG-IP. This means that if you have not configured MAC masquerading on the server interface, the server will not accept the new BIG-IP MAC address after failover, because the Sun server has the ARP cached from the BIG-IP that was previously active.

To resolve this issue, configure MAC masquerading on the VLAN that faces the Sun servers.

For information about configuring MAC masquerading, refer to K189: Configuring MAC masquerading.
