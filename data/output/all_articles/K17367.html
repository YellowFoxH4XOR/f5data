Known Issue

If you use the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) to change the sshd log level, the level does not change.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You attempt to change the sshd log level using the tmsh utility.

    For example:

    tmsh modify /sys sshd {log-level error}

Impact

You may be unable to debug sshd errors.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Changing the sshd log level appears to have no effect.
  • All sshd log messages remain at the info level.

Note: Beginning in BIG-IP 11.5.0, the system posts sshd log messages from sshd in /var/log/ltm. In pre-11.5.0 releases, sshd messages appear in /var/log/secure.
