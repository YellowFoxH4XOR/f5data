This demo shows the basic steps in the Configuration utility, performed on a standalone system. It doesn't cover planning, backup, preparation, prerequisites, downloading software, high availability considerations, or testing, which are covered in detail in these articles, all linked below.

It's important that you familiarize yourself with these materials and perform any relevant prerequisites found in them before performing the steps in this demo.
