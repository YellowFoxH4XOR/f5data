Known Issue

A route domain's Virtual Local Area Network (VLAN) list may be synchronized to peer devices.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is in a high availability (HA) configuration.
  • Your BIG-IP system's device group is configured to use incremental synchronization.

    Note: This is the default setting.
  • You have a route domain configuration with multiple associated VLANs.
  • You modify the list of associated VLANs on one of the devices in the device group.
  • From the same device, you perform a configuration synchronization (ConfigSync) to other devices in the device group.

Impact

VLANs may be added or removed from route domain configurations on peer devices. If an added VLAN does not exist on a peer device, the configuration may fail to load.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • After performing a ConfigSync, the route domain configuration on one or more peer devices contains an unexpected list of VLANs.
  • After performing a ConfigSync, the configuration on one or more peer devices fails to load.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can disable incremental synchronization. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.
  2. Navigate to Device Management > Device Groups.
  3. Click the name of the relevant device group.
  4. Select the Full Sync check box.
  5. Click Update.
