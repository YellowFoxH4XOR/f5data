FAQ

  1. Why is F5 designating these SKUs as EoS?

    A: F5 is introducing a more granular set of IPI and BIG-IP PEM URL SKUs for each of the platforms, VIPRION, BIG-IP, and Virtual Edition (VE) for better alignment across IPI and URL filtering offerings.

  2. My customer's SKUs are listed as EoS. How does this impact them?

    A: Customers using any of the SKUs that are designated EoS can continue to use the services and are supported until their subscription ends. After their subscription to the current SKUs ends, they will need to buy one of the new SKUs listed in the previous table for their specific platform.

  3. Can customers buy the SKUs that have been designated EoS?

    A: No, customers must buy one of the replacement SKUs listed in the previous tables for their specific platform.

  4. Which SKUs should customers buy in the future?

    A: Customers can choose from the list of new SKUs listed in the previous tables for their specific platform.

  5. I am not able to find the replacement SKUs in the system.

    A: F5 is processing the replacement SKUs and they will be available on or before the EoS Effective Date.
