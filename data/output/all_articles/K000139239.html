Description

MyF5 search uses generative artificial intelligence (AI) and provides both AI-generated conversational answers and traditional search results. F5 calls the feature that produces AI-generated conversational answers generative answering.

When you enter a question or prompt into the search box, you may receive one or both of the following:

  • A natural language, conversational answer from generative answering, similar to conversational answers you get from Google and Bing search engines
  • Traditional search results

The search page does not display generative answering if it cannot locate enough information in the MyF5 knowledge base about your issue. If you do not receive an answer from generative answering, you can try providing more context to your prompt or rephrasing your prompt.

Procedures

Use generative answering in MyF5 search

  1. Go to MyF5 (my.f5.com).
  2. In the search box, enter a prompt or question related to your issue, such as How to configure multi-tenancy for BIG IP, and then press Enter.

    Generative answering displays in a box at the top of the search results page, and traditional search results display beneath it.

    Note: If generative answering is unable to locate enough information in the MyF5 knowledge base about your issue, the search page does not display an AI-generated answer. You can try again by providing more context or rephrasing your prompt.

Important information about generative AI

Generative AI enables users to quickly create new information using existing resources. Though there have been remarkable recent advancements in this technology, systems that rely on generative AI sometimes make mistakes and produce inaccurate answers.

Generative AI models are essentially pattern-matching algorithms that can generate plausible-sounding text based on the patterns in their training data. However, they do not have a true, human-like understanding of the world, which can lead to factually incorrect or inappropriate answers. For example, generative answering may misinterpret words in a question or make incorrect predictions when putting together words that constitute an answer, resulting in incorrect answers.

If you have questions or concerns about an answer your receive from generative answering, or you want additional assistance with your issue, contact F5 Support.
