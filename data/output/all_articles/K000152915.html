K000152915: F5OS host systems and BIG-IP tenants on the same hardware are unable to communicate on management IPs

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-08-14
Updated
2025-08-28
Applies to
  • F5OS
  • F5OS-A
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000152915

Description

Tenants and host systems operating on the same F5 rSeries hardware (F5OS-A platform) cannot communicate with each other using management IP addresses. Attempts to establish connectivity, such as ping or telnet between host and tenant management IPs, result in errors like "Destination Host Unreachable" and "No route to host." Communication between tenants over out-of-band management networks within the same appliance is also not possible.

Environment

  • rSeries / F5OS-A with multiple BIG-IP tenants
  • Management network

Cause

This behavior is intentional and enforced by the platform’s design. The F5OS architecture isolates host and tenant management networks for security purposes, preventing direct communication between them or among tenants within the same appliance via the management network. Only external access from sources such as jump-hosts is permitted.

Recommended Actions

  • Direct communication between host and tenant, or tenant-to-tenant, on the management network within a single appliance is not supported.
  • For high availability (HA) / device service clustering (DSC), configure HA relationships between tenants located on separate appliances or chassis that are identically configured. This approach provides redundancy and failover at the tenant level.
  • Access to both host and tenant management interfaces should be performed externally through the out-of-band management network.
  • Consult the following official F5 documentation for further guidance on rSeries networking architecture and high availability deployment strategies:
    • The rSeries Networking chapter of the F5 rSeries Planning Guide.
    • The Tenant Level HA within the Same Appliance section under the rSeries High Availability chapter of the F5 rSeries Planning Guide.
