K2148: Reasons why 3-DNS may display a BIG-IP as unknown (blue) status, but display the big3d process for the BIG-IP as available (green)

Topic

This occurs when one or more of the 3-DNS systems in the sync group are unable to communicate with the associated BIG-IP. To troubleshoot this issue, perform the following steps:

  1. Check the iQuery communication between all the 3-DNS systems in the sync group and the BIG-IP that is displaying as blue (status unknown) by running a tcpdump on the 3-DNS and verifying that packets are sent and received on the port that iQuery is configured to run.
  2. Check the /var/log/3dns logs, on both the 3-DNS and the BIG-IP, for any big3d errors.
  3. Issue a 3ndc restart command on each of the 3-DNS systems.

If problems persist after running the 3ndc restart command, contact F5 Technical Support and provide the tcpdump output for any big3d error messages found in the /var/log/3dns log file.
