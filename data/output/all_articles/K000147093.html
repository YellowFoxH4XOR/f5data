What Happened?

You want to know why our F5® Distributed Cloud (XC) Platform flags a security event as a Bot.

Environment

  • F5® HTTP Load Balancer

Resolution/Answer

In order to determine whether an event is originated by bot we take into account headers content (including user-agent value), path/query, and a few additional heuristics.
