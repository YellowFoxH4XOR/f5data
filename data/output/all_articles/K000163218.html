K000163218: Frequent BGP session flaps

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-09-06
Updated
2026-09-06
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.5.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000163218

Description

BIG-IP LTM may experience BGP neighbor flaps when TCP monitor is attached to the pool consisting of BGP peers

Observed symptoms include:

  • BGP messages in /var/log/zebos.log such as:
    • BGP-5-ADJCHANGE: neighbor ... Down Peer closed the session
    • BGP-5-ADJCHANGE: neighbor ... Up
  • The BGP peer dropping both the BFD and associated BGP sessions.

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM
  • BGP configured
  • A TCP monitor configured for the BGP peer.

Cause

The behavior is taking place due to BGP Connection Collision Detection mechanism, which prevents having two parallel BGP session to be formed.

Recommended Actions

  1. Remove the TCP monitor from the BGP peer.
  2. Configure a gateway-icmp monitor for the peer instead of TCP.
