Description
Hardware sensors like Fans, Power supply, Temperature, and so on are not discovered by external SNMP monitoring tools if you use a BIG-IP VE

Environment

  • BIG-IP VE and Cloud platforms
  • External SNMP monitoring tools

Cause
BIG-IP VEs are running from different types of hypervisors, so for virtualization reasons, virtual platforms won't have physical hardware sensors available.

Recommended Actions

  1. There's nothing to be worried about, as those type of sensors won't be available for VE platforms.
  2. Please check your external SNMP vendor for further information to acknowledge possible alerts discovering VE platforms.

Additional Information
None.
