Description

This document is a reference to interpreting IKEv2 log messages. When IKEv2 negotiation fails, the log messages are in general the only helpful place to debug, since the later states of the ISAKMP exchange are encrypted making a packet capture unhelpful.

Currently this document describes one log message:

  • AUTHENTICATION_FAILED
 

Environment

An IPsec site-to-site topology with a BIG-IP as either Responder, Initiator or both.

  • IKEv2 is in use.
  • debug2 is enabled, the default is "info" level:
tmsh modify net ipsec ike-daemon ikedaemon log-level debug2

To revert to the default debug level:
 
tmsh modify net ipsec ike-daemon ikedaemon log-level info
 

Messages

The log messages discussed are all found in the /var/log/ipsec.log file. This file is used for IKEv2 logging.

Note: The /var/log/racoon.log file contains logging related to IKEv1 only.
 

AUTHENTICATION_FAILED

As Responder, the BIG-IP will log a message similar to the following, when it detects that the peer is using a pre-shared key that is different to the locally configured one.

[IKE] (payloads dir=PRE-ENCRYPT at=ikev2_encrypt_packet_construct payl=0x560129402c68 len=36 crc=0xc42ef31e
[IKE] @ . (ikev2_encrypt_packet_construct v=0x560129402c68 l=36 crc=0xc42ef31e
[IKE] @ . . . "oqzAFPnNa6uQaI5K3raiAikgIyAAAAABAAAAJAAAAAgAAAAY")
[IKE] @ . (v2_head i_spi=0xa2acc014f9cd6bab r_spi=0x90688e4adeb6a202 next=41:PAYLOAD_NOTIFY
[IKE] @ . . . ver=0x20 exch=35:IKEV2EXCH_IKE_AUTH flags=0x20 msg_id=0x1 len=36)
[IKE] @ . (hd type=41:PAYLOAD_NOTIFY next=0:NO_NEXT_PAYLOAD byte=0 len=8 off=0x1c)
[IKE] @ . (notify proto=0:NOTIFY_PROTO_NONE spi_size=0 type=24:AUTHENTICATION_FAILED))
[IKE] @</rc_vchar_t payload=0x560129402c68>

As Initiator, the BIG-IP would log that notification of a bad pre-shared key similar to the following:

[IKE] (payloads dir=POST-DECRYPT at=ikev2_input_post_process payl=0x56000d8c1aa8 len=72 crc=0x93013d23
[IKE] @ . (ikev2_input_post_process v=0x56000d8c1aa8 l=72 crc=0x93013d23
[IKE] @ . . . "oqzAFPnNa6uQaI5K3raiAi4gIyAAAAABAAAASCkAAAzvPXFoSdgVpwAAAAgAAAAYBwYFBAMCAQeB"
[IKE] @ . . . "H8Q2CR+Q1kr1TbCG+YTI")
[IKE] @ . (v2_head i_spi=0xa2acc014f9cd6bab r_spi=0x90688e4adeb6a202 next=46:PAYLOAD_ENCRYPTED
[IKE] @ . . . ver=0x20 exch=35:IKEV2EXCH_IKE_AUTH flags=0x20 msg_id=0x1 len=72)
[IKE] @ . (hd type=46:PAYLOAD_ENCRYPTED next=41:PAYLOAD_NOTIFY byte=0 len=12 off=0x1c)
[IKE] @ . (encrypted p=0x56000d8c1ac8 len=8 crc=0x85db6dbe
[IKE] @ . . . 00000: ef 3d 71 68 49 d8 15 a7                         '.=qhI...')
[IKE] @ . (hd type=41:PAYLOAD_NOTIFY next=0:NO_NEXT_PAYLOAD byte=0 len=8 off=0x28)
[IKE] @ . (notify proto=0:NOTIFY_PROTO_NONE spi_size=0 type=24:AUTHENTICATION_FAILED)
 

Recommended Actions

During the IKE_SA_INIT phase, this message can be logged when the Initiator specifies a Peer ID that the Responder cannot locate in the local ike-peer configuration. But this check on Peer ID only happens when verify-id is enabled in config. Check the my ID and verify ID settings in the ike-peer configuration.

During the IKE_AUTH phase, this message can be logged by the BIG-IP in any one of the following cases:

  • The pre-shared key does not match. Check the pre-shared key configuration.
  • The auth signature check failed during a certificate based authentication. Check the certificates in use.
