Known Issue

This is most likely the result of a known issue. If you use the snmp_dca, wmi, or realserver performance monitors, BIG-IP will recommend that you synchronize every time it updates the dynamic ratio.

F5 Product Development tracked this problem as CR48785 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.1.1 and 9.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
