Frequently Asked Questions

  1. Why can’t I access my tenant anymore? What happened? 

    1. Your tenant was part of our legacy Free, Individual, or Team plans, which have officially reached their End of Life. As part of this transition, these plans were removed from the platform on September 28, 2025. Our company is evolving to better serve enterprise customers, and this shift means we are no longer supporting our previous billing plans. 

  2. Can My Deleted Tenant Be Recovered?

    1. Unfortunately, once a tenant is deleted, it cannot be restored. All configuration data associated with the tenant is permanently removed from the platform. 

  3. Billing and Refunds

    1. Please note that the legacy plans were governed by terms that allow us to discontinue services at our discretion. Once a tenant is disabled, all associated billing is halted, and no further charges will be made to your account.
    2. If you believe you were charged in error or have concerns about your billing, we encourage you to reach out to our support team or your account manager for assistance.

  4. What can I do now?

    1. Your best path forward is to work with our Sales team to explore our current offerings. We recommend starting with our Organization Plan, which is designed for long-term, enterprise-grade use.

    2. While this is a significant upgrade from previous monthly plans, it reflects our commitment to delivering robust and scalable solutions.

    3. Our sales team can also guide you through other available offerings that may better suit your needs.

  5. Can I create a new tenant?

    1. Yes, you can absolutely start fresh. Please contact our Sales team to initiate the creation of a new tenant.
    2. We recommend using a new tenant ID.

  6. How do I reach out to the Sales team?

    1. We’ve created a dedicated Business Development Representative (BDR) form for this use case, but you’re also welcome to reach out through any of our standard sales channels.
