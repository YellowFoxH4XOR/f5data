K000152865: BIG-IQ may enter a loop of Elasticsearch and guiserver restarts after upgrade

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-09-22
Updated
2025-09-22
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
  • F5 Distributed Cloud Services
  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management: 8.0.0, 8.1.0, 8.0.X, 8.X.X, 8.4.X, 8.4.0, 8.3.X, 8.3.0, 8.2.X, 8.2.0, 8.1.X
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000152865

Description

After updating BIG-IQ, the device may enter a loop of Elasticsearch and guiserver restarts, which may cause device to enter inoperative mode.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ
  • Software upgrade

Cause

Undetermined at the time of publication.

Recommended Actions

Reboot of the device is able to solve this problem and after that device is operative. 

Additional Information

None.
