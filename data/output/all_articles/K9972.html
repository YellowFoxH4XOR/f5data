Topic

The BIG-IP 6900 and 8900 platforms ship with dual redundant hard drives. The second drive, sdb, is used to provide RAID 1 mirroring between the disks starting in version 10.1.0. Use of the second hard drive other purposes (for example, to install BIG-IP software images or store data) is not supported and may result in a loss of functionality or data.
