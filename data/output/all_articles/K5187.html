K5187: The Configuration utility may not display correctly in any browser on Mac OS

Type
Knowledge
Published
2006-02-17
Updated
2023-02-21
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • Legacy Products
  • 3-DNS: 4.6.4
  • BIG-IP 4.X: 4.6.4
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K5187

Topic

No current Mac OS browsers are supported for the BIG-IP and 3-DNS Configuration utilities.

You may be able to successfully use unsupported browsers; however, F5 Networks recommends that you do not use unsupported browsers.

Among other problems, using an unsupported browser may cause colors to appear incorrectly, navigation may not work properly, data entry may not work or may corrupt data, and the browser may crash.

To learn which browsers are supported, refer to the BIG-IP or 3-DNS Release Notes for the version you are using.
