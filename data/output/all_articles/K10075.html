Topic

The FirePass Emergency Licensing for Disaster Recovery option provides a limited-time license that increases the number of allowed concurrent sessions on a FirePass controller. A company may need this type of license if it has a temporary unplanned increase in users requiring remote access. For example, if one of the company’s branches shuts down due to flooding.

Note: To view the number of concurrent sessions that your FirePass license supports, navigate to the Device Management: Maintenance: Activate License page, and click the Click here to view license details link. Next to Allowed Concurrent Connections is the number of connections (sessions) that your FirePass license supports.

The FirePass emergency license helps ensure business continuity in a disaster recovery situation. This license is installed as an add-on license on the Device Management: Maintenance: Activate License page, and it is valid for 120 days.

If you are interested in purchasing a FirePass emergency license, contact your local F5 Networks sales representative.
