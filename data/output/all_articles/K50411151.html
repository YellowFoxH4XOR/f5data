K50411151: Standby unit may log resets when connections are mirrored

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-04-29
Updated
2025-02-06
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K50411151

Description
When connections are mirrored to the standby unit in an HA pair, the standby unit may log resets.  These resets are not actually placed on the wire, however, and the logging is purely cosmetic.

Environment

  • BIG-IP HA pair
  • Mirrored connections
  • Reset Cause Logging enabled

Cause
 

Recommended Actions

No action is required.  This is purely cosmetic.
Note: This may be exacerbated by enabling mirroring on a Virtual Server with a caching profile.  Mirroring is not supported on a Virtual Server with Caching enabled.
