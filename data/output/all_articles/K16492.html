Known Issue

A fail-safe event may not clear the next-active state on a BIG-IP device.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You configure high availability (HA) with two or more devices in a device trust.
  • You configure any of the following HA fail-safe modes on one or more devices in the device group:
     
    • System
    • Gateway
    • VLANs
  • An HA fail-safe event is triggered on a standby device with a designated next-active state for a traffic group.

Impact

The standby BIG-IP system may fail to take over services in case of failure on the active device, even though the device is designated as the next-active device for that traffic group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The standby BIG-IP system is unable to take over a traffic group in case of failure on the active device.
  • You may view the following tmsh show /cm traffic-group command output showing that the next-active state is not cleared on the standby device after a fail-safe condition is triggered.

    For example:

    ---------------------------------------------------------------
    CM::Traffic-Group
    Name Device Status Next
    Active
    ---------------------------------------------------------------
    traffic-group-1 bigip1-ha.com active false
    traffic-group-1 bigip2-ha.com standby true

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can force the standby device offline so that another device in the device group becomes the next-active device for the traffic group. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: The BIG-IP system will terminate existing connections to local traffic objects, and will not allow new connections.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP Configuration utility.
  2. Navigate to Device Management > Devices.
  3. Click the local device (Self).
  4. Click Force Offline.

Note: To release the unit from its Forced Offline state, click Release Offline.
