Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP LTM Configuration utility displays an option for enabling persistence mirroring with all modes of cookie persistence, even though persistence mirroring is not a valid option for the insert, rewrite, or passive cookie persistence methods.

Enabling the persistence mirroring option with an unsupported cookie persistence method results in the following error message:

01070632:3: Mirroring cannot be enabled on a cookie persistence profile (GUI_insert_cookie) unless the cookie mode is hash.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR54086, CR54223, and CR54411, and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.1.2. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
