Description

After an upgrade from 12.1.x to 13.1.x or later, you observe that the rules in an LTM policy with an all-match strategy execute in a different order.

This may cause issues in certain cases, for example when there are multiple rules that match the request and forward traffic to different pools, and you rely on one of those rules to execute last, so that traffic is sent to the correct pool. If the rule execution order changes, traffic would be sent to a different pool.

Environment

  • LTM policy with all-match strategy
  • Pool selection

Cause

The policy compiler behaviour has been rewritten in version 13.1, so behaviour may differ. The all-match strategy does not fall back to the best-matching rule when there are multiple rules that have conflicting actions. Whatever rule is executed last will take precedence, and the execution order of the rules may change as the policy conditions are modified.

Recommended Actions

Avoid using multiple rules in a single policy that might have conflicting actions with an all-match strategy.
