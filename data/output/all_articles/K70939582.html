Description

You add an entry to your IP Intelligence feedlist, but find that the BIG-IP is not taking action to allow or block the traffic specified

Environment

  • BIG-IP AFM
  • IP Intelligence

Cause

dwbld failed to process the new entry due to a formatting error

Recommended Actions

Review dwbld.log to determine if entry was skipped due to an error, and if so, what element in the entry needs to be corrected
