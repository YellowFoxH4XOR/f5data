Description

On systems with very large configuration you may notice merged process utilizing 100% of a CPU core constantly.

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • Large configuration, ex. tens of thousands of objects such as pools or virtual servers
Cause

Merged process is responsible for collecting statistics. In case of very large configurations collecting statistics from all objects can take longer than statistics interval, leading to one CPU core being constantly utilised by the process.

Recommended Actions

In order to mitigate the problem you can either:
  • Reduce configuration size
  • Increase statistics collection interval:

tmsh modify /sys db merged.merge.interval {value "<1-10>"}

For example to change to interval to 10 seconds:

tmsh modify /sys db merged.merge.interval {value "10"}
