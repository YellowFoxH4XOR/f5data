Known Issue

Description

BIG-IP APM portal access may not render frame or iframe content correctly.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP APM system is configured for portal access.
  • Content provided by the destination resource through portal access contains HTML frames or iframes.
  • An HTML frame or iframe uses a parent.document.write (<html with script>) statement.

Impact

Content does not render correctly.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When attempting to access the destination resource content the user's browser automatically refreshes (appears to flicker) several times and then stops responding.
