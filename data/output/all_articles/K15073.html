BIG-IQ software support policy

The BIG-IQ Standard Support phase is characterized by the introduction of new features, as well as security updates and stability improvements.

For each Major or Minor Release (X.Y.0), the Standard Support period begins with the first customer ship date of that version and continues until the applicable End of Standard Support (EoSD) milestone. Customers who require support beyond the Standard Support period should contact their F5 account manager. During the Standard Support period, F5 may release additional Maintenance and/or Point Releases for eligible Major and Minor software versions. For details regarding currently supported BIG-IQ software releases, refer to the End of Software Development column in the table below.

At the end of a release's Standard Support phase, the release enters its End of Software Development (EoSD) and End of Technical Support (EoTS) milestone. Software versions that have reached their EoSD date are no longer supported with active software development. F5 will not provide software fixes (hotfixes) or consulting services for a version that has reached the EoSD milestone.

BIG-IP ASM attack signature files are updated until the release reaches its EoSD phase.

For software releases, unless specifically declared and announced by F5, EoSD and EoTS milestones are the same.

Note: For more information about Major, Minor, Maintenance and Point release numbering, refer to K8986: F5 product support policies.
