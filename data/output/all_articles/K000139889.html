K000139889: How to update MyF5 account details for non-corporate Email ID?

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-06-04
Updated
2026-07-09
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 16.0.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000139889

Description

You will experience Download Failure in MyF5 if your MyF5 account is still in "review" status. 

 

Environment

  • MyF5
  • Download Failure

 

Cause

Inaccurate or incomplete information provided during the MyF5 account registration process.

 

Recommended Actions

  1.  Login to MyF5, select Resources > Downloads

Figure: Download Menu

  1.  Click on “Fill out the form”.

 

Figure: Download Failure Message

 

  1. A similar form will appear. Pay careful attention to the highlighted portion. Fill in each field with information that meets it's criteria. 

Figure: Download Failure Form

 

  1. Below message indicates that you have successfully submitted the form. The "Fill out the form" will be greyed out for the next 24 - 48hrs while your account is being reviewed.  

Figure: Form submitted

 

  1. During this period, if the "Fill out the form" options is enabled again, it means your earlier submission did not meet the data criteria. Hence, you are required to re-submit with accurate information. Maximum of three attempts are allowed. 

 

 

Additional Information

None.
