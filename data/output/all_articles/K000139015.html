K000139015: EMS Pool's peer list not synchronized to the SDC Pool's peer list

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-12-17
Updated
2024-12-17
Applies to
  • Traffix SDC: 5.1.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000139015

Description

  • The entries in the Pool's peer list in the EMS GUI is not the same as the one showing in the SDC GUI.
  • Dynamic peers shows up in the SDC GUI Pool peer list while its missing in the EMS GUI.

Environment

  • Diameter peer
  • OAM-DB
  • FEP

Cause

This is likely caused by a disconnect or unsynchronization happening between the DRA oamDB and EMS oamDB which results to these inconsistencies.


The pool state or status are updated constantly via the SDC cpf/fep <--> SDC nmsagent <--> SDC oamDB --> EMS oamDB. The webui represents the status that is showing from the SDC and EMS oamDB. Hence what you see in the SDC webui is coming from the SDC oamDB same with what you see in the EMS webui (it also comes from its own EMS oamDB).


Recommended Actions

Restart CM, OAM-DB and WEBUI. Another workaround is to push a dummy config change from the pool peer list in the SDC and save/submit.
