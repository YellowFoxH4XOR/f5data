K000157072: Map Subscription greyed out

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-10-20
Updated
2025-10-27
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000157072

Description

On the MyF5 portal, the Map Subscription option is greyed out on the new subscription and the existing license keys can't be mapped to the modules in the new subscription. 

Environment

  • BIG-IP VE

  • New VE term subscription
  • Original VE term subscription

Cause

The MyF5 email account requires Admin access to the new subscription.

Recommended Actions

The customer can request their local BIG-IP administrator add them to this account as admin.

  • Add, change, or remove MyF5 account users

The customer can open an F5 support ticket to request this access.

  • Contact F5 Support
  • Provide the subscription number, a screen shot of the issue, and customer email

Additional Information

None
