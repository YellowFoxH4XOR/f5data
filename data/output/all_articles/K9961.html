Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When the get_rrs() or get_rrs_detailed() iControl method is called with more than one view or zone pair passed to it, the BIG-IP system fails to properly parse and enumerate the response from the zrd process.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as ID 222388 (formerly CR118955) and it was fixed in BIG-IP 11.0.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM release notes.
