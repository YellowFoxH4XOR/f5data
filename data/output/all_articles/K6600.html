Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The pvad process receives and processes all messages from the mcp daemon, including tamd and tamd authentication messages. As a result, the pvad process may consume higher than normal CPU cycles when processing a high number of authentication requests per second.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR67985 and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 9.2.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the LTM, GTM, ASM, or Link Controller release notes.
