Description
At times you may wish to generate a report on the cases (Service Request) you have opened with F5 Support.  This information can offer insight as a historical record of your team's work with F5 Support.

Environment

  • Reporting/Analytics
  • Case research
  • Account information

Cause
At times you may find the need to run a report on what cases you generated with F5 Support and gain further context about the nature of the cases that were worked as well as who worked on them.

Recommended Actions
Running such a report can be accomplished within the F5 Support Portal.

  1. Open the F5 Support Portal

  2. Select Filter list
    You can use these filters based on your needs.  For example if you wanted to see all cases, you would select:
    Status: Show all
    Severity: Show all
    Date Range: Show all

  3. Apply your desired filters

  4. Uncheck Show only my service requests if you wish to see the cases that other people in your team have worked on.

  5. Scroll to the bottom of the results and select View all service requests

  6. Export page to Excel will then pop up enabling you to pull this information from the Support Portal.

Additional Information

If you find after logging in that you do not seem to have this functionality yet, you can request access by contacting your F5 Account Team.
