Known Issue

Access to access session resources may unexpectedly fail.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are using the BIG-IP Edge Client to establish an access session.
  • Your device has multiple (two or more) physical interfaces that connect to the same local network.
  • The network interface in use for accessing the local network is disconnected.

Impact

Destination resources of the access session become unreachable.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Access to access session destination resources is restored after several minutes.
  • Disconnecting and reestablishing an access session restores access to destination resources.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can disconnect and reestablish the access session. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. On your device desktop, click the BIG-IP Edge Client icon.
  2. Click Disconnect.
  3. After the Edge Client has terminated the session, click Connect.
  4. Provide any required credentials.
