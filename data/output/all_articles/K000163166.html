K000163166: How long does it take to upgrade one BIG-IQ device and 3 DCDs

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-09-02
Updated
2026-09-02
Applies to

  • BIG-IP LTM
Source

Description

An upgrade of a BIG-IQ deployment containing two Central Managers (CMs) and three Data Collection Devices (DCDs) may require approximately 3 hours 45 minutes to 5 hours for the device upgrade phase.

F5 estimates approximately 45–60 minutes per device, depending on the system configuration and the number of managed devices. With five devices total, the estimated duration is:

  • 45 minutes × 5 devices = 3 hours 45 minutes
  • 60 minutes × 5 devices = 5 hours

This is an estimate and does not include preparation, validation, troubleshooting, or post-upgrade re-indexing.

 

Environment

  • BIG-IQ Centralized Management
  • Two BIG-IQ Central Managers configured as an HA pair
  • Three BIG-IQ Data Collection Devices
  • BIG-IQ upgrade involving the CM pair and DCD cluster
  • Applicable upgrade procedures and supported source/target versions must be confirmed before scheduling the upgrade


Cause

Upgrade duration varies based on:

  • BIG-IQ software versions and the selected upgrade method
  • BIG-IQ configuration size
  • Number of managed devices
  • DCD cluster size and health
  • Post-upgrade database and Elasticsearch re-indexing requirements

F5 documents an estimated duration of 45–60 minutes per device. F5’s example for one CM and three DCDs—four devices total—indicates a maximum upgrade time of approximately four hours.

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

  1. Plan for approximately 3 hours 45 minutes to 5 hours for the upgrade of two CMs and three DCDs.
  2. Schedule a maintenance window of at least 5–6 hours to allow time for:
    • Pre-upgrade health checks
    • Software image transfers
    • Device upgrades
    • Post-upgrade validation
    • Unexpected delays or recovery activities
  1. Confirm the supported upgrade path and whether a rolling upgrade is available for the specific source and target versions.
  2. If a regular, non-rolling upgrade is required, plan for statistics collection to be suspended until the upgrade is complete.
  3. If a supported rolling upgrade is available, statistics collection may continue while the DCDs are upgraded incrementally, although brief BIG-IQ downtime may still occur.
  4. Monitor the system after the upgrade. BIG-IQ may become accessible before post-upgrade re-indexing has completed.
  5. If a BIG-IQ device remains unavailable for more than approximately 60 minutes or the upgrade fails, collect a QKView and contact F5 Support.


 

Additional Information


Post-upgrade re-indexing runs in the background. Its duration depends on the size of the BIG-IQ configuration and historical statistics data. During re-indexing, objects and historical statistics may not immediately return expected results; normal results should return after re-indexing completes.
