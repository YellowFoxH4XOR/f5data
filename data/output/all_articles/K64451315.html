Known Issue

On initial connection, with a low maximum segment size (MSS), OneConnect and Proxy MSS experience suboptimal performance.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You configure a virtual server with both OneConnect and Proxy MSS enabled.

    Note: Beginning in BIG-IP 12.1.0, proxy MSS is enabled by default.

  • The first connection to the virtual server establishes a low MSS.

Impact

The system will experience decreased throughput and possible congestion due to small segments.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When a client with low MSS is the first to establish a OneConnect flow pair, and proxy MSS is enabled, the server-side shares the same low MSS. Successive connections from full-MSS clients may use this server-side flow, resulting in suboptimal throughput.

Workaround

In some instances, if you disable proxy MSS, that may be sufficient; however, this, too, has potential to increase segment count and decrease throughput.
