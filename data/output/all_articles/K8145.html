Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. HTTP virtual servers using late-binding features, such as cookie persistence, will not pass more than 2 GB in response to a request. As a result, files or data larger than 2 GB fail to download completely; the file transfer will fail at 2 GB.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR88610n.

Workaround

You can work around this issue by upgrading to the BIG-IP LTM, or using simple persistence in place of cookie persistence.
