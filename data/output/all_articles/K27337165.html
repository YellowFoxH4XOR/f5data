Description

Boot up a standalone device without HA configuration, you will notice the HA status transition from Offline to Active direclty as below:

notice sod[<PID>]: 01140030:5: HA proc_running tmm is now responding.
notice sod[<PID>]: 01140044:5: HA reports tmm ready.
notice sod[<PID>]: 010c006d:5: Leaving Offline for Active for dbvar not redundant.
notice sod[<PID>]: 010c0053:5: Active for traffic group traffic-group-1.
notice sod[<PID>]: 010c0019:5: Active

If boot up device with HA cofiguration, you will see the HA status transition from Offline to Standby firstly and then go to Active if no Active device detected. 

notice sod[<PID>]: 01140044:5: HA reports tmm ready.
notice sod[<PID>]: 010c0044:5: Command:  running enable bd bigstart.
notice sod[<PID>]: 01140029:5: HA proc_running bd fails action is go offline and down links.
notice sod[<PID>]: 01140029:5: HA proc_running bd fails action is go offline and down links.
notice sod[<PID>]: 01140030:5: HA proc_running bd is now responding.
notice sod[<PID>]: 010c006d:5: Leaving Offline for Standby for dbvar is redundant.
notice sod[<PID>]: 010c0052:5: Standby for traffic group /Common/traffic-group-1.
notice sod[<PID>]: 010c0018:5: Standby
notice sod[<PID>]: 010c0049:5: Tmm ready - links up.
notice sod[<PID>]: 010c006d:5: Leaving Standby for Active (best load): NextActive:.
notice sod[<PID>]: 010c0053:5: Active for traffic group /Common/traffic-group-1.
notice sod[<PID>]: 010c0019:5: Active

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • HA

Cause

It is expected behavior, when boot up device with HA configuration, it will change to stanby status firstly, and change to active status when it detects no active device existing.
When boot up stand alone device without HA configuration, it will change to active directly.

Recommended Actions

It is normal to see the status change as above.

Additional Information

None.
