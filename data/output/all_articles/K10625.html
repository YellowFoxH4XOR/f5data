Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP WebAccelerator pvac process may core when processing a large normalized document during a configuration load.

This issue may occur when the BIG-IP WebAccelerator system processes a large normalized document simultaneously as the access logs are rotated as part of the hourly cron job. When the access logs are rotated, a HUP signal is sent to the pvac process, which causes the process to crash.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR128517 and it was fixed in BIG-IP WebAccelerator 10.1.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP WebAccelerator release notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in BIGIP-10.0.1-378.0-HF3 issued for BIG-IP 10.0.1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the cumulative hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
