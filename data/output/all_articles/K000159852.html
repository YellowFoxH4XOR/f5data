Description

When restarting the BIG-IP ASM service on a Standby device in a high availability (HA) pair, both devices may simultaneously enter standby state, causing a brief traffic outage. The Active device unexpectedly transitions to Standby and leaves the Active role, while the Standby device is still completing its ASM service restart and not ready to handle the Traffic yet. Below could be the symptoms:

  • The previous Active Device changed to Standby, and the other Standby device changed state to Offline (during service restart) and finally to Active role
  • Brief traffic disruption during ASM service restart
  • Log message: 'Leaving active in favor of active peer' on previous Active device
  • Log message: 'HA proc_running bd fails action is go offline and down links' on the Standby unit after the 'asm' service restart
  • Log message: 'Not receiving status updates from peer device (Disconnected)' on the Standby unit
  • Log message: 'No failover status messages received for X.XXX seconds' on the Standby unit
  • Log message: 'Leaving Standby for Active: Next Active, peers agree on config' on the Standby unit

Environment

  • BIG-IP ASM version 11.0.0 and later
  • High Availability (HA) configuration with two devices
  • HA daemon monitoring enabled for bd (BIG-IP ASM daemon) process
  • Asymmetric unicast failover configuration where one device lacks the Management IP Address in its unicast address list
  • ASM service restart performed using: 'tmsh restart sys service asm' on Standby unit
  • HA-Group configured on both HA devices as the preferred Traffic-Group's Failover Method with same Score in normal situations

Cause

This behavior is expected by design since BIG-IP ASM 11.0.0. When the ASM service restarts, the bd process temporarily stops responding, causing the HA system to detect this as a failure condition. The HA daemon executes the configured failover action: "go offline and down links," transitioning the device to Offline state, at first step.

The race condition where one Instance changed to Standby and the other device enters 'Offline' --> 'Active' state results from:

  • The bd process being monitored by the HA daemon, and its restart triggering failover actions
  • HA communication gaps during the ASM restart window when TMM interfaces temporarily go down
  • Missing Management IP Address in the unicast failover address list on one device, eliminating all HA heartbeat channels when TMM interfaces are unavailable
  • Failover decision logic where the recovering device sends an Active enforcement message before being fully operational, causing the Active peer to yield control prematurely
  • In case of having similar Failover Score for HA-Group, the device with higher Management IP Address will be the final preferred Active unit [K99462468]

Answer/Recommended Actions

Please consider the below best-practices to avoid such unexpected Failovers: 

  1. Configure the Management IP Address in the unicast failover address list on both HA devices to ensure HA heartbeat communication can continue via the Management interface when TMM interfaces are temporarily unavailable. Verify both devices have consistent unicast address configurations including both TMM interfaces and the Management IP address.
  2. Consider the higher value for "Active Bonus" under HA-Group settings of your preferred device to keep it as Active, in case of having similar Failover Score for both peer. [K16947]
  3. Always schedule ASM or any Service Restarts / Reboot during a planned Maintenance window
