Description
After you have identified and removed the faulty hard disk drive (HDD) from the platform, you can install the replacement drive that you received from F5.

Environment

  • RMA of Hard Drive (HDD) for a BIG-IP 11000 series hardware.
  • Hardware swap

Cause
You may find yourself needing to complete a hard drive swap for a number of reasons:

  • a HDD failure has occurred and you've received a replacement after an RMA
  • RAID status has degraded

Recommended Actions

  1. Erase the "missing" drive as described in K12380: RAID capable BIG-IP platforms report the RAID status as degraded in the Configuration utility after a hard drive replacement
    From the bash:  array --erase WD-WXD1E70HN464
  2. You may now insert the new drive.  Use the following guide for assistance Manual Chapter : Platform Maintenance
  3. Perform a "logical build" of the drive into the array as done in K12756: Repairing disk errors on RAID-capable BIG-IP platforms
    Note: Sometimes the BIG-IP might need a reboot if the new drive has not been recognized. The replication will take couple of hours.
