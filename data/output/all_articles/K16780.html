Known Issue

The High-Speed Bridge (HSB) may drop TCP Segmentation Offload (TSO) packets.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • The VLAN is configured with a small MTU value, usually 750 or lower.
  • The BIG-IP platform contains the HSB chip.

Impact

The system drops large TSO packets, and experiences traffic disruption.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The system may experience periods of traffic disruption, such as monitors marking pool members down and up.

    Note: For information about displaying the tmm/hsb_internal_fsc table, contact F5 Technical Support.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can disable TSO by setting the value of the tm.tcpsegmentationoffload BigDB key to disable, and restarting the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM). To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Disabling TSO may cause increased CPU usage as segmentation offload will be performed in software when TSO is disabled. F5 recommends testing any such changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment. Additionally, restarting TMM temporarily interrupts traffic processing and devices configured as a high-availability pair may fail over.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP command line.
  2. Disable TSO by typing the following command:

    tmsh modify sys db tm.tcpsegmentationoffload value disable

  3. Restart TMM by typing the following command:

    bigstart restart tmm
