Topic

This article applies only to BIG-IP and 3-DNS server appliances.

In rare cases, one of the network interface cards (NICs) installed in BIG-IP or 3-DNS may become loose. When this occurs, the system cannot detect the network card. The First-Time Boot/setup utility will report the following error:

no such interface

To fix this problem, perform the following steps:

Warning: Undertaking this procedure without approval from an F5 support engineer will void your warranty.

  1. Remove the top of the case.
  2. Loosen the screw on the NIC.
  3. Make sure the NIC is properly lined up in the slot and tighten the screw on the card.

If the NIC was not loose or re-seating the NIC did not solve the problem, contact F5 Networks Support for assistance.
