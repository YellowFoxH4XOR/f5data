Known Issue

Setting a low value for the VLAN failsafe timeout for your BIG-IP system may cause the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) to restart.

The VLAN failsafe timeout specifies the number of seconds that must elapse before the BIG-IP system triggers the failsafe action. The default value is 90 seconds and the minimum value is 10 seconds.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You configure a low timeout value for VLAN failsafe.
  • Your BIG-IP system generates VLAN failsafe traffic and receives multiple responses in quick succession.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restart, and devices in a high availability (HA) configuration may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restart.
  • Devices configured in an HA configuration may fail over.
  • Your BIG-IP system generates a TMM core file to the /shared/core directory, which includes a time stamp that correlates with the traffic interruption.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can configure your system's VLAN failsafe timeout value to use the default setting of 90 seconds. For more information, refer to K13297: Overview of VLAN failsafe (10.x - 13.x).

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
