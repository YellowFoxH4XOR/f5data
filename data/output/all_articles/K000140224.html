F5 is announcing the End of Life (EoL) for F5 Leaked Credential Check, an add-on for BIG-IP Advanced WAF, effective July 1st, 2025.

Product support

F5 Leaked Credential Check is no longer available as an add-on service to BIG-IP Advanced WAF and is no longer available on BIG-IP TMOS.

Recommended actions

F5 urges customers with F5 Leaked Credential Check who have active subscriptions past the EoL date of July 1st, 2025 to contact their F5 Account Team to discuss their options, which can include trade-in of active licenses.

Questions

For more information about this announcement, contact your local F5 representative.
