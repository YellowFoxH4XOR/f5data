Known Issue

Global traffic statistics recorded by the BIG-IP system may be incorrect.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have enabled Packet Velocity ASIC (PVA) hardware acceleration.
  • You attempt to view global traffic statistics using the Configuration utility or the Traffic Management Shell (tmsh).

Impact

This issue may impact operations requiring accurate statistics.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Statistics in the following Configuration utility menu are incorrect:

    Statistics > Module Statistics > Traffic Summary > General
