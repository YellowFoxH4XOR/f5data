Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) process may restart when you use the qkview utility on a busy BIG-IP system.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is heavily loaded (100k or more connections).
  • You use the qkview utility to gather system diagnostics.

A new command to list connection table entries was added to the qkview utility. This command may have a significant performance impact when running while the BIG-IP system is very busy.

Impact

The qkview utility may take an unexpectedly long time to run (30 minutes or longer). The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restarting, and systems configured as part of a high availability (HA) group may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP system temporarily fails to process traffic while TMM restarts.
  • In the /var/log/ltm file, you observe messages that appear similar to the following example:

    crit tmm8[12345]: 01010020:2: MCP Connection aborted, exiting

  • The qkview utility runs for an unexpectedly long time (30 minutes or longer).

Workaround

To mitigate this issue, F5 recommends that you run the qkview utility during off-peak hours or during a maintenance window.
