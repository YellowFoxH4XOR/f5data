Description

Idle connections are being reset after 300 seconds despite changing the idle-timeout of the fastl4 profile to a longer value. Reset cause for these swept connections will appear in the packet capture as:

F5RST: Flow expired (sweeper) (idle timeout)

 

Environment

  • Virtual Server, Performance Layer 4
  • SNAT or NAT translation object

 

Cause

There is a SNAT translation for this partition, and the value of its (ip-idle-timeout, tcp-idle-timeout, udp-idle-timeout) has a lower timeout.

 

Recommended Actions

A standalone SNAT or NAT translation has its own configurable settings, and the connection table will honor the lower of the values assigned to idle timeout for connection sweeping.

 

Additional Information

While it is possible to raise the timeout values of your snat-translation object (ip-idle-timeout, tcp-idle-timeout, udp-idle-timeout) as appropriate, this may have a greater scope than the traffic flows you wish to augment. It may be preferable to prevent idle timeout by setting a TCP Keep Alive Value within the FastL4 profile lower than the timeout.
