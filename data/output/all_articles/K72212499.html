Technical Support

F5 maintains generous lifecycle policies that allow customers to continue support and receive product updates. Existing BIG-IP ASM and BEST bundle customers can continue to consume BIG-IP ASM past the EoS date. License renewals will not be available after the April 1, 2021 EoS date, but BIG-IP ASM and BEST bundle customers can continue to renew their support service for BIG-IP ASM.

Public Cloud PAYG

F5 BIG-IP BEST offerings in public cloud with PAYG licenses will reach EoS on July 1, 2023. Private offers will not be available beyond this date. The EoS offerings are replaced with new BEST offerings that include Advanced WAF + Threat Campaigns + IP Intelligence. Existing customers on PAYG plans can continue to consume their BIG-IP BEST past the EoS Date. New deployments will be available with the new BIG-IP BEST + IPI + TC.
