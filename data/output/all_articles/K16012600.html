Known Issue

Some combinations of policy options may cause BIG-IP AAM connections to hang or the system to bypass the connections from caching.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Web acceleration is enabled on a virtual server.
  • The Cache On First Hit feature is enabled in an BIG-IP AAM policy.
  • The Assembly Compression OWS feature is enabled in the same BIG-IP AAM policy.

Impact

Clients may experience hanging or unoptimized connections.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The first connection to the web accelerator may hang.
  • The BIG-IP AAM system will pass subsequent connections with an S10413 code.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can disable the Cache On First Hit or Assembly Compression OWS features in your policy.

Impact of workaround: Optimizations relating to disabled options will not be performed.
