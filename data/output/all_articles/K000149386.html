Environment

  • F5OS.
  • k3s pods are not ready and restarting with CrashLoopBackOff reason.
  • System clock adjusted ahead to earlier time.

Cause

System time change makes k3s cluster token to be invalid.

 

Recommended Actions

Do not change the system clock to an earlier time and always configure stable NTP servers at initial configuration. For how to configure NTP at initial configuration you may follow:

F5 rSeries system initial configuration on F5 TechDocs. For how to configure NTP on F5OS, you may follow: Time settings overview under Manual Chapter : System Settings on F5 TechDocs.
In the case that the pods were already under CrashLoopBack status, you may delete the pods and let the system create it automatically.
Impact of procedure: it will make the relavent services unavailable untill the pods are automatically recreated, tenants might be unavailble and restarted during the process.
kubectl -n <namespace> delete pod <pod-name>
For example,
kubectl -n kubevirt delete pod virt-handler-fd2ej
