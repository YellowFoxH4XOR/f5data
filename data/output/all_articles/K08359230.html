Known Issue

BIG-IP ASM may experience an unexpected failover.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You configured BIG-IP ASM systems in a high availability (HA) group.
  • You set the internal parameters bd_hb_interval_low_platforms and bd_hb_interval to a value greater than 10 seconds.

Impact

The system ignores the internal parameters and failover occurs at 10 seconds.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When you configure the BIG-IP ASM systems for a failover time greater than 10 seconds, a failover condition will trigger at 10 seconds.
  • When you configure either of the internal parameters bypass_upon_asm_down or bypass_upon_load and perform a restart of the BIG-IP ASM service, the system triggers a failover due to the 10-second failover time.
