Environment

BIG-IP APM

Cause

This is caused by https://cdn.f5.com/product/bugtracker/ID1505789.html

Please see the article at K000138683 for further details on the issue

 

Recommended Actions

To resolve the issue the recommendation is to upgrade to 16.1.5 or 17.1.2 and in addition create the vpn.serveriplist db per the article

If you cannot upgrade to 16.1.5 or 17.1.2, then please create a new support case to request an engineering hotfix
