Known Issue

Attempts to process data for a closed connection flow may cause the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) process to restart and produce a core file.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • A packet is received for a connection flow that has already been closed.

This issue is rare, and is most likely to occur in extreme low-memory conditions. When a connection flow is closed (for example, when all pool members become unavailable), TMM may attempt to process traffic for the already closed connection flow.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restarting, and systems configured as part of high-availability pairs may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H487097 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The BIG-IP system fails over to another host in the device group.
  • The BIG-IP system temporarily fails to process traffic.
  • The BIG-IP system generates a TMM core file to the /shared/core directory.
