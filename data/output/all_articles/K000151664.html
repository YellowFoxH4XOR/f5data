Source

Description

Service Check Date not updated after license reactivation. Upgrade failed due to old Service Check date in License.

Environment

  • Software upgrade
  • License Reactivation
  • Service Check Date
  • Valid Support Entitlement

 

Cause

F5 License server replication issue

 

Recommended Actions

1) Confirm that your license/device has a valid Support Contract: K32755669: How to verify support contract end date

2) If the F5 license/device has a valid Support Contract, open a case with F5 Support: K2633: Submit a support case

3) F5 Support will work with our relevant department to fix the replication issue. Then a license reactivation is needed to update the Service Check Date issue.

 

Additional Information

K7727: License activation may be required before a software upgrade for BIG-IP
