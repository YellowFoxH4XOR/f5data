K000138982: Certificate Error on App Tunnel Resource Access

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-03-22
Updated
2024-03-22
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP APM: 17.1.1
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000138982

Description

When accessing an HTTPS link to an internal server through an App Tunnel, users may encounter a certificate error. This is not the case when that resource is accessed directly (without APM).


Environment

App Tunnel

Cause

The main causes for the certificate error in this scenario were identified as follows:


  • Discrepancy in the URL used to access the internal server directly versus through the App Tunnel, leading to a SAN (Subject Alternative Name) mismatch.
  • Self-signed certificates not being trusted by personal devices that do not have the certificate or its issuing CA added to their trusted store.

Recommended Actions

1. Ensure that the FQDN used to connect through the App Tunnel is included in the SAN field of the server's certificate.

2. Verify that the certificate's issuing CA is trusted by the client devices. For self-signed certificates, the certificate must be manually added to the trusted store of each personal device that needs to access the App Tunnel.

3. If self-signed certificates must be used, provide clear instructions to users on how to manually install and trust the certificate on their personal devices.
