Known Issue

In rare cases, VIPRION systems may corrupt data on large User Datagram Protocol (UDP) flows.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are running a VIPRION system.
  • You have a very high throughput of UDP data, with frames greater than 1500 bytes, passing through the VIPRION backplane.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may corrupt UDP data.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Data transferred through a BIG-IP virtual server is corrupted.
