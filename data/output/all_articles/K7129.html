Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When using OSPF in conjunction with a default SNAT on the BIG-IP LTM system, the OSPF traffic will be processed by the SNAT. The SNAT address is applied to the OSPF packets that are forwarded to the host, which can prevent the BIG-IP LTM system from forming adjacencies with OSPF peer systems.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR60048 and CR60096 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.

Workaround

You can work around this issue by removing the offending SNAT and replacing it with a more specific SNAT or SNAT Pool.
