Known Issue

The alarm status LED will not light to indicate there is an error condition.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are running a BIG-IP 6900, 8900, 8950, or 11050 platform.
  • One of the drives in the RAID array fails or is removed from the system.

Impact

You may not know that your drive has failed.

Resolution

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • The alarm status LED will not light as expected to indicate there is an error condition.
  • The RAID Management page of the configuration utility will indicate a RAID status of degraded.
