OpenSSH 5.6 and earlier, when J-PAKE is enabled, does not properly validate the public parameters in the J-PAKE protocol, which allows remote attackers to bypass the need for knowledge of the shared secret, and successfully authenticate. F5 products do not include J-PAKE in the OpenSSH programs and are not affected by this vulnerability. (CVE-2010-4478)
