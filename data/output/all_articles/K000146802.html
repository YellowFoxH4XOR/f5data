Description

  • How do I schedule a F5 DDoS simulation test?
  • Can I validate my F5 Distributed Cloud DDoS & Transit Services DDoS protection?

 

Environment

  • F5®Distributed Cloud DDoS & Transit Services

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

Term & Conditions

  • F5® Distributed Cloud Acceptable Use Policy: Load Testing and DDoS Simulation 
  • F5-initiated DDoS Simulation services are available for F5 Distributed Cloud DDoS & Transit Services customers.  Eligible targets must be within IP prefixes advertised to F5.  Endpoints terminated on the F5 platform such as XC Load Balancers are not eligible for F5-initiated DDoS simulation.
  • F5 Distributed Cloud DDoS & Transit Services customers are eligible for 2 DDoS tests per year of contracted service.
  • This attack simulation is free of charge for F5 Distributed Cloud DDoS & Transit Services customers.
  • This attack simulation tool is also available for potential customers; however, they're allowed only 1 simulation free of charge for the duration of the Proof of Concept (POC).
  • Attacks can only be launched on prefixes protected by F5 Distributed Cloud, for DDoS & Transit Services customers.  
  • F5 Distributed Cloud DDoS & Transit Services customers can only select 2 different attack vectors:
    • TCP SYN Flood
    • UDP Flood
    • UDP Fragmentation Flood
    • ICMP Flood
  • Customers can select up to 2 target IPs F5 Distributed Cloud DDoS & Transit Services
  • The attack bandwidth MUST NOT exceed 70% of the customer internet link's capacity. F5 Distributed Cloud can generate up to 3 Gbps of attack traffic.
  • The attack-test duration MUST not exceed 60 minutes
  • The minimum time to submit the request is 6 business days before the desired test date (leaving enough room for information gathering and approvals).

Requirements

  • Ensure traffic is routing through F5 Distributed Cloud DDoS & Transit Services correctly before scheduling the DDoS
  • No route leaks should exist on the customer prefix. If route leaks are observed the test cannot be performed.

Process

  1. Submit a support ticket to the SOC requesting a test. The request must be submitted at least 6 business days before the desired test date and include all of the following information.

  2. F5 Distributed Cloud will submit authorization to conduct an attack to the 3rd party vendor. It can take up to 5 business days for authorization to be processed.

  3. F5 SOC will schedule the test upon receiving authorization from the 3rd party vendor.

  4. If required, before the attack-test day, the customer should ask the SOC to set up a Zoom session during the DDoS test.

Additional Information

What happens on the attack-test day?

  1. On the attack-test day, if the target is a routed IP, F5 SOC will perform one last check to verify no route leaks exist
  2. F5 SOC will confirm with the customer before launching the attack.
  3. The attack starts and the SOC will mitigate the attack by implementing countermeasures to scrub the malicious traffic.
  4. An attack notification will be sent to the customer following the RTIP specified by the customer
  5. If, at any point during the test issues are observed, inform the SOC immediately so the attack can be ceased.
  6. The SOC reserves the right to stop the attack at any point during the test.

Will I receive a report after the attack test?

  1. Yes, 24 - 48 hours after the attack test is completed, F5 SOC will generate a report that will be shared with the customer.
  2. The report will include the following:
  3. Indicate how much bandwidth was generated by the attack tool
  4. Indicate how effective the DDOS protection
  5. A Snippet of Traffic observed during the attack test(s)
  6. Areas for improvement, recommendations
