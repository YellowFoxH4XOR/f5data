Environment

  • SFP interfaces hung
  • i5x00, i7x00 & i11x00 series
Cause

Devices with SFP interfaces (eg: i5x00, i7x00 & i11x00 series) can be driven into a hung state by repeated unplug and plug of the SFP interface cables.

Recommended Actions

Recovery is via a restart of the bcm56xxd daemon

  /bin/bigstart restart bcm56xxd

Important: The recommendation is to wait at least 10 seconds between each plug or unplug activity
