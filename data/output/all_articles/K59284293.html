Known Issue

An SNMP poll for sysObjectID returns unknown for the platform name.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are performing an SNMP poll on OID .1.3.6.1.4.1.3375.2.1.3.4.1000.
  • You are running on a VIPRION 4400 chassis.

Impact

VIPRION hosts may be incorrectly identified by SNMP management stations.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • An SNMP poll on OID .1.3.6.1.4.1.3375.2.1.3.4.1000 (sysObjectID) returns unknown.
