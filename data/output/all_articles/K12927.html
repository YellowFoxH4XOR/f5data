Known Issue

The PDF files generated by the BIG-IP ASM Charts tool do not include the hostname of the device where the files were generated.

This issue occurs when either of the following conditions are met:

  • Charts are manually generated for export from the Configuration utility
  • Charts are automatically generated by the Charts Scheduler

Impact

It may be difficult to determine which BIG-IP ASM device generated a given chart.

Note: In some versions of BIG-IP ASM the impact of this issue may be mitigated if the IP address of the reporting BIG-IP ASM unit in listed in the chart.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • Charts generated by the BIG-IP ASM do not contain a hostname.
