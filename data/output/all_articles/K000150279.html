Context and background

F5 Access for ChromeOS provides VPN support on Chromebooks, built on Google Chrome App technology. Google recently announced that they are going to deprecate Chrome Apps in July 2025. For more details, refer to End of support for Chrome apps.

Note: This link takes you to a resource outside of MyF5, and the third party could remove the document without our knowledge.

As a result, F5 can no longer support native F5 Access for ChromeOS and will deprecate the product on the same date that Google does.

Alternative path

As an alternative, F5 has certified F5 Access for Android 3.1.3 on Chromebooks. For more information, refer to the F5 Access Apps Compatibility Matrix.
