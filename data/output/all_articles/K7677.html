K7677: The VLAN group exclusion list does not update properly

Known Issue

This is a result of a known issue. If a VLAN group is configured with an exclusion list, the list is associated with any SNAT pools configured on the system. If the exclusion list is changed or removed from the VLAN group, the SNAT pools are not updated. As a result, the traffic that is using the members in the SNAT pool is filtered based on the exclusion list that was initially configured.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR72900 and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.1.3. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.
