Known Issue

BIG-IP APM Secure Web Gateway (SWG) policy execution may fail to complete.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system is configured with an SWG profile.
  • The SWG profile access policy incorporates an iRule event.

Impact

Sessions fail to establish.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Your browser stops responding, and the access session fails to establish.
