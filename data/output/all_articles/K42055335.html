K42055335: Dependency failed for Serial Getty on ttyS0

Type
Support Solution
Published
2021-07-23
Updated
2024-06-10
Applies to
  • Virtual Editions
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 15.1.2
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K42055335

Description

When booting the BIG-IP logs on /var/log/daemon.log  shows similar to:

Job dev-ttyS0\x20.device/start timed out.
Timed out waiting for device dev-ttyS0\x20.device.
Dependency failed for Serial Getty on ttyS0 .
Job serial-getty@ttyS0\x20.service/start failed with result 'dependency'.
Job dev-ttyS0\x20.device/start failed with result 'timeout'.

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM VE 15.1.2

Cause

The virtual machine expects a virtual serial port as /dev/ttyS0, since ttyS0 is not used for the VE it creates the above logs.

  • console=tty0 is needed for VEs.
  • console=ttyS0 is needed for physical devices, as their Serial Console needs to be connected to Serial Port 0.

Recommended Actions

None. This is a a cosmetic error.

Additional Information

For more information, refer to Serial Getty error messages on StackExchange.
