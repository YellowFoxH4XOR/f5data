K000163215: F5 Identity Platform: How to share Google Authenticator access

Published
2026-09-04
Updated

Description

This article explains how authorized team members can temporarily access a MyF5 account using a group email address, for example team@example.com, with Google Authenticator App.

Important: Using a group email address is NOT an F5 best practice. Each user should create and use an individual MyF5 account with an individual email address. The procedure in this article should be used only as a temporary workaround while individual accounts are being created.

Environment

  • MyF5 Portal

  • Auth0 MFA
  • Google Authenticator
  • Group email address

Cause

  • F5 recently changed its MyF5 portal access to Auth0 / MFA.
  • MyF5 requires multifactor authentication in addition to a username and password
  • When a group email address is used, the MFA enrollment must be securely made available to authorized team members.

Recommended Actions

  1. Complete the initial MyF5 MFA enrollment

When prompted, scan the QR code displayed in the browser using the Google Authenticator app.

Note: Do not share this initial enrollment QR code. It is intended for the initial enrollment of the account and must not be distributed to other users.

 

  1. Export the MyF5 account from Google Authenticator on the device that completed the initial enrollment.

For iPhone:

    1. Open Google Authenticator App.
    2. On the top left corner, click on the 3 lines icon and choose Transfer Accounts.
    3. Select Export Accounts, type in your phone pin number.
    4. Select (tick box) only the MyF5 Portal account. If multiple accounts are listed, do not select the others.
    5. Select Next.
    6. Google Authenticator displays an export QR code.
    7. Share the QR code only to authorized team members.
    8. Authorized team member can scan the export QR code with Google Authenticator to add the MyF5 account to their device.

 

For Android:

    1. Open the Google Authenticator App.
    2. On the top left corner, click on the 3 lines icon choose Transfer Codes.
    3. Select Export codes, authenticate your phone access.
    4. Select (tick box) only the My F5 portal specific code you want to share. If multiple accounts are listed, do not select the others.
    5. Select Next.
    6. Google Authenticator displays an export QR code.
    7. Share the QR code only to authorized team members.
    8. Authorized team member can scan the export QR code with Google Authenticator to add the MyF5 account to their device.
