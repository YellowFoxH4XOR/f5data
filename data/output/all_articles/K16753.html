Known Issue

Secondary VIPRION blades may display an incorrect cluster status.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • You have two or more blades installed in the VIPRION system.
  • The cluster status on the primary blade changes.

A race condition may occur when the cluster status is changed on the primary blade; this change may not be updated on the secondary blade, resulting in the secondary blade displaying the incorrect cluster status.

Impact

None. This issue is cosmetic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When viewed on a secondary blade, the Cluster status displays an incorrect status, such as 'offline'.

    For example:

    ------------------------------------------------------------
    Sys::Cluster: default
    ------------------------------------------------------------
    Address 172.2.1.1/24
    Availability offline
    State enabled
    Reason Too many cluster members HA offline.
    Primary Slot ID 1
    Primary Selection Time 03/20/14 05:00
