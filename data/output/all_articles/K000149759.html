K000149759: Traffic Groups Displaying as Active on Both Standby and Active Devices

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-02-12
Updated
2025-03-14
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 16.1.4
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000149759

Description

Traffic groups showing active in standby device and active device. Reviewing configuration appears to be correct. Forcing the traffic group offline in the standby device does not resolve the issue. 

Environment

-BIG-IP VE

-BIG-IP LTM in Device Service Cluster

Cause

Unknown at time of publishing

Recommended Actions

Note: It is recommended that these actions should be done in a maintenance window.

Restart the Standby BIG-IP VE

Additional Information

None
