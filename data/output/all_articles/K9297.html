Topic

Cumulative hotfixes HF-602-6 and HF-602-7 were issued to specific customers, and they were not available on the F5 Networks Downloads site.

After additional testing, F5 Networks Product Development created cumulative hotfix HF-602-7.2 to replace cumulative hotfixes HF-602-6 and HF-602-7. You may download cumulative hotfix HF-602-7.2 or later versions of the cumulative hotfix from the F5 Networks Downloads site.

If you installed cumulative hotfix HF-602-6 or cumulative hotfix HF-602-7, you may need to remove those hotfixes prior to installing cumulative hotfix HF-602-7.2. For more information, refer to K9253: Users may need to uninstall hotfix prior to installing HF-602-7.2.
