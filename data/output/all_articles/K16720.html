Policy Information

F5 is releasing the following new SKUs for BIG-IP APM Virtual Edition (VE):

  • BIG-IP Virtual Edition Access Policy Manager Max (200 Mbps, 2500 Concurrent VPN Users, 10k access session v13)
  • BIG-IP Virtual Edition Access Policy Manager Base (1 Gbps, 200 CCU, 800 Access Sessions, v13)

As a result, F5 is announcing the retirement of the BIG-IP APM VE Add-on Concurrent VPN User License SKUs for 25, 100, and 500, effective July 1, 2015.

In addition, F5 will be removing the following two existing APM license SKUs from the F5 price list as of July 1, 2015, and replacing them with new SKUs, with an effective retirement date of July 1, 2015:

  • BIG-IP Virtual Edition Access Policy Manager Max (200 Mbps, 2500 CCU, 2500 Access Sessions, v13)
  • BIG-IP Virtual Edition Access Policy Manager Base (1 Gbps, 250 CCU, 2500 Access Sessions, v13)

F5 is providing notification to customers and partners, announcing intent to immediately retire the licenses listed in the following table, and removing them from the F5 price list on July 1, 2015.

Technical Support

F5 maintains generous life cycle policies that allow customers to enjoy many years of support and new software releases. Customers whose platforms are designated End of Life (EoL), and who are currently under maintenance contracts, will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the service contract renewal date expires.
