K000146847: Why am I unable to add mTLS certificate of my CA to the configuration?

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-11-08
Updated
2024-11-08
Applies to

  • F5 Distributed Cloud
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000146847

What Happened?

  • You are unable to add certificate of your CA to the configuration.
  • You get an error while adding a mTLS certificate to Load Balancer

 

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud Load Balancer

Resolution/Answer

  • We only support x509 v3 certs as CA certs

Cause

  • The certificate attached is a x509 v1 certificate. 

Additional Information

  • Field Certificate spec.loadbalancer_type.https.tls_certificates_choice.tls_parameters.mtls_choice.use_mtls.trusted_ca_choice.trusted_ca_url does not contain a valid truststore certificate
  • Field spec.loadbalancer_type.https.tls_certificates_choice.tls_parameters.mtls_choice.use_mtls.trusted_ca_choice.trusted_ca_url fails rule
