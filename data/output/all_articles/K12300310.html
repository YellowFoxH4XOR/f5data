Description
Unable to connect to WebUI or SSH of recently upgraded device

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • WebUI
  • SSH
  • Upgrade
  • 13.1

Cause
The packet sizes in an SSH connection and WebUI connection are slightly larger than in previous versions.
If the MTU is set correctly on the BIGIP and the client, ensure that intermediate devices in the network are not enforcing a smaller MTU 
 

Recommended Actions

You can check this by setting a smaller MTU on your client, which will cause your client to send out smaller packets on the network. The link below from Microsoft advises on how to adjust the MTU on a Windows client. If the connections are working, then there is something on the network enforcing a smaller MTU, which has not been made aware to the endpoint of the network. 
 

Additional Information
https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/help/826159/how-to-change-the-default-maximum-transmission-unit-mtu-size-settings
