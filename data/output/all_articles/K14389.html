Known Issue

IPv6 networks are not added to the ZebOS IS-IS local LSP/ISIS database when the router interface is configured as passive.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP is licensed with Advance Routing and ZebOS IS-IS is enabled.
  • The ZebOS IS-IS router interface is configured with the passive-interface setting.

Note: This issue does not affect IPv4 networks.

Impact

The affected IPv6 networks will not be used or announced. This may result in services outages or routing issues.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • IPv6 networks on passive interfaces are not added to the local LSP/ISIS database.
  • You may encounter routing issues or service outages for IPv6 traffic.
