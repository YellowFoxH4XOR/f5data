Policy Information

With the availability of the F5 rSeries r12600-DS, r12800-DS, r12900-DS, r12900DSB, and r12900DSM, F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) for the BIG-IP iSeries i11400-DS, i11600-DS, i11800-DS, i11800DSB, and i11800DSM.

This product notice does not impact any subscription licenses or service agreements. Field Upgrade and Add-on SKUs are not included in this EoS announcement.

Refer to the K4309: F5 hardware product lifecycle support policy for information about BIG-IP platform lifecycles.

The BIG-IP SKUs listed below will reach EoS worldwide effective October 1, 2024.
