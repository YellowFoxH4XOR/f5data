Known Issue

New Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) connections using AES or DES encryption ciphers may stall when the Don’t insert empty fragments SSL profile option is disabled.

Note: The Don't insert empty fragments option is enabled by default.

Impact

New SSL connections stall and fail to complete.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Client connections stall and are unable to successfully connect through the BIG-IP system.
  • Client applications do not receive immediate connection-related diagnostic data.
