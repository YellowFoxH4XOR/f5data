Policy Information

BIG-IP software version 9.3 represents the first maintenance release for the BIG-IP Global Traffic Manager and provides improved stability and performance. This release allows the BIG-IP GTM to replace the 3-DNS product line. As part of our normal product lifecycle that includes periodic retirement of legacy products, F5 Networks is announcing the End of Sale of the 3-DNS products. F5 Networks is providing Advanced Product Notification to partners announcing F5 Networks' intent to retire the 3-DNS products listed below:

Platform Upgrades

Customers whose platforms are designated EoL and who want to use the latest software may take advantage of F5 Networks' technology refresh program. For similar performance as the 3-DNS 520, F5 Networks recommends the BIG-IP GTM 1500 2GB. Additionally, the BIG-IP GTM 6400 is available for users who need higher performance.

Technical Support

F5 Networks maintains generous lifecycle policies that allow customers to enjoy many years of both support and new software releases. Customers whose platforms are designated EoL and who are currently under maintenance contracts will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the Service Contract Renewal date expires.

New sales

The new F5 Networks platforms provide superior performance and functionality and were designed explicitly for our latest generation of BIG-IP version 9.x software.
