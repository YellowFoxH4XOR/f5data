Issue

Old Behavior

In versions prior to BIG-IP 13.1.0.2, the BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) on the Amazon Web Services (AWS) Intelligence Community (IC) marketplace is assigned a platform ID of Z100a.

New Behavior

Beginning in BIG-IP 13.1.0.2, the BIG-IP VE on the AWS IC marketplace is assigned a platform ID of Z100a_icm.

F5 Product Development tracked this change as ID 718831.

Impact

For BIG-IP VE on the AWS IC marketplace, if you upgrade, from a version previous to BIG-IP 13.1.0.2 to BIG-IP 13.1.0.2 or later, the platform ID change causes a license failure because the original Bring Your Own License (BYOL) is associated with the Z100a platform ID.

Additionally, you will encounter the following error message when you attempt to reactivate the license after the upgrade:

Unable to grant license key: Error 12345, Minimum locking information is missing from the Dossier

Recommended Actions

When upgrading from a version previous to BIG-IP 13.1.0.2 to BIG-IP 13.1.0.2 or later, you should first contact F5 Technical Support and request a license Allow-Move. Enabling the license Allow-Move allows you to reactivate your license with the new Z100a_icm platform ID after upgrading to BIG-IP 13.1.0.2 or later.
