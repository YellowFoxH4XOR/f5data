Topic

The Non-Volatile RAM (NVRAM) memory contains namespace metadata information that managed volumes are currently using. If this memory is lost, all managed volumes must reimport all of their back-end shares. To protect against this time-consuming process, the NVRAM chip has a separate battery backup that lasts up to 72 hours.

To charge the NVRAM battery, you must turn on the ARX power supplies and have the ARX Initial interview completed. Just connecting the ARX platform to a power source does not charge the NVRAM battery.

The NVRAM battery must be charged before the ARX can be configured. F5 Networks recommends allowing time for the NVRAM battery to fully charge before attempting the configuration.
