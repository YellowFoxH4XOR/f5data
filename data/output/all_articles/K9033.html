K9033: Detecting Windows Vista clients in a Network Access policy check

Topic

On the Network Access : Resources page under the Policy Checks tab, you can configure an expression in the Operating system service packs field that checks for specific Windows operating systems and service packs on the client system. If you want to detect a Windows Vista system, use WinVI in the expression. For example:

(Win2003 OR WinVI OR (WINXP AND SP2)) AND NOT (WIN95 OR WIN98 OR WIN98SE OR WINME)

For additional examples and information, refer to the FirePass online help for Network Access : Resources on the Policy Checks tab.
