Description:

When upgrading and using custom port lockdown setting you will receive a GUI error with regards to failed lockdown validation after upgrade. 

Environment Sample:
  • BIG-IP SSLO
  • Upgraded to 16.1.0
  • Custom port lockdown
Cause:
ID1042437 - Port lockdown "allow custom" is not supported in SSL Orchestrator

When BIG-IP is in high availability (HA) and the self IP used for HA has the port lockdown configured other then "Allow All" or "Allow Default". Config changes from the SSL Orchestrator UI are not possible.


Recommended Actions: 
Change the port lockdown settings to "Allow All" or "Allow Default"
