K25537532: ''Stats processing delay'' email spam after adding a new DCD

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-06-07
Updated
2025-04-01
Applies to
  • BIG-IQ
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K25537532

Description

After adding a DCD to an existing cluster, you start receiving 'Stats processing delay' emails that do not have the hostname of the managed device any longer in the email body.

Environment

  • BIG-IQ DCD cluster with stats collection
  • managed BIG-IP devices running v13 and higher
  • new DCD added or DCD cluster has been reset either by clear-rest-storage or by removing and re-adding all the members
  • email notifications enabled

Cause

When the DCD cluster is reset, the statistics collection information from the BIG-IPs also get removed. Newly added BIG-IPs will show stats collection has failed and will show the default last collection date of January 01, 1970. Email notifications for these will not show any hostname.

Recommended Actions

  • make sure AVR is provisioned on the managed BIG-IPs
  • restart avrd on the BIG-IPs
