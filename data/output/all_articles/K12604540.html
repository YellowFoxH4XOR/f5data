Known Issue

The bigip_tmsh_error_maps.h file is missing from the /etc/alertd/ directory.

Impact

You may be unable to create alerts for Traffic Management Shell (tmsh) related errors, such as certificate expiration warnings, with no mapping information available.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When creating alerts, you are unable to locate error messages related to tmsh logging.
