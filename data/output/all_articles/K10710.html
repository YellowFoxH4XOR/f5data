Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. When the BIG-IP GTM receives a query that falls through to BIND from a host that is only reachable through the management interface, the response may be sent out the management interface with a source IP of 127.0.0.1.

Note: The BIG-IP GTM should not return responses to queries through the management port. In addition, replies sourced from the loopback address are unlikely to reach the intended destination, since few network devices are configured to pass traffic with a loopback source address.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as part of CR110995, and it was fixed in BIG-IP version 9.4.7. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM Release Notes.
