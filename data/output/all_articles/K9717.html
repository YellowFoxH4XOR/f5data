Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The big3d process, which is responsible for performing BIG-IP GTM probes, has an Active probe queue and a Pending probe queue. The Active queue can maintain up to 255 probes in a pending state. The Pending queue can maintain up to 16,000 probes in a pending state. If both queues fill, any additional probes will be immediately marked down, along with their related objects.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR101175, and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.4.6. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM Release Notes.

Additionally, this issue has been fixed in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.5-HF1. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.
