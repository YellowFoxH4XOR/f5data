Description

While navigating to "Statistics >> Module Statistics : Traffic Summary : General" on GUI, "Packet Velocity Acceleration" (PVA) statistics was not shown.

Figure: PVA statistics available

 

Figure: PVA statistics unavailable

 

Environment

Platform that does not have ePVA chip (refer to K12837).

 

Cause

On BIG-IP platform without ePVA acceleration hardware (e.g., VE on ESXi), GUI PVA statistics is not displayed. This is expected behavior.

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

None.
