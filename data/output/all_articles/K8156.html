Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. Some versions of the BIG-IP software do not support the extended daylight saving interval in the New Zealand time zone. That means that the BIG-IP system considers Daylight Saving Time (DST) in New Zealand to begin on the second Sunday in October instead of the last Sunday in September, and end on the third Sunday in the following March instead of the first Sunday in the following April.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR84864 and it was fixed in BIG-IP 9.3.1 and 9.4.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM, ASM, GTM, WebAccelerator, or Link Controller Release Notes.

Additionally, this issue was fixed in Hotfix-BIG-IP-9.4.3-HF2 issued for BIG-IP 9.4.3. You may download this hotfix or later versions of the hotfix from the F5 Downloads site.

For information about the F5 hotfix policy, refer to K4918: Overview of the F5 Networks critical issue hotfix policy.
