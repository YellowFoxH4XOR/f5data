What Happened?

Is it possible to disable a Load Balancer (LB) so it no longer processes traffic, without removing its configuration from the Console?

 

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud Console
  • Load Balancer

 

Resolution/Answer

Yes, it is.

To disable your Load Balancer:

  1. Locate to your Load Balancer (LB) in the Console
  2. Click the Manage Configuration Option from the "..." on the right-hand side of the Load Balancer List
  3. Click Edit Configuration
  4. Scroll To or Click Other Settings
  5. On the VIP Advertisement dropdown, choose Do Not Advertise
  6. Click Save and Exit
