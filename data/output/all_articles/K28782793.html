Known Issue

The cbrd process may experience high CPU usage.

The cbrd process performs XML content-based routing on the BIG-IP system. However, the cbrd process runs even when the BIG-IP system is not configured to perform content-based routing, regardless of provisioned modules.

Impact

The cbrd process may not run properly. Virtual servers configured with XML profiles may be unable to successfully parse XML documents. Other control plane processes may also be negatively impacted due to lack or resources.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Performance graphs on the BIG-IP system show high CPU usage.
  • Output of the ps or top utilities show high CPU usage for the cbrd process.
  • SNMP polling on the BIG-IP system reports high CPU usage.

Resolution

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can restart the cbrd process using the tmsh restart /sys service cbrd command.

Note: This is a temporary workaround and you may need to repeat it if this issue reoccurs.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
