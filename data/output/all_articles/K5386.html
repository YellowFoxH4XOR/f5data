Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The statistics graphs may not update when the web browser caches the graph images on the 3-DNS Controller or the BIG-IP Link Controller.

F5 Networks Product Development tracked this issue as CR23770 and CR24209.

Workaround

If upgrading is not an immediate option, you can work around this issue by refreshing/reloading the statistics page in your web browser.
