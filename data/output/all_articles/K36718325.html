K36718325: Former active BIG-IQ GUI stuck at ''Waiting for BIG-IQ services to become available'' after auto failover

Type
Support Solution
Published
2021-09-30
Updated
2025-02-06
Applies to

Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K36718325

Description
After an auto-failover in a BIG-IQ HA environment, the former active BIG-IQ GUI will show "Waiting for BIG-IQ services to become available" and not present a login screen. This GUI unavailability is the expected behavior. When an auto failover occurs, the restjavad process is stopped on the former active BIG-IP to prevent it from interoperating with the environment. After an auto failover, manual steps must be taken to restore the HA setup. 

Environment

  • BIG-IQ HA environment with auto failover configured.

Cause
An auto failover event has occurred. 

Recommended Actions

See the following manual section on how to restore the HA configuration:

  • Reestablishing a BIG-IQ auto failover HA configuration after failover

None.
