Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. BIG-IP ASM corrupts custom blocking pages that use double-byte character encoding, such as Shift_JIS. This behavior occurs when you configure the blocking page, apply the security policy, and then load the configuration from a UCS file, or during a configsync operation.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR105152 and it was fixed in BIG-IP ASM 9.3.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP ASM Release Notes.
