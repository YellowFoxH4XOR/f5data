Known Issue

iRules session data lookup from a custom iRules event may produce a Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) core file.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP device is licensed and provisioned with the BIG-IP APM module.
  • Your access profile access policy is configured with a custom BIG-IP APM iRule that gathers access session data.
  • You access invalid/stale access session data stored by a custom iRule.

Impact

The BIG-IP APM system may temporarily fail to process traffic while the TMM process restarts.

When configured as part of a high availability (HA) group, the system may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP system fails over to the standby peer when configured for HA.
  • The BIG-IP system generates TMM core files to the /shared/core directory.
  • At the time of the event, the BIG-IP system generates a SIGSEGV fault and stack trace in the /var/log/tmm file that appears similar to the following example:

    notice ** SIGSEGV **
