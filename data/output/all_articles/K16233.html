Known Issue

Traffic groups may not become active on a redundant system following a failover event.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Multiple BIG-IP devices in a high availability (HA) failover group are configured with a failover mechanism, such as VLAN fail-safe or gateway fail-safe.
  • The devices in the HA failover group are also configured with HA groups.
  • A failover event is triggered, such as VLAN fail-safe or gateway fail-safe, for a system with active traffic groups.

When a system with active traffic groups experiences a failover event, such as VLAN fail-safe or gateway fail-safe, the active traffic groups on that system are forced to standby. However, the HA group scores for the failed system are not decremented to zero and may remain equal to or greater than the other systems within the failover group. As a result, those traffic groups will not become active on any other system within the failover group.

Impact

Traffic will fail for the affected traffic groups.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The HA group scores for a system that experiences a failover event remain unchanged after the failover event.
  • Traffic groups that were active on the failed system do not become active on any of the other systems within the failover group following the failover event.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you should not use repetitive failsafe mechanisms in addition to HA groups. You can configure an HA group to replace the VLAN or gateway fail-safe configuration.

Impact of workaround: The impact of the suggested workaround will depend on the specific environment. F5 recommends testing any such changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.
