Topic

The BIG-IP GTM system will not probe objects through the management interface, because the management interface is specified for management traffic to the BIG-IP GTM system, and is not supported for production traffic.

In order to ensure that the BIG-IP GTM system will not attempt to probe objects through the management interface, you must adhere to the following guidelines:

  • Ensure that the BIG-IP GTM system does not have any objects configured on the management network address space.
  • Ensure that no routes are configured requiring that traffic is sent to or received from objects that are routed through the BIG-IP GTM management interface.
  • Ensure that the default route for the BIG-IP GTM system is not on the management subnet.
