K000137891: Authentication Failures after RADIUS Shared Secret update

Description

You've recently updated the RADIUS Shared Secret in F5 APM and are experiencing authentication failures.

 

Environment

RADIUS Authentication in APM

Recently changed Shared Secret on RADIUS

Cause

Authentication failures after updating the RADIUS Shared Secret occur because the APM system does not always automatically apply these changes. This behavior necessitates a manual restart of certain services. This is not unlikely to happen on APM, similar to how service restarts are required after applying access policy changes.

Recommended Actions

To ensure the updated RADIUS Shared Secret is recognized by the system, restart the relevant services:

restart sys service apmd

restart sys service apm_websso

Based on our experience, restarting just the apm_sso service is usually sufficient. However, this can vary depending on your specific setup, and you may need to follow it up by restarting apmd as well.
