Known Issue

Clock advanced messages may be triggered when a large amount of data is maintained in DNS Express zones.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have DNS Express configured with several hundred thousand to several million records.
  • DNS Express zones are being updated.

Impact

Traffic may be dropped by the BIG-IP system during the DNS Express update.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • In the /var/log/ltm log file, you observe messages similar to the following example when DNS Express zones are updated:

    notice tmm[25454]: 01010029:5: Clock advanced by 121 ticks.

  • No traffic can be passed for the duration of the DNS Express update.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can prevent all updates to your DNS Express zones.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system, but may have an operational impact.
