Description

What is the process to wipe the disk drives on an F5OS-A appliance before returning it for an RMA

 

Environment

BIG-IP rSeries Device running F5OS-A software version 1.0.0 or later with Console Access

  • F5 r2000
  • F5 r4000
  • F5 r5000
  • F5 r10000

 

Cause

In case of Drive failure before returning drive under a RMA

 

Recommended Actions

The "The F5 Disk Erase Utility" process is documented at:

https://techdocs.f5.com/en-us/hw-platforms/f5-plat-hw-essentials/title-storage-drive-maint.html

Consult your company Security policies for guidance. If wiping the drive fully is not required, just changing encryption keys may be an adequate solution.
