Description
HA Connection with peer lost/established every 30 seconds

  • Logs filled with HA connection lost/established messages every 30 seconds:

    Jan 30 11:48:51 LTM-A err tmm1[18719]: 01340002:3: HA Connection with peer 192.168.1.1:32768 for traffic-group /Common/traffic-group-1 lost.
    Jan 30 11:48:52 LTM-A notice tmm1[18719]: 01340001:5: HA Connection with peer 192.168.1.1:32768 for traffic-group /Common/traffic-group-1 established.
    Jan 30 11:49:23 LTM-A err tmm1[18719]: 01340002:3: HA Connection with peer 192.168.1.1:32768 for traffic-group /Common/traffic-group-1 lost.
    Jan 30 11:49:24 LTM-A notice tmm1[18719]: 01340001:5: HA Connection with peer 192.168.1.1:32768 for traffic-group /Common/traffic-group-1 established.

  • ha_stat table shows increasing count of 'expires' at the rate of 1 every 30 seconds:

    tmctl -d blade ha_stat -s tmid,expires
    tmid  expires
    ----- -------
    [0.0]  389642
    [0.1]  391565

Environment
  • BIG-IP pair with connection mirroring enabled
  • MTU setting is larger than 1500
Cause
tcpdump of the HA connection channel (tcp port 1029) shows that sent packets larger than 1500 bytes are not reaching the peer and are being retransmitted. After 30 seconds of retries, the connection is aborted and the 'expires' counter incremented.

Recommended Actions

  1. Verify that the MTU settings are correct

    # tmsh list /net vlan
    net vlan /Common/VLAN_X {
        interfaces {
            HA_TRUNK {
                tagged
            }
        }
        mtu 9198
        tag 4100
    }
     
  2. Verify that switches or any devices in the path support MTU size larger than 1500.
