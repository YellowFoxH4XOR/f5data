Known Issue

When you save a monitor configuration with a send, recv, or recv-disable attribute set to none, the attributes may be deleted.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You configure a monitor with one or more of the following attributes set to none:

    • send
    • recv
    • recv-disable
  • You save the monitor configuration.

Impact

The monitor unexpectedly inherits send, recv, or recv-disable attributes from a parent monitor.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H560021 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The saved monitor no longer contains the send, recv, or recv-disable attributes.
  • The monitor does not accurately mark hosts up or down.
