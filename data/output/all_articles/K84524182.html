Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) on a standby device may restart with an assertion fail valid pcb message when a TCP connection is ended during flow control.

A specific condition known to trigger this issue in normal traffic conditions has not been identified.

Impact

The affected standby device in the device group becomes temporarily unavailable.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The standby BIG-IP system is temporarily unavailable.
  • The standby BIG-IP system generates a TMM core file to the /shared/core directory.
  • In the /var/log/tmm file, you observe a TMM assertion fail message similar to the following example:

    Assertion fail :tmm_panic (format=0x1728f00 "../modules/hudfilter/tcp4/tcp4.c:999: %svalid pcb%s")
