K000092615: SFP is not working in RMA replacement device after restoring UCS

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-11-14
Updated
2024-01-25
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000092615

Description

Third party SFP taken from faulty device is not working in RMA device after restoring UCS. The interface status shows down.

Environment

  • RMA replacement device
  • Third party SFP

Cause

F5 does not support third party SFPs

Recommended Actions

  • Use F5 supported SFPs
  • Contact your F5 sales representative for more information

Additional Information

None
