K000149960: How to resolve Active Alarms for Interface Down Warnings on R5000, R10000 and R12000 series devices

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-02-25
Updated
2025-03-19
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000149960

Description

Interfaces without a link display an "Active Alarms" dashboard warning alert of "Interface down" only for R5000, R10000 and R12000 series devices, but not for R2000 and R4000 series devices.

Environment

  • R5000, R10000 and R12000 series devices
  • F5OS-A versions 1.8.0 and earlier

Cause

ID: 1644293

Recommended Actions

To clear the status, one can select to disable the individual interfaces until they are needed and have an active link.
