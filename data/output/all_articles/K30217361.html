Known Issue

BIG-IP APM access policy Endpoint Security (EPSEC) client-side patch management may fail to detect Jamf Casper Suite on macOS.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • BIG-IP APM is installed with an EPSEC package version prior to 1.0.0-415.0.
  • An access policy is configured with an EPSEC client-side agent to check for patch management Jamf Casper Suite on macOS.

Impact

macOS users may be denied access to the configured BIG-IP APM resources.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • BIG-IP APM incorrectly evaluates access policies for macOS users.
