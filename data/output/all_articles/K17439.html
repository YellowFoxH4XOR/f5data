Known Issue

The IPsec policy flow's nexthop may not change when a monitored gateway pool member has been marked down.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP system is configured to route IPsec flows through a gateway pool.
  • A gateway pool member has been marked down by the associated gateway pool monitor.

Impact

IPsec traffic will fail as the BIG-IP system continues to send IPsec traffic to the down pool member.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • IPsec traffic is failing while one or more gateway pool members are available.
  • Traffic packet captures reveal IPsec traffic being sent to down gateway pool members.
