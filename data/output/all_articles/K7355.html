Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP LTM system sends new connections to disabled nodes for a PVA-accelerated virtual server.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR59548 and CR59548, and it was fixed in BIG-IP LTM 9.1.2, 9.2.3, 9.3, and 9.4. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP LTM Release Notes.

This issue still exists in BIG-IP LTM 9.2.4 and 9.2.5.
