Policy Information

F5 hereby announces the End of Sale (EoS) of F5 Application Services Proxy. The F5 EoS of F5 Application Services Proxy is effective May 31, 2018. This applies to all versions, all software development, and all technical support. This product notice does not impact any F5 Container Ingress Service offerings.

In addition, this is six months after F5 introduced F5 Application Services Proxy 1.1 – a short-term support release.

Technical Support

F5 maintains generous lifecycle policies that allow customers to enjoy short-term and long-term support and new software releases. Customers whose platforms are designated EoS and EoL, who are currently under maintenance contracts, will continue to receive technical support until the service contract renewal date expires.
