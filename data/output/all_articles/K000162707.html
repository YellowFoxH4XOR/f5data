K000162707: Upgrade BIG-IP to 17.1.3.4 or 17.5.1.8 breaks CAC authentication

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-08-13
Updated
2026-08-13
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.1.3, 17.5.1
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000162707

Description

CAC certificate authentication fails during TLS renegotiation. The client sends its certificate and related handshake messages, but BIG-IP terminates the connection with a fatal TLS Handshake Failure alert. Browsers may display ERR_SSL_PROTOCOL_ERROR.

Environment

  • Upgrade to BIG-IP 17.1.3.4 or 17.5.1.8
  • CAC certificate validation used a DoD trusted CA bundle

Cause

The issue is caused by BIG-IP software defect ID2384197, which affects TLS client-certificate processing during CAC authentication renegotiation. After the client presents its CAC certificate, BIG-IP may fail to complete the TLS handshake and generate a fatal Handshake Failure alert.

Answer/Recommended Actions

Request the Engineering Hotfix that contains the fix for Bug ID2384197.

After installation:

  1. Test the CAC authentication flow.
  2. Confirm that the client certificate is presented.
  3. Confirm that the TLS handshake completes without a fatal alert.
  4. Confirm that application access is restored.
  5. Upgrade to a generally available release containing the fix when available.
