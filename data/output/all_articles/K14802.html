Known Issue

Programmatically disabling a Traffic Management Operating System (TMOS) plug-in may cause a memory leak.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You disable a TMOS plug-in from within an iRule.

Impact

Performance may degrade over time, and the system may eventually become unresponsive and then restart due to memory exhaustion.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) memory use steadily increases over time.

Workaround

To work around this issue, do not disable TMOS plug-ins from within an iRule.

Impact of workaround: The impact of avoiding a programmatic change to a plug-in varies depending on customer requirements.
