Issue

Old Behavior

BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) previously had a limit of 500 Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) transactions per second (TPS) per vCPU.

New Behavior

The SSL TPS limit is removed in BIG-IP VE, except for the following:

  • BIG-IP VE systems running older licenses that F5 has declared End of Sale. For more information, refer to K15667: Advance End of Sale announcement for BIG-IP Virtual Edition licenses.
  • BIG-IP 12.1.0.1.x.x and earlier utility-based images on the Amazon Web Services (AWS) marketplace.

Note: You must re-activate your BIG-IP VE license to remove the limit.

Impact

With the limit removed, the BIG-IP VE might achieve better SSL TPS performance.

Recommended Actions

To remove the SSL TPS limit of 500 per vCPU from your BIG-IP VE system, you must re-activate your BIG-IP VE license. For more information about re-activating your BIG-IP VE license, refer to K7752: Licensing the BIG-IP system.
