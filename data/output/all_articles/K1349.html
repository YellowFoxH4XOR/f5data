K1349: Configuring 3-DNS to limit the amount of traffic directed to a host or BIG-IP

Topic

You can configure 3-DNS to limit the rate at which it directs traffic to hosts or BIG-IPs, by using the following procedures:

Limiting traffic to hosts

To configure a packet-per-second limit, per device for non-F5 devices, perform the following steps:

  1. From the navigation pane of the 3-DNS Configuration utility, click Servers.
  2. Click Host.
  3. Click the Limit Settings icon.
  4. Enter the desired setting for the device in the Kilobytes/Second box.

    Note: The total of these rates must be less than or equal to the bandwidth of the data center.

  5. Click Update.

Limiting traffic to BIG-IPs

To configure a packet-per-second limit, per device for the BIG-IP:

  1. From the navigation pane of the 3-DNS Configuration utility, click Servers.
  2. Click BIG-IP.
  3. Click the Limit Settings icon.
  4. Enter the desired setting for the BIG-IP in the Kilobytes/Second box.

    Note: The total of these rates must be less than or equal to the bandwidth of the data center.

  5. Click Update.
