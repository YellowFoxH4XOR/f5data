Topic

Current versions of ARX do not support the Remote Quota Reporting (rquota) protocol. The rquota protocol allows NFS users to view their data storage quotas on NFS servers.

Note: For information about products and protocols qualified for use with the ARX system, refer to K10909: F5 Data Solutions Compatibility Matrix.
