Known Issue

Mirroring information for FastL4 virtual servers may timeout on the standby system.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Connection and/or Persistence mirroring is enabled for a FastL4 virtual server.
  • Mirroring is configured with both Primary and Secondary mirroring connections.
  • The inactive mirroring connection was lost, then re-established.

When an inactive mirror connection is dropped and then re-established, mirroring information for existing fastL4 connections is no longer updated on the standby system. As a result, the idle connection and persistence timers will expire on the standby system after their timeout value.

Impact

FastL4 connections may not continue or persist as expected in the event of a failover.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • The idle connection and/or persistence timers will expire on the standby system after their timeout value.
  • FastL4 connections do not continue as expected following a failover event.
  • FastL4 connections do not persist to the same resource as expected following a failover event.

Workaround

Impact of workaround: Performing the following workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.

To mitigate this issue, you can remove the Secondary mirroring configuration.
