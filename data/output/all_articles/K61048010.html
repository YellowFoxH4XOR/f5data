Known Issue

A RADIUS profile with Subscriber Discovery enabled may cause the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) process to restart and produce a core file.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The Subscriber Discovery feature is enabled in the RADIUS profile.
  • Your BIG-IP system is not licensed for the BIG-IP PEM module.

The Subscriber Discovery feature requires the BIG-IP system to be licensed for the BIG-IP PEM module.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from the TMM restart, and devices configured as a high-availability (HA) pair may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H612899 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • Your BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from the TMM restart.
  • If configured for HA, the affected BIG-IP device fails over to the configured peer device.
  • Your BIG-IP system generates a TMM core file to the /shared/core directory, which includes a time stamp that correlates to the traffic interruption.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can disable the Subscriber Discovery option in the RADIUS profile until you license the system for the BIG-IP PEM module. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: The impact of the suggested workaround will depend on the specific environment. F5 recommends that you test any such changes during a maintenance window and consider the possible impact to your specific environment.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.
  2. Navigate to Local Traffic > Profiles > Services > RADIUS.
  3. Select the affected RADIUS profile by clicking the profile name.
  4. To disable the option, clear the Subscriber Discovery box.
  5. Click Update.

To re-enable the Subscriber Discovery feature after you install a BIG-IP PEM license, you can repeat this procedure and re-select the Subscriber Discovery box to enable the option.
