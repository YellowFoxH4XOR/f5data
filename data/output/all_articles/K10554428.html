Description

You are planning an upgrade of your BIG-IP VE systems and need to determine if they are able to be upgraded

Environment

  • BIG-IP VE
  • ESXi/vSphere

Cause

None

Recommended Actions

Look at virtual disk size of the BIG-IP in vSphere and compare it with values in K14946. If disk size matches one of the LTM or ALL image sizes, you will be able to upgrade your device.
