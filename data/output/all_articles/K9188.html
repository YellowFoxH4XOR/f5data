Known Issue

The BIG-IP system is preconfigured to send coldStart or bigipAgentStart traps upon startup of the local SNMP agent. The local SNMP agent and Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) are both initialized at system startup time. If the BIG-IP configuration uses an SNMP trapsink destination whose egress route is through a TMM interface (such as a self-IP address), and TMM has not fully initialized its interfaces at the time the startup traps are sent, the startup traps cannot be sent.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as ID 246976 (formerly CR106744) and it was fixed in BIG-IP 11.6.0. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP release notes.

Workaround

To ensure startup traps are sent at system startup, you can configure a trapsink destination whose egress route is through the management interface.

Note: For more information, refer to the BIG-IP Network and System Management Guide. For information about how to locate F5 product guides, refer to K12453464: Finding product documentation on AskF5.

Impact of workaround: The impact of the suggested workaround will depend on the specific environment. F5 recommends that you test any such changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.
