Description

Environment

F5OS 1.6.1

Cause

It can be initially observed by seeing the tenants failover to the other device in the DSC. However, what's really happening is the tenants are restarted due to Openshift marking the blade NotReady because it could not update its origin-node status for over a minute.

 

Recommended Actions

 

No workaround available as of the moment
