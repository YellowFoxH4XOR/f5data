Known Issue

The BIG-IP 5000/7000 series platforms may generate power supply read failures.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • The power supply is populated and turned on.
  • The BIG-IP platform is one of the following models:

    • BIG-IP 7000s/7200v (D110)
    • BIG-IP 5000s/5200v (C109)

This issue is related to a lights-out processor (LOP) firmware issue and does not indicate an actual power supply issue.

Note: A related issue occurs on the BIG-IP 2000/4000 and 10000 series platforms. For more information, refer to the Supplemental Information section in this article.

Impact

The power supply continues to function properly, but the Always-On Management (AOM) and the BIG-IP system lose the ability to poll the power supply sensors.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H426893 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium screen.
  • The BIG-IP system may continually generate error messages to the /var/log/ltm file, indicating a power supply read failure for PS1 (obj_id=f4). The error message appears similar to the following example:

    notice chmand[7049]: 012a0005:5: Tmstat::updateSensorTbls: HAL SenSvc error: BourneSeepromDev: LopDev: sendLopCmd: Lopd status: 1 packet: action=1  obj_id=f4  sub_obj=0  slot_id=ff  result=19  len=0  crc=a5c1  payload=   (error code:0x19)
  • The BIG-IP system may continually generate error messages to the /var/log/ltm file, indicating a power supply read failure for PS2 (obj_id=fc). The error message appears similar to the following example:

    012a0005:5: Tmstat::updateSensorTbls: HAL SenSvc error: BourneSeepromDev: LopDev: sendLopCmd: Lopd status: 1 packet: action=1 obj_id=fc sub_obj=0 slot_id=ff result=19 len=0 crc=7b2d payload= (error code:0x19)
  • The error code:0x19 read error messages persist and the BIG-IP system logs these messages approximately every 30 seconds.
  • The power supply LEDs are solid green, indicating that they are present and operating properly.

Workaround

To temporarily work around this issue, you can reseat the power supply that is generating the error messages and clear the LCD display. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: There is no impact for appliances that contain dual power supplies. If you have an appliance that contains a single power supply, turn off the unit before reseating the power supply.

Reseating the power supply that is generating error messages

  1. Determine which power supply is generating the error messages by verifying the power supply identifier in the log message.

    For example:

    obj_id=f4  - indicates power supply #1

    obj_id=fc -  indicates power supply #2
  2. On the affected power supply, turn the power switch to the Off ( 0 ) position.
  3. Remove the AC power cord from the power supply.
  4. Loosen the screw and remove the power supply.
  5. Before attaching the AC power cord, slide the power supply into the power supply bay.
  6. Attach the AC power cord to the power supply.
  7. On the power supply, turn the power switch to the On ( 1 ) position.
