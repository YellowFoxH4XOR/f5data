Known Issue

BIG-IP WebAccelerator and BIG-IP AAM may corrupt PDF files that are partially password protected.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • BIG-IP WebAccelerator or BIG-IP AAM is attempting to linearize a PDF document that is partially password protected. For example, some usage rights are granted without password but other usage rights (such as modify) require a password.

Impact

PDF files may display as blank documents or render incorrectly.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • Affected PDF files may display as blank documents or render incorrectly.

Workaround

You can mitigate this issue by disabling PDF linearization in the acceleration policy. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Disabling PDF linearization causes BIG-IP to proxy all PDFs to the origin web server, and may impact performance greater than the original issue. The impact of disabling linearization depends on the specific environment. F5 recommends that you test any such changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.
  2. Navigate to Acceleration > Web Application > Policies.
  3. Select the affected user-defined acceleration policy.
  4. Click a node in the Policy Tree.
  5. From the Matching Rules menu, choose Acceleration Rules.
  6. On the menu bar, click Assembly.
  7. From the Content Assembly Options list, select Advanced.
  8. Clear the Enable PDF Linearization check box.
  9. Click Save.
  10. Publish the acceleration policy by performing the following steps:
    1. ?Click Publish.
    2. In the Comment field, type a description.
    3. Click Publish Now.
