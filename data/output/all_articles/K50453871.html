Description
After upgrade to 15.1.2.1 100Gb Optical interfaces do not come up

Environment

  • 15.1.2.1
  • 100Gb SFPs in use

Cause
Our firmware was adjusted to more closely match the hardware standard requiring Forward Error Correction (FEC) to be enable on the switch and the BIG-IP

Recommended Actions

Enable Forward Error Correction in the interface options on the BIG-IP.  Make sure that it is also enabled on the relevant switch ports.
Note: It is possible that auto may fail to bring the interface up, in which case please use enabled.

Additional Information
The relevant hardware standard
K6097: Specifications of the Fiber Gigabit Ethernet SFP, XFP, SFP+, QSFP+, and QSFP28 module ports on BIG-IP system platforms
Note: Please be aware that we may also fail to bring up a 100GB interface if third party SFPs are in use.  Third party SFPs are not supported.
