Description

A tenant running on an r5000, r10000, or r12000 series platform, or on a VELOS chassis, may display the same MAC address as the corresponding tenant interface on another appliance or chassis.

 

Environment

  • rSeries r5000/r10000/r12000 
  • Tenants
  • Interface 

Cause

 

This behavior is expected in this specific situation.

The interfaces listed inside a tenant on an r5000, r10000, or r12000 series platform, or on a VELOS chassis, represent individual TMM connections to the system’s internal datapath. These internal TMM links use predefined, hardcoded MAC addresses.

As a result, corresponding tenant interfaces may use the same MAC address on different appliances or chassis. This does not indicate that the appliances are incorrectly sharing the same customer-facing MAC address pool.

The high-end rSeries tenant architecture is described in K000133082: F5OS-A — Is there any difference the way r5000/r10000 and r2000/r4000 manage tenant interfaces. The article explains that r5000/r10000 tenants do not have direct visibility into the underlying physical interfaces. Instead, the tenant sees the interfaces associated with its internal datapath.
