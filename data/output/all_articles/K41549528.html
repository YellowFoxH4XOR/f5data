Description

This article provides a guide on the requirements to open BIG-IP Service Proxy for Kubernetes (SPK) /  Cloud-Native Network Functions (CNF) Support Cases. The main objective is to enable F5 Support to be able to address your suspected product issue effectively. F5 Support supports F5 technologies and would render all necessary assistance to address suspected product issues.
We also understand that your business is unique and dynamic. Some activities may be outside the scope of F5 support. F5 Professional Services (PS) can design, customize, and implement a solution that is right for your IT infrastructure, supports your business goals, and is agile enough to move your business forward. Additionally, F5 Support does not support Service Proxy for Kubernetes (SPK) or Cloud-Native Network Functions (CNF) feature customization and tailored scripts nor verify and validate Method Of Procedure (MOP), as well as migration, optimization and audit. This includes F5 PS deployed solutions that are implemented at the customer request. F5 can render consultative assistance when necessary. However, F5 is unable to take any responsibility for any feature customization and tailored scripts deployed nor MOP used to perform a change. As such, F5 is also unable to guarantee any Root Cause Analysis (RCA).  When such a need arises, we recommend you to talk to a F5 Account Representative so that F5 PS can be engaged.
Please adhere to the guidelines described below so that all needed firsthand analysis is performed prior to opening the SR. If otherwise, similar to Support Case that is outside of the scope of F5 Support, we may have to reject and close your Support Case. Please feel free to open a new Case once you have sufficient analysis performed on suspected SPK / CNF product issue.
F5 Support will provide the necessary assistance to resolve issue to Case that follows the guidelines of this article, however F5 does not guarantee RCA nor its timeline.

Environment

  •  BIG-IP Service Proxy for Kubernetes (SPK)
  •  BIG-IP Cloud-Native Network Functions (CNF)

Cause
None

Recommended Actions

1.  If the issue arises within 14 days from a set of new implementations:

a. If this is led by F5 PS, please have F5 PS to open the SR with F5 Support. F5 would render all assistance to assist on your suspect product issue. F5 PS would continue to interface with you on the issue
b. If F5 PS is not involved and there is no clear evidence of a suspected product issue, please talk to a F5 Account Representative


2.  Problem statement (only one problem description per request)

a. Describe the problem fully, the symptoms of the issue and its current impact (Including business impact),
b. The approximate date/time the issue first occurred, or if issue is still recurring,
c. The number of times the issue has recurred,
d. Any error output provided by the system,
e. Steps to reproduce the issue,
f. Any changes you made to the system before the issue first occurred,
g. Any configuration changes on SPK/CNF and in your network,
h. Any steps you have taken in an attempt to solve the issue,
i. Whether or not the implementation is new,
j. If there are configuration changes, we would first recommend that these changes been roll back to validate whether the issue still persist

                               

    A description of your issue's impact, using the following definitions:

    P1 - Site Down

               Your network or application is down, or critical business functions have stopped due to the issue.

               P2 - Site at Risk
               Your network or application is severely and negatively impacted by the issue.

               P3 - Performance Severely Degraded
               The performance of your network or application has been severely reduced due to the issue.

               P4 - General Assistance Required
               The subject of the case does not currently impact your network or application.

    The hours that you are available to work on the issue, and any alternative contacts who can work on the issue if you are not available.

3.  Product-specific information

The following product information is required when you open a support case:
For specific instructions about how to send this information to F5 Support, refer to K000090853: Exchange files with F5 Support.


4.  Collect diagnostic data refer to "QKView and iHealth" section in https://clouddocs.f5.com as soon as the issue happen, any delay might result in logs data being rotated. 

5.  Provide packet traces

If the issue involves the network, perform a packet trace while the issue is occurring and provide the packet trace when you open the case.
Provide your troubleshooting/analysis on the PCAPs (identifying specific flow/frames) that clearly indicate problem is with SPK / CNF. 
Provide screenshot/Error message discover.
For more information about performing packet traces with tcpdump on BIG-IP SPK / CNF, refer to K53241425: Overview of the tcpdump utility on BIG-IP SPK or K000132805: Overview of the tcpdump utility on BIG-IP Next CNFs .
