Topic

The Energy Policy Act of 2005 is a statute that was passed by the United States Congress on July 29, 2005, and signed into law on August 8, 2005. Part of the Act extends Daylight Saving Time by approximately four weeks, beginning in 2007. Daylight Saving Time in the United States will begin at 2 a.m. on the second Sunday of March and end at 2 a.m. on the first Sunday of November. In most of the countries of Western Europe, including the countries that are members of the European Union, Daylight Saving Time begins at 1 a.m. GMT on the last Sunday of March and ends at 1 a.m. GMT on the last Sunday of October.

F5 Product Development is tracking this issue as CR58321 for BIG-IP version 4.5.x and 4.6.x and a hotfix has been issued. Customers affected by this issue can contact F5 Networks Technical Support, referencing this Solution number and associated CR number, to request status on the hotfix.

For instructions about how to obtain a hotfix, refer to K167: Downloading software from F5.

Installing the hotfix for BIG-IP 4.5.x and 4.6.x

Note: You can locate the BIG-IP version 4.5.x and 4.6.x hotfixes on the F5 Downloads site by selecting your specific version from the version selection drop-down menu.

Note: The hotfix for CR58321 applies to both 4.5.x and 4.6.x.

Important: Installation of this hotfix requires a system reboot.

  1. Download the hotfix and place it in the /var/tmp directory.

  2. Install the hotfix by typing the following command from the command line:

    im HOTFIX-CR58321-1.0-1-BSD_OS-4.1.im

  3. A warning message will appear indicating that installation of this hotfix requires a reboot of the system. Type the letter y to continue the installation process.

    The system will automatically reboot after the installation is complete.

Verifying that the hotfix is installed

To verify that the hotfix is installed, type the following command at the command line:

im -Q | grep HOTFIX-CR58321-1.0-1

For example:

# im -Q | grep HOTFIX-CR58321-1.0-1HOTFIX-CR58321-1.0-1 Mon Jan 22 5:27:15 2007

The date displayed is the date that the hotfix was installed. There is no change to the build or version number after the hotfix is installed.

Workaround

If an upgrade or hotfix installation is not possible before March 11, 2007, you may manually reset the BIG-IP clock ahead one hour, on March 11, 2007 (the new end of Daylight Saving Time). If you do not upgrade before April 7, 2007, you will also need to reset the BIG-IP clock back one hour on or after April 7, 2007 (the old end of Daylight Saving Time), when the BIG-IP performs an automatic time change.

Important: Manually resetting the time is required regardless of whether the BIG-IP is configured to synchronize time to an NTP server.

For instructions about resetting the BIG-IP clock, refer to K3381: Setting the time and date on BIG-IP.
