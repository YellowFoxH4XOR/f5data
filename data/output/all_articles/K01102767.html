Known Issue

BIG-IP APM portal access may ignore the X-Frame-Options header.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP APM system is configured with portal access.
  • Content processed by portal access includes the X-Frame-Options HTTP header within the following content types:

    • <frame>
    • <embed>
    • <object>

Impact

The X-Frame-Options header value is ignored.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Content rewritten by portal access that includes the X-Frame-Options header does not impact the content returned to the user.
  • The intended benefit of the X-Frame-Options header is not applied to content sent to the user.
