Description

The GUI is not accessible even when the BIG-IP listens in the 443 port and a packet capture shows that the packets arrive at it.

Environment

  • There is a Riverbed accelerator in front of the BIG-IP.
  • The httpd and tomcat processes are running fine.
  • There are no java.lang.OutOfMemoryError entries in the  /var/log/tomcat/catalina.out file.
  • The management IP of the BIG-IP is reachable via ICMP.
  • The management IP of the BIG-IP is listening on port 443.
  • A packet capture gathered on the BIG-IP shows S+* in the three way handshake:
156    43.052837    192.168.1.1    107.162.162.40    TCP            S+*, 2701 → 443 [SYN] Seq=0 Win=5840 Len=0 MSS=1460 SACK_PERM=1 TSval=666505136 TSecr=0 WS=4
157    43.052912    107.162.162.40    192.168.1.1    TCP            443 → 2701 [SYN, ACK] Seq=0 Ack=1 Win=28960 Len=0 MSS=1460 SACK_PERM=1 TSval=4258137261 TSecr=666505136 WS=128
158    43.053013    192.168.1.1    107.162.162.40    TCP            2701 → 443 [ACK] Seq=1 Ack=1 Win=5840 Len=0 TSval=666505136 TSecr=4258137261

Cause

The Riverbed accelerator encapsulated the HTTPS traffic of the management IP and creates a false positive to the clients as the page seems inaccessible.

Recommended Actions

Bypass the BIG-IPs management IP on the Riverbed accelerator.
