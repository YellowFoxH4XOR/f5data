Known Issue

Some custom attribute-value pairs (AVPs) are missed in Command-Control Request Update (CCR-U) messages for non-existent policy and Command-Control Request Termination (CCR-T) messages.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • The BIG-IP system sends CCR-U or CCR-T messages when the specified policy received from the Policy and Charging Rules Function (PCRF) does not exist.

Impact

CCR-U and CCR-T may miss some of the subscriber attributes, such as IMSI or E164.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • CCR-U or CCR-T messages sent in response to a non-existent policy may be missing some of the custom AVPs, such as IMSI and E164, even if the AVPs are marked as mandatory.
