Description
After upgrading the BIG-IP system, the Configuration GUI is not accessible
You see entries similar to the logs below in /var/log/ltm:

Jan  1 12:00:00 bigipA emerg mcpd[8198]: 0107070e:0: Software version not covered by service agreement.  Reactivate license before continuing.
Jan  1 12:00:00 bigipA emerg mcpd[8198]: 01070608:0: License is not operational (expired or digital signature does not match contents).

Environment

  • Recently upgraded BIG-IP

Cause
This can occur if the system is not licensed to allow the new TMOS software version.

Recommended Actions

  1. Ensure that the TMOS version you are attempting to upgrade to is allowed by your license's Service Check Date. To obtain the Service Check Date, follow the procedure at: K7727: License activation may be required before a software upgrade for the BIG-IP or Enterprise Manager system. 
  2. Obtain the License Check Date, for the TMOS version you are upgrading to. The License Check Date can be found in the table, in K7727.
  3. If the Service Check Date in your bigip.license file is earlier than the License Check Date for the software version you are attempting to install, you must reactivate the system's license before upgrading.
