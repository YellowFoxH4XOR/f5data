Known Issue

The iControl SOAP portal may return an HTTP 500 error under heavy load.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You open many transactions using iControl SOAP and REST, which increases the load on the portal.
  • The excess load leads to a large number of open transactions that are never closed, and begin to timeout.

Impact

In rare circumstances, the iControlPortal.cgi process may become unresponsive and restart.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • You receive 500 errors when you attempt to query large configurations using iControl SOAP and REST.
  • The iControl portal becomes sluggish or slow, causing timeouts.
  • The iControl portal becomes completely unresponsive requiring a system reboot.
