K23065544: A large number of connections can cause the BIG-IP AFM to failover

Type
Support Solution
Published
2021-12-28
Updated
2026-07-17
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP AFM: 15.1.3
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K23065544

Description

The BIG-IP can failover after handling a large amount of connections.

  • A failover is noted during high peaks of traffic
  • TMM restarts, the BIG-IP device fails over to its peer

 

Environment

Relevant environmental factors specific to the topic

  • BIG-IP device is provisioned for AFM 
  • AFM is configured with firewall rules that log or report translation fields or server side statistics

 

Cause

  • This is caused by a known issue

 

Recommended Actions

The actions or steps required to address the topic covered in the description.

  1. Disable logging and reporting of server-side information in AFM firewall ACLs.
  2. While in a maintenance window, run the following command after following step 1: 

tmsh restart sys service tmm
