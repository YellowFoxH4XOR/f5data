Known Issue

The Round Trip Time (RTT) pool load balancing method may not load balance optimally.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You have a BIG-IP GTM Wide-IP pool configured to use the RTT load balancing method.

Impact

You may experience uneven load balancing between pool members.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • RTT pool members are load balanced inconsistently, according to the RTT method.
