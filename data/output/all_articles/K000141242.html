Description

A BIG-IP administrator may find that some non-ASCII characters are not in the "Detected Keyword" of an event report, but can be seen in the Parameter.

 

ASM performs an "extra normalization" for signatures that includes removal of high ASCII characters.  Multi-byte UTF8-encoded characters look like sequences of high ASCII characters to ASM's signature engine and are stripped before parameter enforcement takes place.  This can result in confusing false positives for applications that mix UTF-8 with ASCII in parameter values.

 

ID436924 introduced a system varable, dont_norm_high_ascii, to turn of the removal of high ASCII characters during the "extra normalization" phase. This setting is available starting in 11.3.0, but is not enabled by default.

Environment

One of the following modules is provisioned:

  • ASM
  • AFM

Procedure

To turn on the dont_norm_high_ascii setting, ssue the following commands from the CLI:

 

  1. /usr/share/ts/bin/add_del_internal add dont_norm_high_ascii 1
  2. bigstart restart asm

Note: The unit will go offline as ASM restarts.
