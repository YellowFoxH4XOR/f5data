Known Issue

The BIG-IP GTM timeout settings may result in incorrect monitoring, and the system may not correctly mark pool members up or down.

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • You have configured a BIG-IP GTM monitor with a timeout value that is less than the interval.

    For example:

    gtm monitor external /Common/sample-timeout {
        defaults-from /Common/external
        destination *:*
        interval 10
        probe-timeout 22
        run /Common/sample_monitor
        timeout 5
    }

Impact

The BIG-IP GTM system may not recognize that the monitored pool member is down, and leave it marked up, or the system may erroneously mark pool members up and return the members as answers in DNS responses, thus directing clients to unreachable nodes.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The system marks pool members up when they should be marked down, or the other way around.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can configure monitors with a timeout value that is greater than the interval value.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
