Known Issue

The BIG-IP system slows down and eventually reboots when the management port has a Layer 2 (L2) loop.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You are using Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP).
  • The network to which the BIG-IP management port is connected has an L2 loop.

Impact

When you configure the BIG-IP blade as part of a high availability (HA) system, it temporarily fails to process traffic and fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP blade becomes slow and eventually reboots.
  • In the /var/log/kern.log file, you observe error messages similar to the following examples:

    Dec 21 18:15:44 hostname warning kernel: eth0: received packet with own address as source address

    Dec 21 18:15:44 hostname warning kernel: ICMPv6 NA: someone advertises our address fe80:0000:0000:0000:0223:e9ff:fe44:7ec0 on mgmt!

    Dec 21 18:19:49 slot1/hostname emerg kernel: BUG: soft lockup - CPU#1 stuck for 67s! [events/1:36]

Resolution

F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. Workaround

To work around this issue, if possible, avoid L2 loops in the network to which the management port is connected.

Impact of workaround: Performing this workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
