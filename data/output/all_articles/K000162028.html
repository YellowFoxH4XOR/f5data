K000162028: Why am I receiving a 301 with rsp_code_details: direct_response?

What Happened?

  • You received a 301 from XC with rsp_code_details: direct_response and don't know what it means.

Environment

  • F5 XC Console
  • F5 XC HTTP LB

Resolution/Answer

  1. What does rsp_code: 301 with rsp_code_details: direct_response mean?
  • A 301 response code indicates an HTTP redirect (Moved Permanently). The rsp_code_details: direct_response means that the response was generated directly by the load balancer or its configuration, not by the backend/origin server.
  1. Why might this occur if you have no direct response routes configured on the LB?
  • In F5 Distributed Cloud, a "direct response" can be triggered by several mechanisms, not just explicit direct response routes on the LB. For example, HTTP-to-HTTPS redirection, maintenance routes, or certain error handling configurations can cause the LB to generate a direct response.
  • A 301 direct response could be seen due to HTTP-to-HTTPS redirection, even if the explicit "HTTP Redirect to HTTPS" option is not enabled on the pertinent LB. If another LB in your tenant has this option enabled and is advertised on the same IP address as the pertinent LB, then a port 80 listener is opened on that IP address, so any requests destined for port 80 and that IP address will be redirected to HTTPS and the 301 direct response will be triggered.
  • If a direct response route was previously configured on the LB but since was deleted and the deletion has not yet fully propagated through all XC edge nodes, then a 301 redirect response could still be triggered. In this case, it can take some time for the deletion to fully propagate. After that, the 301 redirect responses will no longer occur.
