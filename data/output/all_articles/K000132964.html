Description

You added a DCD to your BIG-IQ cluster and want to know how the replication mechanism works.
Will your data be lost if one DCD crashes?
And, what happens if you add another DCD

Environment

  • BIG-IQ CM
  • BIG-IQ DCD

Cause

Informational

 Recommended Actions

BIG-IQ DCDs creates replica shards which are copied to other DCD's in the cluster. Hence, if one DCD crashes you will still have access to the data.
As you add a DCD to a cluster the amount of storage will increase but an amount of this storage will be allocated to the replica shards from the other DCDs.
