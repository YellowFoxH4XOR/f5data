Important: Specifications are subject to change without notification.

The 4300 hardware platform uses redundant 460 watt 110/220 volt AC auto-switching power supplies. The active power supply has the following characteristics:

  • Typical power consumption: 275 watts
  • Typical heat generated: 939 BTU per hour
  • Worst case power consumption: 460 watts
  • Worst case heat generated: 1570 BTU per hour

The typical numbers are empirically determined. The worst case numbers are based on the rating of the power supply and are used to size air conditioned facilities.
