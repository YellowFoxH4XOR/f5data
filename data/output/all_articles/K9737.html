Topic

The BIG-IP 1600, 3600, 6900 and 8900 platforms utilize sophisticated power-saving features to reduce overall heat production and energy consumption. One such feature temporarily reduces CPU voltage and clock speed when the CPU is idle, and returns the CPU voltage and clock speed to normal levels immediately upon activity.

This power-saving feature causes no measurable performance impact. However, applications that test or report CPU speed may briefly detect the slower speed, and incorrectly report the CPU as operating at a slower speed than specified. While the slower speed may be detectable during testing, the effect is negligible and does not affect the CPU's ability to perform at the specified speed when active.
