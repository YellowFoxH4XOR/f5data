Known Issue

Border Gateway Protocol (BGP) Equal Cost Multipath (ECMP) recursive routes are not properly redistributed to the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM).

This issue occurs when the following condition is met:

  • Dynamic routing is configured with multiple ECMP routes reachable through a recursive nexthop.

Impact

Packets are not routed to all ECMP nexthops.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • ECMP recursive routes are not properly redistributed to TMM, resulting in an incorrect routing table.
