Known Issue

Attempting to install BIG-IP 10.0.x on the BIG-IP 1600 and 3600 platforms may fail.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your are running BIG-IP 11.3.0 or 11.4.0.
  • You attempt to install BIG-IP 10.0.x to an alternate boot location.

Impact

The installation fails.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • After booting into BIG-IP 10.0.x, messages similar to the following may repeat on the console:

    INIT: Id "1" respawning too fast: disabled for 5 minutes

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 425222 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies To box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. For information about releases or hotfixes that resolve this issue, refer to the following table:

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can first install BIG-IP 10.2.x, then boot into 10.2.0, and proceed to install 10.0.x.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.
