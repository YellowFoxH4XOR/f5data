Description

MAC in the packets sent by the BIG-IP not following the definition of the MAC masquerade, so packets are dropped in the Cisco routers APIC.

Environment

  • BIG-IP appliance with ePVA available 
  • MAC Masquerade
  • Per-VLAN MAC Masquerade
  • Cisco APIC

Cause

ePVA and per-VLAN MAC Masquerade are incompatible.

Recommended Actions

  1. Confirm the MAC masquerade is configured properly
cm traffic-group /Common/traffic-group-1 {
          ha-order {
             /Common/<device1_in_traffic-group>
             /Common/<device2_in_traffic-group>
         }
         mac <MAC_masquerade>
         unit-id 1
     }
 
  1. Verify per-VLAN MAC masquerade is configured
(tmos)# list /sys db tm.macmasqaddr_per_vlan

true indicates per-VLAN MAC masquerade is configured 
 
  1. Verify ePVA acceleration is being used
