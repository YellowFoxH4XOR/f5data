Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may incorrectly apply the active bonus value to its HA score.

This issue occurs when the following conditions are met:

  • An HA group is configured on the BIG-IP system.
  • The active BIG-IP system has an HA score of zero.

The active bonus is an amount that the BIG-IP system automatically adds to the overall HA score of the device running an active traffic group. This ensures that the device remains active when its score would otherwise temporarily fall below the score of the device running the standby traffic group. If the overall HA score of the active device is zero, the system should not add the active bonus value to its device score as this may prevent the system from failing over.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may not fail over as expected.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP system may not fail over as expected.
  • The active bonus value is incorrectly applied to the system's HA score.

    For example, the following output indicates that the active bonus value is incorrectly applied to the HA score when the overall score would otherwise be zero:

    # tmsh show /sys ha-group detail

    ------------------------
    Sys::HA Group: group_one
    ------------------------
    State enabled
    Active Bonus 10
    Score 10

    ----------------------------------------
    | Sys::HA Group Pool: group_one:pool_one
    ----------------------------------------
    | Threshold 2
    | Percent Up 0
    | Weight 10
    | Score Contribution 0

Workaround

None. Although, you may be able to temporarily modify the HA scoring strategy to mitigate this issue.

For example, you may not need to use the active bonus setting in situations where the bonus value would be greater than the total weight of the HA group.
