Known Issue

Pool members that reside in multiple pools that are configured with a Diameter health check monitor are marked down (unavailable).

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is configured with two or more pools and each one uses a different Diameter monitor.
  • Some or all of the pool members are not unique to the pool member configuration (the same IP:PORT combination exists in multiple pools).

Impact

The state of a pool member is simultaneously marked up (available) and down in different pools.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When viewing a pool member's state across pools, the pool member is marked up in one pool and down in a different pool.
  • Affected pool members appear unstable where the pool member's availability state periodically changes.

Workaround

To work around this issue, when you have only two pools that are configured with a Diameter health check monitor and share pool members, you can prevent the monitors from running health check probes at the same time. To do so, alter the interval configured for each of the Diameter monitors. For example, configure one of the Diameter monitors with an interval of 28 seconds and the other with an interval of 31 seconds.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
