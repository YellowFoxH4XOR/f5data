Topic

The sod (switch-over daemon) utility monitors the named utility on the 3-DNS Controller. If the named process halts for any reason, sod attempts to restart it. If it is unable to do so after ten successive attempts, it sends an alert message. The sod utility also monitors the sync group process, syncd.

In 3-DNS version 3.0 to 4.0, the switch-over daemon is called sod-named.

In 3-DNS version 4.0 and later, the switch-over daemon is called sod-3dnsd.
