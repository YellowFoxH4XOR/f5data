K000156743: [VELOS] - SNMP monitoring may periodically reports some high error rate.

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-10-01
Updated
2025-10-01
Applies to
  • F5OS
  • F5OS-C: 1.8.0, 1.6.X, 1.6.2, 1.6.1, 1.6.0
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000156743

Description

With monitoring the VELOS platform through SNMP, you may notice some significant spikes, usually attached to the error rate of the network interfaces, appearing from time to time.

The VELOS chassis and the hosted applications seem to be working fine, though.


Environment

  • F5OS-C
  • VELOS
  • SNMP


Cause

This may be due to: Bug ID 1624057: BX110 Port Flapping or interface/connectivity issues


Recommended Actions

Those spikes are not relevant and can be discarded as a cause of a potential difficulty with the interfaces.

Upgrade to a fixed software version.


Additional Information

The snmp.log file may show records like those:

  • (Counter32 ifInErrors.33554445.=3954040833)
  • (Counter32 ifOutErrors.33554445.=3954040833)

A packet trace may show very high values to the affected OID: 1.3.6.1.2.1.2.2.1.14.33554445: 3954040833
