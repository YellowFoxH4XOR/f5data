Known Issue

Symmetric deduplication v3 (SDD v3) may stop working indefinitely after a failover event.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You configure your BIG-IP Application Acceleration Manager (AAM) systems to perform symmetric data deduplication across a WAN link.
  • The local and remote BIG-IP AAM systems are each a member of a high-availability (HA) device group.
  • The BIG-IP AAM systems actively perform symmetric deduplication for applicable traffic using the SDD v3 codec.
  • Both BIG-IP AAM systems on each side of the WAN link concurrently experience a failover event.

    Note: In rare cases, a failover event on only one of the BIG-IP AAM systems on either side of the link may cause this issue.

Impact

The amount of traffic sent over the WAN tunnel increases.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • After a failover event, all client connections that traverse the WAN link reset.
  • You observe increased traffic traversing the link
  • Messages similar to the following appear in the /var/log/ltm file:

    01470000:3: iSession: Connection error: isession_dedup_handshake_error:3477: sdd-v3 sdd-v3 endpoint disconnected:40
    01470000:3: iSession: Connection error: isession_handshake_reply:1966: isession_handshake_reply error:40

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can restart all services on all affected BIG-IP AAM systems in the topology. To do so, perform the following procedure:

Impact of workaround: Performing the following procedure causes a traffic disruption while the services on all BIG-IP AAM systems restart. You should perform this procedure during a maintenance window.

  1. Log in to the BIG-IP AAM Advanced shell (bash).
  2. Restart services by typing the following command:

    bigstart restart

  3. Repeat steps 1 and 2 for the remaining BIG-IP AAM systems in the topology.
