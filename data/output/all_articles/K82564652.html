Issue

Old Behavior

In versions prior to BIG-IP BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) 13.1.0.2 and 12.1.3.3, the Pay As You Go (PAYG) VE images available on the public cloud marketplaces, such as Amazon Web Services (AWS), Azure, and Google, include a unique license registration key that requires license reactivation on the F5 license server. You may be required to reactivate these PAYG licenses when you upgrade from these BIG-IP VE PAYG images.

New Behavior

Beginning in BIG-IP VE 13.1.0.2, 12.1.3.3, and later, the PAYG images available on the public cloud marketplaces include a PAYG license that does not allow you to reactivate your license when you upgrade. The Service Check Date (SCD) check has been disabled and is no longer enforced when you upgrade these images.

Impact

For BIG-IP VE 13.1.0.2, 12.1.3.3, and later, you are not required to update the license when you update the software. When you upgrade from these PAYG VE images, the upgrade process does not enforce the SCD, and will not reactivate the PAYG license. If you attempt to manually reactivate these licenses, your attempt will fail.

Recommended Actions

When you upgrade BIG-IP 13.1.0.2, 12.1.3.3, and later PAYG VE images in a cloud marketplace, you do not need to (nor are you allowed to) reactivate the license before you upgrade. The upgrade process does not check or enforce the SCD.
