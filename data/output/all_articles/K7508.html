Topic

You can use email alerts to monitor the health of a FirePass controller. The FirePass controller sends out email alerts based on specific conditions. The email alerts report the error and its associated severity level.

Note: For more information about configuring email alerts, refer to the FirePass Controller Administrator Guide.

Email alert severity levels

Email alert conditions have two severity levels:

  • Warning

    A condition with a Warning severity level requires administrator attention, but it should not impair the performance of the FirePass controller.
  • Alert

    A condition with an Alert severity level requires immediate administrator attention, and it may impair the performance of the FirePass controller.

Email alert formats

The format for an email alert with a Warning severity level appears similar to the following example:

From: <FirePass email From address>
To: <Administrator email address>
Subject: FirePass WARNING message from <FirePass IP Address>
Date: <Date and Time>
Importance: Normal
<Alert message>

The format for an email alert with an Alert severity level appears similar to the following example:

From: <FirePass email From address>
To: <Administrator email address>
Subject: FirePass ALERT message from <FirePass IP Address>
Date: <Date and Time>
Importance: High
<Alert message>

Note: You can configure the <FirePass email From address> and the <Administrator email address> on the Device Management: Configuration: Admin E-Mail page in the FirePass Administrative Console.
