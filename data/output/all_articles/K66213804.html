K66213804: Misleading message ''Policy Not Applied'' persist after Installing an Attack Signature Update

Type
Support Solution
Published
2022-09-30
Updated
2026-07-16
Status
Legacy Content
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP ASM: 15.1.5
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K66213804

Description

The message "Policy Not Applied" persists after the signature switches from Stage to Block.

This change was applied after an attack signature update installation finished successfully.

Environment

  • After Attack Signature Update
  • System has updated the Signature, however, Apply Policy" not done

Cause

This behavior is due to a known issue ID1137357.

Note: At the time of writing public article regarding ID1137357 is unavailable on Askf5 or Bugtracker.

Recommended Actions

Always apply changes to policy post editing signature enforcement or at least before installing an attack signature update.

Additional Information

None.
