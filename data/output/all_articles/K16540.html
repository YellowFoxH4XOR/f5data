Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) process may experience a nexthop reference count leak.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The TMM processes run over an extended period of time.
  • Internal communication between TMM processes over tmm0 are heavy during this time period.

Impact

Over time, a reference count leaks may cause TMM to crash and restart, causing the BIG-IP system to fail over to another device in the high availability (HA) group.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP system reboots and/or fails over to another device if configured for HA.
  • The TMM process generates a core file in the /shared/core directory.
  • In the /var/log/tmm log file, you observe error messages similar to the following example:

    notice panic: ../net/nexthop.c:149:
    Assertion "nexthop ref valid" failed.notice ** SIGFPE **
