Topic

The B4300 series blades may indicate a variance in the CPU temperature readings between blades in a chassis, and between CPUs in a blade.

Description

When you view the CPU temperature using the tmsh show sys hardware command on a VIPRION chassis with B4300 blades installed, the CPU temperature delta can be significant, as seen in the following example output:

CPU Status

Slot Index Temp(degC) Fan Speed(rpm)

1 1 "29" 6887

1 2 35 6683

2 1 "38" 6818

2 2 31 6750

3 1 "31" 6818

3 2 33 6750

4 1 "42" 6958

4 2 38 6887

The CPU sensor on the B4300 series blades is calibrated at its critical high temperature, and the lower the temperature is from this calibrated temperature, the greater the CPU temperature delta can be. Therefore, this variance can be greater at lower ambient inlet air temperatures.

Recommendation

The CPU temperature reported on the B4300 series hardware should be used only as reference data. F5 expects that the reported CPU temperature will vary between blades, especially at lower/cooler ambient temperatures.
