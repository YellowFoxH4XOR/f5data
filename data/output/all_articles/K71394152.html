Description
By having another VPN service installed alongside the Edge Client will usually cause a conflict.
Edge Client may fail to connect to VPN.

Environment

  • BIG-IP APM
  • BIG-IP Edge Client on Windows
  • Third-party VPN clients (Palo Alto VPN client and Zscaler)

Cause
Multiple VPN clients competing for necessary route table adjustments.
This happens sometimes even when the third-party VPN client is disabled.

Recommended Actions 
It is recommended to disable or un-install the third-party VPN service and see if that corrects the issue.
Sometimes, client OS re-image is the final solution, if un-installation of third-party VPN can't restore to the correct status.

Additional Information
F5 does not perform compatibility testing for third-party Virtual Private Network (VPN) products.
