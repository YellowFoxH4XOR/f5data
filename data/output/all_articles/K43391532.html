Known Issue

A BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) licensed with FIPS 140-3, running in AWS China may fail to start in AWS-China.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP VE is running in AWS China.
  • The system is licensed with FIPS 140-3.

Impact

The system does not start and fails to process traffic.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • You observe that the system is unable to start.
