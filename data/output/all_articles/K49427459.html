Known Issue

If an iRule contains a periodic after command, and within it there is another nested periodic after command whose contents are suspended or parked, the Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) produces a core file.

Note: The term suspended is commonly referred to as parked or parking. For more information, refer to K12962: Some iRule commands temporarily suspend iRule processing.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You use a periodic after command within an iRule.
  • You have a further nested periodic after command.
  • The contents of the periodic after command are suspended or parked.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from the TMM restart, and devices configured as a high availability (HA) pair may fail over.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Your BIG-IP system unexpectedly fails over to another system in the device group.
  • Your BIG-IP system temporarily fails to process traffic.
  • Your BIG-IP system generates a TMM core file to the /shared/core directory.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can avoid using nested periodic after commands which are parked within iRules.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
