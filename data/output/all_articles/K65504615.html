Description
With various issues arising in your organization there may be times where you need to see the latest content being generated from F5 Support.

Environment

  • Increase in issues
  • Gaining access to what content F5 Support Engineers are working on

Cause
Needing to increase your scalability of your deployed F5 products.

Recommended Actions

  1. Open MyF5 (https://my.f5.com/)
  2. Run a blank search
  3. Apply a Content Type filter for Support Solution
  4. Select Date on the right side to sort by most-recent date to least-recent date.

This will now display the latest content generated by F5 Support engineers who work directly with customers to resolve issues create this content. Support Solution articles give you fast access to mitigation, workaround, or troubleshooting suggestions.
