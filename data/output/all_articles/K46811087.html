Known Issue

The BIG-IP system may produce excessive ERR_UNKNOWN messages.

This issue occurs when of the following condition is met:

  • Your BIG-IP system is running under heavy load.

Impact

This error is cosmetic and may be safely ignored.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H566105 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Low screen. 
  • In the /var/log/ltm file, you observe an error message that appears similar to the following example:

    err tmm3[12345]: 016e0002:3: Execution of action 'asm enable policy=/Common/test_policy' failed, error ERR_UNKNOWN
