K03513854: ASM Learning Suggestions (tightening and loosening) and the lightning bolt icon

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-10-24
Updated
2025-04-02
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP ASM
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K03513854

Description
in ASM Policy Learning,  the system will provide suggestions on both "tightening"  the ASM detection policy, or loosening the policy based on the traffic being seen by the learning process.    Tightening suggestions are marked with a Lightning bolt,  Loosening suggestions are unmarked, but contain samples of the traffic that triggered the suggestion. 

Environment

  • ASM Policy applied to a Virtual server
  • ASM policy in "Learning" mode.
  • you have suggestions in the learning and blocking results.

Cause
N/A

Recommended Actions

  1. ASM Policy learning mode is designed to compare the "realtime" traffic for a given policy against the signatures enabled for that policy.   When learning, ASM Policy suggestions will be generated in 2 methods.
    1. ASM will suggest "Tightening" a policy when it sees a sufficient amount of "Good" traffic that does not match the policy.   These could be signatures that were not matched by any traffic, or compliance signatures that should be enabled to conform with known security recommendations.   This ensures signatures that are "unusual" for the Virtual server it's applied to, are enabled and will block bad traffic.   These entries are marked with a lightning bolt, and do not contain any sample data to review.
    2. ASM will suggest "Loosening" a policy when it sees more than 1 instance of a "matched signature" being hit for a policy, but may be valid traffic for that application or resource being protected by the ASM Policy.    These entries are not marked, and will contain samples of the "matched" signature traffic so you can review whether the signature should be enforced, or disabled to allow the traffic
    3. The lightening bolt indicates that the learning suggestion is a policy tightening suggestion, no request samples exist because the relevant traffic did not generate any violation. This is reason why it does not show any suggestion.
    4. Tightening are represented by "blue lightning" in  the WebUI "Security >> Application Security: Policy Building: Traffic Learning"
    5. Tightening suggestions are to make the policy more restrictive.
    6. Another way to read tightening suggestions is "because I haven't  seen any sample in "X" requests, I would suggest you to block them, unless you authorise to allow the requests.
Tightening suggestions are very straight forward,   "We have not seen any traffic of this type, so turning on this signature would be a good idea."   

Loosening suggestions are fairly complex, and require review to determine whether the traffic pattern is something to be allowed, or something that requires changes to the application.   A good recommendation would be to review learning suggestions for your ASM Policy weekly, while the learning function is enabled.
