K000152689: When both management ports are disconnected, one of the controllers does not display the Alarm LED.

Type
Support Solution
Published
2025-07-23
Updated
2025-07-25
Applies to
  • F5OS
  • F5OS-C
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000152689

Description

When both management ports are disconnected, one of the controllers does not display the Alarm LED.

Expected Behavior: Both controllers should have the Alarm LED on when the management ports are down.

Environment

  • F5OS
  • Velos Controller
  • Alarm LED

Cause

When both management ports are physically disconnected simultaneously, the standby controller does not trigger the Alarm LED.

Recommended Actions

When testing the management ports, physically disconnect them one at a time.  Both controllers correctly trigger the Alarm LED.

None
