Known Issue

The Session Initiation Protocol (SIP) monitor marks members down when the monitor and pool members use white space differently from one another in the SIP headers.

This issue occurs when a subset of the following conditions are met:

  • The BIG-IP system sends and receives SIP headers in which the white space differs from one another. For example:

    • BIG-IP sends Call-ID:222abcde@example.f5.com
    • BIG-IP receives Call-ID: 123454321@example.f5.com
  • The BIG-IP system sends and receives headers in which the white space differs from one another. For example:

    • BIG-IP sends Call-ID: 222abcde@example.f5.com
    • BIG-IP receives Call-ID:123454321@example.f5.com

Impact

Pool members are marked down and remain unavailable for load balancing.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • The BIG-IP system's SIP monitor erroneously marks pool members down.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can use a different monitor type (such as UDP) to monitor pool members.

Impact of workaround: Performing the suggested workaround should not have a negative impact on your system.
