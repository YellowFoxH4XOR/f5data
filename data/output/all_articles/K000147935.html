K000147935: Why are the interfaces I defined in my fleet's configuration not created?

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-11-08
Updated
2024-11-08
Applies to
  • F5 Distributed Cloud Services
  • F5 Distributed Cloud
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000147935

What Happened?

  • You have a fleet configured
  • You want to set up a multi-node site network using your fleet

Environment

  • Fleets
  • Customer Edge

Resolution/Answer

  • Fleet just gives you a way to configure networking parameters for your interfaces
  • You can't dynamically add any physical interface to your Customer Edge (CE) until it's part of the certified hardware (HW)
  • Depending on the certified HW, your node may only support a limited number of interfaces
  • You cannot use more than that limit
  • To use more you have to redeploy the site using a certified HW which matches your interface requirement
