Policy Information

F5 is announcing the End of Life (EoL) / End of Technical Support (EoTS) for the F5 Anti-Bot Mobile (ABM) SDK, effective July 1, 2023.

The ABM SDK integration with F5 Advanced WAF had been achieved with an F5 technology partner (Appdome) to fuse Advanced WAF bot defense with mobile applications. However, to exceed customer expectations and continuously address the increasingly complex and evolving threat landscape, F5 is replacing the ABM SDK with F5 Distributed Cloud Bot Defense Standard with Mobile SDK Support. F5 has partnered with Promon to provide code-level integration and added functionality from Promon’s Shield product, which customers have the option to use. Certain customers may be eligible for promotions. Please contact your Account Manager for more information.

With this replacement, F5 addresses the increased volume and sophistication of attacker tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs). F5 Distributed Cloud Bot Defense Standard offers capabilities beyond the ABM SDK, such as advanced fraud detection. By leveraging the F5 Distributed Cloud SaaS platform, customers can leverage new security services from F5 including various current and upcoming integrations for anti-fraud and bot detection and protection.

Technical Support

Customers who replace their F5 ABM SDK with a code-based integration with F5 Bot Defense Mobile SDK can continue under their existing agreement with F5.

Customers who replace their F5 ABM SDK with a code-based integration of the F5 Bot Defense Mobile SDK with F5 Distributed Cloud Bot Defense Standard may do one of the following:

  • Perform the integration themselves
  • Enlist F5 Professional Services support to perform the integration
  • License a new F5 no-code integration (through our F5 technology partner, Promon)
