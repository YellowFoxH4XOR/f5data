Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. The BIG-IP GTM tcp_half_open monitor works by marking the service as up (green) after the monitor receives the SYN-ACK packet from the pool member. If the BIG-IP GTM receives a RESET packet from the pool member, the service should be marked as down (red). Currently, the BIG-IP GTM tcp_half_open monitor does not mark the service as down after receiving a RST packet from the pool member.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR75808 and it was fixed in BIG-IP GTM 9.3 and 9.4.1. For information about upgrading, refer to the BIG-IP GTM Release Notes.
