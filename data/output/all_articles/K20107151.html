Description

This article provides the information about two similar-looking DC plug connectors used with various DC power supply units (PSUs). 

To identify the connectors, refer to the photos and F5 PSU part numbers in the following table. Note that these terminal plug connectors are shipped and utilized with different DC PSUs; however, in the PSU models listed, the connectors are rated the same, so they can be used interchangeably.
