Topic

You should consider using this procedure under any of the following conditions:

  • Your F5 product has stopped working as expected.
  • You need to contact F5 product support.

Description

Prerequisites

You must have a MyF5 account to open F5 support cases.

Note: If you don't already have a MyF5 account, go to the SIGN IN page and select Sign up.

Procedure

  1. Open a web browser and log in to MyF5.
  2. In the upper-left corner, select CASE MANAGEMENT.
  3. Select Create new case.
  4. Under Select a product family, select the product family you want assistance with.
  5. Under Tell us about the issue you are experiencing, enter a short description of your problem in Subject and select the Product and Version you want assistance with.
  6. Select Next.
  7. Depending on the Product selected, you may be required to select the NGINX Plus version, Linux distribution or Cloud provider and provide Hostname if available.
  8. For Assign a priority, select the appropriate option. Review the following table for severity descriptions.
    LevelNameDescription
    Priority 1Site DownSoftware or hardware conditions on your F5 instance are preventing the execution of high level commerce or critical business activities, including remote access to critical applications. The instance will not power up or is not passing traffic to the detriment of business-critical applications.

    Security issue: Critical business impact due to an attack or vulnerability.

    Priority 2Site At RiskSoftware or hardware on your F5 instance is significantly impairing business activities, including remote access to critical applications. The instance status is putting your network or commerce at risk.

    Security issue: Severe business impact due to an attack, vulnerability, compliance, or data at risk.

    Priority 3Performance DegradedSoftware or hardware on your F5 instance is degraded and this could further impact your traffic or business-critical applications.

    Security issue: Potential or partial business impact related to mitigation, audit results or vulnerability.

    Priority 4General AssistanceGeneral questions and troubleshooting of non-critical F5 software or hardware instances. Requests for product functionality or solutions which are not currently part of the solution.

    Security issue: General security related questions and/or concerns which are not related to an immediate need.

  9. For Reason for contact, select the response that most closely matches your reason for contacting F5 Support.
  10. For Description, enter information about the issue.
  11. Under Contact details, select your preferred contact method, time zone, and alternate contact.
  12. Select Submit.

After you successfully open the support case, the system redirects you to the My Open Cases page, where you can view the case status, add comments, and upload collected supporting data as needed.
