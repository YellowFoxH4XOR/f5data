Known Issue

A rare deadlock condition can affect the rebooting of user devices.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your device is running the Windows 7 operating system.
  • The device has BIG-IP Edge Client installed.
  • You attempt to reboot the device.

Impact

User devices may stop responding.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • When attempting to reboot the device, the device sporadically displays a black screen and stops responding.
  • While the device is in the unresponsive state, a manual hard reboot of the device is required for recovery.
  • While the device is in the unresponsive state, the device can be accessed using a remote desktop (if the device was previously configured to allow access).
