Known Issue

The Traffic Management Microkernel (TMM) may produce a core file with the Assertion "sack scoreboard population counts valid" failed error message.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The Multipath TCP (MPTCP) option is enabled in the TCP profile.
  • The Selective NACK option is enabled in the TCP profile.
  • TMM processes retransmitted packets configured for Selective Acknowledgement (SACK).

Note: The MPTCP and Selective NACK options are both disabled in the TCP profile, by default.

Impact

The BIG-IP system may temporarily fail to process traffic as it recovers from TMM restarting. If the BIG-IP system is configured as part of a high availability (HA) group, a device failover may occur.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The BIG-IP system fails over to the standby system if the system is configured for HA.
  • The BIG-IP system generates TMM core files to the /shared/core directory.
  • In the /var/log/tmm file, you observe a message that appears similar to the following example:

    panic: ../modules/hudfilter/mptcp/mptcpsf.c:9232: Assertion "sack scoreboard population counts valid" failed.

  • In the /var/log/ltm file, you observe a message that appears similar to the following example:

    notice sod[7056]: 01140045:5: HA reports tmm NOT ready.

Workaround

To work around this issue, you can disable either the MPTCP option or the Selective NACK option in your TCP profile (do not configure both in the same profile). To do so, perform one of the following procedures:

  • Disabling the MPTCP option in your TCP profile
  • Disabling the Selective NACK option in your TCP profile

Disabling the MPTCP option in your TCP profile

Impact of workaround: Modifying your profile configuration may have additional effects on traffic processing. F5 recommends that you test any such changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.
  2. Navigate to Local Traffic > Profiles > Protocol > TCP.
  3. Click the name of the TCP profile you want to modify.
  4. Clear the check box next to Multipath TCP.
  5. Click Update.

Disabling the Selective NACK option in your TCP profile

Impact of workaround: Modifying your profile configuration may have additional effects on traffic processing. F5 recommends that you test any such changes during a maintenance window with consideration to the possible impact on your specific environment.

  1. Log in to the Configuration utility.
  2. Navigate to Local Traffic > Profiles > Protocol > TCP.
  3. Click the name of the TCP profile you want to modify.
  4. Clear the check box next to Selective NACK.
  5. Click Update.
