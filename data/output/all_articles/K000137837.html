Environment

BIG-IQ 7000 series
BIG-IQ VE

Cause

The difference is because /var and /var/config along with /shared and /shared/ucs_backups are combined in Big-IQ VE rather than separate filesystems. This makes these filesystems need more space when moving from hardware to virtual.
