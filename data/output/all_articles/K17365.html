Known Issue

The sysObjectID OID returns a result of unknown for the BIG-IP platform type.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP platform supports and is provisioned for Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP).
  • You have deployed one or more BIG-IP vCMP guests.
  • Your BIG-IP vCMP guest is configured for Simple Network Management Protocol (SNMP) polling.
  • You perform an SNMP query on the vCMP guest for the sysObjectID OID.

Impact

You are unable to remotely determine the BIG-IP platform type using SNMP.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter the following symptom:

  • When you perform an SNMP query on a vCMP guest for the sysObjectID OID, the BIG-IP system returns a result of unknown instead of bigipVcmpGuest.
