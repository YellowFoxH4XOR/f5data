Known Issue

The BIG-IP APM Endpoint Security (EPSEC) Client-Side antivirus check fails to detect database age (db_time) and database version (db_version) for software products classified as anti-malware.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your BIG-IP system runs BIG-IP 14.1.x through BIG-IP 15.0.0.
  • BIG-IP APM is installed with an EPSEC package version prior to 1.0.0 build 850.0.
  • You  configure your access policy with an EPSEC antivirus check to detect database age or database version.

Impact

Those who meet the requirements of the configured antivirus check fail the check and may fail the access policy evaluation.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • After upgrading the BIG-IP system, users are unable to pass the access policy antivirus check for products classified as anti-malware.
  • You observe log messages similar to the following in the /var/log/apm file where the db_version and db_time variables are not set:

    notice apmd[13884]: 014902f7:5: /Common/example_policy:Common:3e6328a0:/Common/example_policy_act_av_software_check_ag: endpoint software detected (sdk=4.3.669.0,type=av,id=538,vendor_id=<vendor_id>,version=<version>,state=1,name=<software_name>,vendor_name=<vendor_name> ,db_signature=543,db_version=,db_time=,last_scan=1557020806,engine_version=,missing_updates=).
