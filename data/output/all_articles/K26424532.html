Description

How frequently should BIG-IPs be rebooted?

Environment

F5 doesn't have specific recommendations as its products range in different software versions and platforms; appliances, Viprions, vCMP hosts & guests, & VEs. Plus the software has improved these last several years so scheduled reboots are not as critical for uptime, as they once were. 

Cause

None.

Recommended Actions

F5 recommends creating a schedule that works for the customer's environment and encourages keeping the BIG-IPs up to date, on current releases (~1yrs). The reboots then accompany these upgrades. 

Additional Information

The two years is a general guideline. Nowadays, the x.1.0 is the long term release which extends for five years (K8986). This moves to a four year term in 16.1.x. F5 periodically offers point releases (4th element, 16.1.0.x) that include minor improvements and fixes which may provide additional opportunities to reboot the device.
