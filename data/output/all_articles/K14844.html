Policy Information

Product Update

F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of the BIG-IP 11000-FIPS and 11050-FIPS platforms and SSD options, effective January 1, 2014.

Hardware EoL Schedule

With the availability of the BIG IP 10200v FIPS card, F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of the BIG IP 11000 and 11050 FIPS platforms.  In addition, F5 is announcing the End of Sale (EoS) of the 1.2TB and 2.4TB solid state drive options for the 11000 Platform.

Technical Support

F5 maintains generous lifecycle policies that allow customers to enjoy many years of both support and new software releases. Customers whose platforms are designated End of Life (EoL) and who are currently under maintenance contracts will continue to receive technical support until the expiration of the service contract renewal date. Customers are encouraged to upgrade their systems before the Service Contract Renewal date expires.

After the EoSD date, January 1, 2017, F5 will not be able to provide software fixes for any technical issues, and F5 will continue to provide technical support for the BIG IP 11000 FIPS and 11050 FIPS platforms on a best effort basis.
