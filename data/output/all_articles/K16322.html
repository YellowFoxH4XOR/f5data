Known Issue

The VIPRION C4800 chassis power supply may display firmware version 0.0 when running the End-User Diagnostics (EUD) utility.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have a VIPRION C4800 chassis.
  • The chassis power supplies were energized within 15 seconds of each other.
  • You run either of the EUD Sensor or Power Supply tests.

Impact

Users cannot obtain the chassis power supply firmware number.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • BIG-IP iHealth lists Heuristic H514849 on the Diagnostics > Identified > Medium page.
  • The EUD Sensor or Power Supply tests display one or more power supply firmware versions as 0.0, similar to the following example:

    Power Supply 1 Firmware Ver: 2.8
    Power Supply 1 Manufacture: LPCS
    Power Supply 2 Model Num: SP0777XXXXXXZ01A
    Power Supply 2 Serial Num: 3A001083
    Power Supply 2 Firmware Ver: 0.0
    Power Supply 2 Manufacture: LPCS
    Power Supply 3 Model Num: SP0777XXXXXXZ01A
    Power Supply 3 Serial Num: 2J000085
    Power Supply 3 Firmware Ver: 0.0
    Power Supply 3 Manufacture: LPCS
    Power Supply 4 Model Num: SP0777XXXXXXZ01A
    Power Supply 4 Serial Num: 2J000128
    Power Supply 4 Firmware Ver: 0.0
    Power Supply 4 Manufacture: LPCS

Resolution

Status

F5 Product Development has assigned ID 495630 to this issue. F5 has confirmed that this issue exists in the products listed in the Applies to (see versions) box, located in the upper-right corner of this article. Workaround

To work around this issue, you must energize all chassis power supplies simultaneously. You should validate that the VIPRION power source can accommodate the increased power load.
