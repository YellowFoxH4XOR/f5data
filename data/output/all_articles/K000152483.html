Description

When replacing or reseating the fan tray of a VIPRION 2000 series chassis, there is a possibility that the following messages may be logged in /var/log/ltm:

Jun 6 00:36:41 slot2/bigip warning clusterd[7911]: 013a0009:4: Blade 2: blade 3 powered DOWN.

Jun 6 00:36:42 slot2/bigip notice clusterd[7911]: 013a0010:5: Blade 2: blade 3 powered up.

In some cases, the following messages may be logged continuously in /var/log/tmm:

Jun 6 00:49:55 slot2/bigip notice MPI stream: connection to node 127.20.W.Y expired for reason: TCP retransmit timeout (tcp.c:14665)

or

Jun 6 00:50:40 slot3/bigip notice MPI stream: connection to node 127.20.X.Z aborted for reason: TCP RST from remote system (tcp.c:4967)

If this occurs, the backplane MPI connection is broken, and the chassis will not be able to properly process traffic. Depending on configuration and traffic condition, you may also notice an increase in TMM memory usage (in "cmp" subsystem).

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • VIPRION 2000 series chassis with multiple blades installed
  • replacing or reseating the fan tray

Cause

Bug ID 1967109

Recommended Actions

Reboot one or more blades where errors are continuously logged in /var/log/tmm. You may also reboot the whole chassis.
