Description

Datasync Obfuscation is scheduled to run a specific time everyday, however, the activity occurred again on the same day.


Environment

  • Various daemons seen restarting in the ltm logs
  • Activity happening again increased CPU usage


Cause

Due to unrelated event related to the Datasync Obfuscation Activity, various  daemons were restarted.

One of the daemons restarted was datasyncd as seen in the /var/log/ltm logs:

"HA proc_running datasyncd fails action is go offline and down links."

And in the /var/log/datasync/datasyncd.log:

DATASYNCD|NOTICE|Jan 01 12:00:00.000|XXXXX|datasyncd_main.c:XXXX|main: daemon started
DATASYNCD|NOTICE|Jan 01 12:00:00.000|XXXXX|datasyncd_main.c:XXXX|main: daemon started

When datasyncd is restarted, the Datasync Obfuscation tasks restarts as well, hence seeing the activity occurring again.


Recommended Actions

Expected behaviour.
