Known Issue

The bcm56xxd process may produce a core file when you configure a QinQ Virtual Local Area Network (VLAN) with Virtual Clustered Multiprocessing (vCMP) provisioned.

This issue occurs when all of the following conditions are met:

  • vCMP is provisioned on your BIG-IP system.
  • You configure QinQ on any of the configured VLANs.
  • You have restarted your BIG-IP system.

Impact

The BIG-IP device goes offline.

Symptoms

As a result of this issue, you may encounter one or more of the following symptoms:

  • The bcm56xxd process repeatedly stops responding.
  • Your BIG-IP system goes offline.
