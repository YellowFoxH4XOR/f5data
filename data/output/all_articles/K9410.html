Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. If a VIPRION chassis is operating using DC power supplies and all blades are removed, one or both of the DC power supplies may turn off. The power supply will remain off until at least one blade is installed and the affected power supply is reset.

F5 Product Development tracked this issue as CR100545 and a new revision of the power supply has been released.

Note: This behavior does not indicate that the power supply is faulty; it is an unintended behavior when the power supply does not detect any blades installed in the chassis. The power supply still functions normally and supplies the necessary power. The newly-revised power supplies will remain on when no blades are installed in the chassis.

To reset the offline power supply, perform the following procedure:

  1. Install at least one blade into the chassis.
  2. Remove the affected DC power supply.

    Wait a few seconds to allow the system to acknowledge that only one DC power supply is installed.

  3. Reinsert the DC power supply.
  4. Verify that one or both of the DC power supplies Power LEDs are lit.
