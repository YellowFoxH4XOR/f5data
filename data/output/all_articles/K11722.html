Known Issue

This is the result of a known issue. FirePass Virtual Edition (VE) is not currently supported in a VMWare ESX environment that utilizes an NFS datastore. Running FirePass VE in this configuration will result in system crashes and data corruption.

Other datastore platforms, including iSCSI and local storage, are not affected.

F5 Networks Product Development is tracking this issue as CR140161.

Note: For information about installing and configuring FirePass VE, refer to the FirePass VE product documentation.
