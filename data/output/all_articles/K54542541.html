Policy Information

F5 does not perform compatibility testing for third-party Virtual Private Network (VPN) products. F5 will support investigations into situations where a third-party VPN product interferes with the desired operation of F5 BIG-IP Edge Client or F5 browser-based VPN access.

To perform such an investigation, F5 requires customers to provide sufficient data, materials, equipment, and/or remote access to the customer's lab (with test accounts) that allow F5 Engineering, Product Support, and Software Development teams to reproduce the observed issue in the lab environment.

If the customer suspects that the F5 client is causing undesired operation of a third-party VPN product, the customer is required to first engage and troubleshoot with the vendor of the third-party VPN product. Customers can use the normal F5 Technical Support process to ask technical questions about F5 VPN client operation.

Recommendations

When opening an F5 Technical Support service request, the request must include the contact information of the third-party vendor engineers that are familiar with the third-party VPN product operations and are actively involved with diagnosing the issue. This information allows F5 Technical Support to gain a clear understanding of the issue and the diagnostic steps taken to identify the issue.
