Description

About MyF5 accounts

You may have access to multiple MyF5 accounts, and each one may have unique subscriptions, products, product trials, and support cases. Your MyF5 homepage provides a summary of the subscriptions, product trials, open cases, and product information related to your MyF5 accounts.

The beginning of the following video demonstrates how to view a MyF5 account.

Prerequisites

You must meet the following prerequisite to use this procedure:

  • Make sure your account has at least one Admin user. If your account does not yet have an Admin user, contact your F5 account team to assign an account user the Admin role.

    For more information about the MyF5 account user roles, refer to K000136095: About MyF5 account user roles and permissions .

Procedures

View your MyF5 accounts

You must have the Basic role to perform this procedure.

  1. In the upper right corner of the MyF5 homepage, select your profile name, and select My Accounts.
  2. To view details about a specific account, select the account name.

If you want to change your name, job title, or phone number in your account, contact F5 Support using chat on MyF5 or call F5 Support .

Note: You must sign in to Myf5 to use chat.

View the active subscriptions associated with your MyF5 accounts

You must have the Viewer role to perform this procedure.

  1. On the MyF5 homepage, under Subscriptions, select the Subscriptions link.
  2. To see subscriptions for only a specific account, type the name of the account in the search bar, and only subscriptions for that account are listed.

View the active trials associated with your MyF5 accounts

You must have the Basic role to perform this procedure.

  1. On the MyF5 homepage, under Trial, select the Trials link.
  2. To see trials for only a specific account, from the Account box, select the MyF5 account for which you want to view active trials and trial offers.

View the status of support cases associated with your MyF5 accounts

You must have the Basic role to perform this procedure.

  1. On the MyF5 homepage, under Open Cases, select the Cases link.
  2. To see support cases for only a specific account, type the name of the account in the search bar, and only cases for that account appear.

View product information associated with your MyF5 accounts

You must have the Viewer role to perform this procedure.

  1. On the MyF5 homepage, select My Products & Plans, and select Product Usage.
  2. To see product information for only a specific account, type the name of the account in the search bar, and only product information for that account appears.
