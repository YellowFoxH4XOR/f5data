K41496100: Clearing the LCD remotely when lcdwarn command does not work

Type
Support Solution
Published
2020-11-03
Updated
2023-02-21
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K41496100

Description

   K11094 shows how to clean LCD message with "lcdwarn" command. However, sometimes messages still remains on LCD after lcdwarn execution.

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • LCD messages not clearing

Cause

   "fpdd", the front panel display daemon keep messages on LCD.

Recommended Actions

  1. From command line,
bigstart restart fpdd

Additional Information

   fpdd
   The front panel display daemon draws screens on the LCD panel and manages LEDs on all platforms.
   Restarting fpdd should not have a negative impact on system.
