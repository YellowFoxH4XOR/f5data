Description

Before software upgrade, it's good practise to reactivate license. But even after reactivation, the license doesn't get updated. Though license reactivation is successful, the "Service check date" doesn't get updated.

Environment

  • BIG-IP LTM and other modules
  • License reactivation

Cause

This happens if the device is out of entitlement (contract/warranty).

Note: The "Service check date" in the license, is date when the device's license was last activated/reactivated or the date the service contract of the device expires, whichever date is earlier.

Recommended Actions

Stop the software upgrade because without the "Service check date" getting updated, the configuration may not load post software upgrade. To get this fixed, reach out to the F5 accounts/sales team and get an entitlement for the device.
