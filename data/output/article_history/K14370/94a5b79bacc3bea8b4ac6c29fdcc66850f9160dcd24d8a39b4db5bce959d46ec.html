Topic

This article documents program-specific procedures required to complete a Trade-in return. Perform these procedures only if you are directed to this article as part of the return process for one of the following programs:

  • Technology Refresh
  • Competitive Replacement

For all other returns, or replacement of potentially defective hardware outside of these programs, refer to K12882: Overview of the F5 RMA process.

Return the hardware within 90 days.

Return instructions

  1. Retain all packaging materials to reuse as return packaging.
  2. Carefully place the parts in the original electrostatic bag and packaging, making sure to package each part separately as they were shipped to you. Do not consolidate parts into fewer boxes. Each individual unit or part of a trade-in will be monitored by the F5 returns management group. Any damages sustained during shipping as a result of insufficient packaging will be invoiced at full list price.
