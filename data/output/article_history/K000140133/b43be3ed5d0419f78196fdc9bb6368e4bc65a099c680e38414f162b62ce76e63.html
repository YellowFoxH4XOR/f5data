Description

[F5OS]Tenant failover when controller failover


Environment

F5OS

Controller

Tenant

Failover

Cause

The self ip added under failover unicast address on tenant is not matching on both sides.

Recommended Actions

Update the unicast address on both tenant to match the same network/vlan and make sure they are reachable to reach other

None
