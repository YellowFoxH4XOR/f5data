Topic

Use this article when you want to learn how to leave feedback for MyF5 articles or the MyF5 site in general.

Description

F5 wants to create content to help you self-serve, and we encourage feedback to help us make your MyF5 experience better and easier. We have two main ways for customers to provide feedback:

  • Article-specific feedback
  • Site feedback

Note: If you encounter any difficulty with using either feedback method, you can instead send your feedback to MyF5@F5.com.

Article-specific feedback

To watch a video demo of this procedure, go to Leave feedback on MyF5 articles.

Leave feedback about an article on MyF5

  1. At the bottom of a MyF5 article, for Was this information helpful?, select Yes or No.
  2. For How can we improve this content?, enter your recommendations or other comments about the article.
  3. For May we contact you directly regarding this feedback?, select Yes or No.
  4. If you selected Yes in the previous step, for Email, enter an email address where F5 can contact you regarding your feedback.
  5. Select Submit.

Site feedback

Leave feedback about your MyF5 site experience

  1. Select the Site Feedback tab, which appears on the side of every MyF5 page.
  2. For What was your primary goal by visiting MyF5 today?, enter your answer.
  3. For How easy was it to achieve your goal today with MyF5?, enter your answer on a range from Very easy to Very difficult.
  4. Select Submit.
  5. For How might we improve? enter your recommendations or other comments about the site.
  6. You have the option to enter an email address where F5 can contact you regarding your feedback.
  7. Select Submit.
