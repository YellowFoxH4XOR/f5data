K000164155: Mirrored connections are not removed from standby device

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-11-09
Updated
2026-11-09
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000164155

Description

After removing LTM vlan and virtual server config with mirroring from the active BIG-IP, the Standby BIG-IP still shows mirrored connections in its connection table.

 

Environment

LTM HA pair with connection mirroring enabled


Cause

When vlan config is deleted the connection cleanup of existing mirrored connections on standby can fail.


Answer/Recommended Actions

After removing mirroring configuration, remove mirrored connections on standby with delete sys conn, tmm restart, or reboot.
