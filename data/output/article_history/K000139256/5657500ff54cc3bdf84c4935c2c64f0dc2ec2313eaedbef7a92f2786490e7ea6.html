K000139256: Scheduled report is empty

Type
Support Solution
Published
2024-04-11
Updated
2024-04-11
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP ASM: 15.1.10
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000139256

Description

The scheduled reports from Bigip are sent to the configured emails, but the content of the report is empty.

Environment

  • Scheduled reports
  • Bigip, Bigiq

Cause

When Bigip is managed by Bigiq the statistics are stored in Bigiq thus the reports on Bigip appear to be empty.


Recommended Actions

Follow the solution of article K82850143.
