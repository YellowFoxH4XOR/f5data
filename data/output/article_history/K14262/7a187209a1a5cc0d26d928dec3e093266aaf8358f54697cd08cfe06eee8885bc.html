Topic

A license key is required to enable the functionality of F5 products. The licensing mechanism is composed of a base registration key that is created to enable features of the F5 product. The base registration key is installed on the F5 product when the product is purchased and used in combination with unique attributes of the F5 product to generate a dossier, which is submitted to the F5 licensing server. The F5 licensing server then generates a license, which the F5 product uses to enable the purchased functionality of the F5 product.

Recommendations

When the BIG-IP system encounters a license issue after a host or hardware migration, contact F5 Support to receive authorization to move the license to the new hardware or BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) platform.

Note: You cannot use a BIG-IP hardware license on a BIG-IP VE or vice versa.
