K000135165: Time difference between GTM "Server Name" and me is 11 seconds

Type
Support Solution
Published
2023-06-22
Updated
2025-02-07
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 14.1.4
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000135165

Description

GTM system report the log similar to the following:


Time difference between GTM "Server Name" and me is 11 seconds.


Environment

GTM iQuery mesh.


Cause

Eventlib timer and system clock are out of sync.


Recommended Actions

Restart gtmd daemon

Reboot the device
