Description

The F5OS management webUI may be unavailable when the HTTPS service rejects the configured management certificate because its RSA key is too small.

In the reported incident, this occurred after an r5800 system was upgraded to F5OS 2.0.0 HR2. The configured certificate contained a 1024-bit RSA public key. Tenants were reported to remain operational.

Environment

- F5OS 2.0.0 HR2

- rSeries r5800 (but applicable to all F5OS hardware)

- Management certificate containing a 1024-bit RSA public key

Cause

The HTTPS TLS library rejects the configured 1024-bit RSA management certificate as too small, preventing successful management HTTPS certificate initialization.

Answer/Recommended Actions

1. From the F5OS CLI, inspect the management certificate: 

  #show system aaa tls state certificate

2. Confirm both the 1024-bit RSA public key and the `SSL_CTX_use_certificate:ee key too small` error. This article addresses that combination, not every certificate-related webUI failure.

3. Replace the management certificate/key pair with a supported certificate using a 2048-bit RSA key. Regeneration with a 2048-bit key resolved the incident recorded in the source case. Use the certificate-generation or installation procedure documented for your F5OS release.

4. After replacement, verify the certificate key size, management webUI access, and whether the same HTTPS initialization error recurs.
