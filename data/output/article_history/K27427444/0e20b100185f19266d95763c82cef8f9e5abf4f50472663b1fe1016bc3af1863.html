Description

You can log in to the VELOS system command-line interface (CLI) to view the cluster status of the entire system. You can view chassis-level information and partition-level information while you are logged in at the chassis level.

Note: If you want to view the status of deployed tenants, you must log in at the partition level.

  • System controllers: Their status, the time they joined/created the cluster, and role(s) in the cluster
  • Installed blades: Their status, the time they joined the cluster, their roles, their available CPUs, memory, and number of pods
  • Partition IDs and their statuses
  • Blade-to partition mapping from the blade perspective

Procedures

  • View cluster status at the chassis level
  • View partition information (status and ID)
  • View blade-to-partition mapping
  • View the status of deployed tenants

View cluster status at the chassis level

To view the cluster status of the system at the chassis level, perform the following procedure:

Impact of procedure: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the CLI of the management address of the system controller as the chassis administrator.
  2. View the status of the system at the chassis level by entering the following command:

    show cluster

View partition information (status and ID)

To view the partition status and ID, perform the following procedure:

Impact of procedure: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the CLI of the management address of the system controller as the chassis administrator.
  2. View the partition status and IDs by entering the following command:

    show partitions

    The CLI returns output similar to the following example:

View blade-to-partition mapping

To view blade-to-partition matching, perform the following procedure:

Impact of procedure: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the CLI of the management address of the system controller as the chassis administrator.
  2. View the blade-to-partition mapping from the blade perspective by entering the following command:

    show running-config slots

    The following example CLI output displays that blade 1 is assigned to PartitionA while blade 2 is assigned to PartitionB:

View the status of deployed tenants

To view the status of deployed tenants for a specific partition, perform the following procedure:

Impact of procedure: Performing the following procedure should not have a negative impact on your system.

  1. Log in to the CLI of the management address of the desired partition as the partition administrator.
  2. View the status of all deployed tenants by entering the following command:

    show tenants
