F5 continuously posts new and updated documents on MyF5. To receive notifications for the most current information, you can subscribe to the following mailing lists, which provide updated information about F5 products and services. Details for each option are provided on the preferences pages.

  • F5 Application Delivery and Security Platform (ADSP) Content, News, and Events
  • F5 AI Content, News, and Events
  • F5 BIG-IP Content, News, and Events
  • F5 Distributed Cloud Services (XC Services) Content, News, and Events
  • F5 NGINX Content, News, and Events
  • F5 Labs Newsletter
  • F5 Labs Weekly Threat Bulletin
  • TechNews
  • Other F5 Communications
  • Promotional Sales Communications

To sign up or modify your settings, go to the F5 Preference Center and select I want to change my marketing email preferences or I want to manage my customer security announcements.

  1. Select your preferred language.
  2. Enter your contact information.
  3. Select the check box(es) for the communications you want to receive.
  4. Select Submit or Update.

You can unsubscribe at any time by returning to this page and updating your preferences, or you can select the Unsubscribe link provided in the footer of each F5 email.
