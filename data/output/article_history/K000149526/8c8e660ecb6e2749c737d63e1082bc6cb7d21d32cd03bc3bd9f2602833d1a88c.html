Description

After purchasing the new subscription it does not show on MyF5

Environment

  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IQ
  • MyF5 Subscriptions
  • Licensing

Cause

The Subscription is not linked to MyF5 account or the role is not added to the profile

Recommended Actions 

Contact your administrator or F5 Support to link the Subscription to MyF5 user profile.

To view the Subscription you need at least viewer role.
