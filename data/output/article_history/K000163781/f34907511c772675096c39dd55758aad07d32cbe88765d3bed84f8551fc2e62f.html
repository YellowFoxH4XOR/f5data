K000163781: F5OS-A does not support changing native management SSH or HTTP/HTTPS listening ports

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-10-20
Updated
2026-10-20
Applies to
  • F5OS Products
  • F5OS-A: 1.8.X, 1.7.X, 1.5.X, 1.8.4, 1.8.3, 1.8.0, 1.7.0, 1.5.4, 1.5.3, 1.5.2, 1.5.1, 1.5.0
Source

Description

F5OS-A does not support changing the default listening ports for management SSH or HTTPS. Custom ports such as 1822 for SSH or 18443 for HTTPS cannot be configured directly in F5OS-A.

Environment

  • F5OS-A

  • Management access requires non-default external ports.

Cause

F5OS-A does not provide a configuration equivalent to BIG-IP TMOS for assigning custom management listening ports.

Recommended Actions

Use the native F5OS-A management ports:

  • SSH: 22

  • HTTPS/WebUI: 443

  • RESTCONF: 8888

  • VCONSOLE: 7001

  • SNMP: 161

To provide external access through ports such as 1822 or 18443, configure port translation on an upstream firewall, NAT device, or other network component. Forward the translated connections to the corresponding F5OS-A management ports.

Do not use BIG-IP TMOS tmsh commands to change F5OS-A management ports.

Additional Information

F5OS-A management-port configuration is separate from BIG-IP TMOS management-port configuration.
