Description

Performing live migration of a BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE) from one hypervisor host to another is supported with certain conditions described as follow.

Environment/Conditions

  • Live migration of BIG-IP VE should be done as HA standby when VE is powered off.
  • F5 do not support live migration of VE as HA active when VE is powered on.
  • This limitation applies to all hypervisor software and to all public cloud services.
  • This limitation applies to BIG-IP VE running on public cloud services Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Oracle Cloud, Amazon Web Services. It does not apply to vCMP Guest and F5OS Tenant, for which there is no concept of Live Migration.
 

Impact

When the live migration is performed outside of the conditions above, the BIG-IP VE may be unable to process network traffic requiring a reboot.

 

Recommended Actions

In case the live migration is performed outside of the conditions above, workaround is introduced as follow

  1. Perform failover to VE instance running on another host.
  2. Shut down the previously active BIG-IP VE.
  3. Migrate it to the new host.
  4. Bring it back up.
