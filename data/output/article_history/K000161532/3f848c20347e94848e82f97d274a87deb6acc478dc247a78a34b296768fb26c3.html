K000161532: rSeries BIG-IP tenant stuck in Pending with "Tenant deployment will be processed when the crypto devices are available"

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-09-17
Updated
2026-10-01
Applies to
  • F5OS Products
  • F5OS-C: 1.8.1
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000161532

Description

On an F5OS rSeries appliance, a BIG-IP tenant remains stuck in the Pending state and fails to start after an appliance or tenant crash/recovery event. The tenant deployment status reports the following message:

Tenant deployment will be processed when the crypto devices are available.

Environment

  • F5OS-A (rSeries)
  • BIG-IP tenant configured with SSL/crypto (QAT) acceleration
  • System recovery following an unexpected tenant or host reboot/crash

Cause

Following a crash and recovery event, QuickAssist Technology (QAT) crypto devices can bind to an incorrect driver. This prevents F5OS from allocating crypto resources to the tenant, leaving the tenant deployment stuck in Pending.

Recommended Actions

  1. Log in to the F5OS CLI as an administrator.
  2. Reboot the appliance to reload the correct QAT driver bindings:

    system reboot

  3. After the system boots up, verify that the tenant transitions out of Pending and starts:

    show tenants

    show tenants tenant <tenant_name>

Additional Information

  • A tenant may also remain in Pending due to insufficient CPU/memory resources, software version mismatches, SELinux/KubeVirt faults, or unloaded QAT plugins after an upgrade.
  • If you require root-cause analysis (RCA) for the preceding crash, generate and collect qkview files from both the F5OS base OS (host) and the BIG-IP tenant before rebooting, if possible.
