Policy Information

This policy is the F5 response and recommendation to customers and their insurance providers when an insurance provider inquires about a Repair Option for units that have been damaged at the customer’s site. F5 understands that insurance providers occasionally offer or inquire about paid recertification of units, on behalf of the customer.

Response

F5 does not offer or accept recertification of units as a means to mitigate failure or damage. Environmental factors, such as water, dust, sea salt, or other elements within a data center, if mitigated after the fact, can still cause future intermittent problems to any number of parts and components throughout the system. F5 will not take the risk of future issues occurring, nor does F5 recommend that customers put their own network at risk.

This policy is in conjunction with the F5 Maintenance Terms and Conditions agreement, which customers receive when they purchase units from F5. For more information, see Section 4 of Maintenance Terms and Conditions.

Recommendation

Customers can contact their Sales or Account Representative, and inquire about purchasing a new unit, with a discount given, where appropriate.
