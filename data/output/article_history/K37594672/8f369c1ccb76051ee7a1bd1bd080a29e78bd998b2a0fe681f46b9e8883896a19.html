Policy Information

F5 Support may use remote-access and screen-sharing tools (Microsoft Teams, for example) to access a customer's system to gain a better understanding of issues affecting their specific product instance.

F5 Support accesses a customer's system only after receiving permission from the customer. When accessing the system, the F5 Support representative does not take control of the remote access session but instead guides the customer through running commands, gathering data, and making configuration changes or other actions as may be necessary to resolve the customer's issue. The F5 Support representative may guide the customer in proper use of syntax and commands but does not run them.

If F5 remote access is not possible, identifying and resolving an outstanding incident may take significantly longer.

For additional information, refer to F5 Maintenance Terms and Conditions.
