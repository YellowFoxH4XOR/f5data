Topic

This article documents the step-by-step procedure for returning defective units under a Return Materials Authorization (RMA) event.

If you are returning your unit from another country than the United States, refer to K12883: F5 RMA Return readme instructions, by country, and select the country name.

Description

Return instructions

Return the defective unit within 10 business days after receipt of your replacement unit. To do so, perform the following procedure:

  1. Retain all F5 original packaging materials to reuse as return packaging.
  2. Carefully place the parts in the electrostatic bag and packaging, making sure to package each part separately, as they were shipped to you. Do not consolidate parts into fewer boxes. F5 will monitor each returned unit. Any damages sustained during shipping as a result of insufficient packaging will be invoiced at full list price.
  3. Locate the prepaid shipping label and place it on the outside of each shipping container. If you cannot locate the return label, request a new one by contacting gslr@F5.com.
  4. Clearly write the following information on the outside of the shipping containers:
    • RMA work order number
    • Serial number

    Write the RMA work order number on the F5-provided 3x5-inch RMA sticker and affix it to the outside of the shipping container (if possible). Shipments that do not have an F5-authorized RMA work order number will be refused at time of delivery and returned to the shipper using collect freight.

  5. Schedule a pickup by calling your local FedEx office and requesting a package return pickup.
  6. Include the following information on the FedEx shipping documentation:
    • Shipment option:
      • US Domestic: Ground
      • International: Express Saver
    • RMA work order number
    • Serial number
    • Return shipping address:

      RMA Dept
      F5 C/O Shyft, Inc
      9000 Foothills Blvd
      Dock Door 19
      Roseville, CA 95747
      Tel: (206) 272-5555
       

  7. Keep a record of the tracking number and ship date; you will need these to track lost shipments. Failure to provide F5 with adequate proof of return for the suspect unit will result in an invoice at full list price for the suspect unit.

If you have questions or concerns regarding your return, contact gslr@f5.com.
