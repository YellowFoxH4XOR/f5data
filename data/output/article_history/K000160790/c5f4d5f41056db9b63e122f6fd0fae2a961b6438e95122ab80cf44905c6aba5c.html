Overview

NGINX support is now available on F5 iHealth, making it easier to understand the health and configuration of your NGINX products in one centralized place. By uploading a support package, you can quickly access key insights about your deployment without manually digging through logs or system files.

This experience is designed to help you:

  • Diagnose issues faster.
  • Easily validate entitlement and product status.
  • Share diagnostic data with F5 Support.

After you upload your support package from NGINX Plus or NGINX Ingress Controller, iHealth generates product-specific insights that enable you to:

  • View product status and entitlements.
  • Access quick links to relevant resources.
  • Review supported commands.
  • Browse configuration and diagnostic files.
  • Search logs for faster troubleshooting.

Use iHealth to analyze NGINX data

To analyze NGINX data in iHealth, you must first upload a support package generated from your NGINX environment. The support package securely captures configuration, runtime, and diagnostic data that iHealth can process and display.

Upload a support package to iHealth

After you obtain the support package, you can upload the file to iHealth to diagnose the health and proper operation of your NGINX system. To do so, perform the following procedure:

  1. Open a web browser and log in to the F5 iHealth site.
  2. Select Upload.
  3. Select Choose.
  4. Go to the support package on your local system.
  5. Select Upload QKView(s).
