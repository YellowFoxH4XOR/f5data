Description

If you work for an organization that owns F5 products, your organization already may have a MyF5 account. If you want to open support cases for your organization's F5 products, you may want to add yourself to an existing account for your organization or F5 product instead of creating a new account.

One reason to add yourself to an existing account is that you can open cases using that account. Then, all of your organization's or product's cases are under one account, where they are easy to reference and track. 

You also may want to see other support cases in the existing account. By default, you can only see the cases you create; however, you may want to change your account user role so that you can see other cases within the existing account. For more information, refer to K000136095: About MyF5 account user roles and permissions.

Finally, F5 recommends that you avoid creating unnecessary accounts. When you create a new account, you may later join an existing account and abandon the one you created. Abandoned accounts linger on MyF5 and potentially create confusion for you, your organization, and F5.

About adding yourself to an existing account

You can add yourself to an existing account by entering the serial number or registration key for an F5 product in the Serial number or registration key field when you are registering. When you do so, MyF5 automatically adds your contact details to the account associated with that product and assigns you the Basic account user role.

Also, MyF5 accounts can have one or more users who are Admins. A user with the Admin account user role can take many actions on a MyF5 account, including adding new users to the account. Not all MyF5 accounts have an Admin, though F5 recommends that they do. You can check within your organization to see if there is an existing account with an Admin who can add you to it.

Prerequisites

You must meet the following prerequisite to use this procedure:

  • You must have the serial number or registration key for an F5 product available when you register for a MyF5 account.

Procedure

Note that registering for an F5 account and a MyF5 account are the same process, except that MyF5 has an additional page with questions unique to MyF5. Therefore, you may see the terms F5 account and MyF5 account used interchangeably in other articles. 

  1. To register for a MyF5 account, go to account.f5.com/myf5. 
  2. Optional: Go to K000089224: Register for an F5 account for step-by-step assistance.
  3. On the Account Information page, in Serial number or registration key, enter the number or key for your F5 product.
