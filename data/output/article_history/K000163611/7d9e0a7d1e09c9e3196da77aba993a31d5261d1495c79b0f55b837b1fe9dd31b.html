What Happened?

  • How to set up a security passphrase to authenticate authorized account users.

Environment

  • F5®Distributed Cloud (XC) 

Resolution/Answer

 

Customer Responsibility:
Customers are responsible for creating, updating, and maintaining their account security passphrase using the XC console. 

 

Security Passphrase Setup:
Login to your account.

Navigate to: Home > Administration > Tenant Settings > Incident Procedures > Edit > Type your account passphrase > Save changes

 

Account Passphrase Example:
Passphrase Example: Secret123
