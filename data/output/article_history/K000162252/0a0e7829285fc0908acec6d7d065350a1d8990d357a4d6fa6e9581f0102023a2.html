Security Advisory Description

On July 15, 2026, F5 released Hardened Release 1 (HR1). F5 urges you to deploy these releases as quickly as you are able. For more information about hardened releases, please refer to A faster release cadence: What's changing at F5, and what you need to do.

On August 13, 2026, F5 announced a change to the frequency of hardened releases. F5 will deliver hardened releases on a six-week cadence instead of monthly. For more information, please refer to Our first hardened release cycle: What we learned and how we’re changing.

F5 is pausing regular security notifications. For information about the next scheduled security notification, refer to K12201527: Overview of F5 security notifications.

To download the latest hardened release for your F5 product, go to Downloads on MyF5, sign in, then select your product group, product line, and product version. For more information, refer to K000090258: Download F5 products from MyF5.
