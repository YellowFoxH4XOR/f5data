Description

After replacing or renewing the device certificate, the iQuery connection continues to use the old certificate.

Environment

  • BIG-IP GTM
  • Replacing device certificate used by GUI and iQuery (System > Certificate Management > Device Certificate Management > Device Certificate)
  • iQuery connection unknown

Cause

There are two possible reasons for such an issue. The certificate used by the BIG-IP device has not been replaced adequately, or gtmd and big3d services have not been restarted (so the new certificate may kick in).

Recommended Actions

Impact of procedure: Performing the following procedure briefly halts iQuery communication with this DNS sync group peer.

  1. Restart big3d and gtmd services 

    bigstart restart big3d gtmd
     
  2. If above step does not resolve the problem, please refer to the DNS sync group peer section of the below article and make sure that all the steps were applied :
    K16951115: Changing the BIG-IP system device certificate using the Configuration utility
