K000163658: Incorrect Country selected during MyF5 account registration

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-10-12
Updated
2026-10-12
Applies to
  • BIG-IP
  • BIG-IP LTM: 17.1.1
Source
https://my.f5.com/manage/s/article/K000163658

Description

If you accidentally selected the wrong country while registering for a MyF5 account, contact F5 Support to request a correction.

The country associated with your profile may affect access to certain F5 services and features.

 

Environment

  • MyF5 portal
  • Account registration

 

 

Cause

The incorrect country was selected during MyF5 account registration.

 

 

Recommended Actions

Contact F5 Support and provide the following information:

    • Email address associated with your MyF5 account
    • First and last name
    • Company name
    • Country currently shown on the account
    • Correct country

    F5 Support will review your request and assist with correcting the country associated with your MyF5 profile.
