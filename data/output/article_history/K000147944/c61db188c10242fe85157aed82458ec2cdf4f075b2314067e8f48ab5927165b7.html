Description

  • You need to reach F5® Distributed Cloud Support but are not sure how to.
  • You want to call the F5® Distributed Cloud SOC for an urgent issue.
  • You need to escalate a case but don't know the process of contacting support.

Environment

  • F5® Distributed Cloud

Answer/Recommended Actions

There are three ways to reach F5 Distributed Cloud Support

  1. Preferred option: Customer portal available at F5® Distributed Cloud Console
  2. Email to support@cloud.f5.com / japan-support@f5.com
  3. Phone

It is recommended to create a ticket using the customer portal but, if the issue is urgent, call us to get immediate assistance. 

Before you submit your ticket

  • Have you searched our Knowledge Base for your issue or question? Distributed Cloud Support writes new articles continuously, based on real questions and issues from our customers.
  • Have you reviewed what to include in the ticket? See section below. Including this information helps our Distributed Cloud support engineers to troubleshoot your issue quickly.

What to include in your ticket

  1. Business Impact
  2. Which F5 Distributed Cloud Service is impacted? (e.g. HTTP Load Balancer, Customer Edge, service policy, k8s, etc.)
  3. What is the namespace?
  4. Description of problem and symptoms (e.g. screenshot of the error message, HAR file)
  5. Logs, packet captures
  6.  What is the site's name, load balancer name?
    1. specific domain
    2. path
    3. URI
  7. When did the problem start (date/time/time zone/region) ?
    1. Frequency: (e.g., Always, Intermittent, Random)
    2. Include any specific timestamps (date/time/time zone)
  8. Steps to reproduce the issue?
  9. Expected behavior (tell us what should happen)
  10. Is this a new implementation or was it working before? if so, what has been modified?
  11. What troubleshooting or testing has been done? 
  12. What browser are you using?

Phone Support

  • When calling, select Option 4 for Distributed Cloud and then:
    • Option 1 for Support.
    • If you require to speak to a Duty Manager please inform the agent who answers your call.
