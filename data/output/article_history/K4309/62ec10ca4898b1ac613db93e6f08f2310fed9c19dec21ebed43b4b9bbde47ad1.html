Policy Information

The F5 hardware product lifecycle support policy defines the stages of the hardware product lifecycle from initial release through retirement. This policy describes the levels of support available for customers with a purchased Support Agreement. This policy intends to provide consistent documentation that aids customers in planning and preparing for their organization's support needs.

Hardware product support milestones

  1. Introduction: limited and general availability
  2. End of Sale (EoS)
  3. End of New Software Support (EoNSS)
  4. End of Software Support (EoSS)
  5. End of Support Contract Renewal (EoSCR)
  6. End of RMA (EoRMA)
  7. End of Technical Support (EoTS)
  8. End of Life (EoL)

For a list of abbreviations used in the lifecycle policies, including detailed definitions, refer to K8986: F5 product support policies.

F5 hardware products progress through two distinct lifecycle phases: Regular Support and Extended Support
The following diagram shows where the product milestones occur.

Figure: Typical hardware product lifecycle

Regular Support Phase

The Regular Support phase for customers begins upon accepting their shipment and continues until the EoSS date. During this phase, customers benefit from a range of services, including repair and maintenance of hardware, software, and firmware, troubleshooting support, and educational resources, per their support contract's terms and the support tier selected. The EoSS date marks the end of Regular Support for hardware products and the start of Extended Support.

Upgrade Advisory: To ensure their products stay updated and secure, customers should discuss product upgrade options with either their F5 representative or an F5 Partner before the Regular Support phase ends. For questions or help in finding an F5 representative or Partner, contact F5 Sales.

Extended Support Phase

The Extended Support Phase begins immediately after the EoSS date expires and ends with the End of Technical Support (EoTS) date.

Important: After the End of Software Support (EoSS), F5 requires hardware products to be operating a supported software version as specified in F5 hardware/software compatibility matrix (K9476) or F5OS hardware/software support matrix (K86001294) to qualify for Extended Support.

During the Extended Support phase, F5 is committed to offering the following services for the hardware product:

  • F5 will actively attempt to answer technical questions, troubleshoot, and isolate problems related to the product.
  • F5 will provide Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) support, as outlined in the support contract and subject to the specific support tier.
  • F5 will supply an abbreviated Failure Analysis (FA) Report whenever F5 deems it necessary.

During the Extended Support phase, it is important to note that F5 will NOT offer the following services for the specified hardware product:

  • F5 will not provide repair or maintenance services for software or firmware defects. This includes not providing patches for Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures (CVE), security updates, software fixes (hotfixes), or related educational services.
  • F5 will not supply a detailed and comprehensive Failure Analysis (FA) Report.
  • F5 will not escalate issues to Engineering Services or Product Development teams.

Important: Hardware and software have independent lifecycles. When a hardware product reaches its End of New Software Support (EoNSS) date, F5 ceases all software development and testing for that product in future software releases. Consequently, hardware that is not running a supported software version listed in K9476: The F5 hardware/software compatibility matrix or K86001294: F5OS hardware/software support matrix will not be supported on that product.

For example, if the End of Software Support (EoSS) for a specific hardware product is set for March 31, 2024, and the End of Software Development (EoSD) or End of Technical Support (EoTS) for software used by this hardware is scheduled for May 1, 2025, then the hardware's EoSS date takes precedence. After March 31, 2024, F5 will support this hardware only if a supported software version listed in K9476: The F5 hardware/software compatibility matrix or K86001294: F5OS hardware/software support matrix is available and in use. If no such software version is listed for this hardware, you must refresh to a new hardware platform.

F5 hardware products, unless expressly stated otherwise by F5, default End of Software Support (EoSS) date is set for three years after the product End of Sale (EoS) date. Additionally, four years after the EoSS date, the product reaches its default End of Technical Support (EoTS) and End of Return Material Authorization (EoRMA) dates. Customers have the option to purchase one additional year of extended support, which must be done in the final year of their existing maintenance agreement before the EoTS date.

For instance, if a customer's maintenance agreement expires on March 31, 2024, and the EoTS date is May 1, 2024, they can extend their maintenance (including EoRMA) until March 31, 2025, by paying for an additional full year. However, no new maintenance agreements can be initiated after May 1, 2024.

F5 reserves the right to change its Product Lifecycle Support Policy at its sole discretion, without notice.

BIG-IP Hardware Product Variant Key

BIG-IP hardware product model numbers that contain a letter following the number indicate special or unique options available to the hardware products. These can be observed either via SNMP polling of the hardware product MIB, viewing the hardware product information reported by iHealth, or using the TMOS Shell (tmsh) command to request the hardware product information.

The following letters following a hardware product number (example XXXXv or iXXXX-F) indicate the following:

DS - Indicates Turbo SSL Hardware Product

DF - Indicates FIPS 140-2 Level Hardware Product

N - Indicates NEBS Level 3 Compliance Hardware Product

s - Indicates the following:

  • No Hardware Compression Available
  • No FIPS Option Available
  • No Virtualization Available

v - Indicates the following:

  • Hardware Compression Included
  • FIPS Option Is Available
  • Virtualization Available (Allows use of vCMP Guests)
