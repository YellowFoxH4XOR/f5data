K000163656: Cannot View Subscriptions after being granted Case Manager role in Subscription Account

Type
Support Solution
Published
2026-10-20
Updated
2026-10-20
Applies to
  • Support Portal
Source

Description

You have already been granted "Case Manager" role in MyF5 portal but cant view the Subscriptions in that Account.

Environment

  • MyF5

  • Case Manager Role
  • Subscription Account

Cause

This is due to the Account being a Subscription based account that needs "View Subscription" permissions which is absent from "Case Manager" role.
Refer to K000136095: About MyF5 account user roles, permissions and statuses

Answer/Recommended Actions

For Users with "Admin" role:
Add "Viewer" role in addition to "Case Manager" role when doing "Add Relationship" in the Account with Subscriptions.

Refer to Change MyF5 account user roles in K000089214: Add, change, or remove MyF5 account users


To find the "Admin" of an Account in MyF5 portal, you may refer to this:

K000130613: How to find the Account Admin from MyF5 Portal


If there is no Admin found in the Subscription based account, contact F5 Support or your F5 Account Manager for assistance.
