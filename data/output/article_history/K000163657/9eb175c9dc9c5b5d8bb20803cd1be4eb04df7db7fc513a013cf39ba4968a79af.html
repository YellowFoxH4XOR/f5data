K000163657: UCS archive size differences after migrating BIG-IP tenants from iSeries to rSeries

Description

UCS backups created on migrated BIG-IP rSeries tenants can be larger than corresponding backups from iSeries systems. In the comparison, rSeries tenant archives were approximately 1.44 MB larger on the active device and 1.47 MB larger on the standby device. The difference did not indicate a configuration discrepancy or migration problem.

Environment

  • BIG-IP iseries

  • BIG-IP rSeries tenant environment

  • UCS backups compared before and after migration from iSeries systems

Cause

Differences in archive compression behavior between iSeries systems and rSeries tenants, combined with growth in configuration-history data such as config/.diffVersions/, can produce different archive sizes. The rSeries tenant archives matched locally generated GNU gzip-1 output byte-for-byte, while the iSeries archives used different compression settings and were slightly smaller(e.g. around 1M-2M) than the corresponding gzip-1 output.

Answer/Recommended Actions

  • No corrective action is required solely because UCS archive sizes differ between iSeries systems and migrated rSeries tenants.

  • UCS archives from rSeries tenants can be used for backup and restore operations.

  • When validating UCS files across platforms, compare extracted configuration and objects instead of relying only on total archive size or byte-for-byte archive equality.

  • Continue normal backup validation procedures for migrated tenants.

Additional Information

The config/.diffVersions/ member payload increase doesn't mainly contribute to the1M-2M difference on ucs size. The configuration content remained operationally equivalent.
