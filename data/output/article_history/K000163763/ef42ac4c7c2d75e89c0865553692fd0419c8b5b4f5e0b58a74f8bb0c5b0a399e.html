Source

Description

rSeries management port flapped repeatedly, with many errors similar to the following observed in /var/log/messages:

igb 0000:02:00.0: exceed max 2 second
igb 0000:02:00.0 mgmt: igb: mgmt NIC Link is Down
igb 0000:02:00.0 mgmt: igb: mgmt NIC Link is Up 1000 Mbps Full Duplex, Flow Control: RX

Environment

  • rSeries
  • Management port

Cause

This is typically indicative of a hardware issue with the cable or port.

Recommended Actions

As an initial troubleshooting step, replace the cable and use a different switch port.

If the problem persists, contact F5 Support for further troubleshooting.
