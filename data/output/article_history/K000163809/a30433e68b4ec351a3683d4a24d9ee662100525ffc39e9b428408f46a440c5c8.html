K000163809: F5 Insight HA Upgrade Job Fails When Automatic Fallback and Always Failback to First Device Are Both Enabled

2026-11-24
Applies to

Source

Description

During a TMOS upgrade of a BIG-IP HA pair with F5 Insight 1.2.2, the Software Installation job can end with Status: Failed even though the upgrade completes and the HA pair operates correctly.


The job log reports:


ha: original-active did not rejoin the cluster as a stable standby within 10m0s (last role "ACTIVE", streak 0/3, err )


The failure occurs when both of the following are enabled:


  • F5 Insight Software Installation job option: Automatic fallback after both instance in HA Pair upgraded
  • BIG-IP traffic-group setting: Always Failback to First Device if it is Available


Environment

  • F5 Insight version 1.2.2
  • BIG-IP HA pair (Active/Standby)
  • TMOS upgrade from 17.1.3 to 17.1.3.4
  • BIG-IP traffic-group setting: "Always Failback to First Device if it is Available" enabled
  • F5 Insight job setting: "Automatic fallback after both instance in HA Pair upgraded" enabled

Cause

With "Always Failback to First Device if it is Available" enabled, the first device is forced back to the Active role. F5 Insight's automatic fallback step expects the original-active device to rejoin as a stable standby. These two behaviors conflict, so the original-active device never reaches a stable standby state, the 10-minute wait times out, and the job is marked Failed.

This issue is tracked under internal BUGID

 

Answer/Recommended Actions

Before running the F5 Insight upgrade job, check the traffic-group setting on the BIG-IP HA pair and use one of the following configurations:


Option 1: Disable "Always Failback to First Device if it is Available" on the BIG-IP traffic-group, and keep "Automatic fallback after both instance in HA Pair upgraded" enabled in the F5 Insight job.

Option 2: Enable "Always Failback to First Device if it is Available" on the BIG-IP traffic-group, and disable "Automatic fallback after both instance in HA Pair upgraded" in the F5 Insight job.

After the job runs, confirm that the job status is Completed and verify the HA pair state.

 

Additional Information

  • he upgrade itself completes and the HA pair works correctly. The failure is limited to the job status.
  • Testing showed the job succeeds only when exactly one of the two settings is enabled.
  • No fix is currently available. Until one is released, check these settings before each upgrade job.
